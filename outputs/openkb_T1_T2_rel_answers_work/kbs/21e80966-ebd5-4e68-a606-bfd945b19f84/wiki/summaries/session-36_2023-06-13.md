## Summary of Session 36 (2023‑06‑13)

This conversation provides practical guidance for preserving a steady **daily prayer routine** during travel, covering:

- **Anchoring prayer times** to personal routines (e.g., right after waking or before sleep) to maintain consistency regardless of location. [[concepts/daily-prayer-routine]]
- **Handling overlapping prayer windows** and time‑zone changes by prioritising rhythm over exact local timings, and using flexible timing after flights or at gates. [[concepts/timing-suggestions]]
- **Respectful airport prayer**: find a quiet, low‑traffic spot (empty gate, corner, seat) without blocking walkways; discretion is key. (airport prayer etiquette)
- **Qibla direction in hotels**: use a compass or dedicated Qibla app, verify near a window to reduce building interference; aim for a “good enough” orientation. [[concepts/qibla-direction]]
- **Syncing prayer times**: manually refresh location in the prayer‑time app after landing; verify against local mosque schedules if needed; ensure phone’s automatic time‑zone setting is enabled. [[concepts/prayer-time-synchronization]]
- **App recommendations**: prefer apps that auto‑update location, allow manual refresh, support multiple calculation methods, and provide precise location access. Enable precise location in phone settings for faster Qibla detection. [[concepts/prayer-apps]]
