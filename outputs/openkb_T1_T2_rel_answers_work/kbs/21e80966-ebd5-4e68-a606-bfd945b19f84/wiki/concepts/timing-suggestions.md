# Timing Suggestions

**Definition**: Providing specific time windows in communication to make rescheduling clear, convenient, and actionable.

## Overview
Timing suggestions are a pragmatic element of [[concepts/message-drafting]] that give recipients exact options rather than vague statements. In the context of the **session‑14_2020‑06‑13** conversation, the assistant generated multiple precise slots (e.g., 8:00 AM, 8:15 AM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM) to accommodate the user's conflict with [[entities/choir]] practice at the [[entities/parish]] while respecting the group's typical [[entities/sunday-morning]] plans.

## Key Aspects
- **Specificity** – Rather than saying “earlier” or “later,” the messages name exact start times, reducing back‑and‑forth.
- **Range Offering** – Presenting both a morning window (8:00 – 8:15) and an afternoon alternative (2:00 or 3:00) gives flexibility and shows consideration for different schedules.
- **Contextual Relevance** – The chosen times align with the user’s constraint of leaving by about 9:15 AM for choir duties, ensuring the suggestion is realistic.
- **Tone Integration** – When combined with a light, upbeat tone ([[concepts/humor-in-communication]]), timing suggestions keep the message friendly while remaining clear.

## Benefits
1. **Clarity** – Recipients can quickly decide which slot works for them.
2. **Efficiency** – Fewer follow‑up messages are needed, streamlining coordination.
3. **Respect for Constraints** – Demonstrates awareness of the sender’s schedule (choir practice) and the group’s typical Sunday routines.

## Example from Source
The final, concise draft used in the conversation reads:
> “Hey friends! My Sunday morning got taken over by choir practice, so I can only hang till about 9:15. If you’re up for meeting at 8:00 or 8:15, I’m in. And if that’s way too early for normal humans, I can do 2:00 or 3:00 in the afternoon instead. I’ll show up much more caffeinated by then.”

This snippet illustrates how timing suggestions are woven into a casual, friendly message.
