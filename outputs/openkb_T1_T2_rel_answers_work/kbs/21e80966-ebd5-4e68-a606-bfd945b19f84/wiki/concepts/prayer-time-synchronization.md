# Prayer Time Synchronization

**Definition**: The process of keeping daily prayer times accurate when traveling across time zones, ensuring that notifications, apps, and manual checks stay in sync with local schedules.

## Key Elements
- **Automatic time‑zone adjustment** – Enable the phone’s “automatic time zone” setting so the device updates immediately after landing.
- **Manual location refresh** – Open the prayer‑time app and refresh the location manually; this resolves delays in GPS or network‑based updates.
- **Cross‑checking** – If the app’s times seem off, compare them with a local mosque’s published schedule or a quick internet search for the city’s timetable.
- **Flexible rhythm** – Prioritise a steady prayer rhythm over exact local timing; pray as soon as practicable after a flight or when settled at a gate.
- **App selection** – Choose apps that auto‑update location, allow forced refresh, and support multiple calculation methods, as highlighted in [[concepts/prayer-apps]].

## Practical Tips from [[summaries/session-36_2023-06-13]]
- After landing, give the phone a moment to settle, then manually sync the prayer‑time app.
- Keep phone’s time‑zone mode set to **automatic** to avoid manual adjustments.
- Use precise location permissions to improve Qibla detection and timing accuracy.
- Verify occasional discrepancies against a local mosque’s website.
