# Daily Prayer Routine

A **daily prayer routine** is a structured set of practices that help an individual maintain regular prayer observances throughout the day, regardless of location or circumstance. The concept emphasizes consistency, flexibility, and respectful adaptation to varied environments.

## Core Elements

1. **Anchor Points** – Tie prayer times to personal habits that stay constant (e.g., immediately after waking, before sleep, or after meals). This creates a reliable cue that works in any setting.
2. **Flexible Timing** – When crossing time zones or encountering overlapping prayer windows, prioritize rhythm over exact local timing. Perform the prayer soon after landing or once settled, accepting minor adjustments.
3. **Respectful Public Prayer** – In places like airports, choose quiet, low‑traffic spots (empty gate, corner, seat) and avoid blocking walkways. Discreet, brief prayers are generally acceptable.
4. **Qibla Orientation** – Use a compass or dedicated Qibla app, preferably near a window to reduce building interference. Aim for a "good enough" direction rather than perfection.
5. **Technology Aid** – Leverage prayer‑time apps that auto‑update location, allow manual refresh, and support multiple calculation methods. Enable precise location permissions for faster Qibla detection.
