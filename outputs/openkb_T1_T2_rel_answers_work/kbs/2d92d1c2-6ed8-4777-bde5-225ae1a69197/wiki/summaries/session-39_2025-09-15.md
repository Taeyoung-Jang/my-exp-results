# Summary of session-39_2025-09-15

**Main Idea**: A simple, realistic morning fuel plan for an active individual, split into an early breakfast and a pre‑workout “booster,” with variations for strength vs. cardio and non‑perishable options.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/macro_balance]]** – Combine protein, slow‑digesting carbs, and healthy fat for steady energy.
- **Hydration** – Start with water before any food or coffee.
- **Early breakfast** – Light meal at 6–7 a.m. (e.g., yogurt + fruit, eggs + toast, smoothie with protein, oatmeal + nut butter).
- **[[concepts/pre_workout_timing]]** – Quick carb‑focused snack 30–60 min before a 10 a.m. workout (≈9:15–9:30).
- **Quick carbs** – Banana, half a granola bar, dry cereal, crackers + peanut butter.
- **Strength vs. cardio fueling** – Slightly more protein/fat on strength days; more carbs on cardio days.
- **[[concepts/non_perishable_fuel]]** – Shelf‑stable protein shake, nut‑butter packets, oat bars, applesauce packets, whole‑grain bread.
- **Energy stability** – Goal is to avoid mid‑morning crash and stay light for the workout.

## Practical Weekly Template
### Early Breakfast (choose 2–3 options, rotate weekly)
1. Small bowl of Greek yogurt with berries.
2. Smoothie: fruit + scoop protein powder + a splash of milk or plant‑based milk.
3. Oatmeal topped with a spoonful of almond butter.
4. (Non‑refrigerated) Shelf‑stable protein shake + an apple.
5. (Non‑refrigerated) Whole‑grain bread with a packet of nut butter.

### Pre‑Workout Snack (choose 2–3 options, 30–60 min before workout)
1. Banana.
2. Half a granola bar.
3. Handful of dry cereal.
4. (Non‑perishable) Small packet of applesauce.
5. (Non‑perishable) Nut‑butter squeeze pack + a few crackers.
6. (Non‑perishable) Oat bar.

### Adjustments by Workout Type
- **Strength training**: Favor snacks that include a bit of protein/fat (e.g., banana + peanut butter, granola bar with nuts).
- **Cardio**: Opt for pure carbs for fast energy (e.g., banana alone, dry cereal).

## Why It Works
- **Steady baseline** from the early breakfast provides lasting fuel without heaviness.
- **Quick carb boost** right before exercise supplies readily available glucose, improving performance.
- **Flexibility** with non‑perishable items makes the plan travel‑friendly and low‑prep.
- **Macro tweaking** (more carbs vs. protein) aligns with the metabolic demands of cardio vs. strength.
