# Non‑Perishable Fuel Options

**Definition**: Shelf‑stable foods and snacks that require no refrigeration or cooking, designed to deliver a quick mix of carbohydrates, protein, and healthy fats for pre‑workout energy.

## Why Use Non‑Perishable Fuel?
- **Convenient**: Easy to pack for travel, busy mornings, or when kitchen access is limited.
- **Quick Energy**: Primarily carb‑focused items raise blood glucose fast, supporting both cardio and strength sessions.
- **Balanced Nutrition**: When paired with a small amount of protein or fat, they help avoid an energy crash.

## Core Options Highlighted in [[summaries/session-39_2025-09-15]]
| Category | Example | Primary Macro | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Protein‑Focused** | Shelf‑stable protein shake + fruit | Protein + carbs | Early breakfast (6–7 a.m.) for steady energy [[concepts/macro_balance]] |
| **Carb‑Focused** | Banana, granola bar half, dry cereal | Carbs | Pre‑workout snack (9:15–9:30) [[concepts/pre_workout_timing]] |
| **Hybrid Packets** | Nut‑butter squeeze pack + crackers | Fat + carbs | Pre‑workout boost, especially on strength days |
| **Pure Carb Packs** | Applesauce packet, oat bar | Carbs | Rapid fuel before cardio sessions |

## How to Choose the Right Option
1. **Timing**: Use a more carbohydrate‑dense snack 30–60 minutes before the workout for fast energy.
2. **Workout Type**:
   - **Strength**: Add a small source of protein or fat (e.g., nut‑butter pack) to support muscle repair.
   - **Cardio**: Favor pure carbs for quick energy.
3. **Portability Needs**: Opt for sealed packets or bars that stay fresh without refrigeration.

## Integrating with the Overall Morning Fuel Plan
- Start the day with water and a minimal, non‑perishable protein source if refrigeration isn’t available.
- Follow with a quick carb‑focused snack right before the 10 a.m. workout to avoid feeling heavy while still delivering energy.
