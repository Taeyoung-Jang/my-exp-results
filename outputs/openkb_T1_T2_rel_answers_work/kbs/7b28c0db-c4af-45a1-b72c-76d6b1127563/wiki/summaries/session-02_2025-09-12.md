## Summary of session-02_2025-09-12

**Date:** 2025-09-12 10:30  

The user requested help crafting short, appreciative yet clear declines for social invitations while focusing on a backyard renovation. Several message variants were produced, covering:

- **Format differences** – text‑message style, email style, group‑chat reply, and ultra‑short “can’t this month” notes.  
- **Tone adjustments** – brief & low‑detail, genuinely bummed, casual, and warmer wording.  
- **Timing cues** – mentions of a specific reconnection window such as “mid‑next month” or “once the reno’s done.”  
- **Variations** – super‑short one‑liner, slightly longer warm text, and a casual group‑chat friendly reply.

- [[concepts/friendly-decline-messages]] – crafting polite, appreciative declines.
