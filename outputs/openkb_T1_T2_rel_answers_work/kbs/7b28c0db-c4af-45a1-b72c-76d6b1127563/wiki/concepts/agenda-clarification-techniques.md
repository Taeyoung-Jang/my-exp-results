# Agenda Clarification Techniques

**Definition**: A set of low‑key, polite prompts used before or during a meeting to surface the main objective or expected outcome, helping participants capture concise action items.

## Why it matters
When meetings lack a clear agenda, it’s hard to know *what one thing to walk away with*—a problem highlighted in [[summaries/session-32_2025-11-12]]. Clear clarification reduces cognitive overload, improves the quality of [[concepts/daily-routine-structure]] note‑taking, and prevents wasted micro‑gaps.

## Core approaches
| Timing | Sample phrasing | Goal |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Pre‑meeting DM** (sent a few minutes before the call) | "Since I’m still getting oriented, is there anything specific you’d like me to listen for or contribute?" | Sets expectations without sounding demanding. |
| **Start‑of‑meeting chat** (if no intro) | "Quick check – what’s the key thing I should focus on here?" | Provides an immediate anchor for note‑taking. |
| **Mid‑meeting gentle probe** | "Just to make sure I’m following, is there a main takeaway I should pay attention to?" | Re‑aligns focus without interrupting flow. |

## Tone considerations
Use a friendly, supportive tone to avoid appearing pushy. See [[concepts/communication-tone-variation]] for examples of how slight wording changes affect perception.

## Integration with daily workflow
- Add the chosen prompt to your **Daily Note** template (see [[summaries/session-32_2025-11-12]]). 
- After each meeting, capture the response in the *What happened* and *What I owe* sections, enabling quick 60‑second summaries.
- Treat the clarification step as a **micro‑reset** rather than a new task, aligning with [[concepts/micro-gap-utilization]].

## Benefits
1. **Clear action items** – knowing the meeting’s focus makes the “Things I Owe” list more accurate.
2. **Reduced context‑switch cost** – you spend less mental energy figuring out the purpose during the call.
3. **Improved confidence** – as a new hire, asking these questions signals engagement without overstepping.
