## Zoom (product)

Zoom is a cloud‑based video‑conferencing platform that enables real‑time virtual meetings, webinars, and collaboration.

### Relevance in session‑32
In the conversation documented in [[summaries/session-32_2025-11-12]], the user’s onboarding schedule is filled with back‑to‑back Zoom meetings. The challenges discussed—such as feeling that the day “vanishes,” managing 5‑10 minute gaps, and clarifying meeting goals—are all framed around the experience of using Zoom for frequent calls.

### Key points from the document
- The user frequently checks the watch during Zoom calls, indicating a need for better time awareness.
- Recommendations include creating a **daily routine** with a clear priority and using the 60‑second post‑meeting note technique, which can be applied within Zoom sessions. [[concepts/daily-routine-structure]]
- Small gaps between Zoom meetings are suggested to be used for **micro‑gap utilization**—either a quick micro‑win (e.g., a bite‑size onboarding module) or a micro‑reset (stretch, water). [[concepts/micro-gap-utilization]]
- To compensate for meetings without clear agendas, low‑key prompting techniques are offered to clarify the meeting’s focus, aligning with **agenda clarification techniques**. [[concepts/agenda-clarification-techniques]]
