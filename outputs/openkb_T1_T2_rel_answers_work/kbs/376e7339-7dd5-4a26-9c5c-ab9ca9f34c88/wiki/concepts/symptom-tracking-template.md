# Symptom Tracking Template

A **Symptom Tracking Template** is a structured, one‑page daily log designed to capture the most relevant observations for patients adjusting levothyroxine after [[entities/thyroid-surgery]]. It helps patients and clinicians identify patterns, evaluate medication timing, and decide when to order labs.

---

## Core Elements of the Template
| Field | What to Record | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Date** | Calendar date | Provides chronological context |
| **Energy level** | low / normal / high | Direct indicator of thyroid hormone effect |
| **Temperature sensitivity** | cold / warm / normal | Thyroid hormones regulate basal metabolism |
| **Mood notes** | brief description | Mood swings often reflect hormone fluctuations |
| **Sleep** | hours + quality rating | Sleep quality interacts with energy and hormone levels |
| **Resting heart rate (morning)** | beats per minute | Simple proxy for basal metabolic rate |
| **Levothyroxine dose time** | HH:MM | Critical for interpreting labs and absorption issues |
| **Time of first food/coffee after dose** | HH:MM | Links to [[concepts/levothyroxine-absorption]] and [[concepts/supplement-interference]] |
| **Additional symptoms** | palpitations, shakiness, fatigue swings, etc. | Captures atypical or transient issues |
| **Supplements/meds taken today** | biotin, iron, calcium, PPIs, etc. | Highlights potential absorption blockers |

## Recommended Tracking Schedule
- **Duration**: Capture data for **2–3 weeks** before an appointment to show patterns, and **4–6 weeks** after any change in dose or timing to allow hormone levels to stabilise.
- **Consistency**: Record the same fields each day; enter the resting heart rate **immediately upon waking** (or at a consistent time each morning) for reliability.

## How to Use the Template
1. **Copy the plain‑text version** into a notebook, spreadsheet, or digital note app.
2. **Fill it out each morning** before taking food or coffee.
3. **Review weekly** to note trends (e.g., days when heart rate spikes coincide with late medication intake).
4. **Bring the compiled log** (2–3 weeks) to your endocrinology visit.

## Connections to Other Concepts
- The timing of food, coffee, and supplements ties directly to [[concepts/levothyroxine-absorption]] and [[concepts/supplement-interference]].
- Recording subjective energy and mood forms a form of [[concepts/qualitative-self-assessment]], complementing objective lab values.
- The template can be combined with other health metrics (e.g., blood pressure) for a holistic view, linking to [[concepts/low-pressure-tracking]] when relevant.
