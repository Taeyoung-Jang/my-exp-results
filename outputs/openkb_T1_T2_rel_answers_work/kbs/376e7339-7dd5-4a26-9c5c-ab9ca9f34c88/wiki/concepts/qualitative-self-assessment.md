# Qualitative Self-Assessment

**Definition**: Qualitative Self-Assessment is a low‑effort, feeling‑based approach to monitoring fitness, nutrition, and health progress by using descriptive, non‑numeric prompts rather than precise measurements.

## Core Principles
- **Focus on perception** – Emphasize how the body feels (e.g., workout ease, energy, sleep quality, temperature tolerance) instead of counting calories or weighing oneself.
- **Simple rating scales** – Use quick “low/medium/high” or “better/same/worse” options to capture trends without detailed logging.
- **Minimal frequency** – Check‑ins are recommended every one to two weeks to keep the practice sustainable.
- **One open‑ended reflection** – A single brief note allows for deeper insight while keeping the process light.

## Example Templates
### General Fitness / Nutrition (from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]])
```
Strength or workout ease: low / medium / high
Daily energy levels: low / medium / high
Sleep quality: better / same / worse
Clothes comfort: better / same / worse
Recovery after activity: faster / same / slower
Mood stability: better / same / worse
One quick note about anything that stood out these past two weeks: ________________
```

### Thyroid Medication & Symptom Tracking (from [[summaries/session-39_2024-12-16]])
```
Date:
Energy level (low / normal / high):
Temperature sensitivity (cold / warm / normal):
Mood notes:
Sleep last night (hours, quality):
Resting heart rate (morning):
Levothyroxine dose time:
Time of first food/coffee after dose:
Any notable symptoms (palpitations, shakiness, fatigue swings, etc.):
Supplements/meds taken today (biotin, iron, calcium, PPIs, etc.):
```
This template captures the same qualitative spirit while adding medication timing and supplement awareness, useful for anyone adjusting levothyroxine dosage.

## Relationships to Other Concepts
- Part of a broader [[concepts/low-pressure-tracking]] strategy that avoids obsessive number‑crunching.
- Can be used alongside a structured [[concepts/symptom-tracking-template]] to provide a richer, yet still low‑effort, picture of health changes.
- Functions as a form of non‑scale progress monitoring, highlighting improvements that are not reflected on a scale.

## Benefits
- Reduces mental load and anxiety associated with constant tracking.
- Encourages sustainable habit formation by keeping the routine feel‑based and flexible.
- Provides actionable insight that can be easily reviewed over time, including for specific contexts such as thyroid hormone management.
