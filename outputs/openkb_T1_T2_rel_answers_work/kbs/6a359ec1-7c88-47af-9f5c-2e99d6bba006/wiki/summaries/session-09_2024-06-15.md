# Summary of session-09_2024-06-15

- **Goal:** Provide practical, low‑effort changes for a more environmentally friendly daily routine focused on food and shopping, with a strict avoidance of disposable packaging.
- **Key Strategies:**
  - Prioritize **reusable containers**, reusable bags, and **zero‑waste food storage** methods (e.g., towel‑lined containers, freezing leftovers). [[concepts/zero-waste-food-storage]]
  - When reusable containers aren’t allowed, use a **Plan B** that selects items naturally packaged with minimal waste and request the lightest possible packaging.  
- **High‑impact swaps for takeout:**
  1. Bring your own container and ask politely.
  2. If refused, request the *simplest* paper or foil wrap. 
  3. Choose dishes that already come in minimal packaging (e.g., paper‑wrapped sandwiches, foil‑wrapped burritos, single‑piece sushi rolls).  
- **Low‑waste grocery tips:** [[concepts/low-waste-grocery-practices]]
  - Buy loose produce and place directly into a cloth bag or basket.
  - Use personal containers at deli, bakery, or cheese counters.
  - Prefer glass or metal packaging over plastic when reusable options aren’t available.  
- **Ready‑made “default orders” & scripts:**
  1. **Coffee shop:** Order a plain brewed coffee/espresso. Script – “If my cup can’t be used, could you give it to me in the simplest paper cup you have?”
  2. **Sandwich shop:** Order a baguette/sub wrapped only in paper. Script – “If you can’t wrap it in my container, could you use just the paper wrap without any extras?”
  3. **Sushi:** Request a roll cut to go, ideally paper‑wrapped. Script – “If outside containers aren’t allowed, could you pack the roll in your smallest, least‑packaging option?”
  4. **Thai/Chinese:** Choose a stir‑fry over rice in a single container. Script – “If you can’t use my container, could you pack it in one container with as little extra packaging as possible?”
  5. **Grocery deli:** Request a single block of cheese or roasted veg wrapped in one layer of deli paper. Script – “If my container isn’t allowed, could you use just one layer of your thinnest paper wrap?”
- **Concept synthesis:** The document introduces a repeatable framework—**reusable container ordering** combined with clear, polite phrasing—to navigate hygiene restrictions while still achieving a low‑waste outcome. [[concepts/reusable-container-ordering]]
