# Low-waste Grocery Practices

Low-waste grocery practices are a set of simple, high‑impact actions that let you shop while keeping disposable packaging to a minimum. They complement broader zero‑waste habits such as [[concepts/zero-waste-food-storage]] and reusable‑container ordering.

## Key Tactics (from [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]])
- **Buy loose produce** and place it directly into a cloth or reusable bag, avoiding pre‑packaged plastic.
- **Use personal containers** at the deli, bakery, and cheese counters; ask staff to place items in your container.
- **Choose glass or metal packaging** over plastic when a reusable option isn’t available – these materials are recyclable and often reusable.
- **Request the lightest possible wrap** when outside containers are prohibited. Sample phrasing: “If my container isn’t allowed, could you use just one layer of your thinnest paper wrap?”
- **Select items that naturally have minimal packaging**, such as a single block of cheese, roasted vegetables, or bulk nuts, which typically receive only a single sheet of deli paper.

## Practical Script Library
| Setting | Example Order | Minimal‑Packaging Request |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------|
| Grocery deli | Block of cheese or roasted veg | “If my container isn’t allowed, could you use just one layer of your thinnest paper wrap?” |
| Produce aisle | Loose fruits/vegetables | “Can I put these directly into my reusable bag?” |
| Bulk foods | Nuts, grains | “I’ll store these in my own container, please.” |

## Benefits
- **Reduces single‑use plastic** and overall waste volume.
- **Saves money** by avoiding packaged premium items.
- **Simplifies waste management** at home, as fewer types of material need to be sorted.
