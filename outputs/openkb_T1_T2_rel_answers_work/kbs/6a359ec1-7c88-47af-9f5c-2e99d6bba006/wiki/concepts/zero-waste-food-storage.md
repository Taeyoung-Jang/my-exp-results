# Zero-waste Food Storage

Zero-waste food storage refers to simple, reusable methods for keeping food fresh longer without adding disposable packaging. The approach emphasizes reusing containers you already own and choosing storage techniques that reduce spoilage, thereby cutting down on food waste.

## Key Practices from [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]]
- **Reusable containers:** Use glass jars, stainless‑steel boxes, or BPA‑free plastic containers instead of single‑use bags.
- **Towel‑lined storage:** Place a dry paper towel or cloth inside a container to absorb excess moisture and keep greens crisp.
- **Freezing leftovers:** Portion and freeze meals promptly to prevent spoilage; label containers with dates.
- **Batch prep with minimal packaging:** Prepare meals in bulk using reusable trays, then portion into individual containers for easy reheating.

## Benefits
- Extends shelf life of produce and prepared foods.
- Reduces the need for disposable plastic wrap, zip‑top bags, and foil.
- Saves money by decreasing food that is thrown away.
