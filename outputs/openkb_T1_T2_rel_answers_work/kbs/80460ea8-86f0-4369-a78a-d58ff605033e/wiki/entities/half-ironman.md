## Half Ironman

The **Half Ironman** (also called Ironman 70.3) is a long‑distance triathlon consisting of roughly a 1.2 mile swim, a 56 mile bike leg, and a 13.1 mile run.  Athletes train for months to balance endurance across the three disciplines while preserving performance and avoiding burnout.

### Key Takeaways for Half Ironman Prep
1. **Periodize** the week: weekday rides for steady volume, weekend run for long‑duration endurance.
2. **Cluster** lower‑body strength early (Monday) to keep the legs fresh for the Saturday run.
3. Use **recovery feedback** to adjust load dynamically.
4. Keep strength work at a moderate **effort metric** (RPE ≈ 7) to balance stimulus and fatigue.

This structured approach helps athletes train efficiently for the **Half Ironman** while minimizing risk of overtraining.
