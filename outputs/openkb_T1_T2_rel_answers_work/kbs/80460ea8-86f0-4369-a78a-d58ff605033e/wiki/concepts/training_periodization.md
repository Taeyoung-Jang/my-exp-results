# Training Periodization

**Definition**: Training periodization is the strategic organization of training into distinct phases or cycles, each with specific objectives, to optimize performance while managing fatigue and recovery.

---

## Overview
Periodization structures the overall training load across time, alternating periods of higher intensity or volume with lighter phases. This approach helps athletes avoid overtraining, supports progressive adaptation, and aligns training with competition schedules.

Key components include:
- **Stress Clustering** – grouping similar stressors (e.g., leg‑intensive work) within a short window to contain fatigue [[concepts/stress_clustering]].
- **Recovery Feedback Loops** – using perceived effort or performance cues to adjust load in subsequent weeks [[concepts/recovery_feedback_loops]].
- **Effort Metrics** – monitoring RPE, heart‑rate, or power to gauge intensity and ensure appropriate progression [[concepts/effort_metrics]].

---

## Application in [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-12]]
The session‑38 plan exemplifies a micro‑periodization strategy for a half‑Ironman athlete:

1. **Weekday “steady rhythm”** – short, predictable sessions (bike rides, light strength) to maintain consistency without excessive fatigue.
2. **Weekend “flex space”** – longer endurance or heavier strength work when time and mental bandwidth are greater.
3. **Stress Clustering** – the primary lower‑body lift and heavy bike ride are placed on Monday, followed by an easy spin on Tuesday, keeping leg stress together early in the week.
4. **Recovery Feedback Loop** – a simple rule to dial back weight if the Tuesday spin feels “dead” within the first ten minutes, providing an immediate cue to adjust the next week’s load.
5. **Effort Metric (RPE)** – the lift is prescribed at an RPE of ~7/10, balancing stimulus and recovery.

The weekly layout (Mon‑Sun) demonstrates a short‑term periodization cycle that can be repeated or adjusted as the race approaches.

## Practical Example
| Day | Activity | Load Focus |
|-----|----------|------------|
| **Mon** | Bike‑trainer ride + lower‑body lift (barbell back squat, Bulgarian split squat, hip thrust) | High leg stress, moderate RPE (~7) |
| **Tue** | Easy spin (recovery) | Low intensity |
| **Wed** | Bike‑trainer ride + core/upper‑body work (dumbbells, cable‑machine) | Light supplemental work |
| **Thu** | Steady bike‑trainer ride | Moderate endurance |
| **Fri** | Full recovery (rest or light movement) | Rest |
| **Sat** | Long run (half‑Ironman specific) | High endurance |
| **Sun** | Optional easy spin or rest | Active recovery |
