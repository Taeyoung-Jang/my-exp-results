## Weekly Structure
- **Weekdays** act as a “steady rhythm” – short, predictable sessions to avoid burnout.  
- **Weekends** are the “flex space” – longer endurance or heavier strength sessions.  
- Suggested layout:  
  - **Mon**: [[entities/bike-trainer]] ride + primary lower‑body lift  
  - **Tue**: Easy spin/recovery  
  - **Wed**: [[entities/bike-trainer]] ride + lighter core/upper‑body work  
  - **Thu**: Steady [[entities/bike-trainer]] ride  
  - **Fri**: Full recovery (rest or light movement)  
  - **Sat**: Long run (weekend endurance)  
  - **Sun**: Optional easy spin or rest  
- Emphasis on **[[concepts/stress_clustering]]** – grouping leg‑intensive work early to protect the weekend run.

## Monday Lower‑Body Lift (45–60 min)
1. **Warm‑up** – Barbell back squat: 2 sets (8 reps light, 5 reps moderate).  
2. **Work sets** – Barbell back squat: 3 × 5‑6 reps, **RPE ≈ 7** (**[[concepts/effort_metrics]]**), 2 min rest.  
3. **Single‑leg** – Bulgarian split squat (with [[entities/dumbbells]]): 2‑3 × 6‑8 reps/leg, 1 min rest.  
4. **Posterior chain** – Barbell hip thrust (using [[entities/barbell]]): 2 × 8‑10 reps, 1 min rest.  
5. **Stability / activation** – Cable lateral hip pull or light step‑up (using [[entities/cable-machine]]): 1‑2 × 8‑10 reps, minimal rest.  

- **RPE guideline**: Moderate effort (≈7/10) – strong enough to stimulate strength but not so heavy that Tuesday’s easy spin feels “dead.”  
- **Dial‑back rule**: If legs feel excessively heavy in the first 10 min of Tuesday’s ride, reduce next week’s load by ~10 % or trim a set from the main squat. This is a simple **[[concepts/recovery_feedback_loops]]** cue.  
- **Timing**: Perform the lift **after** the Monday trainer ride to keep the ride high‑quality and ensure the lift stacks onto the day’s stress without compromising cadence.

## Key Concepts
- **Steady rhythm vs. flex space** – structuring weekday vs. weekend training load.  
- **[[concepts/stress_clustering]]** – grouping leg‑intensive work early to protect later endurance sessions.  
- **[[concepts/effort_metrics]]** – using RPE to balance strength and recovery.  
- **[[concepts/recovery_feedback_loops]]** – a feedback loop for load adjustment.  
- **training periodination** – overall framework for periodizing endurance‑strength integration.  

These ideas support training for a **[[entities/half-ironman]]** and can be expanded in broader concept pages.
