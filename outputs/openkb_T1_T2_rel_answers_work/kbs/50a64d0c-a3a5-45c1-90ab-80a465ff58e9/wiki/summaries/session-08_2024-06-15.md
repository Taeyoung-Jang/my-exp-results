# Summary of session-08_2024-06-15

**Context** – The user asked for a practical weekly planning system that balances work (9‑5), gym (3 times/week), two hobbies (1‑2 times/week), chores, meal prep, and two open friend‑night slots.

**Core suggestions**
- Adopt **[[concepts/theme-days]]**: assign each non‑social day a loose theme (deep‑focus, gym, hobby) rather than a rigid checklist.
- Use **anchor days**: keep the two friend nights completely open as immutable anchors.
- Apply **[[concepts/batching]]** by grouping similar mental modes (admin, deep work, physical activity).
- Insert tiny **chore reset** blocks (10‑15 min) on regular evenings.
- Reserve a **meal prep** block on Sunday evening (60‑90 min) to streamline weekdays.
- Structure the day into **time blocks** – “before work” (morning routine) and “after work” (themed activity).

**Revised weekly grid (after‑work gym, open friend nights)**
- **Monday** – After work: deep‑focus evening + 10‑15 min tidy.
- **Tuesday** – After work: gym.
- **Wednesday** – After work: hobby night.
- **Thursday** – After work: friend night (open).
- **Friday** – After work: friend night (open).
- **Saturday** – After work: gym (mid‑day optional) + optional hobby.
- **Sunday** – After work: 60‑90 min meal prep + wind‑down.

**Swap‑rule checklist** for when a friend night moves:
1. **Protect friend nights** – keep them free; they are the highest priority.
2. **Shift hobby** – move the hobby slot to any open non‑gym evening.
3. **Adjust gym** – relocate the gym session to another after‑work slot or to weekend midday.
4. **Keep chores flexible** – 10‑minute tidy can fit anywhere.
5. **Meal prep** – remains on Sunday unless absolutely necessary, then move to early Saturday evening.

**Key concepts** emerging from this conversation include [[concepts/flexible-scheduling]], [[concepts/theme-days]], and [[concepts/batching]].
