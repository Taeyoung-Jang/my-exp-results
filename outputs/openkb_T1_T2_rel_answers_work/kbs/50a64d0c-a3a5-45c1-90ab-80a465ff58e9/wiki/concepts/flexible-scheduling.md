# Flexible Scheduling

**Definition**: Flexible scheduling is a planning method that structures a week around stable anchor points (e.g., open friend nights) while allowing other activities—work, gym, hobbies, chores—to shift fluidly in response to changing circumstances.

## Core Elements

- **Anchor Days**: Fixed, non‑negotiable slots (the two friend nights) that remain open for social commitments. These provide stability and prevent the schedule from collapsing when plans change.
- **Theme Days**: Each remaining day is assigned a loose theme such as *deep‑focus*, *gym*, or *hobby* rather than a rigid hour‑by‑hour agenda. This aligns with the idea of [[concepts/theme-days]] and reduces over‑booking.
- **Batching**: Similar tasks are grouped together (e.g., admin work, deep work, physical activity) to minimize context switching, echoing [[concepts/batching]].
- **Micro‑Chores**: Short 10‑15 minute tidy‑up blocks are sprinkled throughout the week, making chores low‑impact and easy to relocate.
- **Meal Prep Block**: A dedicated 60‑90 minute session on Sunday evening prepares meals for the upcoming week, ensuring weekday evenings stay light.
- **Swap Rule Checklist**: When a friend night shifts, the schedule follows a priority order—keep friend nights untouched, then move hobby slots, then gym sessions, and finally adjust chores if needed. This rule embodies the flexibility of the system.

## How It Works in Practice

The approach was illustrated in [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-15]], where a user needed to balance a 9‑5 job, three weekly gym sessions, two hobbies, chores, and meal prep while keeping two evenings free for friends. By:

1. Designating Thursday and Friday as open friend nights (anchor days).
2. Assigning Monday to deep‑focus work, Tuesday and Saturday to gym, Wednesday to a hobby, and Sunday to meal prep.
3. Using short chore resets on Monday and Thursday.
4. Applying the swap rule when a social plan moves, the user could shift the gym or hobby without disrupting the overall structure.

## Benefits

- **Resilience**: The schedule can absorb unexpected changes without a cascade of cancellations.
- **Clarity**: Themes provide a simple mental cue for what type of activity belongs on a given day.
- **Efficiency**: Batching reduces mental overhead and transition time.
- **Work‑Life Balance**: Anchor days protect social time, while flexible slots accommodate personal health and interests.
