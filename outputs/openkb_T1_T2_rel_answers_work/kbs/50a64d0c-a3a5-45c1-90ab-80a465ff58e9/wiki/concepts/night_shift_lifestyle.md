# Night‑Shift Lifestyle

**Definition**: A pattern of living where work hours fall during the night, requiring the individual to sleep, socialize, and perform daily tasks on a reversed schedule.

## Core Elements
- **Sleep timing** – Protect a consistent daytime sleep window to ensure recovery.
- **Social rhythm** – Fit social activities into the limited evening before a night shift, often using a single‑evening template.
- **Energy management** – Align high‑energy tasks (e.g., social outings) with personal energy peaks and protect low‑energy periods for rest.
- **Boundary setting** – Clearly define start/end times for outings and communicate limits to friends.
- **Low‑effort connection** – Use brief voice notes, texts, or shared media on non‑social days to maintain relationships without extra energy cost.

## Practical Templates (from [[summaries/session-36_2024-12-13]])
1. **Dinner‑focused pre‑shift night** – Wake around 4 p.m., meet at 6 p.m., end by 7:30 p.m., then wind down before work.
2. **Active outing** – Wake ~3:30 p.m., walk at 5 p.m., finish by 6 p.m., brief stretch before shift.
3. **Stay‑in evening** – Invite friend at 5:30 p.m., wrap by 7 p.m., immediate wind‑down.

Each template incorporates a repeatable wind‑down routine (shower, low lighting, light snack) to transition from social energy to work mode.
