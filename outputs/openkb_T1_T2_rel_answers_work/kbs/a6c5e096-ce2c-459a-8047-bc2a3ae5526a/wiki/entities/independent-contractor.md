# Independent Contractor

**Type:** other

An **independent contractor** is a self‑employed individual who offers services to clients on a project‑by‑project basis rather than being hired as a regular employee. In the context of *session‑10_2024‑07‑15*, the independent contractor seeks a **lightweight routine** that provides structure without mimicking a rigid office schedule.

## Key Practices Mentioned in the Document
- **Daily planning**: A short 30‑minute start‑of‑day session to set 1‑2 top priorities. [[concepts/daily-planning]]
- **Deep‑work blocks**: Protected 2‑3 hour windows (e.g., Tuesday and Thursday mornings) for high‑focus tasks. [[concepts/deep-work]]
- **Admin batching**: Consolidating email, invoicing, and scheduling into a single weekly hour. [[concepts/admin-batching]]
- **Weekly review**: A brief Friday recap to assess completed priorities and adjust the upcoming week. [[concepts/weekly-review]]
- **Accountability**: Simple check‑ins, such as brief messages to peers or end‑of‑day notes, to stay on track. [[concepts/accountability]]
- **Scorecard**: An ultra‑light daily log (priority, result, why moved, main activity) plus a weekly roll‑up to gauge time distribution. [[concepts/scorecard]]
- **Overall approach**: Emphasis on a **lightweight routine** that is flexible yet structured enough to maintain focus and productivity. [[concepts/lightweight-routine]]
