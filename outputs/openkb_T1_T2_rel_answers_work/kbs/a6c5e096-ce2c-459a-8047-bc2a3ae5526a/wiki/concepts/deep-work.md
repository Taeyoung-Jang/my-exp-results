# Deep Work

**Definition**: Deep Work refers to sustained, distraction‑free periods dedicated to cognitively demanding tasks that produce high‑quality output.

## Core Characteristics
- **Uninterrupted**: No notifications, emails, or meetings during the block.
- **Time‑boxed**: Typically 2–3 hours, scheduled at times of peak personal focus.
- **Purpose‑driven**: Aligned with the top priorities set during the daily planning routine.

## How It Appears in Session 10
In the *session‑10_2024-07-15* workflow, Deep Work is scheduled on Tuesdays and Thursdays for a protected 2‑3‑hour window each morning. These blocks are deliberately placed after a brief [[concepts/daily-planning]] session on Monday, ensuring that the most important tasks are identified before the deep‑focus periods begin.

## Practical Tips
1. **Choose peak focus times** – Identify when you naturally have the most energy and schedule Deep Work then.
2. **Set a clear goal** – Before each block, note the specific outcome you aim to achieve.
3. **Eliminate distractions** – Close unrelated tabs, silence devices, and use a “do not disturb” sign if needed.
4. **Track outcomes** – Use the [[concepts/scorecard]] format to note whether the block was successful and any reasons for deviation.
5. **Review weekly** – During the [[concepts/weekly-review]], assess the quantity and quality of work produced in Deep Work sessions.
