# Weekly Review

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A short, regular reflection at the end of the week that checks whether set priorities were met, identifies any shifts, and informs adjustments for the next week.

## Purpose
Weekly Review provides a low‑overhead accountability loop for independent workers. It helps close the feedback loop without imposing a corporate‑style performance audit.

## Core Steps (as outlined in [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-15]])
1. **Allocate a brief slot** – about 15 minutes on Friday.
2. **Compare outcomes** – look at the top 1‑2 weekly priorities and note which were completed, which were moved, and why.
3. **Summarize time distribution** – note the balance of deep‑work, admin, and meetings.
4. **Capture insights** – record what worked well and one adjustment for the following week.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with a **[[concepts/daily-planning]]** routine that sets daily priorities.
- Complements the **[[concepts/scorecard]]** daily log, using its “Result” and “Why it shifted” entries as input.
- Reinforces **[[concepts/accountability]]** by making progress visible to oneself or a peer.
- Fits within a broader **[[concepts/lightweight-routine]]** that balances structure with flexibility.
- Helps evaluate the effectiveness of **[[concepts/deep-work]]** blocks and **[[concepts/admin-batching]]** sessions.


## Benefits
- **Clarity:** Quickly surface mismatches between intended and actual work.
- **Adaptability:** Allows iterative tweaks to the weekly rhythm.
- **Low friction:** No need for sophisticated time‑tracking tools; relies on simple notes.

## When to Use
Any independent professional or remote worker who wants a lightweight way to stay on track without a full‑blown productivity system.
