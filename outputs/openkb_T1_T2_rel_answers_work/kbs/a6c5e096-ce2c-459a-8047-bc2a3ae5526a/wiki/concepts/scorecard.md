# Scorecard

**Definition**: A lightweight tracking system that captures whether daily priorities were met, why they may have shifted, and aggregates a weekly overview of time distribution across deep work, admin, and meetings.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-15]])

### Daily Section
- **Priority 1** and optional **Priority 2** – the top tasks identified during a [[concepts/daily-planning]] session.
- **Result** – a simple *yes / no / moved* indicator.
- **Why it shifted** – a brief note required only when the result is *moved*, prompting reflection and supporting [[concepts/accountability]].
- **Main time spent** – categorised as *deep work*, *admin*, *meeting*, or *mixed* (linking to [[concepts/deep-work]] and [[concepts/admin-batching]]).
- **Notes** – any extra context.

### Weekly Section
- **Weekly priorities actually completed** – tallies the goals set on Monday.
- **Where my time went overall** – high‑level breakdown (deep work / admin / meetings).
- **What shifted and why** – captures patterns that inform future [[concepts/weekly-review]].
- **What worked well** and **What I want to adjust next week** – short reflective prompts.

## How the Scorecard Supports a [[concepts/lightweight-routine]]
- **Minimal overhead**: Only a few lines per day, no time‑tracking software.
- **Binary accountability**: The *yes/no/moved* flags give a quick health check without a heavy reporting system.
- **Feedback loop**: Weekly roll‑up feeds into the next planning cycle, keeping the routine adaptable.

## Benefits
1. **Clarity** – makes daily intention explicit and easy to review.
2. **Reflection** – the *why it shifted* field surfaces blockers and helps refine future planning.
3. **Alignment** – ties daily actions to the broader weekly rhythm, reinforcing the three touchpoints of planning, deep work, and review.
4. **Simplicity** – can be copied into any notes app as plain text.

## Example Template (copy‑paste)
```
DAILY
Priority 1: 
Priority 2 (optional): 
Result (yes / no / moved): 
Why it shifted (if moved): 
Main time spent (deep work, admin, meetings, mixed): 
Notes: 

WEEKLY
Weekly priorities actually completed: 
Where my time went overall (deep work / admin / meetings): 
What shifted and why: 
What worked well: 
What I want to adjust next week: 
```
