## Admin Batching

**Definition**: A lightweight routine that consolidates routine administrative activities—email, invoicing, scheduling, follow‑ups—into one focused time block, freeing larger periods for deep, uninterrupted work.

### Why it matters
- Prevents admin interruptions from fragmenting high‑focus periods.
- Reduces decision fatigue by handling repetitive tasks in one go.
- Aligns with a [[concepts/lightweight-routine]] that keeps the overall workflow simple and adaptable.

### How to implement (from [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-15]])
1. **Choose a day and slot** – e.g., Wednesday midday for a 1‑hour "admin hour."
2. **Set a clear boundary** – close unrelated tabs, silence notifications, and treat the block as non‑negotiable.
3. **Batch the tasks** – email cleanup, invoicing, calendar updates, and any other recurring admin chores.
4. **Close the block** – move immediately to the next activity (often a deep‑work session or a break).

### Sample weekly rhythm
| Day | Time | Focus |
|-----|------|-------|
| Mon | 30 min | [[concepts/daily-planning]] – set weekly priorities |
| Tue | 2‑3 h (morning) | [[concepts/deep-work]] |
| **Wed** | **1 h (midday)** | **Admin Batching** |
| Thu | 2‑3 h (morning) | [[concepts/deep-work]] |
| Fri | 15 min | [[concepts/weekly-review]] |

### Benefits
- **Protects deep‑work blocks**: By isolating admin, you keep Tuesday and Thursday free for sustained focus.
- **Improves accountability**: A predictable admin slot makes it easy to note completion in a simple [[concepts/scorecard]].
- **Supports a low‑overhead workflow**: No need for complex time‑tracking tools; the batch acts as a natural checkpoint.

### Connections to other concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/deep-work]] by shielding it from interruptions.
- Complements [[concepts/daily-planning]] and [[concepts/weekly-review]] as part of a regular rhythm.
- Enhances overall [[concepts/accountability]] when the completion of the admin block is logged in a daily scorecard.
