# Accountability

**Definition**: A lightweight system of self‑monitoring and external check‑ins that helps independent workers stay on track without the overhead of formal management structures.

## Why it matters for independent contractors
- Without a manager, you need a simple way to verify that daily priorities are met.
- It reduces the risk of drift and keeps momentum across weeks.
- Encourages reflective practice, which improves future planning.

## Core practices described in [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-15]]
1. **Morning priority note** – write 1‑2 top tasks and review them at day‑end.
2. **End‑of‑day status tag** – mark each priority as *yes*, *no*, or *moved*.
3. **Peer check‑in** – a brief message to a colleague or friend to announce progress or ask for quick feedback.
4. **Weekly review** – a 15‑minute Friday recap that tallies what was completed, what shifted, and what to adjust.

## Simple accountability workflow
```text
1. Start of day: Write top priorities.
2. Throughout day: Work in deep‑work blocks; batch admin tasks.
3. End of day: Mark each priority (yes / no / moved) and note why if moved.
4. Weekly (Friday): Review outcomes, adjust plan, share brief summary with a peer.
```

By integrating these micro‑checks, an independent contractor can maintain direction and motivation without feeling like they are recreating a corporate management system.
