# Daily Planning

**Definition**: A short, repeatable routine—typically 30 minutes at the start of the workday or week—that identifies the top priorities, outlines a loose schedule, and anchors the day in a lightweight structure. [[concepts/lightweight-routine]]

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-15]])
1. **Pick 1–2 top priorities** – Write them down before any other task.
2. **Map the week** – On Monday, glance at all active projects and decide which days will host deep‑work blocks, admin batching, and a Friday review.
3. **Keep it brief** – The whole process should take no more than 30 minutes; it is a cue, not a detailed plan.
4. **Link to accountability** – Optionally share the list with a peer or log it for yourself to increase commitment. [[concepts/accountability]]

## How It Connects to Other Practices
- **Deep‑Work**: By deciding early which days hold protected focus periods, daily planning ensures those blocks are respected. [[concepts/deep-work]]
- **Admin Batching**: Scheduling a single hour for email, invoicing, and other routine tasks prevents them from intruding on focus time. [[concepts/admin-batching]]
- **Weekly Review**: The planning routine feeds into Friday’s recap, creating a feedback loop that refines future plans. [[concepts/weekly-review]]
- **Scorecard**: After the day ends, a quick “yes/no/moved” check against the priorities recorded during planning completes the accountability loop. [[concepts/scorecard]]

## Benefits for an Independent Contractor
- Provides structure without imposing a corporate‑style schedule.
- Enhances focus by clarifying what truly matters each day.
- Supports self‑accountability through a simple, repeatable habit.
