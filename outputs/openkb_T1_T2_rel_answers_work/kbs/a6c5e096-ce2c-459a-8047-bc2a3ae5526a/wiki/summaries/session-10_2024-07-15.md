# Summary of Session 10 – Independent Contractor Workflow (2024‑07‑15)

**Main contribution**: A concise, low‑overhead system for structuring daily and weekly work when operating outside a traditional office, including a concrete weekly template and a minimal “scorecard” for tracking priorities.

## Key Concepts
- **Lightweight routine** – gentle structure that avoids rigid office‑style schedules. [[concepts/lightweight-routine]]
- **Daily start‑up** – 30‑minute planning window to pick 1‑2 top priorities. [[concepts/daily-planning]]
- **Deep‑work blocks** – protected 2‑3 hour windows (e.g., Tue/Thu mornings) for high‑focus tasks. [[concepts/deep-work]]
- **Admin batching** – a single hour (e.g., Wed midday) for email, invoicing, scheduling. [[concepts/admin-batching]]
- **End‑of‑week review** – 15‑minute Friday recap to assess completed priorities and adjust the next week. [[concepts/weekly-review]]
- **Accountability** – simple check‑ins (messaging a peer, end‑of‑day note) to stay on track without formal supervision. [[concepts/accountability]]
- **Scorecard** – ultra‑light daily log (priorities, result, why moved, main activity) plus a weekly roll‑up that tallies deep work vs. admin vs. meetings. [[concepts/scorecard]]

## Practical Weekly Template
| Day | Time Block | Activity |
|-----|------------|----------|
| **Mon** | 30 min | Planning – set 2–3 weekly priorities |
| **Tue** | 2‑3 h (morning) | Deep‑work (focus‑intensive tasks) |
| **Wed** | 1 h (midday) | Admin batch (email, invoicing, scheduling) |
| **Thu** | 2‑3 h (morning) | Deep‑work |
| **Fri** | 15 min | Review – what was done, what moved, next‑week adjustments |

## Daily Scorecard Template (copy‑paste)
```

Why it shifted (if moved): 
Main time spent (deep work, admin, meetings, mixed): 
Notes: 
```

## Weekly Roll‑up Template (copy‑paste)
```
WEEKLY
Weekly priorities actually completed: 
Where my time went overall (deep work / admin / meetings): 
What shifted and why: 
What worked well: 
What I want to adjust next week: 
```

## Findings & Recommendations
- A **consistent start‑of‑day ritual** anchors the day without enforcing a fixed clock‑in time. [[concepts/daily-planning]]
- **Batching admin** minimizes interruptions to deep‑work periods. [[concepts/admin-batching]]
- The **scorecard** provides binary accountability (yes/no/moved) and prompts reflection on why tasks shifted, keeping the system lightweight. [[concepts/scorecard]] [[concepts/accountability]]
- Weekly review acts as a *soft* manager, offering feedback loops without the overhead of formal productivity tools. [[concepts/weekly-review]]

These pieces together form a flexible framework that can evolve as an [[entities/independent-contractor]]’s energy and project mix change.
