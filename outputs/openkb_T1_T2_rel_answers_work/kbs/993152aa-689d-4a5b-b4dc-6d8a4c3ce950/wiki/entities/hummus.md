# Hummus

**Type:** product

**Description:** Hummus is a smooth, blended dip made primarily from cooked chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, and olive oil. In the context of *session‑37_2025‑09‑15*, it is recommended as a crown‑friendly snack because it can be thinned to a fully smooth consistency, eliminating any bits that could lodge under temporary crowns.

## Key Points from the Session
- **Crown‑safe preparation:** The plan calls for *thinned hummus* (or blended white‑bean mash) that is completely smooth, ensuring no chunky pieces remain.
- **Batch‑prep:** One batch of hummus is prepared on Sunday and divided into five small snack containers for the workweek.
- **Protein source:** While hummus itself provides modest protein, it is paired with Greek yogurt, cottage‑cheese, or protein powder in meals to meet high‑protein goals.
- **Food‑safety category:** Listed under the “generally safe” group when thinned; it becomes a “use caution” item if served with visible chunks or thick texture.
- **Packaging:** Each day a small container is packed alongside the breakfast jar, lunch container, and smoothie jar.
