# Polenta

Polenta is a smooth, cooked cornmeal product that can be prepared to a very soft, spoon‑able consistency. In the **session‑37** meal plan it serves as the main lunch component for **Week 2**, where it is blended with either [[entities/ricotta]] or [[entities/silken-tofu]] to create a silky, high‑protein dish that is safe for users wearing temporary crowns.

## Key Facts from the Plan
- **Batch‑prep**: Approximately **5 cups** of cooked polenta are prepared on Sunday, then mixed with **1½–2 cups** of ricotta or silken tofu to achieve a smooth texture suitable for a one‑cup lunch portion each workday.
- **Nutrition**: The addition of ricotta or silken tofu boosts protein content, aligning with the [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]] goal.
- **Safety**: Because it is fully blended and free of chunks, polenta falls into the **generally safe** category of the [[concepts/food-safety-categories]] framework for temporary crown wearers.
- **Storage**: Portion‑sized containers are used for each weekday lunch, making it easy to transport and reheat at work.
- **Flexibility**: The base can be flavored with herbs, a pinch of salt, or a splash of broth without compromising its soft texture.
