# Ricotta Cheese

Ricotta cheese is a fresh, soft cheese made from the whey leftover after the production of other cheeses. Its mild flavor and creamy texture make it ideal for dishes that need to be smooth and easy to swallow, which is especially important for people with **temporary crowns**.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-15]]
- **Meal role**: Used as a protein‑rich ingredient in the Week 2 lunch component (ricotta‑polenta) and in the ricotta‑herb snack mix.
- **Texture**: Naturally smooth; when blended with polenta, mashed potatoes, or herbs it stays fully pureed, eliminating any bite‑size pieces that could snag on crowns.
- **Nutritional benefit**: Provides high‑quality protein and calcium, supporting the [[concepts/protein-targeting]] goal of the meal plan.
- **Batch prep**: One batch of about 1½ cups ricotta is blended with herbs or mixed into polenta for five lunch portions during the Sunday prep for Week 2.

## Related concepts and entities
- Part of the **soft‑high‑protein meals** strategy: see [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]].
- Works with other soft dairy items like [[entities/cottage-cheese]] and [[entities/greek-yogurt]].
- Combined with [[entities/polenta]] and [[entities/mashed-potatoes]] to create fully blended, crown‑safe lunches.
- Paired with [[entities/silken-tofu]] as alternative protein sources in blended dishes.

## Practical usage tips
- **Blend for safety**: When mixed into soups, polenta, or oatmeal, blend briefly to keep the texture uniformly smooth.
- **Storage**: Keep in airtight containers; a single batch yields enough for a full work‑week of lunches and snacks.
- **Flavoring**: Herbs, a drizzle of olive oil, or a pinch of salt can enhance taste without adding chewy elements.
