# Avocado

**Type:** product

## Overview
Avocado is a nutrient‑dense fruit prized for its creamy texture and healthy fats. Its soft, mashable consistency makes it ideal for diets that require minimal chewing, such as for individuals wearing **[[entities/temporary-crowns]]**.

## Role in Session 37 (Meal Plan)
- Featured as a **smooth, blended snack** in Week 2 of the two‑week rotation (e.g., blended avocado mix for Snack 2).
- Prepared in bulk on Sunday: *blend 2 ripe avocados with a splash of lemon juice* to create a **smooth purée** that can be portioned into five small containers.
- Included in the **batch‑prep checklist** with specific quantities to ensure enough for the work‑week.

## Dietary Benefits
- Provides **healthy monounsaturated fats** and a modest amount of protein, complementing the high‑protein targets of the plan.
- When blended, it fits the **[[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]]** criteria, offering a creamy base without any chunks that could lodge under temporary crowns.

## Safety and Preparation Tips
- **Avoid** any chunky pieces; always blend or mash until completely smooth to prevent particles from getting trapped.
- Pair with other soft ingredients like **[[entities/ricotta]]** or **[[entities/greek-yogurt]]** for added protein and flavor.
- Store in a sealed container in the refrigerator and consume within 3‑4 days to maintain freshness.
