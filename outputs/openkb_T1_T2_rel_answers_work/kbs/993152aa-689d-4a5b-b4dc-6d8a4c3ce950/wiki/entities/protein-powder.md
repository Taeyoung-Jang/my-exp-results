# Protein Powder

**Type:** Product

Protein powder is a powdered dietary supplement that provides a concentrated source of protein, typically derived from whey, casein, soy, pea, or other plant/animal proteins. It is mixed with liquid or blended into foods to boost protein content without adding texture that requires chewing.

## Key Facts from Session 37
- Added to **smoothie bases** for both weeks (≈ 5 cups of milk + 5 scoops protein powder per week) to supply a high‑protein, low‑chew breakfast/snack.
- Mixed into **soft oatmeal** (Week 2) and **blended soups** (Week 1) to increase protein while keeping the texture smooth.
- Combined with **cottage‑cheese pudding**, **ricotta‑polenta**, and **mashed potatoes** to create protein‑enriched, crown‑safe meals.
- Recommended portion: 1 scoop per ~ 1 cup of blended food; total of 4‑5 scoops per day across meals.

## Uses in the Crown‑Friendly Meal Plan
- **Breakfast:** blended into oatmeal for a creamy, high‑protein porridge.
- **Lunch:** stirred into fully blended soups to raise protein without affecting smooth consistency.
- **Snacks:** incorporated into smoothie jars for a quick, sip‑friendly protein boost.
- **Meal Stabilization:** helps maintain satiety and muscle maintenance while the user avoids chewy foods due to temporary crowns.
