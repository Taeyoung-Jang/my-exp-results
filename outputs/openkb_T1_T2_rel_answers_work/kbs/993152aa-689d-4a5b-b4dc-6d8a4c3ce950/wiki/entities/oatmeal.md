# Oatmeal

Oatmeal is a cooked grain product that provides a smooth, high‑protein base for meals designed for people wearing **temporary crowns**. In the *session‑37* plan it is prepared in a large batch on Sunday and portioned for five work‑day breakfasts.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-15]]
- **Batch preparation**: Cook ~3 cups dry oats with ~6 cups milk, stir in 4–5 scoops of [[entities/protein-powder]] and a pinch of cinnamon or honey.  
- **Texture**: After cooking, blend lightly to eliminate any grainy texture, creating a fully smooth consistency that is easy to swallow.
- **Portioning**: Divide the blended oatmeal into five containers (≈1 cup each) for Monday‑Friday breakfasts.
- **Nutritional focus**: Provides a steady source of protein and carbohydrates while remaining soft enough to avoid stressing the crowns.

## Safety and dietary context
- Classified as **generally safe** in the [[concepts/food-safety-categories]] framework because it can be made fully smooth and does not contain hard or sticky particles.
- Fits the [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]] design criteria: soft texture, high protein (via added protein powder), and easy to consume on the go.
- Aligns with [[concepts/protein-targeting]] goals for maintaining adequate protein intake during a limited‑chewing diet.
