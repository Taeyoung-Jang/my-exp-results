# Mashed Potatoes

**Type:** product

Mashed potatoes are a soft, high‑protein accompaniment featured in the two‑week crown‑friendly meal rotation described in [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-15]]. They are prepared in bulk on Sunday and portioned for weekday lunches.

## Key Facts from Session 37
- **Batch‑prep quantity:** Cook about 5 cups of potatoes, mash thoroughly, and blend with either ricotta or [[entities/silken-tofu]] to create a silky texture suitable for easy swallowing.
- **Portioning:** Divide the blended mash into five lunch containers (≈1 cup each) for Monday‑Friday.
- **Protein boost:** Optional addition of 1–2 tablespoons of [[entities/protein-powder]] or a splash of [[entities/greek-yogurt]] to meet the [[concepts/protein-targeting]] goals.
- **Safety:** Because the mash is fully blended, it avoids chunky bits that could lodge under [[entities/temporary-crowns]]. It falls under the “generally safe” category in [[concepts/food-safety-categories]].
- **Meal role:** Serves as the main component of the Week 2 lunch option ("tofu‑mashed potatoes" or "ricotta‑polenta with mash"), fitting the [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]] design.

## Preparation Guidance
- Follow the [[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]] concept for scaling the recipe to five days.
- Use the [[concepts/meal-template-design]] framework to pair the mash with protein‑rich additions and ensure a balanced macro profile.
- Store in airtight containers; reheat gently at work to maintain a smooth consistency.
