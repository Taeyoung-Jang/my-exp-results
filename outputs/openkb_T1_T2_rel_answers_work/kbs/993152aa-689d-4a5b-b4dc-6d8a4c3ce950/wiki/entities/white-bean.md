# White Bean (product)

White bean refers to the cooked, canned variety of *Phaseolus vulgaris* that is used in the meal plan from [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-15]]. In the plan it is **blended and thinned** to create a fully smooth snack that is soft enough for someone wearing [[entities/temporary-crowns]].

## Key facts from the session
- **Batch‑prep**: Two cans of white beans are blended with broth on Sunday to produce **five snack‑size portions** for Week 1. This follows the guidelines in [[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]].
- **Nutritional role**: Provides a plant‑based protein boost (≈7 g protein per ½‑cup serving) and fits the **soft‑high‑protein‑meals** framework [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]].
- **Safety**: When fully blended, white bean is placed in the **generally safe** group for temporary crowns, because there are no solid chunks that could wedge under the restoration. The document advises to **blend and thin** to avoid the “use caution” category that whole beans would fall into [[concepts/food-safety-categories]].
- **Meal integration**:
  - Used as **Snack 2** (white‑bean mash) on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday of Week 1.
  - Alternates with thinned hummus on Tuesday and Thursday.
  - Packaged in a small container each day for easy spoon‑drinking at work.
- **Preparation tip**: After blending, the mash can be strained for an ultra‑smooth texture and stored in airtight snack jars.
