# Cottage Cheese

Cottage cheese is a fresh, soft cheese with a mild flavor and a high protein content. In the **session‑37_2025‑09‑15** meal plan it serves as a core ingredient for the **blended cottage‑cheese pudding** breakfasts and is also mixed into soups and snack purées.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Batch‑prep use**: ~6 cups of cottage cheese blended with fruit purée or honey to create five ≈ 1‑cup breakfast portions. [[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]]
- **Protein source**: Provides a quick, chew‑free protein boost, complementing added [[entities/protein-powder]] and often paired with [[entities/greek-yogurt]] for extra creaminess. [[concepts/protein-targeting]]
- **Safety for temporary crowns**: Its ultra‑soft texture makes it safe for users with [[entities/temporary-crowns]], fitting into the **generally safe** food category. [[concepts/food-safety-categories]]
- **Meal roles**:  
  - Breakfast pudding (flavored with strawberry, peach, honey, blueberry, or banana).  
  - Ingredient in blended soups for lunch, adding richness without requiring chewing.  
  - Component of snack‑size purées when combined with [[entities/white-bean]] or [[entities/hummus]] for added variety.
