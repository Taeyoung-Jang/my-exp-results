# Greek Yogurt

**Type:** product

Greek yogurt is a strained, high‑protein dairy product that features prominently in the crown‑friendly meal plan outlined in [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-15]]. It provides a smooth, soft texture that is safe for users wearing [[entities/temporary-crowns]] and serves as a versatile base for both sweet and savory dishes.

## Key Roles in the Meal Plan
- **Breakfast & Snacks:** Blended into cottage‑cheese pudding, mixed with protein powder for smoothies, and combined with fruit purées for flavored breakfast bowls.
- **Lunch:** Added to blended soups to increase creaminess and protein content without adding chewable particles.
- **Protein Boost:** Works with [[entities/protein-powder]] to meet daily protein targets, aligning with the [[concepts/protein-targeting]] strategy.

## Benefits for Temporary Crown Wearers
- **Soft Texture:** Fully smooth when blended, eliminating the need for chewing and reducing risk of dislodging crowns.
- **High Protein:** Supports muscle maintenance and satiety, fitting the [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]] framework.
- **Versatile Base:** Can be incorporated into multiple meal templates, supporting the [[concepts/meal-template-design]] approach.

## Batch‑Prep Guidance
- When preparing the weekly smoothie base, combine **5 cups of Greek yogurt** with **5 scoops of protein powder** and **3 cups of soft fruit** to create five one‑cup portions. This aligns with the [[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]] recommendations for efficient Sunday preparation.

## Food Safety Category
- Classified as **generally safe** for temporary crowns in the [[concepts/food-safety-categories]] matrix because it is fully smooth, non‑sticky, and does not contain particles that could become trapped.
