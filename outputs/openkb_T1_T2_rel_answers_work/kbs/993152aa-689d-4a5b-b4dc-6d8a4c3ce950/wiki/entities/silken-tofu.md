# Silken Tofu

**Type:** product

Silken tofu is a delicate, custard‑like soy curd that blends easily and provides a boost of plant‑based protein. It is especially useful in diets that require soft, chew‑free foods, such as for individuals wearing [[entities/temporary-crowns]].

---

## Key Facts from Session 37

- **Role in the 2‑week plan:** Used as a protein‑rich additive in savory soups, blended dips, and the polenta/mash meals for Week 2. It keeps the texture smooth and eliminates the need for chewing.
- **Batch‑prep quantity:** One batch of ~5 cups of cooked polenta or mashed potatoes is combined with ~1½–2 cups of silken tofu, then blended to a silky consistency for five lunch portions. The tofu is also blended into the protein‑boosted soups for Week 1.
- **Safety:** Because silken tofu is fully blended, it falls into the **generally safe** category for temporary crowns. It does not contain hard particles or sticky residues that could dislodge a crown.
- **Nutritional contribution:** Adds roughly 8 g of protein per ½ cup serving, complementing other protein sources like [[entities/greek-yogurt]], [[entities/cottage-cheese]], and [[entities/protein-powder]].

---

## How It Supports [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]]

Silken tofu’s smooth texture makes it ideal for:
- **Blended soups** – provides body and protein without creating chunks.
- **Savory dips** – can be mixed with herbs or spices to create a soft, spoonable snack.
- **Meal fortification** – combined with [[entities/polenta]] or [[entities/mashed-potatoes]] to increase protein while keeping the dish crown‑friendly.

---

## Batch‑Prep Guidance ([[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]])

1. **Cook base** (polenta or potatoes) – about 5 cups total.
2. **Add silken tofu** – 1½–2 cups, blended until no lumps remain.
3. **Portion** – divide into five airtight containers for the workweek.
4. **Store** – refrigerate; reheat gently before eating.

---

## Food‑Safety Context ([[concepts/food-safety-categories]])

- **Generally safe** – because it is fully blended, it poses minimal risk of getting trapped under a crown.
- **Use caution** – if left with any residual chunks; always ensure a smooth consistency.
