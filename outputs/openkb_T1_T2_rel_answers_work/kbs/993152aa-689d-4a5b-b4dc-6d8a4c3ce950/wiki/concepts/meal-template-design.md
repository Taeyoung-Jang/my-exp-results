# Meal Template Design

## Overview
Meal Template Design is a simple framework for building nutritious, high‑protein meals with minimal cooking. Each template combines four core elements:
1. **Protein source** (e.g., chicken, tofu, tuna, eggs, Greek yogurt)
2. **Vegetables** (fresh, bagged, or frozen)
3. **Carbohydrate or fiber base** (rice, quinoa, lentils, whole‑grain pasta, potatoes)
4. **Quick sauce or seasoning** to add flavor without extra prep.

This framework was articulated in [[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]] and is meant to be repeatable day‑to‑day.

## Core Structure
| Element | Typical Options | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Protein** | Rotisserie chicken (5‑6 oz), baked tofu (10‑12 oz), canned tuna, 4 eggs, Greek yogurt (1‑1.5 cup) | 40‑50 g protein per main meal [[concepts/protein-targeting]] |
| **Vegetables** | Bagged greens, frozen stir‑fry mix, roasted veggies | Fiber, micronutrients |
| **Carb / Fiber Base** | Microwaveable rice/quinoa, lentil pouch, whole‑grain tortilla, roasted potatoes | Satiety, energy |
| **Sauce / Seasoning** | Soy‑ginger, Mediterranean (olive oil, lemon, oregano), creamy yogurt‑mustard, chili‑crisp + honey | Variety, taste |

## Example Templates from the Source Document
### Meal 1 – Grain Bowl
- Base: microwaveable rice or quinoa
- Protein: rotisserie chicken (5–6 oz)
- Veg: big handful of greens or frozen veg
- Sauce: olive oil + lemon **or** soy‑ginger drizzle
- **Protein:** ~45‑50 g

### Meal 2 – Tofu Stir‑Fry Bowl
- Protein: baked or pre‑marinated tofu (10–12 oz)
- Veg: frozen stir‑fry mix
- Carb: quinoa, rice, or lentil pouch
- Sauce: chili‑crisp + rice vinegar + honey (or soy‑ginger)
- **Protein:** ~45‑50 g

## Snack Integration
Optional snacks add 10‑20 g protein each and can be swapped in when the main meals fall short:
- Greek yogurt (1‑1.5 cup) + berries
- Cottage cheese + fruit
- Canned tuna + crackers
- Jerky + fruit

## Quick Sauce / Seasoning Strategies
1. **Soy‑Ginger:** soy sauce, sesame oil, lime, honey
2. **Mediterranean:** olive oil, lemon, garlic, oregano (optional feta)
3. **Creamy Yogurt:** Greek yogurt, mustard, lemon, black pepper (also boosts protein)
4. **Spicy Chili‑Crisp:** chili crisp, rice vinegar, drizzle of honey

## Recommended Staples for Easy Templates
The design relies on a set of low‑effort grocery staples that require little to no cooking: rotisserie chicken, baked tofu, frozen shrimp, microwaveable grains, bagged greens, frozen veggie mixes, canned beans, tuna, hummus, guacamole cups, and basic sauces. These items are catalogued under [[concepts/grocery-staples-for-minimal-cooking]].
