# Grocery Staples for Minimal Cooking

**Definition**: A curated set of pantry, fridge, and freezer items that let you assemble protein‑rich meals quickly, without extensive cooking or meal‑planning.

## Core Staples
- **Proteins**: rotisserie chicken, baked or pre‑marinated tofu, frozen shrimp, canned tuna, canned salmon, jerky.
- **Grains & Carbs**: microwaveable brown rice, quinoa cups, lentil or bean pouches, whole‑grain tortillas, bean‑based pasta.
- **Vegetables**: bagged greens, pre‑chopped fresh veg, frozen stir‑fry mixes, frozen mixed vegetables.
- **Legumes & Beans**: canned beans (black, chickpeas, kidney), lentils (canned or pouch).
- **Dairy & Alternatives**: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, hummus, guacamole cups.
- **Flavor Boosters**: olive oil, soy sauce, sesame oil, lemon, lime, honey, basic vinaigrette, chili crisp, pesto, mustard.

## How They Support Meal Planning
These staples map directly onto the **[[concepts/meal-template-design]]** outlined in the source document, enabling a repeatable structure of **protein + veg + carb + sauce** with minimal prep. By keeping protein sources (chicken, tofu, tuna, etc.) on hand, you can reliably hit the **[[concepts/protein-targeting]]** goal of 40–50 g protein per meal without large portions or complex calculations.

## Usage Example
From the **[[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]]** blueprint:
- **Meal 1** uses rotisserie chicken, microwaveable rice, and bagged greens, finished with an olive‑oil + lemon drizzle.
- **Meal 2** relies on baked tofu, frozen stir‑fry veg, and quinoa, flavored with a chili‑crisp sauce.

Having these items stocked means each meal can be assembled in under 10 minutes, keeping cooking time low while maintaining nutritional goals.
