# Soft, High‑Protein Meals for Temporary Crowns

**Purpose** – Provide a framework for creating meals that are both easy to chew and rich in protein, specifically for individuals wearing [[entities/temporary-crowns]]. The concept draws on the two‑week rotation described in [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-15]].

---

## Core Criteria

1. **Texture** – Foods must be fully smooth, blended, or pureed; no chunks, skins, or kernels that could lodge under a crown.
2. **Protein Density** – Aim for 20‑30 g of protein per main meal or snack, using high‑quality sources such as [[entities/cottage-cheese]], [[entities/greek-yogurt]], [[entities/ricotta]], [[entities/silken-tofu]], [[entities/protein-powder]], and legumes like [[entities/white-bean]].
3. **Temperature & Acidity** – Serve at moderate temperatures; avoid extremely hot or cold items that can stress temporary cement.
4. **Portion Control** – Prepare single‑serve portions (≈1 cup) to keep meals manageable and reduce the need for reheating large volumes.

---

## Meal Design Framework

The approach aligns with [[concepts/meal-template-design]] and [[concepts/protein-targeting]]:

- **Breakfast** – Soft porridges or blended dairy bases (e.g., protein‑enriched [[entities/oatmeal]] or [[entities/cottage-cheese]] pudding).
- **Lunch** – Pureed soups or smooth starches (e.g., silky [[entities/polenta]] or [[entities/mashed-potatoes]] with added protein).
- **Snacks** – Smoothies, blended bean spreads, or soft cheese mixes (e.g., [[entities/hummus]] thinned with broth, [[entities/avocado]] purée, or [[entities/ricotta]]‑herb spread).

Each category can be varied by flavor (fruit purées, herbs, spices) while keeping the base ingredients constant for batch efficiency.

---

## Batch‑Prep Quantities

Refer to [[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]] for scaling guidance. A typical Sunday session for a five‑day workweek includes:

- **Cottage‑cheese pudding** – 6 cups cottage cheese + 1 cup fruit or honey → 5 breakfast portions.
- **Pureed soup** – 6‑7 cups cooked vegetables + broth → 5 lunch portions.
- **Smoothie base** – 5 cups milk/alternative + 5 scoops protein powder + 3 cups soft fruit → 5 jars.
- **Bean/​hummus spreads** – 2 cans beans blended with broth → 5 snack containers.

These quantities mirror the plan in the source document and support the **soft‑high‑protein** goal.

## Food Safety Categories

The concept integrates the matrix from [[concepts/food-safety-categories]]:

| Category | Reason for Classification | Safe Modifications |
|----------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Avoid** | Sticky or granular foods can dislodge crowns (e.g., regular nut‑butter, rice, tortillas). | Blend into smoothies or soups; thin heavily. |
| **Use Caution** | Foods that require minimal chewing or can trap debris (e.g., pasta, beans, eggs, cheese). | Over‑cook to very soft texture; mash or blend fully. |
| **Generally Safe** | Naturally smooth, low‑adhesion foods (e.g., oats cooked very soft, hummus, smoothies, pureed soups). | Ensure no visible chunks; keep temperature moderate. |

## Emergency Crown Care (Brief)

While not a full medical guide, the concept notes that if a crown feels loose, the user should:
1. Stop chewing on that side.
2. Rinse with warm water.
3. Keep the crown dry and transport it in a small container.
4. Contact the dentist promptly; only consider [[entities/temporary-dental-cement]] if previously approved by the dentist.

## Example Two‑Week Rotation (Excerpt)

*Week 1 – Breakfast*: Strawberry [[entities/cottage-cheese]] pudding; *Lunch*: Tomato‑basil pureed soup with Greek yogurt; *Snack 1*: Berry smoothie; *Snack 2*: White‑bean mash.

*Week 2 – Breakfast*: Protein‑enriched [[entities/oatmeal]] blended smooth; *Lunch*: Silky [[entities/polenta]] with ricotta; *Snack 1*: Mango smoothie; *Snack 2*: Ricotta‑herb purée.

All meals follow the texture, protein, and safety rules outlined above.
