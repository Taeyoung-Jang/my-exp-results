# Batch‑Prep Quantities for a 5‑Day Workweek

**Purpose** – Provide a practical framework for preparing enough food on a single Sunday to cover five workdays (Monday‑Friday) while keeping meals soft, high‑protein, and safe for temporary crowns.

## Core Principles
1. **Portion Size** – Aim for roughly **1 cup (≈240 ml)** of each breakfast, lunch, and snack portion. This size fits standard reusable containers and supplies enough calories and protein without being bulky.
2. **Batch Scaling** – Multiply the single‑portion ingredient amounts by **5** (or slightly more to allow for minor loss during blending or transferring).
3. **Container Planning** – Use identical containers for each meal type (e.g., 5 × 500 ml jars for smoothies, 5 × 350 ml containers for soups) so packing is quick.
4. **Ingredient Consistency** – Choose a limited set of staple ingredients that can be flavored differently each day (e.g., cottage‑cheese pudding, oatmeal, polenta) to minimise cooking steps.
5. **Safety Check** – Ensure every batch is **fully blended and smooth** to avoid any bits that could lodge under temporary crowns.

### Scaling Example
If you need **6 cups** of cottage‑cheese pudding for five breakfasts, the recipe is:
- 6 cups cottage cheese (≈1.5 lb)
- 1 cup fruit purée (or honey) for flavor
- Blend until completely smooth; divide into five 1‑cup jars.

Apply the same 5× multiplier to each batch component.

## Practical Tips
- **Batch‑Blend & Strain**: After cooking soups or oatmeal, run through a high‑speed blender and strain through a fine mesh to guarantee a smooth texture.
- **Portion Containers**: Label each jar with the day (Mon‑Fri) to avoid confusion.
- **Flavor Variations**: Add a different fruit purée or a pinch of cinnamon to each day’s smoothie or pudding to keep variety without extra cooking.
- **Storage**: Keep all prepared items refrigerated; consume within 4‑5 days.
