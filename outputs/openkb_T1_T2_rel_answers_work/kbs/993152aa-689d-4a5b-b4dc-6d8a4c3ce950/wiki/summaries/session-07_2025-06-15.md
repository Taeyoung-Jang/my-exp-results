# Summary of session-07_2025-06-15

**Date:** 2025‑06‑15  

### Goal
Create a minimal‑effort meal blueprint for a user who eats two main meals per day, ensuring ~45‑50 g protein per meal (≈110‑130 g total) with easy‑to‑source staples, snack add‑ons, and quick flavor combos.

### Protein Targets & Portion Guides
- **Daily target:** 110–130 g protein.  
- **Per‑meal target:** 40–50 g protein.  
- **Typical portions** for common foods:  
  - Chicken (rotisserie, cooked): **5–6 oz** → ~40–45 g protein.  
  - Tofu (baked/pre‑marinated): **10–12 oz** → ~40–45 g protein.  
  - Eggs: **4 large** → ~24–28 g protein (usually paired with another protein).  
  - Greek yogurt: **1–1.5 cup** → 15–25 g protein.  
  - Cooked lentils: **1 cup** → ~18 g protein (2 cups ≈ 36 g).  
  - Canned tuna (1 can): **≈1 cup** → 25–30 g protein.

### Daily Blueprint
#### Meal 1 – Grain Bowl
- Base: microwaveable rice or quinoa.  
- Protein: rotisserie chicken 5–6 oz.  
- Veg: bagged greens or frozen veg.  
- Sauce: olive oil + lemon **or** soy‑ginger drizzle (soy sauce, sesame oil, lime, honey).  
- **Protein:** ~45–50 g.

#### Meal 2 – Tofu Stir‑Fry Bowl
- Protein: baked or pre‑marinated tofu 10–12 oz.  
- Veg: frozen stir‑fry mix.  
- Carb: quinoa, rice, or lentil pouch.  
- Sauce: chili crisp + rice vinegar + honey (or soy‑ginger alternative).  
- **Protein:** ~45–50 g.

#### Optional Snacks (add 10–20 g protein each)
- Greek yogurt 1–1.5 cup with berries.  
- Cottage cheese cup with fruit.  
- Canned tuna packet with whole‑grain crackers.  
- Jerky + piece of fruit.

### Quick Sauce / Seasoning Combos
1. **Soy‑Ginger:** soy sauce, sesame oil, lime, honey.  
2. **Mediterranean:** olive oil, lemon, garlic, oregano (optional feta).  
3. **Creamy Yogurt:** Greek yogurt, mustard, lemon, black pepper.  
4. **Spicy Chili Crisp:** chili crisp, rice vinegar, drizzle of honey.

### Recommended Staples (effort‑free)
- Rotisserie chicken, baked tofu, frozen shrimp.  
- Microwaveable grains (brown rice, quinoa cups, lentil pouches).  
- Bagged greens, pre‑chopped veg, frozen stir‑fry mixes.  
- Canned beans, tuna, salmon.  
- Hummus, guacamole cups, olive oil, basic vinaigrette sauces.
