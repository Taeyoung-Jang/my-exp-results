# Summary of Session 37 – Crown‑Friendly Meal Planning (2025‑09‑15)

**Purpose**  
Provide a two‑week, Monday‑through‑Friday meal rotation that is soft, high‑protein, and safe for a user wearing [[entities/temporary-crowns]]. Include detailed Sunday batch‑prep quantities, daily packing lists, and a “loose crown” emergency checklist.

**Key Elements**

- **Meal Structure** – Breakfast, lunch, two snacks per workday; each day varies flavor/ingredients while relying on a few core batch‑prep items.  
- **Batch‑Prep Items** – [[entities/cottage-cheese]] pudding, blended protein soups, smoothies, thinned [[entities/hummus]]/[[entities/white-bean]] mash (Week 1); protein [[entities/oatmeal]], silky [[entities/polenta]] or [[entities/mashed-potatoes]] with [[entities/ricotta]]/[[entities/silken-tofu]], smoothies, ricotta‑herb or avocado purées (Week 2). Quantities for 5 portions are provided (e.g., 6 cups cottage cheese + 1 cup fruit purée).  
- **Packing Guidance** – One container for each meal (breakfast jar, lunch container, smoothie jar, snack container) per day.

- **Food Safety Categorization** – Lists foods as **Avoid**, **Use Caution**, or **Generally Safe**, with explanations of why (e.g., rice grains trap under crowns, nut‑butter stickiness) and mitigation tactics (blending, thinning, over‑cooking). See [[concepts/food-safety-categories]] for the full framework.  
- **Loose‑Crown Checklist** – Immediate steps, when [[entities/temporary-dental-cement]] is appropriate, and criteria for urgent vs. routine dentist contact. This aligns with the broader [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]] guidance on safe eating practices.

**Concepts for Cross‑Document Pages**

- [[concepts/soft-high-protein-meals]] – Criteria and examples of meals that are easy to chew for dental crown wearers.  
- [[concepts/batch-prep-quantities]] – Guidelines for scaling batch‑cooked components to a 5‑day workweek.  
- [[concepts/food-safety-categories]] – Framework for classifying foods as avoid, caution, or safe for temporary crowns.  
- [[concepts/meal-template-design]] – Tips for structuring daily meal containers and portioning.  

**Outcome**  
The user receives a concrete, variation‑rich 2‑week menu, precise prep instructions, a clear safety matrix for common foods, and an actionable emergency protocol, enabling consistent nutrition without jeopardizing temporary crowns.
