## Non‑Emergency Police Line

**Type:** organization

The non‑emergency police line is a local law‑enforcement phone service intended for situations that do not require an immediate 911 response. In the caregiver workflow described in [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-12]], it is listed under the **Emergency / Local** checklist as one of the essential numbers to save alongside hospital contacts.

### Why It Matters for Caregivers
- Provides a safe way to report concerns such as noise complaints, minor accidents, or inquiries about community resources without tying up emergency services.
- Can be added to the iPhone **Focus** setup (see [[concepts/focus-mode-setup]]) so that calls from this number are allowed even when other family contacts are silenced.
- Fits into the recommended naming convention: `Local – Police Non‑Emergency`, helping the caregiver quickly identify the purpose of the call.

### Practical Tips from the Session
1. **Save the number** with the label `Local – Police Non‑Emergency` in the caregiver’s contacts.
2. **Add to the allowed list** in the custom “Caregiving” Focus mode if the caregiver wants it to ring through at all times.
3. **Combine with other local contacts** (hospital, ER) under the same naming scheme to keep the contact list organized.
