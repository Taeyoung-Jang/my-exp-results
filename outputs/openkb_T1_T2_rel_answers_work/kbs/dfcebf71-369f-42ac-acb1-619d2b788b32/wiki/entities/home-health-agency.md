# Home Health Agency

**Type:** Organization

**Overview**
The Home Health Agency referenced in [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-12]] is the provider that supplies in‑home nursing care, scheduling support, and after‑hours on‑call assistance for the caregiver of a mother with dementia.

**Key Contact Recommendations**
- **Main office number** – central point for general inquiries.
- **Scheduler / coordinator line** – direct line to arrange visits or changes.
- **On‑call nurse number** – after‑hours emergency contact for urgent health issues.

These numbers should be saved under a consistent naming convention such as:
- `Mom – Home Health Main`
- `Mom – Home Health Scheduler`
- `Mom – Home Health On‑Call`

The naming pattern aligns with the [[concepts/contact-naming-convention]] used throughout the caregiver’s contact strategy.

**Integration with iPhone Focus Mode**
When setting up a custom Focus (see [[concepts/focus-mode-setup]]), the Home Health Agency contacts are added to the *Allowed People* list so that calls or messages from the scheduler or on‑call nurse bypass silencing. The *Allow Repeated Calls* option (linked to [[concepts/repeated-calls-emergency-handling]]) ensures that any unlisted emergency calls from the agency can still break through if they call twice in quick succession.

**Practical Usage**
- Add the three numbers to a single contact entry named “Home Health Agency” with the above sub‑numbers.
- Include this contact in the caregiver’s iPhone Focus *Allowed* list.
- Use the provided **ask script** (from the checklist) when calling the agency to confirm all relevant numbers.
