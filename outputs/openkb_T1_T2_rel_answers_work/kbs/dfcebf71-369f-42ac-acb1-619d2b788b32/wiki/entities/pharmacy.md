## Pharmacy (Organization)

**Role:** The pharmacy is a key point of contact for medication refills and occasional callbacks in the caregiver’s daily routine.

**Key Facts from session‑35_2025‑02‑12:**
- The caregiver is advised to save the following numbers under a single contact:
  - Main pharmacy line
  - Refill/automated line
  - Text‑callback or verification number (if the pharmacy provides one)
- Recommended naming convention for easy identification on the iPhone lock screen: `Mom – Pharmacy Main`, `Mom – Pharmacy Refill`, `Mom – Pharmacy Text`.
- These contacts are added to the **allowed list** of a custom iPhone [[concepts/focus-mode-setup]] (e.g., a “Caregiving” Focus) so calls and texts come through even when other notifications are silenced.
- The pharmacy numbers benefit from the **Allow Repeated Calls** feature described in [[concepts/repeated-calls-emergency-handling]], ensuring that urgent follow‑up calls break through if the initial number isn’t pre‑saved.
- The overall contact organization follows the [[concepts/contact-naming-convention]] to make the purpose of each number instantly recognizable.
