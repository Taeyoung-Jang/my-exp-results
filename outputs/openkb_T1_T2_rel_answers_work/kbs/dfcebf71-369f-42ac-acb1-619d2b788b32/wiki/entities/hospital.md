# Hospital

**Type:** Organization

**Description:** The local hospital’s main line and ER desk are saved in the caregiver’s contact list for rapid emergency response.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-12]]
- Stored using the naming convention `Mom – Hospital Main` and `Mom – Hospital ER` to make the purpose obvious at a glance.  
- Included in the iPhone **Caregiving** Focus mode’s allowed‑people list when urgent access is required, tying into [[concepts/focus-mode-setup]].
- Paired with [[entities/non-emergency-police]] in the **Emergency / Local** contact group for comprehensive local emergency coverage.
- Recommended to keep both the hospital’s main reception number and a direct ER desk/nurse‑station number for the fastest possible assistance.
- The naming approach follows [[concepts/contact-naming-convention]] to reduce cognitive load and prevent missed calls.
- If the hospital calls from a number not in the allowed list, the **Allow Repeated Calls** setting (see [[concepts/repeated-calls-emergency-handling]]) ensures a second call within three minutes still gets through.
