# iOS

**Type:** product

Apple's iOS is the proprietary mobile operating system that powers iPhones and iPads. In the context of session 35 2024 02 12 (caregiver workflow), iOS provides the built‑in **Focus** feature that can be tuned to prioritize urgent care‑related communications while silencing other noise.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Focus mode configuration** – The caregiver creates a custom Focus (e.g., *Caregiving*) directly in iOS settings, selecting allowed contacts such as the home‑health aide, clinic, and pharmacy. This process is described in [[concepts/focus-mode-setup]].
- **Repeated‑calls handling** – iOS offers an *Allow Repeated Calls* toggle, which lets a second call within a short window break through the Focus filter, useful for true emergencies. See [[concepts/repeated-calls-emergency-handling]].
- **Contact naming** – The caregiver adopts a naming convention like "Mom – Clinic Main" to quickly identify care‑related numbers in the iOS contact list, aligning with best practices in [[concepts/contact-naming-convention]].
- **Device association** – iOS runs on the iPhone, the specific hardware the caregiver uses. For reference see [[entities/iphone]].
