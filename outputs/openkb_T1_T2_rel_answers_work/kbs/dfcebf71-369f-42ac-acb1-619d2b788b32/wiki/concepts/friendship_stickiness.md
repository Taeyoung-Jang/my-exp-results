# Friendship Stickiness

**Definition**: Friendship stickiness is the ability of a friendship to endure over time, rooted in feelings of being seen, safe, and comfortable, and sustained by regular, low‑intensity contact rather than occasional high‑stakes events.

## Core Elements
- **Visibility & Safety**: Both parties feel acknowledged and emotionally secure.
- **Consistency Over Intensity**: Small, repeated interactions (quick check‑ins, shared routines) matter more than rare grand gestures.
- **Fit with Life Context**: The friendship integrates naturally into each person’s daily rhythm without demanding extra emotional gymnastics.

- Repeated, low‑effort moments – like a brief message, a shared coffee, or a regular weekly catch‑up – build trust and keep the bond warm.
- Over‑committing or forcing intense interactions can erode stickiness, especially for busy adults.

## Practical Tips
1. **Micro‑Check‑Ins**: Send a quick text or meme a few times a week.
2. **Shared Routine**: Align a small habit (e.g., coffee on Thursday) that fits both schedules.
3. **Safety Signals**: Show genuine interest, listen actively, and keep conversations judgment‑free.
4. **Avoid Over‑Intensity**: Reserve big events for milestones, not everyday maintenance.
