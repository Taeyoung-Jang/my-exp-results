# Repeated Calls Emergency Handling

**Overview**

Repeated Calls Emergency Handling is a feature of iOS Focus mode that lets a second call from the same number within a short window (usually three minutes) bypass the "silence" rules. This provides a low‑friction way to ensure truly urgent calls—such as those from a doctor, nurse, or emergency contact—reach the caregiver even when they are not on the allowed‑people list.

---

## How It Works (iPhone / iOS)

1. **Enable in Focus** – Within a custom Focus (e.g., *Caregiving*), toggle the **Allow Repeated Calls** option.
2. **Timing** – If a caller attempts a second call within three minutes, iOS treats it as an emergency and lets it ring through.
3. **Scope** – Applies to both calls and FaceTime; messages are still governed by the allowed‑people list.

---

## Practical Application for Caregivers

- **Primary Contacts**: Add the home‑health aide, clinic, and pharmacy to the allowed list for immediate delivery.
- **Secondary Contacts**: Family members and other callers stay off the list but can still get through by calling twice.
- **Medical Numbers**: Since clinics often have multiple numbers, saving all of them under a single contact (see [[concepts/contact-naming-convention]]) ensures they are covered; repeated‑calls acts as a safety net for any unexpected line.
- **Avoiding Noise**: By relying on repeated‑call detection, caregivers reduce the need to list every possible number while still catching urgent outreach.

---

## Benefits

- **Simplicity**: No complex rule‑sets; a single toggle handles emergencies.
- **Reliability**: Leverages the built‑in iOS logic, which is consistent across updates.
- **Low Cognitive Load**: Caregiver can focus on caregiving tasks, trusting that a genuine emergency will break through.
