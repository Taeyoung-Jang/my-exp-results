# Summary of session-09_2024-06-12

**Main focus**: How adults can keep existing friendships strong while gently expanding their social network.

## Key ideas
- **Friendship stickiness** hinges on feeling *seen*, *safe*, and *easy* with one another.  Consistency (small, repeated contacts) matters more than intensity.  [[concepts/friendship_stickiness]]
- **Low‑pressure meeting**: Engage in activities you already enjoy; shared context provides natural conversation starters.  [[concepts/low_pressure_meeting]]
- **Anchor moments**: Designate a regular, low‑effort slot each week for close friends (e.g., a dinner, a show, or a quick catch‑up).  [[concepts/anchor_moments]]
- **Social windows**: Keep two weekly windows – one for nurturing existing ties, one for meeting new people – to avoid overcommitment.
- **Fallback strategies**: When energy or time runs low, use micro‑interactions (quick message, funny photo, brief coffee‑shop chat) or shift to an even lighter activity.

## Suggested weekly rhythm (Mon–Sun)
| Day | Focus | Example activity |
|-----|-------|-------------------|
| **Monday** | Rest / reset | No planned social events |
| **Tuesday** | **Anchor friend** | Cook together, quick dinner, or casual hang‑out |
| **Wednesday** | Open / buffer | No obligations |
| **Thursday** | **New‑people window** | Drop into a casual class, board‑game café, community lecture, or work from a coffee shop |
| **Friday** | Free flow | Anything that pops up organically |
| **Saturday** | Flexible | Friends, errands, or rest – adapt as needed |
| **Sunday** | Reset with mellow social | Walk with a new acquaintance, visit a market, or low‑key meetup |

## Handling chaotic weeks
- Missed **anchor**: Send a brief check‑in, share a funny photo, or propose a simple plan for the next week.
- Missed **new‑people** slot: Switch to a lighter interaction (talk to a coworker, greet a neighbor, reply to a recent contact).

## Practical takeaways
1. **Consistency over intensity** – small check‑ins keep bonds warm.
2. **Leverage existing routines** – turn habitual places into informal networking spots.
3. **Two‑window model** – limits social load while fostering growth.
4. **Micro‑fallbacks** – keep connections alive even when you’re exhausted.
