# Summary of Session 06 (2024‑07‑15)

## Core Approach
- **Two‑part framework**: focus on breathable fabrics and a simple rotation of interchangeable pieces. See [[concepts/breathable_fabrics]] and [[concepts/capsule_wardrobe]].
- Prioritize natural, lightweight fibers (cotton, linen, cotton‑linen blends) and lightly structured or unlined garments to allow airflow.

## Suggested Capsule (12‑15 items)
- 2 lightweight tees  
- 1 heavier tee  
- 1 linen or linen‑cotton button‑down  
- 1 light oxford shirt  
- 1 chambray shirt  
- 1 breathable overshirt  
- 1 pair of linen trousers  
- 1 pair of lightweight chinos  
- 1 pair of dark jeans  
- 1 crewneck or light merino sweater  
- 1 soft unstructured jacket  
- 1 windbreaker or light coat  

These items mix easily, support year‑round layering, and stay airy in warm weather.

## Color Palette
- **Base neutrals**: navy, olive, sand/cream  
- **Accents**: muted burgundy or rust  
- Warm‑skin recommendation: olive & sand; cool‑skin: navy & slate.  
The palette stays cohesive without relying on black/gray. See [[concepts/color_palette]].

## Linen Button‑Downs (3‑piece selection)
1. **Off‑white / cream linen** – clean, versatile for dressier looks.  
2. **Soft washed navy linen** – adds depth, pairs with all neutrals.  
3. **Muted olive linen** – integrates with the olive base and layers well.  

All three share the same breathable linen fabric, ensuring consistent comfort across seasons.

## Seasonal Layering Strategy
- **Summer**: prioritize the off‑white/cream linen shirt; use the navy or olive linen on slightly cooler days.  
- **Transitional (spring/fall)**: the navy oxford (or linen oxford) becomes the daily base; layer the olive linen under lightweight knits or jackets.  
- **Winter**: linen shirts serve as indoor layers; the navy and olive pieces sit under sweaters, overshirts, or the unstructured jacket.  

This rotation maximizes outfit variety while keeping the wardrobe minimal and breathable.
