# Summary of Session 39 (2025‑07‑15)

**Main contribution:** Practical, low‑impact laundry methods to keep work uniforms and everyday clothing fresh without accelerating wear, plus a simple post‑service vinegar rinse to remove industrial detergent scent.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/cold-water-washing]]: Use cold water for all fabrics; reduces fiber damage and detergent residue.  
- [[concepts/mild-detergent]]: Choose unscented, mild detergents (e.g., wool‑silk formulas) to protect fibers and avoid strong scents.  
- Inside‑out washing: Turn garments inside out before washing to protect outer fabric and preserve color.  
- Air‑drying: Air‑dry whenever possible; if using a dryer, select low heat and remove items while still damp.  
- Spot‑cleaning: Clean high‑wear areas (collars, underarms) between washes to extend full‑wash intervals.  
- Mesh laundry bag: Use a mesh bag for knits, silks, and other delicate items to reduce agitation damage.  
- [[concepts/vinegar-rinse]]: Add 1–2 tbsp white vinegar to a cold rinse cycle to neutralize industrial detergent odors without leaving a vinegar smell.  
- Fabric‑specific care: Tailor washing method to fabric type (cotton, poly blends, wool/cashmere, etc.).  
- Delicates routine: Simple at‑home protocol – mild detergent, cold gentle cycle, mesh bag, inside‑out, air‑dry.

## Practical Recommendations
| Fabric | Washing Tips |
|--------|--------------|
| **Cotton** | Cold water, mild detergent, low‑heat dryer or line‑dry; turn inside out. |
| **Poly/blend synthetics** | Cold gentle/quick cycle, less detergent, extra rinse; airflow drying preferred. |
| **Wool, cashmere, other delicates** | Hand‑wash or short cold machine wash in mesh bag, mild detergent, lay flat to dry. |
| **Uniforms from service** | After pickup, run a cold rinse with 1–2 tbsp [[entities/white_vinegar]] per load; air out before storage. |

## At‑Home Delicates Routine (Step‑by‑Step)
1. **Detergent:** Small amount of unscented, mild detergent for wool/silk.  
2. **Preparation:** Turn items inside out; place delicate pieces in a mesh laundry bag.  
3. **Cycle:** Cold water, gentlest cycle (or short cycle if needed).  
4. **Drying:** Gently squeeze water, lay flat or hang; avoid dryer, or use low heat if unavoidable.  
5. **Post‑service scent removal:** Add 1–2 tbsp white vinegar to a cold rinse for uniforms; let air dry.

These guidelines provide a repeatable, low‑effort workflow that keeps uniforms crisp, delicates intact, and eliminates lingering industrial detergent odors.
