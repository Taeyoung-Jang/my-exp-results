# Capsule Wardrobe

A **capsule wardrobe** is a deliberately small collection of clothing pieces that intermix easily, allowing you to create a variety of outfits while keeping the total item count low. The goal is to achieve a functional, cohesive look with minimal decision fatigue and a focus on quality, versatility, and seasonal adaptability.

## Core Principles
- **Limited Quantity**: Typically 12‑15 key items that cover tops, bottoms, outerwear, and a few accessories.
- **Interchangeability**: Each piece should pair well with many others, creating multiple outfits from a few garments.
- **Consistent Palette**: Choose colors that complement each other; this reduces the need for many distinct items.
- **Seasonal Flexibility**: Items should be breathable for warm months and easy to layer for cooler weather.

## Capsule Wardrobe from Session 06
In the conversation on 2024‑07‑15, the assistant outlined a practical capsule focused on **breathable fabrics** and a **color palette** that stays cohesive without relying on black or gray. The recommended items were:

- 2 lightweight tees
- 1 heavier tee
- 1 linen or linen‑cotton button‑down
- 1 light oxford shirt
- 1 chambray shirt
- 1 breathable overshirt
- 1 pair of linen trousers
- 1 pair of lightweight chinos
- 1 pair of dark jeans
- 1 crewneck or light merino sweater
- 1 soft unstructured jacket
- 1 windbreaker or light coat

These pieces form a **capsule wardrobe** that works year‑round, leveraging natural, airy fabrics for comfort in summer and still providing layering options for colder months.

## Benefits
- **Simplicity**: Fewer choices make daily dressing faster.
- **Quality over Quantity**: Investing in higher‑quality items reduces wear and extends lifespan.
- **Travel‑Friendly**: A concise set packs easily.
- **Eco‑Conscious**: Less over‑consumption and waste.
