# Production Scheduling for Weekend Market Baking

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A structured weekly plan that allocates limited evening hours to batch‑produce, proof, and finish baked goods so a solo baker can reliably service a Saturday market stall.

---

## Core Idea
The goal is to turn a small amount of weekday kitchen time (≈2 hours per night) plus a single longer block on Friday (≈6 hours) into a complete inventory for a Saturday market. The schedule relies on:
- **Batch production** of a shared base dough for multiple items.
- **Cold‑proofing** and freezing to extend usable time.
- **Strategic sequencing** so the most time‑intensive steps happen when you have the most available time (Friday).

## Typical Week‑Long Timeline (from [[summaries/session-33_2025-08-12]])
| Day | Time Block | Task | Notes |
|-----|-----------|------|-------|
| **Monday** | 8‑10 pm | Mix one large cookie base dough, split, add flavor mix‑ins, scoop portions, freeze. | Produces two flavors (e.g., chocolate‑chip, oatmeal) ready for Friday bake. |
| **Wednesday** | 6‑8 pm | Mix enriched dough for buns/rolls, initial rise, then refrigerate overnight. | One kilo of flour yields ~24 buns; cold rest improves handling. |
| **Thursday** | 7‑9 pm | Shape chilled enriched dough: 12 cinnamon rolls, 12 cardamom buns; prepare glazes/fillings; return to fridge for proof. | Keeps shaping separate from baking, fits within 2‑hour window. |
| **Friday** | 12 pm‑6 pm (≈6 h) | **Baking & Finishing**: <br>• Bake frozen cookie portions (60‑90 min). <br>• Cool, package cookies. <br>• Bake cinnamon rolls and cardamom buns (30‑45 min each). <br>• Cool, glaze/ice, package. <br>**Packing & Prep**: Load trays, extra packaging, signage, cash float, tools. | All high‑heat work occurs in one block; cooling time fills gaps. |
| **Saturday** | 7‑8 am | Light re‑warm buns, final icing on rolls, load stall, quick surface wipe. | Minimal effort; inventory ready to sell from 9 am. |

## Key Principles
1. **Batch‑share ingredients** – A single enriched dough supports multiple products, reducing prep variety.
2. **Front‑load prep** – Mix doughs early in the week when you have short windows; bake only when you have a larger continuous block.
3. **Cold proof & freeze** – Refrigeration extends dough life, letting you shape on a weekday and bake later.
4. **Inventory sizing** – Start with ~2 dozen rolls, 2 dozen buns, 4 dozen cookies; adjust after a few markets based on sell‑through.
5. **Buffer time** – Use cooling periods as natural buffers; they prevent the schedule from feeling rushed.

## Practical Tips
- **Prep the night before**: Lay out all tools, parchment, and packaging on Friday to avoid last‑minute searches.
- **Label frozen cookie portions** with flavor and date to prevent mix‑ups.
- **Track bake times**: Keep a simple log (date, quantity baked) to fine‑tune future batch sizes.
- **Safety**: Ensure dough stays at safe temperatures (<40 °F for refrigerated, ≤ 4 °C for frozen) to comply with local food‑safety rules.
