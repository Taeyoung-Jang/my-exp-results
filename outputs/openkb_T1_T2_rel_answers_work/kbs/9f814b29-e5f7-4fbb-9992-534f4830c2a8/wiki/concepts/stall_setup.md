# Stall Setup and Operations for Market Vendors

**Definition**: A set of practical steps and considerations that enable a single‑person vendor to prepare, transport, display, and sell baked goods at a weekend market with minimal stress and maximal compliance.

---

## Overview
The concept draws from the detailed workflow outlined in [[summaries/session-33_2025-08-12]], focusing on the transition from kitchen production to on‑site sales. It connects closely with [[concepts/menu_planning]] and [[concepts/production_schedule]] because the stall setup must align with what is produced and when.

---

## Key Elements
1. **Pre‑Market Packing**
   - Pack baked items in display‑ready containers (boxes, trays, bags).
   - Include extra packaging for on‑site restocking.
   - Bundle tools: tongs, napkins, gloves, sanitizer wipes, small trash bag.
   - Prepare signage, price cards, and any required labeling (ingredient list, allergen info).

2. **Stall Infrastructure**
   - Table covering, display stands or risers, and any market‑provided hardware.
   - Tent weights or safety straps if required by the [[entities/saturday-market]].
   - Vendor badge or cottage‑food permit (see [[entities/cottage_food_permit]]) placed in an easily reachable pouch.

3. **Payment Systems**
   - Charged phone and card reader; portable power bank.
   - Cash float with small bills and coins; secure pouch for cash.

4. **Food Safety Essentials**
   - Hand sanitizer spray or wipes.
   - Thermometer (if the market mandates temperature checks).
   - Cleanable surface mat for the vendor to work on.

5. **Timing and Order of Operations**
   - **Friday Night**: Complete baking, cooling, glazing, then pack all items.
   - **Friday Packing Order**: bake → cool → finish → pack products → pack tools → pack display items.
   - **Saturday Morning**: quick re‑warm buns if needed, final icing, check seals, load pre‑packed bins, perform a brief wipe‑down, then set up the stall.

---

## Sample Checklist (Printable)
### Friday Night
- **Baking & Finishing**: bake cookies, cinnamon rolls, and cardamom buns; cool; glaze/ice.
- **Packing**: box/bag goods; add extra packaging; gather tongs, napkins, sanitizer, trash bag.
- **Stall Setup Items**: table cover, display stands, risers, weights, vendor badge.
- **Payments**: charge phone, pack float, secure cash pouch.
- **Food Safety**: thermometer, sanitizer, label packaged items.

### Saturday Morning
- **Baking & Finishing**: re‑warm buns, apply final icing to rolls, verify sealed packs.
- **Load‑Out**: grab pre‑packed bins, cooler bag with ice packs, water, personal snack.
- **Stall Setup**: spread table cover, arrange display, place signs, secure weights.
- **Payments**: ensure phone/card reader powered, cash float ready.
- **Food Safety**: sanitizer at hand, hand wipes/gloves.
- **Don’t Forget**: keys, wallet, phone charger, extra pens, tape, scissors, water, sunscreen, hat.

---

## Integration with Planning
- The **menu** chosen in [[concepts/menu_planning]] determines packaging size and display needs.
- The **production schedule** in [[concepts/production_schedule]] dictates when items are ready for packing, ensuring the Friday night workflow stays within the ~6‑hour window.
- Using a **home oven** ([[entities/home_oven]]) limits batch size, so the checklist emphasizes efficient batch handling and minimal on‑site prep.

---

## Practical Tips
- Stage all Saturday‑morning items by the door on Friday night to reduce morning rush.
- Keep a “ready‑to‑go” tote that contains all recurring items (badge, cash, charger) so nothing is left behind.
- Review market rules each season; permits and safety gear may change.
