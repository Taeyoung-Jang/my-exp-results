## Summary of session-33_2025-08-12

### Key Topics
- **Menu planning**: selecting a focused 3‑4 item menu (cinnamon rolls, cardamom buns, chocolate‑chip & oatmeal cookies) that share a base dough. [[concepts/menu_planning]]
- **Production schedule**: week‑long prep timeline (Mon‑Fri) with limited 2‑hour weekday slots and ~6 h Friday block. [[concepts/production_schedule]]
- **Batch production**: one enriched dough batch → 24 buns (12 rolls, 12 buns); one cookie base batch → 48 cookies (24 each flavor).
- **Inventory management**: starter inventory (2 dozen rolls, 2 dozen buns, 4 dozen cookies) and how to adjust after a few markets.
- **Time management**: detailed time blocks (e.g., Mon 8‑10 pm cookie dough, Wed 6‑8 pm enriched dough, Thu shaping, Fri baking).
- **Food safety**: cold‑proofing, refrigeration, labeling, permit checks, sanitizer, thermometer. Includes reference to a cottage‑food permit. [[entities/cottage_food_permit]]
- **Stall setup**: packing checklist, display, weights, signage, cash float, card reader. [[concepts/stall_setup]]
- **Pricing strategy**: cost‑plus pricing (ingredients + labor + margin).
- **Branding**: simple name, consistent packaging, trust signals.
- **Permits**: local cottage‑food rules and vendor badge requirements.

### Practical Output
- A printable **Friday night + Saturday morning checklist** divided into sections (baking/finishing, packing, stall setup, payments/cash, food safety) with suggested order and “don’t forget” items.

The document serves as a step‑by‑step blueprint for a solo baker using a home oven to launch a weekend market stall (Saturday market) without overnight marathons. [[entities/home_oven]] [[entities/saturday-market]]
