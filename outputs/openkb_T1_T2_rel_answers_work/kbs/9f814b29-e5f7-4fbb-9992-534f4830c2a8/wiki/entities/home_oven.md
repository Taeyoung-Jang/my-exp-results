# Home Oven (product)

A **home oven** is a conventional residential baking appliance that provides the heat source for the small‑batch production outlined in [[summaries/session-33_2025-08-12]]. It is the primary equipment for the solo baker planning a weekend stall at a [[entities/saturday-market]].

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Capacity**: Suitable for a single batch of enriched dough (≈1 kg flour) and one batch of cookie dough, matching the quantities suggested in the production plan.
- **Scheduling**: Works within the limited time blocks of the [[concepts/production_schedule]] (e.g., 2‑hour weekday slots and a 6‑hour Friday bake window).
- **Menu Compatibility**: Enables the preparation of the shared‑dough menu—cinnamon rolls, cardamom buns, chocolate‑chip cookies, and oatmeal cookies—without needing commercial‑grade equipment.
- **Food Safety**: Allows the baker to keep dough refrigerated, perform cold‑proofing, and meet [[entities/cottage_food_permit]] labeling and temperature requirements.
- **Stall Setup**: Since the oven is home‑based, baked goods are packed and transported to the market, tying into the [[concepts/stall_setup]] checklist (packaging, transport, and on‑site display).
