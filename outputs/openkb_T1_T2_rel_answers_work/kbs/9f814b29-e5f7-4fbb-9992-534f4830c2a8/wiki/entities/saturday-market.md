# Saturday Market (event)

**Type:** event

**Description:** A local weekend market that operates on Saturdays, providing a venue for independent vendors to sell food, crafts, and other goods.

**Key Facts from Session 33 (2025‑08‑12):**
- The user secured a lease for a **bakery stall** at this market and must be present for the entire sales window (9 am–2 pm). 
- The market requires vendors to be on‑site for opening, setup, and the full sales period each week, imposing a strict time commitment.
- This setting drives the need for a **compact menu**, efficient **production schedule**, and a detailed **stall setup** checklist to ensure the solo baker can manage baking, packing, and sales without overnight work.
