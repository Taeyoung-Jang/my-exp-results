For you, that might look like covering all required monthly bills first (rent or mortgage, utilities, insurance, groceries, transportation), which often lands around half your take‑home income. After that, you can direct a portion toward long‑term security by putting money into retirement savings and an emergency fund. Whatever remains becomes your flexible or “fun” spending.

A very workable setup is to aim for bills to take roughly half your monthly income, emergency fund contributions around ten percent until you’ve built up at least three to six months of expenses, retirement around fifteen to twenty percent if possible, and the rest going to discretionary spending. For a simple spreadsheet, you only need a few columns: one for the category, one for the planned monthly amount, and one for what you actually spent.
