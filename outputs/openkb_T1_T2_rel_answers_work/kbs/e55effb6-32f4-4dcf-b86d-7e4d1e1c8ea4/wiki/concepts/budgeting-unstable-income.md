# Budgeting for Unstable Income

**Definition**: A budgeting approach that builds a financial plan around the lowest reliable earnings, using separate layers for essential costs and variable surplus to handle income volatility.

## Core Principles
1. **Lowest‑Income Baseline**
   - Establish your *must‑cover* layer based on the lowest monthly income you can reliably expect. This baseline includes housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, minimum debt payments, and a tiny emergency cushion.
2. **Flex Layer**
   - Treat any earnings above the baseline as a *flex* layer. Pre‑define how this extra is allocated so decisions aren’t made on the fly.
3. **Buffer Creation**
   - Allocate a fixed portion of each surplus paycheck to a **no‑call buffer** (see [[concepts/financial-buffering]]). A realistic target is 2 weeks to 1 month of bare‑bones expenses.
4. **Sinking Funds**
   - Direct part of the surplus to savings for irregular or large expenses (car repairs, school supplies, etc.).
5. **Discretionary Spending**
   - Reserve a small, predetermined share for personal wants to avoid feeling deprived.

## Practical Implementation (from [[summaries/session-36_2023-09-15]])
- **Monthly Bare‑Bones Template**
  - Rent/Mortgage: 40–55 %
  - Utilities (electric, gas, water): 7–15 % total
  - Phone/Internet: 4–8 %
  - Groceries: 10–18 %
  - Transportation: 5–10 %
  - Car insurance & payment: 3–12 %
  - Minimum debt & essentials: as required
  - Remaining amount becomes the *emergency cushion* for the month.

- **Weekly Worksheet** (see [[concepts/weekly-financial-planning]])
  - Record weekly paycheck and mandatory bills.
  - After subtracting fixed costs, apply the *paycheck rule*:
    - 40 % → No‑call buffer
    - 30 % → Sinking funds
    - 20 % → Discretionary spending
    - 10 % → Flexible overflow (can be re‑assigned as needed)

## Benefits
- **Stability**: Essentials are always covered, even during “no‑call” weeks.
- **Predictability**: Pre‑set percentages remove emotional decisions at payday.
- **Flexibility**: The weekly worksheet lets you adapt to sudden schedule changes while staying aligned with the monthly baseline.
