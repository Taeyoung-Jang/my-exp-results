# Summary of Session 36 (2023‑09‑15)

This conversation provides a practical framework for people with highly variable work hours—specifically substitute teachers—covering both **monthly budgeting** and a **weekly worksheet** that map back to core financial categories.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/budgeting-unstable-income]]** for unpredictable income: base the budget on the lowest reliable monthly earnings.
- **Bare‑bones monthly template** with percentage ranges for rent, utilities, groceries, transportation, debt, and a small emergency cushion.
- **Handling swing weeks** by separating a *must‑cover* layer from a *flex* layer.
- **[[concepts/financial-buffering]]** / no‑call buffer: aim for a safety net of 2 weeks to 1 month of bare‑bones expenses; initially target two weeks.
- **Sinking funds**: allocate a portion of extra earnings to irregular costs (car repairs, clothing, school supplies).
- **Discretionary spending**: preserve a small, pre‑defined share for personal wants to avoid feeling deprived.
- **[[concepts/weekly-financial-planning]]** and meal prep: anchor predictable routines (meal prep, grading) and keep a flexible list of tasks that can fill any time gaps.
- **Financial cushioning**: maintain both a monetary and time buffer to absorb surprise meetings or last‑minute assignments.

## Monthly Bare‑Bones Budget Template
- **Rent/Mortgage**: 40–55 %
- **Electric**: 3–6 %
- **Gas/Heat**: 2–5 %
- **Water/Trash**: 2–4 %
- **Phone & Internet**: 4–8 % total
- **Groceries**: 10–18 %
- **Transportation (fuel, transit)**: 5–10 %
- **Car Insurance**: 3–6 %
- **Car Payment**: 5–12 % (if applicable)
- **Minimum Debt Payments**: as required
- **Medication/Essentials**: as needed
- **Emergency/Cushion Fund**: any remaining amount, even a small weekly contribution

```
Extra remaining after the lines above: __________

Apply percentage rule to the extra:
- No‑call buffer (40%): __________
- Sinking funds (30%): __________
- Discretionary (20%): __________
- Flexible overflow (10%): __________
--------------------------------------
```

### Paycheck Rule (percentages)
1. **Cover imminent bills** (rent share, utilities, insurance, minimum debt).  
2. **Allocate 40 % of remaining** to the no‑call buffer savings.  
3. **Allocate 30 %** to sinking funds for irregular expenses.  
4. **Allocate 20 %** to discretionary spending (or save if not needed).  
5. **Reserve 10 %** as flexible overflow to shift to any category that needs extra support that week.

## Practical Tips
- Review the upcoming week each Sunday; adjust the floating task list as new hours appear.  
- Batch‑cook flexible meals and keep grab‑and‑go options for unpredictable mornings.  
- Keep a short “30‑minute” and a “1‑hour” errand list to match any time gaps.  
- Periodically compare monthly totals against the bare‑bones plan to ensure the low‑income baseline remains covered.
