# Summary of session-13_2020-09-15

**Document Type:** Conversation transcript (budgeting guidance)

**Main Contribution:** A step‑by‑step monthly budgeting framework for a professor with a consistent $6,800 take‑home salary, including specific dollar allocations, a ready‑to‑paste Google‑Sheets table, and handling of pre‑tax retirement contributions.

---

## Key Concepts
- **Fixed‑cost budgeting** – allocating known monthly expenses (mortgage, utilities, car, etc.) before discretionary items. fixed cost budgeting
- **Percentage‑based allocation** – using rough % rules (≈50% bills, ≈10% emergency fund, ≈10% extra retirement, ≈15‑20% fun) as a starting point. percentage allocation
- **Emergency fund sinking** – targeting $700/month to build a 3‑6‑month safety net. emergency fund
- **IRA / extra retirement** – recommending an additional $700‑$800 monthly IRA contribution alongside a 12% pre‑tax university plan. extra retirement savings
- **Granular discretionary categories** – breaking “fun” spending into Dining Out, Coffee & Snacks, Hobbies, Streaming, Gifts, Travel. discretionary spending
- **Annual/Irregular sinking fund** – a $300/month line for non‑monthly costs (car maintenance, home repairs, conferences, medical). sinking fund
- **Spreadsheet layout** – a simple three‑column (Category, Planned Amount, Actual Amount) table that can be pasted directly into Google Sheets. simple budget spreadsheet

## Handling Pre‑Tax Retirement
The 12 % payroll retirement contribution (pre‑tax) does **not** appear in the take‑home‑pay budget. The recommendation is to note it in a side cell, e.g., "12 % pre‑tax retirement via payroll", to keep it visible without double‑counting.

## Actionable Takeaways
1. **Copy the CSV‑style table** above into a blank Google Sheet; the columns will auto‑populate.
2. **Track actual spend** each month in the "Actual Amount" column to compare against the plan.
3. **Adjust** the discretionary line items if you find you consistently overspend or have extra room for savings.
4. **Monitor** the emergency fund and sinking fund balances; once the emergency fund reaches the 3‑6‑month target, re‑allocate that $700 toward either the IRA or discretionary goals.
5. **Document the pre‑tax retirement** note somewhere in the sheet for complete financial visibility.
