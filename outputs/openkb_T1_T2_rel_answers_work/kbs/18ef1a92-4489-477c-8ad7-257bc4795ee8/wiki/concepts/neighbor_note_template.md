# Neighbor Note Template

**Definition**: A set of concise, low‑stakes written messages that a new resident can place on their door to announce their presence and invite casual interaction.

## Overview
Neighbor note templates are designed to facilitate [[concepts/casual_neighbor_interaction]] without creating pressure. They balance friendliness with brevity, making it easy for both the note‑writer and the reader to engage in a brief, natural exchange.

## Core Variations
1. **Super‑brief** – 1–2 sentences, minimal detail.
   - Example: “Hi, I’m Sam in 4B and just moved in. Feel free to say hi if you see me around.”
2. **Warmer** – Adds a friendly tone and a short invitation.
   - Example: “Hello! I’m Sam in 4B. I’m new to the building and would love to say hello if we cross paths.”
3. **With optional phone number** – Includes contact info for building‑related matters only.
   - Example: “Hi, I’m Sam in 4B. If you need to reach me for anything building‑related, my number is ___, no pressure at all.”

All templates follow the principle of [[concepts/low_pressure_gestures]]: they extend a subtle invitation without demanding a response.

## Placement Guidance
- **Attach to your own door** – Keeps the note visible to neighbors who already pass by and avoids the intrusiveness of slipping notes under doors or posting in communal spaces.
- **Design tips** – Handwrite on a small card, place it slightly off‑center, and use a casual font or simple paper to signal personal intent rather than a formal sign.

## Privacy & Safety Considerations
Including a phone number is optional. When privacy is a concern, omit contact details and rely on face‑to‑face encounters later. This aligns with the [[concepts/privacy_safety]] guidance discussed in the source.
