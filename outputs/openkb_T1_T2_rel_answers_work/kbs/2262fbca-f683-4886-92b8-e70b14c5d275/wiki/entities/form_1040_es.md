# Form 1040‑ES

**Type:** work (IRS estimated tax payment form)

**Purpose:** Provides worksheets and vouchers for individuals, especially self‑employed gig workers, to calculate and submit **quarterly estimated tax** payments to the **IRS**.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-40_2025-03-14]]:**
- Used as part of the **tax safe harbor** method where taxpayers can divide last year’s total tax (found on [[entities/form_1040]]) by four and pay each quarter.
- The form can be mailed with a check or submitted electronically via the IRS “Pay IRS” online portal.
- Gig workers are advised to set aside about **30 %** of each payment (see [[concepts/percentage_allocation]]) and then use Form 1040‑ES to make the quarterly deposits.
- The form aligns with the broader concept of **estimated taxes** [[concepts/estimated_taxes]] and supports a **budgeting rhythm** that includes separate tax‑saving accounts [[concepts/separate_accounts]].
