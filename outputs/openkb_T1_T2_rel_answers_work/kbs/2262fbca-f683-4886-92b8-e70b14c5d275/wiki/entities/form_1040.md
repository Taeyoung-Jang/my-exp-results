# Form 1040

Form 1040 is the standard United States individual income tax return used to report a taxpayer's annual income, deductions, credits, and **total tax** liability. 

## Role in session‑40_2025‑03‑14
- The assistant directs the user to locate the **"total tax"** line on Form 1040 when applying the **tax safe harbor** method (pay‑what‑you‑owed‑last‑year ÷ 4). 
- This line provides the dollar amount needed to calculate the quarterly estimated tax payments for gig‑economy workers.
