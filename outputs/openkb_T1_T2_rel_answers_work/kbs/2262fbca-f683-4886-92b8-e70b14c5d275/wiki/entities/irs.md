# Internal Revenue Service (IRS)

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is the United States federal agency tasked with collecting taxes and enforcing tax laws. In the context of **session-40_2025-03-14**, the IRS is referenced as the authority for:

- Providing the online "Pay IRS" portal used to submit **quarterly estimated tax** payments.
- Issuing Form 1040 and Form 1040‑ES, which are the primary documents for reporting annual tax liability and making estimated tax payments respectively. See [[entities/form_1040]] and [[entities/form_1040_es]].
- Defining the **tax safe harbor** rules that allow gig workers to avoid penalties by paying either 90 % of the current year's tax or 100 % of the prior year's tax, divided into four installments. Related concepts: [[concepts/estimated_taxes]], [[concepts/tax_safe_harbor]].
- Influencing the recommended **percentage allocation** of income for taxes (about 30 % of each gig payment) as a simple budgeting rule. See [[concepts/percentage_allocation]].

The document also notes that many gig workers set up a **separate account** to automatically transfer the tax portion of each payment, aligning with the idea of automatic transfers and a budgeting rhythm. Related concepts: [[concepts/separate_accounts]], [[concepts/automatic_transfers]], [[concepts/budgeting_rhythm]].

For further details on how the IRS is discussed in the budgeting advice, refer to the session summary: [[summaries/session-40_2025-03-14]].
