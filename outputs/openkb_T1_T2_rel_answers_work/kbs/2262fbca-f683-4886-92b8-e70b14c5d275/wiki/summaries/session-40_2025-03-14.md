# Summary of session-40_2025-03-14

**Main topic:** Practical budgeting and tax‑payment strategies for gig‑economy workers in the US.

## Key Points
- **Weekly budgeting approach** – Identify *bare‑minimum* weekly expenses and allocate incoming income into three buckets:
  - **Taxes** (≈30 % of each payment) – covers federal, self‑employment, and state taxes.
  - **Emergency/savings** (5‑10 %) – use a [[concepts/separate_accounts]] for automatic transfers.
  - **Essentials & discretionary spending** – the remainder funds rent, food, gas, etc.
- **Tracking methods** – recommended simple solutions:
  - Separate tax‑savings account (most frictionless) – can be set up with [[concepts/automatic_transfers]].
  - Optional spreadsheet for those who like visual tracking.
  - Budgeting apps are optional but can be added later.
- **Tax safe‑harbor guidance** – two common safe‑harbor options:
  1. Pay **30 % of each payment** as you earn it (easy, penalty‑free).
  2. **Pay‑what‑I‑owed‑last‑year ÷ 4** method:
     - Locate the **“total tax”** line on last year’s [[entities/form_1040]].
     - Divide that amount by four and make equal quarterly payments.
     - Works even if last year’s taxes were covered by W‑2 withholding.
     - If gig work starts mid‑year, only the remaining quarters need payments.
- **Quarterly estimated tax filing** – use the [[entities/irs]] **Pay IRS** online portal or mail [[entities/form_1040_es]]; most states have analogous online portals and forms.

## Practical Steps
1. List weekly essential costs.
2. When a gig shift pays, immediately transfer 30 % to a dedicated tax‑savings account using [[concepts/automatic_transfers]].
3. Transfer 5‑10 % to an emergency‑savings [[concepts/separate_accounts]].
4. Use the remainder for weekly expenses.
5. For safe‑harbor quarterly payments, retrieve last year’s total tax from [[entities/form_1040]], split into four, and pay each due date via the [[entities/irs]] online system (or state portal).
