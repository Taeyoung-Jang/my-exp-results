# Slack (product)

Slack is a cloud‑based messaging and collaboration tool that many teams use for real‑time communication, file sharing, and asynchronous updates.

## Role in the session‑33 guidance
- The weekly pre‑read template is designed to be posted directly in a **Slack** channel so each team member can quickly fill in the required sections.
- By leveraging Slack’s thread feature, action items and owners can be recorded inline, keeping the list visible and up‑to‑date.
- Slack serves as the hub where the **[[concepts/async-pre-read]]** is shared, enabling the team to skim updates before the **[[concepts/weekly-check-in-structure]]** meeting.
- The platform also supports linking to the **[[concepts/action-tracking-doc]]**, allowing the running tracker to be referenced or edited collaboratively.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-06-01]]
- The document proposes a lightweight Slack post format with sections for focus, changes, blockers, and resourcing notes.
- Slack threads are suggested for capturing decisions and assigning owners during the meeting.
- The approach assumes all team members are already using Slack for daily coordination.

## Related concepts
- **[[concepts/async-pre-read]]** – the short, structured update posted in Slack.
- **[[concepts/weekly-check-in-structure]]** – the 20‑30 minute alignment meeting that builds on the Slack pre‑read.
- **[[concepts/action-tracking-doc]]** – the shared document for tracking open actions, budget notes, and hiring status, which can be linked from Slack.
