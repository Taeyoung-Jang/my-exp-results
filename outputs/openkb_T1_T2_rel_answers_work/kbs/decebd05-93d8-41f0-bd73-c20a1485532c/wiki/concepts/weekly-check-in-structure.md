# Weekly Check‑In Structure

**Definition**: A lightweight, time‑boxed (20‑30 min) weekly meeting designed to surface changes, decisions, blockers, and resourcing signals while keeping the focus on alignment rather than status reporting.

## Core Components

1. **Async pre‑read** – team members post a short update before the meeting. The template includes:
   - *Focus for the week* (one‑sentence priority)
   - *What changed since last week*
   - *Blockers or decisions needed*
   - *Resourcing notes* (early budget or hiring hints)
   
   This pre‑read is captured in a Slack message or shared doc and is referenced as [[concepts/async-pre-read]].

2. **30‑minute agenda** – the live meeting follows a tight flow:
   - Rapid round‑up of discussion‑worthy items from the pre‑read.
   - Team review of shared risks or decisions.
   - Confirmation of action items and owners.
   - Brief "meeting snapshot" note for historical context.

   The agenda’s brevity ensures the meeting only covers items that truly need group input; if nothing requires discussion, the meeting can be skipped.

3. **Action‑tracking document** – a single running doc that persists week to week, containing:
   - **Open actions** (owner, next step, target date)
   - **Done** (completed items for audit)
   - **Budget notes** (small purchases, upcoming requests)
   - **Hiring notes** (candidate pipeline status)
   - **Meeting snapshot** (one‑sentence log per week)
   
   This structure is described in [[concepts/action-tracking-doc]].

## Benefits
- **Efficiency**: By collecting status asynchronously, the live meeting is limited to decision‑making and problem‑solving.
- **Transparency**: Action items and resourcing signals live in a shared doc, reducing reliance on separate tools.
- **Predictability**: A fixed 20‑30 min slot creates a rhythm and signals that the structure is working when the meeting is often brief.
- **Early risk detection**: "Resourcing notes" surface budget or hiring implications early without derailing the agenda.

## Implementation Tips
- Require each participant to fill out the pre‑read at least 30 minutes before the meeting.
- Use a single Slack channel or a shared document for the pre‑read and the action‑tracking doc.
- At meeting end, immediately update the "Open actions" section with owners and due dates.
- Archive completed actions in the "Done" section to maintain a lightweight history.
