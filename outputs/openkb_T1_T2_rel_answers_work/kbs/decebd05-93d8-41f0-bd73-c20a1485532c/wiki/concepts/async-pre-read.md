# Async Pre‑Read Template

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A lightweight, structured format that team members fill out before a weekly check‑in, allowing the live meeting to focus only on discussion‑worthy items.

---

## Purpose
The async pre‑read eliminates the need for lengthy status updates during the meeting itself. By capturing each person’s priorities, changes, blockers, and resourcing signals ahead of time, the team can:
- Keep the live sync under 30 minutes.
- Surface budget or hiring implications early.
- Ensure every action item has a clear owner before the meeting ends.

---

## Core Sections (as proposed in [[summaries/session-33_2025-06-01]])
1. **Focus for the week** – One‑sentence top priority.
2. **What changed since last week** – Brief notes on any shifts that affect the team.
3. **Blockers or decisions needed** – Items that truly require group input.
4. **Resourcing notes** – Early signals about budget needs or hiring implications.

Each section is kept deliberately short to promote quick skimming and to keep the pre‑read itself lightweight.

---

## How It Integrates with the Weekly Check‑In
The pre‑read feeds directly into the [[concepts/weekly-check-in-structure]] agenda:
- Participants only discuss items flagged in sections 3 and 4.
- Decisions and owners are recorded inline, then transferred to the [[concepts/action-tracking-doc]].
- The running tracker document is updated in real time, preserving continuity across weeks.

---

## Practical Template (copy‑paste ready for Slack)
```
––––––––––––––––––––––––
Weekly check‑in pre‑read
Focus for the week:
(One sentence on your top priority.)

What changed since last week:
(Anything others should know that might affect plans.)

Blockers or decisions needed:
(Only list items that actually require input.)

Resourcing notes:
(Any early signals about budget needs or possible hiring implications.)
––––––––––––––––––––––––
```

---

## Benefits
- **Time efficiency:** Meetings stay within the 20‑30 minute window.
- **Transparency:** All status information lives in a single, searchable thread.
- **Actionability:** Owners and next steps are captured immediately, feeding the [[concepts/action-tracking-doc]].
