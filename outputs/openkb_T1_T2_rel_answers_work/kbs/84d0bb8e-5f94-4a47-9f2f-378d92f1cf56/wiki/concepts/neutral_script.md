## Neutral Script

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A short, non‑judgmental phrase used during a body‑awareness cue that signals observation and then directs attention back to the ongoing activity.

### Purpose
The neutral script keeps body‑awareness practices from turning into anxiety‑driven health checks. By explicitly ending with an instruction to *move on*, it prevents the mind from lingering on sensations or turning the check‑in into a decision point about whether one is "okay."

### Core Characteristics
- **Brevity:** 5–10 seconds to say or think.
- **Neutral language:** No evaluative words like "good" or "bad."
- **Action‑oriented closure:** Ends with a cue such as “moving on,” “continue,” or a similar phrase that redirects focus to the next task.

### Example Scripts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-03-13]]
1. **"Noticing, then moving on."**
   - Take one easy breath, register whatever is felt, and resume the activity.
2. **"How’s the weather in my body?"**
   - Observe the sensation like a weather report (sunny, cloudy, etc.) and keep going.
3. **Fallback acknowledgment:** "Okay, I hear you," followed by returning to the next action.

### How to Use
1. **Identify a single cue** in your routine (e.g., reaching for an item, waiting at a crosswalk). [[concepts/single_cue_check_in]]
2. **Recite the neutral script** silently or aloud.
3. **Observe briefly** (1–2 breaths) without judging.
4. **Immediately transition** back to the task, using the script’s closing cue.

### Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works with [[concepts/single_cue_check_in]] to embed brief body checks into everyday moments.
- Supports the **if/then** patterns described in [[concepts/if_then_shopping_rules]] by providing the neutral language component.
- Helps avoid **red flags** of over‑monitoring (see [[concepts/red_flags]]).

### Practical Tips
- **Keep a short list** of scripts you like and practice them until they feel automatic.
- **Match scripts to context** – a more casual phrase for shopping, a slightly more formal one for commuting.
- **Re‑evaluate periodically** to ensure the script remains neutral and doesn’t slip into evaluative language.
