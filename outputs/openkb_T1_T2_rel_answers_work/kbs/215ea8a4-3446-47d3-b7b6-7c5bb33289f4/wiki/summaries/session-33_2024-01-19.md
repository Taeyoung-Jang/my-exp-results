# Summary of session‑33 (2024‑01‑19)

**Main focus** – How to sharpen an already solid sleep routine by prioritising the most impactful levers.

## Key Recommendations

1. **Wake‑time consistency**  
   - Anchor the internal clock by waking at the same time each day, including weekends, within a 15‑20 minute window (ideally no more than ±30 min on weekends).  
   - This small adjustment yields the clearest gains in alertness and daily smoothness. See [[concepts/consistent-sleep-schedule]].

2. **Morning light exposure**  
   - After stabilising wake time, increase bright‑light exposure in the early morning to reinforce the [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]].

3. **Caffeine timing**  
   - Cut late‑day caffeine; if further optimisation is needed, shift caffeine earlier in the day. See [[concepts/caffeine_timing]].

4. **Nap strategy**  
   - Target a **10–25 minute** nap taken **early afternoon** (no later than mid‑afternoon).  
   - This provides a quick boost without lowering sleep pressure for bedtime.

5. **If a nap isn’t possible**  
   - Use a “second‑best” approach: brief bright‑light exposure, a short walk, or 10 minutes outdoors to counteract the dip.

## Practical Tips for the Coming Week

- Choose a fixed wake‑up time (e.g., 7:00 am) and stick to it daily, allowing a ±15 min drift on weekends.  
- If a late night occurs, **do not** sleep in for hours; instead, get up near the usual time and consider a short nap if needed.  
- Keep caffeine consumption before the early afternoon and avoid it after 2 pm.  
- Test a 15‑minute nap at 1 pm; note any impact on evening sleep onset.
