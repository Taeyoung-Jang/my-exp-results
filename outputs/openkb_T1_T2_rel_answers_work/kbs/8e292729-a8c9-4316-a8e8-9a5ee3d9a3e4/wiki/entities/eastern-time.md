# Eastern Time

Eastern Time (ET) is one of the primary time zones in the United States. In the context of [[summaries/session-03_2025-06-15]], it is recommended as the **reference time zone** for coordinating meetings across the country.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Reference Zone**: The assistant suggests anchoring all meeting times to Eastern Time and explicitly labeling the zone, leveraging [[concepts/reference_time_zone]].
- **Offset Relationships**: ET is **one hour ahead** of [[entities/central-time]], **two hours ahead** of [[entities/mountain-time]], and **three hours ahead** of [[entities/pacific-time]]. This relationship is described by [[concepts/time_zone_offset]].
- **Daylight‑Saving Consistency**: All four main continental zones, including Eastern Time, shift simultaneously for daylight saving, so the offsets remain constant. See [[concepts/daylight_saving_time]] for details. Exceptions are regions like [[entities/arizona]] and [[entities/hawaii]], which do not observe DST and therefore have different relative offsets.
- **Practical Use**: Example conversions in the session show how a 10:00 AM ET meeting maps to 9:00 AM CT, 8:00 AM MT, and 7:00 AM PT, and how a 3:00 PM ET meeting maps to 2:00 PM CT, 1:00 PM MT, and 12:00 PM PT.
