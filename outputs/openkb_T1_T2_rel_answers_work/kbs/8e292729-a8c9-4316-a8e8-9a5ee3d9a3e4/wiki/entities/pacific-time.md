## Pacific Time (other)

Pacific Time (PT) is the westernmost of the four main continental U.S. time zones. In the context of the conversation *session-03_2025-06-15*, PT is used as the reference for a user located on the West Coast.

- **Offset**: PT is **three hours behind** [[entities/eastern-time]] and **one hour behind** [[entities/central-time]]; it follows the [[concepts/time_zone_offset]] pattern of a one‑hour step per zone moving eastward.
- **Reference zone usage**: The assistant recommends anchoring meeting times to a single [[concepts/reference_time_zone]] (often Eastern Time) and then converting to PT for participants.
- **Daylight‑Saving Time**: PT observes [[concepts/daylight_saving_time]] in sync with the other three main zones, so the three‑hour offset remains constant through DST transitions.
- **Exceptions**: While PT itself follows DST, the document notes that regions such as [[entities/arizona]] (most of it) and [[entities/hawaii]] do **not** observe DST, leading to temporary offset changes relative to PT.

**Key facts from the document**
1. Example conversion: 10:00 AM ET → 7:00 AM PT; 3:00 PM ET → 12:00 PM PT.
2. During the Monday after the spring‑forward change, PT’s offset to ET, CT, and MT stays the same because all four zones shift together.
