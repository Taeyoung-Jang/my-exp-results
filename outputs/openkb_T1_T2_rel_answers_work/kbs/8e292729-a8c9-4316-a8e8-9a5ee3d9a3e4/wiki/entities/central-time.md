## Central Time (CT)

Central Time (CT) is the time zone covering the central United States. It is **one hour behind** [[entities/eastern-time]] and **one hour ahead** of [[entities/pacific-time]], with a two‑hour offset to [[entities/mountain-time]].

- **Reference zone**: Often used as a conversion point when the reference is [[entities/eastern-time]] (see [[concepts/reference_time_zone]]).
- **Offset**: CT is UTC‑6 in standard time and UTC‑5 during daylight‑saving time, reflecting a consistent [[concepts/time_zone_offset]] of one hour from its neighboring zones.
- **Daylight‑saving**: CT observes daylight‑saving time in sync with the other three main continental zones, so the relative offsets stay the same around the spring‑forward and fall‑back transitions (see [[concepts/daylight_saving_time]]). Regions that do not observe DST, such as [[entities/arizona]] and [[entities/hawaii]], experience a temporary shift relative to CT.
- **Example conversions** (from [[summaries/session-03_2025-06-15]]):
  - 10:00 AM Eastern → 9:00 AM **Central** → 8:00 AM Mountain → 7:00 AM Pacific.
  - 3:00 PM Eastern → 2:00 PM **Central** → 1:00 PM Mountain → 12:00 PM Pacific.

Central Time is a key reference when coordinating meetings across the U.S., ensuring that participants in both eastern and western zones can accurately translate meeting times.
