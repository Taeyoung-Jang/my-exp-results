# Mountain Time

Mountain Time (MT) is one of the four main continental U.S. time zones. It is typically **two hours behind Eastern Time** and **one hour ahead of Pacific Time**.

- **Standard offset:** UTC‑7 (which is 2 h behind Eastern Time's UTC‑5).
- **Daylight‑saving offset:** UTC‑6, shifting together with Eastern, Central, and Pacific zones.
- **Daylight‑saving behavior:** Follows the same DST transition as the other three continental zones, keeping the relative offsets constant. [[concepts/daylight_saving_time]]
- **Reference for scheduling:** Often used as a reference zone in coordination guides, as shown in [[summaries/session-03_2025-06-15]]. [[concepts/reference_time_zone]]
- **Key relationships:**
  - Adjacent to [[entities/eastern-time]] (3 h ahead) and [[entities/pacific-time]] (1 h behind).
  - Shares DST changes with [[entities/central-time]].
  - Notable exception: most of [[entities/arizona]] does not observe DST, causing temporary offset differences.

**Conceptual context:** The offset relationship is a classic example of [[concepts/time_zone_offset]] across U.S. zones.
