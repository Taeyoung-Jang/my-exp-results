# Summary: Boston Newcomer Practical Checklist (session‑32_2025‑09‑15)

**Main focus** – A concise, action‑oriented guide for someone who has just moved to Boston, covering the immediate steps after getting a MA driver’s license and a CharlieCard.  The assistant breaks down four practical domains:

## 1. Utilities & Basic Services
- Set up **electricity** (typically **Eversource**) and **gas** (usually **National Grid**) in your name – most accounts can be opened online in minutes.
- Verify that water, internet, and trash services are transferred/activated.
- Keep a copy of the lease or a recent utility bill for later‑use in city applications.

## 2. Resident Parking Permit Process
- **Required documents**:
  1. Proof of Boston residence – lease, recent utility bill, or similar.
  2. Vehicle registration showing the same Boston address.
  3. Massachusetts driver’s license with the Boston address.
- **Steps**:
  1. Update vehicle registration at the **RMV** before applying (strongly recommended).
  2. Submit the online application via the City of Boston website (takes a few minutes).
  3. Expect approval in 1–2 days; if the street isn’t in the system, you may need an in‑person visit.
- **Common gotchas**:
  - Mismatched address between registration and license leads to rejection.
  - New buildings or streets sometimes missing from the system.
  - Annual renewal is not auto‑reminded – set a personal reminder.
- **Temporary work‑arounds while waiting**:
  - Use private parking lots/garages.
  - Park on metered or non‑resident‑only streets.
  - Visitor passes are limited to a few neighborhoods and tied to a household; moving permits only cover a specific moving window.
- **Neighborhood quirks**:
  - North End & Beacon Hill have stricter resident‑only zones and tighter enforcement hours, but the application form and document requirements are citywide.

## 3. Transit & Weather Apps
- **Transit app** – generally faster and more accurate for real‑time MBTA arrivals, leveraging crowd‑sourced updates.
- **MBTA official app** – solid but slower to reflect delays.
- **Weather** – apps with strong storm alerts (e.g., **SnowScope**, National Weather Service app) are preferred during nor’easters.

## 4. Quick “First‑Week” Checklist
| ✅ Action | Details |
|---|---|
| Utilities | Set up Eversource (electric) & National Grid (gas); confirm water/internet. |
| Parking Permit | Update RMV registration → Apply online → Note renewal date. |
| Transit | Install **Transit** app; add MBTA routes you’ll use. |
| Weather | Install **SnowScope** or NWS alerts for nor’easter warnings. |
| Neighborhood Scan | Walk the block to locate grocery stores, coffee shops, libraries, and well‑plowed streets. |
| Temporary Parking | Identify a nearby private garage or permissible street while permit processes. |
