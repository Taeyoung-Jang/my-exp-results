# Summary of session-03_2025-06-15

**Topic:** Scheduling meetings across U.S. time zones and handling daylight‑saving transitions.

- **Reference zone recommendation** – Anchor all times to a single zone (commonly [[entities/eastern-time]]) and always label the zone explicitly. Calendars can then auto‑convert for participants.
- **U.S. continental time‑zone offsets** – [[entities/pacific-time]] = [[entities/eastern-time]]‑3 h, [[entities/mountain-time]] = [[entities/eastern-time]]‑2 h, [[entities/central-time]] = [[entities/eastern-time]]‑1 h. The zones step one hour eastward. [[concepts/time_zone_offset]]
- **Daylight‑saving consistency** – All four main zones switch simultaneously, so relative offsets stay constant. Exceptions: most of [[entities/arizona]] (does not observe DST) and [[entities/hawaii]] (always UTC‑10). [[concepts/daylight_saving_time]]
- **Example conversions** –
  - 10:00 AM [[entities/eastern-time]] → 9:00 AM [[entities/central-time]] → 8:00 AM [[entities/mountain-time]] → 7:00 AM [[entities/pacific-time]]
  - 3:00 PM [[entities/eastern-time]] → 2:00 PM [[entities/central-time]] → 1:00 PM [[entities/mountain-time]] → 12:00 PM [[entities/pacific-time]]
- **DST transition scenario** – Offsets remain unchanged on the Monday after the spring‑forward change for the four main zones; only non‑DST regions see a shift.
