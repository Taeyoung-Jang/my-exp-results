# Summary of session-15_2024-06-15

**Main focus**: Providing practical, quick‑to‑prepare breakfast options and mid‑afternoon snack strategies for people who skip lunch and rely on a two‑meal routine (breakfast & dinner) while maintaining steady energy on a busy schedule.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/meal-timing]]: keeping meals at consistent times to avoid energy crashes.  
- [[concepts/balanced-breakfast]]: combining protein, slow‑digesting carbohydrates, and healthy fats.  
- [[concepts/snack-top-up]]: a small, protein- or fat‑rich snack to bridge the afternoon gap.  
- Busy schedule nutrition: quick, repeatable meals that require minimal preparation.  
- Energy stabilization: avoiding blood‑sugar spikes and crashes through food composition.

## Recommended Breakfast Strategies
1. **Protein‑rich & carb‑balanced** (e.g., eggs + toast, oatmeal + peanut butter + berries, Greek yogurt + granola + fruit).  
2. **Smoothies with protein** (yogurt or protein powder, banana, frozen berries).  
3. **Overnight oats** for multi‑day use.  
4. **Cottage cheese or avocado toast** for lighter yet satiating options.

## Grab‑and‑Go Snack Ideas
- Handful of nuts (almonds, cashews, mixed nuts)  
- Fruit (banana, apple)  
- Cheese stick or protein bar  
- Hummus with crackers or veggie sticks  
- Small portion of mixed nuts or granola bar  

## Sample 5‑Day Plan
| Day | Breakfast | Afternoon Snack |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|
| **Monday** | Oatmeal with peanut butter & berries | Almond pack or banana |
| **Tuesday** | Greek yogurt with granola & fruit | Cheese stick or protein bar |
| **Wednesday** | Scrambled eggs with spinach + toast | Apple or cashews |
| **Thursday** | Yogurt‑based smoothie (banana, berries) | Hummus + crackers or veggies |
| **Friday** | Cottage cheese with fruit & honey | Mixed nuts or granola bar |

The plan emphasizes **consistency**, **minimal prep**, and **nutrient balance** to keep energy levels steady without a midday lunch.

## Practical Tips
- Prepare overnight oats or portioned nuts the night before.  
- Keep water intake steady throughout the day.  
- Aim to eat breakfast at roughly the same time each morning.  
- Choose snacks that combine protein + healthy fat for the best [[concepts/snack-top-up]].
