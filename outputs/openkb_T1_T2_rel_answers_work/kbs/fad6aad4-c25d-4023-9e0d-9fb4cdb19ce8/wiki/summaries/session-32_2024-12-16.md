# Summary of Session 32 (2024‑12‑16)

**Main focus:** Practical strategies for arranging daily meals and snacks so they fit a busy schedule while maintaining energy and avoiding excess hunger.

## Meal Structure & Timing
- Anchor eating to natural hunger cues rather than a rigid clock.
- Typical pattern that works: **small breakfast → balanced lunch → light dinner** with an optional **mid‑afternoon snack**.
- Keep gaps between eating episodes short enough to prevent a “ravenous” phase that drives overeating.
- Consistency (e.g., eating breakfast within the same hour each day) helps regulate appetite.

## Portion‑Size Adjustments
- Evaluate post‑meal satiety 2‑3 hours after eating:
  - Still hungry → increase protein/fiber or overall size.
  - Heavy/sluggish → reduce portions or simple carbs.
- Aim for **150‑250 kcal** at breakfast and **100‑150 kcal** for an afternoon snack, adjusting based on personal energy needs.

## Protein & Fiber for Satiety
- Include a modest amount of protein (4‑7 g) and fiber in each eating occasion to prolong fullness.
- Examples: Greek yogurt, hard‑boiled egg, nuts, nut‑butter, cheese stick, hummus.

## Shelf‑Stable Snack Options
- Choose items that stay fresh in a bag/desk and are low in added sugar:
  - **Mini protein bars** (e.g., RXBAR Mini, KIND Mini) – ~100 kcal, ≤5 g added sugar.
  - **Single‑serve nut‑butter packets** (≈90 kcal) paired with 2‑3 whole‑grain crackers.
  - **Handful of plain nuts** – ~100 kcal per small palm‑sized portion.
  - **Trail‑mix (nut‑heavy, minimal dried fruit)** – control portion to stay within calorie range.
- Look for labels showing **protein or healthy fat 4‑7 g**, **added sugar ≤5 g**, and **total carbs balanced with fiber**.

## Practical Portion Guides
- **Nuts:** a small handful (≈¼ cup) = 100‑150 kcal.
- **Nut‑butter:** half a standard packet (≈½ tbsp) on 2‑3 crackers = 100‑150 kcal.
- **Mini bar:** one bar = ~100 kcal (already portioned).
- **Fruit (e.g., banana):** pair with nut‑butter for additional carbs if needed.

## Energy‑Stabilizing Tweaks
- If late‑afternoon hunger appears, add a **protein‑rich bite** (nuts, cheese, hummus) rather than a carb‑only snack.
- Slightly boost lunch with **slow‑digesting carbs** (quinoa, whole‑grain bread) or extra veggies to extend energy.
- Light dinner should still contain protein and some volume; if morning hunger persists, modestly increase dinner size.
