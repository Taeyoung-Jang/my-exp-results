# Balanced Breakfast

**Definition**: A balanced breakfast pairs protein, slow‑digesting carbohydrates, and healthy fats to provide steady, long‑lasting energy, especially useful for those who skip lunch.

## Core Elements
- **Protein** – eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, or protein powder in smoothies.
- **Slow‑digesting carbohydrates** – oatmeal, whole‑grain toast, fruit, or rice.
- **Healthy fats** – nut butter, nuts, avocado, or seeds.

These components work together to avoid rapid blood‑sugar spikes and subsequent crashes, supporting consistent performance through the morning and into the afternoon.

## Application in Session‑15 (2024‑06‑15)
The session outlines several practical breakfast examples that embody a balanced breakfast:
- Oatmeal with peanut butter and berries
- Greek yogurt with granola and fruit
- Scrambled eggs with spinach and toast
- Smoothie with yogurt or protein powder, banana, and frozen berries
- Cottage cheese with fruit and honey

Each combines protein, carbs, and fats to keep the user satiated until dinner when lunch is omitted.
