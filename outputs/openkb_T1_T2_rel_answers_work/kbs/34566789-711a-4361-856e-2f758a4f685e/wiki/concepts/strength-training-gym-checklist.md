# Strength‑Training Gym Checklist

A practical framework for assessing whether a gym can reliably support a 3 × week barbell routine (squat, bench, deadlift) during peak after‑work hours (5 – 7 pm).  The checklist is derived from the recommendations in [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]] and aligns with broader [[concepts/gym-selection-criteria]].

---

## Core Scoring Items (1 – 5)

| Item | What to Observe | Why it Matters |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **Rack availability** | Count total squat/bench racks and note how many are in use at 5‑7 pm. | Ensures you can perform core lifts without waiting. |
| **Condition of bars & plates** | Inspect for rust, wear, and proper storage. | Safety and smooth lifting experience. |
| **Space around racks** | Walk the floor; see if you can set up a deadlift platform without crowding. | Reduces frustration and injury risk. |
| **Wait times** | Observe a rack for 2‑3 min; note if it frees up. | Direct proxy for peak‑hour bottlenecks. |
| **Ventilation & comfort** | Check airflow, temperature, and overall vibe. | Affects performance on tired workdays. |
| **Layout flow** | See if you can move between lifts without back‑tracking. | Saves time and keeps workouts efficient. |
| **Distance / commute** | Note travel time from home (e.g., [[entities/portland]]). | Convenience drives consistency. |
| **Cleanliness & chalk policy** | Look at floor and bench cleanliness; see if chalk is allowed. | Hygiene and grip performance. |
| **Contract clarity** | Ask explicit cancellation steps; note staff response. | Prevents hidden fees (see [[concepts/gym-tour-red-flags]]). |
| **Price fairness** | Compare cost to the equipment/access you’ll actually use. | Aligns budget with value. |
| **Motivation check** (most important) | "Would I still come here on a low‑motivation day?" | Gauges overall fit for long‑term adherence. |

---

## How to Use the Checklist
1. **Score each item** during a brief (≈10 min) tour, focusing on the 5‑7 pm window.
2. **Total or weigh** scores (the motivation check can be given extra weight).
3. **Compare side‑by‑side** for multiple gyms.
4. **Reflect** after the visit: picture yourself training three times a week—does the gym feel effortless?

---

## Quick Red‑Flag Scan (see [[concepts/gym-tour-red-flags]])
- More than two‑thirds of racks occupied during your visit.
- People hovering or waiting with belts on.
- Staff evasive about cancellation or fees.
- Poor ventilation or noisy environment.
