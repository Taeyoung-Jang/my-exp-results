# Gym Selection Criteria

**Gym selection criteria** is a decision‑making framework that helps you compare fitness facilities based on the factors that most affect long‑term attendance and satisfaction. The guidance derives from the practical advice in [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]].

## Core Dimensions
1. **Equipment vs. Classes**
   - Determine whether you are motivated by a wide range of machines and free‑weight stations or by scheduled, instructor‑led classes.  If you get bored working solo, class variety becomes a higher priority; if you prefer self‑paced lifts, focus on equipment quantity and layout.
2. **Contract Terms**
   - Treat the contract as a reality check.  Key questions include: required commitment length, cancellation notice, any early‑termination fees, and whether monthly rates are locked or can increase.  Clear, written terms signal lower risk of surprise costs.
3. **Peak‑Hour Crowding**
   - Visit the gym during the exact time you plan to work out (e.g., 5 pm‑7 pm).  Observe rack occupancy, wait times, and overall floor flow.  High congestion can turn a good gym into a frustrating experience.
4. **Amenities**
   - Amenities such as saunas, pools, or premium locker rooms should only be weighted if you will actually use them.  Otherwise they can distract from the essentials that drive consistency.
5. **Location & Convenience**
   - Consider commute distance, parking or transit options, and whether the gym fits naturally into your daily routine after work.
6. **Overall Vibe & Cleanliness**
   - A clean, well‑maintained space with friendly (not pushy) staff supports habitual attendance.

## Applying the Criteria
- **Create a short checklist** of must‑haves drawn from the dimensions above.  Score each gym on a 1‑5 scale to quantify how well it meets your personal friction points.
- **Use a pressure‑proof script** (see [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]]) to extract clear answers on pricing and cancellation without getting sold on the spot.
- **Watch for red flags** during the tour – packed weight areas, evasive staff, or unclear contract language – which are detailed in [[concepts/gym-tour-red-flags]].
