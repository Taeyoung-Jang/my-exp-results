## Summary

This conversation provides a practical framework for selecting a gym that supports **strength‑training** goals, especially for after‑work visits (5–7 pm). It includes:

- **Decision criteria**: equipment vs. classes, contract terms, peak‑hour crowding, and amenities.  
- **Custom checklist** tailored to a 3×/week barbell routine, with scoring items such as *rack availability*, *condition of bars*, *crowding at target hours*, and *contract clarity*. This aligns with [[concepts/strength-training-gym-checklist]].  
- **Pressure‑proof script** for asking about pricing, cancellation steps, and fee transparency.  
- **Red‑flag indicators** during tours, notably packed weight areas and evasive staff responses; see [[concepts/gym-tour-red-flags]].  
- **Portland‑area gym types**: large chains, regional clubs, and strength‑focused boutiques; suggested locations include **[[entities/fulcrum-fitness]]**, **[[entities/hollywood-fitness]]**, **[[entities/lloyd-athletic-club]]**, and **[[entities/industrial-strength]]**.  
- **Tour timing advice**: visit between 5:15–6:15 pm, observe rack usage, wait times, and overflow behavior.  
- **Quick 10‑minute assessment**: count total racks, note proportion in use, watch for stalls, and watch for people hovering.
