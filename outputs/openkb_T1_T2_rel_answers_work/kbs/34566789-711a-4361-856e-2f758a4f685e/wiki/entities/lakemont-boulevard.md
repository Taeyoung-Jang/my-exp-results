# Lakemont Boulevard

Lakemont Boulevard is a roadway in **Bellevue**, Washington that provides access to the **Coal Creek Falls** trailhead. It is commonly used by hikers seeking a short, easy out‑and‑back hike, especially in [[concepts/rainy-hike-planning]] scenarios.

## Key Facts from Session 09 (2022‑06‑15)
- The trailhead for the **Coal Creek Falls** hike is located on Lakemont Boulevard.
- The recommended 60–90 minute route follows the main trail straight to the falls and back.
- Parking is available in a small dedicated lot and on the street along Lakemont Boulevard; spots are usually found early in the day.
- The trail can become softly muddy in drizzle, so waterproof shoes are suggested, though sturdy sneakers are acceptable.
