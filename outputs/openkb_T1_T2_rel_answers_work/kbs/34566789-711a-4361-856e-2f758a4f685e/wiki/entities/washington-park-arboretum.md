# Washington Park Arboretum

Washington Park Arboretum is a 230‑acre public park on the shores of Lake Washington in Seattle. It combines formal gardens, wetland trails, and a large collection of trees and shrubs, making it a popular spot for relaxed walks, bird‑watching, and modest outdoor recreation.
