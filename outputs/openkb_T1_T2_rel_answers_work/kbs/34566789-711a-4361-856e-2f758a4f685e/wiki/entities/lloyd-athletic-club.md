# Lloyd Athletic Club

**Type:** Organization

**Location:** Portland, Oregon (see [[entities/portland]])

Lloyd Athletic Club is highlighted in the *session‑40_2024‑06‑15* conversation as one of several Portland‑area gyms suitable for serious strength training. It is noted for having a **well‑equipped weight floor** that is relatively spacious compared to pure‑lifting boutiques, making it a viable option for barbell‑focused routines such as squats, bench presses, and deadlifts.

## Key points from the source
- **Free‑weight setup:** The club provides a decent number of squat racks, benches, and dumbbells. When touring between 5 pm and 7 pm, the recommendation is to assess **rack availability**, **platform openness**, and overall **crowding**.
- **Tour checklist relevance:** The gym should be evaluated using the [[concepts/strength-training-gym-checklist]], especially items like “Space to lift without feeling crowded” and “Contract clarity.”
- **Red‑flag awareness:** As per the [[concepts/gym-tour-red-flags]] concept, watch for staff evasion on pricing or cancellation, and for a packed weight floor during peak hours.
- **Comparison context:** It is listed alongside other Portland options such as [[entities/fulcrum-fitness]], [[entities/hollywood-fitness]], and [[entities/industrial-strength]].

## Practical advice for prospective members
1. **Visit during your typical workout window** (5:15 pm–6:15 pm) to gauge real‑time equipment usage.
2. **Count total racks** and note how many are occupied; aim for at least one‑third of racks free to avoid bottlenecks.
3. **Observe overflow behavior** – hovering lifters or makeshift deadlift spots indicate insufficient capacity.
4. **Ask the standard pricing and cancellation script** (see the conversation) to ensure contract clarity.
5. **Consider commute and parking** as part of the broader [[concepts/gym-selection-criteria]] for consistency.
