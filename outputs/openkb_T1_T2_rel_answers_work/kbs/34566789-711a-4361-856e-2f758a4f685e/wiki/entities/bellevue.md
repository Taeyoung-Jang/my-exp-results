## Bellevue

Bellevue is a city in Washington State, located just east of Seattle. It is known for its suburban neighborhoods, commercial districts, and proximity to outdoor recreation areas.

### Key Points from [[summaries/session-09_2022-06-15]]
- The **Coal Creek Falls** trail, recommended for a short, drizzly‑day hike, is situated in Bellevue.
- The trailhead is accessed via **Lakemont Boulevard**, where a small parking lot and street parking are typically available on weekends.
- The hike is described as a 60–90 minute out‑and‑back suitable for groups, with gentle terrain that drains well even in light rain.
