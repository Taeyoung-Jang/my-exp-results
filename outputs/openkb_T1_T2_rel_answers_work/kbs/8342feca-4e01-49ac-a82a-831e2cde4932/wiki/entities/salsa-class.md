# Salsa Class (event)

**What it is**: A recurring weekday salsa dancing class that the user attends in the early evening. The class starts at **6:45 PM**, lasts about an hour, and is located a **15‑minute drive** from the user's home.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-01-11]]**:
- The user leaves work around **5:30 PM** and arrives home at **6:00 PM**.
- The routine is built around a short “pit‑stop” at home before heading to the class.
- A pre‑packed snack, class clothes, and the salsa bag are positioned by the door to enable a smooth transition.
- The user has created several **[[concepts/after-work-routine]]** scripts (continuous motion, quick reset, two‑minute promise) to bridge the gap between arriving home and leaving for the class.
- The plan relies on **[[concepts/cue-based-habit]]** cues such as the bag, snack, and a single upbeat song.
- A **[[concepts/minimum-viable-fallback]]** is defined for low‑energy days, allowing the user to at least drive to the studio or do a brief warm‑up at home.
- The routine incorporates **[[concepts/energy-management]]** strategies (quick snack, hydration) and **[[concepts/emotional-checkpoints]]** (acknowledging tiredness but keeping feet moving).

**Related concepts**:
- [[concepts/after-work-routine]] – the overall structure of the post‑work transition.
- [[concepts/cue-based-habit]] – physical triggers that prompt action.
- [[concepts/minimum-viable-fallback]] – the low‑effort alternative that still counts toward habit consistency.
- [[concepts/energy-management]] – snack and hydration to sustain energy.
- [[concepts/emotional-checkpoints]] – brief mental checks to acknowledge fatigue without stopping.

**Why it matters**: The salsa class serves as the anchor event around which the user designs habit‑forming scripts, ensuring regular attendance despite after‑work fatigue.
