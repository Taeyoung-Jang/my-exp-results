# After‑Work Routine

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** A cue‑driven sequence that bridges leaving work and heading to a target activity.

---

## Overview
An **After‑Work Routine** is a short, repeatable set of actions designed to prevent the habitual “home‑then‑couch” trap that often follows a workday. By structuring micro‑cues—physical objects, brief rituals, and timing windows—the routine creates momentum that carries a person straight from the office door to the next intended commitment (e.g., a salsa class, gym session, or meeting).

## Core Elements (as illustrated in [[summaries/session-37_2025-01-11]])

| Element | What it looks like | Why it matters |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Physical Cue Setup** | Pack the salsa bag, place it by the door with keys; pre‑arrange clothes, snack, water bottle. | Removes decision friction; the environment nudges the behavior automatically. |
| **Timing Anchor** | Specific target times (e.g., 5:30 PM leave work, 6:00 PM home, 6:20 PM depart). | Gives a clear window that limits the opportunity to sit down and relax. |
| **Micro‑Ritual** | Change into class clothes, grab a snack, play one upbeat song, or set a 2‑minute timer. | Provides a brief purposeful action that signals a mental shift from “work mode” to “activity mode.” |
| **If‑Then Planning** | *If* it’s salsa night, *then* send a quick message like “Heading out = salsa.” | Keeps the intention top‑of‑mind and turns the decision into an automatic trigger. |
| **Fallback Path** | Minimum‑viable version: get to the car, drive to the studio, or do a 5‑minute home warm‑up. | Ensures the habit is maintained even on low‑energy days, preserving consistency. |

## Practical Example (from the source session)
1. **Night before**: pack bag, lay out clothes, place snack front‑center, set reminder.
2. **5:30 PM** – Leave work, mentally rename the commute as “on the way with a quick stop.”
3. **6:00 PM** – Arrive home, *do not sit*: change clothes, grab snack, pick up bag, optionally play a song.
4. **6:20 PM** – Head out; if tired, tell yourself “I’ll reassess after five minutes of driving.”
5. **6:45 PM** – Arrive at salsa class.
6. **Fallback**: if exhausted, drive to studio parking lot and allow a skip, or do a 5‑minute dance warm‑up at home.

## Benefits
- **Consistency**: Reduces reliance on willpower by automating the first steps.
- **Energy Efficiency**: Small, timed actions prevent a full “rest” period that drains motivation.
- **Flexibility**: Scripts can be adapted to other after‑work activities (gym, hobby class, volunteer work).
- **Psychological Safety**: Fallback options keep the habit intact without guilt.
