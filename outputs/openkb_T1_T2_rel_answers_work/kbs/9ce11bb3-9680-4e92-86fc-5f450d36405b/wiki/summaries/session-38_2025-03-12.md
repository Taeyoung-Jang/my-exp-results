## Summary of session‑38_2025-03-12

This document provides a **structured, energy‑conserving routine** for people experiencing mild breathlessness, focusing on a **weekly schedule** and a **complete “Grocery Day” plan**. The key contributions are:

- **Weekly template** that splits each day into a main activity, a supporting task, and ample open space, with suggestions for pacing, break definition, and when to stop vs. push through.
- **Time‑blocked example** for a typical weekday and weekend day, explaining what a “real break” looks like and how to assess fatigue mid‑task.  
  *See [[concepts/time-blocking-for-health]] and [[concepts/real-break-definition]].*
- **Concrete definitions** of *main tasks* (e.g., full grocery trip, laundry) versus *supporting tasks* (e.g., folding a few items, wiping one counter).
- **Low‑effort, satisfying break ideas** (single song, tea ritual, short stretch, brief window view) that feel purposeful without high energy cost.
- **Step‑by‑step “Grocery Day” schedule** with:
  - **Normal‑day version** (9:30 am drive → shop → brief break → pantry round → optional extra round).
  - **Tired‑day variation** (later start, lighter shopping, longer post‑unload rest, one short unpacking round).
  - **Plan B** for unexpectedly high breathlessness: prioritize perishables, allow non‑perishables to stay in bags for several hours, and stop if breathing does not settle.
  - **Warning signs** that merit contacting a clinician (persistent breathlessness, light‑headedness, chest discomfort).
- **Fridge checklist** that can be printed and posted: *Perishables first, sit down, hydrate, wait for normal breathing, keep rounds short, stop if heavy or winded*.
