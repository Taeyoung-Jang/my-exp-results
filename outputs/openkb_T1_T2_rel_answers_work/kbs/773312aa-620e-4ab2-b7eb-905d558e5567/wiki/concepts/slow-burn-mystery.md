# Slow‑Burn Mystery

**Definition**: A slow‑burn mystery is a crime or investigative narrative that prioritises gradual tension, deep immersion in setting, and methodical clue‑gathering over rapid plot twists. The story unfolds at a measured pace, allowing the reader to become absorbed in the atmosphere and the investigative process.

---

## Core Characteristics

1. **Atmospheric Emphasis** – The environment often becomes a character itself, shaping mood and influencing the plot. In the session‑02 discussion, titles like *Jar City* and *Sun Storm* were highlighted for their wintry, isolated settings that enhance the mystery.
2. **Methodical Investigation** – A focus on detailed police work, interviews, and the piecing together of clues. *Jar City* was noted as the most procedural example, exemplifying this trait.
3. **Gradual Tension Build‑Up** – Rather than immediate action, the narrative allows suspense to simmer, creating a lingering sense of dread that intensifies over time.
4. **Character‑Driven Insight** – The investigators are portrayed as smart, reflective, and often emotionally complex, contributing to both the plot and the mood.

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- Related to [[concepts/atmospheric-crime]], which emphasizes how setting contributes to the tone of a crime story.
