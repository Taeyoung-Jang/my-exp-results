# Tracking Template

**Type:** Concept

## Definition
A *tracking template* is a minimalist, printable or hand‑drawn page designed to capture key moments of a reading session without turning the activity into a burdensome project. It emphasizes brevity, presence, and ritual, allowing readers to log a book’s physical details, a memorable line, personal resonance, and a closing note.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-23]])
1. **Title and Dates** – space for the book’s title and the dates you engaged with it.
2. **The Book as an Object** – brief observations about the book’s condition, smell, or markings.
3. **Lines That Stayed With Me** – a spot for a quote or impression that lingers.
4. **What Met Me Today** – a short note on mood, setting, or why a passage resonated.
5. **Closing Note** – a concluding thought or a word you’re carrying forward when you finish the volume.

### Ultra‑short “tired‑day” version
- Date
- One line or feeling
- How I am today

Both versions fit on a single notebook page, making the habit easy to maintain.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Supports an [[concepts/intentional-reading-habit]] by providing a lightweight structure that encourages reflective reading rather than checklist completion.
- Reinforces a [[concepts/reading-ritual]] through repeated, ritualized actions (e.g., noting the book’s scent before reading).
- Can be adapted for thematic projects like [[concepts/slow-burn-mystery]] or [[concepts/scandinavian-noir]] reading journeys, where the focus remains on atmosphere and personal engagement rather than exhaustive analysis.

## Usage Scenarios
- **Personal archival** – building a quiet time‑capsule of how each book met you over a year.
- **Low‑pressure progress tracking** – keeping a thread of continuity without stress.
- **Ritual enhancement** – integrating a tactile, reflective step before and after each reading session.
