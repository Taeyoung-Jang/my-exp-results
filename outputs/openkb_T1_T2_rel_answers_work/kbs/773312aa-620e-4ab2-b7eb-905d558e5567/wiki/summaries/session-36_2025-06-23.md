# Summary of session-36_2025-06-23

**Date:** 2025-06-23  
**Topic:** Designing a low‑pressure, ritual‑focused year‑long reading habit and a simple tracking template.

## Key ideas

- **Intentional reading habit** – shift from checklist to a thematic or seasonal rhythm [[concepts/intentional-reading-habit]].
- **Year‑long reading project** – choose a guiding question, seasonal anchor books, or a personal ritual to give the year meaning without rigid deadlines.
- **Reading ritual** – incorporate small practices (reflection, a warm drink, handwritten notes) before opening each book to anchor presence [[concepts/reading-ritual]].
- **Reading journal** – a low‑effort log that captures mood, setting, and a memorable line rather than analysis.
- **Tracking format** – a one‑page handwritten template with sections for title/dates, object notes, “Lines That Stayed With Me”, “What Met Me Today”, and a closing note [[concepts/tracking-template]].
- **Minimum viable tracking** – on low‑energy days, record only date, a single line or feeling, and current mood to keep the thread alive without stress.

## Suggested template (one‑page)

```
Title and Dates:
______________________________

The Book as an Object
(notes on condition, marks, age)
______________________________________________________________

Lines That Stayed With Me
(quotes or impressions)
______________________________________________________________

What Met Me Today
(mood, place, resonance)
______________________________________________________________

Closing Note
(what you’re carrying forward)
______________________________________________________________
```
