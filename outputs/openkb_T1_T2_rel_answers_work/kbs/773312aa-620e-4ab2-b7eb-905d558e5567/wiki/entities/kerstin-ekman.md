# Kerstin Ekman

Kerstin Ekman is a Swedish writer celebrated for her literary crime fiction. In the conversation **session-02_2024-10-24**, she was highlighted as the author of *Blackwater*, an under‑the‑radar novel noted for its remote, wintry setting and a focus on literary and psychological storytelling rather than a procedural investigation.
