# Jar City

**Type:** work (novel)
**Author:** [[entities/arnaldur-indridason]]
**Original title:** *Mýrin* (Icelandic)
**Publication year:** 2000
**Series:** First book in the *Inspector Erlendur* series.

## Overview
*Jar City* is a cornerstone of [[concepts/scandinavian-noir]] and [[concepts/atmospheric-crime]]. Set in a cold, wintry Reykjavík, the novel follows Inspector Erlendur as he conducts a painstaking, methodical investigation into a decades‑old murder uncovered by modern DNA techniques. The story is celebrated for its strong sense of place, where the stark Icelandic landscape functions as a character itself, and for its slow‑burn pacing that gradually builds tension.
