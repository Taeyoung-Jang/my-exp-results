# Blackwater (Work)

**Author:** [[entities/kerstin-ekman]]

**Genre:** Literary crime, Nordic noir

**Setting:** Remote northern Swedish forests, characterized by stark, wintry isolation.

**Key Themes:** The novel emphasizes mood, memory, and community secrets over a traditional procedural investigation. It showcases a strong **sense of place** and a **slow‑burn** narrative that prioritizes psychological depth.

**Narrative Style:** More literary and psychological than procedural; the story follows multiple perspectives over an extended period, focusing on atmosphere rather than on‑page detective work.

**Relevance to Session:** Mentioned in [[summaries/session-02_2024-10-24]] as an under‑the‑radar recommendation.
