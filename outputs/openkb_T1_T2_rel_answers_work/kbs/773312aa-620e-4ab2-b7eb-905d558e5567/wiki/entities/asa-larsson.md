# Åsa Larsson

Åsa Larsson is a Swedish writer best known for the novel **Sun Storm**. In the conversation of [[summaries/session-02_2024-10-24]] she was highlighted as the author of a book that delivers a strong sense of place, with a wintry, isolated setting in far‑north Sweden and a slow‑burn narrative style.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Sun Storm** was recommended as one of the top three titles for "sense of place" alongside *Jar City* and *He Who Fears the Wolf*.
- The assistant described the novel as offering a stark, cold atmosphere that shapes the mystery, making it a prime example of [[concepts/atmospheric-crime]] and [[concepts/scandinavian-noir]].
- It was also noted for its slow‑building tension, fitting the [[concepts/slow-burn-mystery]] criteria.
