## Wedding

A wedding is a formal ceremony that marks the union of two people in marriage, often accompanied by celebrations, registries, and gift‑giving.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-01_2023-12-26]]
- **Gift‑giving etiquette**: Guests navigate expectations around how much to spend, balancing personal sentiment with social norms.
- **"Cover your plate" myth**: Discussed as a non‑universal rule; couples typically do not track whether gifts match the cost of a guest’s meal.
- **Budget guidance**:
  - Single guest: low‑to‑mid hundreds (e.g., $100–$200).
  - Couple attending together: a modest increase over the single‑guest range.
  - Adjustments for travel expenses are considered acceptable, reducing the expected gift amount.
- **Relationship‑based budgeting**: Gift amount should reflect the closeness of the guest to the couple rather than a fixed dollar amount.
