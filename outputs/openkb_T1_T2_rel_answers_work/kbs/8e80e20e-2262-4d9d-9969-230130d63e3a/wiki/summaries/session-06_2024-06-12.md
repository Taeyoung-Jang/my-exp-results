## Summary of Conversation – Session 6 (2024‑06‑12)

**Scope** – The user seeks a practical, safety‑first workflow for diagnosing a completely dead handheld device that charges via USB‑C and contains a single‑cell Li‑ion battery.  The assistant provides a high‑level, non‑procedural guide and asks for photos to refine the analysis.

### Key Diagnostic Stages

1. **External Power Verification**  
   - Check outlets, cables, adapters, and batteries.  
   - Swap known‑good components to rule out external failure.  
   - [[concepts/external_power_verification]]

2. **Initial Board‑Level Check**  
   - Measure voltage at the input connector (USB‑C jack or barrel jack).  
   - Look for any sign of life (LED flicker, vibration) that hints at partial power.  

3. **Power‑Path “Tree” Walkthrough**  
   - Follow the thickest traces from the input through **protection** (fuse, reverse‑polarity diode/MOSFET), **charging controller / power‑path IC**, **primary regulators** (buck or boost), and **secondary LDOs**.  
   - Verify each stage before moving to the next.  
   - [[concepts/power_path_troubleshooting]]

4. **Multimeter Checklist**  
   - **Diode/Ohms mode (power off):** Detect shorts, cracked solder joints, or open circuits by checking direction‑dependent voltage drops and resistance to ground.  
   - **Voltage mode (powered):** Confirm presence of expected rail voltages at each stage.  
   - [[concepts/multimeter_power_checklist]]

5. **Current‑Limited Bench Supply Test**  
   - Apply a reduced voltage with a low current limit to a suspect rail.  
   - Observe heating or current draw to locate a short without damaging components.  
   - [[concepts/bench_supply_short_detection]]

6. **Scope Diagnostics**  
   - **Enabled but overloaded buck:** Irregular/choppy switching pulse train, possible sagging output.  
   - **Regulator not enabled:** Flat line, no switching activity despite input voltage.  
   - [[concepts/buck_regulator_scope_signatures]]

7. **Physical Inspection & Photo Review**  
   - Request wide‑shot of the USB‑C area and macro shots of IC markings, inductors, and MOSFETs.  
   - Use images to map the **power tree stages** and prioritize inspection points.  
   - [[concepts/photo_based_board_analysis]]

### Safety Emphasis
- Keep probing non‑invasive until the board is proven live.  
- Treat all capacitors as potentially charged; discharge safely before handling.  
- Prefer bench‑supply current limiting over direct short‑circuit probing.
