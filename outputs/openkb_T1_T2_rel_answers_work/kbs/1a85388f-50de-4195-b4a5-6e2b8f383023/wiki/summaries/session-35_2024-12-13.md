# Summary of session-35_2024-12-13

**Date:** 2024‑12‑13  

## Core Problem
The user wants low‑cost ways to guarantee that packages are left at their apartment despite a locked front door and an often‑out‑of‑date buzzer directory.

## High‑Level Strategies
- **Timing orders**: ship early in the week to avoid weekend delays.  
- **Alternative drop‑off locations**: use workplaces, trusted neighbors, or free pickup points such as [[entities/amazon-locker]], UPS Access Points, or local stores.  
- **Clear address & instructions**: always include apartment number, buzzer details, and concise delivery notes.  
- **Leverage free carrier tools**: carrier preference tools such as communication style notifications, delivery instructions, and signature release settings in USPS Informed Delivery, UPS My Choice, and FedEx Delivery Manager.

## Carrier‑Specific Settings
### USPS Informed Delivery
- Enable **carrier release** (allow unattended delivery when permitted).  
- Add a standing note in the address profile: e.g., *“Buzz Unit 3B – name may not appear.”*  
- Turn on all **text/email notifications** for tracking and access issues.

### UPS My Choice
- Set **Default Delivery Location** to *Front Door* (or *Apartment Door*).  
- Activate **Driver Release** (allow leaving without signature).  
- Save a short instruction: *“Buzz 3B – directory may be outdated.”*  
- Enable **delivery alerts** for every package.

### FedEx Delivery Manager
- Choose **Preferred Delivery Location** = *At Front Door*.  
- Provide brief **Additional Instructions**: *“Buzz 3B – I’m home, name may be missing.”*  
- Turn on **day‑of‑delivery** and **attempted‑delivery** notifications.

## Common Gotchas in Apartment Buildings
- Missing or incorrect unit numbers in the directory.  
- Locked front doors that require a buzzer press.  
- Buildings that prohibit unattended drop‑offs in lobbies.  
- Long delivery notes are often ignored; keep instructions under 120 characters.

## Instruction Templates (≤120 chars)
1. `Buzz 3B. Name may not appear in directory.`  
2. `Please buzz 3B for access—directory not updated.`  
3. `Buzz 3B; I’m home. Name may be missing on panel.`  

**Longer optional note** (when field allows):
> *Please buzz 3B for entry. My name may not show on the directory, but I’m home and expecting the delivery.*

## Phone Number Advice
Including a phone number in the delivery note is generally discouraged because drivers are rarely permitted to call and it exposes personal contact information without improving success rates.
