### Summary
The user requested several versions of a short announcement about their office‑based work schedule and preferred ways to be contacted. The assistant produced:
- a standard friendly message,
- a concise 1–2 sentence version,
- a detailed version specifying Slack for quick questions, email for longer/non‑urgent matters, and calendar booking for proper discussions,
- a more casual wording,
- a structured version using bullet points.

- **Communication style** – balancing friendliness with clarity communication‑style

- **Message drafting** – creating multiple variants to match tone and detail level message‑drafting
- **Casual tone vs structured format** – offering both informal and bullet‑pointed versions
