# Email

Email is a digital messaging product commonly used for longer or non‑urgent communications within a team. In the document [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]], email is highlighted as the preferred channel for messages that require more detail or are not time‑critical.

## Role in Communication Preferences
- Serves as the fallback when quick questions are better handled via [[entities/slack]].
- Complements the overall [[concepts/contact-preferences]] outlined for the month’s schedule.
- Supports a balanced [[concepts/communication-style]] that mixes informal chats with more formal written records.

## Related Practices
- The drafting of the announcement messages follows principles from [[concepts/message-drafting]], ensuring clarity about when to use email versus other tools.
- Team members are encouraged to book time on the [[entities/calendar]] for discussions that go beyond what email can efficiently cover.

## Contextual Placement
- The user works primarily from the [[entities/office]] during weekdays, and communicates with the [[entities/team]] using a mix of Slack, email, and calendar invites.
