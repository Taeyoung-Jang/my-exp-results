# USPS (United States Postal Service)

USPS is the United States federal agency responsible for providing postal services across the country, including mail delivery, package handling, and retail services such as post offices and self‑service kiosks.

## Key Facts from Session 35 (2024‑12‑13)
- The user is interested in low‑cost ways to ensure deliveries are left at their apartment door despite a locked front door and an outdated buzzer directory.
- USPS offers a free tool called **Informed Delivery** that lets customers manage delivery preferences, receive **delivery notifications**, and add **delivery instructions** for individual packages.
- Important settings highlighted:
  - Enable **carrier release** (allow unattended delivery when the sender permits it).
  - Add a standing note in the address profile, e.g., *"Buzz Unit 3B – name may not appear."*
  - Turn on all **text/email notifications** for tracking and access issues.
- The assistant advises caution about the **package theft risk** when granting unattended delivery permission.
