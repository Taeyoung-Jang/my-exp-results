# FedEx (Organization)

FedEx Corporation is a major international shipping and logistics provider offering services such as overnight parcel delivery, freight, and supply‑chain solutions.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-13]]
- **FedEx Delivery Manager**: The user can set a default delivery preference of “leave at door” and add concise delivery instructions (e.g., “Buzz 3B; I’m home”).
- **Signature Release**: When a shipment is not signature‑restricted, the “leave at door” setting is honored.
- **Notifications**: Turn on day‑of‑delivery and attempted‑delivery alerts to stay informed of any access issues.
- **Gotchas**: Long notes are often ignored; keep instructions under 120 characters to increase compliance. Providing a phone number in the note is discouraged.

## Practical Tips
1. In the **Delivery Preferences** section, select **Preferred Delivery Location → At Front Door**.
2. Add a brief **Additional Instructions** field: e.g., `Buzz 3B – I’m home, name may be missing.`
3. Enable both **day‑of‑delivery** and **attempted‑delivery** alerts under **Settings → Alerts and Notifications**.

These steps help apartment residents with locked front doors and outdated buzzer directories receive packages reliably without extra cost.
