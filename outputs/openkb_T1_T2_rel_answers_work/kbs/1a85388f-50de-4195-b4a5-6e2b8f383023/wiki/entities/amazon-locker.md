# Amazon Locker

Amazon Locker is a network of secure, self‑service lockers where customers can have online purchases delivered and later retrieve them at their convenience. It is offered at many public locations such as shopping centers, grocery stores, and transit hubs.

## Key Facts from Session 35 (2024‑12‑13)
- Mentioned as a **free alternative** to home delivery to avoid missed deliveries and the need for premium carrier services. 
- Serves as a reliable fallback when a user’s apartment door is locked or the buzzer directory is outdated, reducing **package‑theft risk** and delivery attempts.
- Can be selected during checkout as the delivery address, bypassing the need for **delivery instructions** to carriers like [[entities/usps]], [[entities/ups]] or [[entities/fedex]].

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/delivery-instructions]] – instructions are unnecessary when using a locker because the package is left in a secure, designated spot.
- [[concepts/package-theft-risk]] – lockers mitigate theft risk compared to leaving parcels at a front door.
- [[concepts/carrier-preference-tools]] – carriers often allow users to redirect shipments to lockers via their online portals.
