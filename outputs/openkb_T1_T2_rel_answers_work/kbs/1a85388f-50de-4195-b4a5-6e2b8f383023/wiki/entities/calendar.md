# Calendar

Calendar is a scheduling product that allows users to book time slots for meetings and discussions. In the context of the document [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]], Calendar is recommended as the preferred method for arranging proper, in‑depth conversations.

## Key Points from the Document
- Users are advised to "grab a spot on my calendar" when a topic requires a real discussion.
- Calendar complements other contact methods such as [[entities/slack]] for quick questions and [[entities/email]] for longer or non‑urgent matters.
- The recommendation aligns with the overall [[concepts/contact-preferences]] and [[concepts/message-drafting]] strategies outlined in the session.
