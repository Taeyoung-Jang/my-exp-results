## Carrier Preference Tools

**Definition**: Tools provided by shipping carriers that let recipients configure how, when, and where packages are delivered.

### Why they matter
- Reduce missed deliveries without paying for premium services.
- Allow users to specify *leave‑at‑door* options, signature release, and delivery notifications.
- Particularly useful for apartment dwellers with locked front doors or outdated buzzer directories.

### Major free tools (as of 2024)

| Carrier | Tool Name | Key Settings |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| [[entities/usps]] | **Informed Delivery** | • Enable *carrier release* (allow unattended delivery).<br>• Add a standing delivery note such as “Buzz 3B – name may not appear.”<br>• Turn on all text/email **delivery alerts**. |
| [[entities/ups]] | **My Choice** | • Set **Default Delivery Location** to *Front Door / Apartment Door*.<br>• Activate **Driver Release** for signature‑free parcels.<br>• Save a concise instruction: “Buzz 3B – directory not updated.”<br>• Enable per‑package alerts. |
| [[entities/fedex]] | **Delivery Manager** | • Choose *Preferred Delivery Location* = *At Front Door*.<br>• Provide brief **Additional Instructions** (e.g., “Buzz 3B – I’m home”).<br>• Enable day‑of‑delivery and attempted‑delivery notifications. |

### Common Gotchas
- Missing unit numbers or outdated buzzer directories can still cause “no access” attempts.
- Blanket “leave‑at‑door” permissions increase [[concepts/package-theft-risk]]; consider the security of your lobby.
- Long delivery notes are often ignored; keep instructions under 120 characters.

### Practical Tips for Apartment Residents
1. Keep the instruction concise and confident (e.g., “Buzz 3B. Name may not appear”).
2. Update the note in each carrier’s portal whenever building access changes.
3. Combine carrier tools with free pickup points like [[entities/amazon-locker]] for maximum flexibility.
