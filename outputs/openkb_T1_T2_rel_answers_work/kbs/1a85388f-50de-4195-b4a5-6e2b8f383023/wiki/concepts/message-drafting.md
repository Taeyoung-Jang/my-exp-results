## Message Drafting

Message drafting is the process of composing short, purposeful communications that match the intended tone, audience, and medium. It involves selecting the right level of formality, structuring information for quick comprehension, and highlighting preferred contact methods.

### Key Elements
- **Audience awareness** – Tailor language to the [[entities/team]] while keeping it friendly yet professional.
- **Tone selection** – Balance between a casual voice and clear instructions, a topic explored in [[concepts/communication-style]].
- **Contact clarity** – Explicitly state preferred channels (e.g., [[entities/slack]] for quick questions, [[entities/email]] for longer topics, and [[entities/calendar]] for scheduled discussions), reflecting principles from [[concepts/contact-preferences]].
- **Structure** – Use concise paragraphs or bullet points to improve readability.

### Example from Session
In the document "session-01_2024-06-15", multiple drafts were created to convey the same schedule information:
- A **standard friendly version** that mentions being based in the [[entities/office]] during weekdays.
- A **short version** limited to 1‑2 sentences.
- A **detailed version** that specifies contact preferences: Slack for quick queries, email for longer/non‑urgent matters, and calendar invites for in‑depth discussions.
- A **casual version** and a **structured bullet‑point version**.

These variations illustrate how message drafting adapts content length, tone, and format to meet different communication needs while maintaining clarity.
