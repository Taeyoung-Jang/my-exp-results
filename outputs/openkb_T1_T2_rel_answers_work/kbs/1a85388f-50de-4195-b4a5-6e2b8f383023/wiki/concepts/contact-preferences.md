# Contact Preferences

**Contact preferences** refer to the recommended ways to reach someone based on the nature and urgency of the communication. In the context of the *session-01_2024-06-15* announcement, the preferences are:

- **Slack** – best for quick questions or brief interactions.
- **Email** – preferred for longer, non‑urgent messages that need to be tracked.
- **Calendar invites** – used when a proper, scheduled discussion is required.

These preferences aim to balance **[[concepts/communication-style]]** with efficiency, ensuring teammates know the most appropriate channel for each scenario. The guidance was crafted as part of a **[[concepts/message-drafting]]** process to create clear, friendly messages for the **[[entities/team]]**.

The source document also notes that the sender will be **[[entities/office]]**‑based during weekdays, which influences the expectation that in‑person interactions are possible when needed. 

For the full text of the original announcement, see [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]].
