# Delivery Instructions

**Definition**: Delivery instructions are short, actionable messages left for parcel carriers (e.g., [[entities/usps]], [[entities/ups]], [[entities/fedex]]) that clarify how to access a residence and where to place a package when the recipient is not directly reachable.

## Core Elements
- **Clarity** – Use direct language; avoid long explanations.
- **Location Details** – Mention buzzer/unit number and any quirks (e.g., "directory may not show my name").
- **Permission** – State that unattended drop‑off is acceptable if the carrier’s signature‑release setting is enabled.
- **Security Awareness** – Acknowledge potential [[concepts/package-theft-risk]] but keep instructions brief to avoid encouraging theft.

## Best‑Practice Templates (≤120 chars)
1. `Buzz 3B. Name may not appear in directory.`
2. `Please buzz 3B for access—directory not updated.`
3. `Buzz 3B; I’m home. Name may be missing on panel.`

**Longer optional note** (when the field allows more characters):
> Please buzz 3B for entry. My name may not show on the directory, but I’m home and expecting the delivery.

## How Delivery Instructions Fit Within Carrier Preference Tools
- [[concepts/carrier-preference-tools]] let users set default drop‑off locations and enable **signature release**. Delivery instructions complement these settings by giving concrete access information.
- In the USPS Informed Delivery dashboard, the "Add Delivery Instructions" link adds a standing note for the address.
- UPS My Choice’s "Delivery Instructions" field accepts the short templates above and works with the "Driver Release" toggle.
- FedEx Delivery Manager’s "Additional Instructions" field behaves similarly.

## Interaction With Communication Style and Message Drafting
- Effective delivery instructions adopt a concise [[concepts/communication-style]] that respects the carrier’s limited time.
- Drafting the note follows [[concepts/message-drafting]] principles: keep it under 120 characters, use active verbs, and avoid apologetic language.

## Contact Preferences
- Generally **do not include a phone number** in the instruction field, as carriers are rarely permitted to call and it adds privacy risk. Use the carrier’s built‑in notification system instead (see [[concepts/contact-preferences]]).

## Real‑World Example
The guidance above derives from the practical scenario described in [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-13]], where a user with a locked front door and an outdated buzzer directory needed concise, reliable instructions for each carrier.
