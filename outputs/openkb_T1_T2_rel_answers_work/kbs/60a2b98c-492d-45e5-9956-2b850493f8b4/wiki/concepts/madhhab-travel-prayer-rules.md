# Madhhab‑specific Rules for Travel, Shortening (Qasr) and Combining (Jamʿ) Prayers

This concept explains how the four major Sunni madhhabs differ in applying the travel‑related rulings of **qasr** (shortening) and **jamʿ** (combining) for the five daily prayers. The details are drawn from the discussion in [[summaries/session-14_2023-06-11]].

---

## Core Definitions
- **Qasr** – Reducing the four‑rakʿah prayers (Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) to two rakʿahs when a traveller meets the minimum distance and intention criteria.
- **Jamʿ** – Performing two consecutive prayers at one time, either **Jamʿ Taqdim** (earlier) or **Jamʿ Taʾkhir** (later). In practice this usually means praying Dhuhr with Asr and Maghrib with Isha together.

---

## Minimum Travel Distance
| Madhhab | Minimum distance (approx.) |
|---------|----------------------------|
| **Hanafi** | ~48 mi / 77‑80 km (one‑way) |
| **Shafi‘i** | ~48 mi (same practical threshold) |
| **Hanbali** | ~48 mi (similar to Shafi‘i) |
| **Maliki** | ~48‑50 mi (slightly flexible) |

All schools agree that a genuine out‑of‑town journey qualifies; the exact measurement is only relevant for borderline cases.

---\n## Duration of Travel Status
| Madhhab | Stay limit before qasr/jamʿ no longer applies |
|---------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Hanafi** | 15 consecutive days of intended residence |
| **Shafi‘i** | 4 days |
| **Hanbali** | 4 days |
| **Maliki** | 4 days (counted similarly) |

If the traveller plans to stay beyond the limit, the travel rulings cease and regular prayers resume.

---

## Combining (Jamʿ) Permissions
- **Hanafi**: Generally **does not permit** jamʿ for ordinary travel, rain, or hardship. The only canonical allowance is at **Arafah** and **Muzdalifah** during Hajj. In daily life Hanafi scholars suggest “sorting” – praying Dhuhr at the end of its window and Asr at the beginning, keeping each prayer technically in its own time.
- **Shafi‘i**: Allows jamʿ during travel, and also for hardship such as rain or illness. Both Jamʿ Taqdim and Jamʿ Taʾkhir are permissible.
- **Hanbali**: Similar to Shafi‘i; permits jamʿ in travel and for legitimate difficulties (e.g., severe weather).
- **Maliki**: Permits jamʿ in travel and, in some opinions, for rain, but is more cautious than Shafi‘i/Hanbali.

---

## Practical Hanafi Office Approach (From the Source)
- Pray each salah **within its prescribed time**, preferably at the **edges** of lunch or afternoon breaks.
- Use “sorting” rather than true jamʿ: Dhuhr at the latest possible minute, Asr immediately after its start; similarly for Maghrib/Isha.
- If a meeting truly blocks prayer, perform the missed prayer **as soon as the obstacle lifts**, still within the overall time window if possible.
