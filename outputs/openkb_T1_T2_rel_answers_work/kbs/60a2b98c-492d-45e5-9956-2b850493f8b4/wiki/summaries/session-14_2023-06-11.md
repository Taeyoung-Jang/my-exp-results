# Summary of session-14_2023-06-11

**Main contribution** – A practical framework for Sunni Muslims (especially Hanafi) to weave core acts of worship (salah, dhikr, Qur’an, adab) into a 9‑5 work schedule, handle travel‑related shortening/combining, and avoid guilt or waswasa.

## Daily routine integration
- Treat the five daily prayers as **anchors**; fit them into existing pauses (pre‑commute, lunch break, end‑of‑day).  
- Use **tiny dhikr blocks** (e.g., SubhanAllah, La ilaha illa Allah) after each prayer or during commutes.  
- Keep a **small Qur’an habit** (½ page or a few verses) after Fajr and before bed, or during the commute.  
- Embed **adab** in workplace interactions (patience, honesty, gentleness) as lived worship.  
- This aligns with [[concepts/daily-worship-integration]].

## Discreet work‑place prayer
- Keep a compact prayer mat in a bag or desk.  
- Use empty conference rooms, quiet corners, or a parked car for Dhuhr/Asr.  
- If meetings block you, pray **as soon as the obstacle lifts**; a short delay is permissible.  
- If you cannot step out, employ the “end‑of‑window” strategy (praying Dhuhr at the end of its time, then Asr as soon as its time begins), which is accepted in Shafi‘i and Hanbali and tolerated in Hanafi.  

## Missed prayers & Qur’an days
- **Qada** rule: one missed prayer = one make‑up prayer; no special formulas needed.  
- Adopt a “pray and move on” mindset; avoid replaying the mistake.  
- If unsure whether a prayer was missed, assume it was performed and continue.  

## Travel: qasr & jamʿ
- **Qasr**: shorten 4‑rak‘ah prayers to 2 when on a legitimate journey.  
- **Jamʿ**: combine Dhuhr‑Asr and Maghrib‑Isha during travel; also allowed for rain/illness in most schools except Hanafi.  
- Hanafi specifics:  
  - Minimum distance ≈ 48 mi / 77‑80 km.  
  - Travel status ends after **15 days** of intended stay.  
  - Jamʿ only permitted at [[entities/arafah]] and [[entities/muzdalifah]] (Hajj); otherwise rely on “sorting” within prayer windows.  
- Other madhhabs (Shafi‘i, Hanbali, Maliki) allow jamʿ during travel, rain, or hardship with shorter stay limits (≈4 days).  
- These details are covered by [[concepts/madhhab-travel-prayer-rules]].

## Managing waswasa
- View missed acts as **normal human slip‑ups**, not sins.  
- Make up once, then let go; avoid obsessive checking.  
- Emphasize sincerity and return over perfection.  
- Guidance aligns with [[concepts/waswasa-management]].

## Practical Hanafi office approach
- Pray each salah **within its time**, preferably at the **edges** of lunch or afternoon breaks.  
- Use quick, focused prayers; no need to combine for work pressure.  
- If truly impossible, pray immediately after the obstacle, still within the time if feasible.  
- This respects Hanafi rulings while keeping the routine sustainable.
