# Allah (other)

**Description:** Allah is the unique, absolute God in Islamic theology, the creator and sustainer of the universe.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-14_2023-06-11]]**
- The assistant advises that when a prayer is missed, one should simply make it up later, trusting that **Allah** does not keep a punitive score.
- The discussion emphasizes returning to worship and seeking forgiveness from **Allah** without falling into guilt or obsessive worry (waswasa).
- The practical routine presented is framed as a way to stay connected to **Allah** throughout the workday via prayer, dhikr, and Qur’an recitation.
