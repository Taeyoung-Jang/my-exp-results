# Hanbali School of Thought

The **Hanbali** school is one of the four major Sunni Islamic legal (fiqh) schools, founded by **Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal**.  It is known for its strict reliance on the Qur'an and authentic hadith, with comparatively limited use of analogical reasoning (qiyāsa) and consensus (ijmāʿ).

## Key points from the session‑14 summary

- In the context of daily worship integration for a busy 9‑5 schedule, the Hanbali approach allows *"sorting"* (praying Dhuhr at the end of its window and Asr as soon as the Asr window opens) as a way to handle tight meeting schedules.  This is mentioned alongside the Hanafi and Shafi‘i positions.
- Regarding travel, the Hanbali school **does permit** combining (jamʿ) the prayers Dhuhr‑Asr and Maghrib‑Isha when a person is on a legitimate journey, and also allows shortening (qasr) the four‑rakʿah prayers to two rakʿahs.
- The Hanbali stance on combining for hardship (e.g., rain, illness) is more flexible than the Hanafi view, aligning with the broader Sunni consensus that combining is permissible in travel and certain emergencies.
