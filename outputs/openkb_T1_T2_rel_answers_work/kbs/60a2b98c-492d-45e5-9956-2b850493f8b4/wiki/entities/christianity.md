# Christianity

Christianity is a global religious tradition founded on the life, death, and resurrection of **Jesus Christ**. It encompasses a wide variety of denominations, theological perspectives, and cultural expressions, but shares core beliefs in the divinity of Christ, the significance of the cross, and the call to discipleship.
