# Qur’an (work)

The **Qur’an** is the primary holy text of Islam, believed to be the word of [[entities/allah]] revealed to the Prophet Muhammad. In the context of *session‑14_2023‑06‑11*, the Qur’an is highlighted as a key component of a practical daily worship routine for a busy Sunni Muslim.

## Role in Daily Worship Integration
- Suggested habit: recite a small portion (e.g., half a page or a few verses) after **Fajr**, during the commute, or before bedtime. This aligns with the broader framework of [[concepts/daily-worship-integration]] that encourages embedding sacred practices into existing daily pauses.
- The routine emphasizes **consistency over volume**, encouraging brief, regular engagement rather than lengthy sessions that could lead to burnout.

## Managing Missed Qur’an Sessions
- When a Qur’an reading is missed, the guidance mirrors the approach to missed prayers: simply resume the next day without guilt. This practice ties into [[concepts/waswasa-management]], which advises treating slip‑ups as normal human moments and avoiding obsessive self‑critique.

## Interaction with Travel and Prayer Rules
- While travel‑related shortening (qasr) and combining (jamʿ) primarily affect the five prayers, the same principle of flexibility can be applied to Qur’an reading: during travel, one may combine short recitations with prayer times or listen to audio recitations while on the move. The relevant jurisprudential details are covered in [[concepts/madhhab-travel-prayer-rules]].

## Related Entities
- The Qur’an is central to the faith of [[entities/sunni-islam]] adherents and is respected across the major Sunni madhhabs, including [[entities/hanafi]], [[entities/shafi-i]], [[entities/hanbali]], and [[entities/maliki]].
