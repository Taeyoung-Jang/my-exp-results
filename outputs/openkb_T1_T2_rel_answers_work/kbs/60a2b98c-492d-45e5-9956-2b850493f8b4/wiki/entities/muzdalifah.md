# Muzdalifah

Muzdalifah is a flat plain located between Arafah and Mina during the Islamic pilgrimage of Hajj. It serves as a gathering place for pilgrims to perform **combined prayers** (jamʿ) and to collect pebbles for the symbolic stoning ritual.

## Relevance in the session‑14 document
- The document notes that, according to the **Hanafi** school, jamʿ is generally not allowed for everyday hardship, but it **is permitted at Arafah and Muzdalifah** during Hajj. This highlights Muzdalifah’s unique status in the context of **[[concepts/madhhab-travel-prayer-rules]]**.
- The mention underscores the distinction between regular travel‑related prayer concessions (qasr and jamʿ) and the specific allowances for Hajj rites.
