# Budgeting Template (Four‑Section System)

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A four‑section budgeting framework that orders must‑pay, sinking funds, savings, then flexible spending.

---

## Overview
The *Budgeting Template* is a practical structure for organizing personal finances on a recurring basis. It breaks every pay period into four priority sections:

1. **Must‑pay essentials** – non‑negotiable bills such as rent, car lease, utilities, insurance, and minimum debt payments.  
2. **True expenses / sinking funds** – predictable but non‑monthly costs (e.g., car maintenance, gifts, annual memberships) that are saved for gradually.  
3. **Savings** – contributions to emergency funds, retirement, or other financial goals.  
4. **Flexible spending** – discretionary money for groceries, gas, eating out, personal items, and other variable costs.

This ordering guarantees that core obligations are covered first, then builds resilience through sinking funds and savings, and finally allocates the remaining discretionary cash.

## Key Features
- **Priority‑driven:** Fixed, non‑negotiable expenses are funded before any discretionary spending.
- **Bi‑weekly friendly:** Each monthly amount is split in half, allowing the template to be applied to each paycheck.
- **Sinking‑fund approach:** Small, regular allocations smooth out irregular, larger expenses.
- **Rule of thumb for flexibility:** Aim for 15‑25 % of take‑home pay as the flexible pool after covering fixed bills.

## How It Works (example from [[summaries/session-32_2024-06-15]])
Assume a bi‑weekly net income of $2,400.

| Section | Calculation (per paycheck) | Result |
|---------|----------------------------|--------|
| Must‑pay essentials | Half of monthly fixed bills ($2,605) | $1,302.50 |
| Sinking funds | Annual total $1,340 ÷ 26 pay periods | $51.50 |
| Savings (emergency fund starter) | Fixed amount per paycheck | $100.00 |
| **Flexible pool** | Remaining cash | **≈ $946** |

The template can be implemented using budgeting buckets, separate accounts, or simply labeled line items within a budgeting app.
