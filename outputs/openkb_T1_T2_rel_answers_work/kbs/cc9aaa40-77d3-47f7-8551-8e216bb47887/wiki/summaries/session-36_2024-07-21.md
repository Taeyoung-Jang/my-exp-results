- **Prioritize layout**: Arrange furniture for a comfortable flow before adding décor. This establishes a functional base for later styling. [[concepts/room-layout]]

- **Lighting hierarchy**: After layout, focus on warm, low‑key lighting for evenings, especially when daytime sunlight is abundant. This fits within a broader [[concepts/room-lighting-plan]] and can be approached with [[concepts/layered-lighting]] techniques.

- **Textiles as finishing touches**: Use throw blankets, rugs, and cushions to add softness without clutter.

- **Managing bright daytime exposure**: For south‑facing, sun‑flooded rooms, rely on a single, dimmable lamp rather than multiple fixtures to create a gentle evening ambience. [[concepts/minimal-lighting]]

- **Lamp type recommendation**: Choose a floor lamp with a fabric shade that diffuses light downward, placed near the seating area (e.g., beside or slightly behind the sofa) for ambient illumination. [[concepts/minimal-lighting]]

- **Bulb specifications**: Use a warm‑white LED (≈2700 K) with 450–600 lumens, preferably dimmable, to balance coziness and sufficient light without glare.

- **Curtains for transition**: Light, sheer or textured curtains soften the shift from bright day to evening, reducing harsh contrast without serving as primary light sources.

- **Shade vs. uplight**: A lamp with a fabric shade diffuses light and minimizes glare and harsh shadows, making it more suitable than an uplight in a bright room.

- **Overall goal**: Achieve a **cozy atmosphere** by balancing layout, warm lighting, and soft textiles while keeping visual clutter minimal. [[concepts/cozy-atmosphere]]
