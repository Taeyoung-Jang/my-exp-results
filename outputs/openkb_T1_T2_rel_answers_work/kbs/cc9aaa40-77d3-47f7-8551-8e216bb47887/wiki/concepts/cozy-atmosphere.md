# Cozy Atmosphere

A **cozy atmosphere** is a feeling of warmth and comfort in a room, achieved by balancing spatial layout, gentle lighting, and soft textiles while keeping visual clutter to a minimum.

## Core Elements

1. **Room Layout**  
   - Arrange furniture for natural flow and easy movement.  
   - A well‑planned layout forms the foundation for any cozy design.  
   - See [[concepts/room-layout]] for detailed strategies.

2. **Layered Lighting**  
   - Start with warm, dimmable light sources after the layout is set.  
   - In bright, sun‑flooded rooms, a single floor lamp with a fabric shade works best, providing a soft, downward glow without glare.  
   - For specifics on lamp type and bulb specs, refer to [[summaries/session-36_2024-07-21]].

3. **Textiles**  
   - Add throw blankets, rugs, and cushions to introduce tactile softness.  
   - Textiles finish the space without adding visual clutter.

## Practical Tips from Session 36

- **Lighting choice**: Use a warm‑white (≈2700 K) dimmable LED bulb, 450‑600 lumens, in a floor lamp with a fabric shade.  
- **Curtains**: Light, sheer curtains help transition from bright daytime to a calmer evening mood.  
- **Minimal fixtures**: Prioritize a single lamp over multiple fixtures to keep the room minimal and focused.
