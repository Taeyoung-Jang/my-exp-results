# Euphorbia (genus)

Euphorbia is a diverse genus of flowering plants ranging from tiny succulents to massive trees.  Many species produce a milky latex that can cause skin irritation and is toxic if ingested, making them unsuitable for households with cats or dogs.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-21]]
- Listed among plants to **avoid** for pet‑safe indoor décor because of its toxic sap.
- Mentioned alongside jade plant and snake plant as common interior choices that are actually hazardous for pets.
- Recommended to replace with pet‑safe alternatives such as [[entities/hawthornia]] or [[entities/gasteria]] when creating a low‑maintenance, pet‑friendly corner.
