# Sempervivum (succulent genus)

Sempervivum is a genus of hardy, rosette‑forming succulents often called "hen and chicks." They are prized for their ability to thrive in hot, dry climates and to tolerate periods of neglect, making them ideal for compact living spaces.

## Key Characteristics
- **Pet safety**: Classified as a [[concepts/pet_safe_succulents]]; Sempervivum is non‑toxic to cats and dogs, unlike many common houseplants such as [[entities/jade_plant]] or [[entities/euphorbia]].
- **Light tolerance**: Handles bright, indirect light well. If exposed to intense afternoon sun, leaves may develop a reddish tint, which serves as a cue per [[concepts/light_intensity_cues]] that the plant needs a bit more shade.
- **Low water demand**: Can survive occasional missed waterings, fitting the advice for drought‑tolerant plants in a [[concepts/first_thing_you_see_corner]] setup.
- **Compact growth**: Forms tight rosettes that stay small, perfect for a narrow wall shelf or windowsill in a tiny apartment.

## Practical Uses (from [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-21]])
- Suggested as a pet‑safe alternative to traditional cacti for a grounding “first‑thing‑you‑see” corner on a shelf by the door.
- Recommended to pair with a small landscape print or a smooth stone to create a calm, desert‑inspired scene.
- Advises rotating the pot weekly and providing some shade during the harshest afternoon sun to prevent leaf scorch.

## Related Entities
- Other low‑maintenance, pet‑safe succulents mentioned alongside Sempervivum include [[entities/gasteria]] and [[entities/hawthornia]].
- Compare with less suitable options such as [[entities/jade_plant]] (toxic) and [[entities/euphorbia]] (irritating sap).

## Care Tips
1. Place in bright, indirect light; watch for washed‑out or reddish leaf edges as a sign of overexposure.
2. Water sparingly; allow the soil to dry out completely between waterings.
3. Provide good drainage to prevent root rot.
4. Rotate the pot every week for even growth.
