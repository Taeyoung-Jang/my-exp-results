# Haworthia (succulent genus)

Haworthia is a genus of small, slow‑growing succulents native to Southern Africa.  The plants form tight rosettes of thick, fleshy leaves that store water, making them tolerant of infrequent watering and low‑humidity indoor conditions.

## Key traits
- **Pet‑safe** – Non‑toxic to cats and dogs, often recommended as a household‑friendly alternative to more hazardous succulents.  See [[concepts/pet_safe_succulents]].
- **Light tolerance** – Thrives in bright, indirect light; can handle a sunny windowsill but should be shielded from harsh, direct afternoon sun to avoid leaf scorch.  Light‑stress signs are described in [[concepts/light_intensity_cues]].
- **Low maintenance** – Requires watering only when the soil is completely dry; tolerates occasional neglect without drooping.
- **Size** – Compact growth habit (typically under 6 inches tall), perfect for narrow shelves, nightstands, or windowsills.

## Recommended use in small‑space grounding corners
In the context of creating a calming “first‑thing‑you‑see” corner for a tiny apartment in a hot, dry climate (see [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-21]]), Haworthia can be paired with:
- A narrow wall shelf near the door or bedroom.
- A small, matte clay or terra‑cotta pot that complements desert‑tone décor.
- A personal object such as a favorite photo or a smooth stone to complete the scene.

These elements align with the [[concepts/first_thing_you_see_corner]] approach, offering an instant visual anchor that feels alive yet unobtrusive.

## Practical tips
1. **Placement** – Position Haworthia near a window with filtered light; a sheer curtain can diffuse intense midday sun.
2. **Rotation** – Rotate the pot a quarter turn each week to promote even growth.
3. **Watering** – Water sparingly; allow the soil to dry completely between waterings.
4. **Monitoring** – Watch for the washed‑out or reddish leaf edges that signal excessive light, as outlined in [[concepts/light_intensity_cues]].

By incorporating Haworthia into a deliberately designed corner, you gain a resilient, pet‑safe green element that reinforces a sense of home and calm each morning.
