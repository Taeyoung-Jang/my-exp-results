# Jade plant (Crassula ovata)

**Type:** other

**Description:** A popular succulent with thick, glossy, rounded leaves that is commonly used as a decorative indoor plant.

## Key Facts
- **Scientific name:** *Crassula ovata*.
- **Common names:** Jade plant, money plant, friendship tree.
- **Care needs:** Prefers bright, indirect light and well‑draining soil; tolerates periods of neglect, making it a low‑maintenance houseplant.
- **Toxicity:** Contains compounds that are toxic to cats and dogs; ingestion can cause vomiting, lethargy, and loss of appetite. Because of this, it was mentioned in [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-21]] as a plant to **avoid** when selecting pet‑safe succulents for a grounding "first‑thing‑you‑see" corner.
- **Relation to concepts:** The plant is relevant to [[concepts/pet_safe_succulents]] (as an example of a *non‑safe* choice) and [[concepts/light_intensity_cues]] (its leaves can show light‑stress symptoms such as discoloration).
