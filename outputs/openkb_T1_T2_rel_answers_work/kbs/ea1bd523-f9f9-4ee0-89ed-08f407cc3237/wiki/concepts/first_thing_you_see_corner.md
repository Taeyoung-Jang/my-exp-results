# First‑Thing‑You‑See Corner

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A deliberately arranged mini‑scene positioned at eye‑level when you wake, designed to provide an immediate sense of belonging and calm in a new or small living space.

---

## Core Elements
1. **Location** – Typically a nightstand, dresser top, narrow shelf, or windowsill that is the first spot your eyes meet after opening your eyes.
2. **Minimalist Composition** – 2‑3 items maximum, keeping the area uncluttered and purposeful.
3. **Sensory Anchor** – Combines a visual cue with a tactile or aromatic element (e.g., a small plant, a smooth stone, a personal photo).
4. **Low‑Maintenance** – Uses items that tolerate missed watering and require little daily care, crucial for busy mornings.

---

## Benefits
- **Psychological grounding:** Immediate visual familiarity reduces the “new‑place” anxiety.
- **Spatial ownership:** Small, personal objects reinforce a sense of control over the environment.
- **Ease of upkeep:** Choosing drought‑tolerant, pet‑safe succulents ensures the corner stays attractive even with occasional neglect.

---

## Implementation Tips
- Keep the arrangement **asymmetrical** but balanced to avoid visual clutter.
- Rotate any plant pots weekly to promote even growth.
- Use neutral or warm tones that echo the surrounding décor, especially in hot, dry climates.
