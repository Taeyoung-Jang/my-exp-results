# Light Intensity Cues for Plants

**Definition**: Observable changes in a plant’s appearance that signal excessive light exposure before permanent damage occurs.

## Key Visual Indicators
- **Color wash‑out**: Leaves become pale or lose their characteristic hue.
- **Reddish/orangey margins**: Edge coloration shifts toward red or orange, a early warning of stress.
- **Leaf texture changes**: Slight crisping or a leathery feel can precede scorching.

These cues appear quickly, often within days of a plant being placed in a spot with intense, direct afternoon sun.

## Practical Application
- **Adjust placement**: Move the plant a few inches back from the window or provide a sheer curtain when you notice the above signs.
- **Rotate regularly**: Turning the pot weekly ensures even light distribution and reduces localized stress.
- **Monitor after relocation**: When setting up a [[concepts/first_thing_you_see_corner]] or any new morning ritual, check the plant’s leaves for the cues before finalizing its spot.
