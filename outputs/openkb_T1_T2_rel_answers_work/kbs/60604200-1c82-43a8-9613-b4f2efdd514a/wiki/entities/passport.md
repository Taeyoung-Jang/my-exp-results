## Passport

A **passport** is a U.S. government‑issued travel document that serves as primary proof of U.S. citizenship. In the context of federal onboarding for compliance roles, it is frequently required to satisfy [[concepts/eligibility-verification-documents]] when establishing identity and citizenship eligibility before a security clearance investigation.

### Role in Government Onboarding
- Accepted as a **required** citizenship document when paired with a government‑issued photo ID (e.g., [[entities/drivers-license]]) or on its own.
- Considered an **original** document; copies are generally not acceptable for the initial eligibility check.
- Recognized for all clearance levels—[[concepts/security-clearance-levels]] (public trust, Secret, Top Secret) as a valid proof of citizenship.

### Accepted Document Combinations
| Combination | Acceptable for Eligibility? |
|-------------|-----------------------------|
| Passport book alone (original) | ✅ |
| Passport **card** alone (original) | ✅ (acceptable for citizenship proof) |
| Driver’s license + original birth certificate ([[entities/birth-certificate]]) | ✅ |
| Driver’s license + Certificate of Naturalization | ✅ |
| Driver’s license + Consular Report of Birth Abroad | ✅ |
| Enhanced driver’s license ([[entities/enhanced-drivers-license]]) *must* be paired with a citizenship document (e.g., passport) | ✅ |

### Original vs. Copy Requirements
- **Passport book** and **passport card**: must be presented as originals.
- When scanned for secure upload, a clear digital copy may be accepted only if HR explicitly permits it; otherwise, the physical original is required.

### Related Entities

- [[entities/drivers-license]] – commonly paired with a passport or birth certificate for identity verification.
- [[entities/enhanced-drivers-license]] – provides identity verification but still requires a separate citizenship document.
