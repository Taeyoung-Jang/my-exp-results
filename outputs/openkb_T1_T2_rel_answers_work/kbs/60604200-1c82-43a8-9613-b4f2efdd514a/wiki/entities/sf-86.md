# SF‑86 (Questionnaire for National Security Positions)

The **SF‑86** is the official questionnaire used by U.S. federal agencies to collect detailed personal, employment, and foreign‑contact information for individuals seeking access to classified information. It is a core component of the [[concepts/background-check-process]] and is required for most **secret** and **top secret** clearances, as defined in [[concepts/security-clearance-levels]].

## Key Facts from Session 38 (2024‑12‑16)
- **Timing:** In the onboarding flow described in [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]], HR typically asks for "proof of eligibility" (identity and citizenship documents) **before** the start date. The full SF‑86 is usually submitted **after** this initial eligibility verification, not as a prerequisite for the start date.
- **Relation to Eligibility Verification:** The eligibility step focuses on documents such as a passport, passport‑card, or birth certificate, while the SF‑86 gathers the deeper personal history needed for a suitability or security‑clearance investigation. This distinction is highlighted in the discussion of [[concepts/eligibility-verification-documents]].
- **Complementary Forms:** The SF‑86 is often paired with the **SF‑85** for positions that only require a public‑trust investigation. See [[entities/sf-85]] for the lower‑level counterpart.
- **Content Scope:** The questionnaire asks for up to ten years of address history, employment history, foreign travel, foreign contacts, and financial information. These look‑back periods align with the requirements for different clearance tiers (public trust, secret, top secret) mentioned in the session notes.

## Typical Use Cases
- **Security Clearance Applications:** Required for positions involving access to classified material.
- **Public‑Trust Investigations:** Used when a lower‑level background investigation is needed, sometimes in conjunction with the SF‑85.
- **Federal Employment Onboarding:** Submitted after initial identity verification and before the clearance investigation proceeds.
