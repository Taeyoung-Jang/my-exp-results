# Enhanced Driver’s License (EDL)

**Type:** product

An **Enhanced Driver’s License (EDL)** is a state‑issued photo ID that combines the functions of a normal driver’s license with limited proof of U.S. citizenship. It is accepted for identity verification and can be used at land or sea border crossings between the United States, Canada, and Mexico, but it does not replace a passport for international air travel.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]]
- Mentioned as an **acceptable identity document** when paired with a separate citizenship document (e.g., passport, birth certificate) for eligibility verification in federal onboarding.
- Not sufficient on its own for citizenship proof; must be combined with a citizenship document for clearance eligibility.
- Typically required in its **original form**; copies are rarely accepted unless HR explicitly allows a secure upload.
