# Passport Card

The **Passport Card** is a wallet‑size, U.S. government‑issued identification document that proves U.S. citizenship. It is accepted for domestic travel, border crossings at land ports of entry, and, importantly, as a **proof of eligibility** for government employment and security clearance processes.

## Key Facts from Session 38 (2024‑12‑16)
- **Eligibility verification**: In the onboarding packet for a federal compliance role, the Passport Card is listed as an acceptable citizenship document alongside a passport book and an original birth certificate. It satisfies the requirement for [[concepts/eligibility-verification-documents]] when paired with a government‑issued photo ID such as a [[entities/drivers-license]].
- **Original required**: The document must be presented in its original form (not a photocopy) for the initial eligibility check. Copies are generally not accepted unless HR explicitly permits a secure upload.
- **Clearance level relevance**: The Passport Card is sufficient for the eligibility step for all clearance tiers—[[concepts/security-clearance-levels]] (public trust, secret, top secret). The deeper clearance investigation (SF‑86 or SF‑85) follows after eligibility is confirmed.
- **Common document combinations**:
  - Passport Card **alone** (original) – acceptable for citizenship proof.
  - Driver’s license or state ID + Passport Card – satisfies both identity and citizenship verification.
- **Privacy note**: The card is a **nice‑to‑have** document for the eligibility stage; it should not be submitted alongside unnecessary financial records (e.g., tax returns) unless specifically requested.
