# SF‑85 (Questionnaire for Public Trust Positions)

The **SF‑85** is a U.S. federal form used to evaluate the suitability of candidates for **public‑trust** positions. It collects personal, employment, and background information to support a **background check** and any required **security‑clearance** investigations.

## When It Is Used
- Typically required after an employer has verified basic **eligibility‑verification‑documents** (e.g., ID and citizenship) but before a formal clearance investigation begins.
- Often paired with the **SF‑86** for higher‑level clearances; the SF‑85 is the standard for public‑trust roles that do not need a classified clearance.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]]
- The onboarding packet mentions that HR may request proof of eligibility **before** a start date, and that the **SF‑85** (or **SF‑86**) is submitted **after** the initial eligibility check.
- The session clarifies that employers usually do **not** expect the full questionnaire at the very first step; they first want identity and citizenship documents.
- The document emphasizes asking HR whether the SF‑85 must be submitted upfront, as the request can vary by agency.

## Relationship to Other Processes
- Part of the broader **[[concepts/background-check-process]]**, providing the detailed personal data needed for investigations.
- Connected to **[[concepts/eligibility-verification-documents]]** because the form builds on the identity and citizenship proof already collected.
- The level of scrutiny for the SF‑85 aligns with the **[[concepts/security-clearance-levels]]** applicable to public‑trust positions (generally lower than Secret or Top‑Secret clearances).
