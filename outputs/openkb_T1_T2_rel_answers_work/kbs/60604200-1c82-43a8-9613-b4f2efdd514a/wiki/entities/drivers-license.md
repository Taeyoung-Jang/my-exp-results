# Driver’s License (Product)

**Description**: A government‑issued photo identification card used to verify identity in employment and security‑clearance processes.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]]**:
- Required as one half of the identity proof for eligibility verification [[concepts/eligibility-verification-documents]].
- Paired with a citizenship document (e.g., [[entities/passport]], [[entities/passport-card]], or [[entities/birth-certificate]]) to satisfy onboarding requirements.
- Acceptable for all clearance levels [[concepts/security-clearance-levels]] when combined with the appropriate citizenship proof.
- Must be presented as an original document; copies are generally not accepted unless HR explicitly allows scanned versions.
- Can be used together with an [[entities/enhanced-drivers-license]] for identity, but still needs a separate citizenship document.

**Typical usage in onboarding**:
- Submitted during the “proof of eligibility” stage before a start date is set.
- Part of the standard [[concepts/background-check-process]] to confirm the applicant’s identity.
