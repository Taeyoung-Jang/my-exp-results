# Summary of session-38_2024-12-16

**Topic:** Onboarding for government‑compliance positions, focusing on proof of eligibility, document requirements, and clearance timelines.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/background-check-process]] and [[concepts/security-clearance-levels]]
- [[concepts/eligibility-verification-documents]]
- Clearance levels: public trust, secret, top secret
- Required forms: [[entities/sf-86]], [[entities/sf-85]]
- Documentation handling (originals vs. copies) and privacy considerations

## Typical Onboarding Flow
1. **Standard background check** – verifies identity, employment, education, and criminal record.  
2. **Eligibility verification** – HR requests proof of identity and U.S. citizenship before a start date.  
3. **Clearance investigation** – if the role requires access to classified information, a suitability review or security‑clearance investigation follows, using the appropriate SF‑86/SF‑85.

## Checklist Overview
### Required (basic eligibility)
- Government‑issued photo ID (driver’s license or state ID) – [[entities/drivers-license]]
- Proof of U.S. citizenship (passport book, [[entities/passport-card]], or original birth certificate – [[entities/birth-certificate]])
- Name‑change or naturalization documents, if applicable
- Address history  
  - Public Trust: 5–7 years  
  - Secret: 7 years  
  - Top Secret: 10 years  
- Employment history  
  - Same look‑back windows as address history

### Commonly Requested (often after initial eligibility)
- Supervisor contact information
- Personal reference contacts
- Disclosure of foreign travel, contacts, or dual‑citizenship (usually narrative, not documents)

### Nice‑to‑Have (for personal reference)
- Old leases, utility bills, W‑2s, offer letters
- Notes on short‑term unemployment, travel, or schooling

## Accepted Identity/Citizenship combos
- Passport book **alone** (original) – [[entities/passport]]
- Passport **card** alone (original) – [[entities/passport-card]]
- Driver’s license/state ID + original birth certificate – [[entities/drivers-license]] + [[entities/birth-certificate]]
- Driver’s license/state ID + Certificate of Naturalization
- Driver’s license/state ID + Consular Report of Birth Abroad
- Enhanced driver’s license (must still pair with a citizenship document) – [[entities/enhanced-drivers-license]]

### Originals vs. Copies
- Passports, naturalization certificates, birth certificates: **original** (or certified copy for birth certificates)  
- Driver’s license/state ID: **original**  
- Copies are rarely accepted unless HR explicitly allows secure uploads.

## What NOT to Submit Unprompted
- Tax returns, pay stubs, bank statements, or other financial records – keep them private unless specifically requested.

## Handling Gaps in History
- Provide a **continuous** timeline; label gaps as “unemployed,” “between jobs,” “travel,” or “in school.”  
- Honest, clear labeling prevents suspicion; investigators care more about missing time than brief unemployment periods.

## Timing of Forms
- “Proof of eligibility” usually means showing the documents, not submitting the full [[entities/sf-86]]/[[entities/sf-85]].  
- The clearance questionnaire is typically provided **after** the offer is tentatively accepted, not before the start date.
