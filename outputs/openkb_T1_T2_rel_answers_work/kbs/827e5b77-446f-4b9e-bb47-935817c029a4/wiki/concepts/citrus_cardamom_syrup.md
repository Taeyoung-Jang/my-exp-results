# Citrus‑Cardamom Syrup

## Overview
Citrus‑cardamom syrup is a thin, aromatic glaze designed to add a subtle burst of citrus and spice to morning baked goods and coffee without overwhelming sweetness. It is used primarily to brush lightly onto croissants or to perfume cappuccino milk, keeping pastries crisp while providing a memorable scent and flavor.

## Standard Batch (Full‑size)
- **Ingredients**: ½ cup sugar, ½ cup water, one strip of orange peel, 2‑3 lightly cracked cardamom pods.
- **Method**: Combine sugar and water in a small saucepan, add peel and pods, and simmer 5‑7 minutes until fragrant. Strain, cool, and store in the refrigerator.
- **Shelf life**: About one week refrigerated.

## Small “Weekend” Batch
- **Ingredients**: 2 Tbsp sugar, 2 Tbsp water, one strip of orange peel, one lightly cracked cardamom pod.
- **Method**: Same as above but simmer only a few minutes until the sugar dissolves and aroma is released. Strain and cool.
- **Yield**: A few spoonfuls—enough for a couple of croissants.
- **Shelf life**: Several days refrigerated.

## Application to Croissants
1. Warm the croissant freshly out of the oven.
2. Using a soft pastry brush (or fingertip), apply the thinnest possible coat of syrup—just a brief kiss of glaze.
3. The residual heat helps the moisture evaporate quickly, preserving the flaky layers.

## Application to Cappuccino
- Lightly rub a fresh orange peel over the milk pitcher before steaming to release essential oils, then steam as usual. This “perfumes” the milk with citrus aroma without adding bitterness.

## Recommended Orange Varieties
- **Navel orange** – readily available, fragrant, and easy to zest. [[entities/navel_orange]]
- **Cara Cara orange** – optional for a sweeter, slightly floral note. [[entities/cara_cara_orange]]
