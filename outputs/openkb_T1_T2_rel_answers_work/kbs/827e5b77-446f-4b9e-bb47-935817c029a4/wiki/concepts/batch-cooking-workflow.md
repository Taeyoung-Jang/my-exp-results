# Batch Cooking Workflow

A **batch cooking workflow** is a repeatable, time‑boxed process for preparing core components of a week’s meals in one or two sessions. The goal is to minimize daily cooking effort, reduce waste, and ensure consistent nutrition while staying within dietary constraints (e.g., gluten‑free). The workflow described in [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]] follows these key phases:

## 1. Planning & Staples Selection
- Identify a compact set of **core staples** (15‑20 items) that can be combined in multiple ways. In the source document these include rice, gluten‑free pasta, tortillas, proteins, canned beans, tomatoes, broth, vegetables, oil, and a gluten‑free spice blend.
- Align staples with a broader **gluten‑free meal planning** strategy to avoid hidden sources of gluten.

## 2. Primary Batch Session (Sunday)
| Step | Approx. Time | Quantity |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Rice | 0:00‑0:10 | 2 cups dry (≈6 cups cooked) |
| Roast Chicken | 0:05‑0:45 | 2 lb thighs + 2 onions + 4 carrots |
| Brown Ground Meat | 0:10‑0:20 | 1 lb turkey or beef |
| Soup Assembly | 0:20‑0:50 | 2 cans beans, 2 cans tomatoes, 4 cups broth, carrots, greens |
| Pasta | 0:50‑1:00 | 8 oz gluten‑free pasta |
| Cool & Store | 1:10‑2:00 | Portion into airtight containers; keep raw veg separate |

- All components are cooked within a ~2‑hour window, then cooled and stored for the week.

## 3. Mid‑Week Reset (Wednesday)
- Quick 20‑30 min review to replenish any depleted items:
  - Cook an extra cup of rice if low.
  - Thaw or roast an additional pound of chicken.
  - Chop fresh vegetables and refresh tortillas.
- This step keeps the workflow flexible without a full re‑batch.

## 4. Assembly of Daily Dinners
- Each dinner pulls from the pre‑cooked pool:
  - **Stir‑fry**, **pasta**, **tacos**, **soup**, **fried rice**, **braise**, and **omelet** are built using the same base ingredients.
- Minimal extra cooking (e.g., sautéing, reheating) is required, typically 5‑15 minutes per meal.

## 5. Storage & Reheating Guidelines
- **Rice & pasta**: Refrigerate 4‑5 days; add a splash of water when microwaving.
- **Cooked proteins**: 3‑4 days; freeze a portion for later use.
- **Soup**: Up to 5 days; freezes well.
- Reheat gently on stovetop or microwave to preserve texture.

## 6. Label Awareness
- While batch cooking, be vigilant about **label red flags** in any packaged sauces, broths, or spice blends used. See [[concepts/label-red-flags]] for specifics.
