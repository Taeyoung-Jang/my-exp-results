## Cara Cara Orange

Cara Cara orange is a **product** — a pink‑fleshed mutation of the navel orange, known for its low acidity and subtle, floral‑sweet taste. It is mentioned in the *session‑39_2025‑06‑15* conversation as an optional orange variety for zesting the citrus‑cardamom syrup, valued for its aromatic quality that pairs well with cardamom.

- **Botanical note**: Like regular navel oranges it is seed‑less and easy to peel.
- **Flavor profile**: Sweet, low‑acid, with a hint of berry‑like nuance that makes it a favorite for desserts and brightening drinks.
- **Culinary use in the session**: Suggested as an alternative to a standard navel orange when zesting the milk for a cappuccino or adding zest to the citrus‑cardamom syrup, because its fragrant peel enhances the aromatic “morning‑bloom” vibe.
