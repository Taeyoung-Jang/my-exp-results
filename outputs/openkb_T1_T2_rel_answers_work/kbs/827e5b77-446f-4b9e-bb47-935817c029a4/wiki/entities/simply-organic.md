## Simply Organic

**Type:** Organization  

**Overview:** Simply Organic produces a wide range of organic spice blends, many of which are certified gluten‑free.

**Relevance to session‑10:** In the “Gluten‑Free Meal Planning” guide (see [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]), Simply Organic is cited as a recommended source for gluten‑free spice blends, helping to avoid the [[concepts/label-red-flags]] often found in generic seasoning mixes.

**Related concepts:** [[concepts/gluten-free-meal-planning]], [[concepts/label-red-flags]]

**Comparable brands:** Pacific Foods, Primal Palate, and Boar’s Head also offer gluten‑free products, but Simply Organic is specifically highlighted for its spice blends.
