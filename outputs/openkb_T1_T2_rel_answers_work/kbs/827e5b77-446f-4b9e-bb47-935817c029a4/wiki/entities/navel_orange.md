# Navel Orange

**Type:** product

Navel orange is a seedless variety of sweet orange, prized for its easy‑to‑peel skin and abundant, juicy flesh. It is especially valued for its aromatic zest, which makes it a common choice in culinary applications that require a bright citrus note without excessive bitterness.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]]
- Recommended as the preferred orange for the citrus‑cardamom syrup used in a signature café pastry.
- Described as fragrant, easy to zest, and not overly sharp, making it ideal for the light syrup glaze on croissants.
- Suggested as a good alternative to Cara Cara orange, which offers a slightly floral aroma.

## Usage tips
- For zesting, use a microplane or fine grater to scrape only the bright orange part of the peel, avoiding the bitter white pith.
- When adding zest to milk for cappuccino, gently rub the peel over the pitcher to release volatile oils without steeping the zest directly, preventing bitterness.
