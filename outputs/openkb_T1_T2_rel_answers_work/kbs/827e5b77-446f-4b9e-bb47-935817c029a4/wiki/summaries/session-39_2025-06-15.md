## Summary

### Ideal morning café
- Simple, thoughtful menu: quality coffee, a few teas, fresh flaky pastries, optional light breakfast plates.  
- Vibe: calm, warm wood, soft music, natural light; unhurried but not sleepy. [[concepts/morning_cafe_design]]

### Signature item
- Citrus‑cardamom morning bun: laminated croissant‑style dough with citrus sugar and cardamom, caramelized edges. [[concepts/signature_pastry]]

### Signature pair upgrade
- Light brush of citrus‑cardamom syrup on croissant.  
- Add a pinch of orange zest to cappuccino milk (rub peel over pitcher).

### Citrus‑cardamom syrup recipe
- **Standard batch**: ½ cup sugar + ½ cup water, orange peel strip, 2‑3 cracked cardamom pods, simmer 5‑7 min, strain, cool. Keeps ~1 week refrigerated. [[concepts/citrus_cardamom_syrup]]
- **Small “weekend” batch**: 2 Tbsp sugar + 2 Tbsp water, one strip orange peel, one cardamom pod, simmer a few minutes. Yields a few spoonfuls; store several days.

### Keeping croissants crisp
- Apply syrup while pastry is warm with a very thin coat (brush or fingertip dab); heat evaporates excess moisture, preserving flakiness. (food texture preservation)

### Orange variety
- [[entities/navel_orange]] recommended; [[entities/cara_cara_orange]] optional for floral note.
