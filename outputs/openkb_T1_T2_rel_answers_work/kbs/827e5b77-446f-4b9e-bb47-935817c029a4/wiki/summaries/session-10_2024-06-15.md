# Summary of session‑10_2024‑06‑15

This document provides a printable, gluten‑free 7‑day dinner plan for two adults, built around 15–20 core staples. It includes a detailed Sunday batch‑cooking timeline, a mid‑week reset checklist, day‑by‑day dinner usage of pre‑prepared components, and concise label‑red‑flag and safe‑swap boxes for common gluten‑containing ingredients.

## Core Staples
- Rice, gluten‑free pasta, corn/cassava tortillas, eggs, chicken thighs, ground turkey/beef, canned beans, canned diced tomatoes, gluten‑free broth, onions, garlic, carrots, leafy greens, frozen mixed vegetables, olive oil, gluten‑free tamari or coconut aminos, potatoes, gluten‑free spice blend.

## Prep Timeline (Sunday, ~2 hr) [[concepts/batch-cooking-workflow]]
1. **Rice** – 2 cups dry → ~6 cups cooked.  
2. **Roast chicken** – 2 lb thighs with onions & carrots, 40 min.  
3. **Brown ground meat** – 1 lb turkey or beef.  
4. **Soup** – 2 cans beans, 2 cans tomatoes, 4 cups broth, carrots, greens, simmer 30 min.  
5. **Pasta** – 8 oz gluten‑free, under‑done, tossed with oil.  
6. **Cool & store** – Pack rice, pasta, chicken, ground meat, soup; keep raw veg separate.

## Mid‑week Reset (20‑30 min)
- Cook extra 1 cup rice if needed.  
- Thaw/roast 1 lb extra chicken.  
- Chop fresh veg, refresh tortillas & greens.

## 7‑Day Dinner Rotation
| Day | Meal | Pre‑pped components used |
|-----|------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Chicken stir‑fry | Roasted chicken, veg, rice, tamari |
| 2 | Tomato‑garlic pasta | Cooked pasta, canned tomatoes, greens |
| 3 | Tacos | Ground meat, sautéed onions, beans, tortillas |
| 4 | Bean‑vegetable soup | Soup batch |
| 5 | Fried rice | Leftover rice, eggs, frozen veg |
| 6 | Chicken braise | Remaining chicken, tomatoes, onions, rice/potatoes |
| 7 | Omelet/frittata | Eggs + any leftover veg |

## Label Red Flags [[concepts/label-red-flags]]
Wheat, barley, malt, malt extract, yeast extract, modified starch without a gluten‑free label, “natural flavors” without certification, and any sauce not marked gluten‑free.

## Safe Swaps [[concepts/gluten-free-meal-planning]]
- **Soy sauce** → tamari (gluten‑free) or coconut aminos.  
- **Broth** → cartons explicitly labeled gluten‑free (e.g., [[entities/pacific-foods]]).  
- **Spice blends** → certified gluten‑free brands such as Simply Organic or Primal Palate, or single‑ingredient spices.  
- **Sausages/Deli meats** → look for a clear “gluten‑free” statement and no barley malt, wheat starch, or vague flavorings.
