## Department of Motor Vehicles

**Type:** organization

The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is the state-level government agency responsible for issuing driver’s licenses, ID cards, and handling vehicle registration and title matters. In the context of moving, the DMV is a key touchpoint for several address‑update and vehicle documentation tasks.

### Roles highlighted in [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-15]]
- **Driver’s license address change** – Typically requires an in‑person visit to update the physical card, although a few states permit online updates.
- **Out‑of‑state vehicle registration and title transfer** – The DMV verifies identity and ownership, conducts VIN inspections, and may require emissions or safety tests before issuing new plates, stickers, and titles.
- **Document requirements** – Out‑of‑state transfers need the out‑of‑state title, current registration, a valid driver’s license or state ID, proof of new residence (lease, utility bill, etc.), proof of insurance, and lender information if the vehicle is financed.
- **Process flow** – Check‑in → identity/ownership verification → VIN/emissions inspection → title processing → registration issuance → plate and sticker hand‑out.
- **Interaction with other agencies** – Works with the United States Postal Service ([[entities/usps]]) for address verification and with insurance providers for proof of coverage.
