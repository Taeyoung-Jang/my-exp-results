# De Pijp

De Pijp is a bustling district in the heart of [[entities/amsterdam]] renowned for its dense urban fabric, extensive bike infrastructure, and a lively mix of cafés, bars, and shops.

## Key Characteristics
- **Car‑light living**: The area’s compact layout and abundant bike lanes make it easy to live without a car, aligning with [[concepts/car_light_living]].
- **Walkability & transit**: Streets are pedestrian‑friendly and well‑served by trams and buses, supporting strong [[concepts/walkability]] and [[concepts/transit]] options.
