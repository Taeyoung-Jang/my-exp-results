## Vesterbro (place)

Vesterbro is a lively district in central Copenhagen, Denmark, known for its strong **walkability**, extensive **bike infrastructure**, and a thriving social scene that makes it easy to settle in quickly. 

### Key points from [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-15]]
- Highlighted as one of the top neighbourhoods for **car‑light living** alongside Nørrebro. 
- Offers a mix of cafés, bars, and cultural venues that support the typical **weeknight social routine** of meeting friends for drinks or a gym class. 
- Its dense, mixed‑use layout exemplifies the criteria of [[concepts/walkability]] and [[concepts/transit]] discussed in the document.
