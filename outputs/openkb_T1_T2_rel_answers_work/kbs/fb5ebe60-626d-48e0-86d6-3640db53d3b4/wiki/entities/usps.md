# U.S. Postal Service (USPS)

**Type:** organization  
**Description:** The United States federal agency responsible for mail delivery and postal services.

## Role in moving

- Provides the official **Change‑of‑Address (COA)** form used in the first‑week tasks of the [[concepts/moving_checklist]].
- Updating your address with USPS is the foundational step for ensuring that all subsequent mail (bank statements, insurance notices, voter registration confirmations) reaches your new residence.
- The COA can be submitted online at the USPS website or in‑person at a local post office; the submission triggers forwarding of mail for up to 12 months.

## Interaction with other entities

- Works in tandem with the [[entities/department_of_motor_vehicles]] when updating driver’s‑license address, as many states require proof of a recent USPS COA filing.
- Often referenced alongside [[entities/usps]] (the same organization) for consistency across wiki pages.
