## Copenhagen

Copenhagen is the capital city of Denmark, known for its high **walkability**, extensive **bike infrastructure**, and reliable **transit** system. In the context of the "session‑06_2024‑06‑15" discussion, Copenhagen was highlighted as one of the top cities for people transitioning from Seattle because it offers a car‑light lifestyle, mild weather, and a social texture that encourages spontaneous weeknight activities.

### Key Points from the Session
- **Neighborhoods for car‑light living**: [[entities/norrebro]] and [[entities/vesterbro]] are cited as ideal districts where groceries, cafés, and transit stops are within a short bike ride.
- **Social routine**: Typical weeknight activities include biking to a gym class, meeting friends for a quick drink at a local bar, and enjoying low‑key, casual gatherings.
- **Why it suits Seattle transplants**: The city mirrors Seattle’s laid‑back, outdoorsy rhythm while providing stronger [[concepts/walkability]] and [[concepts/transit]] options, and a climate that feels familiar.
