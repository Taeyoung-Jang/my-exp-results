## Core checklist overview
- Provides a **four‑week moving checklist** that prioritises identity and mail updates first, then utilities, vehicle paperwork, and finally “quiet” items that are often forgotten.  
- Highlights **deadline‑ish items** that vary by state (DMV address change, driver’s‑license renewal, voter registration, car registration/title updates, emissions tests).

## Week‑by‑week breakdown
| Week | Primary focus | Typical tasks |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| **1** | Identity & mail | USPS change‑of‑address, update employer, bank, insurance, doctors; driver’s‑license/ID update (10‑30 days), voter registration |
| **2** | Utilities & subscriptions | Transfer/close utilities, update online shopping/streaming accounts, adjust recurring deliveries |
| **3** | Vehicle paperwork | Update car registration, title address, insurance; schedule emissions test or VIN check if required |
| **4** | Overlooked items | Payroll tax address, pet microchip registry, emergency contacts, local memberships (library, gym), scout new area services |

## Document gathering before starting
- Driver’s license / state ID (link to [[entities/department_of_motor_vehicles]] if needed)  
- Passport (optional but useful)  
- Lease or purchase agreement (proof of residence)  
- Recent utility bill  
- Vehicle title & insurance information  
- Social Security number (for DMV forms)  
- Loan/lender information if the car is financed  

## Car‑related paperwork details
- **Driver’s license** – usually requires an **in‑person** DMV visit for address change; a few states allow online updates.  
- **Vehicle registration** – often can be updated **online** unless moving from another state (then in‑person for VIN/emissions verification).  
- **Title** – needs updating only on **inter‑state moves**; generally requires an **in‑person** visit.  
- **Insurance** – address changes can be made **online or by phone**; advisable to update before visiting the DMV.

## Out‑of‑state registration / title transfer steps
1. **Prepare documents**: out‑of‑state title, current registration, driver’s license/ID, proof of new address (lease, utility bill, etc.), proof of insurance, lender info (if financed).  
2. **Check‑in**: DMV verifies identity and ownership.  
3. **Vehicle inspection**: VIN check (often on‑site) and any required emissions or safety test.  
4. **Title processing**: Update or issue new title.  
5. **Registration**: Pay fees, receive new plates and stickers.  
6. **Finalizing**: Receive updated registration and, if applicable, new driver’s‑license card.  

> **Tip:** Update insurance **before** the DMV visit to avoid delays.
