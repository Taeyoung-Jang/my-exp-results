# Traveling Mailbox

Traveling Mailbox is a virtual mailbox **product** that provides a stable mailing address for people who move frequently, such as digital nomads, RV travelers, or short‑term renters.

## Key Features (from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]])
- **Stable address** – Acts as a permanent mailing address independent of your current residence, supporting the [[concepts/stable-mail-address]] strategy.
- **Scanning speed** – Same‑day or next‑day envelope scans, giving quick visibility of incoming mail.
- **Forwarding** – Allows forwarding of physical items, though forwarding incurs postage plus a small handling fee.
- **Plan pricing** – Plans start in the low‑teens USD per month; higher tiers increase scan quotas and storage.
- **Limitations** – Scan quotas per plan tier may lead to overage fees if mail volume is high.

## Typical Use Cases
- Managing official mail (banks, IRS, DMV) while keeping a consistent address.
- Handling time‑sensitive items (e.g., credit‑card replacements) by forwarding once a stable location is known.
- Reducing the risk of lost mail during frequent moves.
