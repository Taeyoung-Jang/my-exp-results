# UPS (United Parcel Service)

UPS is a major logistics and package delivery company that provides a network of physical storefronts and service centers where customers can pick up shipments, hold packages for later collection, and manage deliveries when they lack a stable residential address.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]]
- Recommended as part of a **[[concepts/package-pickup-strategy]]** for individuals who move frequently.
- Serves as a reliable alternative to home delivery, allowing items to be retrieved at a UPS store even when the user's current location is temporary.
- Works alongside other carrier options like [[entities/fedex]] and [[entities/amazon-lockers]] to create a flexible **[[concepts/mail-workflow]]**.

## Related Concepts
- **[[concepts/mail-workflow]]** – the overall system of handling mail and packages efficiently.
- **[[concepts/package-pickup-strategy]]** – using carrier locations instead of residential delivery.
- **[[concepts/stable-mail-address]]** – the need for a consistent mailing address, which UPS pickup points help support.
