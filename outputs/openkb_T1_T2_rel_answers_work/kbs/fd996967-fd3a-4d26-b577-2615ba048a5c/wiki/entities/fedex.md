## FedEx

FedEx is a major international **organization** that provides courier, shipping, and logistics services. In the context of the *session‑13* discussion, FedEx is highlighted as one of the key carriers whose **pickup locations** can be used as part of a **stable mail address** strategy for people who move frequently.

- **Pickup/hold capability**: FedEx stores allow users to ship packages to a fixed location and collect them later, which helps avoid reliance on a temporary residential address. This aligns with the [[concepts/package-pickup-strategy]] for maintaining a reliable **mail workflow** while on the move.
- **Comparison with other carriers**: FedEx is mentioned alongside [[entities/ups]] and Amazon Locker services as viable alternatives to home delivery.
- **Relevance to virtual mailbox users**: When a user cannot forward time‑sensitive items like a replacement credit card, FedEx hold services provide a safe fallback.

For a concise overview of how FedEx fits into the broader mail‑management recommendations, see the summary of the source document: [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]].
