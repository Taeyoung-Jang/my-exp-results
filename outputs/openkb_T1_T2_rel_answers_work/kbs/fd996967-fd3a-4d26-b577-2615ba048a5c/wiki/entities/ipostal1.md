# iPostal1

**Type:** Product

iPostal1 is a virtual mailbox provider that offers a permanent mailing address independent of a user's physical residence. It is frequently recommended for people who move often, such as digital nomads or short‑term renters.

## Key Facts from Session 13 (2024‑07‑15)
- **Pricing model:** Base plans appear inexpensive, but scanning, forwarding, and extra handling are billed separately, which can increase total cost.
- **Service variability:** Mail handling speed depends on the specific location of the iPostal1 mail center; some locations provide fast, consistent scanning while others may be slower.
- **Scanning speed:** Not guaranteed as same‑day; varies by center, unlike the more predictable same‑day/next‑day scans offered by Traveling Mailbox.
- **Forwarding fees:** Users pay postage plus handling fees for each forwarded item; there is no bundled forwarding allowance.
- **Use case fit:** Works well as a [[concepts/stable-mail-address]] for anyone needing a reliable address while changing residences frequently.
- **Gotchas:** Because pricing is a‑la‑card, users should monitor scan quotas and extra fees to avoid surprise charges.
