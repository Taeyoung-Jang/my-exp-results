# Amazon Lockers

Amazon Lockers are self‑service parcel pickup locations operated by Amazon. They allow customers to have online orders delivered to a secure locker rather than a residential address, providing a stable pickup point that works well for people who move frequently.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]]
- Recommended as part of a [[concepts/package-pickup-strategy]] for handling packages when a permanent residential address is unavailable.
- Useful for receiving time‑sensitive items without relying on home delivery; the user can collect the package at a convenient time.
- Complementary to other pickup options such as [[entities/ups]] stores, [[entities/fedex]] offices, and [[entities/amazon-lockers]] themselves.

## Relationship to broader concepts
- Supports a stable mail system by acting as a "home base" for package delivery within the [[concepts/stable-mail-address]] workflow.
- Fits into the overall [[concepts/mail-workflow]] by providing a predictable endpoint for inbound parcels.

## Practical considerations
- Packages are held for a limited time (usually three days) after delivery, after which they are returned to Amazon.
- Users receive a unique pickup code via email or SMS to open the locker.
- No additional scanning or forwarding fees apply, making it a cost‑effective alternative to virtual mailbox forwarding for occasional parcels.
