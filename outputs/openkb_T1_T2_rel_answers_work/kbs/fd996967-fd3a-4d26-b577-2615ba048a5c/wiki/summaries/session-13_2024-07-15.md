- **Mail organization**: Use a single physical inbox tray for immediate triage; file, digitize, or discard items after processing.  
- **Subscription tracking**: Keep a running list with renewal dates in a notes app, calendar, or dedicated tracker.  
- **Stable home base**: Adopt a virtual mailbox or rented P.O. box as a permanent mailing address, independent of your current residence — see [[concepts/stable-mail-address]].  
- **Package handling**: Prefer carrier pickup locations such as UPS stores, FedEx offices, or Amazon lockers, or use hold‑for‑pickup services rather than home delivery — see [[concepts/package-pickup-strategy]].  
- **Time‑sensitive items**: Scan‑first virtual mailboxes allow rapid awareness; forward urgent mail once a concrete address is known, or use trusted contacts or branch pickup for items like replacement credit cards.  
- **Service comparison**:  
  - **Traveling Mailbox** – plans start in the low‑teens USD per month, same‑day/next‑day scanning, forwarding fees include postage + handling, limited scan quota per tier — [[entities/traveling-mailbox]].  
  - **iPostal1** – lower base price but scanning, forwarding, and handling charged separately; service speed varies by location — [[entities/ipostal1]].  
- **Common gotchas**: Some banks and government agencies may require a physical residential address; keep the virtual mailbox as mailing address and list your actual temporary residence when required. Complete USPS Form 1583 for mailbox authorization — [[entities/usps-form-1583]].  
- **Address strategy for institutions**:  
  - **Banks**: Use virtual mailbox for mail, update residential address only for significant changes.  
  - **IRS**: Primarily needs mailing address; virtual mailbox suffices for correspondence.  
  - **DMV**: Update driver’s license address only when moving to a new state or meeting residency duration rules.  
- **Workarounds without a trusted person**: Request bank card delivery to a local branch for in‑person pickup, or use carrier hold services at post offices, UPS, FedEx stores, or Amazon lockers — [[entities/ups]], [[entities/fedex]], [[entities/amazon-lockers]].  

These practices create a predictable [[concepts/mail-workflow]] that minimizes lost items despite frequent relocations.
