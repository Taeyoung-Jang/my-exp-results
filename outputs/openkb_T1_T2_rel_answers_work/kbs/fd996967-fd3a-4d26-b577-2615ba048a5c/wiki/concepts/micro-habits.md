# Micro‑Habits

**Definition**: Tiny, repeatable actions that can be performed in a few minutes each day to establish comfort, routine, and a sense of ownership in a new living space.

## Core Principles
1. **Low effort, high impact** – Choose actions that take 2‑30 minutes and provide an immediate feeling of order or pleasure.
2. **Anchor points** – Pair each micro‑habit with a physical location (e.g., entry drop‑zone, work surface, evening lamp) to create mental cues.
3. **Consistency** – Perform the habit at the same time each day to reinforce neural pathways and make the environment feel "home" faster.
4. **Layering** – Start with one or two habits, then gradually add more without overwhelming yourself.

## Benefits
- **Rapid acclimation** – Small wins create a perception of progress, reducing the stress of moving.
- **Reduced decision fatigue** – Fixed routines eliminate the need to decide where to put everyday items.
- **Clear mental separation** – Pairing habits with specific zones helps divide work and leisure spaces.
- **Scalable** – New habits can be introduced without demanding additional purchases.
