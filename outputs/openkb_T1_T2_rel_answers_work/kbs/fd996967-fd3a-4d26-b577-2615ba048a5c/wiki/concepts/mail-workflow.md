# Mail Workflow

A **mail workflow** is a repeatable process that keeps physical and digital correspondence, packages, and subscription notices organized, especially for people who move frequently.

## Core Steps
1. **Inbox triage** – Keep a dedicated physical tray near where mail lands. Anything that needs attention goes there immediately and is processed once a day.
2. **Processing** – After triage, each item is either:
   - Filed in a permanent location,
   - Photographed and archived digitally, or
   - Discarded if no longer needed.
3. **Subscription tracking** – Maintain a single list (notes app, calendar, or dedicated tracker) with renewal dates and set a reminder a week before each renewal.

## Stable Mail Address
- Use a **virtual mailbox** or rented P.O. box as a permanent mailing address that does not change with your residence. This satisfies banks, the IRS, and other institutions while keeping a reliable point of contact. [[concepts/stable-mail-address]]
- Authorization requires USPS Form 1583. [[entities/usps-form-1583]]

## Package Pickup Strategy
- Prefer carrier‑based pickup locations over home delivery: UPS stores, FedEx offices, Amazon lockers, or other locker services. This removes the need to know your exact address in advance. [[concepts/package-pickup-strategy]]
- For urgent items (e.g., replacement credit cards), request delivery to a local branch or use carrier hold services.

| **iPostal1** | Lower base price | Varies by location | Separate charges for scanning, forwarding, handling | Service quality depends on specific mail center |

Both services let you scan envelopes quickly, decide whether to forward, store, or shred items, and act as the **stable mail address**.

## Institutional Address Management
- **Banks**: Keep the virtual mailbox as your mailing address; update the residential address only for significant changes.
- **IRS**: Primarily needs a mailing address; the virtual mailbox works for all correspondence.
- **DMV**: Update the driver’s license address only when moving to a new state or when residency rules require it.

## Workarounds Without a Trusted Person
- Request banks to send replacement cards to a local branch for in‑person pickup.
- Use carrier hold services (USPS, UPS, FedEx) to keep packages at a store until you can collect them.
