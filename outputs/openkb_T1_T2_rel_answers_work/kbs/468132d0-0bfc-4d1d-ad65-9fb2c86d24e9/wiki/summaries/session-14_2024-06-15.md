# Summary of Session 14 (2024‑06‑15)

This document delivers a practical routine framework for a standard 8 am–4 pm workweek, covering **hour‑by‑hour schedules**, **flexible low‑energy alternatives**, **specific chore checklists with minute estimates**, and a **pantry‑freezer dinner rotation with a supporting grocery list**.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/weekday-scheduling]]** – Two primary versions (morning workout vs. after‑work workout) with a 30‑minute commute built in.
- **[[concepts/exercise-timing]]** – Short 30‑minute workout blocks placed either before the commute or after returning home.
- **low‑energy routine** – A “bare minimum” mode that drops structured exercise, simplifies dinner, and limits chores to a single quick task.
- **[[concepts/chore-planning]]** – Weekday‑specific chores (laundry, kitchen reset, common‑space reset, bathroom touch‑up, quick tidy) each limited to 20–30 minutes, with a 10‑minute fallback.
- **time budgeting** – Rough minute estimates for each chore step to aid adherence.
- **[[concepts/pantry-meals]]** – Fully pantry/freezer‑based dinner options that avoid fresh ingredients, including non‑pasta/non‑tortilla alternatives.
- **grocery planning** – Minimal staple list to support the dinner rotation.

## Schedule Overview
### Morning‑Workout Version
- **6:30 – 7:15** – Wake, brief workout, shower.  
- **7:30** – Depart for a 30‑min commute; arrive at work by 8:00.  
- **8:00 – 16:00** – Work.  
- **16:30** – Commute home.  
- **17:00 – 18:00** – Dinner.  
- **18:00 – 18:30** – Chores (per weekday plan).  
- **18:30 – 22:00** – Free time / wind‑down.  
- **22:30** – Bed.

### After‑Work‑Workout Version
- Same wake & commute times.  
- **16:30 – 17:00** – Home, brief unwind.  
- **17:00 – 17:30** – Workout.  
- **17:30 – 18:30** – Dinner.  
- **18:30 – 19:00** – Chores.  
- **19:00 – 22:00** – Free time / wind‑down.  
- **22:30** – Bed.

### Bare‑Minimum Mode (low‑energy days)
- Skip structured workout.  
- Prepare a 10‑minute “bare minimum” dinner (e.g., frozen dumplings, canned soup).  
- Perform only one quick 10‑minute chore from that day’s list.  
- Rest of the evening is unstructured downtime.

## Weekday Chore Checklists (20‑30 min) with 10‑min Fallback
- **Monday – Laundry**  
  - 5 min: gather clothes and start one load  
  - 5 min: switch to dryer later  
  - 10‑15 min: fold essentials only  
  - *Bare minimum*: start & switch load only.  

- **Tuesday – Kitchen Reset**  
  - 10 min: load/rinse dishes  
  - 5‑10 min: wipe counters and stove  
  - 5‑10 min: quick sweep if time  
  - *Bare minimum*: dishes + one counter wipe.  

- **Wednesday – Common‑Spaces Reset**  
  - 10 min: put stray items away  
  - 5‑10 min: straighten cushions, surfaces  
  - 5‑10 min: quick floor/table tidy  
  - *Bare minimum*: pick up most visible clutter.  

- **Thursday – Bathroom Touch‑Up**  
  - 5 min: wipe sink and counter  
  - 5 min: toilet bowl quick scrub  
  - 5‑10 min: spot‑clean mirror, swap hand towel  
  - *Bare minimum*: wipe sink and quick toilet swish.  

- **Friday – Quick Home Tidy**  
  - 10‑15 min: walk through rooms, put things back  
  - 5‑10 min: empty small trash cans  
  - *Bare minimum*: reset the room you use most.
