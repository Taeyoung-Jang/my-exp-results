# Chore Planning

**Definition**: A structured approach to assigning short, repeatable household chores to specific weekdays, each limited to a 20‑30 minute window with a 10‑minute “bare‑minimum” fallback.

## Core Elements
- **Weekday‑specific focus** – each day targets a single area (laundry, kitchen, common spaces, bathroom, overall tidy).  
- **Time budgeting** – chores are broken into rough minute estimates (e.g., 5 min gather laundry, 10–15 min fold) to make adherence easier.  
- **Bare‑minimum mode** – on low‑energy days a condensed 10‑minute version of the same task is provided, ensuring the home stays manageable without overload.  
- **Integration with daily schedule** – chore blocks are placed after dinner (around 18:00‑19:00) in the [[concepts/weekday-scheduling]] outlined in the source, leaving evening leisure time intact.

## Example Checklist (derived from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]])
| Day | Main Block (20‑30 min) | Bare‑minimum (≈10 min) |
|-----|-----------------------|------------------------|
| **Monday** | Laundry: start load (5 min), switch to dryer (5 min), fold essentials (10‑15 min) | Start & switch load only |
| **Tuesday** | Kitchen reset: load/rinse dishes (10 min), wipe counters & stove (5‑10 min), quick sweep (5‑10 min) | Dishes + one counter wipe |
| **Wednesday** | Common‑spaces reset: put stray items away (10 min), straighten cushions & surfaces (5‑10 min), quick floor/table tidy (5‑10 min) | Pick up most visible clutter |
| **Thursday** | Bathroom touch‑up: sink & counter wipe (5 min), toilet scrub (5 min), mirror spot‑clean & towel swap (5‑10 min) | Sink wipe + quick toilet swish |
| **Friday** | Quick home tidy: walk through rooms & put things back (10‑15 min), empty small trash cans (5‑10 min) | Reset the room you use most |

## Benefits
- **Predictability** – knowing exactly what to do each day reduces decision fatigue.  
- **Flexibility** – the 10‑minute fallback lets the system adapt to low‑energy days without breaking the habit.  
- **Time efficiency** – minute‑level estimates keep tasks within realistic limits, supporting a balanced routine alongside [[concepts/exercise-timing]] and other daily anchors.

## Connections
- Works in tandem with [[concepts/weekday-scheduling]] to embed chores into a broader daily routine.  
- Complements [[concepts/exercise-timing]] by allocating post‑dinner windows that do not conflict with workout slots.
