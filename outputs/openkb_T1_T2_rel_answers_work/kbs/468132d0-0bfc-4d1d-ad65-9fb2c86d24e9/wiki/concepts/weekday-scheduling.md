# Weekday Scheduling

**Definition** – Weekday Scheduling is the practice of arranging work, commute, exercise, meals, chores, and leisure into a repeatable, time‑bounded framework for Monday‑Friday days that follow a regular job schedule.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]])
- **Work block** – Fixed 8:00 am – 4:00 pm.
- **Commute** – 30 minutes each way, built into start/end times.
- **Exercise timing** – Two interchangeable 30‑minute slots:
  - *Morning workout* (≈6:45 – 7:15) before the commute.
  - *After‑work workout* (≈5:00 – 5:30) after returning home.
  - Tied to the [[concepts/exercise-timing]] concept.
- **Dinner** – Simple 20‑30 minute meals, often pantry‑freezer based.
- **Chore blocks** – 20‑30 minute weekday‑specific tasks with a 10‑minute fallback, linked to [[concepts/chore-planning]].
- **Low‑energy “bare minimum” mode** – Skips structured exercise, uses a quick dinner, and limits chores to a single 10‑minute task.

## Typical Daily Flow (Morning‑Workout Version)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:30 – 6:45 | Wake, brief prep |
| 6:45 – 7:15 | Exercise (morning) |
| 7:15 – 7:30 | Shower & get ready |
| 7:30 – 8:00 | Commute |
| 8:00 – 16:00 | Work |
| 16:00 – 16:30 | Commute home |
| 17:00 – 18:00 | Dinner |
| 18:00 – 18:30 | Chores (per day) |
| 18:30 – 22:00 | Free time / wind‑down |
| 22:30 | Bed |

## After‑Work‑Workout Variant
The only shift is moving the 30‑minute exercise block to 17:00 – 17:30, followed by dinner at 17:30 – 18:30 and chores at 18:30 – 19:00. All other blocks remain the same.

## Flexibility & Bare‑Minimum Mode
- **Exercise** – Omitted on low‑energy days.
- **Dinner** – Choose a 10‑minute pantry/freezer option (see [[concepts/pantry-meals]]).
- **Chores** – Perform only the 10‑minute fallback from the day’s checklist.
- The rest of the evening stays open for rest or passive leisure.

## Integration with Other Concepts
- [[concepts/chore-planning]] provides the detailed weekday‑specific checklists that fit within the 20‑30 minute window.
- [[concepts/exercise-timing]] outlines the two workout placement options.
- [[concepts/pantry-meals]] supplies the quick dinner repertoire that supports the schedule’s low‑prep requirement.

## Benefits
- Predictable structure reduces decision fatigue.
- Built‑in flexibility accommodates fluctuating energy levels.
- Short, bounded chores prevent evening overload.
- Consistent commute and work anchors anchor the day, while exercise and meals are deliberately slotted.
