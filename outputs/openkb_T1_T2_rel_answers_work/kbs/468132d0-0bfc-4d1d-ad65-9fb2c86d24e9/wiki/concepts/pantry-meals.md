# Pantry Meals

**Definition**  
Pantry Meals are quick, low‑prep dinner options that rely primarily on pantry and freezer staples, designed for low‑energy weekdays.

## Core Meal Options (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]])

- **Frozen dumplings** served with soy sauce.
- **Frozen ravioli** topped with [[entities/jarred-pasta-sauce]].
- **Mixed frozen vegetables** heated with a can of [[entities/canned-beans]] or [[entities/canned-lentils]].
- **Canned soup** paired with [[entities/frozen-bread]] or frozen rolls.
- **Instant rice** combined with [[entities/canned-lentils]] and basic seasonings.
- **Non‑pasta / non‑tortilla alternatives** such as the soup‑and‑bread combo or the rice‑lentil bowl.

All meals can be prepared in 10–15 minutes and require no fresh produce.

## Role in a Low‑Energy Weekday Routine

Pantry Meals support the **bare minimum** mode described in the schedule: they eliminate cooking time, reduce decision fatigue, and fit within the 10‑minute dinner window. They complement the chore timing outlined in [[concepts/chore-planning]] and can be slotted after a brief unwind period before the nightly wind‑down.

## Integration with Weekly Planning

When combined with the **weekday scheduling** framework ([[concepts/weekday-scheduling]]), Pantry Meals provide a reliable dinner anchor that leaves the evening free for relaxation or optional exercise (see [[concepts/exercise-timing]]). Their short prep time aligns with the 20–30 minute chore blocks, allowing a seamless transition from chores to dinner.
