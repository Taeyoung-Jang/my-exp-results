## Frozen Ravioli

**Type:** Product

### Overview
Frozen ravioli is a convenience food—pre‑shaped pasta parcels filled with cheese, meat, or vegetables, flash‑frozen for long‑term storage. It requires only heating, making it ideal for rapid meals.

### Role in the Session‑14 Routine
- Listed in the **bare‑minimum pantry/freezer dinner rotation** for low‑energy evenings (see [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]]).
- Paired with [[entities/jarred-pasta-sauce]] for a quick dinner that fits within a 10‑15 minute prep window.
- Supports the **[[concepts/pantry-meals]]** concept by providing a ready‑to‑heat protein‑rich option without fresh ingredients.
- Integrated into the **[[concepts/weekday-scheduling]]** plan as part of the dinner suggestions for both the morning‑workout and after‑work‑workout schedules.

### Suggested Meal Use
1. **Heat** frozen ravioli according to package instructions (usually 3‑5 minutes boiling).
2. **Combine** with [[entities/jarred-pasta-sauce]] for flavor.
3. **Optional add‑ins**: toss in a handful of [[entities/frozen-vegetables]] for extra nutrients, or serve alongside [[entities/frozen-bread]] for a simple side.
