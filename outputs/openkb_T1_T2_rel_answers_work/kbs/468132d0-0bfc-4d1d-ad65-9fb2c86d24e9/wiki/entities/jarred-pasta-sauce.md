# Jarred Pasta Sauce

Jarred pasta sauce is a ready‑to‑use, shelf‑stable condiment that provides a quick tomato‑based base for meals. In the **session‑14_2024‑06‑15** routine, it is listed as a core pantry staple for low‑effort dinners, especially when paired with frozen items like [[entities/frozen-ravioli]] or as a flavor boost for other quick dishes.

## Key Facts from the Session 14 Document
- **Role in Meal Planning**: Recommended in the *bare‑minimum pantry/freezer dinner* rotation to create a fast pasta‑style meal without fresh ingredients.
- **Grocery List Inclusion**: Appears on the concise grocery list that supports the suggested dinner options.
- **Complementary Items**:
  - Works with [[entities/frozen-ravioli]] for a ready‑made ravioli dinner.
  - Can be combined with [[entities/frozen-dumplings]] for a simple sauce‑dressed dumpling plate.
  - Pairs well with [[entities/frozen-vegetables]] for a quick mixed‑veggie pasta.
- **Related Concepts**:
  - Part of the broader [[concepts/pantry-meals]] strategy that emphasizes pantry‑only ingredients.
  - Supports the [[concepts/weekday-scheduling]] approach by reducing dinner prep time.

## Typical Uses
1. Heat a saucepan, add the jarred sauce, and simmer for 5‑7 minutes.
2. Stir in cooked frozen pasta, ravioli, or dumplings.
3. Optionally top with basic seasonings such as salt, pepper, or [[entities/basic-seasonings]] for extra flavor.

## Storage & Shelf Life
- Store unopened jars in a cool, dry pantry.
- Once opened, refrigerate and use within 5‑7 days.
