# Basic Seasonings

**Type:** Product

Basic seasonings are the essential pantry staples—salt, pepper, chili flakes, dried herbs—that add flavor to quick meals. In the *session‑14* routine they appear in the grocery list that supports the [[concepts/pantry-meals]] plan and the 20–30 minute weekday chore schedule.

## Role in the Schedule
- Listed under the “Pantry/freezer grocery staples” to enable fast, low‑prep dinners.
- Used when preparing bare‑minimum meals such as frozen dumplings with soy sauce or instant rice with canned lentils.
