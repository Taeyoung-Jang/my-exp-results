# Frozen Vegetables

Frozen vegetables are a convenient, shelf‑stable food item that can be stored long‑term in the freezer and quickly prepared. In the context of the **session‑14_2024‑06‑15** routine, they serve as a core ingredient for low‑energy, pantry‑freezer meals.

## Key Facts from the Document
- Listed among the recommended staples for a "bare minimum" dinner rotation (e.g., *frozen veggies plus a can of beans*).
- Paired with **[[entities/canned-beans]]**, **[[entities/canned-lentils]]**, or **[[entities/canned-soup]]** to create balanced, quick meals.
- Supports a flexible weekday schedule by requiring only 10‑15 minutes of heating, fitting within the [[concepts/weekday-scheduling]] framework.
- Works alongside other freezer staples such as **[[entities/frozen-dumplings]]**, **[[entities/frozen-ravioli]]**, and **[[entities/frozen-bread]]**.
- Complemented by basic seasonings (**[[entities/basic-seasonings]]**) to add flavor without fresh ingredients.
