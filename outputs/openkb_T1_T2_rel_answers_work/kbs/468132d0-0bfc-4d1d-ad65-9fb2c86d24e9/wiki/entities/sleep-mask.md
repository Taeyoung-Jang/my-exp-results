# Sleep Mask

A **sleep mask** is a soft, light‑blocking accessory worn over the eyes to simulate darkness, helping the wearer fall asleep when ambient light is present. 

## Key points from [[summaries/session-37_2024-12-15]]
- Recommended as part of a night‑shift routine: wear sunglasses on the commute home, then put on a sleep mask immediately upon arrival to signal "night mode" to the body. 
- Works together with [[entities/blackout-curtains]] to create a fully dark sleeping environment, especially important when trying to sleep during daylight hours.
- Supports the broader strategy of managing [[concepts/light-exposure]] and using a [[concepts/gradual-sleep-shift]] approach to adjust to a flipped work schedule.

## Related items
- [[entities/sunglasses]] – used on the commute to reduce bright light before the mask.
- [[entities/blackout-curtains]] – complementary light‑blocking solution for the bedroom.

## Usage tips
1. Choose a mask that fits comfortably and blocks light completely.
2. Pair it with a quiet, low‑light wind‑down routine (e.g., dim lamps, no screens).
3. Keep the mask clean and store it in a breathable pouch to maintain hygiene.
