# Frozen Dumplings

**Type:** Product

## Overview
Frozen dumplings are pre‑made, flash‑frozen parcels of dough filled with meat, vegetables, or a combination. They require only brief heating—typically boiling, steaming, or pan‑frying—for a fast, satisfying meal.

## Role in [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]]
In the *session‑14* routine guide, frozen dumplings are listed as one of the core **bare‑minimum pantry/freezer dinner** options. They provide a protein‑rich, low‑prep dinner that fits within the 10‑15 minute cooking window for low‑energy evenings. The document recommends pairing them with a simple sauce such as soy sauce.

## Suggested Use
1. Boil or steam according to package instructions (usually 4‑6 minutes).
2. Drain and drizzle with soy sauce or a splash of [[entities/jarred-pasta-sauce]] for variety.
3. Add a side of [[entities/frozen-vegetables]] for extra nutrients.
