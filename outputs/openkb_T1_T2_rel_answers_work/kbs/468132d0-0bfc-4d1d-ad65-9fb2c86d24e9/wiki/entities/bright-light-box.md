# Bright Light Box

## Overview
- A bright‑light therapy device that delivers approximately **10,000 lux** and is used to simulate natural daylight for alertness and circadian alignment.

## Recommended Usage for Night‑Shift Workers
- **Timing:** Use during the first 2–3 hours of the shift (e.g., midnight–1 a.m. for a 9 a.m. bedtime). [[summaries/session-37_2024-12-15]]
- **Duration:** 20–30 minutes, positioned at arm’s length; you do not need to stare directly at it.
- **Cut‑off:** Stop at least **six hours** before the intended bedtime (e.g., cease by 3 a.m. for a 9 a.m. sleep time) to avoid interference with sleep.
- **Settings:** Standard 10,000‑lux intensity; can be adjusted lower if overly stimulating.

## Benefits
- Boosts alertness during the early part of the night‑shift.
- Provides a strong “morning” cue that helps anchor the circadian rhythm.
- Can reduce reliance on caffeine when timed correctly.
