# Instant Rice

**Type:** product

Instant rice is a pre‑cooked, dehydrated rice that rehydrates in a few minutes with hot water or in the microwave. It is prized for its speed, convenience, and long shelf‑life, making it a staple for quick meals.

## Key facts from the Session 14 routine
- Listed in the **bare‑minimum pantry/freezer dinner rotation** as a base for fast meals such as *instant rice + canned lentils* or paired with frozen vegetables.
- Included in the **simple grocery list** that supports the low‑energy dinner plan.
- Suggested as a **time‑budgeted pantry staple** for efficient weekday cooking.
