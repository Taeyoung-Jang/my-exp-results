## Summary of session‑38 (2025‑03‑16)

**Main focus:** Advice for planning and executing the first month of a year‑long fellowship in Copenhagen, emphasizing three priorities – budget calibration, low‑pressure social routines, and clear work‑time boundaries.

### Key concepts
- **Housing as foundation** – Secure housing first; it determines cost structure and daily logistics.
- **Iterative budgeting** – Spend the first week gathering data, then create a rough budget in week 2, refine in weeks 3‑4.
- **Low‑pressure social habits** – Small, repeatable activities (café visits, language cafés, community swims) build familiarity without feeling forced.
- **Work‑day boundaries** – Define a default start/stop time early; protect the stop‑time to avoid over‑working.
- **Neighborhood scouting** – Walking the area, testing transport routes, and locating grocery spots give context for both budgeting and social life.

### 30‑day checklist (weekly bullets)
#### Week 1 – Foundations
- Open/finalise a local bank account; verify transfers.
- List fixed costs (rent, utilities, transit, phone).
- Perform 2–3 grocery runs to map prices.
- Test commute routes to the lab.
- Walk the neighbourhood; note cafés, parks, shops.

#### Week 2 – Early budgeting & social habit
- Review week‑1 spending; sketch a rough budget.
- Acquire a **Rejsekort** or monthly transit pass.
- Establish one low‑pressure social habit (e.g., weekly café visit).
- Attend a lab social or informal lunch.
- Define default work hours; stick to them.

#### Week 3 – Refinement & expansion
- Adjust budget based on actual patterns.
- Identify preferred grocery stores for price & convenience.
- Add another social touchpoint (meetup, interest group).
- Guard work stop‑time; resist “just one more hour”.
- Explore a new walking or bike route.

#### Week 4 – Fine‑tuning & weekend outing
- Re‑check spending; fine‑tune any outliers.
- Build a simple, budget‑friendly meal routine.
- Keep social habits that feel natural; drop others.
- Re‑commit to work boundaries before they solidify.
- Plan a small weekend outing to a new neighbourhood.

### Copenhagen‑specific low‑pressure social ideas
- **Weekly language café** at a local library – informal conversation practice.
- **Outdoor swimming spots/saunas** (Islands Brygge, Nordhavn) – casual chats.
- **Community events** at Kulturhuset Indre By or library talks.
- **Casual cycling or beginner running clubs** – low‑energy, social exercise.

### Takeaway
By treating the first month as a series of data‑gathering and habit‑building weeks, you can calibrate your budget, embed easy social routines, and set sustainable work boundaries, making the year‑long Copenhagen fellowship feel like a smooth transition rather than a stressful reset.
