**Assistant:** *Great Expectations* has a similar emotional pull to *Jane Eyre*, but it’s a bit steadier and less gothic. If you want something a bit lighter but still substantial, *The Scarlet Letter* also works: short, intense, and thematically rich without spiraling off into endless commentary.
