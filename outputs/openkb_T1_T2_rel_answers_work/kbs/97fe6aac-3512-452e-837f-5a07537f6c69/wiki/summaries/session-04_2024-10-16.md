# Summary of session-04_2024-10-16

**Date:** 2024‑10‑16

**Topic:** Helping the user choose classic literature that aligns with their taste for strong character arcs, moral dilemmas, sharp dialogue, and emotionally intense yet plot‑driven stories.

## Key Preferences Identified
- **Likes:**
  - Strong [[concepts/character-arc]]
  - Moral/ethical dilemmas ([[concepts/moral-dilemma]])
  - Sharp dialogue
  - Emotionally intense (not melodramatic) narratives
