# Character Arc

A **character arc** is the transformation or growth a protagonist undergoes from the beginning to the end of a story. It captures changes in beliefs, motivations, relationships, and self‑understanding, driving emotional engagement and thematic depth.

---

## Why It Matters

- Provides the narrative spine that keeps readers invested.
- Connects personal stakes to larger **[[concepts/moral-dilemma]]** themes.
- Works best in **[[concepts/plot-driven-narrative]]** structures where tension builds around the character’s decisions.
