# Plot‑Driven Narrative

A **plot‑driven narrative** is a story structure that keeps the reader moving forward with clear goals, escalating conflict, and minimal sidetracking exposition. The focus is on action and the unfolding of events rather than on lengthy description, philosophical digressions, or static scenes.

## Key Characteristics
- **Forward Momentum:** Each chapter or scene pushes the story toward a defined climax.
- **Clear Stakes:** The protagonist’s goals and obstacles are explicit, creating sustained tension.
- **Limited Digression:** Descriptive or philosophical passages are brief and serve the plot.
- **Strong Character Arcs:** Even though the emphasis is on plot, characters still experience meaningful growth, tying into the [[concepts/character-arc]] concept.
- **Moral/Ethical Challenges:** Plot‑driven works often incorporate [[concepts/moral-dilemma]] moments that propel decisions and plot twists.

## Benefits for Readers
- **Engagement:** Continuous plot movement keeps readers emotionally invested without feeling stalled.
- **Accessibility:** The clear structure makes it easier for readers who dislike extensive exposition.
- **Thematic Depth:** Plot‑driven stories can still explore deep themes through the characters’ decisions and dilemmas.
