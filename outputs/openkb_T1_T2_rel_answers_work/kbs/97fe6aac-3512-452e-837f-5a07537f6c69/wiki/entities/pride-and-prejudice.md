# Pride and Prejudice

**Type:** work

**Author:** Jane Austen (1813)

**Genre:** Romantic fiction, social commentary

**Length:** Approximately 432 pages (varies by edition)

---

## Overview
Pride and Prejudice is a seminal work of English literature that follows the evolving relationship between Elizabeth Bennet and Fitzwilliam Darcy, set against the backdrop of early‑19th‑century British society. The novel is celebrated for its witty dialogue, keen observations of class and gender dynamics, and a tightly plotted narrative that balances humor with serious social critique.
