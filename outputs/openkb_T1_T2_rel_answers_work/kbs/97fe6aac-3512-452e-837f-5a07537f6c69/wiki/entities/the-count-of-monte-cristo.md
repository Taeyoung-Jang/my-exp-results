# The Count of Monte Cristo

**Author:** Alexandre Dumas ( père )
**Publication:** 1844–1846 (original serialization)
**Genre:** Adventure, historical fiction

---

## Overview
The Count of Monte Cristo tells the story of Edmond Dantès, a young sailor falsely imprisoned who escapes, discovers a massive treasure, and engineers an elaborate plan of revenge against those who betrayed him. The narrative moves through many locales and social strata, keeping a tight, forward‑driving plot.

---

## Themes and Concepts
- **Revenge vs. Justice** – Dantès wrestles with moral questions about retribution, aligning with the theme of [[concepts/moral-dilemma]].

- **Plot‑Driven Action** – The novel’s relentless pacing exemplifies a [[concepts/plot-driven-narrative]], avoiding long digressions.
