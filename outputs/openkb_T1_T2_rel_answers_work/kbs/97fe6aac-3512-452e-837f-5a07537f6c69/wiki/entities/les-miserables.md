# Les Misérables

**Author:** Victor Hugo

**Publication:** 1862 (original French edition)

**Genre:** Historical fiction, social novel

**Length:** Typically 1,200–1,400 pages; however, a streamlined edition is suggested for readers seeking a tighter narrative.

## Relevance in the Session
In the conversation documented in [[summaries/session-04_2024-10-16]], *Les Misérables* was highlighted as one of the **“bridge classics.”** It was recommended because it:

- Offers a **clear, plot‑driven narrative** that keeps momentum without excessive digression [[concepts/plot-driven-narrative]].
- Explores deep **moral dilemmas** such as justice, redemption, and compassion, aligning with the user's interest in ethical conflict [[concepts/moral-dilemma]].
- Features memorable, evolving characters, providing a strong **character arc** throughout the story [[concepts/character-arc]].

The assistant advised selecting a **streamlined edition** to stay within the user's preferred page range and to avoid the sprawling nature of the full text.

## Related Works Suggested
