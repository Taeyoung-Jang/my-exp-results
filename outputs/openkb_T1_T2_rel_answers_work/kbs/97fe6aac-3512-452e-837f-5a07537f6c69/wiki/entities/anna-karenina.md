# Anna Karenina

**Author:** Leo Tolstoy (published 1877)

**Genre:** Realist novel, literary classic

**Length:** Roughly 800 pages in most editions, making it longer than the 300–600 page range the user prefers.

**Key Themes:** The novel delves deeply into [[concepts/character-arc]] and [[concepts/moral-dilemma]] through the tragic love affair of Anna and the parallel lives of other characters. While it offers a rich, emotionally intense narrative, its pacing can be slower and includes extensive social commentary, which may feel digressive compared to tighter [[concepts/plot-driven-narrative]] works.

**Relevance to Session 04 (2024‑10‑16):** In the conversation, [[entities/anna-karenina]] was suggested as an alternative to *Great Expectations* for readers seeking deeper moral weight. It was noted that the novel is longer and slower in places, making it a potential next read for those ready to handle its breadth, but it was also placed in a “maybe later” category compared to more plot‑driven suggestions.
