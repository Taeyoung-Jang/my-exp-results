## Moby‑Dick

**Type:** work

### Overview
Moby‑Dick is Herman Melville’s 1851 novel that follows Captain Ahab’s obsessive hunt for the great white whale. It is renowned for its rich symbolism, detailed maritime descriptions, and exploration of existential themes.
