# Great Expectations

**Author:** Charles Dickens

**First published:** 1861 (serial), 1862 (book)

**Typical length:** ~500 pages, fitting the 300–600 page range the user prefers.

---

## Overview
Great Expectations follows the orphan Pip as he navigates ambition, love, and identity. The novel is celebrated for its strong **[[concepts/character-arc]]**, where Pip evolves from a naïve boy into a self‑aware adult, and for its sustained **[[concepts/moral-dilemma]]** as he confronts the consequences of his desires.

The story maintains a **[[concepts/plot-driven-narrative]]** with relatively little digression, which aligns with the user’s dislike for long, meandering sections.
