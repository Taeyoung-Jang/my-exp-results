## Summary of session-07_2021-05-15

**Context**: A user plans a short 2–3 night mountain cabin getaway and asks for guidance on choosing, comparing, and vetting rental cabins.

### Key Considerations for Choosing a Cabin
- **Location** – proximity to trails, scenery, and desired mountain area.
- **Essential amenities** – reliable road access, proper heating/cooling, comfortable bed, clean bathroom, functional kitchen basics, and good cleanliness reviews.
- **Nice‑to‑have extras** – mountain views, fireplace, hot tub/sauna, fast Wi‑Fi, outdoor seating, extra comforts (games, hammock, upgraded kitchen gear).
- **Practical factors** – typical weather for travel dates, road conditions, and ease of access.

### Simple Checklist: Must‑haves vs. Nice‑to‑haves
- **Must‑haves**: safe access, heating, bed & bath quality, trail proximity, basic kitchen gear, clean‑room reviews.
- **Nice‑to‑haves**: scenic view, fireplace/fire pit, hot tub/sauna, Wi‑Fi speed, deck/outdoor seating, extra leisure items.

### Scoring Sheet (Text‑Only Template)
```
Dealbreakers (yes/no):
  • Reliable road access: ___
  • Heating works well: ___
  • Cleanliness: ___
  • Wi‑Fi usable: ___
If any are “no,” skip this listing.

Location (weight 3): ___ / 5
Comfort (weight 3): ___ / 5
Amenities (weight 2): ___ / 5
Access & Practicality (weight 3): ___ / 5
Total Score = (Location×3) + (Comfort×3) + (Amenities×2) + (Access×3)
```
- The rubric lets you compare 3–5 cabins quickly, focusing on the four weighted categories. See [[concepts/scoring-rubric-template]] for a reusable framework.

### Host‑Question Template
```
Hi! Before booking, could you confirm a few things?
- Is the access road consistently passable for a regular car during the season we’re visiting?
- Is the heating reliable and easy to control?
- How stable is the Wi‑Fi, and is there decent cell service for major carriers?
- If there’s an outage, is there any backup heat or power source?
- How far is the cabin from the nearest trailhead and the closest grocery store?
Thanks so much.
```
- This set of queries aligns with [[concepts/host-communication-checklist]].

### Quick Decision Process
1. Verify **dealbreakers** – any “no” eliminates a listing outright.
2. Score the remaining cabins with the weighted rubric.
3. If scores are close, choose the cabin that gives the strongest intuitive “yes” feeling.
