# Wi‑Fi (product)

Wi‑Fi is a wireless networking technology that provides internet connectivity without cables. In the context of short‑term cabin rentals, it is considered a **must‑have** amenity for guests who need reliable connectivity for work, communication, or entertainment.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-07_2021-05-15]]
- Listed among the essential amenities in the **must‑haves** checklist for cabin selection.
- Included in the host‑communication checklist: guests are advised to ask hosts about Wi‑Fi stability and speed.
- Scored as part of the **Amenities** category in the [[concepts/scoring-rubric-template]], influencing the overall cabin rating.
- Hosts are prompted to confirm Wi‑Fi usability when answering the standard pre‑booking questions.
