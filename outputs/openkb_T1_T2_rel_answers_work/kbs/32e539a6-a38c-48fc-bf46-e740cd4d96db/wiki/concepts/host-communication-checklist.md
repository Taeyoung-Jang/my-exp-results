## Host Communication Checklist

**Definition**: A reusable list of concise questions renters can send to cabin hosts to verify critical amenities and practical details before booking.

### Core Questions (from [[summaries/session-07_2021-05-15]])
1. **Access Road** – *Is the access road consistently passable for a regular car during the season we’re visiting?*
2. **Heating** – *Is the heating system reliable and easy to control?*
3. **Wi‑Fi** – *How stable is the Wi‑Fi for basic work and video calls?* (also see [[entities/wifi]])
4. **Cell Service** – *Is there decent cell service for major carriers?*
5. **Backup Power/Heat** – *If there’s an outage, is there any backup heat or power source?*
6. **Proximity** – *How far is the cabin from the nearest trailhead and the closest grocery store?* (relevant for trips to the [[entities/blue-ridge-mountains]])

### How to Use the Checklist
- **Copy‑paste** the list into a short, polite message to the host.
- **Record** the host’s answers alongside each potential cabin in your [[concepts/scoring-rubric-template]].
- **Dealbreaker filtering**: If any answer is a clear “no” to a non‑negotiable item, rule the listing out before scoring.

### Relationship to Other Concepts
- Helps operationalize the **must‑have** criteria described in [[concepts/cabin-selection-criteria]].
- Works in tandem with the scoring sheet from [[concepts/scoring-rubric-template]] to streamline decision‑making.

### Example Host Message
```text
Hi! Before booking, could you confirm a few things?
- Is the access road consistently passable for a regular car during the season we’re visiting?
- Is the heating reliable and easy to control?
- How stable is the Wi‑Fi, and is there decent cell service for major carriers?
- If there’s an outage, is there any backup heat or power source?
- How far is the cabin from the nearest trailhead and the closest grocery store?
Thanks so much.
```
