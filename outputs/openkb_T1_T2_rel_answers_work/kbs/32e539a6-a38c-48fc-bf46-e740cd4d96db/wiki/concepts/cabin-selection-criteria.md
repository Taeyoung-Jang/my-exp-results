# Cabin Selection Criteria

## Overview
A structured set of factors to evaluate and compare short‑term mountain cabin rentals, helping travelers choose a suitable cabin efficiently.

## Core Categories
- **Location**: Proximity to trails, scenic views, distance to the nearest grocery store or trailhead, and overall accessibility.
- **Essential Amenities (Must‑haves)**: Reliable road access, functional heating/cooling, comfortable bed, clean bathroom, basic kitchen gear, and positive cleanliness reviews.
- **Nice‑to‑have Amenities**: Mountain view, fireplace or fire pit, hot tub/sauna, fast Wi‑Fi, outdoor seating or deck, extra comforts such as games, hammock, or upgraded kitchen tools.
- **Practical Considerations**: Typical weather for travel dates, road conditions, cell service reliability, and backup power/heat options.

## Decision Tools
- **Dealbreaker Checklist**: Binary yes/no items for critical factors (e.g., road access, heating, Wi‑Fi usability). Any “no” eliminates the listing instantly.
- **Scoring Rubric**: Weighted evaluation across four categories—Location (weight 3), Comfort (weight 3), Amenities (weight 2), Access & Practicality (weight 3). The total weighted score identifies the top candidates. See [[concepts/scoring-rubric-template]] for the template.
- **Host Communication**: A concise set of questions to confirm dealbreakers and practical details with the host. See [[concepts/host-communication-checklist]] for the recommended questionnaire.

## Application Example
These criteria were applied in a planning session for a 2–3 night cabin getaway in the **Blue Ridge Mountains**. The user used the dealbreaker checklist and weighted scoring rubric to narrow down 3–5 listings and selected the cabin that gave the strongest intuitive “yes” feeling.
