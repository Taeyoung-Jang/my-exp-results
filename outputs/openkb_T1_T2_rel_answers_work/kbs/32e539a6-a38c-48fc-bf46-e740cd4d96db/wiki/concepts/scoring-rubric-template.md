# Scoring Rubric Template

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A lightweight weighted rubric for comparing short‑stay cabin rentals.

## Overview
The *Scoring Rubric Template* is a simple, text‑only framework designed to help travelers evaluate and rank cabin listings quickly without getting bogged down in excessive detail. It combines binary **dealbreaker** checks with a weighted scoring system across four primary categories:

1. **Location** (weight 3) – proximity to trails, scenery, and overall setting.
2. **Comfort** (weight 3) – quality of bed, bathroom, heating/cooling, and overall livability.
3. **Amenities** (weight 2) – presence of nice‑to‑have features such as Wi‑Fi, fireplace, hot tub, etc.
4. **Access & Practicality** (weight 3) – road accessibility, parking, reliability of utilities, and other logistical factors.

The total score is calculated as:
```
Total = (Location × 3) + (Comfort × 3) + (Amenities × 2) + (Access & Practicality × 3)
```
Higher totals indicate a better overall fit.

## Dealbreaker Section
Before applying the weighted scores, the template includes a **Dealbreakers** checklist (yes/no) for critical must‑haves such as:
- Reliable road access
- Functional heating
- Cleanliness
- Usable Wi‑Fi

Any "no" answer triggers an immediate exclusion of the listing, streamlining the decision process.

## Usage Example
A user can copy‑paste the following plain‑text sheet and fill in scores for each cabin being considered:
```
Dealbreakers (yes/no):
  • Reliable road access: ___
  • Heating works well: ___
  • Cleanliness: ___
  • Wi‑Fi usable: ___
If any are “no,” skip this listing.

Location (weight 3): ___ / 5
Comfort (weight 3): ___ / 5
Amenities (weight 2): ___ / 5
Access & Practicality (weight 3): ___ / 5
Total Score = (Location×3) + (Comfort×3) + (Amenities×2) + (Access×3)
```
The sheet is intentionally minimalistic, making it easy to compare **3–5** cabin options side‑by‑side.
