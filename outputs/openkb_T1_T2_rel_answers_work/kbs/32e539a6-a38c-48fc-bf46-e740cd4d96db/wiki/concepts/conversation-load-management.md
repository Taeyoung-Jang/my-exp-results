# Conversation Load Management

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Managing the number of active early‑stage dating conversations to keep the experience enjoyable and sustainable.

---

## Core Principle
Maintain a manageable conversation load—typically **2‑3 active chats**—to prevent the dating process from feeling like a part‑time job or customer‑support queue.

## Why It Matters
- **Cognitive overload:** Juggling many chats leads to mixed details, delayed replies, and mental fatigue.
- **Reduced authenticity:** When overloaded, people default to scripted responses, undermining genuine interaction.
- **Burnout risk:** Excessive messaging drains energy and can cause disengagement from the dating process.

## Key Indicators of Overload
- Forgetting personal details shared by matches.
- Feeling drained after replying.
- Treating messages as tasks rather than conversations.
- Losing interest or feeling obligated to continue.

## Practical Recommendations
1. **Set a ceiling:** Aim for no more than **two to three** concurrent conversations.
2. **Monitor energy levels:** If you notice fatigue, pause new matches and focus on existing chats.
3. **Prioritize quality:** Choose conversations where curiosity and genuine interest are present.
4. **Schedule breaks:** Regularly step back from the app to recharge.
5. **Use clear signals:** When reaching your limit, politely let new matches know you’re focusing on current conversations.
