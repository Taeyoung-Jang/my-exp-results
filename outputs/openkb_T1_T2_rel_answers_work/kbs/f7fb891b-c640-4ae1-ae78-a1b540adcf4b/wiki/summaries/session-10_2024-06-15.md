## Summary of session-10_2024-06-15

- Emphasizes [[concepts/boundary_setting]] by using polite, predictable responses, limiting favor requests, and providing short neutral declines.

- Supplies two alternative versions of the key script: a super‑casual doorstep phrasing and a more formal text‑message version.  
- Lists several brief “polite no” lines for refusing extra requests without creating tension.
