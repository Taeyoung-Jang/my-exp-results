# Paramedic

A **paramedic** is an advanced pre‑hospital emergency medical professional who provides higher‑level medical care than an Emergency Medical Technician (EMT).  Paramedics undergo longer, more intensive training that includes detailed anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, advanced airway management, ECG interpretation, and clinical rotations.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-07-04]]
- **Training depth**: Longer programs covering anatomy, pharmacology, advanced assessment, and hands‑on clinical experience.
- **Scope of practice**: Can start IVs, administer a broad range of medications, interpret ECGs, manage airways with advanced equipment, and make complex clinical decisions in the field.
- **Roles compared to EMT**: While EMTs handle basic life support (CPR, AED, splinting, simple meds), paramedics perform advanced life support and have greater medical authority and responsibility.
- **Study recommendations**: Early focus on core anatomy (cardiovascular, respiratory, neuro), a functional overview of protocols, and medical terminology. Suggested a 2‑week study plan with daily anatomy and protocol blocks, color‑coded tabbing, and a quick‑index template for packet navigation.
