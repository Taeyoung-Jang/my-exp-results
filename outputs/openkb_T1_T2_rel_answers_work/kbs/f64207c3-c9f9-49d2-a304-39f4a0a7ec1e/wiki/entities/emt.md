# Emergency Medical Technician (EMT)

An **Emergency Medical Technician (EMT)** is the foundational pre‑hospital health professional who delivers basic life‑support care. EMTs are trained to assess patients, perform CPR, operate an AED, administer oxygen, control bleeding, apply splints, and use a limited set of medications.

## Key Facts from session‑39 (2025‑07‑04)
- **Scope of practice**: Focuses on rapid assessment, stabilization, and safe transport to a medical facility.  
- **Training emphasis**: Basic anatomy, physiology, medical terminology, and calm, organized stress management are highlighted as early study priorities for new EMTs and future paramedics.
- **Comparison to paramedic**: The conversation stresses that EMTs handle the basics, while **paramedics** perform advanced interventions such as IV insertion, extensive pharmacology, ECG interpretation, and airway management.  
- **Relevant concepts**: See [[concepts/differences-between-emt-and-paramedic]] for a detailed contrast, and [[concepts/paramedic-training-priorities]] for guidance on advancing from EMT to paramedic.
