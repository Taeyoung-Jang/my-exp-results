# Automated External Defibrillator (AED)

**Type:** product

## Overview
The Automated External Defibrillator (AED) is a lightweight, portable device that delivers a therapeutic electric shock to restore normal heart rhythm in cases of sudden cardiac arrest. It is designed for use by first responders such as Emergency Medical Technicians (EMTs) and, with additional training, by paramedics.

## Role in Emergency Medical Work
- **EMT scope:** AEDs are a core tool for EMTs, enabling rapid defibrillation on scene as part of basic life support. This aligns with the foundational skills outlined in [[concepts/differences-between-emt-and-paramedic]].
- **Paramedic scope:** While paramedics have broader medication and advanced airway capabilities, the AED remains a primary device for early cardiac rhythm management before advanced interventions.
