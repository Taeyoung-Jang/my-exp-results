# Study Plan Design for Paredic Programs

**Definition**  
A structured approach to organizing early study activities for new paramedic students, balancing anatomy review, protocol familiarization, and effective packet organization.

## Overview
Derived from the practical advice given in [[summaries/session-39_2025-07-04]], this concept outlines how to create a short‑term (typically two‑week) study plan that prepares trainees for the intensive first weeks of a paramedic program.

## Core Priorities
1. **Anatomy first** – focus on cardiovascular, respiratory, and neuro systems.  
2. **Protocol orientation** – skim structure, locate sections, and add color‑coded tabs.  
3. **Terminology & pathophysiology** – read alongside anatomy to build context.

These priorities echo the broader [[concepts/paramedic-training-priorities]] framework.

## Two‑Week Day‑by‑Day Schedule
| Day | Anatomy Focus (≈1 hr) | Protocol Work (≈30 min) |
|-----|----------------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Cardiovascular overview | Flip whole packet; place main tabs |
| 2 | Heart anatomy details | Read general assessment section |
| 3 | Respiratory overview | Add Cardiac, Respiratory, Trauma, Pediatrics tabs |
| 4 | Respiratory anatomy details | Read cardiac protocol summaries |
| 5 | Neuro overview | Add sticky flags for contraindications |
| 6 | Neuro anatomy details | Read respiratory protocols |
| 7 | Review cardio/resp/neuro | Read trauma protocols |
| 8 | Cardio refresher | Read pediatric protocols |
| 9 | Resp refresher | Review all tabs; adjust as needed |
|10 | Neuro refresher | Deep read one protocol |
|11 | Mixed review | Deep read another protocol |
|12 | Mixed review | Practice rapid section location |
|13 | Full anatomy review | Quick scan of every protocol heading |
|14 | Light anatomy recap | Navigation practice using tabs only |

## Tabbing & Quick‑Index Scheme
- **Red** – Cardiac  
- **Blue** – Respiratory  
- **Green** – Trauma  
- **Yellow** – Pediatrics  
- **Orange** – Assessment / General  
- **Sticky flags** – contraindications, special notes  

Create a one‑page “quick index” (see source) to tape inside the front cover, listing tab colors and flag purposes for rapid reference.

## Study Habits
- Consistent daily blocks (1 hr anatomy, 30 min protocol).  
- Verbal self‑explanation of each system to reinforce retention.  
- Calm, organized approach under pressure; practice with mock scenarios.
