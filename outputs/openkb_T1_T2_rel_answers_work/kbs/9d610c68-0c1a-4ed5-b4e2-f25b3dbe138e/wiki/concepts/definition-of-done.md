# Definition of Done

**Overview**
A concrete, checklist‑style threshold that signals when a dissertation draft can be considered complete for the current revision cycle, allowing the author to move on to the next milestone without endless tweaking.

**Key criteria (from [[summaries/session-36_2024-06-15]]])**
- The main argument reads clearly and consistently from introduction through conclusion.
- Each chapter explicitly supports the central claim.
- All committee‑requested revisions are fully addressed.
- Citations are complete, accurate, and consistently formatted.
- Figures, tables, and appendices are final and correctly referenced.
- The manuscript meets all graduate‑school formatting requirements.
- No remaining typographical or readability issues remain.
- Nothing left undone would delay submission or affect the defense.

**How it works**
1. **Define the threshold upfront** – before a revision pass, list the items above that must be satisfied.
2. **Separate revision passes** – use [[concepts/revision-passes]] (structural, clarity, surface) and stop each pass once its specific criteria are met.
3. **Integrate with planning** – treat the checklist as a milestone within [[concepts/weekly-rhythm]] or [[concepts/milestone-based-sprint-planning]] to keep momentum and avoid burnout.

**Benefits**
- Provides a clear stopping point, reducing scope creep.
- Enables time‑boxed work sessions and better energy management (see [[concepts/capacity-capping]]).
- Supports consistent progress tracking across weeks and toward defense readiness.
