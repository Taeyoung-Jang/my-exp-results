# Weekly Rhythm

**Weekly Rhythm** is a templated schedule that allocates regular time blocks for three core activities during the final dissertation phase: 

1. **Dissertation editing** – applying focused [[concepts/revision-passes]] such as structural, clarity, and surface edits.
2. **Committee and administrative coordination** – handling emails, scheduling, and submission logistics.
3. **Defense‑readiness preparation** – brief sessions for slides, talk outline, anticipated questions, and logistics.

The concept was introduced in [[summaries/session-36_2024-06-15]] and provides two intensity variants:

## High‑Energy Week
- **Monday** 9‑11 am edit block, 2‑3 pm admin.
- **Tuesday** 9‑12 am deep edit.
- **Wednesday** 9‑10 am cleanup, 10:30‑11 am defense prep.
- **Thursday** 9‑12 pm intensive edit.
- **Friday** 9‑10 am polish, 2‑3 pm admin.
- **Saturday** 10‑10:20 am weekly planning note, 10:20‑10:40 am defense prep.
- **Sunday** full rest.

## Low‑Energy Week
- **Monday** 10‑11 am edit, 3‑3:30 pm admin.
- **Tuesday** 10‑12 pm focused edit.
- **Wednesday** 10‑10:30 am cleanup, 10:30‑10:50 am defense prep.
- **Thursday** 11‑12 pm edit.
- **Friday** 10‑10:30 am polish, 2‑2:15 pm admin.
- **Saturday** 11‑11:05 am planning note, 11:05‑11:20 am defense prep.
- **Sunday** full rest.

Both templates maintain the **three pillars** while scaling the duration of each block to match personal energy levels. By embedding short defense‑prep touchpoints (≈30 min on Wednesday, 15‑20 min on Saturday) the rhythm ensures steady progress toward the dissertation’s [[concepts/definition-of-done]] without overwhelming the primary editing work.

The Weekly Rhythm can be combined with other planning approaches such as [[concepts/milestone-based-sprint-planning]] to align weekly goals with larger project milestones.
