## Jira

Jira is a software product used for planning, tracking, and managing agile work, particularly sprint backlogs and story points. In the *session-01_2022-06-15* discussion, the assistant proposes a sprint plan that can be dropped into **Jira** (and Confluence) to manage the migration track and its capacity‑capped work.

### Key facts from the session
- The sprint has an overall capacity of ~40 story points, with a suggested **capacity‑capping** of 12‑15 points for the migration effort. [[concepts/capacity-capping]]
- The migration initiative is treated as a separate **migration‑track** that runs alongside regular sprint work. [[concepts/migration-track]]
- Milestones are defined using a **milestone‑based sprint planning** approach to keep work granular and visible. [[concepts/milestone-based-sprint-planning]]
- The plan includes backlog items, definitions of done, and artifacts that will be stored in **Confluence**, linking the two tools together. [[entities/confluence]]
- The concrete sprint plan (goal, milestones, backlog items, capacity suggestion, next milestone) is documented in the summary of the session. [[summaries/session-01_2022-06-15]]
