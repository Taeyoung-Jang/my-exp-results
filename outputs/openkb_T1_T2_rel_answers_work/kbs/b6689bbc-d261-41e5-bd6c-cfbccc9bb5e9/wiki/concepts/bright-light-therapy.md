# Bright‑Light Therapy as a Sunlight Substitute

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Artificial bright‑light therapy provides a controlled light exposure that can substitute for natural sunlight, especially for circadian regulation and mood support.

---

## Definition
Bright‑light therapy (BLT) involves the use of a light source that delivers a high intensity of illumination, typically around **10 000 lux**, to simulate the effect of outdoor sunlight on the body’s internal clock.

## How It Works
- The retina detects the bright light and sends signals to the suprachiasmatic nucleus, the brain’s master clock.
- This exposure synchronises circadian rhythms, influencing melatonin production, sleep‑wake timing, and mood.
- It is most effective when used in the **morning**, aligning with the body's natural sensitivity to early‑day light. See [[concepts/morning-light-benefits]].

## Recommended Specifications
- **Intensity:** Approximately 10 000 lux at a comfortable viewing distance.
- **Spectrum:** Broad‑spectrum white light, avoiding UV.
- **Duration:** 10‑30 minutes per session, often combined with other daily activities (e.g., reading, work).
- **Device Example:** See [[entities/10k-lux-light-therapy-lamp]] for a typical product meeting these criteria.

## When to Use Bright‑Light Therapy
- **Limited Outdoor Access:** Days when outdoor exposure is impractical (e.g., winter, heavy workload).
- **Late Outdoor Breaks:** If outdoor time occurs after midday, BLT can provide the missing morning cue.
- **Consistent Routine:** When a reliable, repeatable light cue is needed, BLT offers a predictable alternative.

## Comparison with Outdoor Light
- **Direct Sun vs. Lamp:** Direct sunlight offers a broader spectrum and higher intensity, but BLT approximates the critical brightness needed for circadian signalling.
- **Integration Strategies:** BLT can complement outdoor exposure; using a lamp in the morning and stepping outside later adds cumulative benefit. For strategies on blending indoor and outdoor exposure, see [[concepts/integrating-outdoor-time]].

## Practical Guidance
1. **Morning Session:** Place the lamp on a desk and sit within the recommended distance while performing routine tasks.
2. **Consistency:** Use daily, especially on days with little or no outdoor time.
3. **Safety:** Avoid staring directly at the light; ensure the device is labeled for light‑therapy.
