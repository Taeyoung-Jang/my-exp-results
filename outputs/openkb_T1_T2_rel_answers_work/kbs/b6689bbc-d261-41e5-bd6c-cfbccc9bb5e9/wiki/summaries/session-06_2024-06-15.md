## Summary of session‑06_2024-06-15

- **Goal:** Offer realistic ways for people with mostly indoor schedules to obtain sufficient sunlight exposure and guidance on using bright‑light lamps as a supplement.
- **Key recommendations:**
  - Integrate short outdoor moments into existing activities (e.g., walk part of a commute, park farther away, coffee break outside, quick post‑lunch loop). [[concepts/integrating-outdoor-time]]
  - Aim for **10–15 minutes** of outdoor light, preferably in the **morning** or around **midday**; even a few minutes help set circadian cues. [[concepts/morning-light-benefits]]
  - When outdoors, **direct sun** is more effective than shade, but any open‑sky exposure counts.
  - If outdoor exposure is unreliable, use a **bright‑light lamp** delivering ~**10 000 lux** placed close enough to the eyes without glare; look for devices marketed as *light‑therapy* or *bright‑light* rather than ordinary desk lamps. [[concepts/bright-light-therapy]] (see [[entities/10k-lux-light-therapy-lamp]] for a typical specification)
- **Decision guide:**
  - Prioritize outdoor exposure when you can get a morning break outside; it generally outranks a lamp for circadian impact.
  - Use a lamp when mornings are consistently indoors, daylight is limited (e.g., winter), or outdoor time is only later in the day.
  - Combining both has **no downside** – the lamp provides a reliable baseline, and outdoor moments act as a bonus.
- **Break timing:**
  - One slightly longer outdoor stint (>5 min) is marginally more effective than several 2–3 minute bursts, but consistency matters more than exact duration.
  - If only micro‑breaks are possible, any exposure still contributes.

### Practical tips
1. **Commute tricks:** walk part of the route, bike, or get off the bus one stop early.
2. **Workplace tweaks:** keep a desk near a sunny window, open curtains immediately after waking, and place the lamp on the desk for a 10‑minute morning session while reading.
3. **Break strategy:** schedule the first break of the day outdoors; if not possible, default to the lamp.
4. **Safety:** avoid staring directly at the sun; enjoy ambient outdoor light.
