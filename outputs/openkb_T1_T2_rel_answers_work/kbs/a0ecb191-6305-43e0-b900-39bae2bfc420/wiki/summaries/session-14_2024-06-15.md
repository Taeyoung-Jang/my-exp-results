## Summary of Session 14 (2024‑06‑15)

- **Goal:** Help the user improve a work‑from‑home routine by reducing auditory distractions during calls and deep‑focus work.
- **Primary recommendation:** Invest first in **over‑ear noise‑cancelling headphones** (e.g., Anker Soundcore Space Q45 or Sony WH‑CH720N) because they both block ambient noise and provide better built‑in microphones than a laptop’s mic.
- **Secondary gear tip:** If a dedicated mic is needed later, choose a cardioid or “voice‑only” model, but pair it with headphones to avoid hearing the surrounding chaos.
- **Software settings:** 
  - **Zoom:** Settings → Audio → Noise Suppression → **High** (or Auto). Enable “Automatically adjust microphone volume” and keep “Suppress background noise for calls only” on.
  - **Microsoft Teams:** Profile → Settings → Devices → Noise Suppression → **High**. Disable “High fidelity music mode” and use headset output.
- **Quick mic tricks for laptop built‑in mic:**
  - Position the laptop so noise sources are behind it.
  - Raise the laptop on a stand or books to bring the mic closer to the mouth.
  - Use a cheap foam pop filter or cut‑down mic cover to soften background sounds.
- **Routine & scheduling tactics:**
  - Block the day into **quiet blocks** (for calls and deep work) and **noisy blocks** (lighter tasks).
  - Use calendar alerts to remind of predictable noisy windows.
  - Employ visual cues (e.g., “on a call” light) to signal availability.
  - Consider “push‑to‑talk” mode during especially noisy periods.
- **Product comparisons:** The Soundcore Q45 tends to outperform the Sony WH‑CH720N at cancelling voices/TV noise, while the Sony may deliver slightly more natural voice reproduction.
