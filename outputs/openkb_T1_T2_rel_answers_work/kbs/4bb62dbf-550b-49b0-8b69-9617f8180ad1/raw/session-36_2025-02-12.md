**User:** I’m trying to get better at meal planning for busy weeknights—what are some practical strategies to make dinners easier without getting bored of eating the same thing all week?

**Assistant:** One of the easiest ways to make weeknight dinners smoother is to prep building blocks rather than full meals. For example, cooking a batch of grains, roasting a tray of vegetables, or marinating some protein ahead of time gives you mix‑and‑match parts you can turn into completely different dinners throughout the week. It keeps things flexible so you don’t feel like you’re eating repeats.

Another helpful trick is choosing recipes that share ingredients but don’t feel similar. If you buy a bunch of cilantro or a carton of mushrooms, plan two or three dishes that use them in different ways so nothing goes to waste.

You can also rotate “themes” instead of specific dishes—like tacos one night, pasta another, stir‑fry another—so you get variety without reinventing the wheel every week.

And if you’re really pressed for time, having a few shortcut ingredients on hand, like pre‑chopped veggies, frozen dumplings, or a good jarred sauce, can turn a simple meal into something satisfying with almost no effort.

Small habits like these add up and make busy nights feel much easier without falling into the same‑meal-every-day rut.
