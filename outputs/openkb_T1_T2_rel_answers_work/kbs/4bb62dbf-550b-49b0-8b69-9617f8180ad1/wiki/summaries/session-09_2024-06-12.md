# Summary of session-09_2024-06-12

**Date:** 2024‑06‑12  

## Overview
This document proposes a practical, experiment‑driven approach to learning cooking fundamentals. It recommends starting with a handful of **foundation techniques** and builds a **4‑week checklist** (2–3 sessions per week) that targets a single “knob” (variable) each session, paired with quick sensory criteria for judging success and a few riff ideas. The plan is tailored for a vegetarian kitchen using a **cast‑iron skillet** and a **sheet pan**.

## Learning Approach
- **Core techniques first** – mastering sautéing, roasting, pan sauces, and simple doughs provides transferable skills.  
- **Single‑variable experimentation** – each session focuses on one adjustable factor (e.g., heat, pan crowding, cut size) to develop intuition.  
- **Sensory feedback loop** – clear visual/taste cues guide whether the tweak succeeded.  
- **Low‑stakes, repeatable dishes** – allow for improvisation without strict recipe adherence.  

## Weekly Checklist
### Week 1 – Sautéing
| Session | Dish Template | Experimental Knob | What to Look / Taste For | Riff Ideas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skillet vegetable sauté | Heat level | Edge browning without burning or mush | Swap onions/shallots; butter vs oil |
| 2 | Skillet tofu or mushrooms | Pan crowding | Good browning when spaced; steaming when crowded | Vary cut shapes; finish with soy, lemon, or vinegar |
| 3 | Mixed veg sauté | Cut size | Uniform finish – no mushy pieces | Add herbs at end; try a quick spice blend |

### Week 2 – Roasting
| Session | Dish Template | Experimental Knob | What to Look / Taste For | Riff Ideas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Sheet‑pan mixed vegetables | Oven temperature | Higher temp → caramelized edges; lower temp → even cooking | Different seasoning mixes; adjust oil amount |
| 5 | Roasted root vegetables | Oil level | Light gloss for browning without sogginess | Add citrus squeeze after roast; whole garlic cloves |
| 6 | Mixed veg roast | Cut size | Centers stay tender while outsides crisp | Swap squash & onions; finish with yogurt or pesto |

### Week 3 – Pan Sauce (vegetarian)
| Session | Dish Template | Experimental Knob | What to Look / Taste For | Riff Ideas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Seared mushrooms with sauce | Amount of fond (sear depth) | Darker, richer sauce from deeper sear | Use thyme vs rosemary; butter vs oil |
| 8 | Veggie base sauce (shallots, zucchini, peppers) | Deglazing liquid | Wine adds brightness, broth adds savoriness, water stays mild | Add splash of cream; finish with mustard |
| 9 | Simple reduction sauce | Reduction time | Sauce coats spoon lightly – not runny or syrupy | Add lemon squeeze; stir in herbs |

### Week 4 – Simple Dough (pasta / flatbread)
| Session | Dish Template | Experimental Knob | What to Look / Taste For | Riff Ideas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|10| Homemade pasta or flatbread dough | Hydration | Softer, more extensible dough with more water; avoid stickiness | Add olive oil; incorporate chopped herbs |
|11| Same dough, varied rest time | Rest period | Dough stretches without tearing, rolls evenly | Roll thinner or thicker; sprinkle cheese |
|12| Shape & thickness experiment | Thickness | Even cooking and texture – thin noodles cook fast, thick bread chewier | Wide vs narrow noodles; skillet flatbread |
