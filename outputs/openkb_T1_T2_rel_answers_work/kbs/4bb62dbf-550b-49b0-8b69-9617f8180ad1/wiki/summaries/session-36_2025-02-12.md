# Summary of session-36_2025-02-12

**Main contribution**: A tightly‑structured, time‑boxed Sunday prep workflow that produces a 5‑night dinner plan using a single grain ([[entities/quinoa]]), two proteins ([[entities/chicken-thighs]], [[entities/tofu]]), and a sheet‑pan of roasted broccoli, peppers, and onions, plus two versatile sauces. The plan includes precise cooking windows, uniform container labels with “use‑by” dates, and a repeatable “grab order” to make weeknight meals automatic.

## Key Concepts
- **Batch cooking as building blocks** – preparing grains, proteins, and veggies separately to mix‑and‑match across meals. [[concepts/building_blocks]]
- **Detailed prep timeline** – 2:00‑2:15 quinoa, 2:15‑2:35 roasted veg, 2:20‑2:45 chicken, 2:20‑2:35 tofu, 2:40‑2:55 sauce blending. *(no wikilink)*
- **Container labeling system** – concise labels (e.g., “Quinoa — Sun Prep — Use by Fri”) that include day, prep source, and expiration. [[concepts/meal_prep_labeling]]
- **Fridge organization & grab order** – grain on top shelf, veggies middle, proteins bottom, sauces in door; nightly retrieval follows grain → veg → protein → sauce. [[concepts/grab_order]]
- **Cross‑week sauce prep** – cilantro‑lime yogurt sauce ([[entities/cilantro-lime-sauce]]) and soy‑ginger sauce ([[entities/soy-ginger-sauce]]) prepared once on Sunday, usable for multiple cuisines. *(no wikilink)*
- **Theme rotation without repeats** – each night uses the same base but different flavor profiles (Mexican bowl, stir‑fry, curry, Mediterranean, comfort pesto). *(no wikilink)*

- Follow the exact Sunday schedule to finish prep by ~3:00 PM.
