# Chicken Thighs

**Type:** product

Chicken thighs are the primary animal‑protein component in the Sunday batch‑cooking workflow described in [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-12]]. They are baked on the prep day, portioned into clear containers, and labeled **“Chicken Thighs — Sun Prep — Use by Thu.”**

## Role in the Meal System
- Serve as a **building block** for multiple dinner themes (Mexican bowl, curry bowl, comfort pesto bowl) – see [[concepts/building_blocks]].
- Stored on the lower fridge shelf alongside [[entities/tofu]] to keep proteins together.
- Retrieved in the nightly **grab order** after the grain and vegetables – see [[concepts/grab_order]].
- Paired with [[entities/quinoa]] (grain base) and [[entities/sheet-pan]] roasted broccoli, peppers, and onions.
- Complemented by the two sauces prepared on Sunday: [[entities/cilantro-lime-sauce]] and [[entities/soy-ginger-sauce]].

## Labeling & Organization
The labeling follows the **meal‑prep labeling** system outlined in the document, using concise, date‑stamped tags to ensure quick identification – see [[concepts/meal_prep_labeling]].

## Key Facts from the Document
- Cooked from 2:20 PM to 2:45 PM on Sunday.
- Cooled, portioned, and stored by 3:00 PM.
- Use‑by date set to Thursday to ensure freshness throughout the week.
- Integrated into a 5‑night plan that alternates between chicken and tofu for variety.
