## Cilantro‑Lime Sauce

**Type:** Product  
**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-12]]

**Description**: A bright, tangy yogurt‑based sauce flavored with fresh cilantro and lime juice, prepared in bulk on Sunday and used throughout the week to add a fresh Mexican‑style note to dinner bowls.

**Key facts from the session**:
- Prepared during the Sunday batch‑cooking window (2:40‑2:55) alongside a soy‑ginger sauce.
- Stored in a small jar in the fridge door, labeled “Cilantro‑Lime — Sun Prep — Use by Fri”.
- Paired with quinoa, roasted vegetables, and chicken thighs on Monday and with tofu on Thursday, fitting into the nightly *grab order*.
- Acts as a **building block** that can be combined with the same grain and veg base to create different cuisine themes. [[concepts/building_blocks]]
- Complements the **meal prep labeling** system by including prep day and use‑by date on its label. [[concepts/meal_prep_labeling]]
- Retrieval follows the standardized **grab order**: grain → vegetables → protein → sauce. [[concepts/grab_order]]

**Usage pattern**:
1. Grab the grain container, then the roasted veg container, then the assigned protein container, and finally the Cilantro‑Lime Sauce jar according to the nightly grab order.
2. Drizzle over the bowl, stir, and serve.
