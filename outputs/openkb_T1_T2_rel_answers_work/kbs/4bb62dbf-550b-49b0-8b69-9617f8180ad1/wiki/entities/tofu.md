# Tofu

**Type:** product

**Overview**
Tofu is a versatile soy‑based protein that features as one of the two main proteins in the Sunday batch‑cooking system described in [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-12]]. It provides a plant‑based alternative to meat and pairs well with a variety of sauces and flavor profiles.

**Key facts from the session**
- Prepared on Sunday alongside quinoa, chicken thighs, and a sheet‑pan of roasted broccoli, peppers, and onions.
- Cooked from 2:20 PM to 2:35 PM, then portioned into a clear container.
- Labeled with a uniform tag: `Tofu — Sun Prep — Use by Fri` to support the [[concepts/meal_prep_labeling]] system.
- Stored on the bottom fridge shelf next to [[entities/chicken-thighs]] for easy retrieval according to the nightly [[concepts/grab_order]].
- Paired with two pre‑made sauces: [[entities/soy-ginger-sauce]] (Asian stir‑fry night) and [[entities/cilantro-lime-sauce]] (Mexican‑style bowl).

**Role in the batch‑cooking workflow**
- Serves as a **building block** that can be combined with the grain [[entities/quinoa]] and roasted vegetables [[entities/sheet-pan]] to create distinct cuisines across five nights. [[concepts/building_blocks]]
- Enables a **theme rotation** without repetition by swapping sauces and protein focus each evening.

**Practical tips**
1. **Cooking method** – pan‑sear or sauté in a hot skillet (e.g., cast‑iron skillet) for a golden crust before cooling.
2. **Storage** – keep in a moisture‑tight container; label clearly as above.
3. **Grab order** – retrieve tofu third (after grain and veggies) on Tuesday, Thursday, and any other night the plan calls for the plant‑based protein.
