# Sheet Pan

A sheet pan is a flat, rectangular metal tray typically used in ovens for roasting, baking, and cooking a variety of foods. In the context of the **session‑09_2024-06-12** cooking plan, the sheet pan serves as the primary tool for the Week 2 roasting experiments.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Purpose:** Enables the **experiment‑driven learning** approach by providing a consistent surface to test variables such as **oven temperature**, **oil level**, and **cut size** during vegetable roasting.
- **Sessions Referenced:**
  - *Session 4*: Sheet‑pan vegetables – knob: oven temperature.
  - *Session 5*: Roasted root vegetables – knob: oil level.
  - *Session 6*: Mixed veg roast – knob: cut size.
- **Learning Benefits:** Aligns with the [[concepts/sensory-feedback-loop]] by offering visual cues (browning, caramelization) that help assess the effect of each experimental knob.
- **Complementary Tool:** Often paired with a [[entities/cast-iron-skillet]] for complementary skillet‑based sauté sessions in Week 1.
