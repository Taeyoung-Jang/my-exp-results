# Soy‑Ginger Sauce

**Type:** product

**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-12]]

## Overview
Soy‑Ginger Sauce is a quick‑mix, savory condiment prepared in a single batch on Sunday. It combines soy sauce, fresh ginger, garlic, and a touch of sweetener (e.g., honey or maple syrup) to create a flavor profile that works across multiple cuisines throughout the week.

## Key Facts from Session 36 (2025‑02‑12)
- **Prep timing:** Blended between 2:40 – 2:55 PM during the Sunday batch‑cooking session.
- **Labeling:** Stored in a small glass jar labeled “Soy‑Ginger — Sun Prep — Use by Fri.”
- **Storage location:** Placed in the fridge door for easy access during the nightly “grab order.”
- **Usage pattern:** Paired with tofu on Tuesday and Thursday, and with chicken thighs on Wednesday and Friday, providing a consistent yet varied flavor base.
- **Role in workflow:** Serves as a **building block** that can be applied to different meal themes, supporting the [[concepts/building_blocks]] approach to batch cooking.
- **Integration with meal prep system:** The sauce is part of the standardized [[concepts/meal_prep_labeling]] and follows the nightly [[concepts/grab_order]] (grain → veggies → protein → sauce).

## Typical Ingredients (not exhaustive)
- Soy sauce
- Fresh ginger, minced
- Garlic, minced
- Honey or maple syrup
- Rice vinegar (optional) 
- Sesame oil (optional)

## How to Use
1. **Stir‑fry:** Add a tablespoon while sautéing tofu and roasted vegetables for an Asian‑style bowl.
2. **Marinade:** Toss chicken thighs with a thin coat before reheating for a quick flavor boost.
3. **Finishing sauce:** Drizzle over reheated quinoa and veggies just before serving.
