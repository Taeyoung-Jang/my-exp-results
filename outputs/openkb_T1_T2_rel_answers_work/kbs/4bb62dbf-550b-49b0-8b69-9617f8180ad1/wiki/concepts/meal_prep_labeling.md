# Meal Prep Labeling

**Definition**: A systematic approach to labeling meal‑prep containers with concise, uniform information (e.g., food item, prep source, and use‑by date) to enable rapid identification and selection of components during busy weeknights.

## Core Principles
- **Consistency**: Use the same label format for every container, such as "[Food] — Sun Prep — Use by [Day]".
- **Visibility**: Write on clear tape or a marker directly on the container so the label is readable at a glance.
- **Prioritization**: Include the most critical decision points (food type, prep day, expiration) to eliminate mental overhead.

## Implementation from [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-12]]
- **Label examples**:
  - "Quinoa — Sun Prep — Use by Fri"
  - "Roast Veg — Sun Prep — Use by Fri"
  - "Chicken Thighs — Sun Prep — Use by Thu"
  - "Tofu — Sun Prep — Use by Fri"
  - "Cilantro‑Lime — Sun Prep — Use by Fri"
  - "Soy‑Ginger — Sun Prep — Use by Fri"
- **Container placement**: Grain on top shelf, vegetables middle, proteins bottom, sauces in the door. This layout works hand‑in‑hand with the [[concepts/grab_order]] routine.
- **Time‑boxing**: Labels are applied during the Sunday prep window (2:40‑2:55) after cooking and cooling, ensuring they are ready before storage.

## Benefits
1. **Speed** – At 6 PM, the grab order reduces decision time to seconds.
2. **Reliability** – Clear use‑by dates prevent waste and keep food safety top‑of‑mind.
3. **Scalability** – The same system can be extended to other meals, snacks, or batch‑cooked items.
4. **Cognitive load reduction** – By externalizing the “what’s where” knowledge, you free mental bandwidth for other tasks.
