# Herdez (product)

**Type:** product

## Overview
Herdez is a commercial brand that produces a range of Mexican‑style salsas, sauces, and related condiment products. The brand is widely available in North America and is frequently cited as a reliable, peanut‑free option for consumers with nut allergies.
