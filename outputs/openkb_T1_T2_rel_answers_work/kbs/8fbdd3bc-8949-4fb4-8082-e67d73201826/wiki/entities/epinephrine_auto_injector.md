## Epinephrine Auto‑Injector

**Type:** product

**Description:** A compact, pre‑filled device designed to deliver a rapid intramuscular dose of epinephrine (adrenaline) during anaphylactic reactions.

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-31_2023-09-06]]
- Used as a first‑line emergency treatment for severe food‑allergy reactions.
- Decision to discontinue carrying it after a **supervised oral food challenge** is individualized and depends on prior reaction severity, confidence in sustained tolerance, and overall health factors.
- Patients are advised to resume carrying the injector if they experience any new or repeatable immediate‑type symptoms (e.g., oral itching, hives, gastrointestinal distress, light‑headedness) after re‑introducing the food.
