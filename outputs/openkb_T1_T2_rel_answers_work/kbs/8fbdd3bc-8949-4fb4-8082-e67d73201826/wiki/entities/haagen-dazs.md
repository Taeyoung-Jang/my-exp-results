# Häagen‑Dazs

**Category:** Premium ice‑cream (product)

## Overview
Häagen‑Dazs is a well‑known brand of ice‑cream that offers a range of single‑flavor varieties such as vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, and mint‑chip. In the context of the *session‑08_2021‑09‑06* conversation, it was recommended as a reliable, peanut‑free option for a dessert sundae bar when guests have severe peanut allergies.

## Relevance to Peanut‑Allergy Safety
- The brand’s single‑flavor products are generally produced on dedicated lines, reducing the risk of cross‑contamination with peanuts.
- Recommended low‑risk flavors include vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, and mint‑chip (provided the mix‑ins are also nut‑free).
- When selecting Häagen‑Dazs, the conversation advises checking the label for any “may contain peanuts” or “processed on shared equipment” statements, as manufacturing facilities can change.

## Suggested Use in a Get‑Together
- Serve individual bowls of Häagen‑Dazs alongside a separate set of nut‑free toppings (fresh berries, banana slices, chocolate syrup, caramel sauce, nut‑free sprinkles).
- Follow the [[concepts/food-safety-serving-setup]] guidelines: use dedicated scoops for each flavor, keep toppings in separate containers, and place clear signage indicating “nut‑free, please use this scoop only.”

## Related Brands Mentioned
- Other peanut‑free ice‑cream options referenced in the same discussion include Tillamook.  
- For broader snack choices, the conversation also listed Simple Mills, Triscuit, Sabra, Ithaca, Frontera, and Herdez as reliable peanut‑free brands.
