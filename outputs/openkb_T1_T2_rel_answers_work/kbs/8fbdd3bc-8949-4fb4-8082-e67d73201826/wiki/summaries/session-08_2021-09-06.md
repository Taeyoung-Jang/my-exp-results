## Peanut "Watch List"
- Peanut oil, peanut flour, ground nuts, mixed‑nut products  
- Generic terms: “nut paste”, “nut meal”  
- Packaged cookies, granola bars, candies  
- Asian‑style sauces (e.g., satay, hoisin) that may contain hidden peanuts  
- Labels stating **"may contain peanuts"** or **"processed on shared equipment"**

## Allergy‑Safe Communication
- Ask guests casually: *“I’m planning the menu and want to make sure everyone can enjoy everything—any allergies or no‑go foods I should know about?”*  
- Keep the tone light; frame it as part of ordinary planning. See [[concepts/allergen-communication]] for more detail.

## Dessert‑Table Setup Tips
- Separate bowls for each ice‑cream flavor with dedicated scoops.  
- Separate containers/spoons for toppings.  
- Small signage (e.g., “nut‑free – use this scoop only”).  
- Position the dessert area away from other foods to reduce accidental cross‑contact. See [[concepts/food-safety-serving-setup]].
