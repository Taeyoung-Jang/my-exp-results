# Summary of Session 37 (2024‑10‑30)

**Main focus:** Practical, water‑efficient drip‑irrigation setup and schedule for establishing desert‑style plants (agave and ocotillo) on sandy soil with a gravel surface.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/desert-landscaping]] – design principles that prioritize low water use, simple plant palettes, and clean, stable ground.  
- [[concepts/drip-irrigation]] – precise, low‑flow watering method; importance of matching emitter flow to plant needs.  
- [[concepts/establishment-phase-watering]] – deep, infrequent watering to encourage root penetration during the first 8 weeks.  
- soil‑type impact – how sandy, fast‑draining soils affect frequency and duration of watering.  
- rain offset – adjusting drip schedules when natural precipitation provides a sufficient soak.  
- multi‑emitter strategy – using two emitters to broaden moisture distribution and reduce crown rot.

## Irrigation Priorities
1. **Map water zones** and verify emitter placement before plant selection.  
2. Choose **drought‑tolerant plants** (e.g., [[entities/agave]], [[entities/ocotillo]]) with adequate spacing.  
3. Maintain a **clean, gravel‑covered ground** to reduce evaporation and weed pressure.

## Watering Numbers & Schedule
| Plant | Target volume per watering | 1 GPH emitter | 2 GPH emitter |
|-------|---------------------------|--------------|--------------|
| [[entities/agave]] (establishment) | 3–5 gal | 3–5 hr | 1.5–2.5 hr |
| [[entities/ocotillo]] (establishment) | 4–6 gal | 4–6 hr | 2–3 hr |

- **First 4 weeks:** run [[entities/drip-emitter]]s once per week (≈1 hr for two 2 GPH ocotillo emitters; ≈2 hr total for two 1 GPH agave emitters).  
- **Weeks 5‑8:** extend interval to 10‑14 days if soil dries fully between cycles.  
- **Rain adjustment:** skip a cycle only when rain wets the soil a few inches deep; light sprinkles do not count.

## Emitter Placement Recommendations
- **Agave:** Prefer two 1 GPH emitters spaced around the base to create a wider wet zone and avoid crown rot.  
- **Ocotillo:** One emitter is usually sufficient; two 2 GPH emitters can be used for a 1‑hour weekly run to achieve the target volume.

## Practical Tips
- After each run, check soil moisture 2‑3 in. deep; if still damp, delay the next cycle.  
- Adjust duration rather than frequency when temperatures rise; aim for deeper, less frequent watering.  
- Periodically refresh the gravel layer to keep the surface neat and to further reduce evaporation.
