# Summary of session-05_2022-10-30

**Main goal**: Provide inexpensive, quick‑win methods to make a room feel warmer, reduce drafts, and manage moisture around windows.

## Key Recommendations
- Install removable [[concepts/window_insulation]] film for an immediate barrier.  
- Apply **weatherstripping** to moving parts of the window and **caulk** to rigid seams.  
- Use a [[entities/dehumidifier]] or improve ventilation to prevent condensation.  
- Add [[entities/thermal_curtains]] or heavier fabric panels to retain heat and limit moisture buildup.  
- Regularly clean the window area to stop mold growth and check for loose exterior caulk.

## Weatherstripping vs. Caulk
- **Weatherstripping**: best for sash and track areas that move; foam or rubber strips are easy to cut and press in place.  
- **Caulk**: best for fixed seams where the frame meets the wall or trim; use paint‑able exterior‑grade silicone or latex‑silicone [[entities/exterior_grade_silicone_caulk]].  
- Recommended order: **caulk first** (needs curing time), then **weatherstripping**.

## Tools & Materials Checklist
- [[entities/medium_density_adhesive_foam_weatherstripping]] (window/door grade).  
- [[entities/exterior_grade_silicone_caulk]].  
- Caulk gun, razor scraper/putty knife, rag, mild cleaner.  
- Optional: small [[entities/dehumidifier]] or moisture absorber.

## Weekend DIY Plan
### Saturday (Caulking)
1. Scrape off old caulk, clean seams.  
2. Apply a fresh bead, smooth with a damp finger.  
3. Let cure for a few hours to overnight (dry to touch by evening).

### Sunday (Weatherstripping)
1. Install foam weatherstripping on sash or frame where drafts remain.  
2. Test window operation; adjust if needed.  
3. If condensation appears, briefly vent the window, run a dehumidifier, and ensure curtains don’t block airflow.

## Prioritized Impact Plan
- **Biggest impact**: seal trim‑to‑wall gaps with caulk on Saturday.  
- **Sash leaks**: add weatherstripping on Sunday.  
- If drafts persist after caulking, touch up specific spots with extra caulk.

## Quick Draft Diagnosis (No Tools)
- Close window fully, run the back of your hand around the perimeter.  
- Strong airflow at moving parts → **sash leak**.  
- Strong airflow at outer edge where frame meets wall/trim → **trim/frame leak**.  
- A thin tissue can also reveal fluttering at the leak point.

## Weatherstripping Placement for [[entities/double_hung_window]]
- Vertical sides of each sash where they meet the tracks.  
- Horizontal meeting rail where upper and lower sashes close.  
- Top edge of the upper sash if it’s loose.  
- Avoid the bottom edge that needs to drain water.
