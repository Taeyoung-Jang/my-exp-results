# Dehumidifier

A **dehumidifier** is a portable appliance used to lower indoor humidity levels by extracting moisture from the air. In the context of [[summaries/session-05_2022-10-30]], it is suggested as an optional tool to manage condensation that can occur after sealing windows with [[concepts/caulking]] and [[concepts/weatherstripping]].

## Key Points from the Session
- Recommended as a supplemental measure if condensation appears after applying window‑insulation film or sealing drafts.
- Helps keep the room comfortable in coastal environments where dampness can settle quickly, especially after the late‑October frost.
- Can be used in conjunction with other moisture‑control strategies such as improving ventilation and using [[concepts/window_insulation]] film.

## Related Products & Materials
- Works well alongside [[entities/medium_density_adhesive_foam_weatherstripping]] and [[entities/exterior_grade_silicone_caulk]] when performing the weekend DIY plan for draft reduction.
- Complementary to [[entities/thermal_curtains]] which also aid in retaining heat and reducing surface condensation.

## Practical Usage Tips
1. **Placement**: Position the dehumidifier near the window after sealing to capture any residual moisture.
2. **Operation**: Run it continuously for the first 24‑48 hours post‑sealing, then adjust based on humidity readings.
3. **Maintenance**: Empty the water collection bucket regularly and clean the filter to maintain efficiency.
