# Agave (plant)

**Type:** other  
**Description:** Drought‑tolerant succulent commonly used in desert‑style landscaping.

## Overview
Agave is a slow‑growing succulent native to arid regions. It is valued for its striking rosette form, low water needs, and ability to thrive in sandy, well‑drained soils.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-37_2024-10-30]]

- **Water requirement during establishment:** 3–5 gallons per watering.
- **Drip‑emitter recommendations:**
  - With a 1 GPH emitter → run 3–5 hours.
  - With a 2 GPH emitter → run 1.5–2.5 hours.
  - Preferred setup: two 1 GPH [[entities/drip-emitter]] placed a short distance apart around the base to spread moisture and reduce crown rot.
- **Schedule:**
  - Weeks 1‑4: water once per week.
  - Weeks 5‑8: extend to every 10‑14 days, allowing the soil to dry fully between cycles.
- **Soil context:** Sandy substrate with a layer of decomposed granite; fast drainage favors deep, infrequent watering.
