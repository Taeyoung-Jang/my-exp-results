# Medium‑Density Adhesive Foam Weatherstripping

**Type:** product

Medium‑density adhesive foam weatherstripping is a flexible, self‑adhesive strip designed for sealing the moving parts of windows and doors. It is commonly sold in rolls that can be cut to length and pressed onto the frame or sash.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-05_2022-10-30]]
- Recommended as the primary **[[concepts/weatherstripping]]** material for double‑hung windows.
- Ideal for the vertical sides of the sash, the horizontal meeting rail, and any track where the window slides.
- The adhesive backing allows quick installation without tools; simply cut to size and press firmly.
- Works best when paired with **[[entities/exterior_grade_silicone_caulk]]** for sealing fixed seams (trim/frame) before applying the foam.
- Part of a budget‑friendly weekend DIY plan: caulk on Saturday, foam weatherstripping on Sunday.

## How It Relates to Other Items
- Used alongside **[[entities/exterior_grade_silicone_caulk]]** to address both **[[concepts/caulking]]** and **[[concepts/weatherstripping]]** needs.
- Often installed on **[[entities/double_hung_window]]** units to reduce drafts.
- Complements **[[entities/thermal_curtains]]** and optional **[[entities/dehumidifier]]** for overall moisture and temperature control.
- Contributes to overall **[[concepts/window_insulation]]** strategy.

## Practical Tips
1. **Preparation:** Clean the surface where the strip will be applied; remove old caulk or debris.
2. **Cutting:** Measure the length needed, cut with scissors or a utility knife.
3. **Application:** Peel the backing and press the foam onto the sash or track, ensuring a tight seal.
4. **Testing:** After installation, close the window and feel for drafts; adjust if necessary.
