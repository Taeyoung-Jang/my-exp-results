# Establishment Phase Watering

[[concepts/establishment-phase-watering]] describes the deep, infrequent watering approach used during the first
8 weeks (or about two months) after planting drought‑tolerant species such as [[entities/agave]] and [[entities/ocotillo]].
It is a core sub‑practice of [[concepts/desert-landscaping]] and relies on precise [[concepts/drip-irrigation]].

## Why it matters
- Encourages roots to grow deeper rather than staying near the surface.
- Reduces overall water use by avoiding frequent light watering that evaporates quickly.
- Fits sandy or fast‑draining soils where moisture disappears rapidly.

## Typical parameters (from [[summaries/session-37_2024-10-30]])
| Plant | Target volume per watering | Typical emitter flow | Approx. run time | Frequency (first 8 weeks) |
|-------|---------------------------|----------------------|------------------|----------------------------|
| Agave | 3–5 gal | 1 GPH → 3–5 hr  <br> 2 GPH → 1.5–2.5 hr | 3–5 hr (1 GPH) or 1.5–2.5 hr (2 GPH) | Weekly first month, then every 10‑14 days |
| Ocotillo | 4–6 gal | 1 GPH → 4–6 hr <br> 2 GPH → 2–3 hr | 4–6 hr (1 GPH) or 2–3 hr (2 GPH) | Weekly first month, then every 10‑14 days |

### Adjustments
- **Soil type:** Sandy soils drain fast, so maintain the deep‑water, long‑interval schedule.
- **Rainfall:** Skip a scheduled cycle only when rain wets the soil a few inches deep; light sprinkles do not count.
- **Emitter arrangement:** For agave, two low‑flow emitters spaced around the base are preferred to spread moisture and protect the crown; ocotillo can use a single emitter or two 2 GPH emitters for a 1‑hour weekly run.

## Practical checklist
1. Set emitter flow (1 GPH or 2 GPH) based on available hardware.
2. Calculate run time to achieve the target volume.
3. Run the cycle, then check soil moisture 2–3 in. deep.
4. If soil is still damp, postpone the next cycle.
5. After 8 weeks, gradually extend the interval while maintaining deep soak volumes.

Establishment Phase Watering is a low‑maintenance, water‑conserving strategy that sets the foundation for a thriving, drought‑tolerant landscape.
