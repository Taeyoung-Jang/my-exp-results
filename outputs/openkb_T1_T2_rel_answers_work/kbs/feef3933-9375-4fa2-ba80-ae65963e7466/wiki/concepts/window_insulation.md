# Window Insulation

**Definition**: Methods and materials used to reduce heat loss, block drafts, and control moisture at window assemblies.

## Core Techniques

1. **Removable window‑insulation film** – a clear, shrink‑able plastic sheet applied directly to the glass.  It creates an additional airtight barrier that significantly cuts drafts with minimal cost.
2. **Thermal curtains** – heavy fabric panels that add a layer of insulation, retain warm air, and limit condensation on the glass surface.  See [[entities/thermal_curtains]].
3. **Weatherstripping** – foam or rubber strips applied to the moving parts of a window (sash, tracks).  While primarily a draft‑seal, it also reduces thermal bridging at the sash edges.  Related concept: [[concepts/weatherstripping]].
4. **Caulking** – sealing fixed seams where the window frame meets the wall or trim to stop air infiltration.  Use paintable exterior‑grade silicone caulk for durability.  Related concept: [[concepts/caulking]].
5. **Dehumidification** – controlling interior moisture after sealing to prevent condensation on the newly insulated glass.  Small portable units are often sufficient.  See [[entities/dehumidifier]].

## Practical Weekend Implementation (from [[summaries/session-05_2022-10-30]])

- **Saturday**: Prioritize caulking exterior seams to stop the largest drafts.  Apply a fresh bead of exterior‑grade silicone, smooth it, and allow it to cure (few hours to overnight).
- **Sunday**: Install removable insulation film on the glass, then apply weatherstripping to the sash and tracks.  Finish by hanging thermal curtains.

## Quick Draft Diagnosis

- Run the back of your hand or a thin tissue around the window perimeter.  Air movement at the sash indicates a **sash leak** (addressed with weatherstripping).  Air movement at the frame‑to‑wall edge indicates a **trim/frame leak** (addressed with caulking).

## Benefits

- Reduced heating costs and more consistent indoor temperature.
- Lower risk of condensation and mold growth.
- Improved comfort without major renovations.
