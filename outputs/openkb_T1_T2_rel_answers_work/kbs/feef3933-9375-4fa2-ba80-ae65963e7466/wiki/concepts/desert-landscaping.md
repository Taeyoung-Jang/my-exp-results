# Desert Landscaping

**Definition**: Desert landscaping is a design approach that emphasizes water‑efficiency, minimal maintenance, and the use of drought‑tolerant plants to create attractive outdoor spaces in hot, dry climates.

## Core Principles
1. **Efficient Irrigation** – Use precise, low‑flow systems such as drip irrigation to deliver water only where it is needed.  [[concepts/drip-irrigation]]
2. **Plant Selection** – Choose a limited palette of native or well‑adapted succulents and desert shrubs (e.g., [[entities/agave]], [[entities/ocotillo]]) that require little water once established.
3. **Soil & Ground Cover** – Incorporate sandy or gravelly substrates that promote rapid drainage and reduce evaporation. Periodic replenishment of gravel keeps the area tidy and stable.
4. **Establishment‑Phase Watering** – During the first 8 weeks, provide deep, infrequent waterings to encourage root penetration before transitioning to a more hands‑off schedule. [[concepts/establishment-phase-watering]]
5. **Weed Management** – Early removal of small weeds prevents them from competing for moisture and reduces long‑term maintenance.

## Irrigation Strategy (from [[summaries/session-37_2024-10-30]])
- **Mapping Water Zones**: Identify where each plant’s root zone lies and place emitters accordingly.
- **Emitter Flow Matching**: Use 1 GPH or 2 GPH emitters to deliver 3‑6 gallons per plant per session, adjusting duration based on flow rate.
- **Scheduling**:
  - Weeks 1‑4: Water once per week (≈1 hour for two 2 GPH ocotillo emitters; ≈2 hours total for two 1 GPH agave emitters).
  - Weeks 5‑8: Extend to every 10‑14 days, ensuring the soil dries completely between cycles.
- **Rain Offsets**: Skip a scheduled watering only when a rain event wets the soil a few inches deep.

## Benefits
- **Water Conservation** – Deep, infrequent watering reduces overall consumption.
- **Reduced Labor** – Fewer watering events and simple plant palettes lower routine upkeep.
- **Aesthetic Appeal** – Gravel and clean ground surfaces maintain a tidy look while minimizing evaporation.
