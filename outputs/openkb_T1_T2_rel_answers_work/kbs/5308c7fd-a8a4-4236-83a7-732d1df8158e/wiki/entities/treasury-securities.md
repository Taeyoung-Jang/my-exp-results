# Treasury Securities

Treasury securities are debt instruments issued by a national government—most commonly the U.S. Treasury—to finance public spending. They are considered among the safest investments because they are backed by the full faith and credit of the issuing government.

## Key facts from the session‑15 discussion
- Recommended as the defensive component of a mixed‑asset portfolio to improve downside protection when the equity side (stocks) and corporate bond funds suffer losses.
- Suggested to make up **about 50 % to two‑thirds** of the fixed‑income slice, with the remainder allocated to corporate bond funds for income.
- Their low credit risk helps reduce overall portfolio drawdowns during market stress.
