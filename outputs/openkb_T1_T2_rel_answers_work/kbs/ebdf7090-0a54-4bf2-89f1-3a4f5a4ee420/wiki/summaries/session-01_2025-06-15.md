# Summary of session-01_2025-06-15

**Date:** 2025‑06‑15  

## Core Advice
- Use a **visually lossless** [[concepts/mezzanine-codec]] for both intermediate chunks and the final master (ProRes on macOS, DNxHR on Windows).
- Keep the export at full 8K resolution, original frame rate, and avoid extra scaling or heavy effects during the render.
- Leverage a [[concepts/proxy-workflow]] during editing to keep the timeline responsive, then switch back to full‑resolution media for the final export.

## Chunked Export Workflow for [[entities/adobe-premiere-pro]] (Windows / DNxHR)
1. **Chunk length:** 5–10 minutes per segment – long enough to limit file count, short enough to prevent crashes.  
2. **Method:** Duplicate the master sequence and trim each copy to cover a clean, back‑to‑back range (preferred over manual in/out cuts to avoid gaps/overlaps).  
3. **Export settings for each chunk:**  
   - **Container:** QuickTime (MOV) – can also use MXF if required by delivery specs.  
   - **Codec:** DNxHR **HQ** (10‑bit) – switch to **HQX** only for 10‑bit log footage demanding maximum fidelity.  
   - **Resolution:** Full 8K (e.g., 7680×4320 for UHD, 8192×4320 for DCI).  
   - **Bit depth:** 10‑bit.  
   - **Audio:** PCM, 48 kHz, 24‑bit.  
   - **Render at Maximum Depth:** *Off* (unless specific color work demands it).  
   - **Use Maximum Render Quality:** *Off* for 1:1 scaling; turn on only if scaling is performed.  
4. **Stitching:** Create a new empty 8K sequence, import the chunk files in order, and export **once more** using the identical DNxHR preset – this is effectively a re‑wrap, preserving quality.

## Off‑loading Render Work
- **[[entities/adobe-team-projects]]** – share the project to a more powerful workstation.  
- **Cloud workstations** such as **[[entities/aws-ec2-g-series]]** or **[[entities/paperspace]]** – rent a GPU‑enabled VM, install Premiere, and render remotely.  
- **Export XML/EDL** and hand the project to a collaborator with a higher‑spec machine.

## Recommendations Recap
- **Start with DNxHR HQ** (or HQX for 10‑bit log) in MOV containers for both chunks and final master.  
- **Chunk length:** 5‑10 min, duplicate‑sequence method to avoid timeline gaps.  
- **Offload** via [[entities/adobe-team-projects]] or cloud VMs if local hardware fails.  
- **Enable** only necessary render quality options to keep render time reasonable.  

**Next steps:** Provide the exact 8K delivery specification (UHD, DCI, anamorphic) to fine‑tune resolution and pixel‑aspect settings.
