# Career Center

**Type:** organization

The Career Center is a university service that assists students in exploring career options, developing job‑search skills, and connecting with industry professionals.  In the context of *session‑12_2020‑04‑14*, the Career Center is recommended as a primary channel for arranging brief informational interviews with students and professionals in computer science, business, and psychology.

## Role in the Session
- Suggested as a resource to **set up 1‑2 short chats per field** (15‑minute informational interviews) during the first week of the decision‑making plan.
- Provides **access to alumni networks** and **career‑center contacts**, facilitating introductions to working professionals.
- Can offer **workshops or quick tutorials** that complement the low‑commitment experiments outlined in the plan.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/career-decision-making]] – The broader process of weighing interest, market trends, and personal fit.
- [[concepts/low-commitment-experiments]] – The Career Center helps coordinate the short‑term experiments (interviews, mini‑projects).
- [[concepts/reflection-tracking]] – Students are advised to log their interview reflections, a practice the Career Center may support through templates or counseling sessions.
