# LinkedIn

**Type:** Organization

**Description:** LinkedIn is a global professional networking platform that enables users to connect, share, and discover career opportunities.

**Relevance to session-12 (2020‑04‑14):** In the suggested 4‑week decision‑making plan, LinkedIn is recommended as a channel to reach out to working professionals for brief informational interviews. These interviews are part of the [[concepts/low-commitment-experiments]] used to explore potential majors such as computer science, business, and psychology, supporting the broader [[concepts/career-decision-making]] process.
