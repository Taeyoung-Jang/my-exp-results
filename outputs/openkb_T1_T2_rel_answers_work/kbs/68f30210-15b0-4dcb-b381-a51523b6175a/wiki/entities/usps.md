# USPS

**Type:** Organization  

**Description:** The United States Postal Service (USPS) is the national mail delivery service of the United States, operating as an independent government agency.

## Role in address consistency

In the context of [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]], USPS is listed under the “Nice to check” tier of the [[concepts/prioritized_checklist]] for maintaining [[concepts/address_consistency]]. It is a delivery‑heavy account where address mismatches typically cause only minor inconveniences (e.g., misrouted packages).

## Verification needs

USPS generally requires only standard login credentials; it does not normally impose extensive identity verification beyond password or two‑factor authentication.
