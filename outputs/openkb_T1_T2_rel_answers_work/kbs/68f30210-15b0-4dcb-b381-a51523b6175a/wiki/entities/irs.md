# Internal Revenue Service (IRS)

**Type:** organization

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is the United States government agency tasked with collecting federal taxes and enforcing tax laws.

## Relevance in Session 13 (2024‑06‑15)

In the *must‑check* portion of the address‑verification checklist, the IRS appears as a critical item that should have the correct mailing and billing address. The session‑13 summary notes that the IRS (referenced via [[entities/irs]]) typically requires multi‑step identity verification, such as security questions, two‑factor authentication, or document upload, before allowing an address change.

## Relationship to Key Concepts

- The IRS is part of the broader strategy of maintaining **[[concepts/address_consistency]]** by ensuring that the primary tax authority reflects the user’s current address.
- It fits into the **[[concepts/prioritized_checklist]]** as a *must‑check* entity, alongside banks, credit bureaus, and the DMV.
- Updating the IRS aligns with the **[[concepts/source_of_truth]]** approach: the IRS should be updated after the user’s primary source of truth (e.g., their main bank) is corrected, ensuring downstream consistency.
