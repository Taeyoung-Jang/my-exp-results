# Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)

The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is a state-level **organization** responsible for issuing driver licenses, vehicle registrations, and maintaining associated records. In the context of personal data hygiene, the DMV is highlighted as a **must‑check** entity for address verification.

## Role in Address Consistency
- The DMV is listed in the *must‑check* tier of the **prioritized checklist** for confirming that your mailing address is up‑to‑date across critical services. [[concepts/prioritized_checklist]]
- Keeping the DMV record current is essential because many downstream services (e.g., banks, credit bureaus) may rely on it as a secondary source of truth for address validation. [[concepts/source_of_truth]]
- An accurate DMV address contributes to overall **address consistency**, reducing the risk of missed legal notices or registration renewals. [[concepts/address_consistency]]
