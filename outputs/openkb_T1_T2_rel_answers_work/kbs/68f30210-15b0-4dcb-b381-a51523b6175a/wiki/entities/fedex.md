## FedEx (Organization)

FedEx (Federal Express) is a multinational courier delivery services company that provides shipping, freight, and logistics solutions worldwide.

### Relevance in Session 13 (2024‑06‑15)
In the *Nice to check* tier of the address‑verification checklist, FedEx is listed alongside other delivery‑heavy services such as UPS and USPS. Ensuring your address is up‑to‑date with FedEx helps prevent misdelivered packages and aligns with the broader goal of **[[concepts/address_consistency]]**.

### Checklist Placement
- **Tier:** Nice to check
- **Reason:** Primarily for convenience; FedEx rarely requires extra identity verification beyond standard login credentials.
- **Action:** Verify the stored shipping address in your FedEx online account or through any third‑party platforms (e.g., e‑commerce sites) that use FedEx for fulfillment.

### Related Concepts
- Maintaining a **[[concepts/source_of_truth]]** for your address ensures updates propagate to services like FedEx.
- The **[[concepts/prioritized_checklist]]** framework categorizes FedEx under less‑critical, yet still useful, services.
