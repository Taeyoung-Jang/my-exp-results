# Equifax

**Type:** Organization (credit bureau)

Equifax is one of the three primary credit bureaus in the United States, alongside Experian and TransUnion. It collects and maintains consumer credit information, which lenders and other institutions use for identity verification, credit checks, and risk assessment.

## Role in Session 13 (2024‑06‑15)
- Listed under the **Must check** category of the address consistency checklist.  Ensuring your address is up‑to‑date with Equifax is critical because errors can affect credit reports and downstream services that rely on credit‑bureau data.
- Updating Equifax typically requires multi‑step identity verification, similar to other high‑risk services.
