# Summary of Session 13 (2024-06-15)

**Main Idea**: Establish a single *source of truth* for your address and billing details, then verify that information across key categories using a three‑tiered checklist (Must, Should, Nice).

## Key Concepts
- **Source of Truth** – Central location (e.g., primary bank) where the address is first updated. [[concepts/source_of_truth]]
- **Address Consistency** – Keeping the same address across banks, credit bureaus, government records, and online accounts. [[concepts/address_consistency]]
- **Prioritized Checklist** – Structured into *Must check*, *Should check*, and *Nice to check* groups. [[concepts/prioritized_checklist]]
- **Identity Verification** – Extra steps (security questions, two‑factor authentication, document upload) required by high‑risk services.
- **Password Manager Integration** – Storing address and payment info for autofill and consistency.

## Checklist Overview
### Must check
- Primary bank and credit card accounts  
- Three major credit bureaus: [[entities/experian]], [[entities/equifax]], [[entities/transunion]]  
- [[entities/dmv]] record  
- Tax authority account (e.g., [[entities/irs]] or local tax office)  

> These services typically require multi‑step identity verification.

### Should check
- Mobile carrier  
- Utilities (electric, water, gas)  
- Insurance providers  
- Payroll/benefits portals  
- Major payment hubs: [[entities/amazon]], [[entities/paypal]], [[entities/apple]], [[entities/google]]  

> Usually protected by two‑factor authentication but no extensive document checks.

### Nice to check
- Delivery‑heavy accounts: [[entities/ups]], [[entities/fedex]], [[entities/usps]]  
- Streaming services  
- Loyalty programs  
- Gym memberships  
- Smaller subscription boxes  

> Primarily for convenience; minimal verification.

## Practical Tips
1. **Update the source of truth first** and let changes propagate to linked services.  
2. **Maintain a short running list** of services; cross off each after updating.  
3. **Leverage a password manager** to store and autofill address/payment data, making mismatches obvious.  
4. **Periodically audit** the Must and Should groups to ensure ongoing consistency.  

By confirming the *must* items, you secure legal and financial integrity; the *should* and *nice* groups smooth daily operations and prevent minor inconveniences.
