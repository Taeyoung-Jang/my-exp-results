# Summary of Session 33 (2025‑02‑13)

**Scope** – Provides a concrete, screenshot‑friendly plan for managing sleep, nutrition, and housing while working rotating night shifts.

## Key Concepts
- **Anchor Sleep** – A fixed daily rest block that remains constant despite schedule changes. [[concepts/anchor_sleep]]
- **Shift‑Work Nap Strategy** – Targeted napping before and after double shifts to prevent cumulative fatigue. [[concepts/shift_work_nap_strategy]]
- **Night‑Shift Nutrition Kit** – Minimal, non‑perishable foods and sleep aids that fit in a bag. [[concepts/night_shift_nutrition_kit]]
- **Apartment Hunting for Shift Workers** – Criteria and outreach questions focused on daytime quiet, blackout capability, and HVAC performance. [[concepts/apartment_hunting_shift_workers]]
- **Daytime Noise Mitigation** – Checklist items to evaluate a unit’s suitability for daytime sleeping. [[concepts/daytime_noise_mitigation]]

## Sleep Schedules (Exact Times)
### Normal 10 p.m.–6 a.m. Shift
- Home ≈ 7:10 a.m.  
- Wind‑down: 7:10 – 8:30 a.m.  
- **Anchor sleep**: 8:30 a.m. – 12:30 p.m.  
- Awake/errands/meals: 12:30 – 4:00 p.m.  
- **Second sleep**: 4:00 – 7:00 p.m.  
- Light meal before shift: 7:00 – 9:30 p.m.  
- **Caffeine cutoff**: ≤ midnight  

### 2 p.m.–6 a.m. Double Shift
- Home ≈ 7:10 a.m.  
- Wind‑down: 7:10 – 8:30 a.m.  
- **Anchor sleep**: 8:30 a.m. – 12:30 p.m.  
- Pre‑double meal: 12:30 – 1:15 p.m.  
- If only a 20–30 min break: use for a power nap **or** eat/hydrate.  
- Post‑double **crash sleep**: 7:10 – 9:30 a.m.  
- Reset awake block: 9:30 – 12:30 p.m.  
- **Anchor sleep** again: 12:30 – 4:30 p.m.  
- Light evening, optional early second sleep.  
- **Caffeine cutoff**: ≤ 10 p.m.  

### 6 p.m.–6 a.m. Flip Shift
- After prior normal shift, home at 7:10 a.m.  
- Wind‑down: 7:10 – 8:30 a.m.  
- **Anchor sleep**: 8:30 a.m. – 12:30 p.m.  
- Awake block: 12:30 – 3:00 p.m.  
- **Adjustment sleep**: 3:00 – 6:00 p.m.  
- Meal + commute: 6:00 – 6:45 p.m.  
- Shift start: 6 p.m.  
- **Caffeine cutoff**: ≤ 11 p.m.  

## "Always‑in‑Bag" Kit (Sleep + Food)
- Contoured eye mask  
- Soft foam earplugs  
- Small travel pillow  
- 1–2 favorite protein bars  
- Portion of mixed nuts  
- Fresh, bruise‑resistant fruit (apple or mandarin)  
- 2–3 electrolyte powder packets  

## Apartment Outreach Text Template (6‑8 Questions)
```
Hi, I work night shift and need to confirm a few things before touring.
1. Is the unit quiet during daytime hours? Any regular noise sources?
2. Are blackout curtains allowed, and can the windows accommodate tension rods?
3. How strong is the AC, and does the bedroom get direct sun/heat?
4. Where is the unit in the building (near high‑traffic areas like pools or mailrooms?)
5. Total monthly cost with fees, and earliest move‑in date?
Thanks—happy to tour ASAP if it fits.
```

## Day‑Sleep Tour Checklist (10 Items)
1. Hallway noise level (doors, elevators)  
2. Outside noise with windows closed (traffic, construction)  
3. Bedroom sun exposure during the day  
4. Window frames support blackout curtains or rods  
5. AC output in the bedroom (temperature drop)  
6. How quickly the room cools after AC on  
7. Nearby loud sources (dumpsters, playgrounds, dog areas)  
8. Proximity to elevators or stairs (foot traffic)  
9. Parking‑lot noise audible from bedroom windows  
10. Wall thickness – tap for echo, indicating thin walls
