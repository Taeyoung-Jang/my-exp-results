# Night‑Shift Nutrition Kit

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A compact set of sleep aids and non‑perishable foods designed for night‑shift workers on the go.

---

## Overview
The Night‑Shift Nutrition Kit is a lightweight, portable collection of items that support both sleep quality and nutritional needs during irregular, night‑or‑evening work schedules.  It is intended to minimize reliance on vending‑machine snacks, reduce decision fatigue, and provide the sensory tools (light‑mask, earplugs, travel pillow) needed for effective crash‑sleep in variable environments.

---

## Core Components
| Category | Items (example) | Reason / Benefit |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Sleep Aids** | Contoured eye mask, soft foam earplugs, small travel pillow | Blocks light and noise, creates a consistent sleep micro‑environment regardless of where you crash. |
| **Protein & Energy** | 1‑2 favorite protein bars, mixed nuts (almonds, peanuts, cashews) | High‑calorie, shelf‑stable, quick to eat; sustains energy during long shifts. |
| **Fresh Fruit** | An apple or a mandarin (easy to transport, low‑bruising) | Provides natural sugars, vitamins, and hydration without refrigeration. |
| **Electrolyte Hydration** | 2‑3 single‑serve electrolyte powder packets | Replaces salts lost through caffeine or sweating and reduces dependence on sugary drinks. |

---

## How to Use
1. **Before Shift:**
   - Eat a protein bar and fruit to fuel the start of the night.
   - Take a few sips of water with an electrolyte packet.
2. **During Breaks:**
   - Snack on nuts or a second protein bar to keep blood‑sugar stable.
   - If a brief rest period is available, use the eye mask and earplugs for a power‑nap (20‑30 min). 
3. **Crash‑Sleep Anywhere:**
   - Deploy the mask, earplugs, and travel pillow to create a dark, quiet sleep pocket, whether in a break room, a car, or a temporary lodging.
4. **After Shift:**
   - Re‑hydrate with another electrolyte packet and have a light protein snack before the main anchor sleep.

---

## Rationale
Night‑shift workers often face:
- **Irregular meal times** leading to unhealthy snack choices.
- **Limited kitchen access** making fresh meals impractical.
- **Variable sleeping environments** where light and noise are uncontrolled.

By standardizing a few reliable items, the kit reduces decision‑making load, improves nutrient intake, and supports consistent sleep hygiene.
