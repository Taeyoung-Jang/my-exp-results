# Address Consistency

**Definition**: The practice of maintaining an identical residential or billing address across all personal, financial, governmental, and online service records.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents missed bills, legal notices, and identity‑verification failures.
- Reduces the risk of fraud by eliminating mismatched data that can trigger security alerts.
- Streamlines the update process by allowing a single *source of truth* to propagate changes.

## Core Components
1. **Source of Truth** – Designate one account (typically a primary bank or credit‑card issuer) where the address is first updated. All other accounts should be synchronized to this reference point. [[concepts/source_of_truth]]
2. **Prioritized Checklist** – Verify the address in three tiers:
   - **Must**: primary bank, credit bureaus, [[entities/dmv]], and tax authority (e.g., [[entities/irs]]).
   - **Should**: mobile carrier, utilities, insurance, payroll portals, and major payment hubs like [[entities/amazon]], [[entities/paypal]], [[entities/apple]], [[entities/google]].
   - **Nice**: delivery services ([[entities/ups]], [[entities/fedex]], [[entities/usps]]), streaming platforms, loyalty programs, and smaller subscriptions.
   This structure is outlined in the [[concepts/prioritized_checklist]] concept.
3. **Identity Verification** – High‑risk services (banks, credit bureaus, DMV, tax authority) often require additional proof (security questions, document upload). Keeping the address consistent reduces the frequency of these extra steps.

## Practical Workflow (derived from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]])
1. **Update the source of truth** – Change your address in the primary bank account first.
2. **Run through the Must checklist** – Confirm the address with the three credit bureaus ([[entities/experian]], [[entities/equifax]], [[entities/transunion]]), the DMV, and the IRS.
3. **Proceed to the Should list** – Verify with your mobile carrier, utilities, insurance providers, payroll portals, and major payment hubs.
4. **Finish with Nice items** – Check delivery‑heavy accounts and any other services you use regularly.
5. **Use a password manager** – Store the address for autofill; any mismatched field will stand out during login or checkout.

## Benefits of Maintaining Address Consistency
- **Legal compliance** – Ensures government agencies have the correct location for official correspondence.
- **Financial reliability** – Avoids declined payments or delayed refunds.
- **Time savings** – One update cascades, reducing the need for repetitive manual edits.
- **Security** – Consistent data lowers red‑flags in fraud‑detection systems.
