### Summary of session‑06 (2024‑06‑15)

**Topic**: The user sought help writing concise, warm text messages that set personal boundaries without appearing flaky. The focus was on handling *last‑minute* (same‑day) invitations across various relationship contexts.

**Key contributions**:
- Provided template messages for **close friends**, **acquaintances**, **group chats**, **one‑on‑one catch‑ups**, and **family**.
- Offered variations:
  1. **Alternative‑date offer** – suggests another day the user can attend.
  2. **Clean decline** – brief, no over‑explanation.
- Ensured all messages stay under ~200 characters for easy texting.
- Adjusted tone to be **firm yet kind** for family, and added an “I’m in next time” vibe for group chats.

**Concepts introduced**:
- boundaries
- last‑minute invites
- text etiquette
- assertive communication
- relationship context (friends, acquaintances, family)
- tone management (warm, sincere, firm)
- character limit for texts

These concepts can be expanded into cross‑document concept pages to explore broader strategies for *social boundary setting* and *digital communication style*.
