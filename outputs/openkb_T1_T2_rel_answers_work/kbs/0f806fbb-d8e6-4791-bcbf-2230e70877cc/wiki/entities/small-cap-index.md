# Small‑Cap Index

A **small‑cap index** is a market‑weighted collection of stocks from companies with relatively low market capitalizations. It offers broader exposure to the small‑cap segment than buying individual stocks, providing diversification while retaining the higher volatility typical of this market slice.

## Role in Session 14
In the **risk‑tolerance testing** outlined in [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]], the small‑cap index is suggested as a *moderately volatile* bucket alongside a stable [[entities/broad-index-fund]] and a more conservative [[entities/dividend-growth-fund]] or [[entities/sector-etf]].

- **Purpose**: Acts as the “higher‑volatility” option that remains within a responsible investment framework, allowing users to observe emotional reactions without feeling like they are gambling.
- **Allocation**: Recommended to receive a modest dollar amount (e.g., $100–$500) during the experiment so that emotional responses mirror those of larger positions while keeping financial risk low.
- **Volatility Profile**: Expected to show daily swings of 3–10 % and occasional short‑term drops of 20 % or more, which helps differentiate it from the smoother performance of a broad index fund.

## Connections to Concepts
- **[[concepts/investing-styles]]** – The small‑cap index exemplifies a *growth‑oriented* style that sits between ultra‑conservative and high‑risk speculative approaches.
- **[[concepts/risk-tolerance-testing]]** – It serves as a concrete instrument for measuring how an individual handles increased market turbulence.
- **[[concepts/behavioral-finance-guardrails]]** – Participants are encouraged to set behavioral limits (e.g., price‑check frequency, sell‑only‑on‑fundamentals) while holding the small‑cap index to assess stress levels.

## Practical Takeaways
- Use the small‑cap index as a **test bucket** to gauge personal comfort with volatility.
- Keep the allocation small to focus on **behavioral signals** rather than monetary outcomes.
- Compare emotional responses against those from the [[entities/broad-index-fund]] and other selected assets to refine your preferred **investing style**.
