# Broad Index Fund

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A diversified fund that tracks a wide‑market index (e.g., S&P 500), offering low volatility and steady long‑term growth.

## Key Facts from Session 14 (2024‑06‑15)
- Served as the **baseline bucket** in a small‑scale risk‑tolerance experiment.
- Defined as the *steady* investment choice against which a more volatile bucket (e.g., a sector ETF or small‑cap index) is compared.
- Typical "normal‑sized dip" for this fund is 2 % over a few days and 5–10 % over several weeks, providing a benchmark for emotional responses.
- The experiment recommends allocating a modest amount (e.g., $100–$500) to observe behavior without significant financial risk.
