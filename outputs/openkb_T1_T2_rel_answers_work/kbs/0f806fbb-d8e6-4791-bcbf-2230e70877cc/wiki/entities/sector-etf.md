# Sector ETF

**Type:** Product

A **Sector ETF** is an exchange‑traded fund that concentrates its holdings in a specific industry or market segment (e.g., technology, healthcare, energy). In the context of the *session‑14_2024-06-15* experiment, a sector ETF is recommended as a **moderately volatile** investment bucket for risk‑tolerance testing.

## Role in Risk‑Tolerance Experiment
- Paired with a baseline **[[entities/broad-index-fund]]** to contrast steady versus more active exposure.
- Serves as a *mid‑point* between very low‑risk assets and high‑risk speculative bets, allowing the investor to experience noticeable price swings without feeling like gambling.
- Suggested allocation: a small, experimental amount (e.g., $100–$500) to observe emotional reactions rather than to seek profit.

## Related Concepts
- **[[concepts/investing-styles]]** – The sector ETF aligns with a more active, growth‑oriented style compared to a pure index‑fund approach.
- **[[concepts/risk-tolerance-testing]]** – Used as a test bucket to gauge how an individual handles volatility and market noise.
- **[[concepts/behavioral-finance-guardrails]]** – Investors can set guardrails (e.g., limited price checks, sell‑only‑on‑fundamentals) while holding a sector ETF to monitor stress levels.

## Comparisons
- **[[entities/dividend-growth-fund]]** – Another moderate‑volatility option that may offer more income stability.
- **[[entities/small-cap-index]]** – Can be more volatile than a sector ETF, useful for a higher‑risk test bucket.
