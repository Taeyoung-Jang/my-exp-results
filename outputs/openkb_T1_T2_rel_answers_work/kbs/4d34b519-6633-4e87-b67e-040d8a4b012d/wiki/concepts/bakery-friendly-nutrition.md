# Bakery‑Friendly Nutrition

**Definition**: Nutrition strategies and snack selections that provide steady energy, are easy to prepare the night before, stay intact in a bag, and avoid contaminating flour‑covered hands.

---

## Key Principles
1. **Portability & Durability** – Foods must survive being tossed in a bag without crushing or breaking.
2. **Low‑Mess Consumption** – Items should be edible without requiring hands that need to stay clean (e.g., no crumbly pastries). 
3. **Steady Energy Release** – Emphasize low‑glycemic, protein‑rich choices to prevent mid‑morning crashes.
4. **Minimal Prep** – Ideally prepared the night before; no reheating needed during the early shift.

---

## Recommended Snack Options (Night‑Before Prep)
| Snack | Prep Details | Why It Fits |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| Small yogurt pouches or tubes | Portion into single‑serve containers; seal tightly | No utensils; easy to squeeze; protein‑rich |
| Firm protein/oat bars (nut‑butter coated) | Store in airtight wrappers | Holds shape, low crumb, balanced carbs & fats |
| Individually wrapped cheese sticks | Keep in foil or plastic wrapper | High protein, no crumbs |
| Apple held by the stem | Wash, dry, store whole; stem acts as handle | Natural bite, low mess |
| Squeeze‑pack nut‑butter packets | Portion into single‑serve sachets | Quick boost of healthy fats, no mess |
| Dried mango or apricot strips (non‑sticky) | Pack in zip‑lock bag | Concentrated carbs, stays tidy |
| Mini chia pudding jars (sealed) | Prepare with milk/juice, seal with lid | Hydrating, fiber‑rich, no spill |
| Drinkable kefir or yogurt drinks | Store in sealed bottles | Probiotic, portable protein |

## Integration with Morning Routines
- Pair snack consumption with **micro‑reset** moments (e.g., a deep breath before eating) to reinforce calm focus. [[concepts/micro-reset]]
- Align snack timing with the **pre‑dawn productivity** schedule: consume between 4:20–4:30 to fuel the main bakery prep block. [[concepts/pre-dawn-productivity]]
- Use snack consumption as a cue in a **cue‑based habit** loop, linking the act of eating to the transition from calm prep to active work. [[concepts/cue-based-habit]]
