# iOS (product)

iOS is the mobile operating system developed by Apple Inc. for its iPhone and iPad hardware. In the context of [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]], iOS is mentioned as the platform where users can locate app permission information before installing an app.

## Key facts from the session
- **Permission lookup**: On iOS, permissions are displayed in the App Store listing under the **App Privacy** and **Data Used** sections. Users scroll past screenshots and the description to see what data categories the app collects.
- **Relevance to evaluation**: The quick **[[concepts/evaluation_scorecard]]** suggested for assessing habit‑tracker and notes apps relies on checking these iOS‑specific permission details.
- **Risk assessment**: The session ties the permission check to the broader **[[concepts/risk_flag_system]]**, where unrelated permissions (e.g., location for a habit tracker) trigger yellow or red flags.
- **Related entity**: The evaluation process also references the **[[entities/app_store]]**, where the iOS permission data is presented.

## Connections
- The iOS platform is a primary environment for applying the **[[concepts/app_evaluation]]** framework described in the session.
- Users can follow the **[[concepts/evaluation_scorecard]]** steps directly within the iOS App Store interface.
