# App Store

Apple's **App Store** is the official digital distribution platform for iOS applications. It provides users with a searchable catalog of apps, user reviews, update histories, and privacy information.

## Role in the session‑05_2024‑06‑15 document

The conversation uses the App Store as the primary source for the rapid [[concepts/app_evaluation]] workflow. Specific checks in the [[concepts/evaluation_scorecard]] rely on data displayed in the store listing:
- **Update history** – last‑updated date shown on the app’s page.
- **Recent reviews** – sortable by date to gauge user sentiment and developer responsiveness.
- **Developer website & portfolio** – links provided in the store listing.
- **Privacy policy** – accessed via the “App Privacy” section.
- **Permissions** – listed under “Data Used” for iOS apps.

These signals feed into the [[concepts/risk_flag_system]] (Pass/Yellow/Red) that guides whether to install an app.

## iOS specific context

On iOS, the App Store’s **App Privacy** and **Data Used** sections are the places where a user can quickly verify permissions before installation, as highlighted in the document. This ties the App Store directly to the [[entities/ios]] ecosystem.
