# Mood‑Heuristic Decision Tool

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A brief self‑assessment used to choose a Saturday "vibe" by identifying whether the person seeks stimulation or softness.

---

## Overview
The Mood‑Heuristic Decision Tool is a lightweight mental shortcut introduced in the Saturday planning framework of [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-10]]. It helps users break the inertia of a “blah” morning by asking a single question:

> **Do I want stimulation or softness?**

Depending on the answer, the user is directed toward a corresponding set of vibe‑based templates:

- **Stimulation** → social or outdoorsy vibes
- **Softness** → solo‑quiet, cozy‑at‑home, or gentle‑reset vibes

The heuristic is intentionally simple: it requires no rating scales, no extensive reflection, and can be answered in a few seconds. Its purpose is to provide just enough structure to prevent decision‑paralysis while preserving the spontaneous feel of a weekend day.

---

## How It Works
1. **Morning Check‑In** – When the user wakes up, they pause for a moment and ask the heuristic question.
2. **Interpretation** – 
   - *Stimulation* indicates a desire for active, external engagement. The user then selects a vibe that includes an "anchor" such as meeting a friend for coffee (social) or stepping outside for fresh air (outdoorsy).
   - *Softness* signals a preference for calm, internal activities. The user chooses a vibe like solo‑quiet (reading) or cozy‑at‑home (brew a drink).
3. **Vibe Selection** – The chosen vibe provides a minimal anchor and optional add‑ons, as outlined in the Saturday framework.
4. **Flexibility** – If energy shifts during the day, the user can treat the anchor as a completed chapter and switch to another vibe without feeling like they failed.

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The heuristic ties directly into [[concepts/vibe-based-planning]], which structures Saturday options around “vibes.”
- It relies on the notion of an **anchor activity** (see [[concepts/anchor-activity]]) to give each vibe a low‑effort starting point.

---

## Benefits
- **Speed:** Enables a decision in under 10 seconds.
- **Low Cognitive Load:** No need for exhaustive lists or rating scales.
- **Preserves Spontaneity:** Acts as a gentle nudge rather than a rigid schedule.
- **Adaptable:** Works for any day where a user wants a quick mood‑based direction, not just Saturdays.

---

## Practical Example
A user wakes up feeling “blah.” They ask themselves, “Do I want stimulation or softness?”
- They answer **stimulation** → pick the *outdoorsy* vibe → anchor: five minutes of fresh air → optional add‑on: short walk in a park.
- Mid‑day energy drops → fallback: step onto the balcony for a breath, then transition to a *cozy‑at‑home* vibe if desired.
