# Vibe‑Based Planning

**Vibe‑Based Planning** is a lightweight framework for structuring Saturday (or any leisure day) around a chosen *vibe* rather than a rigid schedule. The approach provides just enough scaffolding to guide activity while preserving spontaneity.

## Core Elements

1. **Vibe Menu** – A short set of themed templates (e.g., social, solo‑quiet, outdoorsy, cozy‑at‑home, gentle‑reset). Each vibe offers:
   - an **anchor** – the one activity you commit to doing, no matter what (see [[concepts/anchor-activity]]). 
   - 2‑3 optional add‑ons that can be pursued if energy permits.
   - a low‑energy fallback for when motivation drops.

2. **Mood‑Heuristic** – A quick self‑question asked each morning: *“Do I want stimulation or softness?”* This helps select the most suitable vibe (see [[concepts/mood-heuristic]]).

3. **Flexible Switching** – The anchor is treated as a *starting point*, not a contract. Once the anchor is completed, the vibe is considered fulfilled, allowing you to shift to another vibe or simply follow the moment without feeling like you failed.

## How It Works (Illustrated in [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-10]])

- **Pick a vibe** using the mood‑heuristic.
- **Do the anchor** (e.g., meet a friend for coffee, read for 15 minutes, step outside for a breath).
- **Add optional activities** if you have the energy.
- **If energy wanes**, fall back to the prescribed low‑effort version.
- **Mid‑day switch** is allowed; the completed anchor counts as the day's contribution, so you can start a new vibe without guilt.

## Benefits
- Reduces decision fatigue by limiting choices to a few clear options.
- Maintains a sense of structure while encouraging spontaneity.
- Provides ultra‑low‑effort anchors for days when even small tasks feel heavy.
- Encourages reflective practice through brief end‑of‑day check‑ins.
