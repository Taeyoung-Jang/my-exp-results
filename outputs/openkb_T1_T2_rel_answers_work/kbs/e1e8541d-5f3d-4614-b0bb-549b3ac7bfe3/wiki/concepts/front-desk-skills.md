# Front‑Desk Skills

**Definition** – The set of interpersonal, operational, and emotional capabilities that allow front‑desk personnel to deliver smooth, guest‑focused service while managing the fast‑paced, high‑stress environment of a hotel.

---

## Core Components

| Skill | Why it matters | How to develop |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Calm under pressure** | Maintains service quality during rushes and unexpected issues. | Practice deliberate breathing, short pauses before actions, and rehearse peak‑time scenarios. |
| **Clear, kind communication** | Reduces guest frustration and prevents misunderstandings. | Use scripts for phone holds and in‑person acknowledgments; model the tone used in [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-09]]. |
| **Anticipation** | Allows proactive problem‑solving (e.g., preparing rooms before arrival). | Observe patterns of guest flow; note recurring issues during shadowing. |
| **Reading guests** | Detects hidden needs and emotions, improving satisfaction. | Develop empathy by listening for tone and body language; tie into [[concepts/emotional-resilience-hospitality]]. |
| **Humor** | Defuses tension and bolsters team morale. | Share light‑hearted comments when appropriate; keep it professional. |
| **Technical fluency** (e.g., **property‑management system**) | Faster transaction processing, fewer errors. | Allocate daily practice time on the [[entities/property-management-system]] after shifts. |

## Practical Tips from Session 33

1. **Prioritize in‑person guests** before answering the phone; use a brief hold phrase: *“Front desk, thanks for calling—may I place you on a brief hold?”*.
2. **Acknowledge wait times** with short statements like *“Thank you for your patience, I’m just finishing this up for you.”*.
3. **Adopt a two‑second pause** before finalizing refunds, room moves, or incidentals to double‑check details.
4. **Confirm identity** for every request, even from familiar‑looking guests, to protect privacy.
5. **Practice the PMS** for at least ten minutes after each shift to build speed and confidence.
