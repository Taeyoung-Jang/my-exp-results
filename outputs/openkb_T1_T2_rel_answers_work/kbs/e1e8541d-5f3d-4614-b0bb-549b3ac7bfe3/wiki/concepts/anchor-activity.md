# Anchor Activity

**Definition** – An *anchor activity* is a tiny, low‑commitment action that serves as the starting point for a chosen Saturday "vibe". It is deliberately simple enough to be done even on low‑energy days, providing a sense of accomplishment and allowing the rest of the day to unfold organically.

## Role in Vibe‑Based Planning

Anchor activities are the core component of the [[concepts/vibe-based-planning]] framework described in [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-10]]. Each vibe (e.g., social, solo‑quiet, outdoorsy, cozy‑at‑home, gentle‑reset) specifies one anchor that must be completed; everything else is optional. By treating the anchor as a *starting point* rather than a contract, users can shift to another vibe later without feeling they have failed.

## Characteristics
- **Minimal duration** – typically 5 minutes to 15 minutes.
- **Low friction** – requires little preparation or resources.
- **Flexible context** – can be performed at home, outdoors, or in a public setting.
- **Scalable** – can be reduced further into a *super‑low‑effort* version (e.g., opening a window for a breath) when energy is very low.

## Examples from the Source Document
| Vibe | Anchor Activity | Super‑low‑effort fallback |
|------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Social | Meet one person for coffee or brunch | Send a quick text and have a brief phone chat |
| Solo‑quiet | 15 minutes of reading or a hobby | Read in bed for a few minutes |
| Outdoorsy | 5 minutes of fresh air outside | Step onto a balcony and take a single breath |
| Cozy‑at‑home | Brew a comforting drink or get into comfy clothes | Curl up with a blanket and do nothing |
| Gentle‑reset | Tidy one tiny area (a drawer, a corner) | Just the tiny tidy, then stop |

## Connection to Mood Decisions
When waking up feeling "blah," the suggested quick decision tool – the [[concepts/mood-heuristic]] of asking *"Stimulation or softness?"* – helps choose a vibe. The chosen vibe then determines which anchor activity to perform.

## Benefits
- **Reduces decision fatigue** – a clear, tiny first step eliminates the need for a full schedule.
- **Preserves spontaneity** – after the anchor, the day remains open to follow curiosity.
- **Mitigates pressure** – because the anchor is intentionally easy, missing optional add‑ons does not count as failure.
