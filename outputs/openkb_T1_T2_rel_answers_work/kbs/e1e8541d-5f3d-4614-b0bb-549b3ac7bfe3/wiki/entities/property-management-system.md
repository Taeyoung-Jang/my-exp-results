# Property Management System (PMS)

A **Property Management System** (often abbreviated *PMS*) is the core software product that hotel front‑desk teams use to handle reservations, check‑ins, check‑outs, billing, room assignments, and communication with back‑of‑house services.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-09]]
- **Early mastery is crucial** – New front‑desk staff are advised to spend extra minutes after each shift practicing the PMS. This reduces stress during peak periods when the system can become a bottleneck.
- **Supports essential tasks** – The PMS is the tool behind handling refunds, rate adjustments, room‑move updates, and incident‑card authorizations mentioned in the session.
- **Links front‑desk workflow** – Efficient use of the PMS feeds directly into the [[concepts/front-desk-skills]] of staying calm under pressure and anticipating guest needs.
- **Onboarding relevance** – Mastery of the PMS is highlighted as a core component of the [[concepts/onboarding-strategies-front-desk]] for new hires.

## Relationship to other entities and concepts
- Used daily by [[entities/front-desk]] personnel.
- Enhances the ability to apply [[concepts/front-desk-skills]] such as clear communication and swift problem‑solving.
- Contributes to a smoother onboarding experience, reinforcing the [[concepts/onboarding-strategies-front-desk]] approach.

## Typical functions
1. **Reservation management** – Create, modify, and cancel bookings.
2. **Guest profile handling** – Store preferences, payment methods, and stay history.
3. **Billing & incidentals** – Track charges, process payments, and issue refunds.
4. **Room allocation** – Assign rooms, manage moves, and update housekeeping status.
5. **Reporting** – Generate occupancy, revenue, and performance reports for management.

## Practical tip for new staff
> *“Spend ten extra minutes after a shift practicing common PMS actions; the time saved during a rush is worth it.”*
