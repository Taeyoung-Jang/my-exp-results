# Summary of session-33_2024-11-09

**Main focus** – Practical insight for new front‑desk staff in hospitality, covering day‑to‑day surprises, essential skills, rapid onboarding tactics, real‑time prioritization, and habits to avoid common mistakes.

## Key Concepts
- **Pacing in hotel front‑desk** – Even slower properties experience sudden rushes; expectation management is crucial.  
- **Emotional resilience** – Guests bring travel stress; staff must stay calm and supportive. [[concepts/emotional-resilience-hospitality]]
- **Front‑desk core skills** – Calm under pressure, clear kindness, anticipation, reading guests, and humor. [[concepts/front-desk-skills]]

## Onboarding Strategies (First Weeks)
1. **Shadow experienced staff** – Observe check‑ins, problem handling, and peak‑time rhythm.  
2. **Master the property‑management system (PMS)** – Short daily practice sessions prevent later bottlenecks. [[entities/property-management-system]]
3. **Map back‑of‑house contacts** – Know the go‑to housekeeper, maintenance, and other support roles.  
4. **Ask micro‑questions early** – Clears confusion before it compounds.  

*These actions accelerate confidence and reduce overwhelm.* [[concepts/onboarding-strategies-front-desk]]

## Multitasking & Prioritization
- **Face‑to‑face guests first** – Physical presence feels most ignored; handle them before answering the phone.  
- **Phone hold script** – “Front desk, thanks for calling—may I place you on a brief hold?”  
- **In‑desk acknowledgment** – Simple statements like “Thank you for your patience, I’m just finishing this up for you.”  
- **Buy‑time phrases** – “I’m waiting on the system to catch up – just a moment.”  

Effective micro‑communications keep guests feeling seen while you resolve issues.

## Common Front‑Desk Mistakes & Preventive Habits
| Mistake | Typical Trigger | Habit to Prevent |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| Refund / rate errors | Clicking too fast | **Pause & double‑check** guest name, dates, folio before confirming. |
| Room move oversights | Forgetting to update keys, housekeeping status | **Mentally walk through** the guest’s stay after each move. |
| Incidentals / card issues | Assuming card on file | **Verify authorization** each transaction. |
| Privacy breaches | Sharing info without identity proof | **Confirm identity** and use neutral wording (e.g., “I can pass a message”). |

Adopting a two‑second pause, verbal confirmation of key details, and a “protect‑info‑unless‑certain” mindset dramatically cuts error rates.

## Quick Takeaways
- Expect rapid pace and emotional load; prepare with resilience and humor.  
- Early weeks: shadow, practice the PMS, learn support contacts, ask questions.  
- Prioritize in‑person guests, use brief hold scripts, and give reassurance cues.  
- Build habits of pausing, verbal confirmation, and strict identity verification to avoid costly mistakes.
