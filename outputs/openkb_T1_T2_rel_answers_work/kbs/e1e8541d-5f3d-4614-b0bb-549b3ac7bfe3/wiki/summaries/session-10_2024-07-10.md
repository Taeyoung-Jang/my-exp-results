## Summary of session-10_2024-07-10

**Date:** 2024‑07‑10  

### Core Idea
A lightweight framework for Saturday planning that keeps spontaneity alive while offering gentle structure through **[[concepts/vibe-based-planning]]** menus and ultra‑low‑effort anchors.

### Key Concepts & wikilinks
- **Vibe‑based menu** – A set of 3‑5 Saturday “templates” (social, solo‑quiet, outdoorsy, cozy‑at‑home, gentle‑reset) each with:
  - a single **[[concepts/anchor-activity]]** (non‑negotiable starter activity)
  - 2‑3 optional add‑ons
  - a low‑energy fallback
- **Anchor** – The minimal action that counts as doing the vibe (e.g., meet a friend for coffee, 15‑minute reading, five minutes outside).
- **Low‑energy fallback** – A lighter version of the anchor for when energy drops (e.g., phone chat, bedside reading, a breath on the balcony).
- **Mood‑question heuristic** – Quick self‑check “Stimulation or softness?” to choose a vibe when waking up feeling “blah” [[concepts/mood-heuristic]].
- **Switching without failure** – Treat the anchor as a *starting point*, not a contract; completing it means the vibe is fulfilled, allowing free transition to another vibe.
- **Super‑low‑effort anchors** – Micro‑actions for heavy‑energy days: opening a window for a breath, drinking a glass of water mindfully.

### Practical Templates (pick‑one vibe)
1. **Social** – Anchor: meet one person for coffee/brunch. Add‑ons: wander a market, watch a movie, take a walk. Fallback: rain‑check text + phone chat.  
2. **Solo‑quiet** – Anchor: 15 min of reading/hobby. Add‑ons: explore a café, stroll a bookstore, journal. Fallback: read in bed for a few minutes.  
3. **Outdoorsy** – Anchor: five minutes of fresh air. Add‑ons: longer walk, park visit, photo session. Fallback: step onto balcony for a breath.  
4. **Cozy‑at‑home** – Anchor: comforting home activity (brew a drink, get comfy). Add‑ons: simple cooking, watch a show, light puttering. Fallback: curl up with a blanket.  
5. **Gentle‑reset** – Anchor: tidy one tiny area. Add‑ons: stretch, play music, prep a simple week‑ahead snack. Fallback: just the tiny tidy.

### How to Choose a Vibe
- Ask: **“Do I want stimulation or softness?”**  
  - *Stimulation* → social or outdoorsy  
  - *Softness* → cozy‑at‑home or solo‑quiet  
- If undecided, pick the vibe with the **smallest anchor** that feels doable.

### Handling Mid‑Day Switches
1. Recognize the anchor is complete → “chapter finished”.  
2. Re‑assess mood and select a new vibe or simply follow whatever feels right.  
3. Remind yourself that the goal is mood support, not task completion.

### Takeaway
The framework encourages intentional yet low‑pressure weekend days by giving clear, minimal starting points and flexible pathways, helping users stay spontaneous without the hidden checklist stress.
