# Evergreen Clematis (Clematis armandii)

**Type**: Evergreen vine, horticultural product.

## Overview
Evergreen Clematis (*Clematis armandii*) is a compact, evergreen climbing vine native to southern China. It is prized for its year‑round foliage, fragrant white blooms in spring, and its ability to thrive in USDA zone 7 with minimal care.

## Key Characteristics
- **Growth habit**: Climbs 8–10 ft tall, spreads 3–4 ft horizontally.
- **Foliage**: Glossy, dark green leaves that remain evergreen in zone 7, providing consistent privacy.
- **Flowers**: Small, fragrant white flowers appear in early spring, adding seasonal interest.
- **Root system**: Non‑aggressive, does not damage fence foundations or surrounding plants.
- **Maintenance**: Low‑maintenance; requires only occasional pruning after flowering to keep shape.
- **Cold tolerance**: More cold‑hardy than star jasmine, tolerating typical zone 7 winter lows.

## Recommended Use
In the design outlined in [[summaries/session-34_2025-03-15]], Evergreen Clematis is suggested as the primary **evergreen climber** for modern fence‑mounted panels. It pairs well with slim cedar or metal trellis frames, fulfilling both aesthetic and functional goals of [[concepts/privacy_landscaping]] and [[concepts/modern_trellis_design]].

## Planting & Support Guidelines
- **Support**: Install eyelets or a thin trellis on the fence; the vine will readily attach and climb.
- **Spacing**: Plant about 1 ft from the fence to allow airflow and easy access for pruning, aligning with [[concepts/spacing_guidelines]].
- **Soil**: Well‑drained, slightly acidic to neutral soil; mulch to retain moisture.
- **Water**: Regular watering during establishment; becomes drought‑tolerant once established.
