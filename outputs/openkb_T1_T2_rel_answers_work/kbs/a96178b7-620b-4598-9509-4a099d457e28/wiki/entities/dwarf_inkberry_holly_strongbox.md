# Dwarf Inkberry Holly ‘Strongbox’

**Type**: Evergreen shrub (product)

## Overview
'Dwarf Inkberry Holly ‘Strongbox’' is a compact, non‑invasive cultivar of *Ilex vomitoria* (inkberry holly). It is prized for its tidy, rounded habit and minimal pruning needs, making it a go‑to choice for low‑maintenance planting schemes in USDA zone 7.

## Key Characteristics
- **Size**: 2–3 ft tall × 2–3 ft wide at maturity.
- **Foliage**: Dark green, glossy leaves that retain color year‑round.
- **Growth habit**: Naturally rounded; rarely requires shaping.
- **Root system**: Shallow and non‑spreading, so it won’t damage fences or hardscapes.
- **Hardiness**: Suitable for full‑sun locations in zone 7.

## Landscape Role
- Ideal for the **[[concepts/privacy_landscaping]]** gaps in a modern fence line, providing visual screening without creating a solid wall.
- Aligns with **[[concepts/low_maintenance_plant_selection]]** goals: little pruning, drought‑moderate tolerance, and consistent form.
- Fits the **[[concepts/spacing_guidelines]]** recommendation of planting about 3 ft from neighboring plants and ~1 ft from the fence to allow airflow and easy access.
- Not listed among problematic species in **[[concepts/invasive_species_awareness]]**; it is a well‑behaved, clump‑forming shrub.

## Planting & Care
1. **Site preparation**: Choose a sunny spot with well‑draining soil.
2. **Spacing**: Place the shrub roughly 3 ft from adjacent shrubs (e.g., [[entities/compact_abelia_kaleidoscope]]) and 1 ft from the fence to avoid crowding.
3. **Watering**: Water deeply once a week during the first growing season; thereafter, it tolerates moderate drought.
4. **Pruning**: Minimal; remove only dead or wayward stems after flowering if desired.
