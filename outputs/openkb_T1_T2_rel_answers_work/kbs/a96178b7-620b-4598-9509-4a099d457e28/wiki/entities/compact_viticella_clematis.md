# Compact Viticella‑type Clematis

**Type**: product

**Overview**
A compact viticella‑type clematis, prized for its modest size, abundant seasonal blooms, and minimal upkeep. It is recommended for USDA zone 7 full‑sun locations and works well on modern trellis panels to provide decorative privacy without overwhelming the space.

**Key Characteristics**
- **Growth habit**: Climbing vine, typically reaching 6–8 ft in height with a spread of 3–4 ft.
- **Bloom period**: Long flowering season, producing colorful, fragrant flowers that attract pollinators.
- **Maintenance**: Naturally tidy growth; requires only occasional pruning after flowering to shape and remove dead wood.
- **Root behavior**: Non‑invasive, shallow‑rooted, making it safe for use near fences and structures.

**Design Context**
In the layout described in [[summaries/session-34_2025-03-15]], this clematis is paired with modern, slim panels (see [[concepts/modern_trellis_design]]) to create a clean, structured privacy screen. Its compact form fits the recommended **panel height** of ~7 ft, delivering visual screening while preserving an airy feel.

**Related Plant Selections**
- **Evergreen alternative**: [[entities/evergreen_clematis_armandii]] – a more evergreen option for colder snaps.
- **Companion shrubs**: [[entities/dwarf_inkberry_holly_strongbox]], [[entities/compact_abelia_kaleidoscope]].
- **Grass filler**: [[entities/feather_reed_grass_avalanche]].

**Best Practices**
- **Spacing**: Install the vine about 1 ft from the fence and provide support via eye‑lets or a slim trellis, following [[concepts/spacing_guidelines]].
- **Low‑maintenance focus**: Aligns with [[concepts/low_maintenance_plant_selection]] for gardens where pruning is infrequent.
- **Invasiveness**: Unlike bamboo, wisteria, or honeysuckle, this clematis does not become invasive, adhering to [[concepts/invasive_species_awareness]].

**Use Cases**
- Enhancing **privacy landscaping** while maintaining a modern aesthetic.
- Adding seasonal color to a structured garden without high maintenance demands.
