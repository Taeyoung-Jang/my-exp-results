## Compact Abelia ‘Kaleidoscope’

**Type:** Product (shrub)

Compact Abelia ‘Kaleidoscope’ is a low‑maintenance, evergreen‑like deciduous shrub prized for its profuse, fragrant pink‑lavender blooms that appear from late summer through fall. In USDA zone 7 full‑sun settings it typically reaches **2–3 ft tall and wide**, forming a rounded habit that rarely requires pruning.

### Key attributes from [[summaries/session-34_2025-03-15]]
- **Size & spacing**: Recommended planting **~3 ft** from neighboring plants and **~1 ft** from the fence to satisfy [[concepts/spacing_guidelines]].
- **Role in design**: Used as the **deciduous flowering shrub** in the “privacy landscaping” scheme, providing seasonal colour while keeping the overall layout airy.
- **Maintenance**: Naturally compact; minimal shaping needed, aligning with [[concepts/low_maintenance_plant_selection]].
- **Compatibility**: Paired with evergreen options such as [[entities/dwarf_inkberry_holly_strongbox]] and climbers like [[entities/evergreen_clematis_armandii]] or [[entities/compact_viticella_clematis]].

### Why it fits the proposed layout
- **Privacy without bulk**: Its moderate height blocks sightlines when placed between the modern panels, contributing to [[concepts/privacy_landscaping]] while leaving gaps for light.
- **Non‑invasive**: Unlike plants flagged in [[concepts/invasive_species_awareness]] (e.g., running bamboo, wisteria, honeysuckle), ‘Kaleidoscope’ stays well‑behaved and does not send aggressive root runners into the fence.
- **Aesthetic harmony**: Works well with ornamental grasses such as [[entities/feather_reed_grass_avalanche]] to create a layered, modern look.

### Planting tips
1. **Soil**: Well‑drained, moderately fertile soil; amend with compost if heavy clay.
2. **Sun**: Full sun (6–8 hrs) as specified in the design brief.
3. **Water**: Establish with regular watering for the first season; thereafter drought‑tolerant.
4. **Mulch**: Apply a 2‑in mulch ring to conserve moisture and suppress weeds.
