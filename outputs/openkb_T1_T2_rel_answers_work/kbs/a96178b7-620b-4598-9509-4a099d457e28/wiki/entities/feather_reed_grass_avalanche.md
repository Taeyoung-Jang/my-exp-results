# Feather Reed Grass ‘Avalanche‘

**Type**: Product (ornamental grass)

**Overview**
Feather Reed Grass ‘Avalanche’ is a dwarf, clump‑forming *Pennisetum* cultivar prized for its narrow, upright habit and airy plumes that reach 4–5 ft tall. It thrives in full sun, tolerates USDA zone 7 conditions, and requires minimal watering once established.

**Key Characteristics**
- **Mature size**: ~4–5 ft tall, ~2 ft spread
- **Growth habit**: Compact, non‑spreading clump; excellent for gaps between structural elements
- **Maintenance**: Very low – dead‑heading after bloom and occasional division every few years
- **Root behaviour**: Non‑aggressive, does not damage fences or adjacent plantings

**Design Role in Session‑34 Plan**
In the 40‑ft back‑yard layout (see [[summaries/session-34_2025-03-15]]), ‘Avalanche’ is suggested as the grass option for the in‑between sections. The recommendation is to plant three stems about 18 in apart to form a soft mound that fills visual gaps without becoming a pruning chore.

**Planting Tips**
- Place the clump about 1 ft from the fence to allow airflow and easy access.
- Ensure well‑drained soil; amend with compost if needed.
- Mulch lightly to retain moisture, especially during the first growing season.
