# Summary of session-08_2024-07-14

### Core Problem
The user is distracted by a low‑level highway hum that seeps into a home office through the window. They need low‑cost, non‑studio solutions for detection and mitigation, plus advice on effective masking sounds.

### Recommended Mitigation Strategies
- **Window treatment**: Use heavy, dense curtains (preferably with a thick blackout lining) that extend several inches beyond the frame and are set a short air gap away from the glass. A blanket‑over‑the‑window test can quickly confirm the window’s contribution. [[concepts/window_acoustic_mitigation]]
- **Sound masking**: For low, steady traffic noise, pink or brown noise blends best; white noise can feel harsh. A small fan works equally well. Place the source near the window but away from the microphone—e.g., a corner opposite the desk. [[concepts/sound_masking_strategies]]
- **Plant and furniture placement**: Large leafy plants near the window and a bookshelf opposite can add modest absorption and reduce internal reflections.

### Simple One‑Afternoon Diagnosis Plan
1. **Blanket test** – Cover the window with a heavy blanket; note any drop in hum.  
2. **Ear‑sweep** – Trace the window frame, sill, and corners with your ear; mark louder spots with painter’s tape.  
3. **Vent check** – Listen at HVAC vents; temporarily block with a towel to see impact.  
4. **Door gap test** – Roll a towel against the door threshold; observe changes.  
5. **Interpretation** –  
   - If the blanket helps → upgrade curtains or seal gaps.  
   - If a spot lights up in the ear‑sweep → apply weatherstripping/caulk (airflow leak detection).  
   - If vent or door gaps are the source → seal with sweeps or tape.  
   - If none affect the hum → the noise is structural transmission; rely on masking. [[concepts/indoor_noise_diagnosis]]

### Follow‑Up Actions
- Purchase heavy curtains with blackout lining and install with a slight offset from the wall.  
- Apply weatherstripping or caulk to any identified frame gaps.  
- Add a pink/brown noise generator or a quiet fan in a corner near the window, angled away from the desk microphone.  
- Optionally place a large plant and a bookshelf to improve room ambience.
