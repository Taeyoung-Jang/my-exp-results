# Summary of session‑34 (2025‑03‑15)

**Goal**: Create a private yet airy back‑yard fence line (~40 ft) in USDA zone 7 full sun, using low‑maintenance plants and a modern structural element.

## Layout Overview
- **Three modern panels** (≈4 ft wide each) spaced 6–7 ft apart, mounted on cedar or slim metal frames.  
- **Panel height**: ~7 ft to block sightlines without feeling like a wall.  
- **Planting offset**: ~1 ft from the fence to allow airflow and easy access for maintenance.  
- **In‑between sections**: Either a single shrub or a small clump of grasses per gap, providing intentional rhythm.  
- **Climber support**: Simple eye‑let hooks or a slim trellis attached to the fence; no elaborate structure needed.

## Plant Selections (Zone 7, full sun)
| Position | Plant (Cultivar) | Type | Mature Size | Maintenance notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evergreen shrub (gap) | **Dwarf Inkberry Holly** ‘Strongbox’ | Evergreen shrub | 2–3 ft tall × 2–3 ft wide | Rounded habit, non‑spreading roots, minimal pruning |
| Deciduous flowering shrub (gap) | **Compact Abelia** ‘Kaleidoscope’ | Deciduous shrub | 2–3 ft tall × 2–3 ft wide | Long bloom period, stays tidy |
| Grass clump (gap) | **Feather Reed Grass** ‘Avalanche’ (or ‘Karl Foerster’) | Ornamental grass | 4–5 ft tall × 2 ft spread | Plant 3 stems ~18 in apart for a soft mound |
| Evergreen climber (panel) | **Evergreen Clematis** *Clematis armandii* | Evergreen vine | 8–10 ft tall, spreads 3–4 ft | Handles colder snaps better than star jasmine |
| Flowering climber (panel) | **Compact Viticella‑type Clematis** (e.g., ‘Jackman’s Early Scarlet’) | Deciduous vine | 6–8 ft tall, modest spread | Seasonal blooms, easy pruning |

## Plants to Avoid (invasive or high‑maintenance)
- Running bamboo – [[entities/running_bamboo]]  
- Wisteria – [[entities/wisteria]]  
- Vigorous honeysuckle – [[entities/honeysuckle]]

These species tend to **invade** surrounding areas, **push** against fences, and demand frequent pruning.

## Key Concepts & Practices
- [[concepts/privacy_landscaping]]: Using structured panels combined with selective planting to achieve visual screening while preserving openness.  
- [[concepts/modern_trellis_design]]: Slim, minimalist supports that guide climbers without overwhelming the space.  
- [[concepts/low_maintenance_plant_selection]]: Choosing species with natural compact habits and non‑aggressive root systems.  
- [[concepts/invasive_species_awareness]]: Recognizing and excluding plants that become problematic over time.  
- [[concepts/spacing_guidelines]]: Maintaining ~1 ft from fence and ~3 ft between shrubs (or 18 in between grasses) to reduce crowding and simplify care.  
- Panel height considerations: selecting ~7 ft height to balance privacy and openness.  
- Zone 7 planting guidelines: matching plant hardiness to climate for reliable winter performance (plain text, not a linked concept).

**Result**: A repeatable, low‑effort design that delivers privacy, modern aesthetic, and seasonal interest without the need for intensive trimming or invasive plant management.
