# Summary of Session 31 – Glitter‑Free Fundraiser Assembly Line  

**Date:** 2025‑06‑15  

This document provides a practical, step‑by‑step system for running a school fundraiser envelope‑stuffing line that minimizes mess, especially glitter fallout. It includes a **text diagram**, **volunteer count tables** for 8, 10, and 12 participants, a **lead‑person script**, and a **10‑minute cleanup plan**.

## Key Concepts  
- [[concepts/assembly_line_workflow]] – Linear, one‑direction flow from a “messy quarantine” corner to a clean exit.  
- **Messy quarantine area** – Designated spot for any potentially glittery tasks (unpacking, sealing decorative pieces).  
- **Clean zone** – All downstream stations (folding, stuffing, sealing, labeling/boxing) handle only pre‑cleaned items.  
- [[concepts/floater_role]] – A flexible volunteer who restocks supplies and clears scraps, preventing bottlenecks.  
- **Volunteer allocation** – Specific headcounts per station for 8, 10, and 12 volunteers.  
- **Lead script** – Concise briefing covering rules, mistake handling, and flow enforcement.  
- [[concepts/cleanup_plan]] – Structured 10‑minute shutdown routine that traps glitter in the quarantine area and leaves the workspace clean.  

## Layout Overview (text diagram)  
```
Entrance → Table A (Messy Quarantine) → Table B (Folding) → Table C (Stuffing) → Table D (Sealing) → Table E (Labeling & Boxing) → Out
```  
- **Table A**: Isolated corner; 1‑2 volunteers depending on size.  
- **Table B‑E**: Straight line or gentle L‑shape to maintain forward motion.

## Lead Script (one‑page)  
> “Thanks for being here. We’re keeping this a glitter‑free zone, so use only the supplies provided and no personal tools. Everything moves in one direction from Table A through Table E. Stay at your station unless asked to move. If you make a mistake, place the item in the red **Fix Me** tray—don’t try to fix it yourself. All glitter‑potential work is done at Table A; everything you receive is already sealed and clean. Need more supplies or see a pile? Wave the floater over. Your only job is the step in front of you.”  

## 10‑Minute Cleanup Plan  

1. **Announce shutdown** – Freeze all in‑progress work.  
2. **Floater collects** loose pieces and deposits them in a sealed tray.  
3. **Volunteers wipe** their tables with damp cloths (e.g., [[entities/baby-wipes]]) and shake items away from the floor.  
4. **Lead bundles** trash, ties bags, and removes bins.  
5. **Messy‑Quarantine crew** folds the table cover inward like a taco, trapping any stray [[entities/glitter]].  
6. **Move finished boxes** to the clean exit area.  
7. **Final sweep** – quick visual check for specks before sweeping/vacuuming.  

## Supplies Checklist (for the lead person)  

- Large shallow trays for each station  
- Small trash bins (one per 2‑3 stations)  
- Painter’s tape to mark flow lanes  
- [[entities/baby-wipes]] or damp cloths  
- Red “Fix Me” tray  
- Floater’s supply cart (extra envelopes, labels, gloves)  
- Sealed container for glitter‑prone decorative pieces  

This systematic approach ensures volunteers stay clean, the line stays efficient, and glitter stays confined to a single controlled area.
