# Summary of session-01_2023-06-15

- Provides practical, low‑pressure [[concepts/conversation-starters]] for a community support group attendee who comes alone after a spouse’s passing.  
- Supplies ultra‑short one‑liners for initial greetings (e.g., “Mind if I sit here?”) and polite ways to exit a conversation (e.g., “I’m going to take a quick breather, I’ll be back in a minute”).  
- Offers concise responses to direct questions such as “Are you here with anyone?” and “What brought you here?” that balance honesty with brevity.  
- Gives brief, matter‑of‑fact phrasing for mentioning the loss of a spouse and includes a technique to gently shift the subject afterward.  
- Recommends simple [[concepts/emotional-coping]] strategies: pause, acknowledge, step aside, and brief breathing to manage unexpected emotions during conversation.
