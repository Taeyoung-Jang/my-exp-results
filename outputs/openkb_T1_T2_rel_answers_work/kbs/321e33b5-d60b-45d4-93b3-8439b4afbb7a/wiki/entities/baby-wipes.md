## Baby Wipes

Baby wipes are single‑use, moistened disposable wipes commonly used for quick surface cleaning. In the context of a **school‑fundraiser** envelope‑stuffing assembly line, they serve as a fast‑acting tool for removing stray glitter and other small debris.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-15]]**:
- Included in the **lead’s 10‑minute cleanup plan** as one of the essential supplies for each station. [[concepts/cleanup_plan]]
- Distributed to volunteers at each workstation so they can wipe down surfaces immediately after a spill, helping to keep the line glitter‑free. [[entities/glitter]]
- Used by the designated **floater** to maintain a clean flow and restock supplies, supporting the overall **assembly line workflow**. [[concepts/floater_role]] [[concepts/assembly_line_workflow]]

**Typical usage in the fundraiser setting**:
1. After a glitter‑prone task, volunteers use a baby wipe to clean the tabletop before moving to the next step.
2. During the shutdown phase, the floater collects baby wipes to quickly absorb any remaining dust, preventing glitter from spreading to the exit area.
3. Baby wipes are disposed of in the small trash bins placed at each station, minimizing cross‑contamination.

These wipes are valued for their convenience, disposability, and effectiveness in maintaining a tidy, efficient work environment during high‑volume volunteer activities.
