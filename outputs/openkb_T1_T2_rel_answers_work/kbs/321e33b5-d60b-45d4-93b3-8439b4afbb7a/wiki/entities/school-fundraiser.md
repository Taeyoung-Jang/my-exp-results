# School Fundraiser (event)

**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-15]]

The *School Fundraiser* described in session‑31 is a community‑driven event where volunteers assemble and ship fundraising envelopes. The core innovation is a structured [[concepts/assembly_line_workflow]] designed to keep participants clean and the workspace glitter‑free.

## Key Features
- **Messy quarantine area:** A dedicated corner (Table A) handles any potentially glittery tasks (unpacking, sealing decorative pieces) before items enter the clean zone.
- **Linear flow:** Stations progress from folding → stuffing → sealing → labeling/boxing, preventing back‑tracking and cross‑traffic.
- **Volunteer allocation:** Adjustable headcounts for 8, 10, or 12 volunteers, with a designated [[concepts/floater_role]] to restock supplies and clear scraps.
- **Supplies:** Includes large shallow trays, small trash bins, painter’s‑tape lane markers, and [[entities/baby-wipes]] for quick clean‑ups.
- **Glitter control:** All glitter‑prone materials are sealed at the quarantine stage; [[entities/glitter]] never leaves that area.
- **Lead script:** Provides volunteers with clear rules (no personal craft tools, stay at your station, use the “Fix Me” tray for mistakes).
- **Cleanup:** A concise 10‑minute [[concepts/cleanup_plan]] ensures any stray glitter is contained and the room is left tidy.

## Impact
By treating the fundraiser setup as a staged workflow, the event minimizes downtime, reduces the risk of volunteers leaving covered in craft fallout, and creates a repeatable template for future school‑based fundraising activities.
