# Floater Role in Volunteer Operations

The **floater role** is a dedicated volunteer who moves between stations on a fundraiser assembly line to keep the workflow smooth. Rather than performing a fixed task, the floater monitors supply levels, clears scraps, and steps in where a station is lagging, preventing the line from grinding to a halt.

## Core Responsibilities
- **Supply Management:** Carry a cart of extra envelopes, labels, gloves, and other needed items; replenish stations before they run empty.
- **Scrap & Mess Control:** Collect stray pieces (e.g., glitter, torn paper) and place them in designated trash bins, helping maintain a clean zone.
- **Bottleneck Relief:** Observe the flow; if a station slows down, temporarily assist or redirect traffic to keep the line moving forward.
- **Communication Bridge:** Act as the point‑of‑contact for the lead person, relaying any issues that arise during the shift.

## Placement in the Workflow
The floater operates alongside the linear [[concepts/assembly_line_workflow]] described in the fundraiser plan. While most volunteers stay at a fixed station (folding, stuffing, sealing, labeling), the floater circulates primarily between the **stuffing** and **sealing** areas, where bottlenecks are most common.

## Benefits
- **Reduced Downtime:** Immediate replenishment eliminates waiting periods.
- **Mess Containment:** By quickly gathering scraps, the floater supports the overall [[concepts/cleanup_plan]] and keeps glitter confined to the quarantine zone.
- **Flexibility:** The role adapts to varying volunteer counts (8, 10, or 12), scaling the number of floaters as needed.

## Example from Session 31
In the "glitter‑free envelope‑stuffing assembly line" described in [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-15]], a single floater was assigned to drift between the stuffing and sealing tables. This person ensured that trays stayed stocked, trash bins were emptied, and any stray glitter was promptly removed, allowing the main volunteers to focus solely on their specific tasks.
