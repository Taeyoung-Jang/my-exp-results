# Structured Cleanup Plan

**Definition**: A timed, ordered procedure for ending a volunteer assembly‑line activity that isolates and contains residual mess (e.g., glitter) and restores the workspace to a safe, clean state.

## Core Elements
1. **Announcement & Freeze** – The lead announces the end of the session, and all volunteers stop work instantly to avoid moving partially‑completed items.
2. **Floater Collection** – The designated [[concepts/floater_role]] gathers loose pieces (glitter, scraps) into a sealed tray.
3. **Surface Wipe** – Each volunteer wipes their station with a damp cloth (e.g., [[entities/baby-wipes]]) and shakes out any debris without scattering it onto the floor.
4. **Trash Consolidation** – The lead bundles trash bags, ties them, and removes bins from the area.
5. **Messy‑Quarantine Closure** – Volunteers at the "messy quarantine" corner fold the table covering inward like a taco, trapping any stray glitter inside.
6. **Final Transfer** – Finished boxes are moved to a clean exit zone, away from the line.
7. **Visual Sweep** – A quick walk‑through checks for visible specks before any sweeping or vacuuming begins.
