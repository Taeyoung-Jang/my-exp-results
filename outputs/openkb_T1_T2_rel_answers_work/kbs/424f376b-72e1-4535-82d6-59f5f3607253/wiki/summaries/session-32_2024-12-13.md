# Summary

The assistant offers practical habits to reduce morning chaos after moving to a new place.

### Core habits
- **Night‑before preparation** – lay out clothes, keys, bags, and any car accessories.  
- **Consistent wake‑up time** for the first weeks to let the body adjust.  
- **Small calming start** (stretching, water) before checking the phone.  
- **Buffer time** – add a 5‑minute cushion to avoid feeling rushed.

### Car‑related shortcuts
- **Parking predictability** – park in the same area when possible and take a quick photo of a nearby landmark; for variable street spots, repeat the photo or say the cross‑street aloud as a verbal cue. [[concepts/parking-photo]] [[concepts/verbal-cue]]  
- **Windshield cover** – use a cover with built‑in door flaps to stay secured; after use, shake off water, fold the wet side inward, and store in a plastic tote. Dry it on a rail or shower rod. [[concepts/windshield-cover-management]]  
- **Remote start** (if available) to warm the car while you get ready. [[entities/remote-start]]  
- **Scraper storage** – keep it in a door‑pocket slot or attach with a small elastic loop/Velcro so it’s always reachable.

### Overall approach
Combine **night‑before prep**, **consistent wake‑up**, **buffer time**, and the above car tricks to create a repeatable, low‑stress morning routine. [[concepts/morning-chaos-reduction]]
