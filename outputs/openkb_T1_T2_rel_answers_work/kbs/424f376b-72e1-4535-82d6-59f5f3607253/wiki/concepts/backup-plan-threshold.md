# Backup Plan Threshold

**Definition**: The specific amount of transit delay (in minutes) at which a commuter decides to abandon the planned mode and switch to a predefined backup option.

## Key Details from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]
- The session recommends a **10‑15 minute** window as a practical cutoff.
- The threshold is meant to balance patience with the need to keep the morning routine calm.
- Users are encouraged to pick a point within this range that feels personally stress‑free.

## How to Set Your Threshold
1. **Observe** your typical delays over a week.
2. **Choose** a cutoff that feels comfortable (e.g., 12 minutes).
3. **Document** the decision so you can act quickly when a delay occurs.

## When to Apply the Threshold
- During a **light‑rail** commute when real‑time apps show a delay exceeding the set minutes.
- When you need to maintain the **[[concepts/morning-chaos-reduction]]** goal of a calm start.

## Choosing a Backup Mode
Once the threshold is met, decide among alternatives based on speed, weather, and convenience:
- **Bus** – if a parallel route is available soon.
- **Rideshare** – when bus headways are long or you’re already behind.
- **Bike‑share** – in good weather and safe route conditions.

These choices tie into the broader evaluation of **[[concepts/commute-options]]**, weighing cost, reliability, and stress.
