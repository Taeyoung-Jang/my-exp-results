# Windshield Cover Management

**Description:** Managing windshield covers to stay secure, dry, and easily accessible.

## Overview
Windshield cover management involves choosing the right type of cover, securing it against wind, handling moisture after use, and storing it in a way that keeps the car interior clean. Effective management reduces morning friction, especially after moving to a new location where you may need to set up new routines.

## Key Practices from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-13]]

1. **Choose a cover with built‑in door flaps**
   - Covers that tuck into the car doors stay in place even on windy mornings.  
   - The flaps eliminate the need for straps or magnets and prevent the cover from blowing off.

2. **Quick removal and moisture control**
   - After removing the cover, shake it off thoroughly.
   - Fold the cover with the wet side facing inward.
   - Store the folded cover in a cheap plastic tote or reusable grocery bag placed in the trunk to keep the rest of the car dry.

3. **Drying the cover**
   - Once home, drape the cover over a rail, shower rod, or similar elevated surface for a few minutes.  
   - This allows the cover to air‑dry, preventing a damp, musty smell.

- **Reliability:** A secured cover means you won’t waste time re‑attaching it.
- **Cleanliness:** Proper storage prevents moisture from soaking the trunk and interior.
- **Efficiency:** Quick removal and drying streamline the morning routine.
