# Summary of session-35_2025-07-10

**Main contribution**: a structured yet gentle 15‑20 minute morning routine for people in a quiet home, with low‑energy alternatives and a checklist format.

### Key ideas
- **Comforting rituals**: start with a small, pleasant activity (e.g., favorite playlist, warm drink) to turn quiet into warmth.
- **Outdoor cue**: a brief 5‑minute walk (or a window‑opening swap) provides the "someone’s here" feeling the user misses.
- **Grounding tasks**: light stretching, breathing, or a tiny chore (watering a plant, tidying a corner) create momentum without pressure.
- **Low‑energy swaps**: each step includes an easier alternative so the routine can be maintained on tougher mornings.
- **Checklist format**: each activity is paired with an approximate time allocation (1‑2 min, 5 min, etc.) for easy tracking.

### Routine checklist (approx. 15‑20 min)
1. **Wake up & dress** – 1–2 min. *Low‑energy:* stay in bed, just sit up.  
2. **Step outside** – 5 min walk. *Low‑energy:* open a window for a minute of fresh air.  
3. **Warm drink & window sit** – 5–7 min. *Low‑energy:* take any drink, sit anywhere.  
4. **Light movement** – 2–3 min stretch or deep breaths. *Low‑energy:* stay seated, breathe slowly.  
5. **Grounding task** – 2–3 min (water plant, tidy a corner, set out items). *Low‑energy:* notice one calming element in the room.
