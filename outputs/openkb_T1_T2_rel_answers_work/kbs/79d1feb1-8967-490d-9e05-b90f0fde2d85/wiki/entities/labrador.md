# Labrador Retriever

Labrador Retriever is a widely‑known dog breed celebrated for its friendly temperament, high energy, and strong desire to please people. While generally sociable, Labs can mask stress signals, leading owners to misinterpret their body language.

## Breed Traits
- **Temperament:** outgoing, eager to engage with humans and other dogs.
- **Physical traits:** medium‑large size, sturdy build, short dense coat, "happy mask" of an open mouth and wagging tail.
- **Typical behavior:** loves retrieving, swimming, and being part of family activities.

## Key Insights from [[summaries/session-02_2024-07-10]]
- Labs often present a **"happy mask"**: open mouth, tail wag, and seemingly relaxed posture even when they feel uneasy.
- Stress in Labs may be visible as:
  - Stiffened body while the head and neck appear excited.
  - Narrowed or slower tail wag, early or sustained panting without exertion.
  - Quick shake‑off after an interaction that seemed neutral or uncomfortable.
- Owners should watch for **subtle mismatches** between facial/neck excitement and overall body tension.
