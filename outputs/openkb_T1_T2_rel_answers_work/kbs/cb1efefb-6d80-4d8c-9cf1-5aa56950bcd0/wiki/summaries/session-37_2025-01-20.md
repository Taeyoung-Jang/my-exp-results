# Summary of Session 37 (2025‑01‑20)

## Overview
This document outlines how to set up a kitchen **allergy‑safe zone**, identifies the most overlooked cross‑contamination traps, recommends the safest material choices, and provides a concrete 48‑hour checklist for cleaning and replacing items while minimizing flour dust aerosolization.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/allergy-safe-zone]] – dedicated prep area with separate tools, storage, and cleaning habits.  
- [[concepts/cross-contamination-traps]] – shared surfaces (cutting boards, sponges, cloths), porous materials, and hidden dust reservoirs.  
- [[concepts/material-decontamination]] – stainless steel and hard plastics are easiest to sanitize; wood and bamboo are harder to clean.  
- [[concepts/flour-dust-handling]] – treat flour dust as a thin surface film; use damp wiping, avoid dry sweeping, and run ovens hot to burn off residue.  
- [[concepts/damp-wipe-technique]] – keep cloths slightly damp to capture particles rather than loft them into the air.  
- [[concepts/hepa-vacuum-use]] – low‑suction HEPA vacuum with hose attachment for corners and floors; avoid bagless models without HEPA.  
- [[concepts/avoid-dry-cleaning]] – no dry cloths, brooms, or fans during cleaning to prevent re‑aerosolization.  
- [[concepts/kitchen-reset-checklist]] – a step‑by‑step 48‑hour plan covering supplies, zone order, and cautions.

## Cross‑Contamination Traps (Often Overlooked)
1. **Shared surfaces & tools** – cutting boards, wooden spoons, colanders, baking sheets retain residues.  
2. **Sponges & cloths** – spread allergens when reused without thorough sanitization.  
3. **Counters wiped with the same cloth** – transfer hidden particles.  
4. **Appliance crevices** – vents, mixer motor heads, oven fans collect settled flour.

## Material Recommendations
- **Best:** [[entities/stainless-steel]], smooth hard plastics, high‑quality silicone (non‑textured) – see [[entities/silicone-utensils]].  
- **Least reliable:** Wood, bamboo (porous, retain oils and proteins).  
- **Priority replacements:** Cutting board → stainless/plastic, toaster, colanders, spatulas with detachable heads, wooden utensils.

## Cleaning Fixed Items
- **Oven / convection oven:** Run a hot cycle, then wipe interior with a damp cloth.  
- **Stand mixer:** Disassemble removable parts, wash, and wipe motor head.  
- **Vents:** Remove cover if possible, wash or damp‑wipe.  
- **Pantry shelves:** Slow top‑to‑bottom damp wipe; let dry before restocking.  
- **General surfaces:** Countertops, cabinet fronts, backsplash – damp wipe only.

## 48‑Hour Reset Checklist (Supplies & Cautions)
### Day 1 – Morning
- Gather supplies: damp‑wipe cloths, mild cleaner, paper towels, HEPA vacuum with hose, trash bags.  
- **Caution:** Keep windows closed, fans off, no dry sweeping.  
- **Task:** Clear pantry, lift items straight up, damp‑wipe each shelf, dry before restocking.

### Day 1 – Afternoon
- Clean main kitchen surfaces top‑to‑bottom (upper cabinets → backsplash → counters → lower cabinets).  
- Use only damp cloths; vacuum edges with HEPA hose.  
- Introduce new safe‑zone items (cutting board, toaster, dedicated sponge) after surfaces are dry.

### Day 1 – Evening
- **Appliances:**  
  - Stand mixer – disassemble, wash, wipe motor head.  
  - Oven – run hot cycle, cool, damp‑wipe interior.  
  - Stovetop – damp wipe.  
- **Avoid:** Using any appliance that circulates air (convection) until cleaning is complete.

### Day 2 – Full Day
- Walk‑through: wipe missed spots (light switches, handles, appliance tops).  
- Vacuum floor with HEPA hose (low suction).  
- **Optional airing:** Open a window briefly **after** all wiping/vacuuming is done.  
- Final check: ensure all new tools are stored in the designated allergy‑safe zone.

## Practical Takeaways
- Establish a visually distinct **allergy‑safe zone** and keep its tools separate.  
- Replace high‑risk porous items first; prioritize cutting board and toaster.  
- Adopt the [[concepts/damp-wipe-technique]] and [[concepts/hepa-vacuum-use]] to keep flour dust from becoming airborne.  
- Follow the [[concepts/kitchen-reset-checklist]] to methodically reset the kitchen without creating dust storms.  
- Confirmation from your [[entities/allergy-allergist]] underpins the safety plan.
