# Silicone Utensils

**Type:** Product

**Description:** Non‑porous silicone kitchen tools that are easy to clean and suitable for allergy‑safe zones.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-01-20]]:**
- Recommended as a safe material because it is smooth, non‑textured, and can be thoroughly washed. [[concepts/material-decontamination]]
- Works well alongside stainless steel; both are among the easiest surfaces to fully decontaminate. [[entities/stainless-steel]]
- Should be used in the designated [[concepts/allergy-safe-zone]] and kept separate from porous tools that can trap allergens. [[concepts/cross-contamination-traps]]
- When cleaning, apply the [[concepts/damp-wipe-technique]] to avoid re‑aerosolizing flour dust. [[concepts/avoid-dry-cleaning]]
- Include silicone utensils in the [[concepts/kitchen-reset-checklist]] for the 48‑hour kitchen reset plan.

**Practical recommendations:**
- Choose high‑quality, non‑textured silicone items.
- Wash with hot, soapy water and rinse thoroughly; a final damp wipe ensures no residue.
- Store them in the allergy‑safe zone, clearly labeled or color‑coded.

**Related entities:** [[entities/allergy-allergist]] (provides guidance on safe materials).
