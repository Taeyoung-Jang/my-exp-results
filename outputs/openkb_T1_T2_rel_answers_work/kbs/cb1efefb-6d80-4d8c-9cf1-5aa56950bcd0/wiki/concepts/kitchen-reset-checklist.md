# 48‑Hour Kitchen Reset Checklist

**Purpose** – Provide a concrete, timed workflow for turning a regular kitchen into an [[concepts/allergy-safe-zone]] while minimizing cross‑contamination and airborne flour dust.

---

## Core Principles
- **Separate tools and storage** – dedicated cutting board, toaster, sponge, and utensils stored in a visually distinct area.
- **Use safe materials** – prioritize [[concepts/material-decontamination]] (stainless steel, hard plastic, silicone) over porous wood or bamboo.
- **Damp cleaning** – employ the [[concepts/damp-wipe-technique]] for every surface to capture particles rather than loft them.
- **Avoid dry cleaning** – no sweeping, dry cloths, or fans; follow [[concepts/avoid-dry-cleaning]].
- **Control dust** – use a HEPA‑filtered vacuum with a hose attachment as described in [[concepts/hepa-vacuum-use]] and handle [[concepts/flour-dust-handling]] by treating dust as a thin film.
- **Mind hidden traps** – be aware of [[concepts/cross-contamination-traps]] such as shared cutting boards, sponges, and appliance crevices.

---

## Supplies Checklist
- Micro‑fiber cloths kept **slightly damp** with mild cleaner
- Paper towels (for quick wipes)
- HEPA vacuum with hose attachment (low suction) – avoid bagless models without HEPA
- Trash bags for discarding old items
- New safe‑zone items (stainless‑steel cutting board, silicone spatulas, dedicated toaster, separate sponge)
- Optional: label stickers or color‑coded bins for visual separation

---

## Day 1 – Morning (Pantry Focus)
1. Close windows, turn off ventilation fans.
2. Gather all supplies.
3. Remove pantry items **lift‑up** (no sliding) to avoid dust disturbance.
4. **Damp‑wipe** each shelf from top to bottom; let dry before restocking.
5. Vacuum shelf edges with HEPA hose.

## Day 1 – Afternoon (Surface Sweep)
1. Work top‑to‑bottom on kitchen surfaces:
   - Upper cabinet fronts
   - Backsplash
   - Countertops
   - Lower cabinets
2. Use only damp cloths; avoid dry dusting.
3. Vacuum corners and trim with HEPA hose.
4. Place new safe‑zone tools (cutting board, toaster, dedicated sponge) on the cleaned surfaces **after they are dry**.

## Day 1 – Evening (Appliance Deep‑Clean)
1. **Stand mixer**: disassemble removable parts, wash, wipe motor head with damp cloth.
2. **Oven / convection oven**: run a full hot cycle, then cool and **damp‑wipe** interior walls.
3. **Stovetop**: damp‑wipe surface and knobs.
4. **Vent covers**: remove if possible, wash or wipe, replace.
5. Do **not** use any appliance that circulates air (e.g., convection setting) until cleaning is finished.

## Day 2 – Full Day (Final Touch‑Up & Verification)
1. Walk‑through: check light switches, handles, appliance tops; **damp‑wipe** any missed spots.
2. Vacuum the floor again with HEPA hose (low suction).
3. **Optional airing**: once all wiping and vacuuming are complete, open a window briefly to refresh air.
4. Verify that all new tools are stored in the designated [[concepts/allergy-safe-zone]] area and that no old porous items remain.

---

## Cautions (What **Not** to Do)
- **No dry cloths, brooms, or sweeping** – they loft flour dust.
- **Do not turn on fans or ventilation** until after the cleaning pass is complete.
- **Avoid aggressive shaking** of pantry items; lift straight up.
- **Do not use a bagless vacuum without HEPA filtration** – it can release trapped particles.

---

## Outcome
Following this checklist creates a low‑risk kitchen environment within 48 hours, sharply reduces hidden flour reservoirs, and establishes a repeatable routine for maintaining an [[concepts/allergy-safe-zone]].
