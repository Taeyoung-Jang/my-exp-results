# Allergy‑Safe Zone

## Overview
An **Allergy‑Safe Zone** is a clearly defined section of the kitchen where food preparation for allergy‑sensitive individuals occurs. It relies on dedicated tools, separate storage, and consistent cleaning habits to eliminate hidden sources of allergens.

## Core Elements
- **Physical separation**: Use a distinct countertop or island space, marked with a unique color or label.
- **Dedicated equipment**: A stainless‑steel or hard‑plastic cutting board, a separate toaster, and a dedicated sponge or paper‑towel set.
- **Exclusive storage**: Store the individual’s ingredients on a dedicated shelf or bin to prevent drips or cross‑contact.
- **Visual cues**: Keep the zone’s tools and containers visually distinct from the rest of the kitchen.

## Materials
Prioritize items that are easy to sanitize:
- [[entities/stainless-steel]] surfaces and hard plastics (smooth, non‑porous) are preferred.
- High‑quality [[entities/silicone-utensils]] are acceptable if non‑textured.
- Avoid porous materials such as wood or bamboo for high‑contact items.

## Cleaning Practices
- Follow the [[concepts/damp-wipe-technique]]: keep cloths slightly damp to capture particles rather than loft them.
- Never use dry cloths, brooms, or sweeping; see [[concepts/avoid-dry-cleaning]].
- For flour residues, apply [[concepts/flour-dust-handling]] methods: hot‑run the oven, then damp‑wipe interiors.
- Use a low‑suction [[concepts/hepa-vacuum-use]] with a hose attachment for corners and shelves.

## Implementation Checklist
The step‑by‑step plan from [[summaries/session-37_2025-01-20]] aligns with the [[concepts/kitchen-reset-checklist]]:
1. **Prepare supplies** – damp cloths, mild cleaner, paper towels, HEPA vacuum, trash bags.
2. **Clear and wipe pantry** – top‑to‑bottom damp wipe, dry before restocking.
3. **Introduce dedicated tools** – place new cutting board, toaster, and sponge in the zone.
4. **Wipe main surfaces** – cabinets, backsplash, countertops using damp cloths.
5. **Clean appliances** – stand mixer, oven (hot cycle + wipe), stovetop.
6. **Final walk‑through** – vacuum floor, wipe handles, ensure all new items remain within the zone.
