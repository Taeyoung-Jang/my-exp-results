# Damp‑Wipe Technique

**Definition**: A cleaning method that uses a lightly dampened cloth or paper towel to trap and remove dust, flour, and allergen residues, preventing them from becoming airborne.

---

## Overview
The damp‑wipe technique is recommended for kitchens where food allergens or flour dust are concerns. By keeping the cleaning surface moist, particles adhere to the cloth rather than being lofted into the air, which is essential for maintaining an [[concepts/allergy-safe-zone]].

---

## Why It Matters
- **Prevents cross‑contamination**: Dry wiping or sweeping can re‑suspend allergens, defeating other safety measures like dedicated tools (see [[concepts/cross-contamination-traps]]).
- **Controls flour dust**: Flour settles as a thin film; a damp wipe removes it efficiently without the need for aggressive scrubbing (see [[concepts/flour-dust-handling]]).
- **Supports other cleaning practices**: Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/hepa-vacuum-use]] for edges and corners, and aligns with the overall [[concepts/kitchen-reset-checklist]] plan.

---

## Key Steps
1. **Prepare a damp cloth**: Use warm water with a mild, residue‑free cleaner; wring out until the cloth is just wet, not dripping.
2. **Wipe surfaces top‑to‑bottom**: Start with high surfaces (upper cabinets, shelves) and work down to counters and floor edges.
3. **Press, don’t scrub**: Press the cloth onto the surface to let particles adhere; avoid vigorous rubbing which can spread residue.
4. **Change cloths frequently**: When the cloth becomes visibly dirty, discard or rinse it to avoid re‑depositing allergens.
5. **Dry surfaces**: After wiping, use a clean dry towel or let air‑dry before placing clean tools back into the area.

---

## When to Use
- **Pantry shelves** before restocking (as outlined in [[summaries/session-37_2025-01-20]]).
- **Appliance interiors** after a hot‑run cycle (oven, convection oven).
- **Countertops and cabinet fronts** during the kitchen reset checklist.
- **Any area where a sponge or dry cloth would stir up dust** – replace with a damp wipe to honor [[concepts/avoid-dry-cleaning]].
