# Polk Signa S2/S3

Polk Signa S2/S3 is an affordable 2.0/2.1 soundbar (typically under $200) that includes a dedicated **voice‑boost** or **speech enhancement** preset. In the context of [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-15]], it was recommended as one of three budget soundbars that improve the intelligibility of audio description tracks and dialogue when watching TV from another room.

## Key points from the session
- **Dialogue clarity:** The soundbar’s speech‑enhancement mode directly supports the [[concepts/dialogue-enhancement]] concept, making narration and dialogue stand out over music and effects.
- **Audio description support:** By boosting spoken content, it helps listeners follow shows that rely on [[concepts/audio-description]] while multitasking (e.g., cooking).
- **Comparison:** It is mentioned alongside other budget options such as [[entities/vizio-v-series-2-1]] and [[entities/samsung-hw-t400-t450]], all of which offer similar voice‑boost features.
- **Use case:** Ideal for half‑watching scenarios where users need clear speech from a distance, complementing practices like using subtitles and adjusting TV settings (night mode, dynamic range compression).

## Practical tips
1. Enable the **Voice Boost** or **Speech Enhancement** preset in the soundbar’s menu.
2. Pair it with TV settings such as **dialogue enhancement**, **night mode**, or **dynamic range compression** to further flatten loud‑quiet swings.
3. If the TV or streaming app allows separate volume control for the audio‑description track, raise that track slightly above the main mix.

By focusing on speech clarity, the Polk Signa S2/S3 helps users maintain comprehension of plot and dialogue even when their visual attention is divided.
