## Stranger Things

**Type:** work (TV series)

**Platform:** [[entities/netflix]]

**Key attributes (as noted in [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-15]]):**
- Recognized for **clear audio description** that helps listeners follow the story while multitasking. [[concepts/audio-description]]
- Occasionally suffers from a **busy sound mix** where music and effects can mask narration, making it less ideal for listening from another room.
- Suitable for **half‑watching** due to its strong dialogue and episodic structure, though viewers should be aware of the occasional mix complexity. [[concepts/half-watching]]
- Benefits from **dialogue‑enhancement** settings on TVs or soundbars to improve speech intelligibility. [[concepts/dialogue-enhancement]]

**Recommendations:**
- Use subtitles alongside audio description for dual reinforcement.
- Enable TV or soundbar features such as "dialogue boost," "night mode," or dynamic range compression to mitigate the busy mix.
- For the most reliable half‑watching experience, consider titles like [[entities/reacher]] or [[entities/ted-lasso]], which have steadier mixes.
