# Vizio V‑Series 2.1

**Type:** product

**Overview**
The Vizio V‑Series 2.1 (model V21‑H8 and similar refreshes) is a low‑cost soundbar system praised for its voice‑boost or **dialogue enhancement** mode, making it a popular choice for users who watch TV while multitasking, such as cooking in another room.

**Key characteristics from session-37**
- **Price range:** typically under $200, placing it firmly in the budget category.
- **Audio focus:** includes a dedicated *voice‑boost* setting that lifts speech and audio‑description tracks above background music and effects.
- **Configuration:** a 2.0 satellite speaker pair plus a subwoofer; while it lacks a separate center channel, its processing compensates for dialogue clarity.
- **Recommended settings:** enable the soundbar’s *dialogue* or *voice* mode, combine with TV‑level options like [[concepts/dialogue-enhancement]], night mode (if available), and **dynamic range compression** to keep narration audible from another room.

**Relevance to half‑watching**
Users who practice [[concepts/half-watching]] benefit from its ability to keep audio description intelligible without constantly rewinding. Pairing the Vizio V‑Series 2.1 with subtitles (see [[concepts/subtitle-distraction]]) further improves comprehension when glancing at the screen.
