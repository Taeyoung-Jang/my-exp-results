# Half‑Watching

**Definition**: A viewing approach where the audience watches TV or streaming content while simultaneously engaging in other activities, accepting that full visual attention is not possible.

## Core Characteristics
- **Selective focus**: Relies on audio cues, subtitles, and occasional pauses rather than continuous visual monitoring.
- **Genre suitability**: Works best with shows that have clear, repetitive structures—comedies, procedurals, reality TV—while dense, visual‑heavy dramas are less forgiving.
- **Audio tools**: Utilizes **audio description** to fill visual gaps and may boost the description track volume for clarity [[concepts/audio-description]].
- **Subtitles**: Keeping subtitles on provides a quick visual anchor for spoken content, reducing the need to stare at the screen.
- **Break points**: Viewers often pause at natural scene changes or commercial beats to mentally recap the plot.

## Practical Tips (from [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-15]])
1. **Choose appropriate content** – prioritize series with straightforward narratives and minimal reliance on visual storytelling.
2. **Leverage subtitles and description** – enable both to capture dialogue and visual context simultaneously.
3. **Adjust audio settings** – enable **dialogue enhancement**, **voice boost**, or **night mode** to accentuate speech and narration [[concepts/dialogue-enhancement]].
4. **Volume management** – raise the description track slightly above the main mix to keep it audible over background sounds.
5. **Use pause‑recap technique** – at natural breaks, pause briefly to consolidate understanding.

## Related Concepts

## Benefits
- Allows consumption of entertainment while completing household tasks.
- Reduces need for repeated rewinding, saving time.
- Enables enjoyment of a broader range of content through strategic selection and tech adjustments.

## Limitations
- Not suitable for highly visual or plot‑dense series where missing details impairs comprehension.
- May still require occasional rewinding for critical moments.
