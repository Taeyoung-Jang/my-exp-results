# Summary of session-05_2023-06-15

**Main contribution:** Provides multiple email drafts for a contractor outreach, focusing on tone, brevity, and natural phrasing of union membership.

## Key points
- Three tone variants were created: **standard**, **formal**, and **casual**. Each includes a line about union status. These drafts illustrate principles of [[concepts/email-drafting]] and [[concepts/professional-tone]].
- The standard version was tightened to 4‑5 sentences and re‑worded for natural flow.
- Suggested subject line options: "Interest in Upcoming Opportunities" and "Checking In About Possible Work".
- Revised union line to emphasize good standing without sounding formal: e.g., "feel free to confirm my union membership—I’ve been in good standing with the [[entities/electrical-workers-union]] since last year."
- Provided 2‑3 alternate one‑liners for the union sentence, offering flexible phrasing for contractor communication and showcasing [[concepts/union-membership-verification]].

## Practical takeaways
1. Keep outreach emails brief (4‑5 sentences) while clearly stating interest and availability.
2. Use natural language for union verification, such as "confirm my union membership" or "check my union standing."
3. Offer multiple tone options to match the recipient’s culture and the desired level of formality.
