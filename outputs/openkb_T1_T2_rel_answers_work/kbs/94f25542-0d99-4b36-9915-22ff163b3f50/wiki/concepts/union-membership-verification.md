# Union Membership Verification

## Definition
Union Membership Verification refers to the practice of explicitly offering a recipient the ability to confirm a contractor’s affiliation and good standing with a labor union. It is commonly used in outreach emails to reassure potential clients about compliance and credibility.

## Purpose in Contractor Outreach
- Demonstrates transparency and legitimacy.
- Provides a quick reference for employers who may require union‑covered labor.
- Helps maintain a professional yet approachable tone without appearing overly formal.

## Common Phrasing (as seen in [[summaries/session-05_2023-06-15]])
1. "feel free to confirm my union membership—I’ve been in good standing with the electrical workers' union since last year."
2. "You can check my union membership anytime—I’m in good standing with the electrical workers' union."
3. "If needed, you can confirm my union standing; I’ve been with the electrical workers' union since last year."

These variations avoid jargon like "cardholder" and use natural language that aligns with [[concepts/professional-tone]].

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/email-drafting]]**: The verification line is a component of a well‑structured outreach email, placed after expressing interest and before the closing.
- **[[concepts/professional-tone]]**: Crafting the verification statement balances professionalism with a conversational tone, ensuring the message is clear but not stiff.
