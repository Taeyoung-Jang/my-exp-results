# Name Change Process

**Definition:** A systematic approach to changing a legal name and propagating that change across personal, governmental, financial, and digital records.

---

## Overview
The name change process begins with a court order and then moves outward through a series of dependent updates. The core challenge is the **[[concepts/administrative-ripple-effect]]**, where each system that stores the old name must be corrected to avoid future mismatches.

## Core Steps (Prioritized Order)
1. **Obtain Certified Copies** – Order 3‑5 certified copies of the court order to have originals available for institutions that demand them.
2. **Update Government IDs**
   - **Social Security Administration** – Use a certified copy to update your SSN record. [[entities/social-security-administration]]
   - **Department of Motor Vehicles** – Update driver’s license or state ID once the SSA record reflects the new name. [[entities/department-of-motor-vehicles]]
   - **Passport** – Submit the court order plus updated SSN proof.
3. **Financial Accounts**
   - Banks and credit‑card issuers require a certified copy and a photo ID.
   - Update the three major credit bureaus: **Equifax**, **Experian**, **TransUnion**. [[entities/equifax]] [[entities/experian]] [[entities/transunion]]
4. **Employment & Benefits**
   - HR/payroll systems usually accept a copy of the order.
   - Health, dental, and other insurance providers often need the certified copy.
5. **Utilities & Services** – Most providers accept a copy or electronic upload.
6. **Medical Records** – Primary care and specialist portals generally accept scanned copies.
7. **Digital Presence** – Update email usernames, social media handles, and subscription accounts. For platforms that require proof, use the certified copy; otherwise most changes are self‑service. [[concepts/email-drafting]]

## Common Forgotten Accounts
The following tend to be overlooked until a problem surfaces, causing the biggest hassles:
- Retirement and investment accounts
- Small‑scale subscriptions and niche services
- Airline frequent‑flyer and hotel loyalty programs
- Old email usernames that cannot be changed easily
- Any account that holds financial information (e.g., PayPal, Venmo)

These are captured under **[[concepts/common-forgotten-accounts]]**.

## Tracker Template
A practical spreadsheet‑style table helps monitor progress. The template includes columns for:
- Category
- Priority (1‑3)
- Account / Agency
- Proof Required
- Method (In‑Person / Mail / Online)
- Date Submitted / Date Confirmed
- Status
- Notes

The template is provided in the source summary **[[summaries/session-35_2024-12-16]]** and can be copied directly into a note‑taking app or spreadsheet.

## Tips for Institutions Requiring Original Documents
- Bring originals and ask if they can photocopy and return them on the spot.
- For mail submissions, use tracked delivery and include a prepaid return envelope.
- Keep a spare certified copy at home for future requests.
