# Department of Motor Vehicles

**Type:** organization

The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is the state agency responsible for issuing driver’s licenses, state identification cards, and related vehicle services.

## Role in the name‑change process
In the checklist from [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-16]], updating the DMV record is listed as a **high‑priority** step after Social Security. The DMV typically requires:
- A certified copy of the court order confirming the legal name change
- Updated Social Security documentation
- Personal identification (e.g., current driver’s license)

The update is usually performed **in‑person** at a local DMV office, though some states allow mail or online submissions for certain changes.

## Interaction with other entities
- The DMV works closely with the [[entities/social-security-administration]] to verify the new name against the Social Security record.
- It is part of the broader [[concepts/administrative-ripple-effect]] that occurs when a legal name change propagates through government and private systems.
