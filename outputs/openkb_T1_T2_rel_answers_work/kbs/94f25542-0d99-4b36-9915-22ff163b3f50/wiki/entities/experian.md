# Experian

**Type:** organization

**Brief:** Experian is one of the three major U.S. credit bureaus.

## Overview
Experian collects and provides consumer credit information to lenders, landlords, and other authorized parties. It is frequently mentioned in personal‑administrative tasks because many institutions require a credit‑report check when a legal name change is processed.

## Role in session‑35_2024‑12‑16
In the name‑change checklist from [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-16]], Experian appears as one of the **Credit Bureaus** that should be updated after a legal name change. The document advises:
- Adding Experian to the prioritized tracker template under the "Credit Bureaus" section.
- Supplying a certified copy of the court order (or an uploaded scan) as proof of the name change.
- Using online or mail methods to submit the update request.

## Practical Tips (from the session)
- Order **3‑5 certified copies** of your court order so you have originals to submit to Experian while keeping spares.
- When contacting Experian, ask if they will accept a scanned copy of the certified order; many agencies will, but some may still request an original.
- Keep a record of the **date submitted** and **date confirmed** in your name‑change tracker template to ensure the update is processed.
