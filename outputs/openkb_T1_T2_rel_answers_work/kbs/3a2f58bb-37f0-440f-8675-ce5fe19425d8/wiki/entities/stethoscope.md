# Stethoscope

A **stethoscope** is a handheld medical instrument used by healthcare professionals to listen to internal body sounds such as heartbeats and lung activity. In the context of [[summaries/session-38_2025-01-09]], the stethoscope is mentioned as a practical location to keep an **emergency snack** for quick access during unpredictable hospital on‑call shifts.

## Key Facts from Session 38
- The device is commonly stored alongside other essential tools like the [[entities/pen_light]] at the [[entities/nurses_station]].
- It serves as a convenient anchor point for the [[concepts/emergency_snack]] stash, reinforcing the idea of **tiny anchors** that help maintain nutrition and energy when schedules shift.
- The practice of placing a snack near the stethoscope aligns with the broader strategy of [[concepts/anchor_habits]], where small, repeatable cues support health on rotating [[entities/hospital_on_call_shift]] schedules.
- By integrating the snack with the stethoscope, clinicians can quickly refuel without disrupting workflow, aiding overall [[concepts/emotional_stability]] and reducing stress.
