# Protein Bar

A **protein bar** is a compact, shelf‑stable snack that provides a balanced mix of protein and carbohydrates. In the context of rotating on‑call schedules, it serves as a convenient **emergency snack** that can be grabbed in seconds, helping to prevent energy crashes when meals are delayed.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-01-09]]
- Recommended as one of the primary **grab‑and‑go** options for staff on [[entities/hospital_on_call_shift]].
- Chosen for its **non‑crumbly** nature, making it easy to store alongside other tools such as a [[entities/stethoscope]] or [[entities/pen_light]].
- Integrated into the **anchor habits** routine for hunger management: quick chew, brief pause, then back to duties.
- Fits within the broader concept of [[concepts/emergency_snack]] and supports overall [[concepts/anchor_habits]] for health stability.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/emergency_snack]] – the broader category of portable nutrition solutions.

- [[concepts/pre_sleep_wind_down]] – while not a snack, the protein bar can be consumed (if needed) before a short wind‑down to avoid hunger‑induced wakefulness.

## Practical Tips
- Keep a few bars in the same drawer or pocket where you store your [[entities/nurses_station]] supplies.
- Choose varieties low in added sugars to avoid a quick spike and crash.
- Pair with a sip of water to aid digestion and hydration.
