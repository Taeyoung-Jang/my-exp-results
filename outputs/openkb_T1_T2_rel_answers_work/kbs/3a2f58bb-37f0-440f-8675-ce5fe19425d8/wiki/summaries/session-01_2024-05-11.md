# Summary

This document captures a conversational guide on building **emotional stability** through **low‑intensity, flexible anchor habits** and an **if/then protocol** for handling unexpected changes. The assistant recommends :

- Selecting a few **non‑negotiable micro‑rituals** (e.g., a slow breath, brief stretch, sip of water, shoulder relaxation) that can be performed anywhere regardless of schedule.
- Using **grounding techniques** such as a quick pause, naming the change, or a brief sensory action to reduce the impact of sudden disruptions.
- Structuring the habits into a concise **if/then plan** with memorable trigger phrases (e.g., “before I open email”, “when plans change”).
- Practicing these cues in low‑stress moments to develop an **automatic practice** rather than a rigid routine.
- Reviewing the plan briefly each day (e.g., while coffee brews) to keep it familiar without feeling like a chore.
