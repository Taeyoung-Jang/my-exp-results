# Emergency Snack

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** Portable, balanced mini‑meals for quick nutrition during unpredictable schedules.

---

When work hours shift erratically—such as during a [[entities/hospital_on_call_shift]]—the body’s need for fuel doesn’t wait for a regular mealtime. An **Emergency Snack** is a pre‑planned, grab‑and‑go food item (or small collection) kept within easy reach so you can refill energy in under a minute.

## Core Characteristics
1. **Portability** – fits in a bag, pocket, or the drawer next to the [[entities/stethoscope]] or [[entities/pen_light]].
2. **Balanced macros** – a mix of protein and carbohydrate to stabilize blood‑sugar and stave off cravings.
3. **Shelf‑stable** – no refrigeration needed; safe for long‑haul storage at the [[entities/nurses_station]].
4. **Quick consumption** – can be eaten or sipped without preparation beyond opening a package.

## Recommended Items (from session‑38)
- **Protein bar** – non‑crumbly, high‑protein option.
- **Mixed nuts & dried fruit packets** – natural fats and carbs.
- **Instant oatmeal cup** – just add hot water.
- **Shelf‑stable yoghurt** or Greek yoghurt tubes.
- **Tuna or chicken packets** with crackers.

These items are mentioned in the source summary and can be stored together in a single "snack kit" alongside the [[entities/stethoscope]] or other frequently‑used tools.

## How to Use the Emergency Snack
1. **Detect the cue** – notice you’re feeling low energy or it’s been >3‑4 hours since your last meal.
2. **Grab the kit** – reach for the nearest snack container.
3. **Consume mindfully** – chew slowly for at least a minute to signal satiety.
4. **Reset** – after eating, take a brief mental pause (a few deep breaths) before returning to work.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of a broader set of [[concepts/anchor_habits]] that provide micro‑level stability.
- Supports [[concepts/emotional_stability]] by preventing irritability linked to low blood‑sugar.
- Works alongside [[concepts/morning_reset]] and [[concepts/pre_sleep_wind_down]] as part of a flexible routine.
- Can be combined with [[concepts/grounding_techniques]] (e.g., mindful chewing) for added calm.
