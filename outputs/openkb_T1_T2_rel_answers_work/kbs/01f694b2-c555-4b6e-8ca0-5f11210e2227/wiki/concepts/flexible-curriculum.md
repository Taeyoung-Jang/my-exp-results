## Flexible Curriculum

A **flexible curriculum** is an educational blueprint that intentionally keeps multiple pathways open while progressively deepening expertise. It is built around a **common core** of transferable skills—math, introductory programming, statistics, writing/communication, and a lab science—and then layers track‑specific electives that can pivot toward policy/economics or data/technology.  

### Core Principles
- **Breadth first:** The first two years emphasize broad exposure (e.g., math, programming, humanities) so students can discover interests without premature specialization. 
- **Early real‑world testing:** Summer internships, research assistantships, or self‑directed projects are woven into each year to provide concrete feedback on fit. 
- **Skill anchors:** Core courses act as anchors that make later specialization possible without re‑learning fundamentals. 
- **Decision checkpoints:** By the end of year 2, students evaluate major commitment using three signals – engagement in an intermediate course, identifiable job pathways, and completion of at least one real‑world experience.
