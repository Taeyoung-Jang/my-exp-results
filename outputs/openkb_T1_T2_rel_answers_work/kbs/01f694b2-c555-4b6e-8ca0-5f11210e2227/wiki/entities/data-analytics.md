# Data Analytics (Minor/Certificate)

**Type:** other

**Overview**

Data Analytics is a minor or certificate program that teaches students how to collect, clean, analyze, and visualize data using statistical and computational methods. It is highlighted in [[summaries/session-12_2019-04-14]] as a high‑leverage option for undecided undergraduates because it builds **core skills** that are valuable across many majors.

**Key Facts from Session 12**

- Listed among the **high‑leverage minors/certificates** that keep both policy/econ and data/tech pathways open. [[concepts/high-leverage-minors]]
- Strengthens quantitative literacy, a cornerstone of the **common core** curriculum. [[concepts/core-skills]]
- Supports a **flexible curriculum** by providing transferable analytical tools regardless of the chosen major. [[concepts/flexible-curriculum]]
- Pairs well with other recommended entities such as **Computer Science Fundamentals** and **Public Policy**, enabling students to blend technical and policy expertise. [[entities/computer-science-fundamentals]] [[entities/public-policy]]

**Why It Matters**

- **Employability:** Employers in both the public and private sectors look for data‑driven decision‑making abilities.
- **Versatility:** The minor equips students to handle data‑heavy tasks in economics, public policy analysis, software development, and research.
- **Early Exposure:** Completing introductory data‑analytics courses by the end of Year 2 aligns with the suggested decision checkpoints for committing to a major.

**Typical Coursework**

1. Introduction to Data Analysis (statistics, Excel, or R)
2. Data Visualization (Tableau, PowerBI, or Python libraries)
3. Applied Statistics or Econometrics
4. Database Fundamentals (SQL)
5. Capstone Project (real‑world data problem)
