## Public Policy (Minor/Certificate)

**Type:** other

**Description:** A structured program of study that introduces students to the processes, institutions, and analytical tools used in creating and evaluating public policies. It is often offered as a minor or certificate that can be combined with a wide range of majors.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-12_2019-04-14]]:**
- Recommended as one of the high‑leverage minors that keep both policy/econ and data/tech pathways open because it provides contextual knowledge of government and societal systems while remaining compatible with quantitative and technical coursework.
- Pairs well with data‑oriented minors such as [[entities/data-analytics]] and technical foundations like [[entities/computer-science-fundamentals]].
- Supports the **flexible‑curriculum** approach by giving students a policy lens that can be applied to many career trajectories, from consulting to public‑interest technology.
