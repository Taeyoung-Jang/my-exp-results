# Team Lead (New Role)

**Type:** other

**Description:** This entity represents the newly inherited team lead position described in [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-20]]. The role involves guiding a six‑person hybrid team (three remote, three in‑office) through the first weeks of leadership by setting up clear expectations, lightweight routines, and transparent ownership structures.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Facilitate shared agreements** – co‑create expectations with the team rather than imposing rules. [[concepts/shared-agreements]]
- **Establish lightweight, purpose‑driven routines** such as brief async updates, mid‑week check‑ins, and regular one‑on‑ones. [[concepts/lightweight-routines]]
- **Iterate team norms** – treat the initial set of norms as a draft to be refined based on feedback. [[concepts/iterative-team-norms]]
- **Use simple async‑update prompts** to surface priorities, blockers, and weekly progress. [[concepts/async-update-prompts]]
- **Maintain a decision‑log‑lite** that records date, decision, owner, and follow‑up location in a single line. [[concepts/decision-log-lite]]
- **Map ownership with a RACI‑lite table** (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) for each inherited project. [[concepts/racilite-ownership]]
- **Coordinate hybrid team communication** ensuring equal visibility for remote and in‑office members via shared channels and video‑enabled meetings. [[concepts/hybrid-team-coordination]]

## Context from Session 34
- The role is taken on after accepting a leadership position for a small, hybrid team with existing project confusion.
- The first 2–3 weeks focus on one‑on‑one conversations, weekly rhythm establishment, and co‑creating a concise ownership map.
- Measurable signals to track success include reduced last‑minute surprises, faster blocker resolution, and clearer updates.
