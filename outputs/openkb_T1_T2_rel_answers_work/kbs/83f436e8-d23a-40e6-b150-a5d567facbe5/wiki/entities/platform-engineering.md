# Platform Engineering (work)

**Overview**
Platform Engineering is the discipline that designs, implements, and maintains the foundational infrastructure, tooling, and services that enable product teams to deliver value quickly and reliably. It encompasses areas such as reliability, automation, cost efficiency, and developer experience.

**Key Points from [[summaries/session-07_2022-07-20]]**
- The document proposes a set of high‑impact goals for a platform engineer, including:
  - Leading reliability initiatives to cut recurring incidents by **20‑30 %**.
  - Building automated remediation mechanisms that resolve major failure classes without human intervention.
  - Driving efficiency projects that reduce compute or storage costs by **10‑20 %** while maintaining SLA performance.
  - Defining and meeting SLOs with **>99.9 %** availability for two consecutive quarters.
- These goals are framed as **[[concepts/ic-track-ambition]]** objectives, emphasizing technical depth over people‑management.
- The suggested OKRs are crafted to be **[[concepts/review-proof-okrs]]**, with clear attribution to the engineer’s ownership and explicit evidence‑collection methods (dashboards, incident stats, design‑doc approvals).
- The approach reflects **[[concepts/technical-leadership-without-management]]**, positioning the platform engineer as a technical lead who drives architectural direction, standards, and tooling.

**Related Entities**
- While the focus here is on platform engineering, the document also discusses **[[entities/distributed-systems]]** as a complementary domain where similar IC‑track strategies apply.

**Typical Activities**
- Designing and implementing platform‑wide reliability frameworks (e.g., auto‑recovery, observability stacks).
- Optimizing resource utilization through cost‑analysis and performance tuning.
- Creating and maintaining developer‑experience tools (build pipelines, deployment automation).
- Establishing service‑level objectives (SLOs) and monitoring compliance.
- Mentoring engineering teams on best practices and onboarding them to new platform capabilities.

**Why It Matters**
Effective platform engineering amplifies the impact of product teams, reduces operational overhead, and safeguards the organization’s technical health, directly aligning with the ambition of technical individual contributors seeking high‑impact, leadership‑by‑expertise roles.
