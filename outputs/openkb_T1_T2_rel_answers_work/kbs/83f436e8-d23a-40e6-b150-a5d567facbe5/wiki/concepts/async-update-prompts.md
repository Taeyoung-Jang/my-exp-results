# Async Update Prompts

**Definition**: Short, recurring questions posted in a shared channel that team members answer asynchronously to communicate weekly priorities, blockers, achievements, and support needs. The prompts are meant to be lightweight, purpose‑driven, and co‑created so they feel like a shared agreement rather than a top‑down requirement.

## Core Purpose
- Provide visibility for both remote and in‑office members, supporting [[concepts/hybrid-team-coordination]].
- Reduce the need for status‑checking meetings by surfacing the most relevant information in a predictable format.
- Enable the team lead to track measurable signals such as blocker resolution time and update completeness.
- Serve as a lightweight complement to [[concepts/lightweight-routines]] and [[concepts/iterative-team-norms]].

## Typical Weekly Rhythm (from [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-20]])
| Day | Prompt | Goal |
|-----|--------|------|
| **Monday** | *“What are your top one or two priorities this week, what might block you, and what support do you need?”* | Align on upcoming work and surface early blockers. |
| **Friday** | *“What did you finish this week, what are you carrying forward, and what support do you need for next week?”* | Capture progress, reflect on outcomes, and plan assistance. |

These prompts are posted in the team’s existing async channel (e.g., Slack, Teams) and answered by each member in a sentence or two. The team lead also answers the prompts to model the behavior.

## Design Guidelines
1. **Keep it short** – 1‑3 lines per participant; avoid lengthy narratives.
2. **Make the purpose explicit** – tie each question to a concrete coordination problem (e.g., “prevent last‑minute surprises”).
3. **Invite co‑creation** – solicit feedback after the first week and adjust wording as needed, reinforcing [[concepts/shared-agreements]].
4. **Link to decision tracking** – if a response reveals a decision point, record it in the simple [[concepts/decision-log-lite]] format.
5. **Maintain visibility** – ensure both remote and office‑based teammates see the updates; consider pinning the prompt or using a shared document.

## Sample Prompt Templates
- **Monday Prompt**: 
  - *Priority*: “My top focus this week is X.”
  - *Blockers*: “Potential blocker: Y.”
  - *Help needed*: “I need Z from the team.”
- **Friday Prompt**:
  - *Done*: “Completed A and B.”
  - *Carry‑over*: “Continuing work on C.”
  - *Support*: “Need input on D before next week.”

## Benefits
- **Transparency**: Everyone knows who is working on what without extra meetings.
- **Speed**: Quick identification of blockers leads to faster resolution.
- **Data for improvement**: The pattern of responses feeds into the measurable signals used to assess the health of communication and ownership.
- **Low overhead**: Requires only a few minutes per person per week, fitting well with [[concepts/lightweight-routines]].

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- The prompts support the **RACI‑lite ownership** approach ([[concepts/racilite-ownership]]) by surfacing who is *Responsible* for each priority.
- They complement the **decision log** ([[concepts/decision-log-lite]]) by providing the context for each logged decision.
- Used together with the weekly schedule, they reinforce the **iterative team norms** process ([[concepts/iterative-team-norms]]) by making norm adjustments visible.
