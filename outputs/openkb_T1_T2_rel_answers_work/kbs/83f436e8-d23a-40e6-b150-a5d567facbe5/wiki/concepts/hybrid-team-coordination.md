# Hybrid Team Coordination

**Definition**: A set of lightweight practices that give remote and in‑office members the same level of visibility, communication, and ownership, preventing information silos in a blended work environment.

---

## Why It Matters
In the *session-34_2024-07-20* discussion, a 6‑person hybrid team struggled with unclear project ownership and uneven information flow. The leader needed a simple framework that would work for both remote and on‑site participants without adding busywork.

Key pain points identified:
- Confusion over who owned which tasks.
- Missed updates because some members relied on informal office chatter.
- Difficulty surfacing decisions in a timely manner.

## Core Practices

### 1. Shared Async Update Prompts
Use brief, recurring prompts to surface priorities and blockers for the whole team regardless of location. The prompts are covered under [[concepts/async-update-prompts]] and typically run on Monday and Friday.

### 2. Lightweight, Purpose‑Driven Routines
A predictable weekly rhythm keeps everyone aligned while staying minimal. This aligns with the broader idea of [[concepts/lightweight-routines]] and includes:
- **Monday**: async priority check‑in.
- **Wednesday**: 15‑minute video‑enabled team sync (remote participants join via video, office participants gather in a room).
- **Friday**: async wrap‑up reflecting on progress and support needs.

### 3. Decision Log Lite
Document decisions in a single‑line format (date, decision, owner, follow‑up location). See [[concepts/decision-log-lite]] for the exact template. The log lives in a shared channel so both remote and in‑office staff can see it instantly.

### 4. RACI‑Lite Ownership Mapping
A stripped‑down RACI table (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) for each inherited project clarifies who does what without the overhead of a full matrix. Refer to [[concepts/racilite-ownership]] for the template.

### 5. Iterative Team Norms & Shared Agreements
Norms are introduced as a **first draft** and refined together, embodying the principles of [[concepts/iterative-team-norms]] and [[concepts/shared-agreements]]. This collaborative approach reduces the feeling of top‑down mandates.

## Implementation Checklist
1. **Send the first team message** (see summary) outlining the new rhythm and inviting co‑creation.
2. **Schedule 30‑minute 1:1s** with each member to learn preferred work styles and capture ownership clues.
3. **Post Monday async prompt** and collect responses.
4. **Hold Wednesday video sync**; surface any early decisions.
5. **Log decisions** in the Decision Log Lite.
6. **Draft RACI‑Lite tables** for each project and share them for validation.
7. **Gather feedback** at the end of week three and adjust the routine.

## Benefits
- **Equal visibility**: Remote staff receive the same updates as on‑site staff via async channels and recorded video syncs.
- **Clear ownership**: RACI‑Lite and Decision Log Lite eliminate ambiguity.
- **Low overhead**: Routines are brief, purpose‑driven, and iteratively refined, avoiding bureaucracy.
- **Improved trust**: Shared agreements and iterative norms foster a collaborative culture.
