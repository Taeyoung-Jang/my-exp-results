# Lightweight Decision Log

A **lightweight decision log** is a minimalistic record that captures the essential details of a decision in a single line. Its purpose is to make ownership and follow‑up visible without creating extra administrative overhead.

## Core Elements
- **Date** – when the decision was made.
- **Decision summary** – a brief statement of what was decided.
- **Owner** – the person responsible for delivering the outcome.
- **Follow‑up location** – where updates will be shared (e.g., weekly check‑in, async update channel).

**Typical format**
```
YYYY‑MM‑DD — Decision summary; Owner; Follow‑up location
```
*Example*: `2024-07-22 — Prioritize feature X this sprint; Maria; updates in Friday async wrap‑up.`

## Why Use a Lightweight Log?
- **Reduces busywork** – only four fields, one line per entry.
- **Ensures clarity** – ownership and next steps are explicit.
- **Fits hybrid teams** – a shared text file or channel note is accessible to both remote and in‑office members, supporting [[concepts/hybrid-team-coordination]].
- **Supports iterative norms** – the log can be adjusted as the team refines its processes, aligning with [[concepts/iterative-team-norms]].

## Relationship to Other Practices
- Works hand‑in‑hand with brief **async update prompts** (see [[concepts/async-update-prompts]]) that surface progress and blockers.
- Complements **lightweight routines** such as short daily or weekly check‑ins ([[concepts/lightweight-routines]]).
- Reinforces **shared agreements** by documenting the outcomes of co‑created decisions ([[concepts/shared-agreements]]).
- Can be referenced in the **RACI‑lite ownership** table for clarity on who is responsible and accountable ([[concepts/racilite-ownership]]).

## Adoption Steps (as described in [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-20]])
1. **Introduce the log** in the first team meeting and explain its one‑line format.
2. **Add the log** to the channel or doc the team already uses for communication.
3. **Record each decision** immediately after it is made, using the template.
4. **Review weekly** during the Friday async wrap‑up to ensure owners are on track.
5. **Iterate** the format if the team feels any field is unnecessary or missing.

By keeping the decision log lightweight, teams gain transparency and accountability without the friction of heavy documentation.
