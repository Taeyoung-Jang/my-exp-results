# RACI‑Lite Ownership Mapping

**Definition**: A lightweight adaptation of the classic RACI matrix that captures only the most essential roles—Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed—using a short text block per project. It is designed for fast adoption by small, agile teams and avoids the bureaucracy of full‑scale responsibility charts.

---

## Why Use RACI‑Lite?
- **Clarity without overload** – Provides just enough information to eliminate confusion about who owns what, especially in hybrid settings where visibility varies. [[concepts/hybrid-team-coordination]]
- **Supports iterative norms** – Can be drafted, shared, and refined as part of an ongoing [[concepts/iterative-team-norms]] process. [[summaries/session-34_2024-07-20]]
- **Fits lightweight routines** – Aligns with brief weekly syncs and async check‑ins, keeping the ownership conversation short and actionable. [[concepts/lightweight-routines]]
- **Keeps documentation minimal** – Pairs well with a simple decision log to capture who decided what and where updates will appear. [[concepts/decision-log-lite]]

---

## Core Structure
For each project, list the following four lines:
```
Project: <Project Name>
Responsible: <Who drives the work>
Accountable: <Final decision‑maker>
Consulted: <People whose input shapes the work>
Informed: <Stakeholders who need visibility>
```
Only one name per role is required, but multiple names can be added if the team prefers.

## Applying RACI‑Lite in Session 34
During the first week, the new team lead used one‑on‑ones to surface current ownership ambiguities. The resulting RACI‑Lite tables were shared in the team channel for co‑creation:

```
Project: Feature X Redesign
Responsible: Maria
Accountable: Alex (Team Lead)
Consulted: Jamie, Priya
Informed: All team members
```

The team discussed the draft live, made quick edits, and then logged the final version in a shared doc. This short iteration reinforced the principle of **shared agreements** rather than imposed rules. [[concepts/shared-agreements]]

## How to Maintain the Map
1. **Create** during onboarding or after a project hand‑over. 
2. **Publish** in a commonly accessed location (e.g., the team’s async channel or a lightweight wiki). 
3. **Update** whenever roles shift—prefer a single line edit over a full redesign. 
4. **Reference** in the decision log entries to tie decisions to owners. [[concepts/decision-log-lite]]
