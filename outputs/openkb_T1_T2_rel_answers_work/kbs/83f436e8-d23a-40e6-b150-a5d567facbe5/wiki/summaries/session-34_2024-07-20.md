# Summary of session-34_2024-07-20

**Main contribution**: Practical advice for a new team lead to establish clear, collaborative expectations, lightweight routines, and a simple ownership framework for a 6‑person hybrid team.

## Key Concepts
- **Shared agreements** – frame expectations as co‑created norms rather than top‑down rules. [[concepts/shared-agreements]]
- **Lightweight, purpose‑driven routines** – brief daily/weekly check‑ins tied to real coordination needs. [[concepts/lightweight-routines]]
- **Modeling behavior** – demonstrate the communication style and availability you expect. *(plain text)*
- **Iterative team norms** – treat team norms as a first draft to be refined together. [[concepts/iterative-team-norms]]
- **One‑on‑one cadence** – 30‑minute weekly or bi‑weekly personal syncs focused on alignment and unblocking. *(plain text)*
- **Async update prompts** – simple Monday and Friday questions to surface priorities, blockers, and progress. [[concepts/async-update-prompts]]
- **Decision log** – a one‑line record (date, decision, owner, follow‑up location) to keep ownership visible without busywork. [[concepts/decision-log-lite]]
- **Measurable signals** – fewer last‑minute surprises, faster blocker resolution, clearer updates, and higher commitment follow‑through. *(plain text)*
- **Hybrid team coordination** – ensure equal visibility for remote and in‑office members through shared async channels and video‑enabled mid‑week check‑ins. [[concepts/hybrid-team-coordination]]
- **RACI‑lite ownership map** – a minimal table (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) for each inherited project. [[concepts/racilite-ownership]]

## Concrete 2–3 Week Plan
1. **Week 1**
   - Conduct short individual one‑on‑ones to learn preferred work styles and current blockers.
   - Post a **Monday async prompt**: “Top 1‑2 priorities this week, potential blockers, support needed.”
   - Hold a brief **mid‑week team check‑in** (video for remote members) to surface early decisions.
   - Summarize ownership insights and share a draft **RACI‑lite table** for each project.
2. **Week 2**
   - Run the established routines consistently.
   - Use one‑on‑ones to clarify project ownership and document decisions in the **decision log**.
   - Track **measurable signals** (e.g., blocker resolution time, update completeness).
3. **Week 3**
   - Refine routines based on feedback (adjust async frequency, tighten meeting agenda).
   - Publish the final **team norms** draft and invite edits.
   - Review the **decision log** and RACI‑lite tables for accuracy.

## Sample Weekly Schedule (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- **Monday**: Async prompt + optional quick personal check‑in.
- **Wednesday**: 15‑minute team sync (video for remote participants) – align on progress, surface decisions.
- **Friday**: Async wrap‑up prompt – “What moved, what’s in motion, support needed next week.”

### 1:1 Agenda (30 min)
1. Personal check‑in (2 min)  
2. Current ownership & progress (8 min)  
3. Blockers & needed support (8 min)  
4. What’s going well / learning (6 min)  
5. Quick recap & next steps (6 min)

## Decision Log Format (single line)
```
YYYY‑MM‑DD — Decision summary; Owner; Follow‑up location (e.g., weekly check‑in)
```
Example: `2024-07-22 — Prioritize feature X this sprint; Maria; updates in Friday async wrap‑up.`

## Ownership Mapping (RACI‑lite) Template
```
Project: <Name>
Responsible: <Who drives the work>
Accountable: <Final decision maker>
Consulted: <Who provides input>
Informed: <Who needs visibility>
```
Fill in for each inherited project during the first week.

## Facilitating the First Team Meeting
- Frame the discussion as **aligning on the current picture**, not assigning new roles.
- Present the draft RACI‑lite tables and ask “Does this match how you see the work?”
- Invite real‑time edits; emphasize co‑creation and the goal of reducing confusion.
- Reinforce that the norms and ownership map are **first drafts** that will evolve.
