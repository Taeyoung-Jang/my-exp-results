# Summary of Session 11 – Spanish Learning Routine (2024‑06‑15)

This document outlines a **grammar‑first language‑learning framework** for Spanish, offering a concrete **7‑day schedule** (Mon–Sun) with exact time blocks for **grammar study**, **speaking practice**, and **listening immersion**, plus a **2‑week topic map** that cycles core grammar topics and pairs each with a speaking prompt and a beginner‑friendly listening source.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/grammar-first-approach]] – Prioritizing grammar while maintaining daily speaking and listening.  
- [[concepts/time-block-scheduling]] – Fixed daily blocks: 20 min grammar, 5 min speaking, 10‑15 min listening (30‑45 min total).  
- [[concepts/topic-cycling]] – Rotating through essential Spanish grammar topics over two weeks.  
- Spanish (entity) – The target language for the routine.  
- YouTube (entity) – Suggested platform for some listening sessions.  

## 7‑Day Schedule (Mon–Sun)
| Day | Grammar (20 min) | Speaking Prompt (5 min) | Listening (10‑15 min) |
|-----|------------------|--------------------------|-----------------------|
| **Mon** | *ser* vs *estar* – identity & location | “Who am I, where am I, how do I feel?” | Slow‑Spanish news podcast |
| **Tue** | *ser* vs *estar* – temporary states | Describe room objects & feelings today | Beginner YouTube video |
| **Wed** | Irregular present verbs (tener, hacer, salir…) | Daily routine using those verbs | Graded learner dialogue |
| **Thu** | Direct & indirect object pronouns | Talk about things you buy, give, show | Short dialogue or app exercise |
| **Fri** | Preterite forms | What I did yesterday | Storytelling podcast (past events) |
| **Sat** | Imperfect forms | Past weekend habits or descriptions | Beginner narrative YouTube video |
| **Sun** | Preterite vs. imperfect comparison | Mini‑story mixing scene setting & actions | Beginner podcast with personal anecdotes |

## 2‑Week Topic Map (Day 1–14)
- **Days 1‑2:** *ser* / *estar* – identity, location, states.  
- **Days 3‑4:** Irregular present‑tense verbs.  
- **Days 5‑6:** Direct & indirect object pronouns.  
- **Days 7‑8:** Preterite vs. imperfect (Day 7 comparison).  
- **Days 9‑10:** Simple future tense – plans.  
- **Days 11‑12:** Conditional tense – hypothetical wishes.  
- **Days 13‑14:** Present subjunctive – desires, recommendations, mixed with future/conditional.  

Each day’s **speaking prompt** directly applies the grammar point, reinforcing production, while the **listening source** aligns with the topic’s difficulty level.

## Takeaway
By structuring daily study into **fixed time blocks** and **rotating grammar topics**, learners can keep **grammar progression** at the forefront without sacrificing **speaking fluency** and **listening comprehension**. The schedule is adaptable to any 30‑45 min daily window and can be extended with additional topics as proficiency grows.
