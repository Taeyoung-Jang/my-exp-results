# YouTube (Organization)

YouTube is a global online video‑sharing service that hosts a vast range of user‑generated and professional content. It is frequently used as a source for language‑learning material, especially beginner‑friendly Spanish lessons.

## Mention in [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]]

In the “Spanish Learning Routine” document, YouTube is recommended on several days as the **listening source**:

- Tuesday: a beginner YouTube video for listening practice.
- Saturday: a beginner narrative YouTube video.

These suggestions illustrate how the platform supports the [[concepts/grammar-first-approach]] by providing authentic yet approachable audio input that aligns with the daily **[[concepts/time-block-scheduling]]**.

## Related concepts

The schedule described in the document follows a **[[concepts/time-block-scheduling]]** pattern and cycles through grammar topics (**[[concepts/topic-cycling]]**). YouTube serves as one of the media options that enable this approach.
