# Easy‑Reach Zone

**Definition**: The *Easy‑Reach Zone* is a horizontal band in a home that lies roughly between the user’s knees and shoulders (waist‑to‑shoulder height). Items placed within this zone can be accessed without bending, stretching, or climbing, which improves ergonomics and safety, especially for those with mobility limitations.

**Origin**: This concept was introduced in [[summaries/session-36_2025-11-11]], where the assistant recommended reorganizing daily essentials into an easy‑reach band to accommodate a user wearing a walking boot.

## Core Principles
1. **Height Range**: Identify a band approximately 40–120 cm from the floor, adjusting for the individual’s height and any assistive devices.
2. **Frequency‑Based Placement**: Store items used daily or multiple times a day within the zone; reserve higher or lower storage for occasional or heavy items.
3. **Clear Zones per Room**: Apply the zone consistently across rooms (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, entryway) to create predictable access points.
4. **Safety First**: Keep the zone free of obstacles; ensure pathways remain wide.

## Practical Applications
- **Kitchen**: Place dishes, mugs, utensils, spices, and everyday pans on mid‑height shelves or countertop turntables. Example storage items: [[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]], [[entities/compact_craft_tool_tote]], [[entities/divided_cleaning_caddy]].
- **Bathroom**: Keep toiletries, skincare, medication, and towels on the vanity top or a waist‑height shelf. Useful accessories include [[entities/over_the_door_caddy]] and [[entities/tension_rod_shelf]].
- **Bedroom**: Store most‑worn clothes, pajamas, and accessories in mid‑drawer compartments or on a low shelf. Options: fabric bins, rolling drawers.
- **Entryway**: Locate keys, wallet, shoes, and everyday grab‑items on a [[entities/narrow_console_table]] or freestanding shoe rack like [[entities/freestanding_shoe_rack]].

## Complementary Concepts
- **[[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]]** – Offers adhesive hooks, tension‑rod shelves, and other tools that enable the Easy‑Reach Zone without permanent installation.
- **[[concepts/one-trip_caddy]]** – Encourages portable, waist‑height containers that can be moved between rooms while staying within the Easy‑Reach Zone.

## Benefits
- Reduces physical strain and risk of falls.
- Increases efficiency of daily routines.
- Supports recovery from injuries that limit bending or climbing.
