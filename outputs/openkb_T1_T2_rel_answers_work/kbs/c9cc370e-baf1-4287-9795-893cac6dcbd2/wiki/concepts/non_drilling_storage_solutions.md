# Non‑Drilling Storage Solutions

## Definition
Non‑drilling storage solutions are ways to organize home items without using screws, nails, or permanent mounts. They rely on adhesive products, freestanding units, and portable containers placed within the [[concepts/easy-reach_zone]].

## Core Techniques (from [[summaries/session-36_2025-11-11]])
- **Stick‑on hooks and adhesive shelves** – simple tapes or suction‑based mounts for lightweight items.
- **Over‑the‑door caddies** – hang on door backs without hardware. Example: [[entities/over_the_door_caddy]].
- **Tension‑rod shelves** – pressure‑fit rods that support shallow trays, useful in bathrooms and closets. See [[entities/tension_rod_shelf]].
- **Freestanding shoe racks** – stand on the floor and provide organized storage without wall attachment. [[entities/freestanding_shoe_rack]].
- **Narrow console tables** – low‑profile tables placed at waist height for quick drop‑off zones. [[entities/narrow_console_table]].
- **Plastic shower caddies** – sturdy, wide‑based bins with a single handle; can sit on counters or shelves. [[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]].
- **Divided cleaning caddies** – small, compartmentalized organizers for tools and supplies. [[entities/divided_cleaning_caddy]].
- **Compact craft‑tool tote** – lightweight tote used as a portable kitchen or bathroom caddy. [[entities/compact_craft_tool_tote]].

## Benefits
- **No damage to walls or furniture** – ideal for renters or temporary setups.
- **Easy re‑configuration** – units can be moved as needs change.
- **Supports the [[concepts/one-trip_caddy]] approach** by keeping essential items in portable containers.

## When to Use
Apply these solutions in any room where the [[concepts/easy-reach_zone]] is being established: kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and entryway.
