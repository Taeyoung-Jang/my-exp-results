# Time Suggestion

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A strategy for offering concrete temporal options (e.g., day, weekend, specific time slots) to streamline scheduling while maintaining politeness.

---

## Core Elements
1. **Relative Temporal References** – Using phrases like "first weekend next month" or "Saturday of the first weekend" to avoid exact dates that may be error‑prone.
2. **Specific Time Slots** – Providing a small set of clear options (e.g., "11 am or 1 pm") to reduce back‑and‑forth negotiation.
3. **Contextual Constraints** – Aligning the suggestion with external factors such as project deadlines or weather considerations, as seen in the home‑renovation scenario.
4. **Politeness & Warmth** – Framing the suggestion within courteous language to preserve the relationship [[concepts/politeness_in_message]].

## Best Practices
- **Limit options** to 2‑3 times to avoid overwhelming the recipient.
- **Use relative dates** when exact dates may change or be uncertain.
- **Match tone** to the overall message style; a formal note may use "late morning or early afternoon," whereas a casual note might say "around 11 or 1."
- **Tie the suggestion** to a clear reason or constraint, reinforcing the necessity of the proposed timing.
