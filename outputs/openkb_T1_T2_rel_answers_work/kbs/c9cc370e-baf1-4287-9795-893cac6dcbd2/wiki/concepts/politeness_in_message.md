# Politeness in Message

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Politeness in Message refers to the set of linguistic and pragmatic strategies used to convey respect, consideration, and a willingness to maintain a positive relationship while delivering a request, apology, or change in plans.

---

## Core Elements
1. **Apology or Acknowledgment** – Recognize the inconvenience caused to the recipient.
2. **Clear Explanation** – Briefly state the reason for the change (e.g., a home renovation deadline before bad weather).
3. **Warm Tone** – Use friendly, personable language rather than overly formal or rigid phrasing.
4. **Concrete Alternative** – Offer a specific, workable suggestion (e.g., "Saturday of the first weekend next month, around 11 am or 1 pm").
5. **Closing Appreciation** – Express eagerness to meet and gratitude for the recipient’s flexibility.

---

## Relation to Other Concepts
- The choice of **tone** (casual, formal, concise) interacts with politeness; see [[concepts/message_tone_variation]] for how tone adjustments affect perceived politeness.
- Providing a **time suggestion** with clear options helps convey respect for the recipient’s schedule; see [[concepts/time_suggestion]].

## Example from Source Document
In the session **"session-03_2025-10-28"**, the assistant crafted a polite, warm message:

> "I just wanted to let you know that I’m tied up with the renovation through the end of the month, since I need to get everything finished before the weather turns. I’d still really love to see you. Would Saturday of the first weekend next month work for you, maybe around 11 am or 1 pm?"

This example demonstrates all core elements: an apology/acknowledgment, a brief explanation, a warm tone, a concrete time suggestion, and a friendly closing.

---

## Practical Tips
- **Keep it brief** but include all essential components.
- **Personalize** the language to reflect the relationship (e.g., "I’d really love to see you").
- **Avoid exact dates** if uncertain; use relative references like "first weekend next month" to reduce errors.
- **Offer multiple time options** to show flexibility.
