# Compact Craft‑Tool Tote

A compact, low‑base plastic tote featuring a single sturdy handle. It is lightweight, about the size of a lunchbox, and designed to stay stable when carried with one hand.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-36_2025-11-11]]
- Recommended as a **stable one‑handed caddy** for the kitchen in the "[[concepts/one-trip_caddy]]" strategy.
- Fits within the **"[[concepts/easy-reach_zone]]"** (knee‑to‑shoulder height) when placed on a waist‑level countertop corner.
- Ideal for holding everyday cooking essentials such as oil, salt, pepper, a spatula, and kitchen shears.
- Supports **non‑drilling storage** solutions because it can sit on existing surfaces without installation, complementing ideas like [[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]].

## Related Entities
- Similar alternatives mentioned: [[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]], [[entities/divided_cleaning_caddy]].

## Usage Tips
1. Place the tote on a waist‑height surface (e.g., a narrow console table or countertop edge) to stay within the easy‑reach zone.
2. Keep the contents light and balanced; heavier items should go to the bottom of the tote.
3. Pair with other **non‑drilling storage** tools like stick‑on hooks or adhesive shelves for a fully drill‑free setup.
