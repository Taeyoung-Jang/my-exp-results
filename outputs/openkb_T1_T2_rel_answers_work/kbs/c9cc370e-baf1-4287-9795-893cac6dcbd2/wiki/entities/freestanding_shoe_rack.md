# Freestanding Shoe Rack

A freestanding shoe rack is a lightweight, floor‑standing storage unit designed to hold everyday footwear at waist height. It requires **no drilling or permanent installation**, making it ideal for quick, non‑invasive home re‑organization.

## Key Points from Session 36
- Recommended for the **entryway** to keep shoes within the [[concepts/easy-reach_zone]] so the user does not need to bend or reach up.
- Fits the **one‑trip caddy** strategy: it can serve as a stable base for a small grab‑and‑go basket that holds keys, wallet, and other essentials.
- Aligns with [[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]] such as over‑the‑door caddies, tension‑rod shelves, and adhesive hooks that were suggested elsewhere in the same session.
- Works well alongside other suggested products like a [[entities/narrow_console_table]] for a nearby drop‑zone, or a [[entities/over_the_door_caddy]] for additional organization.

## Related Items Mentioned in the Same Session
- **Plastic Shower Caddy** – [[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]]: another low‑profile, non‑drilling storage option for the bathroom.
- **Tension Rod Shelf** – [[entities/tension_rod_shelf]]: provides extra mid‑height storage without hardware.

## Placement Advice
- Position the rack on a flat surface at **waist height** near the door, preferably next to a **narrow console table** for a convenient surface to drop off items.
- Keep the rack stable by placing it on even flooring and avoiding over‑loading with heavy boots or equipment.
