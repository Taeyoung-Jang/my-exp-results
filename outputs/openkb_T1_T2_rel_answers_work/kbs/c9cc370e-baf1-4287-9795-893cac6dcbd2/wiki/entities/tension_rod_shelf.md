# Tension‑Rod Shelf

A **tension‑rod shelf** is a lightweight, adjustable shelving unit that fits between two walls or within a door frame without any drilling. It typically consists of a telescoping metal rod and removable shelf panels that can be raised or lowered to the desired height.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-11-11]]
- Recommended as a **non‑drilling storage solution** for the bathroom and other rooms where permanent installation is not possible.  
- Ideal for placing everyday items (toiletries, skincare, meds) within the **easy‑reach zone** (knee‑to‑shoulder height) to reduce bending or stretching, especially useful while using a walking boot.
- Can be positioned on a **waist‑height surface** or directly inside a door frame, keeping essential items accessible without permanent fixtures.
- Works well alongside other inexpensive organizers such as [[entities/over_the_door_caddy]] and stick‑on hooks.

## Benefits
- **No Tools Required** – Installs via tension alone, preserving walls and avoiding damage.
- **Adjustable Height** – Easily moved up or down to match the user’s preferred reach zone.
- **Portable** – Can be taken down and re‑installed elsewhere if room layouts change.
- **Cost‑Effective** – Typically inexpensive and reusable.

## Suggested Uses
- **Bathroom**: Store daily toiletries, medication, and towels on a mid‑height shelf to keep them within the [[concepts/easy-reach_zone]].
- **Bedroom**: Hold accessories or small clothing items on a tension‑rod installed across a closet rod.
- **Entryway**: Create a temporary catch‑all for keys and mail when a permanent console is not feasible.

## Placement Tips
1. Measure the width of the space and adjust the rod to a snug fit.
2. Position the shelf at **waist height** (roughly 90‑100 cm from the floor) for optimal ergonomics.
3. Keep heavier items near the back or on a sturdy base to maintain stability.
