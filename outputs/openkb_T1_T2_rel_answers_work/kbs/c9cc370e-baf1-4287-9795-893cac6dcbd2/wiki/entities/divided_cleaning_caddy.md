# Divided Cleaning Caddy

**Type:** Product

The **Divided Cleaning Caddy** is a low‑profile, wide‑based plastic organizer with multiple compartments. It is highlighted in [[summaries/session-36_2025-11-11]] as a cheap, stable option for the **[[concepts/easy-reach_zone]]** and the **[[concepts/one-trip_caddy]]** strategy.

## Key Features
- **Stable one‑handed use:** Low centre of gravity and a single central handle keep it steady when carried with one hand, matching the advice for a walking boot.
- **Compartmentalized layout:** Separate sections let you keep small items (sponges, scrub brushes, sprays) organized without creating a top‑heavy load.
- **No‑drill installation:** Can be placed directly on countertops or waist‑height shelves, aligning with the [[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]] approach.
- **Size:** Roughly the size of a lunchbox, making it easy to slide onto a counter corner or a mid‑height shelf.

## Recommended Placement
- **Kitchen:** Position on a waist‑height corner of the counter where the caddy is within the **[[concepts/easy-reach_zone]]**. It serves as the kitchen’s **[[concepts/one-trip_caddy]]** for oil, salt, pepper, a spatula, and kitchen shears.
- **Other rooms:** Though primarily suggested for the kitchen, it can be repurposed in any room where a lightweight, compartmentalized carrier is needed.

## Related Products
- **[[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]]** – another low, sturdy caddy often used in bathrooms.
- **[[entities/compact_craft_tool_tote]]** – a similarly sized tote suitable for crafts or kitchen tools.

## Usage Tips (Do/Don’t)
- **Do** keep heavier items (e.g., bulk spray bottles) in the bottom compartments.
- **Don’t** overload the caddy or stack tall items that shift the centre of gravity.

By integrating the Divided Cleaning Caddy into the easy‑reach system, you reduce bending and stretching while maintaining a tidy, tool‑free environment.
