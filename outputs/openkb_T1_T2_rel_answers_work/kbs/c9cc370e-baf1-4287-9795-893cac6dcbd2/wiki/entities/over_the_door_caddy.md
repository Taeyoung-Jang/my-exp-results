# Over‑the‑Door Caddy

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A hanging organizer that attaches to the back of a door using tension rods or hooks, providing quick‑access storage without drilling.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-11-11]]**
- Recommended as a cheap, non‑drilling storage option for the bathroom.
- Helps keep daily toiletries and small items within the [[concepts/easy-reach_zone]].
- Supports the [[concepts/one-trip_caddy]] approach by allowing a portable set of essentials to stay in a fixed, waist‑height spot.
- Often paired with other lightweight organizers such as [[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]] or [[entities/tension_rod_shelf]].

**Typical uses**
- Storing toothbrushes, razors, travel‑size shampoos, and medication.
- Placing on a mid‑height bathroom shelf or directly on the door at waist height.

**Advantages**
- No tools or drilling required, aligning with [[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]].
- Easy to install, move, or remove.
