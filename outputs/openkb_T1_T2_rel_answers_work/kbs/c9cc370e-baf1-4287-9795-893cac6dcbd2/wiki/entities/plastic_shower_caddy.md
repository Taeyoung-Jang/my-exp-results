# Plastic Shower Caddy

**Type:** product

## Overview
The **plastic shower caddy** is a compact, lightweight organizer made of sturdy plastic. It features a flat base, a single central handle, and low side walls that keep items secure while remaining easy to lift with one hand.

## Key Features
- Low, wide footprint for stability
- Single, ergonomically‑shaped handle
- Made of durable, water‑resistant plastic
- Open grid or slots for quick access to small items
- Typically the size of a lunchbox, keeping weight low

## Recommended Uses in the Home
- Serves as a **[[concepts/one-trip_caddy]]** in the kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and entryway
- Perfect for placing in the **[[concepts/easy-reach_zone]]** (knee‑to‑shoulder height) to avoid bending or stretching
- Works well with **[[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]]** such as adhesive hooks, tension‑rod shelves, and over‑the‑door organizers

## Pairings with Other Entities
- Can be combined with **[[entities/over_the_door_caddy]]** for additional bathroom storage
- Complements **[[entities/divided_cleaning_caddy]]** when a layered cleaning setup is needed
- Works alongside **[[entities/compact_craft_tool_tote]]** for a cohesive kitchen or craft area organization

## Placement Advice
- Position on a waist‑height surface (counter corner, vanity top, narrow console table) to stay within the easy‑reach zone
- Ideal spot: a **[[entities/narrow_console_table]]** near the entry door for quick grab‑and‑go
