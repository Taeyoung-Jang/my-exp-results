# Summary of Session 36 (2025‑11‑11)

## Core Idea  
Introduce an **easy‑reach zone** — the space between knee and shoulder height — to reduce bending, stretching, and clutter, especially while using a walking boot. [[concepts/easy-reach_zone]]

## Room‑by‑Room Checklist  
- **Kitchen**: Move dishes, glasses, mugs, utensils, spices, and daily pans to mid‑height shelves. Cheap helpers: countertop turntables, under‑shelf hanging baskets, stick‑on hooks.  
- **Bathroom**: Keep daily toiletries, skincare, medication, and towels in the same zone. Use [[entities/over_the_door_caddy]], [[entities/tension_rod_shelf]], and small countertop drawers.  
- **Bedroom**: Store most‑worn clothes, pajamas, and everyday accessories at mid‑drawer height or on an easy‑reach shelf. Options: fabric bins, under‑bed rolling drawers, hanging closet organizers.  
- **Entryway**: Place keys, bags, shoes, and other grab‑items at waist height. Solutions include [[entities/freestanding_shoe_rack]], stick‑on key hooks, and [[entities/narrow_console_table]].

## “One‑Trip” Caddy Strategy  
Create a lightweight, portable caddy for each room to hold the essentials you frequently move between spaces. [[concepts/one-trip_caddy]]

- **Kitchen caddy**: oil, salt, pepper, spatula, kitchen shears. Position on a waist‑level counter corner.  
- **Bathroom tote**: toothpaste, face wash, moisturizer, hairbrush. Keep on the vanity top or a mid‑height shelf.  
- **Bedroom basket**: glasses, lip balm, charger, meds. Place on a dresser or nightstand at waist height.  
- **Entryway drop‑zone**: keys, wallet, earbuds, sunglasses. Sit on a small console table near the door.

## Stable One‑Handed Caddy Recommendations  
- Low, wide base with a single central handle.  
- Materials: small plastic shower caddy, compact craft‑tool tote, soft‑sided fabric tote with firm bottom insert.  
- Size: roughly that of a lunchbox.

| Room | Options |
|------|----------|
| Kitchen | 1. [[entities/plastic_shower_caddy]] with molded handle 2. [[entities/compact_craft_tool_tote]] 3. [[entities/divided_cleaning_caddy]] |
| Bathroom | 1. Narrow shower caddy with flat base 2. Small makeup‑organizer tote 3. Soft toiletry bin with insert |
| Bedroom | 1. Handled fabric tote 2. Diaper‑caddy style organizer 3. Shallow plastic bin with central handle |
| Entryway | 1. Handled mail‑sorter caddy 2. Mini tool tote 3. Handbag‑style organizer |

Placement for all caddies: on waist‑height surfaces (counter corners, vanity tops, dressers, narrow console tables) to avoid reaching up or down.

## Do / Don’t List for Caddy Use  
**Do**: keep heavier items at the bottom, limit contents to essentials, inspect regularly and remove excess.  
**Don’t**: stack tall bottles, add bulky or breakable items, rely on a single skinny handle point for all weight.

## Non‑Drilling Storage Ideas  
- Stick‑on hooks & adhesive shelves.  
- Over‑the‑door racks & tension rods.  
- Freestanding racks and rolling drawers. [[concepts/non_drilling_storage_solutions]]
