# Miscommunication Prevention

**Definition**: Strategies and habits that help a team spot, clarify, and resolve misunderstandings before they become friction points.

## Why it matters
In a small, long‑standing team, even brief lapses can create tension if left unchecked. The session‑01_2024‑06‑15 conversation highlights that unchecked confusion often stems from:
- Assuming shared context without verification.
- Over‑ or under‑communicating, which either buries the core point or sounds abrupt.
- Letting small misunderstandings sit too long.

## Core Practices (from [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]])
1. **Ask clarifying questions early** – When something feels ambiguous, pose a simple question instead of guessing. 
2. **Reset the conversation** – Use phrasing like “Hold up, I think we might be talking about different parts of this—can we reset for a sec?” to pause and realign.
3. **State the shared understanding** – Summarize your interpretation and invite confirmation: “Just to make sure we’re aligned, here’s how I’m understanding it… does that match everyone else?”
4. **Balance brevity with warmth** – Provide enough context to be clear without sounding blunt; add a friendly tone cue (e.g., “Thanks” or “When you get a moment”). This links to [[concepts/clarity-with-warmth]] and [[concepts/contextual_clarity]].
5. **Address issues promptly** – Don’t let a minor miscommunication linger; a quick “Heads up, I’m seeing a possible snag…” helps keep the team on the same page.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/clarity-with-warmth]] – The practice of coupling clear requests with a warm tone to reduce perceived abruptness.
- [[concepts/contextual_clarity]] – Providing just enough context so messages are understood without overload.
