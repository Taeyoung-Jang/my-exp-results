# Natural Introduction

**Definition:**  
A friendly, low‑pressure approach to meeting new colleagues by sharing a brief, non‑work detail and asking about their role or work style.

**Key Practices**
- Treat the interaction as a quick check‑in rather than a performance.
- Offer a light personal tidbit that is easy to remember but not overly rehearsed.
- Follow up with open‑ended questions such as “What’s your preferred way of collaborating?” or “What does smooth collaboration look like from your side?”
- Show active listening: remember their name, reference something they said later, and keep tone aligned with the environment.

**Examples from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]]**
- General intro: “Hey, I’m [Name]. I just joined the team this week and I’m excited to get settled in. I’ll be working on [area]. Nice to meet you.”
- Manager: “I’m really looking forward to working with you… What would you like me to focus on first?”
- Peer: “I’ve heard I’ll be partnering with you often… What’s your preferred way of collaborating?”
- Cross‑team: “I’m still getting a feel for how our teams work together… What does smooth collaboration look like from your side?”

**Benefits**
- Reduces pressure on both parties, fostering genuine connection.
- Increases memorability through a personal touch and attentive follow‑up.
- Sets a collaborative tone early, lowering risk of miscommunication.
