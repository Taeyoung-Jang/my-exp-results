# Summary of Session 33 (2024‑06‑15)

**Main focus:** Practical playbooks for transit agencies to minimise rider disruption during major rail upgrades, concrete rider “Plan B” checklists, and reusable advocacy templates to pressure agencies for better service.

---

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/public-transit-disruption-management]]** – Strategies agencies can employ to limit downtime and keep riders informed.  
- **[[concepts/real-time-communication]]** – Frequency, format, and placement of updates during unpredictable events.  
- **[[concepts/rider-contingency-planning]]** – Decision‑tree thresholds, apps, backup routes, and packing essentials for riders.  
- **Gate closure playbook** – Step‑by‑step cadence (minute‑0, minute‑3, every 5 min) with announcement and signage templates.  
- **Service update cadence** – Recommended five‑minute refresh cycle and explicit “awaiting next estimate” messaging.  

---

## Agency Recommendations
1. **Work‑window planning** – Night/weekend closures, single‑tracking, bundled upgrades.  
2. **Replacement service** – Frequent, clearly signed [[entities/shuttle-bus-service]].  
3. **Communication protocol** –  
   - Deploy 2‑3 staff at gate the moment metering begins.  
   - Announce reason, first‑update time, and repeat every 5 min.  
   - Whiteboard/printed sheet at gate and near turnstiles with current pattern.  
   - Digital displays refresh every 2 min; show “Awaiting next estimate” when data is stale.  
4. **Templates** –  
   - **Unknown ETA:** “Service is temporarily paused … We do not have an estimated arrival time yet. Next update at __.”  
   - **Shuttle buses:** “Train service is suspended between __. Shuttle buses run every few minutes from __.”  
   - **Single‑tracking:** “Trains are sharing one track … Expect 15‑20 min headways. Allow extra travel time.”  

---

## Rider “Plan B” Checklist
- **Pre‑trip:** Enable push notifications on your preferred [[entities/transit-app]]; set a personal bail‑out threshold (e.g., >10 min delay).  
- **Decision tree:**  
  - *Flexible job (≤5 min late):* Wait one delay cycle; if delay >10‑15 min, switch.  
  - *Zero‑tolerance job:* Skip train if any *major delay* or *service paused* notice appears.  
- **Backup route:** Have one alternative (bus, [[entities/bike-share]], or parallel line) tried at least once; store in app.  
- **Essentials:** Charged phone, headphones, transit card with buffer balance, logged‑in [[entities/bike-share]]/[[entities/ride-hail]] app.  
- **On‑site actions:** When threshold reached, leave station immediately, follow staff directions to shuttle or bike‑share.

## Advocacy Documentation Template
```
Date and time: 
Station: 
Issue observed: 
What was missing or not working: 
How long the problem lasted: 
Impact on riders: 
Photo taken: yes/no 
311 or agency report filed: case number ___
```
Collect timestamped photos and log entries daily.

---

## Weekly Summary Templates
### 1. Short 311/Agency Complaint (character‑limit friendly)
```
Repeated issues at [station] this week: gate closures without staff, ETA screens stuck, missing shuttle signs. Occurred on [dates]. Caused crowding and confusion. Case numbers/photos available.
```
### 2. Longer Email to Council/Transit Board
```
Subject: Persistent service‑management problems at [station]

Hi [Name],
I’m writing to share a weekly summary of recurring problems at [station] during the current construction period. On [dates], riders experienced unstaffed gate closures, frozen real‑time screens, and missing or unclear shuttle‑bus signage. These issues created unsafe crowding and left riders without reliable instructions.

**Ask list:**
- Staff at gates whenever metering begins.
- Five‑minute update cadence announced at entrance and on platform.
- Accurate shuttle‑bus signage at gate and street level.
- Real‑time screens that explicitly state when estimates are unknown.
- Updated whiteboard/printed sheet reflecting service changes.

**Case numbers:**
[Paste case numbers]

**Photos:**
[Attach or link]

Thank you for your attention. I’m happy to provide more detail.
```
