# Transit Board

The **Transit Board** is the regional authority that governs and oversees public transit agencies, sets service standards, and allocates funding for infrastructure projects.  It is the primary decision‑making entity that can implement policies such as staff deployment at station gates, real‑time update cadences, and shuttle‑bus signage requirements.

## Role in session‑33 (2024‑06‑15)
- Receives weekly rider‑generated summaries and complaints about service disruptions (see [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]). 
- Can mandate improvements in [[concepts/public-transit-disruption-management]] such as:
  - Staffing gates during metering events.
  - Enforcing a five‑minute update cadence across all communication channels.
  - Requiring clear, posted information for [[entities/shuttle-bus-service]] and single‑tracking operations.
- Works alongside the [[entities/city-council]] and other oversight bodies to ensure that the transit agency’s actions align with broader city policy.
- Responds to escalations from the [[entities/rider-union]] and public complaints filed through [[entities/311]].

## Interaction with Riders
- Riders are encouraged to use a [[entities/transit-app]] for real‑time alerts, set push notifications, and follow the recommended [[concepts/rider-contingency-planning]] checklists.
- Advocacy groups often direct riders to contact the Transit Board with documented evidence of gate closures, stale ETAs, and missing signage.
