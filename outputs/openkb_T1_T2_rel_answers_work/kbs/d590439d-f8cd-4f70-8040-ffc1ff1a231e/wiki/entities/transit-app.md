# Transit App

**Type:** product

**Description:** A mobile application that delivers real‑time service alerts, push notifications, and alternative route planning for public‑transit riders.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]
- **Real‑time communication:** The app is recommended to display service alerts on the home screen and push notifications for specific lines, supporting the [[concepts/real-time-communication]] strategy.
- **Rider contingency planning:** Users are advised to set a personal “bail‑out threshold” (e.g., 10‑15 min delay) within the app, tying into [[concepts/rider-contingency-planning]].
- **Backup route integration:** The app should store at least one pre‑tested alternate route—such as a frequent bus, a [[entities/bike-share]] option, or a parallel rail line—so riders can switch quickly.
- **Essential preparation:** The checklist suggests keeping the phone charged, headphones ready, and the transit card loaded, all managed via the app.
- **Advocacy support:** Riders can use the app to quickly capture screenshots of stale ETAs or missing signage for documentation when filing reports with [[entities/311]] or communicating with the [[entities/transit-board]].
