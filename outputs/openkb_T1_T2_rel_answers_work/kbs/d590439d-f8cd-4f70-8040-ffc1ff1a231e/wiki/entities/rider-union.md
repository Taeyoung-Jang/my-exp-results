# Rider Union

**Type:** organization

**Description:** Rider Union is a collective of transit riders that advocates for reliable service, clear communication, and equitable treatment during construction and other disruptions.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]**
- The document suggests that the Union could help amplify rider‑generated evidence (photos, timestamps, case numbers) when submitting complaints to [[entities/311]] or communicating with the [[entities/city-council]] and [[entities/transit-board]].
- Rider Union can coordinate with other groups such as [[entities/rider-union]] and share templates for weekly summaries and short 311 complaints.
- The Union may also promote the use of [[entities/transit-app]] for real‑time alerts and encourage riders to adopt a structured [[concepts/rider-contingency-planning]] approach.
