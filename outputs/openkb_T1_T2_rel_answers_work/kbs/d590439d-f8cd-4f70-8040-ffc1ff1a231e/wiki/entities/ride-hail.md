# Ride‑Hail

Ride‑Hail is an on‑demand car service product that riders can summon via a mobile app. In the context of **session‑33_2024‑06‑15**, it is highlighted as one of the alternative travel options commuters should have ready when public‑transit service is disrupted.

## Key Facts from Session 33
- The assistant recommends that riders keep a **ride‑hail app** logged in alongside bike‑share and transit‑app tools as part of a **Plan B** contingency.
- Ride‑hail is presented as a “quick pivot” option for riders who need to leave the station immediately once a delay exceeds their personal bail‑out threshold.
- It is included in the suggested checklist of essential items: a charged phone and ready‑to‑use ride‑hail app.
