# Bike‑Share

**Type:** product

Bike‑Share refers to a city‑wide network of short‑term rental bicycles that riders can unlock via a mobile app or station kiosk. In the context of [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]], bike‑share is highlighted as a key component of a rider’s **Plan B** contingency plan during rail construction disruptions.

## Role in Rider Contingency Planning
- Listed as one of the recommended backup modes in the **rider plan B checklist** (see [[concepts/rider-contingency-planning]]).  
- Riders are advised to have the bike‑share app logged in and a charged phone ready so they can switch to cycling for the last‑mile when gates are closed or delays exceed personal thresholds.
- Suggested as an alternative when shuttle‑bus signage is missing or real‑time updates are stale.

## Interaction with Transit Services
- Works alongside **[[entities/transit-app]]** for route planning and availability checks.
- Can be coordinated with **[[entities/shuttle-bus-service]]** to provide a seamless multimodal experience: e.g., a shuttle drops riders near a bike‑share dock.
- Advocacy efforts may involve reporting gaps in bike‑share integration to **[[entities/311]]**, the **[[entities/city-council]]**, or the **[[entities/transit-board]]**.

## Benefits Highlighted in the Document
- Provides a quick, reliable fallback when rail service is paused.
- Reduces crowding at stations by diverting some riders to bicycles.
- Enhances overall resilience of the public‑transport network, supporting the goals of [[concepts/public-transit-disruption-management]].

## Suggested Improvements
- Ensure real‑time bike‑share dock availability is displayed on station digital screens, aligning with the recommended [[concepts/real-time-communication]] cadence.
- Publicize bike‑share options more prominently in agency signage during construction periods.
