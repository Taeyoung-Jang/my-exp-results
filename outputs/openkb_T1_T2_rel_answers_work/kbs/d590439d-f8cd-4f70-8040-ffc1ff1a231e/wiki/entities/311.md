# 311

**Type:** Organization (city government non‑emergency service)

**Brief description:** 311 is a municipal contact center that receives resident complaints, service requests, and information inquiries, often providing case numbers for follow‑up with city departments.

## Role in transit‑disruption context
During the rail‑upgrade disruptions described in [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]], riders were advised to log each observed problem (gate closures, stale ETAs, missing signage) through the 311 system. 311 creates a formal case number, which can be compiled into weekly summaries for escalation to the [[entities/city-council]], [[entities/transit-board]], or local rider advocacy groups.

## Key facts from the session‑33 document
- Riders should file a 311 report for every incident, capturing date, time, location, description, and photo evidence.
- The report generates a case number that strengthens the evidence base for agency accountability.
- Weekly aggregation of 311 case numbers and photos is recommended for email outreach to the [[entities/city-council]] and the [[entities/transit-board]].
