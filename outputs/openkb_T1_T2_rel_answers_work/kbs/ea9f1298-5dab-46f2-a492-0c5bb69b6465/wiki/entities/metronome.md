## Metronome

Metronome is a **product** that generates regular, audible beats to help musicians maintain a consistent tempo while practicing. In the context of guitar learning, it is introduced after a player has solidified chord shapes and smooth transitions.

### Role in session-08_2025-04-20
- Recommended to be **re‑introduced** once a guitarist can execute a chord change cleanly about ten times in a row.
- Suggested initial setting: change chords **every two clicks**, then progress to every click before adding a strumming pattern.
- Used as a tool for moving from **accuracy → consistency → speed**, reinforcing steady timing without exposing underlying technique flaws.
