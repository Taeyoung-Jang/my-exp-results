# C Major Chord (work)

**Type:** work

**Brief description:** A basic major triad on the guitar, consisting of the notes C, E, and G.

## Role in session-08_2025-04-20

In the conversation, the C Major chord is part of the G ↔ C transition drill. The advice recommends using a *pivot finger* on the B‑string 3rd fret to minimise hand movement when switching between **G Major** and **C Major**. Practitioners are instructed to:
- practice the shape without a metronome until it feels comfortable,
- perform a slow back‑and‑forth drill (about 15 minutes per session),
- aim for whole‑shape placement rather than one‑finger‑at‑a‑time.

These steps follow the [[concepts/shape-first-tempo-later]] approach and the [[concepts/whole-shape-placement]] principle, while also applying [[concepts/tension-management-guitar]] to keep the hand relaxed.
