## Brix JarKey

Brix JarKey is a renter‑friendly, adhesive‑mounted, V‑shaped jar opener designed for **one‑handed** operation.  It attaches to the underside of a cabinet with a peel‑off adhesive that removes cleanly, making it suitable for apartments and other temporary living spaces.

### Key Features
- **Adhesive Mounting:** Uses a removable adhesive pad that does not damage cabinet finishes when removed.
- **V‑Shape Teeth:** The V‑shaped groove with small teeth grips jar lids, allowing the user to twist the jar using only the functional hand.
- **One‑Handed Use:** Once positioned, the jar can be slid into the opener and turned without needing the injured hand for stabilization.
- **Placement Tips:** Position the opener under the front edge of a cabinet, close to the countertop edge, so the jar can be guided in and twisted without reaching back.
- **Removal:** Warm the adhesive with a low‑heat hair dryer for ~20 seconds, then peel off slowly.  Any residue can be loosened with a dab of cooking oil and wiped clean with mild soap.

### Comparison
The Brix JarKey works similarly to the **EZ Off Jar Opener** but may require a slightly more precise bite on the lid.  Users reporting tall or heavy jars often find the EZ Off a bit more forgiving, while the Brix still provides reliable grip when positioned correctly.
