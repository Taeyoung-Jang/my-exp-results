# Adhesive Under‑Cabinet Jar Opener

**Definition**: A non‑permanent, adhesive‑mounted V‑shaped device that grips a jar lid, allowing the user to twist the jar using only the functional hand.

## Key Characteristics
- **Removable adhesive**: Uses a peel‑off adhesive pad that leaves no residue on cabinet surfaces, making it suitable for renters.
- **V‑shaped teeth**: The interior teeth bite into the lid, providing sufficient grip to overcome tight seals without requiring finger strength.
- **One‑handed operation**: The jar is slid into the opener with the good hand, the opener holds the lid, and the user twists the jar while the other (injured) hand remains uninvolved.
- **Placement tips**: Install the opener just under the front edge of a cabinet, near the counter edge, so the jar can be accessed comfortably with a single hand.

## Practical Use Cases (from [[summaries/session-32_2025-06-01]])
- Opening tightly sealed sauce or pickle jars while a left hand is immobilized.
- Managing tall or heavy jars where a wall‑mounted opener isn’t feasible.
- Providing a consistent alternative to the tap‑the‑lid or hot‑water methods, which involve more force or uncertainty.

## Maintenance & Removal
1. **Warm the adhesive** with a low‑medium hair dryer for ~20 seconds.
2. **Peel slowly** to avoid pulling paint or finish.
3. **Clean residue** using a dab of cooking oil on a cloth, followed by mild soap rinse.
