# Summary of Session 40 (2025‑02‑13)

**Main contribution:** A concise, printable moving‑week plan that covers packing categories, a timeline, a simple color‑coded labeling system, clothing rules of thumb, and a “use‑up” food strategy—including freezer and condiment handling.

## Packing Checklist
- **Suitcase (daily‑life kit)** – 5‑7 mix‑and‑match outfits, underwear/socks, sleepwear, light jacket, one work outfit, toiletries, meds, first‑aid, laptop & chargers, important docs, towel, comfort item.  
- **Essentials Box** – Basic dishes & utensils, one pot/pan, dish soap, paper towels, trash bags, lightbulbs, extension cord, small toolkit, toilet paper, shower‑curtain (if needed), basic toiletries, bedding set.  
- **Open‑Last Boxes** – Décor, books, extra bedding/towels, serving/baking gear, off‑season clothes, hobby supplies, bulky “settle‑in‑later” items.  
- **Load‑Early Boxes** – Archived papers, rarely used appliances, tools, cleaning supplies, extra cables, storage bins, board games, spare blankets, duplicates.  

These categories follow the [[concepts/packing-checklist-strategy]].

## Timeline (5‑day lead‑up)
1. **5 days out** – Pack all “load‑early” items, start stacking boxes.  
2. **3‑4 days out** – Finish “open‑last” boxes, break down furniture.  
3. **2 days out** – Reduce home to suitcase items and immediate‑need items.  
4. **Day before** – Zip suitcases, stage boxes, unplug/defrost.  
5. **Move‑out day** – Load truck: early items first, then open‑last, keep suitcases & essentials box within arm’s reach.  

The timeline aligns with the [[concepts/move-timeline-planning]].

## Labeling System
Use **colored tape** plus a simple number code (e.g., *Green‑3*). Suggested colors:  
- Green = Kitchen  
- Blue = Bedding  
- Yellow = Bathroom  
- Red = Fragile/Important  

Keep a phone note mapping colors to categories; boxes stay anonymous to others.

## Clothing Rules of Thumb
Pack layers and neutrals for weather flexibility. Suggested mix: 3 everyday outfits, 1 work outfit, 1 comfy outfit, and 1 warm/light top layer. Choose wrinkle‑resistant fabrics that can be reworn.

## Food Strategy – “Use‑Up Sprint”
Prioritize perishable fridge/freezer items for quick meals (stir‑fries, pasta, eggs, soups, burrito bowls). Avoid restocking bulk items; buy only small, finish‑quick portions if needed. Keep a minimal **bridge pantry** for after move‑out: oatmeal packets, granola bars, nuts, peanut butter, instant noodles, shelf‑stable soup. This follows the [[concepts/food-use-up-plan]].

## Freezer & Condiments
- **Freezer:** Use up opened veg, dumplings, meat; keep unopened solid‑frozen items only if you can move them directly to a new freezer. Otherwise discard or give away.  
- **Condiments:** Keep everyday sauces (soy, hot, mustard, mayo). Seal each bottle in a plastic bag, stand upright in a small tote. Toss or gift rarely used or near‑empty bottles. Clean fridge/freezer 24 hours before move‑out to avoid leaks and sticky residues.
