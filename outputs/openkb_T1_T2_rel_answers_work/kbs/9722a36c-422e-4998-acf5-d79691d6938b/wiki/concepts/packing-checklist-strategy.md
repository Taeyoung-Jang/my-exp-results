# Packing Checklist Strategy

## Overview
A practical framework for organizing the items you need to pack when you have a short “in‑between” week between moving out and moving in. It defines four packing categories, a timeline for when to pack each group, and a minimalist color‑coded labeling system.

## Packing Categories
- **Suitcase (daily‑life kit)** – Essentials you will use every day: 5–7 mix‑and‑match outfits, underwear, sleepwear, a light jacket, one work‑appropriate outfit, toiletries, meds, first‑aid basics, laptop and chargers, important documents, a towel, and a comfort item.
- **Essentials Box** – Items required immediately after you unload: a basic dish set, one pot/pan, dish soap, paper towels, trash bags, lightbulbs, extension cord, small toolkit, toilet paper, shower curtain (if needed), basic toiletries, and a bedding set.
- **Open‑Last Boxes** – Things you can defer until you settle in: décor, books, extra bedding/towels, serving/baking gear, off‑season clothing, hobby supplies, and other bulky items.
- **Load‑Early Boxes** – Items that can be packed well before move‑out: archived papers, rarely used appliances, tools, cleaning supplies, extra cables, storage bins, board games, spare blankets, and duplicates.

## Timeline
The approach aligns with the [[concepts/move-timeline-planning]] from the source session:
1. **5 days before move‑out** – Pack all **Load‑Early** items.
2. **3‑4 days before** – Complete **Open‑Last** boxes and disassemble furniture.
3. **2 days before** – Reduce the home to only **Suitcase** items and any immediate‑need items.
4. **Day before** – Zip the suitcase, stage boxes near the exit, and unplug/defrost appliances.
5. **Move‑out day** – Load the truck in order: Load‑Early, then Open‑Last, keeping the suitcase and Essentials Box within arm’s reach.

## Labeling System
A low‑tech, color‑coded method using only marker and colored tape:
- **Green** – Kitchen items
- **Blue** – Bedding
- **Yellow** – Bathroom
- **Red** – Fragile/important
Each box receives the color plus a sequential number (e.g., *Green‑3*). Keep a phone note mapping colors to categories for quick reference.
