# Move Timeline Planning

**Definition** – A step‑by‑step, short‑term schedule that coordinates packing categories, labeling, and move‑out actions to minimise chaos during the transition week.

## Core Timeline (derived from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]])

| Day before move‑out | Main actions |
|--------------------|--------------|
| **5 days out** | • Pack all **Load‑Early** items (archived papers, rarely used appliances, tools, etc.).<br>• Stack boxes in a staging area.<br>• Begin labeling with the simple color‑code system. |
| **3‑4 days out** | • Complete **Open‑Last** boxes (decor, books, extra bedding, hobby supplies).<br>• Disassemble any furniture that can be taken apart early.<br>• Start a **use‑up food sprint** to reduce fridge load. |
| **2 days out** | • Reduce the home to only **Suitcase** items and the **Essentials Box**.<br>• Verify that all daily‑life essentials are packed and reachable.<br>• Unplug appliances, defrost freezer if needed. |
| **Day before** | • Zip and seal suitcases.<br>• Stage boxes near the exit for easy loading.<br>• Double‑check labeling and color key.
| **Move‑out day** | • Load the truck in order: **Load‑Early** → **Open‑Last** → keep **Suitcase** and **Essentials Box** at arm’s reach.<br>• Perform a quick walkthrough to ensure nothing is left behind. |

## How it connects to other concepts
- The timeline works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/packing-checklist-strategy]], which defines *what* to pack in each category.
- It incorporates a brief **food‑use‑up plan** (see [[concepts/food-use-up-plan]]) to minimise waste and avoid a fridge‑full of leftovers.
- The color‑coded labeling system referenced in the timeline can be informed by ergonomics considerations, though ergonomics is not the primary focus here.

## Practical Tips
- **Stick to the schedule:** Even a single day shift can cause a cascade of last‑minute decisions.
- **Use a printable checklist:** Pair the timeline with a checklist for each day to track completion.
- **Keep the color key handy:** A phone note or a small printed sheet speeds up box identification during loading.
