# Food Use‑Up Plan

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** A short‑term strategy to consume existing food before moving to avoid waste.

---

## Overview
The *Food Use‑Up Plan* is a focused, low‑effort approach for the week between deciding to move out and the actual move‑out day. Its goal is to clear out perishable items, minimize the need for new take‑out meals, and reduce the amount of food that must be packed or discarded.  The plan is built around three core actions:

1. **Inventory & Prioritize** – List everything in the fridge, freezer, and pantry, then flag anything that will perish within the next 7‑10 days.
2. **Meal Sprint** – Design a simple, flexible menu that uses those flagged items as the primary ingredients. Think one‑pot stir‑fries, pasta sauces, eggs, soups, and burrito‑bowl‑style mixes that can absorb a variety of veg, protein, and leftover grains.
3. **Minimal Restocking** – If a new ingredient is needed, buy only a small amount that can be finished quickly (e.g., a few fruits, a bag of greens, a rotisserie chicken). Avoid bulk or long‑shelf‑life purchases.

## Key Tactics from Session 40 (2025‑02‑13)
- **“Use‑up sprint”**: Treat the week as a sprint to finish perishable items rather than a prolonged meal plan.
- **Bridge pantry**: Keep a tiny stash of shelf‑stable foods (oatmeal packets, granola bars, nuts, instant noodles, peanut butter) for after the move, so you’re not forced to rely on takeout.
- **Freezer handling**: Use up opened frozen veg, dumplings, and meat; keep only unopened solid‑frozen items if you can move them directly to a new freezer. Otherwise, discard or give away.
- **Condiment strategy**: Retain only everyday sauces (soy, hot sauce, mustard, mayo), seal each in a plastic bag, and store upright in a small tote to avoid leaks.

## Step‑by‑Step Action List
1. **Day 1 – Inventory**
   - Write down all items in the fridge, freezer, and pantry.
   - Highlight perishable items (fresh produce, dairy, opened frozen goods, cooked leftovers).
2. **Day 2‑3 – Meal Sprint**
   - Create a flexible menu: 
     - *Stir‑fry*: any veg + frozen protein or dumplings + soy sauce.
     - *Pasta*: sauce base + leftover meat/veg + cheese.
     - *Egg dishes*: omelet, frittata, or scramble using veg and cheese.
     - *Soup/Bowl*: broth + veg + beans or rice.
   - Cook twice a day; store leftovers in reusable containers.
3. **Day 4 – Minimal Restocking**
   - If you’re low on a staple, buy a small portion (e.g., a single banana bunch, a bag of salad greens, a rotisserie chicken). 
4. **Day 5 – Freeze/Store**
   - Pack any remaining unopened frozen foods for transport only if you’ll have a new freezer immediately; otherwise, consider donation.
5. **Day 6 – Final Clean‑out**
   - Throw away anything that has gone bad or cannot be safely moved.
   - Wipe down fridge/freezer interiors.
6. **Day 7 – Bridge Pantry Prep**
   - Assemble a small “survival kit” of shelf‑stable snacks and quick‑cook items to keep in the suitcase or essentials box.

## Benefits
- **Cost‑effective:** No need for expensive takeout or last‑minute grocery runs.
- **Waste reduction:** Most perishable items are consumed or donated instead of being thrown away.
- **Simplified moving day:** Fewer boxes, less weight, and no risk of leaks from sauces or melted frozen foods.
