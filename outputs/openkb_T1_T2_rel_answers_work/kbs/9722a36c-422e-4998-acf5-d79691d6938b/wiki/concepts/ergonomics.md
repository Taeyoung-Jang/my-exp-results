# Ergonomics

**Definition**: Ergonomics is the practice of designing and arranging work environments, tools, and tasks to fit the human body, minimizing strain and maximizing comfort and efficiency.

## Core Principles
- **Posture Alignment**: Adjust chair height and screen level so the spine stays neutral, shoulders relaxed, and eyes level with the top of the monitor. This prevents hunching and neck strain.
- **Micro‑breaks**: Incorporate brief, frequent movements—standing, stretching, or shifting weight—to reduce static muscle load.
- **Equipment Positioning**: Arrange frequently used items within easy reach to avoid excessive reaching or twisting.
