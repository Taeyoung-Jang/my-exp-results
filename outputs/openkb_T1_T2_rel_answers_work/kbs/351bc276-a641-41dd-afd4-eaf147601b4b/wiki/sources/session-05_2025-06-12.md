**User:** I’m trying to get better at planning my week so I actually have time for the things I care about—do you have a good approach for mapping out a realistic weekly schedule without it feeling overly rigid?

**Assistant:** A nice middle ground is to think of your week in loose “blocks” rather than strict hour‑by‑hour plans. Start by picking just a few priorities for the week—the things that really matter to you—and decide roughly when they tend to fit best, like mornings for focused work or evenings for hobbies. It also helps to leave some open buffer time each day. That way the unexpected stuff doesn’t throw everything off, and you don’t feel boxed in. At the end of each day, do a quick check‑in and adjust the next day a little if needed. It keeps your plan alive without becoming a strict schedule.
