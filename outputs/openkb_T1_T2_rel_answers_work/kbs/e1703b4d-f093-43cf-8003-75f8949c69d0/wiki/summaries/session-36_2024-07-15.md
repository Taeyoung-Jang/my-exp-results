## Summary

The conversation outlines the essential, non‑glamorous tasks to prepare when buying, selling, or moving out of a house. It focuses on organizing paperwork, utility and system information, creating a detailed house‑info packet, and handling smart‑home devices securely.

### Core Priorities
- Consolidate **property paperwork** (deed, disclosures, warranties, permits, insurance, manuals).  
- Document **utility and system shut‑offs** (water, gas, electricity, irrigation).  
- Conduct a **systems walkthrough** (HVAC, water heater, electrical panel, detectors, gutters).  
- Record a **maintenance history** (service dates, repairs, pest control, paint colors).

### House‑Info Packet Checklist
A printable, sectioned checklist covering:
1. **House Systems and Safety**  
2. **Utilities and Services** – see [[concepts/utility-management]]  
3. **Appliances, Equipment, and Warranties**  
4. **Maintenance and Repair History** – see [[concepts/maintenance-scheduling]]  
5. **House Quirks and Helpful Notes**  
6. **Smart Home Handoff** – see [[concepts/smart-home-transfer]] (thermostat, doorbell camera, garage opener, etc.)  
7. **What You Keep** vs **What Not to Give** (logins, security codes, personal accounts).

### Fill‑in Template
Provides a one‑page layout with blanks for owners to write locations, provider names, dates, and reset steps, making the packet easy to complete and hand over.

### Smart Home Transfer
Specific sub‑section lists each smart device, indicates whether a factory reset has been performed, and records transfer instructions, ensuring the new owner can set up devices without inheriting the seller’s accounts.

### Retained Documents
Advises keeping digital copies of closing documents, receipts, permits, photos, and any non‑transferable warranties for future protection.
