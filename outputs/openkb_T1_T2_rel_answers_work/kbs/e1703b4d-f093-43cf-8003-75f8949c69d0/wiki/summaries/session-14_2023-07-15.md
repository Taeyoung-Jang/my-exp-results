# Summary of Session 14 (2023‑07‑15)

**Purpose**: Provide a practical, climate‑specific checklist and upgrade plan for homeowners to minimize water‑damage risk and avoid insurance claims. See [[concepts/homeownership-disaster-prevention]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Maintenance cadence**: Split actions into *monthly*, *seasonal* (quarterly), and *annual* tasks, focusing on gutters, alarms, roof inspection, dryer vents, and exterior walkways. See [[concepts/maintenance-scheduling]].
- **Cold‑climate focus**: Emphasize protection against ice dams, frozen pipes, and basement seepage. See [[concepts/cold-climate-water-risk-mitigation]].
- **Top 5 high‑impact upgrades**:
  1. **[[entities/attic-insulation]] & air‑sealing** – prevents warm air from escaping, reducing ice‑dam formation. *(Cost: $1‑3 k)*
  2. **[[entities/heat-trace-cable]]** on roof edges, gutters, and vulnerable plumbing – keeps critical surfaces above freezing. *(Cost: $2‑5 / ft)*
  3. **[[entities/water-leak-sensor]] + [[entities/automatic-shutoff-valve]]** – early detection and rapid water cut‑off. *(Sensors $30‑100 each; valve $400‑1,000 installed)*
  4. **[[entities/sump-pump]] with **[[entities/battery-backup]]** – protects basement during heavy snow melt or power loss. *(Cost: $300‑1,200)*
  5. **Exterior drainage & foundation sealing** – downspout extensions, re‑grading, crack sealing. *(Cost: minimal to a few hundred dollars)*
- **12‑month timeline** (quarter‑based):
  - **Q1**: Install attic insulation/air‑seal.
  - **Q2**: Upgrade exterior drainage & foundation sealing.
  - **Q3**: Add heat‑trace cables; install leak‑sensor + shut‑off system.
  - **Q4**: Install/upgrade sump pump (with backup) before winter.
- **If limited to two projects**: Prioritize attic insulation/air‑sealing and the leak‑sensor + shut‑off system, as they address the majority of ice‑dam and burst‑pipe scenarios.
- **Quick verification**:
  - *Attic*: Check for visible joists, cold drafts, or uneven insulation.
  - *Leak‑sensor need*: Identify pipes in unheated spaces (basement, crawlspace, exterior walls) or older plumbing.
