# Impact‑Rated Storm Shutters

**Type:** product

Impact‑rated storm shutters are specially engineered window coverings designed to withstand high wind pressures, flying debris, and hail. They are commonly installed on homes in regions prone to hurricanes, severe thunderstorms, or heavy snow loads.  

## Key Facts from Session 14 (2023‑07‑15)

- **Purpose**: Reduce storm‑related water and wind damage to the home envelope, thereby lowering the likelihood of insurance claims.
- **Cost range**: Approximately $100 – $500 per window, depending on material (aluminum, steel, or polycarbonate) and installation complexity.
- **Installation timing**: Recommended as a *high‑impact upgrade* for cold‑climate homes that experience occasional heavy snow and wind; can be installed any season but should be in place before the first major winter storm.
- **Risk mitigated**: Limits window breakage that can lead to interior water intrusion, structural compromise, and personal injury.
