# Heat‑Trace Cable

**Type**: Product

**Description**: An electrically powered cable that can be installed on roof edges, gutters, and exposed plumbing to keep those surfaces above freezing temperatures.

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-14_2023-07-15]]
- **Purpose**: Prevents ice‑dam formation on roofs and stops frozen‑pipe bursts, directly addressing the most common water‑damage risks in cold, snowy climates.
- **Installation timing**: Recommended in the third quarter (late summer/early fall) before winter sets in, as part of the seasonal maintenance schedule.
- **Cost**: Roughly **$2‑5 per foot installed** (materials plus labor).
- **Maintenance**: Inspect periodically for wear or damage; ensure power supply remains reliable, optionally pairing with a [[entities/battery-backup]] for critical sections.
