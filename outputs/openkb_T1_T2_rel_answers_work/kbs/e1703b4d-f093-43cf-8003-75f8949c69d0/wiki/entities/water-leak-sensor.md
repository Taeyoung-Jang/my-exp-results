## Water Leak Sensor

A **water leak sensor** is a compact, battery‑powered device that monitors for the presence of moisture in areas prone to leaks (under sinks, near water heaters, in basements, etc.). When moisture is detected, the sensor sends an alert to a smartphone or home automation hub, allowing homeowners to intervene before water spreads and causes costly damage.

### Key Facts from Session 14 (2023‑07‑15)
- **Cost range:** roughly **$30 – $100 per unit**.
- **Recommended placement:** sinks, toilets, laundry machines, water heater, and basement floor.
- **Integration:** often paired with an **[[entities/automatic-shutoff-valve]]** to automatically stop water flow when a leak is confirmed.
- **Benefit:** early detection reduces risk of flood damage, mold growth, and expensive repairs, especially in **cold‑climate** homes where frozen pipes can burst.

### Role in Cold‑Climate Water‑Risk Mitigation
In a cold climate, frozen pipes and ice dams are common sources of water intrusion. Deploying water leak sensors helps homeowners catch:
- Burst pipes caused by freezing.
- Leaks from thawing ice dams on roofs.
- Basement seepage during spring melt.

This aligns with the broader strategy outlined in [[concepts/cold-climate-water-risk-mitigation]] and supports overall [[concepts/homeownership-disaster-prevention]].

### Installation & Maintenance
1. **Mount** the sensor on a wall or base near the target area.
2. **Test** the alert function after installation.
3. **Replace batteries** annually (or as indicated by the device).
4. **Connect** to a home automation system or smartphone app for real‑time notifications.
