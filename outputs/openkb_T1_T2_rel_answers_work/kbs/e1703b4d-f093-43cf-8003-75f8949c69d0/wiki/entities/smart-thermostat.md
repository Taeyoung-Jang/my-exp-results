## Smart Thermostat

A **smart thermostat** is a programmable, internet‑enabled temperature control device that can be managed via a smartphone app, voice assistant, or web portal. In the context of homeownership handoff, it is one of several smart‑home components that should be reset and removed from the seller’s accounts before transfer to the new owner.

### Key Facts from Session 36 (2024‑07‑15)
- Listed under the “Smart Home Handoff” section of the printable house‑info packet template.
- The checklist asks the seller to note the *brand/model*, confirm that a **factory reset** has been performed, and record any transfer steps or setup instructions for the buyer.
- Emphasized as a device whose **login credentials and account links must not be handed over**; instead, the seller should provide a clean slate for the new owner.
