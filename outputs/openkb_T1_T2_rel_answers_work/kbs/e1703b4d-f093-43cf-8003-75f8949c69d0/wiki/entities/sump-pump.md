# Sump Pump

A **sump pump** is a mechanical device installed in a home’s basement or crawlspace to remove water that accumulates from groundwater, snow melt, or roof runoff. By automatically pumping water away from the foundation, it helps protect the structure from water intrusion, mold, and costly damage.

## Key Facts from Session 14 (2023‑07‑15)
- **Purpose in cold climates**: Mitigates basement seepage during freeze/thaw cycles and heavy snow melt.
- **Recommended timing**: Install or upgrade in the fourth quarter, *before the winter season*, to ensure protection when storms and power outages are most likely.
- **Cost range**: Approximately **$300 – $1,200** depending on pump capacity, type (submersible or pedestal), and installation complexity.
- **High‑impact upgrade**: Pair with a **battery backup** system to keep the pump running during power outages common in winter storms.
- **Prioritization**: Listed as one of the top 5 upgrades in the [[concepts/cold-climate-water-risk-mitigation]] strategy and appears in the quarterly **maintenance schedule** outlined in [[concepts/maintenance-scheduling]].
