# Doorbell Camera

A **doorbell camera** is a smart‑home product that combines a video doorbell with a built‑in camera, allowing homeowners to see, record, and interact with visitors remotely via a smartphone or other device.

## Key Facts from Session 36 (2024‑07‑15)
- **In the handoff checklist** the doorbell camera is listed under the *Smart Home Handoff* section.
- The template asks for the **brand/model**, whether a **factory reset** has been performed, and for **transfer instructions** to be left for the new owner.
- Sellers are advised **not to give personal logins**; instead they should remove the device from their account and provide the new owner with setup steps.
- This aligns with the broader recommendation to treat smart‑home devices as part of the **[[concepts/smart-home-transfer]]** process, ensuring privacy and security during a **[[concepts/homeownership-handoff]]**.

## Recommended Handoff Steps
1. Record the **brand and model** of the doorbell camera.
2. Perform a **factory reset** (or remove the device from your account) to clear personal credentials.
3. Provide the new owner with **setup instructions**, including how to add the device to their own smart‑home ecosystem.
4. Include any **manuals or warranty information** in the house‑info packet.
