# Attic Insulation

**Type:** Product

Attic insulation refers to the thermal barrier installed on the attic floor or roof deck to reduce heat loss, improve energy efficiency, and keep warm indoor air from escaping into the attic space. In cold, snow‑prone climates, proper attic insulation and air‑sealing are the most effective single measure for preventing ice‑dam formation and reducing the risk of frozen‑pipe bursts.

## Key Facts from Session 14 (2023‑07‑15)
- **Primary benefit:** Keeps warm air from rising into the attic, which mitigates ice dams on the roof – a major water‑damage risk in cold climates.
- **Recommended timing:** Install or upgrade during the first quarter of the year (Q1) before winter conditions set in.
- **Cost range:** Approximately **$1,000‑$3,000** depending on attic size, insulation type (fiberglass batts, blown‑in cellulose, spray foam), and the extent of air‑sealing needed.
- **Risk assessment cue:** If you can see exposed joists, feel cold drafts, or notice uneven or thin insulation when accessing the attic, the upgrade will have high impact.
- **Relation to other actions:** Works in tandem with **heat‑trace cable** on roof edges and the **water‑leak sensor** + **automatic shut‑off valve** system to provide a layered defense against water damage.
