# Homeownership Handoff

**Definition**: A systematic process for providing a new homeowner with all essential information, documentation, and access details needed to operate, maintain, and secure the property from day one, while retaining critical records for the seller.

---

## Overview
When a property changes hands, the transition can be smooth or chaotic depending on how well the outgoing owner prepares the handoff. The concept focuses on compiling a **House Information Packet** that includes:
- Consolidated **property paperwork** (deed, disclosures, warranties, permits, insurance, manuals).
- Detailed **utility and system information** (water, gas, electric shut‑offs, HVAC, filters, detectors).
- A concise **maintenance history** (service dates, repairs, pest control, paint colors).
- A dedicated **smart‑home handoff** section for devices such as thermostats, doorbell cameras, and garage door openers.
- Clear delineation of what the seller **keeps** versus what is **shared** with the buyer.

These elements align with best practices from [[summaries/session-36_2024-07-15]].

---

## Key Components

### 1. Property Paperwork
- Deed, closing statements, and any recorded disclosures.
- Warranty cards for appliances, roof, HVAC, and structural components.
- Permits and inspection reports.
- Manuals for major systems and appliances.

### 2. Utility & System Information
- Locations of the **main water shutoff**, **gas shutoff**, and **electrical panel** (including sub‑panels).
- Contact details for water, power, gas, trash, internet, and any specialty services.
- Filter sizes for HVAC, refrigerator, and other appliances.
- Smoke and CO detector locations with last‑replacement dates.

> This aligns with [[concepts/utility-management]].

### 3. Maintenance & Repair History
- Dates of HVAC service, roof inspections, water‑heater maintenance, and any major repairs.
- Pest‑control records, paint color specifications, and notes on recurring quirks.
- Organizing this history aids future scheduling and ties into [[concepts/maintenance-scheduling]].

### 4. Smart Home Transfer
- List each smart device (e.g., smart thermostat, doorbell camera, garage door opener). 
- Perform factory resets, remove the device from personal accounts, and provide setup instructions.
- Include a checklist for resetting Wi‑Fi passwords or creating guest networks.

> Detailed guidance is covered in [[concepts/smart-home-transfer]].

### 5. What to Keep vs. What to Give
| Keep (Seller) | Give (Buyer) |
|---|---|
| Closing documents, receipts, permits, move‑out photos | Paperwork packet (paperwork, warranties, manuals) |
| Personal utility account logins, Wi‑Fi passwords, security codes | Utility provider names and account numbers (no passwords) |
| Smart‑device accounts and personal data | Reset devices with new setup instructions |

## Printable Checklist (Excerpt)
A one‑page, fill‑in template can be printed and completed during the move‑out process. The checklist includes sections for **House Systems and Safety**, **Utilities and Services**, **Appliances & Warranties**, **Maintenance History**, **House Quirks**, **Smart Home Handoff**, and **Documents You Keep**.

---

## Benefits of a Structured Handoff
- Reduces buyer confusion and post‑sale disputes.
- Preserves seller liability protection through documented records.
- Enables smooth continuation of smart‑home functionality for the new owner.
- Facilitates future **maintenance scheduling** and budgeting.
