# Parking Strategy

A focused approach to choosing parking spots and routes that reduces walking distance and time when handling multiple errands, especially while using a walking boot.

## Overview
Parking strategy is a crucial component of effective [[concepts/errand-planning]] and works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/curbside-pickup]] to keep trips quick and low‑impact on mobility.

## Key Tactics (from [[summaries/session-34_2025-10-08]])
- **Target the front row of the Kohl’s entrance** that faces the main drive aisle. This area turns over fastest because shoppers often leave after quick Amazon returns, increasing the chance of a close spot.
- **Avoid re‑parking** between Kohl’s and Old Navy; a short walk from Kohl’s to Old Navy is faster than circling the lot for a new space.
- **Circle once only** if the front row is full; this limits extra walking and keeps the distance minimal.
- **Leverage curbside options** (Target Drive‑Up, CVS drive‑thru) to stay in the car whenever possible, further reducing the need for parking near store entrances.

## Practical Example
When following the Saturday morning plan, the driver parks in the Kohl’s front row, completes the Amazon return at [[entities/kohls]], then walks a few steps to [[entities/old-navy]] for the clothing return. This minimizes walking, avoids unnecessary re‑parking, and keeps the total trip under two hours.
