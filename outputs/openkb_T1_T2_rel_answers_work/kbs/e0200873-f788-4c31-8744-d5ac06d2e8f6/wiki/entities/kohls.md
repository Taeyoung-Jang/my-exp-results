# Kohl’s (organization)

Kohl’s is a department‑store chain that, in the context of [[summaries/session-34_2025-10-08]], serves as the location for an Amazon return using a QR code (no box required). It is situated in a busy shopping center together with Old Navy, providing a large parking lot where a specific **front‑row parking strategy** is recommended to minimize walking while in a walking boot.

## Key facts from the session
- **Purpose in the errand plan:** Acts as the first stop in the Kohl’s/Old Navy center for the Amazon return before proceeding to Old Navy for a clothing return.
- **Parking recommendation:** Aim for the front row of the Kohl’s entrance facing the main drive aisle; this spot tends to turnover quickly and keeps the subsequent walk to Old Navy short.
- **Return process:** Scan the Amazon QR code at the Kohl’s customer service desk; the transaction is typically completed in under two minutes.
- **Strategic role:** Part of an overall [[concepts/errand-planning]] workflow that leverages minimal walking and efficient sequencing of curbside and in‑store actions.
