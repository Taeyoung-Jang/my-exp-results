# Member Spotlight

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A short, customized paragraph used in club newsletters to introduce a featured member, typically 3–5 sentences, balancing friendliness and professionalism.

## Core Elements
- **Tone variation** – Writers may choose formal, professional‑casual, or casual tones depending on the audience. See [[concepts/tone-variation]] for guidance.
- **Placeholders** – Standard tokens `[Name]`, `[Role]`, and `[Contribution]` allow easy personalization without rewriting the whole copy. See [[concepts/placeholder-usage]].
- **Narrative perspective** – Usually written in third‑person from the club’s viewpoint.
- **Length constraint** – Often limited to three sentences for concise newsletter space.
- **Subtle personal references** – Optional mentions (e.g., a member’s Buddhist practice) should be woven subtly so they inform the tone without dominating the message.

## Example from Source
The concept is demonstrated in the drafting process of a professional‑casual member spotlight paragraph in [[summaries/session-11_2023-05-24]], where the assistant produced several three‑sentence versions, incorporated placeholders, adjusted tone, and made the Buddhist reference understated.

## Usage Guidelines
1. **Select tone** appropriate for the newsletter’s overall voice.
2. **Insert placeholders** for the member’s name, role, and a specific contribution.
3. **Add optional personal detail** (e.g., a practice or hobby) in a subtle manner that supports the narrative.
4. **Keep to the sentence limit** to maintain brevity.
5. **Proofread** for consistency and ensure the paragraph reads naturally when placeholders are replaced.
