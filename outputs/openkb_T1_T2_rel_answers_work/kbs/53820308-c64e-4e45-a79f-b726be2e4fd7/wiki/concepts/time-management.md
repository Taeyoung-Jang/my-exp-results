# Time Management

**Definition**: The systematic planning and allocation of limited time resources to achieve goals efficiently while minimizing stress.

## Core Elements
- **Prioritization**: Identifying high‑impact tasks (e.g., county‑clerk visit) and ordering them based on urgency and dependencies.
- **Time Blocking**: Reserving specific calendar slots for distinct activities such as errands, appointments, or preparation sessions.
- **Buffer Allocation**: Adding extra time around fixed‑hour commitments (e.g., clerk business hours) to accommodate travel or unexpected delays.
- **Dependency Mapping**: Understanding which tasks must precede others, like confirming clerk requirements before scheduling the appointment.

## Application in [[summaries/session-38_2025-05-24]]
The session demonstrates a practical use of time‑management techniques:
1. **Call the county clerk early (Tuesday)** to determine walk‑in vs. appointment needs, thereby informing the schedule.
2. **Block Tuesday after 3 PM** for quick errands (prescription, bank) that aren’t bound by business‑hour limits.
3. **Reserve Wednesday morning** for the clerk visit if no appointment is required, otherwise keep it flexible for an appointment slot.
4. **Keep Friday open** as a catch‑all day for any remaining tasks, providing a buffer for the clerk appointment, package return, or other errands.

These steps illustrate **time blocking**, **dependency mapping**, and **buffer allocation** in action, helping the user reduce the cognitive load of juggling multiple commitments.
