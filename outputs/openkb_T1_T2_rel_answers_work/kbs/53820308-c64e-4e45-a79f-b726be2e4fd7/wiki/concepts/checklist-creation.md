# Checklist Creation

**Definition**: A systematic approach to assembling a concise, reusable list of items and questions that prepares a person for a specific interaction—most often a phone call or in‑person visit—to ensure all required information is on hand and no steps are missed.

## Why it matters
- Reduces cognitive load and the chance of forgetting critical documents.
- Speeds up interactions with bureaucratic offices, aligning with [[concepts/time-management]].
- Supports efficient scheduling when appointments are required, linking to [[concepts/appointment-planning]].

## Core Elements (as used in [[summaries/session-38_2025-05-24]])
- **Personal identifiers**: Full legal names of both parties.
- **Photo ID options**: Driver’s license, state ID, passport, or military ID.
- **Proof of address**: Utility bill, mail, or other official correspondence if the ID lacks an address.
- **Vital records**: Certified birth certificates; prior‑marriage documents (divorce decree or death certificate) if applicable.
- **Event details**: Planned ceremony date.
- **Parish paperwork**: Any forms already supplied by the officiant or parish.
- **Note‑taking tools**: Space to record fees, accepted payment methods, and timing information.
- **Targeted questions**: 
  - "Is there any waiting period or processing time I should plan for?"
  - "Does anything expire or need to be completed within a certain window?"

## How to create a checklist
1. **Identify the interaction goal** – e.g., filing marriage‑license paperwork.
2. **Gather required documents** – list each category (IDs, proofs, records).
3. **Add context‑specific items** – such as parish paperwork or ceremony date.
4. **Formulate key questions** – focus on deadlines, fees, and appointment logistics.
5. **Format for quick reference** – bullet points, checkboxes, or a screenshot‑friendly layout.

## Benefits observed in the source session
- The user could quickly verify they had all necessary items before calling the county clerk.
- The checklist enabled a clear, structured call script, minimizing back‑and‑forth.
- It provided a flexible framework that could be reused for future civic appointments.
