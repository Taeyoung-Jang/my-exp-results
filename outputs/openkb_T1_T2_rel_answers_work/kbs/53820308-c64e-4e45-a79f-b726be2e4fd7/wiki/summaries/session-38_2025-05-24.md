# Summary of session-38_2025-05-24

## Core Request
The user asked for help planning a week of errands tied to a marriage license, including:
- Visiting the county clerk
- Coordinating with the officiant/parish prep sessions
- Completing routine errands (prescription pick‑up, package return, bank stop)
- Understanding what to ask the clerk and how to prioritize tasks.

## Proposed Scheduling Approach
- **Call the county clerk early (Tuesday)** to confirm walk‑in vs. appointment, required documents, fees, and processing time. [[concepts/appointment-planning]]
- Use **Tuesday after 3 PM** for quick errands that don’t depend on business‑hour hours. [[concepts/time-management]]
- Reserve **Wednesday morning** for the clerk visit if no appointment is needed; otherwise keep it flexible for an appointment slot. [[concepts/time-management]]
- Keep **Friday** open as a catch‑all day for any remaining tasks (package return, clerk appointment, etc.).

## Detailed Call Script (Updated)
```
Hi, I’m calling to check your process for handling marriage‑related paperwork. I’m coordinating this with my officiant and the parish’s paperwork schedule, so I want to make sure I come in fully prepared. Do you take walk‑ins or do I need an appointment? Could you tell me which documents you typically want people to bring, what the fees are and how you prefer people pay, and about how long the visit or appointment usually takes? I just want to plan my timing and avoid any repeat trips.
```
This script integrates the need to align with parish paperwork without delving into legal specifics.

## Checklist (Screenshot‑Ready Bullet List)
**Checklist to have in front of you**
- Full legal names for both partners
- Government‑issued photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, passport, or military ID)
- Proof of address if not on the ID (utility bill, mail, etc.)
- Certified birth certificates (if available)
- Prior‑marriage documents if applicable (certified divorce decree or death certificate)
- Planned ceremony date
- Any paperwork already provided by the parish/officiant
- Means to take notes on fees, payment methods, and timing

**Notes to ask**
- “Is there any waiting period or processing time I should plan for?”
- “Once we start the paperwork, does anything expire or need to be completed within a certain window?”

These items form a typical‑items checklist for marriage‑license filings and can be reused for similar civic processes. [[concepts/checklist-creation]]
