## Summary

This session produced a set of ready‑to‑use drafts for recruiting volunteers to a **[[entities/neighborhood-clean-up]]** event. Two versions were created—a concise 2‑3 sentence notice and a more detailed announcement. Both include **[[concepts/placeholders]]** for date, time, location, parking information, sign‑up link, and a contact for late arrivals, enabling quick personalization.

Key elements added to the messages:
- Kids are welcome with adult supervision.
- Notice of limited extra supplies (bags, grabbers) while encouraging volunteers to bring gloves, water, and comfortable shoes.
- Clear parking instructions and a late‑arrival contact.
- An upbeat, friendly tone without being cheesy.

The drafts serve as a template for future community event communications, illustrating how to balance brevity, detail, and logistical clarity.

### Core Concepts
- [[concepts/volunteer-recruitment-message]] – structuring concise and detailed outreach.  
- [[concepts/placeholders]] – using bracketed tokens for rapid customization.  
- [[concepts/community-event-logistics]] – covering timing, location, parking, and contact info.  
- kids welcome policy – integrating family‑friendly guidelines.  
- supplies management – communicating limited resources and volunteer contributions.  
- upbeat tone – maintaining positivity without over‑sentimentality.
