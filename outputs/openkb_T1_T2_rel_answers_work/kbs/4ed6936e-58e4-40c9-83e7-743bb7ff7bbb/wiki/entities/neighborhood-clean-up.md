# Neighborhood Clean‑Up (event)

**Overview**
The Neighborhood Clean‑Up is a local community event focused on improving the cleanliness and safety of the neighborhood’s public spaces. It relies on resident volunteers to pick up litter, sort recyclables, and maintain shared areas.

**Key Details from Session 15 (2024‑04‑20)**
- **Purpose**: Mobilize neighbors to collaboratively clean the area.
- **Volunteer Recruitment**: Draft messages were created to attract additional helpers, featuring an upbeat tone and clear calls to action. See the related [[concepts/volunteer-recruitment-message]] for the message structure.
- **Logistics**: The outreach includes placeholders for date, time, location, parking, and a contact for late arrivals, illustrating the use of [[concepts/placeholders]]. It also outlines essential items for volunteers (gloves, water, shoes) and mentions limited extra supplies (bags, grabbers).
- **Family Friendly**: Kids are welcome with adult supervision, emphasizing the event’s inclusive nature.
- **Supplies Management**: While a few extra bags and grabbers are provided, participants are encouraged to bring their own, linking to the concept of [[concepts/community-event-logistics]].
