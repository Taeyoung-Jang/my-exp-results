## Overview
The assistant outlines a quick mental checklist for deciding whether to stick with a known favorite dish or try a rotating [[entities/daily-special]].

## Key Points
- **Identify the restaurant’s strength** – signature dishes or frequent kitchen output signal safe bets. [[concepts/restaurant-strength]]
- **Read the kitchen vibe** – a busy, confident kitchen encourages risk; a chaotic one suggests staying simple. [[concepts/kitchen-vibe]]
- **Use the default dish as a home base** – deviate only when a clear reason exists (seasonal ingredient, familiar flavor twist, chef enthusiasm). [[concepts/default-dish]]
- **Ask the server targeted questions**:
  - “Have you tried it yourself?”
  - “How are people liking it so far?”
  - “What’s the story behind this special?”
  These elicit genuine opinions and reveal the special’s purpose. (server cues)
- **Red‑flag answers** – server hasn’t tried it, gives vague or generic replies, shows hesitation, or cannot explain the dish’s intent. In such cases, stick with the known favorite. (red‑flag answers)
- **Seasonal & ingredient focus** – specials highlighting fresh, seasonal ingredients are higher‑value bets. (seasonal ingredients)
- **Risk assessment** – compare the special’s similarity to known preferences; a small twist is lower risk than a totally unfamiliar dish. (risk assessment)

## Practical Takeaway
Treat your go‑to order (e.g., [[entities/grilled-salmon]]) as a baseline, and only substitute it when the server’s feedback and the dish’s context provide a compelling, concrete reason to try something new.
