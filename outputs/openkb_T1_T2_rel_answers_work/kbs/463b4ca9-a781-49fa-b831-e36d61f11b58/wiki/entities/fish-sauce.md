# Fish Sauce (product)

Fish sauce is a clear, amber-colored liquid made from fermented fish (often anchovies) and salt. It is a staple condiment in many Southeast Asian cuisines, adding umami depth and a subtle briny note to dishes.

- Provides saltiness and umami, often added to sauces, marinades, soups, and dressings.
