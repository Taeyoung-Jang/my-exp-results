# Natto

**Type:** product

Natto is a traditional Japanese fermented soybean product characterized by a sticky, stringy texture and a pungent, earthy flavor. It is often cited as an example of an intense fermented food that beginners might find challenging.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-39_2025-11-14]]
- **Flavor profile:** described as "strong aroma" and an "acquired taste," making it a candidate for the "gentler cousin" pivot strategy.
- **Gentler alternatives:** The session suggests using miso‑based soups or stews as a milder way to experience fermented depth without natto's intensity.
- **Pivot line suggestion:** When a server mentions natto, you can ask, "What’s the miso‑leaning option that gives me the depth without the intensity?"
