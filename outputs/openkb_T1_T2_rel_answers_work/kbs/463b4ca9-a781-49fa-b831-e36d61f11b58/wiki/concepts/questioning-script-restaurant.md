# Restaurant Questioning Script

**Definition** – A short, ordered set of polite questions diners can ask restaurant staff to gauge flavor intensity, spice level, texture, and any surprising elements before committing to an unfamiliar dish.

## Core Script
1. *“If I’m new to this dish, is the flavor **mild** or **bold**?”* – differentiates approachable seasoning from strong 
2. *“How **spicy** is it compared to what most people expect here?”* – gauges heat without using vague terms
3. *“What’s the **texture** like?”* – catches unexpected mouthfeel (e.g., slime, crunch)
4. *(Optional) “Is there anything that **surprises** first‑time tasters?”* – surfaces funky or fermented notes

### Handling vague answers
- *“If you were recommending this to a first‑timer, would you call it an **easy** choice or a **deeper cut**?”*
- *“What’s the **gentler cousin** of this dish?”*

## When to Use It
- **Small‑plate, tasting‑menu, or food‑hall venues** where staff expect curiosity.
- **Menus without translations or clear descriptions** – the script extracts concrete cues.
- Situations where you want to **avoid overspending** by ordering a single exploratory plate rather than a full entrée.
