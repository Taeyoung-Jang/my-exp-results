# Summary of session-13_2025-05-09

**Document title:** Conversation session 13 (2025‑05‑09)

**Purpose:** To give the user a concrete, high‑focus three‑day work sprint template for completing a thesis submission, including hour‑by‑hour deep‑work blocks, daily and pre‑submission checklists, and two tailored unavailability messages.

### Key Elements
1. **Daily rhythm** – Same structure each day:
   - 9:00‑9:15 settling in  
   - **Deep‑work Block 1** 9:15‑10:45 (60‑90 min) – see [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]]  
   - Break 10:45‑11:15  
   - Light admin/prep 11:15‑12:00  
   - Lunch 12:00‑13:00  
   - **Deep‑work Block 2** 13:00‑14:30 – see [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]]  
   - Short debrief/break 14:30‑15:00  
   - Light wrap‑up 15:00‑16:00  
   - **Daily Review** 16:00‑16:15 – see [[concepts/daily-review]]

2. **Day‑specific thesis tasks**
   - **Day 1 – Analysis & Results**
     - Finish any remaining data analysis, generate figures/tables.
     - Draft the full *Results* section.
     - **Must‑finish checklist:** analysis completed, figures ready, Results draft complete.
   - **Day 2 – Discussion & Introduction**
     - Draft/revise *Discussion* (reasoning‑heavy).
     - Gather citations, integrate literature.
     - Write/revise *Introduction* to match final shape.
     - **Must‑finish checklist:** Discussion draft, Introduction draft, all citations collected.
   - **Day 3 – Formatting & Final Proof**
     - Apply department template: headings, spacing, references, captions.
     - Assemble supplementary files, create submission package.
     - Perform full proofread for typos, consistency, and PDF export.
     - **Must‑finish checklist:** formatted manuscript, supplementary package, final proofread.

3. **Pre‑submission checklist** (run after Day 3) – verify that all sections are present and consistent, figures/tables correctly labeled, references formatted per style guide, abstract within word limit, PDF export clean, and backups stored in at least two locations.

4. **Managing distractions & urgent messages**
   - Create one or two “message windows” each day (e.g., 11:00‑11:15, 15:00‑15:15) – see [[concepts/message-windows]].
   - Silence notifications outside windows; allow a starred contact or single app to bypass silence for true emergencies.

5. **Unavailability messages**
   - **To [[entities/supervisor]]:**
     > “I’m entering a focused three‑day submission sprint and will be offline except for true emergencies related to the thesis. If anything urgent comes up, please text or call. I’ll send the final version as soon as it’s ready.”
   - **To [[entities/friends-and-family]]:**
     > “I’m disappearing for the next three days to finish my thesis before the deadline. I won’t be checking messages much, but if something important comes up, call or text and I’ll see it. Back to normal after Thursday.”
