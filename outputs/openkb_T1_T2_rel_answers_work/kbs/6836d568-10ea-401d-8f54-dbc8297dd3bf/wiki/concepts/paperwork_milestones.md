# Paperwork Milestones

Paperwork Milestones is a pragmatic approach for turning routine administrative tasks into motivating checkpoints. The method combines a simple note‑template with a reward‑scaling principle, encouraging completion by pairing each “done” moment with a pre‑chosen treat.

---

## Core Components

### 1. Task‑Tracking Template
The template captures the essential elements of any task:

```
Task: 
Done when: 
Steps: 
Reward I’ll give myself: 
Date finished: 
How it felt:
```

It ensures clarity on what constitutes completion, outlines actionable steps, and pre‑selects a reward.  See [[concepts/task_tracking_template]] for the template details.

### 2. Reward Scaling
Rewards are matched to the *emotional weight* of the task rather than its duration. The scaling tiers are:
- **Small annoyance** → coffee, snack, or similar quick treat.
- **Medium dread** → dinner out, movie night, or a modest personal indulgence.
- **High‑stress milestone** → mini‑getaway, overnight stay, or a larger experience.

This principle prevents over‑rewarding while keeping the incentive proportional.  See [[concepts/reward_scaling]] for the scaling logic.

---

## How It Works
1. **Define the task** and its success criteria in the "Task" and "Done when" lines.
2. **Break it into steps** to create a clear roadmap.
3. **Choose a reward** beforehand using the scaling guide.
4. **Track progress** by marking each step as you go, adding timestamps if desired.
5. **Celebrate** immediately upon completion, noting the experience in the "How it felt" field.

## Example from Session 39
The concept was illustrated in a real‑world example where the user received a signed committee confirmation and rewarded themselves with a weekend train trip. The entry looked like:

```
Task: Final committee sign‑off at the department office.
Done when: I had the stamped confirmation sheet in hand.
Steps: Go to the office, check in, wait for review, receive the signed confirmation.
Reward I gave myself: Booked the weekend train trip I’d been putting off.
Date finished: Today.
How it felt: Like a huge exhale I’d been holding for months.
```

This entry demonstrates the full workflow of a *big milestone* and the appropriate scaling of the reward.

---

## Benefits
- **Increased motivation** by linking paperwork to pleasant outcomes.
- **Clear progress tracking** reduces overwhelm.
- **Flexible**: works for any administrative context, from address updates to tax filing.
- **Scalable rewards** keep incentives balanced over time.
