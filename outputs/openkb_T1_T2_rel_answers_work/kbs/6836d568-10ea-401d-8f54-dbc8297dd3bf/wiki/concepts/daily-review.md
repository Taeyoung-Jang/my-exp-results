# Daily Review

**Definition**: A short, structured end‑of‑day check‑in that captures what was accomplished, identifies any blockers, and sets clear objectives for the next day.

**Purpose**
- Consolidates progress from the day's [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]].
- Prevents mental carry‑over, reducing burnout.
- Provides a concrete hand‑off point for multi‑day sprints such as the thesis sprint described in [[summaries/session-13_2025-05-09]].

**Typical Structure (from the session‑13 plan)**
1. **Time allocation** – 15 minutes (e.g., 16:00‑16:15).
2. **What to note**
   - Completed outcomes of each deep‑work block.
   - Items that remain unfinished or need follow‑up.
   - Any unexpected insights or obstacles.
3. **Next‑day framing** – Choose a single primary goal and outline the two deep‑work blocks for tomorrow.

**Benefits**
- Gives a clear “stopping point” that protects evening downtime.
- Enables quick re‑orientation when returning after a break or weekend.
- Supports accountability when informing stakeholders (e.g., [[entities/supervisor]] or [[entities/friends-and-family]]) about progress.

**Relation to Other Concepts**
- Works in tandem with [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]] to create a cyclical rhythm of focus and reflection.
- Complements [[concepts/message-windows]] by reducing the need for ad‑hoc status updates during the day.

**Implementation Tips**
- Keep a dedicated notebook or digital note ready for the review.
- Use a consistent template to make the process automatic.
- Review the checklist at the end of each sprint day to ensure the next day's plan is concrete.
