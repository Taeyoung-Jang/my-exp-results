# Reward Scaling

**Type:** Concept

## Definition
Reward Scaling is the practice of adjusting the size and significance of a reward to correspond with the perceived emotional difficulty or stress of a bureaucratic task, rather than its time duration.

## Core Principles (from [[summaries/session-39_2025-05-23]])
1. **Emotional Weight Over Time** – Evaluate how mentally taxing a task feels. The heavier the dread, the larger the reward.
2. **Proportional Rewards** –
   - *Small annoyance*: treat with a modest treat such as a coffee or pastry.
   - *Medium dread*: reward with a nicer dinner, a movie night, or a favorite snack.
   - *High‑stress milestone*: reward with a mini‑getaway, weekend trip, or another memorable experience.
3. **Pre‑selection** – Choose the reward before starting the task, creating a clear incentive and an immediate “exhale” moment upon completion.
4. **Avoid Over‑rewarding** – Keep larger rewards reserved for truly significant milestones to maintain their motivational impact.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/paperwork_milestones]] which defines the checkpoints where rewards are applied.
- Utilises the structure provided by [[concepts/task_tracking_template]] to record the task, criteria, steps, and chosen reward.

## Practical Application
When you log a task using the template, decide the reward tier based on how stressful the task feels:
```markdown
Task: …
Done when: …
Steps: …
Reward I’ll give myself: [choose appropriate tier]
```
For example, updating an address (low weight) gets a coffee, renewing a driver’s license (medium weight) gets a dinner, and filing taxes (high weight) gets a weekend getaway.

## Benefits
- **Motivation** – Links completion with immediate pleasure.
- **Scalability** – Easy to adapt as task difficulty varies.
- **Balance** – Prevents reward inflation, keeping large rewards special.
