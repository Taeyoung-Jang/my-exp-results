# Thesis Submission (event)

**Overview**: The thesis submission sprint is a high‑intensity, three‑day work block designed to finish analysis, writing, formatting, and final proofing before a hard deadline.

**Key Facts from [[summaries/session-13_2025-05-09]]**
- **Duration**: 3 days (May 9 – May 11, 2025).
- **Daily Rhythm**: Structured around two [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]] per day, each 60‑90 minutes, with scheduled breaks, a light admin period, and a [[concepts/daily-review]] at the end of the day.
- **Day‑Specific Tasks**:
  - *Day 1*: Complete data analysis, generate figures/tables, draft the Results section.
  - *Day 2*: Write/revise Discussion and Introduction, gather citations.
  - *Day 3*: Apply formatting, assemble supplementary files, perform final proofread.
- **Checklists**: Each day has a “must‑finish” checklist; after Day 3 a **pre‑submission checklist** ensures sections, references, abstracts, PDF export, and backups are all in order.
- **Distraction Management**: Uses [[concepts/message-windows]] to limit message checking to specific times and silences other notifications.
- **Communication Plan**: Tailored unavailability messages for the [[entities/supervisor]] and for [[entities/friends-and-family]] to set expectations during the sprint.

**Related Concepts**
- [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]] – the core time‑blocking technique used throughout the sprint.
- [[concepts/daily-review]] – brief end‑of‑day reflection to capture progress and set next‑day goals.
- [[concepts/message-windows]] – controlled periods for handling urgent communications.

**Related Entities**
- [[entities/supervisor]] – receives a formal notice of limited availability.
- [[entities/friends-and-family]] – receive a casual notice of limited availability.
