# Friends and Family

**Type:** other

**Description:** A group of personal contacts—friends and family members—who may be informed about the author's limited availability during an intensive thesis‑submission sprint.

## Role in [[summaries/session-13_2025-05-09]]
The document provides two tailored unavailability messages, one of which is directed at **Friends and Family**. The message asks them to use a simple call or text only for truly important matters while the author focuses on [[entities/thesis-submission]].

- **Purpose:** Sets clear expectations, reduces interruptions, and protects the author’s deep‑work periods defined by [[concepts/deep-work-blocks]].
- **Relation to other entities:** Contrasts with the more formal notice sent to the [[entities/supervisor]], emphasizing a lighter tone.
