# Seasonal Maintenance Planning

Seasonal Maintenance Planning is a systematic method for organizing home upkeep activities around the four seasons, ensuring that critical tasks are performed at the optimal time to protect the property, control costs, and reduce the risk of damage.  The concept is especially relevant for owners of a [[entities/coastal_bungalow]] where salt air, moisture, and temperature swings amplify wear and tear.

## Why It Matters
- Aligns with [[concepts/maintenance_budgeting]] by spreading expenses across the year, making the 1‑2 % annual budget more predictable.
- Supports [[concepts/preventative_maintenance]] by catching issues early before they become costly emergencies.
- Directly addresses [[concepts/moisture_control]] and [[concepts/coastal_corrosion]] challenges unique to coastal environments.
- Provides a framework for deciding when a DIY approach is feasible versus when to engage a professional, tying into [[concepts/DIY_vs_professional_costs]].

## Seasonal Task Overview (from [[summaries/session-38_2024-06-15]])

### Early Spring
- **Gutter & downspout cleaning** – remove debris to ensure water is directed away.
- **Roof inspection** – look for missing shingles or corrosion.
- **Exterior outlet test** – verify weather‑proof covers and check for rust.
- **Attic moisture check** – spot damp patches or mold.

### Late Spring
- **Exterior caulk touch‑up** – use [[entities/marine_grade_silicone_caulk]] or polyurethane for durability.
- **Screen and hardware rinse** – rinse salt, then apply a protective coat of [[entities/boeshield_t9]].
- **Door/window binding check** – oil hinges with a marine‑grade silicone spray.

### Mid‑Late Summer
- **Crawlspace/basement moisture inspection** – run a [[entities/dehumidifier_35_50_pt]] if humidity exceeds 55 %.
- **Plumbing corrosion review** – replace any fittings that show rust; prefer [[entities/316_stainless_steel]] for replacements.
- **General interior ventilation** – keep air moving to deter mold.

### Early Fall
- **Gutter clearing before storms** – repeat spring cleaning.
- **Weather‑stripping inspection** – ensure doors and windows seal properly.
- **Exterior hardware rinse** – remove accumulated salt before winter.

### Early Winter
- **Draft detection** – check for swollen wood or gaps; re‑oil hinges.
- **Attic post‑rain inspection** – look for new moisture ingress.
- **HVAC/heat‑pump humidity control** – verify the system maintains indoor humidity at 45‑55 %.

## Decision Triggers for Professionals
- Persistent mold or moisture after cleaning.
- Corrosion on plumbing, electrical panels, or exterior outlets.
- Roof leaks or gutter overflow that cannot be resolved DIY.
- HVAC system unable to regulate humidity despite filter changes.
