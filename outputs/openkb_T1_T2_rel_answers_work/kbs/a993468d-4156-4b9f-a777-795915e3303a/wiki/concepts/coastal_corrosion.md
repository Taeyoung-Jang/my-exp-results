# Coastal Corrosion and Moisture Management

## Overview
Coastal properties are exposed to salt‑laden air, high humidity, and frequent moisture swings that accelerate metal corrosion, wood swelling, and mold growth. Effective management combines material selection, regular cleaning, moisture control, and a structured maintenance schedule.

## Key Challenges
- **Salt corrosion** on exterior hardware, fasteners, and electrical fixtures.
- **Moisture intrusion** leading to mold, mildew, and wood rot.
- **Wood swelling** that can cause doors and windows to stick.
- **Accelerated wear** of plumbing and HVAC components in damp conditions.

## Recommended Practices
| Area | Actions | Typical Cost | Recommended Specs |
|------|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Hardware** | Rinse all metal bits with fresh water after storms; apply corrosion‑resistant lubricant. | DIY <$20; pro $100‑200 | Use [[entities/316_stainless_steel]] for exterior fasteners; lubricate with [[entities/boeshield_t9]]. |
| **Sealing** | Re‑caulk joints where water can seep. | DIY $10‑15 per tube; pro $100‑200 per job | Apply [[entities/marine_grade_silicone_caulk]] or polyurethane marine‑grade caulk. |
| **Moisture Control** | Operate dehumidifier to keep indoor RH 45‑55 %. | Unit $150‑300; electricity a few dollars/month | Choose a [[entities/dehumidifier_35_50_pt]] sized for a small bungalow. |
| **Inspection** | Monthly visual sweep for rust, swelling wood, and mold; seasonal deeper checks. | Mostly free; professional inspections $150‑250 per visit. |
| **Mold Cleaning** | Spot‑clean with a non‑bleach, hydrogen‑peroxide‑based cleaner. | DIY $10‑30; pro if widespread. |

### Monthly Quick Checks (10‑15 min)
- Walk the exterior for fresh rust or salt deposits.
- Inspect doors/hinges; oil with marine‑grade silicone spray.
- Run dehumidifier; empty water reservoir.
- Verify HVAC filters and quick visual check for condensation.

### Seasonal Tasks (see [[concepts/seasonal_maintenance]])
- **Spring:** Clean gutters, rinse hardware, inspect roof.
- **Summer:** Full crawlspace moisture audit, re‑apply caulk.
- **Fall:** Clear gutters again, repeat hardware rinse, check for drafts.
- **Winter:** Verify heat‑pump humidity control, inspect attic for damp spots.

## Budget and Planning
- Allocate **1‑2 % of home value** annually for maintenance (see [[concepts/maintenance_budgeting]]). Coastal homes tend toward the higher end due to accelerated wear.
- Prioritize **DIY** for routine rinsing, caulking, and dehumidifier operation; schedule professionals for HVAC service, plumbing corrosion checks, and electrical panel inspections. Refer to [[concepts/DIY_vs_professional_costs]] for cost balancing.
