## Boeshield T‑9 Corrosion‑Resistant Lubricant

Boeshield T‑9 is a **marine‑grade silicone spray** formulated to create a thin, water‑repellent film that protects metal surfaces from rust, salt creep, and wear. In the **coastal bungalow** maintenance plan from [[summaries/session-38_2024-06-15]], it is recommended for:

- **Exterior hinges, latches, and railings** to keep them from seizing in salty, damp air.
- **Preventative maintenance** on any hardware that is regularly exposed to sea spray, complementing the use of **316 stainless steel** fasteners (see [[entities/316_stainless_steel]]) and **marine‑grade silicone caulk** for sealing gaps (see [[entities/marine_grade_silicone_caulk]]).

### Key Benefits
- **Corrosion resistance:** Helps mitigate issues highlighted in [[concepts/coastal_corrosion]].
- **Moisture control:** Works alongside dehumidification efforts to reduce interior humidity spikes, relating to [[concepts/moisture_control]].
- **Ease of application:** Suitable for DIY homeowners, fitting into the [[concepts/DIY_vs_professional_costs]] framework for low‑cost, frequent upkeep.

### Typical Use in a Coastal Setting
1. **Rinse hardware** with fresh water after a storm.
2. **Apply Boeshield T‑9** to hinges, door knobs, and any metal fixtures.
3. **Re‑oil** moving parts if needed; the spray provides a protective base.
4. **Repeat** during each seasonal check (see [[concepts/seasonal_maintenance]]) or whenever salt buildup is visible.
