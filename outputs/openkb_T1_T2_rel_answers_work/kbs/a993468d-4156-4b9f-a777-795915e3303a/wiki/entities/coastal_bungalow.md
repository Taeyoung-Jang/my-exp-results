## Coastal Bungalow (place)

**Entity:** Coastal Bungalow – a typical small house located near the sea, subject to salt air, high humidity, and occasional damp conditions. The entity is used in [[summaries/session-38_2024-06-15]] as a concrete example for creating a maintenance checklist and comparing renting vs. owning.

### Key Facts from the Session
- **Location Characteristics:** Close to a ferry terminal, exposed to salt‑air corrosion and moisture that can cause wood swelling, mold, and metal corrosion.
- **Maintenance Priorities:** Emphasis on moisture control, corrosion mitigation, and regular inspections of roof, gutters, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems.
- **Budget Guidance:** Suggested annual maintenance budget of about 1–2 % of the home’s value, with specific cost ranges for DIY versus professional tasks.
- **Monthly Checklist:** Includes quick exterior inspections, dehumidifier operation, filter changes, and hardware rinsing.
- **Seasonal Checklist:** Aligns with [[concepts/seasonal_maintenance]] – spring gutter cleaning, summer crawl‑space moisture checks, fall hardware rinsing, winter draft inspections.
- **Product Recommendations:** 
  - Fasteners: [[entities/316_stainless_steel]] for exterior hardware.
  - Caulk: [[entities/marine_grade_silicone_caulk]] (polyurethane or marine‑grade silicone).
  - Dehumidifier: [[entities/dehumidifier_35_50_pt]] (35–50 pt capacity suitable for a small bungalow).
  - Lubricant: [[entities/boeshield_t9]] for hinges and metal fixtures.
