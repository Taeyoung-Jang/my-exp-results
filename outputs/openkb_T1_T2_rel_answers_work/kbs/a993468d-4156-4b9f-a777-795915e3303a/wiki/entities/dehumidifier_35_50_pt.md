# 35–50 Pint Per Day Dehumidifier

**Type:** Product

## Overview
A compact dehumidifier capable of removing **35 to 50 pints of moisture per day** from the indoor air. Designed for small homes, especially coastal bungalows where salt air and high humidity accelerate moisture‑related issues.

## Key Specifications
- **Capacity:** 35‑50 pints/day (≈ 16‑23 L/day)
- **Coverage Area:** Up to ~1,200 sq ft, ideal for a single‑story bungalow.
- **Power Consumption:** Typically 300‑500 W, depending on humidity level.
- **Features:** Continuous drainage option, built‑in humidistat, auto‑restart after power loss.

## Recommended Use
- Run continuously during damp seasons to maintain indoor humidity between **45 % and 55 %**.
- Pair with regular **[[concepts/moisture_control]]** practices such as venting, gutter cleaning, and salt‑water rinsing of exterior hardware.
- Include in the **[[concepts/maintenance_budgeting]]** plan as a recurring expense (initial purchase + electricity).
- Integrate into the **[[concepts/seasonal_maintenance]]** schedule for spring and fall humidity spikes.

## Cost Estimate (from session‑38)
- **Purchase price:** $150‑$300 (depends on brand and energy rating).
- **Operating cost:** ~$5‑$10 per month in electricity for a typical bungalow.
