# Summary of Session 38 (2024‑06‑15)

## Rent vs Own Decision Framework
- Highlights practical factors: **maintenance responsibility**, hidden costs (property taxes, insurance, HOA, yard work), lifestyle preferences, and stability vs flexibility.  
- Suggests key questions to assess personal situation (life stability, expected stay length, cash availability, appetite for projects).  
- Introduces the concept of evaluating **daily lived experience** rather than pure financial calculus. [[concepts/rent_vs_own]]

## Maintenance Budgeting for an Older Small House
- Recommends budgeting **1‑2 % of home value per year**, leaning toward the higher end for older properties.  
- Emphasizes early detection of system issues to avoid cascade failures.  
- Provides a **first‑year preventive checklist** focusing on roof/gutters, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems. [[concepts/maintenance_budgeting]] [[concepts/preventative_maintenance]]

## General Month‑by‑Month First‑Year Checklist
- Organizes tasks into monthly focus blocks (system inspections, routine upkeep, seasonal chores).  
- Lists essential tools and spare parts: adjustable wrench, screwdriver set, plunger, flashlight, step stool, spare bulbs, furnace filters, exterior‑grade caulk, plumber’s tape.  
- Gives rough **DIY vs professional cost ranges** for each activity. [[concepts/DIY_vs_professional_costs]]

## Coastal Bungalow Adaptation
- Adjusts checklist for **salt‑air, damp climate**: priority on moisture, corrosion, mold, and wood swelling.  
- **Top 5 first‑30‑day items**: clear roof/gutters, rinse & oil hardware, run dehumidifier, mold inspection, HVAC/heat‑pump service.  
- Provides monthly tasks with cost estimates and **product specifications** (e.g., [[entities/316_stainless_steel]] hardware, marine‑grade polyurethane or silicone caulk, 35‑50 pt dehumidifier). [[concepts/coastal_corrosion]] [[concepts/moisture_control]]

## Printable One‑Page Maintenance Sheet
- Divides into:  
  1. **Monthly quick checks** (10‑15 min each).  
  2. **Seasonal tasks** (spring, summer, fall, winter).  
  3. **“Call a pro” triggers** for mold recurrence, corrosion, severe drafts, HVAC humidity issues, roof leaks.  
- Includes a concise **shopping list** with specs only (no brand names), referencing [[entities/coastal_bungalow]], [[entities/316_stainless_steel]], [[entities/marine_grade_silicone_caulk]], [[entities/dehumidifier_35_50_pt]], and [[entities/boeshield_t9]].  
- Offers a template ready for printing and reuse each year. [[concepts/seasonal_maintenance]]

## Key Takeaways
- Decision between renting and owning hinges on **responsibility tolerance** and **life stability**.  
- Allocate **1‑2 % of home value** annually for maintenance; front‑load inspections in the first year.  
- In coastal environments, prioritize **corrosion mitigation**, **moisture control**, and **regular hardware rinsing**.  
- Use a **simple monthly/seasonal workflow** to keep upkeep manageable and cost‑effective.
