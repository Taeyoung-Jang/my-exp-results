# Policy Update After Move

When you change residences, it’s essential to inform your renters insurance provider of the new location and review the policy details. This concept synthesizes the practical steps highlighted in [[summaries/session-06_2023-07-15]].

## Why Update?
- **Coverage applicability:** Insurers need the correct address to ensure the policy covers the proper premises.
- **Premium adjustment:** Location‑based rating factors such as crime rates, fire‑service response, and building construction can affect the premium.
- **Accurate risk assessment:** New unit characteristics (e.g., floor level, security features) may influence underwriting.

## Information Insurers Typically Request
- Full new address, including unit number.
- Building type and construction details.
- Floor level and any security measures (locked entry, intercom, monitored alarms).
- Presence of roommates or additional insured persons.

## Steps to Perform a Policy Update
1. **Locate the declarations page** of your existing policy to confirm the current coverage type (e.g., replacement cost vs. actual cash value).
2. **Contact your insurer** (phone, online portal, or chat) and provide the new address and unit details.
3. **Review limits and deductible** to ensure they still match the value of your belongings in the new space.
4. **Consider scheduled item riders** for high‑value personal items (laptops, jewelry, camera gear) if the new unit’s inventory has changed.
5. **Ask about premium impact** and any potential discounts (e.g., for upgraded security systems).

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- Updating the address is a core part of [[concepts/renters_insurance]] management, as the policy’s scope and pricing depend on location.
- If you share the unit with a roommate, you’ll need to address [[concepts/roommate_policy]] considerations—whether they are added as a named insured or obtain a separate policy.
- The overall process of keeping the policy current is captured by [[concepts/policy_update]].

## Checklist
- [ ] Provide new address (unit number, floor, building type).
- [ ] Verify security features are noted.
- [ ] Confirm coverage type (replacement cost vs. actual cash value).
- [ ] Update limits, deductible, and add any necessary riders.
- [ ] Inquire about premium changes.
- [ ] Document any roommate additions or removals.
