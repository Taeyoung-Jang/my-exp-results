# Communication Boundary

**Definition**: A communication boundary is a deliberate limit placed on conversational depth or frequency to safeguard emotional wellbeing and personal energy.

## Core Elements
- **Purpose**: Prevents overwhelming the speaker, especially after stressful events.
- **Expression**: Typically conveyed through brief, clear statements (e.g., “I’m not up for discussing this right now”).
- **Contextual Use**: Often appears in messages that share personal updates while signaling a need for space.

## Example from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-15]]
In the email draft, the user adds a line that sets a boundary: *“I’m feeling pretty drained, so I’m not up for getting into details right now… thanks for understanding.”* This phrasing:
1. Acknowledges the emotional state.
2. Limits further inquiry.
3. Provides gratitude for respect.

## Benefits
- Reduces the risk of emotional overload.
- Encourages respectful interaction.
- Helps the speaker manage energy after demanding situations.

## Implementation Tips
1. **State the boundary plainly** – avoid ambiguous language.
2. **Pair with a brief rationale** to foster understanding.
3. **End with appreciation** to maintain relational tone.
