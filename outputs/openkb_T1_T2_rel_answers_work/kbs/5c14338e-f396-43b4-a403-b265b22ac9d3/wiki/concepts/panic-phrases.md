# Panic Phrases

**Definition**: A concise set of pre‑learned sentences that learners can deploy in real‑time conversation to buy time, clarify meaning, or steer the interaction back to the target language.

## Why They Matter
- Reduce anxiety during **micro‑interactions** by providing a fallback option.
- Support the **speaking‑as‑show‑up** mindset: you’re present, not perfect.
- Enable smoother language switches, reinforcing a **balanced language learning** approach.

## Core Phrase Categories (from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]])
### 1. Freezing / Need More Time
- “Je cherche mes mots, une seconde.”
- “Pardon, je reformule.”
- “Attendez, je veux être sûr d’avoir compris.”

### 2. Didn't Hear / Ask for Slower Speech
- “Vous pouvez répéter un peu plus lentement ?”
- “Désolé, j’ai pas bien entendu.”
- “Vous pouvez répéter avec d’autres mots ?”

### 3. Handling English Switches
- “Si possible, je préfère continuer en français. Même si je fais des erreurs, ça m’aide beaucoup.”
- “Je peux continuer en français, si ça ne vous dérange pas.”

### 4. Confirmation & Reformulation
- “Laissez‑moi reformuler pour voir si j’ai compris.”
- “Je comprends, mais j’ai du mal à répondre.”

## How to Use Them
1. **Integrate** them into each day’s mission (see the 7‑day plan in [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]]). 
2. **Practice** saying each phrase aloud until it feels natural.
3. **Apply** the phrase immediately when you feel a slip; this keeps the conversation flow and reduces the urge to abandon French.
4. **Reflect** after each interaction: note which phrase helped most and whether you needed to switch languages.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/micro-interactions]] by giving low‑stakes rescue options for brief exchanges.
- Complements [[concepts/self_correction_checklist]]: after using a panic phrase, you can later review the moment and self‑correct.
- Aligns with [[concepts/balanced_language_learning]] by balancing confidence‑building tools with authentic practice.
