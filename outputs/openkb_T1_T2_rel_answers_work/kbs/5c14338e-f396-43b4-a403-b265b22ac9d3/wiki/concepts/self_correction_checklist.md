# Self‑Correction Checklist

## Overview
The **Self‑Correction Checklist** is a concise, learner‑driven tool for reviewing short French writing pieces. It prompts the writer to verify that the intended grammar, vocabulary, and naturalness are present before seeking external feedback. This checklist is a core component of the writing‑first learning plan described in [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]].

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of a broader **[[concepts/balanced_language_learning]]** strategy that pairs brief study with immediate production.
- Supports the **[[concepts/writing_first_approach]]** by encouraging autonomous quality control before correction.

## Checklist Items
After completing a writing task, run through the following questions:
1. **Target Grammar** – Did I use the specific grammar point I was studying?
2. **Agreement & Endings** – Are my verb endings, gender agreements, and plural forms correct?
3. **Natural Flow** – Does the sentence sound clear and natural when read aloud?
4. **Reuse of Input** – Did I incorporate at least one word or phrase from the recent reading or study material?

If any answer is **No**, revise the text until the answer becomes **Yes**.

## How to Use It
- Apply the checklist immediately after each writing exercise (paragraph, sentences, diary entry, etc.).
- Use it as a self‑editing step before sending the text for correction by a tutor or language partner.
- Over time, the checklist helps internalize error patterns and improves autonomous writing accuracy.
