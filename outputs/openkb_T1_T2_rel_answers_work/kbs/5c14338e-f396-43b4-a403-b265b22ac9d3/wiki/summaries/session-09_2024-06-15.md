# Summary of session-09_2024-06-15

## Document overview
This conversation outlines a **structured, writing‑centric weekly plan** for learning French, balancing brief **grammar/vocabulary study** with daily **writing practice**. It also supplies a **self‑correction checklist** and explicit instructions for sending short French texts for correction.

## Key concepts
- **Balanced language learning** – small, focused study sessions paired with immediate production [[concepts/balanced_language_learning]]
- **Writing‑first approach** – prioritises written output over speaking, suitable for learners uncomfortable with oral conversation [[concepts/writing_first_approach]]
- **Weekly rhythm** – three study days (Mon, Wed, Fri) and four input‑focused days (Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun) with defined time blocks
- **Self‑correction checklist** – quick questions to verify grammar use, agreement, naturalness, and reuse of input [[concepts/self_correction_checklist]]
- **Feedback loop** – learner sends raw French text; assistant returns corrected version and explanations

## Weekly schedule (summary)
| Day | Activity | Time | Output | Submission |
|-----|----------|------|--------|------------|
| **Mon** | Grammar/vocab + short paragraph | 20 min + 10‑15 min | Paragraph using new material | Send paragraph for correction |
| **Tue** | Light reading + short sentences | 15 min | 3‑4 sentences inspired by reading | Send sentences |
| **Wed** | Grammar/vocab + new paragraph | 20 min + 10‑15 min | Paragraph on a new topic | Send paragraph |
| **Thu** | Reading/listening + summary | 15 min | Brief summary (4‑5 sentences) | Send summary |
| **Fri** | Light study + casual text message | 20 min + 10‑15 min | Text‑message style paragraph | Send message |
| **Sat** | Longer diary‑style entry | 15‑20 min | Diary entry about the week | Send entry |
| **Sun** | Optional input + reflective sentences | 15 min | 2‑3 reflective sentences | Optional send |

## Self‑correction checklist (quick guide)
1. Did I use the targeted grammar point?  
2. Are verb endings and agreements correct?  
3. Does the sentence sound natural when read aloud?  
4. Did I reuse at least one word/phrase from the input or study?

If any answer is *no*, revise the text until it feels smooth.

## How to submit texts
- Paste the French text **plainly**, no special formatting needed.
- Include only the target sentences/paragraph for each day.
- The assistant will return corrections, explanations, and a polished version.
