# Summary of session-32_2023-06-15

This document provides a practical template for assessing an investor’s [[concepts/risk-tolerance]] and shaping an asset allocation that balances an aggressive growth tilt with diversification safeguards. It introduces a [[concepts/scoring-rubric]] used to score six personal risk dimensions (time horizon, liquidity, drawdown tolerance, concentration, income stability, emergency fund, and behavior risk) on a 0–5 scale, aggregates the scores into total‑score bands (conservative, moderate, aggressive), and outlines guardrails for portfolio composition.

## Key Concepts Covered
- **Investment styles** – growth vs. value and how they relate to temperament. [[concepts/investment-styles]]
- **Risk tolerance factors** – time horizon, liquidity, drawdown tolerance, concentration, income stability, emergency fund, behavior risk. [[concepts/risk-tolerance]]
- **Scoring rubric** – 0–5 rating per category, summed to a maximum of 35, with three posture bands (0‑14 = Conservative, 15‑24 = Moderate, 25‑35 = Aggressive). [[concepts/scoring-rubric]]
- **Portfolio diversification guardrails** – three allocation buckets:
  1. **Speculative single names** 0–10%
  2. **Sector/theme funds** 10–30%
  3. **Broad index/value core** 60–90%
  These illustrate the principle of a stable core supporting an aggressive tilt. [[concepts/diversification]]
- **Rebalancing rule** – semi‑annual review plus a drift trigger of 5–10 percentage‑point deviation from target ranges. [[concepts/rebalancing]]

## Practical Checklist (Spreadsheet‑Ready)
### Scoring Table
| Category | Score 0 anchor | Score 5 anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Time horizon | Need funds within 1–2 years | No need for funds 10+ years |
| Liquidity needs | May need access soon | No foreseeable need |
| Drawdown tolerance | Panic‑sell on drops | Calmly endure drops |
| Concentration risk | Few positions dominate | Broadly spread holdings |
| Income stability | Irregular/uncertain | Stable, predictable |
| Emergency fund | < 1–2 months expenses | 6+ months expenses |
| Behavior risk | Frequent impulsive changes | Rare impulsive changes |

### Total‑Score Bands
| Posture | Total score |
|---|---|
| Conservative | 0–14 |
| Moderate | 15–24 |
| Aggressive | 25–35 |

### Allocation Guardrails
| Bucket | Example range |
|---|---|
| Speculative single names | 0–10% |
| Sector / theme funds | 10–30% |
| Broad index / value core | 60–90% |

### Rebalancing Rules
| Rule | Description |
|---|---|
| Calendar | Rebalance twice per year |
| Drift trigger | Adjust when any bucket drifts > 5–10 percentage points from its target |

## How to Use
1. **Self‑assessment** – Fill in the 0–5 scores based on personal history and current financial situation.
2. **Determine posture** – Sum the scores and locate the total‑score band to decide whether an aggressive, moderate, or conservative allocation style is appropriate.
3. **Apply guardrails** – Use the bucket ranges as starting points; adjust within the ranges to reflect the chosen posture.
4. **Monitor** – Follow the semi‑annual calendar check and the 5–10 % drift trigger to keep the portfolio aligned.

*All numbers are illustrative templates, not personalized investment advice.*
