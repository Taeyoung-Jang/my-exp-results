# One‑Big‑Meal Routine

**Definition**: A dietary pattern where the primary calorie intake occurs in a single, substantial evening meal, supplemented by one or two lightweight, desk‑friendly snacks earlier in the day to maintain [[concepts/energy-stability]].

## Core Principles
- **Primary meal**: A larger, balanced dinner that provides the majority of daily macronutrients and calories.
- **Mini fuel‑ups**: One to two small, protein‑ or fat‑rich snacks (e.g., yogurt, nuts, cheese sticks, a banana with peanut butter) consumed mid‑morning and/or mid‑afternoon.
- **Desk‑friendly**: Snacks should be quiet, low‑mess, and easy to eat while working.
- **Flexibility**: Timing is loosely based on a 3‑4 hour eating rhythm but does not require strict meal times.

## Benefits
- Supports [[concepts/energy-stability]] without demanding multiple full meals.
- Keeps appetite low during the day while preventing the mid‑afternoon energy crash.
- Simplifies meal planning by focusing preparation on a single dinner.
- May gradually shift appetite earlier as the body adapts to regular small fuel‑ups.

## Practical Implementation (from [[summaries/session-38_2025-03-16]])
1. **Select desk‑friendly snack options**: portable yogurt, protein shake, trail mix, cheese stick, banana, crackers with nut butter, hummus & pre‑cut veggies.
2. **Schedule**: 
   - *Mid‑morning* (~10 am): small yogurt or banana + peanut butter.
   - *Mid‑afternoon* (~2 pm): cheese stick or handful of nuts, or a quick smoothie.
   - Adjust to one snack if appetite is very low.
3. **Maintain a balanced dinner**: include protein, complex carbs, vegetables, and healthy fats.
