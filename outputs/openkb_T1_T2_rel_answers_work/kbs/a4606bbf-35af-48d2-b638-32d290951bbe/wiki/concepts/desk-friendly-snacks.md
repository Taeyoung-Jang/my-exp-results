# Desk‑Friendly Snacks

**Definition**: Portable, low‑mess foods that can be eaten at a desk without interrupting workflow.

## Overview
Desk‑friendly snacks are a core part of a **mini fuel‑up** strategy for people who prefer a single large dinner but need steady energy throughout the day. The idea is to choose items that are quiet, easy to handle with one hand, and require little to no preparation, so they can be consumed while working.

## Key Characteristics
- **Quiet**: Minimal crunch or rustle that might disturb coworkers.
- **Low‑mess**: No sauces that spill, no crumbs that fall, and no strong odors.
- **Portable**: Individually packaged or easy to portion into a small container.
- **Nutrient‑dense**: Provide protein, healthy fats, or fiber to support [[concepts/energy-stability]].

## How to Use Them
1. **Plan timing**: Align snacks with a mini‑fuel‑up schedule (e.g., mid‑morning ~10 am, mid‑afternoon ~2 pm). [[concepts/mini-fuel-up]]
2. **Prepare ahead**: Keep a stash in your desk drawer or a nearby fridge so you don’t need to leave your workstation.
3. **Portion control**: Stick to a single‑serving size to avoid over‑eating before the big dinner. [[concepts/portion-control]]
4. **Rotate variety**: Switching between dairy, nuts, fruit, and protein bars maintains interest and covers a range of nutrients.

## Benefits
- Maintains [[concepts/energy-stability]] without major breaks.
- Supports a **one‑big‑meal routine** by providing just enough calories to prevent crashes. [[concepts/one-big-meal-routine]]
- Enables **meal‑pattern flexibility** for those with low early‑day appetite. [[concepts/meal-pattern-flexibility]]
