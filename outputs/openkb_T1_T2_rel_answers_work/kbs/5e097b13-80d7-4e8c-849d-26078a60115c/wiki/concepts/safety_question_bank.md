# Safety Question Bank

A **Safety Question Bank** is a short, reusable set of day‑one safety queries that help new apprentices demonstrate awareness, protect themselves, and integrate smoothly into a hands‑on work environment.

---

## Why a Safety Question Bank matters
- **Psychological safety**: Asking clear, practical questions signals that the apprentice cares about a safe workspace without trying to show off, which encourages inclusive communication [[concepts/psychological_safety]].
- **Low‑effort touchpoints**: Simple, concrete questions create easy entry points for building rapport with mentors and peers [[concepts/low_effort_touchpoints]].
- **Orientation day**: Integrating these questions into the early briefing helps turn a generic orientation into an actionable safety check [[concepts/orientation_day]].
- **Workplace community**: When apprentices ask the right questions, they contribute to a culture where safety information flows naturally among team members [[concepts/workplace_community]].

---

## Core question categories (derived from [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-15]])
1. **Hazard reporting**
   - "Who should I tell if a power tool sounds off or isn’t working right?"
2. **First‑aid & incident forms**
   - "Where are the first‑aid kits and incident‑report forms kept?"
3. **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures**
   - "What’s the lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedure for the machines we’ll be using?"
4. **Personal protective equipment (PPE) management**
   - "If my PPE (gloves, goggles, etc.) doesn’t fit or is damaged, who do I talk to for a replacement?"
5. **Emergency shutdown**
   - "Where are the emergency‑stop buttons or pull‑cords for my work area?"

---

## How to use the bank
- **Before the first shift**: Review the list and pick 2‑3 questions that feel most relevant to the specific shop floor.
- **During orientation**: Slip a question into the natural flow of conversation; for example, after a brief tour, ask about the location of first‑aid kits.
- **After the first question**: Take note of the answer in a personal safety log – this reinforces memory and creates a quick reference for future tasks.

---

## Extending the bank
Apprentices can add tailored questions for specific tools or processes they encounter. Over time, the bank can evolve into a shared resource within the [[concepts/workplace_community]] to help newcomers across cohorts.
