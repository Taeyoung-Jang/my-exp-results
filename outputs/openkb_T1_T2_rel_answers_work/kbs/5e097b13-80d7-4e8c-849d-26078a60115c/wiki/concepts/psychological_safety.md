# Psychological Safety

**Definition**: Psychological safety is the shared belief that a team is a safe space for members to express ideas, admit mistakes, and be themselves without fear of negative consequences.

## Core Elements
- **Trust**: Team members trust that their contributions are valued.
- **Risk‑free expression**: People feel comfortable sharing opinions, asking questions, and highlighting errors.
- **Supportive response**: Mistakes are treated as learning opportunities rather than reasons for blame.

## Role in Workplace Community
In the conversation from [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]], psychological safety was identified as the foundation for a genuine [[concepts/workplace_community]]. The later discussion in [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-15]] showed how psychological safety also underpins a new apprentice’s ability to ask practical safety questions, seek clarification on name recall, and engage during an orientation day without anxiety. When newcomers feel psychologically safe, they are more likely to use the suggested low‑effort name‑recall phrases and pose day‑one safety queries from the [[concepts/safety_question_bank]] without fearing judgment.

## Connection to Low‑Effort Touchpoints
Low‑effort, optional interactions—such as quick chat updates, coffee‑pairing, or short voice notes—reinforce psychological safety by providing frequent, low‑stakes opportunities for people to be seen and heard. These [[concepts/low_effort_touchpoints]] help maintain a safe atmosphere even when formal meetings are scarce.

## Practical Indicators
- Team members openly admit mistakes.
- Questions and new ideas are welcomed.
- Feedback is given constructively.
- Individuals feel comfortable reaching out with personal updates.
- New hires confidently use [[concepts/name_recall_strategies]] phrasing and ask safety‑related questions during [[concepts/orientation_day]] without hesitation.

## Benefits
- Higher engagement and innovation.
- Reduced fear of failure.
- Stronger interpersonal connections, leading to a resilient [[concepts/workplace_community]].
- Smoother onboarding experiences where apprentices feel safe to speak up about tools, PPE, and lockout/tagout procedures.
