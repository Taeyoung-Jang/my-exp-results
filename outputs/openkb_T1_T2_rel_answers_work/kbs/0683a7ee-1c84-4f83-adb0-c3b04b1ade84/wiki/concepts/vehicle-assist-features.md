## Vehicle Assist Features

Vehicle Assist Features are built‑in or add‑on technologies that reduce driver workload, increase situational awareness, and make tight‑city maneuvers—especially parking—more predictable and less stressful.

### Core Features Highlighted in [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]
- **Backup camera** – Provides a clear view of the area directly behind the vehicle, eliminating guesswork when reversing. [[entities/backup-camera]]
- **Parking sensors** – Emit audible alerts as the car approaches obstacles; sensitivity can be adjusted in the vehicle settings. [[entities/parking-sensors]]
- **360‑degree camera system** – Stitching of multiple cameras gives a bird’s‑eye view, making parallel parking almost effortless. [[entities/360-degree-camera]]
- **Automatic parking assist** – Takes over steering (and sometimes throttle) to guide the car into a spot with minimal driver input. [[entities/automatic-parking-assist]]
- **Light steering and small turning radius** – Mechanical design traits that allow the vehicle to pivot sharply, ideal for narrow streets.
- **Large windows and good mirror coverage** – Improves visual reference points; can be fine‑tuned with outward‑adjusted mirrors. [[entities/passenger-side-mirror]]

### Related Adjustments and Practices
- **Mirror and sensor adjustments** – Setting mirrors slightly farther outward and increasing sensor sensitivity help maintain clear curb cues. [[concepts/mirror-and-sensor-adjustments]]
- **Parallel‑parking routine** – The presence of these assist features integrates smoothly with a repeatable parking workflow, reducing reliance on pure visual estimation. [[concepts/parallel-parking-routine]]

### Why They Matter in Urban Driving
In dense city environments, space is limited and traffic flow is unpredictable. Vehicle Assist Features provide:
1. **Early information** about obstacles, allowing slower, more controlled maneuvers.
2. **Reduced mental load**, letting drivers focus on overall traffic rather than precise positioning.
3. **Increased safety**, as visual blind spots are mitigated by cameras and sensors.

By combining these technologies with proper mirror placement and calibrated sensor settings, drivers of compact cars—such as hatchbacks—can achieve smoother, more confident parking and navigation in tight streets.
