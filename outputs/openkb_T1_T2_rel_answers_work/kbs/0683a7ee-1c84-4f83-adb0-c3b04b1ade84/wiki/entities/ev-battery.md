# EV Battery

**Type:** product

**Summary:** The EV Battery provides the electric energy for plug‑in vehicles and is a major expense when it fails.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-06_2022-06-15]]:**
- Recognized as a classic [[concepts/big-ticket-repairs]] because replacement can cost several thousand dollars and adds little resale value.
- Likelihood of failure rises after roughly ten years of service or 150–200 k miles, similar to other major components.
- Early warning signs such as reduced range, charging anomalies, or dashboard alerts fall under [[concepts/early-warning-maintenance]].
- The high replacement cost influences [[concepts/ownership-decision-factors]] when owners decide whether to keep or replace the vehicle.
