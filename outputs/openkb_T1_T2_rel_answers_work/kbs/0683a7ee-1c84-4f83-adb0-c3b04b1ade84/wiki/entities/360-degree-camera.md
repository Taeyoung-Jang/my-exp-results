# 360‑Degree Camera

A **360‑degree camera** is a vehicle‑assist product that stitches feeds from multiple cameras around the car into a single overhead view. This surround‑view helps drivers see obstacles and curbs that are difficult to judge from a single perspective.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]
- Described as a feature that makes parallel parking "almost effortless" by removing the guesswork in tight urban spots.
- Recommended alongside other aids such as a [[entities/backup-camera]] and [[entities/parking-sensors]] for a comprehensive parking‑assist suite.
- Mentioned in the context of reducing stress while driving in dense city environments, especially when combined with proper [[concepts/mirror-and-sensor-adjustments]].

## Related concepts
- [[concepts/vehicle-assist-features]] – broader category that includes the 360‑degree camera, backup cameras, and automatic parking assist.
- [[concepts/mirror-and-sensor-adjustments]] – adjustments that complement the camera by improving visual cues and sensor feedback.
- [[concepts/parallel-parking-routine]] – the routine where a 360‑degree camera provides visual support for the visual checkpoints.

## Typical benefits
- Enhanced visibility of curbs, nearby vehicles, and pedestrians during low‑speed maneuvers.
- Reduced reliance on side mirrors and manual distance estimation.
- Greater confidence for drivers of small hatchbacks and other compact vehicles in tight spaces.
