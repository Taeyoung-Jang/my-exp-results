# Convex Stick‑On Mirror

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A small, adhesive‑backed convex mirror that attaches to the passenger‑side mirror, providing a wider field of view to help drivers see curbs, obstacles, and blind‑spot areas while parking in tight urban spaces.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]**:
- Recommended as a low‑cost add‑on to improve curb visibility during parallel parking.
- Placed on the passenger‑side mirror to reduce blind spots and give a clearer view of the curb without adding clutter.
- Works alongside other [[concepts/mirror-and-sensor-adjustments]] such as outward‑adjusted mirrors and calibrated parking sensors.
- Particularly useful for small hatchbacks with short rear overhangs, where precise visual cues are critical.
- Enhances the effectiveness of the [[concepts/parallel-parking-routine]] by providing an easy visual reference for the curb line.
