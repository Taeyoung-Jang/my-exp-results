# Backup Camera

A **backup camera** is an on‑board video system that displays what is behind the vehicle while reversing, helping drivers avoid obstacles and park more safely.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]
- Identified as a major **vehicle‑assist feature** that *takes away the guesswork in tight spots* during city driving. [[concepts/vehicle-assist-features]]
- Recommended alongside **parking sensors** for early warning of curbs and other obstacles. [[entities/parking-sensors]]
- Works in concert with **mirror and sensor adjustments** to improve curb visibility and reduce blind spots. [[concepts/mirror-and-sensor-adjustments]]
- Enhances the **parallel‑parking routine** by providing a clear view of the rear wheel and curb distance. [[concepts/parallel-parking-routine]]
- Mentioned as a less expensive alternative to a full **360‑degree camera** system, though both serve similar purposes. [[entities/360-degree-camera]]

## Related Adjustments & Add‑ons
- Pairing the backup camera with a **convex stick‑on mirror** on the passenger‑side can further improve curb sightlines. [[entities/convex-stick-on-mirror]]
- Properly setting the **passenger‑side mirror** outward reduces blind spots and complements the camera view. [[entities/passenger-side-mirror]]

## Benefits for City Driving
- Reduces stress by allowing drivers to *slow down earlier* and make smoother maneuvers.
- Increases confidence when parking in narrow streets or parallel‑parking in tight spaces.
- Provides visual feedback that supplements auditory cues from parking sensors.
