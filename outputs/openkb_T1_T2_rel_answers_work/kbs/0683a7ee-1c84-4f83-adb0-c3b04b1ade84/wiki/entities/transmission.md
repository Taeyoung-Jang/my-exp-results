# Transmission

**Type:** product

The transmission is a core vehicle **product** that transfers power from the **engine** to the drive wheels, enabling the car to change speed and torque.

## Role in ownership decisions
In the *session‑06* conversation, the transmission is highlighted as one of the major **big‑ticket repairs** that can push a driver toward replacing a vehicle.  When repair costs for the transmission approach the annual cost of a new car loan, owners often consider the car no longer predictable and may opt for a swap.

## Typical failure timeline
- **Age/mileage:** Problems become more likely after roughly **10 years** or **150–200 k miles**, though this varies by make and maintenance history.
- **Warning signs:** Rough or delayed shifting, unusual noises, slipping gears, and fluid leaks are early indicators that the transmission may need costly repair.

## Maintenance & early‑warning cues
Routine checks such as monitoring transmission fluid level and condition, and listening for shifting anomalies, are part of the [[concepts/early-warning-maintenance]] checklist recommended for keeping an eye on potential transmission issues.
