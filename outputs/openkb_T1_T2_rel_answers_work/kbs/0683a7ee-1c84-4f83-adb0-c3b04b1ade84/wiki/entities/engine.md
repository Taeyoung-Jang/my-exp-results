# Engine

**Type:** product

The engine is the heart of a car, converting fuel (or electricity in hybrid/EV designs) into mechanical power that drives the wheels. In the context of [[summaries/session-06_2022-06-15]], the engine is highlighted as one of the key components whose condition heavily influences ownership‑decision factors such as cost, reliability, and predictability.

---

## Role in ownership decisions

- **Cost vs. reliability:** When an engine starts requiring frequent repairs, its upkeep cost can approach the monthly payment of a newer vehicle, prompting owners to consider replacement.
- **Predictability threshold:** A rough or unstable idle, loss of power, or overheating signals that the engine may no longer be predictable, a core concept in [[concepts/ownership-decision-factors]].

---

## Common big‑ticket failure

Engine repair is listed among the classic [[concepts/big-ticket-repairs]] that often trigger a vehicle swap because the expense is high and resale value gains are minimal. Typical failure modes include:
- Major internal wear or seized components
- Blown head gasket or cracked block
- Catastrophic timing‑belt/chain failure

---

## Early‑warning signs (see [[concepts/early-warning-maintenance]])

- **Rough idle or knocking noises**
- **Higher than normal temperature gauge**
- **Reduced fuel efficiency**
- **Smoke from exhaust**
- **Check‑engine light that flickers or stays on**

Detecting these cues early lets owners address minor issues before they become costly engine overhauls.

---

## Related components

The engine interacts closely with other major systems:
- [[entities/transmission]] – transfers engine power to the wheels.
- [[entities/hybrid-battery]] and [[entities/ev-battery]] – supplement or replace engine power in hybrid/EV models.
- [[entities/suspension]] – may exhibit symptoms (e.g., vibrations) when the engine is misfiring.
- [[entities/air-conditioning-system]] – places additional load on the engine, especially when faulty.

---

## Maintenance tips

- Check engine oil level and quality monthly.
- Monitor coolant levels and watch for leaks.
- Listen for unusual sounds during normal drives.
- Respond promptly to any warning lights or temperature spikes.

Following a simple routine aligns with the checklist from the session and helps keep the engine— and the whole vehicle—operating predictably.
