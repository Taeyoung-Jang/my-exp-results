# Automatic Parking Assist

*Type:* Product

Automatic Parking Assist is a driver‑aid technology that can steer the vehicle into a parking space with little or no driver steering input. It is typically engaged when the vehicle detects a suitable parking spot and uses inputs from sensors and cameras to control steering, while the driver controls braking and acceleration.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]
- Mentioned as a **stress‑reducer** for city driving, especially in crowded streets.
- Works together with other [[concepts/vehicle-assist-features]] such as backup cameras and parking sensors to make parallel parking “almost effortless.”
- Recommended as a desirable upgrade for drivers seeking smoother, more predictable parking routines.
- Complements the step‑by‑step [[concepts/parallel-parking-routine]] by handling the steering portion, allowing the driver to focus on speed control and final adjustments.

## Related Features and Components
- Relies on inputs from [[entities/backup-camera]] and [[entities/parking-sensors]] to detect obstacles and curb proximity.
- Often paired with a [[entities/360-degree-camera]] system for a full surround view.
- Benefits from proper [[concepts/mirror-and-sensor-adjustments]] such as calibrated sensor sensitivity and optimal mirror positioning.

## Practical Benefits
- Reduces the need for precise visual cue timing when aligning the car to the curb.
- Lessens the chance of common mistakes like turning too early or reversing too fast.
- Particularly useful for small hatchbacks with short overhangs, where manual steering corrections can be more pronounced.
