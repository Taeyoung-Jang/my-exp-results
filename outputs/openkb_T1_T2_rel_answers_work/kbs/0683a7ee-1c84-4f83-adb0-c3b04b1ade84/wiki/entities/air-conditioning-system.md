# Air Conditioning System

## Overview
The air conditioning system (often shortened to A/C) is a **product** installed in automobiles to remove heat and humidity from the cabin, providing passenger comfort.

## Relevance in the session‑06 discussion
- Mentioned as one of the **big‑ticket repairs** that can push owners toward replacing a vehicle when the repair cost approaches the car’s residual value.  
- Highlighted as an **early‑warning maintenance** item: fluid leaks, loss of cooling performance, or unusual noises are signals to inspect the system before a major failure occurs.
- While not a primary cost driver for most owners, a failed A/C can be expensive to replace and may influence the **ownership‑decision‑factors** considered when evaluating whether to keep or replace a car.

## Typical failure signs
- Reduced cooling efficiency or strange odors when the A/C is on.
- Audible hissing or grinding noises from the compressor.
- Visible refrigerant leaks under the vehicle.
- Dashboard warning lights related to climate control flickering.

## Maintenance tips (aligned with the checklist from the session)
- **Inspect refrigerant levels** and hoses monthly, especially in hot climates.
- Look **under the car weekly** for signs of fluid drips.
- Respond promptly to any **warning light** that indicates A/C system issues.
- Have the system **diagnosed** at the first sign of performance loss to avoid costly component replacement.
