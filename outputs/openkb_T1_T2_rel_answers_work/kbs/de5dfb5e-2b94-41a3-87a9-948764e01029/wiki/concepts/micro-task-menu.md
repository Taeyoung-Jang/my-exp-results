# Micro‑Task Menu

**Definition**: A compact, categorized collection of tasks that can be completed in 5–20 minutes, designed to be used when a day is fragmented into many small gaps.

## Purpose
The Micro‑Task Menu helps people maintain productivity on unpredictable days by providing ready‑made options that fit into brief time slots, reducing decision‑fatigue and avoiding endless reshuffling of a larger to‑do list.

## Structure
- **Categories**: Typically 3–4 broad buckets such as:
  1. **Quick Communication** – short emails, messages, confirming appointments.
  2. **Mini‑Admin** – tidying a desk, filing a document, clearing a few inbox items.
  3. **Creative/Thinking Micro‑Tasks** – drafting a paragraph, sketching an idea, reviewing notes.
  4. **Maintenance** – updating a spreadsheet, checking a task board, performing a quick system check.
- **Item Limit**: Keep each category to 3–5 items, all realistically doable in ≤15 minutes.
- **Eligibility Rule**: Only include a task if it is useful and can be completed without needing extensive preparation or resources. Larger or more complex tasks belong on the regular to‑do list.

## How It Connects to Other Concepts
- The menu is used within the **[[concepts/flexible-blocks]]** framework, filling the small blocks that sit between larger **[[concepts/anchors]]**.
- By limiting the number of tasks, it supports the **two‑update rule** (morning plan and midday reset) described in the source summary.

## Creation Tips (from [[summaries/session-15_2025-06-16]])
1. Identify the types of micro‑tasks you frequently encounter.
2. Group them into the categories above.
3. Limit entries to keep the list lightweight.
4. Store the menu in an easily accessible place (notes app, sticky, or a digital checklist).
5. Review and refresh the menu during your scheduled plan updates.

## Benefits
- **Speed**: Choose a task instantly when a gap appears.
- **Focus**: Keeps you working rather than planning.
- **Flexibility**: Works with any day structure, especially highly fragmented schedules.
- **Mental Relief**: Reduces the feeling of a growing backlog.
