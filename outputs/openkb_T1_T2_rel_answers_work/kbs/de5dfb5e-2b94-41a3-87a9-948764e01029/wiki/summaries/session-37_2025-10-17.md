# Summary of session-37_2025-10-17

**Date:** 2025‑10‑17  

## Core ideas
- **Evening time blocks** can be shaped into intentional routines rather than default scrolling sessions.  
- A **[[concepts/transition-ritual]]** (e.g., 10 min to change clothes, get a drink) helps shift from work mode to personal time.  
- Two‑hour stretches can follow a **reset → meaningful activity → calming wind‑down** pattern.

## Template plans (concrete examples)
1. **Quiet creative**  
   - 10 min transition (change clothes, make tea)  
   - 40 min reading  
   - 30 min hands‑on creative (sketching, knitting, small project)  
   - 20 min soft landing (music, facial routine)  
2. **Active‑then‑cozy**  
   - 10 min walk or stretch  
   - 50 min simple cooking (e.g., pasta, bowl meal) and eating mindfully  
   - 30 min optional media (one episode) or book  
3. **Flexible energy‑based** (aligned with [[concepts/flexible-blocks]])  
   - If energized: 1 h of goal‑oriented activity (guitar, language, personal project) then shower + light reading.  
   - If tired: start with restorative activity (warm shower, reading) and only move to engaged task if energy returns.

## Scrolling cutoff rule
- Use a **mechanical cue**: place the phone across the room when the main activity begins.  
- Set a **single 10‑minute timer** (use [[entities/phone-timer]]) labeled “scroll time” for the evening’s end.  
- When the timer ends, the phone stays put and you transition to bed.  
- This approach removes reliance on willpower and follows [[concepts/scrolling-cutoff-mechanics]].

## Takeaway
By framing the two‑hour evening window with a simple structure and a concrete scrolling limit, you gain flexibility, avoid autopilot scrolling, and can adapt to varying energy levels each night.
