# Summary of session-15_2025-06-16

**Main contribution:** A set of realistic, adaptable time‑management tactics for days that are highly fragmented and constantly changing.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/anchors]]** – Core non‑negotiable tasks (or brief stabilising moments) that are scheduled regardless of chaos.
- **[[concepts/flexible-blocks]]** – Small, adaptable time slots (10–15 min or 30–60 min) that are filled with tasks after anchors.
- **midday reset** – A brief mid‑day review to adjust the plan, limiting plan changes to two moments per day.
- **two‑update rule** – Update the schedule only in the morning and at the midday reset to avoid endless reshuffling.
- **[[concepts/micro-task-menu]]** – A concise, categorized list of tasks that reliably fit into 5–20 min gaps.
- **task capture** – Quick “catch‑bin” method (notes app, voice memo, sticky) for interrupt‑driven ideas.

## Practical Advice
1. **Identify anchors** – Even a 5‑minute commitment (e.g., morning setup, short check‑in) serves as a stabilising point.
2. **Design flexible blocks** –
   - *10–15 min*: email replies, quick messages, tidying, small decisions.
   - *30–60 min*: writing, coding, deep reading, planning.
3. **Create a micro‑task menu** – Use 3‑4 categories (quick communication, admin, creative mini‑tasks, maintenance) with 3‑5 items each, all ≤15 min.
4. **Apply the two‑update rule** – Sketch a rough morning plan, pick one or two anchors, then adjust at the midday reset; avoid plan changes outside these windows.
5. **Capture interruptions efficiently** – Record a minimal cue (e.g., “email Sam about report”) in a trusted capture tool; sort during the next reset.
6. **Adapt to fragmented days** – Treat anchors as brief stabilising moments; rely on the micro‑task menu to fill frequent 5–20 min gaps.

## Template Example
```
Morning Anchor (5 min): Review top priority
Flexible Block 1 (10‑15 min): Choose a micro‑task
Flexible Block 2 (30‑60 min): Deep work task
Midday reset – update plan
Afternoon Anchor (5 min): Key meeting or call
Flexible Blocks …
Day End Cutoff – wrap up
```

## Benefits
- Provides structure without rigid scheduling.
- Reduces decision fatigue by pre‑defining task sizes.
- Limits context‑switching through the two‑update rule.
- Keeps a lightweight to‑do list via the micro‑task menu.
