# Sleep Hygiene and Seasonal Adjustments

**Definition**: Strategies for maintaining consistent, restorative sleep by adapting bedtime, wake time, and environmental cues to seasonal variations in daylight and temperature.

## Core Principles

1. **Consistent Wake Time**
   - Keeping the same wake‑up hour each day stabilises the body’s circadian rhythm, even when daylight hours shift.
2. **Season‑Adjusted Bedtime**
   - In winter, consider going to bed slightly earlier to match earlier evening darkness and reduced natural light exposure.
3. **Light Exposure**
   - Use bright indoor lighting or a sunrise‑style lamp immediately after waking to provide a strong zeitgeber when natural light is limited.  
   - If stepping outside is too cold, stand near a bright window or briefly open a door for a flash of outdoor light.
4. **Hydration Before Caffeine**
   - Drink water right after the light cue and before caffeine; this supports metabolism and prevents dehydration that can be exacerbated by dry winter air.
5. **Gentle Morning Movement**
   - A short (2‑3 min) stretch routine warms the body, reduces morning stiffness caused by colder temperatures, and reinforces the wake‑up signal.

## Seasonal Adjustments Highlighted in [[summaries/session-01_2025-04-15]]

- **Winter Mornings**: Emphasise a “ramp‑up” approach with soft lighting, brief indoor movement, and delayed caffeine (≈10 min after water).  
- **Very Cold Days**: If outdoor light is uncomfortable, substitute a bright window or a quick glance through an open door for the light cue while preserving movement and hydration steps.
- **Heat/Long‑Day Seasons**: While the source focused on cold conditions, the same principles apply in reverse—lighter meals, cooler bedroom temperatures, and maintaining consistent wake time.
