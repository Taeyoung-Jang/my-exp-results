# Morning Routine for Cold/Dark Days

**Definition**: A concise, low‑effort morning protocol designed to counteract the lethargy caused by low temperature and limited daylight, helping maintain sleep quality, mood, and energy.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Consistent Wake‑Time** – Keep the same wake‑up hour throughout the year; consider going to bed slightly earlier in winter to support natural circadian shifts. [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]]
2. **Immediate Light Exposure** – Turn on a bright indoor lamp or sunrise‑style light as soon as you sit up. If possible, step outside for 1–2 minutes of natural light; on very cold days, use a bright window or briefly open the door to catch outdoor light. [[concepts/light_therapy]]
3. **Gentle Movement** – Perform 2–3 minutes of easy stretches (overhead arm raises, shoulder rolls, slow squats) to warm the body and stimulate circulation.
4. **Hydration First** – Drink a glass of water right after the light and movement phase.
5. **Delayed Caffeine** – Wait roughly 10 minutes after water intake before consuming coffee or tea, allowing endogenous wake‑up hormones to act first.

---

## Step‑by‑Step 15–20 Minute Plan

| Time (min) | Action |
|------------|--------|
| 0–1        | Switch on bright lamp/sunrise light. |
| 1–3        | Gentle stretch sequence (arms, shoulders, squats). |
| 3–5        | If feasible, step outside for a minute of daylight; otherwise, stand by a bright window or open the door briefly. |
| 5–6        | Drink a glass of water. |
| 6–16       | Optional quiet activity (reading, journaling) while waiting. |
| 16–20      | Have coffee/tea. |

---

## Adaptations for Extremely Cold Mornings

- **Indoor Light Substitution**: Use the brightest window available and supplement with a strong indoor lamp.
- **Brief Outdoor Glimpse**: Open the door and stand just inside for 30 seconds to capture a flash of exterior light without fully exposing yourself to the cold.
- **Maintain Movement**: Keep the stretch portion unchanged; it provides essential warmth.

---

## Benefits

- Improves alertness by leveraging both *light* and *movement* cues.
- Supports consistent circadian rhythm despite seasonal daylight variation.
- Enhances hydration and optimal caffeine absorption.
- Flexible enough to accommodate harsh weather without skipping key cues.
