## Overview
This document captures a practical system for keeping social energy high with minimal planning. It introduces a **"deck of cards"** of 40 low‑effort activity ideas, a quick **60‑second pick rule**, reusable Notes templates, sample weekly schedules, ready‑to‑copy invitation texts, and a concise decision script to avoid overbooking.

## Activity Deck [[concepts/deck-of-cards]]
- 40 numbered "cards" split into **Weeknights (1‑20)** and **Weekends (21‑40)**.
- Each card is tagged with one of five categories: Social, Solo‑around‑people, Active, Food/Drink, Culture.
- Cards are designed to be within ~20 minutes travel and require no advance tickets.

## Low‑Effort Activity Ideas [[concepts/low-effort-activity-ideas]]
- Emphasize short, spontaneous outings (e.g., quick drink, 20‑minute walk, pop‑in to a gallery).
- Balance solo and social options to suit energy levels.

## 60‑Second Pick Rule [[concepts/pick-rule]]
1. Ask: *Do I have energy to talk to people?* → pick the first easy Social card.
2. If not, ask: *Can I leave the house at all?* → pick the easiest Solo‑around‑people card.
3. If still no, commit to a 10‑minute outing using the shortest Active or Food/Drink card and reassess.

## Notes Template (plain‑text)
```

• Sun daytime:

Deck (quick options to drop in)
• Social:
• Solo‑around‑people:
• Active:
• Food/Drink:
• Culture:
``` 
The template includes sections for pre‑filled plans, a reusable deck, and a rule for how many slots to lock.

## Sample Week (plain‑text)
- **Weeknight 1:** Quick drink at a nearby bar (Card 1)  
- **Weeknight 2:** Read/journal at a cozy café (Card 5)  
- **Weeknight 3 (optional):** Dessert stop on the way home (Card 11)  
- **Saturday daytime:** Farmers market wander with a friend (Card 21)  
- **Saturday night (optional):** Casual bar hop (Card 24)  
- **Sunday daytime:** Photo‑walk through an interesting block (Card 40)

## Copy‑Paste Invitation Texts (plain‑text)
1. “I’m heading to ___ around 7. Join if you feel like it.”  
2. “Quick drink at ___. Low‑key, come by if you’re free.”  
3. “Walking over to ___ for a short wander. Want to tag along?”  
4. “Tiny food crawl near ___. Swing through if you’re nearby.”  
5. “People‑watching at ___ tonight—zero pressure, drop in.”  
6. “Dessert stop at ___. Feel free to drop in.”  
7. “Exploring ___ this afternoon. Happy for company, but fine solo.”  
8. “I’ll be at ___ until about 9. Come say hi if you’re in the area.”  
9. “Last‑minute: heading to ___ in 10 min. Up for it?”  
10. “I’m checking out ___ tonight—super chill, join if you want.”

## Decision Script for Invitations (plain‑text)
1. **Check capacity:** *Is my week already at two weeknight and two weekend slots?* – If **no**, accept.  
2. **Prioritize:** If **yes**, ask *Do I want this more than one of my locked slots?* – If **no**, decline.  
3. **Swap:** If **yes**, choose which locked slot to replace.  
4. **Validate load:** *Will the swap keep my week fun, not overloaded?* – If **yes**, confirm; otherwise keep original plan.

## Scheduling Rule (plain‑text)
- **Lock exactly two weeknight and two weekend blocks by Sunday night.**  
- Any new invitation must **replace** a locked slot; you never add on top of the four.  
- This prevents over‑commitment when excitement spikes.

## Key Takeaways
- Use the **deck of cards** as a quick menu of low‑effort outings.  
- Apply the **60‑second pick rule** for on‑the‑spot decisions.  
- Pre‑fill a limited number of slots each week using the Notes template.  
- Communicate with concise, open‑ended invites and a decision script to stay in control.
