# Deck of Cards

The **Deck of Cards** is a curated set of 40 concrete, low‑effort activity ideas designed to help you keep social energy high without heavy planning. It originated in [[summaries/session-39_2024-05-12]] and is organized by:

- **Timeframe** – Weeknight (cards 1‑20) vs. Weekend (cards 21‑40)
- **Category** – Social, Solo‑around‑people, Active, Food/Drink, Culture
- **Constraints** – ≤ 20 minutes travel, no advance tickets, minimal prep

## Structure

| Card # | Description | Category |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Quick drink at a nearby bar you haven’t tried yet | Social |
| 2 | One‑hour dinner with a friend at a casual spot within 20 min | Social |
| 3 | Drop into a no‑RSVP meetup at a café or bar | Social |
| … | … | … |
| 20 | Try a new takeout spot and eat it outside somewhere lively | Food/Drink |
| 21 | Wander a farmers market with a friend | Social |
| 22 | Coffee walk with someone in a new‑to‑you neighborhood | Social |
| … | … | … |
| 40 | Do a photo‑walk through a visually interesting block | Active / Solo‑around‑people |

*Only a subset is shown for brevity; the full list follows the same pattern and is ready for copy‑paste into a notes app.*

## How It Works

1. **Deck metaphor** – Treat the list as a shuffled deck of cards. When you need a plan, draw the top card that fits your current energy level.
2. **Category suits** – Weeknights: pick one suit (category) and select the first feasible card. Weekends: pick two suits and combine cards (e.g., a brunch card + a walk card).
3. **Bias toward constraints** – All cards are pre‑screened for ≤ 20 min travel and no ticketing, aligning with the user’s stated preferences.

## Related Concepts

- The deck supports the [[concepts/pick-rule]], a 60‑second decision flow for choosing a card when you’re tired.
- It fits within a broader **low‑effort activity** framework described in [[concepts/low-effort-activity-ideas]].
- When you regularly host variations of these cards, you can evolve a **rotating‑hosting** routine, linked to [[concepts/rotating-hosting]].
- Consistently using the deck helps build stable **social routines**, as discussed in [[concepts/social-routines]].

## Usage Tips

- **Pre‑fill** two weeknight and two weekend slots each Sunday (see the scheduling rule in the source summary).
- Keep the deck in a notes app for quick access; you can copy‑paste the numbered list directly.
- When an invitation arrives, run the [[concepts/pick-rule]] decision script. If you need to swap, replace a pre‑filled slot rather than adding on top.
