# Low‑Effort Activity Ideas

**Definition**: A collection of easy, low‑commitment activities designed to keep social energy high while requiring little forethought, short travel time (≈20 minutes), and no advance ticketing.

## Source
This concept is derived from the ideas presented in [[summaries/session-39_2024-05-12]], which organized 40 concrete “cards” into weeknight and weekend options.

## Core Characteristics
- **Proximity**: All activities are within roughly a 20‑minute travel radius.
- **No Advance Booking**: No tickets, reservations, or RSVPs required.
- **Brief Duration**: Typically 30 minutes to 2 hours, allowing easy insertion into a day.
- **Flexible Social Context**: Includes both social (e.g., quick drink, bar hop) and solo‑around‑people options (e.g., café people‑watching).
- **Category Tags**: Each idea is labeled as Social, Solo‑around‑people, Active, Food/Drink, or Culture.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The full list of 40 specific ideas is organized as a **[[concepts/deck-of-cards]]** that can be shuffled and drawn on demand.
- When deciding on the spot, the **[[concepts/pick-rule]]** provides a 60‑second decision flow that prioritizes these low‑effort options.
- These activities support broader **[[concepts/social-routines]]** by offering repeatable, low‑stress outings that can be rotated throughout the week.

## Example Activities (selected from the deck)
- **Social**: Quick drink at a nearby bar you haven’t tried; casual bar hop in a new part of town.
- **Solo‑around‑people**: Read or journal at a cozy café; people‑watch at a busy café patio.
- **Active**: 20‑minute exploratory walk in a new neighborhood; short bike ride to a coffee shop.
- **Food/Drink**: Try a new happy‑hour spot; two‑stop mini food crawl on the same block.
- **Culture**: Browse a nearby bookstore until closing; drop into a gallery with evening hours.

These ideas can be mixed and matched, forming a reusable “menu” that keeps calendars full without causing burnout.
