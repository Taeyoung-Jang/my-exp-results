# Pick Rule

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A concise, 60‑second decision flow that helps you select a low‑effort activity from your "deck of cards" when you’re tired after work or otherwise low on energy.

## Core Steps (from [[summaries/session-39_2024-05-12]])
1. **Ask:** "Do I have the energy to talk to people?"   
   - **Yes:** Choose the first easy **Social** card from your deck (e.g., quick drink at a nearby bar).   
   - **No:** Continue to step 2.
2. **Ask:** "Can I leave the house at all?"   
   - **Yes:** Pick the easiest **Solo‑around‑people** card within 20 minutes (e.g., read or journal at a cozy café).   
   - **No:** Continue to step 3.
3. **Commit to a short outing:**   
   - Choose the shortest **Active** or **Food/Drink** card (e.g., a 10‑minute walk, grab a dessert).   
   - Leave the house for at least 10 minutes; once out, re‑evaluate and often you’ll continue the activity.

## Why It Works
- **Speed:** Designed to be completed in under a minute, preventing indecision.
- **Energy‑aligned:** Directly matches your current social energy level to the appropriate activity category.
- **Low friction:** Relies on pre‑defined cards, so you never need to brainstorm from scratch.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- It operates on the **[[concepts/deck-of-cards]]**, using the pre‑generated list of 40 weeknight/weekend ideas.
- It complements **[[concepts/low-effort-activity-ideas]]** by providing a rapid selection mechanism for those ideas.
- It can be integrated into a broader **[[concepts/social-routines]]** framework for maintaining consistent, energizing social habits.
