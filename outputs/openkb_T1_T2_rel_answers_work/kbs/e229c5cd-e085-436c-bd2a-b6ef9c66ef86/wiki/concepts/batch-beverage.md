# Batch Beverage Preparation

**Definition**: The process of creating a sizable, single‑batch drink (alcoholic or non‑alcoholic) in advance of an event, then storing and dispensing it efficiently for guests.

## Why it matters in event‑menu planning
- Saves time on the day of the event, allowing the host to focus on hot dishes and guest interaction.
- Guarantees consistent flavor and strength across servings.
- Reduces the need for bar staff or constant mixing, which is ideal for small gatherings of 25‑30 people.

## Core steps (make‑ahead cooking principle)
1. **Select a recipe that scales** – a mocktail or cocktail that tolerates dilution and chilling. The session‑08 menu uses a citrus‑ginger punch that fits a 2‑gallon dispenser.
2. **Calculate batch size** – aim for 2 gallons (256 oz) for 25 guests; increase to about 2.5 gallons for 30 guests.
3. **Mix base ingredients** – combine citrus juice, ginger syrup, and cranberry juice. This can be done the day before and stored in the refrigerator. [[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]]
4. **Add water** – fill the remainder of the dispenser with still water. Keep sparkling water separate to add just before service, preserving carbonation.
5. **Chill** – keep the base cold; add ice and sparkling water on the day of the event.
6. **Serve** – provide cups, garnishes (citrus slices), and a ladle or pump for self‑service.

## Example from the source document
The elevated menu in [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-12]] includes a batch mocktail recipe:
- 48 oz fresh citrus juice
- 24 oz ginger syrup
- 32 oz cranberry juice
- Still water to bring the total to 2 gallons
- Sparkling water (1–2 L) added at serving time

This drink is stored as a [[entities/mocktail]] base the night before and completed with sparkling water on the day of the event.

## Benefits
- **Consistency** – every glass has the same balance of sweet, tart, and spice.
- **Efficiency** – no need for per‑guest mixing; guests can self‑serve.
- **Flexibility** – the base can be adjusted for alcoholic variations by adding a spirit batch.
