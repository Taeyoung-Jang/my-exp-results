# Event Menu Planning

**Definition**: Event menu planning is the process of selecting, sizing, and sequencing dishes and beverages to create a cohesive dining experience that fits the event’s size, vibe, and logistical constraints.

## Core Elements (as illustrated in [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-12]])
- **Guest count & portions** – Use per‑person assumptions (e.g., 2 bone‑in chicken thighs per guest) to calculate total weights and counts.
- **Balanced course structure** – Appetizers, main proteins (including a vegetarian option), starch/grain, green salad, and a self‑serve drink station.
- **Make‑ahead potential** – Identify items that can be fully cooked the day before to reduce day‑of stress. See [[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]] for a deeper dive.
- **Batch beverage preparation** – Provide a non‑alcoholic mocktail in a large dispenser, complementing water and optional wine/beer. Relates to [[concepts/batch-beverage]].

## Practical Steps from the Session
1. **Determine protein quantities**
   - [[entities/chicken-thighs]]: 50 thighs (~19 lb) for 25 guests, 60 thighs (~23 lb) for 30 guests (2 thighs per person).
   - Provide a vegetarian counterpart such as a **mushroom‑leek galette** – [[entities/mushroom-leek-galette]].
2. **Calculate sides**
   - Grain: dry [[entities/farro]] 3 lb (25) / 3.5 lb (30).
   - Greens: 3.5 lb mixed tender greens (25) / 4.25 lb (30).
3. **Appetizer planning**
   - Olives and nuts (≈1.5 lb olives, 1 lb nuts for 25). 
   - Warm bite pieces (50–60 pieces) using mushrooms and cheese.
4. **Drink station**
   - Batch mocktail – [[entities/mocktail]] recipe for a 2‑gallon dispenser.
   - Include sparkling & still water, optional wine (white‑wine) – [[entities/white-wine]].
   - Dressings and cooking fats use [[entities/olive-oil]].

## Timeline
| Phase | Tasks |
|-------|-------|
| **Day‑before** | Braise chicken with aromatics and stock, bake galettes, cook farro, roast vegetables, prepare olives/nuts, assemble warm bites, mix mocktail base, portion dressing. |
| **Day‑of** | Reheat chicken and galettes, toss farro salad, dress green salad, add sparkling water to mocktail, arrange platters, serve. |

## Decision Factors
- **Space constraints** – Choose a main that holds well (braised chicken) rather than a large roasted fish.
- **Dietary inclusivity** – Provide a vegetarian main that mirrors the meat’s components.
- **Service style** – Self‑serve stations for drinks reduce staffing needs.

## Benefits
- Reduces on‑the‑day workload by front‑loading prep.
- Ensures consistent portion sizes and avoids shortages.
- Creates a menu that feels elevated yet remains practical for a 25–30‑person gathering.
