# Summary of Session 8 – Elevated Event Menu

**Context**: The user is planning a small‑scale event (25‑30 guests) and requested a concrete, store‑ready shopping list for an elevated menu featuring [[entities/chicken-thighs]] and a mushroom‑leek galette, plus sides, appetizers, and a batch mocktail. They also asked for clear *day‑before* tags and exact quantities without ranges.

## Key Concepts & Findings
- **[[concepts/event-menu-planning]]** for a mid‑size gathering emphasizes make‑ahead components and a single meat main with a vegetarian counterpart.  
- **[[entities/chicken-thighs]]**: assume 6 oz bone‑in thighs, 2 thighs per guest → 50 thighs (≈19 lb) for 25, 60 thighs (≈23 lb) for 30. Include sufficient braising liquid (3 qt stock + 750 ml wine for 25; 4 qt stock + 1 L wine for 30) to keep the meat moist in a chafing dish.  
- **[[entities/mushroom-leek-galette]]** as the vegetarian main; use 2‑3 pastry sheets, 4–6 lb mushrooms, 6–9 leeks, and 8–12 oz shredded cheese.  
- **[[entities/farro]]** salad: 3 lb dry farro (25) / 3.5 lb (30) with 6–7 lb roasted vegetables; dressing of olive oil and lemon juice.  
- **Green salad**: 3.5 lb greens (25) / 4.25 lb (30) with cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, and vinaigrette.  
- **Appetizers**: marinated olives, mixed nuts, and 50/60 warm bite pieces (tart squares or stuffed mushrooms).  
- **[[entities/mocktail]]** (2‑gallon dispenser): 48 oz citrus juice, 24 oz ginger syrup, 32 oz cranberry juice, topped with still water; optional extra batch for 30 guests.  
- **[[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]]** tags identify items that can be fully cooked or assembled a day earlier, reducing day‑of workload.

## Day‑Before Preparation (✓) 
- Fully braise the chicken thighs and store in their liquid.  
- Bake or par‑bake the mushroom‑leek galettes.  
- Cook the farro, roast the vegetables, and prepare the olive‑oil dressing (keep separate).  
- Prepare olives & nuts, season and refrigerate.  
- Make the warm bite pieces; cool and store.  
- Mix the mocktail base (citrus, ginger, cranberry) and chill.

## Day‑Of Execution (✗) 
- Reheat the braised chicken and galettes.  
- Toss the farro salad with roasted veg and dressing.  
- Assemble the green salad, dress lightly.  
- Add sparkling water to the mocktail, serve.  
- Plate appetizers and open the chafing dish.
