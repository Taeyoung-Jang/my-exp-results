# Clear Matte Varnish

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** A clear, matte-finish brush‑on varnish used to seal finished air‑dry clay pieces with minimal sanding.

## Key facts from session‑37 (2025‑03‑11)

- Recommended as the final protective coating for pinch‑pot or dish projects made with [[entities/das-clay]] or [[entities/crayola-air-dry-clay]].
- Applied directly after optional [[entities/acrylic-paint]] without needing sanding, supporting a [[concepts/low-mess-creative-setup]].
- Provides a durable, non‑glossy finish while keeping the workflow simple for users with [[concepts/shaky-hand-accommodations]].

## Usage tips

1. Ensure the clay piece is completely dry.
2. Apply a thin, even layer with a soft brush.
3. Allow to dry according to the manufacturer’s instructions; a second coat can be added for extra protection.

## Related concepts & entities

- Part of the finishing step in the air‑dry clay hand‑building workflow described in [[summaries/session-37_2025-03-11]].
- Often used together with [[entities/acrylic-paint]] for color and [[entities/clear-matte-varnish]] for seal.
- Complements low‑mess tools such as the [[entities/silicone-baking-mat]] and [[entities/rubber-clay-tool]].
