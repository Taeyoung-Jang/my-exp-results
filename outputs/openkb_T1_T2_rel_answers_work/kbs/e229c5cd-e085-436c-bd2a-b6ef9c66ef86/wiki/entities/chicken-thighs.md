# Chicken Thighs

**Entity**: [[entities/chicken-thighs]] – bone‑in, skin‑on chicken thigh, typically ~6 oz each.

## Role in the Event Menu
- Featured as the **meat main** in the elevated menu for the 2024‑06‑12 event (see [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-12]]).
- Chosen for its ability to stay moist when **braised** and to hold flavor in a chafing dish, fitting the needs of [[concepts/event-menu-planning]].

## Quantities
- **25 guests**: 50 thighs (≈19 lb).
- **30 guests**: 60 thighs (≈23 lb).
- Served **2 thighs per person** to ensure generous portions after cooking loss.

## Braising Components (Make‑Ahead)
- Aromatics: onions, carrots, celery, garlic.
- Liquid: 3 qt chicken stock + 1 × 750 ml white wine for 25; 4 qt stock + 1 L wine for 30.
- Herbs & seasoning (olive‑oil, salt, pepper) – see [[entities/olive-oil]] and [[entities/white-wine]].
- All ingredients can be assembled and **braised the day before**, then reheated on the event day, demonstrating [[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]].

## Storage & Reheat
- Cool in its braising liquid, store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.
- Reheat gently in the same liquid in a chafing dish to prevent drying.

## Related Items
- Often paired with a vegetarian main such as the [[entities/mushroom-leek-galette]].
- Served alongside side dishes like [[entities/farro]] salad and a mocktail beverage ([[entities/mocktail]]).
