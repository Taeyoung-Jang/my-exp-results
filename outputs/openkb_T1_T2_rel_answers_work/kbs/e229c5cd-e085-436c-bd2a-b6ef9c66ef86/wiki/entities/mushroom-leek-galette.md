# Mushroom‑Leek Galette

**Type**: work (vegetarian main dish)

The *Mushroom‑Leek Galette* is a savory, flaky pastry filled with sautéed mushrooms, leeks, and shredded cheese. It was proposed in the **session‑08_2024‑06‑12** event menu as the vegetarian counterpart to the braised chicken thighs.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Portioning**: Two galettes for 25 guests, three for 30 guests. Each galette serves roughly 12‑13 people.
- **Ingredients (per galette)**:
  - Pastry sheet (store‑bought puff or short‑crust) – 1 sheet per galette
  - Mushrooms – 2 lb per galette (total 4 lb for 25, 6 lb for 30)
  - Leeks – 3 large leeks per galette (6 large for 25, 9 large for 30)
  - Shredded cheese (gruyère or cheddar) – 4 oz per galette (8 oz total for 25, 12 oz for 30)
  - Olive oil – used for sautéing the filling [[entities/olive-oil]]
- **Make‑ahead**: The filling can be prepared a day before, and the galettes can be fully baked or par‑baked the day before and simply reheated on the day of the event. This aligns with the [[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]] approach.
- **Serving**: Warmed briefly in a moderate oven before plating; can be served alongside the farro salad and green salad.

## Preparation Overview
1. **Sauté** the mushrooms and leeks in olive oil until softened; season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
2. **Combine** with shredded cheese to create the filling.
3. **Lay** a pastry sheet on a baking tray, spread the filling, fold edges to create a rustic edge.
4. **Bake** at 375 °F (190 °C) for 25‑30 minutes until the crust is golden.
5. **Cool** and store in airtight containers if making a day ahead; reheat at 300 °F for 10‑15 minutes before serving.
