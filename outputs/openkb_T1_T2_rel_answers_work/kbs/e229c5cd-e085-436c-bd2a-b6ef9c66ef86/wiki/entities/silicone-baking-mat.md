# Silicone Baking Mat

**Type:** product

**Description:** A flexible, heat‑resistant silicone sheet with a non‑stick surface, commonly used in kitchens and crafting to provide a clean, reusable work area.

## Key Facts from Session 37 (2025‑03‑11)
- Recommended as the primary **[[concepts/low-mess-creative-setup]]** for hand‑building air‑dry clay in a small apartment.
- Works well with the **[[concepts/air-dry-clay-hand-building]]** approach because it contains clay crumbs and moisture, preventing mess on furniture.
- Its smooth surface pairs nicely with the **[[concepts/shaky-hand-accommodations]]** strategy, letting users press and smooth clay with a palm or sponge without fine finger control.
- Suggested alongside a block of **[[entities/das-clay]]** or **[[entities/crayola-air-dry-clay]]**, a set of **[[entities/rubber-clay-tool]]**, and a kitchen sponge for shaping and smoothing.
