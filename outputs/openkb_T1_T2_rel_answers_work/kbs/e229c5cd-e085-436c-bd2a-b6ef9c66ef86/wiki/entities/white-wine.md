# White Wine (750 ml)

A 750 ml bottle of dry white wine, selected for its acidity and flavor profile, used as a key component of the braising liquid for the [[entities/chicken-thighs]] in the elevated menu plan.

## Role in the Event Menu
- **Braising liquid**: Combined with chicken stock, onions, carrots, celery, and herbs to create a moist cooking environment for the chicken thighs, ensuring they stay tender and flavorful.
- **Quantity**:
  - For 25 guests: 1 × 750 ml bottle.
  - For 30 guests: 1 × 1 L bottle (rounded up to a standard size).
- **Day‑before preparation**: The wine is added to the braising pot on the day before, allowing the flavors to meld while the chicken cooks low‑and‑slow.

## Related Concepts & Entities
- Part of the overall [[concepts/event-menu-planning]] and [[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]] strategy for the event.
- Complementary to the [[entities/olive-oil]] used for sautéing aromatics.
- Mentioned in the [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-12]] summary of the planning document.

## Notes
- The wine is not used in the [[entities/mocktail]]; its purpose is strictly culinary.
- Choose a dry white such as Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Grigio to avoid overly sweet notes in the braise.
