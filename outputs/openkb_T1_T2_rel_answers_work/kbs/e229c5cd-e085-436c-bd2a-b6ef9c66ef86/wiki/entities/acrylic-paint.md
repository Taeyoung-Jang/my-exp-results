# Acrylic Paint

**Type:** product

Acrylic paint is a fast‑drying, water‑based paint commonly used to add colour and finish to crafts such as air‑dry clay pieces. In the context of the low‑mess, shaky‑hand‑friendly clay setup described in [[summaries/session-37_2025-03-11]], acrylic paint is recommended because it adheres directly to dry clay without requiring extensive sanding or priming.

## Key Facts from Session 37
- **Direct application:** The paint can be brushed onto dried clay surfaces straight away, simplifying the finishing process.
- **Compatibility with low‑mess setup:** Works well with the [[concepts/low-mess-creative-setup]] approach, avoiding extra steps that could create dust.
- **Complementary sealing:** After painting, a clear matte varnish (see [[entities/clear-matte-varnish]]) is applied to protect the colour and add durability.
- **Shaky‑hand accommodation:** Because acrylic paint is applied with broad brush strokes, it aligns with [[concepts/shaky-hand-accommodations]] and does not demand fine motor control.

## Usage Tips
1. **Choose a brush size** that matches the scale of the piece; larger brushes reduce the need for steady hand movements.
2. **Apply thin, even coats** to avoid drips; let each coat dry before applying the next.
3. **Seal with clear matte varnish** for a durable, low‑glare finish.
