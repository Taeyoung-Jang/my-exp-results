## Farro

Farro is an ancient cereal grain known for its chewy texture and nutty flavor. It was selected for the **elevated event menu** as a substantial, make‑ahead side that pairs well with both the braised chicken thighs and the mushroom‑leek galette.

### Key facts from the session‑08_2024-06-12 document
- **Quantity**: 3 lb for 25 guests; 3.5 lb for 30 guests (dry weight).  
- **Role**: Forms the base of a warm farro‑and‑roasted‑vegetable salad, dressed with olive oil and lemon juice.  
- **Preparation**: Can be cooked the day before; keep separate from vegetables and dressing until the day of serving.  
- **Related items**: Uses **olive oil** for the dressing and pairs with the **mushroom‑leek galette** and **braised chicken thighs**.

### Usage tips for events
1. **Cook in bulk**: Rinse the grain, simmer in water (2 : 1 ratio) for about 20‑25 minutes until al dente.  
2. **Cool quickly**: Drain and spread on a tray to stop cooking; refrigerate overnight.  
3. **Combine on service day**: Toss with roasted vegetables, dressing, and a pinch of salt just before guests arrive.

Farro provides a gluten‑containing but nutritionally dense alternative to rice or quinoa, adding visual appeal and a satisfying bite to the menu.
