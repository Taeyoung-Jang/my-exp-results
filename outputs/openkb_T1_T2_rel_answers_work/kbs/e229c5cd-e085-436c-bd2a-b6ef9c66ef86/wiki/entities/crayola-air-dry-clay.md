# Crayola Air‑Dry Clay

Crayola Air‑Dry Clay is a pre‑mixed, oven‑free modeling material that dries to a solid, lightweight form. It is marketed for beginners and hobbyists who want a **low‑mess** and easy‑to‑use clay.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-03-11]]
- Recommended as a **sturdy, non‑fussy** option for users with shaky hands.
- Works well with the **hand‑building** approach described in [[concepts/air-dry-clay-hand-building]].
- Pairs nicely with a **low‑mess creative setup**: silicone baking mat, chunky‑handle tools, and damp sponges (see [[concepts/low-mess-creative-setup]]).
- When combined with the suggested tools—such as [[entities/rubber-clay-tool]] and a [[entities/silicone-baking-mat]]—the clay can be shaped using broad palm motions, reducing the need for fine finger control.
- Finishing can be done with **acrylic paint** ([[entities/acrylic-paint]]) and sealed with **clear matte varnish** ([[entities/clear-matte-varnish]]) without extensive sanding.

## Typical Uses
- Creating pinch pots, small dishes, or decorative objects.
- Projects where the creator prefers **shaky‑hand accommodations** (see [[concepts/shaky-hand-accommodations]]).
- Activities that emphasize tactile, rhythmic creation rather than detailed sculpting.

## Practical Tips
1. Keep a **damp sponge** handy for smoothing surfaces; it eliminates the need for dry sanding and minimizes dust.
2. Work on a **silicone baking mat** to contain scraps and make cleanup easy.
3. Use tools with **large, ergonomic handles** (e.g., [[entities/rubber-clay-tool]]) to reduce grip strain.
4. After drying, apply acrylic paint directly, then seal with clear matte varnish for durability.
