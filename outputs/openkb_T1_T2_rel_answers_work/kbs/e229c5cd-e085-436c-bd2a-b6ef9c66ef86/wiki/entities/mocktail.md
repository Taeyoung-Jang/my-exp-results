# Mocktail (Citrus‑Ginger Punch)

**Type**: product

The Citrus‑Ginger Punch mocktail is a non‑alcoholic batch beverage designed for a small event of 25‑30 people. It combines fresh citrus juice, ginger syrup, cranberry juice, and still water, topped with sparkling water at serving time.

## Key Facts (from [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-12]])
- **Quantity**: 2‑gallon dispenser (256 oz total). For 25 guests: 48 oz citrus juice, 24 oz ginger syrup, 32 oz cranberry juice; for 30 guests the recipe can be scaled up to 60 oz citrus, 32 oz ginger, 40 oz cranberry.
- **Preparation**: All liquid ingredients (citrus juice, ginger syrup, cranberry juice) are mixed the day before and chilled. Sparkling water is added on the day of service.
- **Serving**: Presented in a dispenser with ice, optional citrus garnish slices, and a separate container of sparkling water for guests to top up.
- **Related Concepts**: [[concepts/batch-beverage]] for the batch preparation method and [[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]] for the day‑before mixing.
- **Related Entities**: [[entities/mocktail]] (self‑reference), [[entities/olive-oil]] (used elsewhere in the menu), [[entities/white-wine]] (used in the braised chicken).

## Usage in Event Menu
The mocktail serves as the non‑alcoholic centerpiece of the drink station, complementing wine, beer, and water. Its bright citrus‑ginger flavor balances the savory menu items and offers a refreshing option for all guests.
