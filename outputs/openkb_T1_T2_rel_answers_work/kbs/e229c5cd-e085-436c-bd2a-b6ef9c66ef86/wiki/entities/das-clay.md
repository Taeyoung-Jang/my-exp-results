# DAS Air‑Dry Clay (product)

**Type:** product

## Overview
DAS Air‑Dry Clay is a white, non‑kiln‑firing clay that hardens at room temperature. It stays soft enough for broad, palm‑based shaping while drying to a solid, durable finish.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-03-11]]
- Recommended by the assistant as a **sturdy, low‑mess** option for users with shaky hands.
- Works well with a **low‑mess creative setup**: silicone baking mat, damp sponge, and chunky‑handle tools.
- Allows the maker to rely on **hand‑building** motions rather than fine finger work.
- Pairs nicely with finishing options like **acrylic paint** and **clear matte varnish** without extensive sanding.

## Recommended Setup
- **Work surface:** [[entities/silicone-baking-mat]] to contain crumbs and protect tables.
- **Tools:** [[entities/rubber-clay-tool]] or other wooden/rubber tools with large, rounded handles.
- **Cleaning:** A small bowl of water and a soft kitchen sponge for smoothing and cleanup.

## Benefits for Shaky‑Hand Accommodations
- The clay’s softness lets users **press and roll** with palms, reducing the need for precise finger control.
- Textural irregularities from minor tremors become **characterful surface details** rather than flaws.
- Using a **damp sponge** for smoothing eliminates the need for dry sanding, keeping the workspace dust‑free.
