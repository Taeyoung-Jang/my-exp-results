# Olive Oil (product)

Olive oil is a liquid fat extracted from olives, prized for its flavor and versatility in cooking. In the **elevated menu** outlined for a 25‑30‑person event, olive oil serves several key roles:

- **Dressing the farro salad** – 1 cup is listed for 25 guests (1.25 cups for 30) to emulsify the lemon‑juice dressing.  
- **Sautéing aromatics** – used with onions, carrots, and celery when braising the chicken thighs.  
- **General seasoning** – added to roasted vegetables and the mushroom‑leek galette.

The ingredient is part of the **make‑ahead cooking** strategy ([[concepts/make-ahead-cooking]]) because the dressing can be prepared the day before and stored. It also contributes to the **batch beverage** preparation as a possible optional garnish for the mocktail, though the documented recipe does not call for it directly.

## Quantities (from the session‑08 menu)
- **25 guests**: 1 cup (≈ 8 oz) olive oil
- **30 guests**: 1.25 cups (≈ 10 oz) olive oil
