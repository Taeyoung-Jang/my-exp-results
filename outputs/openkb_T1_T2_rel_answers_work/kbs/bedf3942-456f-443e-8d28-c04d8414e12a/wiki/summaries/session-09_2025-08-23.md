**Summary**

This document captures a conversation about designing hands‑on science labs that balance excitement with safety, focusing on a **15‑minute opening script** for a sophomore dissection lab. It outlines strategies such as **workspace zones** and **role assignment**, a **quick safety demonstration** using [[entities/goggles]], [[entities/gloves]], [[entities/scalpel]], the [[entities/sink]] and the [[entities/waste-bin]], and a **group safety‑pass** system, and provides a printable checklist with timestamps and a safety‑pass card.

**Findings & ideas**  
- Structure the first 10‑15 min as a “reveal” to spark curiosity before rules.  
- Demonstrate the scalpel‑set‑down motion and let students mime it.  
- Use a short, firm safety briefing covering goggles, gloves, and waste handling.  
- Implement a **group safety pass** card with three criteria: safe set‑down, waste location, and ready protective gear.  
- Conduct a rapid tabletop check (≈4 min) to verify each group meets the criteria before handing out tools.
