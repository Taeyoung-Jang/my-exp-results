# Summary of Session 37 (2025-09-13)

**Main Topic:** Best practices for resigning from a job while protecting future reputation and ensuring smooth final‑pay processing.

## Key Points

- **Professional Notice**: Provide written resignation with a polite tone, clear end date, and reference any contractual obligations (e.g., non‑competes, equipment return).
- **Transition Handoff**: Document current work, create concise handoff notes, and ensure colleagues know where to find resources to avoid post‑departure blame.
- **Final Pay & Benefits**:
  - Verify PTO/vacation payout policy.
  - Confirm inclusion of commissions, bonuses, and reimbursements.
  - Ask about the timing of the final paycheck.
  - Determine health insurance end date and request COBRA paperwork.
  - Review 401(k) rollover options.
- **Equipment & Access Return**:
  - Return physical items (laptop, phone, badge, keys, credit cards, tools).
  - Disable digital access (log out, hand over passwords, return security tokens).
  - Understand that many employers tie final‑pay release to confirmed return of property.
- **HR Confirmation**: Request written confirmation of:
  - Items returned.
  - Date of final paycheck processing.
  - PTO payout status.
  - Health coverage termination and COBRA details.

## Practical Output

- **Email Draft**: A concise template to email HR confirming badge/key return and asking for written confirmation of the above items.
