# Hands‑On Science Activities

## Definition
Hands‑On Science Activities are practical, inquiry‑based experiences where learners directly manipulate materials, tools, and specimens to explore scientific concepts.

## Key Elements (from [[summaries/session-09_2025-08-23]])
- **Balance curiosity and overwhelm** – provide enough structure to keep students confident while preserving the sense of discovery.
- **Workspace zones** – clearly defined areas for materials, messy work, and finished products to prevent chaos.
- **Role assignment** – simple group roles (gatherer, measurer, recorder) focus attention and reduce competition for tools.
- **Brief safety demonstration** – a quick, visual demo of the safe scalpel‑set‑down motion, goggles, gloves, and waste handling.
- **Group safety pass** – a three‑item checklist (safe set‑down, waste location, protective gear ready) that must be demonstrated before tools are released.
- **Printable checklist** – a minute‑by‑minute script for the first 10‑15 minutes of a dissection lab that reinforces the above practices.
