# Scalpel

**Type:** Product  

**Description:** A handheld cutting instrument with a thin blade, used for precise dissection in biology labs.

**Key points from session‑09 (2025‑08‑23):**
- Demonstrated during the first 10‑15 minutes of the lab to model safe handling.
- Safety script emphasizes the **scalpel set‑down motion**: blade points down, placed on the tray, moved only when both group members know its location.
- Students mime the motion with empty hands before receiving the real tool.
- Integrated into the **Group Safety Pass**: students must show the set‑down motion, have goggles and gloves on, and know the waste location before receiving the scalpel.
- Placed on the workspace tray; sharp items stay on the tray until disposal.
