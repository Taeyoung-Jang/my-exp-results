## Lab Sink

The **lab sink** is the designated washing station in a science classroom where students clean dissection tools, gloves, and any spilled materials. In the *Dissection Lab Opening Checklist* (see [[summaries/session-09_2025-08-23]]), the sink is highlighted as part of the quick safety routine that reminds students where to rinse sharps like the [[entities/scalpel]] and dispose of liquid waste.

### Role in the session
- Mentioned during the 4‑6 minute safety segment to show where students should place wet tools and rinse their hands after handling specimens.
- Reinforces the [[concepts/lab-safety-protocol]] by providing a clear location for immediate cleanup, reducing the risk of cross‑contamination.
- Integrated into the **workspace zones** concept, separating the "cleaning zone" (sink) from the "messy zone" where specimens are handled.

### Connections to other elements
- Students are instructed to have their [[entities/gloves]] and [[entities/goggles]] on before moving to the sink for rinsing.
- Waste that cannot be washed down the drain is placed in the [[entities/waste-bin]] nearby.
- The sink supports the overall goal of engaging [[concepts/hands-on-science-activities]] while maintaining a safe, orderly environment.

### Practical tips
1. Keep a towel and disinfectant nearby for quick wipe‑downs.
2. Ensure the sink area is uncluttered so students can focus on proper cleaning motions.
3. Verify that each group demonstrates the rinse step during the "group safety pass" check.
