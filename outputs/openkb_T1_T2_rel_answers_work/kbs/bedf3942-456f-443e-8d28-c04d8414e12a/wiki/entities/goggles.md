## Laboratory Goggles

**Type:** product  

**Description:** Clear, impact‑resistant eye protection used in science labs, especially during dissection activities.

### Role in the session-09_2025-08-23 dissection lab
- Required before any student can handle a [[entities/scalpel]] or other sharp tools.
- Mentioned in the opening script as part of the three safety criteria on the **Group Safety Pass Card**.
- Students must have goggles **on**, gloves **on**, and a clear workspace before receiving tools.

### Safety connections
- Part of the overall [[concepts/lab-safety-protocol]] discussed in the session.
- Works together with [[entities/gloves]] and the designated [[entities/waste-bin]] and [[entities/sink]] cleanup stations.
- Helps maintain a safe environment that supports [[concepts/hands-on-science-activities]] without overwhelming students.
