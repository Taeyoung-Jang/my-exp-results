# Waste Bin

A **waste bin** is a designated container used in laboratory environments to collect used or disposable items, especially sharps such as scalpel blades, as well as any other waste generated during hands‑on activities.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-09_2025-08-23]]
- **Location and role**: Placed at each workstation as part of the **workspace zones** strategy to keep the mess organized and to provide a clear drop‑off point for contaminated materials.
- **Safety integration**: Included in the brief safety briefing that covers the **lab‑safety‑protocol**; students are instructed to place *sharps* (e.g., [[entities/scalpel]]) on the tray and then deposit them in the waste bin.
- **Checklist mention**: The opening checklist explicitly tells students, “*Waste goes here*,” and the *group safety pass* requires students to point out the correct waste location before receiving tools.
- **Related equipment**: Works in conjunction with other safety gear such as [[entities/gloves]] and [[entities/goggles]] and is situated near the [[entities/sink]] for easy cleanup.

## Connections
- Part of the broader **[[concepts/hands-on-science-activities]]** approach that balances excitement with order.
- Reinforces the **[[concepts/lab-safety-protocol]]** by providing a dedicated spot for disposal of hazardous items.
- Helps define **[[concepts/workspace-zones]]** by demarcating the “messy” area of the lab.
