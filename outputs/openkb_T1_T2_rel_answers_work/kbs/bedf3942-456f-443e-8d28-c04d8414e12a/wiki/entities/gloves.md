# Laboratory Gloves

Laboratory gloves are disposable protective hand coverings used in science labs, especially in dissection activities, to prevent contamination and protect students from sharp tools and biological specimens.

## Role in the Session‑09 Dissection Lab

- **Safety prerequisite**: In the opening checklist of [[summaries/session-09_2025-08-23]], gloves are required before any student may handle a [[entities/scalpel]] or other sharp instruments.
- **Group Safety Pass**: One of the three criteria on the “Group Safety Pass Card” is that each group must have gloves on, along with goggles and a clear workspace.
- **Integration with other safety items**: Gloves are paired with [[entities/goggles]] and a clean tray; waste is directed to the [[entities/waste-bin]] and liquid to the [[entities/sink]].

## Connection to Broader Concepts

- Demonstrates the principle of [[concepts/lab-safety-protocol]] by providing a barrier against cuts and contamination.
- Supports [[concepts/hands-on-science-activities]] by allowing students to handle specimens safely, enabling active learning.
- Fits within the idea of [[concepts/workspace-zones]] where protective gear defines the “clean” zone.

## Practical Notes

- Gloves should be put on **before** any demonstration of the scalpel set‑down motion.
- Inspect gloves for tears before the activity begins; replace any damaged pair.
- Dispose of gloves in the designated waste receptacle after the lab.
