## iMessage

**Type:** Product

### Overview
iMessage is Apple's proprietary messaging service that allows users to send texts, photos, videos, and other data over the internet between iOS and macOS devices. It supports features such as read receipts, typing indicators, and iMessage apps.

### Mention in [[summaries/session-35_2025-09-17]]
In the conversation documented in the summary, the user asked for a "super‑short one‑sentence version" that would fit in an iMessage preview. The assistant provided two concise messages tailored for close friends and for acquaintances/coworkers, explicitly referencing the message length constraints of iMessage.
