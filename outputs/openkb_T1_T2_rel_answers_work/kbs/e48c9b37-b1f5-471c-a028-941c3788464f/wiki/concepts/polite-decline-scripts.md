## Polite Decline Scripts

**Definition**: A set of short, friendly wording patterns that let you turn down last‑minute social invitations while maintaining warmth and avoiding the perception of being flaky.

**Purpose**: Supports a healthy balance between social engagement and personal downtime by giving you ready‑to‑use responses that respect both your needs and the inviter’s feelings.

### Core Principles
1. **Honesty with brevity** – State your current need (e.g., “recharging” or “keeping tonight low‑key”) without over‑explaining.
2. **Positive framing** – Express appreciation for the invitation and suggest an alternative time.
3. **Consistency** – Using a repeatable line reduces decision fatigue and reinforces your boundary.
4. **Tailoring to relationship** – Slightly warmer language for close friends, more neutral for acquaintances.

### Sample Scripts from [[summaries/session-12_2025-06-17]]
- General: "I’m keeping tonight low‑key, let’s plan another time."
- Close friend: "I wish I had the energy, but I’m wiped. Rain check? I want to be present when we hang out."
- Alternative phrasing: "That sounds fun, but I’m recharging tonight. What does your week look like?"
- Quick decline: "I’m laying low tonight, but I’d love to catch up later this week."

### How They Fit Into Broader Practices
Polite decline scripts complement **[[concepts/calendar-based-self-care]]** by turning downtime into a scheduled, defendable commitment, and they reinforce the **[[concepts/social-downtime-balance]]** approach of monitoring energy levels before saying yes.

### Implementation Tips
- **Prepare a list** of 2‑3 scripts that feel authentic; keep them in your phone’s notes for quick copy‑paste.
- **Match tone** to the inviter: use warmer language with close friends, keep it neutral with acquaintances.
- **Follow‑up**: When you suggest an alternative, propose a concrete future time to keep the connection alive.
- **Practice the pause**: Before replying, pause, check your calendar, and then select the appropriate script.

By integrating these scripts into daily communication, you can protect your personal energy while preserving healthy social relationships.
