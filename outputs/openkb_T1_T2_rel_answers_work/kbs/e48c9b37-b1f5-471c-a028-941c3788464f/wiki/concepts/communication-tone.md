# Communication Tone

**Definition**: The deliberate selection of language, formality, humor, and empathy to suit a specific audience and purpose when crafting messages.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Audience Segmentation** – Tailoring tone for close friends, acquaintances, or professional contacts. In the *session-35* interaction, three distinct tones were produced:
   - Super‑casual/funny for friends.
   - Sincere for personal acquaintances.
   - Neutral, concise for coworkers.
2. **Formality vs. Informality** – Adjusting the level of formality influences perceived warmth and respect. The casual version used playful metaphors ("pigeon", "bakery‑time"), while the neutral version employed straightforward language.
3. **Emotional Framing** – Even brief notes can convey care. The sincere draft expressed empathy and explained the schedule change, avoiding the impression of neglect.
4. **Length Constraints** – Tone must be maintained even in ultra‑short formats, such as a single‑sentence iMessage preview.

## Application in Session 35

The assistant generated multiple message variants based on the user’s request, demonstrating practical *communication-tone* adaptation:
- **Funny/Casual** – Uses humor to soften the inconvenience of a weird schedule.
- **Sincere** – Prioritizes honesty and personal connection.
- **Group‑Chat** – Balances brevity with clear information for a mixed audience.
- **Short iMessage Preview** – Condenses the core message while preserving an approachable tone.

These examples illustrate how tone shifts can maintain relationships despite schedule constraints.
