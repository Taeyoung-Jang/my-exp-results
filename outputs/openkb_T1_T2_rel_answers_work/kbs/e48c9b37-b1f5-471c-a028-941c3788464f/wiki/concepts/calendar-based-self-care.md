# Calendar‑Based Self‑Care

**Definition:** Calendar‑Based Self‑Care is the practice of deliberately scheduling personal downtime on a calendar, treating it with the same priority as social appointments to create a sustainable balance between interaction and rest.

---

## Core Principles
1. **Treat Rest as an Appointment**
   - Block out specific evenings or time slots for relaxation, hobbies, or simply doing nothing, just as you would schedule a meeting.
2. **Visibility for Conflict Management**
   - By having downtime visible, you can quickly assess whether a new invitation fits without over‑committing.
3. **Consistent Review**
   - Regularly review the calendar to gauge how you feel after scheduled activities; adjust blocks if you notice chronic fatigue or isolation.

## Benefits
- **Reduces Over‑Commitment:** Clear visual cues prevent accidental double‑booking of social events and personal recovery time.
- **Improves Energy Management:** By spacing social nights with scheduled rest, you avoid chronic drain and maintain higher quality interactions.
- **Facilitates Polite Declines:** Having a documented downtime slot provides a truthful, simple explanation when turning down last‑minute invitations, linking to [[concepts/polite-decline-scripts]].

## Implementation Tips
1. **Choose a Consistent Color** in your digital or paper calendar for self‑care blocks.
2. **Set Reminders** to signal the start of a downtime period, reinforcing the boundary.
3. **Review Weekly:** At the end of each week, note any feelings of fatigue or isolation and adjust the upcoming schedule accordingly.
