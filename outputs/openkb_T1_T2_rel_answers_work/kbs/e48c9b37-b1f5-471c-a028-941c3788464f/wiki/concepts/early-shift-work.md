# Early Shift Work

**Definition**: Early Shift Work refers to employment that requires starting work before sunrise, often leading to a compressed daily routine and altered communication patterns.

**Key characteristics**
- Work hours begin in the pre‑dawn period (e.g., bakery shifts starting around 4–5 am).
- The schedule forces an early bedtime, typically with a personal "Do Not Disturb" window starting around 9 pm.
- Availability for social contact is limited to late afternoon or early evening.

**Implications for communication**
- People on early shift schedules need to set clear [[concepts/availability-windows]] for when they can be reached.
- Adjusting the [[concepts/communication-tone]] (e.g., using friendly, explanatory messages) helps manage expectations among friends, groups, and coworkers.
- Planning personal time around the shift can support [[concepts/calendar-based-self-care]] by ensuring rest and downtime.
