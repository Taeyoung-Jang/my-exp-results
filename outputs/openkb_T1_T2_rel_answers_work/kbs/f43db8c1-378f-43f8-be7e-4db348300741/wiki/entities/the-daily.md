# The Daily (work)

**Type:** work

**Description:** A daily news podcast produced by The New York Times, offering concise episodes that typically run 15–20 minutes.

## Key Facts from Session 6 (2024‑09‑15)
- Recommended as a **"ramp‑up"** option for morning commutes, fitting neatly into a 10–20 minute block.
- Its short format makes it ideal for **crowded‑car scenarios**, allowing listeners to stay engaged without needing visual attention. See [[concepts/crowded-car-strategies]].
- Can be part of a broader **commute ritual** that includes a single purpose activity during the ride. See [[concepts/commute-ritual]].
- Works well with a **minimal packing** setup (phone + earbuds) to keep the pouch light. See [[concepts/minimal-packing]].
