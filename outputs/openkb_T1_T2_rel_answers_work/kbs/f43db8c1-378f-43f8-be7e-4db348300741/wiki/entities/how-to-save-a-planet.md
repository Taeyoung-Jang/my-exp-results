## How to Save a Planet (work)

**Type:** work (podcast series)

**Description:** A concise, self‑contained podcast that delivers episodes lasting roughly 20 minutes, focusing on sustainability, climate solutions, and environmental storytelling.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-06_2024-09-15]]:**
- Recommended as a **morning “ramp‑up”** script for commuters using light‑rail.
- Episodes fit neatly into a 15–25 minute commute window, making it ideal for a quick, energizing start to the day.
- Suggested alongside other short‑form podcasts such as [[entities/planet-money]] and [[entities/the-daily]] for variety.
- Works well with [[concepts/commute-ritual]] approaches that favor audio‑only content, especially in crowded‑car situations covered by [[concepts/crowded-car-strategies]].
- Pairs nicely with a minimal packing strategy (see [[concepts/minimal-packing]]) – only a phone and earbuds are needed to enjoy the show during transit.

**Why it matters for commuters:**
- Provides substantive yet bite‑sized content that can be consumed without visual attention, allowing riders to stay hands‑free.
- The clear, self‑contained episodes reduce the need to track progress across multiple parts, supporting repeatable daily routines.

- Similar short‑form podcasts: [[entities/planet-money]], [[entities/the-daily]]
- Other recommended audio options for mindless fun: [[entities/comedy-bang-bang-snack-minis]], [[entities/maintenance-phase-minis]]
