# Settle‑In Window

## Definition
A short, structured period at the start of an on‑call shift (typically 5–10 minutes) during which the on‑call engineer logs in, pulls up dashboards, reviews recent changes, and receives a handoff of any active alerts before taking full responsibility.

## Rationale
- Reduces **immediate triage** pressure and prevents responding “half‑blind.”
- Provides a clear mental transition from off‑call to on‑call mode, supporting better **sleep management** and **burnout prevention**.
- Aligns with broader [[concepts/on-call-sustainability]] practices by treating the shift as a predictable, managed process rather than a chaotic interruption.

## Implementation Steps
1. **Set a settle‑in window** of 5–10 minutes at the beginning of each shift.
2. **Communicate** the window to the team, requesting that the previous on‑caller retain ownership of pages until the handoff.
3. **Log in** to required tools, open monitoring dashboards, and scan recent deployments or incidents.
4. **Document** any handoff notes and confirm readiness before assuming alert ownership.
5. **Adjust** the window length as needed, possibly integrating with existing [[concepts/check-in-windows]] or [[concepts/time-blocking]] schedules.

## Benefits
- Improves response quality by ensuring context is available before action.
- Lowers cognitive load and **interruption‑management** demands during the critical early minutes of a shift.
- Contributes to sustained performance and reduces the risk of **burnout** over time.
