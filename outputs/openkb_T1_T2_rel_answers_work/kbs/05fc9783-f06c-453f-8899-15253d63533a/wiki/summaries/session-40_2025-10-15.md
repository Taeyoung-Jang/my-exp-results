- Preventing burnout ([[concepts/burnout-prevention]])
- Drafting concise Slack proposals for a settle‑in window ([[concepts/settle-in-window]])

## Key Strategies
- **Negotiate clear expectations** – define what constitutes a page, response windows, and responsibilities. Written guidelines reduce mental load and can be framed as a [[concepts/check-in-windows]] practice.
- **Sleep hygiene** – go to bed earlier, keep evenings low‑key, return to sleep immediately after a page, and use short naps the next day.
- **Boundaries** – decide which activities to avoid during on‑call (e.g., alcohol, long drives) and limit extra project work. Communicate these limits to protect mental bandwidth.
- **Burnout monitoring** – track page frequency and repeat incidents; push for reliability work when patterns emerge. This aligns with [[concepts/burnout-prevention]] efforts.
- **Recovery time** – ensure at least one quiet day after the shift ends to reset.

## Managing Immediate Triage Expectations
- Propose a **settle‑in window** (5–10 min) before taking alerts. Frame the request around quality and context, not availability.
- Consistently deliver high‑quality responses to shift expectations and reduce pressure for “instant” triage.

## Sample Slack Messages
- Direct, diplomatic, and explicit‑time versions are provided for proposing a 5‑10 minute handoff (e.g., “handoff at 9:10”).
