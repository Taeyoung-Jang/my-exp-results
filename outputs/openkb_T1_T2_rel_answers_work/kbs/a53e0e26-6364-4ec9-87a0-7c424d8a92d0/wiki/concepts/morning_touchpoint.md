# Morning Touchpoint

**Definition**: A *morning touchpoint* is a small, tangible cue placed in a readily‑visible spot at the start of the day (e.g., a pen, stone, coin, or key) that prompts a quick reflective question or action, helping to interrupt habitual, automatic behavior.

## Core Characteristics
- **Physical and Tactile**: Relies on a concrete object rather than digital notifications, aligning with [[concepts/tactile_ritual]].
- **Low‑time Commitment**: Designed to be engaged with in under a minute, supporting [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]].
- **Intentional Prompt**: Accompanies a simple question such as “What do I want to pay attention to today?” to foster [[concepts/intentional_morning_routine]].
- **Automation Disruption**: Acts as a manual counter‑measure to background automation and autopilot thinking, linking to [[concepts/background_automation]].

## Benefits Highlighted in Session 32
- **Reduces Mental Load**: By externalising the decision‑making cue, it frees mental bandwidth for the day's tasks, connecting to [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]].
- **Provides Consistency without Rigid Checklist**: Offers a repeatable habit that feels like a *touchpoint* rather than a burdensome list.
- **Scalable to Different Vibes**: Can be paired with any of the 3‑line prompt formats described in the session, such as those in [[concepts/paper_prompt_card]] or the [[concepts/morning_settings_template]].
- **Simple Organization**: The cue can be kept on a desk, bedside table, or in a small bowl, requiring no filing system—an approach echoed in the quick‑review tip [[concepts/quick_review_method]].

## Typical Implementation Steps (from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-13]])
1. **Select an Object** – Choose a small, distinct item (e.g., a stone, coin, key, or pen).  
2. **Place It Visibly** – Put it where you’ll see it first thing (kitchen counter, nightstand, etc.).  
3. **Define a Prompt** – Pair the object with a single question or intention (e.g., “What should I focus on today?”).  
4. **Engage Briefly** – When you notice the object, answer the prompt aloud or mentally, then move on.

## Variations & Extensions
- **Mood‑Based Touchpoint**: Use colored beads to represent different desired moods.
- **Goal‑Tracking Touchpoint**: Attach a tiny sticky note with the day’s priority.
- **Rotating Touchpoint**: Change the object weekly to keep the cue fresh.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- Works alongside [[concepts/paper_prompt_card]] as a physical trigger to fill out a short daily card.
- Complements the [[concepts/morning_settings_template]] by providing the initial cue that signals when to write the settings.
- Serves as a practical example of [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]] and [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]] in daily habit design.
