# Quick Review Method

**Definition**: A lightweight, paper‑based technique for scanning the last few days' morning‑settings entries to spot patterns and decide on one tiny adjustment for the upcoming day.

## Core Elements
1. **Location** – At the top of a notebook page where the 3‑line "morning settings" template is written each morning.
2. **Scope** – Review the most recent 3‑4 entries only; the goal is a quick visual sweep, not a full journal.
3. **Process** –
   - Flip back to the previous pages.
   - Read each three‑line block.
   - Note repeated words, shifts in tone, or emerging themes.
   - Choose a single, actionable tweak for the new day (e.g., change the "primary setting" wording or add a new focus).
4. **Outcome** – Maintains the habit of reflection without adding mental load, keeping the routine low‑maintenance.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- Part of an [[concepts/intentional_morning_routine]] that uses [[concepts/morning_touchpoint]] and [[concepts/tactile_ritual]] cues.
- Complements the [[concepts/paper_prompt_card]] and [[concepts/morning_settings_template]] by providing a closing loop.
- Supports [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]] and [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]] by keeping review time under two minutes.

## Example Implementation
```
--- Today’s Mode: Focus
--- Primary Setting: Finish report outline
--- Note to Self: Stay present in meetings
```
After a few days, flip back:
- Notice the word "focus" appears frequently → maybe broaden to "clarity".
- Adjust the next day's "Primary Setting" accordingly.
