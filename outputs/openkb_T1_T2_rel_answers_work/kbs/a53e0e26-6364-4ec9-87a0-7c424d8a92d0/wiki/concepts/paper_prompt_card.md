# Paper Prompt Card

A **Paper Prompt Card** is a tiny, analog template—typically a small index card or notebook page—containing three concise prompts that can be filled out in under two minutes each morning. The format is deliberately low‑maintenance, offering a tactile cue that interrupts autopilot and helps users set an intentional tone for the day.

## Core Features
- **Three‑line structure**: Each line serves a distinct purpose (e.g., mood/intention, primary priority, quick reminder).
- **Variations**: The concept supports multiple vibe‑sets, such as practical, reflective, calming, and momentum‑focused versions.
- **Physical cue**: The card is a tangible object that can be placed on a desk, in a bowl, or clipped to a notebook, reinforcing the habit through touch.
- **Rapid review**: Users can flip through recent cards to scan for patterns without engaging in extensive journaling.

## Example Prompt Sets (from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-13]])
1. **Practical**
   - *Today’s anchor task…*
   - *Must‑remember detail…*
   - *One small win to aim for…*
2. **Reflective**
   - *How I want to show up…*
   - *What I’ll give my best energy to…*
   - *Something I want to notice today…*
3. **Calming**
   - *What I’m carrying this morning…*
   - *What I can set down…*
   - *Where I want my attention to rest…*
4. **Momentum‑focused**
   - *One step forward…*
   - *What supports that step…*
   - *End‑of‑day feeling I’m aiming for…*

## Usage Tips
- Keep a small stack of blank cards in a bowl or binder clip; pull the top one each morning.
- After a week, move older cards to an envelope to prevent clutter while preserving a brief history.
- Treat the card as a “settings panel” rather than a journal entry—no need for elaborate writing, just concise prompts.
