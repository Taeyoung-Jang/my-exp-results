# Morning Settings Template

**Definition**: A minimal, paper‑based “settings panel” that can be rewritten each morning in under two minutes to frame the day’s focus, priority, and a brief reminder.

## Core Structure
The template consists of three short lines, each prompting a specific type of input. The basic format is:

```
Today’s Mode: 
Primary Setting: 
Note to Self: 
```

Variations adapt the wording to different tones:
- **Practical** – “Today’s Anchor Task”, “Must‑remember Detail”, “One Small Win”.
- **Reflective** – “How I Want to Show Up”, “What I’ll Give My Best Energy To”, “Something I Want to Notice”.
- **Calming** – “What I’m Carrying”, “What I Can Set Down”, “Where I Want My Attention to Rest”.
- **Momentum‑Focused** – “One Step Forward”, “What Supports That Step”, “End‑of‑Day Feeling I’m Aiming For”.

These options are designed to stay under **2 minutes** to fill in, keeping the routine low‑effort and **low maintenance** [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]].

## How It Works
1. **Physical cue** – Keep a small notebook or a dedicated page ready on your nightstand or desk.
2. **Rapid fill‑in** – As soon as you start the day (e.g., while coffee brews), write the three lines.
3. **Immediate reference** – The completed panel sits at the top of the page, visible throughout the day to guide actions and decisions.

The template functions as a **tactile ritual** [[concepts/tactile_ritual]] that interrupts autopilot and reduces **mental load** [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]] by externalizing intent.

## Organization & Review
- **Stacking** – Keep the notebook pages in a simple stack; the newest page is always on top.
- **Periodic scan** – Every few days, flip back through the last 3‑4 pages and **scan** the settings (no writing required). Look for repeated words or shifts in tone and pick one tiny adjustment for the next day. This quick visual sweep is the **quick review method** [[concepts/quick_review_method]].

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of an **intentional morning routine** [[concepts/intentional_morning_routine]].
- Often paired with a **paper prompt card** [[concepts/paper_prompt_card]] for a physical cue.
- Can be combined with a **morning touchpoint** [[concepts/morning_touchpoint]] (e.g., a stone or coin) to trigger the template fill‑in.
