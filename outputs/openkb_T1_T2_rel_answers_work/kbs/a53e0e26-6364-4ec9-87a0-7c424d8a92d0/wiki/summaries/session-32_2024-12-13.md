## Summary of session-32_2024-12-13

The conversation explores how to make morning choices more hands‑on by using **tiny tactile rituals** and a **paper‑based “settings” card** that can be filled in under two minutes.

### Key ideas
- **Touchpoint rituals** – place a physical cue (pen, stone, coin) and ask a quick question to break autopilot. [[concepts/morning_touchpoint]]
- **3‑line prompt cards** – four variations are offered, each with a distinct vibe (practical, reflective, calming, momentum‑focused). [[concepts/paper_prompt_card]]
- **Organization** – keep cards in a simple stack or binder clip; periodically move older cards to an envelope.
- **Morning settings template** – a notebook version with three lines such as “Today’s Mode / Primary Setting / Note to Self” and its variants. [[concepts/morning_settings_template]]
- **Fast review** – flip through the last few pages, scan for repeated words or shifts, and pick one tiny adjustment for the new day. [[concepts/quick_review_method]]
