# Smart Bulb

A **smart bulb** is a connected lighting device that can be programmed or controlled remotely to adjust brightness, color, and scheduling. In the context of *session‑04_2024‑06‑15*, smart bulbs are highlighted as a core component of a low‑maintenance smart‑home starter kit.

## Key Points from the Session
- **Background automation**: When set to turn on at sunset and off at bedtime, the bulb operates without further user interaction, exemplifying [[concepts/background_automation]].
- **Low‑maintenance design**: Once the schedule is defined, the bulb requires little to no ongoing tweaking, aligning with [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]].
- **Mental load reduction**: By handling lighting decisions automatically, smart bulbs reduce the user's cognitive burden, supporting [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]].

## Typical Use Cases
- **Time‑based lighting**: Lights automatically switch on at dusk and off at night.
- **Scene activation**: Integrated with a [[entities/smart-speaker]] or hub to create voice‑triggered “night mode” scenes.
- **Energy saving**: Coupled with a [[entities/smart-thermostat]] to dim lights when the house is unoccupied.

## Relation to Other Entities
- Works alongside **smart‑speaker** for voice control.
- Often paired with a **smart‑thermostat** for coordinated home automation.
- Can be managed via a **[[entities/smartphone]]** app for scheduling and firmware updates.
