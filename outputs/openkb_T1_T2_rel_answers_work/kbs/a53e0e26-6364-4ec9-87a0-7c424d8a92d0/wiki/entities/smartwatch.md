# Smartwatch

**Type:** product

A smartwatch is a wearable computer that combines the functionality of a traditional watch with advanced features such as health tracking, notifications, and app interaction. In the context of the session‑04 discussion, smartwatches are highlighted as examples of technology that can act as an "invisible helper" when they operate passively and require little user intervention.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]]
- **Passive health tracking** – Features like continuous heart‑rate monitoring, sleep tracking, and step counting work automatically and do not demand user input, embodying [[concepts/background_automation]] and reducing mental load.
- **Potential pitfalls** – More proactive functions such as workout auto‑detection, fall‑detection sensitivity adjustments, or frequent stand‑reminders often need frequent tuning, which contradicts [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]] and can increase mental load.
- **Design criteria** – The document emphasizes that smartwatches should prioritize [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]] by offering clear defaults, context‑aware behavior, and minimal notifications.

- [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]] – Designing the smartwatch to require little upkeep after initial setup.
