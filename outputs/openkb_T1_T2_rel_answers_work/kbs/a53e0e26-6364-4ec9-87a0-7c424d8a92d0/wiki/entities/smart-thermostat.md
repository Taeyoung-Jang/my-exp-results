# Smart Thermostat

A **smart thermostat** is a connected temperature‑control device that can automatically adjust heating and cooling based on occupancy, schedules, or external conditions. In the context of the *session‑04_2024‑06‑15* discussion, it is highlighted as a core component of a low‑maintenance smart‑home starter kit.

## Key Characteristics
- **Background automation** – operates quietly, switching to eco mode when you leave and restoring comfort settings on return. [[concepts/background_automation]]
- **Low‑maintenance design** – after initial setup, it requires little to no ongoing tweaking. [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]]
- **Reduces mental load** – frees the user from manually adjusting temperatures, contributing to a simpler daily routine. [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]]

## Role in the Minimal‑Management Setup
The document recommends pairing the smart thermostat with:
- **Smart bulbs** for time‑based lighting [[entities/smart-bulb]]
- **Smart speaker** or hub for central control and simple voice‑activated scenes [[entities/smart-speaker]]

Together, these devices provide a cohesive, "set‑it‑and‑forget‑it" ecosystem that emphasizes invisible assistance rather than additional tasks.
