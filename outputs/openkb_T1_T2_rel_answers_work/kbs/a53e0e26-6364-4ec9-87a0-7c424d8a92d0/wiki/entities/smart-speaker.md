## Smart Speaker

A **smart speaker** is a voice‑activated audio device that serves as a central hub for controlling other smart home products such as [[entities/smart-bulb]] and [[entities/smart-thermostat]]. In the discussion of a *minimal‑management* smart‑home setup (see [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]]), the smart speaker is highlighted as one of the three core components needed to achieve reliable, low‑effort automation.

### Key Points from the Session
- **Central control**: It links together lights, thermostat, and other devices, enabling simple voice commands like a “night scene” that dims lights and plays calming music.
- **Background automation**: By handling routines based on time or presence, the speaker supports [[concepts/background_automation]] without requiring ongoing user intervention.
- **Low‑maintenance design**: The recommended automations (e.g., sunset lighting, eco‑mode thermostat) exemplify [[concepts/low_maintenance_design]], meaning once set up they rarely need tweaking.
- **Mental‑load reduction**: By providing a single, natural‑language interface, the smart speaker reduces the cognitive effort needed to manage multiple devices, aligning with [[concepts/mental_load_reduction]].

### Typical Use Cases
1. **Voice‑activated scenes** – e.g., saying "good night" to dim lights, lower temperature, and start a soothing playlist.
2. **Scheduling** – setting up time‑based actions (sunset lights, bedtime routines) that run automatically.
3. **Presence detection** – integrating with a smart thermostat to switch to eco mode when the home is empty.
