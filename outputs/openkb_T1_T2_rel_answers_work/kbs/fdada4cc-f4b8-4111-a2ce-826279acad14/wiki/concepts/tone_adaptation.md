# Tone Adaptation

**Definition**: The practice of modifying language, tone, and structure of self‑introductions to align with the expectations of a specific audience or communication channel.

## Key Elements
- **Audience awareness** – recognizing whether the reader is a hiring manager, tech team, or casual Slack audience.
- **Formality level** – shifting from formal, concise phrasing for a resume to a more conversational style for a Slack intro.
- **Length constraints** – tailoring content to 1‑2 sentences for brief bios, or 2‑3 sentences for Slack messages.
- **Value focus** – emphasizing universal workplace values (e.g., mindfulness, collaboration) while omitting detailed religious specifics to stay inclusive.
