# Manager Conversation Strategies

**Definition**: Practical approaches for discussing workplace prayer accommodations with a manager, balancing clarity, brevity, and professionalism.

## Core Principles
1. **Keep it brief** – Highlight the request in one or two sentences.
2. **Frame as a logistical need** – Emphasize minimal time impact and use of existing quiet areas.
3. **Offer flexibility** – Show willingness to work around team schedules and to use short breaks.
4. **Stay calm and professional** – Use a neutral tone that acknowledges the manager’s perspective.

## Sample Scripts (from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]])
### Very brief version (busy manager)
> "I’ve started praying during the day and just need a quiet spot for a few minutes around lunch and late afternoon. Is there a room I can use briefly so I’m out of the way?"

### Detailed version (if questions arise)
> "I’ve recently begun observing the daily prayers. It only takes a few minutes, usually during my lunch period and once later in the afternoon. I’m looking for a small, quiet space I can step into so I’m not disrupting anyone. I’ll keep it quick, and it won’t affect my work or availability. I just want to make sure I’m using a space that’s okay with you."

### Handling push‑back about break time
> "I understand. The prayer itself is very short, and I can time it within my lunch break or any flexible moments in the afternoon. I’m not asking for extra time—just a place to step into briefly. I’m happy to work around the team’s needs."

## Application Tips
- **Choose the right moment** – Request a quick one‑on‑one or send a concise email.
- **Know the space options** – Identify meeting rooms, empty offices, or a car before the conversation.
- **Prepare a fallback** – If a dedicated room isn’t available, suggest using a discreet corner or your vehicle.
- **Document the agreement** – Follow up with a short email summarising the agreed arrangement for reference.
