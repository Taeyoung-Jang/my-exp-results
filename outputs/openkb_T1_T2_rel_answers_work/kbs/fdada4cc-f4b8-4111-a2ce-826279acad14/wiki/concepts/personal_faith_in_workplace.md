# Personal Faith in the Workplace

**Definition**: The practice of referencing one’s personal religious identity or values in workplace introductions and communications in a way that is authentic, inclusive, and professionally appropriate.

## Core Elements
1. **Value‑focused framing** – Emphasize universal virtues (e.g., mindfulness, respect, curiosity) drawn from faith rather than doctrinal specifics.  
2. **Professional tone** – Use concise, collaborative language that aligns with team culture, especially in tech or engineering environments.  
3. **Avoiding overshare** – Skip detailed rituals, prescriptive statements, or comparative religious commentary to maintain workplace neutrality.

## Guidance from session‑06_2022‑06‑15
- Provide multiple length tiers (brief, medium, personal) to suit different contexts (team bios, Slack intros).  
- Offer variations that **name** the faith (e.g., "practicing Hindu") and versions that **omit** the explicit mention while preserving the same values.  
- Include tips on what to avoid: too much detail about rituals, and language that could be interpreted as prescriptive or comparative.
- Suggest tone adaptations for tech teams, Slack style, and engineering‑focused introductions, keeping each to 1‑3 sentences.
