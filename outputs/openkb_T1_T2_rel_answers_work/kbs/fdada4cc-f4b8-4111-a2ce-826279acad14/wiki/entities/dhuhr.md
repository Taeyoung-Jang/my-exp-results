# Dhuhr (midday prayer)

Dhuhr is the second of the five daily Islamic prayers, performed after the sun has passed its zenith. In a typical 9 am–5 pm work schedule, Dhuhr usually falls during the lunch break.

## Workplace practice (from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]])
- **Timing**: Align the prayer with the middle of the lunch window.
- **Location options**: a quiet corner, an unused meeting room, a storage area, or even a parked car if privacy is needed.
- **Preparation**: Keep wudu (ablution) refreshed during the morning to make the break quick and unobtrusive.
- **Routine integration**: Treat Dhuhr as a short pause within the lunch period, similar to the overall [[concepts/daily_prayer_routine]].
