## Summary

This document records a back‑and‑forth where the user requests help crafting short “about me” blurbs for a new job/team introduction. The assistant supplies:

* **Three length tiers** – a very brief, a medium, and a slightly more personal version – all highlighting the user’s practicing Hindu identity while keeping the tone professional.
* **Guidance on what to avoid**, e.g., detailed ritual descriptions and prescriptive language, emphasizing inclusive, value‑focused wording.
* **Tailored tech‑team variations**, both naming Hinduism and omitting it, each condensed to 1‑2 sentences.
* **Slack‑friendly intros** – four extra options with distinct vibes (energetic, concise, engineering‑y, warm) limited to 2‑3 sentences and optionally sprinkling a non‑work interest.

Key ideas include:

- **Balancing personal faith with professional presentation** – using values (mindfulness, curiosity, respect, balance) rather than doctrinal specifics. [[concepts/personal_faith_in_workplace]]
- **Tone adaptation for different audiences** – casual tech‑team language, concise Slack style, and a more engineering‑focused voice. [[concepts/tone_adaptation]]
- **Inclusive language practices** – avoiding prescriptive or comparative religious statements to maintain workplace neutrality. [[concepts/inclusive_language]]
- **Structure of self‑introductions** – brief opening, core values, optional personal hobby, and a forward‑looking closing. (see summary of the source document)
- **Slack introduction patterns** – greetings, value statement, brief personal hook, and enthusiasm for collaboration. (see summary of the source document)

The assistant also offers to further tailor the text to specific company cultures, indicating an iterative, user‑centered approach. [[summaries/session-06_2022-06-15]]
