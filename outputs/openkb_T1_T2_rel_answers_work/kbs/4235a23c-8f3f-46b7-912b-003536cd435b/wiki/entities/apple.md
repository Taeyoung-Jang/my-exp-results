# Apple

Apple Inc. is the multinational technology company that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and online services. It is the creator of the iPhone and the iOS operating system, which includes the location‑permission framework discussed in [[summaries/session-05_2025-06-15]].

## Role in the document
- Provides the iOS platform where users manage location settings such as *While Using the App*, *Ask Next Time*, and *Precise Location*.
- Supplies system services like **Significant Locations**, **Find My**, and **Location‑Based Alerts**, which can trigger the location icon without exposing data to third‑party apps.
- The document advises users on how to adjust these iOS settings to balance privacy with app functionality, reflecting Apple’s built‑in privacy controls.
