# Smartwatch

A **smartwatch** is a wearable product that combines time‑keeping with digital features such as health and fitness tracking, notifications, and app integration. In the context of [[summaries/session-35_2025-09-15]], the smartwatch is mentioned as one of the tools users employ to monitor running activity.

## Key Points from Session 35
- The user considers a smartwatch an alternative to a phone for tracking runs, appreciating the convenience of glance‑state data.
- Concerns are raised about **privacy vs. functionality tradeoff** when the device automatically records location and performance metrics.
- Recommendations include turning off **auto‑start** and using **hidden live metrics** modes to keep the experience motivating without feeling intrusive.
- The smartwatch supports **habit formation** by providing simple, regular feedback on consistency rather than obsessing over pace or distance.
