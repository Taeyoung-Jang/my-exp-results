# Summary of session-35_2025-09-15

## Core Advice
- Start with **easy, enjoyable runs** and blend walking when needed; increase run portions gradually to avoid burnout. This aligns with [[concepts/sustainable_running]].
- **Consistency** is the primary focus early on – aim for regular outings rather than chasing pace or distance.
- Vary routes, times of day, and scenery to keep the routine from feeling monotonous.

## Tracking Strategies
- **Turn off auto‑start** in your [[entities/running_app]] so tracking begins only when you press “go.”
- Use **selective tracking**: record only 2–3 runs per week, choosing runs that represent your typical routine (e.g., one easy run, one slightly longer or more intentional run).
- Enable **hidden live metrics** or a “minimal display” mode to hide pace/distance during the run, reviewing a summary afterward.
- Consider a “run‑only” mode that pauses data screens or suppresses real‑time alerts.

## Motivation & Mindset
- Treat the app as a **quiet black box** that provides feedback after the fact, reducing performance pressure.
- Focus on **intrinsic motivation** – notice how you feel, energy levels, and mood rather than just numbers. See [[concepts/intrinsic_motivation]].
- Small, steady wins (e.g., weekly frequency) reinforce habit formation. See [[concepts/habit_formation]].
