# Sustainable Running

**Definition**: Sustainable Running is a balanced, long‑term approach to running that prioritizes gradual progression, enjoyment, and the avoidance of burnout or boredom.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Start Easy & Enjoyable**
   - Begin with short, relaxed runs mixed with walking.  
   - Gradually increase the running portion as the body adapts.
2. **Consistency Over Intensity**
   - Prioritize regular outings (e.g., several times per week) before focusing on pace or distance.
3. **Variety to Prevent Boredom**
   - Change routes, times of day, or scenery to keep the routine fresh.
4. **Mindful Tracking**
   - Use selective tracking (2–3 runs per week) and hide live metrics to keep data motivating rather than pressuring.
   - Choose runs that represent typical effort (e.g., one easy run, one slightly longer run).
5. **Intrinsic Motivation**
   - Focus on how the run feels, energy levels, and mood rather than pure numbers.
   - Celebrate small, steady wins such as weekly frequency.

## Practical Tips

- **Turn off auto‑start** in your running app; start recording only when you want.
- **Use a "minimal display"** mode to hide real‑time pace/distance.
- **Select 2–3 representative runs per week** to log, mixing easy and slightly longer sessions.
- **Vary routes** weekly to keep the experience fresh.
- **Reflect post‑run** in a journal: note mood, energy, and any observations beyond numbers.

---

## Benefits

- Reduces risk of injury and burnout.
- Maintains long‑term engagement with running.
- Provides meaningful data without overwhelming pressure.
- Supports overall fitness and mental well‑being through consistent, enjoyable activity.
