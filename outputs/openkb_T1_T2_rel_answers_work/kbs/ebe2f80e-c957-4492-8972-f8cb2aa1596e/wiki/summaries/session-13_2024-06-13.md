## Summary of session-13_2024-06-13

**Purpose**: The user requested a concrete, non‑repetitive weekly dinner rotation (Mon‑Sat) with clear recipes, cook times, leftovers for lunch, and an exact grocery list that distinguishes must‑buy items from pantry staples. They later asked to replace the salmon night with a vegetarian high‑protein option and to change the Friday "nicer meal" away from risotto while keeping ingredient overlap.

### Final Dinner Plan (Mon‑Sat)
| Day | Dish | Cook Time | Key Steps (3‑5) | Lunch Leftovers |
|-----|------|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Monday** | Chicken‑Veggie Stir‑Fry | 20 min | 1. Slice chicken, half bell pepper, half zucchini. 2. Sauté chicken, add veg & spinach. 3. Finish with soy sauce, serve over rice. | Small portion of stir‑fry & rice |
| **Tuesday** | Turkey Tacos / Burrito Bowl | 20 min | 1. Cook ground turkey with garlic & seasoning. 2. Add remaining bell pepper & zucchini. 3. Serve in tortillas or over rice, squeeze lemon. | Small turkey/veg portion |
| **Wednesday** | Cherry‑Tomato Spinach Pasta | 15‑20 min | 1. Boil 3‑4 oz pasta. 2. Sauté cherry tomatoes. 3. Add spinach, toss with pasta & Parmesan. | None (unless extra made) |
| **Thursday** | Veggie + Chickpea Skillet (high‑protein) | 15 min | 1. Sauté onion & garlic. 2. Add drained chickpeas + any leftover veg. 3. Season with lemon, salt, pepper; serve over rice or tortillas. | Small chick‑pea mix |
| **Friday** | Tomato‑Spinach Parmesan Gnocchi ("nicer" meal) | 20 min | 1. Sauté onion & garlic, add cherry tomatoes. 2. Stir in spinach until wilted. 3. Add shelf‑stable gnocchi, finish with Parmesan. | One small serving |
| **Saturday** | Personal Flatbread / Pizza | 15 min | 1. Spread flatbread with olive oil or tomato sauce. 2. Top with any remaining cheese & veg. 3. Bake until crisp. | None |

### Updated Grocery List (Quantities)
- **Proteins** (must‑buy)  
  - 1 small chicken breast (~6 oz)  
  - 4 oz ground turkey  
  - 1 can chickpeas (14‑15 oz)  
- **Produce** (must‑buy)  
  - 1 bell pepper  
  - 1 small zucchini  
  - 6 oz fresh spinach  
  - 1 pint cherry tomatoes  
  - 1 small yellow onion  
  - 1 head garlic  
  - 1 lemon  
- **Dairy / Cheese** (must‑buy)  
  - 3‑4 oz Parmesan wedge  
- **Carbohydrates** (must‑buy)  
  - 1 pack small tortillas or 1 flatbread  
  - Small box pasta (use 3‑4 oz)  
  - 1 package shelf‑stable gnocchi (12‑16 oz, use ~½)  
- **Pantry Staples** (P)  
  - Rice (or microwave pouch)  
  - Olive oil, salt, pepper  
  - Soy sauce / stir‑fry seasoning  
  - Taco seasoning or chili powder  
  - Tomato sauce (optional for flatbread)
