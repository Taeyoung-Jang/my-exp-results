# Evening Routines

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A framework for short, low‑effort evening scripts that can be performed with one hand, combining calm audio, minimal tactile tasks, and a gentle wind‑down.

---

## Overview
Evening routines are deliberately designed sequences that help transition from the day’s activities to a restful state. The approach outlined in [[summaries/session-34_2024-11-12]] focuses on:
- **One‑handed execution** to accommodate a wrist brace.
- **No‑app, free audio** sources to avoid subscription fatigue.
- **Gradual intensity**: from super low‑energy to slightly engaging, ending with sleep‑inducing ambience.

## Core Components
1. **Audio Backbone** – A calming soundtrack or narrative (e.g., a Librivox chapter, a YouTube rain loop) drawn from [[concepts/free-audio-resources]].
2. **One‑Handed Activity** – Simple, tactile actions such as:
   - Making a warm drink.
   - Watering a plant.
   - Gentle scalp massage.
   These align with [[concepts/one-handed-activities]].
3. **Wind‑Down Phase** – Soft ambient sounds or a silent timer to cue sleep.
4. **Device Setup** – Hands‑free control using [[entities/iphone]] features like *Hey Siri* and *Voice Control*.

## Script Templates
The source provides three ready‑made scripts that can be rotated:
- **20‑minute script** – Light drink, short listening, brief activity, final quiet listening.
- **45‑minute script** – Longer listening block, modest task (plant watering or face wash), then ambient sleep sounds.
- **90‑minute script** – Extended listening, tactile ritual, mid‑session snack or tidying, ending with pure ambience.
Each script follows the same three‑stage pattern: **Listen → Engage → Wind‑down**.

## Audio Recommendations (Free & Ad‑Free)
- YouTube channels: **Nemo’s Dreamscapes**, **Relaxed Guy**, **Relaxing White Noise**, **Calmed By Nature**, **Miracle Forest**, **Michael Sealey**.
- Librivox recordings: *The Wind in the Willows* read by [[entities/alan-winterrowd]], *Anne of Green Gables* read by [[entities/karen-savage]], and *Little Women* (Version 2).
These resources are curated to avoid sudden loud intros or ads.

## iPhone Hands‑Free Control
1. Enable **Hey Siri**: *Settings → Siri & Search → Listen for “Hey Siri”* and allow use when locked.
2. Turn on **Voice Control**: *Settings → Accessibility → Voice Control*.
3. Set a silent timer to stop playback: Open **Clock** → *Timer* → *When Timer Ends* → scroll to **Stop Playing** (entity [[entities/clock-app]]).
4. Use voice commands such as:
   - “Hey Siri, pause” / “play”
   - “Hey Siri, skip forward thirty seconds”
   - “Hey Siri, volume down”
   - “Hey Siri, set a timer for 30 minutes” (will invoke the silent *Stop Playing* action).

## Benefits
- **Ergonomic**: No strain on the immobilized wrist.
- **Low Cognitive Load**: Minimal decision‑making, fostering relaxation.
- **Flexibility**: Scripts can be mixed, matched, or shortened.
- **No Subscriptions**: Relies entirely on free, public‑domain content.
