# Free Audio Resources

**Definition**: A curated collection of free, ad‑free audio sources that can be used in one‑handed evening routines without requiring subscriptions or intrusive ads.

## YouTube Channels & Trusted Titles
- **Nemo’s Dreamscapes** – *Lofi Rainy Jazz • 1940s Rainy Night Ambience* and *Rainy Night in a Cozy Room • Vintage Ambience*. These videos are long (hour‑plus), have a vintage visual style, and contain no commercial breaks. [[entities/nemos-dreamscapes]]
- **Relaxed Guy** – *10 Hours Rainfall (No Music)*, a steady rain track with no overlay music. [[entities/relaxed-guy]]
- **Relaxing White Noise** – *Gentle Rain Sounds for Sleeping (8 Hours)*, pure rain without sudden volume changes. [[entities/relaxing-white-noise]]
- **Calmed By Nature** – *Cozy Cabin Ambience | Fireplace & Rain Sounds* (3‑hour loop). [[entities/calmed-by-nature]]
- **Miracle Forest** – *Rain on Tent • Camping Ambience* (3‑hour loop). [[entities/miracle-forest]]
- **Michael Sealey** – *Deep Sleep Hypnosis (quiet background)*, a minimal‑voice track that fades out quickly. [[entities/michael-sealey]]

## Librivox Public‑Domain Audiobooks
- *The Wind in the Willows* read by **Alan Winterrowd** – warm narration with consistent volume. [[entities/alan-winterrowd]] [[entities/the-wind-in-the-willows]]
- *Little Women* (Version 2) – a multi‑reader edition known for gentle pacing. [[entities/little-women]]
- *Anne of Green Gables* read by **Karen Savage** – soothing, steady voice. [[entities/karen-savage]] [[entities/anne-of-green-gables]]
- All recordings are available on **Librivox**, which guarantees royalty‑free, ad‑free streaming. [[entities/librivox]]

## How the Resources Fit Into Evening Scripts
The audio selections are referenced in the [[summaries/session-34_2024-11-12]] to populate three one‑handed scripts (20 min, 45 min, 90 min). Each script uses a **listening phase** (ambient music or a public‑domain story), a brief **one‑handed activity** (e.g., making a tea, watering a plant) and a final **wind‑down phase** (rain or soft narration). This structure aligns with the broader [[concepts/evening-routines]] and the need for [[concepts/one-handed-activities]].

## Selection Criteria
- **No subscriptions** – all videos are freely accessible on YouTube; all books are in the public domain on Librivox.
- **Minimal interruptions** – chosen uploads have explicit “no ads” or “no interruptions” in the description and a consistent thumbnail that identifies the official channel.
- **Calm tone** – recordings avoid sharp spikes, loud music, or overt self‑help narration.
- **Length** – each track runs for at least an hour, allowing uninterrupted playback for the full script duration.

## Technical Tips for iPhone Users
To start playback hands‑free, enable **Hey Siri** and **Voice Control** on the iPhone. Then use commands such as “Hey Siri, play *Lofi Rainy Jazz*” and “Hey Siri, set a timer for 20 minutes” with the timer set to **Stop Playing** in the Clock app to silence audio silently.
