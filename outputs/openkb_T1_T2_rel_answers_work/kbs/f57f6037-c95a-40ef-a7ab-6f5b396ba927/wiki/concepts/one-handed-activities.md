# One‑Handed Activities

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Practical, low‑effort actions that can be performed using only the non‑braced hand, especially for winding down in the evening.

---

## Overview

One‑handed activities are central to the evening scripts created in [[summaries/session-34_2024-11-12]]. They focus on gentle, calming actions that avoid strain on a immobilized wrist while still offering a sense of routine or mild engagement. The goal is to provide variety without requiring apps, subscriptions, or complex equipment.

## Core Characteristics

| Characteristic | Details |
|---|---|
| **Low physical demand** | Movements can be completed with a single hand; no gripping or fine‑motor tasks that stress the wrist. |
| **Minimal setup** | Items are pre‑positioned (e.g., phone on a stable surface) so the activity can start without fiddling. |
| **Sensory focus** | Often paired with audio or tactile sensations (warm cloth, scent, ambient sound). |
| **Scalable engagement** | Can be classified as super‑low energy, lightly engaging, or sleep‑supporting, matching the structure of [[concepts/evening-routines]]. |

## Example Activities from the Source

1. **Warm drink preparation** – Using the free hand to boil water, steep tea, and hold the mug.
2. **One‑handed scalp or face massage** – Gentle motions with a warm washcloth or a small handheld massager.
3. **Plant watering** – Carrying a small watering can or cup to a nearby plant.
4. **Tidy a small surface** – Straightening a desk or clearing a nightstand, moving items one at a time.
5. **Color‑by‑number or simple drawing app** – Tap‑only interaction; no dragging required.
6. **Listening to curated audio** – Paired with a set‑and‑forget phone position, leveraging [[entities/iphone]] voice control.

## Integration with Evening Scripts

The three scripted routines (20‑, 45‑, 90‑minute) each weave one‑handed activities into three phases:

- **Listen & settle** – Begin with a calm audio track from [[concepts/free-audio-resources]].
- **Mild activity** – Insert one‑handed tasks (e.g., tea ritual, plant watering) to provide gentle engagement.
- **Wind‑down** – Return to pure listening or ambient sound, assisted by a silent sleep‑timer via the [[entities/clock-app]].

These phases align with the broader [[concepts/evening-routines]] framework, ensuring the activities support relaxation rather than productivity.
