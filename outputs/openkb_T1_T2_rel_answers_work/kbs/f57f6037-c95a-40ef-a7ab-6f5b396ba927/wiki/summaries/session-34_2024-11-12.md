# Summary of session-34_2024-11-12

**Main contribution**: A detailed guide for creating low‑effort, one‑handed evening scripts (20‑, 45‑, and 90‑minute) that avoid apps and subscriptions, paired with curated free audio resources and step‑by‑step iPhone voice‑control setup.

## Key Ideas

- **One‑handed evening routines** – scripts grouped by duration, each composed of calm listening, minimal tactile activity, and a wind‑down phase. [[concepts/evening-routines]]
- **Free audio recommendations** – specific YouTube channel names and exact video titles (e.g., *Nemo’s Dreamscapes – Lofi Rainy Jazz • 1940s Rainy Night Ambience*), plus reliable Librivox recordings with reader names for verification. [[concepts/free-audio-resources]]
- **iPhone one‑handed setup** – enabling *Hey Siri* and *Voice Control*, configuring large on‑screen buttons, and positioning the device to avoid further interaction. [[entities/iphone]] [[entities/siri]]
- **Silent sleep‑timer** – using the Clock app’s *Stop Playing* timer to automatically cease audio without an audible alarm. [[entities/clock-app]]
- **Avoiding ads/interruptions** – tips for confirming the correct upload (channel verification, thumbnail cues) and confirming Librivox reader information. [[entities/librivox]]

## Practical Takeaways

1. **Script drafting** – combine a chosen audio track, a simple one‑handed task (e.g., warm drink, plant watering), and a final ambient phase. [[concepts/one-handed-activities]]
2. **Setup checklist** – enable Siri, enable Voice Control, set Auto‑Lock to *Never* (temporarily), and place the phone on a stable surface.
3. **Command cheat‑sheet** – `Hey Siri, pause/play`, `skip forward/backward thirty seconds`, `volume up/down`, `set a timer for X minutes` (pre‑configured with *Stop Playing*).
4. **Verification steps** – check channel name and thumbnail on YouTube; confirm reader name on Librivox before playing.
