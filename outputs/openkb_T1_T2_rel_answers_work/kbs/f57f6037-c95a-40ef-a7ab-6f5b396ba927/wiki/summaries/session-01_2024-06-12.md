# Summary of session-01_2024-06-12

**Date:** 2024‑06‑12

## Core Idea
A detailed, time‑boxed weekly plan that protects focused woodworking blocks while accommodating chores, exercise, and downtime. The plan is broken down into concrete 90‑minute Tuesday/Thursday sessions and 3‑4‑hour Saturday sessions, tailored to a small side‑table project built from rough 4/4 stock using mortise‑and‑tenon joinery and a simple overlay drawer with side‑mounted runners.

## Weekly Rhythm
- **Weeknight slots (6:30‑10:30 pm)** – Two dedicated “shop nights” (Tue & Thu) of ~90 minutes each, plus lighter evenings for exercise or chores.
- **Saturday block** – 3‑4 hours of uninterrupted “deep work” in the shop.
- **Rules** – “No errands after 6:30 pm on shop nights” and a “screen buffer” until the shop block ends.

## Session‑by‑Session Checklist
| Week | Day | Duration | Focus | Stop Point & Staging |
|------|-----|----------|-------|----------------------|
| **1** | Tue | 90 min | Mill leg blanks from rough 4/4 stock (flatten, plane, rip) | Four squared leg blanks ready; taper layout tools staged |
|      | Thu | 90 min | Mill apron stock, layout leg tapers | Aprons squared; taper marks laid out; jig staged |
|      | Sat | 3‑4 h | Cut leg tapers, mortise‑and‑tenon joinery, test‑fit base | Parts dry‑fit, clamps, glue, cauls staged for next night |
| **2** | Tue | 90 min | Tune joinery, adjust tenons/mortises, stage glue area | Cleaned‑up fit, glue, clamps ready |
|      | Thu | 90 min | Glue‑up of the base only | Base clamped, drying; drawer stock staged |
|      | Sat | 3‑4 h | Build overlay drawer (cut parts, rabbets, glue, side runners) | Functional drawer assembled, sandpaper & finish staged |
| **3** | Tue | 90 min | Surface prep: flush joints, sand base & drawer | Surfaces ready for finish |
|      | Thu | 90 min | Apply first finish coat (wipe‑on oil/varnish) | Parts positioned for drying |
|      | Sat | 3‑4 h | Light scuff‑sand, final coats, hardware install, final touch‑ups | Completed side table ready |

## Task Allocation Strategy
- **Weeknight tasks** – Low‑friction, easy‑to‑pause work (layout, milling small pieces, joinery tuning, glue‑up, sanding, first finish coat).
- **Saturday tasks** – High‑impact, setup‑heavy work (taper cuts, mortise‑and‑tenon cutting, full drawer construction, final finishing).
