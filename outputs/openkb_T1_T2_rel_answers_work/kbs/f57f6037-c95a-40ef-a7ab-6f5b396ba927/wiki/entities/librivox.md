# Librivox

Librivox is a volunteer‑run organization that provides free public‑domain audiobooks.  Recordings are hosted online and can be streamed or downloaded without cost, making it a popular source for low‑effort, one‑handed listening.

## Role in the session‑34 guide
The document **[[summaries/session-34_2024-11-12]]** recommends several specific Librivox recordings as part of its calm evening scripts:
- *The Wind in the Willows* read by **[[entities/alan-winterrowd]]** – a warm, steady narration ideal for the 20‑minute script.
- *Little Women (Version 2)* – a multi‑reader edition noted for its gentle domestic tone.
- *Anne of Green Gables* read by **[[entities/karen-savage]]** – praised for its soothing, consistent voice.

These recordings are used to provide ad‑free, non‑intrusive audio content that fits the **[[concepts/free-audio-resources]]** and **[[concepts/one-handed-activities]]** strategies outlined for evening routines.

## Why Librivox fits the evening‑routine framework
- **No subscriptions or ads** – aligns with the goal of avoiding apps and paid services.
- **Public‑domain content** – offers a wide range of classic literature that is calm and narrative‑driven, suitable for winding down.
- **Easy one‑handed control** – recordings can be started in a browser or app and left to play while the user engages in simple tactile tasks (e.g., making a drink, watering a plant) as described in the **[[concepts/evening-routines]]**.
