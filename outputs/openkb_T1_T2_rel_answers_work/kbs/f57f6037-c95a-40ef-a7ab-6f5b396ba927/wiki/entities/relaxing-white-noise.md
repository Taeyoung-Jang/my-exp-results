## Relaxing White Noise

**Type:** Organization (YouTube channel)

**Overview**
Relaxing White Noise is a YouTube channel that publishes extended recordings of natural sounds, primarily rain, without music or spoken commentary. The videos are designed for background listening and are free of advertisements and sudden volume changes, making them suitable for low‑effort, one‑handed evening routines.
