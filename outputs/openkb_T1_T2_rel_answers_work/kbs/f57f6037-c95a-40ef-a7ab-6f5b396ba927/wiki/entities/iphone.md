## iPhone

The iPhone is Apple’s line of smartphones, referenced in **session‑34_2024-11-12** as the device used to enable hands‑free, one‑handed evening routines.  The user leverages built‑in iOS features such as **Hey Siri**, Voice Control, and the Clock app’s timer to start, pause, and stop free audio resources without using the braced wrist.

### Key facts from the session
- **Voice activation** – Settings → Siri & Search → “Listen for ‘Hey Siri’” and “Allow Siri When Locked” let the user issue commands like “Hey Siri, pause” or “set a timer for 20 minutes.”
- **Voice Control** – Settings → Accessibility → Voice Control provides commands like “Tap play,” “Tap pause,” and “Go home,” enabling fully hands‑free operation.
- **Auto‑Lock adjustment** – Extending or disabling Auto‑Lock prevents the screen from sleeping during the routine.
- **Audio playback** – The iPhone runs YouTube videos (e.g., from [[entities/nemos-dreamscapes]]) and Librivox audiobooks, with the device placed on a stable surface after starting playback.
- **Silent sleep timer** – Using the **Clock app**’s Timer tab, selecting “Stop Playing” under “When Timer Ends,” and then invoking it via Siri creates a quiet audio cutoff without a loud alarm.
