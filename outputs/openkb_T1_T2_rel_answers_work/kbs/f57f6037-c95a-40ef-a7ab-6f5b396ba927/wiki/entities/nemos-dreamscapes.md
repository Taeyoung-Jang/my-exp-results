# Nemo’s Dreamscapes

Nemo’s Dreamscapes is a YouTube channel that curates vintage‑style ambient videos—primarily rain, lo‑fi jazz, and cozy indoor soundscapes—designed for uninterrupted, low‑energy listening. The channel’s uploads are ad‑free and have become a go‑to source for one‑handed evening routines that avoid apps or subscriptions.

**Key facts**
- **Platform**: YouTube (public, free to watch).
- **Content focus**: Long‑form ambient loops such as rain, vintage jazz, and fireplace sounds, all presented with minimal introductions.
- **Typical titles referenced**: "Lofi Rainy Jazz • 1940s Rainy Night Ambience" and "Rainy Night in a Cozy Room • Vintage Ambience" (search the exact titles on the channel).
- **Use case in the wiki**: Recommended in [[summaries/session-34_2024-11-12]] as a core audio source for 20‑, 45‑, and 90‑minute one‑handed evening scripts.
- **Related concepts**: [[concepts/evening-routines]], [[concepts/free-audio-resources]], [[concepts/one-handed-activities]].
- **Related entities**: [[entities/calmed-by-nature]], [[entities/relaxed-guy]], [[entities/relaxing-white-noise]], [[entities/michael-sealey]], [[entities/miracle-forest]].

The channel’s consistent, low‑stimulus soundtracks make it ideal for users looking to unwind without the distraction of ads, subscription prompts, or complex setup steps.
