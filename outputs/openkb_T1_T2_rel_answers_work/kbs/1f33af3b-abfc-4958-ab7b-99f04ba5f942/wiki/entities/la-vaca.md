## La Vaca

**Type:** Organization

**Location:** [[entities/barcelona]]

### Overview
La Vaca is a coworking venue in Barcelona known for its relaxed atmosphere and frequent afternoon social activities such as drop‑in coworking sessions, language exchanges, and short fitness or creative workshops.
