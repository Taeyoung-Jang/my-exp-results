## Barcelona

Barcelona is the capital of Catalonia, a major Mediterranean port city in Spain renowned for its architecture, arts scene, and diverse neighborhoods. It serves as the backdrop for the weekly routine template described in [[summaries/session-31_2025-02-12]], which leverages the city's distinct districts to blend work, exploration, and social interaction.

### How Barcelona Supports the Routine
The city's layout allows a **[[concepts/time-blocking]]** approach where a fixed "exploration window" (15:00‑17:30) can be spent in any of the listed neighborhoods, each offering lively street life, cafés, and community events. This aligns with **[[concepts/routine-anchoring]]**, giving a predictable rhythm while still providing variety.

The template also exemplifies **[[concepts/social-integration]]** by embedding language exchanges, coworking meet‑ups, and short workshops into the afternoon slot, turning everyday city navigation into intentional social opportunities.
