# Ciutadella Park

**Type:** place

## Overview
Ciutadella Park is one of Barcelona's largest and most popular public parks, located near the city centre. It features a lake, expansive lawns, monuments, and a range of recreational facilities, making it a key spot for relaxation and outdoor activities.
