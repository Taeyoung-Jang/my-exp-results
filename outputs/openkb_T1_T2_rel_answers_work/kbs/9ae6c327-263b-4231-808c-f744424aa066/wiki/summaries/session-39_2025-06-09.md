# Summary of Session 39 (2025‑06‑09)

**Goal:** Create a low‑effort weekday dinner system for an early‑shift bakery worker who needs meals ready by 7:30 PM and finished by 9 PM.

## Key Ideas
- **Batch / base cooking**: Prepare a neutral starch ([[entities/microwave-rice]] or dry rice) and a tray of roasted vegetables on Sunday.  
- **Protein shortcuts**: Use [[entities/rotisserie-chicken]], canned beans, pre‑cooked lentils, or other ready‑to‑heat proteins.  
- **Assembly‑style meals**: Combine the pre‑cooked base, vegetables, and protein with a quick sauce; each dinner ≤ 15 min.  
- **No‑stovetop option**: Replace the gnocchi night with a microwave‑ready baked potato topped with veggies, chicken, and yogurt/cheese.  
- **Mild flavor focus**: No spicy ingredients; sauces rely on [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/plain-yogurt]], [[entities/olive-oil]], honey, and dried herbs.

## Sunday Prep Checklist (≈ 45 min) [[concepts/meal-prep]]
1. Cook rice (pot ≈ 20 min) *or* set out 3–4 microwave rice packets.  
2. Roast baby carrots, broccoli florets, and cherry tomatoes on a sheet pan (≈ 25 min).  
3. Shred the [[entities/rotisserie-chicken]] and store in a container.  
4. Cool and portion the roasted vegetables.  
5. All components ready for quick weeknight assembly.

## 5‑Day Dinner Schedule [[concepts/quick-dinner-assemblies]] [[concepts/weeknight-time-management]]
| Day | Meal (≤ 15 min) |
|-----|----------------|
| **Mon** | Warm rice & roasted veg, add chicken, finish with [[entities/lemon]] & [[entities/olive-oil]]. |
| **Tue** | Microwave‑ready baked potato, split, top with roasted veg, chicken, and [[entities/plain-yogurt]] or cheese. |
| **Wed** | Warm a can of mild white beans, serve over rice + veg, sprinkle cheese or dressing. |
| **Thu** | Heat pre‑cooked lentils or mild soup, stir in chicken, optional side of rice. |
| **Fri** | Bowl of microwave rice, frozen peas (microwaved), leftover chicken, spoon of yogurt or mild dressing. |

## Grocery List (single‑person weekly quantities)
- 1 [[entities/rotisserie-chicken]]  
- 3–4 microwave rice packets **or** 1 bag dry rice  
- 1 bag baby carrots  
- 1 bag broccoli florets  
- 1 box cherry tomatoes  
- 1 bag frozen peas or mixed veg  
- 2 cans mild white beans  
- 1 pack pre‑cooked lentils **or** 1 mild prepared soup  
- 2 microwave‑ready potatoes  
- 2 [[entities/lemon]]  
- 1 small tub [[entities/plain-yogurt]] **or** mild dressing  
- 1 small bunch greens  
- Cheese, [[entities/olive-oil]], salt, optional honey & dried herbs  

## Mild Sauce / Dressing Ideas
1. **Lemon‑yogurt drizzle** – [[entities/plain-yogurt]] + [[entities/lemon]] juice + pinch of salt.  
2. **Honey‑herb oil** – [[entities/olive-oil]] + [[entities/lemon]] + tiny honey squeeze + dried herbs.
