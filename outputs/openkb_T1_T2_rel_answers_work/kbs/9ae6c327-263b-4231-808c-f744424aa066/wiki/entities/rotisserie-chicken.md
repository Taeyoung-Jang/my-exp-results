# Rotisserie Chicken

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** A ready‑cooked whole chicken sold by grocery stores, often used for quick meals.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-09]]:**
- Shredded on Sunday as part of a batch‑prep routine.
- Serves as the main protein for four of the five weekday dinners.
- Combined with rice, roasted vegetables, beans, lentils, or a microwave‑baked potato.
- Paired with simple mild sauces such as lemon‑yogurt drizzle or olive‑oil lemon dressing.
