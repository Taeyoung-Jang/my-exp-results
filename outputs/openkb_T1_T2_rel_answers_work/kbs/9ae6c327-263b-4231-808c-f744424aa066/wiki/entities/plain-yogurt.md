# Plain Yogurt

Plain yogurt is an unsweetened dairy product that serves as a versatile, mild sauce or dressing.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-09]]
- Suggested as one of the **quick, low‑effort sauce options** for the Friday dinner bowl.
- Paired with lemon, olive oil, or herbs to add freshness without extra cooking time.
- Works well with microwave‑ready components like [[entities/microwave-rice]] and the baked potato from [[entities/microwave-baked-potato]].
- Aligns with the overall **[[concepts/meal-prep]]** strategy of preparing a base on Sunday and assembling meals in ≤ 15 minutes.
- Supports the **[[concepts/quick-dinner-assemblies]]** approach by providing a ready‑to‑use creamy element.
- Fits into the **[[concepts/weeknight-time-management]]** goal of finishing dinner by 9 PM.

## Related Entities
- [[entities/lemon]] – often mixed with plain yogurt for a citrus‑yogurt drizzle.
- [[entities/olive-oil]] – another mild dressing component mentioned alongside yogurt.
- [[entities/rotisserie-chicken]] – a protein that can be combined with yogurt‑based sauces.

## Usage Tips
- Whisk with a squeeze of [[entities/lemon]] and a pinch of salt for a quick lemon‑yogurt drizzle.
- Stir into cooked vegetables or rice for extra creaminess.
- Top a microwave‑baked potato for a light, protein‑rich meal.
