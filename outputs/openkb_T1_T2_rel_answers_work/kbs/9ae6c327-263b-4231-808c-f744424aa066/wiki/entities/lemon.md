## Lemon

Lemon is a fresh citrus fruit that provides a light, acidic finish to several meals in the **weekday dinner system** described in [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-09]].

### Key roles in the plan
- **Flavor boost**: Squeezed over warm rice, roasted vegetables, and shredded [[entities/rotisserie-chicken]] to add brightness without extra cooking time.
- **Sauce component**: Combined with plain yogurt to create a quick **lemon‑yogurt drizzle** used on the baked‑potato night and other bowls.
- **Time‑saving**: Requires no prep beyond a quick squeeze, supporting [[concepts/quick-dinner-assemblies]] and [[concepts/weeknight-time-management]].

### Relation to broader concepts
- Integrated into the **meal‑prep** workflow on Sunday, where the fruit is stored whole and only sliced when needed, exemplifying [[concepts/meal-prep]].
- Acts as a natural, low‑effort seasoning that keeps the weekly menu varied while staying mild.

### Associated items
- Works well with [[entities/olive-oil]], [[entities/plain-yogurt]], and the pre‑cooked components such as [[entities/microwave-rice]] and [[entities/microwave-baked-potato]].
