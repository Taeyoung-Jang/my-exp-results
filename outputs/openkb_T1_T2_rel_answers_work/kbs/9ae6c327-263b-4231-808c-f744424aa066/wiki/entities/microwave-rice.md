# Microwave Rice

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** Ready‑to‑heat rice packets that can be prepared in the microwave in 1–2 minutes.

## Role in the weeknight dinner plan (see [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-09]])
In the “Sunday prep” described in the session, the author suggests buying **3–4 microwave‑ready rice packets** (or cooking a pot of dry rice) to serve as the neutral starch for each weekday meal. The packets are stored in the fridge and quickly reheated on the night of each dinner, keeping total cooking time under 15 minutes.

## How it supports key concepts
- Part of a **[[concepts/meal-prep]]** strategy that front‑loads bulk cooking on Sunday.
- Enables **[[concepts/quick-dinner-assemblies]]** by providing an instant base that can be combined with rotisserie chicken, roasted vegetables, beans, or peas.
- Aligns with **[[concepts/weeknight-time-management]]** by reducing active cooking time to a few minutes after a late shift.

## Typical pairings from the plan
- **[[entities/rotisserie-chicken]]** – shredded and added on top.
- **[[entities/olive-oil]]** and **[[entities/lemon]]** – simple dressings.
- **[[entities/plain-yogurt]]** – for a creamy topping on Friday’s bowl.
- Roasted vegetables (carrots, broccoli, cherry tomatoes) prepared on Sunday.

## Practical tips
- Keep the packets in a stack in the fridge or pantry; microwave each for 1–2 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
- Portion a packet per dinner; no extra seasoning is needed if you finish with a light sauce or a squeeze of lemon.
