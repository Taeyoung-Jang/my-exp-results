# Microwave‑Ready Baked Potato

**Type:** Product

A microwave‑ready baked potato is a pre‑cooked whole potato that can be heated in the microwave in just a few minutes. It is sold in grocery stores, often individually wrapped, and requires no additional cooking equipment beyond a microwave.
