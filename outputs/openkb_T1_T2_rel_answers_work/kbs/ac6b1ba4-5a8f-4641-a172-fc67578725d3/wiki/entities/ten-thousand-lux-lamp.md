## 10,000‑lux Light Therapy Lamp

**Type:** product

**Summary** – The 10,000‑lux light therapy lamp is a daylight‑mimicking device that delivers approximately ten thousand lux of illumination when positioned at the recommended distance (about 1–2 feet). It is commonly used in the morning to signal the start of the day and help regulate the body’s internal clock, especially in regions with limited natural daylight.

### Key Specifications
- **Illuminance:** ~10,000 lux at the manufacturer‑specified optimal distance.
- **Recommended Distance:** 1 – 2 feet from the user, based on model specifications.
- **Color Temperature:** Cool “daylight” spectrum (typically 5,000–6,500 K) for a natural morning feel.
- **Session Length:** 20 – 30 minutes per session, integrated into a routine activity such as breakfast or email.
- **Timing:** Use within the first hour or two after waking; stop by early afternoon to avoid disrupting sleep.

### Usage Recommendations from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-12]]
- **Morning Cue:** Begin the session immediately upon waking, even if it is still dark outside (e.g., Toronto winter mornings).
- **Placement:** Position the lamp off to the side at a slight angle; direct eye exposure is not required.
- **Safety Note:** Users with medical conditions should consult a clinician before regular use.
