# Japan Standard Time (JST)

Japan Standard Time (JST) is the official time zone for Japan, set at **UTC+9** and does **not observe daylight‑saving time**. It serves as the reference point for scheduling across multiple international time zones.

## Role in session‑07_2024‑06‑15
The document [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]] centers on a user located in JST who is coordinating friendships with contacts in various regions. JST is used to:
- Define **[[concepts/overlap-windows]]** for convenient call times with friends in [[entities/us-pacific-time]], [[entities/us-eastern-time]], and [[entities/united-kingdom]].
- Structure **[[concepts/quick-check-ins]]** that can be sent at any JST hour, allowing recipients in other zones to view them later.
- Build a **[[concepts/rotating-call-schedule]]** that alternates meeting times so no single participant consistently faces early‑morning or late‑night calls.
