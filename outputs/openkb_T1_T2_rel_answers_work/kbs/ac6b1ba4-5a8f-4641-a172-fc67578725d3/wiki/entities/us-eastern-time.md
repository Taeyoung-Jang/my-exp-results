# US Eastern Time (ET)

US Eastern Time (ET) is the time zone covering the eastern part of the United States, operating at UTC‑5 during standard time and UTC‑4 during daylight saving time.

## Relevance to session‑07
The session‑07 discussion of **overlap windows** for friends in Japan Standard Time referenced ET as a key comparison zone.  Example overlap suggested was:
- 8–10 am JST ↔ 6–8 pm ET, providing a convenient morning‑for‑Japan / evening‑for‑US‑East window.
