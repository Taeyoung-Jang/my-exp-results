# US Pacific Time (PT)

## Overview
US Pacific Time (PT) is the time zone observed in the western United States, including states such as California, Washington, and Oregon. It is UTC‑8 during standard time and UTC‑7 during daylight‑saving time.

## Relevance to [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]]
- Mentioned as the counterpart to **Japan Standard Time** in the discussion of [[concepts/overlap-windows]] for coordinating cross‑time‑zone friendships.
- Forms one side of the proposed [[concepts/rotating-call-schedule]] that alternates weekly calls with a friend located in the US Pacific zone.
- Example overlap slot from the session: **JST 8:00–9:00 am ↔ PT 15:00–16:00 pm**, illustrating a convenient morning‑evening pairing.
