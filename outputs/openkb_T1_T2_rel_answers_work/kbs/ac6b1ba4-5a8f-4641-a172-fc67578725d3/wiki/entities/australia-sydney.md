# Sydney, Australia

Sydney is Australia’s largest city, located in the state of New South Wales. It operates on Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST, UTC+10) and observes daylight saving, shifting to UTC+11 in summer.

## Relevance to [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]]
In the conversation, Sydney (along with Melbourne) was highlighted as an easy overlap partner for a user in [[entities/japan-standard-time]]. Because Sydney is only one to two hours ahead of JST, most of the user’s available windows line up comfortably with friends in Sydney.

The document recommends using [[concepts/overlap-windows]] such as the user’s early evening (7–9 pm JST) which corresponds to Sydney’s 8–10 pm local time. This minimal time‑difference makes scheduling calls or quick chats straightforward.
