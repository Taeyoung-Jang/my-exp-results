## Overview
The conversation provides practical advice for easing the transition to a new city while addressing the impact of limited daylight on energy levels. It covers habit anchoring, social integration, and detailed recommendations for using a [[entities/ten-thousand-lux-lamp]].

## Settling‑In Strategies
- Treat the first weeks as a **gentle onboarding** rather than a test.
- Establish a few **non‑negotiable habits** (e.g., short morning walk, set mealtime, wind‑down routine) to give your body predictability. [[concepts/routine-anchoring]]
- Become a **regular** at a local spot (café, gym class, library) to map the environment as home through repeated micro‑interactions. [[concepts/social-regularity]]
- Pace yourself; transitions are tiring, so slower progress often leads to faster overall settlement. *(adjustment curve)*

## Managing Daylight with Light Therapy
- **Lamp specs:** Aim for ~10,000 lux; use for 20‑30 minutes each morning while performing a routine activity. [[concepts/light-therapy]]
- **Distance:** Sit 1–2 feet from the lamp, checking the manufacturer’s stated effective distance.  
- **Color temperature:** Prefer a cooler “daylight” spectrum; most reputable lamps already meet this.  
- **Timing:** Start within the first hour or two after waking, even if it’s still dark. Stop by early afternoon to avoid shifting the circadian clock. [[concepts/circadian-rhythm-management]]
- If waking before sunrise (e.g., in [[entities/toronto]] winter), use the lamp immediately upon waking rather than waiting for daylight.

## Preventing Late‑Afternoon Energy Crashes
- **Bright indoor lighting** or a short outside walk can signal that the day isn’t over despite darkness.  
- **Protein‑rich snack** provides a metabolic lift.  
- Create a tiny **“second‑wind” ritual** (e.g., brief stretch, tea, or a 5‑minute activity) around the time cravings for sleep arise. [[concepts/second-wind-ritual]]

## Key Takeaways
1. Anchor your days with a few simple, repeatable habits.  
2. Use a 10,000‑lux lamp early in the morning, sit 1–2 ft away, choose daylight‑balanced color, and cease use by early afternoon.  
3. Combat afternoon slump with brighter light, protein snacks, and a consistent second‑wind routine.
