# Rotating Call Schedule

**Definition**: A structured yet flexible approach that alternates which participant selects the call time, spreading inconvenient early‑morning or late‑night slots evenly across friends in different time zones.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents any one person from repeatedly sacrificing sleep or personal time.
- Keeps friendships strong by pairing the fairness of scheduling with the **[[concepts/quick-check-ins]]** mindset of low‑pressure contact.
- Works hand‑in‑hand with identified **[[concepts/overlap-windows]]** to choose times that are mutually acceptable.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]])
1. **Identify Overlap Windows** – Use the overlap windows for JST vs. US Pacific, US Eastern, UK, Australia, Singapore/Hong Kong to find viable time slots.
2. **Rotate Responsibility** – Alternate weeks (or months) so that one week you pick a slot that favors your morning, the next week you pick a slot that favors the other person’s morning.
3. **Use a Simple Table** – The source document provides a plain‑text table showing week‑by‑week friend, JST time, local time, and notes. The table flags any slot that is a *very late* time for the UK friend (Week 4) and suggests swapping it with a less‑late slot (Week 6).
4. **Maintain Flexibility** – The schedule is deliberately loose; if a week doesn’t work, it can roll over without guilt.
5. **Communicate Expectations** – Pair the schedule with a warm template such as “Time zones are wild, so reply whenever you can. No rush at all.” to keep the tone friendly.

|------|--------|----------|------------|-------|

| 2 | UK | Thu 21:00–22:00 | Thu 12:00–13:00 | Easy midday |

| 4 | UK | Sun 10:00–11:00 | Sun 01:00–02:00 | **Very late – swap** |
| 5 | US Pacific | Sat 10:00–11:00 | Fri 17:00–18:00 | Good overlap |
| 6 | UK | Tue 08:00–09:00 | Mon 23:00–00:00 | Borderline late |
| 7 | US Pacific | Sun 21:00–22:00 | Sun 04:00–05:00 | Early for Pacific |
| 8 | UK | Wed 21:00–22:00 | Wed 12:00–13:00 | Comfortable |

**Adjustment**: Swap Week 4 with Week 6 to avoid the 1–2 am UK slot.

## How to Implement
1. List the friends you regularly call and note their time zones.
2. Determine your own available windows (e.g., weekdays 7–9 am & 8–11 pm, weekends 9–11 am & 7–11 pm).
3. Map each week to an overlap window using the **[[concepts/overlap-windows]]** guide.
4. Alternate the selection of windows each week to ensure fairness.
5. Keep a simple table (like the one above) and flag any extreme slots for later swapping.

## Benefits
- **Equitable**: No single participant bears the burden of odd hours.
- **Low‑Pressure**: Aligns with the spirit of **[[concepts/quick-check-ins]]**, making each call feel like a pleasant catch‑up rather than an obligation.
- **Scalable**: Can be expanded to more than two friends by rotating the responsible party each week.
