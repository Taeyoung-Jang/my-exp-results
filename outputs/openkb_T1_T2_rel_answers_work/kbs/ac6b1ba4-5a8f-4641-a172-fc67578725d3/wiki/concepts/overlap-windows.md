# Overlap Windows

**Definition**: Specific time‑slot pairings that align a person’s availability in one time zone with a convenient window for friends living in other time zones.

## Why they matter
When friendships span continents, the biggest obstacle is finding moments when both parties are awake and not forced to stay up late.  Overlap windows provide a practical way to schedule quick check‑ins or calls without imposing undue strain on either side.

## Example windows from [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]]
| Partner zone | JST window | Partner local time | Typical use |
|--------------|------------|-------------------|------------|
| [[entities/us-pacific-time]] (UTC‑8) | 8–10 am JST | 4–6 pm PT | Morning note for you, late‑afternoon for them |
| [[entities/us-eastern-time]] (UTC‑5) | 8–10 am JST | 6–8 pm ET | Same morning slot, early‑evening for them |
| [[entities/united-kingdom]] (UTC+0) | 4–7 pm JST | 8 am–11 am BST | Your afternoon matches their morning |
| [[entities/australia-sydney]] / [[entities/australia-melbourne]] (UTC+10/UTC+11) | 7–9 pm JST | 8–10 pm AEST | Evening for both, just a 1‑hour shift |
| [[entities/singapore]] / [[entities/hong-kong]] (UTC+8) | Any convenient slot | Same local time | No adjustment needed |

## How to use them
1. **Identify mutual availability** using the table above or a personal schedule.
2. **Treat quick check‑ins** (see [[concepts/quick-check-ins]]) as asynchronous “postcards” that can be read later, reducing the need for immediate replies.
3. **Incorporate into a [[concepts/rotating-call-schedule]]** so that the burden of early‑morning or late‑night slots rotates between friends.
4. **Communicate expectations** with friendly templates (e.g., “Time zones are wild, so reply whenever you can.”).

## Benefits
- Reduces scheduling friction across large time gaps.
- Encourages regular, low‑pressure contact.
- Supports fairness by distributing inconvenient slots.
