# Default Action

**Definition**: A Default Action is a deliberately placed, low‑effort non‑digital activity (e.g., a book, a calming object, or a pair of comfy socks) that you perform automatically before reaching for your phone, serving as a habit anchor to break scrolling loops.

## Why it matters
- Provides immediate, friction‑free alternative to the phone → reduces the impulse to scroll.
- Signals a shift in mental mode, reinforcing the start of a [[concepts/tiny_rituals]] or [[concepts/evening_routine]].
- Helps maintain [[concepts/habit_consistency]] by linking a concrete cue to a desired state.

## Core elements
1. **Readily available** – Keep the item within arm’s reach in the space where you unwind.
2. **Low effort** – Should take less than a minute (e.g., opening a book to a bookmark, feeling a textured object, slipping on socks).
3. **Pleasurable or soothing** – It must be something you enjoy, reinforcing the habit loop.

## How to implement
- Identify a small, pleasant object you can store near your night‑stand or couch.
- Pair it with a cue (e.g., “Before I pick up the phone, I will …”).
- Use it consistently as part of your [[concepts/if_then_cues]] (e.g., *If* the baby finally goes down, *then* grab the book instead of the phone).

## Connections to other concepts
- Works with [[concepts/anchor_habits]] by providing a tangible starter action.
- Complements [[concepts/micro_comfort_ritual]] and [[concepts/grounding_pause]] as part of a micro‑routine.
- Benefits from strategic [[concepts/phone_placement]] to increase physical distance from the device.
- Can be rotated among several options, creating [[concepts/mini_moods]] for varied wind‑down experiences.
