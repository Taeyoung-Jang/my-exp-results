## Blue Ridge Parkway

Blue Ridge Parkway is a 469‑mile scenic parkway that traverses the Appalachian Mountains of Virginia and North Carolina. It is renowned for its panoramic views, historic overlooks, and leisurely driving experience.

### Mention in session‑11 (2022‑12‑18)
- The itinerary suggested using the Parkway as a **road‑based option** for a 7‑10‑day vacation.
- Travelers could either **rent a car in New York City** or take an [[entities/amtrak]] train to Charlottesville or Lynchburg and pick up a vehicle there, shortening the initial drive.
- Driving from [[entities/new-york-city]] to the northern terminus of the Parkway takes about **8‑9 hours**, after which the route can be explored at a relaxed pace.
- A week‑long trip that includes motels, park lodges, and meals was estimated to cost **$1,500‑$2,200**, with stress primarily coming from highway traffic before reaching the Parkway and low stress once on the scenic road.
