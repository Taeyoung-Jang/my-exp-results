## Glacier National Park

Glacier National Park is a protected wilderness area in north‑western Montana, United States.  It is highlighted in the **session‑11_2022‑12‑18** conversation as a nature‑focused, train‑accessible vacation option.

### Key points from the session
- **Access by train**: The park can be reached directly via [[entities/empire-builder]], which stops near the park’s western entrance.  This makes it one of the few national parks that visitors can approach without renting a car.
- **Travel time**: From New York City, the recommended route is [[entities/lake-shore-limited]] to Chicago, then the [[entities/empire-builder]] westward, totalling roughly 45‑50 hours door‑to‑door.
- **Budget**: A 7‑10 day trip is estimated at **$2,500‑$3,500**, largely due to lodging costs inside or near the park and the choice of coach vs. sleeper accommodations.
- **Stress level**: The itinerary is labeled the *least predictable* of the options because both long‑distance trains run on freight‑owned tracks, which can cause delays.  This relates to the broader [[concepts/rail_travel]] and [[concepts/buffer_strategy]] discussion about managing schedule risk.
