# Seattle

Seattle is a large city in Washington state, known for its waterfront, tech industry, and as a key stop on several [[entities/amtrak]] routes, especially the [[entities/cascades]] service and the [[entities/empire-builder]] long‑distance train.

## Relevance in session‑11
- Proposed as the **final destination** (or start) in the NYC → Seattle/Portland itinerary.
- Suggested neighborhoods for visitors: **Capitol Hill**, **Belltown**, and **Queen Anne**, all noted for walkability and transit access.
- The itinerary compares two routing variants that differ in whether an overnight buffer in [[entities/chicago]] is added; the Seattle leg benefits from the low‑stress, reliable [[concepts/rail_travel]] offered by the Cascades corridor.
- Stress rating for the Seattle‑Portland route without a Chicago buffer is 4/5, dropping to 2/5 when an overnight stay in Chicago is used as a [[concepts/buffer_strategy]].

## Transportation links
- Arrives via the [[entities/empire-builder]] (westbound) from Chicago.
- Connects to the [[entities/cascades]] line for the short Seattle–Portland segment.
- Accessible from the Lake Shore Limited via the Chicago transfer.
