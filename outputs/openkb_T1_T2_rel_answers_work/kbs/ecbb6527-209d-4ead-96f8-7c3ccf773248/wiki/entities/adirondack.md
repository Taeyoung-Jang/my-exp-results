# Adirondack (train)

**Type:** work (Amtrak passenger train)

**Route:** Operates daily between [[entities/new-york-city]] Penn Station and [[entities/montreal]], traveling through the Hudson Valley and the Adirondack Mountains.

**Key features**:
- All‑day, single‑seat‑through service; no transfers required.
- Border crossing at the US‑Canada border; delays are common, especially on weekends and during peak foliage weeks. See [[concepts/border_delays]].
- Recommended travel mid‑week (Tuesday‑Thursday) to minimize border wait times, as advised in [[summaries/session-11_2022-12-18]].

**Relevance to itineraries**:
- Suggested as the primary option for a flight‑free NYC → Montreal trip in the session‑11 plan.
- Provides a low‑stress, low‑cost alternative to multi‑leg journeys; aligns with the [[concepts/rail_travel]] strategy.

**Related services**:
- Operated by [[entities/amtrak]].
- Connects conceptually with other long‑distance services like [[entities/lake-shore-limited]] and [[entities/empire-builder]] for cross‑country travel.

**Considerations**:
- Travelers sensitive to delays should employ a [[concepts/buffer_strategy]], such as a flexible arrival day in Montreal.
