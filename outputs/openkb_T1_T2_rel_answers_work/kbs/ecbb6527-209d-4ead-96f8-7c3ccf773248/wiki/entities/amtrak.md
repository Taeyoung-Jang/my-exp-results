# Amtrak

## Overview
- Amtrak is the primary **U.S. intercity passenger rail** operator, providing long‑distance and regional services across the United States.

## Role in session‑11 (2022‑12‑18)
- Used as the carrier for all long‑distance train legs in the proposed itineraries:
  - [[entities/lake-shore-limited]] from New York City to Chicago.
  - [[entities/empire-builder]] from Chicago to Seattle or Portland.
  - The [[entities/adirondack]] line (also operated by Amtrak) for the NYC → Montreal route.
- The document highlights Amtrak’s **delay risk** on the Lake Shore Limited, which informs the proposed **buffer strategy** of an overnight stay in [[entities/chicago]].
