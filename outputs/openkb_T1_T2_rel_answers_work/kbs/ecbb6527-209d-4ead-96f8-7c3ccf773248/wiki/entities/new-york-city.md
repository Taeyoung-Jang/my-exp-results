# New York City

New York City (NYC) is the starting point for the travel itineraries discussed in [[summaries/session-11_2022-12-18]]. The user plans several flight‑free trips departing from NYC using [[entities/amtrak]] services.

## Trips referenced

- **NYC → Montreal** using the [[entities/adirondack]] train, a single‑seat ride that involves a border crossing where [[concepts/border_delays]] are a concern.
- **NYC → Seattle/Portland** via the [[entities/lake-shore-limited]] to [[entities/chicago]], then the [[entities/empire-builder]] to the Pacific Northwest. A [[concepts/buffer_strategy]] of an overnight stay in [[entities/chicago]] is suggested to reduce stress.
- A road‑trip option via the [[entities/blue-ridge-parkway]] after a short Amtrak leg to Charlottesville/Lynchburg (not detailed here).

## Related concepts

- [[concepts/buffer_strategy]] – used to mitigate connection risks on the Chicago leg.
- [[concepts/border_delays]] – relevant for the Adirondack‑Montreal route.
