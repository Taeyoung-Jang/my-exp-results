# Chicago

Chicago is a large city in the U.S. state of Illinois, often referenced as a strategic layover point for long‑distance Amtrak trips.

## Role in session‑11 (2022‑12‑18)
- Serves as the **connection hub** between the [[entities/lake-shore-limited]] (NYC → Chicago) and the [[entities/empire-builder]] (Chicago → Seattle/Portland).  
- Suggested as an **overnight buffer** to reduce stress from possible delays on the Lake Shore Limited.  
- Two buffering options were discussed:
  - Overnight on the **outbound** leg only (adds ~24 h, one hotel night, $150‑$250 cost).  
  - Overnight on **both outbound and return** legs (adds two hotel nights, higher cost, but stress rating drops to 1/5).  
- The city is highlighted in the **[[concepts/buffer_strategy]]** and **[[concepts/rail_travel]]** sections of the itinerary analysis.
