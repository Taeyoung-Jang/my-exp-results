# Portland (Place)

Portland is a vibrant city in Oregon, USA, highlighted in the **NYC → Seattle/Portland** travel plan from the session‑11 discussion. It serves as the second major destination after Seattle when using Amtrak’s rail network.

## Key facts from the document
- **Travel access**: Reached via the [[entities/cascades]] corridor, a short 3‑4 hour Amtrak ride between Seattle and Portland.
- **Suggested neighborhoods** for mid‑range lodging: **Pearl District**, **Northwest**, and **Buckman**, all described as walkable and transit‑friendly.
- **Role in itinerary**: After arriving in Seattle (or starting in Portland), travelers spend **days 4‑6** exploring the city, then take the Cascades train to the other city on **day 7**.
- **Stress considerations**: The Cascades segment is noted as “extremely reliable and low stress,” aligning with the broader [[concepts/rail_travel]] strategy and supporting a **buffer strategy** that mitigates delays on longer connections.
- **Budget**: Daily expenses are estimated at **$170‑$260**, covering coach‑class rail tickets, mid‑range hotels, and meals.
