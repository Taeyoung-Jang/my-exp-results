# Summary of Session 11 (2022‑12‑18)

## Core request
- The user wants **flight‑free vacation itineraries** from **[[entities/new-york-city]]** for 7–10 day trips.  
- Focus on realistic routing, door‑to‑door travel time, rough budget (transport + lodging + food), and a **stress rating** that reflects delay sensitivity and transfer complexity.

## Suggested destinations
1. **[[entities/new-york-city]] → [[entities/montreal]]** (single‑train **[[entities/adirondack]]** trip)  
2. **[[entities/new-york-city]] → [[entities/seattle]]/[[entities/portland]]** (long‑distance Amtrak routes) – with two routing variants:  
   - **No Chicago overnight** (direct **[[entities/lake-shore-limited]]** → **[[entities/empire-builder]]** connection)  
   - **Chicago overnight buffer** (add a night in **[[entities/chicago]]** before the **[[entities/empire-builder]]**, either outbound only or both ways)

## Key concepts & findings
- **Rail‑only travel** is viable for both US and Canada; **[[entities/amtrak]]** provides the backbone for cross‑country connections. [[concepts/rail_travel]]
- **Border delays** on the **[[entities/adirondack]]** are mitigated by traveling **mid‑week (Tue‑Thu)** and avoiding the peak foliage window (late Sept – mid‑Oct). [[concepts/border_delays]]
- **Buffer strategy**: an overnight stay in **[[entities/chicago]]** eliminates the high‑stress hand‑off between the **[[entities/lake-shore-limited]]** and the **[[entities/empire-builder]]**. Adding a buffer on the outbound leg is most valuable for delay‑averse travelers. [[concepts/buffer_strategy]]
- **Seasonal crowd impact**: Fall foliage season (≈ Sept 28 – Oct 15) increases both tourist volume and customs processing time, raising stress for the **[[entities/adirondack]]**.  
- **Cost structure**: coach‑class tickets, mid‑range hotels, and average daily food/lodging give a **per‑day budget** of roughly $140‑$220 for **[[entities/montreal]]** and $170‑$260 for the **[[entities/seattle]]/[[entities/portland]]** trips.  
- **Stress rating** (1 = low, 5 = high) depends on connection tightness, border unpredictability, and road‑driving components.

## Practical recommendations
- **[[entities/montreal]]**: Book a mid‑week **[[entities/adirondack]]** (Tue‑Thu) departure; allow a flexible arrival day for border inspection. Budget ~ $1,500‑$2,000 total.  
- **[[entities/seattle]]/[[entities/portland]]**: If delay‑averse, add **one overnight in [[entities/chicago]] on the outbound leg**; it adds ~$150‑$250 but drops stress to 2/5. Adding a second night on return further reduces stress to 1/5 at modest extra cost.  
- **Road‑based alternative**: **[[entities/blue-ridge-parkway]]** drive can be done by renting a car after a short Amtrak leg to Charlottesville/Lynchburg, but this route is outside the primary table.
