# Summary of session-34_2023-10-12

**Main focus:** How to showcase a framed diploma in a small hallway without it feeling like a résumé wall.

## Placement
- Hang the diploma at eye‑level in a semi‑private spot (e.g., hallway outside the bedroom) to keep it personal rather than a public statement.
- Center the frame over a console table for a clean look; add a plant or a stack of books off‑center to create a balanced vignette.
- Keep the wall uncluttered; the diploma becomes part of a small décor grouping rather than a standalone display.

## Lighting options
- **Table lamp** on the console: use a fabric or frosted‑glass shade for soft, diffused light; aim the light slightly to the side to avoid glare on the glass.
- **Picture light** above the frame: choose a slim, matte‑finish fixture (black, bronze, brushed brass) that is roughly two‑thirds to full width of the frame. This provides focused illumination without a formal gallery feel.
- **Bulb specifications:** warm white 2700‑3000 K, 300‑450 lumens (LED preferred). Warm light creates a cozy atmosphere and reduces harsh reflections.
- **Battery‑powered options:** a quality bar‑style puck light with a warm color temperature and diffuser works fine; avoid cheap, spotty puck lights.

## Styling tips
- Pair the diploma with personal items (photo, favorite print, small plant) to ground the display.
- Use the console surface for additional personality items, keeping the overall look intentional yet relaxed.
- In narrow, low‑light hallways, a picture light can be more effective than a table lamp, freeing up console space.
