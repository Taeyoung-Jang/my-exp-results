# UV‑Protection Sunglasses

**Type:** product

UV‑Protection sunglasses are specially designed eyewear that filter out the majority of ultraviolet (UV) radiation from sunlight, helping to protect the eyes from long‑term damage such as cataracts and macular degeneration.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]
- Recommended as a daily habit for outdoor activities to shield eyes from UV exposure.
- Complement other vision‑care practices like proper **lighting** ([[concepts/lighting-for-vision]]) and ergonomic screen setups ([[concepts/screen-ergonomics]]).
- Mentioned alongside other eye‑comfort aids such as [[entities/artificial-tears]] and [[entities/humidifier]].

## Related Concepts
- **Lighting for Vision:** Proper lighting reduces the need for squinting, but UV‑Protection sunglasses address damage from sunlight that lighting alone cannot mitigate.
- **Screen Ergonomics:** While sunglasses are for outdoor use, the overall eye‑health strategy includes screen‑related habits like the 20‑20‑20 rule and correct posture.
- **Blue‑Light Glasses:** Unlike UV‑Protection sunglasses, blue‑light glasses target screen‑emitted light and are mostly marketing‑driven for daytime use.

## Practical Guidance
1. Choose sunglasses that meet the standards for **100 % UV‑A and UV‑B protection**.
2. Wear them whenever outdoors, even on cloudy days, as UV rays can penetrate clouds.
3. Pair them with regular eye‑care routines—regular breaks, adequate humidity, and artificial tears when needed.
