# Night Gel Lubricant

**Entity type:** product

Night gel lubricant is a topical ocular lubricant formulated to provide longer‑lasting moisture than standard artificial‑tear drops while minimizing visual blur. It is typically used in the evening before sleep to protect the ocular surface overnight.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-15]]
- **When to use:** Recommended when a user wakes up with dry or sticky eyes, or when lids do not close fully during sleep. It offers a middle‑ground between a standard drop and a thicker night ointment.
- **Effect on vision:** Causes mild temporary blur that usually clears after a few minutes; less blur than an ointment, which can persist into the morning.
- **Frequency:** Can be applied once nightly; additional use is optional if dryness is severe.
- **Safety:** Preservative‑free formulations are safe for regular use and do not build up toxicity.

## Practical tips
1. **Apply after lid cleaning:** Use a mild lid cleanser, then apply a thin layer of gel to the lower fornix.
2. **Allow time for absorption:** Wait 2–3 minutes before lying down to reduce transfer onto the pillow.
3. **Monitor comfort:** If persistent blur or irritation occurs, consider switching to a night ointment or consulting an eye care professional.
4. **Environmental support:** In dry indoor climates, a small humidifier can complement gel use.
