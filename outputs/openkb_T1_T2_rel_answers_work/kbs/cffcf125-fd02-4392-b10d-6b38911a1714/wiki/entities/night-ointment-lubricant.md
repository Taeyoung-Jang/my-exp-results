# Night Ointment Lubricant (product)

## Overview
Night Ointment Lubricant is a high‑viscosity, preservative‑free eye ointment designed to be applied before sleep. Its thick texture provides prolonged lubrication of the ocular surface, helping to reduce overnight dryness, especially for people whose lids do not fully close or who experience severe morning irritation.

## Recommended Use
- Apply a small strip (about the size of a grain of rice) to the lower lid **after** a gentle lid‑wash in the evening.
- Use when you notice persistent dryness upon waking, or if you have **meibomian gland dysfunction** that leaves the tear film unstable.
- Typical frequency: **once nightly**; increase to twice per night only under professional guidance.

## Gel vs. Ointment Decision
See [[concepts/gel_vs_ointment_nighttime_lubricants]] for a detailed comparison. In brief:
- **Gel**: lighter, may cause mild blur for 10–15 minutes after application.
- **Ointment**: thicker, provides all‑night moisture but can blur vision into the morning; best for severe dryness or incomplete eyelid closure.

## Safety & Warning Signs
- Preservative‑free formulation is safe for repeated nightly use; no known toxicity.
- If you find yourself needing the ointment **every 1–2 hours** even after sleep, this may signal worsening dry‑eye disease—consult your optometrist.
- Watch for signs listed in [[concepts/dry_eye_warning_signs]] (sharp pain, sudden redness, photophobia, vision change) and seek prompt care.
