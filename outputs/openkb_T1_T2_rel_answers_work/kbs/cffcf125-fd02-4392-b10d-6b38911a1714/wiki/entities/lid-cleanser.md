# Lid Cleanser

**Type:** product

## Overview
Lid cleanser is a gentle, non‑foaming cleaning solution designed for daily eyelid hygiene. It helps remove debris, excess oil, and bacterial buildup from the eyelid margin and lashes, supporting healthy meibomian gland function and reducing dry‑eye symptoms.

## Recommended Use (from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-15]])
- **When:** In the evening, after the warm‑compress routine.
- **How:** Apply a small amount to a clean cotton pad or fingertip, gently wipe the upper and lower eyelid margins, then rinse with lukewarm water.
- **Frequency:** Once daily is sufficient for most users; increase to twice daily if you notice persistent debris or oily lids.

## Benefits
- Keeps the eyelid margin clean, aiding the effectiveness of warm‑compress therapy [[concepts/warm_compress_therapy]].
- Supports healthy meibomian gland function, helping to prevent or alleviate [[concepts/meibomian_gland_dysfunction]].
- Reduces the risk of irritation that can exacerbate [[concepts/dry_eye_warning_signs]].

## Considerations
- Choose a **preservative‑free** formulation to avoid irritation, especially if you use it multiple times per day.
- Avoid cleansers containing harsh surfactants or fragrances, which can disrupt the tear film.
- If you experience redness, burning, or increased dryness after use, discontinue and consult your eye care professional.
