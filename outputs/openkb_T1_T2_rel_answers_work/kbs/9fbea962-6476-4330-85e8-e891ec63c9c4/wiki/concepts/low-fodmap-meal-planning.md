# Low‑FODMAP Meal Planning

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** A systematic approach to creating weekly menus that stay within the strict low‑FODMAP elimination phase while keeping meals varied and satisfying.

## Core Principles
1. **Base Foods** – Choose a small set of safe staples (e.g., plain rice, baked chicken, low‑FODMAP broth) and batch‑cook them once a week.
2. **Portion Control** – Use exact servings (e.g., 1 cup cooked rice, 3–4 oz chicken, ½ cup vegetables) to stay within tolerated amounts.
3. **Flavor Variation** – Apply different herbs, spices, and safe add‑ins each night to change texture and taste without introducing high‑FODMAP ingredients.
4. **Midweek Lifesaver** – Prepare a single, reheatable dish (such as a simple chicken‑rice soup) that can be upgraded with fresh elements when needed.
5. **Safety Checks** – Identify and avoid hidden onion/garlic sources in packaged broths, seasoning blends, and sauces.

## Practical Implementation (from [[summaries/session-33_2025-05-07]])
- **7‑Day Checklist** – Each day lists the base foods plus a specific low‑FODMAP add‑in:
  - Monday: rice, chicken, broth, ½ cup carrots or zucchini.
  - Tuesday: rice, chicken seared in garlic‑infused oil, thyme/oregano, lemon juice.
  - Wednesday: rice, chicken, broth with ginger and spinach.
  - Thursday: rice, chicken, sesame oil, toasted sesame seeds.
  - Friday: rice, chicken, broth with turmeric and black pepper.
  - Saturday: rice, shredded chicken with lemon‑oregano, cucumber or baby spinach.
  - Sunday: rice stewed with broth, carrots or zucchini, chicken pieces.
- **Safe Rotation List** – Herbs/spices (thyme, oregano, rosemary, basil, parsley, chives, scallion greens, turmeric, black pepper, ginger) and vegetables (carrots, zucchini, spinach, cucumber, green beans) in standard portion sizes.
- **Flavor Kits** – Pre‑made jar mixes that add quick variety:
  - Lemon‑herb kit (lemon juice, garlic‑infused oil, thyme/oregano) – see [[concepts/flavor-kit-prep]].
  - Ginger‑sesame kit (grated ginger, sesame oil, tamari) – see [[concepts/flavor-kit-prep]].
  - Golden kit (garlic‑infused oil, turmeric, black pepper).
- **Hidden Risks** – Store‑bought broths and seasoning blends often contain onion or garlic powder; see [[concepts/hidden-onion-garlic-sources]].

## Benefits
- Reduces decision fatigue by reusing a limited set of safe ingredients.
- Keeps meals interesting through texture, herb, and spice changes.
- Supports gut health by staying strictly within low‑FODMAP limits.
- Enables quick, low‑stress cooking on busy weekdays.
