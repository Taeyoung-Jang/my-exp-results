# Flavor‑Kit Preparation

**Concept Type:** Concept

**Definition**: A systematic method for assembling portable, pre‑measured condiment jars ("flavor kits") that can be mixed into base dishes to provide distinct taste profiles without additional cooking time.

---

## Why Use Flavor‑Kit Preparation?
- **Speed** – A ready‑to‑use jar adds flavor in seconds, ideal for low‑energy evenings.
- **Consistency** – Exact measurements keep sodium, spice, and FODMAP limits predictable.
- **Variety** – Rotating kits prevents meal fatigue while staying within the [[entities/low-fodmap-elimination-diet]] restrictions.

---

## Core Elements of a Flavor Kit
| Kit | Ingredients (volume) | Storage Life (fridge) | Typical Use |
|-----|----------------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| **Lemon‑Herb** | 2 Tbsp lemon juice, 1 Tbsp garlic‑infused oil, ½ tsp dried thyme or oregano | 4–5 days | Drizzle over rice‑chicken bowls for a bright finish |
| **Ginger‑Sesame** | 1 Tbsp grated fresh ginger, 1 Tbsp sesame oil, 1 Tbsp plain tamari | 3–4 days | Stir into broth‑based meals for a gentle ginger‑sesame note |
| **Golden** | 2 Tbsp garlic‑infused oil, ½ tsp turmeric, pinch of black pepper | 5–7 days | Mix into broth or rice for a warm, earthy flavor |

> **Note:** All kits avoid onion and garlic pieces; they rely on *infused* oil for a permissible garlic flavor.

---

## Preparation Steps
1. **Gather ingredients** – Use low‑FODMAP certified items. Verify that garlic‑infused oil, tamari, and lemon juice contain no added onion/garlic powders (see [[concepts/hidden-onion-garlic-sources]]). 
2. **Measure** – Use a measuring spoon for each component to ensure repeatable taste and to stay within safe portion limits.
3. **Combine** – Add ingredients to a small, airtight jar (e.g., 2‑oz glass or BPA‑free plastic). Label with kit name and date.
4. **Store** – Keep jars in the refrigerator. Shake before each use to re‑mix settled solids.
5. **Rotate** – Prepare a fresh batch every 3‑5 days to maintain flavor quality and prevent spoilage.

---

## Integrating Kits into a Low‑FODMAP Meal Plan
The kits complement the weekly template described in [[summaries/session-33_2025-05-07]]. For each day, a different kit can be added to the base of rice, baked chicken, and broth:
- **Monday** – No kit (plain base).
- **Tuesday** – Lemon‑Herb kit for a citrusy lift.
- **Wednesday** – Ginger‑Sesame kit for an Asian‑style note.
- **Thursday** – Golden kit for a warm, turmeric‑infused feel.
- **Friday onward** – Repeat or mix kits as desired.

---

## Safety Checklist
- ✅ Confirm all ingredients are listed as **low‑FODMAP** and **onion‑free**.
- ✅ Avoid hidden onion/garlic in commercial tamari or broths (refer to [[concepts/hidden-onion-garlic-sources]]).
- ✅ Use only the portion sizes shown; excess oil can increase fat content beyond personal tolerance.
