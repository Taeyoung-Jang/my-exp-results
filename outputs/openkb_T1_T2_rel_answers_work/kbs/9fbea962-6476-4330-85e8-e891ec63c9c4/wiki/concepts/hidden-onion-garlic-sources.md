# Hidden Onion & Garlic Sources

**Concept definition**: A reference guide to the everyday pantry items, packaged foods, and prepared products that can contain hidden onion or garlic ingredients, which are problematic for strict low‑FODMAP elimination diets.

---

## Why it matters
During a **low‑FODMAP elimination** phase, even trace amounts of onion or garlic can trigger symptoms. The session‑33 conversation emphasizes the need to audit ingredients carefully, especially in pre‑made broths, seasoning blends, and sauces, to keep meals truly elimination‑safe.

---

## Common hidden sources
| Category | Typical examples | What to check for |
|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Store‑bought broths & stocks** | Chicken broth, vegetable stock, bouillon cubes | "onion powder," "garlic powder," or "natural flavor" (often includes onion/garlic extracts) |
| **Seasoning blends & spice mixes** | Italian seasoning, taco seasoning, herb rubs | Ingredient list may list "garlic," "onion," or "garlic/onion powder" even if the label only mentions "herbs" |
| **Sauces & condiments** | Tamari, soy sauce, mustard, vinaigrette, BBQ sauce, ready‑made marinades | Look for "garlic extract," "onion extract," or "flavorings" |
| **Processed snacks & ready meals** | Pre‑made soups, frozen meals, seasoning packets, instant noodles | Often contain hidden aromatics in the flavor packet |
| **Prepared mixes** | Flavor‑kit ingredients (e.g., garlic‑infused oil) | Verify that "infused" oils are truly **garlic‑infused** (acceptable) vs. containing garlic pieces; avoid any that list powdered garlic unless tolerated |

---

## Practical steps
1. **Read labels thoroughly** – focus on the *full* ingredient list, not just the front‑of‑pack claims.
2. **Choose "no onion/garlic" labeled products** – many brands now produce low‑FODMAP‑friendly broth and sauces.
3. **Make your own base** – preparing broth, sauces, and seasoning mixes at home (as suggested in [[concepts/flavor-kit-prep]]) guarantees control over hidden ingredients.
4. **Maintain a checklist** – keep a running list of trusted brands and products to streamline grocery trips.
