# Salt

**Type:** product

**Description:** A basic mineral used to enhance flavor, essential for building layers of seasoning in cooking.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-03_2024-09-05]]
- Recommended as one of the core pantry staples for everyday cooking.
- Emphasized to be added **early** in the cooking process to ensure ingredients taste good as they cook.
- Used in conjunction with other basics like [[entities/black-pepper]], [[entities/garlic]], and [[entities/onion]] to create a balanced flavor foundation.
- Part of the **[[concepts/seasoning-layering]]** approach: early salt, mid‑stage aromatics and spices, late‑stage acid or fresh herbs.
- Works with various acids (e.g., [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/lime]], [[entities/rice-vinegar]]) to finish dishes without becoming sour, as described in **[[concepts/acidity-timing]]**.

## Related entities
- [[entities/black-pepper]] – common companion seasoning.

- [[entities/soy-sauce]], [[entities/miso]] – umami boosters that complement salt.
