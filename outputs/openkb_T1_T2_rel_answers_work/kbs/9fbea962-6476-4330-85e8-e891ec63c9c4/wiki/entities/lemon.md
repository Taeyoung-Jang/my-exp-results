# Lemon

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A citrus fruit that provides bright acidity, commonly used to finish dishes and to add flavor variation in low‑FODMAP meal plans.

## Culinary role in session‑03_2024‑09‑05
- Recommended as the go‑to acid for Indian‑style dishes, added near the end of cooking to lift flavors without overwhelming the palate.
- Illustrates the principle of acidity timing: add a few drops, stir, taste, and adjust to avoid a sour taste.

## Role in low‑FODMAP meal planning (session‑33_2025‑05‑07)
- Key component of the **lemon‑herb flavor kit**: 2 Tbsp lemon juice combined with garlic‑infused oil and dried thyme or oregano, kept for 4–5 days in the fridge.
- Used to brighten bowls, stir‑fry‑style plates, and the midweek “lifesaver” chicken‑rice soup, providing a fresh note without adding FODMAPs.
- Paired with safe herbs and spices such as thyme, oregano, and ginger to create distinct nightly flavors while staying within elimination guidelines.

## Connection to seasoning workflow
- Fits into the three‑stage seasoning layering approach as the final layer that brightens and balances the dish after salts, aromatics, and spices.
- Works alongside other low‑FODMAP safe acids like [[entities/rice-vinegar]] for quick flavor adjustments.

## Related acids and flavors
- Often paired with other finishing acids such as lime (used for Mexican cuisine) and [[entities/rice-vinegar]] (used for stir‑fry dishes).
- Can be complemented by a pinch of [[entities/honey]] to mellow sharp heat when needed.

## Practical tip
- Use a conservative amount, taste, and then decide if more is required; this prevents the dish from becoming sour.
