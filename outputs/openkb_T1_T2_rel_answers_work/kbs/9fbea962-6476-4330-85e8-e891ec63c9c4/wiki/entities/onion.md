# Onion (product)

Onion is a fundamental aromatic vegetable that forms the flavor base for a wide range of cuisines. In the *session-03_2024-09-05* conversation, onions are highlighted as an essential early‑stage ingredient for seasoning layering.

## Key Points from the Session
- **Early seasoning step**: Onions are softened with garlic and a pinch of salt before adding spices, helping to build depth from the start.
- **Flavor layering**: Used alongside garlic, salt, and [[entities/black-pepper]] to create a savory foundation before introducing warm spices like [[entities/paprika]] and [[entities/cumin]].
- **Complementary ingredients**: Paired with acid finishes such as [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/lime]], or [[entities/rice-vinegar]] to brighten dishes.
