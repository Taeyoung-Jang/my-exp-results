## Garlic

Garlic is a fundamental flavor base in many cuisines. In the **seasoning‑layering** approach described in the session, garlic is added early, after the onions have softened, to allow its aroma to develop and to provide a savory foundation for the dish. It is typically sautéed with a bit of oil to **toast** the cloves, which enhances its sweetness and reduces raw sharpness.

### Key points from the session
- Added early in the cooking process alongside **onion** and **salt** to build depth. 
- When combined with oil, garlic benefits from the **spice‑toasting** technique, releasing richer flavors.
- Works well with other pantry staples such as **black‑pepper**, **cumin**, and **paprika** to create layered taste profiles.
- Serves as a bridge between the base aromatics and later additions like acids (lemon, lime, rice‑vinegar) and finishing herbs.
