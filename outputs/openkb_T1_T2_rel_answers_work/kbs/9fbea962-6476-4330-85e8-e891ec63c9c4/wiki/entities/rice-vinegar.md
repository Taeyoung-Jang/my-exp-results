## Rice Vinegar

Rice vinegar is a mild, slightly sweet vinegar made from fermented rice. It is commonly used in Asian cuisine, especially for stir‑fry seasoning and dressings.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-03_2024-09-05]]
- Recommended as the gentlest acid for stir‑fries, added near the end of cooking to brighten flavors without making the dish sour.
- Used in the "acid timing" approach, pairing with other acids like lime and lemon for different cuisines.
- Works well with other pantry staples such as [[entities/salt]], [[entities/garlic]], and [[entities/onion]].
