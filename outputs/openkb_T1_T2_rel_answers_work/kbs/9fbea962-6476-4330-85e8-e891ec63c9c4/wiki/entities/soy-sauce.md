# Soy Sauce

**Type:** product

**Description:** A fermented soy‑based liquid that provides salty umami depth to dishes.

- Paired with other fundamentals such as [[entities/salt]], [[entities/garlic]], [[entities/onion]], [[entities/miso]], [[entities/cumin]], and [[entities/paprika]].

- Works well alongside acid finishes (e.g., [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/lime]], [[entities/rice-vinegar]]) to balance saltiness.
- Complements the principles of [[concepts/spice-toasting]] by adding depth before spices are toasted.
