# Sesame Oil

Sesame oil is a fragrant, nutty‑flavored oil commonly used as a finishing touch in many cuisines, especially Asian stir‑fries and spicy dishes. It adds depth without overwhelming heat and pairs well with acidic ingredients such as lemon juice or rice vinegar. In the low‑FODMAP elimination context, it appears in the **ginger‑sesame flavor kit** (see [[concepts/flavor-kit-prep]]) as a safe, quick‑add option that keeps meals interesting while staying within dietary limits.

## Key points from the sessions
- Listed among "flavored oil" options alongside chili oil for finishing spicy dishes (from [[summaries/session-03_2024-09-05]]).
- Helps "give the heat more depth instead of just sharpness" and works well with a squeeze of acid (lemon, rice vinegar) and fresh herbs.
- Included in the **ginger‑sesame kit** for low‑FODMAP meal planning, providing a nutty base that can be combined with grated ginger and tamari (see [[concepts/low-fodmap-meal-planning]]). The kit keeps 3–4 days in the fridge.
- Suitable for use in flavor‑kit prep to quickly vary the "feel" of meals without adding onion or garlic.
