# Miso

Miso is a traditional Japanese fermented paste made from soybeans, salt, and a kōji starter. It provides a deep umami flavor and salty richness, making it a versatile ingredient in many cuisines.

## Key facts from session‑03_2024‑09‑05
- Listed as one of the essential **pantry staples** for everyday cooking, alongside salt, garlic, onion, and acids like lemon or lime.
- Recommended as an **umami booster** to round out dishes, especially when layering seasonings according to the [[concepts/seasoning-layering]] approach.
- Can be used in place of or together with other salty ingredients such as [[entities/soy-sauce]] to add depth without excessive sodium.

## Typical uses
- Added toward the **mid‑stage** of cooking to allow its flavor to meld with other spices and aromatics.
- Often combined with acids (e.g., [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/lime]]) at the end of a dish to balance richness.
