# Chili Oil

Chili Oil is a flavored oil infused with dried chilies that adds heat and depth to dishes. It is recommended as a finishing addition for spicy meals such as chili, curry, or stir‑fry, providing a layer of heat that is less harsh than fresh chilies and rounding out flavors with a touch of oil.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-03_2024-09-05]]
- Used as a **finishing drizzle** to give spicy dishes more complexity.
- Complements other finishing ingredients like acid (lime, lemon, rice vinegar), herbs, and a pinch of honey.
- Works well alongside other pantry staples such as [[entities/salt]], [[entities/garlic]], [[entities/onion]], [[entities/soy-sauce]], and [[entities/miso]].
