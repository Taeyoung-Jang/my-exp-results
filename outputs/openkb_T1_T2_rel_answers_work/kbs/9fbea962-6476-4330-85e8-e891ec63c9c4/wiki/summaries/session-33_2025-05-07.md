## Summary of session-33_2025-05-07

**Document Type:** Conversation – meal‑planning guidance for a strict [[entities/low-fodmap-elimination-diet]].

**Main Contribution**
- Provides a concrete **7‑day dinner template** that reuses three base foods (plain rice, baked chicken, low‑FODMAP broth) while varying texture, herbs, spices, and safe add‑ins.
- Lists **exact portion amounts** for each meal (e.g., “½ cup cooked carrots,” “3–4 oz chicken”).
- Supplies a **safe‑rotation list** of low‑FODMAP herbs, spices, and vegetables suitable for the elimination phase.
- Details **three flavor‑kit recipes** (lemon‑herb, ginger‑sesame, golden) with precise measurements, storage guidelines, and hidden onion/garlic warnings.
- Includes a brief **check‑list format** for easy daily use and printable potential.

**Key Concepts**
- [[concepts/low-fodmap-meal-planning]] – structuring meals around limited safe foods.  
- Portion control – using specific amounts to stay within elimination limits.  
- [[concepts/flavor-kit-prep]] – pre‑made condiment jars to add variety without extra prep.  
- [[concepts/hidden-onion-garlic-sources]] – common pantry items that may unintentionally break the diet.  
- Checklist format – simple tick‑box layout for weekly adherence.

**Practical Takeaways**
1. **Weekly Checklist** – each day lists rice, chicken, broth, and a safe veg/herb/spice combo with exact servings.  
2. **Safe Herb & Veg List** – thyme, oregano, rosemary, basil, parsley, chives, scallion greens, turmeric, black pepper, ginger; carrots, zucchini, spinach, cucumber, green beans (standard portion sizes).  
3. **Flavor Kits** –  
   - *Lemon‑herb*: 2 Tbsp [[entities/lemon]] juice, 1 Tbsp garlic‑infused oil, ½ tsp dried thyme or oregano. Keeps 4–5 days in the fridge.  
   - *Ginger‑sesame*: 1 Tbsp grated fresh ginger, 1 Tbsp [[entities/sesame-oil]], 1 Tbsp plain tamari. Keeps 3–4 days.  
   - *Golden*: 2 Tbsp garlic‑infused oil, ½ tsp turmeric, a pinch of [[entities/black-pepper]]. Keeps 5–7 days.  
4. **Hidden Risks** – store‑bought broths, seasoning blends, and sauces may contain onion or garlic powder or extracts; verify labels.
