# Summary of Session 3 (2024-09-05)

## Core seasoning workflow
- **Early**: [[entities/salt]] lightly, add aromatics ([[entities/garlic]], [[entities/onion]]) and base spices to bloom.  
- **Mid**: introduce warm spices, dried chilies, chili powders, [[entities/paprika]], [[entities/cumin]].  
- **Late**: finish with acid ([[entities/lemon]], [[entities/lime]], [[entities/rice-vinegar]]), fresh herbs, a touch of fat, and fresh chilies.

## Recommended pantry staples
- [[entities/salt]], fresh [[entities/black-pepper]]  
- [[entities/garlic]], [[entities/onion]]  
- Acid sources: [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/lime]], [[entities/rice-vinegar]], various vinegars  
- Warm spices: [[entities/paprika]], [[entities/cumin]], curry powder  
- Umami boosters: [[entities/soy-sauce]], [[entities/miso]]  
- Chili varieties: dried whole chilies, chili flakes, chili powders, fresh chiles  

## Acid usage by cuisine
- **Mexican**: [[entities/lime]]  
- **Indian**: [[entities/lemon]] or vinegar  
- **Stir‑fry**: [[entities/rice-vinegar]]  
- **Rule**: add a few drops, taste, adjust to avoid sourness. [[concepts/acidity-timing]]

## Toasting spices
- Whole spices: bloom in oil at the start. [[concepts/spice-toasting]]  
- Ground spices: add after aromatics, before burning, let darken slightly.

## Pepper/heat layering plan
- **Early (simmered dishes)**: dried chilies, chili powders, curry powders.  
- **Mid**: chili flakes or spicier paste.  
- **Finish**: fresh chiles for bright heat.  
- **Stir‑fry**: same pattern compressed in time.

## Finishing enhancements for spicy dishes
- Acid splash, a pinch of [[entities/honey]], fresh herbs (cilantro, basil, scallions), flavored oil ([[entities/chili-oil]], [[entities/sesame-oil]], olive oil).

## Reusable three‑wave plan
- Deep heat early, warm heat mid, bright heat late, with finishing acid/herb/oil to round out. [[concepts/seasoning-layering]]
