# Samsung (organization)

Samsung is a global electronics conglomerate that designs, manufactures, and sells a wide range of consumer technology products, including smartphones, smartwatches, televisions, and home appliances.

## Key Points from session‑15_2025‑06‑15
- Mentioned as a source of older smartwatches that can work across different phone ecosystems.
- These older Samsung watches provide a reasonable level of cross‑platform compatibility, though not as seamless as ecosystem‑specific watches.
- Samsung's ecosystem emphasizes tight integration between its phones and wearables, making cross‑brand mixing more challenging for features like notifications, health data sync, and payments.
