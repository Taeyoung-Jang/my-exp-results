# iCloud Photos

**Type:** product

**Overview**

iCloud Photos is Apple’s native cloud storage solution for photos and videos, tightly integrated with the iOS/macOS Photos app. It automatically backs up media taken on devices like the [[entities/iphone]] and syncs them across all Apple devices linked to the same Apple ID.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-38_2025-10-15]]**

- When using a Google‑centric workflow, iCloud Photos does **not** integrate into the native Photos app on iPhone, leading to a separate local camera‑roll library that must later be uploaded to Google Photos.
- iCloud Photos handles features such as Live Photos and full‑resolution originals more seamlessly on iPhone compared to third‑party services.
- The document recommends disabling iCloud syncing for contacts and calendars to avoid duplicate data, but notes that iCloud Photos can remain active if the user prefers Apple’s integration.

**Comparisons**

- Compared to [[entities/google_photos]], iCloud Photos offers tighter OS integration but is limited to the Apple ecosystem.
- Users may choose between iCloud Photos and Google Photos based on their preferred [[concepts/ecosystem]] and the need for cross‑platform sync.
