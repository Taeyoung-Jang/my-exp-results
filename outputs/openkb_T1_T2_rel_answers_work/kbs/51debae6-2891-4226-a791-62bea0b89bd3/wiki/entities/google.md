# Google (organization)

Google is a multinational technology company best known for its search engine, but it also provides a suite of cloud-based productivity and media services that serve as a **source of truth** for many users.

## Role in session-38_2025-10-15
- Recommended as the central ecosystem for syncing **contacts**, **calendars**, **notes**, and **photos** across an iPhone, Windows laptop, and other devices.
- Acts as the hub in a [[concepts/cross_platform_sync]] strategy, allowing seamless data flow without manual maintenance.
- Used to avoid duplicate data by disabling competing iCloud sync features, a practice aligned with [[concepts/duplicate_avoidance]].
- Google Photos was highlighted as an alternative to iCloud Photos, noting its lack of native iOS integration but strong cross‑platform accessibility.

## Key Services Mentioned
- **Google Contacts** – stores contact information; set as default on iPhone to prevent iCloud duplication.
- **Google Calendar** – central calendar used on iPhone and Windows via browser or desktop apps.
- **Google Photos** – cloud photo backup used on iPhone; does not integrate with the native Photos app like [[entities/icloud_photos]].

## Interaction with Other Entities
- Works alongside [[entities/iphone]] and [[entities/windows]] to provide a unified experience.
- Compared to [[entities/icloud]] and [[entities/icloud_photos]] regarding integration depth on Apple devices.
