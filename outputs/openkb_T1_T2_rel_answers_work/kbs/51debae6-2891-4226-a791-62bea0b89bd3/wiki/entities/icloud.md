## iCloud

iCloud is Apple’s cloud‑based ecosystem that provides seamless syncing of **contacts**, **calendars**, **notes**, **photos**, and other data across Apple devices such as iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-38_2025-10-15]]
- Presented as one of the two main **source of truth** options (the other being Google).
- Offers friction‑free integration for Apple users, automatically keeping calendars, notes, contacts, and photos in sync.
- When paired with a Windows laptop, iCloud can lead to duplicate data if its contacts and calendars remain enabled alongside Google; disabling those iCloud services avoids duplication – an example of [[concepts/duplicate_avoidance]].
- iCloud Photos integrates directly into the iPhone’s native Photos app, handling Live Photos and full‑resolution originals more seamlessly than Google Photos, illustrating the importance of a consistent [[concepts/ecosystem]].
- Migration from iCloud to Google (or another cross‑platform system) involves exporting vCard contacts and calendar files, then turning off iCloud sync for those categories to prevent re‑adding data.
