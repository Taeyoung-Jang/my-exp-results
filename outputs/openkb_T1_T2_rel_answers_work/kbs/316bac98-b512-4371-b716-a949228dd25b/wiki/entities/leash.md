# SUP Leash

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A lightweight safety strap that attaches a stand‑up paddleboard (SUP) to the rider’s ankle, preventing the board from drifting away if the rider falls.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-03_2024-07-11]]**
- Recommended as essential beginner gear for SUP to ensure safety on calm lakes or bays.
- Simple to use: clip the leash to the board’s attachment point and the rider’s ankle or calf.
- Helps maintain focus on the adventure without worrying about losing the board.
