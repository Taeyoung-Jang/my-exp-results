# Personal Flotation Device (PFD)

**Type:** Product

## Overview
A Personal Flotation Device (PFD) is a safety garment designed to keep a wearer afloat in water. It is a core piece of equipment for many water‑based activities, including stand‑up paddleboarding (SUP).

## Role in the SUP starter plan
In the conversation summarised in [[summaries/session-03_2024-07-11]], the PFD is listed as part of the **minimum gear checklist** for beginners. It provides essential buoyancy and complies with basic safety recommendations for paddle sports.

## Gear checklist (from the document)
- Inflatable SUP board [[entities/inflatable-board]]
- Adjustable paddle [[entities/paddle]]
- Leash [[entities/leash]]
- **PFD** (this item)
- Optional light wetsuit for cold water
