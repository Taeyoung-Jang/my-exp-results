# Inflatable Stand‑up Paddleboard

An inflatable board designed for **stand‑up paddleboarding (SUP)** that offers easy transport, quick setup, and a low‑impact workout.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-03_2024-07-11]]
- Recommended as the primary piece of gear for a beginner-friendly secondary hobby that complements cycling.
- Lightweight and deflatable, making it ideal for spontaneous outings and fitting into a **[[concepts/weekly-multi-sport-rhythm]]**.
- Pairs with a **[[entities/paddle]]**, **[[entities/leash]]**, and a **[[entities/pfd]]** for a complete, safe SUP kit.
- Supports an **[[concepts/adventure-mindset]]** by allowing exploration of calm lakes, sheltered bays, and scenic shorelines.
- Serves as a **[[concepts/complementary-outdoor-activity]]** that shifts effort to the core and upper body, reducing leg strain from cycling.

## Typical Gear Set
- **Inflatable board** – the core product itself (**[[entities/inflatable-board]]**).
- **Paddle** – adjustable length for efficient propulsion (**[[entities/paddle]]**).
- **Leash** – keeps the board attached to the rider for safety (**[[entities/leash]]**).
- **Personal flotation device (PFD)** – essential safety equipment (**[[entities/pfd]]**).
- Optional: light wetsuit for cooler water conditions.

## Usage Tips
1. Choose a calm water spot (lake, sheltered bay) to start, enhancing the **adventure mindset** without high risk.
2. Keep sessions short (30‑40 minutes) to fit into a weekly rhythm that balances cycling and SUP.
3. Pair paddling with a favorite podcast or playlist to boost motivation.
