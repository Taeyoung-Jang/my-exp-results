## Summary of session-38_2025-03-11

**Topic:** Beginner marathon training – balancing structure, recovery, and sustainable volume.

**Key ideas**
- Use a **loose structure** with anchor days (easy run, longer run, optional pace work) rather than a rigid plan. See [[concepts/anchor-days]].
- Aim for **3‑5 running days** a week; the example focuses on a **4‑day schedule** (Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun).
- Keep weekday runs **45‑60 min** (≈5 mi) and the long run around **one‑third of weekly mileage**.
- Follow a **3‑week build + 1‑week cut‑back** cycle, gradually increasing the long run (9 → 10 → 11 → 12 mi) and then dropping back (≈8 mi). See [[concepts/cutback-week]].
- **Mileage targets:** ~25 mi (Week 1), ~27 mi (Week 2), ~29 mi (Week 3), ~23 mi (Cut‑back Week 4). This reflects a steady weekly mileage progression.
- **Quality work:**  
  - **Strides:** 6‑8 × ~20 s, walk/jog recovery, added at the end of a Tuesday run every other week. See [[concepts/quality-sessions]].  
  - **Short tempo:** 10 min comfortably hard within a Thursday run after a 10‑15 min warm‑up, keeping total run time 45‑60 min.
- **Red‑flag signs** to reduce load: persistent heaviness, worsening sleep, stride‑altering soreness, dread, unusually high effort or heart‑rate at easy pace. These are plain‑text indicators.

**Practical schedule**
| Week | Tue | Thu | Sat (Long) | Sun |
|------|-----|-----|------------|-----|
| 1 (~25 mi) | 5 mi | 5 mi | 10 mi | 2‑3 mi |
| 2 (~27 mi) | 5 mi | 5 mi | 11 mi | 2‑3 mi |
| 3 (~29 mi) | 5 mi | 5 mi | 12 mi | 2‑3 mi |
| 4 Cutback (~23 mi) | 4‑4.5 mi | 4‑4.5 mi | 8 mi | 2 mi |

**Takeaway:** A flexible, anchor‑day‑based plan with incremental mileage, periodic cut‑backs, and modest quality sessions supports endurance growth while preserving life balance and reducing burnout risk.
