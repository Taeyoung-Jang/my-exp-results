# Text Message Scripts

**Definition**: A collection of short, pre‑written messages that help people communicate their scheduling preferences, acceptances, or declines in a clear and polite way.

## Core Purpose
The scripts support the ideas in [[summaries/session-32_2024-06-15]] by providing concrete language for:
- Expressing a **notice‑preference** for short‑notice plans (see [[concepts/notice-preference]]).
- Maintaining **plan‑commitment** while avoiding over‑commitment (see [[concepts/plan-commitment]]).
- Delivering **polite declines** without sounding flaky (see [[concepts/polite-decline-scripts]]).
- Balancing **flexibility vs. overcommitment** (see [[concepts/flexibility-vs-overcommitment]]).

## Script Categories
| Situation | Example Script |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Soft maybe** – you like the idea but need a later check‑in. | "Sounds fun, but I can’t commit that far out yet. Can we touch base a few days beforehand and see where things are at?" |
| **Clear no** – a polite decline. | "I’m keeping my schedule pretty light right now, so I can’t lock that in. Thanks for thinking of me though—hope it’s a great time." |
| **Yes with check‑in** – you’re in but want a reminder close to the date. | "I’m in for now! My schedule tends to shift, so can we do a quick check‑in the week of just to make sure everything still works?" |
| **Tentative yes (energy‑dependent)** – you want to go but need to monitor your wellbeing. | "I’d really like to come, and I’m planning on it. I just want to be honest that it depends a bit on my energy that week. Let’s plan for yes, and I’ll let you know ahead of time if anything shifts." |

## How to Use
1. **Identify the context** – decide whether you need a soft maybe, a firm decline, a conditional acceptance, or a tentative yes.
2. **Copy‑paste** the appropriate script into your messaging app.
3. **Personalize** any details (e.g., specific dates, names) to keep the tone authentic.
4. **Follow up** according to the script’s suggestion (e.g., schedule a check‑in, send a final confirmation).

## Benefits
- Reduces the cognitive load of crafting responses on the fly.
- Maintains consistent communication style, reinforcing your **notice‑preference**.
- Helps preserve relationships by being respectful and transparent.
- Supports a healthy **social calendar baseline** by preventing accidental over‑booking.
