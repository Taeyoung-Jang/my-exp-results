# Social Calendar Baseline

**Definition**: A personal assessment of the amount of social time you can comfortably engage in each week without feeling drained.

## Why It Matters
- Provides a clear reference point for deciding which invitations to accept or decline.
- Helps protect scheduled downtime and prevents over‑booking.
- Supports healthier relationships by aligning social commitments with real energy levels.

## How to Establish Your Baseline (from [[summaries/session-13_2023-06-15]])
1. **Track Current Activity** – For one week, note each social interaction and how you felt afterward.
2. **Identify Patterns** – Look for moments when you felt energized versus exhausted.
3. **Set a Weekly Limit** – Choose a total number of hours or number of events that feels sustainable.
4. **Integrate Flex Space** – Reserve regular “downtime appointments” in your calendar to honor your limit.

## Applying the Baseline
- Before responding to an invite, **pause** and check the calendar against your baseline.
- Use the baseline to communicate your **notice‑preference** (e.g., needing at least two days' notice) in a friendly way.
- Combine with [[concepts/polite-decline-scripts]] to craft respectful refusals that reference your capacity.
