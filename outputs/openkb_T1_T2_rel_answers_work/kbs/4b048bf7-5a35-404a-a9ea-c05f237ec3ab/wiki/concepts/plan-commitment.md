# Plan Commitment

**Definition**: The set of psychological, social, and practical factors that increase the likelihood a planned activity will actually occur.

## Core Components (from [[summaries/session-32_2024-06-15]])
1. **Genuine Interest** – Participants need to want the activity; desire predicts follow‑through.
2. **Schedule Fit** – The plan must slot naturally into existing commitments without creating stress.
3. **Clear Communication** – Explicit, honest sharing of availability and preferences reduces ambiguity.
4. **Shared Commitment** – When all parties express personal buy‑in, accountability rises.

## Practical Takeaways
- **Apply the "Tonight‑test"**: If you wouldn’t want to do the activity tonight, you likely won’t want it weeks later.
- **Reserve “no‑plan” days** to protect bandwidth and avoid over‑commitment.
- **Use clear check‑ins** for long‑term plans, treating future commitments as tentative until a nearer‑term confirmation.
