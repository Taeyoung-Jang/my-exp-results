# Layering Strategy

**Layering Strategy** is the systematic approach of pairing inner garments with a curated set of outerwear to achieve optimal comfort, aesthetic variety, and temperature control without over‑loading a wardrobe.

## Core Principles (drawn from [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-15]])

1. **Outerwear Rotation** – Build a small, versatile trio of outer pieces that overlap in temperature range:
   - A lightweight, water‑resistant **shell** (noted as a generic piece in the source).
   - A mid‑weight jacket, ideally a **[[entities/harrington-jacket]]** in navy for its clean lines and color versatility.
   - A heavier coat such as a **[[entities/wool-topcoat]]** or **[[entities/parka]]** for true cold.
   - See [[concepts/outerwear-rotation]] for a deeper dive.
2. **Layer Diversity** – Change the inner layers (tees, henleys, thin hoodies, sweaters) to give each outer piece a fresh look. This prevents visual repetition while keeping the outer wardrobe minimal.
3. **Color Versatility** – Choose neutral, adaptable colours (e.g., navy) that pair with every denim wash, neutral tee, and sneaker hue. Refer to [[concepts/color-versatility]] for guidelines.
4. **Functional Segmentation** – Assign distinct roles to each outer item:
   - **[[entities/denim-jacket]]** for rugged, textured casual days.
   - **[[entities/hoodie]]** for soft, athletic comfort.
   - **[[entities/harrington-jacket]]** for a clean, smart‑casual edge.
   - This segmentation avoids redundancy and clarifies outfit intent.
5. **Maintenance Awareness** – Consider how often each piece is worn and its care requirements; integrate with [[concepts/clothing-keepers]] to extend lifespan.

## Practical Application

- **Mild days:** Reach for the denim jacket; layer over a simple tee.
- **Cool/athletic vibe:** Choose the hoodie; add a thin hoodie or sweatshirt underneath for extra warmth.
- **In‑between or polished look:** Wear the navy Harrington over a plain tee, knit polo, or thin hoodie, adjusting the inner layer to shift the outfit’s mood.

## Benefits
- **Reduced wardrobe bulk** – Fewer outer pieces but broader coverage.
- **Stylistic flexibility** – Swapping inner layers yields many outfits.
- **Efficient temperature management** – Each piece targets a specific range while overlapping for seamless transitions.
