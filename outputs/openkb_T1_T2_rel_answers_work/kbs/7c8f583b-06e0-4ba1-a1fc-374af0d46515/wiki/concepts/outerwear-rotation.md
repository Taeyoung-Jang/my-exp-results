# Outerwear Rotation

**Definition**: A minimal outerwear system that uses three complementary pieces—a lightweight shell, a mid‑weight jacket, and a heavier coat—to cover most temperature conditions while allowing frequent style variation through interchangeable inner layers.

---

## Core Elements
1. **Lightweight water‑resistant shell** – works from spring through fall; layers over tees, hoodies, or sweaters.
2. **Mid‑weight jacket** – the everyday workhorse (e.g., a [[entities/harrington-jacket]]); pairs with jeans, sneakers, and a range of inner tops.
3. **Heavy coat** – wool topcoat or [[entities/parka]] for true cold days.

These pieces overlap in temperature range, so you can always choose the most appropriate outer layer without needing a large wardrobe.

---

## How It Relates to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/layering-strategy]]** – Outerwear Rotation is a specific application of broader layering principles, focusing on outer layers while varying the garments underneath for visual diversity.
- **[[concepts/color-versatility]]** – Selecting versatile colours (e.g., navy for the mid‑weight jacket) maximises outfit combinations and reduces redundancy.
- **[[concepts/clothing-keepers]]** – A well‑planned rotation reduces wear on individual items and extends their lifespan, aligning with keeper‑mindset practices.

---

## Practical Implementation (from [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-15]])
- **Mild days**: Use the mid‑weight [[entities/harrington-jacket]] or the [[entities/denim-jacket]] for a casual vibe.
- **Cool/athletic days**: Choose a hoodie as the primary outer layer.
- **In‑between or polished looks**: Deploy the Harrington over a tee, henley, or thin hoodie.
- **Cold weather**: Switch to a wool topcoat or parka, layering over the same inner pieces.

By swapping the inner layers (plain tee, long‑sleeve shirt, knit polo, thin hoodie, sweater), the same three outer pieces generate many distinct outfits, preventing the feeling of repetition.

---

## Benefits
- **Simplicity**: Fewer items to purchase, store, and maintain.
- **Flexibility**: Overlapping temperature coverage enables easy daily choices.
- **Style Variety**: Changing inner layers provides visual freshness without additional outerwear.
- **Longevity**: Rotating three key pieces reduces wear frequency on any single item.

---

## Tips for Building Your Own Rotation
1. Pick a neutral, versatile colour for the mid‑weight jacket (navy, charcoal, or olive).
2. Ensure the shell is water‑resistant and lightweight for easy layering.
3. Choose a heavy coat that matches the aesthetic of the other pieces (e.g., wool topcoat for a dressier look, parka for sportier utility).
4. Keep a small selection of diverse inner tops to mix and match.
