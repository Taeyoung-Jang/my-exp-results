# Repair Decision Criteria

**Definition**: A set of factors to evaluate whether an item should be repaired or replaced, balancing functional condition, emotional value, cost, and impact on the item’s character.

---

## Core Criteria

1. **Emotional Attachment**
   - Does the item hold personal memories or a sense of identity?  Items that act as *"keepers"* often merit repair even when functionally worn. [[concepts/clothing-keepers]]
2. **Functional Wear**
   - Assess the specific failure:
     - **Zipper issues** – sticky teeth or a faulty slider are usually low‑cost fixes. [[entities/zipper-slider]]
     - **Lining condition** – patches may suffice if the outer shell is sound; full replacement is justified when the lining is shredded or hanging.
3. **Material Integrity**
   - Check for deep cracks, structural weakening, or irreversible leather damage. When the **[[entities/leather-jacket]]** shell is compromised, repair may not restore the original feel.
4. **Fit Preservation**
   - Any work should maintain the original shape. Ask the shop to use existing stitch holes and avoid altering arm‑hole or hem dimensions.
5. **Cost vs. Replacement Value**
   - Compare repair quotes to the price of a comparable new item. Typical U.S. ballparks from the source:
     - Lining patch: $50‑$150
     - Full lining replacement: $200‑$400
     - Slider swap: $25‑$75
     - Full zipper replacement: $100‑$200
   - Factor in long‑term durability of the repair.
6. **Turnaround Time**
   - Small fixes (patches, slider swaps) often finish within days; full replacements may take 1‑3 weeks.
7. **Future Maintenance**
   - Choose materials that extend life. Synthetic satin or poly blends are popular lining choices for smoothness and durability. [[entities/synthetic-satin]]

## Application Example

In **[[summaries/session-07_2023-10-15]]**, the user considered a brown leather jacket with a sticky zipper and worn lining. Applying the criteria:
- The jacket holds strong emotional value → bias toward repair.
- Zipper slider is the primary issue → inexpensive slider swap.
- Lining shows localized wear → patching preferred unless multiple tears appear.
- Leather surface remains intact → full leather replacement not warranted.
- Estimated costs and timelines align with the user’s budget and patience.

---

## Questions to Ask a Repair Shop

- Will you use existing stitch holes to avoid new needle damage?
- How will you protect edge paint and keep the original fit?
- Can I see samples of previous lining or zipper work?
- Will you provide a written quote and estimated completion date?
