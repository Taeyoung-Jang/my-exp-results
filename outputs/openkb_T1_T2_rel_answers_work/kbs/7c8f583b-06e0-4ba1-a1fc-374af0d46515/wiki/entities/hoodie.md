# Hoodie

**Type:** product

**Description:** A soft, casual sweatshirt with a hood, often made from cotton‑blend fleece, used as a versatile layering piece.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-15]]:**
- Serves as one of the three core outerwear options in the suggested [[concepts/outerwear-rotation]].
- Fits the [[concepts/layering-strategy]] by providing warmth while remaining easy to layer under a Harrington or denim jacket.
- Offers a casual aesthetic that contrasts with the cleaner lines of a [[entities/harrington-jacket]] or the rugged texture of a [[entities/denim-jacket]].
- Color choices are flexible; the discussion highlighted pairing with a navy Harrington, illustrating [[concepts/color-versatility]].
