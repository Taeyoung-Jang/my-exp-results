# Zipper Slider

The **zipper slider** is the movable part of a zipper that engages and releases the teeth, allowing the zipper to open and close. In the context of leather jackets, the slider often becomes the point of friction or sticking, especially when the surrounding leather or zipper teeth are clean but the slider itself wears out or misaligns.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-07_2023-10-15]]
- **Common issue:** The slider may “stick” even when the teeth appear fine, requiring a simple replacement rather than a full zipper swap.
- **Home remedy:** Rubbing a plain graphite pencil along the teeth can improve glide, but the underlying problem is usually the slider’s surface condition.
- **Repair advice:** When describing the problem to a leather shop, phrase it as a "sticking slider" to prompt a targeted swap.
- **Cost & time:** Slider replacement typically costs **$25–$75** and can be completed **same‑day to 2 days**.
