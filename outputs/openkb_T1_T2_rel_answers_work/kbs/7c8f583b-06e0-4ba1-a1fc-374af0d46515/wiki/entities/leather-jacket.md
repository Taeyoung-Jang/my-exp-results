# Leather Jacket

A **leather jacket** is a durable outerwear garment made from animal hide, often valued for its style, warmth, and ability to develop a personalized patina over time. In the conversation from [[summaries/session-07_2023-10-15]] the jacket serves as a concrete example of a **"keeper"** item that users may choose to maintain rather than discard.

## Key facts from the session
- **Item details**: brown leather, zippered pockets, aging lining, occasional sticky zipper.
- **Emotional value**: the jacket is considered a *keeper* because of memories and the way it feels like "mine" – a concept explored in [[concepts/clothing-keepers]].
- **Repair priorities**:
  1. Sticky zipper – easy home fix with a graphite pencil; if unresolved, describe the issue as a **sticking slider** (see [[entities/zipper-slider]]).
  2. Lining – patching for minor wear; full replacement when the lining is shredded or loose. Material options include synthetic satin (see [[entities/synthetic-satin]]), cotton, or heavier twill, each affecting feel and durability.
- **Cost & timing estimates** (U.S. ballparks):
  - Lining patch: $50‑$150, 3‑7 days.
  - Full lining replacement: $200‑$400, 1‑3 weeks.
  - Slider swap: $25‑$75, same‑day to 2 days.
  - Full zipper replacement: $100‑$200, 1‑2 weeks.
- **Shop evaluation checklist**: ask about using existing stitch holes, preserving arm‑hole/hem shape, protecting edge paint, seeing prior work samples, and obtaining a written quote.
