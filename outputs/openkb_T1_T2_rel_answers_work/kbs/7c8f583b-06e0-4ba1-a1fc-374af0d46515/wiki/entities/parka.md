## Parka
A parka is a heavyweight, insulated outerwear piece designed to protect against cold and inclement weather. In the **session‑40_2024‑10‑15** discussion, the parka is presented as one of the three core pieces in an outerwear rotation, serving as the *heavy coat* option for truly cold days.

- **Function**: Provides maximal warmth, usually featuring a down or synthetic fill, a wind‑proof shell, and a high collar or hood.
- **Style role**: Complements lighter items like a lightweight shell, a Harrington jacket, or a denim jacket, giving a *smart‑casual* or *sporty* edge when needed.
- **Placement in rotation**: Paired with the [[concepts/outerwear-rotation]] framework, the parka fills the “heavy coat” slot, while the lightweight shell and mid‑weight jacket cover milder conditions.
- **Related items**: Often compared with a [[entities/wool-topcoat]] for a more formal heavy coat alternative; both serve the same temperature niche but differ in aesthetic.
- **Key recommendation**: Use the parka on days that are colder than the range covered by the Harrington or denim jacket, and layer a hoodie or sweater underneath for added comfort.
