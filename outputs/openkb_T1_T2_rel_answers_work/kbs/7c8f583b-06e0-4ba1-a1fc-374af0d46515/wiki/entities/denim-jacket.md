# Denim Jacket

## Overview
The denim jacket is a classic, rugged outerwear item made from denim fabric. It is lightweight enough for mild temperatures and offers a casual, textured look that contrasts with smoother jackets.

## Role in an [[concepts/outerwear-rotation]]
In the three‑piece rotation recommended in [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-15]], the denim jacket serves as the **mild‑day layer**. It fills the gap between a heavier coat and lighter layering pieces, providing a distinct aesthetic while still being functional for everyday wear.

## Interaction with Other Outerwear
- Pairs well with a [[entities/hoodie]] for added warmth without bulk.
- Complements a [[entities/harrington-jacket]] by offering a more rugged alternative for casual looks.
- Distinct from a [[entities/bomber-jacket]] or [[entities/leather-jacket]], which have sportier or harsher silhouettes.

## Styling Tips from Session 40
- Wear over a plain tee or a light sweater for a clean casual outfit.
- Combine with mid‑wash or dark denim jeans and clean sneakers for a cohesive denim‑on‑denim look.
- Use the denim jacket as a textural contrast when the Harrington jacket is chosen for a smarter vibe.
