# Harrington Jacket

**Type:** product

## Overview
The Harrington jacket is a mid‑weight, zip‑front casual jacket with a stand‑up collar and often a tartan lining. In the context of a minimal wardrobe, it is highlighted as the most versatile mid‑weight piece for everyday casual styling.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-15]]
- **Style choice:** Preferred over bomber and chore jackets for a clean, slightly grown‑up look that pairs well with jeans and sneakers.
- **Color recommendation:** Navy is singled out as the most flexible colour, working with all denim washes, neutral tees, and most sneaker hues.  
- **Role in outerwear rotation:** Serves as the “smart‑casual” layer that sits between a denim jacket and a hoodie, filling a distinct aesthetic niche.
- **Outfit examples:** 
  - White or grey tee + mid‑wash jeans + clean sneakers + Harrington jacket.
  - Light sweatshirt or thin hoodie under the jacket, allowing the hoodie to peek.
  - Knit polo or long‑sleeve tee with darker denim for a slightly dressier vibe.
- **Layering strategy:** Used on milder or in‑between days, or whenever a more polished look is desired without adding bulk.
