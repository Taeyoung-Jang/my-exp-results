# Wool Topcoat

**Type:** product

**Description:** A classic, warm outer garment made from wool, favored for its durability, structure, and ability to elevate casual outfits.

## Role in [[concepts/outerwear-rotation]]
- Recommended as the **heavy coat** in a three‑piece rotation (light shell, mid‑weight jacket, heavy coat).
- Provides coverage for true cold days while maintaining a sleek silhouette.
- Can be dressed down with jeans, sneakers, and a hoodie, or dressed up with smarter pieces for a polished look.

## Styling & [[concepts/layering-strategy]]
- Pair with a **denim jacket** or **hoodie** underneath on milder days for added warmth without bulk.
- Works well alongside a **Harrington jacket** as the mid‑weight layer in the same wardrobe.
- Neutral colours (e.g., charcoal, navy, camel) maximize [[concepts/color-versatility]] and mix easily with a casual jeans‑and‑sneakers wardrobe.
