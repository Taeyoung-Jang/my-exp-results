# Summary of session‑07_2023-10-15

This conversation explores why certain garments become **“keepers”** and offers practical advice for evaluating, repairing, and budgeting leather‑jacket maintenance.

## Key concepts
- **Clothing keepers** – items that persist because of functional fit, emotional memory, or unique wear patterns. [[concepts/clothing-keepers]]
- **Repair vs. replace decision criteria** – assess wear, fit, material integrity, and personal attachment before choosing a fix. [[concepts/repair-decision-criteria]]
- **Leather jacket maintenance** – prioritize repairs that restore wearability (zippers, lining) while preserving the jacket’s character. [[concepts/leather-jacket-maintenance]]
- **Sticky zipper fix** – a graphite pencil on the teeth is a safe home remedy; avoid oils. *(no wikilink)*
- **Slider vs. teeth issues** – describe the problem as a “sticking slider” to the shop for a targeted swap. [[entities/zipper-slider]]
- **Lining material choices** – synthetic satin/poly blends for smoothness and durability; cotton for breathability; heavier twill for rugged use. [[entities/synthetic-satin]]
- **Cost and turnaround benchmarks** – typical U.S. price ranges and time frames for four common jobs:
  1. **Lining patch**: $50‑$150, 3‑7 days.
  2. **Full lining replacement**: $200‑$400, 1‑3 weeks.
  3. **Zipper‑slider swap**: $25‑$75, same‑day to 2 days.
  4. **Full zipper replacement**: $100‑$200, 1‑2 weeks.
- **Shop evaluation criteria** – ask about:
  - Use of existing stitch holes (no new needle damage).
  - Preservation of arm‑hole and hem shape.
  - Protection of edge paint/glazing.
  - Samples of past lining or zipper work.
  - Written quote and clear timeline.
  Red flags include vague estimates, refusal to show examples, or lack of written documentation.

## Practical takeaways
1. **Start with the easiest functional repairs** – sticky zipper first, then lining if the outer leather is sound.
2. **Select lining material based on feel and durability**; request swatches to compare. [[entities/synthetic-satin]]
3. **Use cost/turnaround guidelines** to set expectations and budget.
4. **Vet the leather shop** with the checklist above to protect the jacket’s fit and finish. [[entities/leather-jacket]]

Overall, the conversation provides a decision‑making framework and concrete repair guidance for extending the life of a cherished leather jacket.
