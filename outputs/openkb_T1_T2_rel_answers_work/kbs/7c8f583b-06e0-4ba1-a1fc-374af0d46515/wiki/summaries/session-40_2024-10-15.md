# Summary of session-40_2024-10-15

## Core outerwear framework
- Recommend a three‑piece rotation covering most temperature ranges: 
  1. **Lightweight water‑resistant shell** – spring to fall, layers over tees, hoodies, or sweaters.  
  2. **Mid‑weight jacket** – the workhorse for casual everyday looks.  
  3. **Heavy coat** – wool topcoat or parka for true cold days.  
- The key idea is that the **layers underneath** provide visual variety while the outer pieces stay simple.  
- Related concepts: outerwear rotation, layering strategy.

## Mid‑weight jacket choice for a casual minimal wardrobe
- **Preferred style:** Harrington jacket over bomber or chore jacket because it offers clean lines, a slightly grown‑up feel, and easy pairing with jeans and sneakers.  
- **Best colour:** **Navy** – works with every denim wash, neutral tees, and most sneaker colours. Black can be too stark; olive is more niche.  
- Relevant concept: color versatility.

## Role of a navy Harrington alongside existing pieces
- **Denim jacket** = rugged, textured, more casual.  
- **Hoodies** = soft, athletic.  
- **Navy Harrington** = clean, slightly sharper; fills the “smart‑casual” gap and layers well over hoodies without bulk.  
- Not redundant; each piece occupies a distinct aesthetic and functional niche.  
- Entities referenced: [[entities/denim-jacket]], [[entities/hoodie]], [[entities/harrington-jacket]].

## Sample casual outfit combos
1. White or grey tee + mid‑wash jeans + clean sneakers + navy Harrington.  
2. Light sweatshirt or thin hoodie + jeans + sneakers + Harrington (hoodie peeking).  
3. Knit polo or long‑sleeve tee + darker denim + sneakers + Harrington for a smarter vibe.

## Rotation strategy with three outer layers
- **Mild days:** denim jacket.  
- **Cool or athletic vibe days:** hoodie.  
- **In‑between or when you want a polished look:** navy Harrington.  
- Vary the inner layers (plain tee, henley, thin hoodie, sweater) to avoid repetition.
