## Summary of session-32_2024-10-15

The conversation focuses on how to stay organized when using a highly restricted corporate laptop that prohibits external storage and limits tooling. Key ideas include:

- **Separate work‑inside and personal‑outside layers** – keep all job‑related notes inside the approved internal wiki/notes tool, while high‑level learning goals live on personal devices.
- **Ask IT/manager** for the officially sanctioned note‑taking or task‑tracking application and clarification on what data may be stored there.
- **Simple Markdown templates** for:
  1. **Daily log** – date header, sections for work items, follow‑ups, and non‑sensitive observations.
  2. **Weekly plan** – weekly goals, key tasks, end‑of‑week reflections.
  3. **Learning & questions** – lists of things learned, questions to ask, and follow‑ups.
- **Safety tips** – write in your own words, avoid pasting code, configs, internal names, or any potentially sensitive material.
- **Organization strategy** – use separate weekly pages (e.g., `Weekly-2024-10-15`) and a single rolling daily‑log page, linking new weeks from the top of the daily log. Archive old weeks in an “Archive” folder for easy navigation.
