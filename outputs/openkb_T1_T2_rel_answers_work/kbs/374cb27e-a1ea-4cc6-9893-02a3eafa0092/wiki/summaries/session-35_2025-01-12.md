## Summary of session-35_2025-01-12

**Main focus:** Practical tips for configuring lighting and screen settings to minimise eye strain while reading or studying for extended periods.

### Key recommendations
- **Ambient lighting**: Use soft, even, warm‑to‑neutral lighting. Position light sources to the side of the workspace rather than directly in front of or behind the screen to avoid harsh contrast. [[concepts/ambient_lighting]]
- **Screen brightness & colour**:
  - Match screen brightness roughly to the surrounding light level. [[concepts/screen_brightness_matching]]
  - Prefer a slightly warmer colour temperature, especially in the evening, to reduce blue‑light glare. [[concepts/color_temperature]]
  - Keep the screen at arm’s length and consider increasing text size for comfort.
- **Breaks**: Take brief look‑away breaks to focus on distant objects, giving eye muscles a rest. [[concepts/visual_breaks]]

### Desk lamps & supplemental lighting
- A small, warm desk lamp that illuminates the keyboard/reading area is generally more comfortable than raising screen brightness. [[entities/desk_lamp]]
- In steady low‑light environments (e.g., library stacks), keep the lamp on only when working with paper; otherwise, let the laptop screen match ambient illumination. [[entities/library]]
- Ideal lamp brightness: just enough to raise ambient lighting so the screen isn’t the sole bright object; colour temperature in the warm‑to‑neutral range (≈3000‑4000 K). [[entities/warm_white_led_bulb]]

### Matte screen protectors
- Effective for reducing glare when strong reflections are present, but they can slightly soften image sharpness. [[entities/matte_screen_protector]]
- In low‑steady lighting with minimal reflections, a matte protector is optional; adjusting lamp placement and screen angle often suffices.

### Practical workflow for low‑light library settings
1. Set laptop brightness to match the ambient library light. [[concepts/screen_brightness_matching]]
2. Use a small reading lamp only when transitioning to paper materials. [[entities/reading_lamp]]
3. Maintain a comfortable viewing distance and take regular visual breaks. [[concepts/visual_breaks]]
