# LED (product)

LED (light‑emitting diode) lighting is a solid‑state, energy‑efficient source that can be tuned for specific **lumens** output and **color temperature**.  In the session‑03_2024‑07‑15 guidance, LEDs are recommended for indoor evening illumination because they can provide warm‑white light (≈2200–2700 K) without the stimulating blue‑white output of traditional daylight bulbs.

## Key facts from the document
- **Adjustable intensity**: LEDs allow selection of 400–800 lumens per lamp, suitable for a small apartment’s night‑time lighting.
- **Color temperature control**: Warm‑white LED bulbs (often marketed as "warm white") help signal the body’s [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]] that it is time to wind down.
- **Placement**: Used in [[entities/floor_lamp]] and [[entities/table_lamp]] fixtures to create lower‑level pools of light rather than overhead illumination.
- **Safety & visibility**: Provide enough brightness for tasks while avoiding the harsh, cool‑white daylight cue that can interfere with sleep.
