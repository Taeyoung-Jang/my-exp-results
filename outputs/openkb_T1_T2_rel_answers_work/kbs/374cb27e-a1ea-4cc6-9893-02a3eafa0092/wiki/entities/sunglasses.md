# Sunglasses

**Product type**: Eyewear designed to filter and attenuate incoming light.

## Role in light exposure management
- In the *session-03_2024-07-15* guidance, sunglasses are recommended for **late‑day outdoor use** to soften bright sunlight without compromising safety.
- Darker‑tinted lenses help lower overall **[[concepts/light_intensity]]** reaching the eyes, supporting the body’s transition toward nighttime.
- When combined with a **[[entities/brimmed_hat]]**, they further reduce direct glare while preserving sufficient visibility for outdoor activities.

## Practical recommendations from the document
1. **Three hours before bedtime** – switch to sunglasses with a stronger tint than earlier in the day.
2. **Two hours before bedtime** – continue wearing the sunglasses and seek shade when possible.
3. **One hour before bedtime** – move indoors; the reduced light exposure from the sunglasses eases the shift to indoor **[[concepts/color_temperature]]** lighting.

## Related concepts
- [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]] – how light cues influence sleep‑wake cycles.
- [[concepts/light_intensity]] – the amount of luminous flux reaching the eye.
- [[concepts/color_temperature]] – the hue of light, with warmer tones favoring evening relaxation.

## Associated entities
- [[entities/brimmed_hat]] – complementary accessory used alongside sunglasses for outdoor light attenuation.
