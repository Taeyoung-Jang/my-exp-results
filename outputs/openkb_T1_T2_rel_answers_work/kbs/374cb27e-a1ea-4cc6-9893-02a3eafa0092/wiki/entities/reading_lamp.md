## Reading Lamp

A **reading lamp** is a small, focused light source designed to illuminate books, notes, or a workstation without causing glare or excessive contrast with screen displays. 

### Key points from [[summaries/session-35_2025-01-12]]
- Recommended for use in low‑light environments such as library stacks when switching from screen work to paper reading.
- Should provide warm to neutral light (around 3000‑4000 K) to complement ambient lighting and reduce eye strain.
- Used sparingly: turn on only when needed for paper, keeping ambient illumination balanced with screen brightness.
- Works best alongside a desk lamp or other ambient lighting to maintain a comfortable overall light level.
