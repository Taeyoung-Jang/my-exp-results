# Table Lamp

**Type**: product

A table lamp is a portable lighting device placed on a surface such as a desk or nightstand. In the context of *session-03_2024-07-15*, it is recommended for evening use to provide a gentle pool of light that supports the [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]] without triggering alertness.

## Key characteristics from the session
- **Color temperature**: Warm‑white LEDs in the 2200–2700 K range are suggested, often realized with a **[[entities/warm_white_led_bulb]]**.
- **Brightness**: 400–800 lumens per lamp, enough for visibility but low enough to avoid stimulating the brain.
- **Placement**: Positioned at waist‑ or eye‑level to create a localized pool of light, reducing the overhead “daylight” cue.
- **Usage pattern**: One to three lamps are used in a small apartment, dimmed gradually as bedtime approaches.
