# Warm‑White LED Bulb

**Type:** product

## Overview
The warm‑white LED bulb is a lighting product that emits light in the 2200–3000 K color temperature range. It is designed to provide a comfortable, low‑stimulus illumination that supports the body's [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]] in the evening.

## Key Characteristics
- **Color temperature:** Warm‑white (≈2200–3000 K) – aligns with [[concepts/color_temperature]] recommendations for night‑time lighting.
- **Light intensity:** Typically 400–800 lumens per bulb, offering sufficient visibility without excessive stimulation, linking to [[concepts/light_intensity]].
- **Technology:** Uses [[entities/led]] technology, offering energy efficiency and long lifespan.

## Practical Use (as advised in [[summaries/session-03_2024-07-15]])
- **Placement:** Ideal for low‑level fixtures such as [[entities/floor_lamp]] and [[entities/table_lamp]] to create a “pool” of warm light rather than overhead daylight cues.
- **Scheduling:** Deploy these bulbs in the evening, especially the hour before bedtime, to transition the environment from brighter, cooler light to a softer, warm ambiance.
- **Dimming:** Can be paired with dimmer switches to gradually reduce brightness as sleep approaches.
