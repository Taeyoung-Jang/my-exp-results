# Desk Lamp

**Type:** Product

**Overview**
A desk lamp is a compact lighting device placed on a work surface to provide localized, adjustable illumination. It is commonly used to create side lighting that complements screen displays and reduces visual contrast.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-35_2025-01-12]]**
- Supplies warm to neutral light (≈3000‑4000 K), aligning with [[concepts/color_temperature]] for comfortable viewing.
- Increases overall [[concepts/light_intensity]] in the immediate work area so the laptop screen can be set to match ambient light, supporting [[concepts/screen_brightness_matching]].
- Recommended brightness is just enough to lift ambient lighting without becoming the dominant light source; this helps avoid glare and keeps the screen from standing out as a bright spotlight.
- Works best when paired with regular [[concepts/visual_breaks]] during long reading or study sessions.
- In steady, low‑light environments such as a library, the lamp is typically switched on only when reading paper; otherwise the screen’s brightness alone suffices.
- Often equipped with a warm LED bulb like [[entities/warm_white_led_bulb]] or other [[entities/led]] technology.
