# Brimmed Hat

## Overview
A **brimmed hat** is a headwear product featuring a wide, projecting brim that shades the eyes and reduces direct sunlight intensity. It is commonly used to manage light exposure without sacrificing visibility or safety.

## Role in Light Management
In the *session-03_2024-07-15* guidance, the brimmed hat is recommended as part of a late‑day light‑tapering routine:
- Worn about three hours before bedtime to **soften light intensity** while still being outdoors.
- Combined with darker‑tinted [[entities/sunglasses]] to further reduce the **blue‑light** stimulus that can delay [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]] onset.
- Helps transition the visual environment toward warmer hues, supporting the shift in [[concepts/color_temperature]] that signals evening.

## Usage Guidelines
1. **Timing** – Put on the hat during the last 3 hours before sleep, especially when the sun is still bright.
2. **Complementary Gear** – Pair with sunglasses for added shading; the hat alone provides coarse shading, while sunglasses handle finer light reduction.
3. **Safety** – The brim allows adequate peripheral vision for outdoor activities, preventing the feeling of “living in a cave.”
4. **Material** – Light, breathable fabrics are preferable for comfort during prolonged outdoor exposure.
