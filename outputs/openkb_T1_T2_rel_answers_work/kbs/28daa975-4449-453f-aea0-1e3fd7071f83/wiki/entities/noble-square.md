# Noble Square

**Type:** Place (neighborhood in Chicago)

Noble Square is a small, primarily residential enclave located within Chicago’s West Town district. It is bounded roughly by Ashland Avenue to the east, Noble Street to the north, and the surrounding side streets that spill into Ukrainian Village. The area is known for its tree‑lined blocks, low‑traffic streets, and a mix of historic bungalows and newer mid‑rise apartments.

## Key Characteristics (from [[summaries/session-08_2024-10-13]])
- **Quiet atmosphere:** Frequently highlighted as part of the [[concepts/quiet-rental-neighborhoods]] due to its calm side streets and distance from major nightlife corridors.
- **Parking:** Generally more workable than denser downtown neighborhoods, though street parking can be inconsistent on the commercial stretch of Chicago Avenue. Residents often rely on a mix of street permits and off‑street spaces.
- **Commute:** Offers a short, predictable drive to 500 W Madison St (approximately 10‑15 minutes in normal rush‑hour conditions). The area benefits from proximity to major expressways (Kennedy Expressway) without being directly on them, supporting the [[concepts/commute-time-estimation]] guidance.
- **Nearby amenities:** Close to the cultural and dining options of West Town and Ukrainian Village, while maintaining a quieter residential feel.
