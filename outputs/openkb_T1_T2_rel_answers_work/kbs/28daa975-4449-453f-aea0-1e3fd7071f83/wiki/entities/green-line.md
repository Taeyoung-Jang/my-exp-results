## Green Line (CTA)

The **Green Line** is a rapid‑transit rail line operated by the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA). It runs from **Harlem/Lake** on the north side, through the Loop, and out to **Cottage Grove** on the south‑west side, serving many neighborhoods including **River West**, **West Loop**, **Ukrainian Village**, **West Town**, and the suburb **Oak Park** via the **Green Line** stations.
