## Kennedy Expressway (I-90/94)

The Kennedy Expressway is a key interstate highway running northwest from downtown **Chicago** to the western suburbs. It carries the I‑90 and I‑94 designations and serves as a primary commuter corridor for residents of neighborhoods and towns such as [[entities/oak-park]], [[entities/forest-park]], and parts of [[entities/river-west]].
