# Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)

The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is a government agency responsible for issuing driver licenses, vehicle registrations, and enforcing motor vehicle regulations, including temporary suspensions of driving privileges.

## Role in [[summaries/session-36_2025-02-13]]
In the conversation documented in the session‑36 summary, the user’s license was temporarily suspended by the DMV after a neurologist prescribed a new seizure medication. This suspension created a need for alternative mobility solutions, prompting the user to plan backup transit options, rideshare usage, and low‑pressure communication with friends for rides.
