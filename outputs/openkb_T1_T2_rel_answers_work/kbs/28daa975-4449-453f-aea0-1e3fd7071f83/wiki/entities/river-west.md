# River West (place)

River West is a neighborhood on the west side of downtown Chicago, bounded roughly by the Chicago River to the east, the Kennedy Expressway to the west, and the neighborhoods of West Loop and Fulton River District to the south and north.  It features a mix of newer mid‑rise apartments, converted lofts, and low‑rise residential buildings.

## Key characteristics from the session‑08_2024-10-13 summary
- **Quiet residential feel**: The area is highlighted as a quieter alternative to the bustling West Loop, especially in interior blocks around Hubbard, Erie, and Ogden streets.
- **Commute**: Typical rush‑hour drive times to 500 W Madison range from 10‑15 minutes, stretching to about 20 minutes on heavier traffic days.
- **Parking**: Parking is described as hit‑or‑miss but generally manageable, particularly west of Milwaukee Avenue.  Some streets may have tighter availability.
- **Noise considerations**: Proximity to the Blue Line can cause train noise; the recommendation is to stay at least two blocks away from the elevated tracks to keep noise to a mild background level.
- **Nearby amenities**: Close to the Fulton River District, West Loop, and West Town, offering restaurants, shops, and easy access to the Kennedy Expressway for drivers.
