# Rideshare Services

Rideshare Services are digital platforms that connect riders with drivers for on‑demand transportation. In **session‑36_2025‑02‑13** they are highlighted as a pivotal backup option when public transit is delayed or a personal vehicle is unavailable.

## Key points from the document
- Suggested as a **ready‑to‑use backup** alongside public transit and friends.
- Integrated into the weekly planning template as a column for “rideshare” under each day’s backup plan.
- Mentioned in the context of low‑pressure messaging to friends, offering an alternative to asking directly for a lift.
- Emphasized that having rideshare apps available helps keep the mobility system resilient.
