**Main contribution**: a practical, fill‑in‑the‑blank weekly template that helps users coordinate appointments, errands, work blocks, and travel buffers while planning backup transit or rideshare options, plus several low‑pressure text scripts for requesting rides.

### Key ideas
- **Mobility system thinking** – treat travel time as a planned block rather than an after‑thought [[concepts/mobility-planning]].
- **Two‑window daily layout** – each day is split into a morning and an afternoon/evening window, with fields for address, transit‑friendliness, and ride‑status (confirmed vs. tentative).
- **Backup plan section** – a dedicated line for noting alternative transit routes, rideshare apps, or a specific friend to ask.
- **Low‑pressure messaging** – three template messages that include pickup/drop‑off times while keeping the request friendly and optional [[concepts/low-pressure-communication]].

### Template outline (plain‑text)
```
Day: ______
Morning window: ______
Address: ______
Transit‑friendly? yes/no ______
Confirmed ride ☐   Tentative ☐
Notes (travel + buffer): ______

Afternoon/Evening window: ______
Address: ______
Transit‑friendly? yes/no ______
Confirmed ride ☐   Tentative ☐
Notes (travel + buffer): ______

Backup plan for the day (transit route / rideshare / person to ask): ______
```

### Sample low‑pressure ride‑request texts
1. “Hey, I need to get from ____ to ____ on ____. Pickup would be around ____ and drop‑off around ____. If that happens to fit your day, great—if not, no worries at all.”
2. “Quick check: I’ve got an appointment at ____ on ____. Would a pickup around ____ work for you? Totally fine if you’re busy—I just want to ask before I book something else.”
3. “I’m sorting out a ride for ____ around ____. If you happen to be free to take me to ____, that’d be amazing, but please don’t feel obligated. Just let me know either way.”
