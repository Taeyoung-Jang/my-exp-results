### Recommended “distance from tracks” rule of thumb
- **Within 1 city block** of an elevated line → noticeable train noise.  
- **1–2 blocks** → mild background sound for most buildings.  
- **≥2‑3 blocks** → noise generally negligible, assuming decent insulation.  
- **[[entities/oak-park]] (Green Line, at‑grade)** → keep at least **1½ blocks** from the line.  
- **[[entities/forest-park]] & [[entities/river-west]] (Blue Line)** → aim for **≥2 blocks** buffer for quiet.
