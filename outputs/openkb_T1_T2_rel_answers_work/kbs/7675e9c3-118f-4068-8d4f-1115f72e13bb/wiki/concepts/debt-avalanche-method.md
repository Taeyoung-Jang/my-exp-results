# Debt Avalanche Method

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** The debt avalanche method is a repayment strategy that directs any extra money toward the debt with the highest interest rate while maintaining minimum payments on all other debts. By tackling the most costly debt first, the borrower reduces overall interest expense and typically clears debt faster than alternative methods.

---

## How It Works
1. **List all debts** with their balances, interest rates, and minimum required payments.
2. **Make minimum payments** on every debt each month.
3. **Allocate any surplus cash** (from budgeting or reduced discretionary spending) to the debt with the highest interest rate.
4. **When the highest‑rate debt is paid off**, roll its entire payment amount into the next highest‑rate debt, and repeat until all debts are cleared.

The method assumes APR is nominal and compounded monthly, using the standard monthly‑rate conversion (APR ÷ 12).

---

## Why Choose Avalanche Over Snowball?
- **Interest Savings:** By attacking the highest‑rate debt first, the borrower saves hundreds to thousands of dollars in interest.
- **Speed of Elimination:** Though the snowball method can provide quick psychological wins by paying off the smallest balances, the avalanche often finishes the overall debt load sooner when the interest spread is large (as in this case).

---

## Integration with Budgeting
- The avalanche method relies on a **zero‑based budget** to identify surplus cash that can be directed to debt repayment.
- Maintaining a modest **buffer** (e.g., $100 per month) helps avoid new credit‑card use, supporting the avalanche strategy.
- Building a starter **emergency fund** before allocating all surplus to debt can provide safety without significantly derailing the avalanche timeline.
