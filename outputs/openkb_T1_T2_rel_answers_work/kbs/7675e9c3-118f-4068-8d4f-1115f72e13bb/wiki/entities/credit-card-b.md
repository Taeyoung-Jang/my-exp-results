# Credit Card B

**Type:** Product (credit card)

**Balance:** $3,200

**Annual Percentage Rate (APR):** 19.99%  (≈ 0.016658 monthly rate)

**Minimum monthly payment:** $95

**Role in the user's finances**
- Listed among the user’s debts in [[summaries/session-11_2023-06-15]].
- Treated as a lower‑priority debt in the [[concepts/debt-avalanche-method]] because its interest rate is below that of [[entities/credit-card-a]] but above the personal and student loans.
- Included in the [[concepts/zero-based-budgeting]] plan where the user allocates a $100 buffer, essential expenses, and discretionary limits, while extra funds are first directed toward the higher‑rate Credit Card A.

**Interaction with other financial items**
- **Credit Card A:** Higher‑rate debt that receives extra payments before Credit Card B under the avalanche strategy ([[entities/credit-card-a]]).
- **Personal loan:** After Credit Card B is paid off, its payment amount rolls into the personal loan repayment ([[entities/personal-loan]]).
- **Student loan:** The final debt to be tackled once all higher‑rate obligations are cleared ([[entities/student-loan]]).

**Considerations**
- Building an [[concepts/emergency-fund]] before aggressively paying down credit‑card balances can help avoid new debt.
- The user’s zero‑based budget reserves $100 as a buffer to cover unexpected costs, reducing reliance on credit cards like Credit Card B.
