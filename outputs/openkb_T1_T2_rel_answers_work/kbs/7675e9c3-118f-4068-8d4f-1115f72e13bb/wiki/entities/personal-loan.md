# Personal Loan (product)

**Type**: product

**Related summary**: [[summaries/session-11_2023-06-15]]

## Overview
The personal loan referenced in the budget discussion is a fixed‑rate installment loan with the following characteristics:

- **Original balance**: $12,400
- **Interest rate**: 11.5% APR (nominal, compounded monthly)
- **Monthly payment (minimum)**: $310
- **Role in budget**: Treated as a required debt payment in the zero‑based budgeting plan.  It is the third debt targeted in the avalanche payoff sequence after the two credit cards.

## Interaction with Debt Payoff Strategy
- The loan’s interest rate is lower than the credit‑card rates, so under the [[concepts/debt-avalanche-method]] it is paid after both Credit Card A and Credit Card B are eliminated.
- Once the credit‑card balances are cleared, the full amount previously allocated to those payments (≈ $915 per month) is rolled into the personal loan, accelerating its repayment.

## Financial Impact
- When $915/month is applied, the loan is projected to be paid off in roughly 15 months, adding about $900 in total interest over its life.
- The loan’s balance and payment schedule are central to the overall timeline presented in the session‑11 budget and payoff plan.
