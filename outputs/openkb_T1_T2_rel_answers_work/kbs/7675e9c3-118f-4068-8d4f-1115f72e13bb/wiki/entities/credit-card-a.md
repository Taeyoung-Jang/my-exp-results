# Credit Card A

- **Balance:** $6,800
- **APR:** 24.99% (monthly rate ≈ 2.08%)
- **Minimum monthly payment:** $210
- **Extra payment in budget:** $300 (or $200 while building an [[concepts/emergency-fund]])
- **Total monthly payment in the proposed plan:** $510 (minimum + extra)

## Role in the user's finances
Credit Card A is the highest‑interest debt in the scenario described in [[summaries/session-11_2023-06-15]]. Because of its rate, it is the primary target of the [[concepts/debt-avalanche-method]] strategy. The zero‑based budgeting approach outlined in the same summary allocates a $300 surplus each month specifically to accelerate payoff of this card.

## Interaction with other financial items
- Competes with [[entities/credit-card-b]] (balance $3,200, APR 19.99%) for repayment priority.
- After Credit Card A is cleared, the freed‑up payment amount rolls into the repayment of [[entities/personal-loan]] and later the [[entities/student-loan]].
- The user’s checking balance is tracked under [[entities/checking-account]]; building a starter [[concepts/emergency-fund]] may temporarily reduce the extra payment to Credit Card A.

## Payoff estimates (from the summary)
- **Full $300 extra:** ~14 months to retire, ~ $1,000 total interest.
- **$200 extra for first 10 months (while saving $1,000 emergency fund), then $300:** ~20 months total, ~ $1,500 interest.

These figures assume the APR is treated as nominal and compounded monthly.
