## Counter‑space optimization counter space optimization
- Keep 5‑7 daily‑use items: coffee/tea setup, small cutting board, one utensil in a crock, oil & salt/pepper bottle, and a pleasant item (fruit bowl or plant).
- Store or donate all rarely‑used appliances, duplicate tools, extra cookware, and anything not used weekly.

## Knife storage options knife storage options
- **Knife block**: not space‑efficient for tiny kitchens.
- **Magnetic strip**: best renter‑friendly solution; mount with removable adhesive pads.
- **Drawer**: edge guards or in‑drawer tray as alternatives.

## Renter‑friendly magnetic strip mounting magnetic knife strip
- Use adhesive pads with gel‑type, pull‑tab design (e.g., Command picture‑hanging strips or 3M removable VHB tabs).
- Clean surface with rubbing alcohol, press 30 s, wait 1 h before loading knives.
- Height: eye‑level or just below, directly above prep area.

## Recommended products product recommendations
- Magnetic strips: IKEA Kungsfors stainless, OXO bamboo strip, HMmagnets stainless (Amazon).
- Removable pads: Command “picture hanging” strips, 3M removable VHB tabs.
- Edge‑guard kits: Messermeister silicone‑lined guards (8″ chef, 4″ paring, 10″ bread), Dalstrong Easy‑Grip guards.

## Minimal knife set minimal kitchen utensils
- Chef’s knife
- Paring knife
- Serrated bread/utility knife

## Edge‑guard drawer storage edge guard drawer storage
- Choose silicone‑lined or rubber‑textured guards to prevent slipping.
- Fit guards to blade length; snug fit stays in place longer than hard‑plastic only guards.

**Key takeaways**: Prioritize daily‑use items on the counter, use a magnetic strip with removable adhesive for renters, and keep knives either on the strip or safely in a drawer with silicone edge guards.
