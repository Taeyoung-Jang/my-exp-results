# Summary of session-04_2025-06-15

**Main contribution**: A set of concrete, low‑conflict approaches to split household chores between siblings, including two ready‑to‑use weekly schedules, a flexible "backup rule," and communication tips.

## Key Concepts
- **Fair chore division** – focus on preference, strength, and frequency rather than a strict 50/50 split. fair chore division
- **Open communication** – a calm, collaborative conversation to surface expectations and avoid guesswork. communication in shared living
- **Rotating chores** – alternating dishes to balance daily workload. rotating chores
- **Daily task balancing** – adding a small daily responsibility (trash, counter wipe, quick tidy) to the sibling handling less‑frequent chores. daily task balancing
- **Backup rule** – if one partner is tired or working late, the other covers that day’s chore and the covered partner swaps the same chore the next day, eliminating ad‑hoc negotiation. backup rule
- **Weekly schedule planning** – two template schedules (alternating dishes vs. added daily task) that keep cooking primarily with the user while keeping overall load even. weekly schedule planning
- **Conflict prevention** – brief regular check‑ins and a non‑scorekeeping mindset to keep tension low. conflict prevention

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Start with a joint conversation** to name perceived unfairness and agree on a flexible system.
2. **Leverage strengths**: keep cooking with the person who enjoys it, but share the daily cleanup.
3. **Option 1 – Alternating dishes**
   - You cook every night.
   - Dishes: you on Tue, Thu, Sat; sibling on Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun.
   - Laundry Tue & Sat; Vacuum Sat (as already practiced).
4. **Option 2 – Small daily task for sibling**
   - You cook & do dishes every night.
   - Sibling adds a 5‑10 min daily task (trash, counter wipe, quick sweep).
   - Laundry & vacuuming unchanged.
5. **Backup rule** – automatic swap for the day; the swapped‑over partner takes the same chore next day.
6. **Maintain a brief weekly check‑in** to tweak any imbalance before it escalates.

## Practical Schedule Templates
### Option 1 – Alternating Dishes
| Day | Cooking | Dishes | Laundry | Vacuum |
|-----|---------|--------|---------|--------|
| Mon | You | Sibling | – | – |
| Tue | You | You | Sibling | – |
| Wed | You | Sibling | – | – |
| Thu | You | You | – | – |
| Fri | You | Sibling | – | – |
| Sat | You | You | Sibling | Sibling |
| Sun | You | Sibling | – | – |

### Option 2 – Daily Small Task
| Day | Cooking & Dishes | Small Daily Task | Laundry | Vacuum |
|-----|------------------|------------------|---------|--------|
| Mon‑Sun | You | Sibling (e.g., trash) | Tue & Sat | Sat |

Both options keep the core routine intact while redistributing the daily load for a fairer feel.
