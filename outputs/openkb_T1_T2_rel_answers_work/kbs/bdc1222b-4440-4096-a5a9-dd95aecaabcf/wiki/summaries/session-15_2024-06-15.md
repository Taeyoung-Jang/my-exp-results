# Summary of Session 15 (2024‑06‑15)

## Practical day‑to‑day differences between [[entities/android]] and [[entities/ios]]
- **Control vs. convenience** – [[entities/android]] offers more customization; [[entities/ios]] provides tighter integration and less maintenance.  
- **Data migration** – Moving [[entities/ios]]↔[[entities/ios]] is seamless; [[entities/android]]↔[[entities/android]] varies by brand; crossing platforms ([[entities/android]] ⇄ [[entities/ios]]) often loses app‑specific data.  
- **App ecosystem** – Most major apps exist on both platforms; [[entities/ios]] apps tend to be more polished, [[entities/android]] apps give more freedom and third‑party stores.  
- **Long‑term support** – [[entities/ios]] devices receive 5‑6 years of updates; select [[entities/android]] manufacturers (Pixel, Samsung) now promise 5‑7 years.  
- **Resale & repair** – [[entities/ios]] devices hold value better; [[entities/android]] offers broader hardware variety and easier repair options.

## [[entities/android]] “ecosystem” recommendations for Google‑centric users
- **Pixel** – Runs stock [[entities/android]], minimal bloat, fastest updates, 7‑year update guarantee, most reliable Google backup/restore.  
- **Samsung** – Strong hardware (displays, battery), decent backup tools, longer support windows on flagships, but adds One UI bloat and slower updates.  
- Other brands – Generally more bloat, slower updates, and less reliable backup integration.

## Safe‑bet [[entities/android]] models for 4‑6 year longevity
| Brand | Model(s) | Update promise | Recommended specs |
|-------|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Google Pixel** | [[entities/pixel-9]], [[entities/pixel-9-pro]] (or Pixel 8 series) | 7 years | ≥256 GB storage, highest RAM option |
| **Samsung** | [[entities/galaxy-s24]], S24+, S24 Ultra | 7 years (now) | ≥256 GB, base or higher RAM (Plus/Ultra) |

## Cost‑focused choice: Pixel 9 vs. Galaxy S24 (base)
- **Pixel 9** – Cleaner, stock [[entities/android]], smoother Google backup/restore, fewer preinstalled apps.  
- **Galaxy S24** – Excellent hardware but adds One UI layers, later updates, slightly more maintenance.  
- Verdict for “least annoying” 4‑6 year experience: **Pixel 9**.

## Day‑one actions to ensure complete Google backup/restore
1. Verify **[[entities/google-photos]]** backup is on and fully synced.  
2. Confirm **System backup** (Settings → System → Backup) is enabled and recent; trigger manual backup.  
3. Check **[[entities/google-messages]]/[[entities/rcs]]** backup status.  
4. Prepare **[[entities/google-authenticator]]** transfer (export QR code).  
5. Run a full **[[entities/whatsapp]]** Google Drive backup.  
6. Keep **[[entities/signal]]** installed for device‑to‑device transfer (no Google backup).  
7. Save backup codes for every 2FA‑enabled account.

## Full migration checklist (Pixel 9)
### Before you start
- ✅ Ensure Wi‑Fi, plug in old phone, confirm all cloud syncs (Photos, Messages, WhatsApp, etc.) are complete.  
- ✅ Export Authenticator accounts (do not delete yet).  
- ✅ Record backup codes for all 2FA services.  
- ✅ Verify Signal is ready for direct transfer.

### During setup/transfer
1. Power on Pixel 9, choose **Copy data from old device** (cable or wireless).  
2. Sign in with your Google account – triggers restoration of system backup, apps, call & Wi‑Fi settings.  
3. Accept the most recent cloud backup when prompted.  
4. Let the restore run uninterrupted.

### After the new phone boots
- 📸 Open **[[entities/google-photos]]** – confirm library appears and syncing continues.  
- 💬 Open **[[entities/google-messages]]**, wait for RCS to activate and confirm chat history is present.  
- 🔐 Open **[[entities/google-authenticator]]** – import accounts via QR code; test a few codes.  
- 📞 Install **[[entities/whatsapp]]**, verify number, restore from Google Drive backup.  
- 📱 Install **[[entities/signal]]**, follow the device‑to‑device transfer steps; verify chats before wiping old phone.  
- 🔑 Log into all 2FA‑protected services; confirm codes work.  
- ✅ Only after confirming all above, **factory‑reset** the old device.

## Common gotchas
- Missing Signal chats if transfer isn’t performed before wiping the old phone.  
- Assuming Google backs up Authenticator codes – they must be exported manually.  
- Apps without cloud sync lose local data.  
- Incomplete photo upload leading to missing images after restore.
