# Google Photos (product)

Google Photos is a cloud‑based service that automatically backs up and syncs photos and videos from Android devices. In the context of the **session‑15_2024‑06‑15** migration guide, it is highlighted as a critical component for ensuring a smooth data transfer when moving to a new phone such as a [[entities/pixel-9]] or [[entities/galaxy-s24]].

## Key facts from the session‑15 summary
- **Backup verification**: Users are instructed to open Google Photos on the old device, confirm that "Backup" is enabled, and wait until the library is fully synced before starting the migration.
- **Reliability**: Google Photos is considered a reliable part of the overall [[concepts/google_backup]] strategy, especially for Google‑centric Android ecosystems.
- **Cross‑device restore**: After setting up the new phone, opening Google Photos should immediately show the full library and continue syncing without interruption.
- **Long‑term support**: As part of a broader [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]] plan, Google Photos benefits from Google's commitment to multi‑year updates on devices like Pixel phones.
