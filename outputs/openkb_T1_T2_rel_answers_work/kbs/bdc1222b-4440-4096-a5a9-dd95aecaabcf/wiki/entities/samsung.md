## Samsung (organization)

Samsung is a South Korean multinational corporation that designs, manufactures, and sells consumer electronics, including Android smartphones. In the context of **session‑15_2024‑06‑15**, Samsung is highlighted as a major Android "ecosystem" alternative to Google’s Pixel devices.

### Key points from the session
- **Hardware variety**: Samsung’s Galaxy line, especially the **Galaxy S24**, **S24+**, and **S24 Ultra**, offers high‑end displays, strong battery life, and multiple form‑factor options.
- **Software stack**: Devices run Samsung’s One UI skin on top of Android, which adds pre‑installed apps and some customization layers, resulting in more bloat compared to stock Android.
- **Update commitment**: Samsung now pledges up to seven years of security and OS updates for its flagship S‑series, aligning with the long‑term support promise discussed in [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]].
- **Backup & restore**: Samsung’s backup tools work, but Google‑centric backups (Photos, Messages, Drive) tend to be cleaner on Pixel devices. Samsung users may encounter extra steps when restoring data due to the additional Samsung‑specific services.
- **Overall recommendation**: For users deeply integrated with Google services, Samsung is the second‑best choice after Pixel, offering broader hardware choices at the cost of a slightly more complex software experience.
