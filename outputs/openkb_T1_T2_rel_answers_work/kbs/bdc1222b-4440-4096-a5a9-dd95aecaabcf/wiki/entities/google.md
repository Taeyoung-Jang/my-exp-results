# Google (organization)

Google is a global technology leader that develops and maintains the Android operating system, a suite of cloud services, and the Pixel line of smartphones. In the context of the **session‑15_2024‑06‑15** discussion, Google is highlighted for:

- Providing the **stock Android** experience found on [[entities/pixel-9]] and [[entities/pixel-9-pro]], which ensures minimal bloat and the fastest system updates.
- Offering robust **backup and restore** capabilities through Google Drive, Google Photos, and the system backup service – see [[concepts/google_backup]].
- Powering essential consumer apps such as [[entities/google-photos]], [[entities/google-messages]] (including [[entities/rcs]]), and [[entities/google-authenticator]], all of which are central to a smooth data migration.
- Supporting the broader Android ecosystem, competing with [[entities/apple]]'s iOS platform ([[concepts/android_vs_ios]]), and influencing long‑term phone support decisions ([[concepts/long_term_phone_support]]).

### Key Services Mentioned
- **Google Photos** – cloud photo backup and sync, crucial for preserving media when moving to a new device.
- **Google Messages** – includes RCS messaging; its backup is part of the overall system backup.
- **Google Authenticator** – two‑factor authentication app; requires manual export for migration.
- **Google Drive** – storage backend for WhatsApp chat backups and other app data.

### Role in Device Choice
The conversation recommends Google’s own hardware (Pixel) as the most hassle‑free Android option because it runs Android as Google intends and leverages Google’s backup infrastructure. Samsung’s [[entities/galaxy-s24]] devices are presented as a viable alternative, but they introduce additional software layers and slower updates compared to the pure Google experience.
