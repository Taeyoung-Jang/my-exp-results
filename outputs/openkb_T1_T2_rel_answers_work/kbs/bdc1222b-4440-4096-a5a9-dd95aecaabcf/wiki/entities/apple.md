## Apple (Organization)

Apple Inc. is a global technology company best known for designing and selling the iPhone series, which run the iOS operating system. In the discussion of day‑to‑day smartphone differences, Apple is presented as the provider of a tightly integrated ecosystem that offers:

- **Seamless device migration**: moving between iPhones is described as “almost frictionless” because Apple controls both hardware and software.
- **Long‑term software support**: iPhones typically receive 5–6 years of OS updates, contributing to a feeling of continued support and higher resale value.
- **Polished app experience**: iOS apps tend to be more consistent and refined, as developers target a limited set of devices.
- **Resale and repair considerations**: iPhones hold their resale value better than most Android phones.

These points are contrasted with Android devices such as those from [[entities/google]] and [[entities/samsung]], reflecting the broader [[concepts/android_vs_ios]] comparison. The document also notes that iOS phones receive fewer updates than the 7‑year promises from Google’s Pixel line or Samsung’s flagship Galaxy series, tying into the theme of [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]].
