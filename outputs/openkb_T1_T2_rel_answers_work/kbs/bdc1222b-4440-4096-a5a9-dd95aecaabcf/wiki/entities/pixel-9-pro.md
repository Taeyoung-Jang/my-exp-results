# Pixel 9 Pro

Pixel 9 Pro is Google's 2024 flagship Android smartphone, positioned as the premium model in the Pixel 9 family. It runs stock Android, receives Google’s promised seven‑year update window, and integrates tightly with Google services such as [[entities/google-photos]], [[entities/google-messages]], and [[entities/google-authenticator]].

## Key facts from the session‑15 discussion
- **Long‑term support**: Offers a seven‑year Android update guarantee, making it a top choice for users seeking [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]].
- **Backup/restore reliability**: Works best with Google’s own backup ecosystem, referenced in [[concepts/google_backup]].
- **Hardware recommendations**: Suggested configuration is at least 256 GB storage and the highest RAM option to ensure smooth restores and future‑proof performance.
- **Comparison**: Recommended over the Samsung [[entities/galaxy-s24]] for the "least annoying" 4–6 year experience due to minimal bloat and faster updates.
- **Ecosystem fit**: Ideal for users already deep in the Google ecosystem, providing seamless syncing with services like [[entities/google-photos]] and reliable migration tools.
