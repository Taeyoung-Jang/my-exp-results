# Google Messages

Google Messages is the official messaging app for Android, supporting both traditional SMS and Rich Communication Services (RCS) for enhanced chat features.

## Key facts from the session‑15 discussion
- **Backup & restore**: Messages data is included in the Android system backup (see [[concepts/google_backup]]). During a phone migration, the assistant recommends confirming that RCS is enabled and that the backup timestamp is recent before transferring to a new device like a [[entities/pixel-9]] or [[entities/galaxy-s24]].
- **RCS integration**: The app leverages RCS (referenced as [[entities/rcs]]) to provide read receipts, typing indicators, and higher‑quality media sharing.
- **Role in migration checklist**: In the step‑by‑step migration checklist, users are instructed to verify that Google Messages is set up correctly and that RCS is active before starting the data transfer. After the new phone is set up, they should open Google Messages to ensure the chat history appears and RCS re‑activates.
- **Long‑term support**: As part of the broader discussion on [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]], Google Messages benefits from the same update cadence as the underlying Android version, especially on devices with guaranteed updates such as Pixel phones.
