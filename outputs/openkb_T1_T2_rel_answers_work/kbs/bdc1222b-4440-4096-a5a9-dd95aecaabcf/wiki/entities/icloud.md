# iCloud (product)

**iCloud** is Apple’s cloud‑based storage and synchronization platform that keeps photos, notes, passwords, device backups, and other data consistent across Macs, iPads, iPhones, and other Apple devices.

---

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-13]]
- After consolidating data, the user relies on iCloud to sync **photos**, **notes**, and **passwords** across macOS and iPadOS.
- The checklist emphasizes reviewing iCloud **sync settings** and confirming that categories like Photos, iCloud Keychain, and Notes are enabled.
- For iPadOS, the user should verify that **iCloud Backup** is turned on and shows a recent backup.
- macOS does not perform full device backups via iCloud, but enabling core sync categories ensures data is encrypted and available.

## Practical Guidance (from the session)
1. **Check sync toggles**: Settings ► *your name* ► **iCloud** → ensure Photos, iCloud Keychain, Notes, etc., are turned on.
2. **Verify backup** (iPadOS): Settings ► *your name* ► **iCloud** ► **iCloud Backup** → enabled and recent.
3. **Review device list**: Settings ► *your name* ► scroll to devices → remove any unknown devices to protect iCloud data.
4. **Maintain recovery options**: Add a [[entities/recovery-contact]] via Account Recovery; consider a [[entities/recovery-key]] only if you can store it securely.
5. **Keep trusted phone numbers up‑to‑date** to avoid lock‑outs when accessing iCloud.

## Summary
iCloud provides seamless, encrypted synchronization and backup for Apple users. Proper configuration of sync settings, backups, trusted numbers, and recovery options ensures the service remains secure and reliable.
