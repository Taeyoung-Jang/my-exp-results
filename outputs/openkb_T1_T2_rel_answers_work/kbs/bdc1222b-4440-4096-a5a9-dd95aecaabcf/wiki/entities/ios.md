# iOS (product)

**Overview**  
 iOS is the mobile operating system developed by [[entities/apple]] for the iPhone and other Apple devices. It is known for its closed, tightly integrated ecosystem, providing seamless hand‑off between devices and a uniform user experience.

**Key characteristics**  
- **Integration:** End‑to‑end control of hardware, software, and services, enabling smooth data migration between iPhones.  
- **App quality:** Apps tend to be more polished and consistent because developers target a limited set of devices.  
- **Long‑term support:** Apple typically supplies iOS updates for 5–6 years, contributing to higher resale value and prolonged usability.  
- **Customization:** Compared with [[entities/android]], iOS offers limited user customization and fewer third‑party app stores.

**Comparison with Android**  
The practical day‑to‑day differences are captured in [[concepts/android_vs_ios]], where iOS scores higher on consistency and long‑term convenience, while Android provides greater flexibility and hardware variety.

**Support lifecycle**  
Consistent with the discussion of [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]] in the session, iOS’s multi‑year update commitment makes it attractive for users who prefer a set‑and‑forget device.
