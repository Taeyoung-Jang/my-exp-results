## Galaxy S24

The **Galaxy S24**, **S24+**, and **S24 Ultra** are Samsung’s flagship Android smartphones released in early 2024. They are highlighted in the *session‑15* discussion as the primary non‑Pixel Android alternative for users seeking a long‑term, reliable device.

### Key facts from the conversation
- **Update commitment**: Samsung now pledges up to seven years of software updates for the S24 line, matching the guarantees offered by Google’s Pixel devices. This aligns with the [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]] concept.
- **Hardware variety**: The S24 series provides a range of screen sizes and specifications, with storage options starting at 256 GB and RAM configurations that increase from the base to the Ultra model, ensuring smooth performance over several years.
- **Backup & restore**: While Samsung’s One UI adds extra apps and settings, its backup tools integrate with Google services (e.g., [[entities/google-photos]], [[entities/google-messages]]) and Samsung’s own cloud, though restores may require more steps compared to the stock Android experience of a Pixel.
- **Bloat and customization**: The series includes Samsung‑specific software and pre‑installed apps, which can be disabled but represent additional bloat relative to the stock Android experience.
- **Comparison to Pixel**: In the source document, the Pixel 9 is recommended for the “least annoying” long‑term experience due to minimal bloat and smoother Google backup/restore, while the S24 is praised for its premium hardware and still‑reliable support.
