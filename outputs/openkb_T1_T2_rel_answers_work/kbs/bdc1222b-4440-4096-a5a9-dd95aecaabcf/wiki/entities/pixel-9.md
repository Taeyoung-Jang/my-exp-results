# Pixel 9

**Type:** product

**Manufacturer:** [[entities/google]]

**Release year:** 2024

**Key attributes**
- Runs stock Android with fast, direct updates from Google.
- Promises up to seven years of OS and security updates, aligning with the [[concepts/long_term_phone_support]] goal for Android devices.
- Designed for users deeply integrated with Google services, offering the most reliable [[concepts/google_backup]] experience across Photos, Messages, and other data.
- Offers at least 256 GB storage and the highest RAM configuration for smooth restores and multi‑year performance.

**Why it stands out in the Android ecosystem**
- Minimal bloat compared to many OEM skins, making it the cleanest choice for users who want a hassle‑free experience.
- Direct integration with [[entities/google-photos]], [[entities/google-messages]] (including [[entities/rcs]]), and Google Authenticator, simplifying day‑to‑day data migration.
- Compared to competitors like [[entities/galaxy-s24]], the Pixel 9 generally receives updates sooner and has a more straightforward backup/restore flow.

**Relevant usage scenarios**
- Ideal for users who have locked their data ecosystem into Google accounts and need a phone that avoids manufacturer‑added software layers.
- Recommended for long‑term ownership (4–6 years) due to its update guarantee and stable performance.
