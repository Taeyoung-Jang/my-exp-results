# Android vs iOS

**Definition**: A practical comparison of the two dominant mobile operating systems, focusing on control, integration, data migration, app ecosystems, long‑term support, resale value, and repairability.

---

## Core dimensions

| Dimension | Android | iOS |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Control & customization** | High – users can change default apps, install third‑party stores, and tweak UI elements. | Low – Apple restricts defaults and disallows alternative app stores.
| **Integration** | Varies by manufacturer; Google services integrate tightly, but OEM skins add extra layers. | Tight, end‑to‑end integration of hardware, iCloud, and Apple services.
| **Data migration** | Moving between Android devices is smoother when using Google’s migration tools, but crossing to iOS can lose app‑specific data. | Moving iPhone → iPhone is almost frictionless; crossing to Android often loses data.
| **App ecosystem** | Nearly identical catalog of major apps; quality can vary across devices and manufacturers. | Similar catalog, but apps tend to be more polished because developers target fewer devices.
| **Long‑term support** | 5‑7 years on flagship devices from Google (Pixel) and Samsung; cheaper brands often fall short. | 5‑6 years of OS updates on all iPhone models, with strong support consistency.
| **Resale value** | Generally lower; depreciation faster. | Higher resale value; devices retain worth longer.
| **Repairability** | Wider hardware variety means more repair options; some models are very repair‑friendly. | Repair options are more limited; Apple‑specific parts and services required.

---

## Practical takeaways from [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]]
- **Control vs. convenience**: Android offers deeper control, while iOS provides a "set‑and‑forget" experience.
- **Migration pain points**: Crossing platforms is the hardest part; staying within the same ecosystem (Android→Android or iOS→iOS) is smoother.
- **Update longevity**: iPhones receive consistent updates for 5‑6 years, whereas only Pixel and Samsung currently promise up to 7 years.
- **App quality**: iOS apps often feel more consistent; Android apps can vary due to device‑specific customizations.
- **Hardware & repair**: Android gives more hardware choices and easier repairs, but iPhone resale remains superior.
