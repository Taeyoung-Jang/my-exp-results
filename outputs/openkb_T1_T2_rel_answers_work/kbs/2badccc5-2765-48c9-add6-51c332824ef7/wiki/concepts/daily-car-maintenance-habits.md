# Daily Car Maintenance Habits

**Definition:** Consistent, low‑effort routines that maintain organization, cleanliness, and comfort in a compact vehicle.

## Core Practices (from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-15]])

1. **Two‑minute tidy after every trip** – unload groceries, gym gear, and other items and return them to their designated spots.
2. **Designated “home” for each item** – use a small collapsible trunk organizer, a seat‑back pocket, and a reusable trash bag so nothing rolls around.
3. **Micro‑fiber cloth in a door pocket** – quickly wipe dust and smudges that are more noticeable in a tight cabin.
4. **Separate clean vs. dirty zones** – keep a washable canvas tote or mesh bag for sweaty shoes and muddy clothes while groceries stay in the organizer.
5. **Odor control** – store gym gear in breathable bags with charcoal deodorizer pouches; refresh pouches in sunlight monthly.
6. **Non‑slip solutions** – employ organizers with rubber/Velcro bottoms or a non‑slip mat to prevent cargo from shifting on carpeted floors.

## Benefits
- Maintains a feeling of spaciousness despite limited interior volume (see [[concepts/compact-car-storage]]).
- Reduces odors and moisture buildup, supporting [[concepts/odor-control-in-vehicles]].
- Saves time by preventing clutter buildup that would require longer clean‑ups later.
