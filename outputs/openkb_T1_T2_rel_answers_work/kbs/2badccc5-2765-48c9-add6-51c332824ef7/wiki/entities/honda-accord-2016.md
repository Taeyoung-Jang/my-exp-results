# 2016 Honda Accord

**Type:** product

## Overview
The 2016 Honda Accord is a midsize sedan known for reliability and resale value. In the conversation *session‑03_2024‑06‑15* it serves as the **current vehicle** being evaluated for a keep‑vs‑replace decision.

## Role in [[summaries/session-03_2024-06-15]]
- **Mileage:** ~92,000 miles
- **Recent maintenance (last year):** $1,200 (brakes, tires, battery)
- **Upcoming repairs:** front struts (~$900) and a potential oil‑seep repair (~$1,500)
- **Resale value estimate:** $10,000–$12,000 (private party)
- **Financial context:** The owner is comparing a 2‑year keep scenario against buying a used car in the $22k–$28k range.

## Financial Details Used in the Analysis
- **Keep scenario costs (2 years):**
  - Anticipated repairs: $2,400 (struts + oil repair)
  - Routine maintenance: $500–$700
  - Depreciation impact: modest drop from $10k–$12k to $7k–$9k
- **Replace scenario costs (2 years):**
  - Purchase price of replacement (example): $25,000
  - Down payment: $5,000
  - Loan financing: $20,000 at typical used‑car rates → $8,400–$10,800 in payments
  - Higher insurance: +$400–$600 over two years
  - Depreciation: $3,000–$5,000
  - Maintenance: $300–$500
