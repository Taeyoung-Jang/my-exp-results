# L.L.Bean

**Type:** organization

L.L.Bean is an American outdoor‑apparel and goods retailer founded in 1912.  The brand is especially recognized for its sturdy canvas products, such as the classic Boat and Tote, which are frequently recommended for practical everyday uses.
