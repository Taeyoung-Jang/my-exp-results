# Toyota Camry

The **Toyota Camry** is a popular mid‑size sedan that frequently appears as a potential replacement vehicle in cost‑comparison discussions. In the conversation *session‑03_2024‑06‑15*, the Camry is mentioned alongside the Honda Accord as a 2–4‑year‑old used‑car option with a purchase price in the **$22k–$28k** range.

## Role in the keep‑vs‑replace decision
- The Camry serves as an example of a newer, possibly more reliable vehicle that could replace an older car like a [[entities/honda-accord-2016]].
- It is evaluated using the [[concepts/two-year-cost-framework]] to compare total ownership costs over two years, including loan payments, insurance, depreciation, maintenance, and fuel.
- The decision to switch to a Camry involves assessing [[concepts/keep-vs-replace-decision]] factors such as repair costs on the current vehicle, resale value, and changing lifestyle needs.

## Financial considerations highlighted
- **Purchase price:** $22 000–$28 000 (including taxes/fees).
- **Depreciation:** Expected to follow typical [[concepts/vehicle-depreciation]] trends for a lightly used sedan.
- **Insurance:** Likely higher than for an older Accord, affecting the overall cost analysis.
