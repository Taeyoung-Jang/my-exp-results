# Accord Replacement Range

The **Accord Replacement Range** refers to the set of used‑car options the user is evaluating to replace a 2016 Honda Accord.  In the conversation the user cites a budget of **$22,000 – $28,000** and mentions potential models such as a recent‑year **Toyota Camry**, another **Honda Accord**, or a small SUV.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-03_2024-06-15]]
