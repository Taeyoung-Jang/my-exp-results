# Summary of session-06_2025-06-15

The user asked for help creating a short, friendly forum post announcing a recent product update. The assistant provided multiple template versions, progressively stripped down, each containing:

- A **TL;DR** line.  
- One to three sentences describing the update with **placeholders** for features, areas, settings, and links.  
- Bullet points (or a minimal list) directing readers to the **full announcement** and **changelog**.  
- An invitation for readers to **reply with any issues** they notice.  
- An optional sentence with a placeholder link to the **settings page**.  
- Two tone options: **neutral** and **upbeat**, showing how language can be adjusted without adding new facts.

Key concepts include [[concepts/forum-announcement-drafting]], [[concepts/update-communication]], and [[concepts/tone-adaptation]]. These templates can be reused for future announcements, simply filling in the placeholders with the actual feature names, URLs, and setting locations.
