# Update Communication

Update Communication refers to the practice of notifying users about product or service changes through brief, clear, and actionable messages. Effective update communication balances brevity with enough detail to guide users on what has changed, what actions they may need to take, and where to find more information.

## Core Elements
- **TL;DR Summary** – A one‑line headline that quickly conveys the essence of the update.
- **Placeholders for Specifics** – Slots for feature names, settings, and URLs that can be filled in later, ensuring the template remains reusable.
- **Links to Full Resources** – Direct links to the official announcement and changelog so readers can dive deeper if needed.
- **Tone Adaptation** – Adjusting language to be neutral or upbeat depending on the community’s culture. See [[concepts/tone-adaptation]] for guidance on tone choices.
- **Feedback Invitation** – A short call‑to‑action encouraging users to reply with any issues they encounter after the update.
- **Optional Settings Reminder** – An extra sentence with a placeholder link to the settings page for users who need to verify configuration changes.
