# Forum Announcement Drafting

**Definition**: A set of best‑practice patterns for writing short, clear, and approachable forum posts that inform community members about product updates without hype.

## Core Elements (as illustrated in [[summaries/session-06_2025-06-15]])

1. **TL;DR line** – A one‑sentence snapshot that tells readers the update is live and what the main focus is.
2. **Brief description** – 3‑4 sentences (or even a single sentence for ultra‑short versions) that mention the affected features or areas, using **placeholders** (`[feature placeholder]`, `[announcement link placeholder]`, etc.) for details that will be filled later.
3. **Links** – Separate URLs for the full announcement and the changelog, kept on distinct lines for easy copying.
4. **Call to action** – Invite readers to reply with any issues or feedback after they try the update.
5. **Optional settings reminder** – A sentence with a link to the user’s settings page, included only when needed.

## Tone Adaptation

Two tonal variants are commonly provided:

- **Neutral tone** – Polite and factual, suitable for a broad audience.
- **Upbeat tone** – Slightly more enthusiastic, highlighting the positive feel of the changes.

The choice of tone can be swapped without altering the structural elements, illustrating the concept of [[concepts/tone-adaptation]].

## Update Communication Context

These drafts are part of broader **[[concepts/update-communication]]** practices, ensuring that community announcements are:

- Consistent with the official product release notes.
- Easy to skim for members who may have missed the original announcement.
- Structured to surface actionable items (settings checks, feature re‑configurations).

## Template Sketch

```
TL;DR: [short summary]

[Brief description with placeholders]
Full announcement: [announcement link placeholder]
Changelog: [changelog link placeholder]
If anything acts weird after the update, just reply and let me know.

Optional: You can review your settings here if needed: [settings link placeholder]
```

This template can be copied, filled, and posted directly, streamlining the communication workflow for community moderators and power users.
