## Overview
The session provides a practical, screen‑free evening wind‑down designed for people navigating big life transitions. It emphasizes **reset points**—small, repeatable cues that mark the end of the active part of the day—while keeping the routine flexible.

## Core Concepts
- **reset points** – One or two brief cues (e.g., dimming lights, a playlist) that signal the shift to evening.
- **screen free wind down** – A 30–45 minute script that avoids screens and focuses on gentle activities.
- **low alert audio** – Background sound choices that are warm but not stimulating (soft instrumental playlists, ambient rain, slow‑paced podcasts or familiar audiobooks).
- **mail admin block** – A short, bounded period for handling mail or small admin tasks without turning into a stress‑inducing rabbit hole.

## 30–45 minute Evening Script (starting ~7:30 PM)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **7:30‑7:40** | **Mail/Admin Block** – Play low‑alert audio, sort mail for 10 min, recycle junk, and create a tiny “deal‑later” pile. | Keeps productivity without lingering stress. |
| **7:40‑7:50** | **Gentle Grounding** – Read a few pages, stretch slowly, or do a quiet hobby (knitting, puzzle). | Provides a mental “reset” after the admin window. |
| **7:50‑8:00** (or 8:15) | **Sensory Wind‑down** – Brew tea, take a warm shower, wash face, change into comfortable clothes. | Signals the body that it’s time to settle. |
| **Optional 8:00‑8:15** | Deep breathing, light tidying, or simply sit with the drink while the background sound continues. | Allows extra relaxation if desired. |

## Sound Recommendations
- **Instrumental playlists** – Soft piano, acoustic guitar, or lo‑fi without lyrics.
- **Ambient soundscapes** – Light rain, crackling fire, gentle wind.
- **Low‑alert spoken audio** – “slow living” podcasts, cozy conversational shows, or familiar audiobook chapters that don’t demand plot tracking.

## Practical Tips
- Treat each block as a **self‑contained cue** rather than a to‑do list.
- Limit the mail/admin segment to **10 minutes**; set a timer to prevent drift.
- Choose audio that **fills silence** but stays in the background, preventing the apartment from feeling too still.
- Repeat the script for a week to let the sequence become a predictable, calming glide path into night.

## Outcome
Following the script helps maintain a sense of control during major schedule changes, reduces evening anxiety, and creates a gentle transition to sleep without reliance on screens or distracting television noise.
