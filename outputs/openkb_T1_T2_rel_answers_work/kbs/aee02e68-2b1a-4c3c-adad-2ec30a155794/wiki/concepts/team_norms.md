# Team Norms for Work Hours

**Overview**
Team norms for work hours are collective agreements that guide when meetings and communications should occur, aiming to keep collaboration within regular business hours and reduce after‑hours fatigue. These norms are established to balance flexibility with personal boundaries while ensuring that urgent matters are still addressed.

**Key Elements from the Source**
- **Default to earlier meetings**: Encourage the team to schedule meetings earlier in the day rather than defaulting to late‑day or evening slots.
- **Clear end‑time guidance**: Mention a preferred latest end time (e.g., "available until 5 pm") to set expectations without appearing rigid.
- **Emergency clause**: State that genuine emergencies will still receive a response outside normal hours.
- **Flexibility and async options**: Offer alternatives like earlier windows or asynchronous updates for tasks that might otherwise push meetings later.
- **Pushback handling**: Provide concise, respectful responses when a teammate argues that a time is the only option.

**Implementation Tips**
1. Share a brief note (e.g., via [[entities/slack]]) outlining the new norm and its rationale.
2. Include a short subject line for email communications to signal the policy shift.
3. Use the provided pushback line when needed to keep discussions focused on normal‑hour solutions.
4. Reinforce the norm in recurring meetings and team retrospectives to embed the behavior.

By establishing clear **team norms for work hours**, teams can improve work‑life balance, maintain productivity, and ensure that after‑hours work remains an exception rather than the default.
