# Pine (Wood)

Pine is a soft‑wood species that is cheap, readily available, and easy to work with, making it a popular choice for entry‑level woodworking projects such as shelves, planters, stools, and small side tables.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-03_2023-11-13]]
- **Beginner‑friendly**: Recommended in [[concepts/beginner-woodworking]] for its forgiving nature and ease of cutting.
- **Typical dimensions**: Used in 1×8, 1×3, and 1×2 boards for constructing a 16" × 16" tabletop and a 20" tall side table.
- **Safety**: Because pine cuts cleanly, it reduces the risk of splintering when using basic tools like a [[entities/handsaw]] and a [[entities/drill-driver]].
- **Tools needed**: Measure with a [[entities/tape-measure]], mark with a [[entities/pencil]], cut with a [[entities/handsaw]], and finish with [[entities/sandpaper]].
- **Clamping**: Simple projects can be secured using [[entities/quick-grip-clamp]]s.

## Typical Uses in Beginner Projects
- **Shelves**: 1×8 pine boards cut to 2 ft wide and 8‑10 in deep.
- **Stools**: 1×2 legs with a pine top.
- **Side Table**: Top made from two glued 1×8 pieces; legs from 1×2; aprons from 1×3.
