# Quick‑Grip Clamp

A **quick‑grip clamp** is a small, easy‑to‑use clamping tool favored by beginners in [[concepts/beginner-woodworking]]. It features a single‑hand lever that tightens the jaws quickly, making it ideal for holding short workpieces while cutting, drilling, or assembling.

## Key Facts from Session 03 (2023‑11‑13)
- **Recommended quantity:** Two medium‑size clamps are enough for most starter projects such as a simple shelf or side table.
- **Why it’s suggested:** The clamp’s quick‑adjust mechanism lets a novice keep a workpiece steady without needing both hands, which pairs well with other essential [[concepts/basic-tools]] like a [[entities/tape-measure]], [[entities/drill-driver]], [[entities/handsaw]], [[entities/pencil]] and [[entities/sandpaper]].
- **Safety role:** By securely holding the material, the clamp helps prevent accidental slips, supporting the basic [[concepts/woodworking-safety]] practices emphasized in the guide.
- **Typical use cases:** Holding the tabletop while drilling pilot holes, keeping apron rails aligned when screwing them to legs, and stabilizing cut‑outs for the legs of a side table.

## Related Entities & Concepts

## Practical Tips
1. **Position before tightening:** Place the clamp so the jaws are just beyond the edge of the workpiece; then pull the lever to secure.
2. **Avoid over‑tightening:** A snug grip is sufficient; excessive force can mar the wood surface.
3. **Check alignment:** After clamping, verify that the piece remains square before drilling or cutting.

The quick‑grip clamp is a low‑cost, high‑utility addition to any beginner’s toolbox, enabling safe and efficient construction of simple furniture pieces.
