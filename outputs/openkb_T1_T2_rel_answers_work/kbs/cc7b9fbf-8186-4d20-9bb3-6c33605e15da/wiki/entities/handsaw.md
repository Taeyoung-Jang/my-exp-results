# Handsaw

A **handsaw** is a manual cutting tool with a toothed blade used to trim and shape wooden boards. In the context of beginner woodworking, it is listed among the essential [[concepts/basic-tools]] for getting started with simple furniture projects.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-03_2023-11-13]]
- Recommended as one of the first tools a new woodworker should acquire.
- Used to cut pine lumber (e.g., 1×8, 1×3, 1×2) for the side‑table project.
- Paired with other foundational tools such as a [[entities/tape-measure]], [[entities/pencil]], [[entities/drill-driver]], [[entities/quick-grip-clamp]], [[entities/sandpaper]], and a [[entities/carpenter-square]].
- Safety advice emphasizes securing the workpiece with clamps, keeping hands clear of the blade, and wearing eye protection—principles covered under [[concepts/woodworking-safety]].

## Typical Use Cases
- Rough cutting of board lengths before planning joinery.
- Trimming excess material after dimensions are laid out with a [[entities/pencil]] and checked with a [[entities/carpenter-square]].
- Preparing pine pieces for assembling the tabletop, legs, and aprons of a simple side table.

## Relationship to Other Tools & Concepts
- Complements the [[entities/drill-driver]] for creating pilot holes and driving screws.
- Works alongside [[entities/quick-grip-clamp]] to hold workpieces steady during cuts.
- Part of the broader set of [[concepts/beginner-woodworking]] practices that emphasize low‑cost, low‑skill entry points.
