# Tape Measure

A **tape measure** is a flexible retractable measuring device used to determine lengths and distances, crucial for precision in cutting, fitting, and layout tasks.

## Role in Session 03 (2023‑11‑13)
In the *session‑03_2023‑11‑13* conversation, the tape measure is listed as one of the core **[[concepts/basic-tools]]** a beginner woodworker should acquire first. It is highlighted alongside a handsaw, drill‑driver, clamps, sandpaper, and a pencil for measuring, marking, and ensuring parts are cut to the correct dimensions.

## Key Facts from the Document
- Recommended as part of the essential starter toolkit for **[[concepts/beginner-woodworking]]**.
- Used to measure board lengths for the side table project (e.g., 16‑inch tabletop, 20‑inch legs).
- Emphasized in the safety discussion: accurate measurement reduces waste and the need for re‑cutting, supporting **[[concepts/woodworking-safety]]** best practices.
