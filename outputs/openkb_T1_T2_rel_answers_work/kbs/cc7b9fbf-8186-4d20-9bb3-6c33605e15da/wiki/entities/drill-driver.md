# Drill/Driver

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** A versatile power tool for drilling holes and driving screws, commonly used in beginner woodworking projects.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-03_2023-11-13]]
- Listed among the essential [[concepts/basic-tools]] for a first woodworking toolkit.
- Recommended for tasks such as driving the 1¼" and 1" wood screws that join aprons to legs and attach the tabletop.
- Should be used with appropriate drill bits and the user should follow basic [[concepts/woodworking-safety]] practices (e.g., keep hands clear, let the tool stop before setting down).
- Works together with other starter tools like [[entities/tape-measure]], [[entities/handsaw]], [[entities/quick-grip-clamp]], and [[entities/sandpaper]].
