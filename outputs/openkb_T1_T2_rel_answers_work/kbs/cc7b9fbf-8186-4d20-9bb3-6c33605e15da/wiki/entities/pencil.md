# Pencil (Marking Tool)

A standard wooden **pencil** is a fundamental *basic tool* for any beginner woodworker. It provides a quick, precise way to mark cut lines, drill points, and layout dimensions on lumber.

## Role in the session‑03 guide
- Listed among the essential tools for starting a small side‑table project.  
- Recommended alongside a tape measure, handsaw, drill‑driver, and quick‑grip clamp to allow accurate layout before cutting or drilling.
- Emphasized in the safety discussion: use the pencil to *mark* where to cut rather than guessing, reducing mistakes and material waste.

## Recommended Use
1. **Mark cutting lines** on pine boards before using the handsaw.
2. **Indicate drill locations** on legs and aprons to ensure proper screw placement.
3. **Check dimensions** with a tape measure, then annotate directly on the workpiece.
