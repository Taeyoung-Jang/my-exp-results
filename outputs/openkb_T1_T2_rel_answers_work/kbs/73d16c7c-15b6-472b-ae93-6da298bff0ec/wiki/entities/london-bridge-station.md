# London Bridge Station

London Bridge Station is a major transport interchange in the City of London, serving National Rail, the London Underground (Northern and Jubilee lines) and river services. It sits on the south bank of the River Thames, close to several popular tourist destinations.

## Key facts from session-08_2023-01-15
- **Location in itinerary**: Used as the arrival point after the British Museum via the Underground (Holborn → Bank → London Bridge) and the departure point for travelling to the Tower of London (Jubilee line to Tower Hill) or walking to the South Bank.
- **Proximity to attractions**: Within a short walk of [[entities/borough-market]], [[entities/tower-of-london]], and the [[entities/south-bank]] promenade, making it a convenient hub for a day‑out that requires frequent sit‑breaks.
- **Accessibility relevance**: The station provides step‑free access to platforms and has seating areas and information desks, supporting the [[concepts/accessibility-queue-management]] advice for travelers who need to rest frequently.
