# South Bank

**Type:** Place  
**Location:** Central London, along the River Thames between Blackfriars Bridge and Tower Bridge.

South Bank is a popular cultural and entertainment district known for its riverside promenade, museums, theatres, and numerous public seating areas. It features attractions such as the **Tate Modern**, the **Royal Festival Hall**, and a variety of cafés and open‑air spaces that make it a natural fit for itineraries that require regular sit‑breaks.

### Relevance to Session 08 (2023‑01‑15)

The itinerary suggested in [[summaries/session-08_2023-01-15]] uses South Bank as the optional “must‑see” segment after visiting the British Museum and Borough Market. The route emphasizes:

- **Seat‑break planning** – the promenade offers benches every few minutes, and indoor venues like the Tate Modern foyer and Royal Festival Hall provide reliable rest points. [[concepts/seat-break-planning]]
- **Comfort packing** – lightweight walking shoes and a small portable seat cushion are advised for comfort while strolling the river path. [[concepts/comfort-packing]]
- **Accessibility & queue management** – because the South Bank has dispersed attractions, long standing queues are rare, but visitors are advised to check venue websites for any accessibility provisions. [[concepts/accessibility-queue-management]]
