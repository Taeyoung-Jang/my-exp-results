# British Museum

The **British Museum** is a major public museum in **[[entities/london]]**, renowned for its vast collections of world art and antiquities. In the context of *session‑08_2023‑01‑15*, it is highlighted as an ideal morning venue for a day‑out that requires frequent sit‑breaks.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Opening time** used in the itinerary: 10:00 am, allowing a short entry queue when arriving early.
- **Seating options**: Great Court café, scattered benches in the Great Court, and lobby seating provide convenient rest points for visitors who need to sit every 45–60 minutes. This supports the [[concepts/seat-break-planning]] strategy.
- **Accessibility**: The museum offers timed‑entry tickets and staff assistance for visitors with limited‑mobility, aligning with [[concepts/accessibility-queue-management]].
- **Suggested packing** for a comfortable visit includes comfortable shoes, layered clothing, a small bag with water and snacks, and optionally a portable seat cushion – all part of the [[concepts/comfort-packing]] recommendations.
