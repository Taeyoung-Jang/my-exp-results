# Holborn Station

Holborn Station is an underground railway station in **London** serving the Central line. In the itinerary from [[summaries/session-08_2023-01-15]], it is the departure point after visiting the [[entities/british-museum]] and the connection hub to reach **Borough Market** via the Northern line at London Bridge.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Location**: Central London, within the Holborn district.
- **Lines**: Primarily the Central line; used in the plan to travel one stop to **Bank** (via [[entities/bank-station]]) and then switch to the Northern line.
- **Role in Itinerary**: Acts as the transfer station after the museum visit, enabling a short journey to [[entities/london-bridge-station]] for the market stop.
- **Accessibility**: While not detailed in the session, the station is relevant to [[concepts/accessibility-queue-management]] and [[concepts/seat-break-planning]] because it provides a brief sit‑break opportunity while waiting for a train.
- **Nearby Amenities**: The station’s concourse offers seating and retail, useful for the traveler’s need to pause every 45–60 minutes as advised in [[concepts/seat-break-planning]].
