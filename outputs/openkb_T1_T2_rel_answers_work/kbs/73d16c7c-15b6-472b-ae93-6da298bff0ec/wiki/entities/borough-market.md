# Borough Market

**Type:** Place (food market)

**Location:** Southwark, London, adjacent to [[entities/london-bridge-station]] and the [[entities/south-bank]] area.

**Overview**
Borough Market is a renowned indoor and outdoor market offering a wide range of fresh produce, street food, and specialty stalls. It is a popular destination for locals and tourists alike.
