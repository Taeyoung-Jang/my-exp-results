## Comfort Packing

**Definition**: A set of lightweight, practical items chosen to keep the body comfortable, hydrated, and supported during extended periods of standing or walking.

### Core Elements
- **Footwear** – Prioritise well‑broken‑in, supportive shoes that can be worn for several hours.
- **Layered Clothing** – Light layers allow quick adjustment between cool indoor venues (e.g., museums) and warmer outdoor areas (e.g., markets).
- **Mini‑Bag Essentials**
  - Refillable water bottle
  - Small snack (energy bar, fruit)
  - Lip balm and sunscreen
  - Compact umbrella or foldable tote for sudden weather changes
- **Comfort Aids**
  - Portable seat cushion for hard benches
  - Topical pain‑relief stick or heat patch for arthritis or joint discomfort

### Why It Matters
Packing only what you truly need reduces fatigue from carrying weight and ensures quick access to hydration and pain‑relief, which are crucial when you need to sit every 45‑60 minutes.
