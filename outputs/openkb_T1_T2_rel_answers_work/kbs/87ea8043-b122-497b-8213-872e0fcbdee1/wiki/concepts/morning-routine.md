# Morning Routine

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A set of simple, repeatable habits designed to smooth the transition from sleep to activity on dark, cold mornings, especially for newcomers to a city like Toronto.

---

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-13]])

1. **Light & Hydration**
   - Turn on a warm, soft light before getting out of bed to counter seasonal darkness.
   - Drink a glass of water immediately after waking to re‑hydrate and signal the brain that the day has started.

2. **Pre‑set Outfit & Coffee Gear**
   - Lay out clothes and coffee equipment the night before. This removes decision‑fatigue and creates momentum.
   - Include a reusable mug and coffee beans/grounds ready to brew.

3. **Brief Outdoor Exposure**
   - Step outside for at least one minute, even if it is still dark. The cold air kick‑starts circulation and reinforces the wake‑up cue.

4. **Micro‑Rituals**
   - Pair the walk with a short, enjoyable activity: a 2‑minute journal entry, a favorite podcast snippet, or a simple stretch routine.
   - Keep the ritual under five minutes so it stays low‑effort.

5. **Consistent Timing**
   - Use a fixed wake‑up window (e.g., 7:00‑7:10 am) and aim to be home from the brief walk by 7:45 am. The schedule from the 7‑day plan provides concrete time‑boxes that reinforce the habit.

## Benefits
- Reduces the psychological “brutality” of pre‑dawn wake‑ups.
- Automates decision‑making, freeing mental bandwidth for work.
- Improves circulation and alertness through cold‑air exposure.
- Establishes a positive, predictable start to the day, which supports longer‑term habit formation.
