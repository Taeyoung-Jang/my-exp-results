# Bike Share Toronto

Bike Share Toronto is the city‑run dock‑based bicycle sharing service that lets residents and visitors rent a bike for short trips and return it to any station across the Greater Toronto Area.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-13]]
- Recommended as an optional transport mode in the **7‑day morning exploration** plan for the Annex/Koreatown area, especially when a quick hop between two points is needed.
- Mentioned alongside other essential Toronto tools such as the [[entities/transit-app]] and the [[entities/weather-network]] for planning routes that consider winter conditions.
- Suggested as part of [[concepts/winter-preparation]]: cyclists should equip themselves with proper winter gear (water‑proof gloves, layered clothing) and be aware of icy bike lanes.
- Can support the habit of a brief outdoor exposure in the morning, tying into [[concepts/morning-routine]] and [[concepts/neighbourhood-exploration]] by allowing a swift, low‑effort way to explore new streets.

## Related Entities & Concepts
- **Location**: Primarily used in the [[entities/annex-koreatown]] neighbourhood and throughout [[entities/toronto]].
- **Complementary Services**: Works well with the [[entities/presto]] card for seamless payment across transit and bike‑share.
- **Nearby Points of Interest**: Users often combine a ride with a stop at cafés like [[entities/rustle-still]] or [[entities/hailed-coffee]] during their morning loops.

## Practical Tips
- Download the official *Bike Share Toronto* app (available on iOS and Android) to locate stations, check bike availability, and unlock bikes.
- During winter, check the [[entities/weather-network]] for ice alerts and allow extra time for docking/undocking on slippery platforms.
- Keep a small repair kit or extra tube in your bag for unexpected flats, especially on early morning rides.
