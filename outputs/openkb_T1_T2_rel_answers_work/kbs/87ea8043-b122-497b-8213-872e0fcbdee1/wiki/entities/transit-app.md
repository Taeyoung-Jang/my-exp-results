# Transit (app)

**Type:** product

**Description:** A mobile application that offers live TTC (Toronto Transit Commission) scheduling, route planning, and service alerts, helping users navigate public transit efficiently.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-13]]
- Recommended as a primary tool for **real‑time TTC updates** during the 7‑day morning exploration plan.
- Paired with the official TTC app and Rocketman for redundancy, especially on icy or delayed days.
- Used to check **transit delays** that affect winter commutes, tying into [[concepts/winter-preparation]].
- Supports quick route look‑ups for the *Annex/Koreatown* neighbourhood, aiding the [[concepts/neighbourhood-exploration]] schedule.
- Works alongside [[entities/presto]] for seamless fare payment.
- Complementary to other Toronto mobility tools such as [[entities/bike-share-toronto]] for bike‑share journeys.

## Why it matters for a new Toronto resident
- Enables **time‑boxed morning walks** to stay within a 30–60 minute window by providing accurate arrival times for streetcars and subways.
- Reduces uncertainty on dark winter mornings, supporting the habit of **checking the day's service** as part of a reliable [[concepts/morning-routine]].
- Alerts users to **sidewalk ice** and service disruptions, allowing quick switches to alternate indoor or short‑loop options.
