# University of Toronto

**Type:** organization

**Brief:** Canada’s largest research university, located near the Annex/Koreatown neighbourhood.

## Overview
The University of Toronto (U of T) is a world‑ranked public research university in Toronto, Ontario. Its downtown St. George campus borders the Annex and Koreatown districts, making it a common waypoint for residents exploring the neighbourhood.
