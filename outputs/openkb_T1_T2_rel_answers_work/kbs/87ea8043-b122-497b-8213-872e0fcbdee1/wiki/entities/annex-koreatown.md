# Annex/Koreatown (Toronto)

**Type:** Place

## Overview
- Location: Central Toronto, bounded roughly by Bloor St (south), Bathurst St (west), Dupont St (north), and Spadina Ave (east). Known for leafy residential streets, university proximity, and a concentration of Korean restaurants and shops.

## Key Features for New Residents
- **Transit:** Well‑served by TTC streetcars (Bloor‑Danforth line) and bus routes; PRESTO card required.
- **Cycling:** Numerous Bike Share Toronto docks nearby.
- **Food & Culture:** Koreatown strip along Bloor‑Wellesley offers early‑opening bakeries and restaurants.
- **Green Space:** Christie Pits provides a popular early‑morning walking and skating loop.
- **University Proximity:** Easy walk to the University of Toronto campus, useful for quiet streets and study spots.
