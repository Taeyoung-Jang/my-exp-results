## Summary

The assistant outlines a simple system for coordinating meetings across multiple time zones using the free tool **When2Meet**. Key recommendations include:

- **Reference time zone**: Choose a home zone (e.g., your own) and set it as the grid’s base.
- **When2Meet workflow**: Create a grid, share the link, let participants mark availability, then pick the darkest overlapping slot.
- **Typical availability window**: For small groups, a 9 am – 9 pm window in the reference zone works well; for a user on Eastern European Time (EET) who wants to avoid late nights, a 10 am – 9 pm EET window is suggested.
- **Cross‑zone translation**: The same window maps to 8 am – 7 pm UK time and 3 am – 2 pm US Eastern time, allowing the overlap to fall in the late afternoon/evening for the EET user.
- **Soft deadline**: Ask participants to fill the grid by a specific time (e.g., “by tomorrow evening”) to avoid waiting for last‑minute entries.
- **Handling late responders**: Nudge once or proceed with the best available slot and ask the late responder if they can join, adjusting only if necessary.

These tips give a low‑effort, no‑account method for finding mutually convenient meeting times without manual time‑zone calculations.
