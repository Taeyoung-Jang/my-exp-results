# Study Routine

**Definition**: A structured, repeatable 90‑minute nightly study flow that integrates spaced review, focused work blocks, active recall, and a consistent note‑taking format.

## Core Components (derived from [[summaries/session-10_2024-10-13]])
1. **Spaced Review (0–10 min)** – Quick recall of material from the previous day, two days ago, four days ago, and a week ago. This anchors older knowledge without extending the session.
2. **Focused Work Blocks** – Two 30‑minute periods (10–40 min and 45–75 min) of active engagement with new content (video or textbook). Short 5‑minute break between them.
3. **Active Recall (75–85 min)** – Close the material and test memory of the day's key points, then verify against notes.
4. **Session Closure (85–90 min)** – Note unresolved questions and plan the next lesson.
5. **Adaptation for Low Energy** – When exhausted, shrink to a 10‑minute review, a single 20‑minute focus block, and a brief 2‑minute recall.

## Note‑Taking Template
A reusable format that supports the routine:
```
Topic:
Key ideas (short sentences):
Examples or details I might need later:
Questions to resolve:
Quick summary in two or three lines:
```
See [[concepts/note-taking-template]] for the template details.

## Spaced Review Mechanics
The 0–10 minute slot follows a simple schedule (Day 1, Day 2, Day 4, Day 7). Tracking can be done with a notebook log or index cards – a low‑tech approach described in the source. For more on the concept, see [[concepts/spaced-review]].

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/note-taking-template]]** – Provides the consistent note format used during the focused blocks.
- **[[concepts/spaced-review]]** – Supplies the timing strategy for the initial review segment.
- The routine itself is the central hub that connects these concepts into a cohesive study habit.

## Benefits
- **Predictability** – Fixed timestamps reduce decision fatigue.
- **Flexibility** – Works for both video lessons and textbook chapters.
- **Efficiency** – Light note‑taking and brief recall maximize retention in limited time.
- **Sustainability** – Scalable down on low‑energy nights to maintain habit continuity.
