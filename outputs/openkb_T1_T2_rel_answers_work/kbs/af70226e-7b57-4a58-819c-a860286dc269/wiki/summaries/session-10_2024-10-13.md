# Summary of session-10_2024-10-13

**Main contribution**: A practical, adaptable 90‑minute nightly study routine that integrates [[concepts/spaced-review]], a reusable [[concepts/note-taking-template]], and low‑tech tracking methods.

## Core Routine (90 min)
1. **0–10 min – Spaced Review**  
   - Quick recall of material from 1 day, 2 days, 4 days, and 7 days ago.  
   - Uses a notebook log or index‑card system (see low‑tech tracking).
2. **10–40 min – First Focus Block**  
   - Active engagement with new content (video or textbook).  
   - Apply the note‑taking template.
3. **40–45 min – Short Break**  
   - Physical reset (water, stretch).
4. **45–75 min – Second Focus Block**  
   - Continue new material, maintain light, in‑own‑words notes.
5. **75–85 min – End‑of‑Session Recall**  
   - Close material, test recall, compare with notes.
6. **85–90 min – Closure**  
   - Note unresolved questions and plan next session.

## Adaptations for Exhaustion
- **Reduced session**: 0–10 min review, 10–30 min single focus block, 30–32 min quick recall, then stop.  
- Keeps the habit alive while respecting energy limits.

## Note‑Taking Template
```
Topic:
Key ideas (short sentences):
Examples or details I might need later:
Questions to resolve:
Quick summary in two or three lines:
```
- Use during both video and textbook work.  
- For videos, pause only for key ideas or questions; for text, skim headings then summarize chunks.

## Spaced Review Menu (0–10 min)
| Day | Review Target |
|-----|---------------|
| 1   | Material from previous day |
| 2   | Material from 2 days ago |
| 4   | Material from 4 days ago |
| 7   | Material from 1 week ago |

## Low‑Tech Tracking (no app needed)
- **Notebook Log**: One line per lesson with four future dates; check off after each review.  
- **Index Cards**: Front = lesson title; back = four review dates; pull cards matching today’s date.

## Underlying Concepts
- [[concepts/study-routine]] – Structured flow for efficient learning.  
- [[concepts/spaced-review]] – Timing of recall to strengthen memory.  
- [[concepts/note-taking-template]] – Consistent format for capturing key ideas.  
- Active recall – End‑of‑session testing without notes.  
- Pomodoro‑style work blocks – Short work periods with brief breaks.  
- Low‑tech tracking – Notebook or index‑card system for review scheduling.  
- Habit formation – Maintaining a daily study habit, even on low‑energy days.
