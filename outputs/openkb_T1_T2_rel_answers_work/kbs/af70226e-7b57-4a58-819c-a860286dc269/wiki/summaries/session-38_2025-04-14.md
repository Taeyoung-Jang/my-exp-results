# Summary of session-38_2025-04-14

## Overview
This document proposes a lightweight, 30‑minute nightly learning routine for Python data analysis (pandas + basic statistics). It maps a four‑week curriculum onto a repeatable Mon‑Sun rhythm, suggests effortless progress‑tracking methods, and recommends the Titanic dataset for consistent practice.

## Routine Structure
- **10 min** – Review the previous day’s concept.
- **15 min** – Hands‑on exercise (pandas or stats).
- **5 min** – Quick reflection / log entry.

The routine emphasizes continuity over task completion, aligning with the learning habit and 30 min routine ideas.

## Progress Tracking
Three low‑friction options are offered:
1. **Streak log** – mark attendance, not achievement.
2. **Wins note** – a one‑line “aha” entry per session.
3. **Script folder** – accumulate practice scripts for visual growth.
These support progress tracking without feeling like homework.

## Four‑Week Curriculum
| Week | Focus | Sample Topics |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **1** | Loading & inspection | `read_csv`, `head`, `info`, basic selection, mean/median/std dev.
| **2** | Cleaning & shaping | Missing values (`dropna`, `fillna`), filtering, simple distributions.
| **3** | Grouping & summarizing | `groupby`, aggregations, correlations, comparing group means.
| **4** | End‑to‑end mini‑analyses | Full flow: load → clean → ask a question → compute stats.

Each week follows the same daily rhythm, detailed below.

## Mon‑Sun Pattern (Weekly Rhythm)
- **Monday** – Review previous material, tiny exercise.
- **Tuesday** – Introduce a new pandas or stats technique, apply once.
- **Wednesday** – Pure practice of earlier concepts (no notes).
- **Thursday** – Add a small twist/variation to the Tuesday technique.
- **Friday** – Connect the week’s stats idea to the pandas work.
- **Saturday** – Conduct a concise “mini‑analysis” using the Titanic data.
- **Sunday** – Quick reset: skim notes, set one goal for the next week.

This pattern is captured by weekly rhythm and can be overlaid on the four‑week outline, alternating pandas‑focused and stats‑focused days as shown in the original text.

## Dataset Recommendation & Mini‑Analyses
- **Dataset**: The **Titanic** CSV from Kaggle – small, clean, rich enough for many questions. titanic dataset
- **Six repeatable questions** for Saturday sessions:
  1. Survival rate by gender.
  2. Survival rate by passenger class.
  3. Average age of survivors vs. non‑survivors.
  4. Impact of family size (`SibSp`/`Parch`) on survival.
  5. Fare differences between survivors and non‑survivors.
  6. Survival rate by embarkation port.
These provide quick practice for mini analysis and reinforce both pandas operations and statistical interpretation.
