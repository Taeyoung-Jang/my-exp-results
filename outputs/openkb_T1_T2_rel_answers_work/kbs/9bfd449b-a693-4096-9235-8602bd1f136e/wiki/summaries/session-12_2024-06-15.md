# Summary of session-12_2024-06-15

**Main contribution:** A concrete two‑hour morning routine for focused work, including scripts for handling urgent messages and a lightweight triage checklist.

## Key Concepts
- **Protected morning window** – Reserve the first 1–2 hours of the day for deep work before checking email or chat. [[concepts/protected_focus_time]]
- **Warm‑up ritual** – Brief activity (e.g., coffee, stretch, plan review) to signal the brain that work is beginning. [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]]
- **Deep‑work blocks** – 40–60 minute stretches of uninterrupted work, separated by a short reset. [[concepts/deep_work]]
- **Message sweep** – A brief, timed check (e.g., at 10:45) to handle only true blockers. [[concepts/message_sweep]]
- **Urgent‑message scripts** – Pre‑written replies that set boundaries while offering quick triage. [[concepts/urgent_message_scripts]]
- **Lightweight triage checklist** – 5–7 step process to identify and respond to true blockers without falling into reactive mode. [[concepts/triage_checklist]]

## Concrete Two‑Hour Template (9:00–11:00)
1. **9:00‑9:05** – Warm‑up & review of the desired outcome (no inbox). 
2. **9:05‑9:50** – First deep‑work block. 
3. **9:50‑9:55** – Short reset (stand, water, breath). 
4. **9:55‑10:40** – Second deep‑work block. 
5. **10:40‑10:45** – Wrap‑up notes: what was completed, next steps. 
6. **10:45‑10:48** – Message sweep using the triage checklist.

## Scripts for “Urgent” Pings
- **General response:** “Thanks for the note. I’m heads‑down until 10:45, but I’ll look at this as soon as I come up for air.”
- **If claimed urgent:** “I’m in a focus block right now. If this needs action before 10:45, let me know the deadline or what’s at risk so I can prioritize it properly.”

## Triage Checklist (10:45 Sweep)
1. Limit the sweep to **2‑3 minutes** total. 
2. **Preview only** – glance at subject lines or first sentences; do not open full threads. 
3. Identify **true blockers**: keywords like *blocked, need your approval, waiting on you* or an imminent deadline. 
4. **Quick unblock** – if a single sentence resolves it, reply immediately. 
5. **Deferred unblock** – if more work is needed, send a brief note with a concrete time for a full response. 
6. **Defer** any message without blocker/deadline keywords. 
7. **Close** the inbox/chat as soon as the sweep ends to avoid drift.

## Practical Takeaways
- Structure the morning with a predictable ramp‑up, two deep‑work blocks, and a defined check‑in.
- Use pre‑written scripts to maintain boundaries while remaining helpful.
- Apply the triage checklist to catch genuine blockers without sacrificing focus.
- Treat any message that can wait **> 1 hour** as non‑urgent.
