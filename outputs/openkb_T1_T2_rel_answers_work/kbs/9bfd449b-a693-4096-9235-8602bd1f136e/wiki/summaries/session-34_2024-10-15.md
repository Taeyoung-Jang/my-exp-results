# Summary of session-34_2024-10-15

**Topic:** Managing a morning schedule when daily standups span three time zones.

## Core Recommendations
- **Create a fixed "meeting window"** that clusters all real‑time communication early (e.g., 8:00–9:30). This prevents scattered calls from eroding focus throughout the morning. [[concepts/meeting_window]]
- **Pre‑standup warm‑up ritual (10 min):** At 7:50, review the day’s top priority, define success, and avoid opening email/Slack. Provides mental clarity before the meetings. [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]]
- **Post‑standup reset (brief):** After the last standup, step away for a short break (stretch, hydrate) to create a clean boundary before deep work. [[concepts/reset_break]]
- **Protected deep‑work block (90 min):** Schedule from ~9:40 to 11:10 (or 9:55‑11:25 on an overrun day). Treat it as an immutable appointment; silence notifications. [[concepts/deep_work]]
- **Batch communication checks:** One focused email/Slack sweep after deep work (11:10‑11:30) and a brief triage before lunch on overrun days. Reduces context‑switching. [[concepts/message_sweep]] [[concepts/triage_checklist]]

## Sample Hour‑by‑Hour Schedule (standard day)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 7:50‑8:00 | Warm‑up ritual |
| 8:00‑9:30 | Three standups (across time zones) |
| 9:30‑9:40 | Quick reset break |
| 9:40‑11:10 | Deep‑work block |
| 11:10‑11:30 | Email/Slack sweep |
| 11:30‑12:00 | Lunch prep / transition |

## Adjusted Schedule for Standup Overrun (ends 9:50)
- **Reset:** 2‑minute breather (9:50‑9:52).
- **Deep‑work:** 9:55‑11:25 (full 90 min). [[concepts/overrun_adjustment]]
- **Communication triage:** 11:25‑11:35 (5‑10 min). [[concepts/triage_checklist]]
