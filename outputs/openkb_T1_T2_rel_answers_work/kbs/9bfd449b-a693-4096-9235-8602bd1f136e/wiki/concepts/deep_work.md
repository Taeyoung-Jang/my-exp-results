# Deep Work

**Definition:** Deep Work is a sustained period of focused, distraction‑free effort that pushes cognitive limits and produces high‑value output.

**Why it matters in the morning:** According to [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]], structuring the first 1–2 hours of the day around Deep Work helps build momentum before meetings and messages intrude. It pairs with a [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]] and a brief reset to keep energy high.

**Typical block length:** The session suggests 40–60 minutes per block, which is long enough to gain flow but short enough to avoid fatigue.

**Integration with other concepts**

- **Protected focus time** – The entire two‑hour window is treated as [[concepts/protected_focus_time]], during which notifications are silenced.
- **Message sweep** – After the blocks, a timed [[concepts/message_sweep]] (e.g., at 10:45) reviews only true blockers.
- **Triage checklist** – The sweep follows a concise [[concepts/triage_checklist]] to prevent slipping back into reactive mode.
- **Urgent‑message scripts** – Pre‑written replies from [[concepts/urgent_message_scripts]] reinforce boundaries while remaining responsive.

**Practical template (from the source):**
1. 9:00‑9:05 – Warm‑up ritual and outcome review.
2. 9:05‑9:50 – First Deep Work block.
3. 9:50‑9:55 – Short reset.
4. 9:55‑10:40 – Second Deep Work block.
5. 10:40‑10:45 – Wrap‑up notes.
6. 10:45‑10:48 – Message sweep using the triage checklist.

**Benefits:** Improves focus, accelerates progress on meaningful tasks, and creates a predictable rhythm that reduces decision fatigue throughout the day.
