# Protected Focus Time

**Definition**: A deliberately blocked segment at the start of the workday that is kept free from meetings, email, and other distractions to enable sustained concentration on high‑impact tasks.

## Why It Matters
- Allows the brain to enter a state of deep concentration before reactive demands accumulate.
- Sets a positive momentum for the rest of the day, reducing decision fatigue.
- Supports the habit of tackling the most important outcome first.

## Practical Implementation (from [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]])
1. **Schedule the window** – e.g., 9:00 – 11:00, marked on the calendar as *Protected Focus Time*.
2. **Warm‑up ritual** – begin with a brief activity (coffee, stretch, quick plan review) to signal work mode. See [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]].
3. **Deep‑work blocks** – split the window into two focused periods (e.g., 9:05‑9:50 and 9:55‑10:40) of 40–60 minutes each. Linked concept: [[concepts/deep_work]].
4. **Mid‑session reset** – a five‑minute physical break (stand, hydrate) to maintain energy.
5. **Message sweep** – at the end of the window (e.g., 10:45) perform a short, structured inbox check using a triage process. See [[concepts/message_sweep]] and [[concepts/triage_checklist]].
6. **Urgent‑message scripts** – pre‑written replies to set boundaries while staying responsive. Refer to [[concepts/urgent_message_scripts]].

## Benefits
- Guarantees a protected period for the day's most critical work.
- Reduces multitasking and context‑switch costs.
- Provides a predictable rhythm that teammates can respect.
