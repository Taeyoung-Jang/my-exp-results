# Message Sweep

**Definition**: A short, scheduled review of email and chat notifications designed to capture genuine blockers while preserving a protected focus period.

## Core Elements
- **Timing**: Typically placed immediately after a deep‑work window. In a standard day (standups 8:00‑9:30) this means around **11:10 – 11:30**. On days when a standup overruns (ends 9:50) the sweep is compressed to a brief **11:25 – 11:35** triage.
- **Duration**: Limited to 2–3 minutes on tightly packed schedules, but can extend to up to 20 minutes when the deep‑work block ends earlier and a fuller review is practical.
- **Scope**: Scan only message previews/subject lines; avoid opening full threads unless a blocker is obvious.
- **Criteria**: Look for keywords such as *blocked*, *need your approval*, *waiting on you*, or an explicit near‑term deadline. These indicate a **true blocker**.
- **Action Rules**:
  1. If a true blocker can be resolved with a one‑sentence reply, respond immediately.
  2. If more work is required, send a brief note stating when a full answer will be delivered.
  3. If no blocker or deadline is apparent, defer the message to a later time.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of a broader **[[concepts/protected_focus_time]]** strategy that shields the morning for deep work.
- Works hand‑in‑hand with a **[[concepts/warm_up_ritual]]** to transition smoothly into focus.
- Implements the **[[concepts/triage_checklist]]** as the operational procedure during the sweep.
- May be paired with **[[concepts/urgent_message_scripts]]** for quick, pre‑written replies.
- Coordinates with the **[[concepts/reset_break]]** that follows the standup window, ensuring a clean mental boundary before the sweep.
