## Meeting Window

**Definition**: A *meeting window* is a designated early‑morning time span during which all real‑time, cross‑time‑zone communications (e.g., standups) are grouped together. By concentrating meetings in one predictable block, you minimize scattered interruptions and preserve longer periods of uninterrupted work later in the day.

**Key Elements** (as illustrated in [[summaries/session-34_2024-10-15]]):
- **Timing**: Typically scheduled as soon as the day begins; in the example it runs from 08:00 – 09:30 (or later on an overrun day).
- **Pre‑meeting warm‑up**: A short [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]] (10 min) at 07:50 to review the day’s top priority and set clear intent before entering the window.
- **Post‑meeting reset**: A brief [[concepts/reset_break]] (5‑10 min) immediately after the last meeting to create a mental boundary before deep work.
- **Protected focus time**: The window’s end is followed by a [[concepts/protected_focus_time]] block (90 min of deep work) where notifications are silenced.
- **Message handling**: Email/Slack are deliberately batched after deep work, aligning with the [[concepts/message_sweep]] practice. On days with a meeting overrun, a quick [[concepts/triage_checklist]] replaces the longer sweep.
- **Overrun adjustment**: If a meeting exceeds the scheduled window (e.g., ends at 09:50), the schedule compresses the reset and message sweep but keeps the deep‑work block intact, as described in [[concepts/overrun_adjustment]].

**Why It Works**
- Consolidates unavoidable real‑time interaction into a single, predictable slot, reducing context‑switching.
- Frees up the majority of the morning for focused execution, supporting concepts like [[concepts/deep_work]] and overall productivity.
- Enables clearer communication norms: teammates know when you are reachable for live discussion versus when you are in deep‑work mode.

**Implementation Checklist**
1. Identify the earliest time that overlaps with all required time zones.
2. Block that period in your calendar as a *meeting window*.
3. Add a preceding [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]].
4. Schedule a short [[concepts/reset_break]] right after the window.
5. Follow with a [[concepts/protected_focus_time]] deep‑work block.
6. Plan a [[concepts/message_sweep]] later in the morning; compress if needed using [[concepts/overrun_adjustment]] and a quick [[concepts/triage_checklist]].
