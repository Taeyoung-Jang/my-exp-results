# Triage Checklist

**Definition**: A lightweight, 5–7 step process used during a short, scheduled message sweep to spot genuine blockers and decide whether to respond immediately or defer.

---

## Purpose
The checklist helps maintain the integrity of a protected morning work block by preventing the inbox or chat from pulling you into reactive mode while still ensuring that critical dependencies are cleared.

---

## Steps (as outlined in [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]])
1. **Set a time limit** – spend no more than **2–3 minutes** on the entire sweep.
2. **Preview only** – glance at subject lines or the first sentence of each message; do not open full threads unless a blocker is suspected. 
3. **Identify true blockers** – look for keywords such as *blocked*, *need your approval*, *waiting on you*, or any mention of an imminent deadline. 
4. **Quick unblock** – if a single sentence or brief action can resolve the blocker, reply immediately.
5. **Deferred unblock** – if resolution requires more work, send a short note stating a concrete time you’ll provide a full answer.
6. **Defer non‑blockers** – any message without blocker or deadline cues is postponed for later handling.
7. **Close out** – terminate the inbox/chat session the moment the sweep ends to avoid drift into full‑inbox mode.

## Practical Tips
- Keep the checklist visible (e.g., sticky note, digital reminder) during the sweep.
- Use a timer to enforce the 2‑3 minute limit.
- Regularly review the keywords that signify a true blocker and adjust them to your role’s specific language.
