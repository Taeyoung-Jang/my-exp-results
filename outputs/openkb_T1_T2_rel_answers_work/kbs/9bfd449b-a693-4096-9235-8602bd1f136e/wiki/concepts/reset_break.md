# Reset Break

**Definition**: A short, intentional pause taken immediately after a meeting block to clear the mind, reset focus, and prepare for a protected deep‑work period.

## Key Elements
- **Duration**: Typically 5–10 minutes, but can be as brief as 2 minutes on days when meetings overrun.
- **Activities**: Light physical movement (stretch, walk), hydration, deep breathing, or a quick change of environment. The goal is to create a clear mental boundary without deep distraction.
- **Purpose**: Prevents the cognitive residue of back‑to‑back standups from spilling into the subsequent focus block, thereby preserving the quality of the [[concepts/deep_work]] session.

## Application in Session‑34 (2024‑10‑15)
- After the standup window (8:00‑9:30), a **reset break** from **9:30‑9:40** is recommended.
- On days when a standup overruns to 9:50, the reset is compressed to a **2‑minute breather** (9:50‑9:52) before sliding directly into the deep‑work block.
- The reset is positioned **before** the [[concepts/protected_focus_time]] of 90 minutes, ensuring the focus period starts with a fresh mental state.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works in tandem with [[concepts/warm_up_ritual]] (pre‑meeting priming) and [[concepts/message_sweep]] (post‑focus communication batching).
- Supports the overall **meeting window** strategy [[concepts/meeting_window]] by isolating meeting time from deep work.
- Can be adapted as part of an **overrun adjustment** [[concepts/overrun_adjustment]] when meeting schedules shift.

## Practical Tips
1. **Set a timer**: Keep the break time bounded.
2. **Physical cue**: Stand up, stretch, or walk to the kitchen for water.
3. **Mental cue**: Take three deep breaths or practice a one‑sentence mantra about focus.
4. **Avoid screens**: Keep phones and computers closed to maintain the boundary.
