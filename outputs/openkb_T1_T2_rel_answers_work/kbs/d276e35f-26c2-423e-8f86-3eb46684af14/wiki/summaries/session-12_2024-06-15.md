## Summary of Session‑12 Vegetarian Gluten‑Free Menu

**Document type:** Summary  

### Core Offering
A complete, printable plan for a **vegetarian, naturally gluten‑free menu** serving 6–8 guests. It includes:
- Exact dish names and **quantities** for all ingredients (vegetables, cheese, lentils, chocolate, etc.)
- A **shopping list** grouped by supermarket aisles
- A **make‑ahead timeline** (tasks 2 days ahead, 1 day ahead, day‑of) linked to [[concepts/make-ahead-planning]]
- Specific **cross‑contamination notes** for cheese/fruit boards, dips, and shared ovens/grills linked to [[concepts/cross-contamination-prevention]]
- Safe **serving/labeling guidelines** for the cheese board and dip stations

### Menu Components
1. **Main:** Roasted Vegetable & [[entities/halloumi]] Tray Bake  
2. **Sides:** Warm Lentil & Herb Salad (using [[entities/french-green-lentils]]); Roasted Carrots with Honey‑Cumin Glaze  
3. **Apps:** Caprese Skewers; Cheese & Fruit Board with [[entities/gluten-free-crackers]]; Whipped Feta Dip  
4. **Dessert:** [[entities/flourless-chocolate-cake]]

### Key Concepts & Wikilinks
- [[concepts/gluten-free-cooking]] – strategies for naturally gluten‑free meals without substitute pastas or breads.  
- [[concepts/cross-contamination-prevention]] – dedicated utensils, separate toasters, wiped surfaces, and isolated cracker bowls.  
- [[concepts/make-ahead-planning]] – staging tasks 2 days ahead (bake cake, cook lentils), 1 day ahead (roast veg, prep apps), day‑of (reheat, assemble).  
- Vegetarian party menu – balanced courses that feel “party‑worthy” while staying plant‑based.  
- Shopping list organization – aisle‑grouped list for efficient grocery trips.  
- Prep timeline – visual schedule to keep workload manageable and ensure food safety.  
- Cheese board safety – separating gluten‑free crackers, using individual cheese knives, and clear labeling.

### Practical Takeaways
- **Quantities** are calibrated for 6–8 people (e.g., 4 zucchini, 2‑3 lb carrots, 1 ½ cup uncooked lentils, 8 oz chocolate).  
- **Cross‑contamination risks** highlighted for shared ovens/grills, cheese boards, and dip containers, with mitigation steps per [[concepts/cross-contamination-prevention]].  
- **Label/serve** the cheese & fruit board with a simple note and dedicated utensils to keep gluten‑free items uncontaminated.  
- The **checklist** can be sent to hosts/guests to ensure a safe environment.
