# Halloumi

Halloumi is a semi‑firm, brined cheese originating from Cyprus, known for its high melting point that allows it to be grilled or pan‑fried without losing shape.  In the *session‑12* vegetarian menu it is used as the protein centerpiece in the **Roasted Vegetable and Halloumi Tray Bake**, with **14–18 oz (2–3 blocks)** sliced and browned at the end of roasting.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Quantity**: 2–3 blocks (approximately 14‑18 oz) for 6–8 servings.
- **Role**: Provides a satisfying, “party‑worthy” main in a naturally gluten‑free menu.
- **Preparation**: Added during the last few minutes of roasting so the surface caramelizes while the interior stays soft.
- **Cross‑contamination**: Must be cooked on a clean pan or tray separate from any gluten‑containing foods; use dedicated utensils and keep the serving platter isolated from shared crackers.
- **Storage**: Can be pre‑sliced and kept refrigerated; reheat briefly before serving.
