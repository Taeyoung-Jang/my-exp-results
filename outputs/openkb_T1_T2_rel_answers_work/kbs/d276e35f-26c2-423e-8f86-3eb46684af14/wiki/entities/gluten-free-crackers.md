# Gluten‑Free Crackers

**Type:** product

Gluten‑Free Crackers are packaged snack crackers made without wheat, barley, rye, or any other gluten‑containing grains. In the *session‑12_2024‑06‑15* menu they are featured on the **cheese & fruit board** as a safe accompaniment for guests with celiac disease.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Menu role:** Served alongside cheeses, fruits, nuts, and olives; kept in a separate bowl to prevent crumbs mixing with other foods.
- **Quantity:** Suggested amount is roughly one pound (or an equivalent pre‑packaged size) for a gathering of 6–8 people.
- **Cross‑contamination note:** Place crackers in their own container with dedicated tongs or spoons; avoid sharing plates with gluten‑containing items. See [[concepts/cross-contamination-prevention]] for detailed safety steps.
- **Preparation timeline:** Can be purchased ahead of time; no prep required beyond opening the package on the day of the event. This aligns with the [[concepts/make-ahead-planning]] approach used for the rest of the menu.
- **Dietary context:** Naturally gluten‑free, requiring no flour substitutes; suitable for both vegetarian and gluten‑free diets. Refer to [[concepts/gluten-free-cooking]] for broader guidance.
