# Flourless Chocolate Cake

A dense, rich dessert that is naturally gluten‑free because it contains no flour. It is made from chocolate, butter, sugar, eggs, and a small amount of cocoa powder, yielding a cake that feels indulgent and suitable for a party setting.

## Key Facts from Session‑12
- **Ingredients (per the suggested recipe):**
  - 8 oz dark chocolate
  - ½ cup butter
  - ¾ cup granulated sugar
  - 3 large eggs
  - 2 Tbsp cocoa powder
- **Serves:** 6‑8 people, fitting the vegetarian menu.
- **Preparation:** Can be baked 1‑2 days ahead and chilled overnight for optimal texture.
- **Role in the menu:** Acts as the dessert in the vegetarian, gluten‑free gathering, complementing the savory dishes.
