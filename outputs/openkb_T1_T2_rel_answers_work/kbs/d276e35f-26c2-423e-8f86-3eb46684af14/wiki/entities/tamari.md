# Tamari

Tamari is a **gluten‑free** soy sauce‑like condiment made from fermented soybeans. It is often used as a safe substitute for regular soy sauce in recipes that need to be [[concepts/gluten-free-cooking]] compliant.

## Key facts from **Session‑12**
- Recommended in the vegetarian menu as a **safe swap** for soy sauce to avoid hidden gluten.
- Mentioned alongside other gluten‑free swaps such as certified gluten‑free broths and mustards.
- Serves as a low‑risk ingredient for [[concepts/cross-contamination-prevention]] because it eliminates the need for wheat‑based sauces.
- Can be incorporated into make‑ahead dishes (e.g., dressings, marinades) without additional preparation steps, aligning with [[concepts/make-ahead-planning]].
