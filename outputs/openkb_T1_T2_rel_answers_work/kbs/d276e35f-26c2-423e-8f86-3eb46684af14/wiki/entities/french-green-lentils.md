# French Green Lentils

French green lentils are a variety of *Lentil* *culinaris* known for their peppery flavor and firm texture that holds up well in salads and warm dishes. They are naturally gluten‑free, making them a staple in [[concepts/gluten-free-cooking]] and a reliable protein source for vegetarian meals.

## Key facts from session‑12 (2024‑06‑15)
- **Quantity suggested:** 1½ cups uncooked (≈ 4 cups cooked) for a 6‑8‑person menu.
- **Dish usage:** Form the base of the *Warm Lentil and Herb Salad* side, mixed with olive oil, lemon juice, mustard, parsley, salt and pepper.
- **Preparation tips:** Rinse, simmer in water or gluten‑free broth for 15‑20 minutes until tender but still firm; drain and cool before dressing.
- **Nutritional notes:** High in protein, fiber, iron, and folate; low in fat.
