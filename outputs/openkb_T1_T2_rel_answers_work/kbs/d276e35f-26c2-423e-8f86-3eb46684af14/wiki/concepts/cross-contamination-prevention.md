# Cross‑Contamination Prevention

**Definition:** Strategies and practices to keep gluten‑containing ingredients from contacting gluten‑free foods, especially in shared kitchen environments.

## Why it matters
People with celiac disease rely on strict separation of gluten‑free items. The menu plan in [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]] highlights several high‑risk zones (toasters, shared frying oil, wooden cutting boards, and condiment jars). Even naturally gluten‑free foods can become unsafe if they touch crumbs or oil from wheat‑based dishes.

## Common high‑risk items
- **Toasters & air fryers** – often used for bread or bagels.
- **Shared frying oil** – oil that has cooked wheat‑floured foods can transfer gluten.
- **Cutting boards and knives** – especially wood that absorbs crumbs.
- **Condiment jars** – butter, jam, or sauces that are double‑dipped with a bread knife.
- **Cheese & fruit boards** – mixing gluten‑free crackers with other crackers or placing them on the same platter can spread crumbs.
- **Oven/grill racks** – crumbs from breaded items can linger.

## Practical prevention strategies
1. **Dedicated equipment** – keep a separate toaster, cutting board, knives, and oil for gluten‑free cooking.
2. **Label and separate** – use clearly marked containers for gluten‑free crackers ([[entities/gluten-free-crackers]]) and keep them in a separate bowl.
3. **Wipe surfaces** – clean counters, stovetops, and grill grates before preparing gluten‑free dishes.
4. **Use gluten‑free swaps** – replace soy sauce with tamari ([[entities/tamari]]) and verify all sauces are certified gluten‑free.
5. **Serve with individual utensils** – provide separate tongs, cheese knives, and dip spoons on a cheese board (e.g., halloumi [[entities/halloumi]]) .
6. **Designate a “gluten‑free zone”** on the prep area and communicate it to anyone else cooking.

## Checklist for hosts and guests
- [ ] Have dedicated utensils and cutting boards.
- [ ] Use separate oil for frying or omit frying altogether.
- [ ] Keep gluten‑free crackers in their own bowl; don’t place them on a shared board.
- [ ] Clean all surfaces before and after use.
- [ ] Verify label claims on all packaged items (spices, broths, sauces).
- [ ] Provide a short note or verbal reminder about using the provided utensils only.

## Connection to other concepts
