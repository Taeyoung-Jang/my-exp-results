# Intraocular Pressure Measurement

**Type:** other

Intraocular pressure (IOP) measurement is a routine test performed during eye examinations to assess the pressure inside the eye. Elevated IOP is a primary risk factor for glaucoma, a condition that can lead to irreversible vision loss if untreated.

## Relevance in Session 37 (2025‑06‑15)
The session on post‑surgical eye care recommends that a comprehensive yearly exam include an **intra‑ocular pressure test**. While the primary focus is on monitoring vision stability after refractive surgery, checking IOP helps ensure that any developing glaucoma is caught early.

## Key Points from the Document
- Listed as a standard component of the annual comprehensive exam after refractive surgery.
- No special request from the patient is needed; the clinician will typically measure IOP as part of the routine visit.
- Important for patients with a family history of glaucoma or other risk factors.
