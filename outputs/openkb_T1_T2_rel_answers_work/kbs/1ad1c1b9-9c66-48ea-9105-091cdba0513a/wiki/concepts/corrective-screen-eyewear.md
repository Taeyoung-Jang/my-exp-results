# Corrective Screen Eyewear

Corrective screen eyewear refers to any prescription lenses—single‑vision, progressive, or specialized computer glasses—designed to improve visual comfort while using digital displays. The concept emerged from practical advice in [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-15]], which highlighted how glasses affect monitor placement, font size, and overall ergonomics.

## Core Principles
- **Maintain standard ergonomics**: Even with glasses, the recommended monitor distance (about an arm’s length) and height (top at or slightly below eye level) still apply. Adjustments should ensure the screen sits within the clear zone of the lenses.
- **Progressive lenses**: Users often tilt their heads back because the screen falls outside the mid‑range portion of the lens. Lowering the monitor a few inches and positioning it slightly closer can align the screen with the optimal lens zone.
- **Computer‑specific prescriptions**: Single‑vision computer glasses provide a broader, dedicated viewing area for the typical screen distance, reducing the need for head tilt and minimizing fatigue compared to progressives.

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/eye-strain-mitigation]]**: Corrective eyewear is one tool among many (breaks, lighting, blinking) to alleviate eye strain during prolonged screen use.
- **[[concepts/monitor-ergonomics]]**: Proper monitor distance, height, and font scaling must be coordinated with the wearer’s prescription to keep visual effort low.

## Practical Recommendations
1. **Assess lens zone**: Verify that the screen falls within the part of the lenses that offers clear focus; adjust monitor height or distance accordingly.
2. **Consider dedicated computer glasses** if you experience persistent fatigue, especially when using progressive lenses.
3. **Combine with ergonomic habits**: Take regular breaks, use appropriate lighting, and maintain a comfortable blink rate to maximize comfort.

These guidelines help integrate corrective eyewear into a holistic approach to comfortable screen work.
