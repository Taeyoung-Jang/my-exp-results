# Low‑Pressure Touchpoints

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Small, easy interactions that sustain friendships without feeling like obligations.

---

## Overview
Low‑pressure touchpoints are brief, informal moments of contact that keep a friendship alive without demanding significant time, planning, or emotional effort. They are designed to be *optional* and *flexible*, allowing friends to stay connected even when schedules are packed.

## Key Characteristics
- **Minimal effort** – actions that take seconds to minutes (e.g., a voice note, a meme, a quick text).
- **Low expectation** – each touchpoint is presented as *if it works, great; if not, no problem*.
- **Consistency over intensity** – frequent small interactions are more valuable than occasional large gatherings.
- **Adaptable format** – can be one‑on‑one or group‑focused, asynchronous (text, voice) or synchronous (short call, walk‑and‑talk).

## Examples from [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-16]]
1. **Voice notes** while walking or at the end of the day.
2. **Sharing memes or photos** that remind you of the friend.
3. **“Thinking of you”** quick messages.
4. **Weekly update texts** – short snapshots of daily life.
5. **Ten‑minute calls** during cooking or commuting.
6. **Group chat check‑ins**, meme drops, or a "question of the day" thread.
7. **Brief group video hellos** or shared playlist updates.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Linked to [[concepts/consistency-vs-intensity]] because maintaining regular small contacts builds consistency, which is more sustainable than sporadic intense events.
- Supports [[concepts/flexible-routines]] by offering adaptable, low‑commitment ways to keep in touch that can fit into any routine.

## Practical Tips
- **Frame each interaction as optional**: "If you have a moment, here's a quick note – no pressure."
- **Use a default script** when plans fall through, e.g., *"Looks like this week’s not going to line up, but no worries – let’s do a quick check‑in instead."* (see source summary for wording).
- **Mix modalities**: combine voice, text, images, and short calls to keep the experience fresh.
- **Leverage group dynamics**: a light meme in a group chat can keep everyone engaged without extra coordination.
