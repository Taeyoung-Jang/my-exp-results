# Flexible Routines for Friendship Maintenance

Flexible routines are low‑pressure habits that let friends stay connected without rigid scheduling. They work by offering optional, adaptable touchpoints that fit into varied calendars, turning friendship maintenance into a natural part of daily life rather than a mandated task.

## Core Principles
- **Optionality:** Activities are framed as "if it works this week, great; if not, no stress," keeping the expectation warm but loose.
- **Adaptable formats:** The same routine can shift among a full sit‑down, a quick drink, a short walk, or a brief call while cooking or commuting.
- **Recurring yet light:** Examples include a monthly call, an ongoing text thread, or a brief weekly check‑in that can be shortened to a 15‑minute call when time is tight.
- **Consistency over intensity:** Small, regular interactions build stronger bonds than occasional, high‑effort events. See [[concepts/consistency-vs-intensity]].

## Practices Highlighted in [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-16]]
1. **Monthly optional call** – a scheduled but not mandatory conversation that can be skipped without guilt.
2. **Ongoing chat thread** – a group or one‑on‑one message stream where participants dip in when they have a moment.
3. **Flexible weekly meet‑up** – treated as an optional gathering that can vary in length and activity.
4. **Short “walk‑and‑talk”** – a brief outdoor chat that fits into a commute or errand run.
5. **Ten‑minute call while cooking** – leverages existing routines for connection.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **Low‑pressure touchpoints** – Flexible routines rely on easy, low‑effort interactions such as voice notes, memes, or quick updates. See [[concepts/low-pressure-touchpoints]].
- **Consistency vs. intensity** – By focusing on regular, adaptable contact, flexible routines embody the idea that consistency is more sustainable than sporadic, intensive meet‑ups.

## Benefits
- Reduces perceived obligation, making friendship feel *supportive* rather than *mandatory*.
- Accommodates fluctuating personal schedules and energy levels.
- Encourages a sense of reliability without demanding large time blocks.
