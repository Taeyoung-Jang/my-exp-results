# Runway Mindset

**Definition**: The *runway mindset* frames a bounded time horizon (e.g., two years) as a platform for consistent, low‑intensity actions that compound over time, rather than as a pause or a period of stagnation.

## Origin
The concept was introduced in the discussion of a two‑year “settle‑in” phase in [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-15]]. The assistant suggested treating the upcoming period as a *runway* to support intentional, incremental improvements across five life pillars.

## Core Principles
1. **Time as a resource, not a constraint** – View the fixed duration as a runway that gives you space to build momentum.
2. **Consistency over intensity** – Small, repeatable weekly habits (see [[concepts/habit-formation]]) are preferred to sporadic, high‑effort bursts.
3. **Compound impact** – Regular actions in health, skills, relationships, finances, and personal projects produce measurable gains.
4. **Lightweight review** – A brief monthly check‑in keeps the trajectory aligned without becoming a burden.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/decision-factors]]**: The runway mindset influences the decision‑making process by emphasizing long‑term, low‑friction growth rather than short‑term risk.
- **[[concepts/habit-formation]]**: It relies on habit formation as the engine that drives steady progress during the runway period.

## Practical Application
- **Set a horizon** – Define a concrete period (e.g., 24 months).
- **Choose pillars** – Identify 3‑5 focus areas (health, career, relationships, finances, personal project).
- **Create weekly habits** – Design low‑effort actions that can be executed reliably.
- **Monthly micro‑review** – Spend ~10 minutes reflecting on what worked, what slipped, and adjust the next month’s focus.

By adopting the runway mindset, you turn a static time block into a launchpad for purposeful, cumulative growth.
