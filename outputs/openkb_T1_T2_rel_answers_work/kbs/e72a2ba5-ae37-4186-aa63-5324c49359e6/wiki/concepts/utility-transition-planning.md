# Utility Transition Planning

Utility transition planning is the process of timing and managing the shutdown of existing services and the activation of new ones (electricity, gas, water, internet, etc.) when moving to a new residence. Proper planning prevents service gaps, unexpected fees, and the stress of dealing with utilities on moving day.

## Why It Matters
- **Avoid downtime:** Ensures lights, heat, and internet work as soon as you arrive.
- **Cost control:** Prevents overlapping billing periods and early‑termination penalties.
- **Smooth move:** Reduces last‑minute scramble that can derail a moving checklist.

## Core Steps (derived from [[summaries/session-34_2025-06-15]])
1. **Set dates early (Week 1):**
   - Contact electricity, gas, and water providers to schedule a stop‑off date at your current address and a start‑up date at the new address.
   - Book internet installation for **1–2 days before arrival** to avoid being without service.
2. **Document and track** the utility account numbers, contact details, and scheduled dates in a central checklist. This aligns with [[concepts/moving-checklist-design]].
3. **Re‑confirm dates (Week 3):**
   - Call providers to verify that the shut‑off and start‑up dates are still on schedule.
   - Ensure the internet provider has the correct installation window.
4. **Finalize on moving day:**
   - Take photos of meters for record‑keeping.
   - Verify that all services are active at the new location before the movers leave.

## Timing Overview
| Day Range | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Day 21‑20** | Confirm mover details **and** schedule internet install; set utility stop/start dates. |
| **Day 13‑12** | Re‑confirm utility dates; adjust if needed. |
| **Day 1 (Move day)** | Verify service activation, take meter photos. |

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/moving-checklist-design]]**: Utility transition tasks are a distinct block in a phased moving checklist.
- **[[concepts/address-change-management]]**: Utility accounts are among the top items to update when changing an address.
- **[[concepts/decision-factors]]**: Selecting providers and timing often depends on contract terms, availability, and cost considerations.

## Tips for Falling Behind
If you miss an early step, prioritize:
1. **Confirm mover and utility dates** – this prevents service gaps.
2. **Book internet installation** – a missing internet connection is a major inconvenience.
3. **Update utility addresses** – tie this into the broader address‑change process.

By following this structured approach, you can ensure a seamless transition of essential services during any relocation.
