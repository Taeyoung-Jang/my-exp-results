# Moving Checklist Design

**Definition**: A structured approach to creating moving checklists that break the relocation process into clear, time‑bound phases, balancing essential (must‑do) tasks with optional (nice‑to‑do) items, and integrating utility transitions, address updates, and contingency priorities.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Temporal Phasing**
   - Divide the move into weekly or daily blocks (e.g., a 3‑week plan) rather than a single monolithic list.  
   - Early phases focus on **admin** (movers, utility dates, internet installation), middle phases on **packing** and **address‑change** work, and the final phase on **clean‑up** and move‑day logistics.

2. **Must‑Do vs. Nice‑to‑Do Columns**
   - Highlight critical actions that, if missed, cause major disruptions (e.g., confirming movers, setting utility dates, updating banks).  
   - Optional tasks such as donating items or pre‑cleaning can be deferred without jeopardising the move.

3. **Utility Transition Planning**
   - Schedule shut‑off and start dates for electricity, gas, water, and especially internet (install 1‑2 days before arrival).  
   - Align these dates with the packing schedule to avoid service gaps.

4. **Address‑Change Management**
   - Create a master list of accounts and institutions, then update high‑priority entities (banks, employer, insurance) in the middle of the timeline.  
   - Use the **top‑ten forgotten** locations as a reference point.

5. **Subscription & Service Timing**
   - Pause or cancel recurring subscriptions (gym, boxes, local services) during the 21‑day window to reduce waste.

6. **Fallback Prioritization**
   - A concise “if I fall behind” list that first secures mover/utility confirmation, then bulk‑packs non‑essentials, followed by critical address updates.  
   - This ensures the move remains viable even when schedule slips.
