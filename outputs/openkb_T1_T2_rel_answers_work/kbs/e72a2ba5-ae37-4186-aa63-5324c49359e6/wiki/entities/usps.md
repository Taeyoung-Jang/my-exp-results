# United States Postal Service (USPS)

The United States Postal Service (USPS) is the federal agency responsible for national and international mail delivery, package services, and address‑change support in the United States.

## Role in session‑34_2025‑06‑15
In the 21‑day moving checklist, USPS is mentioned as the entity to handle mail forwarding. The checklist advises:
- Set USPS mail forwarding to start on move‑in day, submitting the request about a week in advance so that mail is captured during the transition.
- Include USPS in the broader **address‑change management** process alongside banks, insurers, and other institutions.

## Key Facts (as cited in the moving checklist)
- USPS forwarding requests need about a **one‑week buffer** before the move.
- The service is essential for ensuring that bills, packages, and personal correspondence follow the new address.
- It is listed among the top ten places people often forget to update after a relocation.
