# Summary of session-34_2025-06-15

**Date:** 2025‑06‑15  

## Core Contribution
A concrete, day‑by‑day moving checklist for a 3‑week relocation window, covering packing, utilities (including internet), USPS forwarding, address‑change locations, subscription management, and a fallback priority list.

## Key Concepts & Guidance
- **Weekly thematic approach** – early weeks focus on admin (movers, utilities, documentation), mid‑weeks on packing and address updates, final week on final packing, cleaning, and moving‑day prep. This aligns with the principles of [[concepts/moving-checklist-design]] and a [[concepts/runway-mindset]] for staged progress.  
- **Must‑do vs. nice‑to‑do** – each day groups essential actions (e.g., confirming movers, setting utility dates) with optional tasks (e.g., donating items, cleaning ahead).  
- **Utilities timing** – set stop/start dates for electricity, gas, water; schedule internet install 1–2 days before arrival, as described in [[concepts/utility-transition-planning]].  
- **USPS forwarding** – submit forwarding request at least a week before move‑in to ensure mail capture; see [[entities/usps]].  
- **Address‑change checklist** – top ten frequently forgotten entities: employer HR/payroll, banks/credit cards, insurance, driver’s license/ID, vehicle registration, medical providers, subscription boxes/meal kits, default online‑shopping addresses, streaming/digital services, loyalty programs. This follows the framework of [[concepts/address-change-management]].  
- **Subscription management** – pause or cancel gym memberships, subscription boxes, and local services during the 21‑day window.  
- **“If I fall behind” priority list** – focus first on mover/utility confirmation, bulk non‑essential packing, then critical address changes; everything else can be deferred.  

## Structured Daily Checklist (copy/paste ready)
```
Day 21  
[ ] Confirm movers…  
[ ] Schedule internet install…  
[ ] Set electricity, gas, and water stop/start dates.  
[ ] Walk through your home and decide what you’re not taking.  
[ ] Order or pick up boxes, tape, labels, and packing paper.

Day 20  
[ ] Start packing things you won’t use again before the move.  
[ ] Create a single note for every account that will need an address change.  
[ ] Gather important documents into one folder so they don’t get packed by accident.

… (continues through Day 1)

Day 1 (Move day)  
[ ] Pack the last of your essentials.  
[ ] Walk through every room, closet, and drawer.  
[ ] Take meter photos if needed for utilities.  
[ ] Keep your first‑night bag with you.
```
