# Summary of session-08_2024-06-15

## Core question
How to decide between a “settle‑in” phase and a “shake‑things‑up” phase, and how to structure a productive two‑year “settle‑in” plan without overthinking.

## Decision factors
- Energy level (heavier vs lighter feeling)  
- Current needs vs growth potential  
- Sense of direction and whether change feels like escape or progress  

These map to the broader concept of [[concepts/decision-factors]].

## Making the “settle‑in” phase count
Focus on **compound‑quiet** improvements:
1. A daily rhythm that grounds you
2. One or two skill or project trajectories that show visible progress
3. A small, intentional social circle

## Two‑year plan structure
The plan is organized around five **pillars**:
- Health
- Skills / Career
- Relationships / Community
- Finances
- Personal Project

Each pillar gets a monthly focus, a set of weekly habits, and a brief monthly review. This design embodies the concepts of goal setting and [[concepts/habit-formation]].

## Default weekly habits (busy‑schedule friendly)
- **Health:** Move 3×/week (20‑30 min) + weekly meal planning.
- **Skills / Career:** One 45‑60 min learning block per week.
- **Relationships / Community:** Reach out to one person weekly; attend one recurring community event.
- **Finances:** 10‑min weekly spending review; verify automated savings/payments.
- **Personal Project:** 15‑20 min “touch‑base” each week.

## Monthly template (one‑page checklist)
```
MONTHLY TEMPLATE

Pillar: Health  
Focus this month: …  
Weekly habits I’m committing to: …  
Notes from last month: …

Pillar: Skills / Career  
Focus this month: …  
Weekly habits I’m committing to: …  
Notes from last month: …

… (repeat for Relationships, Finances, Personal Project)

Short monthly reflection  
- What energized me this month: …  
- What drained me: …  
- One thing I want to lean into next month: …  
- One thing I can let go of: …
``` 

The template encourages a quick monthly review (~10 min) that keeps the process light yet intentional.

## Practical tip
Treat the two‑year horizon as a “runway” rather than a pause. Consistency in the small weekly actions compounds into measurable health, skill, relational, financial, and creative gains without feeling like endless homework. This reflects the concept of [[concepts/runway-mindset]].
