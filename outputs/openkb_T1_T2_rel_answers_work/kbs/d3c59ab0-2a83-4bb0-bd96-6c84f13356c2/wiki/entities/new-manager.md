## New Manager (generic role)

A **new manager** is the individual who assumes supervisory responsibility for an employee after a role transfer during a company reorganization. In the context of the conversation from [[summaries/session-37_2025-06-15]], the new manager is the primary point of contact for:

- **Onboarding coordination** – receiving updates about the employee’s scheduled onboarding and training sessions and confirming how much time can be allocated to early deliverables.
- **Priority clarification** – providing guidance on the most important tasks the employee should focus on during the ramp‑up period.
- **Capacity framing** – understanding and approving the proposed time‑split (e.g., a 60 % onboarding / 40 % deliverable split) to ensure realistic workload expectations.

### Key Facts from the Document
- The employee plans to allocate roughly **60 % of their time** to onboarding and training, leaving the remainder for deliverables. This split is communicated to the new manager to set expectations.
- The employee asks the new manager to **confirm top priorities** and to indicate any tasks that should be started or shadowed during the onboarding period.
- The communication style suggested emphasizes **simple, frequent, and transparent messaging**, aligning with the concept of [[concepts/controlled-ambiguity]] and [[concepts/communication-style]].
