## Old Manager (generic role)

**Type:** other

**Description:** A supervisory figure in the employee’s former team who is kept informed of the employee’s transition, onboarding schedule, and handoff priorities during a company reorganization.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-06-15]]:**
- Receives a concise update outlining the employee’s upcoming onboarding and training blocks.
- Is asked to identify any tasks that should be **elevated** for handoff before the transition finalizes.
- The communication emphasizes a limited but still‑available bandwidth, using a clear, proactive tone rather than a complaint.
- The employee proposes a **60/40 time split** (60 % onboarding, 40 % deliverables) and invites the old manager to confirm any priority adjustments.
- The message aligns with [[concepts/communication-style]] and [[concepts/time-split-framing]], ensuring transparent bandwidth framing.

**Related concepts:**
- [[concepts/communication-style]] – guides the polite, factual tone used in the update.
- [[concepts/controlled-ambiguity]] – reflects the broader context of navigating uncertainty during the reorg.
- [[concepts/core-work-anchor]] – reminds the employee to keep core responsibilities visible.
- [[concepts/onboarding-as-work]] – frames the training period as legitimate work to be communicated.
- [[concepts/team-sync]] – the update helps maintain synchronization between the old and new teams.

**Related entity:**
- [[entities/new-manager]] – the counterpart who receives a similar but forward‑looking onboarding communication.
