# Summary of session-10_2024-06-15

**Key concepts**
- [[concepts/communication-style]]: Different ways teams prefer to share updates (chat, email, quick calls).  
- [[concepts/team-sync]]: A short 10–15 minute meeting to align on working preferences.  
- check‑in meeting: Individual or group check‑ins to improve [[concepts/workplace-relationships]].  
- message drafting: Crafting concise, confident messages for various channels (group chat, 1:1, email).  
- recurring sync: Proposing a standing 15‑minute check‑in every other week.  

**Main ideas & recommendations**
1. **Message variations** – Provide three tailored drafts:  
   - *Group chat*: invites a brief team sync while allowing replies with preferences.  
   - *1:1*: personal invitation to discuss individual workflow.  
   - *Formal email*: polite, structured request.  
2. **Tone adjustment** – Make the group‑chat version sound more confident and natural, offering two tonal options:  
   - *Upbeat/casual* – friendly and energetic.  
   - *Straightforward/neutral* – clear and professional.  
3. **Suggested questions for check‑ins** – Examples to keep the conversation focused:  
   - “What communication method works best for you during the week?”  
   - “Is there anything I can do differently when sharing updates or coordinating tasks?”  
4. **Recurring check‑in line** – Optional sentence to propose a bi‑weekly 15‑minute standing meeting.  

**Potential actions**
- Use the provided drafts as templates for immediate outreach.  
- Choose the tone that matches the team culture.  
- Incorporate the suggested questions to gather actionable feedback.  
- Consider implementing the [[concepts/team-sync]] if the initial check‑in is successful.
