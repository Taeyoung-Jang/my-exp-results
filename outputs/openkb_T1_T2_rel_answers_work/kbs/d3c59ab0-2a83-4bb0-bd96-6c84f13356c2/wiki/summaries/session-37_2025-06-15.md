## Summary of session-37_2025-06-15

### Key Concepts
- **Controlled ambiguity** – Treat a reorg as a period of limited clarity and stay steady until structures settle. [[concepts/controlled-ambiguity]]
- **Core work anchor** – Keep delivering the tasks that directly support your team or customers, even amid change. [[concepts/core-work-anchor]]
- **Communication style** – Use short, direct questions to confirm priorities and stakeholders (e.g., “Is this still the most important thing for me to focus on this week?”). [[concepts/communication-style]]
- **Onboarding as work** – View onboarding and mandatory training as real work, allocating dedicated time rather than squeezing it around existing tasks. [[concepts/onboarding-as-work]]
- **Time‑split framing** – A typical 60/40 split (60 % onboarding, 40 % deliverables) signals commitment to ramp‑up while still contributing; alternatives include a more conservative 70/30 split or an aggressive 50/50 split. [[concepts/time-split-framing]]

### Practical Recommendations
1. **Prioritization** – Identify the core deliverables that matter most to your team/customers and keep them moving.
2. **Communication with managers** – Draft brief, polished messages (4–6 sentences) to both [[entities/old-manager]] and [[entities/new-manager]] outlining:
   - Upcoming onboarding/training schedule
   - Planned handoffs for current tasks
   - Request for confirmation of top priorities
   - Expected time split for ramp‑up vs. deliverables
3. **Message Templates** –  
   - *Old manager*: Emphasize handoff readiness, limited availability, and invitation for any priority adjustments.  
   - *New manager*: State onboarding schedule, propose a 60 % time allocation to ramp‑up, request priority guidance, and offer to incorporate shadowing or prep work.
4. **Alternative time‑split phrasing** –  
   - Conservative: “around seventy percent of my time on onboarding and training while I get grounded.”  
   - Aggressive: “roughly half my time available for early deliverables alongside the onboarding sessions.”

### Actionable Takeaways
- Adopt a **steady‑as‑she‑goes** mindset during the reorg.
- Anchor daily work to the **core value** you provide.
- Communicate bandwidth with **clear, proactive framing**.
- Use a **60/40** (or adjusted) time‑split language when discussing ramp‑up with managers.
- Prepare concise, polished manager messages that cover schedule, handoffs, and priority confirmation.
