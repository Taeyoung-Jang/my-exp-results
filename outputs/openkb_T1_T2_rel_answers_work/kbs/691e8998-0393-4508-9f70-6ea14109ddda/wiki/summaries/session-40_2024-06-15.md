## Summary of Session 40 (2024‑06‑15)

This document outlines a framework for selecting an investing style suited to a 10–15 year horizon and provides a concrete, written policy for a growth‑oriented portfolio. The key components are:

- **Investing style selection** – match the amount of time you want to spend on research with your tolerance for volatility. Hands‑off investors favor a broadly diversified core; active investors can allocate more to satellites but still need a stable foundation.

- **Core / Satellite Allocation** – example target: 65 % broad equity ETFs (core), 25 % high‑volatility satellites (individual stocks, themes), 10 % crypto sleeve. [[concepts/core-satellite-allocation]]

- **Diversification & Position‑Sizing Caps**
  - ETFs ≤ 30 % of total portfolio.
  - Individual stocks ≤ 6 % each.
  - Crypto assets ≤ 3 % each; total crypto ≤ 10 %.
  - Satellite bucket as a whole ≤ 40 % of portfolio.
  - Caps are soft; breaches are addressed by redirecting new contributions before selling. [[concepts/position-sizing]]

- **Rebalancing Rules**
  - **Drift‑band trigger**: intervene when any sleeve drifts > 5 percentage points from its target. [[concepts/drift-band-rebalancing]]
  - **Annual check‑in**: a full portfolio rebalance once a year.
  - **Contribution‑driven rebalancing**: each $2 k monthly contribution is first allocated to under‑weighted allocations, helping keep caps in line without frequent selling. [[concepts/contribution-driven-rebalancing]]

- **Practical Policy Text** – a concise, plain‑language ruleset that can be written down and followed, covering target mix, caps, breach handling, rebalance schedule, and use of monthly contributions.

Overall, the guidance balances a growth‑tilted, high‑volatility appetite with structural safeguards to prevent accidental concentration and to keep the portfolio aligned with long‑term goals.
