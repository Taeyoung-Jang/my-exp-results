# Risk Assessment

## Definition
Risk assessment is the systematic process of identifying, evaluating, and comparing potential negative and positive outcomes of a decision to determine whether a safe (certain) or risky (variable) option aligns with one’s values and goals.

## Core Elements
1. **Identify the real downside** – Pinpoint the concrete consequences if the risky option fails, avoiding exaggerated worst‑case scenarios.
2. **Check the safety net** – Assess whether you can tolerate that downside without jeopardizing essential stability (financial, emotional, professional).
3. **Clarify the upside** – Determine if the potential gain meaningfully advances a priority or goal.
4. **Motivation analysis** – Distinguish whether the pull toward safety or risk stems from genuine preference or fear‑driven avoidance.
5. **Future‑self perspective** – Project which choice you’ll feel proud of later, helping to balance short‑term emotions with long‑term values.

## Summary
Risk assessment integrates objective analysis of downsides and upsides with subjective signals (motivation, emotional tone) to help individuals make informed choices between certainty and potential reward. By following the structured approach from the session, users can quickly verify whether a safe option genuinely fits their priorities or if hidden fear is at play.
