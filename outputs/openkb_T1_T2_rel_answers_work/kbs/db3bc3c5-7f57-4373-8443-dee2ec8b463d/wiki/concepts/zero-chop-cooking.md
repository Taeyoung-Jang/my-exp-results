# Zero‑Chop Cooking

Zero‑Chop Cooking is a minimalist kitchen strategy that eliminates manual chopping by relying on pre‑washed greens, frozen vegetables, microwave‑ready grains, and other ready‑to‑use ingredients. The goal is to assemble balanced meals in minutes, making it ideal for busy weeknights.

## Core Principles
- **Pre‑washed produce** – baby spinach, arugula, and mixed greens that can be tossed directly onto a plate.
- **Frozen or steam‑in‑bag vegetables** – broccoli, peas, and mixed veggie blends that require only heating.
- **Microwave‑ready carbs** – rice, quinoa, or farro packets that cook in 2‑3 minutes.
- **Ready‑to‑eat proteins and add‑ons** – canned beans, pre‑cooked lentils, rotisserie chicken, or deli mains.
- **Flavor boosters** – jarred pesto, vinaigrette, hummus, lemon juice, olive oil, parmesan, or chili crisp that add taste without extra prep.

## Benefits
- **Time savings** – eliminates the need for chopping, slicing, or extensive cooking.
- **Consistency** – a fixed set of staples reduces decision fatigue.
- **Flexibility** – items can be mixed and matched with a variety of mains.

## Example Staples (from [[summaries/session-39_2025-02-10]])
- Microwave rice or quinoa packets
- Frozen broccoli or mixed veggies (steam‑in‑bag)
- Frozen peas
- Pre‑washed baby spinach or arugula
- Cherry tomatoes (ready‑to‑eat)
- Baby carrots
- Canned beans
- Pre‑cooked lentils
- Jarred pesto
- Vinaigrette dressing
- Hummus
- Lemon (squeeze‑only)
- Olive oil
- Parmesan or chili crisp

## Typical Pairings
- **Meatloaf** – microwave quinoa + pesto, or frozen broccoli + lemon.
- **Roasted chicken** – arugula tossed with vinaigrette, or warmed lentils + hummus.
- **Salmon** – microwave rice + peas, or wilted spinach with lemon.
- **Pasta bake** – cherry tomatoes + spinach, or peas stirred in with pesto.
