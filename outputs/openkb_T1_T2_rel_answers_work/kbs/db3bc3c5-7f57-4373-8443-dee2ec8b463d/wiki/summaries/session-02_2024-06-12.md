# Summary of session-02_2024-06-12

## Overview
This session outlines a **beginner‑friendly sauce toolkit** that emphasizes *base ingredients* and flexible **ratio‑based sauce templates**. It also delivers a concrete shopping list with quantities and a **4‑week practice schedule** that cycles through five core sauce types while keeping all techniques active.

## Key Concepts sauce toolkit
- **Pantry staples**: olive oil, neutral oil, vinegar, soy sauce, tomato paste, butter, broth, cream/yogurt.
- **Flavor‑building ingredients**: onions, garlic, lemons, whole spices (cumin seeds, coriander seeds, dried chilies, peppercorns, cinnamon stick, ginger).
- **Techniques** sauce techniques: sweating aromatics, deglazing, reduction, emulsifying, toasting whole spices, making a roux.

## Ratio‑Based Sauce Templates rate based sauce templates
1. **Pan sauce** – equal parts fat (butter/oil) and aromatics, then 1 part deglazing liquid to 2 parts stock (adjust texture). Finish with a knob of butter.
2. **Vinaigrette** – 1 part acid to 3 parts oil, plus a teaspoon of mustard to aid emulsification.
3. **Tomato sauce** – 1 spoon oil + 1 spoon onion, add 1 spoon tomato paste, then 1 can tomatoes + splash water; simmer to reduce.
4. **Simple curry‑style sauce** – toast whole spices, add aromatics, then 1 part tomato to 1 part liquid (broth or coconut milk); reduce and finish with lemon.
5. **Creamy sauce** – 1 spoon butter + 1 spoon flour (roux), whisk in 3‑4 parts warm milk/broth/cream; enrich with mustard, cheese, or herbs.

## Emphasis on Base Ingredients base ingredients
- Replace pre‑made blends with *whole* spices for greater flexibility.
- Use canned tomatoes instead of jarred sauce.
- Keep the pantry lean: only the essential liquids, fats, and aromatics needed for all templates.

## Weekly Practice Plan weekly practice plan
- **Each week** focuses on one primary template while lightly applying the others.
- Example: Week 1 centers on vinaigrettes (multiple variations) and includes a quick pan sauce; Week 2 emphasizes tomato sauce with a daily vinaigrette; Week 3 spotlights curry sauce, sprinkling in a small tomato sauce; Week 4 hones the creamy sauce, with a supplemental curry and pan sauce.

## 4‑Week Schedule four week schedule
| Week | Primary Template | Supplemental Practice |
|------|------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | Vinaigrette | Pan sauce, small tomato‑paste addition |
| 2 | Tomato sauce | Vinaigrette (daily), one pan sauce |
| 3 | Curry‑style sauce | Light tomato sauce, vinaigrette without measuring |
| 4 | Creamy sauce | One curry‑style sauce, one quick pan sauce |

By rotating focus while maintaining exposure to all techniques, the plan builds muscle memory without monotony.
