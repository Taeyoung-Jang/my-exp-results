# Recurring Payment Tracking

## Overview
Recurring Payment Tracking is the practice of collecting, organizing, and regularly reviewing all ongoing subscription and service charges so that you always know **what you’re paying, when it renews, and whether it still provides value**.

## Core Approaches (as highlighted in [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]])

1. **Spreadsheet** – A simple table with columns for service name, cost, billing frequency, renewal date, and source. It can be sorted or filtered to surface upcoming renewals.
2. **Notes / Finance App** – Centralised digital notes or dedicated budgeting apps that let you tag subscriptions and set reminders.
3. **Calendar Alerts** – The most reliable fail‑safe: create an event on the renewal date (or a week before) with a reminder notification.

## Best‑Practice Workflow
1. **Gather sources** – Pull data from app stores, email receipts, and bank/credit‑card statements (see [[concepts/subscription_auditing]] for a full audit process).
2. **Create a master list** – Use one of the approaches above; ensure each entry includes the exact renewal date.
3. **Set proactive reminders** – Calendar alerts a week‑to‑two weeks before each renewal, as recommended in the session.
4. **Regular review** – Refresh the list every 3‑4 months to catch new subscriptions and remove obsolete ones.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/subscription_auditing]] to first identify all recurring charges.
- Supports [[concepts/annual_renewal_detection]] by providing a structured place to log annual charges once they are discovered.
- Can be combined with a cost‑over‑time analysis (see [[concepts/cost-over-time]]) to evaluate long‑term financial impact.

## Tools & Tips
- **Google Calendar** or **Apple Calendar** for reminder events.
- **Google Sheets**, **Excel**, or **LibreOffice Calc** for quick spreadsheets.
- Dedicated subscription‑management apps (e.g., Truebill, Bobby) if you prefer a purpose‑built solution.
- Tag entries with the source entity (e.g., [[entities/apple]], [[entities/google]]) to quickly locate app‑store subscriptions.
