## Subscription Auditing

**Definition**: Subscription auditing is a structured workflow for discovering all recurring software charges, assessing their value, and maintaining an up‑to‑date record to avoid unwanted renewals.

### Core Steps
1. **Gather Sources**
   - Collect subscription data from app stores, email receipts, and bank or credit‑card statements. 
   - Identify the origin of each charge (e.g., [[entities/apple]], [[entities/google]], direct vendor via [[entities/stripe]] or [[entities/paddle]]).
2. **Create a Master List**
   - Record each service name, cost, billing frequency, and renewal date in a single spreadsheet or tracking tool.
   - This list supports analysis of cost trends and can be linked to [[concepts/cost-over-time]].
3. **Evaluate Value**
   - Apply a quick [[concepts/feature-evaluation-checklist]]: usage frequency, time‑saving benefit, cost vs. alternatives, and availability of free or cheaper options.
4. **Confirm Renewal Details**
   - Use the transaction description to determine whether the subscription is managed via an app store or directly with the vendor.
   - Access the relevant subscription page (e.g., Apple or Google account settings, or the vendor’s billing portal) to verify plan name and exact renewal date.
5. **Decide Action**
   - Cancel, downgrade, or keep the subscription based on the evaluation.
6. **Set Reminders**
   - Add calendar alerts a week‑two before each renewal. Calendar reminders are a proven fail‑safe for preventing surprise charges.

### Tools & Tracking Options
- **Spreadsheet** – Simple, sortable by renewal date or cost.
- **Notes/Finance Apps** – Centralised view if already in use.
- **Calendar Reminders** – Essential for [[concepts/annual_renewal_detection]] and for keeping renewals top‑of‑mind.
