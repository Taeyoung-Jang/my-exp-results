# Cost Over Time

**Definition**: Assessing the true financial impact of a subscription by translating recurring charges (usually monthly) into a yearly cost, enabling clearer comparison across plans and add‑ons.

---

## Why It Matters

In the conversation from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]], the assistant advises users to “think in terms of total cost over a year rather than the monthly price, because small differences add up.”  This shift in perspective helps:

1. **Expose hidden savings** – A plan that appears cheaper month‑to‑month may become more expensive annually if it lacks bundled features.
2. **Facilitate side‑by‑side comparison** – Annualized numbers line up the cost of a standard tier plus a storage add‑on against a higher tier that includes storage, making the trade‑off obvious.
3. **Support budgeting** – Many organizations plan budgets annually; knowing the yearly figure integrates the subscription into broader financial planning.

---

## How to Calculate

1. **Identify all recurring fees** (base plan, add‑ons, optional extras).
2. **Multiply each monthly price by 12** to get its annual cost.
3. **Sum the annualized amounts** for a total yearly expense.
4. **Compare** this total with the advertised annual price of higher tiers (if any), remembering to adjust for discounts or promotional rates.

> *Example (illustrative)*: 
> - Standard plan: $10 /mo → $120 /yr
> - Storage add‑on: $5 /mo → $60 /yr
> - Total current setup: $180 /yr
> - Next tier (includes 200 GB storage): $15 /mo → $180 /yr
> In this scenario the cost is equal, so the decision hinges on feature differences.
