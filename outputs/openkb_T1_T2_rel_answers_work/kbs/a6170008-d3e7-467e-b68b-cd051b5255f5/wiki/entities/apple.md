# Apple (Organization)

Apple Inc. is a global technology corporation best known for its hardware, software, and digital services. In the context of **session-39_2025-03-14**, Apple appears as a possible source of recurring software subscription charges.

## Role in Subscription Auditing
- The document advises users to inspect bank‑statement transaction lines for keywords such as **Apple**, **iTunes**, **App Store**, or **Play Store** to determine if a charge originates from an Apple‑managed subscription.
- If an Apple‑related charge is identified, the recommended action is to open the **Apple Subscriptions** page on the user’s device to view plan details, cost, and renewal date.
