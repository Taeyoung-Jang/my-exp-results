# Google (Organization)

Google LLC is a global technology company best known for its search engine, advertising platforms, and a suite of consumer services. In the context of [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]], Google is referenced as one of the possible sources of recurring subscription charges through the **Google Play Store**.

## Role in subscription management
- **App‑store subscriptions**: When a bank transaction mentions "Google" or "Play Store," it usually indicates the charge originates from a Google Play subscription. This distinction helps users apply the **source identification** steps described in the document.
- **Verification process**: Users are advised to open the Google Play subscriptions page on their Android device to view plan names, costs, and renewal dates.

## Related concepts
- [[concepts/annual_renewal_detection]] – searching for annual charges often involves checking Google Play receipts.
- [[concepts/subscription_auditing]] – Google Play subscriptions are one of the categories to audit when compiling a master list of recurring payments.
- [[concepts/recurring_payment_tracking]] – after confirming a Google‑based subscription, users can track it via spreadsheets, apps, or calendar reminders.
