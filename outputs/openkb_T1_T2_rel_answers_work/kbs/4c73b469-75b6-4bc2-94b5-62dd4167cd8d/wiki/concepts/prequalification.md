# Loan Pre‑qualification

**Definition**: Loan pre‑qualification is an early, non‑binding assessment where a lender estimates how much you might be able to borrow and at what interest rate, using only a **soft‑pull** of your credit information. It does not affect your credit score and does not constitute a formal loan application.

---

## How It Works
1. **Information Provided**: You typically supply basic personal and financial details such as name, address, employment status, income, and the desired loan amount.
2. **Credit Check**: The lender performs a **soft‑pull** on your credit report. This inquiry is recorded as a [[concepts/soft-pull]] and does not appear on your credit file, so it has no impact on your [[concepts/credit-score]].
3. **Result**: You receive an estimate of loan amounts and possible interest rates. Because no hard credit pull is made, you can complete multiple pre‑qualifications without the risk of a **hard‑inquiry**.

---

## Benefits
- **No Credit Impact**: Since only a soft pull is used, you can shop around freely.
- **Speed**: Pre‑qualification often provides instant or near‑instant feedback.
- **Negotiation Power**: Having multiple pre‑qualification offers lets you compare rates and terms before committing.

---

## Risks & Considerations
- **Marketing Consent**: Pre‑qualification forms may include optional or default marketing consent. Be vigilant for language that would sign you up for calls or mail; see [[concepts/marketing-opt-out]] for ways to reduce unwanted contact.
- **Data Accuracy**: Errors in the information you provide can lead to inaccurate estimates.
- **Limited Guarantee**: An offer is not binding; final loan terms are confirmed only after a full application and a **hard‑inquiry**.

---

## Relationship to Formal Application
When you move from pre‑qualification to a full loan application, the lender will conduct a **hard‑inquiry**. Multiple hard pulls for the same loan type within a short period are treated as a single inquiry under the [[concepts/rate-shopping-window]] (typically about 14 days). Planning your timing helps keep credit impact low.

---

## Practical Tips
- Use a dedicated email address for pre‑qualification forms to keep marketing separate.
- Review each form for statements like “soft inquiry only” to confirm you won’t incur a hard pull.
- After completing pre‑qualifications, you can compare offers and decide which lenders to submit formal applications to within the rate‑shopping window.
