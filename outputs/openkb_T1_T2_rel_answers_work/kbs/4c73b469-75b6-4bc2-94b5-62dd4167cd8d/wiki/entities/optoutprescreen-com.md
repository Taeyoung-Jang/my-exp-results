# OptOutPrescreen.com

OptOutPrescreen.com is a free online service that allows U.S. consumers to opt out of receiving prescreened credit and insurance offers, helping to reduce unsolicited marketing contacts.

The site is referenced in [[summaries/session-03_2024-06-15]] as a recommended step for loan shoppers who want to minimise spam and protect their credit profile.

Using OptOutPrescreen.com is part of a broader [[concepts/marketing-opt-out]] strategy. By opting out, consumers limit the number of marketing‑driven hard inquiries that could otherwise appear on their credit report, which can help maintain a healthy [[concepts/credit-score]].

The service works alongside other tools such as the [[entities/do-not-call-list]] for phone‑based marketing control, providing a comprehensive approach to privacy and credit‑score preservation.
