# National Do‑Not‑Call List

The **National Do‑Not‑Call List** is a United States government‑maintained registry that allows individuals to opt‑out of most telemarketing calls.  By adding a phone number to this list, consumers can significantly reduce unsolicited sales calls, which is especially useful when managing loan‑shopping communications.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-03_2024-06-15]]
- The session advises registering the borrower’s phone number on the **Do‑Not‑Call List** to minimize marketing calls after submitting loan pre‑qualification forms.
- It is presented as one of the practical steps to lower post‑application spam alongside opting out of prescreened credit offers.

## Usage Tips
1. Visit the official website (typically **https://www.donotcall.gov**) to register a phone number.
2. Expect a processing period of up to 30 days before calls cease.
3. The list does not block calls from political organizations, charities, or companies with an existing business relationship.

By leveraging the National Do‑Not‑Call List, loan shoppers can keep their communication channels focused on legitimate lenders and avoid the noise of unsolicited marketing.
