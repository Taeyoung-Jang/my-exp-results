## Summary of Session 3 (2024‑06‑15)

**Goal:** Help the user plan actions over the next few months to boost loan‑approval odds and secure a good interest rate.

### 1. Strengthen Your Credit Profile
- **Check credit reports** for errors and dispute inaccuracies (affects [[concepts/credit-score]]).
- **Reduce credit‑card balances** and avoid new charges; lower utilization can raise scores quickly.
- **Limit new credit inquiries**; each hard pull temporarily signals higher risk.

### 2. Maintain Financial Stability
- Keep bank‑account activity steady; avoid large swings that could look risky.
- Stay in the same job (or avoid major employment changes) during the preparation window.
- Preserve an emergency‑savings cushion to demonstrate liquidity.

### 3. Use Soft‑Pull Pre‑Qualifications Wisely
- Look for wording such as *“soft credit inquiry,”* *“will not affect your credit score,”* or *“pre‑qualify.”*  These do **not** generate a hard pull.
- Typical information requested: name, address, income, employment, and desired loan amount.
- No documents are needed at this stage; results are not reported to credit bureaus.

### 4. Manage Hard‑Pull Applications
- **Rate‑shopping window:** Most scoring models (FICO, VantageScore) treat multiple hard pulls for the same loan type as a single inquiry when they occur within roughly **14 days** (some models up to 30 days).  Submit all formal applications within this window to minimise credit impact.
- Plan a short, intensive application period after soft‑pull research.

### 5. Watch the Fine Print & Minimise Spam
- **Hard‑pull warnings:** Look for phrases like *“pull your credit report”* or *“hard inquiry.”* If unclear, skip the form.
- **Marketing consent:** Optional checkboxes often permit phone, text, email, or partner outreach. Leave unchecked when possible.
- **Spam reduction tactics:**
  1. Opt‑out of prescreened credit offers at [[entities/optoutprescreen-com]].
  2. Register your phone number on the [[entities/do-not-call-list]].
  3. Use a dedicated email address for loan shopping.

Then, group all hard‑pull applications into a 14‑day window and proactively opt‑out of marketing to keep the process smooth and cost‑effective.
