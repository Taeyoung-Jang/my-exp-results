# Mazaki (product)

Mazaki is a Japanese‑style **gyuto** (chef’s knife) made from carbon steel. In the session‑02 conversation it is highlighted as a **mid‑weight** option that balances stiffness, food‑release, and a blade profile suited for both rocking and push‑cutting.

## Key Characteristics
- **Length:** 210 mm (recommended as the optimal daily driver size for most home cooks) – see [[concepts/gyuto-length]].
- **Blade profile:** Gentle curve toward the tip with a relatively flat heel, ideal for mixed **rocking and push cuts** – see [[concepts/blade-profile-rocking-push]].
- **Steel:** Carbon steel, offering a keen edge while requiring regular maintenance (drying, occasional oiling) – see [[concepts/high-carbon-knife-care]].
- **Intended use:** Works well as a versatile, everyday Japanese chef’s knife for serious home cooking.

## Maintenance & Sharpening
- Recommended sharpening routine follows the general advice for high‑carbon knives: touch‑ups on a fine stone (3000–6000 grit) and full sharpening starting on a medium stone (~1000 grit) before finishing on the fine stone – see [[concepts/sharpening-stone-grits]].

## Comparison
- Compared to ultra‑thin “laser” gyutos like **Gesshin Ginga** [[entities/gesshin-ginga]] or **Konosuke HD2** [[entities/konosuke-hd2]], the Mazaki offers a sturdier spine and more forgiving feel for cooks who still rock the blade.
- It is positioned between the lightweight laser category and heavier **workhorse** models such as **Moritaka AS** [[entities/morita-ka]] and **Watanabe pro‑style** [[entities/watanabe-pro]].
