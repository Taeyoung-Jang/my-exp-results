# Yoshikane SKD

Yoshikane SKD is a **mid‑weight Japanese gyuto** (chef’s knife) often offered in a 210 mm (8.3 in) length. In the *session‑02_2024‑09‑15* conversation it was cited as a **balanced option** for home cooks who want a knife that handles both rocking motions and push‑cut techniques comfortably.

## Key Characteristics
- **Length:** 210 mm, matching the recommended size for most home chefs (see [[concepts/gyuto-length]]).
- **Blade Profile:** Features a gentle curve toward the tip with a relatively flat heel, suitable for mixed rocking and push cuts (see [[concepts/blade-profile-rocking-push]]).
- **Steel Type:** Available in a white‑steel (SKD) variant, offering good edge retention while remaining manageable for sharpening.
- **Use Case:** Ideal as a daily driver for mixed‑technique cooking; avoids the ultra‑thin, flat‑spine designs that can feel chattery when rocking.

## Related Care & Maintenance
- When using a high‑carbon or white‑steel gyuto like Yoshikane SKD, regular drying and occasional mineral‑oil coating help prevent oxidation (see [[concepts/high-carbon-knife-care]]).
- For sharpening, a routine of fine‑stone touch‑ups (3000‑6000 grit) and occasional full sharpening on a medium stone (~1000 grit) is recommended (see [[concepts/sharpening-stone-grits]]).

## Context in the Session
This product was highlighted in the discussion of **mid‑weight gyuto options** alongside other examples such as Mazaki and Moritaka. It illustrates the balance between agility and robustness that many serious home cooks seek.
