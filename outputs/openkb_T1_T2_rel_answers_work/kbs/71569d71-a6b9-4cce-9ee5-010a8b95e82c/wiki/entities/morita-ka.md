# Moritaka AS

**Type:** product

**Key facts from the session-02_2024-09-15 discussion**

- Recommended as a **workhorse** gyuto for home cooks who want a sturdy, reliable knife.
- Length suggested: **210 mm** (or 240 mm for larger tasks) – see [[concepts/gyuto-length]].
- Blade profile: thicker spine with a moderate curve, suitable for both rocking and push cuts – see [[concepts/blade-profile-rocking-push]].
- Typically offered in high‑carbon or carbon‑core steel, requiring the care outlined in [[concepts/high-carbon-knife-care]].
- Sharpening routine: medium stone (~1000 grit) for full sharpening, fine stone (3000‑6000 grit) for touch‑ups – see [[concepts/sharpening-stone-grits]].
- Storage: works well in a leather roll if kept dry and lightly oiled – see [[concepts/leather-roll-storage]].
- Steel choice: mid‑range carbon steel balancing edge retention and ease of sharpening – see [[concepts/knife-steel-selection]].
