# Gesshin Ginga

Gesshin Ginga is a Japanese **gyuto** (chef's knife) made from carbon steel. In the *session-02_2024-09-15* conversation, it is cited as a **laser‑type** gyuto that offers a very thin, lively edge while remaining reasonably tough for home‑cook use. It is highlighted as a concrete example of a high‑carbon, low‑profile knife that avoids the extreme fragility of some ultra‑thin blades.

## Key Characteristics
- **Blade length:** 210 mm (recommended size for a daily driver). 
- **Profile:** Laser thin with a gentle curve, suitable for both rocking and push‑cutting when handled correctly. 
- **Steel:** High‑carbon steel, requiring regular drying, oiling, and a sharpening routine of fine (3000‑6000 grit) stones for touch‑ups and medium (≈1000 grit) stones for full sharpening. 
- **Use case:** Ideal for cooks who want a lightweight, precise knife that can handle mixed cutting techniques without excessive fatigue.
