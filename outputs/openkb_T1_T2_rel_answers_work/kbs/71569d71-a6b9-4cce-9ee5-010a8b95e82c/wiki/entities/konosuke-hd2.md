# Konosuke HD2

**Type:** product (Japanese carbon‑steel laser gyuto)

**Description:** The Konosuke HD2 is a high‑carbon, laser‑style gyuto knife (~210 mm) praised for its thin, agile blade while remaining relatively durable for home use.

**Key points from session‑02_2024‑09‑15**
- Classified as a **laser** gyuto: very thin and lively, suitable for precise work but still robust enough for typical home cooking. [[entities/gesshin-ginga]] is another example of a laser gyuto mentioned alongside it.
- Recommended length for a daily driver is **210 mm** (8.3 in), fitting the mixed rocking and push‑cut style described. [[concepts/gyuto-length]]
- Blade profile: gentle curve toward the tip with a relatively flat heel, supporting both rocking and push cuts. [[concepts/blade-profile-rocking-push]]
- Steel: high‑carbon construction, offering a keen edge but requiring careful maintenance. [[concepts/high-carbon-knife-care]] and [[concepts/knife-steel-selection]]
- Sharpening routine: use a fine stone (3000–6000 grit) for touch‑ups and a medium stone (~1000 grit) for full sharpening. [[concepts/sharpening-stone-grits]]
- Storage advice: keep the blade bone‑dry and consider a thin blade guard inside a leather roll to avoid rust. [[concepts/leather-roll-storage]]
