# Allowed Items

**Definition**: The set of personal belongings that most residential treatment or rehab facilities allow patients to keep after the initial intake check.

## Core Categories
- **Clothing**: everyday wear, pajamas, socks, underwear, light jacket or hoodie (no metal accessories).
- **Footwear**: comfortable shoes and shower‑appropriate flip‑flops.
- **Toiletries**: non‑glass, non‑alcohol products such as toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, shampoo, body wash, hairbrush/comb.
- **Personal Comfort**: paperback books, a small journal, pen or pencil, ear‑plugs, eye mask.
- **Electronics & Accessories**: phone charger, wired headphones, basic phone (subject to check), reusable plastic water bottle.

These items are generally **allowed** after a quick visual inspection by staff. They are considered low‑risk and help patients settle in comfortably.

## Relation to Intake Process
The allowance of these items is part of the broader **[[concepts/intake-procedure]]**. During intake, staff review a guest’s bag, separate prohibited items (sharp objects, glass, aerosols, etc.) into a sealed property bag, and return the allowed items for immediate use.

## Communication Context
When phones are temporarily held, facilities often provide **scheduled calls** or supervised phone use, ensuring residents stay connected while still adhering to safety rules. This ties into the **[[concepts/communication-protocols]]** that govern contact with family during the first few days.
