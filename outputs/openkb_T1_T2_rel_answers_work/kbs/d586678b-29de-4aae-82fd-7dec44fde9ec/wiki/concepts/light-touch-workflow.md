# Light‑Touch Workflow

**Definition** – A workshop approach that minimizes wrist strain by placing frequently‑adjusted tools within an ergonomic *arm‑reach zone*, using low‑effort fixtures (e.g., toggle clamps, carrier sleds) and machine‑based precision instead of manual gripping.

## Core Principles
- **Keep the wrist neutral**: Store measurement tools, pencils, hex keys, and small squares in a shallow tray directly under the bench so they can be grabbed with the forearm or hip rather than twisted.
- **Shift work to fixtures**: Build and use jigs that hold the workpiece securely (general clamping board, router‑table sled, drill‑press carrier) so the operator only nudges the material.
- **Use low‑effort clamps**: Toggle clamps are favoured for quick, predictable clamping with minimal wrist motion. See [[concepts/toggle-clamp-advantage]].
- **Leverage machine adjustments**: Dial in depths and feeds via shims or large‑diameter adjustment wheels instead of turning small knobs repeatedly. See [[concepts/shim-based-depth-adjustment]].
- **Organise by reach**: Heavy or grab‑and‑go items (large squares, clamps, jigs) are mounted on a wall rail; lightweight, frequently‑used items stay within the arm’s reach zone. See [[concepts/arm-reach-zone]].

## Practical Elements from [[summaries/session-33_2023-12-13]]
1. **General clamping board** – 12×18 in plywood with two light‑pressure toggle clamps and a grip‑tape surface.
2. **Router‑table carrier sled** – 8×10 in base, rear fence, dome‑shaped palm‑heel handle, one toggle clamp, UHMW sliding base.
3. **Drill‑press carrier** – 6×8 in base, angled dowel handle, wedge pair clamps.
4. **Pull‑out tool tray** – low drawer for squares, gauges, hex keys, pencils.
5. **Wall rail** – holds larger squares, clamps, pre‑built jigs.
6. **Hardware choices** – palm‑style knobs, quick‑release vise add‑ons, large‑diameter bit‑height wheel for easier height adjustments.

## Benefits
- **Reduced repetitive wrist motion** – fewer twist‑cap and pinch actions.
- **Consistent precision** – fixtures and shims provide repeatable references.
- **Faster setup** – quick‑release and toggle mechanisms speed changeovers.
- **Lower fatigue** – most actions are palm‑presses, hip nudges, or single‑stroke flips.
