# Summary of session-14_2022-06-15

**Date:** 2022‑06‑15 14:30  

## Core Recommendations
- **Sharp iron first** – a dead‑sharp, lightly cambered edge with a polished back is the single most important factor for a glassy surface. See [[concepts/sharp-iron-preparation]].
- **Chipbreaker setting** – start with the breaker about **0.1 mm (≈0.004 in)** from the iron edge; adjust closer for very fine shavings or tricky grain, back off for heavier cuts. Refer to [[concepts/chipbreaker-setup]].
- **Frog/bed alignment** – ensure a solid, stable support; move the frog forward only when a tighter mouth is required for difficult grain.
- **Mouth opening** – keep it moderate; tighten only when grain direction causes tear‑out.

## Choosing a Plane
- **Smoothing plane (No. 4 or 4½)** – heavy, bevel‑down, with a tuned chipbreaker is the most versatile for ultra‑fine finishes. See [[entities/no-4-smoothing-plane]] and [[entities/no-4-5-smoothing-plane]].
- **Bevel‑up smoother** – useful when you want to vary the cutting angle via micro‑bevels, but relies on a perfect edge because it lacks a chipbreaker. See [[entities/bevel-up-smoother]].
- **Traditional bevel‑down planes** – with a well‑set chipbreaker provide excellent control on tricky grain.

## Tear‑out Diagnosis
- **Grain direction issues** – appear as streaks in reversing grain; flipping or skewing the plane often resolves them.
- **Setup problems** (see [[concepts/tear-out-diagnosis]]):
  - *Too wide breaker gap* → long, continuous tear‑out.  
  - *Dull iron* → fuzzy surface, higher thrust needed.  
  - *Over‑tight mouth* → starved plane, shavings bunch or jam.

## When to Raise Cutting Angle
- Use a **standard angle + chipbreaker** as the first line of defense.
- If tear‑out persists on highly figured grain, consider:
  - A York‑pitch frog or back bevel on a bevel‑down plane – [[entities/york-pitch-frog]].
  - Higher micro‑bevel on a bevel‑up smoother.
- Higher angles increase effort and slightly alter the surface feel.

## Single Non‑Destructive Tweak
- **Adjust the chipbreaker** – the quickest reversible change. Move it a hair closer, test on the workpiece, and look for cleaner, more controlled shavings and a uniformly smooth surface. If shavings become crinkly or clog, back off. This tweak aligns with [[concepts/chipbreaker-setup]].

## Practical Tips for Evaluation
- Perform a few test passes on the actual board after each tweak.
- Feel for the “snap” of fibers breaking cleanly; observe shavings consistency and surface uniformity.
