# York Pitch Frog

**Type:** product

The York Pitch Frog is a removable frog extension that increases the effective cutting angle of a hand plane. By raising the angle of attack, it helps reduce tear‑out on highly figured or difficult grain when the standard setup (sharp iron, chipbreaker, mouth control) is insufficient.

## Key Facts from Session 14
- Recommended as the **non‑destructive tweak** to try after adjusting the chipbreaker when tear‑out persists on tricky grain.
- Provides a higher cutting angle without permanently altering the plane, making it reversible and easy to experiment with.
- Works well with traditional bevel‑down planes such as the [[entities/no-4-smoothing-plane]] or the larger [[entities/no-4-5-smoothing-plane]].
- Complementary to other setup concepts like [[concepts/chipbreaker-setup]], [[concepts/sharp-iron-preparation]], and [[concepts/tear-out-diagnosis]].

## Usage Tips
1. Install the York Pitch Frog in place of the standard frog.
2. Make a few test cuts on the workpiece; a tighter, cleaner shaving indicates the higher angle is helping.
3. If the plane feels too heavy or the surface loses its “silky” feel, revert to the standard frog.
