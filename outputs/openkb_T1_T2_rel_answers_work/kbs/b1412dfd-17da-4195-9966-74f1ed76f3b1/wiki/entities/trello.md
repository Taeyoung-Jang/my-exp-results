# Trello

Trello is a web‑based **product** that provides a visual board interface for organizing tasks and projects using lists and cards. It supports lightweight workflows, enabling teams to track progress, attach files, and add checklists directly on cards.

## Role in session‑04_2024‑06‑16
In the conversation dated 2024‑06‑16, Trello is recommended as the central place to embed a concise **definition of done** checklist and to serve as the trigger for the **hand‑off signal**. The suggested template places a short “Done when:” list in the card description and uses moving the card to the next column as the cue that the task is ready for the next owner.
