# Handoff Signal

A **handoff signal** is a lightweight, uniform cue that tells the next team member a task has met its "definition of done" and is ready to be picked up. The signal is deliberately simple—often a single action within the primary work‑tracking tool—so it is noticed without adding meetings or extra documentation.

## Core Elements
- **Single, repeatable action** – e.g., moving a Trello card to the next column, tagging the next owner, or posting a templated Slack message.
- **Coupled with a Definition of Done** – the signal is only sent after the checklist for that stage is satisfied.
- **Visibility in existing tools** – the cue lives where the team already looks (Trello, Slack), eliminating the need to check separate logs.

## Why It Matters
- Reduces ambiguity when the usual tacit flow breaks (e.g., a team member is out or a new person steps in).
- Provides a clear, auditable point of transition without extra meetings.
- Supports rapid scaling of small crews who already have strong informal role awareness.

## Practical Template (from [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-16]])
### Trello Card
```
Done when:
- files are in the shared folder
- notes are updated in the card
- blockers are listed (if any)

Ready for next step once the card is moved to the next column and the owner is tagged.
```
### Slack Message
```
[Task name] is ready for you — card moved, files posted, link here: <link>
```
Both templates embed the signal directly in the tools the team uses daily.
