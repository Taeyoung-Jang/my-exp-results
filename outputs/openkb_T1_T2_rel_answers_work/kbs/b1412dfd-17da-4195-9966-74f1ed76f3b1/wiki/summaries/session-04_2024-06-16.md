# Summary of session-04_2024-06-16

**Date:** 2024-06-16

## Core Idea
Provide practical, low‑overhead methods for a small, experienced crew to make role responsibilities, “definition of done”, and handoff signals explicit, using the tools they already use ([[entities/trello]] and [[entities/slack]]).

## Key Concepts
- **Visible Role Ownership** – quickly map who owns what without formal documentation; use a simple shared view or board. [[concepts/role-ownership]]
- **Definition of Done (DoD) per Step** – one‑sentence checklist for each workflow stage, kept where the work lives. [[concepts/definition-of-done]]
- **Hand‑off Signal** – a single, consistent action (e.g., moving a Trello card, tagging a person) that indicates the task is ready for the next owner. [[concepts/handoff-signal]]
- **Lightweight Documentation** – embed the DoD checklist directly in the [[entities/trello]] card description and use a templated [[entities/slack]] message for the cue.
- **Backup Language** – a minimal “backup” protocol that only surfaces when the normal tacit flow breaks (someone out, new member, high load). *(plain text)*

## Suggested Templates
### Trello Card Checklist
```
Done when: 
- files are in the shared folder
- notes are updated in the card
- blockers are listed (if any)

Ready for next step once the card is moved to the next column and the owner is tagged.
```
### Slack Handoff Message
```
[Task name] is ready for you — card moved, files posted, link here: <link>
```
These templates keep the process **lightweight**, **visible**, and **consistent** across the team.

## Practical Steps for Implementation
1. Conduct a short team chat to list each person’s natural strengths and map them to primary responsibilities (visible role map). [[concepts/role-ownership]]
2. Draft one‑sentence DoD statements for each typical stage of your workflow. [[concepts/definition-of-done]]
3. Add the DoD text to the description/template section of every relevant [[entities/trello]] card.
4. Agree on a single hand‑off signal – e.g., moving the card + tagging the next owner. [[concepts/handoff-signal]]
5. Adopt the Slack one‑liner as the standard notification when the card moves. [[entities/slack]]
6. Use a brief weekly 10‑minute stand‑up to surface any blockers and ensure the backup language is being respected.

## Benefits
- Reduces ambiguity when the crew is busy or when a new person steps in.
- Eliminates extra meetings; the signal lives in existing tools.
- Provides a clear, repeatable handoff process that scales with minimal friction.
