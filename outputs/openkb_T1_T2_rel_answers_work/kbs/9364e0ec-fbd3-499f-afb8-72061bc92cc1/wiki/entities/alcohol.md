# Alcohol

**Type:** other

**Description:** An alcoholic drink referenced as part of an evening routine that should not interfere with sleep.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-23]]
- Suggested to be consumed **after dinner**, typically around **8:30 PM** in the proposed weekday schedule.
- The drink is positioned as a **final off‑switch** after work, exercise, and dinner.
- Low‑effort **wind‑down routines** are recommended to follow the drink, helping to avoid feeling “wired” before bedtime.
- The timing aligns with a **caffeine‑cutoff** strategy (no coffee after noon) to support healthy sleep hygiene.

## Interaction with other elements
- Paired with the **caffeine‑cutoff** concept (see [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]]) to ensure stimulants are avoided later in the day.
- Integrated into a broader **daily‑routine‑design** that balances work, meals, exercise, and relaxation (see [[concepts/daily-routine-design]]).
- Complemented by a **wind‑down‑routine** (see [[concepts/wind-down-routine]]) that includes a warm shower, light stretching, and calming activities after the drink.
- Mentioned alongside **caffeine** as a contrasting stimulant, emphasizing the need for a clear separation between the two substances (see [[entities/caffeine]]).

## Practical guidance
- Keep the alcoholic beverage to one portion and enjoy it **after the evening drink window** to minimize impact on sleep.
- Follow the drink with a brief, low‑stimulus wind‑down (e.g., dim lights, herbal tea, gentle stretching) to transition smoothly to bedtime.
- Avoid additional caffeine or high‑sugar snacks after the drink to maintain the sleep‑friendly environment.
