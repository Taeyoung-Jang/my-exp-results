# Summary of Session 14 (2024‑06‑23)

**Main goal**: Provide realistic weekday schedules that respect a *no‑coffee‑after‑noon* rule and *evening‑only alcohol* rule, plus concrete crash‑snack options, desk‑friendly movement breaks, and low‑effort wind‑down routines.

## Schedule concepts
- Three template flows were proposed: **morning‑momentum**, **slow‑start‑strong‑afternoon**, and **early‑movement**. Each balances work blocks (8:30 – 5:30), meals, coffee timing, and exercise.
- The *morning‑momentum* option was expanded into a detailed timeline (wake 7:00, coffee with breakfast, second coffee before 12:00, lunch 12:30, afternoon reset at 2:30‑3:00, workout 5:30‑6:15, dinner 7:00, drink 8:30). This follows the guidelines in [[concepts/daily-routine-design]] and respects the [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]] rule.
- Backup strategies for midday crashes avoid caffeine: protein‑rich snack, posture reset, brief cold‑water splash.

## Crash‑snack ideas
1. Greek yogurt + granola + berries (protein + fiber).  
2. Apple slices with almond/peanut butter (fat + protein).  
3. Whole‑grain crackers + hummus (savory mini‑meal).

These snacks are designed to sustain energy for >30 minutes.

## Desk‑friendly movement breaks
- **2‑minute reset**: stand, shoulder rolls, arm stretch overhead, walk to hallway/window, sit back.  
- **5‑minute micro‑circuit**: calf raises, slow squats, forward fold; boosts circulation without sweating.

## Evening wind‑down routines (60‑90 min before bed)
1. *Soft lighting* → warm shower → back/hip stretch → 15‑20 min quiet activity (reading, light TV). Keep the drink early, then switch to water or herbal tea. This aligns with [[concepts/wind-down-routine]].
2. *Soft landing*: drink, then tidy/kitchen prep, followed by blanket on couch, journaling or a calm podcast, finishing with 4‑in/6‑out breathing.

Both routines aim to avoid feeling “wired” after an evening [[entities/alcohol]] drink.
