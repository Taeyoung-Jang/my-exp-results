# Summary of Session 36 (2024‑10‑21)

**Context**: The user has started a very early shift (wake‑up at 3:30 am) and wants to maintain steady energy, manage caffeine and naps, and preserve some evening social life while aiming to fall asleep around 7:30–8:00 pm.

### Core Recommendations

1. **Gradual Schedule Shift**  
   - Move bedtime earlier in 15‑20 minute increments every few nights.  
   - Align wake‑up time naturally as the rhythm stabilises.

2. **Light Exposure**  
   - Seek bright light immediately after waking to signal the brain for daytime.  
   - Dim lights and limit screens in the evening, at least an hour before intended sleep.  
   - [[concepts/light-exposure]]

3. **Wind‑down Routine**  
   - Reserve 10 minutes of quiet, low‑stimulus activity before bed to cue sleep.  
   - Consistency is key for the body’s internal clock.  
   - [[concepts/wind-down-routine]]

4. **Caffeine Management**  
   - Limit caffeine to the first half of the shift; stop by **≈1 pm** for a 7:30‑8:00 pm bedtime.  
   - If sensitive, end earlier.  
   - Use a modest coffee **just before a short lunch‑break nap** for a “coffee‑nap” boost.  
   - [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]] • [[concepts/coffee-nap]]

5. **Nap Strategy**  
   - Preferred nap window: **late morning / early afternoon** (around the 11:30 am lunch break).  
   - Keep naps **15‑20 minutes**; longer naps impair nighttime sleep.  
   - A brief nap after work is acceptable only if strictly limited to 15 minutes.  
   - [[concepts/nap-strategy]]

6. **Mid‑Afternoon Slump Alternatives**  
   - Light movement: short walk, stretching, fresh air, or a splash of cold water.  
   - Light snack with protein (e.g., nuts, yogurt).  
   - Change of scenery or brief exposure to bright light.  
   - Avoid additional caffeine after the 1 pm cut‑off.  
   - [[concepts/mid-afternoon-slump-management]]

7. **Evening “Micro‑Evenings”**  
   - Accept that long nights out are limited; focus on a concise, enjoyable routine after work (simple dinner, a show, a chat).  
   - Keep lights bright enough to stay awake during the routine, then dim at least an hour before sleep.  
   - Protect **1–2 evenings per week** for social or personal activities; keep the rest low‑key.  
   - [[concepts/micro-evenings]]

### Practical Timeline Example
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 3:30 am | Wake‑up; expose to bright light, hydrate, light snack |
| 4:00 am | Start work shift |
| 10:30 am | Light snack, optional small coffee (if needed) |
| 11:30 am | Lunch break → **15‑20 min nap** (optional coffee‑nap) |
| 12:15 pm | Resume work |
| 1:00 pm | **Last caffeine** of the day (if any) |
| 3:30 pm | Light movement break to counter slump |
| 5:30 pm | End shift, start wind‑down routine (dim lights, quiet time) |
| 7:30 pm | Aim to be asleep |
