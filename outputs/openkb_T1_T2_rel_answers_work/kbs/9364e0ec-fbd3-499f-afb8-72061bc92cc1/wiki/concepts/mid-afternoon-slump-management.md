## Mid‑Afternoon Slump Management

**Overview**
Managing the dip in alertness that commonly occurs in the early afternoon is crucial for anyone on an early shift. The goal is to restore vigor with minimal impact on the subsequent night’s sleep.

**Key Strategies from [[summaries/session-36_2024-10-21]]**
- **Light exposure**: Step outside or face a bright light source for a few minutes to stimulate the circadian system. [[concepts/light-exposure]]
- **Movement breaks**: Short walks, stretching, or a splash of cold water on the face can quickly raise alertness. [[concepts/early-shift-energy-management]]
- **Protein‑rich snack**: A small bite of nuts, yogurt, or cheese provides steady energy without a sugar crash.
- **Caffeine timing**: Limit caffeine to before ~1 pm. If a boost is needed, a modest coffee just before a brief nap can create a “coffee‑nap” effect. [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]] [[concepts/coffee-nap]]
- **Micro‑nap**: A 15‑20 minute nap during a lunch break (around 11:30 am) offers a quick reset with minimal sleep‑inertia. [[concepts/nap-strategy]]

**Practical Implementation**
1. **Pre‑nap caffeine** – If you take a lunch‑break nap, sip a small coffee right before lying down; the caffeine will peak as you wake.
2. **Post‑nap activity** – After the nap, engage in a brief walk or light stretching to fully shake off sleepiness.
3. **Mid‑afternoon boost** – When the slump returns later in the day, use a quick bout of bright light exposure and a protein snack instead of another caffeine dose.
4. **Evening consistency** – Keep evenings low‑key and dim lights at least an hour before your target bedtime to protect sleep quality. [[concepts/wind-down-routine]]
