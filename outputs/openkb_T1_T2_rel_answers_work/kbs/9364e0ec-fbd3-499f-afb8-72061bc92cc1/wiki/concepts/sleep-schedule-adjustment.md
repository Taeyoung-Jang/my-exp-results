# Sleep Schedule Adjustment

**Definition**: A structured approach to moving bedtime and wake‑time earlier in small, consistent steps to align the circadian rhythm with an early‑shift schedule.

---

## Core Principles (derived from [[summaries/session-36_2024-10-21]])

1. **Incremental Bedtime Shifts**
   - Adjust bedtime by 15–20 minutes every 2–3 nights rather than a large jump.
   - Allows the body’s internal clock to adapt without excessive sleep debt.

2. **Consistent Wake‑up Time**
   - Keep the wake‑up time fixed (e.g., 3:30 am) even on days off to reinforce the new rhythm.

3. **Morning Light Exposure**
   - Seek bright natural light immediately after waking to signal daytime to the suprachiasmatic nucleus.
   - Link: [[concepts/light-exposure]]

4. **Evening Light Management**
   - Dim indoor lighting and reduce screen brightness at least one hour before the target bedtime.

5. **Wind‑down Routine**
   - Allocate ~10 minutes to low‑stimulus activities (reading, stretching, meditation) to cue sleep.
   - Link: [[concepts/wind-down-routine]]

6. **Caffeine Cut‑off**
   - Stop caffeine intake by about 1 pm for a desired 7:30‑8:00 pm sleep onset, allowing metabolic clearance.
   - Link: [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]]

7. **Strategic Napping**
   - Short naps (15‑20 minutes) during late morning/early afternoon, preferably aligned with lunch breaks, to boost alertness without disrupting nighttime sleep.
   - Link: [[concepts/nap-strategy]]

8. **Managing Mid‑Afternoon Slump**
   - Use light movement, fresh air, or a protein snack instead of extra caffeine.
   - Link: [[concepts/mid-afternoon-slump-management]]

9. **Micro‑Evenings**
   - Design brief, enjoyable post‑work routines (light dinner, a short show, brief social contact) to maintain a balanced social life without extending the day.
   - Link: [[concepts/micro-evenings]]

10. **Early‑Shift Energy Management**
    - Combine the above tactics to sustain energy throughout an early shift while preserving sleep quality.
    - Link: [[concepts/early-shift-energy-management]]

## Practical Timeline Example
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 3:30 am | Wake, expose to bright light, hydrate |
| 4:00 am | Begin shift |
| 10:30 am | Light snack, optional small coffee |
| 11:30 am | Lunch + 15‑20 min nap (optional coffee‑nap) |
| 1:00 pm | Last caffeine of day |
| 5:30 pm | End shift, start wind‑down (dim lights) |
| 7:30 pm | Aim to be asleep |

---

## Benefits
- Improves sleep onset latency and overall sleep quality.
- Reduces reliance on high‑dose caffeine.
- Enhances daytime alertness and reduces fatigue crashes.
- Allows for a sustainable, socially satisfying evening routine.
