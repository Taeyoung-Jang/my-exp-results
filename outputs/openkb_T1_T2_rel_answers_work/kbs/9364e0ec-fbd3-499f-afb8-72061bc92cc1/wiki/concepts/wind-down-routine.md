# Wind‑Down Routine

A wind‑down routine is a short, low‑effort sequence of activities performed in the final hour‑to‑hour‑and‑a‑half before bedtime to promote relaxation, signal the body that it’s time for sleep, and avoid feeling wired, especially after alcohol or late‑day caffeine.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-23]])
- **Lighting & ambience**: Dim lights after dinner and at least an hour before you plan to fall asleep to cue melatonin production and reduce blue‑light exposure. This aligns with principles of [[concepts/light-exposure]].
- **Warm shower or gentle stretch**: A warm shower or gentle back and hip stretches help release muscle tension and lower body temperature.
- **Drink timing**: Alcohol should be consumed earlier in the evening (e.g., 8:30 pm); afterward switch to water or herbal tea. Limiting caffeine earlier in the day and respecting a [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]] further supports the routine.
- **Quiet activity**: 10‑15 minutes of reading, light TV, or a calming podcast creates a mental cue that the day is winding down.
- **Tidying / prep**: Light kitchen clean‑up or next‑day planning helps close the day and reduces lingering stress.
- **Breathing or journaling**: Simple breathing exercises (e.g., 4‑in/6‑out) or a few journal lines unload thoughts and calm the mind.
- **Consistency for early shifts**: For those on very early schedules (e.g., waking at 3:30 am), keep the routine consistent even on “micro‑evenings” to reinforce the body’s internal clock.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents a post‑drink or late‑caffeine “wired” state that can interfere with sleep onset.
- Supports a broader [[concepts/sleep-schedule-adjustment]] and [[concepts/early-shift-energy-management]] by providing a reliable nightly cue.
- Complements [[concepts/micro-evenings]] by allowing a brief, enjoyable wind‑down after work while still preserving the night‑time sleep window.

## Related Entities
- [[entities/alcohol]] – timing and moderation are integral to the routine.
- caffeine – while not part of the wind‑down itself, its earlier cutoff enhances the effectiveness of the routine.
