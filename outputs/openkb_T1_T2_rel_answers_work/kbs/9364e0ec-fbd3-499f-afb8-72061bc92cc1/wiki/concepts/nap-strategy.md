# Nap Strategy

**Definition**: A set of practices for using brief, strategically timed naps to sustain alertness and protect nighttime sleep, especially for people on early work shifts.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-36_2024-10-21]])

1. **Timing**
   - Aim for a nap during the **late morning or early afternoon**, ideally aligned with a lunch break (e.g., around **11:30 am**).  
   - This window provides a boost without encroaching on the sleep window that begins around **7:30–8:00 pm**.
2. **Duration**
   - Keep naps **15–20 minutes**.  
   - Longer naps increase sleep inertia and can delay the ability to fall asleep at night.
3. **Placement Relative to Caffeine**
   - If a coffee‑nap is desired, consume a modest amount of caffeine **just before the nap**; the caffeine’s effect peaks as you wake, enhancing alertness.
   - Ensure the caffeine intake still respects the overall **caffeine‑cutoff** (no later than ~1 pm) to avoid bedtime interference.  
   - See [[concepts/coffee-nap]] and [[concepts/caffeine-cutoff]].
4. **Alternative Nap Options**
   - A **very short (≈15 min) nap after work** is permissible only if you can guarantee strict time limits; otherwise, it may jeopardize nighttime sleep.
5. **Integration with Overall Energy Management**
   - Pair the nap strategy with other early‑shift tactics such as light exposure, consistent wind‑down routines, and mid‑afternoon activity breaks.  
   - See [[concepts/early-shift-energy-management]], [[concepts/light-exposure]], [[concepts/wind-down-routine]], and [[concepts/mid-afternoon-slump-management]].

## Practical Example
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 11:30 am | Lunch break → **15‑20 min nap** (optional coffee‑nap) |
| 12:00 pm | Resume work, refreshed |
| 1:00 pm | Last caffeine of the day (if any) |
| 3:30 pm onward | Light movement break to counter slump |
| 7:30 pm | Begin wind‑down routine, lights dimmed |

## Benefits
- Restores alertness without the crash associated with longer naps.
- Supports a stable **sleep‑schedule adjustment** for early wake‑times.
- Reduces reliance on additional caffeine in the afternoon.
