# Weighted Scoring Template

The **Weighted Scoring Template** is a concise, phone‑friendly scoring sheet designed to help renters evaluate and compare multiple flats during viewings. It assigns numeric weights to the most critical criteria, aggregates the scores, and produces an overall rating out of a fixed maximum (9 points in the original version).

## Core Structure
- **Noise** – weight **3** (most important). Scores are derived from the [[concepts/noise-scoring-rubric]] (0‑2 for outside noise, inside noise, and a one‑minute silent test) and then normalized to a 0‑3 scale.
- **Commute** – weight **2**. A simple 0‑2 rating based on walking distance, travel time, and reliability of transport.
- **Building vibe / management** – weight **2**. Evaluates maintenance responsiveness, water pressure, heating, and signs of damp.
- **Neighbours** – weight **1**. Considers turnover, friendliness, and observed noise during the silent test.
- **Costs** – weight **1**. Captures council tax band, typical utility bills, and any hidden fees.

The template looks like this (copy‑paste ready):

```
Noise (weight 3): ___ / 3
Commute (weight 2): ___ / 2
Building vibe/management (weight 2): ___ / 2
Neighbours (weight 1): ___ / 1
Costs (weight 1): ___ / 1
Overall note (one sentence): __________
Total: ___ / 9
```

## How to Use It
1. **Prepare** the checklist before the viewing (see [[concepts/apartment-viewing-checklist]] for the full set of on‑the‑spot tests and numeric fields).
2. **Score each category** immediately after the visit, using the rubric for noise and a quick gut‑level rating for the other items.
3. **Calculate the weighted total** by adding the individual weighted scores. The maximum possible score is 9.
4. **Record a brief note** to capture any qualitative impressions that numbers miss.

## Benefits
- **Consistency**: Fixed weights prevent a flashy kitchen from outweighing a noisy environment.
- **Speed**: The template can be filled in within a minute on a phone.
- **Comparability**: All flats are evaluated on the same scale, making side‑by‑side comparison straightforward.
