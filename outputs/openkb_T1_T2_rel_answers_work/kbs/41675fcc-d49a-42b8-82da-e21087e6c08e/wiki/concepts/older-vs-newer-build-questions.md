## Older vs Newer Build Questions

**Purpose**
- Provide a focused set of questions to reveal age‑related issues that can affect comfort, maintenance costs, and long‑term livability.
- Use these questions alongside the general [[concepts/apartment-viewing-checklist]] and the [[concepts/red-flags-apartment-hunting]] guide to spot hidden problems early.

**When to Use**
- During any flat viewing, first determine whether the building is pre‑1990 (typically older) or post‑2000 (typically newer). Tailor the inquiry set accordingly.
- Record answers in the "Numbers to record" section of the checklist and factor any red flags into the [[concepts/weighted-scoring-template]].

---
### Questions for Older Buildings
| Area | Sample Questions |
|------|-------------------|
| **Wall & Sound Insulation** | • How thick are the interior walls? <br>• Are there known noise‑transfer issues with neighbours? |
| **Heating / Boiler** | • What is the age and make of the boiler? <br>• When was the last service performed? <br>• Are there any recurring boiler failures? |
| **Damp & Moisture** | • Have you observed damp patches around windows, corners, or behind furniture? <br>• Are there any known roof or basement leaks? |
| **Plumbing & Wiring** | • Have there been historic plumbing leaks or pipe bursts? <br>• Is the electrical wiring original or has it been upgraded? |
| **Maintenance History** | • What major repairs have been done in the last 5 years? <br>• Are there upcoming maintenance projects that could affect tenants? |

---
### Questions for Newer Buildings
| Area | Sample Questions |
|------|-------------------|
| **Ventilation & Overheating** | • Does the flat overheat in summer? <br>• How is the mechanical ventilation system designed? |
| **Cladding & Safety** | • Has the building undergone cladding safety assessments? <br>• Are there any pending remediation works? |
| **Soundproofing Materials** | • Is the inter‑unit partition concrete, steel, or lightweight panels? <br>• How effective is the built‑in soundproofing? |
| **Window Design** | • What are the window opening limits? <br>• Is there adequate airflow without compromising security? |
| **Energy Efficiency** | • What is the EPC rating? <br>• Are there smart thermostats or energy‑saving fixtures installed? |

---
### Integrating with the Viewing Workflow
1. **Identify building age** early in the viewing and select the appropriate question set.
2. **Add answers** to the checklist (see [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-13]]) under the “Older‑building questions” or “Newer‑build questions” headings.
3. **Score red flags** using the [[concepts/noise-scoring-rubric]] for noise‑related items and the [[concepts/weighted-scoring-template]] for overall weighting.
4. **Document observations** in the "Overall note" field of the scoring template to capture any qualitative concerns.
