# Summary of Session 02 (2024‑06‑15)

This conversation provides a concise, low‑stress approach to basic emergency preparedness, with a focus on **garage‑friendly storage**, **rotation routines**, and **temperature‑sensitive considerations**.

## Key Recommendations
- **First‑weekend checklist**: ~1 gallon of water per person per day for 3 days, shelf‑stable foods you already eat, flashlight with fresh batteries, charged power bank, essential medications, and a small first‑aid kit. Store everything in an accessible spot, preferably off the floor and in sealed bins.
- **Garage storage tips**: Keep containers off the floor, out of direct sunlight, and use sturdy plastic or metal shelving. Seal food in airtight bins to protect against moisture and critters. *(See [[concepts/garage_storage]])*
- **Rotation routine**: Pull 1‑2 items into regular meals each month and replace them on grocery trips; rotate bottled water every 6–12 months and food every 6–12 months. *(See [[concepts/food_rotation]])*
- **Temperature thresholds**: Prolonged temps > 85 °F (≈30 °C) accelerate flavor loss in water and cause oil‑rich foods to go rancid; freezing can deform cans and split plastic jugs.
- **Garage‑friendly foods**: Plain canned vegetables, beans, lentil soups, canned tomatoes/fruit, white rice, pasta, instant noodles. Keep nut butters and boxed meals indoors if possible.
- **Next practical additions**: Backup phone charger (power bank), extra daily medications, reliable light source, comfort items (toiletries, shelf‑stable snacks).

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Trying to do everything at once – leads to overwhelm and abandoned kits.  
- Buying gear you don’t understand or won’t use.  
- Stashing supplies and never checking or rotating them.
