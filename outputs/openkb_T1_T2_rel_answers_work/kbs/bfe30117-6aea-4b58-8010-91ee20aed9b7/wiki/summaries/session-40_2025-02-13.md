# Summary of session-40_2025-02-13

## Context
User seeks space‑saving organization ideas for a small open living‑room/kitchen condo.

## Furniture evaluation
- **Keep**: full‑size sofa (if not oversized), rolling kitchen cart (if it doesn’t block walkways) – [[entities/rolling_kitchen_cart]].
- **Replace or downsize**: large coffee table → smaller coffee table or nesting tables – [[entities/nesting_tables]]; bulky TV stand → slim low‑profile stand or consider wall‑mount – [[entities/low_profile_tv_stand]]; dining table → small round or drop‑leaf table, store extra chairs.
- Emphasize dual‑purpose or lightweight pieces.

## Layout recommendations
- Pull furniture slightly away from walls to create breathing room.
- Position sofa facing TV with enough clearance behind it.
- Center a modest coffee table, maintaining a 30–36 in clear walkway.
- Locate the dining area in the corner nearest the kitchen.
- Place the rolling cart against the sofa or at the kitchen end to integrate it.
- See [[concepts/layout_planning_for_small_spaces]] for broader guidance.

## Vertical storage without drilling
- Tension rods, leaning ladder shelves – [[entities/ladder_style_leaning_shelf]], over‑the‑door pantry rack – [[entities/over_the_door_pantry_rack]], adhesive hooks – [[entities/adhesive_hooks]].
- Tall, narrow freestanding bookshelf or shelving unit placed against a wall – [[entities/narrow_bookshelf]].

## Sizing guidelines
- Coffee‑table width ≈ 2/3 of sofa width → 50–56 in for an 84 in sofa; depth ≤ 20 in.
- Preserve ~18 in between sofa and table and keep a 30–36 in clear path.
- Round tables reduce corner intrusion; nesting tables add flexibility – [[entities/round_accent_tables]] and [[entities/nesting_tables]].
- Slim TV stand: width 48–60 in, depth 14–16 in – [[entities/low_profile_tv_stand]].

## Product‑type suggestions
- Narrow bookshelf (≤ 12 in deep) – [[entities/narrow_bookshelf]]
- Ladder‑style leaning shelf – [[entities/ladder_style_leaning_shelf]]
- Over‑the‑door pantry rack – [[entities/over_the_door_pantry_rack]]
- Adhesive or over‑door hooks – [[entities/adhesive_hooks]]
- Small nesting tables or round accent tables – [[entities/nesting_tables]], [[entities/round_accent_tables]]
- Low‑profile TV stand – [[entities/low_profile_tv_stand]]
