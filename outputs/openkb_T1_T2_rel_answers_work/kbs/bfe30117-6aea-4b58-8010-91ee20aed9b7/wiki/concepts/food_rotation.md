# Food Rotation

**Definition**: A systematic method for regularly using and replenishing stored emergency supplies to keep them fresh, functional, and ready for use.

---

## Why Rotate Food?
- Prevents waste from expired or degraded items.
- Ensures that emergency supplies remain safe and palatable.
- Reduces the risk of relying on compromised resources during a crisis.

---

## Core Steps (Based on [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]])
1. **Monthly Pull‑In**: Each month, select 1–2 items from your stored inventory and incorporate them into your regular meals.
2. **Replace on Restock**: When you use an item, replace it with a fresh equivalent during your next grocery trip.
3. **Water Rotation**: Rotate bottled water every 6–12 months, storing it in a cool, shaded spot.
4. **Food Rotation Timeline**:
   - **High‑fat items (nut butters, boxed meals)**: Replace every 6 months, ideally kept indoors.
   - **Canned goods & dry staples (beans, rice, pasta)**: Replace every 12 months, safe for garage storage.

---

## Practical Tips
- Keep a simple log or checklist near your storage area to track when each item was added or used.
- Use clear, resealable containers to protect items from moisture and pests.
- Store water containers off the floor and away from direct sunlight to minimize temperature exposure.
