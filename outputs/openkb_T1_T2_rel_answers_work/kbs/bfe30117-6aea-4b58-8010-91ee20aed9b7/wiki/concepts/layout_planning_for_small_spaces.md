# Layout Planning for Small Spaces

**Definition**: A set of strategies for arranging furniture, circulation paths, and storage in confined living environments to maximize usable space, visual openness, and functional flow.

## Core Principles (derived from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]])
1. **Create Breathing Room**
   - Pull furniture slightly away from walls rather than pushing everything flat against them. This creates visual space and improves traffic flow.
2. **Define Clear Pathways**
   - Maintain a minimum of 30–36 inches of unobstructed walkway, especially between entry points and key zones (e.g., kitchen).
   - Keep about 18 inches between a sofa and a coffee table to allow easy access without feeling cramped.
3. **Zone Delineation**
   - Position the sofa facing the TV, leaving enough clearance behind the sofa for movement.
   - Locate the dining area in a corner close to the kitchen to keep the living‑room area clean and linear.
   - Integrate auxiliary pieces (e.g., a rolling kitchen cart) against existing furniture or at the end of a zone rather than letting them float in the middle of traffic.
4. **Scale Furniture to Space**
   - Choose coffee tables or nesting tables that are roughly two‑thirds the width of the sofa (≈ 50–56 in for an 84 in sofa) and keep depth ≤ 20 in.
   - Prefer round or compact tables to reduce corner intrusion.
   - Select a slim TV stand (48–60 in wide, 14–16 in deep) to free floor area.
5. **Vertical & Dual‑Purpose Solutions**
   - Use tall, narrow storage like a [[entities/narrow_bookshelf]] or a [[entities/ladder_style_leaning_shelf]] to keep items off the floor.
   - Opt for furniture that doubles as storage (e.g., ottomans with hidden compartments).

## Practical Tips for Rentals
- Use adhesive hooks and over‑the‑door solutions (e.g., [[entities/over_the_door_pantry_rack]]) to avoid drilling.
- Leaning shelves and tension‑rod systems are removable and leave walls intact.
- Choose lightweight, mobile furniture like a [[entities/rolling_kitchen_cart]] that can be repositioned as needed.
