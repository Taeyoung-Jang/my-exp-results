# Emergency Preparedness

**Definition**: A practical, low‑stress strategy for acquiring and maintaining the essential supplies and knowledge needed to survive short‑term emergencies such as storms, power outages, or supply‑chain disruptions.

---

## Core Elements (as outlined in [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]])

1. **Minimal first‑weekend kit**
   - Water: ~1 gallon per person per day for 3 days.
   - Food: Shelf‑stable items you already eat (canned beans, pasta, rice, nut butter, etc.).
   - Essentials: Flashlight with fresh batteries, charged power bank, essential medications, small first‑aid kit.
   - Store in an easily accessible location; keep everything together for quick grab‑and‑go.

2. **Garage storage best practices** – see [[concepts/garage_storage]]
   - Keep containers off the floor, out of direct sunlight, and sealed in plastic bins or on metal shelving.
   - Protect supplies from temperature extremes and critters.

3. **Rotation routine** – see [[concepts/food_rotation]]
   - Integrate 1‑2 stored items into regular meals each month and replace them on grocery trips.
   - Rotate bottled water every 6–12 months; rotate food every 6–12 months, depending on packaging.

4. **Temperature considerations**
   - Sustained temps above ~85 °F (30 °C) accelerate flavor loss in water and cause oil‑rich foods to go rancid.
   - Freezing can deform cans and split plastic jugs; keep items partially full to allow expansion.

5. **Common pitfalls** – see [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]]
   - Trying to acquire everything at once.
   - Buying gear you don’t understand or won’t use.
   - Stashing supplies and never checking or rotating them.

---

## Practical Next Steps
- Add a backup phone charger (power bank) and a reliable light source.
- Keep a small reserve of daily medications.
- Include comfort items like extra toiletries and shelf‑stable snacks.
