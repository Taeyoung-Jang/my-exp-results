# Round Accent Tables

**Type:** product

Round accent tables are small, typically circular tabletop pieces that can serve as coffee tables, side tables, or supplemental surfaces in compact living spaces. Their rounded shape reduces visual clutter and makes traffic flow smoother because there are no sharp corners to obstruct movement.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]]
- Suggested as a **space‑saving** option for the user’s condo where a 30–36 in clear walkway is needed.
- Recommended over a rectangular coffee table when the main walkway cuts through the room, as the round form "feels the least intrusive."
- Should be compact, ideally no deeper than 20 in, and can be paired with a nesting set for flexibility.
- Works well alongside other rental‑friendly pieces such as [[entities/narrow_bookshelf]], [[entities/over_the_door_pantry_rack]], and [[entities/low_profile_tv_stand]].
