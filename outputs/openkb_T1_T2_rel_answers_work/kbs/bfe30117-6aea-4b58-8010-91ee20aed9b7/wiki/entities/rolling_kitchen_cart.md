# Rolling Kitchen Cart

## Description
A wheeled, freestanding storage unit often used in kitchens to hold appliances, pantry items, or serve as an “appliance garage.”

## Key points from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]]
- Mentioned as a piece the user currently owns.
- Recommended to keep if it does not block walkways.
- Suggested placement: against the back of the sofa or at the end of the kitchen counter to integrate it into the flow.
- Can double as an appliance garage, keeping tops clear while storing bulkier items underneath.

## Role in space‑saving organization
- Provides **vertical** storage without permanent fixtures, aligning with [[concepts/vertical_storage_without_drilling]].
- Supports [[concepts/space_saving_organization]] by consolidating items in a mobile unit.
- Fits into the overall [[concepts/layout_planning_for_small_spaces]] strategy by being movable and adaptable.
