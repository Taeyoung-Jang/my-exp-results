## Nesting Tables

**Type:** product

Nesting tables are a set of smaller tables that fit one inside another, allowing them to be stored compactly when not in use and spread out as needed. In the context of a small open‑plan living‑room/kitchen condo, they are recommended as a space‑saving alternative to a single large coffee table.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]]
- **Size guidance:** For an 84‑inch sofa, a single coffee table of about 50–56 in wide and 20 in deep is ideal; nesting tables provide a flexible way to stay within this footprint while still offering surface area when needed.
- **Shape recommendation:** Round or small rectangular nesting tables are suggested because they minimize corner intrusion and help maintain a clear 30–36 in walkway.
- **Flexibility:** They can be pulled apart for use as separate side tables or pushed together to occupy minimal floor space, supporting the goal of keeping a clear path through the room.
- **Rental‑friendly:** No permanent modifications are required; the tables can be moved or removed easily, fitting the rental‑friendly storage approach.

### Usage tips
1. **Measure clearance:** Ensure at least 18 in between the sofa and the outermost nesting table, and maintain a 30–36 in clear path for traffic.
2. **Combine with vertical storage:** Pair nesting tables with a [[entities/narrow_bookshelf]] or [[entities/ladder_style_leaning_shelf]] to keep items off the floor.
3. **Store extra chairs:** When not needed, extra dining chairs can be tucked under a nesting table set, further reducing clutter.
