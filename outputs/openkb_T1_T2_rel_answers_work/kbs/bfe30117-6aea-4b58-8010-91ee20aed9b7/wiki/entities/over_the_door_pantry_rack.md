# Over‑the‑Door Pantry Rack

**Type:** product

An over‑the‑door pantry rack is a hanging storage solution that attaches to the door of a pantry or closet, providing vertical shelves for cans, jars, and other kitchen items without the need for drilling or permanent mounting.  It is especially useful in rentals or small‑space living where wall‑mounts are impractical.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]]
- Recommended as a cheap vertical storage idea that does **not** require drilling holes.
- Suitable for a condo with limited wall space and a small pantry closet.
- Helps keep counters clear by moving pantry items onto the door, supporting a clutter‑free aesthetic.

## Usage tips
1. Choose a rack with adjustable shelves to fit various container heights.
2. Ensure the pantry or closet door can support the weight of stocked items.
3. Pair with clear containers or baskets to maintain a tidy look.
