# Narrow Bookshelf

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A tall, freestanding bookshelf with a depth of about one foot, ideal for small rentals where floor space is at a premium.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]]**
- Recommended as a vertical storage option that requires no drilling.
- Provides pantry‑like storage while keeping the room feeling open.
- Works well in open living‑room/kitchen combos with limited wall space.
- Often paired with other rental‑friendly storage solutions such as [[entities/ladder_style_leaning_shelf]] and [[entities/over_the_door_pantry_rack]].

**Related concepts**
- [[concepts/space_saving_organization]]
- [[concepts/vertical_storage_without_drilling]]
- [[concepts/layout_planning_for_small_spaces]]

**Usage tips**
- Position the bookshelf against a wall; no anchors or screws needed.
- Keep the depth under 12 inches to avoid obstructing walkways.
- Use clear containers or baskets on the shelves to maintain a tidy appearance.
- Combine with [[entities/adhesive_hooks]] for added hanging storage without holes.
