## Garage

A **garage** is a building or part of a residence used for parking vehicles and, in the context of emergency preparedness, for storing supplies. The conversation in [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]] highlights the garage as a common, low‑cost location for keeping water, food, and other emergency items.

### Key Facts from the Document
- **Temperature sensitivity**: Prolonged heat above the mid‑80s °F (≈30 °C) can shorten the shelf life of bottled water and oil‑rich foods, while freezing can damage cans and plastic containers.
- **Storage recommendations**: Keep supplies off the floor, out of direct sunlight, and in sealed plastic bins or on metal shelving to protect against moisture, critters, and temperature swings.
- **Rotation routine**: Rotate bottled water every 6–12 months and food every 6–12 months, pulling a few items into regular meals each month.
- **Garage‑friendly foods**: Plain canned vegetables, beans, lentil soups, canned tomatoes/fruit, white rice, pasta, and instant noodles are resilient to temperature fluctuations.
