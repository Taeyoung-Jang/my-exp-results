# Low‑Profile TV Stand

A **low‑profile TV stand** is a shallow, narrow piece of furniture meant to hold a television while occupying minimal floor space. In the context of the [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-13]] conversation, it was recommended as a replacement for a bulky TV stand in a compact condo.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Ideal dimensions:** 48–60 in wide and only 14–16 in deep, providing enough surface area without crowding the room.
- **Purpose:** Frees up floor area, contributes to a lighter visual aesthetic, and supports the overall **space‑saving organization** strategy.
- **Placement:** Should sit opposite an 84 in sofa, preserving a 30–36 in clear walkway between furniture pieces.
- **Alternative:** Wall‑mounting the TV was mentioned, but for renters who cannot drill, a low‑profile stand offers a practical, non‑permanent solution.

## Usage Tips for Renters
1. **Measure your space** before purchasing to ensure the stand fits within the recommended width and depth.
2. **Choose a stand with hidden storage** (e.g., shallow drawers) to keep media accessories tidy.
3. **Opt for a freestanding model** that can be moved easily if you need to re‑arrange or move out.
