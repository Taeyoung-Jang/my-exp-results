# The City & The City

*The City & The City* is a 2009 novel by author China Miéville. It presents a uniquely [[concepts/weird-worlds]] premise: two distinct cities, Besźel and Ul Qoma, coexist in the same physical location, with residents trained to *unsee* the other city’s inhabitants. The story follows a detective investigating a murder that straddles both jurisdictions, blending noir mystery with speculative world‑building.
