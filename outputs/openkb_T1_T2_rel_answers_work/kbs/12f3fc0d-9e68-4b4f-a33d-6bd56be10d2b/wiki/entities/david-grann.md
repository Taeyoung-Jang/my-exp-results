# David Grann

David Grann is an American journalist and author celebrated for his narrative nonfiction works that blend investigative reporting with novelistic storytelling.  He is best known for books such as *The Lost City of Z* and *Killers of the Flower Moon*, both of which have been praised for their [[concepts/propulsive-pacing]] and [[concepts/narrative-nonfiction]] style.

## Key facts from session-35_2024-12-13
- Mentioned as the author of **The Lost City of Z**, a recommended *expedition/adventure* title that “tracks Percy Fawcett’s final Amazon expedition and Grann’s attempt to retrace it.”
- The book is highlighted for its “mix of adventure, mystery, and atmospheric detail” and noted to have a strong audiobook narration that captures both timelines smoothly.
- Grann’s work is positioned alongside other micro‑history and story‑driven nonfiction recommendations such as *The Ghost Map* and *Five Days at Memorial*.
