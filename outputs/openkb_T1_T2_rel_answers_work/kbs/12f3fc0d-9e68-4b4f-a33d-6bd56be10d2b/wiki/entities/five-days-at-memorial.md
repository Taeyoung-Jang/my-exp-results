# Five Days at Memorial

**Type:** Work (nonfiction)

**Author:** [[entities/sheri-fink]]

**Published:** 2015

**Synopsis**
Five Days at Memorial chronicles the harrowing days after Hurricane Katrina when Memorial Medical Center in New Orleans was flooded, power‑lost, and forced to make life‑and‑death decisions.  The book zeroes in on a single, intense period, making it a classic example of **[[concepts/microhistory]]**.

**Key facts from the conversation (session‑35)**
- Recommended as the top **disaster/trial‑style** pick for readers who love narrative‑driven nonfiction.
- Praised for its **[[concepts/propulsive-pacing]]**, keeping the story moving like a thriller.
- Highlighted for its excellent **[[concepts/audiobook-narration]]**; the narrator maintains a steady, calm rhythm that helps listeners stay oriented during chaotic scenes.
- Described as a “Larson‑adjacent” read, offering the same novel‑like momentum that Erik Larson achieves in his works.
