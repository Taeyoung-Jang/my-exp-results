# The River of Doubt

**Author:** [[entities/candice-millard]]

**Genre:** Narrative nonfiction, adventure, microhistory

**Publication:** 2005 (first edition)

**Synopsis**
The River of Doubt recounts former President Theodore Roosevelt’s 1913–1914 Amazon expedition, focusing on the perilous journey down the uncharted "River of Doubt" and the near‑fatal challenges the party faced. Millard blends meticulous research with a novel‑like narrative, delivering a tightly focused story that reads like a thriller.

**Key Themes & Concepts**
- The work exemplifies [[concepts/narrative-nonfiction]] by turning a historical episode into a compelling story.
- It is a classic case of [[concepts/microhistory]], zeroing in on a single expedition rather than a broad biography.
- Millard’s prose is noted for its [[concepts/propulsive-pacing]], keeping readers turning pages.
- The audiobook production is praised for its engaging [[concepts/audiobook-narration]] that captures the tension of the jungle trek.

**Audiobook**
Listeners appreciate the narrator’s ability to convey both the excitement of the adventure and the historical context without over‑dramatizing, making it a recommended listen for fans of story‑driven nonfiction.
