# Candice Millard

Candice Millard is an American writer celebrated for her narrative nonfiction that brings historical events to life with the pacing of a novel. Her most noted work mentioned in the knowledge base is **_The River of Doubt_**, an account of Theodore Roosevelt’s harrowing Amazon expedition.

## Key Works
- **_The River of Doubt_** – Explores Roosevelt’s 1913–1914 Amazon journey; praised for its immersive storytelling and strong audiobook production. [[entities/the-river-of-doubt]]

## Themes & Style
- **Narrative nonfiction**: Millard blends rigorous research with storytelling techniques, making complex histories accessible and engaging.
- **Microhistory focus**: She zeroes in on a single, dramatic episode—Roosevelt’s expedition—providing depth without overwhelming breadth.
- **Audiobook appeal**: The production quality and narrator’s delivery are frequently cited as enhancing the reading experience.
