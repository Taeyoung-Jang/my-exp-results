# The Lost City of Z

**Type:** work  
**Author:** [[entities/david-grann]]  
**Subject:** The final Amazon expedition of explorer Percy Fawcett.

**Overview**
*The Lost City of Z* tells the true‑story investigation by journalist David Grann into the early‑20th‑century British explorer Percy Fawcett and his obsessive quest to find a rumored lost city in the Amazon jungle. The book weaves archival research, travelogue, and present‑day retracing of Fawcett’s route into a narrative that reads like a novel.
