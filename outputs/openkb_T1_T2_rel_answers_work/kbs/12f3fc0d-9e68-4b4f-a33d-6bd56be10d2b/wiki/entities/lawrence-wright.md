## Lawrence Wright

Lawrence Wright is an American journalist, author, and professor of journalism best known for his Pulitzer‑winning book **_The Looming Tower_** (2006), a detailed narrative of the events leading up to the September 11 attacks.

### Key Works
- **_The Looming Tower_** – a deep‑dive microhistory of the rise of al‑Qaeda and the U.S. intelligence community’s response, praised for its investigative rigor and novelistic pacing.

### Mention in [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-13]]
In the December 13, 2024 conversation, Wright’s *The Looming Tower* was recommended as a **microhistory** that captures a pivotal political moment (the run‑up to 9/11).
