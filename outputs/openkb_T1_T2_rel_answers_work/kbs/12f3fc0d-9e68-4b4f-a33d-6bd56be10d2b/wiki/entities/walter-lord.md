# Walter Lord

Walter Lord (1917‑2002) was an American writer celebrated for his vivid, story‑driven accounts of historical events. His most famous work, *A Night to Remember*, chronicles the sinking of the Titanic with a focus on personal testimonies and minute‑by‑minute detail, making the disaster feel immediate and cinematic.

## Key Facts from Session 35
- Mentioned in the recommendation list as a classic microhistory that reads like a novel.
- *A Night to Remember* was highlighted for its **propulsive pacing** and suitability as an engaging audiobook, with a clean, concise narration that makes it easy to binge.
- The book exemplifies [[concepts/narrative-nonfiction]] and [[concepts/microhistory]], showing how a single event can be explored in depth without overwhelming the reader.
- The session linked his work to the broader theme of **story‑centric nonfiction** alongside titles such as *Five Days at Memorial* and *The River of Doubt*.
