# The Looming Tower

**Type:** Work (nonfiction)

**Author:** [[entities/lawrence-wright]]

**Synopsis** – *The Looming Tower* chronicles the development of al‑Qaeda and the events leading up to the September 11, 2001 attacks. It weaves together investigative journalism, biography, and political history to create a tightly plotted narrative that reads like a thriller.
