# A Night to Remember

**Author:** [[entities/walter-lord]]

**Genre:** Narrative nonfiction, microhistory

**First published:** 1955

**Subject:** The sinking of the RMS Titanic on 15 April 1912.

---

## Overview
"A Night to Remember" is a classic work of **[[concepts/microhistory]]** that reconstructs the Titanic disaster minute‑by‑minute.  Walter Lord weaves eyewitness testimonies, official reports, and personal letters into a tightly paced narrative that reads like a thriller.  The book’s **[[concepts/propulsive-pacing]]** and **[[concepts/narrative-nonfiction]]** style have made it a benchmark for disaster storytelling.

## Audiobook
The audiobook version is noted for being **short, cleanly narrated, and very easy to binge**, aligning with the principles of **[[concepts/audiobook-narration]]**—a clear voice that maintains tension without melodrama.
