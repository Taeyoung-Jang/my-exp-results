# Children of Time

**Type:** Work (novel)
**Author:** [[entities/adrian-tchaikovsky]]
**Genre:** Science fiction, hard‑science, speculative evolution

### Key points from session‑14 (2024‑06‑15)
- Recommended for its [[concepts/weird-worlds]] and thoughtful [[concepts/big-ideas]] about an evolving alien ecosystem.
- Described as “alien ecology that turns out to be surprisingly beautiful and clever”, not focused on body‑horror.
- Highlighted as a readable, engaging story with strong momentum, though not as fast‑paced as thrillers like [[entities/the-fold]]; still praised for its page‑turning quality.
- Suggested as a follow‑up to *The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet* for a palate‑cleanser after heavier reads.

### Related works
- Sequel *Children of Ruin* ([[entities/children-of-ruin]]) continues the theme with a slightly moodier tone.
