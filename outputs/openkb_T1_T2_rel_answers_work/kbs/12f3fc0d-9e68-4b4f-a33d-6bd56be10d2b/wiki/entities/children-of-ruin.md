# Children of Ruin

*Children of Ruin* is the sequel to [[entities/children-of-time]] by [[entities/adrian-tchaikovsky]]. It continues the series' focus on expansive [[concepts/big-ideas]] and richly imagined [[concepts/weird-worlds]], delving further into alien ecology while adopting a somewhat moodier, slower‑paced narrative compared to its predecessor.

In the conversation [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]], the assistant suggested *Children of Ruin* as a direct follow‑up for readers who enjoyed *Children of Time*, emphasizing its continuation of the ambitious world‑building and the shift toward a slightly more contemplative tone.

**Key Points**
- **Author**: [[entities/adrian-tchaikovsky]]
- **Series**: Sequel to *Children of Time* ([[entities/children-of-time]])
- **Themes**: Big‑idea scientific speculation, alien ecology, evolutionary narratives
- **Tone**: Still inventive but moodier and less propulsive than some other recommendations
- **Recommendation Context**: Presented as an option for readers wanting to stay in the same speculative universe before moving to a lighter palate‑cleanser like *The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet*.
