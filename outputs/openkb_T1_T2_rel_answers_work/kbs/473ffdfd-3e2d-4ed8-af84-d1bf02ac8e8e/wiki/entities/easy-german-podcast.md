# Easy German Podcast

**Type:** Work (podcast series)

**Description:** Easy German Podcast is a language‑learning podcast that provides short, native‑speed conversations and interviews in German, often accompanied by transcripts. It is geared toward learners who already have solid reading skills and want to improve listening comprehension.

## Role in the 4‑Week Listening Routine
- **Week 1:** Used as the primary weekday resource. Listeners start at 0.9× speed, skim the transcript after the first listen, then replay at the same speed. This aligns with the [[concepts/supported-listening]] approach.
- **Week 2:** Speed is increased to 1.0×, with transcripts consulted only after a full listen, reinforcing the shift toward [[concepts/progressive-speed-adjustment]].
- **Week 3:** Listeners hear the episode at native speed without any prior text support; transcripts are consulted after listening, and key sections are shadowed—a practice linked to [[concepts/shadowing-practice]].
- **Week 4:** The podcast is consumed at full speed with minimal text aid, serving as a diagnostic tool to gauge native‑speed comprehension.

## Associated Resources and Alternatives
- **Alternative podcasts** mentioned in the plan: Slow German Podcast, Deutsch – Warum Nicht?, Easy German Shorts.
- **Related entities** in the weekly plan include [[entities/kurzgesagt-deutsch]], [[entities/mr-wissen2go]], [[entities/deutsche-welle]], and TV series like [[entities/dark-tv]] and [[entities/charite-tv]].
