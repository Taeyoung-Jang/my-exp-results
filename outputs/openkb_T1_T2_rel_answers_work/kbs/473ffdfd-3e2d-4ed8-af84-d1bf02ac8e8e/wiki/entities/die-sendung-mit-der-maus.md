# Die Sendung mit der Maus

**Type:** work (television program)

**Overview**
Die Sendung mit der Maus is a long‑running German children’s TV show produced by WDR. It combines animated short stories, real‑world footage, and simple explanations, using clear, everyday German that is especially suitable for language learners.

**Relevance to the language‑learning plan**
- Recommended for the **Saturday** sessions in **Week 2** and **Week 3** of the progressive routine described in [[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]].
- The suggested format: watch the first half **with German subtitles** to build confidence, then re‑watch the second half **without subtitles** to practice pure listening. This mirrors the [[concepts/progressive-speed-adjustment]] strategy of gradually removing textual support.
- Serves as an example of **[[concepts/supported-listening]]**, providing a low‑pressure, enjoyable immersion activity that helps avoid burnout.

**Suggested alternatives**
If the show’s style isn’t appealing, the plan also lists other German works for weekend immersion, such as [[entities/dark-tv]] and [[entities/charite-tv]].

**Key takeaways**
- Acts as a bridge between short daily clips and longer native‑speed content.
- Encourages learners to experience native‑speed German in a context that is entertaining and linguistically accessible.
- Fits neatly into the overall framework of moving from heavy text support toward more natural listening, complementing other resources like [[entities/easy-german-podcast]], [[entities/kurzgesagt-deutsch]], and [[entities/mr-wissen2go]].
