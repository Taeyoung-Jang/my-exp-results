# Kurzgesagt auf Deutsch

Kurzgesagt auf Deutsch is the German‑language branch of the popular science‑animation channel Kurzgesagt.  In the **session‑07_2025‑06‑15** language‑learning plan it is used as a core listening resource for progressive ear training.

## How it is used in the weekly routine
- **Week 1 (Tuesday)** – 20‑25 min: watch the first half of a short video at **0.9× speed** with German subtitles, then replay the same half **without subtitles**.
- **Week 2 (Tuesday)** – 20‑25 min: watch at **1.0× speed**, keeping subtitles on for the first **30 seconds** only, then continue unsubtitled.
- **Week 3 (Tuesday)** – 20‑25 min: full‑speed video **with no subtitles**; after finishing, rewatch the first minute **with subtitles** to confirm understanding.
- **Week 4 (Tuesday)** – 20‑25 min: full‑speed video **without subtitles**; if the material feels too fast, the rule is to **shorten the clip** rather than slow it down.
