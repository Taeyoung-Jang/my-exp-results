# Tatort (TV Series)

Tatort is a flagship German crime‑drama television series that has aired since 1970. Episodes are typically 90 minutes long and feature self‑contained investigations by different regional police teams. The series is renowned for its high production values, realistic dialogue, and a wide variety of accents and speaking speeds, making it a valuable resource for language learners.

## Role in the language‑learning routine (session‑07_2025‑06‑15)
- **Week 1, Saturday:** A single scene from *Tatort* is used for the first supported‑listening block. Learners watch the scene first with German subtitles at normal speed (1.0×) and then re‑watch it without subtitles to train ear‑to‑eye transfer.
- **Week 2, Saturday:** The series appears as an alternative to the suggested *Dark* episode, offering another authentic listening source.
- **Week 4, Saturday:** While the primary recommendation is a freely chosen German show, *Tatort* is listed among the optional alternatives for immersive, native‑speed viewing.

## How it supports learning concepts
- Demonstrates **[[concepts/supported-listening]]** by providing a subtitled first pass before a pure‑listening second pass.
- Illustrates **[[concepts/progressive-speed-adjustment]]** as learners shift from slowed or subtitled exposure to full‑speed, no‑subtitle viewing across the four‑week plan.
- Can be paired with **[[concepts/shadowing-practice]]**: learners may repeat short dialogue snippets to improve rhythm and pronunciation.
