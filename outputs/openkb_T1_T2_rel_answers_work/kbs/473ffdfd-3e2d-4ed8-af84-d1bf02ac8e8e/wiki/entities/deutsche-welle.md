# Deutsche Welle

Deutsche Welle (DW) is Germany’s public international broadcaster, providing news, analysis, and language‑learning resources in multiple languages, including German.

## Role in the weekly German listening plan (session‑07_2025‑06‑15)
- **Source for authentic audio**: The plan uses DW’s *Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten* and regular news clips as core listening material.
- **Supported‑listening anchor**: In Week 1, DW content is presented at 1.0× speed because it is already slowed, fitting the [[concepts/supported-listening]] stage.
- **Progressive‑speed adjustment**: Across Weeks 2‑4 the DW clips shift from occasional transcript checks to pure 1.0× listening, illustrating the [[concepts/progressive-speed-adjustment]] approach.
- **Complement to shadowing**: While the plan emphasizes shadowing with podcasts and YouTube videos, DW news provides additional material for rhythm and intonation practice, supporting the overall [[concepts/shadowing-practice]] strategy.
