# Dark (TV Series)

**Type:** work (television series)

**Overview**
Dark is a German science‑fiction thriller that follows intertwined families in the fictional town of Winden as they uncover a time‑travel conspiracy. In the context of the document *session‑07_2025‑06‑15*, Dark is leveraged as a native‑speed listening resource for immersive weekend practice.

**Role in the language‑learning routine**
- Featured in **Week 1**, **Week 3**, and **Week 4** as a weekend activity where learners watch a full scene or episode.
- The routine starts with German subtitles on the first viewing and then repeats the same material **without subtitles** to build comprehension, aligning with the [[concepts/progressive-speed-adjustment]] approach.
- The series provides authentic, natural‑speed German dialogue, supporting the transition from [[concepts/supported-listening]] to full‑speed immersion.
- Learners may also apply [[concepts/shadowing-practice]] by mimicking phrases from the show after the initial listening pass.

**Suggested usage pattern**
1. **First pass** – watch the selected scene/episode at 1.0× speed with German subtitles.
2. **Second pass** – replay the same segment without subtitles to test comprehension.
3. **Optional** – shadow a short dialogue segment to reinforce rhythm and intonation.
4. **After‑listening** – skim the transcript or subtitles only if comprehension gaps remain, keeping the focus on auditory processing.
