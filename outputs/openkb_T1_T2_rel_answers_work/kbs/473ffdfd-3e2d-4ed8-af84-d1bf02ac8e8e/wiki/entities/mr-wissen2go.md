## MrWissen2Go (work)

**Type:** work (educational YouTube channel)

**Description:** MrWissen2Go produces short, fact‑based video essays in German covering history, science, and current affairs. It is frequently recommended as a listening resource for intermediate‑advanced German learners.

### Key facts from the weekly language‑learning plan
- Featured as a core listening resource in the **supported listening** approach of the four‑week program outlined in [[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]].
- Used in Week 1 (Thursday) at 0.9× speed with a transcript check, then again at normal speed (1.0×) in later weeks.
- Serves as a practical example for applying [[concepts/progressive-speed-adjustment]] and [[concepts/shadowing-practice]] during the routine.

### Related concepts and entities
- Part of the **supported listening** strategy: [[concepts/supported-listening]].
- Helps develop **shadowing practice** skills: [[concepts/shadowing-practice]].
- Mentioned alongside other German learning resources such as [[entities/easy-german-podcast]], [[entities/kurzgesagt-deutsch]], [[entities/dark-tv]], and [[entities/charite-tv]].
