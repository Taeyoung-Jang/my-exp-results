# Progressive Speed Adjustment

**Definition**
Progressive Speed Adjustment is a structured approach that gradually shifts listening practice from slowed audio with heavy transcript support to full native‑speed exposure with minimal textual aid. The progression is typically organized in four weekly phases, each with explicit rules for playback speed (e.g., 0.9× vs 1.0×) and when to use subtitles or transcripts.

**Key Elements**
- **Supported listening** in the first week (0.9× speed, transcript skim after the first pass) – see [[concepts/supported-listening]].
- **Speed increase** in week 2, moving to 1.0× for most sessions while still allowing occasional subtitle cues.
- **After‑the‑fact transcript use** in week 3, where transcripts are consulted only after a full‑speed listen, often combined with [[concepts/shadowing-practice]].
- **Native‑speed comfort** in week 4, with pure 1.0× listening and only brief text checks for diagnostics.

**Typical Weekly Schedule** (derived from [[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]])
- **Week 1** – slowed audio (0.9×) with immediate transcript support; resources include [[entities/easy-german-podcast]], [[entities/kurzgesagt-deutsch]], and [[entities/mr-wissen2go]].
- **Week 2** – normal speed (1.0×) for most days; subtitles are limited to the first 30 seconds of a video.
- **Week 3** – full‑speed listening; transcripts are consulted only after listening; includes a shadowing segment each Thursday.
- **Week 4** – sustained native‑speed exposure; subtitles are used only when truly stuck; weekend sessions feature longer shows such as [[entities/dark-tv]] or [[entities/charite-tv]].

**Why it works**
The gradual reduction of external scaffolding forces the auditory system to adapt, improving phonological mapping and real‑time comprehension. Short, high‑frequency bursts of native‑speed material are more effective than prolonged slowed sessions because they preserve natural prosody while still allowing the learner to repeat challenging segments.

**Related concepts**
- [[concepts/supported-listening]] – the foundation phase of the progression.
- [[concepts/shadowing-practice]] – used especially in weeks 3‑4 to reinforce rhythm and intonation.

**Implementation tips**
1. Keep daily blocks short (20‑30 min) to maintain consistency.
2. When a clip feels too fast, shorten its length rather than reducing speed.
3. Track progress by noting how often you need to consult a transcript; the goal is a steady decline.
