# Summary of session‑07_2025-06-15

## Core ideas
- Design a **progressive listening routine** that moves from **[[concepts/supported-listening]]** (slow speed, transcripts) to **native‑speed comprehension**.
- Blend three pillars each week:  
  - **Daily short exposure** (20‑30 min) for habit formation.  
  - **Focused practice** (shadowing, transcript review) to build active listening skills – see **[[concepts/shadowing-practice]]**.  
  - **Enjoyable immersion** (shows, longer podcasts) on weekends to avoid **burnout**.
- Use **speed adjustments** (0.9× vs 1.0×) and **text scaffolding** (transcripts, subtitles) as explicit rules, shifting their role each week.  
- Provide **resource families** with 2‑3 alternatives for each activity:  
  - [[entities/easy-german-podcast]]  
  - [[entities/kurzgesagt-deutsch]]  
  - [[entities/mr-wissen2go]]  
  - [[entities/deutsche-welle]]  
  - [[entities/dark-tv]], [[entities/charite-tv]], [[entities/tatort-tv]], [[entities/die-sendung-mit-der-maus]]
- Include a **fallback strategy**: shorten clips rather than slowing them when material feels too fast.

## Weekly progression
| Week | Goal | Typical weekday activity | Weekend focus |
|------|------|--------------------------|----------------|
| **1** | Supported listening | 0.9× speed, transcript skim after first listen | Subtitled scene + repeat without subtitles |
| **2** | Normal‑speed push | 1.0× speed, transcript only after full listen | Mixed subtitled/unsubtitled viewing |
| **3** | Transcripts → after‑the‑fact | Full‑speed listening, skim transcript later, shadowing | Full episode with subtitles first half only |
| **4** | Native‑speed comfort | Pure 1.0× listening, minimal text, short‑clip repeats | Free‑choice show, subtitles only when stuck |

## Daily structure (example for Week 1)
- **Monday:** [[entities/easy-german-podcast]] segment, 0.9×, skim transcript, re‑listen.  
- **Tuesday:** [[entities/kurzgesagt-deutsch]] video, first half at 0.9× with subtitles, then same half without subtitles.  
- **Wednesday:** [[entities/deutsche-welle]] *Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten* at 1.0×, double listen, transcript after both.  
- **Thursday:** [[entities/mr-wissen2go]] clip, 0.9× → transcript → 1.0×.  
- **Friday:** [[entities/easy-german-podcast]] street interview, 1.0×, subtitles on first minute only.  
- **Saturday:** One scene from [[entities/dark-tv]] or [[entities/tatort-tv]], subtitles first pass, none second.  
- **Sunday:** Review any week‑long item, pure 1.0× listening.

(Weeks 2‑4 follow the same day‑by‑day pattern with updated speed/subtitle rules as outlined above.)

## Takeaways
1. **Start slow, transition fast.** The plan uses intentional speed changes to train auditory perception.  
2. **Make text a diagnostic, not a crutch.** Transcripts/subtitles move from primary aid to occasional check‑ins.  
3. **Keep sessions short and varied** to sustain motivation and avoid overload.  
4. **Iterate resources** – the provided alternatives ensure flexibility and personal preference.  
5. **Measure progress** by reducing reliance on text and increasing pure‑listening days.
