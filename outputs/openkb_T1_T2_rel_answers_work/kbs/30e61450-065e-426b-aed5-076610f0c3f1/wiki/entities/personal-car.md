# Personal Car

**Type:** product

**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-34_2025-04-11]]

## Overview
The personal car is presented in the session‑34 discussion as the **default commuting mode** for users who prioritize structure, privacy, predictability, and door‑to‑door convenience. It satisfies the *commuting‑preferences* of individuals who favor high control over their travel environment and minimal interaction with strangers.

## Key facts from the document
- **Primary role:** Serves as the baseline commuting option when the user wants a structured, fast, and private journey.
- **Personality match:** Aligns with answer patterns dominated by **A** choices in the 12‑question checklist (structured, solitary, predictable, convenience‑focused).
- **Backup relationship:** When the personal car is unavailable, the recommended **backup** is [[entities/ride-share]], which mimics the car’s privacy and convenience without the need to drive.
- **Last‑resort option:** If both the car and ride‑share are inaccessible, the user defaults to [[entities/public-transit]] as a fallback.
- **Scenario usage:** In bad weather, car‑in‑the‑shop, or post‑work exhaustion scenarios, the personal car is the preferred choice if available; otherwise, ride‑share is selected.
