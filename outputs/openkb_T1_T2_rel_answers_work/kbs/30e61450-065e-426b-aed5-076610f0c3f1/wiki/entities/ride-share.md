# Ride‑share

**Type:** product

Ride‑share refers to on‑demand transportation services accessed through mobile apps that connect passengers with drivers using personal vehicles. In the context of the *session‑34_2025‑04‑11* discussion, ride‑share is presented as the preferred backup commuting option for users who value privacy, door‑to‑door convenience, and flexibility without the need to drive themselves.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-34_2025-04-11]]
- **Backup plan:** Suggested as the primary alternative to a personal car when the latter is unavailable or when the user is exhausted, because it retains the personal‑space feel of driving.
- **Scenario suitability:** Ideal for bad‑weather situations and car‑in‑the‑shop scenarios, matching the user’s desire for a controlled, comfortable commute.
- **Relation to concepts:** Aligns with [[concepts/commuting-preferences]] (provides convenience and privacy), fallback‑commuting‑strategies (used as a second‑tier option), and the mode‑recommendation‑matrix that maps questionnaire answers to ride‑share as a recommended mode.

## Related entities
- personal‑car – the default commuting mode for users seeking full control.
- public‑transit – listed as a last‑resort option when neither personal car nor ride‑share is viable.
