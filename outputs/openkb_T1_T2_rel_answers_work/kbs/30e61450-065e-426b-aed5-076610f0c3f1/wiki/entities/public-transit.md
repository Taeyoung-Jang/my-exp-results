# Public Transit

**Type:** product

Public transit refers to a collective transportation service that operates on fixed routes and schedules, typically using buses, trains, subways, or trams. In the context of the *session‑34_2025‑04‑11* document, public transit is presented as one of the commuting mode options evaluated by a personality‑based checklist.

## Key facts from the document
- **Mode in the recommendation matrix** – Listed as a fallback option when neither a personal car nor a ride‑share is available. [[concepts/fallback-commuting-strategies]]
- **Position in the checklist** – Associated with answers that favor flexibility, moderate cost, and a willingness to be around strangers. [[concepts/mode-recommendation-matrix]]
- **Mood impact** – Suggested for users who score more "B" answers (moderate structure, openness to social environment) rather than the dominant "A" pattern that drives a personal car preference. [[concepts/commuting-preferences]]
