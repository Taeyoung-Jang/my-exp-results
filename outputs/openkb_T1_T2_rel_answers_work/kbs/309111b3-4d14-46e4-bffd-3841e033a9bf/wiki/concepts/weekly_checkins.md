# Weekly Check‑ins

**Definition**: A recurring, short‑duration coordination moment—either in‑person or via a group chat—where community gardeners share observations (e.g., pests, watering needs, plant health) and align on immediate actions.

**Key Elements from [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-05]]**
- Conducted **weekly** to keep the garden’s rhythm predictable.
- Can be a quick face‑to‑face huddle at the garden entrance or a brief digital message thread.
- Serves as the primary channel for flagging time‑sensitive issues such as bolting herbs, heat stress, or emerging pest activity.

**How it fits with other coordination tools**
- Works hand‑in‑hand with a **[[concepts/shared_calendar]]** that schedules regular tasks (watering, maintenance) and marks the weekly check‑in slot.
- Often paired with **[[concepts/rotation_stewardship]]**, where the steward for the week leads the check‑in and can make on‑the‑spot decisions within agreed boundaries.
- Supports **[[concepts/flexible_routine_adjustments]]** by allowing the group to quickly tweak routines (e.g., temporary shade, emergency watering) based on real‑time observations.

**Practical Tips**
1. **Keep it brief** – 5‑10 minutes focused on status updates and any urgent actions.
2. **Document outcomes** – Use an on‑site **[[entities/whiteboard]]** or a weather‑proof **[[entities/binder]]** to note decisions made during the check‑in.
3. **Rotate leadership** – Align with the rotation stewardship model so responsibility is shared and fresh eyes spot issues.
4. **Link to the shared calendar** – After the check‑in, update the calendar with any schedule changes (e.g., adjusted watering days).

**Benefits**
- Enhances communication without overwhelming participants.
- Provides a structured yet flexible venue for rapid decision‑making.
- Reduces the risk of duplicated effort or missed tasks by keeping everyone aligned on the latest garden conditions.
