## Shared Calendar

A **shared calendar** is a collaborative scheduling tool that lets community garden members sign up for recurring responsibilities such as watering, weeding, and maintenance. By visualizing who is responsible for each task on a given day, the garden avoids gaps in care and prevents overlapping efforts.

### How it works in practice
- **Task sign‑up:** Members choose slots for regular duties (e.g., watering on Mondays, compost turning on Fridays).  
- **Visibility:** The calendar is accessible to all participants, often via a free online platform or a printed board near the garden entrance.
- **Coordination:** Combined with [[concepts/weekly_checkins]] and [[concepts/rotation_stewardship]], the calendar provides a predictable rhythm while allowing flexibility for unexpected issues.
- **Integration with on‑site logs:** Quick notes about actions taken (e.g., “added shade cloth”) are recorded on a nearby [[entities/whiteboard]] or in a weather‑proof [[entities/binder]]; the calendar entry can be updated with a brief comment linking the two.

### Benefits
- **Reduces confusion:** Everyone knows when it is their turn, minimizing missed watering or duplicate work.
- **Supports rapid decisions:** When a time‑sensitive problem arises (e.g., a heat wave), the current steward can act immediately and note the change in the on‑site log.
- **Enables flexible routine adjustments:** The calendar can be edited on the fly, supporting the principles described in [[concepts/flexible_routine_adjustments]].

### Implementation steps
1. Choose a simple, shareable platform (Google Calendar, a community board, or a printed sheet).  
2. Define the core recurring tasks and their typical frequency.  
3. Invite all garden members to claim slots, rotating responsibilities periodically.  
4. Link the calendar to the garden’s daily log system (e.g., a [[entities/laminated_bed_tag]] at each bed for short notes, or a central [[entities/whiteboard]] for daily updates).  
5. Review the schedule during regular [[concepts/weekly_checkins]] to adjust for seasonal changes or new volunteers.

### Related concepts

- [[concepts/rotation_stewardship]] – rotating point‑of‑contact roles that complement the calendar.  
- [[concepts/flexible_routine_adjustments]] – the ability to tweak the schedule as conditions change.
