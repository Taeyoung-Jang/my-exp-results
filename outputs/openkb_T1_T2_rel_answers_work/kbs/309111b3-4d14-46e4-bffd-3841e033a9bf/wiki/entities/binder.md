# Binder

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** Weather‑proof binder for community‑garden record‑keeping.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-05]]:**
- Recommended as a low‑tech, on‑site logging option alongside a whiteboard.
- Stored in a mailbox‑style box near the garden entrance to keep notes longer than a day.
- Used to record actions such as temporary shade installation, emergency watering, pest observations, and other adjustments.
- Helps maintain a shared log when reliance on a group chat is low.
