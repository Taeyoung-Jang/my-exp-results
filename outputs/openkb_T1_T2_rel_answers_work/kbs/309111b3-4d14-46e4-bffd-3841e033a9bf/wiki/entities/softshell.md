# Softshell

**Type:** product

Softshell is a lightweight, breathable outer garment that provides wind resistance while allowing moisture to escape. In the context of [[concepts/temperature_layering_strategy]], it is recommended as the third (outer) layer to keep the body warm during cold mornings without causing overheating later in the day.

Key points from [[summaries/session-13_2025-04-24]]:
- Acts as a wind‑resistant barrier that remains breathable, making it ideal for variable temperatures.
- Pairs well with a base layer such as [[entities/merino_wool]] and a mid‑layer like [[entities/grid_fleece]] or a [[entities/synthetic_blend]].
- Serves a similar purpose to a [[entities/windbreaker]] but typically offers better insulation while retaining flexibility.
- Chosen for its ability to block chill in the morning and shed excess heat as the day warms, supporting the principles of [[concepts/optimal_fabric_choices]].

**Typical use case**
1. Base layer (e.g., merino wool) for moisture‑wicking and warmth.
2. Mid‑layer (e.g., grid fleece) for added insulation.
3. Softshell outer layer for wind protection and breathability.

This combination enables small, flexible adjustments to daily routines when weather shifts unexpectedly, aligning with [[concepts/flexible_routine_adjustments]].
