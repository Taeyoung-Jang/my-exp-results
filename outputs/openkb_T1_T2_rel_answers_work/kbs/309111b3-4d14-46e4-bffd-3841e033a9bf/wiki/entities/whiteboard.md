# Whiteboard (product)

**Type:** product

A whiteboard is a weather‑proof, reusable writing surface often mounted near the entrance of a community garden.  In the context of [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-05]], the whiteboard is recommended as a low‑tech, on‑site logging tool where gardeners can quickly note daily actions such as adding shade cloth, emergency watering, or pest observations.  It serves as the primary visible log, supporting the garden’s coordination mechanisms like [[concepts/shared_calendar]] and [[concepts/weekly_checkins]].

## Key facts from the source
- **Purpose:** Capture real‑time updates on garden actions to avoid duplication or undoing work.
- **Placement:** Near the garden entrance for easy visibility.
- **Usage pattern:** Anyone can write brief, dated notes; entries are read by subsequent volunteers.
- **Advantages:** Low cost, immediate accessibility, no need for digital devices.
