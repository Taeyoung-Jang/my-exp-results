## Synthetic Blend

**Type:** product  
**Description:** A lightweight, breathable fabric mix—often polyester‑based—designed to retain warmth while wicking moisture.

### Key Characteristics
- **Thermal regulation:** Keeps the wearer warm in cold conditions without overheating later.
- **Moisture wicking:** Pulls sweat away from the skin and dries quickly, preventing a clammy feeling.
- **Flexibility:** Works well as a base‑layer material in layered clothing systems.

### Role in Session‑13 (2025‑04‑24)
In the conversation about adapting routines to sudden cold snaps, [[summaries/session-13_2025-04-24]] recommends synthetic blend fabric for the base layer because it provides warmth, breathability, and quick‑dry performance, especially when transitioning between cold outdoor mornings and warmer indoor settings.
