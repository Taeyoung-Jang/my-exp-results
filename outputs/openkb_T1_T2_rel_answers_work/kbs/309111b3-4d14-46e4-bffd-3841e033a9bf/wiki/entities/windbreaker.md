# Windbreaker

A **windbreaker** is a thin, wind‑resistant outer garment designed to keep the chill out without adding bulk. In the context of the *session‑13_2025‑04‑24* discussion, it is recommended as the third layer in a three‑layer system for handling unexpected temperature shifts.

## Key Facts from the Document
- Serves as the **outer layer** in the suggested layering strategy, providing protection against morning wind while remaining breathable enough to avoid overheating later in the day. 
- Emphasized for its ability to **block wind** without trapping excessive heat, making it ideal for mornings that warm up into sunny afternoons.
- Paired with a **thin base layer** (e.g., merino wool or synthetic blend) and a **mid‑layer** (e.g., light fleece or grid fleece) to create a flexible temperature‑adaptive outfit.
- Highlighted as part of the broader [[concepts/temperature_layering_strategy]] and aligns with recommendations on [[concepts/optimal_fabric_choices]].
