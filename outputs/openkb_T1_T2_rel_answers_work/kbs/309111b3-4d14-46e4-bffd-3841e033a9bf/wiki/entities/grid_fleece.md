# Grid Fleece

**Type:** Product

Grid fleece is a lightweight, breathable synthetic fleece commonly used as a **mid‑layer** in a three‑layer clothing system. It provides warmth without bulk and efficiently wicks moisture, making it suitable for activities that transition between cool outdoor conditions and warmer indoor environments.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-13_2025-04-24]]
- Recommended for short, chilly commutes (10–15 min walk) followed by indoor sitting.
- Offers flexibility for the **temperature layering strategy** discussed in [[concepts/temperature_layering_strategy]].
- Preferred over cotton because it avoids clammy, sweaty‑then‑cold feelings.
- Works well with a thin base layer (e.g., [[entities/merino_wool]] or synthetic blend) and a wind‑resistant outer layer such as a [[entities/softshell]] or [[entities/windbreaker]].

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/optimal_fabric_choices]] – grid fleece is highlighted as a top synthetic option for mid‑layers.
- [[concepts/flexible_routine_adjustments]] – using grid fleece supports adaptable daily routines when weather shifts unexpectedly.

## Usage Tips
1. Pair with a thin, moisture‑wicking base layer for maximum comfort.
2. Layer under a wind‑resistant outer shell to protect against morning chill.
3. Remove or zip the fleece as temperature rises to prevent overheating.
