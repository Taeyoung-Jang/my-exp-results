# Laminated Bed Tag

A **laminated bed tag** is a small, weather‑resistant label or card that can be attached to individual garden beds. Gardeners write brief notes—such as shading actions, watering events, or pest sightings—directly on the tag.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-05]]
- Suggested as a **low‑tech on‑site logging option** for community gardens.
- Works well for **very short notes** but can become cluttered if overused.
- Recommended alongside other logging tools like an outdoor [[entities/whiteboard]] and a weather‑proof [[entities/binder]].

## Typical Uses
- Marking that temporary shade cloth has been added to a bed.
- Noting the date of an **emergency watering**.
- Recording observations of pests or early disease signs.
- Indicating that a plant has been harvested or trimmed because it is bolting.

## Advantages
- **Durable:** Laminated surface protects writing from rain and sunlight.
- **Portable:** Can be moved easily if beds are re‑arranged.
- **Immediate visibility:** Gardeners see the most recent action without needing to check a separate log.

## Limitations
- Limited space; best for **short, single‑sentence updates**.
- If many tags are used without coordination, information can become fragmented.
- Requires a **pen or marker** that writes on laminate.
