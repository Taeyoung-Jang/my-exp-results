# Summary of session-31_2025-06-05  

**Date:** 2025‑06‑05 07:30  

The conversation explores how community gardens can stay organized and make rapid, low‑conflict decisions about watering, pest control, shading, and planting. Key recommendations include:

## Coordination mechanisms
- **[[concepts/shared_calendar]]** for regular tasks (watering, maintenance).  
- **[[concepts/weekly_checkins]]** via in‑person briefings or a group chat to flag emerging issues.  
- **[[concepts/rotation_stewardship]]** who act as the point‑of‑contact for quick decisions.

## Decision‑playbook framework
| Level | Action | Typical examples |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| **Immediate (anyone)** | Temporary, reversible actions | Add shade cloth, emergency watering, harvest/trim bolting herbs, hand‑remove pests |
| **Notify group (no vote)** | Minor adjustments that affect others but are reversible | Change watering schedule, add/remove lightweight supports, re‑plant small quick‑crop plots, flag disease signs |
| **Vote/meeting required** | Permanent or resource‑intensive changes | Permanent bed or irrigation structures, major re‑planting of shared space, budget‑approved purchases, long‑term plantings (shrubs, perennials) |

## Documentation of changes
- **Shared log** – can be a pinned chat message, a cloud note, or an on‑site board where any gardener writes a brief update with the date.  
- **Low‑tech on‑site options**:  
  - Outdoor **[[entities/whiteboard]]** near the entrance for daily notes.  
  - Weather‑proof **[[entities/binder]]** in a mailbox‑style box for longer‑term records.  
  - **[[entities/laminated_bed_tag]]** or clipboards for very brief, location‑specific notes (used sparingly).

The overall approach stresses simple structure, clear boundaries for immediate actions versus group‑consulted actions, and an easy‑to‑use log so later gardeners see what has already been done.
