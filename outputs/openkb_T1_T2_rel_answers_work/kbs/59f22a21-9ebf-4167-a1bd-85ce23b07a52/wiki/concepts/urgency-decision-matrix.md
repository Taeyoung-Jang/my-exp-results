## Urgency Decision Matrix

**Definition**: A structured way to evaluate whether a request or task is truly time‑critical enough to justify shifting a protected recovery block or buffer.

### Core Criteria
| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Immediate impact** | The outcome affects a deadline or deliverable that will be missed today or tomorrow if not addressed. |
| **Uniqueness** | No one else on the team can handle the task in the same time frame. |
| **Consequences** | Failure would cause concrete negative repercussions (e.g., system outage, client breach, lost revenue). |
| **Irreversibility** | The task cannot be postponed without creating additional work later. |

If a request meets **two or more** of the above criteria, it can be classified as *urgent* and may warrant moving a recovery block. Otherwise, it should be declined, deferred, or delegated.

### How to Use It
1. **Pause** – When an invitation lands on a buffer slot or recovery block, quickly run through the matrix.
2. **Score** – Mark which criteria apply.
3. **Decide** –
   - *Urgent*: Communicate a shift using the scripts from [[concepts/boundary-communication-scripts]].
   - *Not urgent*: Politely decline or propose an alternative time.
4. **Document** – Note the decision in your calendar or task manager to keep a record of how often recovery time is moved.

### Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/buffer-time-management]] to keep pre‑ and post‑meeting buffers intact.
- Supports maintaining [[concepts/protected-recovery-blocks]] by providing a clear rule‑set for when they may be moved.
- Complements the [[concepts/weekly-review-ritual]] where you can reflect on any shifts made and adjust future planning.
- Aligns with [[concepts/stress-recovery]] by preventing unnecessary erosion of downtime, which can lead to burnout.

### Benefits
- **Clarity**: Removes ambiguity about what truly warrants a schedule change.
- **Consistency**: Applies the same standard across coworkers and managers.
- **Protection**: Safeguards recovery time, reducing the risk of chronic stress.
