# Weekly Review Ritual

**Definition**: A short, regular (typically 10‑minute) review session that realigns your upcoming week’s schedule, confirms protected recovery blocks, and adjusts tasks based on urgency and energy levels.

## Core Elements

1. **Timing**
   - Ideally performed at the end of the weekend (Sunday evening) or right before a protected recovery block (e.g., Wednesday evening).  
   - Keeps the review fresh in mind before the workweek or a rest period begins.
2. **Agenda**
   - **Scan calendar**: Verify that all **[[concepts/protected-recovery-blocks]]** are still scheduled and uncompromised.
   - **Validate buffers**: Ensure **[[concepts/buffer-time-management]]** slots around heavy meeting days are intact.
   - **Prioritize urgent items** using the **[[concepts/urgency-decision-matrix]]** to decide if any tasks need to shift a recovery block.
   - **Adjust workload**: Move low‑priority items to lighter days, add or trim tasks to maintain a realistic load.
   - **Set intentions**: Note one personal focus for the week (e.g., mindfulness, physical activity) to support **[[concepts/stress-recovery]]**.
3. **Outcome**
   - Updated calendar with clearly marked recovery periods.
   - A concise to‑do list that respects buffers and energy limits.
   - A quick mental check‑in on how you’re feeling, linking to **[[concepts/physiological-self-monitoring]]** if you track fatigue or stress.

## Why It Matters
The ritual prevents the common trap of “plan‑then‑forget,” where you schedule work but neglect the necessary downtime that sustains performance. By anchoring the review to a recovery block, it reinforces the habit of protecting rest and makes it feel like a non‑negotiable part of the week rather than an optional task.

## Tips for Effective Execution
- Keep it brief: set a timer for 10 minutes.
- Use the same location or tool each week to create a cue.
- Record any adjustments in your calendar immediately.
- If you miss a review, do a “quick catch‑up” the next day before the next recovery block.
