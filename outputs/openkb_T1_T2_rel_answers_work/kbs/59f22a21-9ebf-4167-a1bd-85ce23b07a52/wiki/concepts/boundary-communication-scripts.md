# Boundary Communication Scripts

**Definition**: A set of concise, collaborative phrases used to protect scheduled recovery time and buffer periods without appearing inflexible or uncooperative.

## Purpose
- Reinforce *protected recovery blocks* and *buffer-time-management* while maintaining team trust.
- Provide clear alternatives so requests are handled promptly, preserving energy for high‑focus work.
- Align with the *urgency-decision-matrix* by distinguishing truly time‑critical tasks from deferrable ones.

## Core Elements
1. **Contextual framing** – Explain the purpose of the time slot (e.g., “that window is part of my buffer to stay on top of the meeting load”).
2. **Alternative offer** – Suggest a specific later time or next‑day slot.
3. **Collaborative tone** – Use “we” language and show willingness to adjust if the matter is urgent.

## Sample Scripts (from [[summaries/session-40_2024-07-01]])
### Coworker – Buffer Conflict
- *“I’ve got a hold on that window so I can prep and stay on top of the meeting load this week. Could we slide it to a little later, or sometime this afternoon? I’ll be more focused then.”*

### Manager – Buffer Conflict
- *“That slot is part of the buffer I use to stay organized during the heavy meeting days. If this is time‑sensitive, I can shift things around—otherwise, could we move it to a point where I can give it full attention?”*

### Coworker – Evening Recovery Block
- *“I’m offline that evening, but I can take a look first thing tomorrow morning. Will that timing work?”*

### Manager – Evening Recovery Block
- *“I’m offline that evening to keep my energy steady through the week, but I can handle it first thing tomorrow. If it truly can’t wait, let me know and I’ll adjust.”*

## How It Connects to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/buffer-time-management]] to keep pre‑ and post‑meeting buffers intact.
- Supports the structure of [[concepts/protected-recovery-blocks]] by offering graceful ways to defend those periods.
- Relies on the criteria from [[concepts/urgency-decision-matrix]] to decide when a request merits moving a recovery block.
- Can be part of a broader [[concepts/weekly-review-ritual]] where you reflect on boundary adherence and adjust scripts as needed.

## Implementation Tips
- **Template library**: Keep these scripts in a note or document for quick copy‑paste.
- **Personalize**: Insert specific times or project names to make the alternative feel concrete.
- **Practice**: Role‑play with a trusted colleague to sound natural.
- **Review**: During your weekly review, note any repeated push‑backs and refine the language accordingly.
