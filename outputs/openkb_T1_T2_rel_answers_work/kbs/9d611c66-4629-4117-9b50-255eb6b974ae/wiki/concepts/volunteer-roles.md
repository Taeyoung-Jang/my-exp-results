# Volunteer Roles

The **Volunteer Roles** concept describes the core set of tasks that a small team of volunteers performs during a Saturday food pantry. The model, outlined in [[summaries/session-09_2022-05-15]], includes six distinct positions:

1. **Setup/Intake (2 volunteers)** – Unload donated produce, sort items, and prepare intake sheets.
2. **Distribution (2 volunteers)** – Hand out food parcels to clients, manage the line, and answer basic questions.
3. **Logistics** – Oversee inventory levels, restock tables, and handle any on‑site issues.
4. **Floater** – Fill gaps in any role as needed, providing flexibility for the team.

These roles are designed to be simple, repeatable, and evenly distributed among members of a **rotating‑team** ([[concepts/rotating-teams]]). By keeping the role set limited, volunteers can quickly learn their responsibilities, reducing training time and burnout.

The structure works hand‑in‑hand with a **cancellation protocol** ([[concepts/cancellation-protocol]]). Each role has an associated on‑call backup volunteer who can step in if a primary volunteer cannot attend, ensuring the pantry runs smoothly even with last‑minute changes.

The concept emphasizes clarity, shared workload, and adaptability, making it suitable for faith‑based groups, neighborhoods, or clubs that rely solely on volunteers to operate a regular food‑insecurity service.
