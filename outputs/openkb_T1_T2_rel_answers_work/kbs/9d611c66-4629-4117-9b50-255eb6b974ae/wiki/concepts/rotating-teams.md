## Rotating Teams

**Definition**: A volunteer scheduling structure where a small group of members (typically four teams) rotates responsibility for a recurring weekly task, such as a Saturday food pantry, on a month‑by‑month basis.

**Key Elements**
- **Team composition**: Four teams (A‑D), each consisting of the six core [[concepts/volunteer-roles]] (two Setup/Intake, two Distribution, one Logistics, one Floater).  
- **Monthly rotation**: Each team is assigned one Saturday per month, providing a predictable rhythm and preventing burnout.
- **Rotating Team Lead**: Within the six volunteers, one person assumes a brief leadership role for the shift; the lead rotates monthly, ensuring shared responsibility and no permanent coordinator.
- **On‑Call Backup**: Every team designates a standby volunteer who can fill in at short notice, supporting the [[concepts/cancellation-protocol]].
- **Communication**: Teams use a common group‑text channel; members first contact their own team if a cancellation occurs, streamlining response.

**Benefits**
- **Predictability**: Volunteers know the exact week and role they will serve, making it easier to integrate the commitment into their personal schedules.
- **Shared workload**: The load is evenly distributed across the month, reducing the risk of any individual feeling over‑burdened.
- **Leadership development**: Rotating the team lead provides leadership experience to all volunteers without creating a separate staff position.
- **Resilience**: The built‑in backup system and clear cancellation flow keep the pantry running smoothly even when unexpected absences happen.
