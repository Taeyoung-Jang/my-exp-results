# Australia

Australia is a continent‑nation in the Southern Hemisphere. In the context of Easter, it is noted for substituting the traditional Easter Bunny with the **Easter Bilby**, a native marsupial, as a way to raise awareness for wildlife conservation.

## Easter tradition
- The Easter Bilby replaces the bunny in many Australian celebrations, highlighting the importance of protecting native species. This practice is mentioned in [[summaries/session-15_2021-05-02]] as an example of unique [[concepts/easter-traditions]].

## Related concepts
- The focus on renewal and environmental stewardship ties into broader themes of [[concepts/spring-renewal]] and simple home‑based rituals that can be part of [[concepts/home-rituals]] during the season.
