# Hanbali School

The **Hanbali School** (also referred to as the Hanbali madhhab) is one of the four major Sunni Islamic schools of law. It is known for its strict adherence to the texts of the Qur’an and Hadith and for a relatively literalist interpretive approach.

## Key Features Mentioned in Session 32
- **First sitting after two rak‘ahs**: Considered essential for proper prayer structure; skipping it unintentionally does not invalidate the prayer, but it is treated as necessary for completeness.
- **Salawāt in the final sitting**: Required; students are taught to recite the full salawāt on the Prophet before concluding the prayer.
- **Du‘a after salawāt**: Recommended but not obligatory; the school encourages making personal supplications before the taslīm.
- **Use of a phone for quick reference**: A brief glance to remind oneself of the wording is permissible and does not invalidate the prayer, though continuous reading during the prayer is discouraged.
