# Qur'an (work)

**Type:** work

The Qur'an is the central religious text of [[entities/islam]], believed to be the literal word of God as revealed to [[entities/prophet-muhammad]]. It is written in Classical Arabic and divided into 114 chapters (surahs).

## Role in Daily Practice

In the guidance provided during [[summaries/session-32_2023-05-02]], the Qur'an is highlighted as a core element of a beginner’s [[concepts/daily-prayer]] routine. New Muslims are encouraged to:

- Read a translation of the Qur'an daily, even if only a few verses.
- Memorize short Meccan surahs that are easy to recite during the five daily prayers (the *rak‘ahs*).

## Recommended Surahs for Beginners

The session suggests starting with the following short surahs, all taken from the Qur'an:

1. **Al‑Fātiḥah** – essential for every rak‘ah.
2. **Al‑Ikhlāṣ** – "He is Allah, One..."
3. **Al‑Falaq** – a protection against evil.
4. **An‑Nās** – seeking refuge from harmful whispers.

These chapters are chosen for their brevity and frequent use in prayer, making them practical for memorization.

## Connection to Prayer Routine

The Qur'an provides the verses recited in the standing position (*qiyām*) of each prayer. A structured [[concepts/prayer-routine]] often begins with the recitation of Al‑Fātiḥah followed by one of the short surahs listed above. Mastery of these verses supports the gradual build‑up plan outlined in the document, moving from note‑aided prayers to fully memorized ones.
