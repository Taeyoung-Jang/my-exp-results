# Bermuda

Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory in the North Atlantic Ocean. In the context of Easter traditions, it is noted for the unique custom of making and flying homemade kites during the holiday period.

## Easter Custom
During Easter, Bermudians often create colorful kites by hand and launch them, turning the celebration into a vibrant, community‑focused sky‑fest. This practice highlights the island’s maritime culture and the joyful, creative spirit associated with the season.
