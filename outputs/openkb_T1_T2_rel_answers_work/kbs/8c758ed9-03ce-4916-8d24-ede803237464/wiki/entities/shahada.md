# Shahada

**Type:** Event (Islamic declaration of faith)

The **Shahada** is the testimony that "There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah." Reciting this declaration with conviction is the foundational act that brings a person into the Islamic faith.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-32_2023-05-02]]
- The user is preparing to complete the Shahada paperwork at a local **[[entities/mosque]]** next week.
- They are already practising the **[[concepts/daily-prayer]]** and the five daily prayers to be ready for worship after the declaration.
- The session outlines a gentle **[[concepts/prayer-routine]]** to build confidence before and after taking the Shahada.

## Related concepts
- **Daily practice:** The Shahada is often followed by establishing a regular **[[concepts/daily-prayer]]** schedule.
- **Learning the prayer routine:** New converts are encouraged to adopt a step‑by‑step **[[concepts/prayer-routine]]** to integrate prayer into daily life.
- **Islamic jurisprudence:** Different **[[concepts/islamic-schools-of-law]]** (e.g., **[[entities/hanafi]]**, **[[entities/shafi-i]]**, **[[entities/maliki]]**, **[[entities/hanbali]]**) may provide specific guidance on the correct wording and circumstances for reciting the Shahada.

## Significance
- Marks the formal entry into **[[entities/islam]]**.
- Often performed in the presence of witnesses and an **[[entities/mosque]]** authority.
- Enables participation in other core practices such as **[[concepts/daily-prayer]]**, fasting during Ramadan, and the **[[entities/quran]]** study.
