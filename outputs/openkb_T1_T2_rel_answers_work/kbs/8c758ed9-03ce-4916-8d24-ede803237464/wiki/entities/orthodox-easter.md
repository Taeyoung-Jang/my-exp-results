# Orthodox Easter (event)

Orthodox Easter is the principal feast of the Eastern Orthodox Church, marking the resurrection of Jesus. It is calculated using the Julian calendar, which typically places it one to five weeks after the Western (Gregorian) Easter.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-15_2021-05-02]]
- **Timing**: Falls later in spring, often providing a quieter period before the holiday.
- **Seasonal focus**: Emphasizes themes of renewal and connection, aligning with [[concepts/spring-renewal]].
- **Suggested home practices**: A 7‑day checklist of short, low‑effort activities (tidying, walking, egg‑dyeing, note‑writing, simple creative tasks) that build a sense of intentionality without heavy religious ritual.
- **Egg‑dyeing**: Uses natural materials like onion skins, a nod to traditional customs while staying simple.
- **Outdoors option**: A nature walk or planting activity for good weather, reinforcing the renewal motif.
