# Sweden
Sweden is a Scandinavian nation in Northern Europe. In the context of Easter, Swedish children often take part in a unique spring custom where they dress up as little witches and go door‑to‑door, offering hand‑drawn pictures in exchange for sweets—an activity that resembles a seasonal, community‑focused ritual.
