# Maliki School

**Type:** other

The **Maliki** school is one of the four major Sunni schools of Islamic jurisprudence. It is known for a balanced approach to ritual practice, especially in the context of the five daily prayers.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-32_2023-05-02]]
- **First sitting after two rak‘ahs** – Considered important but not invalidating the prayer if missed unintentionally.
- **Salawāt in the final sitting** – Highly recommended; the prayer remains valid even if the salawāt is omitted.
- **Du‘ā after salawāt** – Permitted and allowed, though the school places less emphasis on it compared to some other schools.
- **Using a phone for a quick glance** – A brief, incidental glance to remind oneself does not invalidate the prayer.
