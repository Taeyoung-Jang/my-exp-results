# Easter Bilby (other)

**Overview**
The Easter Bilby is a native Australian marsupial used as an alternative to the traditional Easter Bunny. It serves as a symbol to raise awareness about habitat loss and the endangered status of bilbies.

**Cultural Role**
- Introduced as part of [[concepts/easter-traditions]] to provide a locally relevant, conservation‑focused twist on Easter celebrations.
- Often featured in Australian schools, charities, and seasonal marketing to encourage support for wildlife preservation.
