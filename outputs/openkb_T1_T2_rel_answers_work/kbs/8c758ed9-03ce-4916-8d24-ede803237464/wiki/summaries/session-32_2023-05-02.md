# Summary of session-32_2023-05-02  

**Date:** 2023‑05‑02  

## Core Topics  
- **Daily Islamic practice** – emphasis on the five daily prayers as the rhythm of a modern Muslim life. [[concepts/daily-prayer]]  
- **Step‑by‑step prayer build‑up** – a gentle week‑by‑week plan to move from using notes to praying all five rak‘ahs from memory. [[concepts/prayer-routine]]  
- **Essential surahs & du‘as for beginners** – Surah Al‑Fātiḥah, short Meccan surahs (Al‑Ikhlāṣ, Al‑Falaq, An‑Nās) and basic opening du‘a (“Subḥāna‑ka Allāhumma”).  
- **Full tashahhud and salawāt** – transliteration, meaning, and which parts are required vs. recommended in the prayer.  
- **Differences among the four Sunni schools** (Hanafi, Shafi‘ī, Maliki, Hanbali) regarding:  
  1. The first sitting after two rak‘ahs.  
  2. Inclusion of salawāt in the final sitting.  
  3. Making du‘a after salawāt before the taslīm.  
  4. Whether a brief phone glance invalidates the prayer. [[concepts/islamic-schools-of-law]]

## Practical Guidance  
- **Week 1:** Perform all five prayers with notes; focus on the physical flow.  
- **Week 2:** Choose one prayer to recite from memory each day; add a second after a few days.  
- **Week 3:** Aim to pray most rak‘ahs from memory, using notes only for quick checks.  
- **Week 4:** Complete all five prayers from memory; polish recitation and meanings.  

## Memorization List (Transliteration + Meaning)  
1. **Al‑Fātiḥah** – opening chapter, essential for every rak‘ah.  
2. **Al‑Ikhlāṣ** – “He is Allah, One…”.  
3. **Al‑Falaq** – “From the evil of what He created…”.  
4. **An‑Nās** – “From the evil of the whisperer…”.  
5. **Opening du‘a** – “Subḥāna‑ka Allāhumma”.  

## Tashahhud (Required) & Salawāt (Recommended)  
- **Required portion** (in both first and final sitting):  
  *At‑taḥiyyātu lillāhi … ash‑hadu anna Muḥammadan ‘abduhu wa rasūluh.*  
- **Recommended/strongly emphasized** (final sitting): full salawāt on the Prophet.  
- **Du‘a after salawāt** – permissible and encouraged, especially in the Shafi‘ī tradition.
