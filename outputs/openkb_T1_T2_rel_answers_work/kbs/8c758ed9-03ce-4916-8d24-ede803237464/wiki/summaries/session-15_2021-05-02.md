# Summary of session-15_2021-05-02

**Main Focus**: A practical guide for celebrating Easter—especially Orthodox Easter—with low‑effort, non‑religious activities that emphasize renewal, family connection, and cultural curiosity.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/easter-traditions]]: Brief overview of global customs (Swedish witches‑go‑round [[entities/sweden]], Greek red‑egg cracking [[entities/greece]], Bermuda kite‑making [[entities/bermuda]], Australian Easter Bilby awareness [[entities/australia]] and [[entities/easter-bilby]]).
- [[concepts/spring-renewal]]: Themes of renewal, planting, and seasonal reset that underpin the suggested activities.
- [[concepts/home-rituals]]: Small, repeatable home‑based rituals (tidying, walking, note‑writing) that create a sense of intentionality.

## Structured 7‑Day Checklist (10–30 min each)
| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| **Monday** | Quick tidy of a small personal space (nightstand, countertop). |
| **Tuesday** | 10–15 min walk or sit outdoors with a warm drink, noticing seasonal changes. |
| **Wednesday** | Hard‑boil a few eggs for later dyeing (or prep a simple snack if you don’t eat eggs). |
| **Thursday** | Dye eggs using onion skins: simmer skins, add vinegar, soak eggs until desired hue. |
| **Friday** | Write a single‑sentence hope/intention for the coming months. |
| **Saturday** | Light creative act: arrange found branches/flowers, sketch, or set out a candle. |
| **Holy Saturday** | Prepare a small Easter‑morning element (set table, choose first dish) to start the day calmly. |

## Easter‑Day Options (solo or family)
1. **Indoors – Shared Meal**: Make a relaxed breakfast/brunch with pantry staples and share a round of “one good thing I’m looking forward to.”
2. **Nature – Walk or Plant**: If weather permits, take a slow walk in a green area or plant a seed/herb cutting as a tangible symbol of new beginnings.
3. **Creative – Egg & Note**: Decorate additional eggs (using natural dyes) and write a brief note of appreciation to send later.
4. **Family‑Friendly – Mini Hunt**: Hide small notes, treats, or kind messages around the house for a low‑effort treasure hunt.

## Practical Highlights
- All activities require minimal shopping; most use items already at home (onion skins, eggs, a pen, a candle).
- The plan accommodates both solitary reflection and family participation.
- Emphasizes the universal [[concepts/spring-renewal]] motif while drawing softly on diverse [[concepts/easter-traditions]].
