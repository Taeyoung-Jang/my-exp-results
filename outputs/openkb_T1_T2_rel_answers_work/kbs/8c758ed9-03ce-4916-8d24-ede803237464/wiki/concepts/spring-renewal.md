# Spring Renewal

**Definition**: Spring Renewal refers to the practice of intentional, small‑scale actions that mark the transition into spring, fostering a sense of fresh start, growth, and personal well‑being.

**Relation to Easter**: In the session‑15 discussion, Spring Renewal underpins the proposed 7‑day checklist and Easter‑day activities, emphasizing themes of tidying, walking, planting, and simple creative acts that echo the season’s revitalising energy.

**Key Elements (drawn from [[summaries/session-15_2021-05-02]])**:
- Tidying a small space – quick declutter to create mental clarity.
- Short outdoor walks – noticing seasonal changes and breathing fresh air.
- Preparing and dyeing eggs with natural materials – an artistic expression of new colour.
- Writing hopes or intentions – a personal reset ritual.
- Simple creative arrangements – using found branches, flowers, or candles.
- Planting seeds or herbs – a literal act of growth.

**Connections**:
- Linked to broader cultural practices described in [[concepts/easter-traditions]].
- Overlaps with everyday practices grouped under [[concepts/home-rituals]].

**Practical Tips**:
1. Keep each activity to 10–30 minutes.
2. Use items already at home (onion skins, a pen, a candle) to minimise preparation.
3. Adapt activities for solo or family settings.
4. When weather permits, add an outdoor component such as a walk or planting.

**Why it matters**: Small, repeatable actions help embed a feeling of renewal beyond any specific religious framework, making the season feel purposeful and uplifting.
