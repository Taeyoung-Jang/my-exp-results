# Islamic Schools of Law

The four major Sunni schools—[[entities/hanafi]], [[entities/shafi-i]], [[entities/maliki]], and [[entities/hanbali]]—share many core principles for [[concepts/daily-prayer]] but differ in details of the sitting portions of the prayer. The nuances are especially relevant for beginners following a [[concepts/prayer-routine]] as outlined in [[summaries/session-32_2023-05-02]].

## 1. First Sitting (after two rak‘ahs)
| School | Requirement |
|--------|--------------|
| Hanafi | Considered necessary for proper structure, but not a pillar of the prayer. |
| Shafi‘i | Treated as required for correct prayer order. |
| Maliki | Important but missing it unintentionally does not invalidate the prayer. |
| Hanbali | Seen as essential for completeness. |

The required text in this sitting is the basic **tashahhud** up to the shahada line (*ash‑hadu anna Muḥammadan ‘abduhu wa rasūluh*). All schools agree this part must be recited.

## 2. Salawāt in the Final Sitting
| School | View on Salawāt (sending blessings on the Prophet) |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Hanafi | Generally required and taught as part of the final tashahhud. |
| Shafi‘i | Required; many curricula treat it as obligatory. |
| Maliki | Highly recommended, but the prayer remains valid without it. |
| Hanbali | Required; commonly taught as obligatory. |

## 3. Du‘ā After Salawāt (Before Taslīm)
| School | Recommendation |
|--------|----------------|
| Hanafi | Recommended but not obligatory. |
| Shafi‘i | Strongly encouraged; often included in taught prayers. |
| Maliki | Permitted with less emphasis. |
| Hanbali | Recommended. |

## 4. Using a Phone or Notes During Prayer
All four schools allow a brief, incidental glance at a phone or paper to remind oneself of the wording, especially for new Muslims. Continuous reading is discouraged, but a quick glance does **not** invalidate the prayer.

## 5. Summary Table
| Aspect | Hanafi | Shafi‘i | Maliki | Hanbali |
|--------|--------|----------|--------|----------|
| First sitting tashahhud | Needed for structure | Required | Important, not invalidating if missed | Essential |
| Salawāt in final sitting | Required | Required | Recommended | Required |
| Du‘ā after salawāt | Recommended | Strongly encouraged | Permitted | Recommended |
| Phone/notes glance | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed |

These differences are modest; the overall goal across the schools is to maintain a coherent [[concepts/prayer-routine]] that supports a steady rhythm of worship.
