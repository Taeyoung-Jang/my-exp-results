# Summary of session-13_2021-07-14

**Date:** 2021‑07‑14

## Core topics
- **U.S. immigration status categories** – brief breakdown of temporary visitor status, work‑authorized non‑immigrant status, permanent residency (green card), and humanitarian/family‑based statuses. [[concepts/immigration_status_categories]]
- **Permanent resident (green‑card) compliance** – what a green‑card holder must track when moving states or changing employment. [[concepts/green_card_compliance]]
- **Key documents** – green card, passport, original approval notice, Social Security card; distinguish renewal‑required items (physical green card, conditional 2‑year card) from non‑renewable items. immigration documents
- **Address update requirement** – filing USCIS AR‑11 change‑of‑address within ten days of moving, even without pending applications; keep confirmation as proof. [[concepts/address_update_ar11]]
- **Job change impact** – no notification needed to USCIS; status remains unchanged. (no specific concept page)
- **State‑level updates** – driver’s license, vehicle registration, USPS address; not immigration‑specific. (no specific concept page)

## Practical checklist for green‑card holders
1. **Track card expiration** – ten‑year card renew every 10 years; two‑year conditional card requires **I‑751** petition before expiry. [[entities/green_card]] [[entities/form_i_751]]
2. **Maintain core documents** – green card, passport, original approval notice, SSN card; keep digital copies of AR‑11 confirmation. [[entities/green_card]] [[entities/passport]] [[entities/social_security_card]] [[entities/ar_11_form]]
3. **File AR‑11** – within **10 days** of any address change, regardless of pending cases. [[entities/uscis]] [[entities/ar_11_form]]
4. **Update state records** – driver’s license, vehicle registration, mail forwarding.
5. **Monitor travel** – avoid prolonged absences that could jeopardize residency continuity.
6. **Future naturalization** – note residency‑time requirements if pursuing citizenship later.

## Answers to user questions
- **Do I need to notify USCIS when moving?** Yes, submit AR‑11 within ten days.
- **Does a job change affect my status?** No, there is no USCIS notification requirement.
- **What documents need renewal?** The physical green card (10‑year) or conditional card (2‑year) needs renewal; others (passport, SSN card) do not expire in the same way.
- **What confirmation to keep?** Save the AR‑11 submission receipt (PDF, screenshot, or email).
