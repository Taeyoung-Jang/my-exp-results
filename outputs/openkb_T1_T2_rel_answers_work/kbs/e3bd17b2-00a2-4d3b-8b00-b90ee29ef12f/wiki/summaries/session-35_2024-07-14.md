## Overview
This summary captures the essential guidance for a first‑time U.S. passport application after naturalization, covering required documents, photocopy standards, payment handling, processing timelines, and options for anxious applicants.

## Required Documents [[concepts/required_documents]]
- **Certificate of Naturalization** – original (reviewed by the acceptance agent) **and** a clear photocopy. [[entities/certificate_of_naturalization]]
- **Government‑issued photo ID** (e.g., driver’s license) – bring original and a front‑and‑back photocopy.
- **DS‑11 application form** – complete but do **not** sign until instructed by the acceptance facility. [[entities/ds_11_form]]
- **Passport photo** – one compliant photo meeting size and background rules. See [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]].
- **Payment** –
  - State Department fee: check or money order. [[entities/us_state_department]]
  - Acceptance facility fee: often cash or debit card; some locations accept cards.

## Photocopy Guidelines
- Black‑and‑white copies on plain white 8.5×11" paper.
- Single‑sided, full page visible.
- Driver’s license copies: front and back on separate pages.

## Processing Times & Timing
- Standard processing: roughly **6–8 weeks**, longer during peak travel seasons.
- For travel in **October**, apply by **May or June** to allow a comfortable cushion.
- **Expedited service** is available for an additional fee if needed.

## Acceptance Facility Interaction
- Agent reviews the original naturalization certificate, then sends it with the application; it is returned separately by mail after processing. [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]]
- Two separate payments are typical: one for the State Department fee (check/money order) and one for the facility’s service fee (often cash/card).

## Alternatives for Mailing Concerns
- **In‑person passport agency appointments** are allowed for urgent travel (within 2 weeks, or 4 weeks with a visa). This avoids mailing the original certificate. [[entities/us_passport_agency]]
- No workaround using a passport card for a first‑time application; the original naturalization certificate is required.
- Use a **trackable USPS service** to mitigate anxiety about mailing the original document.

## Key Concepts
- **Passport application process** – end‑to‑end steps from document gathering to receipt. [[concepts/passport_application_process]]
- **Passport photo requirements** – size, background, and quality specifications. [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]]
- **Expedited service** – faster processing for an extra fee.
- **Payment methods** – distinction between State Department fees and acceptance facility fees.
- **Urgent passport agency** – in‑person option for time‑critical travel.
