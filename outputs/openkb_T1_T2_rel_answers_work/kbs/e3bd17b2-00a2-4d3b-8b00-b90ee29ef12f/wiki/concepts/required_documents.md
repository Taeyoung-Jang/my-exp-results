# Required Documents for Passport

This concept outlines the essential paperwork a naturalized U.S. citizen must present when applying for a first passport. The details are drawn from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]].

## Core Documents [[concepts/required_documents]]

1. **Certificate of Naturalization** – Bring the original for the acceptance agent to review and a clear black‑and‑white photocopy. The original is mailed with the application and returned separately by the [[entities/us_state_department]].
2. **Government‑issued Photo ID** – Typically a driver’s license. Provide the original and a front‑and‑back photocopy.
3. **DS‑11 Application Form** – Complete the [[entities/ds_11_form]] but do **not** sign until instructed by the [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]].
4. **Passport Photo** – One compliant image that meets the [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]] (size, background, quality).
5. **Payment** –
   - State Department fee: check or money order.
   - Acceptance facility fee: cash, debit card, or sometimes credit card, depending on the location.

## Supporting Details

- Photocopies should be black‑and‑white, single‑sided, on plain white 8.5×11" paper.
- The acceptance agent verifies the original documents, signs the form, and forwards the packet to the [[entities/us_state_department]].
- The original Certificate of Naturalization is returned to the applicant by mail after processing.

## Timing and Alternatives

- Standard processing takes about 6–8 weeks; apply by May‑June for travel in October.
- For urgent travel, an in‑person appointment at a [[entities/us_passport_agency]] can be used, allowing direct hand‑off of documents and avoiding mailing the original certificate.
