# Passport Photo Requirements

## Overview
A passport photo must conform to specific standards for size, background, and overall quality to be accepted in a U.S. passport application. It is a mandatory part of the [[concepts/required_documents]] for a first‑time passport.

## Key Details from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]]
- **Compliance:** The photo must meet the official size and background specifications set by the U.S. Department of State.
- **Acquisition:** Applicants can obtain a compliant photo at many pharmacies, photo centers, or other authorized vendors.
- **Application Context:** The photo is submitted with the DS‑11 form during the [[concepts/passport_application_process]] at a [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]] and is reviewed by the [[entities/us_state_department]].

## Standard Requirements (U.S. Passport)
- **Dimensions:** 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm).
- **Background:** Plain white or off‑white, free of shadows, textures, or objects.
- **Head Position:** Full face, centered, and neutral expression; both ears should be visible.
- **Quality:** Clear, sharp focus with no visible pixels, grain, or distortion; no glasses with glare, hats, or other accessories that obscure the face.
- **Color:** Color photo (black‑and‑white is not accepted).

## Practical Tips
1. **Use an authorized photo service** that advertises "passport‑ready" to ensure the image meets the size and background rules.
2. **Check the photo before submission** for any shadows, improper lighting, or reflections.
3. **Carry the printed photo** to the acceptance facility; digital submissions are not accepted for paper passport applications.
