# Passport Application Process

The **passport application process** for a first U.S. passport—especially after naturalization—covers gathering required documents, completing the [[concepts/required_documents]] and [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]], handling payments, and understanding processing timelines.

## Core Steps
1. **Prepare Documents**
   - Bring the original [[entities/certificate_of_naturalization]] and a clear black‑and‑white photocopy.
   - Provide a government‑issued photo ID (e.g., driver’s license) with original and front‑and‑back photocopy.
   - Complete the [[entities/ds_11_form]] (DS‑11) but leave the signature blank until the [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]] agent instructs you to sign.
   - Include one compliant passport photo that meets the [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]].
2. **Visit an Acceptance Facility**
   - The agent verifies the original citizenship proof, copies, and signs your application.
   - Payment is split: a check or money order for the [[entities/us_state_department]] fee and a separate fee for the acceptance facility (often cash or debit card).
   - The original naturalization certificate is mailed with the packet and later returned to you separately by the State Department.
3. **Submit and Wait**
   - Standard processing typically takes **6–8 weeks**, longer during peak seasons.
   - For travel in October, apply by May or June to provide a safety cushion.
   - Expedited service is available for an additional fee if tighter timing is required.
4. **Urgent/In‑Person Option**
   - For urgent travel (within 2 weeks, or 4 weeks with a visa), you can schedule an appointment at a [[entities/us_passport_agency]] to hand‑deliver documents, avoiding regular mailing.
   - This still requires the original certificate; there is no passport‑card workaround for first‑time applicants.

## Photocopy Requirements
- Black‑and‑white on plain white 8.5×11" paper.
- Single‑sided, full‑page visibility.
- Driver’s license copies: front and back on separate pages.

## Timing Recommendations
- **Standard**: Apply 2–3 months before intended travel.
- **Expedited**: Apply 1–2 months before travel if you can pay the extra fee.
- Use a trackable USPS service to reduce anxiety about mailing the original certificate.
