# U.S. Passport Acceptance Facility

**Type:** organization

The U.S. Passport Acceptance Facility is a designated location—often a post office, library, or municipal office—where applicants submit their first‑time passport paperwork for review and forwarding to the [[entities/us_state_department]].

---

## Core Functions

- **Document Verification**: Agents examine the original [[entities/certificate_of_naturalization]] (or birth certificate for non‑naturalized citizens) and required ID, ensuring they meet the standards outlined in [[concepts/required_documents]].
- **Form Handling**: Applicants bring a completed [[entities/ds_11_form]] (or other applicable forms); the agent witnesses the signature and packages the application.
- **Payment Collection**: Two separate payments are typical: the State Department passport fee (check or money order) and the facility’s service fee, which may be paid by cash, debit card, or sometimes credit card depending on the location.
- **Photograph Compliance**: The facility may provide guidance on meeting [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]] for a passport‑acceptable photo.

---

## Interaction with the Passport Process

The acceptance facility is a key step in the overall [[concepts/passport_application_process]]:
1. Applicant gathers required documents and a compliant photo.
2. They present originals and photocopies (black‑and‑white, single‑sided, 8.5×11) to the facility.
3. Agent verifies, witnesses the signature on the [[entities/ds_11_form]], and sends the packet to the State Department.
4. The original Certificate of Naturalization is mailed back to the applicant separately after processing.

---

## Practical Tips (from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]])

- **Bring Originals & Copies**: Both the original Certificate of Naturalization **and** a clear photocopy are required.
- **Payment Prep**: Prepare two checks/money orders (State Department fee and facility fee) and verify whether the location accepts cards for its own fee.
- **Timing**: Apply early (May–June) for travel in October to allow the standard 6–8 week processing window.
- **Mail Confidence**: Use trackable USPS service when mailing the application packet.
