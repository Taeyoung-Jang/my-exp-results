# Form I‑751

**Type:** other

Form I‑751, officially titled *Petition to Remove Conditions on Residence*, is a United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) form used by conditional permanent residents to request the removal of the two‑year condition attached to their green card.

## Key Facts (as referenced in [[summaries/session-13_2021-07-14]])
- **Purpose:** Allows holders of a conditional [[entities/green_card]] (typically issued after a marriage‑based immigration) to transition to a regular ten‑year green card.
- **Filing Window:** Must be filed within the 90‑day period before the conditional card’s expiration.
- **Submission:** Filed with [[entities/uscis]] either online or by mail, accompanied by supporting documentation (e.g., proof of a bona‑fide marriage).
- **Outcome:** Upon approval, the conditions are removed and the resident receives a standard permanent‑resident card; denial can lead to removal proceedings.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The process is part of overall [[concepts/green_card_compliance]], which includes tracking card expiration dates and filing required petitions.
- While unrelated to address changes, maintaining an up‑to‑date address via the AR‑11 form (see [[concepts/address_update_ar11]]) is also a compliance requirement for permanent residents.
