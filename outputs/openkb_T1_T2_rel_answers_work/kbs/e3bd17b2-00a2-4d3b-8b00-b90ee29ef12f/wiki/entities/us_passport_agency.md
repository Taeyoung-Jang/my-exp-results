# U.S. Passport Agency

**Type:** organization

The U.S. Passport Agency is a division of the [[entities/us_state_department]] that handles in‑person passport services, especially for urgent or time‑sensitive travel needs.  It provides a direct alternative to the standard mail‑in process at a [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]], allowing applicants to submit original documents such as the [[entities/certificate_of_naturalization]] without mailing them.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]]
- **Urgent appointments** are available for travelers who need a passport within two weeks (or four weeks with a visa).  These appointments let applicants hand over required documents directly, bypassing the standard mailing step.
- The agency processes the same required documents as the regular process, including the completed [[entities/ds_11_form]], a valid ID, passport photo, and the original proof of citizenship (e.g., naturalization certificate).
- It works in coordination with the [[entities/us_state_department]] for the actual passport issuance, while the acceptance facility handles the initial fee collection and document verification for standard applications.
