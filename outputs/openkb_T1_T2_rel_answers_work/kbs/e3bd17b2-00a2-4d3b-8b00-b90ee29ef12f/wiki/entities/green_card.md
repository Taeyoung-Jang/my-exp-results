# Green Card

The **Green Card** is the informal name for the United States permanent resident card, a government‑issued document that grants an individual the right to live and work indefinitely in the **[[entities/united_states]]**.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2021-07-14]]
- **Validity**: Physical cards are typically valid for ten years; a two‑year conditional card requires a petition to remove conditions before expiration (**[[entities/form_i_751]]**).
- **Renewal**: Cardholders must file for renewal using the appropriate USCIS form when the card nears its expiration.
- **Document set to keep**: Green Card, **[[entities/passport]]**, original approval notice, and **[[entities/social_security_card]]**. These should be stored securely and copies of any USCIS filings (e.g., AR‑11 address update) retained as proof.
- **Address updates**: Permanent residents must submit an **AR‑11** change‑of‑address form within ten days of moving, even with no pending applications (**[[concepts/address_update_ar11]]**, **[[entities/uscis]]**).
- **Job changes**: Changing employers does not affect Green Card status and requires no USCIS notification (**[[concepts/green_card_compliance]]**).
- **State‑level actions**: Moving states triggers updates to driver’s licenses and vehicle registration, but not to immigration status.
