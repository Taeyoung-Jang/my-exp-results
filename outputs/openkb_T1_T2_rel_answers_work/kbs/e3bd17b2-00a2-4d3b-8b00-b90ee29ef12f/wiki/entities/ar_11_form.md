# AR‑11 Change‑of‑Address Form

**Type:** other

The AR‑11, formally known as the **Change of Address** form, is the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) requirement for all non‑citizen residents—such as green‑card holders—to notify the agency of any change of residence within the United States.

## Key facts from the conversation (session‑13)
- **When to file:** within **ten days** of moving to a new address, even if the resident has no pending USCIS applications.  
- **How to file:** online through the USCIS website; the system provides a confirmation screen or email that should be saved as proof of filing.  
- **Purpose:** keeps the agency’s records current, which is important for any future immigration filings, notices, or possible audits.
- **Related documents:** the form is distinct from other immigration paperwork such as the **I‑751** petition for conditional residents, but it often appears alongside the green‑card ([[entities/green_card]]) and passport ([[entities/passport]]) when compiling a personal immigration file.
