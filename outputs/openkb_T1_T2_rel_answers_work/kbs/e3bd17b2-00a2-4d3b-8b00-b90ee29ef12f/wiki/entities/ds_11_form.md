# DS‑11 Form

The **DS‑11 Form** is the official U.S. Department of State application used by individuals applying for their first passport, including those who have recently naturalized.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]]
- **Purpose**: Required for first‑time passport applicants and for those replacing a lost or damaged passport.
- **When to Use**: Must be completed when applying at a [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]] after naturalization; the form is not signed until instructed by the acceptance agent.
- **Submission Process**: Submit the completed, **unsigned** form along with:
  - Original [[entities/certificate_of_naturalization]] and a clear photocopy.
  - Original government‑issued photo ID (e.g., driver’s license) and a front‑and‑back photocopy.
  - One compliant passport photo (see [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]]).
  - Payment: a check or money order for the State Department fee (handled by [[entities/us_state_department]]) and a separate fee for the acceptance facility.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/passport_application_process]] – the overall workflow that includes filling out the DS‑11, presenting documents, and obtaining the passport.
- [[concepts/required_documents]] – the set of documents that must accompany the DS‑11, such as the naturalization certificate and ID copies.
- [[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]] – specifications for the passport photo submitted with the DS‑11.

## Additional Notes
- The DS‑11 must be **hand‑delivered** to an acceptance facility; it cannot be mailed directly by the applicant.
- For urgent travel, applicants may visit a [[entities/us_passport_agency]] in person, but the DS‑11 is still required.
- The form is publicly available on the U.S. State Department website and can be printed prior to the appointment.
