# Passport (product)

A **passport** is an official government‑issued travel document that verifies a holder's identity and citizenship, allowing international travel and entry into foreign countries.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-13_2021-07-14]]
- Mentioned as one of the core documents a U.S. permanent resident should keep alongside the green card and the original approval notice.
- Recommended to keep a physical copy and a digital backup for proof of identity and for use in employment verification processes.
- Unlike the green card, a passport does not require renewal for immigration status purposes, though it must be renewed when it expires for travel.
