# United States (place)

The United States is a federal republic spanning North America. In the context of **session-13_2021-07-14**, it is the jurisdiction whose immigration system governs the discussed visa and residency categories.

## Key immigration concepts referenced
- The document outlines several major [[concepts/immigration_status_categories]] relevant to people living in the United States, including temporary visitor status, work‑authorized non‑immigrant status, permanent residency (green card), and humanitarian/family‑based statuses.
- For permanent residents, the guidance focuses on [[concepts/green_card_compliance]], emphasizing tracking the expiration of the physical green card (ten‑year or conditional two‑year) and maintaining core documents such as a [[entities/green_card]], [[entities/passport]], and [[entities/social_security_card]].
- When a resident changes address within the United States, they must file the USC CIS AR‑11 form. This requirement is captured under [[concepts/address_update_ar11]] and involves submitting the [[entities/ar_11_form]] within ten days of moving.

## Relevant agencies and forms
- **USCIS** (U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services) is the federal agency that processes green‑card applications, AR‑11 address updates, and the petition to remove conditions on a two‑year green card, which uses [[entities/form_i_751]].
