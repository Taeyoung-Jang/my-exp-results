## US State Department

The **U.S. Department of State** (often referred to as the State Department) is the federal executive department responsible for issuing U.S. passports, managing diplomatic relations, and overseeing the country's foreign policy.

### Role in passport issuance
- Collects the passport fee and processes the **[[entities/passport]]** application.
- Receives the original **[[entities/certificate_of_naturalization]]** when a naturalized citizen applies for a first‑time passport.
- Returns the naturalization certificate separately after processing.
- Sets processing timelines discussed in **[[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]]**, typically six to eight weeks for standard service and offers an **expedited** option for an additional fee.

### Related concepts and entities
- Part of the broader **[[concepts/passport_application_process]]** and **[[concepts/required_documents]]**.
- Works with **[[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]]** where applicants submit their paperwork and make the first payment.
- Coordinates with **[[entities/us_passport_agency]]** for urgent, in‑person applications.
- Sets the standards for **[[concepts/passport_photo_requirements]]**.

### Key facts from the session-35 discussion
- The State Department fee must be paid by **check or money order**; credit cards are not accepted for this portion.
- Processing time can be several months during peak travel seasons; applying in May or June is advised for travel in October.
- The Department returns the original naturalization certificate separately after the passport is issued.
