# Certificate of Naturalization

The **Certificate of Naturalization** is the primary proof of U.S. citizenship issued to individuals who have completed the naturalization process. It is an official government document that confirms the holder’s status as a naturalized citizen of the United States.

## Key Facts (from [[summaries/session-35_2024-07-14]])
- **Original required for passport applications** – When applying for a first‑time U.S. passport, the original certificate must be presented to the [[entities/us_passport_acceptance_facility]] for verification and then mailed with the passport packet.
- **Photocopy needed** – Applicants must also provide a clear black‑and‑white photocopy on plain 8.5×11" paper, single‑sided, as part of the [[concepts/required_documents]] for the passport.
- **Returned after processing** – The State Department returns the original certificate separately by mail after the passport is issued; it is not retained permanently by the acceptance facility.
- **In‑person alternative** – For urgent travel, applicants can schedule an appointment at a [[entities/us_passport_agency]] to submit the certificate directly, avoiding regular mailing delays.
- **Payment considerations** – The passport fee (paid to the [[entities/us_state_department]]) requires a check or money order, while the acceptance facility may accept cash, debit card, or other methods for its service fee.
