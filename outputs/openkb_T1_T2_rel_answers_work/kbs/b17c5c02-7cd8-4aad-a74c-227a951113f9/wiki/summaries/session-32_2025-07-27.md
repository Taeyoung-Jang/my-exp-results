## Core Request
User wants a practical tool to compare staying in the current rental versus moving, and later a revised tool for making the most of the current lease year.

## Decision Checklist & Scoring System
- Simple 1‑to‑5 scoring for each factor.  
- Categories: Money, Commute/Time, Flexibility, Lifestyle Fit, Stability/Stress.  
- Suggested weights (Money 3, Commute 2, Flexibility 3, Lifestyle 2, Stability 2) → [[concepts/scoring_system]].  
- Total score = sum(weight × score). Higher total indicates the preferred option.
