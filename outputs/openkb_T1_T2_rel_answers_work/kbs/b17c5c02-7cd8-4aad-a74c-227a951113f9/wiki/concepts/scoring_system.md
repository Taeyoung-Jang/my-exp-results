## Scoring System

The **Scoring System** is a lightweight, numeric framework introduced in [[summaries/session-32_2025-07-27]] to help compare the option of staying in a current rental versus moving to a new place. It converts qualitative factors into a quantitative total, making the decision clearer.

### How It Works
1. **Define Categories** – Identify the decision factors (e.g., Money, Commute/Time, Flexibility, Lifestyle fit, Stability/Stress). The categories used in the source document are:
   - Money (rent + upfront costs)
   - Commute / time
   - Flexibility
   - Lifestyle fit / comfort
   - Stability / stress level
2. **Assign Weights** – Each category receives a weight that reflects its relative importance (e.g., Money = 3, Commute = 2, Flexibility = 3, Lifestyle = 2, Stability = 2). Weights allow customization to personal priorities.
3. **Score Each Option** – For both "Stay" and "Move", give a score from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) for each category based on how well the option satisfies that factor.
4. **Calculate Totals** – Multiply each score by its weight and sum across categories:
   ```
   Total = Σ (Weight × Score)
   ```
   The option with the higher total is the preferred choice.

### Example from the Session
| Category | Weight | Stay Score | Stay Total | Move Score | Move Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Money | 3 | 4 | 12 | 2 | 6 |
| Commute / time | 2 | 3 | 6 | 5 | 10 |
| Flexibility | 3 | 5 | 15 | 3 | 9 |
| Lifestyle fit | 2 | 3 | 6 | 4 | 8 |
| Stability | 2 | 4 | 8 | 3 | 6 |
**Total Stay** = 47, **Total Move** = 39 – staying scores higher.

### When to Use It
- **Binary decisions** such as "stay vs. move".
- **Annual planning** where the focus shifts to budgeting and timeline (see [[concepts/budget_stability]] and [[concepts/decision_checklist]] for related frameworks).

### Benefits
- **Transparency:** Makes assumptions explicit through weights and scores.
- **Flexibility:** Users can adjust categories, weights, and scoring criteria.
- **Speed:** Quick to fill out with a simple table.
