# Broad Market Index Fund

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A diversified, low‑cost fund that tracks the entire stock market.

**Key points from session-02_2020-06-15:**
- Recommended as the core holding for a simple, low‑maintenance portfolio.
- Emphasized for its broad exposure and low fees, aligning with the principle of [[concepts/low-cost-index-investing]].
- Suggested to be paired with a modest bond allocation to provide stability.
- The bond side may include [[entities/investment-grade-corporate-bond]] or [[entities/us-treasury-securities]] depending on the investor’s risk tolerance.
