## Total Bond Fund

A **Total Bond Fund** is a pooled investment product that aggregates a variety of fixed‑income securities—typically a blend of government debt (e.g., [[entities/us-treasury-securities]]) and corporate bonds (often [[entities/investment-grade-corporate-bond]]s).  It offers investors a single‑ticket way to gain exposure to the broader bond market without having to select individual issues.

### Key Characteristics
- **Diversified Bond Exposure** – Holds both Treasuries and corporate bonds, providing a balance of credit quality and yield.
- **Convenient Allocation** – Serves as a ready‑made solution for the **bond‑allocation role** in a portfolio, simplifying the decision of how much of the bond bucket should be devoted to stabilization versus income.  See [[concepts/bond-allocation-role]].
- **Potential Trade‑off** – Because it mixes higher‑risk corporates with safer government debt, the stabilizing effect can be softer than a pure Treasury position.  This is a point highlighted in the discussion of [[summaries/session-02_2020-06-15]].
- **Cost Considerations** – May have higher expense ratios than a pure Treasury fund, though many aim to be low‑cost and broadly diversified, aligning with the principles of [[concepts/low-cost-index-investing]].

### When It Might Be Chosen
- Investors who want **broad bond market exposure** in a single fund.
- Those who prefer a **hands‑off approach** and do not wish to allocate separate portions to Treasuries and corporates.
- Situations where the convenience of a single fund outweighs the desire for a cleaner, Treasury‑only ballast.

### Comparison with Treasury‑Only Strategies
- **Pure Treasury Strategy** – Emphasizes credit‑risk‑free ballast and clear behavior during equity sell‑offs; typically involves intermediate‑term Treasuries (see [[concepts/intermediate-term-treasury-strategy]]).
- **Total Bond Fund** – Provides a blend that can improve yield but may increase correlation with equities if the corporate slice leans toward lower credit quality or longer duration.
