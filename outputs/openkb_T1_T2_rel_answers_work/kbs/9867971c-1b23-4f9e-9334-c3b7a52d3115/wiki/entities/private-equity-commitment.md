# Private‑Equity Commitment

**Type:** Other

**Description:** A financial pledge made to a private‑equity fund that is not yet drawn down. It represents capital that the investor has committed to provide when the fund issues a capital call.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2023-06-15]]**:
- Total commitment: **$600,000**
- Amount already called: **$240,000**
- Uncalled (still reserved): **$360,000**
- Treated as a liquidity‑draining reserve in the [[concepts/liquidity-framework]] and [[concepts/reserve-buckets]] approach.

**Relevance to personal finance strategy:**
- The uncalled portion is considered unavailable cash and should be accounted for as a separate reserve bucket when evaluating the adequacy of a [[entities/high-yield-savings-account]] balance.
- It influences the overall liquidity floor alongside personal emergency funds and [[entities/rental-property]] reserves.

**Related entities and concepts:**
- [[entities/high-yield-savings-account]] – the cash vehicle used to hold reserves.
- [[entities/rental-property]] – other long‑term, illiquid assets paired with private‑equity commitments in the liquidity analysis.
- [[concepts/liquidity-framework]] – the three‑bucket model (personal emergency fund, rental reserve, private‑equity reserve).
- [[concepts/reserve-buckets]] – the classification of cash needs that includes the private‑equity reserve.
