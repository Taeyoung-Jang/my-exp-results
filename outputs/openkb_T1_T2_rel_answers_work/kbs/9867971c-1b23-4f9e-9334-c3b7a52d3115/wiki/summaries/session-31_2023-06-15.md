# Summary of Session 31 (2023‑06‑15)

**Main contribution**: A practical, three‑bucket liquidity framework that matches personal cash, rental‑property reserves, and private‑equity capital‑call reserves to determine a healthy high‑yield savings (HYSA) balance, illustrated with a concrete worksheet and green/yellow/red thresholds.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/liquidity-framework]] – Matching assets to time‑horizon and risk tolerance.  
- [[concepts/reserve-buckets]] – Grouping cash into a personal emergency fund, rental reserves, and private‑equity reserves.

## Framework Overview
1. **Personal Emergency Fund**  
   - Formula: `Monthly Spending × Desired Months`.  
   - Example uses 6 months → $11,500 × 6 = **$69,000**.

2. **Rental Reserve**  
   - Total monthly carrying cost = sum of PITI/HOA for all properties.  
   - Apply a reserve factor (1.5×) and add average monthly repair cost.  
   - Example: $15,000 × 1.5 = $22,500 + $1,500 (repairs) = **$24,000**.

3. **Private‑Equity Reserve**  
   - Full uncalled commitment is reserved.  
   - Example: **$360,000** (uncalled portion of [[entities/private-equity-commitment]]).

**Liquidity Floor** = Emergency Fund + Rental Reserve + Private‑Equity Reserve = **$453,000**.

## HYSA Thresholds
- **Green**: ≥ $453k (fully funded).  
- **Yellow**: $400k – $450k (close but not fully covered).  
- **Red**: < $400k (insufficient liquidity).
