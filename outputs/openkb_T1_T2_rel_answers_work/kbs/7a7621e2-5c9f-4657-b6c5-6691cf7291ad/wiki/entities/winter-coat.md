# Winter Coat (product)

**Overview**
A winter coat is a warm, functional outerwear piece designed to protect against cold and inclement weather. It is a core item in a streamlined winter wardrobe.

**Key Recommendations from [[summaries/session-31_2025-04-13]]**
- **Quantity**: Keep 2–3 winter coats to avoid redundancy. Typical roles include an everyday coat, a dressier coat, and an optional heavy‑duty coat for severe climates.
- **Selection Criteria**: Follow a [[concepts/closet-decluttering]] approach—retain coats that are warm, in good condition, and that you actually reach for during the season. Discard bulky, uncomfortable, or duplicate items.
- **Storage**: Store coats on sturdy hangers, clean them beforehand, and keep them in a well‑ventilated space. Use a breathable [[entities/garment-bag]] rather than plastic covers to prevent musty odors and preserve shape. This aligns with the [[concepts/winter-outerwear-storage]] guidelines.
- **Role Definition**: Apply a [[concepts/coat-selection-strategy]] by assigning each coat a distinct function (e.g., deep‑winter workhorse, active‑day jacket, or dressy option) to avoid overlap.

**Typical Variants**
- **Long down parka**: Often the anchor piece for extremely cold, snowy regions. See related [[entities/down-parka]] for a specific example.
- **Short insulated jacket**: Suited for milder days or active outings; complements the heavier coat.

**Practical Tips**
1. Try the coat on to assess fit and comfort before deciding to keep it.
2. Ensure the coat is clean before storage to maintain freshness.
3. Choose breathable storage solutions to preserve the coat’s shape and material.
