# Closet Decluttering

**Definition**: A systematic approach to reduce wardrobe items by evaluating their recent usage, fit, condition, and personal excitement, aiming for a streamlined, functional closet.

---

## Core Principles (from [[summaries/session-31_2025-04-13]])

1. **Usage Test** – If you didn’t wear the item at all last season, it’s a strong candidate for removal.
2. **Fit & Feel** – Try the piece on; it should still fit comfortably and make you feel good.
3. **Condition Check** – Keep only items that are clean, warm, functional, and free from damage.
4. **Duplicate Elimination** – Discard pieces that duplicate the role of another garment.
5. **Emotional Response** – Retain items you are genuinely excited to wear again.

These steps form the backbone of the **gut‑check** method recommended in the session.

## Practical Workflow

1. **Gather** all clothing in one place.
2. **Sort** into three piles: Keep, Donate/Recycle, Unsure.
3. **Apply the Usage Test** – Move any item not worn last season to the Donate/Recycle pile.
4. **Fit & Feel** – Try on the Keep pile; move anything that feels off to Unsure.
5. **Condition Review** – Inspect for stains, tears, or wear; only clean, functional pieces stay.
6. **Final Decision** – If still Unsure, place the item in a “maybe” box and revisit after a set period (e.g., 30 days).

---

## Benefits

- Reduces visual clutter and decision fatigue.
- Highlights gaps in your wardrobe, informing smarter purchases.
- Extends the lifespan of kept items by providing proper storage.
