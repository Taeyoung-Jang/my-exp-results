## Summary of Session 11 (2024‑07‑15)

- **Purpose**: Provide practical advice on whether to keep a heavy wool coat and how to store it long‑term while preventing moth damage, mildew, and shape loss.
- **Key Recommendations**:
  - **Keep or Donate**: Retain the coat if it fits your style, is high‑quality, and you may wear it occasionally; otherwise consider donating.
  - **Cleaning Before Storage**:
    - Dry‑clean only if the coat is dirty or stained; otherwise a gentle brush and airing out is sufficient.
    - Ensure the coat is completely dry and free of body oils before packing.
  - **Hanging vs. Folding**:
    - Prefer hanging on a wide‑shouldered hanger to maintain shoulder structure.
    - If humidity is high, a breathable cotton/canvas garment bag with moisture absorbers works; loosely fold in a fabric box only if needed.
  - **Breathable Garment Bags**: Use cotton or canvas bags (or non‑woven if preferred) and leave a small gap around the coat for airflow.
  - **Moth Prevention**:
    - Use natural deterrents such as cedar blocks, sachets, or cedar‑wood hangers.
    - Refresh or sand cedar blocks once or twice a year; replace sachets as scent fades.
  - **Moisture Control**:
    - Place silica‑gel or charcoal dehumidifier pouches near the coat.
    - Avoid sealed plastic bags that trap humidity.
  - **Maintenance Checks**:
    - Inspect the coat monthly during storage for moisture, mildew, or signs of moth activity.
