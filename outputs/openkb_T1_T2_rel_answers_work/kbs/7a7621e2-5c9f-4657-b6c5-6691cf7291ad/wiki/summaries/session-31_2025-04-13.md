# Summary of session-31_2025-04-13

**Topic:** Practical advice for deciding which winter clothes to keep, how many coats are reasonable, storage tips, and building a streamlined outerwear lineup.

---

## 1. Decluttering Criteria
- Use a **gut‑check**: if you didn’t wear the item last season, consider letting it go.
- Try the piece on; assess fit, comfort, and excitement to wear it again.
- Keep items that are **warm, functional, and in good condition**; discard bulky, uncomfortable, or duplicate pieces.

> This aligns with the broader [[concepts/closet-decluttering]] approach of minimizing unused garments.

---

## 2. Reasonable Number of Winter Coats
- Typical recommendation: **2–3 coats**.
  - **Everyday coat** – versatile for most cold days.
  - **Dressier coat** – suitable for work or evenings out.
  - **Heavy‑duty coat** – optional, for locations with severe winter conditions.
- More than three usually leads to redundancy.

---

## 3. Storage Best Practices
- Hang coats on sturdy hangers to preserve shoulder shape.
- Ensure coats are **clean** before storage.
- Store in a **well‑ventilated area**; avoid plastic covers that trap moisture.
- Use a breathable [[entities/garment-bag]] if possible.

> These tips correspond to the [[concepts/winter-outerwear-storage]] guidelines.

---

## 4. Building a Core Outerwear Lineup
- Identify lifestyle needs and climate:
  1. **Everyday warm coat** – balanced warmth vs. bulk.
  2. **Sharper, tailored option** – wool or structured coat for professional settings.
  3. **Rugged coat** – only if the local winters demand heavy protection.
- Keep the lineup focused on distinct use‑cases to avoid overlap.

---

## 5. Long Down Parka vs. Short Insulated Jacket
- **Long down parka**: ideal anchor for deep‑winter, snowy conditions; provides maximum coverage and wind protection with minimal layering. (See [[entities/down-parka]])
- **Short insulated jacket**: works for milder days or active outings; lighter and less bulky.
- Distinguish each coat’s role:
  - Parka → deep‑winter workhorse.
  - Insulated jacket → active or transitional weather.
  - Dressier coat → aesthetic focus while still warm.

> This decision framework is part of [[concepts/coat-selection-strategy]] and the discussion of layering versus a parka.

---

## 6. Avoiding Redundant Coats
- Assign **distinct jobs** to each coat (e.g., deep‑winter, active, dressy).
- Review overlap periodically; remove any that duplicate functions.

---

**Key takeaways:** Apply a simple usage‑based filter, limit core coats to 2‑3 with defined roles, store them properly, and choose a long down parka as the primary winter anchor while complementing it with a lighter jacket and a dressier option.
