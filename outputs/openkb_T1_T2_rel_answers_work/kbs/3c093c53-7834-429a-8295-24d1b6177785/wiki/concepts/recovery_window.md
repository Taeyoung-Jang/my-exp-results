# Recovery Window

**Definition**: The *Recovery Window* is a minimum 48‑hour interval between any two heavy physical tasks (e.g., digging, hauling, edging) to allow the body to fully recuperate before the next demanding effort.

**Key Details from [[summaries/session-07_2024-07-17]]**
- The user identified a personal need for a full forty‑eight hours after a heavy landscaping day.
- The assistant built a weekly plan that spaces heavy days (Monday and Thursday) exactly one day apart, guaranteeing the required recovery window.
- Heavy sessions are capped at roughly 90 minutes, with a real break in the middle, to stay within the window while still making progress.
- Light‑day activities are explicitly low‑strain (tool tidy‑up, brief watering, planning) to avoid unintentionally breaking the window.

**Practical Guidelines**
1. **Identify heavy tasks** – any activity that involves sustained lifting, kneeling, bending, or repetitive motions (e.g., clearing a flower bed, soil amendment, mulching).
2. **Schedule heavy days** – place them at least two full days apart (e.g., Monday → Thursday). This creates the 48‑hour *Recovery Window*.
3. **Limit duration** – aim for 1–1.5 hours of work with a mid‑session break; stop before fatigue sets in.
4. **Use light days for** – planning, tool organization, short watering, scouting, or minor adjustments that do not tax muscles.
5. **Monitor soreness** – mild, even stiffness is acceptable; sharp or asymmetric pain signals the need to extend the recovery period.

**Related Concepts**
- [[concepts/task_scheduling_for_physical_recovery]] – broader strategies for aligning tasks with bodily recovery needs.
- [[concepts/work_intensity_pattern]] – the pattern of alternating heavy and light work days to manage overall workload.

**Benefits**
- Reduces risk of overuse injuries and chronic fatigue.
- Improves consistency of progress on yard projects.
- Allows the body to adapt and become stronger over time.

**Implementation Example**
From the source session, a two‑week plan for a 12 × 6 ft flower bed follows the *Recovery Window*:
- **Week 1**: Monday – 1.5 h clearing; Thursday – 1.5 h finishing clearing & edging.
- **Week 2**: Monday – 1.5 h soil amendment; Thursday – 1 h planting (optional mulching next heavy day).

By respecting the 48‑hour interval, each heavy session feels manageable and the overall project proceeds without burnout.
