# Task Scheduling for Physical Recovery

**Task Scheduling for Physical Recovery** is a practical framework that aligns yard‑work activities with the body’s need for rest, ensuring consistent progress without excessive fatigue. It draws on the principles outlined in [[summaries/session-07_2024-07-17]].

## Core Elements

1. **Recovery Window** – Maintain at least a 48‑hour gap between any high‑intensity work session. This creates enough time for muscle repair and prevents cumulative strain. [[concepts/recovery_window]]

2. **Work‑Intensity Pattern** – Use a *heavy‑day / light‑day* rhythm. Heavy days are scheduled on non‑consecutive weekdays (e.g., Monday and Thursday) and are limited to ~90 minutes of focused effort. Light days involve truly low‑strain activities such as planning, brief watering, or tool organization. [[concepts/work_intensity_pattern]]

3. **Task Grouping** – Bundle tasks that require the same type of physical effort (digging, hauling, edging) into a single heavy session. This avoids repeated micro‑stressors across days.

4. **Time Caps** – Set explicit time limits for each heavy day (e.g., 1.5 hours). Include a mid‑session break to reset posture and energy.

5. **Light‑Day Safeguards** – Identify “sneaky heavy” tasks (extended kneeling, hauling bags, intensive hand‑weeding) and reserve them for heavy days. Keep light‑day tasks to brief, non‑lifting actions.

6. **Optional Add‑Ons** – On a light day when feeling good, safe add‑ons include yard scouting, light pruning, and equipment tidy‑up. These do not compromise the next heavy session.

7. **Soreness Assessment** – Distinguish mild, even stiffness (acceptable) from sharp or asymmetric pain that limits normal movement (sign to back off). Adjust the schedule accordingly.

## Application Example

The concept was applied to a two‑week plan for renovating a 12 × 6 ft flower bed:
- **Week 1 Heavy Days** – Clearing and edging (≈90 min each).
- **Week 2 Heavy Days** – Soil amendment (≈90 min), planting (≈60 min), mulching (≈60 min on a subsequent heavy day).
- **Light Days** – Tool preparation, plant selection, spot checks, light watering.

By respecting the 48‑hour buffer and the heavy/light pattern, the project progresses steadily while minimizing fatigue.
