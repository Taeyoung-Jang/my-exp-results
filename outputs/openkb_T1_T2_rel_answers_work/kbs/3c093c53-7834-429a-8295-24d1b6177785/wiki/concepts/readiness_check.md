## Equipment‑Free 5‑Minute Readiness Check

**Source:** [[summaries/session-38_2025-03-15]]

The **Equipment‑Free 5‑Minute Readiness Check** is a concise, no‑equipment protocol designed to help anyone who has performed physically demanding work determine whether they can proceed with a light‑activity day or need a full rest day. It blends mobility, simple strength moves, and a brief cardio pulse, then uses clear pass/fail thresholds.

---
### Structure & Timing
| Phase | Duration | Activity | Target Reps / Notes |
|-------|----------|----------|---------------------|
| 1️⃣ Mobility Warm‑up | 1 minute | Shoulder rolls, arm circles, hip circles, followed by **5 controlled body‑weight squats**. | Observe any stiffness or awkwardness. |
| 2️⃣ Strength Segment | 90 seconds | **Push‑ups** (8‑10 reps) – choose standard, incline, or kneeling version. <br> **Squats** (12‑15 reps) – steady tempo. | Aim for an **RPE ≤ 3** (see [[concepts/rpe_threshold]]). |
| 3️⃣ Cardio Pulse | 30 seconds | Light march, jog in place, or brisk walk across the room. | After stopping, allow **30 s recovery**. |
| 4️⃣ Recovery Check | 30 seconds | Monitor breathing and heart‑rate return. | Breathing should normalize within **30 s** (see [[concepts/breath_recovery_threshold]]). |

---
### Pass / Fail Criteria
- **Pass**
  - Mobility feels smooth; no joint restriction.
  - Strength reps feel easy, with **RPE ≤ 3**; movements are coordinated and not unusually heavy.
  - Post‑cardio breathing returns to baseline within **30 s**; perceived effort stays low.
- **Fail**
  - Noticeable stiffness or awkwardness in mobility.
  - Strength feels shaky, heavy, or RPE **≥ 5**.
  - Breathing remains elevated beyond the 30‑second window or feels labored.
  - Any of these signs suggest **hidden fatigue** (see [[concepts/hidden_fatigue]]) and a true rest day is advisable.

---
### Why It Works
- **Sleep** and **protein intake** are the primary recovery drivers ([[concepts/sleep_recovery]], [[concepts/protein_intake]]). The check assumes those basics are met and focuses on the acute functional state.
- **Hydration** supports circulation and muscle function, influencing how quickly the brief cardio pulse resolves ([[concepts/hydration]]).
- The mobility portion aligns with [[concepts/mobility_work]] to prime joints without adding fatigue.
- By using an **RPE threshold** and a **breath recovery threshold**, the test quantifies subjective effort and objective physiological recovery.

---
### Practical Tips
- If standard push‑ups are too hard, switch to **incline** or **knees‑on‑floor** versions – keep the rep range the same.
- Replace squats with a **sit‑to‑stand** from a chair if knee issues exist.
- Perform the check in the morning after a night’s sleep or before the first major activity of the day.

---
### Integration with Daily Planning
This quick assessment can be part of a larger **task‑scheduling** strategy for physical recovery ([[concepts/task_scheduling_for_physical_recovery]]) by informing whether the day’s workload should be **light** or **restful**, especially after high **work intensity patterns** ([[concepts/work_intensity_pattern]]).
