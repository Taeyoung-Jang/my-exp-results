# RPE Threshold for Recovery Assessment

The **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) threshold** is a simple numeric cue used in the 5‑minute readiness check described in [[summaries/session-38_2025-03-15]]. It helps decide whether the day should be treated as a light‑activity day or a full rest day.

## Core Idea
- **RPE Scale:** 0 = no effort, 10 = maximal effort.
- **Target Threshold:** Aim for an RPE of **3 or lower** during the strength and cardio portions of the check. This aligns with a light‑to‑moderate effort that indicates adequate recovery.
- **Fail Threshold:** An RPE of **5 or higher**, or any sensation of heaviness, shakiness, or excessive strain, signals lingering fatigue and suggests taking a true rest day.

## How It Is Applied in the Readiness Check
1. **Strength Segment** – 8‑10 push‑ups (or a modified version) and 12‑15 body‑weight squats. The effort should feel like a 3/10 on the RPE scale.
2. **Cardio Pulse** – 30 seconds of light marching or jogging in place. After stopping, breathing should return to baseline within 30 seconds, and the perceived effort should stay at or below 3/10.
3. **Decision Rule**
   - **Pass:** All movements stay at or below the 3 RPE threshold and breathing normalizes quickly.
   - **Fail:** Any movement exceeds the 5 RPE threshold, feels unusually heavy, or breathing remains elevated.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The RPE threshold is a practical implementation of the broader [[concepts/rpe_threshold]] idea.
- It is part of the overall [[concepts/readiness_check]] framework for quick recovery assessment.
- By catching hidden fatigue (see [[concepts/hidden_fatigue]]), the threshold helps avoid over‑training.
- The breathing recovery component ties into [[concepts/breath_recovery_threshold]].
- Maintaining the threshold supports an optimal [[concepts/recovery_window]] after physical work.

## Practical Tips
- Use a simple 1‑10 verbal rating after each set; no equipment needed.
- If you cannot perform standard push‑ups, substitute with incline or knee push‑ups but keep the same RPE target.
- Consistently applying the threshold over weeks can improve your intuition for daily recovery needs.
