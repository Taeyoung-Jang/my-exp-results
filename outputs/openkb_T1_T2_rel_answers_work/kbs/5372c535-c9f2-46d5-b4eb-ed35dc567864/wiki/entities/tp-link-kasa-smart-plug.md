# TP‑Link Kasa Smart Plug

**Type:** product

A reliable Wi‑Fi enabled smart plug that can be scheduled to turn power on or off for compatible appliances. In the context of the morning routine outlined in [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]], it is used to power a drip‑coffee maker automatically, ensuring coffee starts brewing just before the alarm goes off.

## Key Facts from the Session Document
- Recommended alongside the **Amazon Smart Plug** as a simple, no‑frills automation solution.
- Works with coffee makers that have a **physical on/off switch** that stays on after power loss (e.g., [[entities/mr-coffee-12-cup-switch]], [[entities/black-decker-switch-brewer]]).
- Enables the **smart home automation** concept to reduce decision‑making friction in the [[concepts/morning_routine_design]] and supports [[concepts/behavioral_habit_stacking]] by making coffee preparation automatic.
- The plug is programmed to power the brewer roughly five minutes before the alarm, creating a consistent cue that helps break the snooze habit.

## How It Fits Into the Routine
1. **Night‑before setup:** Plug the coffee maker into the TP‑Link Kasa Smart Plug, load water and grounds, set the brewer’s switch to **ON**.
2. **Automation:** Use the Kasa app to schedule the plug to turn on 5 minutes before the alarm, so the coffee begins brewing as soon as you get out of bed.
3. **Morning flow:** The aroma of fresh coffee serves as a cue for the first wake‑up action (e.g., splash water on face) and helps you stick to the 15‑20 minute out‑the‑door plan.
