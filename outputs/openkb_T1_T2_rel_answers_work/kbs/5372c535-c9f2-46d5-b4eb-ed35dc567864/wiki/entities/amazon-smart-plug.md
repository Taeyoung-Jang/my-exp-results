# Amazon Smart Plug

The **Amazon Smart Plug** is a Wi‑Fi enabled power outlet that lets you turn devices on or off remotely via the Alexa app or voice commands. It is praised for its simple setup, reliable scheduling, and seamless integration with Amazon’s smart‑home ecosystem.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]
- Recommended as one of two reliable smart plugs for automating a coffee maker in a morning‑routine workflow.
- Works best with appliances that have a **physical on/off switch** that stays in the "on" position when power is cut and restored.
- Allows you to schedule the coffee maker to start brewing a few minutes before your alarm, supporting the [[concepts/smart_home_automation]] approach to reduce friction in the [[concepts/morning_routine_design]] process.
- By automating the coffee brew, it helps reinforce the habit‑stacking pattern described in [[concepts/behavioral_habit_stacking]].

## Usage tips
1. Verify the coffee maker’s switch stays on after power loss before connecting it to the plug.
2. Use the Alexa app to set a daily routine that powers the plug on 5 minutes before your alarm.
3. Place the plug in an outlet near the coffee maker for a tidy setup.
