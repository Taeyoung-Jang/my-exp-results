## Black+Decker Switch Brewer

**Type:** product

The Black+Decker Switch Brewer is a simple, inexpensive drip‑coffee machine that features a physical on/off switch that remains in the *on* position when power is restored. This characteristic makes it compatible with smart‑plug automation, allowing users to schedule brewing overnight and have coffee ready when the alarm goes off.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]
- **Automation‑ready:** Works with a smart plug (e.g., [[entities/tp-link-kasa-smart-plug]] or [[entities/amazon-smart-plug]]) because the switch stays on after a power cut.
- **Night‑before setup:** Load water reservoir, add grounds, set the switch to **on**, and plug into a smart plug; the machine will start brewing when power is restored.
- **Safety tip:** Verify the switch behavior by turning the machine on, unplugging, and plugging back in; it should resume brewing automatically.
- **Routine role:** Recommended as the drip‑coffee option in a 15‑20 minute morning routine designed to reduce snoozing and streamline coffee preparation.
