# Mr. Coffee 12‑cup Switch Brewer

**Type:** Product (drip coffee maker)

**Overview**
The Mr. Coffee 12‑cup Switch Brewer is a classic, affordable drip coffee machine that features a physical on/off switch which stays in the "on" position when power is cut. This characteristic makes it suitable for use with [[entities/tp-link-kasa-smart-plug]] or [[entities/amazon-smart-plug]] to automate brewing each morning.

**Key Facts from [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]**
- **Smart‑plug compatibility:** Because the brewer’s switch remains on after a power interruption, a smart plug can safely power it on at a scheduled time, allowing coffee to start brewing automatically before the alarm.
- **Routine integration:** It is recommended in a [[concepts/morning_routine_design]] that emphasizes "prep‑the‑night‑before" and a quick 15–20 minute out‑the‑door flow.
- **Safety tip:** Verify the physical switch stays on by turning the machine off, unplugging, and plugging back in; it should resume brewing without needing to press the button.
- **Product pairing:** Often suggested alongside the TP‑Link Kasa or Amazon Smart Plug for reliable home automation.

**Why It Works for Habit Building**
The reliable automation of coffee brewing supports [[concepts/behavioral_habit_stacking]] by coupling the cue (alarm) with the reward (fresh coffee aroma) without adding decision‑making friction. The simplicity of the machine aligns with the principle of minimizing steps in a morning habit loop.
