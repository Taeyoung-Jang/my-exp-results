# Keurig

**Type:** product  
**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]

Keurig is a single‑serve pod coffee machine (often called a “K‑cup” brewer) referenced in the user’s morning‑routine plan. In the suggested routine, the Keurig cannot be fully automated with a smart plug because it requires a manual button press to start brewing. Night‑before preparation includes:

- Filling the water reservoir.
- Placing a coffee pod beside the machine.
- Setting out a mug and any other items (clothes, bag, keys).

In the morning, after silencing an alarm placed across the room, the user walks to the Keurig, inserts a pod, and presses the brew button. While the coffee brews, they perform a quick “wake‑up” action (e.g., splash water on the face). The first cup is enjoyed on the spot, and a second pod can be brewed into a travel mug for the commute.

The routine ties into broader concepts of habit formation and smart‑home support:

- [[concepts/morning_routine_design]] – structuring a low‑friction start‑to‑day sequence.
- [[concepts/smart_home_automation]] – using smart plugs for drip brewers (the Keurig version relies on manual start).
- [[concepts/behavioral_habit_stacking]] – pairing coffee brewing with a brief physical cue.

Potential smart‑plug companions for automating other appliances in the routine include [[entities/amazon-smart-plug]] and [[entities/tp-link-kasa-smart-plug]].
