# Caffeine Timing

**Caffeine Timing** refers to the strategic placement of coffee or other caffeinated beverages within a daily routine to maximize alertness while minimizing negative side‑effects such as jitteriness, crashes, or interference with physiological measurements.

## Core Principles
1. **Delay Until After Initial Activities**
   - Wait 10–20 minutes after waking before drinking caffeine. This allows natural cortisol levels to peak and reduces the perception of a sudden energy jolt.
2. **Pair With Food**
   - Consume caffeine together with or immediately after a light, protein‑rich breakfast (e.g., yogurt with berries, whole‑grain toast with nut butter, quick oatmeal). This buffers stomach acidity and smooths the stimulant’s impact.
3. **Avoid Pre‑Measurement Consumption**
   - If you monitor blood pressure or heart rate first thing in the morning, postpone caffeine until after the reading. Caffeine can transiently raise heart rate and blood pressure, skewing the measurement.
4. **Consistent Timing**
   - Keep the caffeine intake time consistent day‑to‑day. This habit stacking aligns with overall [[concepts/morning_routine_design]] and supports predictable energy patterns.

## Benefits
- **Steadier Energy:** Reduces the risk of a post‑caffeine crash.
- **Improved Physiological Accuracy:** Prevents caffeine‑induced spikes that could affect early‑morning [[concepts/blood_pressure_measurement]].
- **Lower Anxiety/Jitter:** Food buffers the stimulant, lessening nervousness.
