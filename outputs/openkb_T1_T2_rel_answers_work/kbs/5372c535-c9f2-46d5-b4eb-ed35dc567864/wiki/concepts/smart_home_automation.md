# Smart Home Automation

**Definition**: Smart home automation refers to the use of network‑enabled devices (e.g., smart plugs, thermostats, lights) that can be programmed or remotely controlled to perform everyday tasks with minimal manual effort.

## Core Elements
- **Smart plugs** act as simple on/off relays that can be scheduled or triggered via apps/voice assistants. Popular, reliable options mentioned in the source are the [[entities/tp-link-kasa-smart-plug]] and the [[entities/amazon-smart-plug]].
- **Appliance compatibility** matters: devices like the [[entities/mr-coffee-12-cup-switch]] and [[entities/black-decker-switch-brewer]] have a physical power switch that stays on when power is cycled, making them suitable for smart‑plug control. Pod machines such as [[entities/keurig]] or [[entities/nespresso]] lack full automation because they require a manual brew button.

## Application in a Morning Routine
The session‑10 summary demonstrates a concrete use case: 
1. **Night‑before setup** – Load the coffee maker, place the mug, and plug the brewer into a smart plug.
2. **Scheduled activation** – Configure the smart plug to power the brewer a few minutes before the alarm, so the coffee starts brewing automatically.
3. **Habit integration** – The alarm is placed across the room to force a standing motion, then the user drinks the first cup while performing a quick “wake‑up” action. This links to broader ideas of [[concepts/morning_routine_design]] and [[concepts/behavioral_habit_stacking]].

## Safety Considerations
- Verify the appliance has a **true physical on/off switch** that remains on after a power loss. A simple test: turn the machine on, unplug it, then plug it back in; it should resume brewing.
- Keep the brewing area dry and ensure the water reservoir is properly seated.
- Avoid leaving brewed coffee in the carafe overnight; discard any leftover liquid for freshness.

## Benefits
- **Reduced friction**: Automating the brew eliminates a decision point, increasing the likelihood of sticking to the routine.
- **Consistency**: Timed activation delivers the same coffee‑ready experience each morning.
- **Scalability**: The same smart‑plug approach can extend to other appliances (lights, heaters, etc.) to streamline multiple aspects of daily life.
