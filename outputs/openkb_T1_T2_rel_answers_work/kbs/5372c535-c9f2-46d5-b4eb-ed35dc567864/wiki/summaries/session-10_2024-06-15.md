## Summary of session-10_2024-06-15

**Purpose:** Provide a concrete, time‑boxed (15‑20 min) morning routine that eliminates snoozing, incorporates two cups of coffee, and leverages smart‑plug automation. The user also wants variations for drip coffee vs. pod machines, product recommendations, safety tips, and a way to taper coffee intake.

### Core Routine Steps (Night‑before)
1. **Prepare coffee maker** – Load grounds, add water, place mug, and ensure the machine’s power switch stays **ON**.  
2. **Set up automation** – Plug the brewer into a smart plug (e.g., [[entities/tp-link-kasa-smart-plug]] or [[entities/amazon-smart-plug]]) and schedule it to turn on 5 min before the alarm or use a timer function on the brewer.  
3. **Lay out apparel & essentials** – Clothes, bag, keys, etc., near the door.  
4. **Place alarm** – Across the room at chest/arm‑height so you must stand to silence it.

### Morning Flow (15‑20 min)
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 0 min | Alarm rings; stand, turn off, walk to kitchen. |
| 1 min | Coffee starts (auto‑brew) or press brew button (pod machine). |
| 2‑7 min | Pour **Cup 1**, drink while doing a quick wake‑up move (face splash, stretch, or step outside). |
| 7‑12 min | Pour **Cup 2** into travel mug, get dressed, grab pre‑packed items. |
| 12‑20 min | Final checks, exit house. |

### Device‑Specific Variants
- **Drip‑coffee version** – Works with a smart plug because the brewer’s switch stays **ON** when power cycles. Recommended models: *[[entities/mr-coffee-12-cup-switch]]* and *[[entities/black-decker-switch-brewer]]*.  
- **Pod‑machine version (Keurig/Nespresso)** – Cannot fully automate; you still need a manual brew button. Night‑before steps include filling the water tank, placing a pod, and setting out the mug. Use either [[entities/keurig]] or [[entities/nespresso]].

### Smart Plug Recommendations
- **TP‑Link Kasa Smart Plug** – Reliable Wi‑Fi, easy scheduling.  
- **Amazon Smart Plug** – Integrates with Alexa, simple on/off control.

### Safety & Maintenance Tips
- Verify the brewer has a **physical on/off switch** that stays on after power loss.  
- Keep the brewing basket seated and dry; leaving water/grounds overnight is generally safe for most models.  
- Avoid leaving brewed coffee in the pot overnight – discard after use.  
- If you prefer fresher grounds, store pre‑measured grounds in a sealed container and add them right before brewing (adds ~10 s).

### Reducing Coffee Volume
- Brew full pot but serve a **smaller first cup** and a **half‑cup travel mug** instead of a full second cup.  
- Maintain the same ritual timing; only the volume changes to prevent perceived deprivation.  
- After 1‑2 weeks, further reduce the travel portion if desired.
