## Summary of Session 35 (2024‑12‑15)

The conversation focuses on designing a realistic, low‑stress **morning routine** that fits within a 20‑30 minute window and supports consistent **blood‑pressure** readings. Key recommendations include:

- **Wake‑up consistency**: Get out of bed promptly, sit for a minute, breathe, and avoid phone use before measuring blood pressure.
- **Measurement protocol**: Take the blood‑pressure reading right after waking, keeping movement, conversation, and stress to a minimum.
- **Hydration first**: Drink a glass of water immediately after the reading.
- **Quick stretch/brief walk**: Light movement to wake the body without elevating heart rate.
- **Simple, balanced breakfast**: Protein‑rich options such as yogurt with berries, whole‑grain toast with nut butter, or quick oatmeal to maintain steady energy.
- **Caffeine timing**: Delay coffee until the latter half of the 30‑minute period (after the reading and breakfast) to avoid spikes that could affect the measurement.
- **Avoidance**: Skip salty or sugary foods right before the reading, avoid caffeine, intense exercise, rushed movements, and phone/social‑media distractions.

### Practical 30‑Minute Schedule
1. **0‑2 min** – Sit up, breathe, prepare for blood‑pressure check.
2. **2‑4 min** – Take the reading (quiet, relaxed posture).
3. **4‑6 min** – Drink water.
4. **6‑10 min** – Light stretch or brief walk.
5. **10‑20 min** – Prepare/eat a quick protein‑focused breakfast.
6. **20‑30 min** – Enjoy coffee (with or immediately after the breakfast).
