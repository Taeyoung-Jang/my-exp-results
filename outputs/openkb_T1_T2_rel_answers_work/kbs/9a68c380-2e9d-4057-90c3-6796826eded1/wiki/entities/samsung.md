# Samsung (Organization)

Samsung is a South Korean multinational conglomerate known for its consumer electronics, especially the Galaxy line of smartphones, tablets, wearables, and its smart‑home platform **SmartThings**.

## Role in Session 09 (2024‑06‑15)
In the discussion on evaluating personal technology [[concepts/ecosystem-evaluation]], Samsung was highlighted as a middle‑ground option:
- **Integration:** Provides a fairly unified experience when staying within the Galaxy hardware family and using SmartThings for home automation.
- **Dependency:** Still relies on Google services for many core functions, making its integration less seamless than [[entities/apple]] and more dependent on external services.
- **Lock‑in:** Presents a moderate level of lock‑in; users can benefit from Samsung’s ecosystem while retaining some flexibility due to its Android foundation.

## Resilience & Lock‑in Mitigation
The session recommended strategies that apply to Samsung users to improve [[concepts/automation-resilience]] and reduce [[concepts/lock-in-mitigation]]:
- Duplicate critical automations using native SmartThings rules rather than third‑party bridges.
- Keep data in cross‑platform formats (e.g., notes, files) to avoid being tied solely to Samsung services.
- Maintain a secondary trusted device (Android or iOS) with the same authentication methods as a fallback.
