# Google (Organization)

Google is a global technology company that provides a broad, cloud‑based ecosystem spanning many hardware brands and platforms.

## Role in the Session‑09 Ecosystem Evaluation
- In the comparative analysis, Google’s ecosystem was described as **flexible** and **cloud‑centric**, offering great services that work across a wide range of devices.
- It was positioned between Apple’s tightly‑controlled stack and Samsung’s hardware‑focused approach, making it a middle‑ground choice for users who value variety and customization.
- The session highlighted Google’s **potential for lock‑in** being moderate, mainly due to reliance on its cloud services rather than a single hardware line.
