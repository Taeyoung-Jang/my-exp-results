# Arequipa

Arequipa is a major city in the **southern Peruvian highlands**, known for its colonial architecture, volcanic landscape, and a rich tradition of Andean textile production.  Local artisans have long practiced **hand‑woven** techniques using natural fibers such as wool and alpaca, often employing natural dyes like cochineal and indigo.
