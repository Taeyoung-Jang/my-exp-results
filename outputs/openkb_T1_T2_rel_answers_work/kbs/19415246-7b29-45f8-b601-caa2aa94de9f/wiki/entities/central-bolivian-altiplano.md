# Central Bolivian Altiplano

The Central Bolivian Altiplano is a high‑elevation plateau in the heart of Bolivia, encompassing parts of the Cochabamba and La Paz departments. It is home to Indigenous Aymara and Quechua communities that have maintained distinctive weaving traditions for centuries.

## Textile relevance
- Traditional garments from the Altiplano often use **hand‑spun wool** and natural dyes such as **cochineal** reds and **indigo** blues, producing the irregular tonal qualities described in [[concepts/textile-authentication]].
- Common weave structures include plain weave for everyday ponchos and tapestry weave for ceremonial panels, exemplifying the characteristics outlined in [[concepts/handwoven-characteristics]].
- Motifs may carry cultural significance; respectful handling aligns with the guidance in [[concepts/cultural-sensitivity-andine-textiles]].
