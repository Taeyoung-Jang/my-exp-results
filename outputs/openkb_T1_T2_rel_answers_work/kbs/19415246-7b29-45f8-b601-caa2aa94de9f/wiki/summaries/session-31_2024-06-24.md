# Summary of session‑31_2024‑06‑24

## Core ideas
- **Zone‑based organization**: assign items to the room where they are used (entryway, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom) to align with daily habits.
- **Clutter‑prevention checklist**: 
  - Return items to their natural spot after use.
  - Keep flat surfaces clear nightly.
  - Perform a two‑minute tidy before bed.
  - When adding a new item, remove an old one.
- **Minimal, removable storage**: recommend low‑profile, no‑drill products for renters (over‑the‑door racks, adhesive hooks, freestanding shelves, rolling carts).
- **Specific dimensions** for tight spaces (e.g., ≤4 in deep door hooks, 7‑9 in deep shoe rack, 5‑6 in wide rolling cart).
- **Dedicated homes for “in‑between” items** (sunglasses, reusable bags, tool kit) to keep them out of high‑traffic zones.

## Practical recommendations
| Zone | Item category | Suggested organizer (no‑drill) | Approx. dimensions |
|------|----------------|--------------------------------|--------------------|
| Entryway | Keys, wallet, mail | Small tray or bowl + slim metal hook rack (5‑6 hooks) | <4 in deep, 6‑8 in wide |
| Entryway | Shoes, bags | Lean‑against‑wall shoe rack, over‑the‑door hook or fabric pouch | 7‑9 in deep, 8‑10 in wide |
| Kitchen | Pantry staples | Freestanding tiered shelf or cabinet riser | 10‑12 in wide |
| Kitchen | Cleaning supplies | Under‑sink caddy with handle | 12 in long × 5‑6 in wide |
| Bathroom | Toiletries | Slim rolling cart or countertop drawer organizer | 5‑6 in wide, ≤8 in deep |
| Bathroom | Misc. grooming tools | Over‑the‑door hanging pouch | Flat, fabric, removable |
| Anywhere | Sunglasses | Small open bowl or shallow drawer | 4‑6 in wide |
| Anywhere | Reusable shopping bags | Fold‑into‑one tote hung on door hook | N/A |
| Bedroom/Closet | Tool kit | Compact case (size of hardcover book) placed under bed or on top shelf | ~12 in tall |
