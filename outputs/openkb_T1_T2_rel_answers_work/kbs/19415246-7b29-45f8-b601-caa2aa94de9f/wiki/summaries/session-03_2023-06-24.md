## Authentic identification of Andean textiles
- Distinguish **authentic hand‑woven pieces** from modern replicas by examining **fiber type**, **weave irregularities**, **edge finishes**, and **dye characteristics**.
- Natural fibers (wool, alpaca, cotton) show subtle thickness variation; machine‑spun yarn is uniformly smooth.
- Hand‑woven cloth reveals tiny tension wavering and non‑machine‑perfect pattern joins.
- Traditional natural dyes (e.g., [[entities/cochineal]], [[entities/indigo]], other plant extracts) display tonal depth and uneven fading, unlike the flat saturation of synthetic dyes.

## Hands‑on checks for vintage ponchos
- **Texture test**: feel for hand‑spun irregularities.
- **Weave inspection**: hold to light, look for slight irregular tension.
- **Edge & selvedge**: integral or braided selvedge signals tradition; overlock indicates commercial production.
- **Neck slit finish**: wrapped yarn or loom‑formed openings vs. machine overcast.
- **Repair evidence**: compatible yarn mends suggest age and traditional use.

## Cultural sensitivity
- Certain motifs, color palettes, and panel constructions are **ceremonial or community‑identifying** (e.g., stepped‑diamond motifs, specific ayllu colors). See [[concepts/cultural-sensitivity-andine-textiles]] for guidance.
- Wear such pieces only with permission; otherwise treat them as display items.
- Supporting artisans through **ethical cooperatives** ensures benefit to originating communities.

## Inspection checklist (field‑guide style)
1. Overall silhouette & drape  
2. Fiber feel (hand‑ vs. machine‑spun)  
3. Light‑through weave regularity  
4. Selvedge & neckline condition (photograph all edges)  
5. Pattern clarity & join quality  
6. Dye softness and fading pattern  
7. Repairs or wear signs  
8. Precise measurements (length, width, neck opening)  
9. Provenance notes  

## Glossary (field‑guide style)
- **Neck Slit (Abertura)** – Vertical opening, either cut post‑weave and hand‑reinforced, or woven directly on the loom.  
- **Neck Wrapping** – Hand‑applied yarn wrap protecting the slit; often irregular and tone‑matched.  
- **Loom‑Formed Neck Opening** – Slit formed during weaving, showing clean, uncut edges.  
- **Center Seam / Panel Join** – Hand‑sewn seam with matching wool; may create a subtle interior ridge.  
- **Decorative Join** – Visible raised or zig‑zag stitch used as a stylistic signature.  
- **Integral Selvedge** – Edge created on the loom, dense and stitch‑free.  
- **Braided Selvedge** – Narrow hand‑braided edge, traditional marker.  
- **Blanket Stitch Edge** – Hand‑sewn looping edge, often on repairs.  
- **Overlock Edge** – Machine‑finished looped edge, typical of tourist‑market pieces.  
- **Supplementary‑Weft Border** – Decorative band added with extra colored wefts.  
- **Plain Weave** – Simple over‑under structure, common in everyday ponchos. See [[concepts/handwoven-characteristics]].  
- **Twill Weave** – Diagonal‑rib pattern, slightly more flexible.  
- **Tapestry Weave** – Discontinuous‑weft technique for crisp, thick patterned panels.  
- **Backstrap‑Loom Weaving** – Traditional method yielding narrow panels and precise selvedges.  
- **Hand‑Spun Wool** – Irregular thickness, softer texture.  
- **Machine‑Spun Wool** – Uniform thickness, smoother feel.  
- **Natural Dyes** – Plant or insect derived, uneven but rich (e.g., [[entities/cochineal]], [[entities/indigo]]).  
- **Synthetic Dyes** – Uniform, bright, often flat.

## Recommendations
- Use the checklist and catalog template for each poncho to build a consistent, searchable collection.
- Record **[[concepts/textile-authentication]]**, **[[concepts/handwoven-characteristics]]**, and **[[concepts/cultural-sensitivity-andine-textiles]]** as reusable concepts across the knowledge base.
- When unsure about a motif’s cultural significance, treat the piece as a display item and seek community input before wearing.
- Example provenance entries may reference locations such as [[entities/arequipa]], the [[entities/central-bolivian-altiplano]], or the [[entities/southern-peruvian-highlands]].
