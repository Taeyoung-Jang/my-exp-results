## Summary of Session 32 (2025‑05‑12)

**Topic:** Designing a lightly‑structured weekly routine for people whose schedules have opened up (e.g., retirees).

### Core idea
- Use a small set of **anchors** (3–5) to give each day shape without feeling crowded.  
- Combine [[concepts/daily-ritual-anchor]], a few [[concepts/weekly-maintenance-routine]] touchpoints, and generous open blocks for spontaneity.

### Suggested weekly layout (copy‑paste into a paper planner)

| Day | Anchors |
|-----|---------|
| **Mon** | Morning ritual only; open day |
| **Tue** | Morning ritual; open day |
| **Wed** | Morning ritual; **mid‑week social touchpoint** |
| **Thu** | Morning ritual; open day |
| **Fri** | Morning ritual; **light reset hour** in the afternoon |
| **Sat** | Morning ritual; **creative block** (morning/early afternoon) |
| **Sun** | Morning ritual; open & easy |

### Open‑block menu (10–15 concrete options, grouped)

**Home** – light tidying, plant care, one‑drawer refresh, try a new recipe  
**Social** – call/text a friend, coffee meet‑up, send a quick note  
**Outdoors** – short walk, sit outside 10 min, visit a nearby park/trail  
**Learning** – read a chapter, watch an educational video, practice a skill for 15 min  
**Relaxation** – stretch, tea break, watch something comforting, short nap

### Light tracking method
- Two tiny checkboxes at the bottom‑right of each day:  
  - `☐ Anchor done?`  
  - `☐ Day felt okay?`  
- Tick them as you close the day; no extra notes required.
