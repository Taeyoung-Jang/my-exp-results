# Summary

- **Goal**: Provide a *low‑friction* workflow for freelancers to create invoices, collect payments, and keep basic books without a second‑job workload.  
- **Recommended toolchain**:  
  - **[[entities/stripe]]** – creates invoices, generates payment links, handles [[concepts/automated-payment-reminders]] and supports recurring billing.  
  - A **business bank account** (e.g., [[entities/novo]], [[entities/mercury]]) – separates personal and business cash flow and feeds data to bookkeeping tools.  
  - **[[entities/wave]]** – free lightweight bookkeeping that auto‑imports transactions from the bank and [[entities/stripe]] and learns to auto‑categorize expenses and income.  

- **Key concepts**:  
  - [[concepts/low-friction-invoicing]] – using a single service that covers invoicing and payment collection.  
  - [[concepts/automated-payment-reminders]] – enabling Stripe’s reminder feature to reduce chase‑ups.  
  - Recurring invoices – setting up repeat invoices for ongoing clients.  
  - [[concepts/automatic-transaction-categorisation]] – letting Wave learn from a short initial categorisation pass.  
  - [[concepts/weekly-maintenance-routine]] – a 10‑minute check‑in to correct mis‑categories and verify sync.  

- **First‑week checklist (day‑by‑day)**:  
  1. **Day 1 – Stripe**: create account, add business details, link bank, enable auto‑reminders, allow card + bank transfers, set default terms, save a template, create any recurring schedules.  
  2. **Day 2 – Business bank**: apply/open account, set it as Stripe payout account, turn on any auto‑categorisation the bank offers.  
  3. **Day 3 – Wave**: create account, link bank and Stripe, review default income/expense categories.  
  4. **Day 4 – Train Wave**: categorize a small batch of recent transactions (10‑15 min) to teach the system.  
  5. **Day 5 – Test workflow**: send a test invoice, verify payment lands in Stripe and appears correctly in Wave.  
  6. **Day 6 – Polish**: fine‑tune saved line items, client list, and any mis‑categorized entries in Wave.  
  7. **Day 7 – Hands‑off**: system ready; optionally add more line items or adjust terms.  

- **Ongoing maintenance** (≈10 min weekly): open Wave, review last week’s transactions, correct any mis‑categories, confirm Stripe payouts synced, and adjust invoice templates or recurring schedules as needed.  

*Result*: A streamlined, almost‑hands‑off invoicing and bookkeeping setup that lets freelancers focus on creative work rather than accounting.
