# Mercury

**Type**: organization

Mercury is an online banking platform aimed at startups, freelancers, and small businesses. It provides a dedicated business‑checking account that integrates with modern fintech tools, making it easy to keep personal and business finances separate.

## Mentions in session‑10_2020‑05‑12

* Recommended as a **business bank account** option to pair with Stripe Invoicing and Wave for a low‑friction invoicing and bookkeeping workflow.
* Suggested to enable any built‑in automatic transaction categorisation features, which helps the [[concepts/automatic-transaction-categorisation]] workflow in Wave.
