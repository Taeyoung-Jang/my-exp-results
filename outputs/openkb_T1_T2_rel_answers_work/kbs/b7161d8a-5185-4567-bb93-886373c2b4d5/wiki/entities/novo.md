## Novo

Novo is an online‑only business banking service aimed at freelancers, creators, and small‑to‑medium enterprises. It provides a streamlined account opening process, no‑monthly‑fees structure, and tools that integrate with popular invoicing and bookkeeping platforms.

### Role in the low‑friction invoicing workflow
In the workflow described in [[summaries/session-10_2020-05-12]], Novo is recommended as the **business bank account** that receives payouts from [[entities/stripe]] and feeds transaction data into [[entities/wave]] for [[concepts/automatic-transaction-categorisation]]. By keeping personal and business finances separate, Novo helps automate bookkeeping and supports the overall [[concepts/low-friction-invoicing]] strategy.

### Key features referenced
- **Fast account setup** – online application and quick approval, enabling freelancers to start receiving Stripe payouts within days.
- **Automatic transaction categorisation** – many modern banks, including Novo, tag incoming and outgoing funds, which complements Wave’s categorisation engine.
- **Seamless integration** – direct connections with Stripe for payouts and with Wave for expense syncing, reducing manual data entry.
