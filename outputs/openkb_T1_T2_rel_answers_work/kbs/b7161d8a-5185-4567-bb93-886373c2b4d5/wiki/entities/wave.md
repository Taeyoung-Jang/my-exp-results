# Wave

Wave is a free, cloud‑based bookkeeping tool aimed at freelancers and small businesses. It automatically imports transactions from linked bank accounts and payment processors, learns from early categorisation, and provides basic income‑expense reports for tax filing.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-10_2020-05-12]]
- Recommended as the bookkeeping component of a **low‑friction invoicing** workflow alongside [[entities/stripe]] and a separate business bank such as [[entities/novo]] or [[entities/mercury]].
- Pulls in transactions from both the business bank and Stripe, enabling **automatic transaction categorisation** without manual entry.
- After an initial short categorisation pass, Wave auto‑categorises future expenses, supporting a **weekly maintenance routine** that takes about ten minutes.

## Usage tips
1. Link your business bank account and Stripe account to Wave.
2. Review and adjust the default income and expense categories once to train the system.
3. Perform a brief weekly check‑in to correct any mis‑categorised items and confirm that payouts from Stripe have synced.

Wave thus provides an almost hands‑off bookkeeping solution that lets freelancers focus on their creative work rather than accounting.
