## Stripe

Stripe is a cloud‑based payment processing and financial services platform. In the context of freelance invoicing, **Stripe Invoicing** provides a single tool to create professional invoices, generate payment links, and collect payments via credit cards or bank transfers.

### Key Facts from the Session 10 Summary
- **All‑in‑one invoicing**: Stripe allows freelancers to build invoices, send them with a clickable payment link, and receive funds directly into a business bank account.
- **Automation**: Users can enable [[concepts/automated-payment-reminders]], set default invoice terms, and configure recurring invoices (mentioned as recurring invoices in the checklist) so that repeat billing runs without manual effort.
- **Integration**: Stripe payouts are linked to a separate business bank such as [[entities/novo]] or [[entities/mercury]], keeping personal and business cash flows distinct.
- **Bookkeeping sync**: When paired with [[entities/wave]], Stripe transaction data auto‑imports, enabling [[concepts/automatic-transaction-categorisation]] for streamlined tax reporting.
- **Setup workflow**: The first‑week checklist in [[summaries/session-10_2020-05-12]] allocates Day 1 to creating the Stripe account, linking the bank, and turning on the automation settings.

### Related Concepts
- [[concepts/low-friction-invoicing]] – the overarching goal of using Stripe as a friction‑free invoicing solution.
- [[concepts/weekly-maintenance-routine]] – the brief weekly review that includes confirming Stripe payouts have synced to Wave.

Stripe’s combination of invoicing, payment collection, and easy integration with bookkeeping tools makes it a cornerstone of a low‑maintenance financial workflow for freelancers.
