# Automated Payment Reminders

**Definition**: Automated Payment Reminders are system‑generated notifications—usually via email or SMS—that prompt clients to pay outstanding invoices without the sender having to manually chase them.

---

## Why they matter for freelancers

Freelancers often spend a disproportionate amount of time reminding clients about unpaid invoices.  In the **session‑10_2020‑05‑12** workflow, the assistant recommends turning on Stripe’s built‑in reminder feature as a core part of a low‑friction invoicing setup.  Enabling these reminders:

- Cuts down on administrative overhead.
- Improves cash‑flow timing.
- Allows the freelancer to focus on creative work rather than collections.

---

## Key settings to enable (as per the source document)

1. **Navigate to Stripe → Invoicing → Settings**
2. **Toggle “Automatic payment reminders”** on.
3. Choose the schedule (e.g., reminder 3 days before due, on due date, and 5 days after due).
4. Customize the reminder tone and branding to match the invoice template.
5. Save the changes.

These steps are listed in the day‑by‑day checklist for **Day 1 – Stripe** in the summary of [[summaries/session-10_2020-05-12]].
