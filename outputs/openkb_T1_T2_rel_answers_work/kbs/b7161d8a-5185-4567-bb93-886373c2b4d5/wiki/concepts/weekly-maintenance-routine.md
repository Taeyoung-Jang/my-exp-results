# Weekly Maintenance Routine

**Definition**: A short, recurring (≈10 minutes) review process that ensures a freelancer’s invoicing, payment, and bookkeeping systems remain accurate and automated.

## Why it matters
The routine is essential for the *low‑friction invoicing* workflow described in [[summaries/session-10_2020-05-12]]. By regularly confirming that transactions are correctly categorized and that payment reminders are firing, freelancers avoid the buildup of manual accounting tasks.

## Core steps (≈10 min each week)
1. **Open Wave**
   - Review the past week’s transactions.
   - Correct any mis‑categorized entries, helping Wave improve its [[concepts/automatic-transaction-categorisation]] learning.
2. **Check Stripe payouts**
   - Verify that all invoices sent in the week have been paid and that payouts have synced to the business bank account.
   - Ensure [[concepts/automated-payment-reminders]] are still active and that no invoices are stuck pending.
3. **Quick template audit**
   - Look at the saved invoice template in [[entities/stripe]] for any needed updates (e.g., new line items or changed terms).
4. **Bank account health**
   - Confirm the business bank (e.g., [[entities/novo]] or [[entities/mercury]]) shows the expected deposits and that auto‑categorisation (if offered) is functioning.

## Benefits
- **Minimal time investment** – only a few minutes each week.
- **Keeps automation reliable** – prevents errors from snowballing.
- **Supports tax readiness** – ensures income and expense records stay clean for filing.
