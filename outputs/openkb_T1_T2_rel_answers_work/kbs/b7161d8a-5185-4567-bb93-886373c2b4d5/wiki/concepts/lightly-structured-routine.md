# Lightly Structured Routine

A **lightly structured routine** is a minimal‑weight weekly framework that combines a handful of reliable anchors with generous open blocks, giving days enough shape to feel purposeful without feeling over‑scheduled. It is especially useful for people whose schedules have opened up—retirees, freelancers, or anyone seeking a gentle rhythm instead of a dense calendar.

## Core Components

1. **Daily Ritual Anchor** – A consistent, low‑effort start‑of‑day practice (e.g., wake‑up window, simple breakfast, short reading or stretch).  This creates a daily foothold without imposing obligations.  [[concepts/daily-ritual-anchor]]
2. **Weekly Touchpoints** – One or two recurring activities that add meaning, such as a mid‑week social meet‑up or a weekend creative block.  These serve as “tent poles” for the week.
3. **Open‑Block Intention** – Unfilled periods where the individual decides in the moment what feels good, guided by a brief question or menu of options.  [[concepts/open-block-intention]]
4. **Light Reset Hour** (optional) – A brief weekly tidying‑and‑planning slot that prevents the next week from feeling like a blank void.

## How It Works

- **Anchors** provide predictability.  Because they are few (typically 3‑5), they avoid crowding the schedule.
- **Open blocks** are deliberately left empty; a simple prompting question (e.g., “What would feel good for the next hour?”) turns idle time into purposeful choice without turning it into a task list.
- **Tracking** is kept to two checkboxes per day: *Anchor done?* and *Day felt okay?* – enough data to notice patterns without creating a new job.

## Example Layout (copy‑paste for a paper planner)

| Day | Anchors |
|-----|---------|
| **Mon** | Morning ritual only; open day |
| **Tue** | Morning ritual; open day |
| **Wed** | Morning ritual; **mid‑week social touchpoint** |
| **Thu** | Morning ritual; open day |
| **Fri** | Morning ritual; **light reset hour** in afternoon |
| **Sat** | Morning ritual; **creative block** (morning/early afternoon) |
| **Sun** | Morning ritual; open & easy |

## Benefits
- Reduces decision fatigue by limiting required commitments.
- Keeps days feeling purposeful while preserving spontaneity.
- Simple tracking prevents the system from becoming a burden.
