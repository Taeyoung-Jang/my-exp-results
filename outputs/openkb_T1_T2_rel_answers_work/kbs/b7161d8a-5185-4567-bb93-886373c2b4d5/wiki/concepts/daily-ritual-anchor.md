# Daily Ritual Anchor

**Definition**: A short, repeatable morning practice that serves as a foundational anchor for the day, fostering routine, wellbeing, and a sense of purpose.

## Core Elements
- **Timing**: Performed at roughly the same time each morning, often shortly after waking.
- **Duration**: Typically 15‑20 minutes, enough to be deliberate without feeling burdensome.
- **Activities**: May include a simple breakfast, light reading, gentle stretching, mindfulness, or a brief check‑in with one’s feelings.

## Why It Matters
- Provides a **predictable start** that reduces decision fatigue.
- Acts as a **non‑negotiable anchor** in a lightly‑structured routine, helping to frame the day's rhythm.
- Encourages **mind‑body connection**, supporting mental clarity and physical readiness.

## Application in Session‑32 (2025‑05‑12)
In the weekly template suggested for retirees, the Daily Ritual Anchor appears **every day** as the first anchor:
- Wake around a consistent window.
- Make a slow breakfast.
- Spend 15‑20 minutes reading, stretching, or simply sipping coffee.

This anchor is listed in the planner layout for **Mon‑Sun**, ensuring that each day starts with a calming, purposeful habit before open blocks or other weekly anchors (social touchpoint, creative block, reset hour) come into play.
