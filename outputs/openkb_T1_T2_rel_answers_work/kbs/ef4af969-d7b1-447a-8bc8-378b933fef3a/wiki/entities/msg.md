# Monosodium Glutamate (MSG)

**Type:** other

## Overview
Monosodium Glutamate (MSG) is a sodium salt of the amino acid glutamic acid. It is widely used as a flavor enhancer to intensify the savory (umami) taste in processed foods.

## Alternate Names & Hidden Labeling
In ingredient lists, MSG may appear under names such as:
- "yeast extract"
- "hydrolyzed protein"
- "natural flavors"
These alternate terms allow MSG to be present without the explicit label “MSG,” contributing to the issue of [[concepts/hidden_ingredients]].

## Label‑Scanning Relevance
When scanning product labels, look for the above synonyms, especially in the middle or end of the ingredient list where flavor enhancers are often placed. Recognizing these terms helps apply the [[concepts/label_scanning_tips]] strategy of quickly spotting hidden additives.
