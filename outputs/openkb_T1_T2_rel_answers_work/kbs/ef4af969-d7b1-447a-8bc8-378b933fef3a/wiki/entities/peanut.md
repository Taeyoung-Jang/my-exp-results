# Peanut (product)

**Type:** Food ingredient (legume)

Peanut is a widely used protein‑rich legume that frequently appears on packaged‑food ingredient lists under a variety of alternate names. The session‑10_2023-10-31 discussion highlights how peanuts can be concealed in labels and why they pose a high risk for people with severe peanut allergy.

---

## Common alternate names on labels
- **Peanut oil** (including cold‑pressed or expeller‑pressed forms)
- **Groundnuts** or **groundnut oil**
- **Arachis oil** – see [[entities/arachis_oil]]
- **Peanut flour**, **peanut powder**, **peanut extract**
- Latin terms such as **arachis**
- Occasionally hidden within **natural flavors** or **mixed nut flour** (ambiguous cases)

These alternate names are cited in [[concepts/hidden_ingredients]] and are key to the [[concepts/peanut_allergy_checklist]] for allergy management.

---

## Allergy‑related labeling cues
- Explicit statements: “contains peanuts”, “peanut butter”, “peanut paste”
- Precautionary warnings (high‑risk): “may contain peanuts”, “processed on shared equipment with peanuts”, “made in a facility that also processes peanuts”
- Less clear warnings (require verification): “natural flavors”, “vegetable protein”, “protein blend”, “may contain tree nuts only”

The guidance on rapid label scanning is summarized in [[concepts/label_scanning_tips]].

---

## High‑risk product categories
Peanuts often show up unexpectedly in:
- Granola and protein bars
- Baked goods and candies
- Trail mixes and snack mixes
- Asian sauces and flavored cereals
- Veggie burgers and other plant‑based meat alternatives
- Protein‑enhanced crackers and spice blends
