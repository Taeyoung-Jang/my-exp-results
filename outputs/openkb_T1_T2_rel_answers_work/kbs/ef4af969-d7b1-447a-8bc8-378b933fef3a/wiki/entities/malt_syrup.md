# Malt Syrup

**Type:** Product

**Description:** Malt syrup is a thick, sweet liquid produced by processing malted barley or other grains. It is used as a sweetener and flavoring in a variety of processed foods.

**Key facts from the document**
- Cited in *session-10_2023-10-31* as an example of a hidden ingredient name for added sugars.
- Appears on ingredient lists under the name “malt syrup,” which can be overlooked if only searching for the word “sugar.”
- The advice to look for terms ending in “‑ose” (e.g., malt syrup) is part of [[concepts/label_scanning_tips]].
- Recognizing malt syrup helps apply the “first three ingredients” rule for rapid label scanning, a strategy discussed in [[concepts/hidden_ingredients]].
