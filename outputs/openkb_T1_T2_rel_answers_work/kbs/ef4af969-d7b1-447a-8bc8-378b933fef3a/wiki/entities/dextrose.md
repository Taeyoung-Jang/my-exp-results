# Dextrose

Dextrose is a monosaccharide (glucose) used as a sweetener in many packaged foods. In the context of the conversation **session‑10_2023‑10‑31**, it is highlighted as one of the many “hidden” sugar names that can appear on ingredient labels.

## Key Points from the Document
- **Hidden ingredient name**: Dextrose can be listed instead of the word “sugar,” making it a trickier ingredient to spot.  It falls under the broader discussion of [[concepts/hidden_ingredients]].
- **Label‑scanning tip**: When scanning ingredient lists quickly, look for any term ending in “‑ose,” such as dextrose, to flag added sugars.  This is part of the recommended strategy in [[concepts/label_scanning_tips]].
- **Related sweeteners**: Other sugars mentioned alongside dextrose include malt syrup and other “‑ose” compounds.
