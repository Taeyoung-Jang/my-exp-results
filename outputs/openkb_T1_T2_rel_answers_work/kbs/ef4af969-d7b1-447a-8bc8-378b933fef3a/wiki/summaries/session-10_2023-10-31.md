# Summary of session-10_2023-10-31

**Date:** 2023‑10‑31 19:00

## Key Concepts
- **Hidden ingredient names** – sugars, fats, and flavor enhancers often appear under alternate terms (e.g., [[entities/dextrose]], [[entities/malt_syrup]], [[entities/msg]] as yeast extract) [[concepts/hidden_ingredients]].
- **Label‑scanning strategy** – focus on the first three ingredients, then skim for “‑ose” suffixes or “syrup” keywords; check the end of the list for preservatives and colorings [[concepts/label_scanning_tips]].
- **Peanut‑specific terminology** – includes peanut oil, groundnuts, [[entities/arachis_oil]], peanut flour, peanut powder, peanut extract, and other Latin variants.
- **Cross‑contact warnings** – “may contain peanuts”, “processed on shared equipment with peanuts”, and “made in a facility that also processes peanuts” are high‑risk statements.
- **High‑risk product categories** – granola/protein bars, baked goods, candies, trail mixes, Asian sauces, flavored cereals, veggie burgers, and other “energy” or “protein” snacks often hide peanuts.
- **Surprise product types** – protein‑enhanced crackers, spice blends, seasoning rubs, plant‑based meats, and certain sauces may contain peanut flour or oil even when not obvious.
- **Red‑flag vs. ambiguous – checklist** – a concise list of exact ingredient terms and precautionary statements that warrant automatic put‑back, plus a separate list of vague phrases that merit further verification [[concepts/peanut_allergy_checklist]].

## Practical Checklist
### Automatic put‑back (red flag)
- Peanut, peanuts, peanut butter, peanut paste
- Peanut flour, peanut powder, peanut extract
- [[entities/arachis_oil]], cold‑pressed/expeller‑pressed peanut oil, groundnut oil
- Any ingredient list explicitly naming peanuts
- Warning statements: “may contain peanuts”, “contains peanuts”, “processed on shared equipment with peanuts”, “made in a facility that also processes peanuts”

### Ambiguous – verify further
- Natural flavors, mixed nut flour, vegetable protein, hydrolyzed plant protein, protein blend, generic nut flavoring
- Facility statements that mention nuts without specifying which nut (e.g., “may contain tree nuts only”)
- Any vague allergen statement lacking a clear peanut mention

## Recommendations
1. **First‑three‑ingredients rule** – these dominate the product composition.  
2. **Search for “‑ose” and “syrup”** when avoiding added sugars.  
3. **Scan end of ingredient list** for preservatives and colorings.  
4. **When ambiguous language appears**, cross‑check the allergen box or contact the manufacturer.  
5. **Maintain a personal checklist** (red‑flag vs. ambiguous) to streamline decisions at the store.
