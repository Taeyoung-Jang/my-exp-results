# Confidence Building Around Food

**Definition**: A set of mental and practical strategies that help individuals shift from anxiety‑driven eating to a calm, progressive relationship with food after medical restrictions or allergies.

---

## Core Principles (derived from [[summaries/session-37_2024-09-01]])

1. **Treat each meal as a step, not a test**
   - View food experiences as incremental data points rather than pass/fail exams.
   - Celebrate neutral or positive outcomes to reinforce a sense of safety.

2. **Gradual exposure via a food reintroduction plan**
   - Introduce *one* new ingredient per week, starting with a tiny portion (e.g., a teaspoon) and only increase after a symptom‑free observation period.
   - Follow a structured **portion progression**: very small bite → small forkful → normal serving.
   - This aligns with [[concepts/food_reintroduction_plan]].

3. **Systematic logging**
   - Use a simple one‑page log to capture what was tried, portion size, timing, physical sensations, anxiety levels, and confidence rating.
   - Separate sections for home trials and restaurant trials help compare contexts.

4. **Distinguish mild physical reactions from anxiety**
   - Pause and assess any symptoms; if they are mild, give the body more time before retrying.
   - If anxiety symptoms dominate, employ calming techniques (deep breathing, grounding) and note the experience.
   - Seek clinician input for anything beyond mild.

5. **Effective communication with staff**
   - Deploy a low‑key script when calling or speaking with restaurant personnel, focusing on cross‑contact avoidance and menu suitability.
   - Positive staff responses act as a cue for safety and boost confidence.
   - See [[concepts/restaurant_communication]] for the recommended script.

---

## Practical Tools

- **One‑page Reintroduction Log**: Captures food/ingredient, reason for borderline status, portion, timing, symptom notes, confidence check‑in, and next‑step decision.
- **Restaurant Script**: "Hi, I have a food restriction and just want to check how your kitchen handles it…"

---

## Why It Works

- **Predictability** reduces uncertainty, a major driver of food‑related anxiety.
- **Evidence accumulation** (through logs) builds an objective record of tolerance, reinforcing trust in one's body.
- **Social reassurance** via clear communication with restaurant staff creates a supportive environment, further lowering stress.
