# Outreach Script

**Concept type:** `Concept`

## Definition
An *outreach script* is a short, pre‑written message used to re‑establish contact after a period of unavailability (e.g., jury duty, travel, technical outage). It frames the blackout briefly, conveys readiness to act, and asks the recipient to highlight any time‑sensitive items.

## Key Elements
1. **Acknowledgment of the blackout** – a concise note that you were offline.
2. **Request for priority information** – an explicit invitation for the other party to flag urgent items.
3. **Tone** – polite, honest, and non‑defensive; avoids over‑explaining while maintaining professionalism.

## Example Scripts (from [[summaries/session-37_2025-04-26]])
| Audience | Script |
|---|---|
| **Boss / Client** | "Thanks for your patience — I was in court all week for jury duty with my phone off, and I’m catching up now. If anything still needs my attention or has a deadline attached, let me know and I’ll prioritize it." |
| **Coworkers** | "Just coming back up for air after jury duty and getting through my backlog. If there’s anything time‑sensitive I should look at first, point me toward it." |
| **Friends / Family** | "I’m finally catching up after being stuck in jury duty all week with my phone off. If I missed anything important, just tell me what needs attention and I’ll jump on it." |

## Usage Context
Outreach scripts are typically sent **after** completing a rapid [[concepts/triage_checklist]] to identify any pending tasks. They complement the **three‑question filter** ([[concepts/three_question_filter]]) by ensuring that any newly surfaced urgent items are surfaced by the sender’s contacts rather than remaining hidden.

## Benefits
- Saves time by avoiding ad‑hoc phrasing.
- Sets clear expectations for response urgency.
- Helps maintain professional credibility after a communication gap.
