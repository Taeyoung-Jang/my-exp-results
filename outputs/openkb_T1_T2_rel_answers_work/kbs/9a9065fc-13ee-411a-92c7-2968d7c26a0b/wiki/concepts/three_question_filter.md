# Three‑Question Filter

**Definition**: The Three‑Question Filter is a rapid prioritization tool that asks, for each pending item, three concrete questions to decide whether it should be done now, later, or dropped.

## The Three Questions
1. **Real‑World Consequence** – *Will something bad happen if this isn’t handled in the next day or two?*
2. **Stress Reduction** – *Will completing this noticeably lower my stress right now?*
3. **Wait‑ability** – *Can this safely wait without any negative outcome?*

## How It Works
- **Answer “Yes” to Question 1** → place the item in the **Do‑Soon** bucket (today or tomorrow).
- **Answer “No” to Q1 but “Yes” to Q2** → place the item in the **Do‑Later** bucket (later this week) to gain a stress‑relief win.
- **Answer “No” to all three** → the item can be **dropped** or safely ignored.

## Application Context
The filter is embedded in the [[concepts/triage_checklist]] from *session‑37_2025‑04‑26*, where a 10–15 minute “reset” routine sorts a backlog after a disruptive period (e.g., jury duty). It helps turn a chaotic list of missed calls, emails, and physical receipts into a clear, actionable plan.

## Benefits
- **Speed** – Requires only a few seconds per item, fitting into a time‑boxed triage session.
- **Objectivity** – Moves decisions from emotional urgency to concrete outcomes.
- **Scalability** – Works for both digital tasks and physical items such as receipt piles.
