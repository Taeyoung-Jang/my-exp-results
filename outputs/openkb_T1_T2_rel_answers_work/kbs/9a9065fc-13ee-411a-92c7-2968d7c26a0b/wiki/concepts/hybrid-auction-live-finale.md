# Hybrid Auction with Live Finale

**Definition**: A hybrid auction blends a pre‑scheduled online bidding phase with a brief, high‑energy live finale—often streamed or held in‑person—where the final bids for the top items are placed in real time.

---

## Why Use a Hybrid Live Finale?
- **Revenue boost**: A short live finale creates urgency and excitement, typically raising more money than a purely timed online close.
- **Low logistical overhead**: By limiting the live segment to 10‑15 items and keeping the same bidding platform, the process stays simple for volunteers.
- **Remote‑friendly**: Bidders stay on the same platform, so remote participants can follow the livestream and place bids without switching systems.

---

## Core Structure (as described in [[summaries/session-10_2025-04-12]])
1. **Online phase** – The auction runs on a chosen platform (e.g., GiveButter, Auctria) for several days. Bidders register once, with a card on file.
2. **Bid freeze** – At a predetermined time, bids on the top 10‑15 items are locked.
3. **Live finale** – A 1‑hour livestream (or in‑person event) kicks off. The host energizes the crowd while all actual bids continue through the same online interface, ensuring real‑time updates for every participant.
4. **Closing** – When the live timer ends, the platform auto‑charges winners or sends payment prompts.

---

## Key Operational Tips
- **Single‑platform workflow**: Keep registration, bidding, and payment on the same system to avoid confusion for remote bidders.
- **Clear schedule communication**: Publish the preview window, freeze time, and live finale start/end times well in advance.
- **Minimal staffing**: One person monitors the bidding dashboard; another handles host communication and any last‑minute issues.
- **Technology check**: Ensure a stable internet connection for the livestream; the platform’s embedded stream should be tested ahead of time.

---

## Remote‑Bidder Experience
- Mobile‑first bidding UI with instant updates.
- Visible leader indicator and countdown timer.
- Integrated livestream pane side‑by‑side with the bidding panel, so bidders don’t need to switch tabs.
- Automated reminder emails before the finale starts.

---

## Platform Considerations
- **GiveButter** – Very easy for volunteers; built‑in livestream but depends on host bandwidth.
- **Auctria** – Cost‑effective; livestream less polished, may need a separate video source.
- **OneCause** – Polished live‑auction UI; higher price point.
- **Greater Giving** – More traditional UI; heavier on reporting, less intuitive for mobile bidders.
