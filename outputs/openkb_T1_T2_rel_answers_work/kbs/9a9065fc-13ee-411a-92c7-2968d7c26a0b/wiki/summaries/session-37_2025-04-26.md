# Summary of session-37_2025-04-26

**Date:** 2025‑04‑26

## Core Idea
A concise, actionable triage system for quickly regaining control after a disruptive period (e.g., jury duty) and a set of polite outreach scripts for different audiences.

## Key Concepts
- **Triage Checklist** – Time‑boxed steps (voicemail, texts, email, physical receipts) that fit in a 10–15 minute “reset” session.  
- **Three‑Question Filter** – Real‑world consequence, stress‑reduction, and wait‑ability used to sort tasks into *Do Soon*, *Do Later*, or *Drop*.  
- **Receipt Pile** – A dedicated step to surface hidden obligations such as reimbursements or calendar clues.  
- **Outreach Scripts** – Tailored short messages for bosses/clients, coworkers, and friends/family that acknowledge the recent blackout while soliciting urgent items.

## Checklist (copy‑ready)
```
• 3 min: Scan voicemail for deadlines or problems.
• 3 min: Scan texts, missed calls, email for possible fires.
• 2 min: Scan physical items (receipts, tickets) for reimbursements or clues.
• 2 min: Jot a rough list of everything discovered.
• 3‑5 min: Apply the three‑question filter:
  – Yes → today/tomorrow (consequence‑based)
  – No, but stress‑relief → later this week
  – No & irrelevant → drop
• 1 min: Choose one consequence task + one stress‑relief task for tomorrow; distribute remaining items lightly across the week.
```

## Outreach Scripts
* **Boss/Client:**  
> “Thanks for your patience — I was in court all week for jury duty with my phone off, and I’m catching up now. If anything still needs my attention or has a deadline attached, let me know and I’ll prioritize it.”  

* **Coworkers:**  
> “Just coming back up for air after jury duty and getting through my backlog. If there’s anything time‑sensitive I should look at first, point me toward it.”  

* **Friends/Family:**  
> “I’m finally catching up after being stuck in jury duty all week with my phone off. If I missed anything important, just tell me what needs attention and I’ll jump on it.”
