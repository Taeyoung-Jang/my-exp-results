# Summary of session-10_2025-04-12

**Date:** 2025-04-12

## Key Topics & Concepts

- **Community fundraising event ideas** – seasonal spring hooks such as garden parties, challenge‑style games, cook‑offs, outdoor movies, retro fairs, pet parades, and build‑a‑picnic concepts. [[concepts/fundraising-event-themes]]
- **Creative auction formats** – spring showcase, mystery‑reveal, live‑experience items, and hybrid online‑plus‑live finales. [[concepts/auction-theme-ideas]]
- **Live finale vs. timed close** – a short live finale (about one hour) generally raises more money with minimal extra logistics; structure: online preview → freeze bids → live countdown. [[concepts/hybrid-auction-live-finale]]
- **Registration & payment workflow** – single‑registration, card‑on‑file approach; keep bidding on the same platform throughout to avoid confusion for remote bidders.
- **Remote‑friendly platform features** – mobile‑first UI, real‑time bid updates, clear leader indicator, integrated livestream pane, automated reminders, auto‑charge or payment prompts.
- **Platform comparison** – GiveButter, Auctria, OneCause, Greater Giving evaluated for hybrid auctions with live finales. Summarized pros, cons, fees, and volunteer workload.

## Fundraising Event Theme Highlights

- **Garden‑party style**: live music, planting stations, local food.
- **Spring challenge**: team‑based mini‑games, scavenger hunts, donation‑tied progress.
- **Community cook‑off**: tasting, voting, local chefs.
- **Outdoor movie or sunset concert**: relaxed donation atmosphere.
- **Retro fair / pet parade / build‑a‑picnic**: photo‑worthy, shareable moments.

## Auction Theme & Format Guidance

1. **Spring showcase** – curated items from local artists/makers aligned with renewal.
2. **Mystery reveal** – partially hidden items teased before the event to drive early bids.
3. **Live experiences** – lessons, private tours, behind‑the‑scenes access, ideal for hybrid bidding.
4. **Hybrid structure tips**:
   - Limit to a small set of high‑appeal items.
   - Use one bidding platform end‑to‑end.
   - Define clear schedule: preview window → online phase → live finale.

## Live Finale Structure (Simple)

1. Run online auction for a few days.
2. At a set time, freeze bids on the top 10‑15 items.
3. Launch a one‑hour livestream (or in‑person) where the host energizes bidders.
4. Bids continue **inside the platform**; no chat‑based offers.
5. Platform auto‑charges or sends payment prompts immediately after close.

## Registration & Bid Tracking Simplification

- Pre‑event registration with card on file.
- Same platform for both online and live phases eliminates re‑registration.
- One person monitors the dashboard; another handles host communication.
- Real‑time bid feed visible to all participants reduces disputes.

## Platform Recommendations & Comparison

| Platform | Ease for Volunteers | Integrated Livestream | Card‑on‑File | Fee Model | Notable Gotchas |
|----------|--------------------|----------------------|--------------|----------|-----------------|
| **[[entities/givebutter]]** | ★★★★★ (very straightforward) | Simple embed, relies on host internet | Yes | Optional donor‑covered tips; verify processing fees | Stream quality dependent on connection |
| **[[entities/auctria]]** | ★★★★ (slightly more setup) | Basic live broadcast (less polished) | Yes | Low subscription; processing fees apply | UI can feel “software‑ish,” may need onboarding help |
| **[[entities/onecause]]** | ★★★ (best UI, highest cost) | Polished live‑auction UI | Yes | Higher tier pricing | Ensure selected tier includes livestream & payment gateway |
| **[[entities/greater-giving]]** | ★★ (older UI) | Works but less intuitive | Yes | Mid‑range fees | Mobile bidding less smooth; heavier reporting features |

**Bottom line:** For a small volunteer team seeking minimal setup, **[[entities/givebutter]]** is typically the simplest choice; **[[entities/auctria]]** is a cost‑effective alternative if you can handle a modest UI learning curve.
