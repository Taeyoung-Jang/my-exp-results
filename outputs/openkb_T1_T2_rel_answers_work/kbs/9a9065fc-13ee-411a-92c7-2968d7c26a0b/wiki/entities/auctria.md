# Auctria

**Type:** Organization

Auctria provides an online auction platform aimed at nonprofit fundraising. It supports hybrid auctions that combine online bidding with a live‑finale component, allowing all bids to stay within the same system.

## Key Features (as mentioned in [[summaries/session-10_2025-04-12]])
- **Hybrid auction support** – works for both online bidding and a real‑time live finale.
- **Card‑on‑file registration** – bidders register once and can bid throughout the event.
- **Live broadcast** – includes a built‑in but utilitarian livestream for the finale.
- **Low‑cost pricing** – budget‑friendly for small volunteer teams.
- **Mobile‑friendly UI** – functional, though the interface can feel more "software‑ish" and may need a short onboarding period for less‑tech‑savvy bidders.

## Pros & Cons (from the session summary)
| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Affordable, flexible pricing | UI is less polished than some competitors |
| Capable for hybrid events | Onboarding may require extra guidance |
| Simple setup for small teams | Live stream quality depends on host internet |
