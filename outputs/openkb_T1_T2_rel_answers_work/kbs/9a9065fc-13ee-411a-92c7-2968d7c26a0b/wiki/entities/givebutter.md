# GiveButter

**Type:** Organization

**Overview:** GiveButter is a fundraising and event platform that provides integrated online auction capabilities, live‑stream finale support, and a simple card‑on‑file registration flow. It is highlighted in [[summaries/session-10_2025-04-12]] as the easiest option for small volunteer teams running hybrid auctions.

**Key Features Mentioned**
- Single‑registration, card‑on‑file bidding that works for both online and live phases.
- Built‑in livestream embedding for a real‑time “live finale” without needing separate streaming services.
- Mobile‑first bidding interface with real‑time bid updates and clear leader indicators.
- Optional donor‑covered tip model; processing fees apply to payments.

**Why It Stands Out**
- Rated the most straightforward to set up among the platforms compared (GiveButter, Auctria, OneCause, Greater Giving).
- Low volunteer workload: minimal onboarding and a clean UI.
- Potential “gotcha”: livestream quality depends on the host’s internet connection.
