# OneCause

**Type:** Organization

OneCause provides a high‑end, feature‑rich auction platform that supports hybrid events with a real‑time live finale. It stands out for its polished user interface, integrated livestream capabilities, and rock‑solid real‑time bidding.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-10_2025-04-12]]
- **Hybrid‑auction support** – Designed for events that combine online bidding with a live‑streamed finale, keeping all bids on the same platform. [[concepts/hybrid-auction-live-finale]]
- **User experience** – Offers one of the most refined bidder experiences among comparable platforms, with clear bid buttons, leader indicators, and side‑by‑side livestream/bidding panes.
- **Pricing** – Typically the most expensive option in the market; costs depend on the service tier selected. Volunteers should weigh the premium UI against budget constraints.
- **Feature inclusion** – Some advanced features (e.g., embedded livestream, auto‑charge after the finale) may be tier‑specific, so confirm what is bundled in the chosen plan.
- **Comparison** – In the same document, OneCause is contrasted with [[entities/givebutter]], [[entities/auctria]], and [[entities/greater-giving]]; it scores highest on polish and lowest on ease of setup for small volunteer teams.

## Relevant Concepts
- **Auction theme ideas** – OneCause can host themed auctions such as a "spring showcase" or "live‑experience" format. [[concepts/auction-theme-ideas]]
- **Fundraising event themes** – While primarily an auction platform, OneCause can be integrated into broader community fundraising events. [[concepts/fundraising-event-themes]]

## Considerations for Small Volunteer Teams
- **Setup complexity** – Higher than GiveButter; may require a dedicated volunteer to manage platform configuration and livestream logistics.
- **Fees** – Expect higher subscription or usage fees; also factor in standard payment‑processing percentages.
- **Support** – Premium platforms like OneCause often provide dedicated support, which can mitigate some of the added complexity.
