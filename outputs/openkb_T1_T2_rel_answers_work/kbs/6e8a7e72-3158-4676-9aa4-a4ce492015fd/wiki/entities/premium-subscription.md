# Premium Subscription

**Type:** Product

A premium subscription provides access to additional, often advanced features of a service or software. Users typically consider whether the extra capabilities justify the recurring cost.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-10_2024-07-15]]
- **Value assessment**: Users are encouraged to examine how frequently they use premium features and whether those features save time, improve quality, or simply feel nice. This aligns with [[concepts/premium-value-assessment]].
- **Trial‑off experiments**: A short “off‑premium” period (about one week for regular users, up to a month for occasional users) helps reveal true dependence. See [[concepts/trial-off-experiments]].
- **Productivity impact metrics**: Evaluation focuses on concrete outcomes—time taken, result quality, and any measurable slowdown—rather than just the annoyance of habit disruption. Refer to [[concepts/productivity-impact-metrics]].
- **Tracking template**: Users can log daily tasks, note the absence of premium features, and rate the impact (no change, slightly worse, significantly worse). This structured logging supports the assessment process.
