## Subscription Evaluation

**Definition**

Subscription Evaluation is a systematic approach to determine whether a recurring payment service provides sufficient ongoing value relative to its cost. It combines usage frequency, update cadence, alternative options, and a personal loss‑aversion test.

## Core Criteria

1. **Frequency of use** – How often you actually engage with the service.
2. **Ongoing value** – Does the product improve or deliver new features over time?
3. **Alternative availability** – Is there a free or one‑time purchase that meets the same need?
4. **Miss‑it test** – Would you notice or be inconvenienced if the subscription stopped?

These criteria are directly drawn from the discussion in [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]].

## Checklist Before Subscribing
- Will I use it enough to offset the monthly fee?
- Does the subscription add continuous value beyond unlocking static features?
- Is a free or one‑time alternative sufficient?
- Would I miss it if it disappeared tomorrow?

## Tracking Subscriptions

Effective tracking involves consolidating all recurring charges onto a single payment method, placing renewal dates in a calendar, and performing a brief monthly review. This practice aligns with [[concepts/subscription-tracking]].

## Trial Period Guidance

Adopt a short test window of **2–4 weeks**. If you find yourself forgetting the service or having to force its use, cancel it. Successful trials should become effortless parts of your routine. This mirrors the ideas in [[concepts/trial-off-experiments]].
