# Subscription Tracking

**Definition:** A systematic approach to monitor, evaluate, and control recurring subscription services so that they provide ongoing value without accumulating hidden expenses.

---
## Overview
Subscription tracking focuses on making the financial and functional impact of recurring services visible and manageable. It builds on the decision framework described in [[concepts/subscription-evaluation]] and helps enforce the "Would I miss it if it cancelled?" test from [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]].

---
## Key Practices
1. **Centralised payment method** – Use a single credit card or digital wallet for all subscriptions. This creates a single point of reference when reviewing statements.
2. **Calendar reminders** – Record renewal dates in a digital calendar with alerts a week before each charge. This prevents unexpected auto‑renewals.
3. **Monthly rapid review** – Pick one day each month, open the payment method’s transaction list, filter for recurring charges, and ask the "miss‑it" question for each entry.
4. **Categorisation** – Classify subscriptions using [[concepts/subscription-categories]] (e.g., "always‑worth" vs. "rare‑worth") to prioritise review effort.
5. **Trial‑off evaluation** – When trying a new service, limit the test period to 2–4 weeks (see [[concepts/trial-off-experiments]]) and apply the same tracking steps.

---
## Tools & Techniques
- **Spreadsheets or budgeting apps** – List service name, cost, renewal date, and a brief note on the perceived value.
- **Expense‑tracking software** – Many platforms tag recurring payments automatically; use those tags to generate a monthly subscription report.
- **Notification scripts** – For tech‑savvy users, simple scripts can pull upcoming charges from bank APIs and email a summary.

---
## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Directly supports [[concepts/subscription-evaluation]] by providing the data needed for ongoing value assessment.
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/subscription-categories]] to decide which services merit deeper scrutiny.
- Complements [[concepts/premium-value-assessment]] when evaluating higher‑tier or premium plans (e.g., [[entities/premium-subscription]]).
