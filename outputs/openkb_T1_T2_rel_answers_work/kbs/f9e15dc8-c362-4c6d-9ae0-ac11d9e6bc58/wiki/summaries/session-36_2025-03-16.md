**Topic:** Choosing and structuring movement‑based hobbies (dance, yoga, casual fitness) for midday breaks.

### Key Points
- **Initial decision‑making:** Ask about desired feeling (energizing, relaxing, playful) to steer toward dance, yoga, or fitness. [[concepts/decision-factors]]
- **Follow‑up considerations:** Identify satisfying aspects of the current dance class, recovery sensations, and specific goals (strength, calm, flexibility, variety). [[concepts/recovery-assessment]]
- **Suggested questionnaire (8–12 items):**
  1. Weekly commit‑ment capacity.  
  2. Available time per session (including travel/cool‑down).  
  3. Post‑dance soreness or fatigue duration.  
  4. Over‑/under‑used body areas.  
  5. Cravings on non‑dance days (rest, gentle movement, intensity).  
  6. Primary goals (strength, flexibility, stamina, stress relief).  
  7. Preference for a main activity vs. rotating variety.  
  8. Importance of social/ community aspect.  
  9. Mental refreshment vs. need for calm/structure.  
  10. Perceived balance vs. schedule pressure when adding other activities.  

- **Proposed weekly schedule (3 midday sessions):**
  - **Monday:** Gentle yoga to increase mobility and prepare the body.  
  - **Wednesday:** Core dance class – main focus, provides "happily tired" feeling.  
  - **Friday:** Casual strength or low‑impact fitness class for balanced conditioning.  
  - No back‑to‑back high‑intensity sessions; ensures recovery and variety.

### Takeaway
A concise set of reflective questions can guide whether to keep dance as the primary hobby while safely incorporating yoga or fitness, and a simple three‑day weekly plan can provide balance without overloading the schedule.
