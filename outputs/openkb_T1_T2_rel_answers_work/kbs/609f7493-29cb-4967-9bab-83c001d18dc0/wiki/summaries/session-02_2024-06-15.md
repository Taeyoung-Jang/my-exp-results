## Summary of session-02_2024-06-15

**Main focus:** How to evaluate new invitations and commitments, and how to communicate boundaries politely across different relationship contexts.

### Key concepts
- **Commitment evaluation framework** – consider energy, priorities, alignment with values, and realistic weekly impact. [[concepts/commitment-evaluation]]
- **Emotional check** – notice whether the idea feels exciting (green light) or tiring/guilty (red light). 
- **Boundary communication** – structure a "no" or "not right now" with appreciation, honest reason, and an open‑door statement. [[concepts/boundary-communication]]
- **Scaled involvement** – offer a smaller role only when you genuinely have capacity and desire to help. [[concepts/scaled-involvement]]

### Practical scripts (copy‑paste ready)
| Situation | "No" | "Not right now" | "Yes, but small" |
|-----------|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Close friend | *I love that you thought of me… I don’t have the energy right now.* | *I’m interested but my plate is full; check back in a few months.* | *I can help with a tiny, manageable task.* |
| Coworker | *Thanks, but I’m at capacity with my workload.* | *I’m tied up with other priorities; revisit later.* | *I can contribute limitedly, e.g., review a draft.* |
| Community organizer | *Thank you, I’m not able to take on new commitments now.* | *My schedule is tight; let me know about future rounds.* | *I can assist in a very limited way, like a single event.* |

### Warm‑tone variations
- **Close friend:** "I really appreciate you inviting me. I’d love to join, but my routine leaves me with very little spare time, so I can’t give it the attention it deserves. I’m cheering you on!"
- **Coworker:** "Thanks for thinking of me. My schedule is packed and my regular workflow doesn’t leave much extra room, so I can’t take this on without stretching too thin. I appreciate the offer."
- **Community organizer:** "Thank you for reaching out and for the work you’re doing. My day‑to‑day routine is tight, so I don’t have much extra time to commit right now. I’m grateful to be included and wish you success."

### Suggested actions for the reader
1. Run through the **commitment evaluation** checklist before replying to any invite.
2. Choose the appropriate script style (direct "no", "not right now", or "yes, but small") based on relationship and capacity.
3. Practice the **boundary communication** phrasing to keep relationships warm while maintaining personal limits.
