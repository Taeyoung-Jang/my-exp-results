## Summary of session-07_2024-06-15

**Date:** 2024-06-15 14:00  
**Topic:** Communicating personal hangout‑frequency and texting boundaries to a new romantic interest without seeming rude.

### Key Ideas & Phrasing Strategies
- **Casual vs. Thoughtful tones** – Provide a relaxed text line and a more considered in‑person response.  
- **Flirty‑but‑clear language** – Keep the tone warm, add playful hints, and avoid hard numeric rules (e.g., “twice a month”).  
- **Spontaneous‑planner accommodation** – Acknowledge the other person’s impulsive style while stating your own pacing needs.  
- **Handling a hurt reaction** – Offer reassuring replies that separate “interest” from “availability.”  
- **Low‑key, unscripted phrasing** – Embed the boundary into everyday conversation, making it feel natural.  
- **Text‑frequency boundary** – Set limits on daily messaging without dampening the vibe, using gentle, flirty reassurance.  
- **Specific next‑step suggestion** – Pair a boundary statement with a concrete plan (e.g., “Let’s grab a drink next week”).

These strategies relate to the broader ideas of [[concepts/communication_boundaries]], [[concepts/spontaneous_vs_planned_interaction]], and [[concepts/maintaining_vibe_despite_limits]].

### Sample Phrases
| Situation | Sample Phrase |
|---|---|
| **Casual text** | “Hey, just so you know my social battery is limited, so I usually hang out about twice a month. I’m up for meeting, just like a chill pace.” |
| **In‑person thoughtful** | “I really enjoy spending time with you, but I do best with a light schedule. Meeting every couple of weeks is my sweet spot.” |
| **Flirty, non‑numeric** | “I love our vibe and prefer a slow‑burn pace; it makes each meetup feel special.” |
| **Flirty with humor** | “If we hung out constantly I’d probably start liking you too much – I’m a ‘quality over quantity’ kind of person.” |
| **Spontaneous planner** | “I love your spontaneous energy; if I’m free I’m happy to say yes, just need a little more space between hangouts.” |
| **Reassuring after a hurt response** | “It’s not that I don’t want to see you – I just know my limits, and spacing out helps me be fully present.” |
| **Low‑key conversation insert** | “By the way, I’m kind of a slow‑pace person with hangouts. I like having some space between plans—it just works better for my brain.” |
| **Boundary for frequent texting** | “I’m not the best all‑day texter, but I love hearing from you and will get back when I can.” |
| **Specific next plan** | “I love spending time with you, I just move a little slower socially. How about we grab a drink next week?” |
