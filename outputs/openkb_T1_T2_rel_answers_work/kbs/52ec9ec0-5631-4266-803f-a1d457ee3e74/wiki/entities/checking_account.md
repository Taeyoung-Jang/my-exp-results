# Checking Account

A **checking account** is a liquid banking product designed for everyday transactions such as deposits, withdrawals, bill payments, and debit card purchases. In the context of personal finance, it often serves as the primary **buffer** where funds needed for immediate spending are kept readily accessible.

## Role in the Simple Budget Framework
- Acts as the *ready‑to‑use* buffer described in [[concepts/buffer_checking]] and [[concepts/simple_budget_framework]].
- Holds enough cash to cover roughly two months of the user’s highest‑expense month, providing a safety net for spontaneous purchases.
- Periodically replenished from a high‑yield savings account (see [[entities/high_yield_savings_account]]) following the monthly "money day" routine.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-40_2024-12-13]]
- The recommendation was to keep about **$7,000** in checking for a user with a $3,000 high‑month expense, representing a two‑month buffer plus a comfort margin.
- Checking should be topped up **once a month** (or at payday) from the high‑yield savings account.
- Spending from checking is allowed as long as the balance stays above the defined buffer; otherwise, a small transfer from the HYSA is made first.
