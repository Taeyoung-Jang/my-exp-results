# High‑Yield Savings Account Buckets

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Organizing a high‑yield savings account (HYSA) into a small set of labeled sub‑buckets that remain fully liquid while giving each portion a specific saving purpose.

---

## Why Use Buckets?
- Keeps the bulk of your money earning interest instead of sitting idle in a checking account.
- Provides mental separation for different goals (e.g., general savings, travel, larger purchases) without the friction of multiple external accounts.
- Supports a **simple budget framework** that balances intentional saving with day‑to‑day flexibility.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-40_2024-12-13]])
1. **Base Buffer** – First, maintain a cash buffer in your [[entities/checking_account]] (see [[concepts/buffer_checking]]). The remainder goes into the HYSA.
2. **Bucket Count** – Limit to 3‑4 buckets to keep the system simple.
3. **Labeling** – Use the bank’s internal tagging or a personal spreadsheet to mark each bucket (e.g., “General Savings”, “Travel/Fun”, “Big Purchases”).
4. **Liquidity** – All buckets stay in a single HYSA, so they earn the same interest rate and can be moved back to checking instantly when needed.

## Example Allocation (using the $15,000 example)
- **Checking buffer:** $7,000 (covers ~2 × highest month spending)
- **HYSA total:** $8,000, split as:
  - **General Savings:** $4,000
  - **Travel / Fun:** $2,000
  - **Upcoming Large Purchases:** $2,000

These figures illustrate the **high‑yield savings account buckets** concept: the bulk of funds are purpose‑assigned yet remain accessible.

## Practical Tips
- **Single HYSA vs. Multiple Accounts** – Prefer a single HYSA with internal labels for ease of management; separate external accounts are only needed if your bank lacks clear labeling tools.
- **Rebalancing** – During your monthly “money day”, review bucket balances and shift money between them to reflect new priorities.
- **Spontaneous Purchases** – Before spending, verify that your checking balance stays above the buffer; if not, transfer the needed amount from the appropriate HYSA bucket.
