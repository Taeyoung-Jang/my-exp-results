# Weekly Caps

## Definition
Weekly Caps are preset, short‑term spending limits assigned to specific budget categories for each seven‑day period. They provide a clear, low‑stress boundary that helps keep discretionary spending within a manageable range.

## Origin
The concept is illustrated in the **session‑08_2024‑06‑15** budget example, where the user adopts two caps:
- **Groceries:** $65 per week
- **Fun / Misc:** $20 per week

These amounts were chosen to balance necessities with a modest allowance for discretionary items while preserving a buffer fund.

## How It Works
1. **Set the caps** at the start of a budgeting cycle.
2. **Track** each purchase against the weekly amount in a simple running note or minimal app.
3. **Reset** the caps on a consistent day (e.g., Monday or payday) to start the next seven‑day period.
4. **Carry over** any unspent dollars to a buffer fund if desired.

## Adjustments for Calendar Variations
- In months with **five weeks**, the recommended approach is to keep the caps unchanged and cover the extra week using any leftover padding (e.g., the $280 unassigned amount from the example). 
- If the padding is exhausted, apply a **fixed reduction rule** (e.g., lower each cap by $2‑$5) for the following month, keeping the new caps steady to avoid frequent recalculations.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of a broader **[[concepts/simple_budget_framework]]** that groups spending into essentials, discretionary weekly caps, and a buffer.
- The caps help protect the **[[concepts/buffer_fund]]** by limiting overspend on discretionary categories.
