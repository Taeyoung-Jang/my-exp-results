# Summary of session-08_2024-06-15

## Core Proposal
- Use a **three‑bucket budgeting framework**: essentials, discretionary weekly spending, and a buffer. [[concepts/simple_budget_framework]]
- Set **concrete weekly caps**:
  - Groceries: **$65/week**
  - Fun/Misc: **$20/week** [[concepts/weekly_caps]]
- Allocate a **$300 buffer** as an emergency cushion, untouched unless needed. [[concepts/buffer_fund]]
- Track spending with a **single running note** on a phone (or simple app), updating two lines each week and resetting on a chosen day. (simple tracking method)

## Monthly Calculation (4‑week month)
- Fixed monthly bills: **$1,080** (rent/utilities $900, phone $40, transit $80, debt $60)
- Weekly groceries (4 weeks): **$260**
- Weekly fun/misc (4 weeks): **$80**
- Buffer: **$300**
- **Total used:** $1,720 → **Remaining unassigned:** $280 (can serve as padding or roll over).

## Handling 5‑Week Months
- Keep weekly caps unchanged; cover the extra week using the **$280 leftover** or other padding. (adjustment for five‑week months)
- If padding is exhausted, apply a **simple rule**: reduce each weekly cap by a small fixed amount (e.g., $2‑$5) for the next month and keep the new caps steady, avoiding constant recalculation.

## Practical Tips
- Choose a consistent reset day (Monday or payday) to build habit.
- Record each purchase by subtracting from the weekly amount; if you end the week under the cap, move the surplus to the buffer.
- Review the plan after the first month and adjust caps modestly based on actual spend.
