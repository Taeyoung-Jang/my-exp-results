# Progression and Deload

**Definition** – A structured approach to systematically increase training load over several weeks and then reduce volume/intensity (deload) to promote recovery, especially in programs that must protect a recovering knee.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-02_2024-07-14]])

| Phase | Weeks | Load/Volume Adjustments | Focus |
|-------|-------|--------------------------|-------|
| **Initial** | 1‑2 | Conservative weights, shallow ranges, perfect form | Establish baseline, ensure knee comfort |
| **Build** | 3‑4 | Add 2‑5 lb to hinge/bridge exercises *or* add 1 rep per set each week | Gradual overload while monitoring pain |
| **Further Build** | 5‑6 | Continue small increments; optionally add an extra set on one exercise if well‑tolerated | Strength gains without over‑stress |
| **Peak / Consolidation** | 7 (optional) | Keep loads steady, refine technique, no new volume |
| **Deload** | 8 | Halve the number of sets, keep range of motion comfortable, no load increase | Allow tissues to recover, reset for next cycle |

### Practical Checklist
- **Rest intervals** stay the same (60‑90 s) throughout the cycle.
- **Progression triggers**: pain‑free execution, stable knee, ability to complete prescribed reps.
- **Deload cues**: lingering soreness, swelling, or any joint discomfort signals the need to enter the deload week early.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The progression scheme fits within the broader [[concepts/weekly-fitness-structure]] that balances strength, conditioning, and recovery.
- It respects the principles of [[concepts/knee-friendly-exercises]], ensuring load increases never compromise knee safety.

## Implementation Tips
1. **Track loads** in a simple log (exercise, weight, reps, week).
2. **Assess knee response** before each progression step; if any sharp pain appears, revert to the previous load.
3. **Use the deload week** as a mental reset—focus on mobility, light cardio, and perfect form.
4. **Repeat the cycle** after deload, optionally adjusting the starting load based on new strength levels.
