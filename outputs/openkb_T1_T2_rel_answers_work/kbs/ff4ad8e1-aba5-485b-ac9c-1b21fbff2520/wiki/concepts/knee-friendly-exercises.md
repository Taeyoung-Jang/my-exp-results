# Knee‑Friendly Exercises

**Definition** – A set of strength, conditioning, and mobility movements specifically chosen or modified to minimise stress on the knee joint, allowing safe progression during rehabilitation or injury‑prevention phases.

---

## Core Principles
1. **Reduce knee loading** – Replace deep squats, lunges, and high‑impact drills with hinge‑dominant and glute‑focused actions.
2. **Maintain overall balance** – Keep upper‑body work, core stability, and cardiovascular fitness, but select low‑impact cardio modalities.
3. **Progress gradually** – Follow a structured progression (see [[concepts/progression-and-deload]]) and monitor pain signals.
4. **Flexibility for life** – If a day becomes too busy, shift or skip sessions without penalising the program (see [[concepts/weekly-fitness-structure]]).

---

## Typical Knee‑Safe Substitutions (from [[summaries/session-02_2024-07-14]])
| Original Exercise | Knee‑Friendly Alternative |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Back/Front Squat, Deep Lunges | Romanian Deadlift, Supported Split‑Stance Squat (very shallow), Glute Bridge / Hip Thrust |
| High‑Impact Intervals, Running | Stationary Bike, Elliptical, Light Rowing, Flat‑ground Brisk Walk |
| Jumping, Plyometrics | Controlled Mobility flows, ankle‑circles, hip‑circles |

---

## Example Lower‑Body Templates
### Template A
- Warm‑up (march, hip circles, ankle rolls, glute bridges)
- Romanian Deadlift – 3×8‑10
- Glute Bridge / Hip Thrust – 3×10‑12
- Low Step‑Up (shallow) – 2‑3×8 per leg
- Plank – 2×20‑30 s

### Template B
- Warm‑up (same)
- Hamstring Curl – 3×10‑12
- Single‑Leg Romanian Deadlift – 2‑3×6‑8 per side
- Supported Split‑Stance Squat (very shallow) – 2×6 per leg
- Side Plank or Bird‑Dog – 2×20‑30 s per side

Both templates illustrate the **hinge‑first** approach, limiting knee flexion while still loading the posterior chain and glutes.

---

## Low‑Impact Cardio Options
- **Stationary bike** – adjustable resistance, minimal joint impact.
- **Elliptical trainer** – smooth motion, engages both upper and lower body.
- **Rowing machine** – primarily a pulling movement; keep knee bend shallow.
- **Flat‑ground walk** – easy to control pace and range of motion.

Avoid: jumping, sprinting, deep lunges, or any activity that produces sharp knee pain or instability.

---

## Monitoring & Modification Rules
- Stop immediately if you feel sharp pain, giving‑way, or swelling.
- First step: **reduce range of motion**; if pain persists, **reduce load** or switch to a bridge‑type movement.
- If discomfort continues post‑session (swelling, limping), skip lower‑body work the next day and focus on upper‑body or pure mobility.
