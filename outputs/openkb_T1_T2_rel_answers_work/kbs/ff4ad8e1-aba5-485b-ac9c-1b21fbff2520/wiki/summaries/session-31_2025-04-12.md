# Summary of session-31_2025-04-12

**Document type:** Conversational advice (user → assistant) providing a structured, low‑friction movement habit plan for a busy workweek.

## Core Idea
Create a **playful, micro‑session** routine (3–8 min) that fits into a typical Monday‑Friday work schedule, supports basketball and stair‑climbing, and stays at an **RPE ≈ 3** (easy, no sweat). Each day includes two versions: a standing version and a desk‑friendly alternative, plus a 30‑second emergency version.

## Daily Micro‑Session Cheat Sheet
| Day | Standing (time) | Desk‑friendly (time) | Key Moves |
|-----|----------------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Monday** | Hip circles 1 min each direction, ankle rolls 1 min per side, torso twists 2 min | Seated hip circles 1 min, ankle rolls 1 min per side, rib‑cage twists 2 min | Mobility flow for hips, ankles, spine |
| **Tuesday** | Single‑leg balance 40 s/side × 2, slow calf raises 1 min | Seated leg lifts 30 s/side × 2, seated calf raises 1 min | Balance & ankle activation |
| **Wednesday** | Shoulder‑blade squeezes 1 min, chest opener 1 min, neck mobility 1–2 min | Same sequence seated | Posture reset |
| **Thursday** | Half‑range squats 2 min, split‑stance dips 1 min/side | Chair‑hover pulses 1 min, seated leg swings 1 min/side | Light leg strength activation |
| **Friday** | Lateral steps 2 min, shadow shuffles 1–2 min | Seated weight shifts 1 min, tiny “defensive slides” 1–2 min | Coordination tune‑up |

**30‑second emergency version** – any day: 15 s of joint mobility (hip/shoulder/neck) + 15 s of balance (standing or seated).

## Scaling Guidance
If you begin to feel warm or sweaty, **reduce range of motion and slow the tempo** until the effort feels comfortably light again.
