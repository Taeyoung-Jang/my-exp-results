# Session 02 – 2024‑07‑14 Summary

**Main contribution** – A knee‑friendly 7‑day fitness routine that balances strength, conditioning, mobility, and recovery, plus a 3‑day fallback, detailed lower‑body templates (A/B), progression schedule, and clear stop/modify rules.

## Weekly structure
- 2–3 full‑body strength days, 2 lighter conditioning/mobility days, 1–2 rest days.  
- Flexible “slide‑in” approach for busy schedules.  
- [[concepts/weekly-fitness-structure]]

## Knee‑safe modifications
- Replace deep squats/lunges with Romanian deadlifts, glute bridges, shallow step‑ups, supported split‑stance work.  
- Low‑impact cardio options: stationary bike, elliptical, rowing, flat‑ground walk.  
- Avoid jumping, deep lunges, high‑impact intervals.  
- [[concepts/knee-friendly-exercises]]

## Lower‑body templates (checklist format)

**Template A**  
- Warm‑up (5 min march, hip circles, ankle rolls, 10–12 glute bridges)  
- Romanian deadlift 3 × 8‑10, 90 s rest  
- Glute bridge / hip thrust 3 × 10‑12, 60‑90 s rest  
- Low step‑up 2‑3 × 8/leg, 60 s rest  
- Plank 2 × 20‑30 s, 30‑45 s rest  

**Template B**  
- Same warm‑up  
- Hamstring curl 3 × 10‑12, 60‑90 s rest  
- Single‑leg RDL 2‑3 × 6‑8/side, 60‑90 s rest  
- Supported split‑stance squat 2 × 6/leg, 60 s rest  
- Side plank / bird‑dog 2 × 20‑30 s/side, 30‑45 s rest  

## 6–8 week progression & deload
- Weeks 1‑2: conservative load, focus on form.  
- Weeks 3‑4: +2‑5 lb or +1 rep per set.  
- Weeks 5‑6: continue small increments; add an extra set on one exercise if tolerated.  
- Week 7: maintain load, refine technique.  
- Week 8 (deload): halve sets, keep range comfortable, no load increase.  
- [[concepts/progression-and-deload]]

## Stop / modify rules
- Stop immediately on sharp knee pain, instability, or increasing discomfort.  
- First action: reduce range of motion, then lower load.  
- If post‑workout swelling or limping persists, skip lower‑body work next session.  
- Include plain‑text “stop/modify rules” as guidance.

## 3‑day fallback
- Two full‑body strength sessions (using Templates A/B) + one conditioning or mobility day, preserving major movement patterns while respecting knee limits.
