# Pickup Basketball League

**Type:** Event

The pickup basketball league is a weekend, casual basketball gathering the user joined to complement their weekday movement habit. It provides short bursts of sport‑specific movement while keeping the overall weekly load low and enjoyable.

## Role in the Session‑31 Plan
- Serves as the primary [[concepts/playful-micro-movement]] activity on weekends, reinforcing the weekday micro‑session routine.
- Offers a natural [[concepts/low-rpe-activity]] on the court by focusing on fun and skill rather than maximal effort.
- Complements the weekday micro‑sessions by adding variety and social motivation, supporting the principle of [[concepts/progression-and-deload]].

## Key Facts (from [[summaries/session-31_2025-04-12]])
- Joined after the user reported wanting “quick cuts and sprints.”
- Happens once per week, typically on Saturday afternoons.
- Intended to be low‑impact on the body; the user pairs it with knee‑friendly activities during the week (see [[concepts/knee-friendly-exercises]]).
- The user uses the league to maintain a balanced movement habit without excessive fatigue.

## Practical Tips
- Treat each game as a **fun** extension of the weekday micro‑sessions; keep intensity around RPE 3–4.
- Incorporate brief [[concepts/desk-friendly-exercise]] warm‑ups before the game (e.g., ankle rolls, hip circles) to protect the knees.
