# Soy Sauce

**Type:** product

Soy sauce is a dark, salty liquid condiment produced by fermenting soybeans (often with wheat), salt, and water. It is a fundamental seasoning in many Asian cuisines and widely used to add umami flavor to quick, home‑cooked dishes.
