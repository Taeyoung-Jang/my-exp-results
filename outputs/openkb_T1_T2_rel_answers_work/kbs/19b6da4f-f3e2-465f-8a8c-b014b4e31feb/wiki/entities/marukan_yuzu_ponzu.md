# Marukan Yuzu Ponzu

**Product type**: condiment / sauce

Marukan Yuzu Ponzu is a commercially‑available Japanese ponzu sauce that blends yuzu citrus juice, soy sauce, vinegar, and a hint of sweetness.  In the *session‑31_2025‑02‑12* starter‑kit it is highlighted as a bright, low‑spice flavor booster for a variety of takeout meals.

## Key facts from the session
- Recommended brand: **Marukan** (widely sold in Asian grocery aisles).
- Described as “bright and citrusy” – ideal for dishes that benefit from an acidic lift.
- Suggested pairings: fried chicken, dumplings, noodles, tempura, sushi bowls, and any other dishes that need a fresh zing.
- Included as item 2 in the ten‑item low‑spice “chef’s pantry” kit.
