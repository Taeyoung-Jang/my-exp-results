# Dry Pasta

Dry pasta is a dried, shelf‑stable form of wheat‑based noodles that cooks quickly in boiling water. It serves as a versatile base for many fast, minimally processed meals.

## Role in [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]]
- Listed as a core pantry staple for the **Garlic‑olive‑oil pasta** template.
- Provides a convenient, inexpensive protein‑free option that can be paired with fresh add‑ons (e.g., cherry tomatoes, basil) or frozen vegetables (e.g., frozen spinach).
- Supports the **low‑effort meals** approach by requiring only 8‑12 minutes of cooking time.

## Storage & Usage Tips
- Store in a cool, dry place; dry pasta has a shelf life of several years.
- Portion out servings before cooking to avoid over‑cooking.
- Pair with fresh or frozen vegetables and a drizzle of [[entities/olive_oil]] for a balanced, satisfying meal.
