## Stonefire Frozen Mini Naan

**Product type**: frozen ready‑to‑heat flatbread.

### Overview
Stonefire Frozen Mini Naan is a pre‑made, freezer‑stable naan that can be heated in minutes. It provides a soft, slightly chewy bread that pairs well with a variety of cuisines.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-02-12]]
- Listed as item **#10** in the low‑spice starter kit for enhancing takeout meals.
- Recommended for serving with Indian, Middle Eastern, stews, or as a quick side for any takeout dish.
- Suggested as a versatile, low‑effort component of a **[[concepts/chefs_pantry]]**.

### Typical uses
- Heat in the oven or microwave and serve alongside takeout to create a more complete plate, supporting the [[concepts/low_effort_meals]] and [[concepts/takeout_enhancement]] concepts.
- Use as a base for toppings such as [[entities/kewpie_mayo]], [[entities/brightland_olive_oil]], or a drizzle of [[entities/mizkan_seasoned_rice_vinegar]].
- Pair with other starter‑kit items like [[entities/soom_tahini]] or [[entities/bubbies_sauerkraut]] for added texture and flavor.

### Storage & preparation
- Store in the freezer; keep sealed until ready to use.
- Heat according to package instructions (typically 2‑3 minutes in a microwave or 5‑7 minutes in a pre‑heated oven).

### Related entities
- Works well with other pantry staples such as [[entities/brightland_olive_oil]], [[entities/kewpie_mayo]], [[entities/marukan_yuzu_ponzu]], and [[entities/trader_joes_frozen_jasmine_rice]].
