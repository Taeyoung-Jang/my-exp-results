## Rick’s Picks “Phat Beets”

**Type:** Product (pickled vegetables)

### Overview
Rick’s Picks “Phat Beets” are lightly sweetened, mildly spiced pickled beet slices packaged for quick use. They are sold in a resealable jar and keep well in the refrigerator for several weeks.

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-02-12]]
- Recommended as item **5** in the low‑spice “chef’s pantry” starter kit.
- Described as a **mild, sweet pickled beet** that pairs well with salads, grain bowls, and roasted meat dishes.
- Chosen for its ability to add acidity and texture without adding heat, supporting the goal of low‑effort, flavorful takeout enhancements.

### Typical Uses
- Tossed into mixed green salads for a pop of color and tang.
- Added atop grain bowls or quinoa salads as a quick garnish.
- Served alongside roasted chicken or pork to balance richness.
- Used as a side condiment for takeout meals, fitting into the [[concepts/chefs_pantry]] approach.
