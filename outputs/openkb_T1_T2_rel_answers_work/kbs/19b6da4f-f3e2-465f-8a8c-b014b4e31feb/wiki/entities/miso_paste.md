# Miso Paste

Miso paste is a fermented soybean (and sometimes rice or barley) paste that provides a deep umami flavor. It is shelf‑stable until opened and can be kept in the refrigerator for several months.

## Role in the session‑09_2024‑06‑15 document
- Recommended as a **minimal‑processing** ingredient for a quick ramen or broth‑and‑noodle bowl.  
- Serves as a convenient flavor base that pairs with instant ramen, frozen vegetables, and eggs, keeping the meal fast and nutritious.  
- Highlights the principle of using long‑lasting pantry staples to reduce cooking effort while maintaining ingredient control.
