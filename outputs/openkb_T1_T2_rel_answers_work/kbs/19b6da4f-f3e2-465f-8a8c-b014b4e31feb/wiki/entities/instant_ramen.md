## Instant Ramen

Instant ramen is a pre‑cooked noodle product packaged with flavoring, designed to be ready in minutes with hot water. It is valued for its convenience, long shelf life, and low cost, making it a staple in many quick‑meal strategies.

### Role in [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]]
In the "tired‑night" meal templates, instant ramen serves as the core carbohydrate for a **quick ramen / broth‑and‑noodle bowl**. The assistant suggests pairing it with minimal‑process add‑ons such as [[entities/miso_paste]] (or stock cubes), frozen broccoli, frozen corn, eggs, and fresh mushrooms to boost nutrition while keeping preparation time under 20 minutes.

### Typical Upgrades & Pairings
- **Flavor base**: miso paste or a broth concentrate.
- **Vegetables**: frozen broccoli, frozen corn, and fresh mushrooms.
- **Protein**: eggs (soft‑boiled or fried).
- **Optional seasoning**: a drizzle of [[entities/soy_sauce]] or a splash of [[entities/olive_oil]] for richer mouthfeel.

These additions keep the meal aligned with the principles of [[concepts/low_effort_meals]] while preserving ingredient control.

### Benefits for Low‑Effort Meal Planning
- **Speed**: cooks in 3‑5 minutes.
- **Shelf stability**: stores at room temperature for years, supporting [[concepts/pantry_management]] strategies.
- **Flexibility**: can be customized with both frozen and fresh components, allowing rotation between early‑week fresh produce and later‑week pantry staples.
- **Minimal processing**: when enhanced only with whole‑food toppings, the overall dish remains relatively unprocessed.

### Storage & Shelf Life
Instant ramen packets are non‑perishable until opened. Keep them in a cool, dry place. Once the package is opened, reseal the remaining noodles in an airtight container to maintain freshness.
