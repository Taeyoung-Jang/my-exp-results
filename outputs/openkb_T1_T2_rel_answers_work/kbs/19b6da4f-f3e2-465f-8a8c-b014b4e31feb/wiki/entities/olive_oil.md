# Olive Oil

## Overview
Olive oil is a plant‑derived oil prized for its mild flavor, relatively high smoke point, and health‑benefiting monounsaturated fats. It is commonly used for sautéing, drizzling, and as a base for dressings.

## Role in **session‑09_2024‑06‑15**
In the conversation, olive oil appears as a core pantry staple in multiple "tired‑night" meal templates:
- **Garlic‑olive‑oil pasta** – combined with dry pasta, garlic, and frozen spinach.
- **Sheet‑pan chicken or tofu** – tossed with chicken thighs (or tofu) and long‑keeping veggies.
- General guidance for low‑effort, minimally processed meals stresses using olive oil as the primary cooking fat.

These uses illustrate olive oil’s importance for **ingredient control**, quick preparation, and maintaining a simple pantry.
