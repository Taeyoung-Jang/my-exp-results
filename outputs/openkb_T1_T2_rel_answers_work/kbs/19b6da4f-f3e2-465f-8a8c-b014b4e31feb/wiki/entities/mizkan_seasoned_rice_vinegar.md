# Mizkan Seasoned Rice Vinegar

Mizkan Seasoned Rice Vinegar is a bottled condiment made by Mizkan that combines rice vinegar with a light seasoning of sugar and salt. It provides a subtle acidity and a hint of sweetness, making it a versatile lift for many dishes.

## Role in the "Chef's Pantry" Starter Kit
The product appears in the low‑spice starter kit from [[summaries/session-31_2025-02-12]]. It is recommended as a quick splash that can:
- Brighten sushi bowls, salads, and noodle dishes.
- Add a gentle tang to takeout meals without requiring any cooking.
- Complement other pantry items such as [[entities/kewpie_mayo]] and [[entities/marukan_yuzu_ponzu]].

## Typical Pairings
- Sushi bowls and poke
- Light salads and grain bowls
- Noodle salads or cold soba
- Any takeout that could benefit from a touch of acidity, aligning with the [[concepts/takeout_enhancement]] concept.
