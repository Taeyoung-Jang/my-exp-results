# Tofu

**Type:** product

Tofu is a minimally processed soy curd that serves as a versatile, protein‑rich ingredient for fast, home‑cooked meals. In the *session‑09_2024‑06‑15* conversation it is highlighted as a staple for the "sheet‑pan chicken or tofu" template, offering a plant‑based alternative to animal protein.

## Key attributes
- **Storage:** Usually sold frozen or refrigerated; frozen tofu can be stocked long‑term in the pantry.
- **Preparation:** Requires little more than a drizzle of [[entities/olive_oil]], salt, and pepper before roasting or sautéing.
- **Nutrition:** Provides complete protein, low saturated fat, and is a good source of calcium and iron when fortified.

## Role in the session‑09 meal templates
- Listed as a **staple** for the sheet‑pan meal template, interchangeable with [[entities/chicken_thighs]].
- Paired with long‑keeping vegetables such as carrots or potatoes, and a fresh add‑on like zucchini or bell pepper.
- Supports the [[concepts/low_effort_meals]] philosophy by delivering a quick, minimally processed protein option.

## Related concepts and entities
- Preparation often involves [[entities/olive_oil]] and basic seasonings.
- Can be combined with other pantry items such as [[entities/soy_sauce]] for flavor.
- Fits within the broader framework of [[concepts/pantry_management]] and the decision‑making context of [[concepts/eating_out_vs_home_cooking]].
