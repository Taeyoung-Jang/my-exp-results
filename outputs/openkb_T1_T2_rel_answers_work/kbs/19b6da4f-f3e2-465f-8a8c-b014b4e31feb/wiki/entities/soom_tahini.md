# Soom Tahini

**Type:** product

Soom Tahini is a creamy, smooth sesame paste marketed as a versatile condiment. In the *session-31_2025-02-12* starter‑kit it is highlighted for its ability to enrich Mediterranean‑style takeout, grain bowls, and roasted vegetables without any cooking required.

## Key Facts from the Document
- Listed as item **3** in the low‑spice “chef’s pantry” kit.
- Recommended brand: **Soom** (often sold in a 12‑oz jar).
- Suggested pairings include falafel, hummus, salads, and any dish that benefits from a nutty, umami boost.
- Works well alongside other pantry staples such as [[entities/brightland_olive_oil]], [[entities/kewpie_mayo]], and [[entities/marukan_yuzu_ponzu]] to create quick flavor layers.

## Role in the Chef’s Pantry
Soom Tahini exemplifies the [[concepts/chefs_pantry]] idea: a single, ready‑to‑use ingredient that can transform ordinary takeout into a more intentional, restaurant‑like plate. It aligns with the [[concepts/low_effort_meals]] and [[concepts/takeout_enhancement]] concepts by requiring no prep beyond a drizzle or quick stir.

## Typical Uses
- **Mediterranean takeout:** drizzle over falafel wraps or grain bowls.
- **Sauce base:** mix with lemon juice, water, and garlic for a quick tahini dressing.
- **Flavor enhancer:** swirl into soups or stews for added depth.

## Storage & Shelf Life
- Store unopened jar in a cool, dry place.
- Once opened, keep refrigerated; the paste remains good for several months.
