# Summary of Session 40 (2025‑02‑14)

**Topic:** Designing an ultra‑minimal, low‑odor, **no‑cook** food stash that can be prepared with *only cold water* and stays safe at room temperature after opening.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/micro-pantry]]** – a tiny, shelf‑stable collection (8‑10 items) that fits in a small bin.  
- **[[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]]** – choosing items that emit little smell, avoiding tuna, strong cheeses, etc.  
- **[[concepts/single-serve-packaging]]** – foods packaged for one‑time consumption that remain safe without refrigeration.  

Other ideas such as no‑cook meals, cold water preparation, and nutrition estimation are discussed in the text but are not linked to dedicated concept pages.

## Final “Open‑and‑Finish” Micro Pantry (8‑10 items)

| # | Item (example) | Packaging | Purpose |
|---|----------------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Ready‑to‑eat rice pouch (e.g., [[entities/bens-original]]) | Single‑serve, resealable | Base carbohydrate |
| 2 | Cooked bean tetra‑pack (e.g., [[entities/goya]] Chickpeas 70 g) | Single‑serve, no‑refrigeration | Protein & fiber |
| 3 | Single‑serve lentil salad tetra‑pack (ready‑to‑eat) | Single‑serve | Additional protein |
| 4 | Peanut‑butter squeeze packet (e.g., [[entities/justins]]) | Individual packet | Healthy fats & protein |
| 5 | Tomato‑pesto powder pouch (rehydratable) | Resealable dry powder | Flavor & healthy fats |
| 6 | Mini olive‑oil bottle (15 ml) | Small bottle | Dressing & calorie‑dense fat |
| 7 | Wasa crispbread (2‑piece pack) | Small sealed pack | Crunchy vehicle for spreads |
| 8 | Mixed nuts (30 g) | Resealable bag | Snack protein & fats |
| 9 | Dried fruit (raisins or cranberries, 30 g) | Resealable bag | Quick carbs & flavor |
|10| Optional: Ready‑to‑eat rice “cold” variant (e.g., [[entities/ready-rice]]) | Single‑serve | Backup carbohydrate |

All items are shelf‑stable, low‑odor, and safe at room temperature after opening because they are either single‑serve or resealable dry.

## Four No‑Cook Meal / Snack Combos
1. **Rice + Bean + Pesto + Olive Oil**  
   *Ingredients*: 1 pouch ready rice, 1 tetra‑pack chickpeas, 1 tsp pesto powder (rehydrated with a splash of cold water), 1 tsp olive oil.  
   *Nutrition*: ~480‑520 kcal, 15‑18 g protein.

2. **Rice + Peanut Butter + Pesto**  
   *Ingredients*: 1 pouch rice, 2 tbsp peanut‑butter packet, 1 tsp pesto powder.  
   *Nutrition*: ~450‑500 kcal, ~12 g protein.

3. **Crispbread + Peanut Butter + Dried Fruit**  
   *Ingredients*: 2 [[entities/wasa]] crispbreads, 2 tbsp peanut‑butter, a handful (≈30 g) dried fruit.  
   *Nutrition*: ~320‑360 kcal, ~10 g protein.

4. **Trail‑Mix Bowl**  
   *Ingredients*: 30 g mixed nuts, 30 g dried fruit, 1 tbsp peanut‑butter mixed in.  
   *Nutrition*: ~400‑450 kcal, 10‑12 g protein.

## Safety Note
Original suggestions (opened canned chickpeas or cooked‑lentil pouches) require refrigeration after opening. The revised list swaps them for **single‑serve, ready‑to‑eat** packs that can be consumed in one sitting, eliminating the need for a fridge.

## Takeaway
A well‑curated **[[concepts/micro-pantry]]** of 8‑10 shelf‑stable, low‑odor, single‑serve items enables you to assemble nourishing, no‑cook meals using only cold water (or none at all), while staying within a tiny storage footprint and avoiding food‑safety concerns.
