# Micro Pantry

**Definition**: A compact, shelf‑stable collection of 8‑10 items that enables no‑cook, low‑odor meals.

**Origin**: Described in [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-14]] as a solution for minimal‑setup living, especially when only cold water and no refrigeration are available.

**Key Characteristics**
- **Shelf‑stable**: All items remain safe at room temperature after opening.
- **Low‑odor**: Avoids strong‑smelling foods such as tuna.
- **Single‑serve or resealable**: Items are packaged for one‑time consumption or can be resealed safely.
- **No‑cook**: Meals are assembled without heating, using only cold water when needed.

**Typical Item List** (8‑10 items)
1. Ready‑to‑eat rice pouch – [[entities/bens-original]] or similar.
2. Single‑serve cooked bean tetra‑pack – [[entities/goya]] chickpeas.
3. Single‑serve lentil salad pack – [[entities/trader-joes]] style.
4. Peanut‑butter squeeze packet – [[entities/justins]].
5. Tomato‑pesto powder pouch (rehydratable).
6. Mini olive‑oil bottle.
7. Wasa crispbread – [[entities/wasa]].
8. Mixed nuts (resealable bag).
9. Dried fruit (raisins or cranberries).

**Related Concepts**
- [[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]] – focuses on minimizing smell in stored foods.
- [[concepts/single-serve-packaging]] – emphasizes the use of one‑time portions for safety and convenience.

**Use Cases**
- Quick meals for travelers or backpackers.
- Emergency food stash for small living spaces.
- Minimal kitchen setups where cooking appliances are unavailable.

**Benefits**
- Reduces decision fatigue and grocery trips.
- Ensures nutrition (calories and protein) without refrigeration.
- Fits in a small bin or backpack.
