# Justin’s Peanut Butter (Squeeze Pack)

Justin’s is a brand of single‑serve squeeze‑pack peanut butter, marketed for on‑the‑go nutrition. In the context of the **session‑40_2025‑02‑14** discussion, it was recommended as a key component of an ultra‑minimal, low‑odor, no‑cook micro pantry.

## Key Facts from Session 40
- Presented as a **single‑serve packaging** option that can be finished in one sitting, eliminating the need for refrigeration after opening. [[concepts/single-serve-packaging]]
- Chosen for its **low odor** profile, fitting the low‑odor food storage goals of the micro pantry. [[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]]
- Provides a dense source of **healthy fats and protein**, essential for meeting the calorie and protein targets of the suggested no‑cook combos.
- Paired with other items such as ready‑to‑eat rice, bean tetra‑packs, and mixed nuts to create quick meals and snacks.

## Role in the Micro Pantry
The squeeze‑pack format makes it ideal for the **micro‑pantry** concept: it fits easily into a small bin, does not require a utensil for opening, and can be stored at room temperature. [[concepts/micro-pantry]]
