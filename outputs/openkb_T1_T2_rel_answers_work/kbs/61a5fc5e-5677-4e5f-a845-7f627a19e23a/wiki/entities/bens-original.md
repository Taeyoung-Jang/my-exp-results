# Ben’s Original

Ben’s Original (formerly known as Uncle Ben’s) is a **shelf‑stable instant rice** sold in single‑serve pouches. It is marketed as a quick‑cook, ready‑to‑eat carbohydrate that requires only hot or cold water to hydrate.

## Key Facts from Session 40 (2025‑02‑14)
- **Product type:** Instant rice, ready‑to‑eat.
- **Packaging:** Single‑serve pouch that can be opened and finished in one sitting, fitting the [[concepts/single-serve-packaging]] model.
- **Role in a micro pantry:** Serves as the primary base carbohydrate in the suggested **[[concepts/micro-pantry]]** for a minimal, low‑odor, no‑cook food stash.
- **Low‑odor profile:** The product has a mild scent, making it suitable for [[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]] scenarios.
- **Usage examples from the document:**
  - *Rice + Bean + Pesto + Olive Oil* – a high‑calorie, protein‑rich combo cited in [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-14]].
  - *Rice + Peanut Butter + Pesto* – another no‑cook meal leveraging the product’s convenience.
