## Ready Rice

Ready Rice is a brand of **ready‑to‑eat rice** sold in single‑serve, shelf‑stable pouches that can be eaten cold or rehydrated with water. It is highlighted in [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-14]] as a core component of a **[[concepts/micro-pantry]]** designed for low‑odor, no‑cook living.

### Key Features
- **Single‑serve packaging**: each pouch is meant to be consumed in one sitting, aligning with the [[concepts/single-serve-packaging]] principle and eliminating the need for refrigeration after opening.
- **Shelf‑stable**: does not require cooking; can be eaten straight from the pouch or softened with cold water, supporting [[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]] needs.
- **Compact**: fits easily in a small bin alongside other items like [[entities/bens-original]] peanut butter, [[entities/goya]] bean packs, and [[entities/wasa]] crispbread.

### Typical Use Cases
1. **Meal combo**: Mix a pouch of Ready Rice with a tetra‑pack of chickpeas and a drizzle of olive oil for a quick 500 kcal, 15 g protein meal.
2. **Snack**: Combine Ready Rice with peanut butter and pesto powder for a portable, high‑energy bite.

### Related Products
- **Ben’s Original** – another brand of instant rice often used interchangeably with Ready Rice.
- **Trader Joe’s** cooked lentil packs – mentioned as an alternative protein source in the same micro‑pantry.

Ready Rice exemplifies the approach of keeping a **low‑odor, no‑cook** food stash that remains safe at room temperature, as described in the session‑40 micro‑pantry plan.
