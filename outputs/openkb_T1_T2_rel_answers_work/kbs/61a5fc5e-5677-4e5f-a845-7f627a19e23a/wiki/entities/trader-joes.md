# Trader Joe’s

Trader Joe’s is a U.S.-based grocery retailer known for its private‑label products, unique snack items, and focus on value. In the context of [[summaries/session-40_2025-02-14]], Trader Joe’s is referenced as the source of a **shelf‑stable cooked lentil pouch** that was originally suggested for a minimal “micro pantry.”

## Relevance to the micro‑pantry concept
- The lentil pouch from Trader Joe’s was part of a suggested **[[concepts/micro-pantry]]** list for ultra‑compact, low‑odor food storage.
- Because the original pouch required refrigeration after opening, the summary recommends swapping it for a **[[concepts/single-serve-packaging]]** alternative. This highlights Trader Joe’s role in providing convenient, ready‑to‑eat items that fit the **[[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]]** criteria when properly selected.

## Key facts from the session
- Item mentioned: *Trader Joe’s steamed lentils* (single‑serve cooked lentil pouch). 
- Intended use: protein source in a no‑cook, cold‑water‑only pantry.
- Issue identified: the pouch needs refrigeration once opened, prompting a switch to truly “open‑and‑finish” options.
