# Wasa

Wasa is a brand of crispbread sold in small sealed packs. In **session‑40_2025‑02‑14** it is recommended as a compact, low‑odor vehicle for spreads such as peanut butter, making it a core element of a **[[concepts/micro-pantry]]**.

## Key facts from the session
- **Format:** 2‑piece crispbread pack, resealable and ready‑to‑eat.
- **Role:** Provides a crunchy base for toppings without needing any cooking or refrigeration.
- **Attributes:** Low‑odor, long‑shelf‑life, fits easily in a tiny bin alongside other single‑serve items.
- **Nutrition:** Adds carbs and fiber while keeping meals lightweight and portable.

## Relationships
- Supports the **[[concepts/low-odor-food-storage]]** strategy by being odor‑free and stable at room temperature.
- Works well with other single‑serve items like **[[entities/justins]]** peanut‑butter packets and **[[entities/ready-rice]]**.
- Mentioned in the **[[summaries/session-40_2025-02-14]]** summary as part of the final 8‑10‑item micro pantry list.

## Usage examples from the document
1. **Crispbread + peanut butter + dried fruit** – a quick snack delivering ~350 kcal and ~10 g protein.
2. **Crispbread topped with pesto powder** – a low‑odor, no‑cook meal component.

Wasa’s compact packaging and neutral taste make it ideal for minimalist, travel‑oriented food setups.
