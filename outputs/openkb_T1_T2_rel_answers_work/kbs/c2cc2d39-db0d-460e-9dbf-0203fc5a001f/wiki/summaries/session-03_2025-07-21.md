# Summary of session-03_2025-07-21

**Document type:** Conversation transcript – advice on screen use and sleep hygiene.

## Main ideas
- A short **wind‑down window** (30–60 min) without screens is ideal, but if you must use a screen, focus on reducing **intensity**, **color temperature**, and **visual contrast**.
- Key adjustments:
  1. **Brightness**: keep at the lower‑third (phones) or ~20‑30 % (computers).
  2. **Night‑mode / Night Shift / Night Light**: use the *warmest* setting (deep amber).
  3. **Dark mode** on the OS/app level to cut bright UI elements.
  4. **Room lighting**: a single warm lamp (2200‑2700 K, 200‑450 lumens) placed off‑axis; avoid bright overhead lights.
  5. **Blue‑light‑blocking glasses**: amber lenses give extra benefit over clear lenses, but the biggest gain comes from software settings.
- **Timing & environment**: keep a dim warm lamp on during the hour; complete darkness makes the screen’s contrast more alerting.
- **Prioritization**: max out software warmth first; glasses are a helpful secondary layer, especially if devices cannot be warmed enough or you are light‑sensitive.
