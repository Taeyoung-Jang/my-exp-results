# macOS

macOS is Apple’s desktop operating system. In the context of the **session‑03_2025‑07‑21** discussion, macOS is highlighted for its built‑in **Night Shift** and **dark mode** settings that help reduce blue‑light exposure during evening screen use.

## Key points from the document
- **Night Shift**: Users are advised to enable Night Shift and push the warmth slider to the *warmest* setting for the last hour before sleep. This aligns with the recommendation to lower blue‑light impact for better sleep quality.
- **Dark mode**: Turning on dark mode reduces bright UI elements, complementing the low‑brightness setting.
- **Brightness**: The document suggests keeping macOS screen brightness around **20‑30 %** in a dim room during the wind‑down period.
- **Wind‑down routine**: macOS settings are part of a broader [[concepts/wind-down-routine]] that includes a short screen‑free window, warm room lighting, and optional amber glasses.
