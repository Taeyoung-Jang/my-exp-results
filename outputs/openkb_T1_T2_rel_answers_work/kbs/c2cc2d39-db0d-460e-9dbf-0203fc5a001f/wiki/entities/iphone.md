# iPhone

The **iPhone** is Apple’s flagship smartphone line. In the context of the conversation *session‑03_2025-07-21*, it is mentioned as a primary device for applying evening screen‑time adjustments to protect sleep.

## Key points from the document
- **Brightness**: Recommended set to the lower‑quarter of the slider in a dim environment.
- **Night Shift**: Users should move the warmth slider to the *warmest* end, providing a deep amber hue that reduces blue‑light exposure.
- **Dark Mode**: Enable dark mode to lower UI brightness and contrast.
- **Usage tip**: Keep the phone at a comfortable distance (arm’s length) during the final hour before bedtime.
