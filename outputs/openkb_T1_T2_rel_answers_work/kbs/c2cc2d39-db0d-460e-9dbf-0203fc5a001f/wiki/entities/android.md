# Android (product)

- **Overview**: Android is a mobile operating system developed by Google, powering a wide variety of smartphones and tablets.
- **Relevance to session‑03_2025‑07‑21**: In the sleep‑hygiene advice, Android devices were highlighted for their evening screen‑time settings.
- **Recommended wind‑down settings**:
  - **Brightness**: set to roughly the lower‑quarter of the slider when the room is dim.
  - **Night Light**: enable and push the intensity slider to the *maximum* warmth (deep amber).
  - **Additional color‑temperature**: if available, increase the warmth further under display settings.
  - **Dark mode**: turn on to reduce bright UI elements.
- **Why it matters**: These adjustments help mitigate the impact of screen exposure on sleep by lowering blue‑light emission and visual contrast, supporting the concepts of [[concepts/screen-time-and-sleep]] and [[concepts/blue-light-and-circadian-rhythm]].
- **Complementary tools**: Using amber blue‑light‑blocking glasses ([[entities/amber-blue-light-blocking-glasses]]) can provide a modest extra reduction, especially if the device’s night‑light cannot be made warm enough.
- **Comparison**: Similar recommendations apply to other platforms such as [[entities/iphone]], [[entities/macos]], and [[entities/windows]], but Android’s Night Light feature offers a straightforward way to achieve the warmest setting.
