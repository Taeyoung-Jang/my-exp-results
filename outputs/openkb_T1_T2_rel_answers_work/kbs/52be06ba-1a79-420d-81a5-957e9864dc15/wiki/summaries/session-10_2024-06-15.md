## Overview
The assistant explains how to determine a realistic “social rhythm” for meeting friends, emphasizing personal energy patterns and the need for a flexible friendship cadence.

## Determining a Healthy Cadence
- Monitor your own energy: dread → slow down; feeling disconnected → reach out. [[concepts/energy-awareness]]
- Aim for regular but low‑pressure touchpoints (quick messages, photo shares, reactions). 
- Keep in‑person meetings at a frequency that feels sustainable (e.g., twice a month). [[concepts/friendship-cadence]]

## Maintaining Warmth Between Meet‑ups
- Use brief, context‑free messages ("this made me think of you", "hope your week’s okay").
- Allow conversations to pause and resume naturally.
- Be fully present during the actual meet‑up to reinforce connection.

## Communicating Boundaries
- Frame limits as personal energy needs rather than a judgment of the other person. [[concepts/boundary-framing]]
- Pair the boundary with reassurance ("I still value our time").
- Offer alternative low‑effort ways to stay connected.

## Text Templates
**Close friend**
```
Ah I wish I had the energy for more right now, but I’m keeping my in‑person plans to every couple of weeks so I don’t overstretch myself. I’m definitely up for something soon though—how about the week of [date]?
```

**Newer friend**
```
I’ve been trying to be careful with my schedule, so I usually only make plans every couple of weeks. I’d still love to meet up, just at a slower pace. Would [date] work for you?
```

**When pushed after a boundary**
```
I totally get wanting to hang out more, and I really appreciate it. I just have to stick to the every‑couple‑weeks rhythm so I don’t burn out. It’s nothing personal—I just know my limits. I’m still glad to plan something for [date], if that works.
```
These templates keep the tone warm, clear, and non‑apologetic while reinforcing the desired cadence.

## Takeaway
A sustainable friendship rhythm balances personal energy, occasional low‑effort contact, and clear, compassionate communication of boundaries.
