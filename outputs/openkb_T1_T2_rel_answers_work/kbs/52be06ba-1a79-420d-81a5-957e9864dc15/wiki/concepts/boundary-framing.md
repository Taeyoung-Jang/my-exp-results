# Boundary Framing

**Definition**: Boundary framing is the communication technique of presenting personal limits as a matter of one’s own energy or schedule rather than a judgment of the other person, thereby keeping the tone warm and collaborative.

## Core Elements
1. **Self‑focused language** – Phrase the limit in terms of your own needs (e.g., “I’ve been paying attention to my schedule” or “I do best keeping plans every couple of weeks”).
2. **Positive framing** – Emphasize appreciation for the relationship and the desire to stay connected.
3. **Reassurance** – Clearly state that the boundary is not personal (e.g., “It’s nothing personal—I just need downtime”).
4. **Alternative touchpoints** – Offer low‑effort ways to stay in touch between meetings (quick messages, photo shares).

- Reduces the risk of friends perceiving the limit as rejection.
- Encourages mutual respect for personal downtime.
- Maintains relationship warmth while preventing burnout.
