# Energy Awareness

**Definition**: Energy awareness is the practice of noticing and interpreting one's own physical and emotional stamina to decide how often to engage in social activities.

## Key Elements
- **Self‑monitoring**: Pay attention to signs like dread, fatigue, or excitement when planning meet‑ups. These signals indicate whether to slow down or reach out more often. 
- **Signal Interpretation**: 
  - *Dreading plans* → consider reducing frequency. 
  - *Feeling disconnected* → consider increasing contact.
- **Application**: Use energy awareness to set a realistic [[concepts/friendship-cadence]] that respects personal limits while keeping friendships warm.
