# Hub Checking + Autopay

**Definition**: A low‑maintenance personal‑finance system that uses a single checking account as the financial hub, routing all income into it and paying all recurring expenses through automated transfers.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Single hub checking account** – All income is deposited here; all outgoing payments originate from this account.
2. **Autopay for fixed bills** – Set up automatic payments for utilities, rent/mortgage, subscriptions, etc.  Ideally, pay these bills to a credit‑card that itself has autopay to the hub, consolidating due dates into one predictable outflow.
3. **Credit‑card autopay** – Configure autopay to pull the **statement balance** (not the full current balance) so the payment amount is known each month.  See [[concepts/statement-balance-autopay]].
4. **Buffer floor** – Keep a minimum safety‑net in the checking account (a few hundred dollars) to prevent overdrafts.  This is the [[concepts/buffer-floor-checking]] concept.
5. **Due‑date alignment** – When possible, move credit‑card due dates into the same week each month to make cash‑flow timing predictable.  This falls under general [[concepts/credit-card-terms]].
6. **Regular “money reset”** – A brief (10‑15 min) weekly or monthly review to verify the buffer is intact, confirm upcoming autopay dates, and adjust if needed.

---

## Benefits

* **Reduced manual tracking** – Most payments happen automatically; only a short periodic check‑in is required.
* **Overdraft protection** – The buffer floor prevents unexpected shortfalls.
* **Predictable cash flow** – Aligning due dates and using statement‑balance autopay yields a stable, known outflow each month.
* **Scalable** – Works for irregular incomes; the buffer can grow in good months and simply be preserved in lean months.

---

## Implementation Steps (as described in [[summaries/session-39_2025-04-16]])

1. Open a dedicated checking account to serve as the hub.
2. Direct all paychecks, transfers, and other income into this hub.
3. Identify all recurring fixed expenses and set them on autopay, preferably via a credit‑card that also autopays to the hub.
4. For each credit‑card, enable autopay of the **statement balance** only.
5. Establish a buffer floor (e.g., $200‑$300) that you never let the balance drop below.
6. If the card issuer allows, shift due dates so they fall in the same week.
7. Schedule a short monthly “money reset” to verify the buffer, review upcoming autopay dates, and adjust any misalignments.
