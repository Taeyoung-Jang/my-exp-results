# Credit Card Terms and Disclosures

Credit card terms and disclosures are the collection of legal and informational documents that detail the costs, benefits, and rules governing a credit card. Understanding these disclosures is essential for comparing cards and avoiding hidden fees.

## Core Elements
- **Interest rates** – Regular APR, introductory APR, and the **penalty APR** that may be triggered by late payments or other defaults. See [[concepts/penalty-apr]].
- **Fees** – Annual fee, foreign transaction fee, balance‑transfer fee, cash‑advance fee, and late‑payment fee.
- **Grace period** – The time window in which a statement balance can be paid in full without incurring interest.
- **Reward structure** – How points, miles, or cash‑back are earned, any caps per billing cycle, and expiration rules. See [[concepts/reward-structure]].
- **Schumer Box** – A standardized table, usually near the bottom of the application page or on page 1 of the PDF, that summarizes interest rates and fees.
- **Cardmember Agreement / Terms & Conditions** – The full legal contract linked beneath the Schumer Box that expands on all fees, APR triggers, dispute processes, and reward program rules.

## How to Locate the Information
1. **Schumer Box** – Look for a table titled *Interest Rates and Interest Charges* on the application page; it lists regular APR, penalty APR, grace period, and transaction fees.
2. **Cardmember Agreement** – Click the link directly under the Schumer Box (often labeled *Pricing & Terms*, *Terms and Conditions*, or *Cardmember Agreement*). This PDF contains detailed language on penalty‑APR triggers, fee calculations, and other obligations.
3. **Rewards Program Rules** – Usually a separate link next to the agreement; it explains earning rates, caps, expiration, and category changes.

## Quick Checklist
- Regular APR after any intro period
- Penalty APR triggers and duration
- Grace period length
- Annual fee amount and waiver conditions
- Foreign transaction fee percentage
- Balance‑transfer fee (percentage or flat fee)
- Cash‑advance fee and APR
- Reward expiration policy
- Reward caps per billing cycle or category
- Late‑payment fee amount

By systematically reviewing these sections, consumers can compare cards on a level playing field and avoid common pitfalls such as unexpected interest charges or restrictive reward terms.
