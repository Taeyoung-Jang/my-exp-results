# Reward Structure (Expiration, Caps, Categories)

Reward structure refers to the rules that govern how credit‑card rewards are earned, limited, and expire. Understanding these elements helps you choose a card that truly matches your spending habits and avoid losing value.

## Key Elements

| Element | What to Look For | Why It Matters |
|---------|------------------|----------------|
| **Expiration** | Date or activity requirement after which points/cash‑back become void. | Prevents loss of earned value if you don’t use or redeem rewards promptly. |
| **Caps** | Maximum points or cash‑back you can earn in a billing cycle, month, or year for specific categories. | Helps you gauge realistic earnings and avoid over‑estimating rewards. |
| **Categories** | Spend categories (e.g., groceries, travel, dining) that earn higher rates, and any quarterly or annual rotation of those categories. | Aligns the card’s bonus structure with your actual spending patterns. |

## How These Details Appear in Disclosures

- **Rewards Program Rules** – Usually a separate link near the Schumer Box on the application page. Look for sections titled *Earning Points*, *Earning Restrictions*, *Category Limitations*, or *Expiration*.
- **Keywords to Scan For** – “expire”, “maximum”, “per billing cycle”, “up to”, “cap”, “category”, “quarterly”, “annual”. These cues indicate where expiration dates, caps, and category limits are described.

## Practical Checklist (derived from [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]])
1. Locate the *Rewards Program Rules* PDF linked on the card’s page.
2. Search for the terms above to find expiration dates and any activity‑based requirements.
3. Identify any caps: look for phrasing such as “up to X points per billing cycle” or “maximum Y% cash back per year.”
4. Review category tables to see which spend types earn bonus rates and whether they rotate.
5. Confirm whether the issuer can change categories or caps with notice – this is often in the *Credit Card Terms* section.

## Interaction with Other Credit‑Card Concepts
- Reward structure is a subset of broader [[concepts/credit-card-terms]] that also include APR, fees, and penalty mechanisms.
- While not a penalty, the *penalty‑APR* concept interacts when you carry a balance; a high‑APR can offset reward value if you don’t pay in full.

By carefully reviewing the reward structure, you can ensure that the card’s benefits outweigh any fees and that you’ll actually capture the value you expect.
