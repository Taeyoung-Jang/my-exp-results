## Summary of Session 10 (2024‑06‑15)

This conversation walks through **how to compare credit cards** and provides a practical checklist for spotting important terms in the *Schumer Box* and the *Cardmember Agreement*.

### Core considerations
- **APR vs. rewards vs. fees** – If you usually pay the balance in full, prioritize a rewards card; still ensure the regular APR isn’t excessive for occasional balances. See [[concepts/credit-card-terms]] for details on evaluating APR, rewards, and fees.
- **Annual fees & foreign transaction fees** – Weigh them against expected reward earnings.  
- **Penalty APR triggers** – Late payment, missed payment, or default can switch you to a higher rate. Look for language like “We may apply this rate if …”. See [[concepts/penalty-apr]].
- **Grace period** – The window to pay the statement balance without incurring interest; confirm its length.  
- **Reward structure** – Check for expiration dates, caps per billing cycle, and category changes. See [[concepts/reward-structure]].
- **Balance transfer & cash‑advance fees** – Usually a percentage of the transferred amount or cash advance; factor these into cost calculations.

### Where to find the details
- **Schumer Box** – A table near the bottom of the application page (often on page 1 of the PDF) titled *Interest Rates and Interest Charges*. It lists regular APR, penalty APR, grace period, and transaction fees.  
- **Cardmember Agreement / Terms & Conditions** – Linked directly below the Schumer Box; contains full legal language on fees, penalties, and dispute processes.  
- **Rewards Program Rules** – Separate link, usually adjacent to the agreement; outlines earning rates, point expiration, caps, and category changes.

All of the above items fall under the broader topic of [[concepts/credit-card-terms]].

### Quick checklist for card disclosures
1. **Regular APR** – after any intro period.  
2. **Penalty APR** – triggers and how long it lasts. (see [[concepts/penalty-apr]])  
3. **Grace period** – length and any conditions.  
4. **Annual fee** – amount and whether waived.  
5. **Foreign transaction fee** – % per purchase.  
6. **Balance transfer fee** – flat fee or %.  
7. **Cash advance fee** – % + potential higher APR.  
8. **Reward expiration** – dates or activity requirements. (part of [[concepts/reward-structure]])  
9. **Reward caps** – per billing cycle, category limits. (part of [[concepts/reward-structure]])  
10. **Late payment fee** – amount and frequency.  

### Tips for locating the information
- Search PDFs for keywords: “Penalty APR”, “Grace period”, “When it applies”, “Maximum”, “per billing cycle”, “Expiration”.  
- In the Schumer Box, look for rows labeled *APR – Purchase*, *APR – Balance Transfer*, *APR – Cash Advance*.  
- In the rewards rules, focus on sections titled *Earning Points*, *Earning Restrictions*, *Category Limitations*, and *Expiration*.

By following this checklist and scrutinizing the Schumer Box and Cardmember Agreement, you can avoid hidden costs and select a card that aligns with your spending habits and credit‑card usage patterns. [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]
