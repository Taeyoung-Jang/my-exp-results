# Adobe Inc.

Adobe Inc. is a multinational software company best known for its Creative Cloud suite of applications (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, etc.).

## Relevance to Session 37 (2024‑12‑01)

In the **[[summaries/session-37_2024-12-01]]** checklist, Adobe is mentioned as one of the *machine‑tied* applications that should be deactivated before performing a fresh OS reinstall. Deactivating Adobe licenses prevents activation conflicts on the newly built system and preserves the validity of your subscription.

## Key Actions Highlighted
- **Deactivate before reset**: Open each Adobe app, go to *Help → Sign Out* or use the Adobe Creative Cloud desktop app to *Deactivate* the device.
- **Track license details**: Note your Adobe subscription ID and any offline activation files. This aligns with the broader practice of [[concepts/license-tracking]] during a system clean‑up.
- **Re‑activate after rebuild**: Once the OS is reinstalled, sign back into Adobe Creative Cloud to re‑license the apps on the fresh installation.
