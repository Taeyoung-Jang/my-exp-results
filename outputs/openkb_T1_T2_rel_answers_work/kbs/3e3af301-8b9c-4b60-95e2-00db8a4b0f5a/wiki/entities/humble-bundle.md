## Humble Bundle

**Type:** Organization

**Overview**: Humble Bundle is an online platform that sells bundles of digital games and software, often at discounted prices. It provides customers with license keys and receipts that can be managed using a software inventory system.

**Key facts from the document**:
- Listed as the **Purchase Source** for the *RetroSound Pack* in the software inventory template provided in [[summaries/session-08_2024-03-31]].
- The document recommends tracking the receipt and license information obtained from Humble Bundle alongside other software assets.

**Related concepts**:
- [[concepts/software-inventory-management]] – organizing software purchases, installers, and licenses.
- [[concepts/license-tracking]] – managing keys and receipts from sources like Humble Bundle.
- [[concepts/cloud-storage-organization]] – storing Humble Bundle receipts and installers in a cloud‑based folder structure.

**Related entities**:
- [[entities/retro-sound-pack]] – a product acquired through Humble Bundle.

**Usage tip**: When adding a Humble Bundle purchase to your software index, record the source as "Humble Bundle" and store the corresponding receipt and any license keys in a dedicated sub‑folder under your cloud storage hierarchy.
