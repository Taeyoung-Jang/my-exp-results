## Summary of session-33_2024-06-15

**Date:** 2024-06-15 10:00

The conversation provides a step‑by‑step playbook for resolving a medical billing/collections issue while protecting legal rights and credit standing.

### Core Workflow
1. **Start with the hospital billing department** – request an itemized statement, written confirmation of account ownership, and ask if the debt can be pulled from collections.
2. **If the hospital confirms the collector owns the debt**, contact the collection agency using a neutral script and a written **debt‑validation request**.
3. **Validate insurance coverage** – after receiving the itemized bill, query the insurer to confirm claim submission and request re‑processing if needed.
4. **Maintain a paper trail** – keep all communications in writing, log dates/times, and avoid verbal admissions.

### Debt‑Validation Letter (copy‑paste template)
```
[Your Name]
[Address]
[Email]
[Date]

[Collector Name]
[Collector Address]

Hello,
I recently learned you are reporting a collection account under my name. I am requesting written validation of this account so I can understand what it is and confirm its accuracy. Please mark the account as disputed and provide verification before any further collection efforts. Other than sending the requested verification, please limit contact to written communication. This request is not an admission of liability.

Please provide:
- Original creditor name and account number
- Full itemized statement and charge breakdown
- Dates of service / transaction dates
- Documentation of your authority to collect (assignment, placement agreement, etc.)
- Date of last payment or activity reported
- Any other supporting documents

Send all responses in writing to the address above.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
```

### Documents to Request (bullet list)
- Original creditor name and account number
- Full itemized statement of charges
- Dates of service / dates of transaction
- Proof of collector’s authority (assignment, placement agreement, power of attorney)
- Date of last payment or activity on the account
- Any supporting documentation the collector relies on

### Communication Do‑Not‑Say List
- Do **not** admit responsibility or say you owe the debt.
- Do **not** discuss payment plans, amounts, or timelines.
- Do **not** make partial payments or promises to pay.
- Keep statements short, factual, and neutral.

### Statute of Limitations vs. Credit Reporting
- **Statute of limitations** restarts only with a payment, written promise to pay, or admission of liability.
- Simple disputes, validation requests, or asking questions **do not** restart the clock.
- **Credit‑reporting clock** is based on the original delinquency date and continues for seven years regardless of your actions.

### Next Steps When Validation Is Incomplete
1. Send a **follow‑up validation letter** asking for the missing items (itemized statement, authority proof).
2. File a **dispute with credit bureaus** stating the collector has not provided adequate documentation.
3. Keep all correspondence documented.
