# Down‑Payment Savings

**Definition**: A focused savings strategy aimed at accumulating the cash needed for the down‑payment on a home purchase, typically over a multi‑year horizon.

## Core Elements
- **Time horizon**: Usually 2–5 years, aligning with the buyer’s planned purchase window.
- **Target amount**: Varies by market, but commonly 10‑20 % of the expected home price.
- **Funding source**: Surplus income after essential expenses, minimum debt payments, and a minimal safety buffer.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/budgeting]] to identify the monthly surplus that can be allocated.
- Requires a minimal [[concepts/emergency-fund]] (e.g., a 2‑week to 1‑month buffer) before directing larger portions toward the down‑payment.
- Prioritizes eliminating [[concepts/high-interest-debt-repayment]] (such as [[entities/credit-card-debt]]) because high‑interest liabilities erode saving power.

## Practical Playbook (from [[summaries/session-07_2023-05-14]])
1. **Identify surplus**: $1,200 per month after bills and minimum debt payments.
2. **Initial allocation**:
   - $700 to high‑interest credit‑card debt (19 % APR).
   - $300 to the down‑payment fund (the core of this concept).
   - $200 to build a tiny emergency buffer.
3. **Milestone adjustments**:
   - Once a 2‑week buffer is achieved, shift the $200 emergency contribution to the down‑payment fund, increasing it to $500.
   - After a full 1‑month buffer, maintain the split until the credit‑card debt is cleared.
   - When the credit‑card is paid off, redirect the $700 previously used for debt to the down‑payment fund, resulting in roughly $1,000 per month dedicated to [[entities/home-down-payment]].
4. **Outcome**: Assuming the above cadence, the down‑payment fund can grow to approximately $35‑$40 k over three years, providing a strong position for a home purchase.

## Key Takeaways
- **Stability first**: Secure a modest emergency fund before scaling contributions.
- **Debt before savings**: Pay off high‑interest debt to avoid interest that outpaces typical investment returns.
- **Allocate by percentage**: Simple, adjustable splits keep the plan manageable.
- **Track milestones**: Re‑allocate funds as each safety or debt milestone is reached, accelerating the down‑payment accumulation.
