# Warm‑up Destination Selection

**Definition**: The process of picking an initial, short‑term location for a multi‑month trip that minimizes surprise costs, eases logistical complexity, and provides a gentle “warm‑up” to long‑term travel.

---

## Why it matters
- Reduces the chance of unexpected expenses that could jeopardize a larger savings plan.
- Provides a predictable baseline for budgeting before tackling more expensive or logistically complex destinations.
- Helps build travel routines (housing, transport, health insurance) that can be replicated later.

---

## Core criteria (derived from [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-11]])
1. **Visa simplicity** – Prefer visa‑free entry or inexpensive short‑term tourist permits.
2. **Housing deposit predictability** – Look for rentals that require a modest upfront deposit + first month’s rent, with clear refund policies.
3. **One‑time fees** – Identify common extra costs such as cleaning fees, tourist taxes, SIM‑card setup, or local registration fees.
4. **Transport stability** – Reliable, inexpensive public transit or ride‑share options that are easy for newcomers.
5. **Health‑care cost transparency** – Access to affordable medical care and a clear price range for basic visits; factor in travel health insurance premiums.

---

## Application example
In the session‑33 conversation the user evaluated three candidates:
- **Portugal** – higher deposits ($1,000‑$1,500) and tourist taxes; visa‑free but more hidden fees.
- **Mexico City** – moderate deposits ($300‑$700) and low one‑time fees; visa‑free.
- **Malaysia** – smallest deposits ($150‑$350), no visa fee, low medical costs.

Using the criteria above, **Malaysia** emerged as the lowest‑risk warm‑up destination.

## Practical steps
1. List potential destinations.
2. Score each against the five core criteria.
3. Sum the scores; prioritize the highest‑scoring location.
4. Verify current visa policies and any seasonal price fluctuations.
5. Allocate a modest budget for the identified surprise‑cost ranges before committing.
