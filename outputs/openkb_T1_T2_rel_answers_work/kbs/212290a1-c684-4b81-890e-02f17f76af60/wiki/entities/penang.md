# Penang

Penang is a coastal state and city in **Malaysia** that is frequently considered as a low‑risk "warm‑up" destination for long‑term travelers.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-11]]
- **Visa**: Visa‑free entry for most nationalities, so no visa fee applies.
- **Housing deposit**: Typically one to two weeks’ rent, amounting to **$150‑$350** USD, paid **in addition** to the first month’s rent.
- **Cleaning fee**: Usually **$20‑$40** USD as a one‑off charge.
- **Travel health insurance**: Basic policies cost about **$35‑$70** USD per month.
- **Medical visit cost**: Out‑of‑pocket expenses for a basic clinic visit range from **$15‑$40** USD.
- **Other one‑time fees**: Minimal, such as a cheap SIM card (under $10 USD) and no tourist taxes.

## Why it matters for budgeting
Penang scores well on [[concepts/surprise-travel-costs]] and [[concepts/warm-up-destination-selection]] because its upfront costs are modest and predictable. Travelers can allocate a larger portion of their flexible income to savings or discretionary spending rather than covering unexpected expenses.
