# Mexico (Place)

Mexico is a large North American country that frequently appears as a candidate for a **warm‑up destination** when planning long‑term travel.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-11]]
- **Visa/Entry:** Visa‑free for short tourist stays (no visa fee).
- **Housing deposits:** Typically a partial‑month deposit ranging from **$300 – $700** in addition to the first month’s rent.
- **Common one‑time fees:** Cleaning fees of **$15 – $40**, SIM‑card purchase usually under **$15**, and occasional tourist taxes that are modest.
- **Travel health insurance:** Estimated **$45 – $80 per month** for a basic policy covering Mexico.
- **Medical visit cost:** Out‑of‑pocket expenses for a basic private clinic visit are roughly **$25 – $60**.
- **Surprise‑cost profile:** Considered low compared to Portugal and Malaysia, making it a relatively safe choice for minimizing unexpected expenses.
