# Credit Card Debt

Credit Card Debt refers to the $6,000 balance carried on a credit card with a 19 % APR mentioned in [[summaries/session-07_2023-05-14]]. It is treated as **high‑interest debt** and is the primary focus of the repayment strategy.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Balance:** $6,000
- **Interest rate:** 19 % APR (high‑interest)
- **Monthly allocation:** $700 of the $1,200 surplus is directed toward paying down this debt (≈58 % of surplus).
- **Payoff horizon:** Roughly 10 months when paying $700 per month, accounting for interest.
- **Priority:** First priority in the [[concepts/high-interest-debt-repayment]] hierarchy, ahead of building the [[concepts/emergency-fund]] and saving for a [[concepts/down-payment-savings]] goal.
- **Interaction with other obligations:** Student loan debt remains on minimum payments while the focus stays on this credit card balance.

- [[concepts/down-payment-savings]] – the long‑term goal that receives funding after the debt is cleared.
