# Home Down‑Payment Goal

The **Home Down‑Payment Goal** is a near‑term (2–5 year) savings objective to accumulate funds for a house purchase. It is the primary focus of the financial playbook in [[summaries/session-07_2023-05-14]].

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Time horizon:** approximately 3 years.
- **Monthly surplus available:** $1,200 after bills and minimum debt payments.
- **Initial allocation:**
  - $700 toward the high‑interest credit‑card debt (19 % APR) – see [[entities/credit-card-debt]].
  - $300 toward the down‑payment fund.
  - $200 toward building an emergency buffer – see [[concepts/emergency-fund]].
- **Milestone adjustments:**
  1. After reaching a 2‑week emergency buffer, the $200 is re‑directed to the down‑payment fund, changing the split to $700 (debt) / $500 (down‑payment).
  2. Once a full 1‑month buffer is in place, the split remains until the credit‑card is paid off.
  3. When the credit‑card is cleared (≈10 months), the $700 previously allocated to debt joins the down‑payment contributions, yielding roughly $1,000/month toward the goal while continuing a modest $200 emergency fund build‑up.
- **Projected outcome:** Assuming the staged contributions continue, the down‑payment fund is expected to reach roughly **$35 k–$40 k** by the end of the three‑year period.
