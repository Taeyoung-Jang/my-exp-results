# Porto

Porto is a historic coastal city in northern **Portugal** known for its picturesque riverfront, wine cellars, and relatively affordable short‑term rentals. In the context of the *session‑33* conversation it was evaluated as a possible "warm‑up" destination for a multi‑month travel plan.

## Key facts from the discussion
- **Country:** Portugal (see [[entities/portugal]])
- **Travel purpose:** Suggested as a low‑risk first stop to test long‑term travel routines.
- **Typical housing deposit:** $1,000 – $1,500, usually equivalent to one full month’s rent and refundable after the stay.
- **First‑month rent:** Paid in addition to the deposit; short‑term apartments often range from $800 – $1,200 per month.
- **Additional one‑time fees:** Cleaning fee $40 – $100, tourist tax (a few euros per night), SIM‑card purchase under $15.
- **Visa requirement:** Generally visa‑free for short tourist stays for most nationalities; no visa fee.
- **Local transport:** Public transit (metro, buses, trams) is inexpensive and well‑connected, supporting a predictable travel budget.
- **Health considerations:** Private clinic visits cost about $50 – $80; travel health insurance typically $50 – $90 per month.
