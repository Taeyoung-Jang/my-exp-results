# Student Loan Debt

**Entity:** Student loan debt (type: other)

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-07_2023-05-14]]**
- Amount: **$12,000**
- Interest rate: **5 % APR**
- Treated as a **low‑priority liability** compared to the high‑interest credit‑card balance.
- The plan keeps the loan on **minimum payments** while surplus funds are allocated to higher‑cost debt, an emergency fund, and a home down‑payment goal.

**Role in the financial plan**
- Integrated into the overall [[concepts/budgeting]] strategy as a fixed expense.
- Mentioned alongside the higher‑priority [[entities/credit-card-debt]] in the [[concepts/high-interest-debt-repayment]] discussion.
- Contributions to the [[concepts/down-payment-savings]] goal proceed after the credit‑card is cleared; the student loan does not hinder that timeline.
- The emergency cushion built via [[concepts/emergency-fund]] helps ensure the borrower can continue making loan payments even if income fluctuates.
