# Kuala Lumpur (place)

Kuala Lumpur is the bustling capital of Malaysia, known for its modern skyline, cultural diversity, and relatively low cost of living. In the context of **session‑33_2024‑11‑11**, it is presented as one of three candidate "warm‑up" destinations for a long‑term traveler planning a year‑long round‑the‑world program.

## Why Kuala Lumpur is recommended
- **Visa‑free entry** for most nationalities, meaning no upfront visa fee.
- **Housing deposits** are modest, typically **$150‑$350** (equivalent to 1‑2 weeks’ rent) in addition to the first month’s rent.
- **One‑time fees** are minimal: cleaning fees $20‑$40, SIM‑card cost under $10, and no tourist taxes.
- **Transport** is inexpensive and reliable, contributing to predictable monthly expenses.
- **Medical care** is inexpensive; a basic clinic visit costs **$15‑$40** and travel health insurance runs **$35‑$70 per month**.

These factors make Kuala Lumpur the **lowest‑risk** option among the three destinations evaluated, aligning with the recommendation in the document.
