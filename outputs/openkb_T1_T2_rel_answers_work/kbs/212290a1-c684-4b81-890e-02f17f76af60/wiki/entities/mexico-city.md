# Mexico City (CDMX)

Mexico City, often referred to as CDMX, is the bustling capital of Mexico. In the context of a long‑term travel plan, it was evaluated as a potential “warm‑up” destination in [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-11]].

## Key facts from the session
- **Visa entry**: Visa‑free for most short‑term travelers; no entry fee.
- **Housing deposits**: Typically a partial‑month deposit ranging from **$300 – $700**, paid in addition to the first month’s rent.
- **One‑time fees**: Common cleaning fees of **$15 – $40**; SIM card purchase generally under **$15**.
- **Local transport**: Cheap and predictable, covered under general travel budgeting concepts.
- **Travel health insurance**: Estimated **$45 – $80 per month** for a basic policy.
- **Medical visit cost**: Private clinic visits usually cost **$25 – $60** out‑of‑pocket.
