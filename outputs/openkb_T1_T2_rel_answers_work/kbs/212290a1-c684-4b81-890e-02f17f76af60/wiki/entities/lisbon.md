# Lisbon (Place)

Lisbon is the capital city of **Portugal** and was highlighted in the *session-33_2024-11-11* discussion as a possible "warm‑up" destination for a multi‑month trip.

## Key facts from the session
- **Visa:** Short‑term stays are visa‑free for most travelers; no entry fee.
- **Housing deposits:** Typically require a deposit equal to one full month’s rent, ranging from **$1,000 – $1,500** in addition to the first month’s rent.
- **Additional one‑time fees:**
  - Cleaning fees: **$40 – $100**.
  - Tourist taxes: a small per‑night levy (a few euros per night).
  - SIM‑card setup: usually under **$15**.
- **Travel health insurance:** Estimated **$50 – $90** per month for basic coverage.
- **Medical visit costs:** Private clinic visits typically cost **$50 – $80** out‑of‑pocket.

## Relevance to budgeting concepts
- The deposit and fee structure ties into [[concepts/surprise-travel-costs]], emphasizing the need to account for upfront, unpredictable expenses when planning a stay.
- Selecting Lisbon as a first destination aligns with [[concepts/warm-up-destination-selection]], which advises choosing a place that balances affordability, ease of navigation, and predictable costs.
- The financial figures contribute to a broader [[concepts/budgeting]] approach for long‑term travel, especially when comparing against other options like Mexico City or Kuala Lumpur.
