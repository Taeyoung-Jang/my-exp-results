## Malaysia

**Type:** Place (Southeast Asia)

Malaysia is highlighted in [[summaries/session-33_2024-11-11]] as a recommended “warm‑up” destination for long‑term travel. It offers:

- **Visa entry:** Visa‑free for most short‑term tourists, so no entry fee.
- **Housing deposits:** Short‑term rentals in Kuala Lumpur or Penang usually require a deposit of $150‑$350 (one to two weeks’ rent) in addition to the first month’s rent.
- **One‑time fees:** Minimal cleaning fees ($20‑$40) and cheap SIM cards (under $10). No tourist taxes are commonly applied.
- **Local transport:** Predictable and inexpensive public transit.
- **Health costs:** Basic travel health insurance averages $35‑$70 per month; clinic visits typically cost $15‑$40 out‑of‑pocket.
- **Risk profile:** Lowest among the compared options, making it the least likely to generate surprise expenses.

These attributes align with the [[concepts/warm-up-destination-selection]] criteria and demonstrate low [[concepts/surprise-travel-costs]] risk.
