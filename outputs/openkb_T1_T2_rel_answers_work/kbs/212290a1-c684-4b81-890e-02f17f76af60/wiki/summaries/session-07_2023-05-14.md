# Summary of Session 7 (2023‑05‑14)

**Main contribution:** a concrete month‑to‑month *financial planning playbook* that orders priorities, sets target buffers, and proposes allocation percentages for a surplus of $1,200.

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/budgeting]]** – establishing a surplus after fixed expenses.  
- **[[concepts/emergency-fund]]** – two milestones: a 2‑week buffer then a 1‑month buffer.  
- **[[concepts/high-interest-debt-repayment]]** – credit‑card debt at 19 % APR is the top priority.  
- **[[concepts/down-payment-savings]]** – the 2‑5‑year goal of buying a home.  
- **Debt repayment strategy** – allocate the bulk of surplus to high‑interest debt while feeding the safety net and goal.

## Recommended Allocation (Current Stage)
| Surplus | [[entities/credit-card-debt]] (19 % APR) | Down‑payment fund | Emergency fund |
|---------|------------------------------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| $1,200  | **$700** (≈58 %)                         | **$300** (≈25 %)  | **$200** (≈17 %) |

- **Rationale:** fast reduction of debt (most costly) while still building a tiny safety net and contributing to the home goal.

## Milestone‑Based Adjustments
1. **2‑week emergency buffer reached** – redirect the $200 emergency contribution to the down‑payment fund (now $700 to debt, $500 to home).  
2. **1‑month emergency buffer reached** – keep the same split until the credit‑card is cleared; the emergency fund stays steady.  
3. **Credit‑card paid off** – shift the $700 previously earmarked for debt to the down‑payment fund. New split: **$1,000** to home, **$200** to continue building the emergency fund; [[entities/student-loan-debt]] stays at minimum payments.

## Timeline Estimates
- **Credit‑card payoff:** ~10 months (paying $700/mo at 19 % APR).  
- **Down‑payment fund by 3 years:** roughly $35‑$40 k, assuming the staged contributions above and no major income/expense changes.

## Practical Takeaways
- Prioritize *stability* (buffer) before scaling up savings, but don’t wait for a full month buffer if a shorter cushion suffices.  
- Treat high‑interest debt as a *must‑do*; its elimination accelerates all other goals.  
- Use clear percentage splits to keep the plan simple and adjustable as milestones are hit.  
- Keep the student loan at minimum while focusing on higher‑cost liabilities and the home goal.
