# Summary of session-36 (2025‑06‑15)

## Core Physical Decluttering Approach
- **Loop mindset** – treat decluttering as a repeating cycle: **notice → choose → reset** [[concepts/decluttering_loop]].
- Work through **small categories** (e.g., jackets, mugs) one at a time, handling each item physically and deciding its fate.
- Keep only items that are used regularly or genuinely improve life; everything else is donated or given away.
- Assign each retained item a **clear, permanent home**; items without a home signal that they belong in the discard pile.
- Perform **tiny daily resets** (2‑3 min sweeps) to prevent re‑accumulation.

## "One In, One Gone Before It Enters" Rule
- The rule is refined per category to make decisions explicit [[concepts/one_in_one_out_rule]].
  - **Clothes** – a new piece must replace a specific existing piece before entry.
  - **Kitchen gadgets** – new tool replaces a redundant or single‑purpose gadget.
  - **Books / Paper** – new arrival must swap for a similar book or be read/passed on within a set window.
  - **Toiletries** – maintain at most one open product and one backup; a new item enters only when the old one is finished.
- If no existing item can be swapped, the new item stays out, preserving space.

## Digital Photo Inventory System
- **Capture**: Use [[entities/iphone]] Photos solely for snapping items.
- **Master storage**: [[entities/google_drive]] folder **Home Inventory** acts as the single source of truth [[concepts/digital_inventory_management]].
- **Folder structure**:
  - `Home Inventory/Clothes`
  - `Home Inventory/Kitchen`
  - `Home Inventory/Books`
  - `Home Inventory/Everything‑Else`
- **Naming convention**: `<category>_<short‑descriptor>_<MM‑YY>.jpg` (e.g., `jacket_black_puffer_03‑26.jpg`).
- **Workflow**:
  1. Weekly, move new photos from [[entities/iphone]] to the appropriate Drive sub‑folder.
  2. Delete the transferred images from the phone immediately.
  3. When an item is discarded, delete its photo from Drive right away.
  4. Once a month, run a quick duplicate/unused‑photo purge (under 10 min total).

## Weekly Inventory Review Checklist (<10 min) – plain‑text version
1. **Open [[entities/google_drive]] → Home Inventory** and verify that the folder contents match what you currently own.
2. **Delete** photos for any items you have recently let go.
3. **Add** any new item photos that were captured that week (if any).
4. **Clear** the iPhone camera roll of the transferred images.
5. Perform a **2‑minute physical sweep** of the living space to ensure everything is in its designated home.

## Outcomes
- Creates a sustainable, low‑effort habit loop for minimalism in ultra‑small spaces.
- Prevents digital clutter by consolidating photos into a single, well‑structured repository.
- Keeps the weekly maintenance time under ten minutes, reinforcing consistency.
