# Digital Inventory Management

**Definition**: A systematic approach to storing, naming, and maintaining photographs of physical belongings in a single, well‑structured digital repository so that the digital record mirrors the actual inventory without becoming its own source of clutter.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-15]])
- **Capture source** – Use a mobile device (e.g., iPhone) solely for snapping items; do not let the camera roll become a long‑term archive.
- **Master location** – Designate one cloud folder as the authoritative home, typically a Google Drive folder called **Home Inventory**.
- **Folder hierarchy** – Keep the structure minimal:
  - `Home Inventory/Clothes`
  - `Home Inventory/Kitchen`
  - `Home Inventory/Books`
  - `Home Inventory/Everything‑Else`
- **Naming convention** – `<category>_<short‑descriptor>_<MM‑YY>.jpg` (e.g., `jacket_black_puffer_03‑26.jpg`).
- **Weekly workflow**:
  1. Move the week’s new photos from the iPhone to the appropriate Drive sub‑folder.
  2. Delete the transferred images from the phone immediately.
  3. Verify that each photo corresponds to an item you still own; delete any that have been discarded.
- **Monthly housekeeping** – Run a quick duplicate/unused‑photo purge (typically under 10 minutes).

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Supports the [[concepts/decluttering_loop]] by providing a lightweight digital reference that reinforces the “choose → reset” cycle.
- Complements the [[concepts/one_in_one_out_rule]] by ensuring that every new item added to the physical space is also reflected in the digital inventory, making it easy to spot when the rule is being broken.
- Serves as a practical implementation of [[concepts/digital_inventory_management]] itself, illustrating how a minimal‑effort system can keep both physical and digital clutter in check.

## Benefits
- **Low maintenance** – The entire process can be completed in under ten minutes per week.
- **Single source of truth** – Prevents duplicate photo libraries across devices.
- **Visual audit** – Quickly verify what you own without rummaging through drawers.
- **Scalable** – Works for tiny apartments as well as larger homes.
