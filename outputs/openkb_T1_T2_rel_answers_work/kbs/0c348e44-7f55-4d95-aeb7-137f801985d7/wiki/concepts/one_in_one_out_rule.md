# One‑In‑One‑Out Rule

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A practical minimal‑ism habit that requires a new item to replace an existing one before it enters the home, helping maintain a constant, manageable inventory.

---

## Core Principle
The rule operates on the premise that **space is fixed**, so any addition must be balanced by a removal. It converts the abstract “own less” goal into a concrete decision point: *"Can I let go of something now?"* If the answer is yes, the new item is welcomed; if not, the new item stays out.

---

## Category‑Specific Applications (from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-15]])
| Category | How the rule is applied |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Clothes** | When you consider a new shirt, shoes, etc., you must first select a specific existing piece of the same type to donate or store elsewhere. |
| **Kitchen gadgets** | A new specialty tool (e.g., electric can opener) can only be added if a redundant or single‑purpose gadget is removed first. |
| **Books / Paper** | A new book must replace a similar title or be read and passed on within a set window; otherwise it stays out. |
| **Toiletries** | Keep at most one open product and one backup; a new bottle enters only when the current one is used up or discarded. |

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Forms part of a broader **[[concepts/decluttering_loop]]**: notice → choose → reset, where the “choose” step is embodied by the One‑In‑One‑Out decision.
- Works hand‑in‑hand with **[[concepts/digital_inventory_management]]**: as physical items are swapped, corresponding photos are updated in a single Google Drive folder, ensuring the digital record mirrors reality.

---

## Practical Workflow
1. **Capture** the intent to acquire a new item (e.g., notice a sale, receive a gift).
2. **Identify** an existing item of the same category that can be removed.
3. **Remove** the identified item (donate, give away, recycle) *before* the new item enters.
4. **Update** the digital inventory (photo upload, tag) to reflect the change.
5. **Reset** with a quick visual scan of the space to confirm all items have a clear home.

---

## Benefits
- **Predictable inventory size** – prevents gradual creep of possessions.
- **Decision clarity** – reduces decision fatigue by providing a simple, repeatable rule.
- **Digital sync** – ensures that the physical declutter loop stays aligned with the digital photo archive.
