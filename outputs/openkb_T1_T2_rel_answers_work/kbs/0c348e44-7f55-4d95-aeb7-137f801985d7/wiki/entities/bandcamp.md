# Bandcamp (product)

## Overview
Bandcamp is a digital marketplace that lets artists sell music and other media directly to fans. Purchases are typically **DRM‑free**, allowing buyers to keep permanent copies of the files.

## Mention in [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]
In the discussion about balancing convenience with true ownership, Bandcamp is highlighted as a source of DRM‑free digital downloads that complement physical media collections. It is presented as a trustworthy option for acquiring music without the licensing restrictions common to many mainstream platforms.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/drm_free_downloads]] – Bandcamp exemplifies the value of DRM‑free purchases.
- [[concepts/convenience_vs_ownership]] – It offers a convenient digital acquisition method while preserving ownership rights.

## Connections
Bandcamp is part of the broader conversation on media ownership, alongside physical formats like Blu‑ray and CD, and software tools such as HandBrake and Plex that facilitate personal media libraries.
