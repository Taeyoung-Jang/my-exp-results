# Plex

**Type:** Product

**Description:** Plex is a media server platform that organizes and streams personal video, music, and photo collections to a wide range of devices.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]**

- Recommended as the primary media server for serving ripped Blu‑ray and CD collections on a home network, enabling streaming to TVs, smartphones, and tablets.
- Works well with hardware such as [[entities/intel_nuc]] or a NAS, paired with an external Blu‑ray drive for disc ripping.
- Complements disc‑ripping tools like [[entities/makemkv]] and encoding software such as [[entities/handbrake]].
- Provides a convenient way to access owned media, tying into the [[concepts/convenience_vs_ownership]] discussion.
- Mentioned alongside [[entities/jellyfin]] as an alternative media‑server option.
- Supports the overall workflow of balancing ownership with digital convenience as described in the session.
