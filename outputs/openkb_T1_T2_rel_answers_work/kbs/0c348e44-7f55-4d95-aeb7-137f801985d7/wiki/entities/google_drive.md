# Google Drive

Google Drive is a cloud‑based file storage and synchronization service provided by Google. In the context of **session‑36 (2025‑06‑15)** it is used as the **master repository** for a home‑inventory photo system, serving as the single source of truth for item images captured on an iPhone.

## Key Facts from session‑36
- **Primary role**: Stores the "Home Inventory" folder that contains sub‑folders for clothes, kitchen, books, and everything‑else.
- **Workflow**: Photos are taken with an iPhone, then weekly moved to Google Drive and deleted from the phone to avoid duplicate libraries.
- **Naming convention**: Files follow a pattern like `jacket_black_puffer_03‑26.jpg`, making the collection easy to scan.
- **Maintenance**: A quick weekly review (under 10 minutes) ensures the Drive folder mirrors the physical items owned, supporting the [[concepts/digital_inventory_management]] approach.
- **Integration**: Works alongside the iPhone as the capture device, illustrating a simple **capture‑store** pipeline.
