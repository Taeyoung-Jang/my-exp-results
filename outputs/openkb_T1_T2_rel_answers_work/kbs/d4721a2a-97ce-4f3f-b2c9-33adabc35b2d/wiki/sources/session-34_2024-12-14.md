**User:** Can you help me come up with a lightweight system for keeping track of who’s who and what I’m waiting on without turning it into a huge admin project? After the reorg, I’m on calls and message threads all day with a rotating mix of about thirty different names, and it feels like I’m constantly looping in someone new for day-to-day coordination. Any practical workflow ideas (and maybe a template) that won’t fall apart after a week?

**Assistant:** You want something that’s barely a system—just enough structure to keep your brain clear without turning into a second job. Here’s a simple approach that tends to hold up over time.

Think of it as one small spreadsheet plus one lightweight habit.

In the spreadsheet, keep only four columns: name, what they own, what you need from them, and your last touchpoint. Don’t try to capture every detail. Treat it like a living snapshot you update in under a minute whenever you finish a meeting or send a follow‑up. That’s it.

Then add one recurring ten‑minute check‑in on your calendar—daily or every other day. During that window, skim the sheet, bump anything that’s aging, and clear out anything that’s no longer relevant. Because it’s so minimal, the review is quick and painless.

A simple template you can paste into a sheet:

Name  
Current responsibility or domain  
What I’m waiting on  
Last touchpoint (date or quick note)

If you want a tiny bit of tagging, add a column called Status with only a few values, like Waiting, Active, Closed. Keeping the categories very small is what stops the system from ballooning.

This gives you just enough structure to feel grounded without turning into a maintenance chore.
