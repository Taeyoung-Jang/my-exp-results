# Microsoft Outlook (product)

Microsoft Outlook is the email, calendar, and task management application that is part of the Microsoft 365 ecosystem. It is widely used in corporate environments for communication, scheduling, and lightweight project tracking.

## Role in session‑34 (2024‑12‑14)
In the **lightweight‑tracking‑system** described in [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-14]], Outlook serves three core purposes:
1. **Category tagging** – Three custom categories (Owner = blue, Waiting = orange, FYI = green) are created to flag messages related to people‑ownership tracking.
2. **Search‑folder view** – A saved search folder called *Waiting this week* filters messages that have the *Waiting* category and were received in the last seven days, giving a quick dashboard of pending items.
3. **Integration point** – Outlook categories are referenced in the OneNote table (see the “Status” column) to keep the single source of truth synchronized with email activity.

## Key facts from the document
- Outlook categories are *blue*, *orange*, and *green*.
- A **search folder** is used to surface “Waiting this week” items.
- The workflow relies on **quick‑capture** and **daily‑sweep** habits that lean on Outlook tagging.
