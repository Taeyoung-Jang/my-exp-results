# Quick Capture Checklist

**Definition** – A concise, three‑bullet routine designed to capture a person's name, their ownership area, and the immediate request you have from them in roughly 30 seconds. It is the fast‑capture layer of the broader [[concepts/lightweight-tracking-system]] discussed in [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-14]].

---

## Why it matters
- **Speed** – Fits into the tiny gaps between back‑to‑back meetings.
- **Consistency** – Forces the same minimal data fields each time, reducing forget‑fulness.
- **Integration** – Works directly in the OneNote table used for the overall tracking system, and the captured data can be instantly referenced from Outlook or Teams.

---

## The three‑bullet routine
1. **Add the name.**
2. **Add one short ownership phrase.**
3. **Add one short “what I need” phrase plus today’s date.**

These bullets are meant to become muscle memory; the entire action should take no more than thirty seconds.

---

## How to apply it in your toolset
- **OneNote** – Open the shared "People + Ownership" page, place the cursor in the next empty row of the main table, and type the three pieces of information separated by the pipe (`|`) delimiter.
- **Outlook** – When an email or meeting introduces a new person, switch to OneNote (or use the OneNote quick‑note shortcut) and execute the checklist immediately.
- **Teams** – If a new participant appears in a chat, use the same three‑bullet capture before ending the conversation.

---

## Example rows (as they would appear in OneNote)
```
Alex R. | Vendor contracts | Waiting on updated SOW for Q2 | 3/12 – draft coming this week
Priya T. | Build pipeline dependency | Need status on blocker (looped in by Sam) | 3/14 – replied in Teams thread
```

## Relation to other concepts
- Forms the capture front‑end of the [[concepts/minimum-viable-workflow]] that relies on a single OneNote table.
- Supports the overall [[concepts/lightweight-tracking-system]] by ensuring every new stakeholder is entered with the essential context.
- While not directly about [[concepts/workplace-boundaries]], it helps maintain clear communication boundaries by recording who owns what in a stable, low‑maintenance way.

---

## Tips for making it stick
- Keep the page pinned in OneNote while on calls.
- Use a keyboard shortcut to jump to the table (e.g., Alt‑Shift‑N in Windows).
- Treat the checklist as a habit cue: when you hear a new name, pause, run the three bullets, then resume the meeting.
