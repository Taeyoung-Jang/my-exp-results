# Summary of Session 14 (2024‑06‑15)

**Purpose** – Provide a lightweight framework for couples to choose and plan a short weekend trip that feels special without overspending.

## Key Concepts

- **Travel vibe identification** – Decide whether the trip should be *relaxed*, *outdoorsy*, *foodie*, *arts‑focused*, etc. This guides destination selection. [[concepts/travel_vibe]]
- **Destination scoring** – Compare 5‑6 traits (vibe, activities, cost, travel time, crowd level, “special factor”) using a simple three‑point scale (1 = weak, 2 = decent, 3 = great). [[concepts/destination_scoring]]
- **Checklist template** – A one‑page table for quick side‑by‑side comparison of destinations. Includes rows for the traits above and a final impression score. *(no wikilink)*
- **Booking timeline** – Standard weekend trips: book **2‑4 weeks** ahead. For popular weekends or holidays: shift to **4‑6 weeks**. *(no wikilink)*
- **Budget caps & splits** – Set a total weekend budget, then allocate percentages: typically **50 % lodging**, **25 % food**, **25 % activities + transport**. Adjust splits based on travel mode (driving vs. flying). [[concepts/budget_split]]
- **Finding low‑cost special experiences** – Leverage free or cheap local assets: scenic overlooks, short trails, markets, free museum hours, community events, live‑music venues. *(no wikilink)*

## How to Use
1. **Identify the vibe** you both want. [[concepts/travel_vibe]]
2. **Select 2‑4 nearby destinations**.
3. Fill out the **checklist** for each, scoring the traits. [[concepts/destination_scoring]]
4. Apply the **budget split** appropriate to your total budget and travel mode. [[concepts/budget_split]]
5. Adjust the **booking timeline** based on demand (normal vs. holiday).
6. Look for **special experiences** that are free or low‑cost to enrich the trip.

By following these steps, couples can quickly converge on a destination that feels intentional, stay within budget, and still enjoy memorable moments.
