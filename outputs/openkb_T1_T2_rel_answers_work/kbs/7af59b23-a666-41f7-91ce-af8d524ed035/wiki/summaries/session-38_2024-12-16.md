# Summary of session-38_2024-12-16

## Core Advice
- Compare health‑insurance plans by focusing on the three high‑impact numbers: **premium**, **deductible**, and **out of pocket max**. These dominate total cost in both typical and bad‑year scenarios.
- Align the plan’s cost‑sharing structure (copays, coinsurance) with your personal utilization pattern (routine care vs frequent specialist visits or costly prescriptions).

## Detailed Checklist for Plan Comparison
- **Monthly premium**
- **Deductible** (medical and pharmacy, if separate)
- **Out‑of‑pocket maximum**
- **Copay amounts** for primary care, specialist, urgent care, ER
- **Coinsurance %** after deductible
- **Prescription drug tiers** and associated copays/coinsurance
- **Separate pharmacy deductible** (if any)
- **Hospital‑care deductible** (if distinct)
- **Mental‑health visit cost**
- **Telehealth cost**
- **HSA‑eligibility**

### Gotchas to Verify
- In‑network provider and pharmacy lists (**in network**) for the specific plan code
- Prior‑authorization or step‑therapy requirements for your meds (**prior authorization**) 
- Referral needs for specialists
- Out‑of‑network coverage rules and emergency billing
- Treatment of benefits when switching mid‑year (deductible and OOP carry‑over)
- How claims spanning the switch date are handled
- Reference number for the case and confirmation of the effective date

## Paperwork Needed for Switching from Family to Individual
- Proof of a **qualifying life event** (e.g., divorce decree, legal separation, dependent aging out, proof of new coverage for dependents) – **qualifying life event**
- Employer‑provided change‑of‑coverage form (if applicable)
- Any additional documentation the insurer specifies (PDF, scanned copies, etc.)

### Key Questions When Submitting Documents
1. Exactly which documents are required?
2. Preferred upload method and file format?
3. Expected processing time and the effective date rule (event date vs processing date).
4. Will existing deductible / out‑of‑pocket spending roll over?
5. What is the **reference number** for this submission? (**reference number**)
6. Can the rep repeat back the recorded information for verification?

## Phone Script for the Insurance Representative
```
Hi, I’m switching from a family plan to an individual plan, and I want to make sure I submit the right paperwork and that my effective date and cost‑sharing amounts are handled correctly.

1. Which specific documents do you need for this change?
2. Where should I send or upload them, and what file format works best?
3. After I submit, how long does processing take, and will the effective date be based on the qualifying event or the date you finish processing?
4. Will any deductible or out‑of‑pocket amounts I’ve already paid this year carry over to the new individual plan?
5. Please provide a case/reference number for this call and confirm what you see on your end.
```

Using this script helps ensure all critical points—documents, submission location, reference number, effective date, and cost‑sharing continuity—are covered.
