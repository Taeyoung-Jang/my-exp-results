# Travel Vibe Identification

**Definition** – Travel Vibe Identification is the process of deciding the overall mood or theme a couple wants for a short getaway (e.g., relaxed, outdoorsy, foodie, arts‑focused, spontaneous). Pinpointing this vibe early narrows down suitable destinations and informs later scoring and budgeting decisions.

## Why It Matters
- Sets a clear **direction** before researching specific locations.
- Aligns both partners on expectations, reducing conflict during planning.
- Provides a primary criterion for the [[concepts/destination_scoring]] checklist used in the session‑14 guide.

## Steps (as outlined in [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]])
1. **Discuss Preferences** – Each partner lists activities or atmospheres they enjoy on a trip.
2. **Group into Vibe Categories** – Common categories include:
   - *Relaxed* (spa, slow walks)
   - *Outdoorsy* (hiking, nature)
   - *Foodie* (restaurants, markets)
   - *Artsy* (galleries, street art)
   - *Spontaneous* (open‑ended exploration)
3. **Select the Dominant Vibe** – Choose the vibe that best captures the shared goal.
4. **Use the Vibe as a Filter** – When scouting destinations, prioritize places known for that vibe.

## Connecting to Other Concepts
- The chosen vibe becomes one of the five‑six traits scored in the [[concepts/destination_scoring]] matrix.
- After scoring, the overall budget can be allocated using the framework in [[concepts/budget_split]], ensuring the vibe‑driven activities receive appropriate funding.

## Practical Tips
- **Keep it simple** – One word or short phrase (e.g., "outdoorsy") is enough.
- **Be flexible** – If a destination offers a secondary vibe that also appeals, note it for future trips.
- **Document the vibe** – Write it at the top of the checklist template so it stays front‑and‑center during comparison.
