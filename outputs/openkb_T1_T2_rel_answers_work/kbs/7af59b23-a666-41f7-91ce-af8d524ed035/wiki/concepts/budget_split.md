# Budget Split Allocation

**Definition** – A simple method for allocating a total weekend‑trip budget into four main categories: lodging, food, activities, and transport. The approach uses percentage‑based splits that can be adjusted for total budget size and travel mode (driving vs. flying).

## Core Allocation Rules
- **Base percentages** (suitable for most short trips):
  - **Lodging:** 50 % of the total budget
  - **Food:** 25 % of the total budget
  - **Activities + Transport:** 25 % of the total budget, later divided between the two based on travel mode.
- **Adjustments for travel mode**:
  - **Driving:** Allocate a larger share to lodging/food and a smaller share to transport (gas/parking). Example: transport may be ~10 % of the total, with the remainder shifted to lodging or food.
  - **Flying:** Allocate a larger share to transport (flights) and reduce lodging/food accordingly. Example: transport may be ~15‑20 % of the total.

## Example Budget Tables
These tables illustrate how the base percentages are applied and then tweaked for driving vs. flying. Numbers are rounded for clarity.

| Total Budget | Travel Mode | Lodging | Food | Activities | Transport |
|--------------|-------------|---------|------|------------|-----------|
| **$400** | Driving | $200 | $100 | $60 | $40 (gas/parking) |
|          | Flying  | $160 | $90  | $50 | $100 (flight) |
| **$700** | Driving | $350 | $175 | $125 | $50 (gas/parking) |
|          | Flying  | $280 | $150 | $120 | $150 (flight) |

## How to Use the Allocation
1. **Set a total budget** for the weekend (e.g., $400 or $700).
2. **Choose travel mode** (driving or flying).
3. **Apply the appropriate row** from the table above to get initial category amounts.
4. **Fine‑tune** based on actual costs (e.g., cheaper gas, higher‑priced lodging) while keeping the overall total unchanged.
5. **Track spending** against each category during planning to stay within limits.
