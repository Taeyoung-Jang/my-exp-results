**Topic:** Practical survival guide for private‑party vehicle transactions, focusing on California DMV requirements.

**Key Points**  
- **Mindset & Preparation** – Assume delays, over‑prepare documents, and use online DMV services whenever possible. Kindness to staff can smooth interactions.  
- **General Private‑Party Checklist** – Verify title ownership, clean the car, take photos, gather maintenance records, check state‑specific smog/inspection rules, arrange a safe meetup, bring title, [[entities/bill-of-sale]] (optional), ID, and any release‑of‑liability forms, verify buyer’s insurance/license, complete title and bill of sale accurately, file the release‑of‑liability, remove insurance, and handle license plates per state rules.  
- **Common Mistakes** – Forgetting to file the release‑of‑liability, incorrect mileage/price on the title, accepting insecure payment methods, handing over the title before payment clears, and neglecting to cancel insurance promptly.

### California‑Specific Details [[concepts/notice-of-transfer-release-of-liability]]  
- **Forms & Filing**: Use the [[entities/certificate-of-title]]; a bill of sale is optional. File the **Notice of Transfer and Release of Liability** (form [[entities/reg-138]]) online within 5 days (same‑day filing is safest).  
- **License Plates**: Plates generally stay on the vehicle; they are surrendered only for specialty plates, junking, out‑of‑state moves, or when the DMV requests them.  
- **Lost Title**: Request a duplicate with **REG 227** (form [[entities/reg-227]]) before selling.  
- **Loan on Title**: Contact the lienholder; the loan must be paid off before a clean title can be transferred. The lienholder may send the title to you or directly to the DMV.  
- **Post‑Sale Protections**: Keep a copy or screenshot of the online Release‑of‑Liability confirmation, retain the [[entities/bill-of-sale]], and optionally retain a copy of the old registration for your records to contest any later tolls or tickets.

### Practical Checklist for California Private‑Party Sale [[concepts/private-party-vehicle-sale-checklist]]  
1. **Pre‑Listing**  
   - Confirm title in your name; obtain duplicate if lost (REG 227).  
   - Clean vehicle, photograph, collect service records.  
   - Verify state smog/inspection requirements.  
2. **Meet‑up Preparation**  
   - Bring: title, printed [[entities/bill-of-sale]], driver’s license, buyer’s insurance proof, any required release‑of‑liability forms.  
   - Choose a public, safe location; accompany buyer on test‑drive.  
3. **Paperwork Execution**  
   - Fill title: buyer name, sale price, date, mileage, signatures.  
   - Complete bill of sale with matching details.  
   - Give buyer title, bill of sale, and any extra documents.  
4. **After‑Sale Actions**  
   - File Notice of Transfer/Release of Liability online (within 5 days).  
   - Cancel your insurance after the DMV processes the transfer.  
   *Retain copies of filing confirmation, bill of sale, and registration for your records.*

**Takeaway**: By following the checklist, filing the release promptly, and keeping documentation, you avoid most post‑sale headaches such as unexpected tickets, tolls, or title disputes.
