# Bill of Sale

**Type:** work

## Overview
The *Bill of Sale* is a written document that records the essential details of a private‑party vehicle transaction: buyer and seller names, vehicle description, sale price, date, mileage, and signatures. While California does not require a state‑issued bill of sale, it is strongly recommended as proof of ownership transfer and as a reference for both parties.

## Role in a California Private‑Party Sale
- **Verification:** Provides a cross‑check against the information entered on the Certificate of Title.
- **Evidence:** Serves as evidence for the buyer when registering the vehicle and for the seller if disputes arise.
- **Complement to Filing:** Used alongside the **[[concepts/notice-of-transfer-release-of-liability]]** filing (REG 138) to demonstrate that the seller has transferred responsibility.
- **Record Keeping:** Sellers should keep a copy of the signed bill of sale and any related registration documents for their records.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-18]]
- Include **buyer name, seller name, vehicle VIN, make/model, odometer reading, sale price, and date**.
- Ensure the information matches the details on the **[[entities/certificate-of-title]]** to avoid title rejection.
- Sign the document in the presence of the buyer; both parties should retain a copy.
- Use the bill of sale as part of the **[[concepts/private-party-vehicle-sale-checklist]]** to confirm all paperwork is complete before finalizing the sale.
