## REG 227 – Duplicate Title Request Form

**Type:** work (California DMV form)

### Overview
REG 227, officially titled *Application for Duplicate or Paperless Title*, is the form used by the California Department of Motor Vehicles to request a replacement when the original Certificate of Title is lost, stolen, or damaged.  The form must be completed and submitted before a vehicle can be sold or transferred if the original title is unavailable.

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-18]]
- The form is mentioned as the required step when a seller discovers a missing title before a private‑party sale.
- It is processed by [[entities/california-dmv]] and results in a new [[entities/certificate-of-title]] that can be used for the transfer.
- The session advises obtaining the duplicate **before** completing the sale to avoid delays.

### Role in Vehicle Title Management
REG 227 is part of the broader [[concepts/vehicle-title-management]] workflow.  It ensures that the seller retains a valid title, which is essential for completing the [[concepts/private-party-vehicle-sale-checklist]] and for filing the [[concepts/notice-of-transfer-release-of-liability]] after the transaction.

### Related Forms and Entities
- **REG 138** – the online *Notice of Transfer and Release of Liability* that must be filed within five days of the sale.  It works in tandem with the duplicate title to protect the seller from post‑sale tickets.
- **Certificate of Title** – the official document that REG 227 replaces when lost.
- **California DMV** – the agency that issues and processes REG 227.

### When to Use REG 227
1. The original title is lost, stolen, or destroyed.
2. The seller needs a clean title before listing the vehicle.
3. The buyer requires a valid title for registration.

### Process Summary
1. Complete REG 227 with vehicle details, owner information, and reason for request.
2. Submit the form (online or by mail) to [[entities/california-dmv]].
3. Receive the duplicate [[entities/certificate-of-title]].
4. Proceed with the private‑party sale using the new title and file the [[concepts/notice-of-transfer-release-of-liability]].
