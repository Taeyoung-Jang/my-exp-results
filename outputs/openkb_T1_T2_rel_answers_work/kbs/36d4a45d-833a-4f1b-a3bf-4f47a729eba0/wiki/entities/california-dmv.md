# California Department of Motor Vehicles

The **California Department of Motor Vehicles** (often abbreviated as **CA DMV**) is the state agency responsible for vehicle registration, driver licensing, title issuance, and a variety of motor‑vehicle‑related services in the state of California.

## Key Functions Referenced in Session 38
- **Online filing of the Notice of Transfer and Release of Liability** – The DMV provides an online portal to submit the required notice (often associated with **REG 138**) within five days of a private‑party sale. This is highlighted in [[concepts/notice-of-transfer-release-of-liability]].
- **Duplicate title requests** – When a title is lost, owners can request a duplicate using **REG 227**, a form processed by the DMV.
- **Certificate of Title management** – The DMV issues and records the **Certificate of Title**, the legal proof of ownership for a vehicle. See [[concepts/vehicle-title-management]].
- **Guidance for private‑party sales** – The agency’s resources inform owners on the steps needed for a private‑party vehicle sale, aligning with the checklist outlined in [[concepts/private-party-vehicle-sale-checklist]].

## Interaction with Vehicle Owners
- Provides **online services** that reduce the need for in‑person visits, a point emphasized as a way to save time and patience.
- Issues **specialty plates** and handles surrender of plates when owners choose to relinquish them (e.g., personalized or junk‑vehicle scenarios).
- Accepts **payments** and **document submissions** for title transfers, registration renewals, and lien releases.
