## Packed Sand

**Type:** place

**Description:** A firm, moist beach surface that forms at low tide, providing a softer‑than‑pavement but still supportive terrain for running.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2024-12-10]]**
- Acts as a lower‑impact alternative to pavement, reducing joint stress while increasing demand on calf and Achilles muscles.
- Recommended progression: start with ~25 % of each run on packed sand and increase to ~60 % over a 4‑week plan.
- Strength and mobility work tied to this surface includes [[concepts/strength-drills]] (slow calf raises, eccentric heel drops) and [[concepts/mobility-drills]] (ankle circles, calf stretch).
- Monitoring for overuse uses [[concepts/warning-signs]] such as persistent Achilles stiffness or chronic calf tightness.
- The terrain often features a slight slope or camber; manage it by alternating running direction or varying distance from water, as described in [[concepts/shoreside-camber]].
- Running on packed sand typically occurs at low tide, linking it to the entity [[entities/low-tide-running]].
