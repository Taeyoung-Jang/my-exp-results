## Checklist Template
### Daily
- Warm‑up sensors and the [[entities/data-acquisition-system]] to a stable temperature.
- Compare each sensor to a [[entities/reference-barometer]].
- Record raw and reference values, calculate offset.
- Note ambient swings or handling issues.

### Weekly
- Repeat daily steps.
- Verify DAQ channel scaling and version.
- Inspect cables, ports, and fittings for stress.
- Log offsets under the most stable conditions.

### Monthly
- Perform full calibration under controlled ambient conditions.
- Document any adjustments applied.
- Review month‑long offset trends.
- Confirm storage conditions have remained stable.

## Expanded CSV Log Schema
```
timestamp, sensor_id, sensor_type, daq_channel, daq_scaling_version,
warmup_minutes, check_type, raw_reading, reference_reading, offset,
ambient_temp_C, ambient_pressure_hPa, storage_notes, handling_notes
```
- *check_type* distinguishes daily spot checks, weekly verification, and monthly full calibrations.
- Includes warm‑up time and DAQ scaling metadata for traceability.

## Differential Sensor Zero‑Check Procedure
- Use an equalization valve or tee to momentarily expose both ports of the [[entities/differential-pressure-sensor]] to the same ambient air without disconnecting tubing.
- Allow a few seconds for pressure stabilization, then record the zero reading.
- Prefer a short, rigid equalization path to minimize pressure traps and thermal flex.

## Equalization Valve / Manifold Design
- Install a small tee or manifold that joins the two sensor ports to a single common chamber.
- Provide a controlled opening to ambient air for the zero‑check.
- Keep the shared volume low and use low‑leak fittings to reduce bias and ensure repeatability.
- When the valve is closed, the measurement lines revert to normal operation.

## Practical Recommendations
- Store sensors in a temperature‑stable environment to limit altitude‑induced drift.
- Log offsets over weeks to separate true sensor drift from environmental variability.
- Adjust calibration intervals if a steady drift trend is observed.
- Use the expanded CSV schema for easy sorting, plotting, and trend analysis.
