# Summary of session‑40 (2024‑12‑10)

**Main contribution** – a concrete 4‑week progression for increasing running on packed sand, combined with strength/mobility work, warning‑sign monitoring, and practical advice for salt‑film on glasses.

## Key concepts
- [[concepts/softer-surfaces]]: softer terrain (trails, sand) reduces impact but shifts load to stabilising muscles.
- [[concepts/surface-mixing]]: alternating sand with pavement/trails balances joint stress and muscular demand.
- [[concepts/progressive-sand-running]]: weekly % of run on sand (25 % → 33 % → 50 % → 60 %).
- [[concepts/strength-drills]]: slow straight‑leg calf raises, bent‑knee calf raises, eccentric heel drops (3 × 10, 3 × 10, 2‑3 × 8).
- [[concepts/mobility-drills]]: ankle circles, post‑run calf stretch.
- [[concepts/warning-signs]]: persistent Achilles stiffness, tugging/pinching sensation, calves that stay tight after easy days.
- [[concepts/salt-film-management]]: water‑flask rinse, damp shirt wipe, anti‑fog/anti‑smudge wipes.
- [[concepts/shoreside-camber]]: mild slope awareness, alternating direction or run location to equalise ankle load.

## 4‑week schedule (example 4 runs/week)
| Week | Sand % of each run | Strength sessions/week | Notes |
|------|-------------------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | 25 % (early‑week run only) | 2 (short) | focus on technique, easy runs on firm ground |
| 2 | 33 % (mid‑week run) | 2 | increase sand segment, keep strength same |
| 3 | 50 % (early‑week + weekend runs) | 2‑3 | add a third strength day if legs feel good |
| 4 | 60 % (weekend run) | 2‑3 | maintain or drop back if warning signs appear |

### Strength session example
1. **Straight‑leg calf raises** – 3 sets × 10 slow reps  
2. **Bent‑knee calf raises** – 3 sets × 10 slow reps  
3. **Eccentric heel drops** – 2‑3 sets × 8 reps each leg (pain‑free)  

*Total time: 10‑15 min.*

## Monitoring & adjustments
- **Warning signs** → stay at current level or reduce sand %.
- **Recovery** → gentle ankle circles, calf stretch after each run.
- **Camber management** → switch running direction halfway, or vary distance from water line.

## Practical tip for glasses
- Carry a small water flask for a quick rinse.  
- Keep a damp shirt hem for a swipe.  
- Use anti‑fog/anti‑smudge wipes before heading out.
