# Summary of Session 33 – First‑Week Onboarding Checklist & Health‑Plan Verification

**Date:** 2025‑02‑13

## Overview
This session provides a practical, copy‑friendly checklist for new hires covering the essential **first‑week onboarding** tasks, and a detailed guide on how to verify health‑plan enrollment in an HR portal. It highlights the key paperwork, IT setup steps, benefits enrollment checks, and the exact information to capture (e.g., screenshots) when contacting HR.

## Rest‑of‑Week Action Items
- **Direct‑Deposit Confirmation** – Verify routing and account numbers in the payroll portal.
- **Benefits Confirmation** – Ensure the portal shows:
  - Status = *Enrolled* / *Completed*
  - Correct plan name and coverage level (e.g., Employee Only, Family)
  - Effective date (usually hire date or next month)
  - Per‑paycheck premium amount.
- **IT Access Verification** – Log in to email, calendar, chat, HR portal, project tools; test VPN, MFA, and authentication apps.
- **Shared Resources** – Confirm access to shared drives, role‑specific software, and permissions.
- **Mandatory Trainings** – Complete security, compliance, and policy modules.
- **Emergency Contacts** – Add/update in the HR system.
- **Pay Schedule** – Review pay frequency and first‑paycheck date.
- **Manager Check‑In** – Ask about any additional onboarding tasks or tools.
- **Benefits Deadlines** – Note any dependent addition windows or optional plan selection cut‑offs.

## Verifying Health‑Plan Enrollment in the HR Portal
1. Locate the **Benefits Summary** or **Health Plan** section in the HR portal.
2. Look for a **status field** showing *Enrolled*, *Completed*, *Submitted*, or *Confirmed*.
3. Confirm the **plan name**, **coverage level**, and **effective date** are displayed correctly.
4. Verify the **premium amount** per paycheck is listed.
5. For dependents, each should show a status of *Covered* or *Enrolled* (not *Pending Verification* unless documentation is required).
6. If any field is blank, says *Pending*, or shows an unexpected status, treat it as a potential issue.

## Preparing Screenshots for HR
- Capture the **benefits summary page** showing status, plan name, coverage level, and effective date.
- If premiums or dependents are incorrect, screenshot those specific rows.
- Blur or hide sensitive data (full SSN, bank account numbers).
- Include a brief note in the email with:
  - Your full name and start date
  - The plan you intended to select
  - A concise description of the discrepancy

## Next Steps
- Follow the rest‑of‑week checklist.
- Use the verification steps to confirm health‑plan enrollment.
- If issues arise, email HR with the prepared screenshots and details.
