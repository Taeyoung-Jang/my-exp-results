# Summary of Session 11 (2024‑06‑15)

**Purpose**: Help a retired individual design a low‑pressure, repeatable weekly routine that balances physical activity, budget‑friendly hobbies, and volunteer work.

## Key Ideas
- **Weekly routine planning** – Anchor each weekday with a morning walk (9:00‑10:30) and allocate two‑hour blocks for a hobby or volunteer shift. [[concepts/weekly_routine_planning]]
- **Hobby selection** – Emphasize inexpensive, low‑equipment options: watercolor painting, sketching, journaling, phone photography, and creative writing. [[concepts/budget_friendly_hobbies]]
- **Volunteer engagement** – Offer two volunteer categories (food pantry, animal shelter) with a 2‑hour shift either in the late morning (10:00‑12:00) or early afternoon (1:00‑3:00). [[entities/food-pantry]] [[entities/animal-shelter]]
- **Low‑physical‑demand shelter roles** – Front‑desk greeter, visitor assistance, phone/administrative support, scheduling, and light paperwork. [[concepts/low_physical_volunteer_roles]]
- **Rotation schedule** – Over four weeks, rotate the two hobbies and two volunteer options so each is tried multiple times.
- **Free block purpose** – Designated “rest/errands/social” periods provide intentional downtime while keeping flexibility.
- **Volunteer checklist** – Questions to ask when contacting a site: typical shift hours, required training, physical demands, scheduling lead‑time, point of contact, trial shift policy.

## Printable Table (Weeks 1‑4)
```
Week 1
Mon: 9‑10:30 walk; 10:30‑12 watercolor; 1‑3 watercolor
Tue: 9‑10:30 walk; 10‑12 food pantry; 1‑3 rest/errands/social
Wed: 9‑10:30 walk; 10:30‑12 sketching; 1‑3 sketching
Thu: 9‑10:30 walk; 1‑3 food pantry; 10:30‑12 rest/errands/social
Fri: 9‑10:30 walk; 10:30‑12 open; 1‑3 open

Week 2 (watercolor + animal shelter) – same pattern with shelter shifts
Week 3 – repeat Week 1
Week 4 – repeat Week 2
```
