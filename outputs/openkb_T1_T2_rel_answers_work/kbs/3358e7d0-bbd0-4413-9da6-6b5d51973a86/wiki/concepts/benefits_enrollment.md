# Benefits Enrollment

**Definition**: The set of actions a new employee takes to select, confirm, and verify health, dental, vision, and other optional benefits offered by the employer.

## Core Elements
- **Status Indicators** – Look for portal fields that read *Enrolled*, *Completed*, *Submitted*, or *Confirmed*.
- **Plan Details** – Name of the plan, coverage level (e.g., Employee Only, Family), effective date, and per‑paycheck premium.
- **Dependent Coverage** – Each dependent should show a status of *Covered* or *Enrolled*; *Pending Verification* usually means additional documentation is required.
- **Deadlines** – Most companies require enrollment within 7–30 days of start date; note any later windows for adding dependents or changing optional plans.

## Steps for New Hires (see [[concepts/first_week_onboarding]])
1. **Access the HR portal** (e.g., [[entities/hr-portal]]) and navigate to the Benefits or Health Plan section.
2. Verify that the status field shows a confirmed enrollment.
3. Confirm the correct plan name, coverage level, effective date, and premium amount.
4. If you have dependents, ensure each is listed as *Covered*.
5. Record the enrollment deadline in your calendar.
6. Capture screenshots of the benefits summary page, focusing on:
   - Status field
   - Plan name and coverage level
   - Effective date
   - Premium amount
   - Dependent listings (if applicable)
   - **Redact** any sensitive personal data (full SSN, bank details).
7. If any information is missing or incorrect, email HR with the screenshots and a brief note including your full name, start date, and intended plan.

## Integration with IT Setup (see [[concepts/it_access_setup]])
- Ensure you have access to the HR portal and can log in without issues.
- Verify that MFA or authentication apps are configured, as many portals require them for secure access.
- Confirm that you can download or view benefit documents when needed.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Pending Status** – Indicates enrollment has not been processed; double‑check submission or required documents.
- **Missing Premium Info** – May signal an incomplete enrollment; contact HR promptly.
- **Incorrect Dependent Entries** – Verify names, relationship, and coverage dates.

## Why It Matters
Accurate benefits enrollment ensures you receive the correct health coverage from day 1, avoids costly retroactive enrollment issues, and guarantees that payroll deductions are applied correctly.
