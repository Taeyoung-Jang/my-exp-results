# First‑Week Onboarding

**Definition**: A coordinated series of administrative, technical, and practical tasks that a new employee completes during the first week of employment to become fully operational and compliant.

---

## Core Components

1. **HR Paperwork**
   - Complete tax forms (e.g., W‑4) and any state payroll forms.
   - Sign the employment agreement and acknowledge the employee handbook.
   - Submit direct‑deposit information and verify routing/account numbers.
   - Enroll in health and other benefits (see [[concepts/benefits_enrollment]]).

2. **Benefits Confirmation**
   - Verify the health‑plan status shows *Enrolled* or *Completed*.
   - Check plan name, coverage level (Employee Only, Family, etc.), effective date, and per‑paycheck premium.
   - Ensure each dependent is listed as *Covered*.
   - Note any enrollment deadlines in your calendar.

3. **IT Access Setup**
   - Log in to email, calendar, and messaging tools.
   - Access core work systems (HR portal, time‑tracking, project tools).
   - Set up VPN, MFA, and authentication apps.
   - Test permissions for shared drives, role‑specific software, and hardware (laptop, phone).
   - See [[concepts/it_access_setup]] for typical steps.

4. **Equipment Check**
   - Confirm laptop, monitor, phone, and any peripherals are functional.
   - Verify that required software licenses are active.

5. **Manager Alignment**
   - Discuss expectations for the first week and any critical deliverables.
   - Schedule 1:1 meetings with the manager and key teammates.

6. **Mandatory Trainings**
   - Complete security, compliance, and workplace‑policy modules.

7. **Administrative Details**
   - Add emergency contacts in the HR system.
   - Review the pay schedule and first‑paycheck date.
   - Record any benefits‑related deadlines (e.g., adding dependents, optional plan selections).

---

## Verification Checklist (Health‑Plan Focus)

- Locate the **Benefits Summary** page in the HR portal.
- Look for a **status field** that reads *Enrolled*, *Completed*, or *Confirmed*.
- Confirm the **plan name**, **coverage level**, **effective date**, and **premium amount** are displayed.
- For dependents, ensure each shows *Covered* (not *Pending Verification* unless documentation is required).
- If any field is blank, says *Pending*, or appears incorrect, prepare to contact HR.

## Preparing Evidence for HR

- Screenshot the benefits summary showing status, plan name, coverage level, and effective date.
- Capture any rows with incorrect premiums or dependent information.
- Redact sensitive data (full SSN, bank details).
- Include in the email: your full name, start date, intended plan, and a brief description of the discrepancy.
