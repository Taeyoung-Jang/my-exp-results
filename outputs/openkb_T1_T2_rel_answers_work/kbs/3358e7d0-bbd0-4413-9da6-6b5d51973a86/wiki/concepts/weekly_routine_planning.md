# Weekly Routine Planning

**Definition**: A flexible framework that organizes a retiree’s weekdays into balanced blocks of movement, hobbies, volunteering, and intentional downtime, allowing easy adjustment and minimal pressure.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]])
- **Anchor activity** – a daily morning walk or light exercise (9:00‑10:30) that provides routine.
- **Time‑block structure** – two‑hour windows for a primary activity (either a hobby or a volunteer shift) and a secondary “rest/errands/social” block.
- **Hobby rotation** – alternate between low‑cost, low‑equipment pursuits such as watercolor painting and sketching, exemplifying [[concepts/budget_friendly_hobbies]].
- **Volunteer rotation** – switch between a food pantry and an animal shelter, each on a 2‑hour shift (10:00‑12:00 or 13:00‑15:00), with optional lower‑physical‑demand roles like front‑desk greeter or administrative support (see [[concepts/low_physical_volunteer_roles]]).  Relevant entities include [[entities/food-pantry]], [[entities/animal-shelter]], [[entities/community-center]], [[entities/local-library]], and [[entities/volunteermatch]].
- **Free block purpose** – designated “rest/errands/social” periods give intentional downtime while keeping the day structured.
- **Rotation schedule** – over four weeks the hobby and volunteer choices are swapped so each is experienced multiple times, reinforcing variety without overwhelm.
- **Volunteer checklist** – a concise list of questions (hours, training, physical demands, scheduling, point of contact, trial shift) to ask when contacting a site.

## Printable Template
A single table (Weeks 1‑4) lists each day’s activities with exact times, making it easy to print and follow.

## Benefits
- **Structure without rigidity** – consistent anchor times create rhythm while allowing daily choice.
- **Physical and mental health** – regular walks, creative expression, and social interaction improve wellbeing.
- **Community connection** – regular volunteering builds local ties.
- **Scalability** – the framework can be adapted to different interests, time availabilities, or physical abilities.
