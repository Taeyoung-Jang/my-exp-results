## IT Access Setup

Setting up reliable IT access is a core part of the **first week onboarding** process. Proper configuration ensures a new hire can communicate, access necessary resources, and be productive from day 1 onward.

### Key Elements
1. **Email, Calendar, and Messaging**
   - Log in to corporate email and verify inbox sync.
   - Confirm calendar sharing permissions.
   - Ensure chat or instant‑messaging tools (e.g., Teams, Slack) are functional.
2. **Core Business Systems**
   - Access the HR portal, time‑tracking, and any project‑management tools required for your role.
3. **VPN and Remote Connectivity**
   - Install the company VPN client, configure it, and test a connection to internal resources.
4. **Multi‑Factor Authentication (MFA)**
   - Register an authentication app or token as required by the organization and verify it works across all platforms.
5. **Shared Drives and Permissions**
   - Navigate to shared network drives, confirm you can open expected folders, and request additional permissions if needed.
6. **Role‑Specific Software**
   - Install and launch any specialized applications (e.g., IDEs, CRM, design tools) and verify license activation.
7. **Device Checks**
   - Test your laptop, peripherals, and any mobile devices for connectivity, battery health, and proper configuration.

### Checklist (adapted from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-13]])
- Log in to email, calendar, and messaging tools.
- Verify access to core systems (HR portal, time‑tracking, project tools).
- Set up VPN and confirm remote access.
- Register and test MFA on all required platforms.
- Confirm ability to open shared drives and role‑specific software.
- Perform a quick device health check (battery, webcam, microphone).
- Document any errors and contact IT support promptly.

### Why It Matters
A smooth **IT access setup** minimizes lost time, prevents missed deadlines, and reduces the friction of learning new tools. It also supports compliance with security policies by ensuring MFA and VPN usage from the outset.
