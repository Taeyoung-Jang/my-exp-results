# Animal Shelter (Organization)

**Type:** organization

**Description:** A community animal shelter that provides care for stray and abandoned animals and offers volunteer roles such as front‑desk greeter, visitor assistance, and light administrative tasks.

## Key Facts from Session 11 (2024‑06‑15)
- Mentioned as one of two volunteer options for a retired individual designing a weekly routine.
- Suggested volunteer shift: a 2‑hour slot, either 10:00‑12:00 or 13:00‑15:00.
- Recommended low‑physical‑demand roles: front‑desk greeter, phone/administrative support, scheduling, and light paperwork.
- Integrated into a four‑week rotation schedule alongside the food pantry.
