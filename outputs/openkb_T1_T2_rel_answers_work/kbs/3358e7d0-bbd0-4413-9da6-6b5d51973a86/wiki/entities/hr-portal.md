# HR Portal

**Type:** Product

The HR portal is a web‑based system that employees use to complete onboarding tasks, manage payroll information, and enroll in company benefits. It centralizes essential HR functions such as tax form submission, direct‑deposit setup, and health‑plan enrollment verification.

## Key Facts from Session 33
- New hires are instructed to **confirm their direct‑deposit information** and **verify benefits enrollment** within the portal.
- The portal displays a **status field** (e.g., “Enrolled”, “Completed”) that indicates whether health‑plan enrollment is processed.
- Employees can view plan details, coverage levels, effective dates, and per‑paycheck premium amounts.
- The system also allows users to **add emergency contacts**, check the **pay schedule**, and complete **mandatory trainings**.
- For troubleshooting, users may need to capture **screenshots** of the benefits summary page and email HR with the information.
