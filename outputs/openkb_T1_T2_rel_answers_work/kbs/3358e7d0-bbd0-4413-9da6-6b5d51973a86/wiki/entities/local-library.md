# Local Library (Organization)

The **Local Library** is a community‑run organization that provides access to books, periodicals, digital media, and a variety of public programs such as reading groups, workshops, and classes.  It often serves as a hub for information about local volunteer opportunities, with bulletin boards and staff who can connect residents to organizations like food pantries and animal shelters.
