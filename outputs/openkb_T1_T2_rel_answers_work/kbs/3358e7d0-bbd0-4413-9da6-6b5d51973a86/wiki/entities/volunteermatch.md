## VolunteerMatch

VolunteerMatch is a web‑based service that aggregates volunteer listings from organizations such as food banks, animal shelters, community centers, and libraries. It helps users quickly discover nearby opportunities that match their interests and availability.

### Key facts from the session‑11 summary
- Mentioned as one of the easiest ways to locate volunteer spots locally in the weekly routine plan [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]].
- Can be used to find low‑physical‑demand roles at places like an [[entities/animal-shelter]] (e.g., front‑desk greeter, administrative help) or supportive tasks at a [[entities/food-pantry]] and [[entities/community-center]].
- Complements the concept of [[concepts/weekly_routine_planning]] by providing a searchable database that fits into a structured schedule.
- Supports the idea of choosing budget‑friendly, low‑equipment hobbies by freeing up time for activities like sketching or watercolor, which are highlighted in the routine.

### How it fits into the routine
VolunteerMatch can be consulted during the “rest/errands/social” free blocks to browse new opportunities, ensuring the volunteer component of the schedule stays flexible and aligned with the user’s energy level.
