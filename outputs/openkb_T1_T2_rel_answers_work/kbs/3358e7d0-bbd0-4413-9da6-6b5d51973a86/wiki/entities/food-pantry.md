# Food Pantry (Organization)

**Type:** organization

**Description:** A community organization that collects, prepares, and distributes food to individuals and families experiencing food insecurity.

**Key facts from the session‑11 routine plan**
- Suggested as one of two volunteer options for a retired person's weekly schedule.
- Fits a 2‑hour shift either late‑morning (10:00–12:00) or early‑afternoon (1:00–3:00) to allow free‑time blocks for rest, errands, or social activities.
- The routine recommends rotating this volunteer role across weeks 1, 3, and 5 (if extended) to give repeated exposure.
- When contacting a food pantry, the plan advises asking about typical shift hours, required training, physical demands, scheduling lead‑time, point of contact, and trial‑shift policy.
