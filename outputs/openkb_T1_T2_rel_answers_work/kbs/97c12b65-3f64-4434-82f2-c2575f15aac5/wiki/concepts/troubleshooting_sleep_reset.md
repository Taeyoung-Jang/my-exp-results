# If/Then Troubleshooting for Sleep Reset Plans

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A set of conditional, actionable steps to address frequent problems that arise during a two‑week sleep‑reset, helping users adjust without abandoning the plan.

---

## Core Idea
The troubleshooting framework provides **if/then rules** that map specific symptoms or failures to concrete, low‑effort interventions. It is designed for people following the checklist in [[summaries/session-11_2024-01-20]] and aims to keep the reset effective while respecting the limits of a diagnosed sleep disorder.

---

## Key If/Then Rules (from the source)

| Situation | Trigger (If…) | Recommended Action (Then…) |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------------------|
| **Day‑3 fatigue** | You feel noticeably more exhausted than usual after three days of the reset. | Keep the wake‑time anchor, **shorten morning light** to the minimum (5 min) and **reduce exercise intensity** for the next 1‑2 days. |
| **Prolonged sleep onset** | Falling asleep takes **≥ 45 minutes** on multiple nights. | Get out of bed, engage in a quiet, low‑light activity (e.g., reading a dim book) until drowsy, then return to bed. |
| **Early morning awakening** | You wake **significantly earlier** than your intended bedtime window. | Delay the **morning light exposure** by 15‑20 minutes and **dim evening lighting** further; keep bedtime flexible. |
| **Increasing daytime sleepiness** | Daytime alertness drops to a low rating (≤ 2/5) for > 2 consecutive days. | Re‑evaluate caffeine timing, ensure you have a **6‑hour cutoff**, and consider a brief **mid‑day nap** (< 30 min) if medically appropriate. |
| **New or worsened breathing symptoms** | Any new night‑time breathing difficulty or escalation of existing issues. | **Pause the reset** immediately and contact your clinician (see [[concepts/clinician_red_flags]]). |

## How to Use This Concept
1. **Monitor** the daily log fields (sleep latency, awakenings, restfulness, daytime energy, symptom notes) from the reset plan.
2. **Identify** when a metric crosses a threshold described in the table.
3. **Apply** the corresponding "then" action immediately, noting the change in the log.
4. **Re‑assess** after 1‑2 days; if the issue persists, move to the next relevant rule or seek clinical advice.

---

## Example Workflow
- **Day 4**: Log shows a sleep latency of 50 min. 
- **If**: > 45 min latency → **Then**: get out of bed, low‑light activity, return when sleepy.
- **Day 5**: Latency drops to 30 min, confirming the adjustment helped.
