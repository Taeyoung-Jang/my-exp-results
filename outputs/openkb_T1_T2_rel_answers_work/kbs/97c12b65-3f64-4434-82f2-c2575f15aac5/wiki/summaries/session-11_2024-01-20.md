## Summary of session-11_2024-01-20

**Document type:** Conversation transcript – sleep‑habit redesign  

**Main contribution**  
- Provides a concrete, 14‑day checklist (Week 1 & Week 2) for a sleep‑reset tailored to people with a diagnosed sleep disorder.  
- Distinguishes **must‑do** (non‑negotiable) actions from **optional** enhancements for each day.  
- Introduces a brief **daily log template** (5 fields) to track progress without overwhelming detail.  
- Adds an **if/then troubleshooting** guide and a list of **clinician red‑flag** criteria (though the checklist page itself only references them).

### Core elements
1. **Wake‑time anchor** – fixed wake‑up time every day, cornerstone of circadian stability. [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]]  
2. **Morning light exposure** – 10‑30 min within the first hour; Week 2 may extend minutes. [[concepts/light_therapy]]  
3. **Caffeine cutoff** – at least 6 h before intended bedtime. [[concepts/caffeine_and_sleep]]  
4. **Exercise timing** – light‑to‑moderate activity late morning/early afternoon, adjusted for symptoms. [[concepts/exercise_and_sleep]]  
5. **Wind‑down routine** – consistent, calming steps; flexible for disorder‑related flare‑ups. [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]]  
6. **Flexible bedtime window** – 30‑60 min range, go to bed when sleepy.

### Daily structure (Day 1‑7, same each day)
- **Must do**: wake anchor, light, caffeine cutoff, exercise, wind‑down, flexible bedtime.  
- **Optional**: afternoon walk, evening stretch/warm shower, simple sleep log.

### Week 2 adjustments (Day 8‑14)
- Light exposure may be *extended* by a few minutes.  
- Wind‑down steps can be *tweaked* if they aggravate symptoms.  
- Optional additions: reduce screen brightness earlier, brief journaling, adjust room temperature/bedding.

### Daily log template (5 fields)
- Time to fall asleep (estimate)  
- Number of night awakenings  
- Morning restfulness (1‑5)  
- Daytime energy or focus (1‑5)  
- Any symptom changes or red flags

### Supporting guidance (referenced but not repeated in checklist)
- **If/then troubleshooting** for fatigue, prolonged sleep onset, early waking. [[concepts/troubleshooting_sleep_reset]]  
- **Clinician red‑flags**: worsening symptoms, new breathing issues, sharp sleep‑time drop, unsafe daytime sleepiness, confusion or cardiovascular warnings. [[concepts/clinician_red_flags]]
