## Clearance Section

A **clearance section** is a dedicated area in grocery stores and pharmacies where items that are seasonal, overstocked, or nearing expiration are sold at reduced prices.  In the *session‑14_2024‑06‑15* conversation, the clearance section is highlighted as a low‑effort savings tactic: shoppers can make a quick pass by the clearance shelf or markdown cart to capture extra discounts without joining any loyalty program.

### Key Points from the Document
- **Location**: Typically located near store exits or in a dedicated aisle marked "Clearance" or "Markdowns".
- **Product Types**: Includes produce, pantry staples, household basics, and sometimes health‑care items that are being phased out.
- **Savings Strategy**: Checking the clearance section during the in‑store quick scan (see low‑effort‑savings) can add spontaneous savings to a regular grocery list.
- **Effort Required**: Minimal – a brief visual scan while navigating the store.
