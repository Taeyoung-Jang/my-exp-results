## Budgeting App

A **budgeting app** is a software product that connects to a user’s bank accounts, credit cards, and other financial services to automatically import, categorize, and display transactions in a unified dashboard.

### Key Facts from Session 31 (2025‑02‑15)
- The assistant recommends selecting a single budgeting app as the central hub for all spending, ensuring low‑effort tracking.
- It should support **auto‑categorization** and syncing with the bank’s transaction feed.
- The user must grant the app permission to access transaction data while reviewing privacy settings to limit sharing to only the necessary information.
- The app enables a brief weekly review routine (≈10 minutes) to keep budgeting simple and consistent.
