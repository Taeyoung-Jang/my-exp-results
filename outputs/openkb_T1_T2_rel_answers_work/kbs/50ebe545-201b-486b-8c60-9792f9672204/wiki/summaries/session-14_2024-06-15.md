# Summary of session-14_2024-06-15

**Main contribution**: A concise, actionable guide for saving money at grocery and pharmacy checkout without joining loyalty or rewards programs.

---

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/rewards-programs]]** – Only worthwhile when you shop frequently and the program offers sizable discounts without heavy data collection.  
- **[[concepts/low-effort-savings]]** – Emphasizes timing, unit‑price comparison, store‑brand substitution, and clearance browsing as higher‑yield tactics.  
- **[[entities/weekly-ad]]** – A 5‑10‑minute routine to review store weekly ads (no account needed) and extract loss‑leaders, BOGO, and multi‑buy deals relevant to your regular purchases.  
- **price comparison** – Using per‑ounce or per‑tablet unit pricing to avoid overpaying on larger packages that aren’t actually cheaper.  
- **[[entities/store-brand]]** – Store‑brand items usually match ingredient quality and provide a consistent price advantage.  
- **[[entities/clearance-section]]** – Spotting seasonal or overstock items in clearance sections for additional savings.  
- **[[concepts/checkout-script]]** – Ready‑to‑use one‑liners for cashiers who ask for phone numbers or rewards info, plus alternative polite refusals.  
- **privacy concerns** – Avoiding data collection by declining loyalty enrollment.

---

## Practical Checklist

### Weekly Ad Check (2‑3 min)
1. Open the store’s weekly ad (paper or web) – no login required.  
2. Identify items you purchase regularly (produce, meat, dairy, pantry staples, household goods).  
3. Highlight **loss leaders** on the front page that fit your meal plan.  
4. Note any **buy‑one‑get‑one** or multi‑buy offers on items you already use.  
5. Add only the truly valuable sale items to your shopping list.

### In‑Store Quick Scan (5‑7 min)
- **End‑cap & clearance:** Look for unadvertised markdowns and the clearance cart.  
- **Shelf tags:** Spot temporary price drops on staples.  
- **Unit price:** Compare per‑ounce/per‑tablet values; choose the best value.  
- **[[entities/store-brand]]:** Prefer store‑brand when it’s cheaper than the sale price of name brands.  
- **Seasonal produce:** Choose items that are in season and on sale.

### Checkout Script
- Primary line: *“No thanks, I’m skipping the rewards program, but I’m good to go as is.”*  
- If pressed: *“I prefer not to use loyalty accounts, but I’ll take any public sale price.”*  
- Alternate one‑liners:
  - “I’m skipping accounts today, but I’m ready to check out.”  
  - “No loyalty number for me, thanks—just ring me up.”  
  - “I don’t use rewards programs, but I’ll take any standard sale price.”

---

## Takeaway
By limiting loyalty enrollment, exploiting weekly ads, comparing unit prices, and using a brief checkout script, shoppers can capture most discounts with minimal effort and protect their privacy.
