# Summary of Session 31 (2025‑02‑15)

## Overview
This conversation outlines a **low‑effort savings** budgeting system, practical methods for evaluating cash‑back credit cards, a minimal set of spending categories, and quick privacy/security checks when linking a bank to a budgeting dashboard.

## Simple Budgeting Setup
- Choose one primary account (debit/credit or a [[entities/budgeting-app]]) that automatically aggregates all transactions.
- Schedule a brief **weekly check‑in** (≈10 minutes) to review spending trends and adjust the upcoming week.
- Automate data capture to keep the system low‑maintenance, aligning with [[concepts/simple-budgeting]].

## Category Management
- Keep categories **simple**; avoid over‑splitting unless the distinction matters.
- Suggested umbrella categories: **groceries**, **dining**, **transportation**, **housing/bills**, **shopping/household**, **entertainment**, with optional **travel** and **health**.
- Use a **“misc”** bucket sparingly for truly odd‑ball items; convert recurring misc items into proper categories.
- For mixed‑store trips (e.g., [[entities/costco]]/[[entities/target]]) treat the whole purchase under a single category like “household.”
- Tag shared meals as a single expense and note reimbursements separately.
- Place refunds in the same category as the original purchase for clean net calculations.

## Cash‑Back Card Worth‑It Math
1. **Estimate rewards**: multiply typical spend in each bonus category by the card’s reward rate.
2. **Subtract the annual fee**.
3. For rotating categories, only count rewards if you already spend in those areas (no behavior change assumed).
4. If the net reward exceeds the fee by a comfortable margin, the card is likely worthwhile; if it merely breaks even, consider dropping it.

This process aligns with [[concepts/cash-back-evaluation]] and [[concepts/rewards-programs]].

## Privacy & Security Checklist
- Verify third‑party permissions: ensure only the budgeting dashboard has transaction access.
- Turn off optional data sharing (location tracking, marketing analytics) if not needed.
- Enable **two‑factor authentication** for the banking portal.
- Review logged‑in devices and remove any unfamiliar sessions.
- Confirm that only necessary data (transaction details) is shared; avoid granting broader access, following [[concepts/privacy-best-practices]].
