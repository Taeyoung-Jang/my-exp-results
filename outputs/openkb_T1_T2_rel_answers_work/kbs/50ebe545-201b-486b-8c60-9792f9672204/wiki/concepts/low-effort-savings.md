# Low‑Effort Savings Strategies

**Definition**: Simple, low‑time‑investment methods for reducing grocery and pharmacy bills that do not require joining loyalty or rewards programs.

---

## Core Ideas

1. **Timing & Weekly Ads**
   - Review the store’s weekly ad (paper or web) for 2‑3 minutes before shopping.  Identify regular‑purchase items, loss leaders, and BOGO or multi‑buy deals.  This aligns with the checklist in [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]].
2. **Unit‑Price Comparison**
   - Compare per‑ounce or per‑tablet prices on the shelf to ensure the cheapest overall option, rather than assuming larger packages are a better deal.
3. **Store‑Brand Preference**
   - Choose [[entities/store-brand]] products, which generally match name‑brand quality at lower cost.
4. **Clearance Hunting**
   - Visit the [[entities/clearance-section]] in both grocery and pharmacy aisles for seasonal, over‑stock, or soon‑to‑expire items that often carry deep discounts.
5. **In‑Store Quick Scan**
   - While shopping, glance at end‑caps, shelf‑tag price drops, and markdown carts.  These often mirror the savings offered to loyalty members.
6. **Checkout Script**
   - Use a brief, polite refusal line when the cashier asks for a phone number or rewards ID.  See [[concepts/checkout-script]] for sample phrasing.

---

## Benefits
- **Privacy**: No personal data is shared with retailer loyalty databases.
- **Time‑Efficiency**: Requires only 5‑10 minutes per shopping trip.
- **Broad Applicability**: Works across grocery stores, pharmacies, and many other retailers.
