# Summary of session-02_2024-06-15

**Main contribution**: a practical, 2–3 week schedule for reducing [[entities/coffee]] and [[entities/nicotine]] intake without harming productivity, plus guidance on monitoring progress.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/energy-management]]: Align daily habits with natural rhythms to sustain alertness.  
- [[concepts/alertness-maintenance]]: Use light exposure, hydration, and movement breaks instead of relying on stimulants.  
- [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]: Shift coffee earlier, weaken the first cup, and space doses to avoid afternoon crashes.  
- [[concepts/nicotine-reduction]]: Identify low‑priority cigarettes, delay or replace them with brief physical resets.  
- [[concepts/movement-breaks]]: One‑minute stretches, walks, or fresh‑air pauses act as micro‑stimulants.  
- [[concepts/mid-afternoon-slump]]: Counter with a light protein snack, water, and a short movement break.  
- [[concepts/over-reduction-signs]]: Persistent irritability, prolonged headaches, severe concentration loss, or uncontrollable cravings indicate the pace is too fast.

## 2–3 Week Schedule (High‑Level)
### Week 1 – Focus on coffee
- Keep cigarette count unchanged.  
- Make the first cup of [[entities/coffee]] slightly weaker or delay it ~15 min.  
- Space subsequent cups evenly, finish caffeine by early afternoon.  
- Use a short walk or stretch during the typical mid‑afternoon slump.

### Week 2 – Introduce nicotine adjustments
- Maintain the new coffee pattern.  
- Choose the least essential [[entities/nicotine]] (often mid‑morning/afternoon) and delay it 10–15 min.  
- Replace the delay with a 1‑minute movement break.  
- If comfortable, skip that cigarette once or twice, using the same replacement.

### Week 3 – Consolidate and expand
- Keep coffee timing stable.  
- Apply the delay/skip strategy to a second low‑priority [[entities/nicotine]].  
- Continue short physical resets for any missed nicotine cue.  
- Monitor energy, mood, and cravings; adjust pace if [[concepts/over-reduction-signs]] appear.

## Monitoring Progress
- **Positive adjustment**: mild pull or brief dip that resolves after a short break.  
- **Too fast**: ongoing irritability, lasting headaches, major concentration drop, or overwhelming cravings.  
- If any negative signs persist, hold the current level an extra few days before advancing.

## Practical Tips
- Pair [[entities/coffee]] with food, not on an empty stomach.  
- Keep a water bottle nearby; sip regularly.  
- Schedule a light snack (protein + carbs) before the usual slump.  
- Use a timer to remind you of movement or replacement breaks.  
- Track coffee strength, timing, and cigarette count in a simple log.

This schedule offers a gentle, evidence‑based pathway to raise baseline energy, reduce dependence on stimulants, and maintain productivity throughout monotonous workdays.
