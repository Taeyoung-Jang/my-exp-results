# Coffee

Coffee is a brewed beverage made from roasted coffee beans, containing caffeine that stimulates the central nervous system. In the context of the *session-02_2024-06-15* conversation, coffee is discussed as a primary tool for managing daily energy and focus.

## Key Points from the Session
- **Caffeine Timing**: The advice emphasizes adjusting [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] by weakening the first cup, delaying it by about 15 minutes, and spacing subsequent cups earlier in the day to avoid an afternoon crash.
- **Alertness Maintenance**: Coffee is presented as one of several levers for [[concepts/alertness-maintenance]], alongside light exposure, hydration, and movement breaks.
- **Energy Management**: While coffee provides a quick boost, the session stresses that sustainable [[concepts/energy-management]] relies more on sleep hygiene, regular meals, and physical activity than on stimulant reliance.
- **Mid‑Afternoon Slump**: Reducing coffee strength and timing helps mitigate the [[concepts/mid-afternoon-slump]], especially when paired with a light snack and a short [[concepts/movement-breaks]] routine.
- **Interaction with Nicotine**: The plan suggests cutting back on coffee before tackling [[entities/nicotine]] use, as caffeine habits are generally easier to modify.
- **Monitoring Over‑Reduction**: Users are warned to watch for [[concepts/over-reduction-signs]] such as persistent irritability or headaches when decreasing coffee intake too quickly.
