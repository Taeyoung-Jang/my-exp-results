# Over‑Reduction Signs

**Definition**: Observable symptoms that suggest a user is cutting back on [[entities/coffee]] or [[entities/nicotine]] faster than their body can comfortably adapt, potentially compromising focus, mood, or health.

## Key Indicators

1. **Persistent Irritability**
   - Mood stays low or volatile for hours, not just a brief pull.
2. **Prolonged Headaches**
   - Headaches that last more than a short “withdrawal” window (30‑60 minutes) and do not subside with water or a brief break.
3. **Significant Concentration Drop**
   - Inability to regain focus after a short movement break; tasks take noticeably longer.
4. **Strong Rebound Cravings**
   - Cravings that feel overwhelming, leading to an impulsive return to the previous amount of coffee or cigarettes.
5. **Physical Fatigue Beyond Normal**
   - Feeling unusually tired despite adequate sleep, hydration, and nutrition.

## How to Use This Concept

- **Monitoring**: When following the 2‑3 week reduction schedule from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]], track the above signs daily. If any appear consistently, pause the plan or extend the current week before progressing.
- **Adjustment**: Reduce the speed of change—e.g., keep coffee timing steady for an extra few days, or maintain the current cigarette count before attempting the next delay or skip.
- **Recovery**: Counter mild symptoms with the recommended baseline practices: short [[concepts/movement-breaks]], adequate water, a light protein snack, and ensuring consistent [[concepts/energy-management]] habits.
