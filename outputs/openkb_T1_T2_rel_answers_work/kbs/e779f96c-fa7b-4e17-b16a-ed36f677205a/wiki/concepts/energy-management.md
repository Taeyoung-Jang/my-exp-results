# Energy Management

**Definition**: The practice of aligning daily routines—sleep, nutrition, movement, and stimulant use—to support consistent alertness and avoid energy crashes.

---

## Overview
Energy Management is about working *with* the body’s natural rhythms rather than constantly fighting them. By structuring work, breaks, and stimulant intake, you can maintain a steady level of focus throughout the day without the peaks and troughs that lead to fatigue.

Key ideas are drawn from the guidance in [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]], which outlines a practical, 2–3 week plan for reducing reliance on [[entities/coffee]] and [[entities/nicotine]] while boosting baseline energy.

---

## Core Strategies
1. **Consistent Sleep Hygiene** – Regular sleep‑wake times create a reliable baseline for alertness.
2. **Hydration & Light Exposure** – Even mild dehydration impairs focus; brief daylight exposure lifts energy gently.
3. **Movement Breaks** – Short, frequent stretches or walks act as micro‑stimulants (see [[concepts/movement-breaks]]).
4. **Balanced Nutrition** – Light protein‑rich snacks before the typical slump help sustain glucose levels.
5. **Controlled Stimulant Use** – Adjust timing and strength of [[entities/coffee]] and schedule reductions for [[entities/nicotine]] (see [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] and [[concepts/nicotine-reduction]]).

---

## Caffeine Timing
- Shift coffee consumption earlier in the day and weaken the first cup.
- Space doses evenly and finish caffeine by early afternoon to prevent the mid‑day crash described in [[concepts/mid-afternoon-slump]].
- Pair coffee with food to smooth its impact.

---

## Nicotine Reduction
- Identify low‑priority cigarettes (often mid‑morning or early afternoon) and delay or replace them with a brief movement break.
- Gradually skip these cigarettes while using physical resets to avoid withdrawal‑driven energy dips.
- Monitor for signs of over‑reduction (see [[concepts/over-reduction-signs]]).

---

## Movement Breaks
- One‑minute stretches, a quick walk, or stepping outside for fresh air provide a gentle reset.
- Use these breaks during the mid‑afternoon slump or when a stimulant cue is delayed.
- Consistent use of movement breaks supports overall [[concepts/alertness-maintenance]].

---

## Light & Hydration
- Aim for brief exposure to natural daylight each morning and during breaks.
- Keep a water bottle at your desk; sip regularly to avoid the focus‑drag caused by dehydration.

---

## Monitoring Progress
- **Positive adjustment**: mild pull or brief dip that resolves after a short break.
- **Over‑reduction signs**: persistent irritability, lasting headaches, major concentration loss, or overwhelming cravings.
- If negative signs appear, pause the schedule and maintain the current level before advancing.
