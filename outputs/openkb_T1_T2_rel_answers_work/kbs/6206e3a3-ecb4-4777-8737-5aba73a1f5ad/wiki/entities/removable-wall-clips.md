## Removable Wall Clips

**Type:** product

**What they are**

Removable wall clips are small, low‑profile fixtures that can be attached to walls (often with adhesive backs or simple screws) and later removed without damaging the surface. They provide a smooth, predictable path for oxygen tubing, preventing the hose from snagging on doorframes, furniture corners, or floor obstacles.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-13]]**
- Recommended as one of the two essential “starter‑kit” items for people on continuous oxygen.
- Used to create a gentle “track” for tubing, especially in the living‑room, by placing clips along the wall where the tubing would otherwise drift.
- Placement rule of thumb: first clip about an arm’s length from the seated position, then additional clips only at natural turns (corners, doorframes) spaced a few feet apart, ensuring the tubing moves without tension.
- Clips should be low‑profile and easy to reposition as the room layout changes.

**Practical tip**
When testing placement, stand up from the couch, walk toward the hallway, and ensure the tubing glides smoothly. Adjust any clip a couple of inches if you feel pulling.
