# Corner Guards (product)

Corner Guards are soft, removable protectors that fit over the edges of furniture or walls to create a smooth, snag‑free surface for oxygen tubing.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-13]]
- **Purpose:** Reduce the risk of tubing catching on sharp corners of chairs, tables, or doorframes, supporting a low‑exertion environment for continuous‑oxygen users.
- **Placement:** Applied to high‑traffic furniture edges and doorframe corners as part of an [[concepts/ergonomic-home-adaptation]] strategy.
- **Interaction with other tools:** Often used together with [[entities/removable-wall-clips]] to guide tubing along walls, and complement a [[entities/doorstop]] that keeps doors fully open.
- **Benefit:** Provides a gentle “track” for the tubing, helping maintain the smooth path recommended in [[concepts/tubing-routing]].

## Typical Use Cases
1. **Living‑room furniture:** Attach to the front edges of sofas, arm‑chairs, and coffee tables where tubing may pass.
2. **Doorframes:** Fit onto the inner edge of doorways to prevent the tubing from snagging when doors are opened or closed.
3. **Temporary setups:** Easy to install and remove, making them suitable for renters or short‑term home adjustments.
