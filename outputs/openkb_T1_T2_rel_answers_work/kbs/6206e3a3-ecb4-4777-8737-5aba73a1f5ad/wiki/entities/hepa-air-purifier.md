# HEPA Air Purifier

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A small, high‑efficiency particulate air (HEPA) purifier designed for residential spaces, especially useful for people on continuous oxygen therapy.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-13]]:**
- Recommended as the primary **air‑quality** intervention in the 15‑minute and 1‑hour checklists.
- Should be placed near the main seating area in the living room to capture dust and allergens close to the user.
- Works together with other low‑effort measures such as regular fabric laundering and fragrance‑free cleaners to support [[concepts/air-quality-management]].
- Running the purifier on a higher setting during cleaning helps keep airborne particles down, complementing the low‑dust cleaning strategies discussed.
- Paired with [[entities/removable-wall-clips]] and [[entities/doorstop]] as part of a minimal “starter kit” for quick breathing‑ease improvements.
