# Indoor Dog Zone

An **Indoor Dog Zone** is a dedicated, low‑clutter corner or wall area in a living space that provides a senior dog with a predictable, safe, and enriching environment. The concept focuses on consistency, proper flooring, and selective enrichment to support older dogs who have reduced mobility and attention spans.

## Core Elements
1. **Location & Predictability**
   - Choose a quiet corner away from high‑traffic pathways.
   - Keep all items (bed, water bowl, toys) in the same spot to reinforce a reliable routine.
2. **Supportive Bedding**
   - Use a low‑side, cushioned bed that is easy to enter and exit.
3. **Non‑Slipping Flooring**
   - Place a medium‑sized rug with a non‑slip backing under the bed.
   - Add a narrow runner or a second rug to create a clear, safe path to the water bowl.
   - Rug type: low‑pile, dense‑woven cotton or similar texture that provides good traction.
4. **Essential Amenities**
   - Water bowl within easy reach.
   - One or two familiar toys for quick engagement.
5. **Mental Stimulation**
   - Incorporate [[concepts/puzzle_feeders]] such as a [[entities/snuffle_mat]] or a [[entities/wobble_feeder]] to engage the dog's nose and problem‑solving instincts.
   - Introduce these feeders with high‑value treats and simple initial setups to ensure quick success.
6. **Lighting & Visibility**
   - Ensure adequate lighting and a line of sight to the family to reduce anxiety.

## Implementation Steps (based on [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]])
1. **Select the zone** – Identify a calm corner in the living room.
2. **Lay the rugs** – Position a base rug under the bed and a runner or stepping‑stone rug leading to the water.
3. **Arrange essentials** – Set up the supportive bed, water bowl, and a favorite toy.
4. **Introduce enrichment** –
   - Start with a wobble feeder loaded with easy treats, tapping it so kibble drops out.
   - Follow with a snuffle mat where treats are initially visible, then gradually hidden.
5. **Rotate sparingly** – Swap one enrichment item at a time to keep the zone interesting without adding clutter.
