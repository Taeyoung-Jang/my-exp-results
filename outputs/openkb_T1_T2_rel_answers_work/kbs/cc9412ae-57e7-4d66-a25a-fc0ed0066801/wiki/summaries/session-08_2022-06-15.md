# Summary of session-08_2022-06-15

**Main focus:** Practical setup of a calm, predictable indoor zone that keeps an older beagle safe, comfortable, and mentally stimulated.

## Key recommendations
- **Dedicated zone:** Choose a consistent corner or wall area in the living‑room and keep all items (bed, water bowl, toys) in the same spot. ([[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]])
- **Supportive bedding:** Use a low‑side, cushioned bed that’s easy for a senior dog to get in and out of.
- **Non‑slip flooring:** 
  - Place a medium‑sized rug under the bed that extends outward.
  - Add a narrow runner or a second rug to create a clear “path” to the water bowl.
  - Choose low‑pile, woven cotton or dense‑fabric rugs with a non‑slip backing. ([[entities/non_slip_rug]])
- **Low‑clutter layout:** Limit the zone to the essentials (bed, water, 1‑2 toys). Rotate enrichment items rather than adding more.
- **Mental stimulation:** Use puzzle feeders that match a beagle’s nose‑work instincts. ([[concepts/puzzle_feeders]])
  - **Snuffle mat:** Start with treats on top, then lightly tuck them in as confidence grows. ([[entities/snuffle_mat]])
  - **Wobble feeder:** Begin with a few high‑value treats inside and gently tap the feeder so kibble falls out easily. ([[entities/wobble_feeder]])
  - Gradually increase difficulty only after the dog experiences quick successes.
- **Lighting & visibility:** Ensure good lighting and a line of sight to the family so the dog feels secure.
- **Predictability:** Keep the arrangement unchanged; only swap out a single enrichment item at a time.

## Implementation steps
1. **Select zone** – Identify a quiet corner away from high‑traffic paths.
2. **Lay rugs** – Place a base rug under the bed, add a runner from the rug to the water bowl (or use two stepping‑stone rugs).
3. **Set up essentials** – Bed, water bowl, favorite toy.
4. **Introduce enrichment** –
   - Day 1‑2: Put high‑value treats loosely in the wobble feeder; let the dog see food fall.
   - Day 3‑4: Switch to a snuffle mat with treats visible on top, then gradually bury them.
5. **Rotate** – After the dog masters one feeder, rotate to the other while keeping the base zone unchanged.
