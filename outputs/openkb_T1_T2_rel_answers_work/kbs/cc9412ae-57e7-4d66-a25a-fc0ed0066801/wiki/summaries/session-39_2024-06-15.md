## Pet Selection Guidance
- Assess **time availability**, **living space**, **budget**, **energy level**, and **desired interaction** to match a pet’s temperament [[concepts/pet_selection_criteria]].

## Greyhound Care Overview
- Greyhounds need **short bursts of exercise** (10‑20 min walks) and **plenty of rest**.
- They thrive on **predictable routines** and are generally tolerant of **alone time** when structured properly. This aligns with the [[concepts/short_exercise_long_rest]] principle and falls under [[concepts/breed_specific_care]] for greyhounds.

## Sample Weekday Routine for Luma
### Morning (7‑8 am)
- Calm wake‑up, quick bathroom break.
- Breakfast + short 10‑20 min walk for sniffing and stretch.
- Provide safe enrichment (e.g., a treat puzzle) before departure [[concepts/puzzle_feeders]].

### Midday (12‑1 pm)
- If possible, a dog‑walker or neighbor check‑in for a brief stroll or companionship.
- Otherwise, ensure a quiet, comfortable **indoor dog zone** with bed, water, and background noise (soft music/white‑noise) [[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]].

### Evening (5‑7 pm)
- Longer walk (up to 20 min) plus gentle play or a sniff‑scavenger game.
- Dinner, then settle near owner for winding down.

## Signs Luma Might Not Be Coping Alone
- Persistent vocalizing after departure.
- Accidents limited to owner‑away periods.
- Pacing, restlessness, or destructive chewing near exits.
- Over‑clinginess when you return.

## Home Setup for an 8‑Hour Absence
- Prefer a **gated room** over a crate for long periods; allow stretching.
- Provide multiple bedding options.
- Use low‑volume background noise (TV, music, white‑noise).
- Safe enrichment: durable rubber toys, frozen treat toys, a snuffle mat [[entities/snuffle_mat]], or scattered kibble; avoid anything that can be torn apart or swallowed. Puzzle feeders such as a wobble feeder [[entities/wobble_feeder]] are also suitable if they are durable.

## Key Takeaways
- Keep exercise short, rest plentiful, and schedule predictable.
- Offer safe, low‑maintenance enrichment at departure.
- Monitor for stress signs and adjust routine or seek professional help if needed.
