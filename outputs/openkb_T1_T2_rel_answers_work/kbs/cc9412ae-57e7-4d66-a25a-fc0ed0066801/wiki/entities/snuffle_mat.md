## Snuffle Mat

A **snuffle mat** is a fabric-based puzzle product that allows dogs to use their sense of smell to locate hidden treats. It consists of a dense network of strips or pockets where kibble or small snacks can be tucked in, encouraging natural foraging behavior.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]]
- Recommended for senior dogs, especially beagles like [[entities/barnaby]], because it engages their nose work without requiring complex manipulations.
- Ideal for a low‑clutter [[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]]: can be placed in the dedicated corner with the bed and water bowl.
- Works well as part of [[concepts/senior_dog_enrichment]] alongside other simple puzzle feeders.
- When introducing the mat, start with treats placed on the surface so the dog sees a quick reward, then gradually tuck them deeper as confidence grows.

### Usage tips for senior beagles
1. **Placement** – Put the mat on a non‑slip rug (e.g., [[entities/non_slip_rug]]) to keep it stable on hardwood floors.
2. **Start easy** – Sprinkle high‑value treats on top; after the dog sniffs them out, lightly bury a few treats to increase challenge.
3. **Rotate** – Alternate the snuffle mat with a [[entities/wobble_feeder]] to keep the enrichment varied without adding clutter.

### Relation to other concepts
- Part of the broader category of [[concepts/puzzle_feeders]], which also includes wobble feeders and slow‑feed bowls.
- Contributes to a calm yet stimulating environment as described in [[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]].
