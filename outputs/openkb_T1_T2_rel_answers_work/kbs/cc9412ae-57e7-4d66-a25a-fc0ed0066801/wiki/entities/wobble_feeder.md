## Wobble Feeder

A **wobble feeder** is a low‑tech puzzle feeder that releases kibble or treats when the dog nudges or pushes a weighted base. It provides mental stimulation while accommodating the physical limitations of senior dogs.

### Key characteristics from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]]
- **Ease of entry:** Start with a few high‑value treats loosely placed inside so that a single tap produces an immediate reward.
- **Gradual difficulty:** Once the dog experiences quick successes, the feeder can be loaded more densely or the opening narrowed to increase challenge.
- **Suitable for beagles:** Works well with scent‑driven breeds like [[entities/beagle]] (e.g., Barnaby) because it encourages nose work without requiring complex manipulations.
- **Integration with indoor zones:** Can be placed in a predictable [[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]] alongside a supportive bed and a non‑slip rug, keeping the overall layout low‑clutter and calm.
- **Enrichment purpose:** Part of a broader [[concepts/senior_dog_enrichment]] strategy that balances safety with mental activity.

### Practical tips for introduction
1. **Show the payoff:** Gently tap the feeder while the dog watches treats fall out.
2. **Immediate reward:** Let the dog retrieve the treat and praise it, reinforcing the behavior.
3. **Progressive loading:** After a few successful attempts, add more treats or slightly tighten the opening.
4. **Rotate with other feeders:** Alternate with a [[entities/snuffle_mat]] to maintain novelty without overwhelming the dog.
