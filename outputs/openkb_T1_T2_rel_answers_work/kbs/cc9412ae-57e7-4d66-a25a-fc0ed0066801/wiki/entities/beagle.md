# Beagle

Beagle is a breed of hound originally developed for hunting small game, especially rabbits.  They are medium‑sized, have a keen sense of smell, a friendly disposition, and are known for being vocal and energetic.

## Key traits
- **Scent‑driven:** Excellent nose work makes them thrive on sniff‑based enrichment.
- **Social:** Enjoy companionship and tend to bond closely with families.
- **Stubborn but food‑motivated:** Can be headstrong, yet respond well to high‑value treats.
- **Size & mobility:** Small to medium build, generally agile but may develop joint issues with age.
