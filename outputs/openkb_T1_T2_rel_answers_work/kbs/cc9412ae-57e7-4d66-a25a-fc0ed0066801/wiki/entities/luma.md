# Luma (Greyhound)

Luma is a three‑year‑old [[entities/greyhound]] that was recently adopted from a shelter. The owner’s primary concerns are fitting Luma’s temperament and needs into a typical workday schedule that includes about eight hours away from home.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-39_2024-06-15]]
- **Age & Breed:** 3 years old, breed is a greyhound.
- **Exercise Needs:** Requires short bursts of activity (10‑20 minutes walks) rather than long endurance sessions. This aligns with the [[concepts/short_exercise_long_rest]] principle.
- **Routine Preference:** Sensitive to routine; benefits from predictable meal, walk, and rest times.
- **Alone Time:** Generally tolerates being alone if departures are low‑key and the environment is safe and comfortable.
- **Enrichment:** Safe, unsupervised enrichment such as durable rubber toys, frozen treat toys, or a snuffle mat (if gentle) is recommended. These fall under the broader idea of [[concepts/puzzle_feeders]] and [[concepts/breed_specific_care]].
- **Home Setup:** A quiet, gated indoor space (rather than a crate) with multiple bedding options, water, and low‑volume background noise helps Luma relax during the owner’s workday. This relates to the concept of an [[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]].

## Recommended Daily Routine (summary)
- **Morning:** Quick bathroom break, breakfast, 10‑20 min walk, then a safe enrichment item before the owner leaves.
- **Midday:** If possible, a brief check‑in by a dog‑walker or neighbor; otherwise, a comfortable gated room with background noise.
- **Evening:** Slightly longer walk, gentle play or a sniff‑scavenger game, dinner, and quiet time near the owner.

## Signs of Stress
- Persistent vocalizing after departure.
- Accidents occurring only when the owner is away.
- Pacing, restlessness, or destructive chewing near exits.
- Over‑clinginess when the owner returns.

If any of these behaviors appear, the owner should consider adjusting the routine, adding more enrichment, or seeking professional advice.
