## Non‑Slip Rug

A **non‑slip rug** is a floor covering designed with a grippy backing that helps prevent pets from slipping on smooth surfaces such as hardwood. In the context of senior dog care, it provides a safe, traction‑enhanced path between a dog’s resting zone and essential items like a water bowl.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]]
- **Purpose:** Increases stability for older dogs on slick floors, reducing the risk of falls.
- **Recommended placement:** 
  - A medium‑sized rug under the dog’s bed extending outward.
  - A narrow runner or a second rug acting as a "stepping‑stone" path to the water bowl.
- **Preferred material/texture:** Low‑pile, dense‑weave cotton or similar fabric that offers grip without shifting.
- **Benefits:** Creates a predictable, low‑clutter environment that aligns with [[concepts/indoor_dog_zone]] and supports [[concepts/senior_dog_enrichment]].
