# Summary of session‑39_2025-02-13

**Main contribution:** A concrete, low‑effort system for deciding which apps to keep or delete, a weekly declutter routine, and a one‑screen home‑screen template.

---

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/minimalist-phone-management]]** – The overall philosophy of keeping only essential apps and data on the device.  
- **[[concepts/app-decluttering]]** – Five quick questions to evaluate an app’s usefulness.  
- **[[concepts/weekly-phone-maintenance]]** – A two‑minute routine (choose a day, review apps, clear downloads/screenshots/notifications).  
- **[[concepts/home-screen-organization]]** – A prescribed home‑screen structure (dock + two rows) limiting visual clutter.  
- **[[concepts/temporary-reinstall-rule]]** – Reinstall only for a specific, named task and delete immediately after.

---

## Checklist for Keeping or Deleting an App

1. Have I used this in the past week?  
2. Does it solve a real problem for me right now?  
3. Is there another app that already does the same job?  
4. Does it genuinely improve my day, or just fill time?  
5. Would I reinstall it immediately if I deleted it?

*Place the “temporary‑install reminder” at the top of this list:*  
> **Only reinstall an app for one specific task, and delete it as soon as the task is done. If I can’t name the task, I don’t reinstall it.**

---

## One‑Screen Home‑Screen Template

- **Dock:** a messenger (e.g., [[entities/whatsapp]] or [[entities/imessage]]), a calendar (e.g., [[entities/google-calendar]]), the camera, and one daily media/reading app (e.g., [[entities/kindle]]).  
- **Row 1:** most‑used utility (e.g., notes or reminders) + maps (e.g., [[entities/google-maps]]) if used daily.  
- **Row 2:** a single folder “Daily Utilities” containing alarms, quick‑settings, or other brief‑use tools.  
- **No additional rows or second screens.**

---

## Weekly Routine (2 minutes)

1. Pick a consistent day (e.g., Sunday evening).  
2. Open the app list, scroll with fresh eyes.  
3. Apply the checklist; move any “no” apps to the trash.  
4. Clear the *Downloads* folder, *Screenshots*, and dismiss all pending notifications.  
5. Verify the home‑screen follows the template.

---

## Why This Works

By limiting decision points to a few binary questions and a strict visual layout, the system reduces cognitive load, prevents gradual re‑accumulation of apps, and makes weekly maintenance trivial.
