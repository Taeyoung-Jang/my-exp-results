## Summary

- **Goal**: Help user decide which apps belong on the home screen vs. hidden or deleted.  
- **Key concepts**: [[concepts/app-decluttering]], [[concepts/home-screen-organization]], [[concepts/widget-utility]], digital minimalism, productivity workflow.

### Decision criteria
1. **Daily utility**: apps that you use every day without thinking.  
2. **Habit support**: apps that support a goal or routine.  
3. **Frequency**: apps opened within the past week.  
4. **Redundancy & inertia**: apps not opened in weeks, duplicate functionality, or pure storage.

### Practical checklist
1. Remove all icons from the home screen into a temporary folder.  
2. Add back only apps opened **today**.  
3. Review apps used in the **past week** and keep those that support daily routines.  
4. Evaluate the remaining apps: keep them in a secondary folder or delete if unused.  
5. Re‑assess after a few days and adjust.

### Recommended home‑screen size
- Aim for **6–12** apps (including a widget) to stay in the “control‑panel” sweet spot.

### Grouping & prioritization
- **Fast‑response** apps together (personal vs. work chat).  
- **Planning** zone: calendar widget + task manager.  
- **Consume** apps (reading, news) placed lower‑right.  
- Use **one** widget only if it provides quick value (e.g., calendar).

### Takeaway
- Treat the home screen as a **command center**; only keep apps that make the day easier.  
- Regularly revisit to keep clutter low and maintain focus.
