# Google Maps

**Type:** Product

Google Maps is a digital mapping and navigation service used on iPhone devices. In the context of the *session‑08_2024‑06‑15* conversation, it is identified as one of the “movement” apps that should sit on the home screen alongside Spotify to form a cohesive **planning zone**.

## Role in Home‑Screen Organization
- Placed in the second row of the suggested layout, paired with [[entities/spotify]] for quick access to navigation and media while on the go.
- Included in the recommended **8–12 app** range to keep the home screen focused and efficient, adhering to the principles of [[concepts/home-screen-organization]].
- Considered a **core app** rather than a hidden or foldered item because it is used frequently for travel and location‑based tasks.

## Relation to App Decluttering
- The conversation advises moving low‑frequency or redundant apps to folders or deleting them. Google Maps, being a high‑utility navigation tool, **passes the daily‑utility test** and therefore remains visible on the home screen, illustrating the [[concepts/app-decluttering]] criteria of daily relevance and habit support.
