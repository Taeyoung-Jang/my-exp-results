## 1Password

1Password is a password‑manager application that stores and autofills credentials across apps and websites. In the context of the **session‑08_2024‑06‑15** conversation, it is highlighted as a useful tool to keep visible on the iPhone home screen to reduce friction when accessing secure information.

### Key points from the document
- Placed in the **core set** of 8–12 home‑screen items, alongside communication, navigation, and productivity apps.
- Recommended to stay as an icon (rather than a widget) to keep the home screen **clean** while still providing fast access.
- Serves the broader goal of **[[concepts/app-decluttering]]** by being intentionally retained for its functional value.
- Fits within the **[[concepts/home-screen-organization]]** approach of grouping essential tools in a “command center.”

### Related entities and concepts
- Related to other productivity tools mentioned in the session such as [[entities/google-calendar]], [[entities/todoist]], and [[entities/spotify]].
- The recommendation aligns with the principle of keeping only high‑utility apps on the home screen, a core idea in [[concepts/home-screen-organization]].
