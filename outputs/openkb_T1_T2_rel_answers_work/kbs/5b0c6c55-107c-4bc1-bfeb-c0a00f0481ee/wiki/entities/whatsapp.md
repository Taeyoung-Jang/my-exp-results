## WhatsApp (product)

WhatsApp is a cross‑platform, end‑to‑end encrypted messaging app that enables instant text, voice, and video communication.  In the context of the *session‑08_2024‑06‑15* conversation, WhatsApp is listed as the user’s primary personal‑chat application and is recommended as one of the two personal‑chat icons to keep on the iPhone home screen.

### Role in home‑screen organization
* Grouped with iMessage as the personal‑chat pair on the top row of the suggested layout.
* Considered a **core app** because it is used frequently and supports daily communication habits.
* Falls under the broader [[concepts/app-decluttering]] and [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] guidance, where frequently used apps earn a spot on the home screen.
