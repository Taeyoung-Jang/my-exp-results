# Todoist

**Type:** Product

Todoist is a cross‑platform task‑management application that helps users capture, organize, and prioritize their to‑do items. In the context of the *session‑08_2024‑06‑15* conversation, Todoist is highlighted as a core productivity tool to place on the iPhone home screen.

## Role in the Document
- Recommended as part of the **planning zone** alongside a Google Calendar widget.  
- Suggested to sit near the calendar widget because both support daily scheduling and quick task review.  
- Presented as one of the essential apps to keep within the target 8–12 home‑screen icons, helping reduce digital clutter and improve focus.
