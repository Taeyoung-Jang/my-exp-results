# Pocket

Pocket is a read‑it‑later **product** that lets users save articles, videos, and other web content to view later, often used on mobile devices such as the [[entities/iphone]].

## Mention in [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-15]]
In the *session‑08* conversation, Pocket is listed among the user's reading/news apps alongside Apple News and Kindle. The recommendation was to place Pocket in a secondary "Reading" folder rather than on the primary home screen, supporting the [[concepts/app-decluttering]] and [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] approach.

## Related concepts
- [[concepts/app-decluttering]] – deciding which apps belong on the home screen.
- [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] – structuring the home screen layout.
- [[concepts/widget-utility]] – evaluating widgets for quick access (Pocket is not suggested as a widget).
