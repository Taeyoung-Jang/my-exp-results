# Apple News

**Type:** Product

Apple News is a news aggregation application on iOS that delivers curated news stories and personalized feeds. In the context of the *session‑08_2024‑06‑15* conversation, Apple News was identified as the primary reading/news app to keep on the home screen of an iPhone.

## Role in Home‑Screen Organization
- Selected as the single reading anchor among Apple News, Pocket, and Kindle to stay within the target of 8–12 apps.
- Placed on the home screen alongside communication, navigation, and productivity apps to form a balanced **[[concepts/home-screen-organization]]** layout.
- Not used as a widget; the recommendation favoured a minimal widget approach, reserving widget space for a **[[entities/google-calendar]]** widget instead.

## Connection to App Decluttering
- Apple News exemplifies the **[[concepts/app-decluttering]]** principle of keeping only apps that provide daily value and support habitual consumption of news.
- Its inclusion follows the criteria of daily utility and habit support outlined in the session summary.

## Related Entities
- The recommendation mentions other reading apps: **[[entities/pocket]]** and **[[entities/kindle]]**, which are suggested to reside in a secondary folder.
- It is part of an iPhone‑specific workflow, linking to the device entity **[[entities/iphone]]**.
