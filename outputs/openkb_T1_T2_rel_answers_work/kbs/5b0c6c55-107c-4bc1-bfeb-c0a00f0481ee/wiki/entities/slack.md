# Slack

Slack is a team communication platform used for work‑related messaging. In the context of the **session‑08_2024‑06‑15** conversation, Slack was identified as one of the core work‑chat apps to keep on the iPhone home screen as part of an intentional app decluttering strategy.

## Role in the Home‑Screen Layout
- Placed alongside **Microsoft Teams** as the primary work‑chat duo, grouped on one side of the screen for quick access.
- Included in the recommended 8–12 app configuration, contributing to a clear separation between personal and work communication tools.
- Helps satisfy the [[concepts/app-decluttering]] guideline of keeping only frequently used, habit‑supporting apps visible.

## Related Concepts
- The overall approach follows [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] principles, aiming for a “command center” that presents essential apps first.
- A calendar widget (Google Calendar) is suggested for quick glances, aligning with [[concepts/widget-utility]] when widgets provide tangible value.

## Connections to Other Entities
- Works in tandem with other communication apps such as [[entities/imessage]] and [[entities/whatsapp]] (personal chat) and [[entities/microsoft-teams]] (work chat).
- Shares screen real‑estate with productivity tools like [[entities/google-calendar]] and [[entities/todoist]].
- Co‑exists with entertainment and utility apps like [[entities/spotify]], [[entities/google-maps]], and [[entities/1password]].
