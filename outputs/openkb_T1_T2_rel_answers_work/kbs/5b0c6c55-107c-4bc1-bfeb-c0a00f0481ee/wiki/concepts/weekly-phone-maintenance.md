# Weekly Phone Maintenance

**Definition**: A low‑effort, repeatable routine performed once a week to review, prune, and organize the apps and data on a mobile device, ensuring it stays aligned with a [[concepts/minimalist-phone-management]] approach.

---

## Why It Matters

- Prevents gradual app bloat that erodes performance and focus.
- Reinforces the decisions made using the [[concepts/app-decluttering]] checklist.
- Keeps the home‑screen layout consistent with the [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] rules.
- Supports the [[concepts/temporary-reinstall-rule]] by reminding you to remove short‑term installs promptly.

---

## Core Steps (≈2 minutes)

1. **Pick a Consistent Day** – e.g., Sunday evening or the first workday of the month.
2. **Open the App List** – Scroll through with fresh eyes.
3. **Apply the App‑Deletion Checklist** – Evaluate each app against the five questions from [[concepts/app-decluttering]].
4. **Delete or Archive** – Remove any app that fails the checklist.
5. **Clear Auxiliary Files** – Empty the *Downloads* folder, delete old *Screenshots*, and dismiss all pending notifications.
6. **Verify Home‑Screen Layout** – Ensure the dock and rows match the template described in [[concepts/home-screen-organization]].
7. **Log the Outcome (optional)** – Note the number of apps removed or any temporary reinstalls.

---

## Tips for Success

- **Set a Timer** – 2 minutes is enough; the goal is consistency, not perfection.
- **Use a Reminder** – Add a recurring calendar event (e.g., via Google Calendar) to trigger the routine.
- **Keep the Checklist Visible** – Paste the checklist from the session‑39 notes to your notes app for quick reference.
- **Stay Strict on Temporary Installs** – If you reinstall something, write down the exact task; delete it right after completion, per [[concepts/temporary-reinstall-rule]].

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- Works hand‑in‑hand with the structural guidance of [[concepts/home-screen-organization]].
- Reinforces the discipline of [[concepts/temporary-reinstall-rule]].
