# Minimalist Phone Management

## Overview
Minimalist Phone Management is a low‑effort strategy for reducing digital clutter by keeping only the apps and data that provide real, daily value. It combines a concise **app‑decluttering checklist**, a strict **home‑screen organization** rule, a brief **weekly maintenance** routine, and a clear **temporary reinstall** policy.

## Core Components

### 1. App Decluttering Checklist
Use the five‑question checklist to decide whether an app stays on the device:
1. Have I used this in the past week?
2. Does it solve a real problem for me right now?
3. Is there another app that already does the same job?
4. Does it genuinely improve my day, or just fill time?
5. Would I reinstall it immediately if I deleted it?

This checklist is the heart of [[concepts/app-decluttering]].

### 2. Home‑Screen Organization
Adopt a **one‑screen layout**:
- **Dock:** messenger, calendar, camera, and one daily media/reading app.
- **Row 1:** most‑used utility (e.g., notes) and maps if used daily.
- **Row 2:** a single folder for daily utilities (alarms, quick settings).
- No additional rows or second screens.

The layout enforces the principle of [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] and limits visual overload.

### 3. Weekly Phone Maintenance
Set aside a two‑minute slot each week (e.g., Sunday evening) to:
- Review the app list with the checklist.
- Clear the Downloads folder, screenshots, and pending notifications.
- Verify the home‑screen follows the one‑screen template.

This routine embodies [[concepts/weekly-phone-maintenance]].

### 4. Temporary Reinstall Rule
Reinstall an app only for a **specific, named task** and delete it immediately after the task is completed. If you cannot name the task, the app stays uninstalled. This rule is captured in [[concepts/temporary-reinstall-rule]].

## Benefits
- **Reduced decision fatigue** – Fewer apps mean fewer choices.
- **Improved performance** – Less storage and background activity.
- **Clear mental space** – A tidy phone mirrors a tidy mind.
