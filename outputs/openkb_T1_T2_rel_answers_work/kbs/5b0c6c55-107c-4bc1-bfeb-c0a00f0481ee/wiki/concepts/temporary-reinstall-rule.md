# Temporary Reinstall Rule

**Definition**: Reinstall an app only when you have a clearly defined, single‑use task, and uninstall it as soon as that task is completed.

**Why it matters**: Prevents apps from lingering on the device, supporting [[concepts/minimalist-phone-management]] and reducing digital clutter.

**Key steps**
1. Identify the specific task that requires the app.
2. Reinstall the app solely for that purpose.
3. Complete the task.
4. Uninstall the app immediately afterwards.
5. If you cannot name a concrete task, do not reinstall.

**Relation to other concepts**
- Works together with [[concepts/app-decluttering]] by providing a clear criterion for temporary app presence.
- Complements the [[concepts/weekly-phone-maintenance]] routine, which includes reviewing any temporarily reinstalled apps.
- Fits within the broader [[concepts/home-screen-organization]] strategy by keeping the home screen limited to permanent, daily‑use apps.
- Supports the overall [[concepts/minimalist-phone-management]] philosophy.
