# Madison, Wisconsin

Madison is the capital city of Wisconsin, situated on an isthmus between Lake Mendota and Lake Monona. It is highlighted in [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-10]] as a primary stop on a week‑long, dog‑friendly road trip with a senior Labrador.

## Role in the Itinerary
- Serves as the first overnight destination after departing from [[entities/chicago]].
- Recommended driving time from Chicago is about three hours, fitting the [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]] guideline of short daily legs.
- Offers shaded rest stops along I‑90 such as the Belvidere Oasis and Turtle Creek wayside.

## Dog‑Friendly Attractions
- **UW Arboretum** – Featured as an easy, flat, and shaded walking area; ideal for senior dogs. See [[entities/uw-arboretum]].
- **Picnic Point** – Lakeshore path with gentle terrain and shade, suitable for short 20–30 minute walks.
- Both locations comply with on‑leash requirements year‑round, aligning with the [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]] concept.

## Nearby Related Places
- **Baraboo, Wisconsin** – Next leg of the trip, reachable in ~1.5 h. [[entities/baraboo-wisconsin]]
- **Wisconsin Dells** – Alternative stop in the same region. [[entities/wisconsin-dells]]
- **Lake Geneva, Wisconsin** – Final destination before returning to Chicago. [[entities/lake-geneva-wisconsin]]
- **Mirror Lake State Park** – Nearby state park offering flat trails for senior dogs. [[entities/mirror-lake-state-park]]
- **Big Foot Beach State Park** – Further south, another dog‑friendly park. [[entities/big-foot-beach-state-park]]

## Practical Tips
- Use the [[concepts/rest-stop-vetting-checklist]] to quickly identify shaded, safe stops along the route.
- For senior dogs with arthritis, follow the [[concepts/senior-dog-arthritis-management]] recommendations: provide a supportive car seat, schedule walks during mid‑morning when joints are warmed, and watch for red‑flag symptoms.
