# UW Arboretum

## Overview
UW Arboretum is a public natural area located in [[entities/madison-wisconsin]]. It offers mostly flat, shaded trails that are highly suitable for senior dogs on road trips.

## Relevance to the road‑trip itinerary
- Recommended as a morning or afternoon walk on Day 1 and Day 2 of the Chicago → Madison → Baraboo/Dells → Lake Geneva loop.
- Provides gentle loops such as the Wingra Woods area and the Grady Tract, both with soft footing and plenty of tree cover.
- Easy water access at nearby lake‑shore paths without rocky edges.

## Dog‑friendly features
- Trails are on‑leash required, flat, and shaded – aligning with the [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]] guidelines.
- Ideal for dogs with arthritis, supporting the recommendations in [[concepts/senior-dog-arthritis-management]] (short walks, warm‑up before car rides).

## Practical tips
- Arrive early to avoid crowds and keep walks within the 20–30 minute window.
- Bring a collapsible water bowl; the arboretum has water fountains nearby.
- Use the [[concepts/rest-stop-vetting-checklist]] to confirm the parking area has ample green space before stopping.
