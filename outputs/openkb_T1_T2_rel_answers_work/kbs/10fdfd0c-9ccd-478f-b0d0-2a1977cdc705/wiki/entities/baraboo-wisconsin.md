# Baraboo, Wisconsin

Baraboo is a city in Sauk County, Wisconsin, located a short drive north of the Wisconsin Dells. It serves as a convenient stop on the dog‑friendly road‑trip itinerary outlined in [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-10]].

## Key facts from the itinerary
- **Proximity to dog‑friendly attractions:** Baraboo gives easy access to **Mirror Lake State Park** and the **Wisconsin Dells** area, both highlighted for their flat, shaded trails suitable for a senior Labrador.
- **Suggested rest stops:** The route recommends stopping at the **Dekorra wayside** near the Wisconsin River and the **Cascade Mountain pull‑off**, both state‑run locations offering tree cover and parking for quick breaks.
- **Dog‑friendly walks:** Recommended walks include the Echo Rock Trail and Wild Rice Trail in Mirror Lake State Park, as well as a short shoreline stroll on the lake’s gentle sandy edge.
- **Arthritis‑friendly considerations:** The itinerary notes that Baraboo’s trails are mostly flat, with soft footing and shaded canopy, aligning with [[concepts/senior-dog-arthritis-management]] guidelines for minimizing joint strain.
- **Calming options:** While in Baraboo, owners can employ non‑prescription calming aids such as anxiety vests, familiar blankets, or pheromone sprays, as discussed in [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]].
