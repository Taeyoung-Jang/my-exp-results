# Big Foot Beach State Park

**Type:** Place (State Park)

Big Foot Beach State Park is a Wisconsin state park located on the shores of Lake Geneva.  It is highlighted in the **session-06_2024-06-10** itinerary as a dog‑friendly stop that provides flat, shaded forest trails and gentle grassy lake edges – ideal for senior dogs with arthritis.

## Key Points from the Document
- **Dog‑friendly trails:** The park offers easy, mostly flat loops through forested areas, perfect for 20‑30 minute walks with a senior Labrador.
- **Water access:** Unlike rocky shorelines, the park’s shoreline is grassy and sandy, giving a safe place for a dog to splash without slipping.
- **Pet policies:** Dogs are allowed on‑leash throughout most of the park, though they are restricted from the designated swimming beach.
- **Backup option:** If the main trail becomes busy or hot, nearby residential loops such as Maple Park or Seminary Park provide low‑effort alternatives.
