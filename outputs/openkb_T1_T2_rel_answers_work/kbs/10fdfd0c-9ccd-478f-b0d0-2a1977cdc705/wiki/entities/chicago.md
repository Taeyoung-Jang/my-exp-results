# Chicago

Chicago is the departure point for the seven‑day road trip outlined in [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-10]]. The itinerary is designed around a senior Labrador’s needs, limiting daily drives to **3–4 hours** and emphasizing shaded rest stops and easy, water‑friendly walks.

## Key Points from the Trip Plan
- **Driving limits**: From Chicago the route heads to [[entities/madison-wisconsin]] (≈3 h), then to [[entities/baraboo-wisconsin]]/[[entities/wisconsin-dells]] (≈1.5 h), and finally to [[entities/lake-geneva-wisconsin]] (≈2 h) before returning.
- **Rest‑stop strategy**: Uses the [[concepts/rest-stop-vetting-checklist]] to quickly identify state‑run rest areas with trees and picnic shelters, ensuring shade and safe space for a senior dog.
- **Dog‑friendly travel**: The overall plan follows principles from [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]], such as selecting pet‑welcome hotels, keeping the car cool, and packing essential pet supplies.
- **Arthritis management**: Incorporates advice from [[concepts/senior-dog-arthritis-management]], like using a thick foam pad in the car, timing walks for mid‑morning when joints are warmed, and monitoring red‑flag symptoms.
