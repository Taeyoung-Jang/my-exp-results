## Wisconsin Dells

**Type:** Place

**Location:** Central Wisconsin, USA, situated roughly 1.5 hours west of Madison and 2 hours north‑east of Baraboo.

**Relevance to session‑06_2024‑06‑10**
- Featured as a key stop in the 7‑day dog‑friendly road‑trip itinerary for a senior Labrador.  The plan recommends spending two nights in the Wisconsin Dells area to keep daily driving time short and provide ample rest for the dog.
- Suggested activities include short, flat walks such as the Echo Rock Trail at nearby Mirror Lake State Park and gentle shoreline strolls that offer water access without slippery rocks.
- The location benefits from easy access to shaded rest stops along I‑90/I‑94, typically state‑run rest areas or roadside parks that meet the [[concepts/rest-stop-vetting-checklist]].

**Dog‑Friendly Highlights**
- **Flat, shaded trails:** Mirror Lake State Park (near Baraboo) offers the Echo Rock and Wild Rice trails, both short, level, and tree‑covered – ideal for a senior dog with arthritis.
- **Water access:** Calm shoreline sections at Mirror Lake provide grassy or sandy edges for safe paw contact, avoiding the rocky boulders common at Devil’s Lake.
- **Calming environment:** The low‑traffic, scenic routes support the recommendations from [[concepts/senior-dog-arthritis-management]] and [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]] for stress‑free travel.

**Typical Amenities**
- Pet‑friendly lodging options that allow dogs on leashes, often with ground‑floor rooms and easy outdoor access.
- Numerous dining establishments with outdoor patios where dogs are welcome.
- Convenient parking areas and picnic sites that provide shade and space for water bowls.
