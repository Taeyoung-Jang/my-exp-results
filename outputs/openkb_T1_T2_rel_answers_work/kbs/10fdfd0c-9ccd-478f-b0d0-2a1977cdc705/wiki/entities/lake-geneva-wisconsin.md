# Lake Geneva, Wisconsin

Lake Geneva is a scenic resort town on the southern shore of Geneva Lake in southeastern Wisconsin. In the **session‑06_2024‑06‑10** road‑trip itinerary it serves as the final multi‑night stop before returning to Chicago. The town offers several dog‑friendly walking options that are flat, shaded, and provide gentle water access, making it ideal for senior dogs with arthritis.

## Key Dog‑Friendly Features
- **Flat, shaded walks**: The recommended routes include the **Big Foot Beach State Park** forest trails and a short segment of the **Lake Geneva Shore Path** between Library Park and the Riviera area, both offering soft ground and tree cover.
- **Gentle water edges**: At Big Foot Beach the lake meets grassy/sandy banks, allowing the dog to sip or dip paws without slipping on rocks.
- **Low‑effort backup loops**: If the main trails are busy or warm, nearby residential loops such as the Maple Park neighborhood or Seminary Park provide quiet, shaded pathways.
