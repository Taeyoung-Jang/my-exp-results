# Mirror Lake State Park

Mirror Lake State Park is a Wisconsin state‑run recreation area renowned for its **flat, shaded walking paths** and **gentle shoreline** that provide safe water access for dogs, especially senior dogs with arthritis.

## Key Points from the Road‑Trip Guide (session‑06_2024‑06‑10)
- **Dog‑friendly trails**: The park’s Echo Rock Trail and Wild Rice Trail are short (20‑30 min), mostly level, and covered by mature trees, fitting the recommendations of [[concepts/dog-friendly-travel]].
- **Water access**: The calm, grassy edge of Mirror Lake offers a slip‑free spot for dogs to dip paws, aligning with advice for senior dogs in [[concepts/senior-dog-arthritis-management]].
- **Rest stop considerations**: When traveling the Chicago → Madison → Baraboo/Dells → Lake Geneva loop, Mirror Lake serves as a convenient **mid‑day stop** that meets the rapid vetting criteria described in [[concepts/rest-stop-vetting-checklist]] (state‑run area, ample shade, parking nearby).
- **Nearby attractions**: The park is located near **Baraboo, Wisconsin** and the **Wisconsin Dells**, both mentioned in the itinerary and listed as [[entities/baraboo-wisconsin]] and [[entities/wisconsin-dells]].

## Practical Tips for Visiting with a Senior Labrador
- Use a **thick foam pad or dog bed** in the car before arrival to reduce joint stiffness.
- Schedule the walk for **mid‑morning** after a short pre‑drive stretch to warm joints.
- Keep your dog **on‑leash** at all times; the park allows leashed dogs on all trails but restricts the main swim beach.
- Bring **water and a collapsible bowl**, and watch for red‑flag signs of arthritis flare‑ups (limping, excessive panting).
