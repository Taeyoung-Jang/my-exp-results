# Lease Management and Key Clauses

**Overview**

Effective [[concepts/lease-management]] starts with a clear understanding of the most critical lease provisions. These clauses dictate day‑to‑day responsibilities, protect both tenant and landlord, and help avoid costly disputes.

---

## Core Lease Clauses

| Clause | Why It Matters | Typical Tenant Questions |
|--------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **Rent amount & calculation** | Determines your monthly cost. | How is rent calculated? Are there any additional fees? |
| **Payment schedule & due date** | Sets the timing of cash flow. | When is rent due and what are acceptable payment methods? |
| **Late fees** | Encourages timely payments. | What is the grace period and fee structure for late rent? |
| **Utility responsibilities** | Clarifies who pays for electricity, water, etc. | Which utilities am I responsible for and are any included in rent? |
| **Maintenance & repairs** | Defines who fixes what and response times. | Who handles routine maintenance vs. emergency repairs? |
| **Guest, noise, and pet policies** | Impacts daily living and community harmony. | What are the rules for guests, noise levels, and pets (if any)? |
| **Subletting & assignment** | Controls who can occupy the unit. | Am I allowed to sublet or assign the lease? |
| **Parking & storage** | Addresses extra space needs. | Is parking guaranteed and are there storage units? |
| **Move‑in / move‑out condition requirements** | Protects security‑deposit integrity. | What condition is required at move‑in and move‑out? |
| **Security deposit handling** | Governs how deposits are collected and returned. | How is the security deposit held and what deductions are permissible? |

## Best Practices

1. **Read the lease line‑by‑line** and flag any ambiguous language.
2. **Ask for written clarification** on any clause that impacts cost or access.
3. **Document all agreements** (e.g., inspection notice periods) in email or addenda.
4. **Know local notice laws** to ensure the lease complies with state or city regulations.
5. **Maintain a copy** of the signed lease and any supplemental policies for easy reference.
