# Summary of session-38_2025-02-10

**Topic:** Preparing for a lease move‑in with a strict no‑pets policy and clarifying inspection procedures.

**Key Points**
- **Lease clauses to verify** – rent calculation, payment schedule, late fees, utility responsibilities, maintenance responsibilities, guest/noise/pet/subletting rules, parking, storage, move‑in/move‑out condition standards, security‑deposit handling. See [[concepts/lease-management]].
- **Questions for landlord** – maintenance request process, upcoming building repairs, move‑in logistics (elevator reservations, loading zones, fees), package‑delivery handling, after‑hours emergency repairs, building quirks. See [[concepts/tenant-landlord-communication]].
- **Pet‑policy focus** – understand trigger mechanisms for surprise inspections, notice period, authorized entrants, documentation standards, definition of violations, handling of unfounded complaints, and treatment of temporary situations (pet‑sitting, guest animals, service/assistance animals). See [[concepts/inspection-process]].
- **Concrete checklist** (for email) covering:
  1. Inspection triggers and notice timing.  
  2. Who may enter during an inspection.  
  3. Documentation and evidence standards.  
  4. Procedure for unfounded complaints and tenant response rights.  
  5. Treatment of temporary/incipient animal presence.  
  6. Handling of legitimate service/assistance animals and required documentation.  
  7. Request for written policy despite signed addendum.
- **Polite email draft** – concise, respectful request for clarification and written policy, also asks landlord to confirm compliance with local notice‑requirement laws.

**Takeaway:** A clear, written record of inspection processes and pet‑policy enforcement protects both tenant and landlord from misunderstandings and potential disputes.
