# Caregiver Constraints

**Definition**: The set of practical limits that a primary caregiver experiences, shaping what kinds of work, learning, and hobby activities are feasible.

## Core Elements (as described in [[summaries/session-07_2022-09-15]])
- **Time windows**: Only 30–60 minutes per session, usually in the evenings; some weeks may have **zero** available time.
- **No synchronous communication**: No phone calls, video meetings, or scheduled check‑ins.
- **Flexibility requirement**: Tasks must be interruptible and resumable without penalty.
- **Skill baseline**: Familiarity with spreadsheets, basic writing, and admin work, but skills may be rusty.

## Impact on Work Choices
- Favors **[[concepts/interruptible_work]]** such as spreadsheet cleanup over writing that may need back‑and‑forth.
- Necessitates a **[[concepts/task_vetting_checklist]]** to quickly filter out tasks that could become ongoing or require meetings.
- Drives a **[[concepts/micro_planning]]** approach: breaking goals into 30‑60 minute micro‑sessions.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/interruptible_work]] – work that can be paused and resumed.
- [[concepts/micro_planning]] – planning in tiny, time‑boxed increments.
- [[concepts/task_vetting_checklist]] – checklist to ensure tasks respect caregiver constraints.
- [[concepts/marketplace_search_keywords]] – keywords to locate suitable one‑off gigs.

## Practical Applications
1. **Task Selection**: Use the checklist to verify a task can be completed within a few short sessions and requires no live communication.
2. **Skill Refresh**: Focus on bite‑size learning (e.g., a single spreadsheet function per session) that fits the time window.
3. **Marketplace Search**: Apply specific keywords (see [[concepts/marketplace_search_keywords]]) on platforms like [[entities/upwork]], [[entities/fiverr]], [[entities/remoteok]], and [[entities/weworkremotely]] to find compatible gigs.
