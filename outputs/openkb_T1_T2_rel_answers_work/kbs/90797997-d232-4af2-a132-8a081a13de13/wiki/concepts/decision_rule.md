# Decision Rule

**Definition**: A concise, 2–3 question checklist used on Fridays to decide whether to follow a low‑energy (minimum‑viable) or higher‑energy weekend template.

---

## Overview
The Decision Rule helps translate how you feel at the end of the work week into concrete planning choices for the weekend. By answering three simple prompts, you quickly determine the appropriate version of your [[concepts/weekend_template]].

---

## Core Questions
1. **Body Check** – *How does my physical/mental energy feel?*  
   - Energized → consider the higher‑energy template.
   - Drained or tired → lean toward the low‑energy version.
2. **Social Craving** – *Do I want company or solitude?*  
   - Craving connection → higher‑energy (adds a social block).
   - Preferring quiet → low‑energy (keeps open space).
3. **Capacity Assessment** – *Does adding another plan feel exciting or heavy?*  
   - Exciting → higher‑energy.
   - Heavy → low‑energy.

---

## How to Use It
1. **On Friday afternoon**, pause for a minute and answer the three questions.
2. **Select the template**:
   - If 2+ answers point to “low‑energy”, apply the minimum‑viable weekend schedule.
   - If 2+ answers point to “higher‑energy”, use the more active version.
3. **Adjust as needed** – The rule is a guide, not a rigid mandate; you can tweak blocks after the decision.

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/intentional_weekend_planning]] by providing a decision point for the level of intentionality.
- Supports [[concepts/energy_management]] by aligning weekend structure with current energy levels.
- Complements [[concepts/boundary_setting]]: the chosen template often dictates how you set boundaries (e.g., sending a low‑energy decline message).
- Forms part of a broader **micro‑planning** approach, breaking down weekend planning into small, actionable decisions.

---

## Example Prompt
> *“I’m feeling pretty wiped after this week, I don’t really want to meet anyone, and the thought of another plan feels heavy.”* → Choose the low‑energy template.
