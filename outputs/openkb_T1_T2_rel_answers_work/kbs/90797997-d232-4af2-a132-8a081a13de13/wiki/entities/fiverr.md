## Fiverr

Fiverr is an **online freelance marketplace** where individuals and businesses can post short‑term, one‑off gigs such as spreadsheet cleanup, data entry, or simple automation tasks. It is frequently cited as a source of **[[concepts/interruptible_work]]** because tasks can be taken, completed in a single sitting, and do not require scheduled meetings or phone calls.

### Relevance to session‑07_2022‑09‑15
The document "[[summaries/session-07_2022-09-15]]" recommends Fiverr alongside other platforms (Upwork, RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely) as a place to find **[[concepts/marketplace_search_keywords]]** like "spreadsheet cleanup" or "data cleanup". The suggested workflow relies on the **[[concepts/task_vetting_checklist]]** to ensure gigs are truly interruptible and fit within limited 30–60 minute slots, aligning with the caregiver’s **[[concepts/caregiver_constraints]]**.

### How it fits the micro‑planning approach
Using Fiverr supports **[[concepts/micro_planning]]**: you can browse for a task in one short session, complete the work in one to three 30‑minute bursts, and then move on. This keeps the effort low‑pressure and allows you to reassess each week.
