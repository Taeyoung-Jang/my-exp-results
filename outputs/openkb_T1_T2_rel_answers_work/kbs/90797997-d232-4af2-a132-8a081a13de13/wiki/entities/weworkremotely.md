# We Work Remotely

We Work Remotely is an online job board that lists remote, freelance, and contract positions across a variety of industries. It was cited in [[summaries/session-07_2022-09-15]] as one of the marketplaces where a caregiver can find **one‑off spreadsheet cleanup** or data‑entry tasks that fit a limited 30–60 minute schedule.

## Key facts
- **Category:** Remote‑work job marketplace (organization).
- **Typical listings:** Short‑term projects such as "spreadsheet cleanup," "CSV formatting," or "data deduplication" that have a fixed scope and no required meetings.
- **Relevance to concepts:**
  - Supports [[concepts/interruptible_work]] by offering tasks that can be paused and resumed.
  - Aligns with [[concepts/micro_planning]] for breaking a larger income goal into tiny sessions.
  - Uses [[concepts/marketplace_search_keywords]] like "spreadsheet cleanup" or "one‑off" to filter suitable gigs.
  - Complementary to the [[concepts/task_vetting_checklist]] for quickly assessing task suitability.
  - Helpful for individuals facing [[concepts/caregiver_constraints]] who need flexible, low‑commitment work.
- **Related platforms:** Similar marketplaces include [[entities/upwork]], [[entities/fiverr]], and [[entities/remoteok]]. Community discussions about these sites often happen in [[entities/discord]] channels.

## How it was used in the session
The assistant recommended checking We Work Remotely (alongside Upwork, Fiverr, RemoteOK) and searching with keywords such as "spreadsheet cleanup" or "data cleanup" to locate one‑time, interruptible gigs that respect the caregiver's time constraints.
