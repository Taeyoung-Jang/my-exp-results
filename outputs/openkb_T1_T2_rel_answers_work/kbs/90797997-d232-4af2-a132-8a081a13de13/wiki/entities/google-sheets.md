## Google Sheets

Google Sheets is a cloud‑based spreadsheet application that allows users to create, edit, and collaborate on spreadsheet files online. It supports a wide range of functions, data‑visualisation tools, and integrations with other Google Workspace products.

### Relevance to **session‑07_2022‑09‑15**
The caregiver in this session plans to improve and monetize their spreadsheet skills using short, interruptible work sessions. Google Sheets is cited as one of the platforms on which they can practice functions such as **SUMIF**, **VLOOKUP**, and **FILTER**, and later offer **spreadsheet cleanup** or **data‑tidying** services to clients.

### Key Points from the Document
- **Micro‑planning**: The 8–12 week plan recommends spending 30–60 minute slots learning individual functions in Google Sheets before taking on paid tasks. [[concepts/micro_planning]]
- **Interruptible work**: Tasks like cleaning up a sheet or deduplicating rows are highlighted as ideal because they can be started and stopped without penalty. [[concepts/interruptible_work]]
- **Task vetting**: When selecting jobs, the user should ensure the scope is limited to a single Google Sheets file and can be finished in a few short sessions. [[concepts/task_vetting_checklist]]
- **Marketplace search**: Suggested keywords for finding one‑off spreadsheet gigs include “Google Sheets tidy” and “Google Sheets cleanup.” [[concepts/marketplace_search_keywords]]
- **Caregiver constraints**: The plan is specifically designed around the caregiver’s limited evening availability and occasional weeks with no time. [[concepts/caregiver_constraints]]
