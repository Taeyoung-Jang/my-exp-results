# Summary of session-15_2025-06-15

**Date:** 2025-06-15

## Core Topic
The conversation centers on evaluating active versus passive commuting options—biking, public transit (including bike‑to‑transit), and driving—through the lenses of **safety**, **cost**, and **day‑to‑day practicality**.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/active_commuting]]: Using biking or mixed modes to incorporate exercise into the daily commute.
- [[concepts/mode_comparison]]: Systematic comparison of biking, transit, and driving based on safety, cost, reliability, and practicality.
- reliability measurement: Tracking door‑to‑door times, wait times, and missed connections to assess consistency.
- safety assessment: Evaluating lighting, visibility, and comfort at stops and on routes.
- cost analysis: Accounting for fares, parking, bike storage fees, fuel, and bike maintenance.
- [[concepts/commute_tracking]]: A structured template for two‑week data collection, including baseline bike metrics and AM/PM breakdowns.

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Baseline Establishment** – Use the existing bike commute as a performance baseline (time and cost) for all comparisons.
2. **Holistic Door‑to‑Door View** – Measure the entire journey, not just transit schedules, to capture real‑world experience.
3. **Two‑Week Trial** – Collect data over multiple days to capture normal variation and identify patterns in reliability and safety.
4. **Template Enhancements** – The provided tracking template now captures:
   - Daily bike baseline (time & cost)
   - Separate AM and PM entries
   - Time and cost deltas versus the bike baseline
   - Safety/comfort notes per trip segment
5. **Decision Guidance** – After the trial, compare average deltas and qualitative safety notes to decide which mode best balances activity, predictability, and expense.

## Practical Output
- A **plain‑text tracking template** (shown in the conversation) that users can duplicate for each day of the test period.
- Guidance on how to interpret the collected data to make an informed commuting choice.
