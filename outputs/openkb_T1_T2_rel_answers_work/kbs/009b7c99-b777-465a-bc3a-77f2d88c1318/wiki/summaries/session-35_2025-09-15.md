**Summary of session‑35_2025‑09‑15**

*Document type:* Conversation – advice on adapting a morning routine to a temporary mobility limitation, specifically a walking boot.

### Key Concepts
- **Night‑before preparation** – lay out clothes, boot, mobility aid, water bottle, phone, and breakfast items so nothing needs to be searched for in the morning. [[concepts/night_before_preparation]]
- **Boot‑centric morning stations** – small clusters of needed items placed strategically in three rooms:
  - **By the bed**: boot (if removable), water bottle, phone, first‑layer clothing.
  - **Bathroom**: toothbrush, face wash, deodorant, other essentials kept together for seated use.
  - **Kitchen “launch pad”**: travel mug, pre‑portioned breakfast, coffee‑maker mug, bag/keys near the exit. [[entities/travel_mug]]
- **Single‑chair placement** – the bedroom chair/stool is the most valuable single addition; it enables seated dressing and reduces balance strain.
- **Timed template (30‑45 min)** – flexible phases with “low‑soreness” vs. “more‑sore” variants:
  1. **0‑10 min** – sit up, stabilize, boot adjustments, basic hygiene (mostly seated on sore days).
  2. **10‑25 min** – dressing while seated, using a reacher or long‑handled shoehorn, pulling pants over the boot in small gathers. [[entities/reacher]]
  3. **25‑45 min** – kitchen station work: brew coffee into a leak‑proof travel mug, grab pre‑prepared food, finish grooming, then exit.
- **Must‑have tools** – stable armless chair, travel mug with secure lid, reacher or long‑handled shoehorn. [[entities/walking_boot]]
- **Stair safety** – use handrail, lead with stronger leg uphill, boot side first downhill, move one step at a time, avoid carrying items on the first climb. [[entities/handrail]]
- **Carrying items** – keep hands free with a cross‑body bag, hoodie pocket, or phone in waistband; place coffee on a step if needed, otherwise wait until stable.

### Practical Checklists
#### Night‑before checklist
- Lay out the boot (or removable insert) and place beside the bed.
- Set out the next‑day outfit; keep pants, socks, and under‑garments within arm’s reach.
- Fill a water bottle; place the phone on the nightstand.
- Prepare breakfast: portion cereal, fruit, or a ready‑to‑eat snack; place the mug near the coffee maker.
- Pack the work bag, keys, and any needed medication by the door.

#### Morning sequence (low‑soreness)  
1. Sit up, wiggle toes, adjust boot straps.
2. Perform a quick bathroom routine seated (brush teeth, wash face).
3. Dress seated: pull on underwear, socks, pants, then stand to slip the pant leg over the boot; use a reacher/shoehorn if needed.
4. Walk to the kitchen station, brew coffee into the travel mug, grab pre‑portioned food, eat while seated if desired.
5. Grab bag/keys and exit.

#### Morning sequence (more‑sore)  
- Add 1‑2 min extra seated time for breathing and stability checks.
- Keep all dressing items within immediate reach; skip non‑essential accessories.
- Perform kitchen tasks solely from a single spot; avoid walking back and forth.

### Safety & Energy‑Saving Tips
- **Stairs:** use the handrail, one step at a time, avoid carrying items on the first ascent.
- **Carrying:** use a travel mug with a tight lid; keep phone/keys in a cross‑body bag or waistband; set items on a step if needed, otherwise wait until fully steady.
- **Energy management:** prioritize “good enough” over perfection; break tasks into short steps with brief rests.
