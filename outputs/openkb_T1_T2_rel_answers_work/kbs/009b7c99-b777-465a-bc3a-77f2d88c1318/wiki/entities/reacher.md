# Reacher (grabber tool)

**Type:** product

A **reacher** (also called a grabber) is a lightweight, extendable hand‑held tool that lets a person pick up or pull objects from a distance, reducing the need to bend, stretch, or balance on one leg.  It typically consists of a long pole with a trigger‑activated clasp at the end.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-35_2025-09-15]]
- Recommended as one of the **must‑have tools** for a boot‑friendly morning routine.
- Helps with **night‑before preparation** by allowing the user to place clothing, socks, or a water bottle within easy reach while still seated.
- Supports **morning routine planning** by enabling the user to retrieve items (e.g., phone, keys, coffee mug) without extra steps or loss of balance.
- Works well alongside other mobility aids such as a **[[entities/walking_boot]]** and a stable **[[entities/handrail]]**.
- Often used together with a **[[entities/travel_mug]]** to set the mug in place before lifting it, minimizing spill risk.

## Typical Uses
- Pulling a shoe or boot strap when the boot is on the floor.
- Grabbing a phone or remote from a night‑stand without twisting the torso.
- Retrieving a pre‑portioned breakfast container from a higher shelf during the **[[concepts/morning_routine_planning]]** phase.
- Assisting with **[[concepts/night_before_preparation]]** tasks like placing a water bottle on the bedside table.

## Benefits
- Reduces strain on the lower back and hips, important for users managing temporary mobility limitations.
- Enhances safety by limiting the need to reach over obstacles, complementing **[[concepts/mobility_aid_optimization]]** strategies.
- Increases efficiency of the morning flow, helping keep the routine within the 30‑45 minute window.
