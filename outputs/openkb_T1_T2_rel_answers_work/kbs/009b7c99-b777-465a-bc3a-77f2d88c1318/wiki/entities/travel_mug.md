# Travel Mug

A **travel mug** is a leak‑resistant, portable coffee container designed to keep beverages secure while the user’s hands are otherwise occupied. In the context of a walking boot, it becomes a cornerstone of an efficient morning routine.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-35_2025-09-15]]
- **Leak‑resistant lid** – prevents spills when walking on uneven surfaces or navigating stairs.
- **One‑hand operation** – allows the user to hold the mug at the side while the other hand manages the boot or a bag.
- **Placement in the kitchen “launch pad”** – set out the mug next to the coffee maker the night before, reducing the number of steps needed in the morning.
- **Energy‑saving** – eliminates the need to pause and set down a beverage, keeping the flow of the [[concepts/morning_routine_planning]] smooth.

## Role in a boot‑friendly routine
1. **Night‑before preparation**: Position the travel mug on the counter beside the coffee machine as part of the [[concepts/night_before_preparation]] checklist.
2. **Morning station**: Use the mug as part of the kitchen station to brew coffee, then carry it hands‑free while moving to the door.
3. **Safety**: When tackling stairs, the sealed lid allows the user to keep the mug on a step or in a cross‑body bag without risk of spilling, complementing other mobility aids such as the [[entities/walking_boot]] and [[entities/reacher]].

## Related tools and concepts
- **Mobility aid optimization** – pairing the travel mug with a stable chair in the bedroom and a reacher helps streamline dressing and reduce balance challenges. ([[concepts/mobility_aid_optimization]])
- **Morning routine planning** – the travel mug is listed among the essential items in a boot‑centric [[concepts/morning_routine_planning]] strategy.
