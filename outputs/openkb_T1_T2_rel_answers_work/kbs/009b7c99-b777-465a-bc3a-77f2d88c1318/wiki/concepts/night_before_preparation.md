# Night‑Before Preparation

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A set of proactive steps taken the evening prior to a day that includes a temporary mobility limitation (e.g., a walking boot). The goal is to reduce decision‑making, limit unnecessary movement, and conserve energy in the morning.

## Why It Matters
When a walking boot slows you down, the first minutes after waking are often the most energy‑intensive. By arranging everything you need the night before, you create a predictable flow that minimizes bending, reaching, and back‑tracking. This aligns with [[concepts/morning_routine_planning]] and supports overall [[concepts/mobility_aid_optimization]].

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-35_2025-09-15]])
- **Boot & Mobility Aids**: Place the boot (or removable insert) and any assistive devices within arm’s reach of the bed.
- **Clothing**: Lay out the full outfit, including socks, underwear, pants, and any accessories you plan to wear. Keep the pants positioned so the leg with the boot is easy to pull over.
- **Hydration & Phone**: Fill a water bottle and set it on the nightstand with the phone or alarm device.
- **Breakfast Setup**: Position a travel mug (e.g., [[entities/travel_mug]]) next to the coffee maker, and pre‑portion simple breakfast items (cereal, fruit, yogurt) on the counter or in an easily reachable fridge spot.
- **Bag & Keys**: Pack the work bag, place keys, medication, and any other essentials by the door.
- **Tools**: Keep a reacher or long‑handled shoehorn ([[entities/reacher]]) nearby for dressing without bending.

## Practical Checklist
1. **Boot** – Verify straps are even; set the boot beside the bed.
2. **Outfit** – Arrange clothing in the order you’ll put them on (underwear → socks → pants → shoes/boot).
3. **Water & Phone** – Fill bottle, place phone on nightstand.
4. **Coffee & Breakfast** – Place travel mug near coffee maker, pre‑portion food.
5. **Bag & Keys** – Pack bag, put keys and meds by the door.
6. **Assistive Tools** – Position reacher/shoehorn within easy reach.

## Benefits
- **Energy Conservation** – Reduces the number of trips and lifts needed in the first 30 minutes.
- **Safety** – Limits the need to stand or bend when still groggy, lowering fall risk.
- **Predictability** – Creates a repeatable “station” that becomes muscle memory, easing anxiety.
