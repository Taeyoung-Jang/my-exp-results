## Morning Routine Planning

Morning Routine Planning is a systematic method for organizing the first hours of the day to reduce chaos, conserve energy, and maintain safety when dealing with temporary mobility limitations such as a walking boot.

### Core Elements
- **Night‑before preparation** – Lay out the boot, clothing, water bottle, phone, and pre‑portioned breakfast items before sleep.  See [[concepts/night_before_preparation]] for the detailed checklist.
- **Boot‑centric stations** – Create small “stations” in each key room (bedroom, bathroom, kitchen) so you move in a single direction without backtracking.  This relies on the presence of the [[entities/walking_boot]] and nearby essential items.
- **Timed template (30–45 min)** – Divide the routine into three flexible phases:
  1. **Stabilize (0‑10 min)** – Sit up, adjust the boot, use the [[entities/handrail]] if needed, and perform basic hygiene while seated.
  2. **Dress (10‑25 min)** – Dress while seated using a stable chair; employ a [[entities/reacher]] or long‑handled shoehorn to pull pants over the boot.
  3. **Kitchen & exit (25‑45 min)** – Use a single kitchen “launch pad” with a [[entities/travel_mug]] and pre‑prepared food; finish grooming and grab keys before leaving.
- **Key tools** – A stable, armless chair (ideally in the bedroom), a leak‑proof travel mug, and a reacher are the top three aids that most improve efficiency and safety.
- **Safety on stairs** – Use the handrail on every step, lead with the stronger leg when going up, and keep hands free; carry items only after you are fully stable.

### Adaptations for Varying Soreness
- **Low‑soreness days** – Faster transitions, optional standing for bathroom tasks, and a more streamlined dressing sequence.
- **More‑sore days** – Add extra seated minutes, keep all clothing within arm’s reach, skip non‑essential accessories, and perform kitchen tasks from a single spot.

### Benefits
- Reduces unnecessary bending and walking, conserving energy.
- Provides a predictable rhythm that lowers anxiety.
- Enhances safety by minimizing balance challenges and stair mishaps.
