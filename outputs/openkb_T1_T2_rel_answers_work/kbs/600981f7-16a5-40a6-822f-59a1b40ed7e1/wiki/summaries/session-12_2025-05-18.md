# Summary of session-12_2025-05-18

**Date:** 2025-05-18

## Core Contribution
A concise, reusable framework for planning client migration work across a week, including time‑blocking schedules, communication boundary scripts, an expanded migration checklist template, a cutover runbook section, and a lightweight client‑status tracking table.

## Key Elements

### 1. Time‑blocking schedule
- **Morning focus block:** 9:00‑10:20 (migration tasks) with a 10‑minute prep buffer 10:20‑10:30.  
- **Meeting window:** 11:00‑13:00 (both Thu & Fri).  
- **Afternoon reset & review:** 13:00‑13:15 reset, 13:15‑14:00 follow‑up/review.  
- **Optional wrap‑up:** 15:00‑15:30 Friday weekly close.

### 2. Slack/meeting boundary scripts
- **Non‑urgent script:**  
  > "Hey! I’m in a heads‑down migration block right now. If it’s not urgent, I’ll respond after 10:20. If it is time‑sensitive, let me know."

- **Urgent triage script:**  
  > "Got it. I’m in a focus block, but if you can give me a one‑sentence summary of the issue, I’ll help triage quickly and point you in the right direction."

(Reference to [[entities/slack]] for the messaging platform.)
