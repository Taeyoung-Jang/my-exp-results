# Summary of session-31_2025-06-08

**Document type:** Conversation transcript – practical advice for a new employee preparing for day‑one.

## Core Advice
- **First‑day impression** – quietly establish reliable foundations (device, security, organization) without overt showmanship. [[concepts/first_day_impression]]
- **Night‑before setup (≈1 hour)** – focus on high‑impact, low‑risk actions that prevent lock‑outs and keep the environment default‑compliant. [[concepts/secure_onboarding]]

## Priority Checklist (High‑Impact, Safe to Do Tonight)
1. **Password manager** – install/configure, add all work credentials.  
2. **Multi‑factor authentication (MFA)** – enable everywhere it’s offered and verify successful log‑ins.  
3. **System updates** – apply required OS/firmware updates; reboot if needed.  
4. **Device security settings** – set screen‑lock timeout, enable device encryption if not already on.  
5. **Email basics** – confirm send/receive, set a simple professional signature.  
6. **Collaboration tools** – log into Teams or Slack (or equivalent) to appear reachable. [[entities/microsoft-teams]] [[entities/slack]]

## What to Defer Until After Meeting Manager/IT
- Creating complex email filtering rules.  
- Modifying VPN, network, or proxy configurations.  
- Installing additional software beyond required baseline.  
- Changing default security policies or group policies.  
- Re‑organizing shared‑drive folder structures.  
- Adjusting advanced device policies (e.g., power‑settings, remote‑management agents).

## Underlying Themes & Concepts
- **Secure onboarding** – establishing personal security hygiene early (password manager, MFA, updates). [[concepts/secure_onboarding]]
- **Low‑profile initiative** – showing competence without appearing overbearing.  
- **Organizational alignment** – deferring policy‑dependent customization until guidance is received.  
- **Efficiency under time constraint** – ordering tasks by impact to fit a ~1‑hour window. [[concepts/efficient_task_prioritization]]
