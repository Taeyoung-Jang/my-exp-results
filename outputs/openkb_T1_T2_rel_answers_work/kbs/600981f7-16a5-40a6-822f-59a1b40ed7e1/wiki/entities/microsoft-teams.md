# Microsoft Teams

Microsoft Teams is a cloud‑based collaboration product from [[entities/microsoft]] that combines chat, video meetings, file sharing, and app integration for workplace communication.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-31_2025-06-08]]
- **First‑day readiness**: New employees are advised to log into Teams (or Slack) the night before their first shift so they are reachable on day one. This aligns with the [[concepts/first_day_impression]] of appearing prepared without being overbearing.
- **Secure onboarding**: Setting up Teams is part of the high‑impact, low‑risk tasks recommended for a secure onboarding routine, alongside enabling MFA and configuring a password manager. See [[concepts/secure_onboarding]].
- **Task prioritization**: Including Teams in the initial checklist reflects the [[concepts/efficient_task_prioritization]] approach—focus on tools that enable communication before customizing any settings.

## Typical usage
- Joining team channels and direct messages.
- Attending video calls and virtual meetings.
- Accessing shared files stored in associated SharePoint sites.
- Integrating with other productivity apps (e.g., Planner, OneNote).

## Related entities
- [[entities/slack]] – an alternative team messaging platform mentioned alongside Teams.
