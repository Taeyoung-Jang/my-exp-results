# Floating Anchors

**Definition**: Floating Anchors are adaptable time ranges rather than fixed clock times that provide reliable reference points for essential daily activities such as sleep, eating, and wind‑down rituals. By anchoring to a *window* instead of an exact hour, individuals can maintain physiological consistency even when their schedules shift.

## Core Principles
1. **Range‑Based Scheduling**
   - Choose the earliest and latest feasible times for a given activity and treat any moment within that span as acceptable. This creates flexibility while preserving the cue that the body relies on.
2. **Order Preservation**
   - Keep the sequence **fuel → activity → wind‑down → sleep** intact, even if the exact timing slides. The order, not the clock, drives circadian stability.
3. **Cue Consistency**
   - Use the same pre‑sleep or pre‑meal rituals (e.g., dim lights, screen‑free wind‑down) regardless of when the window starts, reinforcing the body’s internal signals.
4. **Adaptation on the Fly**
   - When an event runs long, shift the entire block later rather than truncating it. If a sleep window is squeezed, move the main rest to a later slot (e.g., after a committee call) to protect a meaningful chunk of rest.

## Benefits
- Reduces mental load of strict clock‑watching.
- Improves sleep quality by preserving consistent pre‑sleep cues.
- Supports steady energy levels via predictable nutrition windows.
- Increases resilience to schedule overruns and unexpected tasks.
