## Bedtime Routine

A **bedtime routine** is a repeatable sequence of activities performed before sleep that signals the body it is time to wind down. By consistently reducing stimulation, incorporating physical relaxation, and clearing mental clutter, the routine helps align the circadian rhythm and improve sleep onset.

### Core Elements
- **Stimulus reduction** – dim lights, silence phone notifications.
- **Physical relaxation** – washing the face, brushing teeth, warm shower, or gentle stretching.
- **Mental unwinding** – brief "brain dump" of pending thoughts, breathing or grounding exercises.
- **Timing consistency** – aim for the same lights‑out window each night (e.g., 10:30 PM).

### 5‑Minute Mini‑Routine (Hectic Nights)
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| **10:25** | Dim lights, put phone in sleep mode. |
| **10:26** | Quick physical wind‑down (wash face, brush teeth). |
| **10:28** | One‑two slow breaths and a brief note‑dump of thoughts. |
| **10:30** | Lights out, in bed. |

### 20–30 Minute Full Routine (Regular Nights)
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| **10:00** | Dim lights, finish current activity. |
| **10:05** | Warm shower or gentle stretching. |
| **10:15** | Calming activity (light reading, short journal entry). |
| **10:25** | Get into bed, prepare for lights‑out. |
| **10:30** | Lights out. |

### In‑Bed Coping Strategies
- **Brain dump**: silently jot down lingering thoughts on a notepad or phone (no bright light).
- **Breathing / grounding**: focus on slow breaths or sensations of the sheets to break mental loops.

### If/Then Planning for Common Hiccups
- **Late dinner** → Switch to the 5‑minute version.
- **Unexpected work message** → Read only; reply in the morning unless urgent.
- **Caffeine too late** → Emphasize physical relaxation (shower, extra dimming) and possibly extend the routine.
- **Wake‑up at 3 AM** → Keep lights low, perform a quick brain dump, repeat breathing, and let the body drift back.
