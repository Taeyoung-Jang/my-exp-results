# If/Then Planning

**If/Then Planning** is a simple, proactive strategy for turning potential disruptions into manageable steps. By defining a concrete *if* condition (a trigger) and linking it to a specific *then* action (the response), the approach helps maintain consistency in routines—especially bedtime habits—without the need for on‑the‑spot decision making.

## Core Elements
- **Trigger (If):** A recognizable event that could derail a routine (e.g., late dinner, unexpected work message, late‑night caffeine, early‑morning awakening).
- **Response (Then):** A pre‑decided, low‑effort action that keeps the overall plan on track (e.g., switch to the 5‑minute mini‑routine, defer replying until morning, add extra physical relaxation, perform a brief grounding exercise).

## Application in Session 02 (2024‑07‑14)
The document outlines several *if/then* rules to handle common bedtime hiccups:
- **If** dinner runs late → **Then** immediately adopt the 5‑minute routine.
- **If** an unexpected work message arrives → **Then** read only, respond the next morning unless urgent.
- **If** caffeine is consumed late → **Then** emphasize physical relaxation steps (warm shower, extra dimming) and possibly extend the routine.
- **If** you wake at 3 AM → **Then** keep lights low, write down thoughts, use a breathing exercise, and let sleep return naturally.

These rules illustrate how *If/Then Planning* transforms potential obstacles into predictable actions, preserving the overall **sleep consistency** goal.

## Benefits
- Reduces mental load at the moment of the trigger.
- Increases the likelihood of sticking to the intended **bedtime routine**.
- Provides a clear, repeatable pathway that reinforces habit formation.
