## Summary

The document provides a practical guide for building a consistent **sleep schedule** through two timestamped bedtime routines: a 5‑minute “mini‑routine” for hectic evenings and a 20–30 minute “full routine” for regular nights. It emphasizes **stimulus reduction**, **physical relaxation**, and **mental unwinding** to cue the body for sleep.

### 5‑minute mini‑routine (timestamped)
- **10:25** – Dim lights, silence phone.  
- **10:26** – Quick physical wind‑down (wash face, brush teeth).  
- **10:28** – One to two slow breaths and a brief brain dump of pending thoughts.  
- **10:30** – Lights out, in bed.

### 20–30 minute routine (timestamped)
- **10:00** – Dim lights, finish current activity.  
- **10:05** – Warm shower or gentle stretching.  
- **10:15** – Calming activity (light reading, short journal entry).  
- **10:25** – Get into bed, prepare for lights‑out.  
- **10:30** – Lights out.

### In‑bed coping strategies
- Perform a silent brain dump on a notepad or phone.  
- Use a simple breathing exercise or grounding technique (e.g., notice sounds, feel the sheets).

### If/then quick‑fix plan for common hiccups
- **Late dinner** → Skip to the 5‑minute version.  
- **Unexpected work message** → Read only; reply next morning unless urgent.  
- **Caffeine too late** → Emphasize the physical components (shower, extra dimming) and extend the routine if needed.  
- **Wake‑up at 3 AM** → Keep lights low, write down thoughts, repeat breathing, and allow natural return to sleep.
