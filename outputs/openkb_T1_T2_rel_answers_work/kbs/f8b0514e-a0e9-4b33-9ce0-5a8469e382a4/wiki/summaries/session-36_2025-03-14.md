## Summary of session‑36 (2025‑03‑14)

- **Gradual bedtime shift** – Move bedtime earlier by ~30 min every 1‑2 nights to avoid a sudden shock. See [[concepts/gradual-bedtime-adjustment]] and [[concepts/bedtime_routine]].
- **Consistent wake‑time** – Keep the same wake‑time on days off, anchoring the circadian rhythm; a small shift (≈ 1 hour) is usually tolerable, but > 3–4 hours disrupts the schedule. This aligns with [[concepts/sleep_consistency]].
- **Wind‑down ritual** – Dim lights, limit screens, and repeat a calming activity before bed to signal sleep readiness. [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]]
- **Light exposure** – Seek bright light immediately after waking and keep evenings dark to reinforce the new morning. [[concepts/light-exposure-for-circadian-entrainment]]
- **Meal and caffeine timing** – Eat the first meal soon after waking; avoid caffeine later in the shift to aid sleep onset. [[concepts/meal-and-caffeine-timing]]
- **Weekend/day‑off strategy** – Stay close to the work schedule; an extra hour of sleep or a bedtime delayed by up to 1‑1.5 hours is generally safe. Larger deviations reset the internal clock. *(no dedicated concept page)*
- **Nap vs. sleeping in** – When a late night is unavoidable, maintain the normal wake‑time and take a short nap (20‑40 min) later rather than sleeping in, which would shift the rhythm.
- **Optimal nap timing** – Limit naps to early afternoon, finishing by ~2 PM, to preserve sleep pressure for a 9 PM bedtime. [[concepts/nap-timing-and-sleep-pressure]]
