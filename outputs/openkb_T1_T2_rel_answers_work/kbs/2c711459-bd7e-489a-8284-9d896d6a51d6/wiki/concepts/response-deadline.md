# Response Deadline

**Definition**
A response deadline is a clear, time‑bound request for a reply, typically expressed in business or customer‑service communications (e.g., “please respond within five business days”).

## Why it matters
- Sets expectations for both parties.
- Helps keep issues from lingering.
- Provides a concrete metric for follow‑up and escalation.

## Example from [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-16]]
In the email templates created for a repair‑kiosk complaint, the author added a clause:

> “I’d appreciate a response within **five business days**…

This deadline gave the recipient a specific timeframe to provide the requested diagnosis, data‑recovery status, refund options, and safety information about a swollen lithium‑ion battery.

## Best practices (from [[concepts/email-drafting]])
- State the deadline early in the request.
- Use business‑day language (“business days”) to avoid holidays.
- Explain why the timeframe is needed (e.g., to make a decision).
- Offer a brief consequence for missed deadlines (e.g., escalation).

**Key takeaway:** Including a well‑defined response deadline improves clarity, accountability, and the likelihood of a timely resolution.
