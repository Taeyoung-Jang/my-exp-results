# Attachment Guidelines

**Definition**: Best practices for including images or other attachments in customer‑service communications to provide clear evidence while protecting privacy and safety.

## Core Principles
1. **Relevance** – Attach only files that directly support the issue (e.g., a clear photo of a swollen battery).  
2. **Privacy** – Redact or blur any personal data, serial numbers, or sensitive information before sending.  
3. **Safety** – Ensure the attachment does not depict unsafe handling of hazardous components; a simple, well‑lit shot of the device is sufficient.
4. **File Size & Format** – Use common formats (JPEG, PNG, PDF) and keep each file under 2 MB to avoid email delivery problems.
5. **Contextual Description** – Reference the attachment in the body of the email so the recipient knows what to look for.

## Application in [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-16]]
In the email drafts for a repair kiosk incident, the assistant advised:
- Attaching a photo of the swollen battery can help the kiosk assess the situation quickly.
- Avoid showing personal data or unsafe handling of the battery.
- Include a brief note in the email body indicating the attachment’s purpose.
