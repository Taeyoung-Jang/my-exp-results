# Data Recovery Policy

**Definition**: A data recovery policy outlines the procedures, responsibilities, and communication standards that a repair service must follow when attempting to retrieve user data after hardware failure, such as a swollen lithium‑ion battery.

## Relevance to Session 35
The user’s request in [[summaries/session-35_2024-12-16]] explicitly asks for:
- Confirmation of any data‑recovery attempts made on the device.
- Written documentation of the diagnosis and steps taken.
- Clear information on what options are available if data cannot be recovered.

These points illustrate the practical need for a transparent data recovery policy in consumer‑focused repair contexts.

## Core Elements of a Data Recovery Policy
1. **Assessment & Diagnosis**
   - The service must provide a written diagnosis that details the condition of the device and the feasibility of data recovery.
2. **Attempt Documentation**
   - Any data‑recovery attempts, tools used, and outcomes should be recorded and communicated to the customer.
3. **Outcome Communication**
   - If recovery is successful, the method of data return (e.g., encrypted backup) must be described. If unsuccessful, the policy should explain why and what alternatives exist (e.g., third‑party services).
4. **Safety & Handling**
   - For cases involving swollen batteries, the policy must address safety precautions, battery disposal, and whether the device is safe for transport. This aligns with [[concepts/swollen-lithium-battery-handling]].
5. **Refund / Credit Options**
   - When data cannot be recovered or the device is beyond repair, the policy should specify refund or credit mechanisms.
6. **Response Timeline**
   - The service should commit to a response deadline (e.g., five business days) to keep the customer informed, as highlighted in the email drafts via [[concepts/response-deadline]].

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Effective communication of the policy benefits from good [[concepts/email-drafting]] practices, ensuring the request is clear and actionable.
- Framing the request as a constructive [[concepts/customer-complaint]] helps maintain a cooperative tone.
- Including relevant photos can support the claim, following [[concepts/attachment-guidelines]].

## Practical Tips for Consumers
- Ask explicitly for a written diagnosis and a summary of any data‑recovery steps taken.
- Inquire about the safety of transporting the device and the status of the swollen battery.
- Request information on refund or credit options if the repair cannot be completed.
- Set a reasonable response deadline to ensure timely follow‑up.

By understanding and invoking these policy elements, customers can more effectively navigate repair situations involving potential data loss.
