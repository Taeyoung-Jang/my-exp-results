# Fitbit

Fitbit is a consumer electronics **organization** that designs and sells fitness‑oriented wearables, including the popular **Fitbit Charge** series.  In the discussion of **[[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]]**, Fitbit was highlighted as a strong candidate for users prioritizing **[[concepts/battery-life-optimization]]** over a full‑featured smartwatch experience.

## Key points from the session
- **Battery longevity**: Fitbit Charge‑style bands routinely achieve a week‑plus battery life, making them attractive for users who dislike daily charging.
- **Feature trade‑offs**: Compared to full smartwatches, Fitbit devices offer a simpler UI, fewer third‑party apps, and limited on‑wrist interactivity.  This aligns with the **[[concepts/smartwatch-vs-fitness-band]]** comparison where fitness bands focus on core health tracking.
- **Health tracking**: While Fitbit provides reliable heart‑rate and sleep monitoring, the session noted that **[[concepts/health-tracking-accuracy]]** can vary across models, and the most accurate sensors are typically found in dedicated sport‑focused brands.
- **Display technology**: Many Fitbit bands use low‑power LCD or e‑ink displays rather than **[[entities/amoled]]**, which helps preserve battery life.

Fitbit remains a go‑to option for users who value extended battery life and core fitness features over the richer app ecosystems found in brands like **[[entities/garmin]]**.
