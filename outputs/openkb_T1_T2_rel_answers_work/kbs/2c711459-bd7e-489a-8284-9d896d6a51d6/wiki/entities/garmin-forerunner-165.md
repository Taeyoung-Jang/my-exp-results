# Garmin Forerunner 165

**Type:** product

## Overview
The Garmin Forerunner 165 is a GPS‑enabled smartwatch positioned as a health‑centric device with an emphasis on long battery life. It targets users who need reliable notifications, workout and run tracking, and accurate sleep monitoring while avoiding the power draw of an always‑on AMOLED screen.

## Key Features
- **Display:** Non‑AMOLED LCD, which conserves power compared to bright OLED panels.
- **Sensors:** Built‑in heart‑rate monitor, SpO₂ sensor (optional night‑time use), and accelerometer for activity detection.
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth notifications for calls, messages, and calendar alerts.
- **GPS:** Standard GPS tracking for outdoor runs; multi‑band GPS is available but drains more power.
- **Battery Life:** Typically 7–10 days with moderate use; can extend further by disabling night‑time SpO₂ and limiting GPS sessions.

## Battery‑Life Optimization
The device aligns with the [[concepts/battery-life-optimization]] approach by:
- Using a low‑power LCD screen instead of [[entities/amoled]].
- Allowing users to turn off continuous SpO₂ monitoring.
- Offering a standard GPS mode that balances accuracy and power consumption.

## Health Tracking Accuracy
Garmin emphasizes [[concepts/health-tracking-accuracy]] through validated heart‑rate and sleep algorithms, making it a strong choice for athletes prioritizing data quality.

## Position in the Wearable Landscape
Compared to other options discussed in [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]]:
- It provides more smartwatch‑like notifications than a typical [[entities/fitbit-charge]] band.
- It offers better battery life than most full‑featured [[entities/garmin-forerunner-255]] models with AMOLED displays.
- It sits between the pure fitness focus of a [[entities/fitbit]] band and the richer UI of a hybrid [[entities/withings-hybrid-watch]].

## Trade‑offs
- **Display:** Less vibrant and interactive than AMOLED screens.
- **App Ecosystem:** Limited third‑party apps compared to a true smartwatch.
- **Features:** Advanced smartwatch functions (e.g., voice assistants) are pared down to preserve battery.

## Recommended Settings for 7‑10 Day Battery
1. Disable all‑night SpO₂ tracking.
2. Use standard GPS rather than multi‑band when possible.
3. Keep screen brightness low and rely on auto‑sleep.
4. Trim notifications to essential apps only.
