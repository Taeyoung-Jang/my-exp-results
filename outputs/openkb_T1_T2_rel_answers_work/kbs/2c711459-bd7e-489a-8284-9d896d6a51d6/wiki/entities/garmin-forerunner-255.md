# Garmin Forerunner 255

**Type:** Product

The **Garmin Forerunner 255** is a mid‑tier fitness‑focused smartwatch in the [[entities/garmin]] lineup. It targets users who want reliable **[[entities/gps]]** tracking, solid **[[concepts/health-tracking-accuracy]]**, and decent notification support while maintaining a **7‑10 day** battery life.

## Key Features (as highlighted in [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]])
- **Display:** Efficient LCD (non‑[[entities/amoled]]) screen that conserves power compared to bright always‑on OLED panels.
- **Battery Life:** Typical endurance of 7–10 days under normal use; longer when GPS and high‑brightness features are limited.
- **Health Sensors:** Accurate heart‑rate monitoring, reliable sleep tracking, and optional SpO₂ monitoring (can be disabled at night to save power).
- **GPS Performance:** Supports standard GPS positioning; multi‑band GPS is available but drains the battery faster.
- **Notifications:** Handles call, text, and app alerts without the heavy app ecosystem of full smartwatches.
- **Durability:** Water‑resistant and built for everyday wear during gym sessions, runs, and outdoor activities.

## Battery‑Life Considerations
- The primary battery drains are **always‑on display**, high **[[entities/amoled]]** brightness (if used), continuous **[[entities/gps]]** tracking, and constant **SpO₂** monitoring.
- To achieve the 7‑10 day target, users are advised to:
  1. Use the LCD mode and keep the screen off when not needed.
  2. Disable all‑night pulse‑ox tracking.
  3. Prefer standard GPS over multi‑band during workouts.
  4. Limit notifications to essential apps only.

These practices align with the broader [[concepts/battery-life-optimization]] strategies discussed in the session.

## Comparison Context
- Positioned between the more feature‑rich **[[entities/garmin-vivoactive]]** series and the ultra‑light **[[entities/garmin-forerunner-165]]**. The Forerunner 255 offers a balance of smart features and battery efficiency, making it a recommended choice for users prioritizing both health data and week‑long wearability.
- In the **[[concepts/smartwatch-vs-fitness-band]]** discussion, the Forerunner 255 sits closer to a smartwatch but retains the endurance typical of fitness‑band‑style devices.
