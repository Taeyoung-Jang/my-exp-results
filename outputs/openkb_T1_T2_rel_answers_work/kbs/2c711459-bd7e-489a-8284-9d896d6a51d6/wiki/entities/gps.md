## GPS

**GPS (Global Positioning System)** is a satellite‑based navigation service that provides location and time information to devices on Earth. In the context of wearable technology, GPS enables accurate outdoor activity tracking such as runs, bike rides, and hikes.

### Role in session‑04_2024-06-15
- The assistant highlighted **GPS** as a major battery drain on Garmin wearables, especially when using **multi‑band GPS** or continuous tracking during workouts.
- Recommendations included switching to **standard GPS** mode and limiting GPS sessions to extend the device’s battery toward the desired 7–10 day range.
- The discussion linked GPS usage directly to the broader theme of [[concepts/battery-life-optimization]].

### Battery Impact
- **Always‑on or frequent GPS**: consumes significant power, shortening battery life.
- **Multi‑band GPS**: offers higher accuracy but uses more energy than single‑band GPS.
- Reducing GPS frequency or disabling it when not needed is a key strategy for achieving longer battery life on wearables.
