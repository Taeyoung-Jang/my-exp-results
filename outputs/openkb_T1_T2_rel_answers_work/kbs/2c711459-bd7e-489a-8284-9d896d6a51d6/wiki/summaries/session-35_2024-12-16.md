# Summary of session-35_2024-12-16

**Date:** 2024-12-16  

## Overview
The user sought help crafting firm‑but‑polite email templates to request clearer policies and follow‑up after a problematic visit to a repair kiosk with a swollen lithium‑ion battery. The assistant provided two versions (to mall management and to the kiosk company), added placeholders, a response deadline, and a safety inquiry about transporting the device.

## Key Elements of the Emails
- **Placeholders** for kiosk name, mall name, location, date, and time.  
- **Bullet‑point request list** covering:
  - Written diagnosis  
  - Data‑recovery attempt confirmation  
  - Refund/credit options  
  - Procedure for swollen batteries  
  - Safety of transport and battery disposition status  
- **Response deadline** of five business days.  
- **Advice on attachments:** Photos are useful if they don’t expose personal data or unsafe handling.

## Actionable Takeaways
1. Use concise placeholders to personalize each email.  
2. List specific information you need to streamline the follow‑up.  
3. Include a deadline to prompt timely responses.  
4. Ask explicitly about the safety and disposition of the swollen battery.  
5. Attach clear photos when appropriate, avoiding any personal or hazardous details.
