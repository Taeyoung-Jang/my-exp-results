# Summary of session-15_2023-06-15

**Context** – The user earns $4,200 /mo after tax and has fixed monthly expenses totaling $3,645, leaving $555 discretionary income. They carry a $38,000 student‑loan balance (4.5%‑6.2% interest) with a $410 minimum payment, $2,500 in an emergency fund, and a 4% 401(k) contribution.

**Key concepts**
- essential expenses – rent, utilities, groceries, transportation, health costs, phone, and the loan minimum, amounting to **$2,845 /mo**.
- emergency fund – target of 3 months of essentials → **≈ $8,500**.
- budgeting – allocation of the $555 surplus between extra loan payments and savings.
- student loan repayment – accelerated payoff using the surplus.
- financial hybrid plan – a split‑phase approach that first builds the emergency cushion, then ramps up loan payments.

## Outcome estimates
- **Emergency fund** reaches the $8,500 target in about **15 months**.
- **Loan payments**: $560 /mo during the build phase, then $970 /mo thereafter.
- With an average interest rate of **5.5 %**, the $38,000 balance is projected to be cleared in roughly **5.5 years** total.

The hybrid approach gives short‑term cash‑flow stability while still accelerating loan payoff compared to the minimum‑payment‑only scenario.
