## Overview  
A concise method for organizing important legal and financial records, defining what to keep, what to shred, how long to retain, and how to protect both paper and digital copies.

## Paper and Digital Folder Structure  
- **Identity & Vital Records** – birth certificates, passports, SSN cards, marriage/divorce papers, name‑change orders.  
- **Property & Long‑Term Agreements** – titles, deeds, major loans, vehicle registrations.  
- **Taxes – [Year]** – one folder per tax year (keep 7 years).  
- **Active Policies & Contracts** – current insurance policies, leases, employment agreements.  
- **Current Year Financial** – monthly statements, bills, receipts (keep 1 year).  
- **Permanent Originals** (digital subfolder) – scans of all forever items.  

These mirror the same names in a secure cloud folder.

## Retention Timeline  
| Category | Retention |
|----------|-----------|
| Forever | Identity, property, long‑term agreements |
| 7 years | Tax returns, major financial records |
| 1 year | Routine statements, bills |
| Immediate | Anything not needed for legal or tax purposes – shred. |

See [[concepts/record-retention]] for the underlying principles.

## Security Best Practices  
- Store paper forever items in a fire‑proof lockbox or safe.  
- Use a reputable password manager for account logins.  
- Scan and upload documents to an encrypted cloud service; avoid local unencrypted copies.  
- Keep a locked shredding bin for disposable papers.  

These align with [[concepts/security-best-practices]].

## Updating Records – Notifications Log Template  
Create a simple table (e.g., in a spreadsheet or note) with columns:  
- Organization  
- Update Type  
- Date Sent  
- Method (online, email, mail, in‑person)  
- Contact Person  
- Confirmation ID/Reference  
- Confirmation Received Date  

Save this as **Notifications Log**.

## Common Recipients Checklist  
1. Banks & credit unions  
2. Credit‑card issuers  
3. Employer / HR system  
4. Health, dental, vision insurers  
5. State agencies (DMV, licensing boards)  
6. Utilities & service providers  
7. Schools, medical offices, professional memberships  

## Proof & Correspondence Management  
- Create a **Proof & Correspondence** folder alongside the log.  
- Store scans of court orders, discharge papers, receipts, confirmation emails, screenshots, and mailed‑letter photos.  
- Keep the log and its proof folder within **Identity & Vital Records** for long‑term reference.
