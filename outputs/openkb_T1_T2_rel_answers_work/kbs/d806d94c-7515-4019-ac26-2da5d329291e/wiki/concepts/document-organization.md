## Document Organization

**Document Organization** is the practice of arranging both paper and digital records in a clear, consistent hierarchy so that important items are easy to locate, retain for the appropriate period, and protect against loss or unauthorized access. The concept is illustrated in the guidance provided in [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]].

### Core Principles
1. **Mirror Physical and Digital Structures** – Use identical folder names for paper storage and cloud storage (e.g., "Identity & Vital Records", "Property & Long‑Term Agreements", "Taxes – 2023", "Active Policies & Contracts", "Current Year Financial").
2. **Simple Naming** – Choose explicit, self‑describing names that require no extra mental mapping.
3. **Retention Alignment** – Align each folder with a retention timeline (see [[concepts/record-retention]]):
   - *Forever* for identity, property, and long‑term agreements.
   - *7 years* for tax returns and major financial records.
   - *1 year* for routine statements and bills.
   - *Immediate shredding* for disposable items.
4. **Security Integration** – Store forever items in a fire‑proof safe, encrypt cloud copies, and manage passwords with a password manager (see [[concepts/security-best-practices]]).
5. **Update Workflow** – When a legal change occurs (e.g., court order), use a **Notifications Log** template to track contacts and a **Proof & Correspondence** folder to keep supporting documents.

### Practical Folder Layout
| Folder (Paper & Digital) | Contents | Retention |
|--------------------------|----------|-----------|
| **Identity & Vital Records** | Birth certificates, passports, SSN cards, marriage/divorce papers, name‑change orders | Forever |
| **Property & Long‑Term Agreements** | Deeds, titles, major loan documents, vehicle registrations | Forever |
| **Taxes – [Year]** | Tax returns, supporting schedules | 7 years |
| **Active Policies & Contracts** | Current insurance policies, leases, employment agreements | Until renewed/replaced |
| **Current Year Financial** | Monthly statements, bills, receipts | 1 year |
| **Permanent Originals** (digital sub‑folder) | Scans of all forever items | Forever |

### Benefits
- **Speed** – Knowing exactly where a document lives eliminates search time.
- **Reliability** – Consistent retention periods reduce the risk of premature disposal.
- **Security** – Centralized safe and encrypted cloud storage protect high‑value items.
- **Scalability** – The same structure works for individuals and small households.
