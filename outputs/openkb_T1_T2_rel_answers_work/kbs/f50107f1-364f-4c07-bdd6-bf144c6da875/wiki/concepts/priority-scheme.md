# Priority Scheme

**Definition**: A concise, color‑coded coding system (P1, P2, P3) used on moving boxes to indicate handling priority and placement order.

## Components

| Code | Meaning | Typical Color | Handling Advice |
|------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| **P1** | Must stay with you or travel in your personal vehicle | **Red** | Keep the box in the car; unpack immediately. |
| **P2** | Goes on the truck but should be unloaded first | **Yellow** | Load near the door of the truck; unload right after P1 boxes. |
| **P3** | Standard items; can be loaded anywhere and unpack later | **Blue** | Load anywhere; no special handling required. |

## How to Apply
1. **Label Structure** – Add a single line `Priority: P1` (or P2/P3) to the standard label format described in [[concepts/packing-system]].
2. **Color Sticker** – Affix a small sticker or piece of tape in the matching color next to the priority line for instant visual identification.
3. **Communication** – Brief movers on the meaning of the three colors before loading begins.

## Benefits
- **Speed**: Movers can glance at a box and know instantly how to treat it.
- **Safety**: High‑value or irreplaceable items (e.g., passports, medication) receive explicit handling.
- **Organization**: Reduces confusion when unloading multiple rooms.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with the **color‑and‑number** approach from [[concepts/packing-system]].
- Complements the **open‑first‑box** checklist in [[concepts/open-first-box]] by ensuring its contents are marked P1.
- Can be logged alongside other visual cues using [[concepts/visual-cue-logging]].
