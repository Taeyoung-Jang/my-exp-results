# Open‑First Box

**Definition**: A designated box packed with the most essential items you need within the first hours or days after arriving at a new home.

**Purpose**: Prevents chaos by providing immediate access to toiletries, medications, clothing, documents, tech chargers, basic tools, and a few comfort items, allowing you to settle in without rummaging through all boxes.

**Key Components (from [[summaries/session-33_2025-06-17]]):**
- Toiletries and medications
- Chargers and power bank
- Two changes of clothes
- Important documents (ID, passport, lease, insurance)
- Laptop and daily‑use technology
- Small first‑aid basics
- Cleaning wipes or spray
- Multi‑tool or scissors
- Bedding or pillow
- Snacks, water, and a mug
- Any sentimental or irreplaceable small items you want with you

**Integration with other concepts:**
- Works hand‑in‑hand with the [[concepts/packing-system]] that organizes all other boxes by room and priority.
- Often marked as **P1** in the [[concepts/priority-scheme]] so it stays with you or is loaded last.
- Can be reinforced with a reminder system (e.g., a checklist) similar to [[concepts/reminder-system]].

**Implementation Tips:**
1. Assemble the items in a sturdy box or bag before the move.
2. Keep the box with you during loading so it remains accessible.
3. Label it clearly, e.g., 'Open‑First Box – P1 – Essentials'.
4. Add a quick copy‑and‑paste checklist to your notes app for future moves.
