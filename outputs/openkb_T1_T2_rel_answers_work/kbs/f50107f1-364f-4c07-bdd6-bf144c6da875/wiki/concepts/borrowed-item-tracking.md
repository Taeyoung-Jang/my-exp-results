# Borrowed Item Tracking

Borrowed Item Tracking is a practical approach for keeping tabs on items you’ve borrowed from others, ensuring they are returned promptly and without awkward reminders.

## Core Components

1. **Visual Cue Logging** – Take a quick photo of the borrowed item and store it with a short note in your phone or a note‑taking app. This creates a visual reminder of what you have and where it is. [[concepts/visual-cue-logging]]
2. **Reminder System** – Set a calendar or reminder event for the expected return date. If the exact date is unknown, create a “check‑in” reminder a week later and snooze weekly until you know the next meeting. [[concepts/reminder-system]]
3. **Designated Storage Spot** – Keep all borrowed items in a single, obvious location at home (e.g., a shelf, basket, or corner). This makes it easy to scan the spot and see what isn’t yours.
4. **Proximity Placement** – Store the borrowed item near things you always take when you leave the house (keys, bag, shoes) to reduce the chance of forgetting it.

## Communication Tips

When it’s time to return an item, use short, friendly messages that acknowledge you still have the item and propose simple return options (e.g., drop‑off or bring it next time you meet). Sample texts are provided in the source document.

## Benefits

- Reduces reliance on memory alone.
- Provides a visual and timed prompt for follow‑up.
- Minimizes awkwardness by offering clear, low‑pressure return logistics.
