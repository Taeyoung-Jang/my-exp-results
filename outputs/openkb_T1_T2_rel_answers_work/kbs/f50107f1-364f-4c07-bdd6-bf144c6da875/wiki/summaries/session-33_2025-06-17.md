## Packing & labeling system  

Use a color‑and‑number scheme per room (e.g., colored stickers + box number) and a consistent label layout:  

- `Room: <Room>`  
- `Box #: <Number>`  
- `Contents: <Brief description>`  
- `Priority: <P1/P2/P3>` (optional)  
- `Notes: <Additional info>`  

See [[concepts/packing-system]] for the overall approach.

## Open‑first box checklist  

Keep a dedicated box with items needed immediately after arrival. Recommended items include:  

- Toiletries & medications  
- Chargers & power bank  
- Two changes of clothes  
- Important documents (ID, passport, lease, insurance)  
- Laptop & daily‑use tech  
- Small first‑aid basics  
- Cleaning wipes or spray  
- Multi‑tool or scissors  
- Bedding or pillow  
- Snacks, water, and a mug  
- Any sentimental or irreplaceable small items  

See [[concepts/open-first-box]].

## Three‑level priority scheme  

Introduce fast codes `P1`, `P2`, `P3` with matching colored stickers:  

- **P1 (Red)** – Must stay with you or ride in your car.  
- **P2 (Yellow)** – On the truck but unload first.  
- **P3 (Blue)** – Can wait; standard loading.  

This scheme helps movers instantly identify handling order. See [[concepts/priority-scheme]].

## Top 10 “can’t‑replace quickly” items to keep out of the truck  

1. Passports, IDs, and other key documents  
2. Medications  
3. Laptop and everyday tech  
4. External drives or backups  
5. Jewelry or small valuables  
6. Sentimental keepsakes  
7. Wallet and banking information  
8. House and car keys  
9. Small emergency kit  
10. Any item required within the first 24 hours (e.g., work device, childcare essentials)  

These recommendations provide a streamlined, repeatable process for moving, ensuring critical items are accessible and high‑priority boxes are clearly identified.
