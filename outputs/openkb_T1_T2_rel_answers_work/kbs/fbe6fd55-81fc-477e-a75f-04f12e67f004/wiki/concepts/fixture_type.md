# Lighting Fixture Types

Lighting fixture types determine how light is distributed, how much glare is produced, and how easily the illumination can be adjusted. The primary categories discussed in [[summaries/session-01_2025-01-20]] are:

- **Flush mount** – sits directly against the ceiling, providing the most even wash of light. Ideal for low ceilings and small home offices where a uniform ambient light level is desired.
- **Semi‑flush mount** – hangs a short distance from the ceiling, allowing a slightly broader spread and reducing the “spotlight” effect while still keeping the fixture close to the ceiling.
- **Pendant** – hangs lower on a rod or chain; best suited to rooms with higher ceilings. When paired with a good [[concepts/diffuser]] (e.g., frosted dome or fabric drum shade) it can deliver focused yet soft illumination, but without proper diffusion it may create glare.
- **LED panel or integrated LED fixture** – consists of a flat panel with built‑in LEDs behind a diffusing layer, offering a large, even light source that minimizes hot spots.

## Key Considerations

1. **Brightness (lumens)** – Choose fixtures capable of delivering roughly **1,500–2,500 lumens** for a small, dim room, as recommended in the session summary. The total lumen output is a crucial factor regardless of the fixture style.
2. **Diffusion** – A quality [[concepts/diffuser]] (frosted, opal, or fabric shade) is essential to spread light evenly and reduce glare. Multiple lower‑wattage LEDs or panel designs typically provide better diffusion than a single high‑watt bulb.
3. **Glare reduction** – Fixtures that hide the light source behind a diffuser or diffusing panel lower the risk of direct glare on the eyes and work surfaces.
4. **Control flexibility** – Pairing any fixture type with a [[concepts/dimmer_switch]] allows fine‑tuning of light levels for different times of day, improving comfort and eye health.

## Choosing the Right Fixture

- **Small home office with low ceiling**: Prefer a **flush** or **semi‑flush mount** with a frosted dome diffuser.
- **Room with higher ceiling**: A **pendant** can be used if it incorporates a high‑quality diffuser and you need a decorative element.
- **Maximum even lighting**: An **LED panel** or **integrated LED fixture** with built‑in diffusion provides the most uniform illumination and is especially effective when paired with dimming control.

By evaluating the space’s size, ceiling height, and desired brightness, you can select the fixture type that best balances illumination quality, glare reduction, and aesthetic preference.
