# Bias Light Strip

A **bias light strip** is a thin, flexible LED lighting element designed to be mounted around the back edge of a monitor. Its primary purpose is to provide a uniform, low‑intensity illumination on the wall behind the screen, reducing the contrast between the display and the surrounding environment and thereby lessening eye strain.

## Key Facts from Session 32 (2025‑07‑23)
- **Placement**: Installed behind the monitor, wrapping the back edge for an even halo effect.  
- **Dimmability**: Recommended to be dimmable so the brightness can be matched to ambient daylight levels.  
- **Color temperature**: A neutral white range of **5000–6500 K** is suggested to avoid tinting screen colors.  
- **Brightness**: Should emit enough lumens to create a subtle glow without overwhelming the workspace.
- **Benefits**: Improves visual comfort, balances screen‑to‑wall contrast, and complements ergonomic monitor placement.
