# Monitor

A monitor is a visual output device commonly used in home workspaces. In the discussion of lighting for a small home office (see [[summaries/session-01_2025-01-20]]), the monitor is mentioned as the focal point for **bias lighting**, a subtle backlight placed behind it to reduce eye strain and improve visual comfort.

## Key points from the session
- **Bias lighting**: Recommended as a finishing touch to complement the main overhead illumination. It provides a gentle glow that eases the contrast between the bright screen and the surrounding darkness.
- **Placement**: The bias light should be positioned behind the monitor, creating an even wash of light on the wall behind the screen.
- **Purpose**: Primarily for comfort rather than mood; it helps maintain consistent visual perception during extended screen use.
- **Interaction with other lighting**: Works alongside a neutral white overhead light (≈4000‑4500 K) and a warmer task lamp to balance overall illumination.
