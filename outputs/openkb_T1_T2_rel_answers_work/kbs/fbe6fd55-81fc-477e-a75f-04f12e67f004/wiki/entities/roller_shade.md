# Roller Shade

**Type:** Product

A **roller shade** is a window covering that rolls up onto a tube, allowing adjustable control of incoming light. In the context of home office glare management, the following facts were highlighted in [[summaries/session-32_2025-07-23]]:

- **Glare reduction:** Light‑filtering roller shades are recommended over sheer curtains for strong afternoon sun because they provide finer control of brightness, directly supporting [[concepts/glare_reduction]].
- **Openness rating:** An openness of **3–5 %** is suggested. For most of the day, a **5 %** openness offers a good balance between glare mitigation and maintaining a bright room, while **3 %** is reserved for periods of intense, blinding sunlight.
- **Fabric choice:** Using a **solar‑screen fabric** (see [[entities/solar_screen_fabric]]) improves glare and heat control compared to regular light‑filtering fabrics, while still allowing a clear view outside.
- **Placement impact:** Positioning the roller shade on a left‑hand window helps reduce direct glare on the monitor, complementing optimal [[concepts/monitor_placement]] (e.g., angling the screen away from the window and increasing distance).
- **Adjustability:** The shade can be partially lowered during peak sun hours and fully raised when natural light is softer, offering dynamic control without darkening the entire space.

These characteristics make roller shades a practical solution for creating a comfortable, well‑lit workspace that minimizes eye strain.
