## LED Bulb

LED bulbs are solid‑state lighting devices that provide high efficiency, long life, and instant illumination. In the context of the **session‑01_2025-01-20** discussion, they are recommended as the primary lighting choice for a small home office.

### Key facts from the session
- **Color temperature**: Use LED bulbs in the **4000‑4500 K** range to achieve a neutral white light that is clear for work without being harsh. [[concepts/color_temperature]]
- **Brightness**: Aim for a combined output of **1,500‑2,500 lumens** for the main overhead fixture to adequately light a dim space. [[concepts/lumens]]
- **Diffusion**: Choose fixtures with a **frosted or opal diffuser**, fabric drum shade, or LED panel with a diffusing layer to minimise glare and create even illumination. [[concepts/diffuser]]
- **Fixture configuration**: Multiple lower‑wattage LED bulbs or integrated LED panels are preferred over a single high‑watt bulb to spread light evenly. This works well with **flush** or **semi‑flush** mount fixtures. [[concepts/fixture_type]]
- **Control**: Pair LED bulbs with a **dimmer switch** to adjust brightness for evening work and reduce eye strain. [[concepts/dimmer_switch]]
- **Supplementary lighting**: While LED bulbs provide the main light, a warmer task lamp and optional bias lighting behind a **monitor** can fine‑tune the visual environment. [[entities/monitor]]

### Relevance to the document
The recommendations for LED bulbs are directly drawn from the guidance in [[summaries/session-01_2025-01-20]], which emphasizes their role in creating a bright, comfortable, and flexible lighting setup for productivity.
