# Solar Screen Fabric

**Type:** Product

Solar Screen Fabric is a specialized textile used in roller shades to control sunlight. It is designed to **reduce glare** and **block heat** more effectively than standard light‑filtering fabrics while still allowing a clear view to the outside.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-32_2025-07-23]]
- Recommended for strong afternoon sun on a left‑hand window.
- Works best with a **3–5 % openness** rating; 5 % is a balanced choice for most of the day.
- Provides superior **glare reduction** compared to regular light‑filtering shades, making it ideal for home office setups.
- Helps maintain a bright room without the need for blackout curtains.

## Related Concepts & Entities
- Used in conjunction with [[entities/roller_shade]] to create an effective window treatment.
- Contributes to overall **glare reduction** ([[concepts/glare_reduction]]) in a workspace.
- When paired with a monitor, proper placement ([[concepts/monitor_placement]]) and ambient lighting (e.g., bias lighting) further improve visual comfort.

## Practical Guidance
1. Choose a solar‑screen fabric roller shade with ~5 % openness for balanced daylight and glare control.
2. Install the shade on the window that receives intense afternoon sun.
3. Combine with a dimmable bias light (e.g., [[entities/bias_light_strip]]) to even out screen contrast.
