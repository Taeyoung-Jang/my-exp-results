**Primary recommendation**: use neutral white [[entities/led_bulb]]s (~4000‑4500 K) for overhead illumination, paired with an adjustable warmer task lamp and optional bias lighting.  

**Overhead fixture priority**: install a high‑output, well‑diffused source delivering about 1,500‑2,500 [[concepts/lumens]] to overcome naturally dim spaces.  

**Diffusion methods**: choose frosted/opal domes, fabric drum shades, or LED panels with a diffusing layer to minimise glare; prefer multiple lower‑wattage [[entities/led_bulb]]s over a single high‑watt bulb for even light distribution.  

**Fixture types**: flush or semi‑flush mounts give the most even wash in low‑ceiling rooms; pendants are suitable only with sufficient ceiling height and proper diffusers.  

**Control flexibility**: a [[concepts/dimmer_switch]] is strongly advised to adjust brightness for evening comfort and eye health.  

**Supplementary lighting**: floor or table lamps can be added later for mood tuning, while bias lighting behind a [[entities/monitor]] serves as a comfort‑focused finishing touch.
