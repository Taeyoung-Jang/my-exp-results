## Summary of session-10_2024-06-15

**Main contribution**: A practical, step‑by‑step workflow and template for extracting key ideas from online video lectures — whether watching the video or using only the transcript — and retaining them effectively.

### Key strategies
- **Purposeful preview** – decide the learning goal before starting any video or transcript. [[concepts/purposeful-preview]]
- **Selective note‑taking** – capture only claims, definitions, processes, and examples; ignore filler. [[concepts/selective-note-taking]]
- **Micro‑rewinds** – if something is missed, rewind just the relevant snippet instead of re‑watching large sections.
- **Immediate self‑explanation** – restate the main point in your own words right after finishing.
- **Brief follow‑up** – review notes later (≈30 s) and add a concrete personal example to cement memory.

### Transcript‑focused workflow (20‑30 min lecture)
1. **Quick skim** (≤1 min) – map structure, spot section breaks and repeated phrases.
2. **Focused read‑through** – highlight sentences that present a claim, definition, process, or example.
3. **Triage for video jumps** – jump only when a line references a visual cue, strong emphasis, or a demo (see the *Triage Rule* below). [[concepts/triage-rule]]
4. **Micro‑video checks** – watch the few seconds needed for visual or tonal context, then return to the transcript.
5. **Three‑sentence summary** – write a concise recap in your own words.
6. **Personal anchor** – note one way you could apply the idea.

### Reusable "Transcript Learning Template"
```
**1. Quick Skim**
- What are the major sections or topic shifts?
- Any repeated terms or obvious “big idea” phrases?

**2. Focused Read‑Through**
- Highlight clear claims, definitions, processes, and examples.
- Ignore filler, small talk, or off‑topic stories.

**3. Triage Rule for Video Jump**
Jump to video only if the section includes:
- A visual reference (e.g., “as you can see…”, “this chart…”).
- Strong emphasis or tone‑dependent meaning (e.g., “the crucial part is…”).
- A demo, gesture, or anything that needs visual context.
Otherwise stay transcript‑only.

**4. Quick Summary**
- In 2–3 sentences, state the core idea of the lecture in your own words.

**5. Personal Anchor**
- Add one example, use case, or connection to something you already know.
```
