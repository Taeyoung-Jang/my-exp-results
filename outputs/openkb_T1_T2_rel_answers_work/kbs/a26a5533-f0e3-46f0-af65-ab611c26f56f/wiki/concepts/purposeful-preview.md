# Purposeful Preview

**Definition**: Purposeful preview is the practice of deciding, in the seconds before starting a video or reading a transcript, exactly what you aim to learn. This intentional framing directs attention toward relevant information and filters out irrelevant details.

## Why it matters
- **Focus**: By stating a goal, you prime your brain to notice key ideas rather than trying to absorb everything passively.
- **Efficiency**: It reduces wasted time scrolling or re‑watching content that does not serve your objective.
- **Retention**: A clear purpose makes the subsequent self‑explanation step (restating ideas in your own words) more effective.

## How to apply (from [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]])
1. **Pause before play** – spend 10 seconds thinking: *What specific insight or skill do I need from this lecture?*
2. **Write a short intent** – jot a one‑sentence goal on a sticky note or in your notes app.
3. **Keep the goal visible** – refer back to it while you skim or watch; if a section doesn’t serve the goal, skip or skim it.
