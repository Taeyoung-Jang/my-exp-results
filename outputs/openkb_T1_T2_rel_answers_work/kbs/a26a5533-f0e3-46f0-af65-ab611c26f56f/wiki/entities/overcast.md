# Overcast

Overcast is a popular podcast player for the **iPhone** that emphasizes simple audio clipping and easy sharing. In the context of the audio‑first learning workflow discussed in [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-16]], Overcast provides the following key capabilities:

- **Clip‑saving** – Users can tap a single button to extract a short snippet from any episode, creating a portable audio highlight. This supports the [[concepts/active-listening]] practice of capturing important moments for later review.
- **Share integration** – Clips can be sent directly to a custom [[entities/apple-shortcuts]] workflow (the "Append to Audio Inbox" shortcut) which appends them to an [[entities/apple-notes]] "Audio Inbox" note. This exemplifies the [[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]] for consolidating audio content.
- **Minimal screen interaction** – The clipping interface is designed for quick taps, aligning with a low‑visual‑effort approach to learning.

### Role in an Audio‑First Knowledge System
Overcast pairs with other tools mentioned in the session, such as **Voice Dream Reader** for text‑to‑speech, **Noted** or **AudioPen** for voice‑note capture, and **Apple Notes** as the central hub. By feeding podcast clips into the same note as TTS highlights and transcribed voice notes, Overcast helps maintain a single, audio‑first repository for review.
