# Apple (Organization)

Apple provides the iOS ecosystem that underpins the audio‑first learning workflow described in the session.

## Key iOS features referenced
- **Speak Screen** – a built‑in text‑to‑speech accessibility option that reads highlighted text aloud. [[concepts/text-to-speech]]
- **Apple Notes** – used as a single "Audio Inbox" where podcast clips, TTS highlights, and voice‑note summaries are stored. [[entities/apple-notes]]
- **Apple Shortcuts** – a share‑sheet shortcut that appends shared audio, text, or files to the Notes hub, enabling automation of the workflow. [[entities/apple-shortcuts]] [[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]]

## Platform
- The workflow is designed for the **iPhone**. [[entities/iphone]]

## Integration with other tools
- Third‑party apps such as **Overcast**, **Airr**, **Voice Dream Reader**, **Noted**, and **AudioPen** can share content directly to Apple Notes via the Shortcut. [[entities/overcast]], [[entities/audio-pen]], [[entities/noted]], [[entities/voice-dream-reader]]

## Related concepts
- The approach leverages **active listening** techniques to improve retention. [[concepts/active-listening]]
