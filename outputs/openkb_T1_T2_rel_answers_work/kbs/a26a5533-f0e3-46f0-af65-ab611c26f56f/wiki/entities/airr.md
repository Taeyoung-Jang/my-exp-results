## Airr (product)

Airr is a podcast application designed for iPhone that lets listeners **highlight specific moments** in an episode and automatically **creates a text transcript** of those highlights. This makes it easy to **search, read, or share** key points from podcasts without having to manually transcribe them.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-16]]
- Mentioned as an alternative to [[entities/overcast]] for saving podcast clips.
- Provides a **clip‑saving feature** that turns highlighted audio into searchable text, supporting the **audio‑first** workflow described in the session.
- Can share clips and transcripts directly to [[entities/apple-notes]] via the iPhone **Share Sheet** or a custom [[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]] built with [[entities/apple-shortcuts]].
- Works well on [[entities/iphone]] and integrates with other audio tools like [[entities/noted]] and [[entities/audio-pen]] for a unified "Audio Inbox".

### How it fits into an audio‑first learning workflow
1. **Capture** – While listening to a podcast, tap the Airr highlight button to mark a moment.
2. **Generate** – Airr automatically produces a text excerpt of the highlighted segment.
3. **Share** – Use the iOS Share Sheet to send the audio clip and its transcript to a Shortcut that appends the content to an Apple Notes file (the central hub for audio learning).
4. **Review** – Open the Notes file, tap the embedded audio, or let **Speak Screen** read the transcript, keeping screen interaction minimal.
