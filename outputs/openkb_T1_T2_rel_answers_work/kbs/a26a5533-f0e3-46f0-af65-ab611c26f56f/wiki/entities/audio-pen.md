# AudioPen

**Type:** product

AudioPen is a mobile app for the **iPhone** that captures quick voice recordings, automatically transcribes them, and generates concise summaries. In the workflow described in [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-16]], it is recommended for turning informal voice‑note takeaways into searchable text without requiring extensive screen interaction.

## Core Features
- **Instant recording** – tap‑and‑record interface designed for minimal friction.
- **Automatic transcription** – speech is converted to text in the background.
- **Summarization** – AI‑driven short summaries are attached to the original audio.
- **Export & integration** – recordings and transcripts can be shared directly to [[entities/apple-notes]] or routed through a [[entities/apple-shortcuts]] shortcut that appends them to a central "Audio Inbox" note.

## Role in the Audio‑First Workflow
1. **Capture**: User records a thought or insight using AudioPen.
2. **Process**: The app transcribes and creates a brief summary.
3. **Share**: Via the iOS Share Sheet, the audio file and its transcript are sent to a custom [[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]] (e.g., "Append to Audio Inbox").
4. **Review**: All items accumulate in a single [[entities/apple-notes]] file, where they can be played back or read aloud using iOS **Speak Screen**.

## Benefits
- Supports **[[concepts/active-listening]]** by turning spoken ideas into retrievable content.
- Enables **[[concepts/selective-note-taking]]** without manual typing.
- Complements other tools like [[entities/overcast]] for podcast clipping and [[entities/voice-dream-reader]] for text‑to‑speech of articles.
