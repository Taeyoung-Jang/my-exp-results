## Noted (product)

**What it is**: Noted is a mobile application for the iPhone that lets users capture short voice recordings and automatically generates transcriptions and concise summaries. It is designed for minimal screen interaction, making it ideal for an *audio‑first* workflow.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-16]]**:
- Suggested as a tool for capturing quick voice‑note takeaways with automatic transcription.
- Works well with the proposed **[[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]]**: users can share recordings directly to an "Audio Inbox" note via an Apple Shortcut.
- Supports the **[[concepts/selective-note-taking]]** approach by turning spoken thoughts into searchable text without manual note‑taking.

**Related concepts**:
- **[[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]]** – the share‑sheet shortcut that appends Noted recordings to Apple Notes.
- **[[concepts/selective-note-taking]]** – using Noted to transform brief spoken ideas into organized, searchable notes.
- **[[concepts/active-listening]]** – encourages listening to transcribed summaries later, reinforcing retention.

**Related entities**:
- [[entities/iphone]] – the platform on which Noted runs.
- [[entities/audio-pen]] – another app mentioned for similar voice‑to‑text functionality.
- [[entities/apple-notes]] – the hub where Noted’s outputs are stored.

**Typical usage**:
1. Record a thought or reminder in Noted while on the go.
2. Use the Share sheet to send the audio and its transcript to the **"Audio Inbox"** note via the custom shortcut.
3. Later, open the note, tap the audio to listen again, or let Siri read the transcript using **Speak Screen**.

**Why it matters**: Noted simplifies the capture‑and‑review loop for audio learners, reducing reliance on visual interfaces and supporting the eye‑strain‑reduction strategy outlined in the session.
