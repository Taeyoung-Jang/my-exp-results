# Voice Dream Reader

**Type:** Product

Voice Dream Reader is a **text‑to‑speech** application for the **iPhone** that can read articles, PDFs, and scanned documents aloud with natural‑sounding voices.  In the conversation of [[summaries/session-34_2024-12-16]], it was highlighted as one of the best all‑purpose options for audio‑first learning because it handles multiple file formats and offers pleasant voices suitable for long listening sessions.

## Key Facts from Session 34
- Recommended for turning written content into spoken form with minimal effort.  
- Supports articles, PDFs, and even scanned documents.  
- Provides a library of voices that are comfortable for extended periods of listening.  
- Can be integrated into a broader **audio‑first workflow** using [[concepts/shortcuts-workflow]] to send highlights or excerpts directly to [[entities/apple-notes]] (or any other hub).  
- Works smoothly on [[entities/iphone]] devices.

## Related Concepts & Entities

- [[entities/apple-shortcuts]] – used to automate sharing of audio or text from Voice Dream Reader.
