## Summary

- The user requested short, friendly availability messages for their team, highlighting focused work hours (2–5 pm) and preferred contact methods.  
- Assistant provided formal and casual drafts, then shortened them to 3–4 lines and added a meeting‑booking guideline.  
- The final casual version was softened to a friendly preference rather than a rule.

**Key concepts**  
- [[concepts/availability-messaging]]: concise communication of personal work windows and contact options.  
- [[concepts/focus-zones]]: reserving afternoon hours for deep work.  
- [[concepts/communication-preferences]]: using [[entities/slack]] for non‑urgent matters, text/call for urgent issues.  
- [[concepts/meeting-clustering]]: requesting avoidance of meetings during focused periods.  
- Tone variations: formal vs casual, balancing friendliness and clarity.
