## Slack

Slack is a cloud‑based team messaging product that enables real‑time text communication, file sharing, and integrations across organizations.

In the document **session‑39_2024‑12‑07**, Slack is recommended as the primary channel for non‑urgent requests: the user asks team members to *"Slack me and I’ll reply after 5"* and to reserve texts or calls for truly urgent matters. This guidance is part of an overall **availability messaging** strategy and reflects specific **communication preferences** for managing deep‑work periods (focus zones) in the afternoon.
