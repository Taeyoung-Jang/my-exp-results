# Summary of session-13_2024-06-15

**Purpose** – Provide a practical method for building a realistic weekly routine that balances productivity, rest, chores, exercise, and personal growth without feeling overly regimented.

## Core Questions
- What natural energy peaks do you have?
- Which commitments are non‑negotiable?
- How do you define “productive” and “restful” for yourself?
- How much flexibility versus structure feels sustainable?

These questions inform the selection of **anchors** – recurring time‑blocks that give the week its rhythm.

## Structured Template
| Day | Morning option | Evening fallback |
|-----|----------------|------------------|
| **Monday** | 6:30–7:15 am workout | 6:30–7 pm light workout, quiet night |
| **Tuesday** | — | 8–9 pm chores/admin |
| **Wednesday** | 6:30–7:15 am workout | 6:30–7 pm light workout, easy night |
| **Thursday** | — | 8–9 pm reading/learning |
| **Friday** | 6:30–7:15 am workout | 5:30–6 pm lighter session, relaxed night |
| **Saturday** | 9–10 am longer movement | Open evening |
| **Sunday** | 10:30–11:30 am home reset | Slow, unstructured evening |

### Anchor Duration Rule of Thumb
- **Workout**: 30–45 min
- **Chores/Admin**: ≤ 60 min
- **Reading/Learning**: 30–60 min
- **Downtime**: ≥ 1 hour (longer on weekends)

## Flexible Template – “Pick‑4‑Blocks” Menu
- [ ] Short workout – 6:30–7:15 am **or** 6–6:45 pm (≈ 40 min)
- [ ] Chores/Admin – 7:30–9 pm (≈ 1 h)
- [ ] Reading/Learning – any evening, 30–60 min
- [ ] Weekend slow‑start – no obligations before 10 am, with optional longer walk or workout

Choose any four blocks that fit your weekly energy; days can be swapped freely, and one evening should remain untouched for breathing room.

## Recovery Strategies
- **Missed Tuesday chores/admin**: Move a reduced 30–40 min version to the next light evening; prioritize only top items.
- **Missed workout**: Accept the skip; optionally add a 10–15 min walk that day but keep the regular workout schedule unchanged.

The approach emphasizes rhythm over rigid schedules, allowing the routine to survive “bad weeks” without snowballing. It draws on principles from [[concepts/weekly-routine-design]], uses [[concepts/time-blocking]] to define anchor windows, and applies [[concepts/anchor-recovery]] for handling missed blocks.
