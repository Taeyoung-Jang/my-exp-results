# Summary of session-34_2024-12-13

**Document type:** Conversation transcript providing actionable advice for a new county government employee.

## Core Concepts
- **Onboarding Checklist** – A timeline split into *week before*, *night before*, and *day one* tasks, covering paperwork, ID, badge/temporary pass, equipment, and security rules.
- **Badge Access** – Explanation of delayed badge issuance, temporary visitor passes, and entry-point guidance.
- **Security Training** – Required government trainings (ethics, cybersecurity, harassment, records handling) and how to obtain early access.
- **HR Communication** – Specific questions to ask HR about badge pickup, entrance selection, training rollout, and IT activation.
- **Supervisor Communication** – Targeted inquiries for the manager regarding meeting location, workflow expectations, and handling of missing logins.
- **Day One Logistics** – Practical steps for arrival, check‑in, equipment handling, and note‑taking.
- **Chain of Command** – Understanding reporting hierarchy before the first day.

## Checklist Overview
### A week before
- Complete all HR forms (tax, direct deposit, background/security).
- Verify photo ID and badge photo are on file.
- Ask HR about early access to mandatory trainings and temporary visitor pass.
- Confirm start‑time, building address, and equipment availability.
- Touch base with supervisor on start‑time and location.

### Night before
- Pack government ID, notebook, charger, and any required documents.
- Review security policies (device restrictions, visitor sign‑in, floor access).
- Re‑read the first‑day email for entrance instructions.
- Map commute and plan to arrive early.

### Day one
- Present ID at reception; obtain badge or temporary pass.
- Sign in using visitor log if badge isn’t active.
- Expect IT to activate email/system logins later in the morning.
- Use downtime for introductions, chain‑of‑command mapping, and office norm observation.
- Log into systems as soon as credentials are ready; report any issues.

## Key Takeaways
- Proactively finish all administrative tasks to avoid day‑one delays.
- Clarify badge/temporary‑pass logistics with HR to ensure building entry.
- Request early access to mandatory trainings where possible.
- Keep concise, practical communication with both HR and the supervisor to appear prepared, not high‑maintenance.
- Use early‑day downtime for networking and understanding office procedures.
