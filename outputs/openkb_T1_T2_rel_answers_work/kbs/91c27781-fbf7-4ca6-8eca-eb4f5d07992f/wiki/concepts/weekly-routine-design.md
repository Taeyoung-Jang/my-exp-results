# Weekly Routine Design

**Definition** – A method for creating a repeatable weekly schedule that balances productivity, rest, chores, exercise, and personal growth without imposing a rigid, boot‑camp style structure.

## Core Principles
1. **Identify natural rhythms** – Ask about energy peaks, non‑negotiable commitments, and personal definitions of productivity and rest.
2. **Use anchors** – Repeating time‑blocks (e.g., workout, chores, reading) that give the week its rhythm.
3. **Choose a structure level** – Either a **structured template** with fixed anchor days or a **flexible template** that offers a “pick‑4‑blocks” menu.
4. **Apply recovery** – When an anchor is missed, shift it minimally rather than over‑loading the rest of the week.

## Structured Template (example from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]])
- **Morning workout**: 6:30–7:15 am on Mon, Wed, Fri (evening fallback 6:30–7 pm if needed).
- **Tuesday evening**: 8–9 pm chores/admin.
- **Thursday evening**: 8–9 pm reading/learning.
- **Weekend blocks**: longer movement on Sat (9–10 am) and home reset on Sun (10:30–11:30 am).
- **Anchor duration rule of thumb**: workouts 30–45 min, chores ≤ 60 min, reading 30–60 min, downtime ≥ 1 hour.

## Flexible Template – Pick‑4‑Blocks Menu
- Short workout (6:30–7:15 am or 6–6:45 pm, ~40 min).
- Chores/admin (7:30–9 pm, ~1 h).
- Reading/learning (any evening, 30–60 min).
- Weekend slow‑start (no obligations before 10 am) with optional longer movement.

Choose any four blocks that fit the week; days can be swapped freely, and one evening remains untouched for breathing room.

## Recovery Strategies
- **Missed chores/admin**: Move a reduced 30–40 min version to the next light evening, focusing on top items.
- **Missed workout**: Accept the skip; optionally add a 10–15 min walk that day, but keep the regular workout schedule unchanged.

These strategies embody the concept of [[concepts/anchor-recovery]].
