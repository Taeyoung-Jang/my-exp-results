# Notes App

**Type:** Product

The **Notes App** is a digital note‑taking tool that the user employs to keep structured, copy‑ready checklists for their pre‑work routine on night shifts. In the conversation captured in [[summaries/session-33_2025-08-15]], the user asks for a checklist formatted for direct insertion into the app, highlighting its role as a practical habit‑support tool.

## Key Facts from the Session
- The user wants a **checkbox list** that can be copied verbatim into the Notes App for daily use.
- The checklist includes steps such as packing lunch, starting the coffee maker, placing a badge note, eating dinner, checking essentials (including a charger), grabbing lunch, pouring coffee into a travel mug, and heading out.
- A **one‑minute backup version** and a **tiny reset routine** are also provided, showing the app’s flexibility for quick reference.
- The checklist is designed to support a **pre‑work ritual** that aligns with the concepts of [[concepts/pre-work-ritual]] and [[concepts/habit-stacking]], allowing the user to chain small actions into a reliable flow.
- The backup plan reflects the idea of a **minimum fallback** strategy, akin to [[concepts/minimum-fallback]], for days when time is scarce.
- The routine is tailored for **night‑shift productivity**, connecting to [[concepts/night-shift-productivity]].

## Related Entities and Concepts
- The checklist references physical items such as a **[[entities/coffee-maker]]**, a **[[entities/travel-mug]]**, and optional **[[entities/bottled-caffeine]]** for the one‑minute version.
- The habit‑anchoring approach aligns with [[concepts/habit-anchoring]] (though not directly linked here, it underpins the routine’s stability).

## Usage
Users can create a new note in the Notes App, paste the provided checklist, and check off items each day. The app serves as a central hub for habit reinforcement, enabling quick visual cues and ensuring consistency across irregular schedules.
