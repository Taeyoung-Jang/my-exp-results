# Distribution Center

**Type:** Place  
**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-33_2025-08-15]]

## Overview
The distribution center is the user’s night‑shift workplace referenced in *session‑33*. It is the setting for a structured [[concepts/pre-work-ritual]] that helps the user transition smoothly from home to work.

## Key facts from the conversation
- The user works the night shift at the distribution center and needs a concise 10–15 minute routine before leaving.
- The routine relies on **habit stacking**: packing lunch at 4 p.m., starting a [[entities/coffee-maker]], placing a badge‑note, and checking essentials.
- When time is scarce, a one‑minute “fallback” version is employed, aligning with [[concepts/minimum-fallback]].
- The overall goal is to boost [[concepts/night-shift-productivity]] by reducing decision fatigue and scrambling.

## Related tools & items

- [[entities/notes-app]] – where the checklist is stored for quick copy‑and‑paste.
- [[entities/travel-mug]] – holds the brewed coffee for the commute.
