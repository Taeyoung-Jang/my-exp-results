# Themed Rotation

**Type:** Concept

**Definition**: A habit‑building strategy that assigns broad, recurring themes (e.g., movement, learning, creative) to specific days of the week, while allowing the exact activity within each theme to vary according to energy and interest.

## Core Principles

1. **Day‑based Themes** – Each weekday is associated with a high‑level habit category, providing a gentle rhythm without rigid daily tasks.  
2. **Activity Flexibility** – Within a theme, users can choose from several low‑friction options (different workouts, lessons, or creative micro‑tasks), preserving variety and motivation.  
3. **Anchoring Routine** – A brief, consistent start‑up ritual (phone away, water, one‑minute decision) signals the transition into the habit block.  
4. **Minimum Fallback** – A clearly defined, ultra‑short action (e.g., two minutes of stretch, one page of notes, or a single sketch line) guarantees streak continuity on low‑energy evenings.

## How It Appears in the Source Document

The concept is illustrated in [[summaries/session-02_2025-06-15]], where a weekly schedule is proposed:
- **Monday & Wednesday:** Movement evenings with options like a short strength routine, a video workout, or a walk.
- **Tuesday & Thursday:** Learning evenings with language flashcards, coding exercises, or concept reviews.
- **Friday/Weekend:** Creative evenings with writing, sketching, or editing tasks.

Each night begins with a **quick‑start ritual** that anchors the habit, embodying [[concepts/habit-anchoring]]. The schedule also defines explicit **minimum fallback** actions for each theme, linking to [[concepts/minimum-fallback]].

## Benefits
- **Structure with Flexibility:** Provides enough predictability to reduce decision fatigue while preserving freedom to adapt daily.
- **Progress & Variety:** Allows steady progress in each domain through varied micro‑activities, preventing boredom.
- **Streak Protection:** The minimum fallback ensures the habit streak remains unbroken, reinforcing consistency.
