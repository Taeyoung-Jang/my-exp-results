## Summary of session-02_2025-06-15

**Main contribution:** A practical framework for designing small, consistent evening habits that balance structure with flexibility, including themed weekly schedules, starter‑activity options, and unambiguous minimum fallback actions.

### Key Concepts
- **Habit anchoring** – linking new habits to existing evening routines (e.g., a phone‑away ritual). [[concepts/habit-anchoring]]
- **Themed rotation** – assigning lightweight themes (movement, learning, creative) to specific days while keeping daily actions flexible. [[concepts/themed-rotation]]
- **Progress‑driven variety** – varying content within each theme (different workouts, lessons, creative tasks) to sustain motivation.
- **Minimum fallback** – a clearly defined, ultra‑short activity that counts toward the streak on low‑energy nights. [[concepts/minimum-fallback]]

### Weekly Schedule (1–2 hour evening block)
| Day | Theme | Quick‑Start Ritual | Core Options (2‑3) | Minimum Fallback |
|-----|-------|-------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| **Monday** & **Wednesday** | Movement | Phone away, water, 1‑min decision | 1️⃣ Short strength routine 2️⃣ Follow‑along video 3️⃣ Brisk walk | 2 min light stretch or short walk |
| **Tuesday** & **Thursday** | Learning | Phone away, water, 1‑min decision | 1️⃣ Language flashcards 2️⃣ Coding exercise 3️⃣ Concept review + short video | 1 min review of notes or read one page |
| **Friday** (or weekend) | Creative | Phone away, water, 1‑min decision | 1️⃣ Write a paragraph 2️⃣ Sketch a quick idea 3️⃣ Edit a photo/video | Open project & add one sentence/line or make a tiny edit |

### Design Principles
1. **Start small** – begin with a habit that feels doable on the worst day.  
2. **Anchor to existing cues** – the ritual creates a mental switch into habit time.  
3. **Keep variety within themes** – rotate specific activities to avoid boredom while maintaining overall focus.  
4. **Define an unambiguous minimum** – ensures streak continuity even on tired evenings.  
5. **Flexible time allocation** – the 1–2 hour window can be filled partially; the minimum guarantees progress.

### Suggested Starter Routines (per theme)
- **Movement:** Change into workout clothes after dinner, 5‑min warm‑up, then 20‑30 min of chosen activity.  
- **Learning:** Set up a frictionless workspace, 10‑min recap of prior material, then a focused 15‑30 min practice.  
- **Creative:** Open the project, do a quick “entry” action (sketch, write, edit) for 5‑10 min, then continue if energy permits.

### Outcome
The model helps users create sustainable evening habits that align with their energy patterns, desire for progress, and need for variety, while providing a clear fallback to maintain streak integrity.
