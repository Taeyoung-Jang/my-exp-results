# Summary of session-37 (2025-02-11)

## Core framework
- Treat chores as an ongoing conversation rather than a one‑time negotiation.
- Use a **point‑person** model that can shift weekly. [[concepts/point_person]]
- Ask grounding questions about time, energy, disliked tasks, and perceived unfairness (chore fairness questions).

## Chore‑meeting template [[concepts/chore_meeting_template]]
1. **Check‑in** – each partner shares what went smoothly, what felt heavy, any tension.  
2. **Look ahead** – upcoming schedule changes or events.  
3. **Assign point‑person** for each core chore (cooking, cleaning, laundry, errands) for the coming week.  
4. **Reflection questions** – does the split feel fair? any hidden dislikes? any small tweaks?

## Low‑effort tracking
- Shared note with a weekly snapshot of who is point‑person for each chore; resets each week to keep it from feeling like a scorecard.

## Handling schedule spikes [[concepts/flexible_chore_adjustment]]
- Agree in advance that the partner with more bandwidth temporarily takes on extra tasks.
- Re‑evaluate at the next weekly check‑in.

## Example splits
```
---------------------------------------------------------------
PLAN                  | Weeknights | Weekends | Behind‑the‑Scenes
---------------------------------------------------------------
Conservative Split    | You: Dinner + kitchen reset
                      | Partner: All meals + full kitchen cleanup
                      | Partner: Groceries / weekly stock‑up
---------------------------------------------------------------
More Equal Split      | You: Lead dinners (choose meals, cook, do basic dishes, store leftovers)
                      | Partner: All weekend cleanup + 1–2 meals
                      | Partner: Sunday meal prep for two weeknights + bathrooms/floors
---------------------------------------------------------------
Busy Season Split     | You: Mon–Thu dinners + quick reset
                      | Partner: Fri dinner + all weekend meals and full reset
                      | Partner: Groceries, errands, supplies
---------------------------------------------------------------
```

## Two‑week trial check‑in script (weekly check‑in)
> "Let’s do a quick review of the last two weeks. Did this version feel fair for both of us? Is anything feeling heavier than expected, or something we want to swap or simplify? I’m happy to adjust together so the system stays balanced with our schedules."

## Weekly checkbox list (simple verification)
```
[ ] Dinners went as planned
[ ] Kitchen stayed at our agreed “baseline”
[ ] Groceries/stock‑up done
[ ] Weekend reset completed
[ ] Laundry handled
[ ] Any swaps or adjustments noted
```
