# Green Card

**Type:** other

The **green card** is the informal name for the United States Permanent Resident Card, which grants the holder lawful permanent residency in the United States. It serves as proof of the holder’s right to live and work indefinitely in the country.

## Key Facts from Session 13 (2022‑06‑15)
- The document is mentioned as an alternative phrasing for *lawful permanent resident* status.
- In professional contexts such as resumes and LinkedIn profiles, the term *green card holder* is considered informal; more formal language like "U.S. permanent resident" or "lawful permanent resident" is recommended.
- When filling out job applications, selecting the **Permanent Resident** option is appropriate, and the holder does not require visa sponsorship.
