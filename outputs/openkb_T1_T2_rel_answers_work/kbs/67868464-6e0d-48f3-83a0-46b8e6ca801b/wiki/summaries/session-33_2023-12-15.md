# Summary of session-33_2023-12-15

**Topic:** Determining the correct "naturalization number" to enter on the California online voter‑registration portal and how to deal with the form’s digit‑only validation.

## Key Points
- The portal asks for the *naturalization number* from the **Certificate of Naturalization**, not the A‑Number, USCIS #, or receipt number found on USCIS letters. naturalization number
- The certificate’s unique **Certificate Number** is typically located in the top‑right corner of the document and may contain letters and digits. certificate number
- California’s form often incorrectly states the number must be 7–8 digits, causing validation errors when letters are included.
- The accepted workaround is to **remove any letters** and enter the full string of digits, preserving any leading zeros. form validation
- Users should not truncate the digit string to the first 7–8 digits; the entire digit sequence (after stripping letters) should be entered. leading zeros
- If uncertainty remains, contacting the local county elections office can provide confirmation, though the digit‑only entry is generally sufficient. California voter registration

## Practical Advice
1. Locate the **Certificate Number** on your naturalization certificate.
2. Strip any alphabetic characters, leaving only the numeric portion.
3. Enter the full numeric string (including leading zeros) into the CA voter‑registration form.
4. If the form still rejects the entry, verify with your county elections office.
