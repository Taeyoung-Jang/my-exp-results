# iPhone (product)

The iPhone is Apple's line of smartphones running the iOS operating system. In the context of corporate **Mobile Device Management (MDM)**, iPhones can be placed in a **supervised** or **managed** state, limiting user control over apps and settings.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2024-12-13]]
- The user’s device is **supervised** (Settings → General → VPN & Device Management shows a managed profile), which restricts deletion of managed apps but still allows moving icons.
- The document outlines a step‑by‑step process for reorganizing the home screen:
  1. Long‑press an app icon to enter edit mode.
  2. Create or use a folder and drag all gray “restricted” (managed) icons into it.
  3. Move the folder to the right‑most home‑screen page, making the left‑most page the personal default.
  4. No iOS setting exists to stop new managed apps from auto‑appearing; they must be moved manually.
- The **App Library** does not prevent supervised apps from appearing on active pages, but can be used to keep personal apps out of sight after organization.
