# Smartphone

**Type:** product

## Overview
A smartphone is a portable, touchscreen-equipped computer that combines telephony, internet connectivity, and a wide range of applications (apps). It serves as the primary platform for personal digital tasks, including communication, productivity, entertainment, and utility functions.

## Relevance in [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]]
The document provides a systematic approach for **app decluttering** on a smartphone. It introduces a **staging folder** strategy and a **scoring rubric** to evaluate which apps to keep, stage, or delete. The process is designed specifically for the smartphone environment, leveraging built‑in usage statistics and cloud‑based backup features.
