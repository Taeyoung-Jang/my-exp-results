## IT Department

The **IT Department** is the internal organization responsible for provisioning, configuring, and enforcing mobile device management (MDM) policies on employees' smartphones. In the context of *session‑39_2024‑12‑13*, the IT Department:

- Enrolled the user's phone in a **mobile‑device‑management** system, resulting in supervised iOS restrictions and gray placeholder icons on the home screen.
- Controls the deployment of managed apps, which automatically appear on the device’s home screen and cannot be blocked by the user.
- Provides the policies that dictate whether a device is under full‑device supervision or a lighter work‑profile arrangement.
- Serves as the point of contact for users seeking a work/personal profile split or alternative solutions such as a second phone.
