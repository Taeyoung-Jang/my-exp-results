## App Scoring Rubric

A lightweight decision‑making framework introduced in [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-15]] for assessing whether a smartphone app should be **kept**, **staged**, or **deleted**. The rubric assigns a score from 0 to 4 based on four criteria, each reflecting a different aspect of an app's value and cost.

### Scoring criteria
| Criterion | Points | What to consider |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| **Function uniqueness** | 0‑2 | Does the app perform a task that no other installed app can replace? More unique functionality yields higher points. |
| **Ease of reinstallation** | 0‑1 | Are the app’s data and settings synced to the cloud, making re‑installation low‑risk? Higher points for easy recovery. |
| **Intended usage pattern** | 0‑1 | Is the app designed for occasional, purposeful use (e.g., travel, creative tools)? A point is awarded when occasional use is expected. |
| **Friction (storage/notifications)** | 0 or ‑1 | Does the app consume a lot of storage or generate annoying notifications? Subtract a point if it adds noticeable friction. |

### Decision thresholds
- **Keep** – total score **3–4**
- **Stage** – total score **1–2**
- **Delete** – total score **0 or below**

### Example applications
| App | Function uniqueness | Re‑install ease | Intended use | Friction | Total | Decision |
|-----|----------------------|------------------|-------------|----------|-------|----------|
| Field recorder | 2 | 0 | 1 | 0 | **3** | Keep |
| Transit app (occasional) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | **3** | Keep |
| Password manager | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 | **4** | Keep |
| Redundant photo editor | 0 | 1 | 0 | -1 | **0** | Delete |

### How it relates to the broader process
The rubric is a core component of the overall [[concepts/app-decluttering-process]], working alongside the [[concepts/staging-folder]] approach. First, an app’s recent usage is checked; then, ambiguous apps are placed in a staging folder. Finally, the rubric is applied to decide the ultimate fate of each app.
