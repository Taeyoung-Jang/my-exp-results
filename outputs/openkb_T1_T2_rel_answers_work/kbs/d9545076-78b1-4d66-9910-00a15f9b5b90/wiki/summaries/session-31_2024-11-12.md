# Summary of session-31_2024-11-12

**Topic:** How to bolster a rental application when credit is sub‑optimal and explore legitimate alternatives such as higher deposits, prepaid rent, and guarantor services.

## Key Points

- **Landlord evaluation criteria** beyond income: credit history, prior rent‑payment record, eviction history, landlord/ employer references, job stability, and background checks. See [[concepts/landlord_evaluation_criteria]].
- **Straightforward ways to strengthen an application**:
  - Prepare organized documentation (pay stubs, ID, employer contact, reference letters).
  - Supply strong references from previous landlords or managers.
  - Offer a brief, honest explanation for credit blemishes.
  - Include a concise “renter intro” note to convey reliability.
  - Propose favorable lease terms (earlier move‑in, longer lease, proof of savings).
  - These practices align with [[concepts/rental_application_strengthening]].
- **Alternatives for weak credit**:
  - Higher security deposit (subject to legal caps).
  - Prepaying one or more months of rent.
  - Using third‑party guarantor services, e.g., [[entities/the_guarantors]], [[entities/rhino]], [[entities/insurent]].
- **Common guarantor services & costs**:
  - Typically charge a one‑time or annual fee ranging from ~0.5 × monthly rent to ~1 × monthly rent.
  - May operate as an insurance‑style premium.
  - Overview in [[concepts/guarantor_services_overview]].
- **Risks & safeguards** for prepaid rent or larger deposits:
  - Verify state‑law limits on security deposits (often 1‑2 months’ rent).
  - Ensure payments go to the official landlord/management account and are documented in writing.
  - Watch for pressure to pay outside normal channels (potential scam).
- **Questions to ask property managers about guarantor services**:
  1. Which guarantor companies are accepted?
  2. What type of coverage or approval letter is required?
  3. At what stage of the application should guarantor paperwork be submitted?
  4. Preferred submission method (portal, email, etc.)?
  5. When must the guarantor fee be paid?
- **Contract terms to double‑check before paying guarantor fees**:
  - One‑time vs. recurring fee.
  - Coverage duration and renewal requirements.
  - Conditions that could void coverage.
  - Refundability (generally non‑refundable).
  - Obligations to update income or employment status.
