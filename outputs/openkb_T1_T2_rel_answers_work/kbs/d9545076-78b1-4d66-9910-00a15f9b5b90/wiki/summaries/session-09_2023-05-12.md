# Summary of Session 9 (2023-05-12)

This conversation provides a detailed strategy for applying for a travel rewards credit card and later refinancing an auto loan while protecting a 750‑point credit score.

## Key Recommendations
- **Apply for the travel card first.** Card issuers are more sensitive to hard inquiries than auto lenders. [[concepts/hard_inquiries]]
- **Wait 4–6 weeks** after the card approval before beginning auto‑loan rate shopping. This allows the inquiry to settle and the new account to age slightly.
- **Rate‑shop the auto refinance within a ~14‑day window.** Scoring models treat all auto‑loan inquiries in that period as a single hard inquiry. [[concepts/auto_loan_rate_shopping]]
- **Keep post‑card‑opening utilization extremely low.** Pay down the balance before the statement closing date so the reported balance stays near zero. [[concepts/credit_utilization]]
- **Determine the statement closing date** via the online account or issuer support; the balance on the statement is what is reported to bureaus.
- **If a large purchase is needed for the sign‑up bonus,** make multiple payments and ensure the balance is nearly paid off before the statement closes (e.g., leave ~$10 reported). Leaving a tiny balance is fine; zero is equally good for the score.

## Sample Timeline
1. **Week 0:** Apply for the travel card.
2. **Within days:** Make the $2,500 purchase for the bonus.
3. **Immediately:** Pay $2,400, then pay the remaining ~ $10 a day or two before the statement closes.
4. **Weeks 5–6:** Begin auto‑loan rate shopping, keeping all lender pulls within a 14‑day window. [[concepts/auto_loan_rate_shopping]]

The plan outlined ensures the credit score remains stable when lenders pull the report for the auto refinance.
