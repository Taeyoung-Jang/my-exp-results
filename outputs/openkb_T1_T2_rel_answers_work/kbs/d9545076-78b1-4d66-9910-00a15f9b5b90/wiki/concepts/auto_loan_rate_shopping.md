## Auto Loan Rate Shopping

**Definition**: Auto loan rate shopping is the process of obtaining multiple auto‑loan quotes from different lenders within a brief, defined period (typically about two weeks) so that credit scoring models treat the resulting hard inquiries as a single inquiry.

### Why It Matters
- Scoring models, such as FICO, group auto‑loan related hard inquiries that occur within a short window into one inquiry, minimizing the negative effect on a credit score.
- This allows borrowers to find the best interest rate without suffering multiple score drops.

### Key Points from [[summaries/session-09_2023-05-12]]
- **Timing**: Begin auto‑refinance rate shopping **4–6 weeks** after applying for a new travel credit card to let the card inquiry settle.
- **Shopping Window**: Keep all lender pulls within **approximately 14 days**. Inquiries made within this window are treated as a single hard inquiry.
- **Score Impact**: A single grouped inquiry typically costs only a few points and fades quickly.

### Interaction with Other Concepts
- Related to [[concepts/hard_inquiries]]: Understanding how hard inquiries are counted is essential for effective rate shopping.
- Related to [[concepts/credit_utilization]]: While the focus is on inquiries, maintaining low utilization on existing credit lines (including new cards) supports a stable score during the shopping period.

### Practical Tips
1. **Plan the timeline** – Apply for any new credit cards first, then wait the recommended 4–6 weeks before starting rate shopping.
2. **Set a clear window** – Mark a two‑week period on your calendar and aim to complete all lender applications within it.
3. **Monitor your credit report** – Verify that the inquiries are grouped as expected and that no unexpected hard inquiries appear.
4. **Maintain low utilization** – Keep balances low on existing cards during the shopping window to avoid additional score volatility.

By following these guidelines, borrowers can secure the most favorable auto‑loan terms while preserving their credit health.
