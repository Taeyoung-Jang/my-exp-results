# Guarantor Services Overview

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** An explanation of third‑party guarantor services that renters can use to strengthen a rental application when their credit score is insufficient.

---

## What are guarantor services?
Guarantor services are companies that act as a financial backer for a tenant, guaranteeing payment of rent to the landlord if the tenant defaults. They operate similarly to an insurance policy: the tenant pays a fee, and the service commits to cover missed rent up to a specified amount for the lease term.

## Why renters use them
* **Weak credit scores** – as highlighted in [[summaries/session-31_2024-11-12]], a credit score in the low‑600s can make an application harder to approve.
* **No co‑signer available** – renters without a friend or family member who can co‑sign can still provide a guarantee through a service.
* **Speed and convenience** – many property managers are already familiar with these services, allowing quicker approval.

## Common providers (mentioned in the source)
* **[[entities/insurent]]** – a national guarantor service.
* **[[entities/rhino]]** – offers rent‑guarantee insurance.
* **[[entities/the_guarantors]]** – another widely used guarantor company.

## Typical cost structure
Fees usually range from **0.5 to 1 month’s rent**, either as a one‑time payment or an annual premium. The exact amount depends on the tenant’s credit, income, and the property’s rent.

## How to introduce guarantor services to a landlord
1. Ask directly: “Do you accept guarantor services, and if so, which ones?”
2. Confirm the required documentation (approval letter, coverage details).
3. Clarify submission method (portal, email, etc.) and timing within the application process.

## Key contract terms to review before paying
* **Fee type** – one‑time vs. recurring.
* **Coverage duration** – does it cover the entire lease or only the initial term?
* **Void conditions** – income changes, lease extensions, or policy violations that could cancel coverage.
* **Refundability** – most fees are non‑refundable.
* **Renewal requirements** – whether a new fee is needed for lease renewal.
