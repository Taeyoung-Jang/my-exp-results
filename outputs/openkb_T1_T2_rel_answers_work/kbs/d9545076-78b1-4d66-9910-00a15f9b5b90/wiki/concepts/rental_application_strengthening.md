# Rental Application Strengthening

**What it is**: A set of strategies renters can use to present themselves as reliable tenants, especially when credit scores are less than ideal.

## Overview
Landlords evaluate applicants using a range of factors grouped under [[concepts/landlord_evaluation_criteria]].  Beyond income, they look at credit history, prior rent‑payment record, eviction history, references, and job stability.  *Rental Application Strengthening* focuses on proactive steps that address these criteria and provide additional assurances.

## Documentation Checklist
- Recent pay stubs
- Photo ID
- Employer contact information
- Prior‑landlord or property‑manager reference letters
- Bank statements or proof of savings
- A brief, honest note explaining any credit blemishes
- A short “renter intro” paragraph that conveys responsibility and friendliness

Having these items organized and ready signals reliability and reduces friction in the review process.

## Leveraging Lease Terms
- Offer to move in sooner than the advertised date.
- Propose a longer lease term (e.g., 18‑24 months) to show commitment.
- Highlight any savings or emergency funds you have available.

These concessions can offset concerns about credit risk.

## Alternative Guarantees for Weak Credit
| Option | How it helps | Key considerations |
|--------|--------------|--------------------|
| **Higher security deposit** | Provides the landlord with extra financial cushion. | Many states cap deposits at 1‑2 months’ rent; verify local limits. |
| **Prepaid rent (1‑2 months)** | Shows ability to pay upfront and reduces perceived risk. | Get a written receipt and confirm the payment goes to the official management account. |
| **Guarantor services** | A third‑party backs your lease obligations. | Refer to [[concepts/guarantor_services_overview]] and the specific providers [[entities/the_guarantors]], [[entities/rhino]], [[entities/insurent]]. |

## Questions to Ask Property Managers About Guarantor Services
1. Which guarantor companies do you accept? (e.g., The Guarantors, Rhino, Insurent)
2. What type of coverage or approval letter is required?
3. At what stage of the application should the guarantor paperwork be submitted?
4. How should the documents be delivered (portal, email, etc.)?
5. When does the guarantor fee need to be paid – before approval or after acceptance?

## Contract Terms to Review Before Paying a Guarantor Fee
- Whether the fee is **one‑time** or **recurring** (annual renewal).
- **Coverage duration** and whether it automatically renews for lease extensions.
- Conditions that could **void the coverage** (e.g., income changes, breach of lease).
- **Refundability** of the fee (typically non‑refundable).
- Any obligations to **update income or employment** information during the lease.
