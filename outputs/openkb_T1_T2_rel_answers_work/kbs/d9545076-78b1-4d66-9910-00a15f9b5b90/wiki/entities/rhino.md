# Rhino

**Type:** Organization (guarantor service)

**Overview:** Rhino provides a third‑party guarantee for renters who have low or imperfect credit scores, allowing them to obtain tenancy by covering rent payments in the event of default.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2024-11-12]]:**
- Mentioned as one of the common national guarantor companies alongside The Guarantors and Insurent.
- Typical fee structure ranges from about half a month’s rent to one month’s rent, charged either as a one‑time fee or an annual premium.
- Landlords may request a guarantor approval letter or certificate from Rhino during the application process.

**How it fits into rental application strategy:**  
Rhino is part of the broader set of options described in [[concepts/guarantor_services_overview]] for strengthening a rental application when credit is sub‑optimal. Using Rhino can complement other tactics such as offering a higher security deposit or prepaid rent, as discussed in [[concepts/rental_application_strengthening]].
