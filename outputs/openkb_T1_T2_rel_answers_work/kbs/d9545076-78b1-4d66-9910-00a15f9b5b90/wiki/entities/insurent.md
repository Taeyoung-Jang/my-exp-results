# Insurent (Organization)

Insurent provides a third‑party guarantor service that landlords often accept as an alternative to strong credit scores. In the context of [[summaries/session-31_2024-11-12]], Insurent is mentioned alongside other guarantor companies such as [[entities/the_guarantors]] and [[entities/rhino]].

## Key Facts from Session‑31
- **Service offering**: Acts as a guarantor for renters, guaranteeing lease payments if the tenant defaults.
- **Common usage**: Frequently cited by property managers when applicants have credit scores in the low 600s or other credit concerns.
- **Fee structure**: Typically charges a one‑time or annual fee that falls in the range of about half a month’s rent to one month’s rent, similar to other providers.
- **Acceptance**: Landlords may require an approval letter or proof of coverage; it’s advisable to ask the property manager which guarantor companies they accept.
- **Contract considerations**: Renters should check coverage duration, renewal terms, non‑refundable fees, and any conditions that could void the guarantee.
