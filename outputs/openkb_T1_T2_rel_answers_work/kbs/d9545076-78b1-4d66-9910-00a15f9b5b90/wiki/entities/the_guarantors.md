## The Guarantors

**Type:** Organization

**Overview**
The Guarantors is a national guarantor service that provides rent‑guarantee insurance for renters who need additional assurance for landlords, especially when their credit history is limited or contains blemishes.

**Key Services**
- Acts as a third‑party guarantor, covering rent payments if the tenant defaults.
- Typically charges a one‑time fee ranging from about half to one month’s rent, though exact pricing varies with credit and income factors.
- Coverage usually spans the lease term and may need renewal for lease extensions.

**Relevance to Rental Applications**
- Mentioned in [[summaries/session-31_2024-11-12]] as one of the common guarantor companies landlords may accept.
- Helps satisfy [[concepts/landlord_evaluation_criteria]] when a tenant’s credit score is low.
- Part of the broader set of options described in [[concepts/guarantor_services_overview]] and contributes to overall [[concepts/rental_application_strengthening]].

**What Tenants Should Verify**
1. **Acceptance:** Ask the property manager if they accept The Guarantors.
2. **Coverage Requirements:** Confirm what type of approval letter or coverage proof is needed.
3. **Timing:** Determine at which stage of the application the guarantor paperwork should be submitted.
4. **Submission Method:** Clarify whether documents are uploaded via a portal, emailed, or handled another way.
5. **Fee Payment:** Understand whether the guarantor fee is due before approval or after acceptance.

**Contract Terms to Review**
- Whether the fee is a one‑time charge or recurs annually.
- Length of coverage and renewal requirements for lease extensions.
- Conditions that could void the coverage (e.g., failure to update income).
- Refundability of the fee (generally non‑refundable).
- Obligations to notify the guarantor of employment or income changes during the lease.
