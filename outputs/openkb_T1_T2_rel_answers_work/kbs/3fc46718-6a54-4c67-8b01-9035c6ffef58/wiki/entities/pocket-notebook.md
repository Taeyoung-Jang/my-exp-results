# Pocket Notebook

**Type:** product

## Overview
A pocket notebook is a compact, paper‑based organizer designed to be carried at all times.  In the context of the *session‑40_2025‑07‑24* discussion, it serves as a low‑tech tool for capturing to‑dos, structuring spoken communication, and providing a tangible focal point for daily planning.

## Key Roles in the Document
- **Task Capture:** The notebook’s front side holds a simple "To‑dos" list, enabling quick jot‑downs of errands such as visiting the [[entities/post-office]] or picking up a prescription.
- **Speech Planning:** The back side is dedicated to a "What I want to say" section, helping the user, who is recovering from a [[entities/stroke]], to order thoughts before phone calls (e.g., a call to Dr. Rivera).
- **Focus & Progress Tracking:** A "Today’s focus" line with a checkbox provides a minimal progress indicator, linking to the concept of [[concepts/focus-and-progress-tracking]].
- **Simple Layout:** The recommended page layout follows the [[concepts/simple-notebook-layout]] template, with clear headings and spacious lines for handwriting.
- **Cognitive Benefits:** Using a pocket notebook leverages the [[concepts/cognitive-benefits-of-writing]] to aid memory and reduce anxiety.
