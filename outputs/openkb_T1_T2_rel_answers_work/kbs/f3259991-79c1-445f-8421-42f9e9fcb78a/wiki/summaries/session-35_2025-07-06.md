# Summary of Session 35 (2025-07-06)

**Main contribution**: A practical toolkit of short, timed anchor routines that help people maintain core habits—sleep wind‑down, minimal workout, and daily planning—while traveling or working irregular schedules. It also offers a concise rule of thumb for handling short‑term time‑zone jumps.

## Key Ideas
- **Anchor routines to invariant cues** rather than clock times, enabling habits to move with a shifting day. [[concepts/routine_anchoring]]
- Provide **exact, timed step‑by‑step scripts** (10 minute max) for each anchor, plus **2‑minute fallback versions** for low‑energy or transit days. [[concepts/fallback_routines]]
- Introduce a **time‑zone adaptation rule**: stay on home sleep schedule for trips < 3 nights; otherwise shift to local time, while keeping cue‑based wind‑down and light exposure consistent. [[concepts/time_zone_adaptation]]

## Anchor Checklists
### Sleep wind‑down script (10 min) and fallback (2 min)
- Silence devices, dim lights, wash face, change clothes, calm media, breathing.
- Fallback reduces to device silence, face wash, brief breathing.

### Minimal workout script (10 min) and fallback (2 min)
- Overhead stretch, squats, wall push‑ups, hip hinge, mobility rolls.
- Fallback limited to stretch, shoulder rolls, deep breaths.

### Daily planning script (10 min) and fallback (2 min)
- Open notes, write “one must‑do”, list key meetings/travel prep, choose a personal action, quick reality check.
- Fallback: write a single most‑important sentence.

## Time‑Zone Handling
- **Rule of thumb**: fewer than 3 nights → keep home sleep schedule; three nights or more → adopt local time.
- Consistently apply wind‑down and morning‑light cues regardless of clock.
