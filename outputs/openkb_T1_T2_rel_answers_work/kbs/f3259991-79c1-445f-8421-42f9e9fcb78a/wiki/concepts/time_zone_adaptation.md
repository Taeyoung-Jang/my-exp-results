# Time Zone Adaptation

**Definition**: A set of practical rules for managing sleep, routines, and cues when moving across time zones, especially on brief travel stays.

## Core Rule of Thumb
- **Stay on home sleep schedule** if the trip lasts **fewer than three nights**.
- **Shift to local time** for trips of **three nights or more**.
- Regardless of the rule, keep **anchor routines** (wind‑down, workout, planning) tied to invariant cues rather than clock time.

## How It Works
1. **Cue‑based wind‑down** – Perform the sleep wind‑down script as soon as you are alone in your room, independent of the local hour. This maintains a consistent pre‑sleep signal for your brain.
2. **Morning light exposure** – Get natural light as soon as you wake, which helps reset the circadian rhythm to the new time zone.
3. **Anchor routines** – Use the same short, timed scripts from [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-06]] for sleep, workout, and planning. The scripts are designed to be flexible and can be executed at any local time.
4. **Fallback versions** – On especially low‑energy days, the 2‑minute fallback routines (see [[concepts/fallback_routines]]) ensure the habit cue is still delivered without a large time commitment.

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- The approach relies on **[[concepts/routine_anchoring]]**: tying habits to stable triggers (e.g., “first sip of coffee”) rather than specific clock times.
- When the primary routine cannot be performed, the **[[concepts/fallback_routines]]** provide a minimal version that still reinforces the habit loop.

## Practical Example
- You arrive in a new city with a 2‑hour time difference and have only one night. Keep your usual bedtime cue (wind‑down script) and sleep at your home‑based time, using the fallback if you’re exhausted. The next morning, expose yourself to daylight, then gradually shift the next night’s wind‑down to the local evening if you’ll stay longer.

## Benefits
- Reduces the cognitive load of constantly recalculating schedules.
- Maintains habit continuity, supporting productivity and mental well‑being during travel.
- Minimizes jet‑lag impact on short trips.
