## Apps/Utilities (Top‑Level Folder)

**Entity:** `Apps/Utilities` – a top‑level folder used to store installers, scripts, and other technical odds‑and‑ends.

### Purpose and Scope
- Acts as a dedicated home for **software installers**, **executable scripts**, and **utility files** that are not part of active work or personal content.
- Keeps these items separate from core content buckets such as [[entities/work]], [[entities/personal]], [[entities/finances]], [[entities/media]], [[entities/reference]], and [[entities/archive]].

### Boundary Rules (what does *not* belong)
- Do **not** place regular work documents, personal files, financial records, media assets, or reference materials here. Those belong in their respective top‑level folders.
- Files that are part of an ongoing project should stay in the project’s folder under [[entities/work]] or the appropriate bucket until they are truly standalone utilities.

### Naming Convention
- Follow the general naming pattern recommended in [[concepts/naming_conventions]]: `ToolName_YYYY‑MM` for folders (e.g., `ffmpeg_2025‑06`).
- Individual files can use `ToolName_Description_YYYY‑MM‑DD.ext` for clarity.

### Relationship to Folder Hierarchy
- Part of the overall [[concepts/folder_hierarchy]] suggested in the “session‑10_2025‑06‑15” guidance. It provides a lightweight scaffold that helps avoid clutter in the other buckets.
