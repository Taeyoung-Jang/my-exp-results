# Work (Top‑Level Folder)

**Entity type:** other

The **Work** top‑level folder is the primary container for all job‑related documents, active professional projects, and administrative files. It is one of the recommended high‑level buckets in the folder hierarchy outlined in [[summaries/session-10_2025-06-15]].

## Core purpose
- Holds anything tied to your employment, client work, or any ongoing professional activities.
- Excludes purely personal items, financial records, media assets, reference material, archived content, and utility scripts.

## Boundary rules (what does *not* belong)
- **Personal** files belong in [[entities/personal]].
- Money‑related documents belong in [[entities/finances]].
- Photos, videos, and audio belong in [[entities/media]].
- Stable information for later use belongs in [[entities/reference]].
- Completed or inactive projects should be moved to [[entities/archive]].
- Installers, scripts, and technical odds‑and‑ends belong in [[entities/apps_utilities]].

## Organization within Work
- **Projects** – active project folders named using the convention `ProjectName_YYYY‑MM` (see [[concepts/naming_conventions]]).
- **Admin** – administrative paperwork such as contracts or schedules.
- **Archive** – a sub‑folder for projects that have been finished; moving a project here separates active from completed work without duplicating files (see [[concepts/folder_hierarchy]]).

## Interaction with other concepts
- The folder structure follows the broader [[concepts/folder_hierarchy]] guidance.
- Files that belong to multiple buckets can be cross‑referenced with tags, while the folder remains the primary home.
- An “Inbox” or “Downloads” area should be emptied regularly and sorted into Work or other top‑level folders as appropriate, following the [[concepts/inbox_management]] approach.
