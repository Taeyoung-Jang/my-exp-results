# Finances (Top‑Level Folder)

**Type:** other  
**Description:** Central repository for all money‑related documents, such as taxes, banking records, receipts, and budgeting files.

## Role in the file‑system
- Holds only financial information; excludes general paperwork or non‑monetary documents.
- Serves as one of the recommended 5‑8 top‑level buckets in the folder hierarchy [[concepts/folder_hierarchy]].

## Boundary rule
> *What doesn’t belong*: Items that are purely personal, work‑related, media, or reference material should be placed in their respective top‑level folders (e.g., [[entities/personal]], [[entities/work]], [[entities/media]], [[entities/reference]]).

## Example sub‑folders (from the session‑10 example)
```
Finances
 Taxes
 Banking
 Receipts_2026
```

## Interaction with other concepts
- Naming follows the project/file naming conventions described in [[concepts/naming_conventions]].
- Files arrive through the inbox/downloads flow and are promptly moved here, following the inbox management rules [[concepts/inbox_management]].
