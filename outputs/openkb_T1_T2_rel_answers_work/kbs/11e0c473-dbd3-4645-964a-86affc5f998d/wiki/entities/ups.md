## UPS (United Parcel Service)

**Type:** organization

**Description:** UPS provides global package delivery, freight, and logistics services. In the moving checklist context, it is highlighted as a carrier whose delivery‑management tools can help prevent mis‑delivered parcels during a residential move.

### Key Points from [[summaries/session-32_2025-02-15]]
- The checklist recommends using carrier‑specific reroute services such as **UPS MyChoice** to redirect shipments that might otherwise go to the old address.
- This tip is part of the broader **[[concepts/package-misdelivery-prevention]]** advice aimed at ensuring packages are sent to the correct new residence during the transition period.

### Related Entities and Concepts
- Similar carrier services are offered by [[entities/fedex]] and the national postal service [[entities/usps]].
- The recommendation aligns with the overall **[[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]]**, which schedules address‑related actions before, during, and after moving.

### Practical Use
When planning a move, owners should:
1. Sign up for UPS MyChoice ahead of the move.
2. Set delivery alerts for incoming packages.
3. Use the reroute feature to forward any shipments addressed to the old residence to the new address or a temporary hold location.

These steps help mitigate the risk of lost or delayed parcels while the address change processes are being completed.
