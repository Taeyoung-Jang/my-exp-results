# United States Postal Service (USPS)

The United States Postal Service (USPS) is the national postal operator of the United States, responsible for mail delivery, package services, and address change utilities.

## Role in Moving Checklist (session-32_2025-02-15)
- **Mail Forwarding**: The checklist advises setting up USPS mail forwarding **now** so it starts on or before moving day. This service acts as a safety net for any stray mail that still goes to the old address.
- **Timing**: It is listed under the *"Now"* category in the [[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]] and is the first step in the overall [[concepts/address-update-workflow]].
- **Package Safety**: While USPS forwarding helps catch missed items, the checklist also recommends additional [[concepts/package-misdelivery-prevention]] steps (e.g., using carrier‑specific reroute tools) to avoid mis‑delivered packages.
