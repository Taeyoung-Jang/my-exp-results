# Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)

The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is the state‑level government organization that issues driver’s licenses, registers vehicles, and maintains residential address records for motor‑vehicle owners.

## Key points from the moving checklist
- **Category:** Government update in the "address update workflow" for moving. [[concepts/address-update-workflow]]
- **Recommended timing:** *Before movers* (1–2 weeks prior to the move) to satisfy legal deadlines that typically range from 10–30 days. This timing is aligned with the overall moving checklist timeline. [[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]]
- **Role in the checklist:** Listed among essential items to update alongside USPS mail forwarding, voter registration, and tax agency addresses. See the source summary for the full context. [[summaries/session-32_2025-02-15]]
- **Interaction with other entities:** Works in concert with other address‑related organizations such as the United States Postal Service [[entities/usps]] and the Internal Revenue Service [[entities/irs]] during a relocation.

## Why it matters
Updating your address with the DMV ensures that:
1. Your driver’s license and vehicle registration reflect your new residence, keeping them legally valid.
2. Insurance companies receive the correct garaging address, which can affect rates and coverage.
3. Future correspondence (e.g., renewal notices, traffic citations) is sent to the correct location.
