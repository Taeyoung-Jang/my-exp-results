## PayPal

**Type:** organization

PayPal is a digital payments service that allows users to send and receive money online. In the context of a move, PayPal is one of the financial accounts that should have its address information updated to ensure that billing and verification processes continue to work smoothly.

### Key Points from [[summaries/session-32_2025-02-15]]
- **When to update:** Listed under the *Financial* category, the recommendation is to update PayPal “Now” – early in the moving process, alongside banks and credit cards.
- **What to update:** Both the residential (home) address and the billing address linked to any stored cards. This ensures that PayPal can correctly verify identity and process payments.
- **Why it matters:** An outdated address can cause payment failures, delays in two‑factor‑authentication code delivery, and issues with linking bank accounts.

### Related Concepts
- The need to change address information for PayPal ties into the broader **[[concepts/address-update-workflow]]** used for moving.
- The timing recommendation aligns with the **[[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]]** that categorizes tasks as "Now", "Before movers", etc.
- Maintaining a correct address helps avoid mis‑delivered communications, complementing **[[concepts/package-misdelivery-prevention]]** strategies.

### Related Entities
- Like other online retailers and payment services, PayPal should be considered alongside [[entities/amazon]] for coordinated address updates.
