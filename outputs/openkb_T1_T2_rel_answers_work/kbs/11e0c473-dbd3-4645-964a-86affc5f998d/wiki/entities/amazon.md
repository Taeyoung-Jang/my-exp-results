# Amazon (Organization)

Amazon is a multinational technology company best known for its e‑commerce platform, as well as services like Amazon Prime, AWS, and digital media.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-32_2025-02-15]]
- **Default shipping address:** The moving checklist advises updating the default shipping address in the Amazon account early ("Now" timing) to prevent packages being sent to the old residence.
- **Billing address:** Amazon also stores a billing address that must match the address on the user's credit cards; the checklist flags this as a critical step in the [[concepts/address-update-workflow]].
- **Timing recommendation:** Included in the "Now" section of the [[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]] alongside banks, credit cards, and other retailers.
- **Package mis‑delivery prevention:** The document suggests temporarily deleting the old address from Amazon and pausing auto‑shipments, which aligns with the [[concepts/package-misdelivery-prevention]] concept.
