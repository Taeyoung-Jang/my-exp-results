# FedEx

FedEx is a major international courier, freight, and logistics provider. In the moving checklist from [[summaries/session-32_2025-02-15]], FedEx is highlighted as one of the carrier services whose delivery‑management tools (e.g., **FedEx Delivery Manager**) can be used to reroute packages that might otherwise go to the old address during a move.

## Key Points from the Session‑32 Checklist
- **Package mis‑delivery prevention**: The checklist advises using carrier‑specific reroute services such as FedEx Delivery Manager, alongside UPS MyChoice and USPS Informed Delivery, to ensure time‑critical shipments arrive at the new residence.
- **Timing**: This recommendation falls under the "After you’re settled" and "Moving week" phases of the [[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]] to catch any stray parcels.
- **Relation to address‑update workflow**: Properly updating the shipping address with FedEx complements the broader [[concepts/address-update-workflow]] that distinguishes billing, shipping, and home addresses across services.

## Practical Tips Involving FedEx
1. **Enroll in FedEx Delivery Manager** before the move to gain control over incoming shipments.
2. **Set delivery instructions** for any expected parcels to redirect them to your new address or hold at a FedEx location.
3. **Monitor tracking** through the FedEx website or mobile app during the moving week.

These actions help ensure a smoother transition by preventing packages from being delivered to the former home.
