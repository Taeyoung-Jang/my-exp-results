## Moving Address Update Checklist – Summary

### Overview
Provides an exhaustive, category‑organized checklist for updating personal address information when moving, including a timeline (Now / Before movers / Moving week / After) and a “top 10 most commonly forgotten” list.

### Key Concepts
- **Address update workflow** – distinguishing home address, billing address, and shipping address across services. [[concepts/address-update-workflow]]
- **Timing categories** – “Now”, “Before movers”, “Moving week”, “After you’re settled”. [[concepts/moving-checklist-timeline]]
- **Government updates** – USPS forwarding, DMV, voter registration, IRS/state tax. [[entities/usps]], [[entities/dmv]], [[entities/irs]]
- **Financial updates** – banks, credit cards, employer, investment accounts, PayPal/Venmo. [[entities/paypal]]
- **Insurance updates** – car, renters/homeowners, health insurance. 
- **Healthcare updates** – doctors, dentists, pharmacy, mail‑order prescriptions. 
- **Utility updates** – electric, gas, water, trash, internet. 
- **Online account & shopping management** – default shipping vs billing addresses, 2FA mailing addresses. [[entities/amazon]]
- **Subscription management** – magazines, meal kits, pet supplies, streaming services. 
- **Commonly forgotten items** – Amazon default address, 2FA mailing, prescription‑by‑mail, pet microchip, tax agency address, etc. 
- **Package mis‑delivery prevention** – keep one active shipping address, pause auto‑shipments, use carrier reroute tools. [[concepts/package-misdelivery-prevention]]

### Timeline Breakdown
| Timing | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Now** | Set USPS forwarding, update banks, credit cards, employer, billing addresses, Amazon default address, 2FA mailing address, health/auto insurance. |
| **Before movers (1‑2 weeks)** | DMV, voter registration, utilities shut‑off/turn‑on, doctor/dentist offices, pharmacy, investment accounts, PayPal/Venmo, insurance if not done earlier. |
| **Moving week** | Double‑check all major billing/shipping addresses, confirm Amazon/retailers, ensure prescription refills are redirected, update cloud‑service home address. |
| **After settling** | Clean up straggling subscriptions, alumni/loyalty programs, pet microchip registry, check forwarded mail, finalize tax‑agency address if needed. |

### Top 10 Frequently Missed Updates
1. Default Amazon shipping & saved addresses
2. Billing addresses for credit cards used online
3. 2FA or backup‑code mailing addresses
4. Mail‑order prescription address
5. Voter registration
6. Pet microchip registry
7. PayPal/Venmo billing address
8. Streaming‑service billing info
9. IRS or state tax address change (Form 8822)
10. Alumni, loyalty, and other annual‑mail organisations

### Quick Tips for Package Safety
- Delete or deactivate old shipping addresses in major retailers for the first weeks.
- Pause automatic shipments until the new address is active.
- Use carrier‑specific reroute services (UPS MyChoice, USPS Informed Delivery, FedEx Delivery Manager). [[entities/ups]], [[entities/usps]], [[entities/fedex]]

The document also includes a printable, Google‑Doc‑style checklist with checkboxes, timing columns, and notes fields for each item.
