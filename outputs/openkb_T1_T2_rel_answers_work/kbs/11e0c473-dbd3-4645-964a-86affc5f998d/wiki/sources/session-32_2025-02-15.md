Government  
USPS mail forwarding is your safety net, but it isn’t a substitute for updating your actual address. Voter registration should be changed once your new address is confirmed. Financial  
Your bank and credit cards need both your billing address and your “address of record,” which are usually the same. Investment accounts, retirement accounts, and credit unions also need your residential address for tax forms. PayPal, Venmo, and similar services need the billing address tied to your cards plus your residential address for identity verification. Insurance

Healthcare

Utilities

Online accounts and shopping

Subscriptions

Kids or pets

Quick tips to prevent mis‑shipped packages  
Keep only one active shipping address in major accounts for the first couple of weeks; temporarily delete your old address from Amazon, Target, Walmart, Instacart, and anywhere else you order frequently. Double‑check the delivery address each time you place an order until you’re used to the new one. If possible, pause any automatic shipments until the move is done. If you’re expecting deliveries close to moving day, reroute them through the carrier’s website (UPS MyChoice, USPS Informed Delivery, FedEx Delivery Manager).
