# Address Update Workflow

**Definition**: A systematic process for updating a person's home, billing, and shipping addresses across government, financial, insurance, healthcare, utilities, online accounts, and subscriptions during a move.

## Core Elements
1. **Identify address types**
   - **Home address** – the primary residence used for legal and tax purposes.
   - **Billing address** – the address linked to payment instruments (credit cards, bank accounts) and invoicing.
   - **Shipping address** – where physical goods are delivered.
2. **Categorize contacts**
   - Government (USPS, DMV, voter registration, IRS)
   - Financial (banks, credit cards, employer, PayPal)
   - Insurance (car, renters/homeowners, health)
   - Healthcare (doctors, dentists, pharmacy, mail‑order prescriptions)
   - Utilities (electric, gas, water, trash, internet)
   - Online accounts & shopping (Amazon, other retailers, 2FA mailing addresses)
   - Subscriptions (magazines, meal kits, pet supplies, streaming services)
3. **Apply a timeline**
   - **Now** – immediate actions like USPS forwarding, banking, credit‑card billing updates, Amazon default address, 2FA mailing address.
   - **Before movers** – DMV, voter registration, utilities setup, doctor/clinic updates, PayPal, insurance.
   - **Moving week** – double‑check major billing/shipping addresses, confirm carrier settings, update cloud‑service home address.
   - **After settling** – clean up stragglers, alumni/loyalty programs, pet microchip registry, tax‑agency address if needed.
4. **Cross‑check address consistency**
   - Ensure billing addresses match the address on file for each payment method.
   - Verify shipping addresses point to the new residence for any pending deliveries.
   - Update autofill/address books on browsers and mobile devices.
