# Summary of session-13_2024-06-15

**Main topic:** How to manage and synchronize different location fields (mailing address, billing address, tax residence, current city) across services, and a practical checklist for updating them.

## Key Concepts
- **Address taxonomy** – distinguishes mailing address, billing address, tax residence, and current city as serving distinct audiences (mail carriers, payment processors, government agencies, social circles) [[concepts/address-taxonomy]].
- **Common pitfalls** – assuming all fields must match, forgetting critical updates, using temporary addresses for legal purposes [[concepts/address-consistency-pitfalls]].
- **Legal‑significant address** – full street address required for government records, banking, credit‑card billing addresses, and tax filings [[concepts/legal-significant-address]].
- **Lightweight address use** – city‑only fields are acceptable for social profiles, job platforms, and any service that does not verify identity.
- **PO Box / forwarding risks** – acceptable for pure mail delivery but problematic when used in place of a legal residence for banks, insurers, or government forms.

## Recommended Update Order (Flow)
1. **Government records** – DMV/driver’s license, voter registration, tax filings [[entities/dmv]].
2. **Financial institutions** – banks and credit‑card billing addresses.
3. **Employer / payroll** – tax forms and pay statements.
4. **Insurance providers** – policies that depend on residence.
5. **USPS** – change‑of‑address or forwarding services [[entities/usps]].
6. **Subscriptions & memberships** – magazines, retail accounts, delivery services.

This sequence moves from the most legally binding entities to convenience‑oriented services, reducing the chance of mismatches.

## Practical Checklist
- Verify the **legal residence** (full Chicago street address) for:
  - Driver’s license / state ID [[entities/chicago]]
  - Voter registration
  - Tax filings
  - Bank and credit‑card accounts
  - Insurance policies
- Update **billing address** to match the bank’s record (if already changed).
- Notify **employer/payroll** of the new residence for accurate tax withholding.
- Submit **USPS** change‑of‑address or forwarding instructions [[entities/usps]].
- Adjust **subscriptions** and **delivery services** to the new address or PO Box as appropriate.
- For profiles with only a “current city,” set it to *Chicago* unless the service explicitly ties it to verification.

## When Not to Use "Chicago" as Full Address
- Situations requiring a **different legal/tax address** (e.g., maintaining a secondary residence for tax purposes).
- Services that **reject PO Boxes** for verification (most banks, insurers, and government forms).

## Takeaway
Treat each address field according to its purpose, prioritize updates from legally binding entities down to convenience services, and keep city‑only fields aligned with actual living location while preserving full street details for legal‑significant records.
