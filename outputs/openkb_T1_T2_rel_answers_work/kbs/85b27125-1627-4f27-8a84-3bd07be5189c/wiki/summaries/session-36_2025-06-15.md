# Summary of session‑36 (2025‑06‑15)

## Overview
This conversation provides practical advice for quickly feeling at home in a new London neighbourhood, focusing on **Highbury Fields**. It culminates in a detailed, low‑pressure **14‑day plan** that blends walking loops, “regular” spots, non‑pub social activities, and essential first‑week administrative tasks.

## Key Concepts
- **Settling into a new home** – establishing routines, unpacking, creating a personal anchor space. [[concepts/settling-into-new-home]]
- **Neighbourhood familiarisation** – walking the area, identifying cafés, pubs, shops, and community hubs. [[concepts/neighborhood-familiarization]]
- **Non‑pub social activities** – yoga, running groups, volunteer mornings, community classes. [[concepts/non-pub-social-activities]]

## Tailored 14‑Day Plan (Highbury Fields)
| Day | Main Activities |
|-----|-----------------|
| **1** | Set up **Citymapper** and **TfL Go**; register on the council tax portal; short stroll through the centre of Highbury Fields. |
| **2** | Register with a local GP (e.g., Highbury Grove or Ritchie Street); walk the **Highbury Barn loop** (Fields → Da Mario → shops → back). |
| **3** | Choose first “regular” café (Fink’s or Little Highbury); linger for a coffee to start recognising staff. |
| **4** | Perform the **Canonbury loop** (north end of Fields → Alwyne Road residential streets → back). |
| **5** | Pick a second regular spot – a grocery (Waitrose or independent shop) or bakery in Highbury Barn. |
| **6** | Attend a drop‑in class at **Move Three Sixty** or a beginner yoga session near the Fields. |
| **7** | Casual wander down Upper Street to observe nearby amenities (no errands). |
| **8** | Re‑visit the chosen café or shop to reinforce familiarity. |
| **9** | Repeat the Highbury Barn loop with a slightly altered path; note shortcuts. |
| **10** | Join a low‑key running or walking group that meets in Highbury Fields. |
| **11** | Home‑settling evening – unpack remaining boxes, arrange a cosy corner, cook a simple meal. |
| **12** | Re‑run the Canonbury loop at a different time of day to sense rhythm changes. |
| **13** | Visit a third regular spot – weekend market, local deli, or another favourite shop. |
| **14** | Final larger loop (Fields → Highbury Barn → Canonbury → home); finish with tea/coffee at the regular café. |

## Administrative Tasks (First Week)
- **GP registration** – locate the nearest NHS surgery, bring proof of address.  
- **Council tax** – log onto the Islington Council website, submit tenancy details.  
- **Transport cards** – top‑up an Oyster card or set up contactless travel.  
- **Utility set‑up** – confirm gas, electricity, internet providers.  
- **Local safety** – sign up for neighbourhood watch or **Nextdoor** alerts.

## Suggested Apps & Resources
- **Citymapper** – real‑time navigation across London.  
- **TfL Go** – official transport updates.  
- **Nextdoor** – hyper‑local news and offers.  
- Local subreddit (e.g., r/Islington) – community chatter.  
- Instagram page “Islington Life” – visual guide to events and spots.  
- Meetup / Eventbrite – find low‑commitment activities near Highbury Fields.
