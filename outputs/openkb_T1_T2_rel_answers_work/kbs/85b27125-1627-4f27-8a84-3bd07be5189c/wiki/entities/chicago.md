# Chicago

Chicago is the third‑largest city in the United States, located in the state of Illinois.  In the context of **session‑13_2024‑06‑15**, Chicago serves as the user’s current legal residence and the target location for updating various address fields.

## Relevance to address management
- The user wants all **legal‑significant addresses** (driver’s license, voter registration, tax filings, banking, insurance) to reflect a full street address in Chicago.
- For **lightweight address fields** such as a profile’s “current city,” Chicago is the appropriate city‑only value.
- The document warns against using a PO box or alternate city for any service that requires a **legal‑significant address**.
