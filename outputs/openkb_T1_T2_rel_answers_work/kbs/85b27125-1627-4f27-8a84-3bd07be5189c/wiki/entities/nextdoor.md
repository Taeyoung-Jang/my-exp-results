# Nextdoor (product)

Nextdoor is a hyperlocal social networking **product** that connects residents within a specific postcode or neighbourhood, enabling them to share updates, ask for recommendations, and stay informed about local events.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-15]]
- Mentioned as a useful tool for **neighbourhood familiarisation** when moving to a new area, especially in London’s Highbury Fields district.
- Suggested alongside other local apps such as [[entities/citymapper]] and [[entities/tfl-go]] for getting oriented in a new city.
- Helps users find community groups, volunteer opportunities, and low‑pressure ways to meet people, complementing [[concepts/non-pub-social-activities]].
- Recommended as part of a broader strategy for **settling into a new home** by staying connected with nearby residents.

## Related concepts
