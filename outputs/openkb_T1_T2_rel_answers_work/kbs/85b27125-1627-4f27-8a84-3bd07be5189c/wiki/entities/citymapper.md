# Citymapper (product)

Citymapper is a mobile application that provides real‑time, multimodal route planning for public transport, walking, cycling, and ride‑hailing services. It is widely used in London to help newcomers and residents navigate the city efficiently.

## Role in [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-15]]
In the *session‑36* conversation, Citymapper was recommended as one of the essential tools for a smooth settlement in a new London neighbourhood, specifically for:
- Quickly finding routes to and from the Highbury & Islington station.
- Exploring the Highbury Fields area on foot and by public transport.
- Supporting the broader [[concepts/settling-into-new-home]] process by reducing travel friction.
