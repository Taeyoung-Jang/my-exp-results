## TfL Go

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** Transport for London’s official app for live travel information and journey planning.

### What it is
TfL Go provides real‑time tube, bus, Overground, and rail service updates, journey planning, and service disruption alerts across London.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-15]]
- Recommended in the first‑day checklist for newcomers to set up alongside Citymapper.
- Helps users navigate the Highbury Fields area and plan walks around the neighbourhood.
- Mentioned as part of essential London‑specific admin and mobility tools that support [[concepts/settling-into-new-home]] and [[concepts/neighborhood-familiarization]].
- Serves as a low‑pressure alternative to pub‑centric socialising by enabling users to join walking or running groups in the park.

### Related apps & resources

### Practical tips
1. Download from the App Store or Google Play during your first week.  
2. Enable push notifications for service alerts.  
3. Use the “journey planner” feature to map the two suggested walking loops around Highbury Fields.  

These steps help new residents quickly become confident moving around London and support a smooth transition into the area.
