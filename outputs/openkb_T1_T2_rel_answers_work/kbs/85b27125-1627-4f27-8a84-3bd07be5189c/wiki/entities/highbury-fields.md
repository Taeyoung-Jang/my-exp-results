## Highbury Fields

Highbury Fields is a large public park in the London Borough of Islington, situated near the Highbury & Islington tube and Overground station.  It serves as a central green space for local residents, offering walking routes, casual sports areas, and venues for community events.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-15]]
- **Location:** Adjacent to Highbury & Islington station, bordered by Upper Street, Canonbury Road and the residential streets of Alwyne Road.
- **Walking loops:** Two recommended loops – the **Highbury Barn loop** (through the park toward the Highbury Barn shops) and the **Canonbury loop** (north side of the park through quiet residential streets) – are used to help newcomers become familiar with the neighbourhood [[concepts/neighborhood-familiarization]].
- **Regular spots:** Nearby cafés such as **Fink’s** and **Little Highbury Café**, and the **Highbury Barn** market area, are suggested as "regular" places to visit and become a familiar face.
- **Non‑pub social options:** The park hosts running groups, drop‑in yoga sessions, and volunteer mornings, providing low‑pressure ways to meet people [[concepts/non-pub-social-activities]].
- **Local resources:** The area is well‑served by transport apps like [[entities/citymapper]] and [[entities/tfl-go]], and community platforms such as [[entities/nextdoor]].
- **Administrative context:** New residents are advised to handle London‑specific admin tasks (GP registration, council tax, etc.) while using the park as a personal anchor for settling in [[concepts/settling-into-new-home]].
