# United States Postal Service (USPS)

The United States Postal Service (USPS) is the federal agency responsible for mail delivery, postal services, and change‑of‑address processing across the United States.

## Role in address management
- Provides the official **change‑of‑address** service that redirects mail to a new residence, which is crucial when updating a **legal‑significant address**.
- Accepts both street addresses and PO boxes for mail receipt, but many **legally significant** processes (e.g., banking, insurance) may reject PO boxes in favor of a full street address.
- Mentioned in the session‑13 discussion as the final step after updating government and financial records.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]
- Recommended updating USPS after government (DMV, voter registration) and financial institutions.
- Highlighted that using a PO box with USPS is fine for simple mail forwarding but can cause issues for services that require a full street address, as described in [[concepts/address-consistency-pitfalls]].
- Emphasized the need to keep the USPS address consistent with the **legal residence** in [[concepts/legal-significant-address]] when applicable.
