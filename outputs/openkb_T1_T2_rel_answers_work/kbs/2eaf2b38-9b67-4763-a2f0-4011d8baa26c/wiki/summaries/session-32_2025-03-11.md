# Summary of Session 32 (2025-03-11)

**Main focus**: Practical ways to keep a home comfortable as the weather shifts, with an emphasis on indoor humidity, humidifier setup, and protecting wood furniture.

## Seasonal comfort adjustments
- Use fans and early‑morning/evening breezes in warm months; lightweight fabrics (cotton, linen) and lighter throws.  
- In cooler, drier periods, introduce thicker curtains, rugs, textured fabrics, and a small **[[entities/humidifier]]** to add moisture.  
- Keep furniture a short distance from vents or radiators for steady airflow.  
- Manage sunlight: let it in for warmth or filter it for cooling.  
- See [[concepts/seasonal_comfort]] for broader guidance.

## Recommended indoor humidity
- Target **35 %–45 %** relative humidity during cold/dry seasons to reduce static and avoid condensation or mold.  
- Monitor with a basic **[[entities/hygrometer]]**.  
- See [[concepts/indoor_humidity]] for details.

## Simple humidity‑raising methods
- Operate a small **[[entities/humidifier]]** on a moderate, steady setting.  
- Passive options: air‑dry a small laundry load indoors, leave bathroom door open after a warm shower.

## Humidifier placement & wood safety
- Position the unit **a few feet (≈1‑2 m)** away from wood surfaces such as tables.  
- Elevate the humidifier on a nightstand, shelf, or other stable surface to encourage upward mist dispersion.  
- Aim mist toward the center of the room, not directly at furniture.  
- Verify correct placement by observing a steady humidity rise **without** damp patches on wood.  
- Adjust distance or mist angle if moisture settles on surfaces.  
- See [[concepts/humidifier_placement]] for best practices.

## Preventing condensation & mold
- Keep humidity within the 35‑45 % window; higher levels risk window fogging and mold growth.  
- Ensure good air circulation; avoid placing humidifiers in corners where mist can pool.
