# Summary of Session 15 (2024‑07‑12)

**Topic:** Practical, low‑tech methods to detect and address abnormal indoor humidity.

## Key Takeaways
- **Symptoms of humidity imbalance** – static shocks, dry skin, cracked wood for low humidity; muggy feel, window condensation, musty odors, damp fabrics for high humidity. indoor humidity signs
- **First verification step** – place a cheap hygrometer in the affected room, record baseline humidity and temperature. hygrometer usage
- **Prioritizing potential sources** – hidden water leak > moisture ingress through walls/foundation > insufficient ventilation/dehumidification.

## 24‑Hour No‑Tool Diagnostic Plan
1. **Baseline measurement** – note hygrometer reading.
2. **Visual leak scan** – inspect under sinks, around toilets, washer, water heater, exposed pipes; feel for wet spots.
3. **Ventilation test** – run bathroom/kitchen fans; hold a paper/tissue near the grille; it should hold if airflow is adequate. Record humidity change over two hours.
4. **Basement/Crawlspace check** – briefly enter; compare hygrometer reading to upstairs. Damp air or wet walls suggests structural moisture.
5. **Condensation vs. intrusion test** – after drying surfaces, observe where moisture reappears (cold exterior walls = condensation; patchy, cool spots = intrusion). condensation vs intrusion
6. **HVAC/dehumidifier verification** – confirm condensate line drips during cooling; run a standalone dehumidifier in a closed room and watch humidity shift.

### Interpreting Outcomes
- **Rapid humidity drop with fans** → ventilation issue.
- **Localized wet spots on plumbing** → active leak.
- **Higher humidity in basement/crawlspace** or damp foundation walls → moisture ingress.
- **Moisture returning on cold surfaces** → condensation.

## Quick “Condensation or Intrusion?” Plastic‑Wrap Test
1. Tape a piece of clear plastic tightly to the damp exterior wall section.
2. Leave for a few hours.
3. **Moisture on room‑side of plastic** → condensation from indoor air.
4. **Moisture on wall‑side or dry plastic with still‑damp wall** → water intrusion through wall. plastic wrap test

## Action Recommendations
- Fix any identified leaks immediately.
- Improve air exchange: run fans longer, crack windows, consider an exhaust vent.
- If structural moisture is suspected, address foundation drainage or waterproofing.
- Use a dehumidifier sized for the space if ventilation alone is insufficient.
