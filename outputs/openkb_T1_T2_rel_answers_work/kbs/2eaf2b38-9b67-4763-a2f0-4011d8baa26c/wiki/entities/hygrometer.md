# Hygrometer (product)

A hygrometer is a measuring instrument that displays the **relative humidity** of indoor air. It is commonly used to ensure that humidity stays within a comfortable and safe range, especially when adjusting climate controls such as humidifiers.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-32_2025-03-11]]
- Recommended to pair with a humidifier to keep indoor humidity between **35 %–45 %** during cold, dry months.
- Users should monitor the reading on a basic hygrometer to avoid excess moisture that could cause condensation or mold.
- Helps identify when static electricity issues arise, like the shocks experienced on wooden furniture.

## Practical tips
1. Place the hygrometer at breathing height, away from direct drafts or heat sources, for an accurate reading.
2. Check the display regularly, especially after adjusting the humidifier settings.
3. If the hygrometer indicates humidity above 45 %, reduce humidifier output or improve ventilation to prevent mold.
4. In very dry conditions (below 35 %), consider increasing humidifier output or adding passive moisture sources (e.g., indoor drying of laundry).
