# Seasonal Comfort

**Definition**: Seasonal Comfort refers to the set of practical adjustments made within a home to maintain a balanced, pleasant indoor environment as outdoor weather shifts throughout the year.

## Core Elements
- **Airflow**: In warm months, use fans and open windows during early‑morning or evening breezes to keep the space fresh without creating drafts. In cooler periods, keep furniture a short distance from vents or radiators to ensure steady airflow.
- **Textiles**: Swap lightweight fabrics (cotton, linen) and lighter throws for summer; introduce thicker curtains, rugs, and textured fabrics for winter to retain heat.
- **Sunlight Management**: Allow sunlight in to provide natural warmth when needed, or filter it with curtains/blinds to prevent overheating.
- **Humidity Control**: Aim for a relative humidity of **35 %–45 %** in cold, dry seasons to reduce static electricity and avoid condensation. See [[concepts/indoor_humidity]] for details on optimal ranges and monitoring.
- **Humidifier Placement**: When using a humidifier, position it a few feet away from wood furniture and elevate it to promote upward mist dispersion, preventing moisture from settling on surfaces. Refer to [[concepts/humidifier_placement]] for best practices.

## Practical Tips from Session 32
- Use a small humidifier on a moderate setting and track humidity with a hygrometer.
- Passively raise humidity by air‑drying a small indoor laundry load or leaving the bathroom door open after a warm shower.
- Keep wood tables and other furnishings at least a few feet from the humidifier and, if possible, on a raised surface to avoid warping.
- Adjust curtains and throws seasonally to modulate perceived temperature.
- Regularly check for condensation on windows to stay within the safe humidity range.
