# Humidifier Placement and Wood Safety

**Type:** Concept

## Definition
Guidelines for positioning a humidifier in a living space to achieve a target indoor relative humidity (typically 35‑45 %) while preventing moisture damage—such as warping, surface dampness, or mold—on wood furniture and finishes.

## Key Recommendations from [[summaries/session-32_2025-03-11]]
- **Distance:** Place the humidifier a few feet (about 1–2 m) away from wooden tables, cabinets, or other wood pieces.
- **Elevation:** Position the unit on a stable, elevated surface (nightstand, shelf, or tabletop) so the mist rises and disperses before reaching lower surfaces.
- **Mist Direction:** Aim the mist toward the center of the room, not directly at wood surfaces. If the humidifier has a directional nozzle, angle it upward.
- **Monitoring:** Use a hygrometer to track humidity levels; keep within the 35‑45 % range to avoid condensation on windows and mold growth.
- **Adjustment:** If damp patches appear on wood, increase the distance, raise the unit higher, or further angle the mist upward.

## Why Placement Matters
- **Wood Sensitivity:** Wood can absorb moisture quickly, leading to swelling, warping, or finish damage when exposed to direct mist.
- **Even Humidity Distribution:** Proper spacing and elevation allow the mist to blend with room air, providing uniform humidity without localized wet spots.
- **Preventing Condensation:** Maintaining the recommended humidity range reduces the risk of window fogging or surface condensation that can affect wood.

## Practical Tips
1. **Start Small:** Run the humidifier on a moderate setting for a few hours and check the hygrometer.
2. **Observe Surfaces:** Look for any moisture rings or dampness on nearby wood; adjust placement accordingly.
3. **Routine Checks:** Re‑measure humidity each morning; seasonal temperature shifts may require repositioning.
4. **Avoid Direct Sprays:** If the humidifier creates a visible mist stream, use a diffuser or set it to ultrasonic mode for finer output.

By following these placement rules, homeowners can enjoy the comfort benefits of added moisture while safeguarding their wooden furniture.
