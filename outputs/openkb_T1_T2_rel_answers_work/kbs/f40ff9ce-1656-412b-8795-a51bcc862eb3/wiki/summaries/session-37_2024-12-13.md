# Summary of Session 37 – Late‑Shift Weekly Routine (2024‑12‑13)

## Overview
The document provides a practical, consistent weekly schedule for a 12 pm–8 pm work shift, including sleep timing, meal windows, workout blocks, weekend adjustments, a simple grocery list, meal‑prep ideas, and snack combos. It emphasizes front‑loading nutrition, morning workouts, and light evening snacks to avoid late‑night grazing.

## Key Concepts
- **Shift‑aligned sleep**: Wake at ~7:45 am, bedtime by 11:15 pm to keep circadian rhythm steady. [[concepts/shift-aligned-sleep]]
- **Shift‑meal timing**: Breakfast (8:15 am), early lunch (11:15 am), main shift meal (3:30–4:00 pm), optional work snack (6:00 pm), light evening snack (9:00 pm). [[concepts/shift-meal-timing]]
- **Morning workout**: 20‑30 min session after breakfast; easier than post‑work exercise.
- **Weekend consistency**: Same wake/bed times, adjusted activity blocks (light cardio/stretch on Saturday, rest or gentle movement on Sunday). This uses a form of [[concepts/time-blocking]].

## Strength Workouts (Home)
All exercises use [[entities/dumbbells]] or [[entities/resistance-bands]].

- **Monday (Workout A)**: Goblet Squats, Bent‑Over Rows, Glute Bridge, Overhead Press, Dead Bug/Plank.  
- **Wednesday (Workout B)**: Reverse Lunges, Floor Chest Press, Romanian Deadlift, Band Pull‑Apart, Side Plank.  
- **Friday (Workout C)**: Step‑Ups, Single‑Arm Row, Single‑Leg Bridge, Arnold/Lateral Raise, Core Circuit.  

Each session: 3 sets of 8‑15 reps, ~20‑30 min total.

## Meal Planning
- **Breakfast rotations**: eggs & toast, oatmeal + fruit, protein smoothie (using [[entities/protein-powder]]).
- **Mid‑day bowl**: rice/quinoa, protein such as [[entities/chicken-breast]] or tofu, mixed veggies.
- **Shift meal**: leftovers or hearty sandwich (hummus, turkey, beans).
- **Evening snack**: [[entities/greek-yogurt]], fruit, small wrap, or protein shake.

### Grocery List (core items)
[[entities/chicken-breast]], ground turkey or tofu, eggs, [[entities/greek-yogurt]], berries, bananas, mixed greens, spinach, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, frozen veggies, rice or quinoa, oats, whole‑grain bread or wraps, hummus, black beans, shredded cheese, olive oil, nuts, basic seasonings, salsa or vinaigrette.

### Meal‑Prep Ideas
1. **Batch‑meal‑prep bowls**: cook six servings of rice/quinoa, roast a tray of vegetables, and prep a protein like chicken or tofu. Portion into containers for the 3:30–4:00 pm shift meal or easy lunch. [[concepts/batch-meal-prep]]
2. **Breakfast jars**: overnight oats or yogurt parfaits prepared 3‑4 at a time.
3. **Wrap packs**: whole‑grain wrap, protein, veggies, and hummus, made two at a time and stored in the fridge.

## Snack Combos
- **6:00 pm work snack** (≈1 serving):  
  - 1 banana + 1 handful nuts  
  - 1 single‑serve Greek yogurt + a few berries  
  - 1 cheese stick + 1 handful grapes  
  - 2 Tbsp hummus + baby carrots
- **9:00 pm evening snack** (≈½–1 cup):  
  - ½–1 cup Greek yogurt + ¼ cup berries  
  - 1 scoop protein powder + water/almond milk (protein shake)  
  - Small whole‑grain wrap with 1 slice turkey + greens

## Rule of Thumb for Skipping the 9 pm Snack
- Skip if you felt satiated after the 3:30–4:00 pm meal **and** the 6:00 pm snack.  
- Skip when the urge is habit or boredom rather than true stomach‑level hunger.  
- Aim to keep the evening snack under 150 kcal to avoid sleep‑disrupting energy spikes.
