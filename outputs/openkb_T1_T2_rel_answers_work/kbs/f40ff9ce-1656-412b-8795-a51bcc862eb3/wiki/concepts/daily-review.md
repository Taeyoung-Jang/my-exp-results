# Daily Review

**Definition**: A short, structured reflection performed at the end of each day to assess what worked, what felt rushed, and what can be improved for tomorrow.

## Purpose
- Reinforces effective habits by highlighting successes.
- Identifies obstacles or bottlenecks in the daily schedule.
- Provides data for iterative tweaks to the routine, ensuring it remains realistic and aligned with energy levels.

## Key Elements (as applied in [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]])
1. **What worked** – Note tasks completed in focused work blocks and any buffer periods that helped smooth transitions.
2. **What felt rushed** – Flag moments where activities ran into the next block without sufficient buffer.
3. **Adjustments** – Decide on concrete changes, such as extending a buffer or shifting a task to a different time slot.

## Process (quick checklist)
- **5‑minute review** after the wind‑down routine.
- Record insights in a notebook or digital note.
- Update the next day's schedule with any buffer adjustments or task re‑prioritizations.

## Benefits
- Encourages continuous improvement of the routine.
- Reduces the feeling of constantly reacting to unforeseen events.
- Strengthens self‑awareness of personal energy peaks and troughs.

- [[concepts/time-blocking]] – the review informs how to refine time‑blocks for better alignment with energy levels.
