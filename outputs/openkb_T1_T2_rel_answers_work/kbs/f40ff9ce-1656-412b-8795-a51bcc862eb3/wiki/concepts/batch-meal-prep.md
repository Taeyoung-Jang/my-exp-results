# Batch Meal Prep

**Definition**: Batch Meal Prep is the practice of cooking large quantities of food in a single session and portioning them for future meals, enabling consistent nutrition while minimizing daily cooking time.

## Why It Matters for a Late‑Shift Schedule
- Aligns with [[concepts/shift-meal-timing]] by ensuring that breakfast, early‑lunch, and the main shift meal are ready without last‑minute scrambling.
- Supports [[concepts/time-blocking]] by reserving a dedicated prep window (e.g., Sunday afternoon) that frees up weekdays for work and recovery.
- Provides a **buffer** against unpredictable days; having pre‑made containers reduces the need for impulse snacks.
- Facilitates a quick [[concepts/daily-review]]—you can glance at your ready‑made meals and confirm they meet your nutritional goals.

## Core Steps (as used in [[summaries/session-37_2024-12-13]])
1. **Choose staple proteins** – e.g., grilled [[entities/chicken-breast]], baked tofu, or ground turkey.
2. **Cook a bulk grain** – rice, quinoa, or oats for breakfasts.
3. **Roast or steam a variety of vegetables** – frozen veggies, spinach, carrots, etc.
4. **Portion into containers** matching the daily schedule:
   - *Morning*: oatmeal or a protein‑rich breakfast jar.
   - *Pre‑shift lunch*: a grain‑protein bowl.
   - *Shift meal*: leftovers or a hearty sandwich.
5. **Label with date and meal slot** to keep the routine transparent.

## Example Meal‑Prep Set
- **Grain‑Protein Bowls**: 6 servings of quinoa, roasted mixed veggies, and [[entities/chicken-breast]] strips.
- **Breakfast Jars**: Overnight oats with berries and a scoop of [[entities/protein-powder]].
- **Wrap Packs**: Whole‑grain wraps, hummus, turkey slices, and sliced cucumber.

## Benefits
- **Time efficiency**: One cooking block replaces daily meal prep.
- **Nutritional consistency**: Same macro balance each day supports energy stability during the 12 pm‑8 pm shift.
- **Reduced snacking**: With ready meals, the temptation for late‑night grazing diminishes.
- **Cost savings**: Bulk buying of staples like [[entities/chicken-breast]] and grains lowers per‑meal expense.
