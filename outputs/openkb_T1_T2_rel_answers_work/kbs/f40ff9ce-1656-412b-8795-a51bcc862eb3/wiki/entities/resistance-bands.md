# Resistance Bands

Resistance bands are lightweight, elastic fitness tools used for strength training, mobility work, and rehabilitation. In the **session‑37_2024‑12‑13** routine they serve as an alternative to dumbbells for the home strength workouts on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

## Key facts from the schedule
- **Home workouts**: All listed exercises (e.g., Bent‑Over Rows, Band Pull‑Apart, Lat Pulldown) can be performed with resistance bands, making them ideal for a compact home gym.
- **Versatility**: Bands provide variable resistance, useful for both lower‑body (squats, lunges) and upper‑body (presses, rows) movements.
- **Portability**: Easy to transport for travel or to use at work during a break.
