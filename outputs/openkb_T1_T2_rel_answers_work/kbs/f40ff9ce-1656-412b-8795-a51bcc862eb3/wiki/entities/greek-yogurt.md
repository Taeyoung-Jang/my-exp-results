# Greek Yogurt

Greek yogurt is a thick, high‑protein dairy product that appears throughout the **session‑37** weekly routine. It is highlighted as a convenient, nutritionally dense option for both **breakfast** and **evening snacks**.

## Key Uses in the Schedule
- **Breakfast**: One of the rotation options (e.g., yogurt with berries or mixed into a smoothie) served around 8:15 am.
- **6:00 pm work snack**: Paired with a few berries or a small handful of nuts for a quick, non‑messy bite.
- **9:00 pm light snack**: Recommended portion is **½–1 cup** of Greek yogurt with a handful of berries or mixed with a scoop of **[[entities/protein-powder]]** to add extra protein without disrupting sleep.

## Portion Guidance
- Typical serving size: **1 cup** (approximately 240 ml) for a satisfying snack.
- For the evening snack, keep the portion to **½–1 cup** to stay under ~150 kcal, supporting the goal of a calm bedtime.

## Connection to Concepts
- The timing of Greek yogurt aligns with **[[concepts/shift-meal-timing]]**, ensuring meals are spaced to prevent late‑night cravings.
- Consistent snack timing aids **[[concepts/shift-aligned-sleep]]** by avoiding heavy intake close to bedtime.
- Using yogurt in pre‑made **[[concepts/batch-meal-prep]]** breakfast jars supports efficient **[[concepts/time-blocking]]** of morning routines.
