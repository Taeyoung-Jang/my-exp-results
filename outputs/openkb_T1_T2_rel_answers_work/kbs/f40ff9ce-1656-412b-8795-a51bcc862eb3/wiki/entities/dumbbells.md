## Dumbbells

Dumbbells are a set of hand‑held weights commonly used for resistance training. In the **session‑37** routine they serve as the primary equipment for the three weekly strength‑training sessions (Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Typical exercises listed include:

- Goblet Squats
- Bent‑Over Rows
- Overhead Press
- Romanian Deadlift
- Single‑Arm Row
- Arnold Press

These movements are performed in 20‑30‑minute **time‑blocking** windows after breakfast, fitting the overall schedule outlined in [[summaries/session-37_2024-12-13]]. The plan emphasizes a simple home gym setup, pairing dumbbells with **resistance‑bands** (see [[entities/resistance-bands]]) for added variation.

### Role in the Weekly Routine
- **Morning workouts**: Dumbbells enable concise, effective strength sessions before the shift, supporting the **shift‑aligned‑sleep** pattern by keeping evenings free for wind‑down.
- **Flexibility**: Adjustable weight ranges allow customization to individual strength levels while keeping the equipment footprint minimal.
- **Integration with other concepts**: The use of dumbbells aligns with the broader **time‑blocking** strategy for structuring the day and complements the **shift‑meal‑timing** plan by providing a consistent, quick workout slot.

### Practical Tips
1. Choose a pair that covers a range (e.g., 5‑25 lb) to progress over weeks.
2. Store them within easy reach of the breakfast area to streamline the post‑breakfast workout.
3. Combine with bands for hybrid movements when seeking extra resistance.
