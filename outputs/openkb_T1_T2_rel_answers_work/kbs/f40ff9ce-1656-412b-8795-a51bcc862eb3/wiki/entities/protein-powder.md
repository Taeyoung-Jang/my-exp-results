## Protein Powder
**Type:** Product  

Protein powder is a powdered dietary supplement that can be mixed with water, milk, or a plant‑based beverage to create a quick protein shake. In the weekly routine from [[summaries/session-37_2024-12-13]] it is recommended as a 9:00 pm light snack, providing about 20 g of protein while staying low in calories and not disrupting sleep.

### Uses in the Schedule
- **Evening snack (9 pm):** One scoop of protein powder blended with water or almond milk forms a simple, satiating shake. This aligns with the [[concepts/shift-meal-timing]] principle of a small, protein‑rich snack to curb hunger before bed.
- **Post‑workout recovery:** Though not explicitly mentioned in the document, many users take a protein shake after the morning strength session to support muscle repair, fitting naturally into the [[concepts/time-blocking]] of the workout block.

### Related Items
- Alternative snack options include [[entities/greek-yogurt]].
- Often stocked alongside other workout gear such as [[entities/dumbbells]] and [[entities/resistance-bands]].

### Practical Tips
- Use a single‑scoop (≈30 g) to keep the snack around 150 kcal.
- Mix with cold water or unsweetened almond milk for a light texture that won’t interfere with sleep.
