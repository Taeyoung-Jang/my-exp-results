# Summary of session-31_2025-03-14

## Core hardware priorities
- **GPU** is the top priority for Resolve, Blender Cycles, and After Effects. Emphasize **VRAM capacity** (≥ 16 GB safe minimum, 24 GB preferred) over raw gaming FPS. [[concepts/gpu-vram]]
- **CPU** comes second: prefer high **single‑core boost clocks** (8‑12 cores) for day‑to‑day responsiveness; very high core counts (16‑24) only help long CPU‑only renders or heavy multitasking.
- **RAM**: 64 GB is the floor for comfortable 8K editing; 128 GB eliminates most paging concerns.
- **Storage layout**: Fast NVMe drives are essential. A two‑drive setup (OS/apps + media/cache) works, but a third dedicated cache/scratch NVMe noticeably reduces bandwidth contention in heavy workflows. [[concepts/nvme-storage]]
- **Common overlooked bottlenecks**: storage contention, thermal throttling on laptops, insufficient VRAM, and RAM‑to‑disk spilling.

## Budget split for a desktop build
| Component | Recommended allocation |
|-----------|------------------------|
| GPU (with VRAM) | Largest share – aim for ≥ 16 GB VRAM |
| CPU (high‑clk)   | Secondary – 8‑12 cores with strong boost |
| RAM (capacity)   | Next – 64‑128 GB |
| NVMe storage      | 2‑3 drives; third drive for cache is a performance gain |

## Workstation extras
- **ECC RAM** – stability luxury, not essential for creative work.
- **Thunderbolt** – useful for fast external drives or docks, optional.
- **10 GbE networking** – valuable when moving large media to NAS or between workstations.
- **RAID** – primarily for redundancy or capacity; does not usually outperform a single fast NVMe for speed.

## Post‑upgrade workflow optimization
1. **Drive separation**:  
   - Drive 1: OS and applications.  
   - Drive 2: Project files and raw footage.  
   - Drive 3: Resolve cache, AE disk cache, optimized media.  
2. **Cache settings**:  
   - Resolve: use efficient proxy/cache formats (ProRes or DNxHR). [[entities/prores]] [[entities/dnxhr]]  
   - After Effects: increase disk‑cache size and place it on the dedicated fast drive. [[entities/after-effects]]
3. **Thermal management**: set a slightly higher base fan speed to keep GPU/CPU temperatures low, preventing throttling on sustained loads.
4. **Backup routine**: automate daily sync of project files and active media to an external drive or NAS; simple and reliable beats manual copying.
