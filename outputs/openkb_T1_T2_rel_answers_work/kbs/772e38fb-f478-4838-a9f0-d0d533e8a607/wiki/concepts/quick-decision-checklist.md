# Quick Decision Checklist

A concise, five‑question heuristic designed to help you evaluate whether a restaurant, cafe, or activity is worth trying in under a minute. It balances mood, social proof, cost, curiosity, and a low‑stakes “okay” tolerance.

---

## The Checklist (yes/no answers)
1. **Mood match** – Is this place aligned with the mood I’m in?
2. **Social proof** – Do the latest photos or comments look generally acceptable?
3. **Cost/effort** – Is the price or effort level right for me right now?
4. **Curiosity spark** – Do I feel at least a little curious or excited about trying it?
5. **Acceptable okay‑ness** – If it’s just “okay,” am I still willing to give it a short try?

---

## How to Use It
- **Speed:** Run through the questions mentally while scanning a listing or map; each answer takes a second.
- **Decision rule:** If the majority of answers are “yes,” go ahead. If not, consider an alternative or apply a low‑risk trial (see [[concepts/risk-reduction-for-new-places]]).
- **Contextual cue:** Pair the checklist with a brief “five‑minute rule” – give yourself a short window to test the spot before committing fully.

---

## Benefits
- Reduces analysis paralysis by providing a fixed, repeatable process.
- Aligns choices with current emotional state and practical constraints.
- Encourages curiosity while keeping expectations realistic.
