# Summary of session-03_2024-07-17

## Main request
User wants help drafting a text to decline a large music festival, staying friendly but firm, and suggest low‑key alternatives.

## Provided solutions
- Initial template.  
- Three tone variations: super casual, more direct, friendly low‑interaction.  
- List of low‑key summer alternatives: backyard hang, beach evening, park picnic, small local show, lake day.  
- Tailored messages for group chat and 1:1, with a concrete alternative (beach evening).  
- Final homebody version for the group chat, adding a line to shut down “one‑day” pressure and swapping the beach for a chill evening at home or a quiet park hang.

## Takeaways
- Use warm, concise language; state personal preference; avoid opening negotiation.  
- Offer a single, concrete alternative to keep the conversation simple.  
- Include a gentle boundary statement to pre‑empt pressure.
