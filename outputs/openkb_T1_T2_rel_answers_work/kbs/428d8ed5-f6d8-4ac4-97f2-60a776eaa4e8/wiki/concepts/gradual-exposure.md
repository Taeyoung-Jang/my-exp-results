# Gradual Exposure

**Definition**: A systematic approach that introduces a person to increasingly challenging crowd situations, allowing the nervous system to adapt and reduce overstimulation.

## Core Principles
1. **Start Small** – Begin with low‑intensity environments (e.g., a quiet café) and gradually move to larger gatherings.
2. **Controlled Progression** – Incrementally increase exposure duration, density, and sensory load.
3. **Supportive Context** – Use a trusted companion, a clear plan, or a designated “home base” spot to maintain a sense of safety.
4. **Self‑Monitoring** – Pay attention to early signs of fatigue or overstimulation and pause or retreat as needed.

## Application in Session 36 (2025‑03‑15)
- The assistant recommended easing into crowded events rather than jumping straight into overwhelming situations, echoing the gradual exposure framework.
- Practical tips included:
  - Choosing a spot with some breathing room.
  - Having a buddy to shift focus from the crowd to shared experience.
  - Using a plan (knowing exits, bathrooms, and a “home base”).
- These steps helped the user transition from feeling threatened to treating the crowd as an exploratory environment.
