# Mulch Strategy

**Definition:** A set of practices for selecting and placing mulch materials to improve drainage, moderate soil temperature, and reduce moisture‑related problems for winter‑hardy herbs.

## Core Principles
1. **Light, breathable layer** – Apply a thin mulch (1–2 in) and keep it pulled back 1–2 in from the plant stems to avoid trapping moisture against woody bases. [[concepts/mulch_strategy]]
2. **Material choice** – Use fast‑drying, low‑matting options such as **[[entities/pine_needles]]**, **[[entities/shredded_leaves]]**, or **[[entities/straw]]**. **[[entities/pea_gravel]]** is also acceptable, especially for lavender, but it provides little thermal buffering.
3. **Spacing** – For first‑year **[[entities/rosemary]]** and **[[entities/lavender]]**, a 6‑inch ring of mulch or gravel around each plant ensures adequate airflow and keeps the root zone drier.
4. **Seasonal adjustment** – Maintain normal fall watering, then reduce as temperatures fall. Water the day before an anticipated freeze to keep roots from desiccating, then return to light mulching.
5. **Integration with frost protection** – Mulch works alongside **[[concepts/frost_protection]]** (e.g., breathable frost cloth) but should not replace it; moisture‑permeable mulch prevents dampness under the cloth.

## Recommended Materials
| Material | Benefits | Considerations |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| **[[entities/pine_needles]]** | Dries quickly, lightweight, acidifies soil slightly (good for rosemary) | May need occasional topping up |
| **[[entities/shredded_leaves]]** | Readily available, improves organic matter | Can mat if applied too thick |
| **[[entities/straw]]** | Good insulation, easy to spread | Can retain moisture if too thick |
| **[[entities/pea_gravel]]** | Excellent drainage, low moisture retention | Provides little insulation; best as a ring rather than full‑bed cover |

## Application Steps (based on [[summaries/session-38_2023-01-15]])
1. Clear any debris around the base of each herb.
2. Lay a **6‑inch radius** ring of the chosen mulch material, leaving a 1‑2 in gap from the stem.
3. If using gravel, spread a **thin layer** (≈1 in) to keep the soil surface breathable.
4. Monitor after heavy rain or snow; if mulch becomes compacted or soggy, gently lift and fluff it.
5. Combine with **[[entities/frost_cloth]]** during extreme cold snaps, removing the cloth during warm daytime periods.
