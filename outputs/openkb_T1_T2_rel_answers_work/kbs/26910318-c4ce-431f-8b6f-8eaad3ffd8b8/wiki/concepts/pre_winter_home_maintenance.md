# Pre‑Winter Home Maintenance

**Definition**: A systematic checklist of inspections, repairs, and preparations performed in late fall to safeguard a house against the stresses of cold weather, snow, and ice.

---

## Overview
Winter can expose homes to water intrusion, frozen pipes, roof overload, and reduced heating efficiency. Conducting a focused pre‑winter maintenance routine reduces the risk of costly damage and ensures that outdoor projects and hobbies can proceed safely.

## Key Tasks (Late Fall / Late November)
- **Clear gutters and downspouts** – remove leaves and debris to prevent ice dams.
- **Inspect roof** – check for missing shingles, soft spots, and proper ventilation.
- **Seal drafts** – apply weather‑stripping or caulk around windows and doors.
- **Insulate exposed plumbing** – especially in basements, crawl spaces, and exterior walls.
- **Service heating system** – furnace or boiler inspection and filter replacement.
- **Prepare outdoor tools** – position shovels, snowblowers, roof rakes, and de‑icers for easy access.
- **Shut off and drain exterior water lines** – prevent frozen‑pipe bursts.
- **Check foundation drains and window wells** – ensure they are clear of leaves and debris.

## Early‑December Check‑up
- Verify that vents and exterior drains remain unobstructed after the first snowfall.
- Re‑inspect the roof for early ice formation along eaves.
- Confirm indoor humidity is within a healthy range to avoid condensation.

## Ongoing Winter Monitoring
- Watch for **ice dams** and address attic heat loss promptly. See [[concepts/ice_dam_prevention]].
- Assess snow load on the roof after heavy storms.
- Periodically check the basement for moisture, musty odors, or staining.
- Ensure snow does not block vents, siding, or heat‑pump units.
