# Lavender

Lavender is a woody, aromatic herb often cultivated for its fragrant flowers and foliage. In the context of winter herb‑bed care, it is discussed alongside rosemary as a hardy but moisture‑sensitive plant.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-38_2023-01-15]]
- **Mulch preferences:** Works well with light, fast‑drying mulches such as [[entities/pine_needles]], [[entities/shredded_leaves]], straw, or a ring of [[entities/pea_gravel]].
- **Cold‑snap warning signs:** Drooping, darkened, or mushy stems indicate frost damage.
- **Frost protection:** A breathable [[entities/frost_cloth]] can be draped over the plant during cold snaps; it should remain loose to avoid trapping moisture.
- **Watering:** Continue normal fall watering, then reduce as temperatures drop; give a moderate watering before an expected freeze to prevent root desiccation.
- **Pruning:** Light shaping only; defer heavy pruning until spring unless tissue is clearly dead.
