# Frost Cloth

**Type:** Product

Frost cloth is a lightweight, breathable fabric designed to shield plants from cold temperatures and wind without trapping excess moisture. It is commonly used in gardening to provide temporary frost protection during sudden cold snaps.

## Key Characteristics
- **Breathability:** Allows air and moisture to pass through, reducing the risk of mold and rot.
- **Flexibility:** Can be draped directly over plants or loosely lifted with stakes.
- **Reusable:** Often made of durable material suitable for multiple seasons.

## Usage in Winter Herb Care
In the *session-38_2023-01-15* conversation, frost cloth was recommended for protecting first‑year rosemary and lavender plants during cold spells. The advice emphasized:
- Draping the cloth directly over the plants while keeping it loose.
- Removing the cloth during daytime warmth to allow drying and light exposure.
- Avoiding airtight coverings that could retain moisture.

These practices align with broader [[concepts/frost_protection]] and [[concepts/herb_winter_care]] strategies.
