## Pine Needles

Pine needles are a lightweight, fast‑drying organic mulch that gardeners often spread around herb plants such as [[entities/rosemary]] and [[entities/lavender]]. In the context of [[summaries/session-38_2023-01-15]], pine needles are recommended as one of several suitable mulch options because they shed water quickly and help keep the soil surface from staying wet, reducing the risk of stem rot during winter.

### Key Points from the Session
- **Moisture Management:** Pine needles allow moisture to evaporate, preventing the soggy conditions that rosemary and lavender dislike.  
- **Placement:** Like other mulches, they should be laid in a light layer and kept at least 1–2 in away from the plant stems to avoid moisture trapping.  
- **Comparison:** Mentioned alongside straw, shredded leaves, and pea gravel as alternatives; each offers quick drainage, but pine needles are especially valued for their natural availability in many garden settings.
