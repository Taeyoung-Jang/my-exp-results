# Shredded Leaves

**Type:** product

Shredded leaves are an organic mulch material that dries quickly and allows good airflow. In the context of winter care for first‑year rosemary and lavender, shredded leaves are recommended as a *light mulch* that should be placed **1‑2 inches away from the plant stems** to avoid trapping moisture and causing stem rot.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-38_2023-01-15]]
- Serves as a suitable option alongside straw, pine needles, or pea gravel for winter mulching. 
- Helps moderate soil moisture fluctuations without creating a wet barrier against the woody base of [[entities/rosemary]] and [[entities/lavender]].
- Works well for **[[concepts/mulch_strategy]]** by providing a breathable cover that can be easily removed or adjusted during cold snaps.
- Contributes to overall **[[concepts/herb_winter_care]]** by protecting roots while allowing airflow to reduce mildew risk.

## Practical Use
- Apply a thin layer over the soil around each plant, ensuring a small gap from the stems.
- Pair with a breathable **[[entities/frost_cloth]]** if a frost event is expected; the mulch will not impede the cloth’s protective function.
- Replace or refresh the shredded leaf mulch each season as it decomposes.
