# Straw

**Type:** product

**Description:** Straw is a dry, stalky material harvested from cereal crops, commonly used as a light mulch in gardens.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2023-01-15]]
- Recommended as one of the possible light mulches for first‑year rosemary and lavender beds.
- Should be applied in a thin layer and kept 1–2 in away from plant stems to avoid excess moisture retention.
- Provides quick‑drying coverage that helps moderate soil temperature without creating a damp environment.
