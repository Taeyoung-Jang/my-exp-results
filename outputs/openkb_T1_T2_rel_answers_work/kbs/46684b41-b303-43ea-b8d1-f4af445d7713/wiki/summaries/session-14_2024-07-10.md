# Summary of Session 14 (2024‑07‑10)

**Topic:** Building a consistent sleep schedule while allowing flexible weekends.

## Key Concepts
- **Consistent sleep schedule** – aim for a regular bedtime window rather than an exact minute. [[concepts/consistent-sleep-schedule]]
- **Wind‑down routine** – simple cues (dim lights, phone away, short reading) to signal bedtime. [[concepts/wind-down-routine]]
- **Weekend drift** – the tendency to sleep later on weekends; problematic when the shift exceeds 1–2 hours.
- **Morning light exposure** – getting natural light soon after waking to anchor the circadian rhythm.
- **Sleep hygiene checklist** – caffeine cutoff, short early naps, low‑stim evening. [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]]

## Practical Recommendations
1. **Choose a “ballpark” bedtime** (e.g., 10:30 – 11:00 pm) and keep it roughly consistent.  
2. **Create a gentle wind‑down**: dim lights, no screens 60 min before bed, brief reading.  
3. **Limit weekend drift**: keep the Saturday/Sunday wake‑up no more than 1–1.5 h later than weekday time.  
4. **Expose yourself to daylight** within 30 min of waking to reinforce the internal clock.  
5. **Maintain daytime habits** – steady meals and light activity support nighttime sleep.

## 4‑Weekend Step‑Down Plan (from 9:00 am to 7:30–8:00 am)
| Weekend | Saturday Wake‑up | Sunday Wake‑up |
|---------|------------------|---------------|
| 1 | 8:30 am | 8:30 am |
| 2 | 8:00 am | 8:00 am |
| 3 | 7:45 am | 7:45 am |
| 4 | 7:30 am – 8:00 am | 7:30 am – 8:00 am |

*Shift by 20‑30 minutes each weekend; pair with immediate morning light and light movement.*

## Sunday‑Night Checklist (to ease Monday)
- **Bedtime window:** 30 min earlier than your usual weekday bedtime, but no earlier than needed for adequate sleep.  
- **Screens:** power off at least 1 hour before bed.  
- **Caffeine cutoff:** no caffeine after 2 pm.  
- **Naps:** keep any nap ≤20 min and finish before 3 pm.  
- **Light:** spend 10‑15 min outside within an hour of waking.  
- **Environment:** dim lights, cool room, gentle sounds if needed.  

Following this gradual schedule and the Sunday‑night routine should reduce Monday sleep‑inertia and keep your overall energy and mood more stable.
