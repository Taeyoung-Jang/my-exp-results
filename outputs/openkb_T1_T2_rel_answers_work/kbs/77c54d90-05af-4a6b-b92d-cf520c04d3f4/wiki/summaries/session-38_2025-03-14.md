# Summary of Session 38 (2025‑03‑14)

## Overview
A one‑page printable worksheet designed for volunteers at Brazil tenant‑assistance desks. It provides a quick‑scan checklist of common lease red flags, Brazil‑specific terminology, a “Receipts & proof” section, and a set of polite follow‑up message templates.

## Key Concepts
- **Lease red flags** – items to watch for in rent increase, deposit, repairs, entry, penalties, and move‑out clauses. [[concepts/lease-red-flags]]
- **Brazilian lease terminology** – Portuguese keywords such as *reajuste*, *garantia*, *caução*, *benfeitorias*, *vistoria de entrada/saída*, *multa rescisória*, *honorários advocatícios*, *prazo de devolução da caução*. [[concepts/brazilian-lease-terminology]]
- **Follow‑up templates** – ready‑to‑copy messages for rent increase clarification, deposit questions, repair responsibilities, entry notice, move‑out checklist, legal‑fees clause, and deposit return details. *(no dedicated concept page)*
- **Receipts & proof** – checklist ensuring tenants obtain receipts for deductions, payment proof, and a signed lease copy. [[concepts/tenant-documentation-checklist]]

## Worksheet Structure
1. **Before you sign** – three checkboxes for numbers, vague terms, and written clarifications.  
2. **Red‑flag sections** – each with a checkbox list:  
   - Rent increase (*reajuste / atualização*)  
   - Deposit / security (*garantia / caução / depósito caução*)  
   - Repairs & maintenance (*reparos / manutenção / benfeitorias*)  
   - Entry & inspection (*entrada / vistoria de entrada / vistoria de saída*)  
   - Penalties, termination & legal fees (*multa rescisória / honorários advogatícios*)  
   - Move‑out checklist (*checklist de saída / laudo de vistoria*)  
   - Receipts & proof (*comprovantes / recibos / cópia do contrato*)  
3. **Follow‑up message templates** – short, polite copy‑paste texts for each red‑flag area.  
4. **Notes / Questions** – free‑form lines for tenants to record queries.

## Intended Use
The worksheet helps volunteers guide tenants through lease review, ensuring they identify risky clauses, request clarifications in writing, and keep proper documentation before signing.
