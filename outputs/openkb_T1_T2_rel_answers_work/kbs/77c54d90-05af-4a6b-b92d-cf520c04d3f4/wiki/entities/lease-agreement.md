# Lease Agreement (Work)

## Definition
A lease agreement is the written contract between a landlord and a tenant that sets out the terms of renting a residential unit.

## Relevance in Session 38
In [[summaries/session-38_2025-03-14]], the lease agreement is the focus of a printable worksheet for volunteers at the Brazil tenant‑assistance desk. The worksheet highlights common [[concepts/lease-red-flags]] and Brazil‑specific terminology found in typical lease agreements.

## Key Clauses Discussed
- **Rent increase (reajuste)** – notice period, discretionary increases.
- **Deposit / security (garantia / caução)** – refundable vs. non‑refundable language.
- **Repairs & maintenance (benfeitorias)** – allocation of responsibility.
- **Entry & inspection (vistoria de entrada/saída)** – notice requirements.
- **Penalties, termination & legal fees (multa rescisória, honorários advocatícios)** – automatic penalties and fee responsibilities.
- **Move‑out checklist (checklist de saída / laudo de vistoria)** – itemized condition report.

These sections map to the checklist items in the worksheet and correspond to the concepts listed in [[concepts/brazilian-lease-terminology]].

## Documentation Checklist
The worksheet includes a [[concepts/tenant-documentation-checklist]] section urging tenants to obtain:
- Receipts for any deposit deductions
- Proof of all payments
- A copy of the fully signed lease agreement

## Stakeholders
- [[entities/landlord]] – provides the lease agreement.
- [[entities/tenant]] – reviews and signs the lease.
- Volunteers from [[entities/brazilian-tenant-assistance-desk]] help tenants interpret the agreement.

## Communication Context
The follow‑up message templates are crafted for a polite but firm tone, reflecting appropriate [[concepts/formality-level]] and [[concepts/language-choice]] for Portuguese‑speaking tenants.
