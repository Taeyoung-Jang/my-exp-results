# Customer Support

Customer support refers to the service function where representatives assist customers with inquiries, problems, or requests related to a product or service. In the context of the **session-12_2024-06-15** conversation, several practical guidelines are highlighted for effective communication within customer support interactions.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]]
- **Language choice**: When a customer switches from Portuguese to English, agents should mirror the language choice but may politely suggest staying in Portuguese for clarity if needed. This aligns with the broader concept of [[concepts/language-choice]].
- **Formality level**: In higher‑stakes support scenarios, a slightly more formal tone helps convey respect and reduces the risk of misunderstanding, linking to [[concepts/formality-level]].
- **Communication context**: Customer support is identified as a distinct [[concepts/communication-contexts]] where power dynamics (service provider vs. client) influence tone and clarity.
- **Avoiding complex negotiations**: Agents are advised to steer away from detailed negotiations in a non‑native language, recommending written follow‑ups or involving a bilingual colleague. This connects to the entity [[entities/bilingual-support]].

## Recommended Phrases for Language Switches
- *“Sure, I can continue in English. Let me know if anything needs clarification.”*
- *“I can answer in English if needed, but for detailed issues it’s clearer for me in Portuguese. Could we keep the conversation in Portuguese or switch to written details?”*

These phrases illustrate how to apply the concepts of **language choice**, **formality**, and **communication context** in real‑time support.
