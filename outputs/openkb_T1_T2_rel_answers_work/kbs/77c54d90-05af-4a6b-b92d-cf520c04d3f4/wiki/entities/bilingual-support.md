# Bilingual Support

**Type:** other

**Description:** A support strategy that brings in a bilingual individual to facilitate accurate and clear communication when language barriers arise, especially in high‑stakes situations.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]]
- Recommended as a fallback when a conversation shifts from Portuguese to English and the discussion involves complex or detailed information.
- Suggested for use in:
  - [[entities/customer-support]] interactions to avoid misinterpretation of issues or resolutions.
  - Workplace chats where precise details are necessary.
  - Disagreements or sensitive negotiations where nuance matters.
- The strategy emphasizes **mirroring** the interlocutor’s language choice while offering the option to involve a bilingual colleague for clarity.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/language-choice]] – Deciding which language to use based on context and stakeholder needs.
- [[concepts/communication-contexts]] – Understanding the stakes and dynamics of the conversation.
- [[concepts/formality-level]] – Adjusting tone to maintain respect and prevent escalation.

## Practical Usage
When you sense the conversation may become nuanced or error‑prone, you can say something like:
> "I can respond in English, but for any detailed points it might be clearer if we involve a bilingual colleague."

This phrasing frames the suggestion as a clarity‑enhancing measure rather than a reluctance to cooperate.
