# Tenant (type: other)

A **tenant** is an individual who occupies a residential unit under a lease agreement, typically on a month‑to‑month basis or a 12‑month contract. In the Brazilian context, tenants often encounter Portuguese contract language and need to verify that lease terms are clear, fair, and legally compliant.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-03-14]]
- Volunteers at the **Brazilian tenant‑assistance desk** help tenants review lease clauses line‑by‑line.
- Tenants are provided with a printable **worksheet** that flags common lease red‑flags such as rent increase (*reajuste*), deposit (*garantia/caução*), repairs (*benfeitorias*), entry/inspection, penalties, and legal‑fees clauses.
- The worksheet includes Brazil‑specific Portuguese terminology and a “Receipts & proof” section to ensure tenants collect documentation like payment receipts and a signed copy of the lease.
- Tenants are encouraged to use **polite follow‑up message templates** to request clarifications or revisions from the landlord before signing.

## Typical Actions
1. **Review** the lease using the worksheet to spot any red‑flags.
2. **Request** clarifications or revisions via the provided message templates.
3. **Collect** receipts, proof of payment, and a copy of the fully signed lease.
4. **Document** any questions or notes in the worksheet’s “Notes / Questions to Ask” section.
