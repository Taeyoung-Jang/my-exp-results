# Brazilian Tenant Assistance Desk

**Type:** Organization

The **Brazilian Tenant Assistance Desk** is a volunteer‑run service that assists renters in Brazil with understanding and negotiating lease agreements. Volunteers meet with tenants, typically on a weekly basis, to walk through lease clauses line‑by‑line, identify common **[[concepts/lease-red-flags]]**, and help draft polite follow‑up messages before signing.

## Key Activities
- Conduct one‑on‑one lease reviews for tenants on month‑to‑month or 12‑month contracts.
- Provide printable worksheets that include checkboxes for red flags, Brazil‑specific terminology, and a **[[concepts/tenant-documentation-checklist]]** covering receipts, proof of payment, and signed lease copies.
- Offer template messages for clarifying rent increases, deposits, repairs, entry rights, move‑out checklists, and legal‑fee clauses.
- Use bilingual support (Portuguese/English) to ensure tenants understand contract language.

## Relationships
- Serves **[[entities/tenant]]** clients who are negotiating with **[[entities/landlord]]** owners.
- Relies on **[[entities/bilingual-support]]** to translate key terms like *reajuste*, *caução*, and *honorários advocatícios*.
- Aligns its communication style with appropriate **[[concepts/formality-level]]** and **[[concepts/language-choice]]** for tenant‑landlord interactions.
