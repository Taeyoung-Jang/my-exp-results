## Tenant Documentation Checklist

A concise list of documents and proof items tenants should obtain before signing or during tenancy, derived from the worksheet in [[summaries/session-38_2025-03-14]].

### Core items
- **Receipts for any deposit deductions** – ensures any withheld amount is justified.
- **Proof of every payment** (rent, security deposit, fees) – receipts, bank statements, or transfer confirmations.
- **Copy of the fully signed lease agreement** – with all annexes and addenda.

### Supporting documentation
- **Entry and exit inspection reports** – *vistoria de entrada/saída*.
- **Itemized move‑out checklist** – *checklist de saída*.
- **Correspondence related to red‑flag clauses** – e.g., requests for clarification on rent increase (*reajuste*), legal‑fees clause, etc.

### Why it matters
These documents help tenants verify that the lease complies with expected standards and protect them against hidden obligations. Missing documentation often masks unfavorable terms highlighted in [[concepts/lease-red-flags]] and [[concepts/brazilian-lease-terminology]].

### How to use
1. Tick each box during lease review.
2. Keep physical or digital copies in a dedicated folder.
3. Refer to the checklist when drafting follow‑up messages; consider appropriate [[concepts/formality-level]] and [[concepts/language-choice]] for tone.

This checklist supports effective communication with the [[entities/landlord]] and is especially useful for volunteers at the [[entities/brazilian-tenant-assistance-desk]] assisting [[entities/tenant]]s.
