## Language Choice

**Definition**: Language Choice is the process of selecting the appropriate language (e.g., Portuguese or English) and the appropriate level of formality for a given communicative situation.

### Core Elements
1. **Context Sensitivity** – Assess whether the interaction is low‑stakes (friendly chat) or high‑stakes (customer support, workplace dispute).  
2. **Relationship & Power Dynamics** – Consider who has more authority or service responsibility.  
3. **Clarity Needs** – When details involve money, deadlines, or technical specifications, prioritize precision.  
4. **Language Mirroring** – Match the interlocutor’s language choice while staying one notch more formal.

### Practical Checklist (from [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]])
- Is the situation tense or could become tense?
- Is there a power/service dynamic?
- How well do you know the other person?
- How critical is accuracy?
- What language is the other person using?
- If unsure, lean toward a slightly more formal tone.

### Phrase Templates for Language Switches
| Setting | Portuguese‑first Phrase | English‑first Phrase |
|---------|------------------------|----------------------|
| **Customer Support** | "I can answer in English if needed, but for detailed issues it’s clearer for me in Portuguese. Could we keep the conversation in Portuguese or switch to written details?" | "Sure, I can continue in English. Let me know if anything needs clarification." |
| **Workplace Chat** | "I can switch to English, sure. For anything detailed it’s much clearer for me in Portuguese; would that be okay, or should we handle specifics in a written note?" | "No problem switching to English. If we get into more detailed points, it might be easier to put them in writing so I don’t misunderstand." |
| **Disagreement** | "I can answer in English, but it’s clearer for me in Portuguese. Would it be alright if we keep it that way, or handle the specifics in writing?" | "I can respond in English, of course. If we need to discuss complex details, perhaps we can write them down or involve a fully bilingual colleague to ensure accuracy." |
