# Lease Red Flags

**Definition**: A set of lease clause patterns that can expose tenants to unexpected costs, loss of rights, or unfair obligations. Recognizing these red flags helps tenants negotiate clearer terms before signing.

## Core Red‑Flag Categories

- **Rent Increase (reajuste / atualização)**
  - No notice period specified.
  - Clause allows increase “at any time” or at landlord discretion.
  - Amount or frequency is undefined.

- **Deposit / Security (garantia / caução / depósito caução)**
  - Non‑refundable deposit without explanation.
  - Extra fees not discussed during walkthrough.
  - Deposit rules not tied to actual damage or unpaid rent.

- **Repairs & Maintenance (reparos / manutenção / benfeitorias)**
  - Tenant held responsible for major or structural repairs.
  - Language uses “may repair” rather than a firm landlord obligation.
  - Tenant must fix appliances that came with the unit.

- **Entry & Inspection (entrada / vistoria de entrada / vistoria de saída)**
  - Landlord can enter without notice (non‑emergency).
  - No clear notice requirement for entry.
  - Inspection terms are vague or missing.

- **Penalties, Termination & Legal Fees (multa rescisória / honorários advocatícios)**
  - Large automatic penalties for early termination.
  - Landlord may terminate without notice.
  - Clause forces tenant to pay “any and all” legal fees.

- **Move‑Out Checklist (checklist de saída / laudo de vistoria)**
  - No itemized condition report provided.
  - No clear evaluation criteria for deductions.

- **Receipts & Proof (comprovantes / recibos / cópia do contrato)**
  - Lack of receipts for any deposit deductions.
  - No proof of payments (rent, deposit, fees).
  - Missing a copy of the fully signed lease.

## Why These Matter
Identifying these red flags enables tenants to request clarifications, negotiate balanced language, and retain documentation that protects their rights. They are especially relevant in Brazil where contracts often use specific Portuguese terms.
