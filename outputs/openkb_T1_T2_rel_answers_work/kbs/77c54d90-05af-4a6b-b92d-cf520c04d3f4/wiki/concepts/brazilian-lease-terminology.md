## Brazilian Lease Terminology

Brazilian residential leases are written in Portuguese and use a set of standard legal terms that tenants often encounter. Understanding these terms helps tenants identify potential red flags and ask clear questions when reviewing a lease.

| Portuguese term | English translation / meaning | Typical context |
|------------------|------------------------------|----------------|
| **reajuste** (or **atualização**) | rent increase | Clause describing how and when rent may be adjusted, often tied to inflation indexes. |
| **garantia** / **caução** / **depósito caução** | security deposit / guarantee | Amount held to cover damages or unpaid rent; may be labeled "non‑refundable" which requires clarification. |
| **benfeitorias** | improvements / fixtures | Refers to alterations made to the property; clauses may assign responsibility for such works. |
| **vistoria de entrada** / **vistoria de saída** | move‑in / move‑out inspection | Documentation of the unit’s condition before and after tenancy; often linked to the **move‑out checklist**. |
| **multa rescisória** | termination penalty | Fee imposed if the tenant ends the lease early; may be disproportionate and needs negotiation. |
| **honorários advocatícios** | legal fees | Clause that may require the tenant to pay "any and all" legal costs; a common red flag. |
| **prazo de devolução da caução** | deposit return timeline | Specifies when and how the security deposit will be returned, and what documentation will be provided. |

### Why These Terms Matter

Each of the above terms can hide clauses that are overly discretionary or unfavorable to the tenant. Recognizing them enables tenants to:

- Cross‑check the lease against common **[[concepts/lease-red-flags]]**.
- Request clarifications using polite, written follow‑up messages (see the worksheet in **[[summaries/session-38_2025-03-14]]**).
- Ensure they collect the necessary **[[concepts/tenant-documentation-checklist]]** items such as receipts and a signed copy of the lease.

### Practical Tips

1. **Highlight** each term in the contract and verify its definition with the landlord.
2. **Ask for written clarification** if any term is vague, overly broad, or seems to shift responsibility to the tenant.
3. **Document** any agreed changes in an addendum to the lease.

Understanding Brazilian lease terminology is a foundational step toward confident, informed tenancy negotiations.
