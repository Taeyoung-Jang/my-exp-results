# Unified Delivery Tracking

**Definition**: A unified delivery tracking approach aggregates tracking numbers from different shipping carriers into a single application or dashboard, providing centralized visibility and alerts for all incoming parcels.

## Why It Matters
- Simplifies management of shipments when you receive packages from **[[entities/ups]]**, **[[entities/fedex]]**, and **[[entities/usps]]**.
- Reduces the mental load of checking multiple carrier websites, especially for frequent movers.
- Supports the broader **[[concepts/mail-organization]]** workflow by keeping the "needs action" pile up‑to‑date.

## Core Components
1. **Tracking Number Capture** – Enter or forward tracking numbers from order confirmations into the app.
2. **Carrier Integration** – The app connects to APIs or scrapes status pages of major carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS, etc.).
3. **Central Dashboard** – A single view lists all active shipments, sorted by expected delivery date.
4. **Notifications** – Push or email alerts inform you when a package is out for delivery or has arrived.

## Practical Tips from [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]]
- **Add numbers immediately**: When you place an order, paste the tracking number into the app right away.
- **Set delivery alerts**: Enable notifications so you can retrieve packages promptly and avoid loss.
- **Combine with a stable address**: Pair the tracking system with a **[[concepts/permanent-address-strategy]]** (e.g., a private mailbox) to ensure all parcels arrive at a reliable location, regardless of where you temporarily reside.

## Benefits for Mobile Lifestyles
- Enables you to **forward** packages from your temporary residence to a permanent mailbox without losing sight of each shipment.
- Provides a quick audit when reviewing your **weekly mail processing routine**.
