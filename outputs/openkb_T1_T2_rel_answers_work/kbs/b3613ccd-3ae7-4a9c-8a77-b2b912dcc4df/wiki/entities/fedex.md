# FedEx (Organization)

FedEx is a major private carrier that provides package delivery and logistics services worldwide. In the conversation **session-04_2024-06-15**, FedEx is referenced as one of the carriers whose tracking numbers can be entered into a **unified tracking** system to monitor shipments in a single dashboard.

## Key Points from the Document
- Recommended to use a **unified tracking** app that aggregates numbers from UPS, USPS, and FedEx for easy monitoring of deliveries.
- Suggests leveraging FedEx’s carrier account features to **redirect deliveries**, **hold packages**, or send items to **pickup locations** when the user is in temporary housing.
- Serves as part of a broader **mail‑organization** strategy that includes consolidating inbound parcels and maintaining a stable mailing address.
