# Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)

The **Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)** is a state-level government agency responsible for vehicle registration, driver licensing, and related services.

## Relevance in the session‑04_2024-06-15 document
- The conversation recommends keeping a small checklist of **address‑related accounts** such as banks, insurance, the **DMV**, and employer HR, reviewing it once or twice a year to ensure mailing addresses stay current.
- When using a **permanent‑address strategy** (e.g., a private mailbox), the DMV may require an **actual residential address** for verification; the advice is to provide the current living address separately while keeping the mailbox for general mail.
- The document suggests setting calendar reminders to update the DMV after each move, aligning with the broader [[concepts/mail-organization]] approach of consistent address management.
