# United States Postal Service (USPS)

## Overview
The United States Postal Service (USPS) is an independent agency of the U.S. federal government responsible for providing postal service across the United States, including mail delivery, package handling, and various ancillary services.

## Key Facts from Session 04 (2024-06-15)
- The document discusses using **USPS forwarding** as a temporary solution when moving frequently. It notes that USPS forwarding does **not** work in reverse for commercial mailbox addresses, so users should keep a stable mailbox and only forward mail from their temporary residence.
- A reminder is suggested to set up and renew USPS forwarding before it expires after each move.
- The advice recommends treating USPS as part of a broader **[[concepts/mail-organization]]** system, alongside private mailbox services and carrier-specific solutions.

## Role in Mail Organization Strategies
- **Temporary Forwarding**: Useful for short‑term address changes but limited in scope; must be paired with a permanent mailing address strategy.
- **Integration with [[concepts/permanent-address-strategy]]**: The stable address (e.g., private mailbox) works in concert with USPS forwarding to ensure mail reaches the user without constant account updates.
- **Interaction with Tracking**: While USPS offers its own tracking, the document promotes a **[[concepts/unified-tracking]]** app that aggregates numbers from USPS, UPS, FedEx, and other carriers for a single view.
