# Summary of session-31_2025-02-15

## Decision criteria for installing a developer/public beta
- Evaluate **risk vs curiosity**: want new features (e.g., on‑device transcription, automation shortcuts) vs tolerance for bugs, crashes, battery drain.
- Ensure a **reliable backup** that can be restored (full device image, iCloud/ iTunes).
- Verify **essential app compatibility** before installing.
- Relates to [[concepts/beta_software_decision]] and broader [[concepts/upgrade_decision_criteria]].

## Initial troubleshooting steps
1. **Reboot** the device – clears many transient issues.
2. **Battery drain** – give the system 1–2 days to re‑index; monitor top‑draining apps and off‑load if needed.
3. **App crashes** – check for updates; many developers push quick fixes for betas.
4. **Overheating** – close intensive background tasks, limit simultaneous workloads while the beta settles.

These steps align with [[concepts/beta_troubleshooting]].

## Roll‑back threshold
- Repeated crashes or **essential apps failing** (banking, calls, messaging).
- Persistent **excessive heat** or battery drain that interferes with daily use.
- Spending more time on **work‑arounds** than on actual work.
When these conditions are met, restore the backup and revert to the stable release. (No specific wikilink; described as a practical rule.)

## Performance‑optimisation checklist (keep transcription & automation functional)

| Keep enabled | Turn off / limit | Adjust (don’t disable) |
|--------------|------------------|------------------------|
| **[[entities/siri]]** on‑device processing | Background App Refresh for rarely‑used apps | **[[entities/5g]]** Auto (instead of 5G On) |
| **[[entities/shortcuts]]** automations | System Analytics & Improvements, “Share iPhone Analytics”, “Share with App Developers” | Bluetooth scanning for non‑essential apps |
| **[[entities/icloud-drive]]** (needed for shortcut data) | Location for non‑essential apps → “While Using” | Always‑On Display / raise Auto‑Lock to 30 s |
| Required Location Services for shortcuts | “Allow Apps to Request to Track” | |

- After installing, **charge and idle** the phone for 1–2 hours so indexing completes, reducing battery/heat spikes.
