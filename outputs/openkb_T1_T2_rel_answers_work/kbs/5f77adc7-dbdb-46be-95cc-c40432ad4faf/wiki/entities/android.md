# Android (Product)

## Overview
- **Android** is Google’s mobile operating system powering a wide range of smartphones and tablets.

## Relevance to session-31
- The conversation in [[summaries/session-31_2025-02-15]] compares iOS and Android update streams when deciding whether to install a developer/public beta.
- The decision‑making process follows the principles of [[concepts/beta_software_decision]] and [[concepts/upgrade_decision_criteria]]: weighing curiosity against risk, checking essential‑app compatibility, and ensuring a reliable backup.
- Suggested troubleshooting steps (reboot, monitoring battery, checking app updates, addressing overheating) map to [[concepts/beta_troubleshooting]].
- The performance‑optimisation checklist (tuning background refresh, location, analytics) aligns with [[concepts/beta_performance_optimizations]].

## Key considerations when beta testing Android
- **Backup strategy** – use a full device image or Google’s backup to enable a quick restore.
- **App compatibility** – verify that critical apps (banking, work tools) run on the beta build.
- **Resource monitoring** – watch battery drain, heat, and crash frequency; set a personal rollback threshold based on daily‑use impact.
