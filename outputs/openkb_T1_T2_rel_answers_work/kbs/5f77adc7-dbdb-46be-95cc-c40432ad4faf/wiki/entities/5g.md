# 5G (other)

5G is the latest generation of cellular network technology, offering faster data rates and lower latency than its predecessors. In the context of the **session‑31_2025‑02‑15** beta discussion, 5G is mentioned as a setting to keep enabled for automation shortcuts that rely on internet access, but the recommendation is to use the "5G Auto" mode rather than forcing a constant 5G connection to reduce heat and battery drain.

## Key points from the session
- **Keep enabled**: 5G (or 5G Auto) should remain on because many automation shortcuts and on‑device transcription features depend on a reliable network connection.
- **Adjust, don’t disable**: Switch from "5G On" to "5G Auto" to let the system select the most efficient radio mode, helping mitigate excess power consumption during beta testing.
