# Summary of Session 15 (2024‑07‑14)

This document provides a practical framework for a worker on a **4 AM – 12 PM shift** to structure daily routines that protect sleep quality. It outlines two primary schedule patterns, adaptations for bad‑sleep nights, detailed timing of meals, caffeine, naps, workouts, and offers portable meal ideas.

## Schedule Patterns
- **Early Sleeper pattern** – light lunch after shift, stay active, mid‑afternoon workout, early dinner (~5 PM), lights dim by 7 PM, bedtime at 8 PM. [[concepts/early-sleeper-pattern]]
- **Planned Nap pattern** – short 25‑30 min nap right after lunch, workout or errands post‑nap, dinner around 5 PM, wind‑down at 7:30 PM, bedtime at 9 PM. [[concepts/planned-nap-pattern]]

## Nap Control
Set a 25‑30 min alarm, nap in a bright environment, keep blankets light, place phone across the room. Early nap timing (12:30‑1 PM) prevents deep sleep.  

## Caffeine Timing
Front‑load caffeine: first dose at shift start, optional second dose no later than 8 AM. No caffeine after the nap to avoid evening alertness.  

## Workout vs. Rest Day Variations
- **Workout days:** exercise around 2‑3 PM after nap.  
- **Rest days:** replace workout with light activities (reading, walk) while keeping the same overall timeline.  

## Bad‑Sleep Adjustment
When only 5‑6 h of sleep the night before, extend nap to ≤45 min, keep caffeine early only, replace intense workouts with mobility or a light walk, maintain dinner and wind‑down times.  

## Meal Composition
- **Shift breakfast:** steady carbs + protein (e.g., yogurt & berries, egg‑cheese wrap, overnight oats).  
- **Lunch before nap:** light, balanced bowls (chicken‑veggie grain bowl, tuna‑white‑bean salad, hummus‑veggie wrap).  
- **Dinner:** early, balanced (roasted chicken/tofu, veggies, modest carbs).  
- **Optional evening snack:** small fruit, yogurt, or toast with nut butter.  
Emphasize steady energy, avoid heavy creamy sauces, large pasta/fried portions, and sugary foods that trigger a post‑lunch crash.

## Portable Meal Ideas
Quick, transportable options for each slot are provided, supporting the overall routine without added preparation burden.

Overall, the guide integrates [[concepts/shift-work-sleep-hygiene]] by aligning activity, nutrition, and caffeine to the unique early‑morning work window, offering flexible templates that can be trialed week‑by‑week.
