# Summary of session‑38_2025-01-11

**Main contribution**: A practical, time‑boxed morning‑routine template for anyone who trains at sunrise and returns home around 9 am, covering light exposure, post‑workout nutrition, caffeine strategy, planning, and deep‑work blocks, plus adjustments for poor sleep or low appetite.

## Key concepts
- **Morning light & movement** – the foundational habit that synchronises the circadian clock and boosts alertness. [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]]
- **Post‑workout nutrition** – balanced protein‑carb‑fat meal or smoothie to prevent mid‑day energy crash. [[concepts/post_workout_meal]]
- **Caffeine timing & dose** – 80‑120 mg between 9–10 am, cut‑off around noon to avoid late‑day slump. [[concepts/caffeine_management]]
- **Planning window** – 9:30‑10:00 am brief review of top 1‑2 priorities.
- **Deep‑focus block** – 10:00‑12:00 am uninterrupted work while post‑exercise arousal is high.
- **Adaptations** – lighter meals or delayed caffeine on poor‑sleep or low‑appetite days.

## Recommended 9:00 – 12:00 template
| Time | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| **9:00‑9:30** | Post‑gym meal | Protein + carbs + a little fat (e.g., eggs + toast, yogurt + granola, or protein‑oat smoothie). |
| **9:30‑10:00** | Planning | Scan calendar, pick 1‑2 high‑impact tasks, note quick wins. |
| **10:00‑12:00** | Deep work | Focused, distraction‑free block for the most cognitively demanding tasks. |

### Adjustments for special cases
- **Poor sleep**: choose an easy‑digest snack (smoothie, yogurt), push caffeine to 9:30‑10:00, keep the deep‑work block but stay aware of fatigue.
- **Low appetite**: start with a small bite (banana, protein shake) then have a fuller meal around 10 am.

## Caffeine recommendation
- **Amount**: 80‑120 mg (≈ one standard cup of coffee) once in the morning.
- **Cut‑off**: no later than **12:00 pm** (or earlier if caffeine‑sensitive) to protect evening sleep. [[concepts/caffeine_management]]

## Simple “stay‑full” smoothie recipe
```text
1 scoop protein powder (20‑25 g protein)
½ frozen banana
¼ cup rolled oats
1 cup milk or plant‑based milk
1 tbsp peanut butter or Greek yogurt
Blend until smooth.
```
Provides protein, complex carbs, and healthy fats for sustained satiety.

## Take‑away habit priority
If you must keep only 2‑3 habits, protect:
1. **Morning light + movement** (already established). [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]]
2. **Post‑workout balanced meal** – the single biggest guard against mid‑day crash. [[concepts/post_workout_meal]]
