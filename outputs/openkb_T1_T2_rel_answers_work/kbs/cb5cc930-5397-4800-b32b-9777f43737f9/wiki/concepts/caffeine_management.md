# Caffeine Management

**Definition**: Strategies for selecting the right amount of caffeine, scheduling its intake, and setting a daily cutoff to maximize alertness while minimizing mid‑day crashes and sleep disruption.

---

## Core Principles
- **Dose**: 80–120 mg (roughly one standard cup of coffee) taken once in the morning.
- **Timing**: Consume between **9:00 am and 10:00 am**, ideally after a balanced post‑workout meal.
- **Cut‑off**: No caffeine later than **12:00 pm** (or earlier for caffeine‑sensitive individuals) to avoid interference with evening sleep.
- **Contextual Adjustment**: On poor‑sleep nights, shift caffeine slightly later (9:30‑10:00 am) and pair with a light, easily digestible snack.

---

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- Aligns with [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]] by supporting the natural morning cortisol peak and avoiding late‑day stimulation that can delay melatonin onset.
- Complements [[concepts/post_workout_meal]]: taking caffeine with or shortly after a protein‑carb‑fat meal smooths the caffeine curve and reduces jitteriness.
- Useful for people following the [[concepts/early-sleeper-pattern]] who need a clear caffeine window to preserve an early bedtime.
- Can be integrated with [[concepts/planned-nap-pattern]] by ensuring no caffeine within 4‑6 hours before a scheduled nap.
- Relevant for [[concepts/shift-work-sleep-hygiene]] where timing of caffeine must be adapted to irregular work hours.

---

## Practical Application (from [[summaries/session-38_2025-01-11]])
1. **Morning dose**: 80‑120 mg between 9‑10 am after returning from sunrise gym session.
2. **Meal pairing**: Combine with a post‑workout breakfast (e.g., eggs & toast, yogurt & granola, or protein‑oat smoothie) to prolong the energizing effect.
3. **Mid‑day cutoff**: Stop caffeine by noon to prevent a 2‑3 pm slump and protect nighttime sleep quality.
4. **Adjustment tips**:
   - If sleep was poor, choose a lighter snack (smoothie or yogurt) and delay caffeine to 9:30‑10 am.
   - If appetite is low, start with a small bite (banana, protein shake) before the full meal, keeping caffeine timing unchanged.

---

## Benefits
- Sustains steady energy throughout the morning work block (10‑12 am).
- Reduces risk of an afternoon energy crash.
- Supports consistent sleep onset and quality.
- Enhances the effectiveness of other morning habits such as light exposure, movement, and focused planning.

---

## Implementation Checklist
- [ ] Measure caffeine amount (≈80‑120 mg).

- [ ] Pair with a balanced post‑workout meal.
- [ ] Set a reminder to stop caffeine by noon.
- [ ] Adjust dose/timing on poor‑sleep or low‑appetite days.
