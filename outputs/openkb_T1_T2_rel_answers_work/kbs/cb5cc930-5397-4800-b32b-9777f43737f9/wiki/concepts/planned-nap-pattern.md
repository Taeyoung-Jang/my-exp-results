# Planned Nap Pattern

**Definition**: The Planned Nap Pattern is a daily routine designed for people who work early morning shifts (e.g., 4 AM – 12 PM) that incorporates a brief, timed nap right after lunch to reset alertness without disrupting nighttime sleep.

## Core Components
- **Wake‑up**: Around 3 AM to allow enough time before the shift starts.
- **Caffeine**: First dose at the start of the shift, optional second dose no later than 8 AM; none after the nap.
- **Lunch & Nap**: Eat a light, balanced lunch at noon, then nap for 25–30 minutes (typically 12:30 – 1:00 PM). The nap is taken in a bright environment, with an alarm and phone placed across the room to prevent oversleeping.
- **Post‑nap Activities**:
  - **Workout days**: Exercise around 2 – 3 PM.
  - **Rest days**: Light activities such as reading, a walk, or errands.
- **Dinner**: Early evening, around 5 – 5:30 PM, balanced protein, carbs, and vegetables.
- **Wind‑down**: Begin at 7:30 PM, dim lights, avoid screens.
- **Bedtime**: Aim for 9 PM to secure a full 8‑hour sleep block before the next shift.

## Adaptations for Bad‑Sleep Nights
When the prior night yields only 5–6 hours of sleep, the pattern adjusts by:
- Extending the nap to a maximum of 45 minutes (still under an hour).
- Keeping caffeine limited to early‑shift doses only.
- Replacing intense workouts with low‑impact mobility or walking.
- Maintaining the same dinner and wind‑down times to preserve rhythm.

## Meal Guidance
- **Shift Breakfast**: Portable combos like yogurt‑berries, egg‑cheese wrap, or overnight oats.
- **Lunch**: Light yet filling options such as a chicken‑veggie grain bowl, tuna‑white‑bean salad, or hummus‑veggie wrap.
- **Dinner**: Early, balanced meals (roasted protein, veggies, modest carbs).
- **Evening Snack** (optional): Small fruit, yogurt, or toast with nut butter.
- **Foods to Avoid**: Heavy creamy sauces, large pasta/fried portions, and sugary items that cause post‑lunch crashes.

## Benefits
- Prevents a prolonged afternoon slump by providing a controlled reset.
- Keeps caffeine exposure early, reducing interference with nighttime sleep.
- Offers flexibility for workout vs. rest days while keeping a stable sleep‑wake schedule.
