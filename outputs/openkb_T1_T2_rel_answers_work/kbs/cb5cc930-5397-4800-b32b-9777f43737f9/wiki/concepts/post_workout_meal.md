# Post‑Workout Meal

**Definition**: A post‑workout meal provides a balanced mix of protein, carbohydrates, and a modest amount of fat soon after exercise to replenish glycogen, support muscle recovery, and stabilise blood‑sugar levels, thereby reducing the likelihood of an energy slump later in the day.

---

## Why it matters
- Aligns with the body's natural **circadian rhythm** by delivering nutrients during the post‑exercise alert phase, which helps maintain consistent energy levels throughout the morning. [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]]
- Supports the effectiveness of later caffeine intake by preventing rapid glucose spikes and crashes; pairing caffeine with food yields smoother, longer‑lasting focus. [[concepts/caffeine_management]]

---

## Recommended composition (based on [[summaries/session-38_2025-01-11]])
| Macro | Typical range | Example foods |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| **Protein** | 20‑30 g | Eggs, Greek yogurt, protein powder, lean meat |
| **Carbohydrates** | 30‑50 g | Whole‑grain toast, oats, fruit, honey |
| **Fat** | 5‑10 g | Nut butter, avocado, a drizzle of olive oil |

### Quick solid options
- **Eggs + whole‑grain toast** (protein + carbs + healthy fat)
- **Greek yogurt + fruit + granola**
- **Leftovers from dinner** (e.g., chicken‑rice‑veggies) if warm food is preferred

### Simple “stay‑full” smoothie (from the same source)
```text
1 scoop protein powder (20‑25 g protein)
½ frozen banana
¼ cup rolled oats
1 cup milk or plant‑based milk
1 tbsp peanut butter or Greek yogurt
Blend until smooth.
```
This blend supplies protein, complex carbs, and fat for sustained satiety.

---

## Timing & caffeine coordination
- **When to eat**: Within 15‑30 minutes of finishing the workout (around 9:00 am for sunrise gym goers). 
- **Caffeine**: Consume 80‑120 mg (≈ one cup coffee) **with** or **shortly after** the meal, ideally by 9:30‑10:00 am, to avoid a jittery spike on an empty stomach and to keep the caffeine cut‑off around noon. [[concepts/caffeine_management]]

---

## Adjustments for special situations
- **Poor sleep**: Choose an easy‑digest option (smoothie or yogurt) and possibly delay caffeine to 9:30 am to soften the stimulant effect.
- **Low appetite after training**: Start with a small bite (banana, protein shake) and follow with a fuller meal around 10:00 am once hunger returns.
