# Shift Work Sleep Hygiene

**Definition**: Practices and routines that help people on irregular or early‑morning shifts preserve sleep quality, manage daytime alertness, and reduce the risk of chronic sleep debt.

## Core Elements

1. **Consistent Sleep‑Wake Timing**
   - Aim to go to bed and wake up at the same clock times each day, even on days off. This stabilises the circadian rhythm.
2. **Strategic Caffeine Use**
   - Front‑load caffeine at the start of the shift and, if needed, a second dose no later than 8 AM. Avoid any caffeine after the early‑day nap to prevent evening alertness. [[concepts/shift-work-sleep-hygiene]]
3. **Planned Short Nap**
   - A 25‑30 minute nap immediately after lunch (around 12:30‑1 PM) helps refresh without entering deep sleep, mitigating the post‑lunch crash. Use an alarm, bright environment, and keep blankets light.
4. **Meal Timing & Composition**
   - **Shift Breakfast**: steady carbs + protein (e.g., yogurt & berries, egg wrap, overnight oats). 
   - **Lunch Before Nap**: light, balanced meals (e.g., chicken‑veggie grain bowl, tuna‑white‑bean salad). 
   - **Dinner**: early, balanced (roasted protein, veggies, modest carbs) around 5 PM. 
   - **Evening Snack**: optional small fruit or yogurt if needed.
   - Avoid heavy, sugary, or fried foods at lunch, which exacerbate the crash.
5. **Physical Activity Scheduling**
   - Schedule workouts after the nap (2‑3 PM) on workout days, or replace with light mobility on rest or bad‑sleep days. This aligns activity with the body’s natural energy dip.
6. **Wind‑Down Routine**
   - Dim lights by 7‑7:30 PM, limit screen exposure, and maintain a calm pre‑bed environment to cue sleep readiness.

## Adaptations for Bad‑Sleep Nights
- Extend the nap to a maximum of 45 minutes (still under an hour). 
- Keep caffeine limited to early‑shift doses only. 
- Substitute intense workouts with gentle movement (stretching, walk). 
- Preserve dinner and wind‑down times to maintain overall schedule consistency.

## Schedule Patterns Supporting Shift Work Sleep Hygiene
- The **early‑sleeper pattern** front‑loads activity and targets an 8‑hour sleep window before the next shift. [[concepts/early-sleeper-pattern]]
- The **planned‑nap pattern** incorporates a short post‑lunch nap and flexible workout timing. [[concepts/planned-nap-pattern]]
