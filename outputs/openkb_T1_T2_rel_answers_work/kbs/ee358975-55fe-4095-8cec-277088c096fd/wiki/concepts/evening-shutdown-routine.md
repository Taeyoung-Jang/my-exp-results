# Evening Shutdown Routine

A **Evening Shutdown Routine** is a concise, repeatable set of actions designed to cue the brain that the workday is over, create a sense of security, and ease the transition into personal time. It is especially useful when the workspace shares the same physical environment as the living space.

## Core Components

1. **Physical boundary cue** – Close the office door and perform a short desk reset (≈20 seconds). This marks a clear separation between work and home. [[concepts/physical-boundary-cue]]
2. **Environmental shift** – Switch to dedicated evening lighting (warm, low‑intensity) to change the visual atmosphere. [[concepts/transition-ritual]]
3. **Body cue** – Take a slow breath or do a brief shoulder roll to signal the body that the day is finished. [[concepts/transition-ritual]]
4. **Comfort habit** – Initiate a soothing activity, such as pouring a favorite drink or changing into relaxed clothing.
5. **Optional security check** – Quickly verify doors are locked and blinds are closed (≤30 seconds) for added peace of mind.

## Printable Checklist (under 1 minute per step)

```
[ ] Close office door and do a 20‑second desk reset
[ ] Switch to evening lighting
[ ] Take one slow breath or shoulder roll
[ ] Start a comfort cue (drink, clothes, etc.)
[ ] Optional: Quick security check (doors locked, blinds closed)
```

## Managing Temptation to Re‑enter Work Mode

If the urge to reopen the door arises, pause for about ten seconds, then jot the pending thought on a “tomorrow” note. This acknowledges the task without breaking the routine.
