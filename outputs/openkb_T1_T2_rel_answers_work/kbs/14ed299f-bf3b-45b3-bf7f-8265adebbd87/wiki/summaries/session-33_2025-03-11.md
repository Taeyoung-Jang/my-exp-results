# Summary of session-33_2025-03-11

**Document type:** Training plan & guidance for a charity stair‑climb event.

## Core Goal
Provide a concrete, 8‑week progressive workout schedule (Mon–Sun) that balances stair‑specific conditioning, low‑impact cardio, and knee‑friendly strength work, while flagging red‑flags for injury and offering detailed warm‑up, cool‑down, and form cues.

## Weekly Structure (updated)
- **Monday:** Easy cardio (25‑35 min) on bike or treadmill.  
- **Tuesday:** [[concepts/knee-friendly-strength-conditioning]] routine (split‑stance sit‑backs, low step‑ups, glute bridges, core).  
- **Thursday:** Non‑impact conditioning on treadmill or bike; includes **steady** and **interval** formats with explicit incline & speed ranges.  
- **Saturday:** Main stair‑climb session (progressive volume, steady vs interval patterns, set/rest scheme).  
- **Sunday:** Rest (or light stretching).

The plan progresses volume and intensity from Week 1 to Week 6, then tapers Weeks 7‑8.

## Thursday Treadmill Options
- **Steady days:** 6‑10 % incline, 2.8‑3.3 mph walking speed.  
- **Interval days:** Moderate phase = 8‑12 % incline, same speed; Easy phase = 3‑5 % incline, same speed. Speed is kept constant; only incline changes (optional tiny speed bump if needed). See [[concepts/treadmill-interval-design]] for the interval structure.

## Strength Routine (knee‑friendly)
- **Supported split‑stance sit‑backs:** 2 × 8‑10 reps per side.  
- **Low step‑ups:** 2 × 8 reps per leg on a 6‑10 in step; focus on a slow eccentric (lower) phase, use light rail support.  
- **Glute bridges:** 2 × 12‑15 reps.  
- **Core hold (plank or dead‑bug):** 20‑30 s × 1‑2.

### Assessing Step Height
If you feel pressure at the front of the knee or the knee travels far past the toe, lower the step until the shin stays more vertical and the movement feels hip‑driven.

## Warm‑up & Cool‑down for Saturday Stairs
- **Warm‑up:** 3‑4 min easy walk, 1 min gentle marches/step‑ups, ankle & hip mobility (ankle circles, hip openers).  
- **Cool‑down:** 2 min slow walk, then static stretches (calf, quad, hip) ~30 s each.

## Form Cues to Reduce Calf Load
- Plant the **whole foot** on each step, not just forefoot.  
- Use a **mid‑foot/heel drive**; let hips push forward.  
- Keep a **tall torso**, avoid leaning too far forward.  
- Lightly hold the handrail for balance only—do not pull.  
- Maintain a **steady, slightly slower cadence**; breathe through the nose.

## Stop‑Signs / Back‑Off Triggers
- Sharp knee pain not easing after warm‑up.  
- Persistent calf tightness altering stride.  
- Swelling around knee or Achilles.  
- Limp or altered gait.

If any appear, replace stair work with low‑impact cardio (bike or incline walk) and resume only after pain‑free daily movement.
