# Facebook Marketplace

Facebook Marketplace is a classifieds service integrated into the Facebook social network, allowing users to list, browse, and communicate about items for sale within local communities. It functions as a digital marketplace where individuals and businesses can post listings for a wide range of goods, including housing, furniture, vehicles, and more.

## Role in Session 11 (2024‑07‑10)
During the conversation in [[summaries/session-11_2024-07-10]], the user requested a friendly message template for contacting a seller via a "Craigslist/FB Marketplace DM." The assistant provided a version tailored for a direct message on Facebook Marketplace, emphasizing a conversational tone while still covering essential pre‑screening questions such as floor level, elevator availability, parking, laundry, pet policy, utilities, internet options, and potential construction or noise issues.
