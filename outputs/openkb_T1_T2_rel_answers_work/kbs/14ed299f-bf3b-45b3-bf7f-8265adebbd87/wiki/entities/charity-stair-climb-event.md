# Charity Stair‑Climb Event

**What it is**: A fundraising stair‑climb held in the **downtown tower** to raise money for local charities. Participants repeatedly ascend and descend the building’s glass‑walled stairwell over a weekend.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-11]]**:
- Date of planning session: 2025‑03‑11.
- Training focus: 8‑week progressive program that balances stair‑specific work, low‑impact cardio, and **knee‑friendly strength conditioning**.
- Main training concepts used: [[concepts/stair-climb-training-progression]], [[concepts/knee-friendly-strength-conditioning]], and [[concepts/treadmill-interval-design]].
- Weekly schedule: Mon‑Sun layout with dedicated stair sessions on Saturdays and non‑impact conditioning on Thursdays.
- Safety cues: stop signs for knee pain, calf tightness, swelling, or altered gait.

**Related entities**:
- The event takes place in [[entities/downtown-tower]].

**Purpose**: Encourage community participation, promote physical fitness, and generate charitable donations.

**Training resources**: The plan outlined in the session summary provides participants with a structured preparation pathway, emphasizing joint‑friendly exercises and form cues to reduce calf load during stair climbing.
