# Pre‑Screening Questions

Pre‑screening questions are a set of essential inquiries that renters ask landlords or agents **before** scheduling a tour. Their purpose is to quickly determine whether a property meets the renter’s basic requirements, saving time and avoiding unnecessary visits.

## Core Questions (from [[summaries/session-11_2024-07-10]])
- **Floor level** – Which floor is the unit on? Are first or second‑floor units only?
- **Elevator availability** – Does the building have an elevator?
- **Parking** – What parking options are available and are there any associated fees?
- **Laundry** – Is there in‑unit laundry, on‑site laundry facilities, or a nearby laundromat?
- **Pet policy** – Are pets allowed, and are there any restrictions or fees?
- **Move‑in costs** – What are the expected move‑in fees, security deposit, and any other upfront costs?
- **Utilities** – What is the average cost of utilities (electricity, water, gas, etc.)?
- **Internet providers** – Which internet service providers are available in the building?
- **Construction / noise** – Are there any upcoming construction projects or known noise issues?

## Why Use Pre‑Screening Questions?
- **Efficiency:** Quickly filters out properties that don’t meet non‑negotiable criteria.
- **Budget control:** Clarifies financial obligations beyond rent.
- **Comfort:** Ensures the living environment aligns with personal preferences (e.g., floor level, noise levels).
- **Decision confidence:** Provides enough information to decide whether to proceed with a tour.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- These questions are incorporated into various message templates described in [[concepts/apartment-inquiry-templates]].
- The tone of the inquiry can be adjusted according to the communication channel, as discussed in [[concepts/communication-tone]].

## Practical Use
When contacting a landlord or property manager—whether via a formal email, a brief text, or a platform‑specific DM (e.g., on [[entities/craigslist]] or [[entities/facebook-marketplace]])—including a concise set of pre‑screening questions helps convey seriousness and professionalism while obtaining the necessary details upfront.
