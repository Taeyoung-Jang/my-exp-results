# Stair‑Climb Training Progression

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A structured, 8‑week plan that gradually increases stair‑specific volume while integrating low‑impact cardio, knee‑friendly strength work, and recovery strategies.

---

## Core Structure
- **Monday:** Easy cardio (25–35 min) on bike or treadmill.
- **Tuesday:** Knee‑friendly strength routine (see [[concepts/knee-friendly-strength-conditioning]]).
- **Thursday:** Non‑impact conditioning on treadmill or bike.  Steady days use 6–10 % incline at 2.8–3.3 mph; interval days follow the [[concepts/treadmill-interval-design]] of moderate incline (8–12 %) with the same speed, alternating with low‑incline recovery.
- **Saturday:** Main stair‑climb session – volume and intensity increase week‑by‑week.
- **Sunday:** Rest or light stretching.

## Weekly Progression
| Week | Monday | Tuesday | Thursday | Saturday (Stair) |
|------|--------|---------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | 25–30 min easy cardio | Knee‑friendly strength | 25–30 min steady incline walk/bike | 3 × 8–10 min steady climbs, 2–3 min rest |
| 2 | Same as Week 1 | Same | 30 min steady, slightly higher grade | 3 × 10 min steady |
| 3 | 30 min cardio | Same | 1 min moderate / 1 min easy intervals, 16–18 min total | 2 × 12 min steady + 8 min easy cooldown |
| 4 | Same | Same | 20 min steady moderate | 15 min steady, 3 min rest, 10 min steady |
| 5 | 30–35 min cardio | Same | 3 min moderate / 1 min easy intervals, ~20–24 min | 20 min continuous, 3 min rest, 15 min second climb |
| 6 | Same | Same | 90 s moderate / 90 s easy, 20–22 min | 30 min continuous (or 2 × 15–18 min) + 8–10 min cooldown |
| 7 | 25–30 min cardio | Light strength | 18–20 min steady | 20–25 min continuous at event pace |
| 8 | 20–25 min cardio | Optional light strength or rest | 10–15 min easy incline walk/bike | Rest (or light stretch) before event |

## Key Elements
- **Progressive overload:** Stair time increases from ~30 min total in Week 1 to up to 30 min continuous by Week 6, then tapers.
- **Interval integration:** Introduced in Weeks 3, 5, 6 to develop aerobic power without excessive joint load.
- **Taper:** Weeks 7‑8 reduce volume to ensure freshness for the event.
- **Warm‑up & Cool‑down (Saturday):** 3–4 min easy walk, 1 min gentle marches/step‑ups, ankle‑hip mobility; post‑session 2 min walk + 30 s static stretches for calves, quads, hips.
- **Form cues to reduce calf strain:** Whole‑foot placement, mid‑foot/heel drive, tall torso, light hand‑rail support, controlled cadence.
- **Stop‑signs:** Sharp knee pain, persistent calf tightness, swelling, limp—swap stair work for low‑impact cardio.
