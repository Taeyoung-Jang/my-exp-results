# Apartment Inquiry Templates

**Definition**: A set of pre‑crafted communication templates that help prospective renters gather essential information about a listing while clearly stating floor‑level preferences.

## Core Components
- **Pre‑screening questions** covering:
  - Floor level and elevator availability
  - Parking options
  - Laundry facilities
  - Pet policy
  - Average utility costs
  - Internet provider options
  - Move‑in fees
  - Upcoming construction or noise concerns
- **Tone variations** tailored to the communication channel:
  - Brief text‑message style
  - Detailed email version
  - Friendly marketplace DM (e.g., on [[entities/craigslist]] or [[entities/facebook-marketplace]])
  - Formal request for property‑management firms
- **Neutral phrasing** for floor preference, emphasizing "first or second‑floor units" without sounding odd.

## How It Was Developed
The templates were iteratively refined in [[summaries/session-11_2024-07-10]], starting from a basic inquiry and expanding to include additional questions and tone adjustments. The process showcases the interplay between [[concepts/pre-screening-questions]] (what to ask) and [[concepts/communication-tone]] (how to ask it).

## Usage Guidelines
1. **Select the appropriate tone** based on the recipient (text, email, marketplace DM, formal management).
2. **Customize placeholders** (e.g., unit address, landlord name) while retaining the core question structure.
3. **Maintain neutrality** when stating floor preferences to keep the message professional and courteous.
4. **Add or remove items** as needed, but keep the set of pre‑screening questions comprehensive to avoid unnecessary tours.

## Example Templates
- **Short Text**: "Hi! I'm interested in this unit. Could you confirm the floor, elevator, parking, laundry, pet policy, and move‑in costs? I'm only looking at first or second‑floor units. Thanks!"
- **Detailed Email**: "Hello, I would like to confirm the floor level, elevator presence, parking, laundry setup, pet policy, average utilities, internet options, and any move‑in fees. Additionally, please inform me of any upcoming construction or noise issues. I am focusing on first‑ or second‑floor apartments."
- **Friendly Marketplace DM** (e.g., on Craigslist/Facebook Marketplace): similar wording with a conversational tone.
- **Formal Management Request**: a polished version with courteous language and full detail request.
