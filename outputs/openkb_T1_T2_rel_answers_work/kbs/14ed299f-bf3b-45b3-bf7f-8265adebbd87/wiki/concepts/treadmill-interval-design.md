# Treadmill Interval Design

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** Structured treadmill workouts that vary incline (and optionally speed) to create moderated and easy phases, serving as a low‑impact substitute for stair‑climbing sessions.

---

## Overview
Treadmill Interval Design provides a reproducible template for Thursday conditioning days in the 8‑week stair‑climb training plan. The core idea is to keep the treadmill speed constant and manipulate the incline to toggle between work and recovery phases. This approach mimics the cardiovascular and muscular demands of stair climbing without the joint‑impact of actual stairs, supporting the overall [[concepts/stair-climb-training-progression]] while remaining knee‑friendly.

---

## Core Parameters
| Parameter | Steady‑State Days | Interval Days |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Incline (work phase)** | 6–10 % | 8–12 % |
| **Incline (recovery phase)** | N/A (single grade) | 3–5 % |
| **Speed** | 2.8–3.3 mph (walk) | Same speed across both phases (2.8–3.3 mph) |
| **Duration** | 25–30 min total | 16–22 min total, depending on interval length |
| **Structure** | Continuous walk at set incline | Alternate work / easy minutes (e.g., 1 min work / 1 min easy) or 90‑sec blocks |

### Interval Timing Options
- **Early weeks (Week 3, 4):** 1 min moderate / 1 min easy, repeat 8–9 times (≈16–18 min).
- **Mid‑program (Week 5, 6):** 3 min moderate / 1 min easy, repeat 5–6 times (≈20–24 min).
- **Later weeks (Week 7, 8):** Return to 1 min moderate / 1 min easy, but reduce total time to 15–20 min for tapering.

---

## Implementation in the 8‑Week Plan
The concept is directly applied in the Thursday sessions described in [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-11]]. Each week follows the same weekly cadence (Mon‑Fri training, Sat stair‑climb, Sun rest) with Thursday alternating between steady and interval formats as the volume progresses. By keeping speed constant, the athlete maintains a stable gait pattern, reducing the risk of knee strain—a key concern addressed by [[concepts/knee-friendly-strength-conditioning]].

---

## Benefits
- **Joint protection:** Lower impact than stairs; incline change isolates effort to the posterior chain without excessive knee flexion.
- **Cardiovascular specificity:** Simulates the aerobic demand of stair climbing, aiding adaptation to sustained effort.
- **Scalability:** Adjust incline or interval length to suit fitness level or recovery status.
- **Simplicity:** No need to constantly tweak speed; the treadmill controls the work‑recovery transitions via incline.
