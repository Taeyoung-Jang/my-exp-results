# CALM Routine

**Definition**: A concise two‑minute micro‑break that uses the acronym **C‑A‑L‑M** (Cup, Adjust, Look, Measure) to provide a rapid mental reset during brief waiting periods, such as warming a bottle.

---

## Overview
The CALM Routine is designed for moments when you have only a minute or two of idle time. It strings together four simple actions that require no equipment and can be performed silently. The routine draws on principles of [[concepts/micro-break]] and [[concepts/mindfulness]] to quickly lower stress and improve focus.

---

## Steps (0:00–2:00)
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| **0:00–0:30** | **C – Cup**: Rub hands together, cup them over your eyes, inhale slowly, then exhale longer.
| **0:30–1:00** | **A – Adjust**: Lower the hands, roll the shoulders back once or twice, letting them settle lower on the exhale.
| **1:00–1:30** | **L – Look**: Choose a distant object, soften your gaze until edges blur (soft focus).
| **1:30–2:00** | **M – Measure**: Take one full, deliberate breath, feeling the chest rise and fall.

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **Micro‑breaks**: CALM is a structured example of a [[concepts/micro-break]] that targets breathing, posture, and visual grounding.
- **Mindfulness**: Each component encourages present‑moment awareness, a core element of [[concepts/mindfulness]].
- **Habit formation**: Pairing the routine with a consistent external trigger (e.g., the sound of a bottle warming) leverages [[concepts/habit-formation]] principles to make the practice automatic.
- **Body scan**: The final breath can be seen as a micro version of a [[concepts/body-scan]], bringing brief attention to bodily sensation.

---

## Cues & Habit Tips
- Use short, non‑cheesy cue phrases such as **“Pause here,” “Two minutes for me,”** or **“No scroll, just breathe.”**
- Anchor the start of the routine to the moment the bottle is placed in the warmer; the visual or auditory cue acts as a trigger.
- Keep the script identical each time to reduce decision fatigue, especially when sleep‑deprived.
