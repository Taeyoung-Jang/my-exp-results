# Body Scan

**Definition**: A guided mindfulness practice that moves attention systematically through the body, noticing sensations without judgment.

## Core Elements
- **Sequential focus**: Start at the feet and travel upward (or vice‑versa), giving each region a brief period of awareness.
- **Non‑judgmental noticing**: Observe sensations—temperature, pressure, tension—without trying to change them.
- **Breath anchoring**: Periodically return to the breath to reset attention, especially after the scan.

## How it appears in [[summaries/session-04_2022-06-15]]
In the June 15, 2022 conversation, the body‑scan was presented as the preferred 20‑minute morning routine.  The suggested structure was:
1. **First 5 minutes** – settle posture and notice natural breathing.
2. **Next 10 minutes** – conduct a slow body‑scan from the feet up through the legs, torso, shoulders, and face.
3. **Final 5 minutes** – return to breath awareness, noticing any ease or tension.

For restless mornings, the scan can be **speeded up** and enriched with **soft labeling** of sensations (e.g., “warm”, “tight”, “relaxed”).

## Benefits
- Helps transition from a sleepy or restless state to a more alert, present one.
- Provides a structured anchor for the mind, useful when thoughts are busy.
- Encourages a holistic sense of embodiment, improving overall mindfulness.
