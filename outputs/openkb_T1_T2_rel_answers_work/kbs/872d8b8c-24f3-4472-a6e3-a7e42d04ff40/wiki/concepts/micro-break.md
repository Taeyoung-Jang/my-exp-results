# Micro‑break

A **micro‑break** is a short, intentional pause—typically lasting from a few seconds up to two minutes—designed to give the brain and body a quick reset. The practice is rooted in *mindfulness* and can be performed anywhere, without equipment, making it ideal for busy moments like waiting for a bottle to warm.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-37_2024-06-15]])
- **Warm‑palms pause** – Rub hands together, cup them over the eyes, and take a slow inhale followed by a longer exhale.
- **Shoulder roll** – Gently roll the shoulders back and let them settle lower on the exhale.
- **Soft‑focus gaze** – Choose a distant object and let the vision blur, reducing visual strain.
- **Chest‑breath grounding** – Place a hand on the chest, feel one full breath rise and fall.
- **Weight shift** – Stand, shift weight onto the heels briefly, then return to neutral.
- **Head‑to‑toe scan** – Perform a quick [[concepts/body-scan]] by noticing tension from crown to toes and releasing a single spot.

These actions map onto the **CALM** acronym (Cup, Adjust, Look, Measure) described in the source, providing a memorable framework for rotating through micro‑breaks.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The **CALM routine** ([[concepts/calm-routine]]) structures micro‑breaks into a repeatable sequence, especially useful during predictable waiting periods.
- Consistent practice supports **habit formation** ([[concepts/habit-formation]]) by pairing the break with an external cue (e.g., the sound of a bottle warming).
- Incorporating a brief **body‑scan** ([[concepts/body-scan]]) deepens the restorative effect, turning a simple pause into a mindful moment.
- Overall, micro‑breaks are a practical expression of everyday **mindfulness** ([[concepts/mindfulness]]), helping reduce stress without requiring dedicated meditation time.

## Practical Tips
1. **Anchor to a cue** – Use a routine event (like a bottle warming) as the trigger.
2. **Keep it tiny** – Stick to the same 30‑second segments to reduce decision fatigue.
3. **Use simple language** – Cue phrases such as “Pause here” or “Two minutes for me” reinforce the habit.

By integrating these brief resets into daily life, micro‑breaks provide a low‑effort, high‑impact tool for maintaining calm and focus throughout a hectic day.
