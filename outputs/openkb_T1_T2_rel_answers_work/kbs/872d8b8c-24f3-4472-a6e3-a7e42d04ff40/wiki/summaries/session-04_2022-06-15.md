# Summary of session-04_2022-06-15

**Main contribution**: Practical advice for beginners to start and sustain a 20‑minute daily mindfulness practice, with adaptable structures for different states (e.g., restless mornings).

## Key concepts
- **Start tiny** – anchor the first breath to an existing habit (e.g., morning drink) to avoid “extra work.”
- **Micro‑check‑ins** – brief moments of awareness throughout the day reinforce the habit.
- **Chunking vs. single block** – two 10‑minute sessions or one 20‑minute block; choose what feels repeatable.
- **Basic 20‑minute structure**:
  1. 5 min: settle posture & notice natural breath.
  2. 10 min: [[concepts/body-scan]] from feet to face.
  3. 5 min: return to breath, notice ease/tension.
- **Breath‑focused alternative** – continuous breath attention, expanding to ambient sounds in the final minutes.
- **Busy‑mind adaptation** – use breath counting (1‑10) and mental labeling ("thinking", "feeling", "hearing") to give the mind a scaffold.
- **Morning habit recommendation** – the body‑scan version suits waking routines; it eases transition from sleep to alertness.
- **Restless‑morning tweak** – speed up the body‑scan, add soft labeling of sensations, and insert brief breath‑counting before resuming the scan.

## Practical takeaways
- **Tiny initiation**: One mindful breath with your morning drink.
- **Check progress**: Notice moments you previously rushed past (mood, tension, surroundings).
- **Flexibility**: Switch between chunked or single‑session formats based on schedule and preference.
- **Structure for focus**: Use a clear minute‑by‑minute outline to give the mind a scaffold, especially on busy days.
- **Labeling**: Simple tags for thoughts/sensations keep attention anchored without forcing silence.
