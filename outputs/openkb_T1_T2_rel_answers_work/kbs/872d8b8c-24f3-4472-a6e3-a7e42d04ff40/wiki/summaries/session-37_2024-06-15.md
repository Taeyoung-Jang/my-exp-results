## Document Overview
The conversation outlines practical **micro‑break** strategies for moments of only a minute or two, culminating in a compact **CALM** routine tailored for the two‑minute bottle‑warming window that many new parents face.

## Key Concepts
- **Micro‑break** – brief, quiet resets such as warm‑palms pause, shoulder roll, soft focus, chest breathing, weight shift, and head‑to‑toe scan. [[concepts/micro-break]]
- **CALM acronym** – *Cup, Adjust, Look, Measure* to remember the sequence of actions. [[concepts/calm-routine]]
- **Breathing techniques** – single slow inhale/exhale and full conscious breath.  
- **Grounding** – hand on chest, weight shift, visual softening.  
- **Habit formation** – pairing the routine with an external cue (bottle warming) and using concise cue phrases. [[concepts/habit-formation]]

## Micro‑break Ideas (1‑minute)
1. Warm‑palms pause – rub hands, cover eyes, breathe.  
2. Slow shoulder roll – exhale while dropping shoulders.  
3. Soft‑focus gaze – pick a distant object, let vision blur.  
4. Chest‑breath grounding – hand on chest, feel one breath.  
5. Heel‑to‑center weight shift – briefly balance on heels.  
6. Head‑to‑toe body scan – notice a single tension release. [[concepts/body-scan]]

## Tailored 2‑Minute Bottle‑Warming Routine
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| **0:00‑0:30** | Rub hands, cup eyes, take one slow inhale and longer exhale. |
| **0:30‑1:00** | Lower hands, perform one or two shoulder rolls, let shoulders settle lower. |
| **1:00‑1:30** | Choose a far object, soften gaze until edges blur. |
| **1:30‑2:00** | Finish with one full, intentional breath, feeling chest rise and fall. |

## Cue Phrases (non‑cheesy)
- “Pause here”
- “Two minutes for me”
- “No scroll, just breathe”

## Tips for Sticking to the Routine
- **Trigger pairing** – let the sound or sight of the bottle entering the warmer serve as the automatic start signal.  
- Keep the sequence **identical** each time to reduce decision fatigue, especially when sleep‑deprived.  
- Use a **tiny, consistent script** so the half‑asleep brain can follow without conscious planning.  

## Takeaway
A concise, repeatable two‑minute micro‑break anchored to an everyday parenting task can provide a quick mental reset, improve calmness, and become a sustainable habit even on minimal sleep.
