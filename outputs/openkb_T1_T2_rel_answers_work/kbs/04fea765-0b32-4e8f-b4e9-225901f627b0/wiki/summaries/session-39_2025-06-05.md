# Summary of session-39_2025-06-05

- **Core principle**: Shape the garage’s default behavior so that tidiness requires less effort than mess. Provide each category a **permanent home** (tools, seasonal items, sports gear) using open shelves or wall hooks, and label everything in plain language for quick identification.

- **Landing zone concept**: Create a small, intentional spot near the door for “temporary parking” of stray items. Keep it deliberately tiny so it fills quickly, acting as a cue for a rapid reset.

- **Recommended landing‑zone setup**: A shallow wall shelf equipped with a single open bin. Alternatives (narrow table, hooks + tray) work but tend to attract more items. The shelf‑plus‑bin solution is the lowest‑maintenance and preserves floor space.

- **Bin size & rules**:
  - Choose a bin roughly the size of a shoebox to a small tote—large enough for a few items, small enough to become full within a week.
  - **Rules**: place only items you’ll address in the next few days, empty the bin when it’s full, and relocate repeatedly‑appearing items to a permanent location.

- **Maintenance habit**: Perform 1–2 minute “quick tune‑ups” each time you enter the garage to prevent snowballing clutter.

- **Supporting practices**: Use clear labeling on shelves and bins, and keep categories visibly separated.

These steps collectively support effective garage organization and sustain a tidy space over the long term.
