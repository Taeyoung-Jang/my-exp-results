## Summary of session-07_2025-06-15

This conversation provides a practical framework for establishing a **sustainable [[concepts/writing-routine]]** that balances ongoing memoir drafting with revision, structure planning, and [[concepts/burnout-prevention]]. Key elements include:

- **Daily micro‑writing block** (≈30 min) to keep the narrative thread alive, even when the main focus is on revising existing material.
- **Weekly thematic rotation**:
  - **Monday & Thursday:** Tiny drafting touchpoints to maintain voice continuity.
  - **Tuesday:** Light revision – skim pages and smooth rough edges.
  - **Wednesday:** Structural/arc thinking – assess chapter order, emotional flow, and gaps.
  - **Friday:** Tone/energy check – a brief reread or mood note to gauge fatigue.
  - **Saturday (long session):** Deep shaping – larger‑scale revisions, restructuring, or stitching sections together.
  - **Sunday:** Full rest day for mental recharge.
- **Accountability** kept gentle via weekly check‑ins or simple written reflections rather than formal reporting.
- **Progress tracking** without relying on word counts, using a minimalist **session log** that records:
  - Completion status (Done/Skipped)
  - Brief notes on what was tackled (e.g., “drafted paragraph on childhood memory” or “revised chapter outline”)
  - A weekly reflection on overall energy and next‑week readiness. (See [[concepts/progress-tracking]] for the approach.)
- **Blank‑brain strategies** for days when inspiration stalls:
  - Re‑read the last paragraph and add a single sentence.
  - Write a one‑sentence summary of the next scene.
  - Free‑write for five minutes about the specific blockage.
  - Perform a quick “underlining true statements” exercise on recent text.

The template and tracker are presented in a copy‑paste‑ready format, encouraging habit formation without turning tracking into another task. The overall goal is **consistency**, **flexibility**, and **self‑care**, ensuring the memoir project progresses while the writer avoids burnout.
