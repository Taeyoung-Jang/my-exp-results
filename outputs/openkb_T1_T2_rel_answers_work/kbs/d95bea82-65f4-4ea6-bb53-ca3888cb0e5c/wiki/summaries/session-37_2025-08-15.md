## Summary
The document records a conversation where the user seeks practical, low‑effort ways to make a strong first‑week impression at a new job and asks for concise scripts for Slack and quick‑intro messages after a day of HR onboarding. The assistant advises:

- **Adopt a curious, steady approach** – focus on listening, reliability, and brief, genuine interactions rather than overt eagerness. [[concepts/first-week-onboarding-strategies]]
- **Use natural micro‑moments** (kitchen, hallway, before meetings) to say hello and gauge interest. [[concepts/relationship-building]]
- **Leverage small tasks** as opportunities for clarifying questions that foster contact. [[concepts/communication-scripts]]
- **Craft ultra‑brief messages** that reference the completed onboarding session and respect busy schedules.

### Example Scripts
- **Slack to manager**: "Hi! I just wrapped the HR onboarding sessions and wanted to check in. What should I focus on first over the next day or two?" (manager priorities)
- **Message to teammate**: "Hi! I’m just coming out of the HR onboarding gauntlet and getting settled. If you have 10–15 minutes sometime this week, I’d love a quick intro." (introductory meeting)
- **Passing‑by note**: "Hey, I’m new—just finished the onboarding sessions. Just wanted to say hi. If you have a free minute later this week, I’d love to hear what you’re working on." (brief messaging)

### Core Concepts
- **First‑week impression** – balancing enthusiasm with restraint. [[concepts/first-week-onboarding-strategies]]
- **Relationship building** – incremental, low‑pressure contact. [[concepts/relationship-building]]
- **Communication scripts** – reusable, concise wording for managers, peers, and casual encounters. [[concepts/communication-scripts]]
- **Respectful timing** – acknowledging colleagues’ workload and offering flexibility. (respectful timing)
