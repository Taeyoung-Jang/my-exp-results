# Relationship Building

**Definition**: A set of low‑pressure, genuine actions for establishing trust and rapport with new colleagues, especially during a first‑week onboarding period.

## Core Principles
1. **Micro‑moments** – Use brief encounters in the kitchen, hallway, or before meetings to say hello and show interest.  
2. **Contextual relevance** – Tie your outreach to recent events (e.g., just finished HR onboarding) to keep it natural.
3. **Respect for time** – Emphasize a short, flexible meeting length and acknowledge colleagues’ workload.
4. **Listening first** – Prioritize observing team rhythms and asking thoughtful, concise questions.

## Practical Applications from [[summaries/session-37_2025-08-15]]
- **Slack to manager** – A brief note that references completing onboarding and asks for immediate priorities.
- **Intro message to teammate** – A short request for a 10‑15 minute chat, framed as “just coming out of the HR onboarding gauntlet.”
- **Passing‑by interaction** – A quick greeting that signals openness without demanding time.

These scripts embody the **relationship‑building** approach: concise, context‑aware, and respectful of busy schedules.
