# First‑Week Onboarding Strategies

**Definition**: A set of low‑effort, relationship‑focused actions that help new hires establish credibility, build rapport, and integrate smoothly during their initial week without appearing overeager.

## Core Principles
1. **Curious, steady engagement** – Prioritize listening, reliability, and incremental learning over grand gestures. [[concepts/first-week-onboarding-strategies]]
2. **Micro‑moment outreach** – Use brief, natural encounters (kitchen, hallway, pre‑meeting) to say hello and gauge interest. [[concepts/relationship-building]]
3. **Leverage starter tasks** – Treat early assignments as conversation starters; ask concise clarifying questions. [[concepts/communication-scripts]]
4. **Respectful timing** – Acknowledge busy schedules and keep interactions brief and flexible.

## Practical Scripts (from [[summaries/session-37_2025-08-15]])
- **Slack to manager**: "Hi! I just wrapped the HR onboarding sessions and wanted to check in. What should I focus on first over the next day or two?"
- **Intro to teammate**: "Hi! I’m just coming out of the HR onboarding gauntlet and getting settled. If you have 10–15 minutes sometime this week, I’d love a quick intro."
- **Passing‑by note**: "Hey, I’m new—just finished the onboarding sessions. Just wanted to say hi. If you have a free minute later this week, I’d love to hear what you’re working on."
