# Progress Tracking

**Progress tracking** is a lightweight approach to monitoring writing activity that focuses on consistency and qualitative notes rather than quantitative metrics like word counts. It is designed to support a sustainable [[concepts/writing-routine]] while minimizing the risk of burnout, aligning with principles from [[concepts/burnout-prevention]].

## Core Elements (as outlined in [[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]])

1. **Minimalist Session Log**
   - A one‑line entry for each day indicating whether the scheduled block was completed (`Done`/`Skipped`).
   - A brief free‑form note describing what was accomplished (e.g., “drafted paragraph on childhood memory” or “light revision of chapter 3”).

2. **Weekly Reflection**
   - At the end of the week, add a short comment on overall energy, any obstacles faced, and readiness for the next week. This helps identify patterns that could lead to fatigue.

3. **No Word‑Count Emphasis**
   - The tracker avoids numeric targets, reducing pressure and keeping the focus on showing up and making incremental progress.

4. **Copy‑Paste Ready Template**
   ```markdown
   Week of ______
   Mon: Tiny draft touchpoint. Notes: ______
   Tue: Light revision. Notes: ______
   Wed: Structure/arc thinking. Notes: ______
   Thu: Tiny draft touchpoint. Notes: ______
   Fri: Tone/energy check. Notes: ______
   Sat: Long shaping session. Notes: ______
   Sun: Rest. How I feel heading into next week: ______
   ```
   This template can be placed in any note‑taking app or plain‑text file.

## Benefits

- **Consistency over volume**: Encourages daily habit formation without the stress of hitting arbitrary word targets.
- **Self‑awareness**: Regular notes surface what types of work feel most draining, informing adjustments to the [[concepts/writing-routine]].
- **Flexibility**: The format can be adapted for any writing project, from memoirs to fiction, and scales from short‑term sprints to long‑term endeavors.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- **[[concepts/writing-routine]]** – Progress tracking is a complementary tool that provides feedback on the effectiveness of a routine.
- **[[concepts/burnout-prevention]]** – By keeping tracking simple and non‑punitive, it helps writers avoid the mental fatigue associated with overly aggressive metrics.
