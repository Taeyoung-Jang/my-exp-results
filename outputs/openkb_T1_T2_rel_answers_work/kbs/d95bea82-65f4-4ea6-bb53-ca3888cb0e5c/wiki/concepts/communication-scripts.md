# Communication Scripts

**Definition**: A set of brief, adaptable message templates designed to help new employees initiate conversations, request priorities, and build rapport without overwhelming busy colleagues.

## Core Principles
- **Brevity** – Keep each script under two short sentences.
- **Contextual cue** – Mention that you’ve just completed onboarding to explain why you’re reaching out.
- **Polite framing** – Offer flexibility and respect the recipient’s time.
- **Actionable ask** – Include a clear, low‑effort request (e.g., a quick intro, a priority list).

## Example Scripts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-08-15]]
### To a manager (Slack)
> "Hi! I just wrapped the HR onboarding sessions and wanted to check in. What should I focus on first over the next day or two?"

### To a teammate (intro request)
> "Hi! I’m just coming out of the HR onboarding gauntlet and getting settled. If you have 10–15 minutes sometime this week, I’d love a quick intro."

### To someone you catch in passing
> "Hey, I’m new—just finished the onboarding sessions. Just wanted to say hi. If you have a free minute later this week, I’d love to hear what you’re working on."

## Usage Tips
1. **Tailor the tone** to match your team's communication style (formal vs. informal).
2. **Send during natural pauses** (e.g., after meetings, during lunch) to increase likelihood of a response.
3. **Iterate** – adjust phrasing based on the feedback you receive.
4. **Store** these scripts in a personal note or quick‑access document for easy reuse.
