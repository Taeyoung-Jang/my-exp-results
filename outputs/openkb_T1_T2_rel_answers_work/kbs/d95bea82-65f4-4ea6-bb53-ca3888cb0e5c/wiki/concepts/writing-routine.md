# Writing Routine

A **writing routine** is a repeatable schedule that creates a reliable habit of writing, balancing productivity with self‑care. It emphasizes short, consistent daily blocks, themed weekly rotations, gentle accountability, and low‑friction progress tracking.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-07_2025-06-15]])

1. **Daily micro‑writing block** – Typically 30 minutes at the same time each day to keep the narrative thread alive.
2. **Weekly thematic rotation** – Assigns light themes to each weekday (e.g., tiny drafting, light revision, structure/arc thinking, tone check) and reserves a longer weekend session for deeper shaping.
3. **Rest day** – A full day off (often Sunday) to allow mental recharge.

## Accountability

Accountability is kept gentle: a weekly check‑in with a friend or a brief written reflection, avoiding the pressure of formal reporting.

## Progress Tracking

Instead of word counts, a simple session log records completion status and brief notes. This aligns with the concept of [[concepts/progress-tracking]].

## Burnout Prevention

Key strategies include:
- Keeping writing blocks short and predictable.
- Building in regular rest days.
- Using low‑stakes tracking to reduce performance anxiety.
These practices tie directly to [[concepts/burnout-prevention]].

## Sample Template

A copy‑paste‑ready weekly template (see the source summary) might look like:

```
Week of ______
Mon: Tiny draft touchpoint. Notes: ______
Tue: Light revision. Notes: ______
Wed: Structure/arc thinking. Notes: ______
Thu: Tiny draft touchpoint. Notes: ______
Fri: Tone/energy check. Notes: ______
Sat: Long shaping session. Notes: ______
Sun: Rest. How I feel heading into next week: ______
```

## Benefits

- **Consistency**: Regular short sessions build momentum.
- **Flexibility**: Themes can shift based on project needs.
- **Sustainability**: Balances output with mental health, reducing risk of burnout.
