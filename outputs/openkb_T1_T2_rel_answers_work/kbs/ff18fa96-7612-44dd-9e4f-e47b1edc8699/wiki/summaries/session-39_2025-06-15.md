# Summary of session-39_2025-06-15

**Date:** 2025‑06‑15 14:30  
**Topic:** Cold‑weather survival – personal comfort, vehicle preparation, and quick ice removal.

## Key Takeaways

### Staying Warm Outdoors
- **Layering**: base layer (moisture‑wicking) → insulating mid‑layer → wind‑blocking outer layer. [[concepts/layering-for-cold-weather]]
- **Extremities**: protect hands, head, and neck to prevent major heat loss.
- **Pre‑warm core**: light activity or indoor heat before heading out reduces perceived cold.

### Car Care in Cold Temperatures
- **Engine start**: a short warm‑up (minute or two) is sufficient; avoid long idling.
- **Fuel**: keep tank at least half full to avoid fuel‑line freezing.
- **Battery**: ensure good charge; cold reduces capacity.
- **Tire pressure**: check more frequently; cold air lowers pressure.
- **Winter‑grade washer fluid**: keep topped up to stay liquid overnight. [[entities/winter-grade-washer-fluid]]

### Quick Morning Routine (5‑minute windshield‑clear)
1. Start engine, set **defroster** low/medium (front & rear).  
2. While glass warms, **brush off snow/salt** and use a **plastic scraper** on softened spots.  
3. If ice is stubborn, spray **commercial de‑icer** (lukewarm water OK, no boiling).  
4. After ice clears, finish defrosting and drive off. [[concepts/quick-windshield-deicing]]

### Night‑Before One‑Minute Checklist
- **Lift wipers** or place a **windshield cover/towel** over glass.  
- Verify **winter‑grade washer fluid** level.  
- Choose a **parking orientation** that shields the windshield from wind or faces the morning sun. *(no matching concept page, kept as plain text)*

### Emergency Car Items for Severe Cold
- Plastic scraper with brush  
- Small bottle of de‑icer spray  
- Waterproof gloves  
- Blanket  
- Portable jump‑starter pack [[entities/portable-jump-starter]]  
- Sand or **kitty litter** for traction [[entities/kitty-litter]]
