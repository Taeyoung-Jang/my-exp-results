## Vehicle Winter Maintenance

**Definition**: A set of preventive actions and quick‑response habits that keep a vehicle reliable, safe, and comfortable during cold‑weather months.

### Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]])
1. **Engine warm‑up** – Start the car and run the defroster on low/medium for a minute or two; long idling is unnecessary and wastes fuel.
2. **Fuel management** – Keep the gas tank at least half full to reduce the risk of fuel‑line freeze‑up.
3. **Battery health** – Verify charge and connections; cold temperatures dramatically lower battery capacity.
4. **Tire pressure** – Check pressure more often, as cold air contracts and can cause under‑inflation, affecting traction.
5. **Winter‑grade washer fluid** – Top up with a low‑freezing formulation (see [[entities/winter-grade-washer-fluid]]) to maintain clear visibility.
6. **Windshield protection** – Use a cover or towel and lift wipers the night before to cut down on ice buildup; see [[concepts/quick-windshield-deicing]].
7. **Emergency kit** – Include a plastic scraper, de‑icer spray, waterproof gloves, a blanket, a portable jump‑starter ([[entities/portable-jump-starter]]), and sand or kitty litter ([[entities/kitty-litter]]) for traction.

### Routine Checklist
- **Night before**: lift wipers, cover windshield, verify washer‑fluid level.
- **Morning start‑up**: start engine, set defroster low/medium, glance at tire pressure, ensure battery terminals are clean.
- **During the day**: monitor fuel level, keep an eye on tire pressure if temperature swings, and keep the emergency kit accessible.
