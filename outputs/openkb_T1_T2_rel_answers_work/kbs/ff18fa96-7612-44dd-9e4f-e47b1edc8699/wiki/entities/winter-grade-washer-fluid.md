# Winter‑grade Washer Fluid

**Type:** product

Winter‑grade washer fluid is a specially formulated windshield cleaning solution that remains liquid at freezing temperatures. It is designed to prevent the fluid from thickening or freezing in the reservoir, pump, and spray nozzles, ensuring reliable visibility for drivers in cold weather.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]]
- **Essential for night‑before prep:** The conversation highlights topping up winter‑grade washer fluid as one of the only two actions needed in a one‑minute pre‑morning checklist.
- **Prevents ice build‑up:** By staying liquid, the fluid helps melt light frost on the windshield and reduces the amount of ice that must be scraped off.
- **Complement to de‑icing routine:** When combined with a quick de‑icing spray, it speeds up ice removal without damaging glass.

## Why It Matters
Cold temperatures can cause standard washer fluids to freeze, rendering the windshield wipers ineffective and forcing drivers to scrape ice manually. Using a winter‑grade formulation mitigates these issues, saving time and reducing the risk of scratches.
