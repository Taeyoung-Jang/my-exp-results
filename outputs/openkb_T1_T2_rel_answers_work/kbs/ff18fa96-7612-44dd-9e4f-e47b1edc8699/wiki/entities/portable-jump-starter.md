# Portable Jump Starter

**Type:** product

A portable jump starter is a compact, rechargeable battery unit designed to provide the high‑current boost needed to start a vehicle when the onboard battery is too weak, especially in cold conditions.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]]
- Recommended as part of an **emergency car kit** for winter mornings when batteries often fail.
- Useful for quickly getting the engine running so the **defroster** can melt ice on the windshield.
- Should be stored in the vehicle year‑round, ready for sudden cold‑weather battery loss.

## Related Concepts
- Included in the broader **[[concepts/vehicle-winter-maintenance]]** checklist for keeping a car reliable in low temperatures.
- Supports the **[[concepts/quick-windshield-deicing]]** routine by ensuring the engine can start promptly to power the defroster.

## Related Entities
- Often stored alongside other winter supplies such as **[[entities/kitty-litter]]** for traction and **[[entities/winter-grade-washer-fluid]]** for unfrozen windshield cleaning.
