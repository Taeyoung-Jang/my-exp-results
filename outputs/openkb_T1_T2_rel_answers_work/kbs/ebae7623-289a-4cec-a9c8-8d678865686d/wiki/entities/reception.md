# Reception

**Type:** Organization

Reception serves as the main point of contact for anyone entering a building, handling inquiries, directing visitors, and providing essential information about access routes.

## Role in Accessibility
- Acts as the first line of assistance for visitors needing step‑free routes, similar to the duties of the [[entities/front-desk]] and the [[entities/accessibility-office]].
- Can confirm which entrance is closest to a public elevator, whether any stairs are present, and if the elevator is operational and not restricted by badge access.
- Offers backup suggestions when the preferred elevator is unavailable or restricted, ensuring a smooth, accessible visit.

## Interaction with Pre‑Visit Planning
- Reception staff are a key resource in the [[concepts/pre-visit-planning]] process, providing real‑time verification that complements online research such as floor plans or virtual tours.
- They help visitors execute [[concepts/building-layout-planning]] by clarifying on‑site conditions that may not be reflected in online resources.

## Reference in Documentation
- The importance of contacting Reception is highlighted in the summary of [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]], where a recommended phone and email script specifically addresses Reception as the contact point for accessibility information.
