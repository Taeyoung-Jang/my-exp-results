# Front Desk

The **Front Desk** is the staffed reception point at the entrance of many public and private buildings, responsible for greeting visitors, answering inquiries, and providing directions and access information.

## Role in [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]
In the session‑13 conversation, the Front Desk is highlighted as the most reliable source for confirming:
- The nearest step‑free entrance to a public elevator.
- Whether any stairs are present on the route.
- Current elevator operation status and any badge‑restricted access.
- Backup entrance and elevator options if the preferred route is unavailable.

## Interaction with [[concepts/accessibility-information]]
The Front Desk often serves as the liaison for accessibility concerns, directing callers to the building’s **accessibility office** or providing the needed details directly. It plays a key part in [[concepts/pre-visit-planning]] by offering up‑to‑date, human‑verified information that may not be reflected online.
