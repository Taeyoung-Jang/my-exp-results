# Accessibility Office

The Accessibility Office is a dedicated department within many public and private buildings that oversees compliance with accessibility standards and assists visitors with mobility challenges.

## Role in Session 13
In the conversation from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]], the Accessibility Office is highlighted as a primary point of contact for confirming step‑free entrances, public elevator availability, and any badge‑restricted or maintenance issues. Reaching out to this office before a visit is recommended as part of [[concepts/pre-visit-planning]].

## Typical Responsibilities
- Providing [[concepts/accessibility-information]] such as which entrances are step‑free and which elevators are publicly accessible.
- Coordinating with [[entities/front-desk]] and [[entities/reception]] staff to ensure visitors receive up‑to‑date guidance.
- Maintaining records of elevator maintenance schedules and badge‑access restrictions.
- Advising on alternative routes or backup plans when the preferred elevator is unavailable.

## How to Contact
When planning a visit, the suggested approach is to call or email the Accessibility Office directly, using a concise script that asks about entrance proximity, stair‑free routes, and elevator status. This aligns with best practices in [[concepts/building-layout-planning]] and [[concepts/pre-visit-planning]].
