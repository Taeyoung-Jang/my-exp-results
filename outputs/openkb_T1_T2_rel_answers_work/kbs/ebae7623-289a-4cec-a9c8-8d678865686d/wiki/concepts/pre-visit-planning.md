## Pre-Visit Planning

**Definition**: A systematic approach to gathering information and confirming logistics before arriving at an unfamiliar building, with a focus on step‑free routes and accessible elevators.

### Core Elements
1. **Online Research**
   - Locate floor plans, interactive maps, virtual tours, and street‑view images on the building’s website.  
   - Identify main entrances, stair locations, and elevator banks.
2. **Accessibility Information**
   - Look for dedicated accessibility or visitor‑information pages that list step‑free entrances and public elevators. 
3. **Direct Communication**
   - Contact **[[entities/front-desk]]**, **[[entities/reception]]**, or the **[[entities/accessibility-office]]** to verify:
     - The closest entrance to a public elevator.
     - Whether the route contains any stairs.
     - Current elevator status and badge restrictions.
4. **Scripted Inquiry**
   - Use a concise phone or email script (see [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]) to ask precise questions and request backup options if needed.
5. **On‑Site Confirmation**
   - Arrive early to reconfirm directions with on‑site staff and adapt to any last‑minute changes.

### Benefits
- Reduces uncertainty and travel stress.
- Ensures a fully step‑free route, avoiding inaccessible stairs.
- Provides a fallback plan if elevators are restricted or under maintenance.
