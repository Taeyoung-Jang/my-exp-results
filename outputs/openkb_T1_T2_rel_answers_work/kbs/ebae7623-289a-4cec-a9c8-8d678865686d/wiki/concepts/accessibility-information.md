# Accessibility Information

Accessibility information refers to publicly provided details about a building’s step‑free entrances, public elevators, and any restrictions (such as badge‑only access or maintenance) that affect visitors who cannot use stairs. It is essential for pre‑visit planning and ensuring a smooth, accessible experience.

## Key Elements
- **Entrances**: Identification of main doors that are wheelchair‑friendly or otherwise step‑free.
- **Elevator Access**: Confirmation that elevators are public, operational, and not behind badge‑only doors.
- **Service Status**: Up‑to‑date information on elevator maintenance or temporary closures.
- **Alternative Routes**: Backup entrance and elevator options when the primary route is unavailable.

## Sources of Accessibility Information
1. **Building Websites** – Often contain dedicated pages titled "Accessibility" or "Visitor Information" that list step‑free entrances and elevator details.
2. **Direct Contact** – Calling or emailing the [[entities/front-desk]], [[entities/reception]], or the building’s [[entities/accessibility-office]] provides the most reliable, real‑time confirmation.
3. **Online Maps & Virtual Tours** – Supplementary tools that help visualize layout but may lack current service status.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- This concept is a core component of [[concepts/building-layout-planning]], which involves understanding the overall floor plan and vertical routes.
- It supports [[concepts/pre-visit-planning]] by giving visitors actionable data to arrange a step‑free route before arrival.

## Example Application
In the session‑13 discussion (see [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]), a user was advised to locate accessibility information on the building’s website, then confirm specifics by contacting the front desk or accessibility office. Providing clear, up‑to‑date accessibility information reduces uncertainty for visitors with mobility constraints and helps them avoid barriers such as locked elevators or unexpected stairs.
