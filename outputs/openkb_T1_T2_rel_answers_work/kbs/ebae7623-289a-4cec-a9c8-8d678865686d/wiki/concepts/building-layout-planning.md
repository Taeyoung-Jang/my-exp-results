# Building Layout Planning

**Definition**: The process of gathering and synthesising information about a building’s floor plans, entrances, stairways, elevators, and other vertical routes so visitors can navigate efficiently and safely before they set foot inside.

## Core Elements
1. **Online Resources**
   - Floor plan PDFs, interactive maps, and virtual tours posted on the building’s website.
   - Street‑view and satellite imagery to locate exterior doors and signage.
   - These sources provide a high‑level view of the building layout and help identify potential entry points.

2. **Staff Communication**
   - Direct contact with the **[[entities/front-desk]]**, **[[entities/reception]]**, or an **[[entities/accessibility-office]]** to verify which entrances lead to public elevators and whether any routes are restricted or under maintenance.
   - A concise script (see [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]) ensures that all necessary accessibility questions are asked.

3. **Accessibility Considerations**
   - Identify step‑free routes from the chosen entrance to the destination floor.
   - Confirm that elevators are public, operational, and not behind badge‑only access.
   - Ask for backup entrances/elevators in case the primary option is unavailable.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- This concept is a subset of [[concepts/pre-visit-planning]], focusing specifically on spatial and vertical navigation.
- It relies heavily on [[concepts/accessibility-information]] to ensure that the route meets accessibility needs.

## Practical Workflow (derived from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]])
1. **Search the building’s website** for floor plans, maps, or virtual tours.
2. **Use street‑view tools** to visualise exterior entrances.
3. **Contact staff** (front desk, reception, or accessibility office) with a prepared script to confirm:
   - Closest entrance to a public elevator.
   - Absence of stairs on the route.
   - Current elevator service status and badge restrictions.
4. **Document the confirmed route** and note any backup options provided by staff.
5. **Arrive early** to verify directions on‑site and adjust if necessary.

By combining digital research with direct staff verification, visitors can create a reliable, step‑free navigation plan before arriving at an unfamiliar building.
