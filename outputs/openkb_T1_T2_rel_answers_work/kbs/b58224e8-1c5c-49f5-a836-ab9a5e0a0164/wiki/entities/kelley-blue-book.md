## Kelley Blue Book
Kelley Blue Book is a widely‑used product that provides market values for used and new vehicles in the United States. In the context of the *session‑04_2024‑06‑15* conversation, it is recommended as a source for checking a car’s current market value when determining whether a borrower is "upside‑down" on an auto loan and whether [[concepts/gap-insurance-evaluation]] is warranted.

### Key Points from the Session
- The user is advised to compare the loan payoff amount with the vehicle’s market value.
- Kelley Blue Book (along with Edmunds) is cited as a tool to obtain that market value.
- This comparison informs decisions about purchasing [[concepts/gap-insurance-evaluation]] and setting appropriate insurance deductibles.
