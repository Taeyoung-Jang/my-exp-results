# Department of Motor Vehicles

The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) is the government agency responsible for issuing driver licenses, registering vehicles, and maintaining vehicle records. In the context of a car sale, the DMV processes the **transfer‑of‑ownership** paperwork, records the **release‑of‑liability**, and may require the surrender of license plates.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- **Transfer‑of‑ownership form**: Sellers must complete the DMV‑required form and submit a release‑of‑liability to avoid future liability for tickets or tolls.
- **Plate surrender**: In many jurisdictions the DMV collects the plates when ownership is transferred; a receipt is provided as proof.
- **Record verification**: After the sale, owners can look up the vehicle’s record or title status on the DMV portal to confirm that they are no longer listed as the registered owner.
- **Documentation**: The DMV issues receipts for plate surrender and liability release, which should be retained for at least a year as part of [[concepts/document-retention-periods]].
