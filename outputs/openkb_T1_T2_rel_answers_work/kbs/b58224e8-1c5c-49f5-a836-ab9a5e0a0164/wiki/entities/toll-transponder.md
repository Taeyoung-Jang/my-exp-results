# Toll Transponder

**Type:** Product  

A toll transponder is an electronic device (or associated account) that automatically pays tolls when a vehicle passes a toll plaza. It is typically linked to a vehicle’s license plate or VIN.

### Relevance to vehicle‑ownership transfer
- After selling a car, the transponder account should be **closed or transferred** to avoid unwanted charges.
- The advice in [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]] places this step **after** returning plates and filing the release‑of‑liability with the [[entities/dmv]].

### Documentation
- Keep the cancellation confirmation (email or screenshot) for a few months, as suggested by [[concepts/document-retention-periods]].
