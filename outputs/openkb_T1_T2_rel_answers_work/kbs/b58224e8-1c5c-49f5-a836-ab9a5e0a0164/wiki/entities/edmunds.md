# Edmunds (Product)

Edmunds is a web‑based platform that provides vehicle pricing, specifications, and market value estimates. In the context of [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]], it is mentioned as a source for checking a car's current market value when assessing whether a loan payoff exceeds that value, a key step in evaluating the need for [[concepts/gap-insurance-evaluation]].

## Key Facts from Session 04
- Used as a reference to determine the **market value** of a car for gap‑insurance considerations.
- Suggested alongside Kelley Blue Book for comparing loan payoff amounts to vehicle worth.
- Part of the decision‑making process for **insurance coverage decisions** such as whether to add gap insurance, linking to [[concepts/insurance-coverage-decision]].

## Related Concepts and Entities
- [[concepts/gap-insurance-evaluation]] – the analysis of loan balance vs. market value.
- [[concepts/insurance-coverage-decision]] – broader framework for choosing coverages.
- [[entities/kelley-blue-book]] – another valuation tool often used in tandem.

## Relevance
Edmunds helps consumers make informed choices about auto insurance add‑ons by providing reliable price estimates that feed into deductible and coverage strategy considerations.
