# Summary of Session 04 (2024‑06‑15)

This conversation walks through **car insurance** decision‑making, focusing on the distinction between must‑have coverages and optional add‑ons, and how to tailor deductibles and extra protections to a driver’s financial situation and vehicle specifics.

## Core Coverages
- **Liability coverage** – required in most states; protects against damage or injuries you cause.  
- **Collision** and **comprehensive** – protect your own vehicle from crashes, theft, weather, etc.

## Optional Add‑Ons Discussed
- **Roadside assistance** – useful for frequent drivers; may be duplicated by credit‑card benefits.  
- **Rental reimbursement** – covers a rental car while your vehicle is in the shop; recommended when you lack a backup vehicle.  
- **Gap insurance** – bridges the difference between loan payoff amount and market value if you’re “upside‑down.”  

## Decision Criteria
| Factor | Guideline |
|--------|-----------|
| **Deductible** | Choose a level you can comfortably pay out‑of‑pocket. $1,000 is suggested for the user who can handle that amount but would struggle with $2,500. See [[concepts/deductible-strategy]]. |
| **Financial comfort** | Align premiums with the expenses you could not absorb (“risk tolerance”). |
| **Vehicle age/value** | Newer, financed cars merit collision, comprehensive, and possibly gap insurance; older cars may skip some. |
| **Loan status** | Compare current payoff balance to market value (e.g., via [[entities/kelley-blue-book]] or [[entities/edmunds]]). If payoff > value, consider gap insurance; if the gap is only a few hundred dollars, skip it. See [[concepts/gap-insurance-evaluation]]. |
| **Backup transportation** | If no secondary vehicle, rental reimbursement is a sensible add‑on. |
| **Existing roadside coverage** | Evaluate credit‑card or membership benefits before adding it to the policy. |

Overall, these factors feed into an [[concepts/insurance-coverage-decision]] process.

## Practical Recommendations for the User
1. **Deductible:** Set at about **$1,000** – balances lower premium with affordable out‑of‑pocket cost. [[concepts/deductible-strategy]]
2. **Rental reimbursement:** Choose a limit of **$30‑$40 per day** for up to **30 days** to cover typical rentals and extended repairs.
3. **Roadside assistance:** Add through the auto policy unless the credit‑card coverage proves reliable.
4. **Gap insurance:** Purchase only if the loan payoff exceeds the car’s market value by a substantial amount; a few‑hundred‑dollar gap is not worth the premium. [[concepts/gap-insurance-evaluation]]
