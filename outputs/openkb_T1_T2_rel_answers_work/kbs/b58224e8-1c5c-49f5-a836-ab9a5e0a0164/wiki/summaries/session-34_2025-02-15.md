# Summary: Car‑Sale Transfer Checklist (session‑34_2025‑02‑15)

**Key concepts**
- **Transfer‑of‑ownership process** – complete DMV/DMV‑like form, sign release‑of‑liability, surrender plates.
- **Common omissions** – forgetting to cancel/transfer insurance, neglecting toll‑transponder accounts, not retaining proof of plate surrender or liability release.
- **Task ordering** – 1) Sign/submit transfer paperwork & release‑of‑liability; 2) Return plates (if required); 3) Cancel or transfer insurance; 4) Close toll/parking accounts; 5) Preserve copies of title, bill of sale, DMV receipts, and cancellation confirmations.
- **Proof & retention** – Keep signed title/bill of sale, plate‑surrender receipt, release‑of‑liability confirmation for ≥ 1 year (or until next registration cycle). Keep insurance/toll cancellation proofs for a few months.
- **Verifying DMV update** – Look up the vehicle’s record or title status by VIN/plate to confirm you’re no longer listed as owner and that the release‑of‑liability is on file.
- **Handling “no‑insurance on file” notices** – Respond with copies of plate‑surrender receipt and liability‑release confirmation; note you no longer own the vehicle.

**Checklist**
1. **DMV paperwork**: Transfer‑of‑ownership form, release‑of‑liability, plate return. ([[entities/dmv]], [[concepts/dmv-release-of-liability]], [[concepts/vehicle-ownership-transfer]])
2. **Documentation to retain**:
   - Signed title or bill of sale
   - DMV receipt for plate surrender
   - Confirmation number/receipt for release‑of‑liability
   - Insurance cancellation email/screenshot
   - Toll‑transponder cancellation proof
3. **Retention period**:
   - Ownership documents: ≥ 1 year (or next registration cycle) ([[concepts/document-retention-periods]])
   - Cancellation proofs: few months
4. **Post‑sale verification**:
   - Query vehicle record or title status online
   - Ensure your name is removed as registered owner
5. **If DMV contacts you about missing insurance**:
   - Send proof of plate surrender and liability release
   - State you no longer own the vehicle; issue resolves.
