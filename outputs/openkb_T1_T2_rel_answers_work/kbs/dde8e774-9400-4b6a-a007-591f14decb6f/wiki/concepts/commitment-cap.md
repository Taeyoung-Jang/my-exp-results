# Commitment Cap

**Definition**: A weekly planning rule that caps the number of scheduled weeknight commitments (e.g., meetings, social plans) to a set maximum, typically two, to preserve energy and downtime.

## Core Elements
- **Cap Value**: The default cap is **two** weeknight commitments per week. A third low‑effort plan may be added only if it feels restorative.
- **Purpose**: Prevents calendar overload, protects mental and physical energy, and creates predictable breathing room.
- **Decision Support**: Works hand‑in‑hand with the [[concepts/decision-tree-commitments]] checklist (priority alignment, energy check, weekly breathing room) to decide whether a new invitation fits within the cap.
- **Weekly Structure**: Integrated into a [[concepts/weekly-schedule]] that designates specific nights for commitments, a protected open night, chores, and downtime.
- **Swap Rule**: If the protected open night (e.g., Monday) is taken, one of the commitment nights is swapped back to a rest night, keeping total “energy‑spend” nights at the capped number.
- **Decline Script**: A concise phrasing to turn down additional plans once the cap is reached: “Thanks for inviting me! I’m at my limit for weeknight plans right now, so I need to pass this time. Please keep me in the loop for future ones.”

## Benefits
- **Habit adherence**: Reinforces consistent weekly rhythms, supporting [[concepts/habit-adherence]].
- **Time blocking**: Encourages deliberate allocation of blocks for work, rest, and social activities, aligning with [[concepts/time-blocking]].
- **Energy management**: Reduces risk of burnout by ensuring regular downtime.
