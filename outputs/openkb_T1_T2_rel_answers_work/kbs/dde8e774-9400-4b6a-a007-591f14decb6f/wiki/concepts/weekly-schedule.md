# Weekly Schedule

**Definition**: A recurring structured plan that allocates academic, work, health, and leisure activities across each day of the week, while incorporating decision‑making rules to limit over‑commitment.

**Core Elements** (from the session‑06_2022-10-14 and session‑38_2024-10-14 documents):
- Fixed anchor points: class times, [[entities/campus-job]] hours, and sleep.
- Morning‑focused study blocks to leverage peak productivity.
- Integration of 8 h/week [[entities/campus-job]] within the 9 AM‑5 PM window.
- Four weekly workouts, with the option to shorten or reduce them during heavy exam periods.
- One protected free evening (typically Friday) to prevent burnout.
- Mostly open weekends, featuring a single longer study block on Saturday and a brief reset on Sunday.
- **Commitment cap**: limit of two weeknight social or meeting commitments per week, enforced by a simple rule set.
- **Decision‑tree for commitments**: a three‑question checklist (see [[concepts/decision-tree-commitments]]) to quickly evaluate new invitations.
- Designated **protected open night** (Monday) that remains free unless an urgent plan arises.
- **Chores/errands night** (Tuesday) dedicated to routine tasks, keeping them off other days.
- **Weekend downtime block**: Saturday morning (e.g., before noon) is kept free for restorative activities.
- **Swap rule**: if the protected open night is taken, one of the two commitment nights (Tuesday or Thursday) is switched back to a rest night, preserving the two‑night cap.

**Time‑Blocking**
- Employs [[concepts/time-blocking]] to assign specific subjects (A‑D) to each study slot, e.g., problem‑set work for Class A on Monday morning, readings for Class B on Monday afternoon, etc.
- In addition to academic blocks, the weekly schedule reserves fixed slots for the two allowed weeknight commitments (commonly Tuesday and Thursday) and blocks the Saturday pre‑noon downtime.

**Adaptation for Exam Weeks**
- During [[entities/midterms]] or [[entities/finals]], shift weight toward study by:
  - Replacing free evenings with extra study blocks.
  - Shortening or reducing workouts.
  - Moving job hours earlier if possible.
  - Retaining one lighter evening for recovery.
- The commitment cap can be temporarily relaxed, but the decision‑tree ensures any added plans still respect overall energy limits.

**Adherence Strategies**
- Links to [[concepts/habit-adherence]] by treating each block as a firm appointment, performing a nightly preview of the next day, and conducting a 20–30 minute weekly review/planning routine on Sunday to adjust allocations and clear distractions.
- Use the decision‑tree checklist before accepting new invites and the concise decline script ("Thanks for inviting me! I’m at my limit for weeknight plans right now, so I need to pass this time…") to maintain the cap.
