## Summary of session‑06_2022-10-14

This document provides a practical framework for an undergraduate student to design a **weekly schedule** that balances classes, study time, a [[entities/campus-job]], workouts, and downtime, and shows how to adapt it for **[[entities/midterms]]** and **[[entities/finals]]**.  Key ideas include:

- **Morning productivity**: front‑load study blocks when the student is most alert.
- **[[concepts/time-blocking]]**: explicit assignment of subjects/tasks (A‑D) to each study slot, e.g., problem‑set work for Class A on Monday morning, readings for Class B on Monday afternoon, etc.
- **Campus job integration**: 8 hours/week placed flexibly between 9 AM–5 PM, without overlapping class times.
- **Workout cadence**: four sessions per week, adjustable to shorter 20‑30 min bursts during heavy exam weeks.
- **Weekend policy**: keep weekends largely open, reserving one longer study block on Saturday and a brief reset on Sunday.
- **Midterm/Final adjustments**: shift job hours earlier, replace free evenings with extra study blocks, reduce workout frequency/intensity, but retain a protected “free” evening (typically Friday) to avoid burnout.
- **Adherence strategies**: treat study blocks as appointments, nightly planning the next day, daily check‑offs, and a **weekly review/planning routine** (20‑30 min on Sunday) to audit deadlines, allocate hours, and set the upcoming Monday plan.  These habits align with [[concepts/habit-adherence]].

### Example Schedule (Mon–Sun)
```
Mon: 07:30–08:00 wake | 08:00–10:00 study (Class A) | 10:00–12:00 class | 13:00–15:00 [[entities/campus-job]] | 17:00 workout | evening light review
Tue: 07:30–08:00 wake | 08:30–10:30 [[entities/campus-job]] | 13:00–16:00 class | 16:30–17:30 workout | evening free
Wed: 07:30–08:00 wake | 08:00–10:00 study (Class D) | 10:00–12:00 class | 13:00–15:00 [[entities/campus-job]] | evening review
Thu: 07:30–08:00 wake | 08:00–10:00 study (rotating) | 13:00–16:00 class | 17:00 workout | evening free
Fri: 07:30–08:00 wake | 08:00–10:00 [[entities/campus-job]] | 10:00–12:00 class | 13:30–15:00 study (wrap‑up) | 15:30 workout | evening fully free
Sat: 08:00–09:00 wake | 09:30–12:30 long study block (focus class) | rest of day open
Sun: 08:00–09:00 wake | 09:30–10:00 short reset (skim all classes) | 10:30‑11:00 weekly review/planning (20‑30 min) | rest of day open
```

### Adaptation for Exam Weeks
- Convert evening free slots (Tue/Thu) into **study blocks**.
- Reduce workouts to 2‑3 short sessions.
- If possible, move some job hours to earlier in the week to free afternoons for intensive study.
- Keep one lighter evening (usually Friday) for mental recovery.

### Tips for Staying on Track
- **Anchor** each day around immutable events (class times, job shifts).
- **Schedule** study blocks as calendar appointments.
- Perform a **nightly preview** of the next day’s tasks.
- Use a **weekly review** on Sunday to adjust allocations and clear distractions, supporting [[concepts/habit-adherence]].
