# Campus Job

**Type:** other

**Description:** A flexible, part‑time position on campus that the student works about eight hours per week, scheduled between 9 AM and 5 PM.

## Role in the weekly routine
- Provides a fixed commitment that must be slotted into the **[[concepts/weekly-schedule]]**.
- Hours are placed around class times and morning study blocks, e.g., Monday 13:00‑15:00, Tuesday 08:30‑10:30, Wednesday 13:00‑15:00, Thursday 08:00‑10:00, Friday 08:00‑10:00.
- During **[[entities/midterms]]** or **[[entities/finals]]**, the student may shift some job hours earlier in the week to free afternoons for extra study.

## Interaction with other elements
- The job’s scheduling is a core example of **[[concepts/time-blocking]]**, demonstrating how to allocate non‑academic obligations.
- It is one of the fixed anchors used in the **[[summaries/session-06_2022-10-14]]** to build a realistic weekly plan.
- Maintaining consistency with the job supports **[[concepts/habit-adherence]]**, as regular work shifts become habit cues that structure the day.

## Key facts from the source document
- Total weekly commitment: ~8 hours.
- Flexible windows: any time between 9 AM and 5 PM.
- Integrated with class blocks (10 AM‑12 PM) and study periods.
- Adjusted during heavy academic weeks to minimize conflict with study time.
