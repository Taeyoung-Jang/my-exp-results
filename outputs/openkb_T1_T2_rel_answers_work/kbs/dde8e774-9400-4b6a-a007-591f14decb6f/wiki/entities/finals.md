# Finals

**Type:** Event

Finals refer to the culminating examinations that occur at the end of an academic term. In the context of the *session‑06_2022-10-14* discussion, Finals are treated as a **heavy‑load week** that requires specific schedule adjustments:

- **Shift study focus:** Evening free slots (e.g., Tuesday and Thursday) are converted into extra study blocks.
- **Reduce workouts:** Workout frequency is cut to two or three short 20‑30 minute sessions.
- **Job flexibility:** Campus‑job hours are moved earlier in the week when possible to free afternoons for intensive study.
- **Protect one lighter evening:** Typically Friday evening is kept free to provide mental recovery and avoid burnout.
- **Maintain core structure:** The underlying [[concepts/weekly-schedule]] and [[concepts/time-blocking]] remain, but the proportion of “academic” blocks increases.

These adaptations echo the broader advice on [[concepts/habit-adherence]] – treating study blocks as appointments and planning ahead to keep the routine intact during demanding periods.

**Related entities and concepts**
- [[entities/midterms]] – another exam period that follows similar but less intense adjustments.
- [[entities/campus-job]] – flexible work that may be rescheduled around Finals.
- [[concepts/time-blocking]] – the method used to allocate specific subjects/tasks to each study slot.
- [[concepts/weekly-schedule]] – the overall framework that is tweaked for Finals.
