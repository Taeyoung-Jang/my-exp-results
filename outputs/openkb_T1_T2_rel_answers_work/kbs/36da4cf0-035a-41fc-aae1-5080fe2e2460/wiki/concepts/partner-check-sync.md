# Partner Check Sync

**Definition** – A short, regular check‑in between partners (often weekly) to synchronize expectations, energy levels, and social commitments for upcoming weekends.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents over‑booking by treating the couple’s shared time as a limited resource, similar to the [[concepts/weekend-shelf-space]] model.
- Aligns both people on the desired weekend vibe (low, normal, or high energy), which ties directly into the [[concepts/energy-budget-planning]] approach.
- Provides a natural moment to apply the "rules of thumb" mini‑policy from the source document, ensuring that joint and solo plans stay within agreed caps.

## Core Steps (as outlined in [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-15]])
1. **Set the vibe** – Decide early in the week (e.g., Sunday evening) whether the weekend will be low‑energy, normal, or high‑energy.
2. **Allocate slots** – Agree on the number of joint and solo plans allowed under the chosen vibe (e.g., 1 joint + 1 solo for a normal weekend).
3. **Review incoming invitations** – When an invite arrives, each partner quickly checks:
   - Do we want this?
   - Does it fit the shared plan?
4. **Communicate decisions** – Use the provided text‑message templates to respond, referencing the partner check when needed (e.g., "Let me check with my partner").
5. **Adjust if needed** – Mid‑week, revisit the plan if energy levels shift or new opportunities arise.

## Benefits
- **Clarity**: Both partners know the weekend capacity, reducing last‑minute conflicts.
- **Efficiency**: Quick decision‑making without endless negotiation for each invitation.
- **Respect**: Each person feels heard, and the relationship is treated as a shared resource rather than an excuse.
