# Privacy Considerations for Emergency Contact Communications

**Overview**

When you ask someone to be your emergency contact and later share follow‑up details, it’s important to balance usefulness with privacy. The source document [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-13]] highlights what to include in a text and what to keep out of it.

**Key Privacy Risks**
- **Sensitive identifiers** such as insurance numbers, full medical histories, or mental‑health details can be misused if a phone is compromised.
- **Location data** (exact home address, frequent travel routes) may expose you to personal safety risks.
- **Over‑sharing** can lead to identity theft or unwanted solicitations.

**What to Avoid in Texts**
- Insurance ID or policy numbers
- Detailed diagnostic or treatment history
- Mental‑health conditions or any information that could be stigmatizing
- Full legal name variations, social security numbers, or passport numbers beyond what is required for travel identification

**Minimum vs. Nice‑to‑Have Information**
The document distinguishes a *minimal* follow‑up (2–3 lines) from a *more complete* version. The minimal set aligns with the essential tier in [[concepts/emergency-contact-info-tiers]]: 
- Allergies (if any)
- Current medications (brief)
- Primary clinic or hospital name

The more complete version adds:
- Doctor’s phone number
- Preferred hospital for emergencies
- A note on who to contact for family matters

These added details are still considered “nice‑to‑have” rather than strictly necessary and should be shared only if the contact is comfortable receiving them.

**Practical Recommendations**
1. **Keep texts short** – limit content to the essential tier.
2. **Share detailed data verbally or via a secure channel** (e.g., encrypted note app) if ever needed.
3. **Ask consent** – ensure the contact understands what information they may receive.
4. **Store sensitive data** (insurance IDs, full medical records) in a protected location, not in SMS.
5. **Review periodically** – update the contact list and associated info as circumstances change.
