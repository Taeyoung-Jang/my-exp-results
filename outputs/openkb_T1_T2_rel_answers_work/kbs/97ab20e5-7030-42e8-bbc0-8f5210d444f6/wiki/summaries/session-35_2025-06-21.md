## Summary of session-35_2025-06-21

This document provides a step‑by‑step framework for taking an extended, truly *unplugged* vacation. It covers three main areas:

1. **Boundary‑setting before departure** – informing stakeholders, designating a backup contact, and creating friction to prevent accidental check‑ins.  
2. **Concrete “Friday → Weekend” checklist** – a chronological set of actions (team notice, task hand‑off, out‑of‑office setup, key logistics, and final sanity check) to ensure a clean break.  
3. **Reusable communication templates** – firmer email and Slack out‑of‑office messages, plus a fill‑in handoff note template with sections for active items, next steps, and known risks.

### Key concepts
- [[concepts/boundary_setting]]: Explicitly tell the team the exact offline dates and who to contact.  
- [[concepts/out_of_office]]: Automated messages that spell out “no checking” and provide a single backup contact.  
- [[concepts/notification_friction]]: Removing work apps, signing out, and turning off notifications to make it harder to peek.  
- [[concepts/delegation_strategy]]: Structured note for the backup person (coverage scope, document locations, urgency criteria, decision‑making permission).  
- [[concepts/launch_checklist]]: Checklist for non‑work logistics (keys, pet care, bills, deliveries) to avoid mid‑vacation interruptions.

### Actionable checklist (condensed)
- **Friday afternoon**: Announce PTO dates, share backup contact, hand off active tasks, set [[concepts/out_of_office]] messages, sign out of work apps, shut laptop.  
- **Saturday**: Confirm key handovers (house keys, pet sitters), disable/delete any remaining work apps, settle personal errands.  
- **Sunday**: Final OOO review, confirm backup’s readiness, mental reset.

### Templates provided
- **Email OOO**: “I’m out on extended PTO and completely offline until **[date]**. I will not be checking email. For urgent matters contact **[name]** at **[email]**.”  
- **Slack OOO**: “I’m on PTO and fully offline until **[date]**. I won’t see [[entities/slack]] messages. Urgent issues: **[name]**.”  
- **Handoff note template** with placeholders for dates, folder links, active items, next steps, and known risks, plus explicit permission for the backup to make decisions.

The material equips the user to **pre‑emptively eliminate work interruptions**, delegate responsibilities confidently, and enjoy a truly restorative break.
