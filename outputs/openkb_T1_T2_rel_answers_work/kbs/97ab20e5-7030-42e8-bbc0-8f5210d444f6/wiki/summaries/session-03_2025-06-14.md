# Summary of session-03_2025-06-14

**Document type:** Conversation transcript – marketing campaign workflow advice

## Core Idea
A compact, repeatable workflow for planning, executing, and reviewing marketing campaigns that minimizes last‑minute scramble. The advice is broken into phases, timelines (4‑week and 6‑week), delegation tips, a “must‑do” minimal version, a launch‑packet template, and a kickoff message.

## Phased Checklist & Timeline
- **Planning phase** – brief, goals, audience, offer, channels, budget, metrics; timeline lock; responsibilities.
- **Build/Production phase** – creative assets, landing pages, tracking, approvals.
- **Launch phase** – final QA, go‑live, early monitoring.
- **Post‑mortem phase** – results vs. goals, lessons learned.

### 4‑Week Runway (owner per step)
| Week | Owner(s) | Key Activities |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Product Marketing (PMM) | Finalize brief, align goals, audience, offer, channels |
| 2 | Creative, Demand Gen, Web/Dev | Draft concepts & copy, build channel plan, start landing‑page & tracking set‑up |
| 3 | Creative, Demand Gen, Web/Dev, Analytics | Produce final assets, configure campaigns, finish pages, validate tracking |
| 4 | Demand Gen, PMM, Analytics | QA checklist, approvals, launch, monitor early data |

### 6‑Week Runway (owner per step) 
- Week 1 – PMM drafts brief  
- Week 2 – Cross‑team alignment (PMM, Demand Gen, Creative, Web/Dev, Analytics)  
- Week 3 – Early creative & heavy‑lift web work  
- Week 4 – Final asset production & full channel setup  
- Week 5 – Dedicated QA, approvals, soft tests  
- Week 6 – Launch, monitoring, early optimization  

## Top 5 “Don’t Skip” QA Checks (pre‑launch) 
1. Verify every link and UTM parameter  
2. Confirm conversion tracking fires correctly  
3. Ensure creative versions match approved assets  
4. Check audience targeting and budgets per platform  
5. Test landing page load & functionality on desktop **and** mobile  

## Minimum “Must‑Do” Workflow (for a busy PMM) 
- **Own**: tight brief (goals, audience, offer, channels, metric) and final approval of core message & top creative concept.  
- **Delegate**: asset creation, campaign build, tracking set‑up, budgets, targeting, version control.  
- **Personal Review before launch**: final landing‑page, one exemplar ad per channel, tracking validation summary.  
- **Personal QA**: links, tracking, mobile landing‑page load.  

## Launch Packet Template (one‑page) 
```
**Campaign Name**
Brief: goal, audience, core message/offer, channels, success metric

**Key Dates**
- Brief finalized: ___
- Assets due: ___
- Build/QA window: ___
- Launch date: ___
- Post‑launch readout: ___

**Owners**
- Product Marketing – brief & message
- Demand Gen – channel setup & launch
- Creative – asset production
- Web/Dev – landing page & tracking
- Analytics – measurement & reporting

**Links**
- Brief: [link]
- Creative folder: [link]
- Landing page preview: [link]
- Tracking plan: [link]
- Build sheet: [link]

**Launch‑Day Checklist**
- ✅ Links & UTMs work
- ✅ Tracking tests pass
- ✅ Landing page loads on desktop & mobile
- ✅ Creative approved
- ✅ Audiences & budgets confirmed
- ✅ Analytics data flowing
- **Go‑live owner & time**: ___
```

## Kickoff Message (Slack/Email) 
```
Hi team — sharing the launch packet for our upcoming campaign: [link].
The brief is final, timelines are locked, and roles are noted inside.
Creative can begin production immediately, web/dev can start on the page & tagging, and demand gen can start building channels.
Analytics, please set up tracking based on the success metric listed.
Let’s aim to have everything ready for QA by the date shown. Ping me if anything looks unclear.
Thanks all — excited to get this one out the door.
```
