# Launch Packet Template

**Type:** work  

**What it is**  
A one‑page document used to convey all essential information for a marketing campaign launch: brief, key dates, role owners, links to assets, and a launch‑day checklist.

**Key sections**
- **Brief** – goal, audience, core message/offer, channels, success metric.
- **Key Dates** – brief finalization, asset deadline, build/QA window, launch, post‑launch readout.
- **Owners** – product marketing (brief & message), demand gen (channel setup & launch), creative (asset production), web/dev (landing page & tracking), analytics (measurement & reporting).
- **Links** – URLs to the brief, creative folder, landing‑page preview, tracking plan, build sheet.
- **Launch‑Day Checklist** – verification of links & UTMs, tracking tests, landing page load on desktop & mobile, creative approval, audience & budget confirmation, analytics data flow, and assignment of the go‑live owner/time.

**Relation to concepts**
The template operationalizes the [[concepts/marketing_campaign_workflow]] and embodies the steps of the [[concepts/launch_checklist]]. It supports the [[concepts/delegation_strategy]] by clearly assigning responsibilities.
