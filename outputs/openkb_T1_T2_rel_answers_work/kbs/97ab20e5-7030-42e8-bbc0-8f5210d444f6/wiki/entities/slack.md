# Slack (product)

Slack is a cloud‑based team messaging and collaboration platform. In the context of the [[summaries/session-35_2025-06-21]] conversation, Slack is referenced as the primary channel for work communication that the user plans to disconnect from during an extended PTO.

## Key points from the document
- **Out‑of‑office (OOO) template** – The user is provided a firm Slack OOO message that states they will be fully offline and directs all urgent matters to a backup contact. [[concepts/out_of_office]]
- **Notification friction** – The guidance recommends signing out of Slack on all devices, removing the app, or turning off notifications to make it harder to peek at messages while on vacation. [[concepts/notification_friction]]
- **Boundary setting** – Slack is mentioned as one of the work tools that must be explicitly excluded from the “unplugged” period, reinforcing the broader boundary‑setting strategy. [[concepts/boundary_setting]]
