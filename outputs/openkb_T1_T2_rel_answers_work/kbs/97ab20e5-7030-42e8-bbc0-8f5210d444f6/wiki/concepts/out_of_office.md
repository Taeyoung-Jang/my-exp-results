# Out‑of‑Office Messaging

**Definition**: An out‑of‑office (OOO) message is a pre‑written notification placed on email, Slack, or other communication channels that clearly states the sender will be unavailable for a defined period, provides a backup contact, and sets expectations that no replies will be read or sent during that time.

---

## Why It Matters

A well‑crafted OOO message is a core element of [[concepts/boundary_setting]]. It creates an explicit boundary with colleagues, clients, and external partners, reducing the risk of “quick question” interruptions. When combined with [[concepts/notification_friction]]—removing work apps or signing out—the message reinforces the friction that protects the vacation period.

---

## Core Components (derived from [[summaries/session-35_2025-06-21]])

1. **Unavailability statement** – State that you are completely offline and will not check messages.
2. **Date range** – Provide the exact return date.
3. **Backup contact** – Name, role, and contact details of the person covering for you.
4. **Firm language** – Use decisive wording (e.g., “I will not be checking” rather than “I might be slow to respond”).
5. **Urgency filter** – Clarify that only the backup contact should be used for urgent or time‑sensitive matters.

---

## Example Templates (from the session‑35 guidance)

### Email OOO
```
I’m out on extended PTO and completely offline until **[return date]**. I will not be checking or responding to email during this time. For anything urgent or time‑sensitive, please contact **[backup name]** at **[backup email]**. Messages sent to me before I return will be read after I’m back.
```

### Slack OOO
```
I’m on PTO and fully offline until **[return date]**. I won’t see or respond to Slack messages while I’m away. For anything that can’t wait, please reach out to **[backup name]**.
```

---

## Best Practices

- **Set OOO before the final hand‑off** – Activate the message after notifying the team and handing off tasks.
- **Synchronize across platforms** – Apply the same wording to email, Slack, and any automated chat responders.
- **Link to backup** – Include the backup contact’s full name, role, and direct email/Slack handle.
- **Stay consistent** – Use the same tone and structure for all OOO messages to avoid confusion.
- **Combine with friction tactics** – Log out of work apps, delete them from the phone, or use [[concepts/notification_friction]] to make it harder to peek.
