# Notification Friction

**Definition**: Notification friction is the practice of creating intentional obstacles that make it harder to access work‑related tools or messages during a planned break, thereby reducing the chance of accidental engagement.

---

## Why It Matters
When someone is on extended PTO, the most common way they get pulled back into work is through a quick glance at a notification. By increasing the effort required to view or respond to work communications, the brain’s default to “just one quick check” is interrupted, supporting a true period of rest.

---

## Core Elements (as described in [[summaries/session-35_2025-06-21]])
1. **Sign out of all work accounts** – Log out of email, Slack, and any project‑management tools on every device.
2. **Remove or disable apps** – Delete work apps from the phone or disable push notifications.
3. **Create physical barriers** – Turn off the laptop, store work devices in a drawer, or use a separate “offline” phone.
4. **Use out‑of‑office messages** – Combine with [[concepts/out_of_office]] to set expectations that you will not be checking messages.
5. **Turn off sync services** – Stop automatic email or calendar sync so new messages do not appear unexpectedly.

---

## How to Implement Notification Friction (step‑by‑step checklist)
- **Before the day off**
  - Open each work app and log out.
  - Delete the app from the phone or disable its notifications (iOS/Android settings → Notifications → Off).
  - Set a strong password on the device and store it away.
- **During the break**
  - Keep devices in a separate location, out of sight.
  - If you must keep a device for personal use, use a distinct profile that does not have work accounts added.
- **When you return**
  - Re‑install or re‑enable apps, log back in, and clear any backlog with the help of your backup contact.

## Benefits
- **Reduced cognitive load** – No need to constantly decide whether to peek at a message.
- **Clearer boundaries** – Teams see that you are truly offline, which improves respect for your time.
- **Higher quality rest** – Less mental interruption supports better recharge.
