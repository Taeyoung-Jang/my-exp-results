# Launch Checklist

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A concise pre‑launch QA set of items that ensure campaign readiness.

---

The **Launch Checklist** is a focused, repeatable set of validation steps that should be completed immediately before a marketing campaign goes live.  It is designed to catch the most common errors that can cause a campaign to fail or under‑perform, while keeping the process lightweight enough for busy product‑marketing managers.

## Core Elements (as outlined in [[summaries/session-03_2025-06-14]])
1. **Link & UTM verification** – Confirm that every URL, tracking parameter, and UTM tag points to the correct destination and is free of typos.
2. **Conversion tracking validation** – Use a test transaction or event to ensure that the analytics platform records conversions correctly.
3. **Creative approval match** – Check that the live assets (ads, emails, social posts) exactly match the versions approved in the brief.
4. **Audience & budget confirmation** – Review targeting settings, audience lists, and budget allocations across all paid platforms.
5. **Landing‑page performance check** – Load the landing page on both desktop and mobile devices, verify page speed, and confirm all interactive elements function.

## How it fits into the broader workflow
The checklist sits at the transition between the **build/production phase** and the **launch phase** of the [[concepts/marketing_campaign_workflow]].  It acts as the final gate before the "go‑live" button is pressed, and its results are typically documented in the **launch packet** (see [[entities/launch_packet_template]]).

## Usage Guidance
- **Timing:** Run the checklist no later than a few hours before the scheduled launch time.  For multi‑day launches, repeat the checklist at the start of each day.
- **Ownership:** The primary owner is usually the demand‑gen lead, with sign‑offs from product marketing (for brief alignment) and analytics (for tracking confirmation).
- **Documentation:** Capture a simple sign‑off sheet (e.g., a checkbox list in the launch packet) that records who performed each check and the outcome.
