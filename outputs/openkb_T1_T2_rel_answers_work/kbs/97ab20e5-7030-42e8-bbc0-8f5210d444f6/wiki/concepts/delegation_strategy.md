# Delegation Strategy for Campaign Execution

**Definition**: A set of principles and role assignments that ensure critical campaign tasks are owned by the right team members, reducing bottlenecks and allowing senior marketers to focus on high‑impact decisions.

## Overview
The strategy originates from the advice given in [[summaries/session-03_2025-06-14]], where a busy product marketer needed a lean workflow for back‑to‑back launches. By clearly delegating routine but essential activities, the marketer can stay hands‑off while still safeguarding quality and data integrity.

## Core Principles
1. **Separate strategic ownership from tactical execution** – the product marketer (or PMM) retains control of the brief, core message, and final data validation.
2. **Assign production tasks to specialists** – creative, demand generation, web/dev, and analytics each own their deliverables.
3. **Limit personal review to three high‑impact items** – final landing page, a representative ad per channel, and the tracking validation summary.
4. **Use a concise launch‑day checklist** to verify that delegated work meets standards before go‑live.

## Delegation Matrix (Typical Roles)
| Phase | Owner | Key Responsibilities |
|-------|-------|----------------------|
| **Brief & Strategy** | Product Marketing (PMM) | Write tight brief (goals, audience, offer, channels, metric) |
| **Creative Production** | Creative Lead | Develop concepts, copy, final assets |
| **Channel Setup** | Demand Generation | Build campaigns in‑platform, set budgets, define audiences |
| **Landing Pages & Tracking** | Web/Dev | Build pages, implement tags, ensure UTM consistency |
| **Measurement Configuration** | Analytics | Configure conversion tracking, create reporting dashboards |
| **Final QA & Approvals** | Demand Gen + Creative Lead + Analytics | Run the [[concepts/launch_checklist]]; PMM reviews only the three items above |

## Minimum "Must‑Do" Workflow for a Busy PMM
1. **Own the brief** – keep it concise and circulate immediately.
2. **Delegate** all asset creation, campaign builds, and tracking implementation.
3. **Approve** only the core message and top‑level creative concept.
4. **Personal Review (pre‑launch)**: final landing page, one exemplar ad per channel, tracking validation summary.
5. **Personal QA**: verify links, confirm tracking fires, test mobile landing‑page load.

## Benefits
- **Reduced context‑switching** for senior marketers.
- **Clear accountability** across functional teams.
- **Faster turnaround** for multiple concurrent launches.
- **Higher quality** through specialist ownership and a focused final review.
