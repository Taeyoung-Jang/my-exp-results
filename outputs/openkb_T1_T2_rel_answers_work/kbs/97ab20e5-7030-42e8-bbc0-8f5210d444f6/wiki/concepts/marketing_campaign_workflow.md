# Marketing Campaign Workflow

**Type:** Concept

## Overview
The Marketing Campaign Workflow is a structured, repeatable methodology that guides teams from initial brief through launch and post‑mortem. It is designed to reduce last‑minute scramble by clearly defining phases, owners, timelines, and essential quality‑assurance (QA) checks.

## Core Phases
1. **Planning** – Create a concise brief covering goal, audience, offer, channels, budget, and success metrics. Align stakeholders and lock the overall timeline.
2. **Build / Production** – Creative produces assets, web/dev builds landing pages and tracking, demand gen configures campaigns, and analytics sets up measurement.
3. **Launch** – Execute a final QA checklist, go‑live, and monitor early performance.
4. **Post‑mortem** – Compare results to goals, document lessons learned, and archive the findings for future reuse.

## Timeline Options
### 4‑Week Runway
| Week | Primary Owner(s) | Key Activities |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Product Marketing (PMM) | Finalize brief, align goals & audience |
| 2 | Creative, Demand Gen, Web/Dev | Draft concepts, channel plan, begin landing‑page & tracking setup |
| 3 | Creative, Demand Gen, Web/Dev, Analytics | Produce final assets, configure campaigns, validate tracking |
| 4 | Demand Gen, PMM, Analytics | QA, approvals, launch, early monitoring |

### 6‑Week Runaway
- **Week 1:** PMM drafts brief
- **Week 2:** Cross‑team alignment (PMM, Demand Gen, Creative, Web/Dev, Analytics)
- **Week 3:** Early creative & heavy web builds
- **Week 4:** Final asset production & full channel setup
- **Week 5:** Dedicated QA, approvals, soft tests
- **Week 6:** Launch, monitoring, early optimization

## Essential Pre‑Launch QA Checks
1. Verify every link and UTM parameter
2. Confirm conversion tracking fires correctly
3. Ensure creative versions match approved assets
4. Check audience targeting and budgets per platform
5. Test landing page load & functionality on desktop **and** mobile

These checks are captured in the broader [[concepts/launch_checklist]].

## Delegation Strategy
To keep senior marketers focused on high‑impact decisions, the workflow adopts a delegation model where:
- The brief and core message are owned by **Product Marketing**
- Asset creation, campaign builds, and tracking setup are delegated to **Creative**, **Demand Gen**, and **Web/Dev** respectively
- **Analytics** handles metric configuration and validation
- Final approvals (message, top‑level creative) and the three critical QA items are personally reviewed by the PMM

The delegation approach aligns with the [[concepts/delegation_strategy]] concept.

## Launch Packet Template
A one‑page “launch packet” standardizes all necessary information (brief, key dates, owners, asset links, launch‑day checklist). The template entity is documented as [[entities/launch_packet_template]].
