# Summary of session-04_2023-06-15

**Main focus**: Providing practical communication templates and strategies for someone who carries ongoing care responsibilities and needs to set boundaries around trip planning and future commitments.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/boundary_setting]]** – Framing limits as flexibility rather than excuses.  
- **[[concepts/communication_strategies]]** – Tailoring messages for different audiences (close friend, group chat, family member).  
- **[[concepts/commitment_flexibility]]** – Using “tentative yes” language and offering alternatives.  
- Care responsibilities – highlighting the impact of caregiving on ability to commit.  
- Alternative plans – suggesting smaller or partial participation options.  
- Confirmation deadlines – setting realistic dates for confirming attendance (2‑3 weeks for weekend trips, 4‑6 weeks for longer trips).  
- Deposit handling – managing deposit expectations, including non‑refundable commitments.  
- Short one‑liners – quick responses for on‑the‑spot pressure.

## Message Templates
1. **Close friend** – Emphasizes personal reassurance and flexibility.  
2. **Group chat** – Includes a clear confirmation deadline (e.g., “I’ll confirm by [date]”).  
3. **Family member** – Provides a “pencil‑in” approach while acknowledging unpredictability.  
4. **Alternative‑offer version** – Proposes a smaller hangout, local meet‑up, or partial participation.  
5. **Deposit‑deadline texts**  
   - *Graceful bow‑out*: Declines due to deposit timing.  
   - *Non‑refundable deposit*: Pays share but warns of possible cancellation.

## Practical Guidance
- **Choosing confirmation dates**: 2‑3 weeks before a weekend trip; 4‑6 weeks for longer trips.  
- **Handling deposits**: Communicate inability to meet deposit deadlines or willingness to pay with a disclaimer about potential non‑attendance.  
- **One‑liners for pressure**: “I’m not able to lock anything in yet, but I’ll let you know if it opens up.” and “I’d love to if I can, but I can’t commit that far ahead right now.”

This summary captures the document’s core advice on respectful, clear communication while managing personal caregiving constraints.
