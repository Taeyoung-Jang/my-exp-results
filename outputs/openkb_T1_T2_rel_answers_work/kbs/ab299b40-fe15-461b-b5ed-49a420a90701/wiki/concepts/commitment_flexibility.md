# Commitment Flexibility

**Definition**: A communication approach that frames agreements as provisional or adjustable, allowing the speaker to honor caregiving duties while remaining open to participation.

## Core Elements
- **Tentative Yes** – Expressing interest without a firm promise, e.g., "I’ll let you know by [date]".
- **Alternative Options** – Offering smaller or partial involvement (local meet‑up, joining for part of an event).
- **Clear Deadlines** – Providing a specific confirmation date so others can plan without indefinite waiting.
- **Boundary Framing** – Positioning flexibility as a need for "room" rather than an excuse.

## Application in Session 04 (2023‑06‑15)
The source document provides practical message templates that embody commitment flexibility:
- A group‑chat message that includes a concrete "confirm by" date (2‑3 weeks for weekend trips, 4‑6 weeks for longer trips).
- Short one‑liners for on‑the‑spot pressure, keeping responses open‑ended yet polite.
- Deposit‑handling texts that either bow out or accept a non‑refundable deposit while still leaving room for last‑minute changes.

These examples show how commitment flexibility helps balance personal caregiving constraints with social expectations.
