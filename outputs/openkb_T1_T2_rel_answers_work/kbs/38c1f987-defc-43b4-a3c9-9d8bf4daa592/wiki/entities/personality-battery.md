# Personality Battery

**Type:** product

**Description:** The Personality Battery is a standardized set of personality‑assessment items, often administered as a forced‑choice questionnaire, that clinicians use to evaluate psychological traits for occupational health clearance.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]**:
- The battery includes many **forced‑choice items** that ask respondents to choose between two statements reflecting typical behavior.
- The user described a situation where a psychologist from the [[entities/occupational-health-department]] walked them through the scoring and clarified wording, causing some sections of the battery to "flip".
- The battery aims to capture *average* rather than *ideal* behavior, emphasizing the importance of interpreting frequency words (e.g., "usually", "more often") and distinguishing between work and home contexts.
- Guidance for answering the battery includes a quick mental **checklist** that focuses on timeframe, frequency, context, and literal wording, linking to the concept [[concepts/checklist-for-forced-choice]].
- Understanding the battery’s structure helps users avoid misinterpretation and over‑thinking, supporting more accurate self‑reporting on personality traits.
