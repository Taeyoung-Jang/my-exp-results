# Wine (product)

Wine is an alcoholic beverage produced by fermenting grape juice, often used in cooking to add acidity, depth, and a touch of sweetness.

## Culinary role
In the context of **session‑05_2024‑06‑15**, wine is mentioned as a possible component of a quick deglaze. A small splash of wine can brighten a dish after the browned fond is lifted, without making the final flavor overly sour.

## Typical use in the documented technique
- Added after spices have bloomed and the aromatics are softened.
- Combined with broth (or water) for a balanced deglaze that reduces the pan temperature and prevents garlic from burning.
- Used sparingly to avoid overpowering the base flavors built from [[entities/onion]], [[entities/garlic]], and [[concepts/foundation-aromatics]].
