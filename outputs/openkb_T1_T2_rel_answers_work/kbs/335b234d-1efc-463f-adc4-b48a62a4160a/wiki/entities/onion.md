# Onion

Onion is a widely used bulb vegetable that forms the core of the **foundation aromatics** in many dishes. In the session‑05_2024-06-15 cooking guide, onion is highlighted as the first ingredient to heat in oil or butter, cooked on medium heat for **5–7 minutes** until translucent and lightly golden. Early salting is recommended to draw out moisture and accelerate softening.

Key points from the document:
- Start the sauté with onion alone; it provides the base flavor before any garlic or spices are added.
- Cook onion for several minutes until just beginning to turn golden; this creates a sweet, mellow background.
- After the onion is softened, **garlic** is added for a brief 30‑60 seconds, then spices are **bloomed** (see [[concepts/blooming-spices]]).
- The onion‑centric base can be deglazed with broth, water, or a mild acid‑balanced liquid (see [[concepts/deglazing]]).
- Variations may include adding [[entities/tomato-paste]] or [[entities/soy-sauce]] after the onion and garlic stage, followed by a quick deglaze.
- A finishing splash of [[entities/lemon]] or [[entities/vinegar]] can brighten the dish without making it sour.
