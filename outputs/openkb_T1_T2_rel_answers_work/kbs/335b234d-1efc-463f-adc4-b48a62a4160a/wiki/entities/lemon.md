# Lemon

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A tart citrus fruit that provides acidity and brightness to dishes.

### Key points from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]
- Added as a **tiny squeeze** after deglazing to give extra brightness without making the dish sour.
- Can be mixed with [[entities/broth]] for a mild, balanced deglaze.
- Recommended as a small splash when you want a hint of citrus acidity in a savory base.

### Related concepts
- [[concepts/deglazing]] – the technique where lemon is used for a gentle acid lift.

### Related entities
- [[entities/onion]] – often part of the foundational aromatic base that lemon finishes.
- [[entities/garlic]] – another aromatic that pairs with lemon in the same sauté steps.
