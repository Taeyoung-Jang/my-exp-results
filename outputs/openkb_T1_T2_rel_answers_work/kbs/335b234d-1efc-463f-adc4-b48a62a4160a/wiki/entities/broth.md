# Broth

**Type:** product

## Overview
Broth is a flavorful liquid made by simmering meat, bones, vegetables, or herbs in water. It serves as a versatile cooking medium, adding depth and moisture to dishes without the heaviness of cream or the intensity of stock.

## Role in [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]
In the cooking technique described in the session, broth is recommended as the primary **deglazing** liquid. It:
- Loosens browned bits (fond) from the pan with minimal impact on overall flavor.
- Provides a subtle savory backdrop that complements the aromatic foundation of onions and garlic.
- Can be combined with a tiny splash of [[entities/lemon]] or a dash of [[entities/wine]] for brightness without making the dish sour.

## Practical Tips
- Use low‑sodium broth to keep seasoning under control.
- Warm the broth before adding it to the pan to maintain temperature and prevent splattering.
- A small amount (a few tablespoons) is sufficient to lift the fond and create a cohesive sauce.
