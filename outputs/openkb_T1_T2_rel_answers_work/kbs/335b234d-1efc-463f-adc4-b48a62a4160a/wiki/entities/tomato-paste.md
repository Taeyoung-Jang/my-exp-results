## Tomato Paste

Tomato paste is a thick, concentrated tomato product made by cooking down tomatoes to remove water and intensify flavor. It is commonly used as a flavor‑building ingredient in a variety of cuisines.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]**
- In the cooking guide, tomato paste is added after the onions and garlic have softened and before deglazing.
- It is cooked for about 1–2 minutes on medium heat to darken slightly and develop a sweeter aroma.
- The paste helps deepen the base flavor without making the dish sour.
