# Soy Sauce

Soy sauce is a fermented liquid condiment made from soybeans, wheat, salt, and water. It provides a salty, umami depth that can quickly lift a dish.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]
- Used as a **variation** in the basic aromatic base technique.
- Added **after the spices have bloomed** (see [[concepts/blooming-spices]]) for about 15 seconds to coat the ingredients.
- Followed immediately by **deglazing** (see [[concepts/deglazing]]) with broth or water, which stops the soy from scorching and integrates its flavor.
- Works well alongside other [[concepts/foundation-aromatics]] like [[entities/onion]] and [[entities/garlic]].

## Typical usage in the session
1. Sauté [[entities/onion]] (and optional aromatics) until translucent.
2. Add [[entities/garlic]] and bloom spices.
3. Stir in a teaspoon of soy sauce, let it **bubble for ~15 seconds**.
4. **Deglaze** with a splash of [[entities/broth]] (or water) to capture the fond and keep the sauce from burning.
