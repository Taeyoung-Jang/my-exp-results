# Vinegar

**Type:** Product

**Description:** Vinegar is an acidic liquid condiment made from fermented ethanol, commonly used in cooking to add brightness and to deglaze pans.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]:**
- Suggested as a quick deglaze additive for a mild acidic lift without making the dish overly sour.
- Recommended as a tiny squeeze at the end of cooking to tighten flavors, alongside lemon.
- Works with the [[concepts/deglazing]] technique to loosen browned bits while providing subtle acidity.
