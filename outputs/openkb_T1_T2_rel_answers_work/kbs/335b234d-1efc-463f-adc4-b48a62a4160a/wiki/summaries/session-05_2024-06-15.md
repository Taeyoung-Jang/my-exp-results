## Summary of session-05_2024-06-15

**Main contribution**: A concise, step‑by‑step method for creating a rich, savory base in everyday cooking using simple aromatics, proper spice “blooming,” timed garlic addition, and quick deglazing, plus two common variations.

### Key Concepts
- **Foundation aromatics** – onions (or shallots) plus garlic, with optional carrots, celery, or ginger depending on cuisine. [[concepts/foundation-aromatics]]
- **Blooming spices** – briefly toasting ground spices in fat for 20–40 seconds to release aroma. [[concepts/blooming-spices]]
- **Garlic timing** – add garlic after onions have softened, cook 30‑60 seconds, then immediately add spices to avoid bitterness. 
- **Deglazing** – use a splash of broth, water, or a mild acid‑balanced liquid to loosen browned bits without adding sourness. [[concepts/deglazing]]
- **Flavor‑building technique** – layering aromatics, spice bloom, and moisture to create depth with minimal effort. 

### Practical Sauté Blueprint (Medium Heat)
1. Heat oil until warm.
2. Add [[entities/onion]]; sauté 5–7 min until translucent and lightly golden.
3. Add [[entities/garlic]]; cook 30–60 sec until fragrant.
4. Add any ground spices; bloom 20–40 sec. [[concepts/blooming-spices]]
5. **Basic deglaze**: pour a small amount of [[entities/broth]] or water; stir to lift fond. [[concepts/deglazing]]

### Variation 1 – Tomato Paste
- After step 3, stir in 1 tsp [[entities/tomato-paste]].
- Cook 1–2 min (darken slightly, sweeten).
- Deglaze as in step 5.

### Variation 2 – Soy Sauce
- Follow steps 1‑4.
- Add 1‑2 tsp [[entities/soy-sauce]] after spice bloom; let bubble 15 sec.
- Deglaze immediately.

### Tips & Tricks
- **Salt early** on onions to draw moisture and soften faster.
- **Buffer garlic** by letting onions act as a heat shield or adding a splash of liquid as soon as garlic turns pale gold.
- **Acid finish** – a tiny squeeze of [[entities/lemon]] or dash of [[entities/vinegar]] after deglazing brightens the dish without making it sour.

These guidelines give a repeatable framework for a flavorful base that can be adapted to many cuisines and sauces.
