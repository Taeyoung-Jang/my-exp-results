## Foundation Aromatics

**Definition**: A set of basic, widely available aromatics—primarily onion (or shallots) and garlic, optionally supported by carrots, celery, or ginger—that form the flavor foundation of many everyday dishes.

**Key Points from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]]**
- Start with **[[entities/onion]]** in a bit of oil or butter, cooking 5–7 minutes on medium heat until softened and lightly golden.
- Add **[[entities/garlic]]** after the onions have begun to soften; cook 30–60 seconds just until fragrant to avoid bitterness.
- Optional secondary aromatics (carrots, celery, ginger) can be introduced with the onions for added depth, depending on the cuisine.
- Salt is added early to the onions to draw moisture and speed softening.

**Related Concepts**
- [[concepts/blooming-spices]] – the next flavor‑building step after the aromatics are in the pan.
- [[concepts/deglazing]] – the technique used after the aromatics and spices have developed flavor, to lift the fond.

**Practical Usage**
1. Heat oil over medium heat.
2. Add onion (or shallot) and optional carrots/celery/ginger; sauté 5‑7 min.
3. Add garlic; sauté 30‑60 sec.
4. Proceed to bloom spices or deglaze as the recipe calls for.

This concept underlies many sauté, stir‑fry, and sauce bases, providing a simple, reliable route to richer, savory dishes.
