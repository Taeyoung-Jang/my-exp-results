# Toll Tag

A toll tag is an electronic transponder that attaches to a vehicle and enables automatic toll payment when passing through toll plazas.

## Key facts from session‑33 (2024‑06‑15)
- Use consistent personal details across the account, as advised in [[concepts/consistent_user_info]].
- Store login credentials securely with a [[concepts/password_manager_storage]] solution.
- Enable [[concepts/auto_reload]] or auto‑pay to keep the balance topped up.
- Provide only essential vehicle information—license plate, state, make, model, year—as outlined in [[concepts/vehicle_info_requirements]].
- The tag can be associated with multiple vehicles, but for occasional rentals use [[concepts/rental_vehicle_handling]] to add the rental plate temporarily and remove it later, preventing [[concepts/double_billing_prevention]].
