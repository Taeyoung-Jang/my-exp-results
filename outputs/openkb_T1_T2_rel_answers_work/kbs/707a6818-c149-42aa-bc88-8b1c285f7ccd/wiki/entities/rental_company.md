## Rental Company

A **rental company** is an organization that provides vehicles to customers on a short‑term basis, often including cars, trucks, or SUVs. In the context of *session‑33_2024‑06‑15*, the rental company is mentioned as a party that could inadvertently bill a driver for tolls if the driver does not manage their [[entities/toll_tag]] correctly.

### Key Points from the Session
- When using a rental car, the driver should add the rental’s license plate to their toll‑tag account only for the exact rental period and then remove it afterward. This prevents the rental company from issuing extra fees or double‑billing the driver. 
- The recommendation aligns with the concepts of [[concepts/rental_vehicle_handling]] and [[concepts/double_billing_prevention]].
