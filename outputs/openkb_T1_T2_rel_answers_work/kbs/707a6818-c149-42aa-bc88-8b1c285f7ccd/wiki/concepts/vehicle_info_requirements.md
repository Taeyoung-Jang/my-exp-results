# Vehicle Information Requirements

**Definition**: The essential vehicle details required by toll tag and similar online services to correctly match a vehicle and avoid billing errors.

## Core Requirements
- **License plate number** (exact spelling and state)
- **Vehicle make** (e.g., Subaru)
- **Model** (e.g., Outback)
- **Year** (e.g., 2024)

These four fields are sufficient for most toll agencies to identify a vehicle. Additional data such as trim level, engine type, or VIN is rarely requested and can introduce mismatches if entered incorrectly.

## Why Keep It Minimal?
- Reduces the chance of **data entry errors** that lead to mis‑billing.
- Simplifies the account setup process, making it easier to maintain consistency across multiple services [[concepts/consistent_user_info]].
- Allows quick updates when changing vehicles without needing to track extra specifications.

## Practical Tips (from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]])
1. **Enter only the basic fields** listed above when creating or editing a toll tag account.
2. **Double‑check the license plate** for typos; a single mistake can cause the system to mis‑associate tolls.
3. **Avoid adding trim or VIN** unless the form explicitly asks for it; most systems ignore these fields.
4. When adding a **rental vehicle**, input the rental’s plate for the exact rental dates and remove it afterward to prevent double billing [[concepts/double_billing_prevention]] and to follow proper rental handling [[concepts/rental_vehicle_handling]].
