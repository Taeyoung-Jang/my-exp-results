# Consistent User Information

**Definition:** Maintaining identical personal details (legal name, email address, and payment method) across all toll‑related services.

**Why it matters**
- Reduces mismatched records that cause billing errors.
- Simplifies account management by allowing a single source of truth.

**Key practices from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]**
1. **Use the exact same legal name** as shown on your driver’s license for every toll tag or online account.
2. **Reuse a single email address** for all registrations, so notifications are centralized.
3. **Choose a stable payment method** (e.g., a credit card you keep unchanged) and keep it consistent across services.
4. Store credentials in a reliable [[concepts/password_manager_storage]] to avoid losing login details.
