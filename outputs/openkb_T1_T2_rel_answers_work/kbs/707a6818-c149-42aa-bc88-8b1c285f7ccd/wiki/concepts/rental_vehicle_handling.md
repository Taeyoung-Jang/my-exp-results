## Rental Vehicle Handling

**Overview**
The *Rental Vehicle Handling* concept describes how to correctly assign and manage a [[entities/toll_tag]] when you drive a rental car or any vehicle you do not regularly own. It focuses on preventing billing errors, avoiding double charges, and keeping your toll account information tidy.

**Key Practices**
1. **Do not move the physical tag** between vehicles for occasional use. Instead, keep the tag on your primary vehicle (e.g., your 2024 Subaru Outback) and adjust the account settings.
2. **Add the rental's license plate** to your toll account for the exact dates you will be using the rental. Remove the plate entry promptly after the rental period ends.
3. Ensure the plate, state, make, model, and year entered match the rental agreement; no need for trim level or VIN unless specifically required.
4. By limiting the tag to a single primary vehicle and using temporary plate entries, you reduce the risk of [[concepts/double_billing_prevention]] and avoid extra fees from the [[entities/rental_company]].

**Interaction with Other Concepts**
- Consistent personal details across services, as described in [[concepts/consistent_user_info]], support accurate matching of temporary plate entries.
- Understanding the minimal vehicle data needed aligns with [[concepts/vehicle_info_requirements]].
- While not directly related, enabling [[concepts/auto_reload]] and storing credentials via [[concepts/password_manager_storage]] helps keep the overall toll account low‑maintenance.

**Example from Session**
The advice in [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]] illustrates these steps: add the rental’s plate for the rental dates, then remove it afterward, keeping the tag on the primary vehicle to avoid double‑billing and extra rental‑company charges.
