## League of Women Voters

The **League of Women Voters** is a nonpartisan, grassroots organization dedicated to encouraging informed and active participation in government. It produces **nonpartisan voter guides** that summarize candidates, offices, and ballot measures without endorsement, helping voters navigate down‑ballot races.

### Role in session‑37_2025‑06‑15
In the **session‑37_2025‑06‑15** checklist, the League of Women Voters is recommended as a trusted source for a quick, neutral voter guide when researching local elections and ballot measures. Its guides are highlighted for their ability to flag non‑partisan races and complex measure coalitions, supporting voters who intend to vote straight‑ticket but need to spot exceptions.
