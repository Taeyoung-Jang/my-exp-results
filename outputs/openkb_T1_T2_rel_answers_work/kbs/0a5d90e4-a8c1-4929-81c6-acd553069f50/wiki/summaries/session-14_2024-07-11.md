# Summary of session-14_2024-07-11

**Main focus:** Providing a practical framework for weekly planning that treats *energy* and *attention* as the limiting resources, and offering concrete tips for scheduling recurring social engagements without over‑committing.

## Key Concepts
- **Energy‑centric planning** – Prioritize activities that sustain energy; schedule them as recurring while keeping variable tasks in flexible blocks. [[concepts/energy-management]]
- **Recurring vs. flexible plans** – Identify grounding routines (sleep, workouts, nourishing social time) for fixed slots; place creative work, optional hang‑outs, and hobbies in open time. [[concepts/recurring-planning]] [[concepts/flexible-scheduling]]
- **Choosing day/time for social plans** – Align the slot with personal energy patterns; avoid high‑focus days (e.g., Tue/Wed) and consider late‑week or weekend timing.
- **Weekend structuring** – Decide between [[entities/saturday]] or [[entities/sunday]] based on whether you want a restful start or a free end to the weekend; prefer Saturday afternoon or Sunday midday for restorative social events.
- **Boundary setting for recurring events** – Anchor the event between established routines, pick nearby locations, and avoid stacking pre/post‑hang plans to keep the activity contained.

## Practical Steps
1. **Identify core grounding activities** (sleep routine, enjoyable workout, nurturing social time). Schedule them repeatedly.
2. **Map personal energy flow** across the week to spot lighter or heavier days.
3. **Select a social slot**:
   - Late‑week (Thu evenings) for most people.
   - If weekends are open, choose Saturday afternoon for a relaxed start or Sunday midday if you keep Saturday free.
4. **Create gentle edges** around the chosen slot:
   - Start after a protected morning routine.
   - End before an evening wind‑down activity.
   - Choose a close‑by venue to limit travel time.
5. **Trial and adjust** – Stick with the chosen day/time for several weeks, then reassess whether it feels supportive or pressure‑inducing.

## Outcomes
- A clearer, energy‑aligned weekly calendar.
- Recurring social engagements that feel restorative rather than draining.
- Preserved flexible time for spontaneity and creative work.
