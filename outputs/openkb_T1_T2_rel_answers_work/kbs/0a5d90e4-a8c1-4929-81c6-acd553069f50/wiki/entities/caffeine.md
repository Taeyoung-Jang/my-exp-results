# Caffeine (product)

**Description:** A widely used psychoactive stimulant found in coffee, tea, soft drinks, and energy products.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-01-10]]

- Recommended to stop consumption **about six hours before the intended sleep time** to minimize lingering alertness. In the session, the user was advised to have the last coffee around **2 a.m.** for an 8 a.m. sleep.
- Caffeine timing is linked to the concept of [[concepts/caffeine_cutoff_timing]], which guides **energy-management** for shift workers.
- While useful for alertness early in a night shift, caffeine should be avoided toward the end of the shift to prevent interference with daytime sleep.

## Related entities and concepts

- Often discussed alongside [[entities/melatonin]], another supplement used to aid sleep.
- Connected concepts: [[concepts/energy-management]], [[concepts/caffeine_cutoff_timing]].
