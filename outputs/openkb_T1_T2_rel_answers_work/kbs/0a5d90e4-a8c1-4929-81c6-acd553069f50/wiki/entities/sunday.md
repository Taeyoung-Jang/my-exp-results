# Sunday

Sunday is highlighted in [[summaries/session-14_2024-07-11]] as a potential day for a recurring social engagement when the user prefers to keep weeknights completely open and reserve Saturday for unstructured time.

## Key points from the conversation
- **Timing:** A Sunday plan should ideally be scheduled for late morning or early afternoon rather than the evening, to avoid the "Sunday night dread" effect and keep the rest of the weekend free for rest and preparation for the upcoming week.
- **Energy focus:** Placing the social activity on Sunday aligns with an **energy‑centric** approach, allowing the user to enjoy the connection without draining the energy needed for a smooth start to Monday.
- **Flexibility:** The recommendation ties into [[concepts/flexible-scheduling]] and [[concepts/recurring-planning]], emphasizing that the slot should be a supportive, recurring anchor rather than a pressure‑inducing obligation.
- **Contrast with Saturday:** If the user values Saturday as a completely open, spontaneous day, Sunday becomes the preferred day for the recurring social event. See also [[entities/saturday]] for the alternative approach.
