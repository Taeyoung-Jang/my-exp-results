# Melatonin (product)

Melatonin is a hormone‑based dietary supplement that can help adjust the body’s circadian rhythm, especially for individuals trying to sleep during daylight hours.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-34_2025-01-10]]
- **Low‑dose recommendation:** 0.5 – 1 mg is suggested for daytime sleepers.
- **Timing:** Take about one hour before the intended sleep time, not immediately upon arriving home.
- **Effectiveness:** Helps signal the body that it is time to sleep, making it easier to fall asleep in a dark, quiet environment.
- **Safety note:** Users should consult a clinician if they have medical conditions or are taking other medications.
