# Summary of Session 40 (2025‑01‑19)

## Core Advice
- Establish a **consistent cutoff** (e.g., 8:00 pm) that marks the start of the wind down routine.
- Treat the cutoff like a non‑negotiable appointment; only urgent tasks justify breaking it.

## Full 20–30 Minute Routine (for an 8:30 pm bedtime)
1. **8:00 pm – Signal shift**: dim lights, place phone out of easy reach but still audible.
2. **Physical transition (5 min)**: wash up, change into comfortable clothes, do a few slow stretches.
3. **Calm activity (10–15 min)**: read a few pages, listen to gentle music, sip a warm drink, or practice light breathing.
4. **Bed entry (by 8:25 pm)**: get into bed, keep the final 5 min quiet for the mind to settle.

## Minimum Version (when behind)
- Immediately put phone away.
- Perform the quickest hygiene step (e.g., brush teeth, change).
- Take **one minute** of deep breathing before lying down.

## Phone Curfew
- Recommended curfew: **7:45 – 8:00 pm** (adjustable).
- Purpose: create a buffer between screen exposure and sleep, supporting sleep hygiene.
- If 8:00 pm feels too strict, start there and shift earlier over time.

## Handling Necessary Phone Use After Curfew
- Keep the phone in a spot that requires standing up (adds friction).
- Define allowed tasks in advance (set alarm, quick message, check next‑day schedule).
- Perform the task, then return the phone to its spot immediately.
- Optional: enable **grayscale** or a focus mode at curfew to reduce visual appeal.

## Supporting Strategies
- **Morning prep before cutoff** (lay out clothes, pack bag, decide breakfast) to reduce evening pressure.
- Use low‑stimulation cues (soft lighting, calming scents) to reinforce the wind‑down signal.
- Associate the bed with rest, not scrolling, by keeping it a “no‑scroll” zone.
