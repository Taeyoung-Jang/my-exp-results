# Google Meet

**Type:** product

Google Meet is Google's browser‑based video‑meeting platform, typically used for real‑time collaboration and remote meetings.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-39_2025-06-15]]
- Recommended as a **whitelisted site** in the Auto Tab Discard configuration so that active meetings are never discarded.
- Suggested to keep Meet open in a dedicated Chrome window when needed, benefiting from Chrome's background throttling while preserving meeting state.
- Included in the list of sites that should stay active alongside Slack, Google Docs, and Google Sheets to prevent interruption of live sessions.

## Relationship to workflow concepts
- Fits into a [[concepts/lightweight-workflow]] by providing a low‑overhead, browser‑native communication tool.
- Supports [[concepts/resource-management]] by avoiding the need for a heavyweight desktop client.
- Ties into [[concepts/tab-management]] practices, especially when using extensions like Auto Tab Discard to manage tab lifecycles.
