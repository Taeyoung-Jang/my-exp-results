# Smell Reduction Techniques

**Definition**: Practical methods for keeping cooking odors low in ultra‑small kitchens (e.g., hot‑plate + mini‑fridge setups).

## Core Strategies

1. **Simmer, don’t fry** – Favor gentle heat (simmering, steaming) over high‑heat sautéing. This aligns with [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] and keeps smoke‑triggered alarms quiet.
2. **Lid on the pot** – Trapping steam reduces the release of aromatic compounds into the air.
3. **Ventilation** – Use a small fan aimed toward an open window or vent; even a desk fan helps move residual steam out of the room.
4. **Immediate cleaning** – Wipe the pot and countertop right after cooking to remove food bits that can continue to emit smells.
5. **Mild aromatics** – Choose citrus (e.g., [[entities/lemon]]), dairy, or mild herbs instead of garlic, onions, or strong spices. Butter, [[entities/cream-cheese]], and [[entities/parmesan]] add richness without lingering odor.
6. **One‑pot focus** – Limiting cookware reduces the number of surfaces that can hold odor‑bearing residues. See [[concepts/one-pot-meals]].
7. **Batch timing** – If you must prep larger quantities, break them into small portions cooked one‑at‑a‑time to avoid long‑running steam clouds (related to [[concepts/batch-cooking]]).

## Typical Low‑Odor Ingredient Choices
- **Butter** and **cream cheese** for richness
- **Lemon juice** for bright flavor with quick dissipation
- **Parmesan** added at the end for umami without a strong smell
- **Frozen vegetables** (e.g., peas, mixed veg) – low odor and quick to heat
- **Pre‑washed greens** – add at the end, minimal aroma

## Application in a Tiny Kitchen
The tips above are drawn directly from the meal plan in [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-09]]. In that context, the user’s constraints (hot‑plate, strict no‑smoke lease, limited fridge space) made it essential to:
- Cook dishes that stay under a simmer (e.g., one‑pot tomato pasta, lentil soup, egg‑rice bowl).
- Finish dishes with a dash of lemon or a knob of butter/cream cheese rather than garlic or chili.
- Keep the pot covered and wipe it clean immediately, limiting any lingering scent.
