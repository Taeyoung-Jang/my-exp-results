# Summary of session-06_2024-07-10

**Document type:** Conversation transcript – practical advice on affordable, time‑efficient home cooking.

## Core Idea
The assistant proposes a **weekly cooking routine** built around four **core recipes** (roasted vegetables, [[entities/rice]], [[entities/lentils]], and pan‑seared [[entities/tofu]]) prepared in a single short **prep day**. By reusing these components, lunches and dinners can be assembled in ~15 minutes, keeping costs low and minimizing leftovers.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/meal-planning]]: Structured weekly plan with defined lunches and dinners.  
- [[concepts/batch-cooking]]: Cook large portions of grains, legumes, and vegetables at once.  
- prep day: One dedicated afternoon (Sunday) to prepare all core ingredients.  
- overlapping ingredients: Shared components across meals to reduce waste.  
- time‑efficient cooking: Meals assembled in under 15 minutes.  
- [[concepts/budget-friendly-meals]]: Use inexpensive staples (rice, lentils, tofu, frozen/seasonal veg).  
- simple recipes: Minimal ingredient lists and straightforward techniques.

## Practical Outline
1. **Prep Day (Sunday)**
   - Roast ~2 cups mixed vegetables (carrots, broccoli, onions).
   - Cook ½ cup dry [[entities/rice]] (≈1.5 cups cooked).
   - Simmer ½ cup dried [[entities/lentils]] (≈1 cup cooked).
   - Pan‑sear ½ block [[entities/tofu]], seasoned with salt or [[entities/soy-sauce]].
2. **7‑Day Schedule** (Lunch + Dinner)
   - **Monday:** [[entities/rice]] + roasted veg + tofu (bowl); same components reheated for dinner with soy‑lemon glaze.  
   - **Tuesday:** Warm [[entities/lentils]] + veg (lunch); lentil soup with broth & veg (dinner).  
   - **Wednesday:** Tofu over lentils (lunch); skillet rice‑veg‑tofu mix (dinner).  
   - **Thursday:** [[entities/rice]] + veg (lunch); tofu‑veg stir‑fry (dinner).  
   - **Friday:** “Clean‑out” bowl or soup combining leftovers.  
   - **Saturday & Sunday:** Flexible – repeat favorites, add simple salads, scrambled egg, or fresh greens.
3. **Grocery List (single‑serve)**
   - 1 block [[entities/tofu]]  
   - ½ cup dry [[entities/lentils]] (or 1 can beans)  
   - ½ cup [[entities/rice]] (or quinoa)  
   - 3‑4 cups mixed vegetables (fresh or frozen)  
   - Carton broth, [[entities/soy-sauce]], olive oil, basic spices (salt, pepper, optional lemon).

## Findings & Recommendations
- **Ingredient overlap** dramatically cuts prep time and waste.  
- **Tofu** is chosen for its quick cooking and adaptability, but **chicken thighs** can replace it with similar steps.  
- **Portion sizing** (half‑block tofu, ½ cup rice, ½ cup lentils) is calibrated for one adult, avoiding surplus.  
- **Flexibility** is built in: small sauce tweaks keep meals interesting without extra work.
