# Summary of session‑33_2025‑03‑09

**Topic:** Cooking strategies for ultra‑small living spaces (hot plate + mini‑fridge) that avoid smoke and lingering odors.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]]** – prioritize simmering, steaming, and gentle heat rather than frying.  
- **[[concepts/one-pot-meals]]** – dishes that require only a single pot (or pot + mug) to minimize dishes and mess.  
- **meal planning) – planning meals that fit a constrained pantry and fridge space.  
- **[[concepts/smell-reduction-techniques]]** – keep lids on, use a fan, clean immediately, choose mild aromatics (lemon, butter, cream cheese).  
- **Compact shopping list** – shelf‑stable, small‑portion staples: [[entities/dry-pasta]], [[entities/instant-rice]], [[entities/lentils]], [[entities/frozen-vegetables]], cherry tomatoes or [[entities/frozen-vegetables]] (spinach), [[entities/prewashed-greens]], [[entities/butter]]/[[entities/cream-cheese]], [[entities/parmesan]], [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/egg]], optional [[entities/soy-sauce]].  
- **[[concepts/batch-cooking]]** – “cook‑once, eat‑once” portions to avoid bulky leftovers.

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Low‑smoke, low‑mess recipes**  
   - *One‑Pot Tomato Pasta*: [[entities/dry-pasta]] + water + cherry tomatoes or frozen spinach; finish with [[entities/butter]] or [[entities/cream-cheese]].  
   - *Ten‑Minute Lentil Soup*: broth or water + [[entities/lentils]] + [[entities/frozen-vegetables]]; finish with [[entities/lemon]], dried thyme, or a pinch of [[entities/parmesan]].  
   - *Egg‑Rice Bowl*: [[entities/instant-rice]] + greens/peas + [[entities/egg]]; finish with a drizzle of sesame oil or a splash of [[entities/soy-sauce]] (optional mild).  

2. **Flavor tweaks without strong odors**  
   - Use citrus ([[entities/lemon]]), mild herbs, dairy ([[entities/butter]], [[entities/cream-cheese]], [[entities/parmesan]]) instead of garlic or hot spices.  

3. **5‑Day Meal Plan (cook‑once, eat‑once)**  
   - **Day 1**: single‑serve tomato pasta.  
   - **Day 2**: single‑serve lentil‑veg soup.  
   - **Day 3**: egg‑rice bowl.  
   - **Day 4**: “creamy rice” with [[entities/instant-rice]], a spoon of [[entities/cream-cheese]] or [[entities/butter]], lemon, and any leftover greens.  
   - **Day 5**: another single‑serve pasta, flavored with [[entities/parmesan]] and [[entities/lemon]].  

   Each day uses only the portion needed; only [[entities/prewashed-greens]] and the small dairy items occupy [[entities/mini-fridge]] space.  

4. **Smell Management**  
   - Simmer under a lid, vent with a small fan, wipe pot and counter right after cooking; prefer butter/cream cheese for richness.  

## Consolidated Shopping List

- [[entities/instant-rice]] (box)  
- 1 can [[entities/lentils]]  
- [[entities/frozen-vegetables]] (mixed or peas)  
- Cherry tomatoes **or** frozen spinach  
- [[entities/prewashed-greens]] (bag)  
- [[entities/butter]] or [[entities/cream-cheese]]  
- [[entities/parmesan]] (wedge or grated)  
- 1 [[entities/lemon]]  
- 1 [[entities/egg]]

This summary captures the core strategies, recipes, and planning approach for cooking in extremely confined, low‑smoke environments.
