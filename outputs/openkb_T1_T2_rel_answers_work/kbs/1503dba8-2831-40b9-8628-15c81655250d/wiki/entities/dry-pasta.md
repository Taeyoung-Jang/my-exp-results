## Dry Pasta

Dry pasta is an uncooked, shelf‑stable form of pasta that only requires water and heat to become a ready‑to‑eat meal. In **session‑33_2025‑03‑09** it is highlighted as a core ingredient for low‑smoke, one‑pot cooking on a [[entities/hot-plate]] with limited space.

### Key facts from the session
- **One‑pot meals**: Used in the *One‑Pot Tomato Pasta* recipe, where pasta cooks together with water, cherry tomatoes or frozen spinach, and finishes with [[entities/butter]] or [[entities/cream-cheese]].
- **Low‑smoke cooking**: Because it simmers rather than fries, dry pasta fits the [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] approach.
- **Meal planning**: Suggested for single‑serve portions in a 5‑day *cook‑once, eat‑once* plan, aligning with [[concepts/meal-planning]] and minimizing leftovers in a [[entities/mini-fridge]].
- **Versatility**: Pairs well with other pantry items like [[entities/instant-rice]], [[entities/frozen-vegetables]], [[entities/lemon]], [[entities/parmesan]], and [[entities/egg]] for protein.
- **Storage**: Long‑lasting, requires only a small storage space, making it ideal for tiny living environments.
