# Cream Cheese

**Type:** product

Cream cheese is a mild, creamy dairy spread that adds richness and a subtle tang without producing strong odors. In the context of ultra‑small kitchens, it serves as a low‑smoke alternative to butter or heavy sauces, helping to keep meals flavorful while adhering to [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] constraints.

## Key Uses in Session 33
- **One‑Pot Tomato Pasta:** Finished with a spoonful of cream cheese instead of garlic or oil, providing silkiness and a gentle flavor boost.
- **Creamy Rice (Day 4 of the 5‑day plan):** Mixed into instant rice with a squeeze of [[entities/lemon]] for a quick, comforting dish that stays aromatic only while eating.
- **Flavor Substitute:** Recommended alongside [[entities/butter]] and [[entities/parmesan]] as mild dairy options that do not linger in the air.
