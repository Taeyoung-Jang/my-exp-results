# Soy Sauce

**Type:** Product

Soy sauce is a fermented liquid seasoning made from soybeans, wheat, salt, and water. It adds umami flavor and saltiness to dishes and is a staple in many cuisines.

## Role in [[summaries/session-06_2024-07-10]]
In the weekly cooking routine outlined in the session, soy sauce is suggested as a simple seasoning for the pan‑seared tofu and for flavoring grain bowls and soups. It contributes to keeping meals **[[concepts/budget-friendly-meals]]** affordable by providing a low‑cost way to enhance taste without requiring many additional ingredients.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/meal-planning]] – Soy sauce is part of the plan’s flexible seasoning strategy, allowing quick flavor changes.
- [[concepts/batch-cooking]] – It can be added in bulk to batches of rice, lentils, or tofu to create a consistent base flavor.
- [[concepts/budget-friendly-meals]] – As an inexpensive pantry staple, it helps maintain a low‑cost menu.

## Related Entities
- [[entities/tofu]] – Paired with tofu for quick, flavorful protein.
- [[entities/rice]] – Often drizzled over rice bowls.
- [[entities/lentils]] – Can be used to season lentil soups.
- [[entities/chicken-thighs]] – Although the plan uses tofu, soy sauce is also a common glaze for chicken thighs.

## Usage Tips
- Add a splash during the final minute of reheating to brighten the dish.
- Combine with lemon juice or a touch of honey for a simple sauce variation.
- Store in a sealed container in the refrigerator to maintain freshness.
