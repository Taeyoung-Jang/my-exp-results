## Parmesan

Parmesan is a hard, aged cheese known for its sharp yet **mellow** flavor profile. In the context of **tiny‑kitchen cooking**, it is highlighted as a low‑smell add‑in that can boost the taste of dishes without generating lingering aromas.

### Uses in session‑33_2025‑03‑09
- Sprinkled on the **one‑pot tomato pasta** to finish the dish with richness.
- Added to the **lentil soup** at the end for a subtle umami boost.
- Paired with **lemon** and **butter** as a flavor alternative to garlic or spicy condiments.
- Recommended as part of a compact shopping list for a [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] and [[concepts/one-pot-meals]] regimen.
