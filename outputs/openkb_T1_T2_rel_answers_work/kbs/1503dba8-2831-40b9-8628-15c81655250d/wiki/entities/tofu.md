# Tofu

**Type:** product

Tofu is a soy‑based, high‑protein food that can be pressed, sliced, and pan‑seared quickly. In the *session‑06_2024‑07‑10* cooking routine it serves as one of four core recipes, providing a versatile protein that pairs with rice, lentils, and roasted vegetables.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Quantity for one person:** half a block (≈150 g) prepared on the prep day.
- **Preparation method:** press, slice, and sear in a hot pan with a little oil; seasoned with salt or soy‑sauce.
- **Role in the meal plan:** used in lunch bowls, dinner skillet meals, and stir‑fry variations, keeping weekly cooking time under 15 minutes.
- **Substitutes:** chicken thighs could replace tofu, but tofu is chosen for simplicity and quick cooking.
