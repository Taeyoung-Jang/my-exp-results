# Hot Plate

A **hot plate** is a small, electric (or sometimes induction) cooking surface that provides a single, flat heating element.  It is popular in micro‑apartments, dorm rooms, and other constrained living environments where a full stove is not allowed or feasible.

## Role in session‑33_2025‑03‑09
In the March 2025 conversation the user’s kitchen consisted of a **hot plate** and a **mini‑fridge**.  All suggested meals—one‑pot pasta, lentil soup, egg‑rice bowl, and variations—were designed to work on that single heating element, avoiding any cooking method that would generate smoke or strong odors.

Key points from the document:
- Only simmering, steaming, or gentle low‑heat cooking is recommended to stay within the lease’s no‑smoke rule. 
- The hot plate is paired with a single pot (or pot + mug) to keep dishes minimal. 
- Recipes are timed for the modest heat output typical of portable hot plates (e.g., 8‑12 minutes for pasta, 10 minutes for soup).
