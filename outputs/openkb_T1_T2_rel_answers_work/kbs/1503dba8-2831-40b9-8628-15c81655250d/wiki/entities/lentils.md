## Lentils

Lentils are small, lens‑shaped legumes that cook quickly, provide plant‑based protein, and are inexpensive. They are a staple in many affordable, home‑cooked meals.

### Role in [[summaries/session-06_2024-07-10]]
In the 7‑day cooking routine presented in the session, lentils serve as one of the four core recipes. They are prepared in bulk on the prep day and then mixed with other components (rice, roasted vegetables, tofu) for both lunches and dinners throughout the week.

### Quantity for One Person
- **Dry lentils:** ½ cup (approximately 100 g)
- **Yield after cooking:** about 1 cup (≈200 g) of tender lentils

These amounts are calibrated to provide enough protein without generating large leftovers.

### Additional Notes
Lentils require only about 20 minutes of simmering, making them ideal for a prep‑day strategy that minimizes weekday cooking time.
