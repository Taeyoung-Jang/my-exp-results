# Instant Rice

**Type:** product

Instant rice is a pre‑cooked, dehydrated rice that rehydrates in a few minutes of hot water. It is prized in ultra‑small living spaces because it requires only a hot plate, minimal water, and a single pot, making it a core ingredient for [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] and [[concepts/one-pot-meals]].

## Usage in Session 33
- Featured in the 5‑day “cook‑once, eat‑once” plan as the base for the Egg‑Rice Bowl, creamy rice, and quick rice bowls.
- Paired with [[entities/egg]], [[entities/prewashed-greens]], [[entities/frozen-vegetables]], [[entities/butter]] or [[entities/cream-cheese]] for protein and flavor without generating smoke.
- Combined with [[entities/lemon]] and [[entities/parmesan]] to add bright, mild taste while keeping aromas low.

## Benefits
- Shelf‑stable and compact, fitting easily in a [[entities/mini-fridge]] or pantry.
- Requires only simmering water, aligning with [[concepts/smell-reduction-techniques]] (keep lid on, use low heat).
- Supports simple [[concepts/meal-planning]] for tight schedules and limited storage.
