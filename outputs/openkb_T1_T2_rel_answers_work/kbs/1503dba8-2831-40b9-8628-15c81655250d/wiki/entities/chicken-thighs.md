# Chicken Thighs

**Type:** Product

Chicken thighs are a dark‑meat cut that remains tender and flavorful when cooked in bulk. In the weekly plan from [[summaries/session-06_2024-07-10]], they are suggested as an alternative to tofu for the core protein component.

## Relevance to the Meal Plan
- Can be prepared during a **[[concepts/batch-cooking]]** session (e.g., roasted or pan‑seared on the prep day).
- Fits well within **[[concepts/budget-friendly-meals]]** because they are generally inexpensive per serving.
- Integral to **[[concepts/meal-planning]]** that relies on overlapping ingredients such as [[entities/rice]], [[entities/lentils]], and [[entities/soy-sauce]].

## Comparison with Other Proteins
- Offers a meat‑based option compared to the tofu suggestion (see [[entities/tofu]]). Both can be seasoned similarly with soy sauce or other pantry staples.

## Suggested Usage
1. **Prep Day:** Season chicken thighs with salt, pepper, and a splash of soy sauce, then bake or pan‑sear until cooked through.
2. **Meal Assembly:** Combine with pre‑cooked rice, lentils, and roasted vegetables for quick lunch or dinner bowls.

By incorporating chicken thighs, the plan maintains flexibility while staying aligned with the core concepts of batch cooking, cost‑effective meals, and structured weekly planning.
