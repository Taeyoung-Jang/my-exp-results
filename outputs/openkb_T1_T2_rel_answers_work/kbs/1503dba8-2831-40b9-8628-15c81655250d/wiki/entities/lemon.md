# Lemon

**Type:** product

Lemon is a small, acidic citrus fruit commonly used as a flavor enhancer in cooking.

## Role in session-33_2025-03-09
In the “low‑smoke, one‑pot” meal plan, lemon is recommended as a mild aromatic add‑in that brightens dishes such as tomato pasta, lentil soup, and rice bowls without creating strong lingering smells. It appears in the shopping list and is suggested as a quick‑finish ingredient to replace garlic or spicy condiments.
