# Mini Fridge (product)

A **mini fridge** is a small, energy‑efficient refrigeration appliance designed to fit in tight living areas such as studio apartments, dorm rooms, or tiny homes. In the context of the *session‑33_2025‑03‑09* conversation, the mini fridge is a core piece of the minimal kitchen setup alongside a [[entities/hot-plate]].

## Role in Low‑Smoke, One‑Pot Cooking
- Stores perishable items like [[entities/egg]], [[entities/prewashed-greens]], butter, cream cheese, and a small amount of parmesan, enabling quick assembly of low‑smoke meals.
- Keeps shelf‑stable staples (dry pasta, instant rice, canned lentils) fresh and easily accessible for [[concepts/one-pot-meals]] and [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]].
- Its limited capacity influences the **cook‑once, eat‑once** approach described in the 5‑day meal plan, encouraging portion‑controlled cooking to avoid bulky leftovers.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] – The mini fridge supports a diet of simmered, steamed dishes that produce minimal odor.
- [[concepts/one-pot-meals]] – Enables storage of the few ingredients needed for single‑pot recipes.

## Key Facts from the Document
- The user’s lease restricts cooking methods that generate smoke; the mini fridge is therefore paired with a hot plate for gentle simmering.
