## Butter

Butter is a dairy product made by churning cream until it separates into butterfat and buttermilk. It provides a smooth, creamy texture and a gentle, buttery aroma that does not linger heavily in small living spaces.

### Role in **session‑33**
- Recommended as a finishing ingredient for the **One‑Pot Tomato Pasta** to create a silky sauce while keeping the dish low‑smoke and low‑odor.  This aligns with the suggestions for [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] and [[concepts/one-pot-meals]].
- Suggested as an alternative to garlic or spicy seasonings to enhance flavor without adding strong lingering smells, supporting [[concepts/smell-reduction-techniques]].
- Used alongside other mild flavor boosters such as [[entities/lemon]] and [[entities/parmesan]] in the meal plan.
