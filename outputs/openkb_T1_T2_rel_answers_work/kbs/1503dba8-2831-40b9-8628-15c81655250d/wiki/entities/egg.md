## Egg

**Type:** product  
**Description:** A versatile protein commonly stored in a mini‑fridge and used in quick, low‑smoke, one‑pot dishes.

### Role in tiny‑kitchen cooking
- Featured in the **Egg‑Rice Bowl** from [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-09]], where an egg is cracked into simmering instant rice and greens to create soft curds.
- Provides protein without requiring additional cookware; the egg cooks directly in the same pot used for rice, fitting the [[concepts/one-pot-meals]] principle.
- Cooked on a [[entities/hot-plate]] with minimal heat, producing virtually no smoke, aligning with [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] and [[concepts/smell-reduction-techniques]].
- Requires only a small amount of fridge space, making it ideal for storage in a [[entities/mini-fridge]].

### Practical tips
- Crack the egg into the pot just before the rice finishes cooking; stir gently for 2 minutes.
- Season with a splash of [[entities/soy-sauce]] or a drizzle of butter/[[entities/cream-cheese]] for richness without strong odors.
- Use fresh eggs that can be kept in the mini‑fridge for up to a week.
