## Prewashed Greens

Prewashed greens are ready‑to‑eat salad greens that arrive washed, trimmed, and packaged for immediate use. In the tiny‑kitchen context of [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-09]] they provide a quick, low‑prep vegetable component that fits inside a [[entities/mini-fridge]] and requires no cooking equipment beyond a pot or mug.

### Key Uses
- Added to the **Egg‑Rice Bowl** and **Creamy Rice** meals to supply fresh texture without extra prep time.
- Paired with low‑smoke, one‑pot dishes such as [[concepts/one-pot-meals]] and [[concepts/low-smoke-cooking]] recipes to keep aroma minimal.
- Supports the **cook‑once, eat‑once** approach in the 5‑day plan, allowing a small daily portion without generating large leftovers.

### Benefits for Small Spaces
- Saves counter space and dishwashing because no washing is needed.
- Extends fridge life when stored in its original sealed pack, making it ideal for a [[entities/mini-fridge]] with limited capacity.
- Contributes to [[concepts/meal-planning]] by offering a versatile, shelf‑stable (refrigerated) protein‑free base that can be mixed with [[entities/instant-rice]], [[entities/dry-pasta]], or soups.

- [[entities/hot-plate]] – the primary heat source for the one‑pot meals that include these greens.
- [[entities/lemon]] and [[entities/parmesan]] – common flavor enhancers paired with greens in the session’s recipes.
