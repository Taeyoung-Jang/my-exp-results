# Summary of session-01_2024-06-15

**Document type:** Conversation transcript (assistant advice).

## Core Idea
A practical, desk‑centric micro‑break routine (30‑60 s every 30 min) designed for people with chronic lower‑back pain who cannot lift heavy or stand long, plus a quick “stop/modify” checklist and ergonomic tweaks.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/micro-breaks]]** – Short, seated resets (30‑60 s) repeated every half‑hour to prevent static postures.  
- **[[concepts/gentle-stretches]]** – Neck turns, shoulder rolls, seated leg extensions, tiny torso swivels, and chest‑opening breaths that keep the spine neutral.  
- **[[concepts/lower-back-red-flags]]** – Sensations (sharp pain, catching, tingling, sustained tension) that signal a movement should be stopped or modified.  
- **[[concepts/desk-ergonomics]]** – Chair lumbar support, foot‑flat positioning, elbow‑near‑body keyboard height, screen at eye‑level to reduce baseline strain.  
- **[[concepts/full-day-schedule]]** – Structured 9 am‑5 pm timeline with 30‑min micro‑breaks and two 2‑3 min seated breathing/chest‑opening breaks.

## Stop/Modify Checklist (Red Flags)
- Sharp, stabbing pain  
- Sudden catching or “locking” of the back  
- Tingling/numbness radiating down the leg  
- Persistent tightening that lasts > 10 s after the movement  
- Any sensation that worsens rather than eases  

If any of the above occur, **stop immediately**, reduce range or duration, and consider a simple breathing pause.

## Ergonomic Tweaks for Lower‑Back Relief
1. **Chair** – Lumbar support aligned with the natural curve; hips at or slightly above knee level.  
2. **Feet** – Flat on the floor or on a footrest; weight evenly distributed.  
3. **Keyboard** – Close enough to keep elbows ≈ 90°, forearms parallel to the floor.  
4. **Monitor** – Top of screen at eye level to avoid neck flexion.  
5. **Desk Height** – Allows forearms to rest comfortably without shoulder elevation.  
6. **Micro‑adjustments** – Small recline changes or seat‑tilt to vary load throughout the day.
