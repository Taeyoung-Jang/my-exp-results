## Overview
The assistant provides a step‑by‑step 4‑week post‑half‑marathon running schedule focused on consistency, easy effort, and injury avoidance.

## Core Recommendations
- **Structure**: 3–4 runs per week (Mon, Wed, optional Fri, Sun long run). Easy runs 30‑40 min, long run 60‑80 min, with gradual time increase.  
- **Run/Walk Intervals**: Initial advice uses 1‑minute run/1.5‑minute walk intervals, progressing to longer runs; the post‑half plan shifts to continuous easy runs.  
- **Motivation**: Set simple weekly goals (e.g., “run 20‑30 min three times”), attach runs to fixed schedule slots, and focus on how you feel afterwards.  
- **Easy‑Day Cues**: [[concepts/easy-day-cues]] such as the talk test, heart‑rate zone, or pace caps to ensure effort stays conversational.  
- **Recovery**: Light walking, gentle stretches, or full rest on off‑days; repeat a week if soreness accumulates.  
- **Injury Awareness**: Distinguish normal muscle soreness (dull, fades) from warning pain (sharp, localized, worsens) such as shin splints; treat the latter with extra rest.

## Handling Missed Sessions
- Miss one day → continue with the next scheduled run.  
- Miss multiple days or feel heavy → repeat the current week before progressing.

## Optional Strides Day
- Friday jog 25‑30 min + 4‑6 short 10‑15 s accelerations, kept below workout intensity.

## Post‑Half‑Marathon Adjustments
- Emphasize true recovery after a race; keep all efforts “conversational.”  
- If shin soreness persists after the long run, downgrade or skip the next run, use gentle walking, and resume when pain resolves.  
- The overall approach aligns with [[concepts/consistent-running]] and the principles of [[concepts/post-half-marathon-recovery]].
