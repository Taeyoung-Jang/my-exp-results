# Micro‑breaks

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Short, frequent pauses (typically 30–60 seconds) taken during desk work to reset posture, stimulate gentle movement, and reduce musculoskeletal strain.

---

## Purpose
Micro‑breaks are designed to:
- Prevent prolonged static loading of the spine, especially the lower back.
- Encourage gentle movement of the neck, shoulders, hips, and torso without heavy lifting or long standing.
- Provide a mental reset that can improve focus and reduce fatigue.

## Typical Pattern
A common micro‑break cadence is **one break every 30 minutes**, lasting **30–60 seconds**. The breaks are mostly performed while seated, with occasional brief standing if tolerated.

## Example Full‑Day Schedule
The schedule proposed in [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]] outlines a 9 am‑5 pm routine:
- 9:30 am – Neck turns & shoulder drops
- 10:00 am – Seated leg extensions
- 10:30 am – Rib‑cage lift & breathing
- 11:00 am – Small torso swivel
- … and so on, repeating the pattern throughout the day with two longer 2‑3 minute seated breathing breaks around noon and 4 pm.

## Implementation Tips
1. **Set a timer** – Use a phone, computer, or dedicated app to cue the break every 30 minutes.
2. **Keep it brief** – Aim for 30–60 seconds; the goal is a reset, not a workout.
3. **Stay seated** – Most movements are performed while seated to avoid stressing the lower back.
4. **Monitor sensations** – Refer to [[concepts/lower-back-red-flags]]; stop if any red‑flag symptoms appear.
5. **Combine with ergonomics** – Adjust chair, monitor, and keyboard as described in [[concepts/desk-ergonomics]] to maximize benefit.

---

## Benefits
- Reduced lower‑back stiffness and pain for individuals with chronic discomfort.
- Improved circulation and reduced fatigue.
- Enhanced focus and productivity due to regular mental breaks.

---

*Micro‑breaks are a low‑impact, scalable strategy applicable to most desk‑bound work environments, especially for those needing to protect a sensitive lower back.*
