# Grab‑and‑Go

**Definition** – Grab‑and‑Go refers to the practice of relying on pre‑prepared, ready‑to‑eat foods (pre‑washed greens, pre‑cooked proteins, canned beans, microwavable grains, etc.) to assemble meals quickly, especially when time, energy, or kitchen resources are limited.

## Core Elements
- **Components** – View food as interchangeable parts: a protein, a fiber source, a whole‑grain or starchy element, and a healthy‑fat addition. This mirrors the [[concepts/components]] mindset.
- **Assembly Meals** – By mixing and matching these components, you create [[concepts/assembly_meals]] without cooking full recipes.

## How it Works in Practice
The approach is illustrated in the cheat‑sheet created for a typical workplace café in [[summaries/session-38_2025-10-16]]. Key tactics include:
1. **Identify available components** – salads (mixed greens, beans, tofu, chicken), grains (quinoa, rice cups), proteins (tuna, hard‑boiled eggs, cheese sticks), and healthy fats (avocado, nuts, seeds).
2. **Combine with minimal heat** – Pair a ready‑to‑eat protein with a microwavable grain and a side of soup, or stack a wrap with pre‑cut veggies and a yogurt cup.
3. **Keep portable snacks** – Pack items like yogurt, cheese sticks, nuts, or granola bars to supplement meals during a shift.

## Benefits
- **Time efficiency** – Meals can be assembled in under five minutes.
- **Nutritional balance** – By deliberately choosing a protein, fiber, and fat, you maintain satiety and steady energy.
- **Flexibility** – Works with any selection of ready foods, adapting to what the café offers on a given day.

## Typical Grab‑and‑Go Combos (examples from the source)
- **Hearty salad + lentil soup** – Mixed greens, grilled chicken, chickpeas, quinoa (when available), feta/avocado, paired with a cup of lentil soup.
- **Turkey sandwich + chili** – Whole‑grain turkey sandwich with a side of chili for extra protein and fiber.
- **Veggie hummus wrap + protein‑rich side salad** – Wrap with a side salad that includes tofu or hard‑boiled egg, roasted veggies, and a drizzle of vinaigrette.
- **Snack pairings** – Yogurt + granola bar; cheese stick + mixed nuts; nut‑butter packet + fruit.

## Implementation Tips
- **Batch when possible** – Double the portion of soups or roasted veggies to have leftovers for future grab‑and‑go meals.
- **Visible go‑to items** – Keep your favorite components front‑of‑mind by ordering them consistently.
- **Light dressings** – Use vinaigrette or a modest drizzle of olive oil to keep meals light for night‑shift digestion.
