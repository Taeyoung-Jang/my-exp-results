# Assembly Meals

**Definition** – A strategy for creating nutritious meals by combining pre‑prepared or easily assembled food items, rather than cooking full recipes from scratch.

## Core principles
- **Component mindset** – View foods as interchangeable parts (protein, grain, veg, healthy fat) that can be mixed and matched. [[concepts/components]]
- **Grab‑and‑go focus** – Rely on items that require little or no cooking: pre‑washed greens, rotisserie chicken, canned beans, microwavable grains, packaged yogurts, etc. [[concepts/grab_and_go]]
- **Balance** – Aim for a combination of protein, fiber, and a source of healthy fat to promote satiety and steady energy. [[concepts/assembly_meals]]

## Typical components (from a standard café line)
- **Proteins** – grilled chicken, shredded chicken, tofu, hard‑boiled eggs, tuna salad.
- **Fiber & carbs** – mixed greens, spinach, romaine, quinoa (when available), chickpeas/black beans, roasted potatoes or whole‑grain bread.
- **Healthy fats** – avocado, nuts/seeds, feta or shredded cheese, olive‑oil‑based dressings.
- **Convenient sides** – soups (lentil, vegetable, chili, chicken noodle), yogurt cups, fruit cups, cheese sticks, granola bars.

## Example combos (derived from [[summaries/session-38_2025-10-16]])
1. **Hearty salad + soup** – Mixed greens + grilled chicken + chickpeas + quinoa (if offered) + feta/avocado, paired with a cup of lentil or vegetable soup.
2. **Sandwich/wrap + soup** – Turkey sandwich or chicken Caesar wrap paired with lentil soup or chili.
3. **Veggie hummus wrap + protein‑rich side salad** – Wrap + side salad with hard‑boiled egg or tofu, roasted broccoli, olive‑oil drizzle; add a yogurt cup for extra protein.
4. **Grain‑anchored bowl + soup** – Quinoa or brown‑rice base + greens + beans + roasted veggies + grilled chicken, served alongside chicken‑noodle or tomato‑basil soup.
5. **Protein box + fruit** – Pre‑made protein box (eggs, cheese, nuts) with a fruit cup or banana.
6. **Snack pairings** – Yogurt cup + granola bar or cheese stick + mixed nuts for quick mid‑shift energy.

## Benefits
- **Speed** – Meals can be assembled in under five minutes.
- **Flexibility** – Works with whatever items are available on a given day.
- **Portability** – Many combos are easy to pack for on‑the‑go consumption.
- **Sustained fullness** – The protein‑fiber‑fat balance reduces cravings during irregular work hours.
