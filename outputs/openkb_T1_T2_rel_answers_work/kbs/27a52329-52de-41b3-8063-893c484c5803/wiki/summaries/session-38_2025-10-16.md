# Summary of Session 38 (2025‑10‑16)

**Main contribution** – A practical cheat‑sheet that translates the user’s typical café offerings into ready‑to‑order, balanced meals and portable snacks for busy, irregular work hours.

## Core concepts
- **components** – Treat foods as interchangeable components (protein, grain, veg, healthy fat) rather than rigid recipes.
- **assembly_meals** – Build meals by assembling pre‑prepared items with minimal cooking.
- **protein, fiber, and healthy fats balance** – Aim for a combination of protein, fiber, and healthy fats for satiety and steady energy.
- **grab_and_go** – Leverage ready‑to‑eat items (pre‑washed greens, rotisserie chicken, canned beans, microwavable grains) to reduce prep time.
- **café line optimization** – Choose from typical café stations (soups, salad bar, sandwich/wrap counter) to create nutritionally complete combos.
- **snack bag basics** – Keep a few non‑perishable, protein‑rich snacks on hand for mid‑shift hunger.

## Default meal combos (pick & pair)
1. **Hearty salad + soup**  
   - Base: mixed greens or spinach.  
   - Protein: grilled chicken or tofu.  
   - Extras: chickpeas/black beans, quinoa (when offered), feta or avocado.  
   - Dressing: light vinaigrette.  
   - Pair with: a cup of lentil or vegetable soup.

2. **Sandwich/wrap + soup**  
   - Choose: turkey sandwich or chicken Caesar wrap.  
   - Pair with: a cup of lentil soup or chili for extra fiber and warmth.

3. **Veggie hummus wrap + protein‑rich side salad**  
   - Wrap: veggie hummus wrap.  
   - Side salad: mixed greens, hard‑boiled egg or tofu, roasted broccoli/cauliflower, a drizzle of olive oil.  
   - Optional add‑on: a yogurt cup for extra protein.

4. **Grain‑anchored bowl + soup**  
   - Bowl: quinoa or brown‑rice base, mixed greens, chickpeas, roasted veggies, grilled chicken.  
   - Pair with: a cup of chicken‑noodle or tomato‑basil soup for comforting variety.

5. **Protein box + side**  
   - Grab a pre‑made protein box (eggs, cheese, nuts) and pair with a fruit cup or banana for carbs.

6. **Soup‑heavy combo**  
   - Choose a hearty soup (chili or lentil) and add a small whole‑grain roll or crackers plus a cheese stick.

## Quick snack backups for the bag
- **Yogurt cup + granola bar** – Immediate protein + sustained carbs.  
- **Cheese stick + mixed nuts or sunflower seeds** – Portable, high‑satiety snack.  
- **Single‑serve nut‑butter packet + apple slices** – Easy energy boost.  
- **Roasted chickpeas (store‑bought) + a piece of fruit** – Crunchy fiber‑rich snack.

## Practical tips
- **Batch‑prep when possible** – Double the portion of soups or roasted veggies to have leftovers for later.  
- **Visible go‑to options** – Keep chosen combos front‑of‑mind by ordering them consistently.  
- **Light dressings & sauces** – Use vinaigrette or a drizzle of olive oil to keep meals light for night‑shift digestion.  
- **Stay varied** – Rotate through the three default patterns to avoid monotony while maintaining nutritional balance.
