# Daily Blueprint Workflow

**Type:** Work

**Overview**
A structured, printable template designed for professionals handling technical drawings. It balances creative warm‑ups with disciplined quality checks, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks during a typical workday.

**Key Facts (from [[summaries/session-10_2024-08-14]])**
- Divides the day into three phases: **Morning Triage**, **Mid‑Day Check‑In**, and **End‑of‑Day Handoff**.
- Each phase includes actionable checkboxes for quick execution.
- Embeds a **Quick QA** section covering dimensions, scale, alignment, annotations, and revision history.
- Introduces a **Stop‑Polishing Rule** to prevent endless refinements.
- Lists the most frequent blueprint errors as a reference.

**Structure**
1. **Morning Triage** – Review new drawings, confirm latest versions, set priorities, and perform a brief creative warm‑up ([[concepts/creative_rhythm]]).
2. **Mid‑Day Check‑In** – Re‑evaluate priorities, verify answered questions, ensure file naming integrity, and take a short mental reset.
3. **End‑of‑Day Handoff** – Document where work stopped, run the Quick QA checklist, and confirm the final saved file is the reviewed version.

**Quick QA Checklist** (aligns with [[concepts/quality_assurance]])
- Dimensions clear, consistent, accurate
- Scale marked and correct
- Major elements aligned properly
- Annotations readable and relevant
- Revision history updated

**Stop‑Polishing Rule**
Submit when the drawing meets all project requirements, all QA items are clean, and any further tweaks would only affect appearance, not function.

**Common Blueprint Mistakes** (see [[concepts/common_blueprint_mistakes]])
- Mismatched or missing dimensions
- Inconsistent or unlabeled scales
- Misaligned structural or reference points
- Outdated notes or revision tags
- Layer visibility or naming errors
