# Summary of session-39_2025-04-14

**Date:** 2025‑04‑14  

## Goal
The user wants a concrete daily routine for job hunting, a reusable cover‑letter checklist/template, a simple method to track applications, and polite follow‑up email templates.

## Daily schedule (9 am‑5 pm)
- **9:00‑9:30** – Wake‑up routine, light breakfast, quick plan review  
- **9:30‑12:00** – Primary job‑search block: select 1‑2 roles, research companies, draft applications  
- **12:00‑13:00** – Lunch break (away from screen)  
- **13:00‑15:00** – Light tasks: polish cover letters, update tracking spreadsheet, send follow‑ups  
- **15:00‑16:00** – Physical break / walk  
- **16:00‑17:00** – Final quick pass: check inbox, note any new opportunities, wrap up  

*Suggested volume:* **2‑3 tailored applications per day** to maintain quality.  

## Cover‑letter checklist & template
### Checklist (mental run‑through)
1. Identify 1‑2 key requirements from the job post  
2. Mention role and company by name in opening  
3. Insert a specific example demonstrating the required skill  
4. End with a human, confident closing  
5. Verify names, dates, links before sending  

### Reusable template (see [[entities/cover-letter-template]])
```
Hi [Name],

I’m applying for the [Role Title] position at [Company] because [brief, specific reason]. Your posting highlights [Key Requirement 1] and [Key Requirement 2]; my experience in [Your Experience] includes [short example] that directly addresses those needs.

I’m excited about contributing to [team/company initiative]. Thank you for your time – I look forward to discussing how I can help.
```

## Application tracking spreadsheet
Suggested columns (lean layout):
- Company name  
- Role title  
- Date applied  
- Job posting link  
- Resume / cover‑letter version used  
- Contact person (if known)  
- Current status (e.g., *Applied*, *Follow‑up 1*, *Interview*)  
- Follow‑up date (planned)  

See [[concepts/application-tracking-spreadsheet]] for the underlying idea.

## Follow‑up cadence
- **First follow‑up:** ~1 week after application  
- **Second (final) follow‑up:** 2 weeks after application (or 1.5‑2 weeks after the first)  
- After two attempts, move on while continuing new applications.  

See [[concepts/follow-up-cadence]] for more detail.

## Follow‑up email templates
### 1. One‑week check‑in
```
Hi [Name],

I hope you’re well. I wanted to check in on my application for the [Role Title] position. I remain very interested and would be happy to provide any additional information.

Thank you for your time.
```

### 2. Final check‑in (≈2 weeks)
```
Hi [Name],

I’m following up to see if there are any updates on the [Role Title] role. I continue to be interested and would appreciate any timeline you can share.

Thanks again for considering my application.
```

### Portal vs. direct recruiter
- **Company portal (no personal contact):** Send the same template to the general hiring inbox or listed recruiting email.  
- **Direct recruiter/hiring manager:** Use the recipient’s name and keep the email personalized; the content remains the same.
