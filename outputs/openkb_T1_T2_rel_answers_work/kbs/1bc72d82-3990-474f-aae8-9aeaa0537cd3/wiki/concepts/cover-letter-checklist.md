# Cover Letter Checklist

**Definition**: A concise mental checklist and reusable template that helps job seekers craft tailored cover letters quickly while maintaining quality.

## Core Checklist Steps
1. **Identify key requirements** – Pull out 1‑2 essential qualifications from the job posting.
2. **Personalize opening** – Mention the role title and company name explicitly.
3. **Show relevance** – Insert a specific example from your experience that directly addresses the identified requirements.
4. **Close confidently** – End with a polite, human closing that expresses enthusiasm.
5. **Validate details** – Double‑check the hiring manager’s name, dates, links, and any numbers before sending.

## Reusable Template (see [[entities/cover-letter-template]])
```
Hi [Name],

I’m applying for the [Role Title] position at [Company] because [brief, specific reason]. Your posting highlights [Key Requirement 1] and [Key Requirement 2]; my experience in [Your Experience] includes [short example] that directly addresses those needs.

I’m excited about contributing to [team/company initiative]. Thank you for your time – I look forward to discussing how I can help.
```
