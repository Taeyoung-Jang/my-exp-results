# Common Blueprint Mistakes

A concise reference of the most frequent errors that appear in architectural and engineering drawings, helping practitioners catch problems early and maintain high standards of [[concepts/quality_assurance]]. The list originates from the daily workflow template in [[summaries/session-10_2024-08-14]].

## Key Mistake Categories

- **Mismatched or missing dimensions** – Dimensions that are inconsistent, contradictory, or omitted entirely, leading to ambiguity in size and placement.
- **Inconsistent or unlabeled scales** – Scale markers that are absent, incorrectly labeled, or differ between sheets, making measurements unreliable.
- **Misaligned structural or reference points** – Core elements (walls, columns, grids) that do not line up across views, causing construction conflicts.
- **Outdated notes or revision tags** – Annotations that reference previous revisions or contain stale information, confusing the construction team.
- **Layer visibility or naming errors** – Incorrect layer assignments, hidden critical layers, or ambiguous layer names that hinder clear communication.

## How It Fits Into the Workflow

These mistakes are explicitly called out in the **Quick QA** section of the [[entities/daily-blueprint-workflow]] checklist. By reviewing each item before finalizing a drawing, designers can ensure that no critical error slips through the cracks.
