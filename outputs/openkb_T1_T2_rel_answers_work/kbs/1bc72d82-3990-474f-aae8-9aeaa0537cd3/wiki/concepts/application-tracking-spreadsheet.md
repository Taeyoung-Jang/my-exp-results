# Application Tracking Spreadsheet

**Definition**
A lightweight, column‑based spreadsheet used to record each job application, its key details, and follow‑up actions.

**Purpose**
Helps job seekers stay organized, avoid duplicate effort, and monitor progress without turning tracking into a second job.

**Core Columns (recommended)**
- Company name
- Role title
- Date applied
- Job posting link
- Resume / cover‑letter version used
- Contact person (if known)
- Current status (e.g., Applied, Follow‑up 1, Interview)
- Planned follow‑up date

**How to use**
1. Add a new row for every role you decide to pursue.
2. Fill in the columns as you gather information from the job posting.
3. Update the *Current status* and *Planned follow‑up date* after each interaction.
4. Review the sheet weekly to spot stalled applications and schedule next steps.

**Link to related concepts**
- Works with the [[concepts/cover-letter-checklist]] to note which version of the cover letter was sent.
- Supports the [[concepts/follow-up-cadence]] by providing a place to record planned follow‑up dates.
- Complements the [[concepts/daily-job-search-schedule]] by feeding the tracking data needed for daily planning.

**Quality considerations**
Keep the sheet simple; extra columns can create friction and reduce consistency. Regularly audit entries for accuracy – a practice aligned with [[concepts/quality_assurance]].
