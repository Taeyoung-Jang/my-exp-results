# Daily Job Search Schedule

A practical, time‑boxed daily routine designed for job seekers to stay focused, productive, and avoid burnout.

## Core Time Blocks (9 am – 5 pm)
- **9:00 – 9:30** – Wake‑up ritual, light breakfast, quick review of the day’s plan.
- **9:30 – 12:00** – Primary job‑search work: select 1‑2 roles, research companies, draft applications.
- **12:00 – 13:00** – Lunch break (away from screens).
- **13:00 – 15:00** – Light tasks: polish cover letters, update the [[concepts/application-tracking-spreadsheet]], send follow‑ups.
- **15:00 – 16:00** – Physical break or walk to reset focus.
- **16:00 – 17:00** – Final quick pass: check inbox, note new opportunities, wrap up.

## Recommended Output
- **2‑3 tailored applications per day** – enough to maintain quality without overload.

## Integration with Other Job‑Search Tools
- Pair the schedule with the [[concepts/cover-letter-checklist]] to ensure each application meets key criteria.
- Use the [[concepts/follow-up-cadence]] to plan follow‑up actions after the 4 pm wrap‑up.
