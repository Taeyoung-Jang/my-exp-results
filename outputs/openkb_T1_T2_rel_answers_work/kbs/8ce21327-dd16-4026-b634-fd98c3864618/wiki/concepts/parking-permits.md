# Parking Permits

**Definition**: A parking permit is an authorized credential issued by a residential community (often managed by the [[entities/hoa]] or [[entities/property-manager]]) that allows a specific vehicle to park in designated spaces.

## Key Details from [[summaries/session-31_2024-12-13]]
- **Quantity per Unit**: Communities typically issue a limited number of permits per unit; the exact count should be confirmed with management.
- **Waitlist Issues**: When additional permits are needed, a waitlist can be long, prompting residents to inquire about interim solutions.
- **Temporary / Paid Options**: Residents may ask whether a paid extra permit or a short‑term/temporary permit is available while on the waitlist.
- **Guest Parking Interaction**: Understanding the relationship between resident permits and [[concepts/guest-parking]] limits (hours, days) helps avoid violations.
- **Documentation**: Request the parking addendum, a map of assigned and guest spaces, and any policy sheets to have written confirmation.
- **Towing Considerations**: Knowledge of the [[concepts/towing-policy]] (warnings, authorization, towing company) is essential when parking outside permitted zones.

## Common Questions Residents Should Ask
1. How many [[concepts/parking-permits]] are issued per unit?
2. Is there a paid option for an extra permit while on the waitlist?
3. Are temporary or transitional permits available during move‑in week?
4. What are the limits for [[concepts/guest-parking]] (e.g., hourly or daily restrictions)?
5. What is the specific towing policy, including warning procedures and authorized tow companies (e.g., [[entities/tow-company]])?
6. Which documents should be provided for reference (parking addendum, space maps, tow‑company info)?
