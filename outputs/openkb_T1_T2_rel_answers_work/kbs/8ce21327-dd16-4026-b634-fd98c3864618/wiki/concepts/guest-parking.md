# Guest Parking

**Definition**: Guest parking refers to designated parking spaces or permits that allow visitors to a residential unit to park on community property without violating HOA or property‑management rules.

## Core Elements

1. **Permit Limits** – Many HOAs and property managers allocate a set number of permits per unit.  The user in [[summaries/session-31_2024-12-13]] is confirming how many permits are issued per unit and whether extra permits can be purchased or temporarily issued.
2. **Time Restrictions** – Guest passes often have hourly or daily caps.  The email draft asks for exact limits (e.g., maximum hours per day, consecutive days allowed).
3. **Overflow Options** – When guest spots are full, residents need guidance on alternative parking for visitors.  The user requests clarification on where guests should park in that situation.
4. **Move‑In Exceptions** – Some communities provide short‑term accommodations such as loading zones or reserved move‑in spots. These are related to guest parking because they affect where visitors and movers can temporarily park.
5. **Documentation** – Residents should request written details: the parking addendum, a map of assigned and guest spaces, and the tow‑company policy.

## Related Concepts

## Practical Guidance for Residents

- **Ask Explicitly**: Verify the number of permits per unit, the guest‑pass time limits, and any paid options for extra permits.
- **Request Documentation**: Secure the parking addendum, space maps, and tow‑company contact info in writing.
- **Clarify Exceptions**: Inquire about move‑in week accommodations and where visitors should park if standard guest spots are full.

By obtaining clear answers and written documentation, residents can avoid inadvertent violations, towing, or fines.
