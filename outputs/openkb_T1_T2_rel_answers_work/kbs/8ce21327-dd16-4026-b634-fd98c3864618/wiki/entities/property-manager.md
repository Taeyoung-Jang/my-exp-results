# Property Manager

**Type:** organization

The Property Manager is the party responsible for day‑to‑day operations of a residential building, including enforcement of parking rules, issuance of permits, and coordination with the HOA and tow companies.

## Role in session‑31_2024-12-13
In the conversation documented in [[summaries/session-31_2024-12-13]], the user prepares to move into a unit that receives a limited number of [[concepts/parking-permits]]. They draft two email versions—one aimed at the Property Manager and another at the HOA—to clarify:
- The exact number of permits issued per unit.
- Limits and conditions for [[concepts/guest-parking]] (hours, days, and overflow handling).
- Availability of paid or temporary permit options while on the waitlist.
- Details of the [[concepts/towing-policy]], including warning procedures, authorization, and the tow company's role.
- Required supporting documents such as the parking addendum, space maps, and tow‑company contact information.
- Possible short‑term accommodations during move‑in week (loading zones, reserved spots, grace periods).

These inquiries illustrate the Property Manager’s involvement in communicating parking policies, coordinating with the [[entities/hoa]] for rule enforcement, and liaising with the [[entities/tow-company]] for towing services.
