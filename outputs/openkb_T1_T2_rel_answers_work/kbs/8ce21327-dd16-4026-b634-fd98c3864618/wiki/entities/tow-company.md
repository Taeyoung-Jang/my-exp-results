# Tow Company

**Type:** Organization

**Description:**
The Tow Company is the external service provider responsible for vehicle removal and towing enforcement within the residential community referenced in [[summaries/session-31_2024-12-13]]. It works under the authority of the [[entities/hoa]] and may coordinate with the [[entities/property-manager]] for operational matters.

**Key Facts from the Document:**
- The resident is asked to request the tow company's contact information as part of the documentation package.
- Understanding the community's [[concepts/towing-policy]] is essential, as the Tow Company enforces that policy, including warning procedures, authorization protocols, and the identity of the contractor.
- The Tow Company may be listed in the parking addendum and map of assigned/guest spaces that the resident plans to request.

**Related Concepts & Entities:**
- [[concepts/towing-policy]] – Governs how and when the Tow Company can act.
- [[entities/hoa]] – The homeowner association that contracts the Tow Company.
- [[entities/property-manager]] – May liaise with the Tow Company for day‑to‑day issues.

**Relevant Documents:**
- Parking addendum (requested from the property manager or HOA)
- Map of assigned and guest parking spaces
- Official tow‑company policy sheet
