# Homeowners Association (HOA)

## Overview
The HOA is the governing body for a residential community that establishes and enforces rules and policies for residents, common areas, and shared services. In the context of the move‑in parking inquiry (see [[summaries/session-31_2024-12-13]]), the HOA is the authority that defines the number of [[concepts/parking-permits]] per unit, guest‑parking limits, and the community’s [[concepts/towing-policy]].

## Key Facts from Session 31
- **Parking permits:** The HOA limits each unit to a set number of permits, and there is a long waitlist for any additional permits.
- **Guest parking:** The HOA sets specific limits on guest‑pass usage (hours/days) and provides guidance on where visitors may park if guest spaces are full.
- **Towing policy:** The HOA outlines the process for warnings, authorization of tow actions, and identifies the responsible tow service.
- **Documentation:** Residents are advised to request the parking addendum, a map of assigned and guest spaces, and tow‑company contact information from the HOA.
- **Move‑in accommodations:** The HOA may offer short‑term exceptions such as loading zones, reserved move‑in spots, or a grace period during the moving week.
