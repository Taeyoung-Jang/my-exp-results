## Summary of session-31_2024-12-13

**Context** – The user is preparing to move into a new residence that issues only a few parking permits per unit. They need a concise, yet assertive, email to clarify parking rules and avoid fines or towing.

**Key Questions to Include**
- How many [[concepts/parking-permits]] are issued per unit?
- What are the limits for [[concepts/guest-parking]] (hours/days)?
- Is there a paid option or temporary permit for an extra spot while on the waitlist?
- What is the [[concepts/towing-policy]] (warning process, authorization, responsible party)?
- Which documents should be provided: parking addendum, map of assigned/guest spaces, tow‑company details?
- Are there any move‑in accommodations (loading zones, reserved move‑in spots, grace period) for the move‑in week?
- Where should visitors park if guest spots are full?

**Email Versions**
1. **Property‑Manager‑Focused** – Emphasizes operational details, paid/temporary options, and immediate move‑in logistics.
2. **HOA‑Focused** – Centers on community rules, enforcement, and formal documentation.

Both versions maintain a polite but firm tone and request the same set of supporting documents to ensure clear written confirmation.

**Concepts Highlighted**

- [[concepts/towing-policy]]
- move-in accommodations

- HOA rules
