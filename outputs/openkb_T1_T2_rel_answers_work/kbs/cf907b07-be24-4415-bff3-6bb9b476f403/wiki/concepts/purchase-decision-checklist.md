# Purchase Decision Checklist

The **Purchase Decision Checklist** is a practical tool for evaluating potential purchases, helping individuals distinguish between genuine needs and impulsive wants. It originated from the conversation in [[summaries/session-07_2024-04-17]] and aligns with broader concepts such as [[concepts/spending-thresholds]] and the [[concepts/wait-period-rule]].

## Core Questions

1. **Will I realistically use this often?**  
   Assesses functional utility over time.

2. **Would I still want it a week from now?**  
   Tests durability of the initial desire.

3. **Am I motivated by stress, boredom, or FOMO?**  
   Identifies emotional triggers that often drive impulse buying.

4. **Does it solve a real problem I have?**  
   Ensures the purchase addresses a concrete need.

5. **Do I already own something that does the job?**  
   Checks for redundancy and potential clutter.

6. **If I had the cash now, would I still trade it for this?**  
   Forces a mental accounting of the monetary trade‑off.

7. **Can I comfortably explain the purchase to a trusted person?**  
   Provides a social accountability check.

8. **Is “limited‑time” pressure influencing me?**  
   Reveals sales‑driven urgency tactics.

9. **Will it add clutter or maintenance to my life?**  
   Directly ties to a clutter‑avoidance mindset.

10. **Is the cost justified by expected value or joy?**  
    Balances price against anticipated benefit.

## How It Connects to Other Concepts

- The checklist works hand‑in‑hand with the **[[concepts/wait-period-rule]]**, which adds a temporal buffer for medium and large purchases.
- It informs the setting of **[[concepts/spending-thresholds]]**, especially when tied to an individual's **[[entities/monthly-take-home-income]]**.
- By surfacing emotional drivers, it helps mitigate impulse purchases and supports a minimalist, low‑clutter lifestyle.

Use this checklist before any significant purchase to create a moment of reflection and ensure that spending aligns with long‑term goals and values.
