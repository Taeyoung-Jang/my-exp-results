# Summary of Session 39 (2025‑04‑17)

**Topic:** Building a simple, phone‑friendly [[concepts/house-brain]] system for managing household tasks and essentials, with a specialized version for new‑parent life.

## Core Ideas
- **House Brain concept** – a single note containing three core sections: *Running Low*, *Quick/Short Tasks*, and *Weekly Essentials* [[concepts/house-brain]].
- **Habit loop** – pair a brief daily check with an existing routine (e.g., morning coffee, bedtime) to reinforce consistency [[concepts/daily-habit-loop]].
- **Low‑maintenance design** – prioritize “how long it takes” over priority, using simple symbols (★, !) for urgency.

## List Structure (General)
1. **Running Low** – items that are near depletion (e.g., milk, dish soap).  
2. **Quick Tasks (≤5 min)** – small actions that can be done instantly.  
3. **Short Tasks** – slightly longer items requiring a few minutes.  
4. **Weekly Essentials** – recurring chores to prevent buildup.

## Daily & Weekly Routine
- **Daily:** Open the note at a fixed cue, skim sections, clear completed items, flag urgent ones. Aim < 1 min.  
- **Weekly:** Spend ~2 min reviewing the *Weekly Essentials* and converting *Running Low* entries into orders or pick‑up plans.  
- **Reminder strategy:** Set a tiny phone notification aligned with the chosen cue until the habit sticks, following the habit loop principle.

## Baby‑Specific Enhancements
- Added a **Baby Essentials** section with explicit **reorder triggers** for common items (diapers, wipes, formula, etc.) [[concepts/reorder-triggers]] and linked to the entity [[entities/baby-essentials]].  
- Introduced a **Next‑Size Tracker** to monitor upcoming size changes for diapers, clothes, and shoes, linked to the entity [[entities/next-size-tracker]], with weekly or monthly checks.  
- Integrated baby‑related tasks into the existing framework (e.g., “restock diaper caddy,” “wash bottles”).

## Practical Takeaways
- Keep everything in **one note** to avoid hunting across apps.  
- Use **simple triggers** (e.g., “half pack left”) to eliminate decision fatigue.  
- Leverage **existing daily anchors** for habit formation.  
- Periodically audit the *Next‑Size Tracker* to stay ahead of growth milestones.
