## Typical Items Listed
- Diapers (current size) – add when the open pack is halfway
- Diapers (next size) – check weekly, add when current size feels snug
- Wipes – add when only one unopened pack remains
- Diaper cream – add when tube is under ¼
- Bottles/nipples – add when down to one clean spare
- Formula – add when one unopened container left
- Breast‑milk storage bags – add when box down to last 10
- Pacifiers – add when down to one backup
- Gas drops – add when bottle is under ⅓
- Swaddles/sleep sacks – add when only one backup is clean
- Changing‑table restock (onesies, burp cloths) – add when drawer is under half full

## Usage Guidelines
1. **Daily Check:** Review the Baby Essentials section during a fixed cue (e.g., morning feed) and mark any items that meet their trigger.
2. **Weekly Review:** Update the Next‑Size Tracker and convert triggered items into orders or shopping plans.
3. **Keep It Simple:** Use symbols like ★ or ! for urgent items; avoid re‑ordering large quantities unless a threshold is reached.
