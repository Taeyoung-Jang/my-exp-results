# Monthly Take‑Home Income

**Entity Type:** other

**Definition**: The amount of money an individual receives each month after taxes and other mandatory deductions. It represents the net income available for personal expenses and savings.

**Key Facts from [[summaries/session-07_2024-04-17]]**
- Used as the reference point to calculate spending thresholds for purchase decisions.
- Example figure: **$3,500** take‑home per month.
- Threshold tiers derived from this income:
  - **Small purchases**: ≈ 1 % of monthly take‑home (≈ $35) – no wait period.
  - **Medium purchases**: ≈ 3 % of monthly take‑home (≈ $105) – 48‑hour wait.
  - **Big purchases**: 7‑10 % of monthly take‑home (≈ $245‑$350) – 1‑week wait.
