# Headache Management for Caffeine Reduction

**Definition**: Practical tactics to prevent or relieve headaches that can arise during a gradual reduction of caffeine consumption.

---

## Core Principles
1. **Small "rescue" doses** – If a headache appears, take a minimal amount of caffeine (e.g., a few sips of regular coffee or a tiny half‑caff serving, roughly ≤20 mg). This should be **smaller** than the usual dose you are cutting back from, so it eases symptoms without undoing the taper.
2. **Hydration** – Increase water intake throughout the day. Dehydration can amplify headache intensity.
3. **Nutrition** – Eat a balanced snack that includes protein or complex carbs (nuts, yogurt, fruit). Blood‑sugar dips often coincide with caffeine withdrawal.
4. **Movement** – A short walk, gentle stretching, or light activity can improve circulation and reduce headache discomfort.
5. **Gradual adjustment** – Follow a structured [[concepts/taper_plan]] (e.g., week‑by‑week swaps like half‑caff, smaller servings, decaf) rather than abrupt cuts, which lowers the chance of severe headaches.
