# Caffeine Taper Plan

A **caffeine taper plan** outlines a step‑by‑step reduction of caffeine intake to avoid withdrawal symptoms while maintaining the benefits of a morning boost. The plan is grounded in a [[concepts/healthy_caffeine_routine]] that balances total milligrams, timing, and personal sensitivity.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Cutoff Time** – Stop caffeine roughly eight hours before bedtime. For a typical 10:30 pm bedtime, the target cutoff is around **1 pm** (or latest **2 pm**) to protect [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]].
2. **Gradual Reduction** – Use smaller portions, half‑caff blends, or decaf alternatives for later‑day drinks. This aligns with the [[concepts/taper_plan]] principle of "one adjustment every few days".
3. **Rescue Strategies** – If headaches or low energy appear, apply a modest "rescue dose" (e.g., a few sips of regular coffee) and support with hydration, food, or light activity, as described in [[concepts/headache_management]].

---

## Week‑by‑Week Schedule (Approx. mg per Day)
| Week | Morning drink (≈12 oz [[entities/drip_coffee]]) | Late‑morning drink | Afternoon / Late‑day drink | Weekend energy drink | Approx. total |
|------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------|---------------------------|----------------------|---------------|
| **1** | 100 mg (unchanged) | Half‑caff (≈50 mg) | Green tea (≈25 mg) after lunch | Energy drink before noon (≈80 mg) | ~175 mg |
| **2** | 100 mg | Small regular coffee or weaker brew (≈60‑80 mg) | Switch green tea to decaf/herbal (≈0‑5 mg) | Same as Week 1 or replace with small coffee | ~160‑180 mg |
| **3** | 100 mg | Small half‑caff or reduced‑strength coffee (≈40‑60 mg) | Decaf/herbal remains | Small coffee or none | ~140‑160 mg |
| **4** | 100 mg | Decaf or skip a few days | Decaf/herbal only | Small coffee or none | ~100 mg |

**Key drinks referenced**: [[entities/drip_coffee]], [[entities/latte]], [[entities/green_tea]], [[entities/energy_drink]].

---

## Practical Tips for Monitoring
- **Cup‑count method** – Treat one standard cup as ~100 mg, half‑cup as ~50 mg. No need for precise milligram tracking.
- **Consistent mug** – Use the same mug for the morning coffee to create a stable baseline.
- **Simple logging** – Note “+1 cup” or “+½ cup” for any additional drinks.

---

## Managing Side Effects
When a headache or fatigue arises:
1. Take a **rescue dose** ≤ 20 mg (e.g., a few sips of regular coffee).
2. Drink water and eat a protein‑rich snack.
3. Take a short walk or stretch.
4. Keep the rescue dose smaller than the previous regular dose to stay on the taper trajectory.
