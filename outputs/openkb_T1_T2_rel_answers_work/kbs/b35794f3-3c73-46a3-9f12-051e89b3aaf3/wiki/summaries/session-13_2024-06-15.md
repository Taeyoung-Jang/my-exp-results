# Summary of Session 13 (2024‑06‑15)

**Main contribution:** A tailored plan to reduce daily caffeine intake while preserving a morning coffee habit, including a recommended cutoff time, week‑by‑week taper steps, approximate milligram totals, and strategies for handling withdrawal headaches.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/healthy_caffeine_routine]] – balancing caffeine amount, timing, and personal sensitivity.  
- [[concepts/caffeine_cutoff]] – stopping caffeine ~8 hours before bedtime (≈1 pm for a 10:30 pm bedtime).  
- [[concepts/taper_plan]] – gradual reduction using half‑caff, smaller servings, and decaf swaps.  
- [[concepts/headache_management]] – rescue doses, hydration, food, and light activity.  
- [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]] – aligning caffeine timing with sleep quality.

## Recommended Cutoff Time
- **Target:** No caffeine after **1 pm** (ideal) or **2 pm** (acceptable) to protect sleep.

## Week‑by‑Week Taper Schedule (approx. mg per day)

| Week | Morning [[entities/drip_coffee]] (12 oz) | Late‑morning drink | Afternoon/late‑day drink | Weekend [[entities/energy_drink]] | Approx. total caffeine |
|------|------------------------------------------|-------------------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | 100 mg (unchanged) | **Half‑caff** (≈50 mg) | [[entities/green_tea]] (≈25 mg) after lunch | Energy drink before noon (≈80 mg) | ~175 mg (weekday) |
| 2 | 100 mg | Small regular coffee or weaker brew (≈60‑80 mg) | Switch green tea to **decaf/herbal** (≈0‑5 mg) | Same as Week 1 or replace with small coffee | ~160‑180 mg |
| 3 | 100 mg | Small half‑caff or reduced‑strength coffee (≈40‑60 mg) | Decaf/herbal remains | Small coffee or no energy drink | ~140‑160 mg |
| 4 | 100 mg | **Decaf** or skip a few days | Decaf/herbal only | Small coffee or skip | ~100 mg (most days) |

## Handling Headaches or Low Energy
1. **Rescue dose:** a few sips of regular coffee or a tiny half‑caff serving (≤20 mg).  
2. **Hydration:** drink water throughout the day.  
3. **Nutrition:** eat a balanced snack (protein/fiber) when symptoms appear.  
4. **Movement:** short walk or light stretching to boost alertness.  
5. Keep rescue doses smaller than the previous regular dose to stay on the taper trajectory.

## Practical Tracking Tips
- Use a simple “cup count” (1 cup ≈ 100 mg, ½ cup ≈ 50 mg) rather than exact milligrams.  
- Maintain a consistent mug for the morning coffee to treat it as a baseline.  
- Log any extra drinks as “+1 cup” or “+½ cup” to stay aware of total intake.

By following this structured taper, you can maintain the morning coffee ritual, stay under the recommended caffeine ceiling, and improve sleep quality without severe withdrawal symptoms.
