# Drip Coffee (product)

**What it is**: A standard home‑brewed, drip‑filtered coffee typically served in a 12 oz mug. In the context of the caffeine‑reduction plan, it provides the primary daily caffeine dose of roughly 100 mg.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]**
- Serves as the unchanged morning caffeine anchor; the taper plan keeps this drink constant throughout all weeks.
- Estimated caffeine content: ~80–120 mg, simplified to 100 mg for tracking.
- The recommendation is to keep the size and strength consistent to maintain a baseline for weekly reductions of other caffeine sources.
