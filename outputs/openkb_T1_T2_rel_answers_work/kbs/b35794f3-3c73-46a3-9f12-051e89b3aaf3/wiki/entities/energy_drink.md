# Energy Drink

Energy Drink is a commercially packaged, carbonated beverage that typically contains a high dose of caffeine, along with sugar, taurine, and other stimulants. It is marketed for quick energy boosts and is often consumed in the afternoon or evening.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]
- In the user's weekend routine, an energy drink is occasionally consumed in the afternoon.
- The assistant recommends limiting consumption to before noon or swapping it for a smaller coffee to support a healthier caffeine routine.
- The suggested taper plan suggests cutting back on the energy drink or reducing its portion size during the fourth week of the program.

## Relationship to Caffeine Management Concepts
- Aligns with [[concepts/healthy_caffeine_routine]] by being a significant source of daily caffeine that may need moderation.
- Influences the [[concepts/caffeine_cutoff]] recommendation; consuming it after 1 pm can interfere with sleep quality.
- Adjusting or eliminating the drink is part of the [[concepts/taper_plan]] to reduce overall caffeine intake.
- If withdrawal symptoms arise, [[concepts/headache_management]] strategies (e.g., hydration, small rescue doses) apply.
- Proper timing contributes to better [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]].

## Comparison with Other Entities
- Unlike [[entities/drip_coffee]], which is usually consumed in the morning, energy drinks are often taken later in the day and contain higher caffeine per serving.
- Compared to [[entities/green_tea]] and [[entities/latte]], energy drinks generally have higher caffeine and sugar content, making them a larger concern for over‑consumption.

## Practical Guidance (from the session)
1. **Cutoff Time**: Aim to avoid energy drinks after 1 pm to respect the eight‑hour sleep buffer.
2. **Taper Steps**: Replace the weekend energy drink with a small coffee or a decaf option during weeks 2‑4 of the taper.
3. **Headache Mitigation**: If a headache occurs after reducing the drink, use a small “rescue” caffeine dose (e.g., a few sips of regular coffee) and stay hydrated.
4. **Tracking**: Count the energy drink as roughly 80 mg of caffeine when estimating daily totals.
