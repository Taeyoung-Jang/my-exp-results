# Latte

Latte is a coffee‑based beverage that combines one or more shots of espresso with steamed milk, often topped with a thin layer of foam. A typical 12 oz latte delivers roughly **60–80 mg of caffeine**, though the exact amount depends on the espresso dose and bean type.

## Mention in Session 13
In the conversation dated 2024‑06‑15, the user reports drinking a **latte around 11 am** as their second caffeine source of the day, following an early‑morning drip coffee. The assistant’s taper plan recommends:
- Swapping the latte for a **half‑caff** version or a smaller regular latte during the second week.
- Eventually moving to a **decaf** or skipping the latte on some days by week four.

These adjustments aim to reduce total daily caffeine while preserving the morning coffee ritual.
