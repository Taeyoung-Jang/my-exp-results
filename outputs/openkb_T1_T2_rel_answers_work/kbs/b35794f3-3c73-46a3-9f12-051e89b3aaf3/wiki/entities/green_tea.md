# Green Tea (product)

**Description:** Green tea is a lightly caffeinated beverage made from the leaves of *Camellia sinensis*. In the context of the session‑13 discussion it provides roughly 25 mg of caffeine per serving.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]
- **Typical use:** Consumed around 3 pm on weekdays to combat afternoon fatigue.
- **Caffeine content:** Approximately 25 mg per cup, contributing to the daily total in the proposed [[concepts/taper_plan]].
- **Role in the taper:** The plan suggests moving the green‑tea slot earlier (right after lunch) and eventually swapping it for a decaf or herbal alternative to stay within the recommended [[concepts/caffeine_cutoff]] (no caffeine after ~1 pm) and support better [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]].
- **Withdrawal considerations:** If reducing green‑tea leads to a headache, the [[concepts/headache_management]] advice recommends a small “rescue” dose, hydration, or a light snack rather than reverting to the original amount.

## Relationships to Other Entities
- Often paired with a morning [[entities/drip_coffee]] as the primary caffeine source.
- Compared to an [[entities/energy_drink]] which contains a much higher caffeine dose and is advised to be limited to before noon.
- Similar in routine to a mid‑morning [[entities/latte]], which the taper plan also recommends reducing.

## Relevance to Concepts
- Part of a [[concepts/healthy_caffeine_routine]] that balances intake, timing, and personal sensitivity.
- Adjustments to green‑tea consumption are a concrete step in the overall [[concepts/taper_plan]].
