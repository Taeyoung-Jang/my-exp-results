# Bright Light Lamp (product)

A **bright light lamp** is a portable artificial light source that emits high‑intensity illumination, often used in the morning to simulate natural sunlight.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-39_2024-12-13]]
- Recommended as an alternative when natural morning light is unavailable or insufficient.
- Exposure to the lamp for at least ten minutes soon after waking helps signal the brain to start the day, supporting a shift in the [[concepts/circadian_rhythm]].
- It works in concert with other [[concepts/sleep_hygiene]] practices such as consistent wake‑up times and evening light reduction.
- By providing a strong light cue, the lamp can facilitate a [[concepts/gradual_shift]] of the sleep schedule, especially when combined with regular morning exposure.
- Maintaining some light exposure on weekends helps with [[concepts/weekend_consistency]], preventing the rhythm from slipping back.

## Typical Usage
1. Turn on the lamp shortly after waking (ideally within 30 minutes).
2. Sit or stand within the recommended distance (usually 16–24 inches) for 10–30 minutes.
3. Use daily for best results; occasional use can still provide a noticeable boost on darker mornings.

## Benefits
- Improves alertness and mood in the early hours.
- Supports the body's internal clock without the need for pharmacological aids.
- Can be combined with other strategies like limiting evening screen exposure and consistent bedtime.
