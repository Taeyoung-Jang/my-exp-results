# Balanced Training Schedule

A **balanced training schedule** is a structured weekly routine that integrates multiple fitness modalities—typically cardio (e.g., swimming), strength training, optional easy cardio, and rest—while managing overall load to avoid burnout. The goal is to distribute effort across the week so that each session supports the others, preserves performance, and allows progressive adaptation.

## Core Principles

1. **Anchor Days** – Fixed cardio sessions (e.g., swim on Monday, Wednesday, Friday) serve as the schedule’s anchors.
2. **Strength Placement** – Heavy or moderate strength days are placed on days adjacent to, but not immediately before, the anchor cardio sessions (e.g., Tuesday and Saturday). A lighter technique‑focused strength day sits between cardio days (Thursday).
3. **Recovery** – At least one full rest or very light‑movement day (Sunday) is built in, with optional easy cardio placed on the lighter strength day to keep overall volume low.
4. **Effort Targeting** – Each lift is prescribed with an effort metric such as RPE or reps‑in‑reserve (RIR) to ensure sessions stay challenging but not overly taxing. See [[concepts/effort_target_rpe_rir]] for details.
5. **Load Adjustment** – Early signs of fatigue trigger load reductions, exercise trimming, or conversion of a strength day to pure mobility. This aligns with the recovery framework described in [[concepts/recovery_adjustments]].

## Example Weekly Template (from [[summaries/session-10_2025-06-16]])

| Day | Activity | Key Features |
|-----|----------|--------------|
| **Monday** | Swim (steady aerobic) | Main cardio anchor; moderate effort |
| **Tuesday** | Heavy Strength (45‑60 min) | Back squat, row, overhead press, RDL, face pulls; 1‑2 RIR |
| **Wednesday** | Swim (steady) | Aerobic focus, avoid excessive sprints |
| **Thursday** | Light Strength + Optional Easy Cardio | Split squats, light RDL, cable rows, lateral raises; 3 RIR; 20‑30 min easy cardio at conversational pace |
| **Friday** | Swim (moderate) | Keep intensity moderate to protect Saturday strength |
| **Saturday** | Moderate‑Heavy Strength | Deadlift, bench press, pull‑ups, seated DB press, core work; 1‑2 RIR |
| **Sunday** | Rest / Light Movement | Full rest or gentle walk/stretch; optional cardio if not done Thursday |

## Benefits
- **Performance Synergy** – Strength work complements swimming by targeting supporting muscle groups (legs, back, shoulders) without compromising swim quality.
- **Fatigue Management** – By alternating high‑ and low‑intensity days and providing a dedicated rest day, the schedule reduces cumulative fatigue.
- **Flexibility** – Optional cardio can be shifted to Sunday if Thursday feels too busy, preserving the overall balance.
- **Scalable Load** – Effort targets allow easy progression (increase weight, add a set) while staying within safe fatigue limits.

## Implementation Tips
- **Assess Availability** – Confirm how many days you can train and the length of each session.
- **Set Goals** – Define primary outcomes (e.g., general fitness, muscle gain, swim endurance) to prioritize exercise selection.
- **Monitor RIR** – Use a simple “reps in reserve” check after each set to stay within the prescribed effort zone.
- **Adjust Promptly** – If you notice lingering soreness or performance drops, apply the load‑adjustment rules from [[concepts/recovery_adjustments]].
