# Grocery Checklist for Quick Meals

A **grocery checklist** is a curated list of shelf‑stable and ready‑to‑heat items that let you assemble balanced meals with minimal cooking.  It supports the [[concepts/minimal_cooking]] approach and fits into a [[concepts/weeknight_meal_planning]] routine by reducing prep time to a few minutes each night.

## Core Items (as used in [[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]])

- **Protein**: [[entities/rotisserie_chicken]] – pre‑cooked, versatile, and can be portioned or frozen for later use.
- **Grains**: [[entities/microwave_rice_pouches]] – 8–9 oz packs that heat in under two minutes.
- **Vegetables**: [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]] – steam‑in‑bag mixes (e.g., broccoli, mixed stir‑fry) that require no chopping.
- **Salad**: [[entities/salad_kit]] – pre‑tossed greens that stay fresh for several days when resealed.
- **Carbohydrate Base**: [[entities/tortillas]] – medium‑size, keep well for wraps or quesadillas.
- **Dairy/Flavor**: [[entities/shredded_cheese]] – a bag of Mexican blend or mozzarella for meltable texture.
- **Legume**: [[entities/canned_beans]] – 15 oz can, drained and rinsed, adds protein and fiber.
- **Sauce/Dressing**: [[entities/vinaigrette]] – a neutral vinaigrette (lemon or balsamic) works across grain bowls, salads, and even quesadillas, providing a single versatile flavor component.

## How the Checklist Supports the Meal Plan

1. **Monday – Grain Bowl**: Combine half a [[entities/microwave_rice_pouches]] with half a [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]] and fresh chicken, drizzle with [[entities/vinaigrette]].
2. **Tuesday – Quesadilla**: Warm two [[entities/tortillas]] with [[entities/shredded_cheese]] and chicken; serve with remaining veggies.
3. **Wednesday – Salad**: Toss half a [[entities/salad_kit]] with chicken and a splash of [[entities/vinaigrette]].
4. **Thursday – Grain Bowl (Thawed Chicken)**: Use the second rice pouch, second veggie bag, and thawed chicken portion; finish with the same vinaigrette.
5. **Friday – Bean & Cheese Melt**: Heat two tortillas with [[entities/shredded_cheese]] and [[entities/canned_beans]]; serve the rest of the salad on the side.

## Storage Tip for Rotisserie Chicken

Split the chicken immediately: keep half in the fridge for the first three days and freeze the other half in a zip‑bag. Thaw the frozen portion in the refrigerator on Wednesday night so it’s ready for Thursday and Friday, avoiding a five‑day fridge window.

## Benefits

- **Speed**: All meals can be assembled in ≤10 minutes.
- **Flexibility**: A single vinaigrette provides flavor variety without extra purchases.
- **Reduced Waste**: Quantities are calibrated to the five‑day plan, minimizing leftovers.
- **Scalability**: The checklist can be expanded or trimmed to suit different dietary preferences while retaining the core principle of quick, low‑effort meals.
