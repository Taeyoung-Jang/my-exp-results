# Summary of Session 10 (2025‑06‑16)

**Purpose** – Provide a concrete, day‑by‑day fitness template that integrates three fixed swim sessions with two strength days, a lighter strength day, optional easy cardio, and recovery adjustments.

## Key Elements

- **Assessment questions** – training frequency, session length, injury status, goals, equipment, and current fitness level.  
- **Weekly structure** –  
  - *Swim*: Mon, Wed, Fri (main cardio anchors).  
  - *Strength*: Tue (heavy full‑body), Thu (lighter technique‑focus), Sat (moderately heavy).  
  - *Rest/Active recovery*: Sun (full rest or gentle movement).  
  This follows the [[concepts/balanced_training_schedule]] approach.  
- **Exercise prescription** – specific lifts, sets, reps, and effort targets using **RPE / reps‑in‑reserve (RIR)**, linked to [[concepts/effort_target_rpe_rir]].  
- **Optional cardio** – 20–30 min easy session best placed on Thursday (or Sunday) at a conversational effort.  
- **Adjustment guidelines** – reduce load, trim a main lift, or convert Thursday to mobility if signs of fatigue appear; see [[concepts/recovery_adjustments]].

## Weekly Template (≈45–60 min strength days)

### Monday – Swim
Steady aerobic swim; keep effort moderate.

### Tuesday – Heavy Strength
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Effort (RIR) |
|----------|------|------|--------------|
| Back Squat | 3‑4 | 5‑8 | 1‑2 |
| Barbell/Cable Row | 3 | 6‑10 | 2 |
| Overhead Press | 3 | 6‑8 | 2 |
| Romanian Deadlift | 2‑3 | 8‑12 | 2‑3 |
| Face Pulls | 2‑3 | 8‑12 | 2‑3 |

### Wednesday – Swim
Aerobic focus; optional short pickups.

### Thursday – Light Strength + Optional Cardio
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Effort (RIR) |
|----------|------|------|--------------|
| Split Squat / Lunges | 2‑3 | 8‑12 per leg | 3 |
| Light RDL or Hip‑hinge | 2‑3 | 10 | 3 |
| Cable Row / Lat Pulldown | 2‑3 | 10‑12 | 3 |
| Lateral Raises (DB) | 2‑3 | 12‑15 | 3 |

Add 20–30 min easy cardio (e.g., brisk walk, light bike) at a conversational pace.

### Friday – Swim
Moderate effort; avoid maximal sprints to preserve Saturday strength.

### Saturday – Moderate‑Heavy Strength
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Effort (RIR) |
|----------|------|------|--------------|
| Deadlift / Trap‑Bar | 3 | 4‑6 | 1‑2 |
| Bench Press (DB or Bar) | 3 | 6‑8 | 2 |
| Pull‑ups / Lat Pulldown | 3 | 6‑10 | 2 |
| Seated DB Press | 2‑3 | 8‑12 | 2‑3 |
| Core (Plank / Cable Chop) | 2‑3 | — | — |

### Sunday – Rest / Light Movement
Full rest or gentle walk/stretch; can host the optional cardio if not done Thursday.

## Recovery & Load Adjustment
- **Early signs of wear** – reduce weight by one increment, drop a main lift, or replace Thursday’s session with mobility work.  
- **Swim‑related fatigue** – lower swim intensity or shorten the set‑count on the affected day.  
- Goal: finish each session with 1‑2 reps in reserve, not exhausted, in line with [[concepts/recovery_adjustments]].
