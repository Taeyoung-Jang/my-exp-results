## Summary of Session 33 (2025‑09‑16)

- **Goal:** Provide a concrete, low‑effort 5‑day weeknight dinner plan that requires little to no cooking, includes a precise grocery checklist, and ensures food safety for [[entities/rotisserie_chicken]].

- **Meal Structure:**  
  - **Monday:** Grain bowl – half a [[entities/microwave_rice_pouches]] pouch, half a [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]] pack, fresh chicken, **versatile sauce**.  
  - **Tuesday:** Chicken‑cheese quesadilla with two [[entities/tortillas]], [[entities/shredded_cheese]], remaining fresh chicken, and the rest of the first veggie pack.  
  - **Wednesday:** Half‑kit salad with the remaining fresh chicken and a [[entities/salad_kit]].  
  - **Thursday:** Grain bowl using thawed frozen‑chicken portion, the second rice pouch, the second veggie pack, and the same sauce.  
  - **Friday:** Bean‑and‑cheese melt (tortillas, shredded cheese, [[entities/canned_beans]]) with the leftover salad and optional thawed chicken.

- **Grocery Checklist (exact amounts):**  
  - 1 [[entities/rotisserie_chicken]]  
  - 2 [[entities/microwave_rice_pouches]] (8–9 oz each)  
  - 2 [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]] (≈10–12 oz each)  
  - 1 [[entities/salad_kit]] (10–12 oz)  
  - 1 pack medium [[entities/tortillas]] (8‑count)  
  - 1 bag [[entities/shredded_cheese]] (≈8 oz)  
  - 1 can [[entities/canned_beans]] (15 oz)  
  - 1 versatile sauce/dressing (recommended: neutral [[entities/vinaigrette]])

- **Chicken Storage Strategy:**  
  - Immediately separate meat; keep half in the fridge for Mon‑Wed.  
  - Freeze the other half in a zip‑bag.  
  - Thaw frozen portion in the fridge on Wednesday night for use Thu‑Fri, avoiding a 5‑day fridge storage window.

- **Key Recommendations:**  
  - Use a **single versatile sauce** (e.g., lemon or balsamic vinaigrette) to simplify shopping and keep flavor flexibility across bowls, salads, and quesadillas.  
  - Choose microwave‑ready rice and steam‑in‑bag vegetables for minimal prep time.  
  - Employ bagged salad kits that stay fresh for several days when resealed.
