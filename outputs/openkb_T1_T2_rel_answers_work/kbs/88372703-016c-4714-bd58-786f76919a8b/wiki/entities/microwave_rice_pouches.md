## Microwave Rice Pouches

**Microwave rice pouches** are pre‑cooked rice portions packaged for quick heating in a microwave. They require no additional cooking, making them ideal for low‑effort weeknight meals.

### Role in the 5‑day plan
- Used as the base grain for the Monday and Thursday grain bowls in the **[[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]]** plan.
- Paired with **[[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]]**, **[[entities/rotisserie_chicken]]**, and a versatile sauce to create balanced meals.
- Provides a fast, portion‑controlled carbohydrate source without the need for a stovetop.

### Benefits
- **Speed:** Ready in 90‑120 seconds, perfect for busy evenings.
- **Shelf‑stable:** Long‑lasting until opened, reducing waste.
- **Portion control:** Typically 8–9 oz per pouch, matching the quantities recommended in the grocery checklist.

### Integration with other items
- Complements **[[entities/tortillas]]** and **[[entities/shredded_cheese]]** for a quick quesadilla fallback.
- Works alongside **[[entities/canned_beans]]** and **[[entities/vinaigrette]]** for a vegetarian grain bowl.
- Fits into the broader **[[concepts/weeknight_meal_planning]]** and **[[concepts/minimal_cooking]]** strategies, reducing prep time while maintaining a balanced diet.

### Purchasing tips
- Look for brands that offer single‑serve pouches (e.g., Ben’s Original, Trader Joe’s, Seeds of Change).
- Choose plain or lightly seasoned varieties to keep the sauce flexible.
