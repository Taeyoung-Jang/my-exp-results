# Medium Flour Tortillas

Medium flour tortillas (typically sold in an 8‑count pack) are a staple in the 5‑day weeknight dinner plan outlined in [[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]]. They provide a fast, low‑effort base for quesadillas, bean‑and‑cheese melts, and can be paired with [[entities/rotisserie_chicken]], [[entities/shredded_cheese]], and [[entities/canned_beans]].

**Key points**
- **Quantity:** One 8‑count pack is sufficient for the weekly plan.
- **Uses:** Quesadillas on Tuesday, bean‑and‑cheese melt on Friday, optional warm wrap for leftovers.
- **Storage:** Keep refrigerated; lasts the week.
