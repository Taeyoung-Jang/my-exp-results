# Rotisserie Chicken

**Type:** product

**What it is**
A fully cooked rotisserie chicken purchased from a grocery store, typically seasoned and ready to eat cold or reheated. It provides a convenient source of protein that requires no cooking, making it ideal for low‑effort weeknight meals.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]]**
- Used as the primary protein in a 5‑day dinner plan that emphasizes [[concepts/minimal_cooking]] and [[concepts/weeknight_meal_planning]].
- The plan calls for **1 rotisserie chicken** total.
- Storage strategy: split the chicken immediately; keep half refrigerated for the first three nights (Monday–Wednesday) and freeze the other half. Thaw the frozen portion on Wednesday night for use on Thursday and Friday.
- Serves multiple dish types: grain bowls with [[entities/microwave_rice_pouches]] and [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]], chicken‑cheese quesadillas with [[entities/tortillas]] and [[entities/shredded_cheese]], and salads with [[entities/salad_kit]].
- Paired with a single versatile sauce (e.g., a vinaigrette) to keep the grocery list minimal, linking to the broader [[concepts/grocery_checklist]] approach.

**Practical tips**
1. **Immediate portioning** – remove all meat from the carcass as soon as you bring the chicken home.
2. **Freezing** – place the half you won’t use right away in a zip‑lock bag, squeeze out air, and freeze. It thaws well in the refrigerator overnight.
3. **Reheating** – microwave in short bursts (30‑45 s) or warm in a skillet to avoid drying out the meat.
4. **Safety** – keep refrigerated chicken for no more than 3‑4 days; freezing extends shelf‑life for the remainder of the week.

**Usage in meal plan**
- **Monday:** Fresh chicken in a grain bowl with rice and veggies.
- **Tuesday:** Fresh chicken in a quesadilla.
- **Wednesday:** Fresh chicken added to a salad kit.
- **Thursday:** Thawed chicken added to a second grain bowl.
- **Friday:** Optional extra thawed chicken in a bean‑and‑cheese melt or served on the side of the remaining salad.
