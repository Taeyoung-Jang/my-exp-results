# Steam‑In‑Bag Frozen Vegetables

Steam‑in‑bag frozen vegetables are packaged portions of assorted vegetables that can be cooked simply by microwaving the sealed bag, which releases steam and cooks the produce without added water. They are a convenient, shelf‑stable protein‑free side or component for quick meals.

## Key Facts from Session 33 (2025‑09‑16)
- **Purpose in the 5‑day plan:** Provides a fast, no‑prep vegetable option for grain bowls, quesadillas, and salads. Two 10‑12 oz packs are bought to cover the week.
- **Storage:** Frozen; keep in the freezer until needed. Can be kept in the pantry for months.
- **Usage examples:**
  - Monday: half of the first bag mixed into a grain bowl.
  - Tuesday: the remainder of the first bag served as a warm side for a quesadilla.
  - Thursday: the whole second bag added to another grain bowl.
- **Benefits:** Minimal cooking effort, consistent nutrition, and long shelf‑life.
