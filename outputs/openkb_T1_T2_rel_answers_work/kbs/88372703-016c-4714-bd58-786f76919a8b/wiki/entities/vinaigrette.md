# Versatile Vinaigrette Dressing

A ready‑to‑use, neutral‑flavored vinaigrette (often lemon‑ or balsamic‑based) that can flavor a variety of quick‑prep meals.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]]
- **Purpose:** Serves as the single sauce for all five dinner nights, eliminating the need for multiple condiments.
- **Versatility:** Works in grain bowls, salads, quesadillas, and warmed vegetable mixes without clashing with other ingredients.
- **Recommended Type:** A simple vinaigrette (e.g., lemon or balsamic) that provides a light, fresh bite.
- **Quantity:** One bottle is sufficient for the week’s plan.

## How It Fits Into the Meal Plan
- **Monday & Thursday (grain bowls):** Drizzled over microwave rice pouches, frozen veggies, and rotisserie chicken.
- **Tuesday (quesadilla):** Lightly spread inside the tortilla before grilling for subtle flavor.
- **Wednesday (salad):** Tossed with the chopped salad kit for a classic dressing.
- **Friday (bean‑and‑cheese melt):** Added as a finishing drizzle to enhance the melt.

## Related Concepts & Entities
- Part of the broader [[concepts/minimal_cooking]] approach by reducing prep steps.
- Integral to the [[concepts/weeknight_meal_planning]] and [[concepts/grocery_checklist]] for low‑effort dinners.
- Often purchased alongside other pantry staples such as [[entities/tortillas]], [[entities/shredded_cheese]], and [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]].

## Practical Tips
- Store in the refrigerator after opening; it stays fresh for several weeks.
- If you prefer a sweeter profile, choose a balsamic‑based vinaigrette; for a sharper taste, opt for a lemon vinaigrette.
- A small amount goes a long way—use sparingly to avoid overpowering the dish.
