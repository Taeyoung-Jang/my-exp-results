# Canned Beans (Black or Pinto)

- **What it is**: Shelf‑stable, pre‑cooked beans (black or pinto) sold in 15 oz cans.  Ready to drain, rinse, and add to meals with no cooking required.
- **Key role in the session‑33 plan**: Listed in the grocery checklist for the 5‑day weeknight meal plan.  Used on Thursday as a protein boost in the grain bowl and on Friday as the main protein in a bean‑and‑cheese melt.
- **Why it matters**: Provides a quick, inexpensive, and versatile protein that aligns with [[concepts/minimal_cooking]] and [[concepts/weeknight_meal_planning]].  It complements other staples such as [[entities/rotisserie_chicken]], [[entities/shredded_cheese]], [[entities/tortillas]], [[entities/steam_in_bag_veggies]], and a single versatile sauce like [[entities/vinaigrette]].
- **Storage**: Keep unopened cans in a cool, dry pantry.  Once opened, transfer leftovers to a sealed container and refrigerate; use within 3‑4 days.
- **Related concepts**: Part of a broader [[concepts/grocery_checklist]] for low‑effort meals and can be swapped with other canned legumes for variety.
- **Reference**: See the detailed 5‑day plan and quantities in [[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]].
