# Shredded Cheese (Mexican Blend)

**Type:** product

## Overview
Shredded Mexican blend cheese is a ready‑to‑use, pre‑shredded cheese mix (typically a blend of cheddar, Monterey Jack, and sometimes queso quesadilla) that adds flavor and meltability to fast meals.

## Role in the 5‑Day Meal Plan
- Used on **Tuesday** for chicken‑cheese quesadillas.
- Included on **Friday** for the bean‑and‑cheese melt.
- Provides a quick source of protein and calcium without any cooking prep.

## Quantity Recommended
- **8 oz bag** (approximately one standard retail package) is sufficient for the entire week’s meals as outlined in [[summaries/session-33_2025-09-16]].

## Storage Tips
- Keep refrigerated; unopened packages last several weeks.
- Once opened, reseal tightly or transfer to an airtight container to maintain freshness.
