## Stress Management

**Definition**: Strategies that lower perceived stress during a commute by addressing friction points and introducing pleasant experiences.

### Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]])
- **Identify friction** – traffic jams, crowded transit, unpredictable wait times.
- **Low‑effort levers** – timing shifts, route tweaks, and later mode changes are covered under [[concepts/commute-optimization]].
- **Metrics** – track stress using the subjective scale from [[concepts/commute-metrics]] (0–5) alongside travel time and variability.
- **Enjoyable add‑ons** – once basic friction is minimized, incorporate music, podcasts, a coffee stop, or a brief walk to turn the commute into a neutral or positive experience.

### Practical Steps
1. **Run a timing experiment** (see [[concepts/timing-experiment]]) to find the least stressful departure slot.
2. **Log each trip** with the template from [[concepts/commute-logging-template]] to capture stress scores and other metrics.
3. **Analyze** the logged data: if stress consistently drops on a particular schedule, adopt that routine.
4. **Add enjoyment** – after achieving a smoother baseline, layer in personal comforts (audio, scenery, coffee) to further reduce stress.

### Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/commute-optimization]] to prioritize simple adjustments before major changes.
- Relies on the data collected via [[concepts/commute-metrics]] and the logging format in [[concepts/commute-logging-template]].
- Informs the choice of departure times explored in [[concepts/timing-experiment]].
