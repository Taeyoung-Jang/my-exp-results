# Commute Optimization

**Definition**: A practical framework for improving the efficiency, predictability, and experience of a regular daily commute through low‑effort adjustments before considering larger changes.

## Core Principles
1. **Prioritize low‑effort levers** – Start with timing tweaks, then explore simple route alternatives, and only later evaluate mode changes such as park‑and‑ride or transit.
2. **Run controlled experiments** – Use a small set of departure times and repeat trials to isolate real effects from day‑to‑day noise.
3. **Track focused metrics** – Measure total travel time, variability between trips, and post‑arrival stress level.
4. **Add enjoyment after friction is reduced** – Incorporate music, podcasts, coffee stops, or short walks to transform the commute into a pleasant ritual.

## Process Overview (based on [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]])
1. **Timing Experiment** – Choose two departure times within a 15‑20 minute flexible window. Alternate them across at least two days each, as described in [[concepts/timing-experiment]].
2. **Route Check** – Compare the regular path with one or two alternatives at the same tested times, noting traffic impressions.
3. **Metrics Collection** – Log each trip using the [[concepts/commute-logging-template]] and capture the three core metrics outlined in [[concepts/commute-metrics]].
4. **Analysis** – After a week (≈ 2‑3 runs per time slot), compare averages to identify the smoother, more predictable schedule.
5. **Stress Management** – Once the optimal timing and route are set, introduce enjoyable activities per [[concepts/stress-management]] to enhance the overall experience.
