# Resistance Band

**Type:** product

## Overview
Resistance Band is a flexible elastic equipment that provides variable resistance for strength, conditioning, and mobility work. It is lightweight, inexpensive, and can be anchored to doors, poles, or other sturdy points to create pull‑down or rowing motions.

## Usage in Session‑38 (2025‑02‑15)
- **Warm‑up:** band pull‑aparts to activate the upper back and shoulders (see [[concepts/full-body-training-patterns]] for movement patterns).
- **Tuesday – Home Superset A:** goblet squat paired with a **band row** or table row as the horizontal pull.
- **Saturday – Home Superset A:** hip‑hinge movement paired with **band pull‑downs** (or high‑anchor rows) for vertical pulling.
- **Saturday – Upper‑back finisher:** face‑pull‑style band work or scapular squeezes to reinforce posture.
- **Mobility/Recovery:** light band shoulder activation drills on off‑days.

## Benefits
- **Portability:** works equally well in a [[entities/gym]] or [[entities/home]] environment.
- **Progressive overload:** increase tension by using thicker bands or stacking bands, aligning with [[concepts/progression-strategies]].
- **Joint‑friendly:** provides smooth, low‑impact resistance, useful when managing shoulder discomfort.
