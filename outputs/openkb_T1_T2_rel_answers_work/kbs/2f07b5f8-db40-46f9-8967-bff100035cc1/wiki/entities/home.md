# Home (place)

Home refers to a residential setting where the full‑body training templates from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-15]] can be performed with limited equipment. It is presented as an alternative to a commercial [[entities/gym]] for the Tuesday and Saturday sessions.

## Key facts from the document
- **Minimal‑equipment version**: The plan includes exercises that require only a few items such as a **dumbbell** [[entities/dumbbell]], a **kettlebell** [[entities/kettlebell]], a **resistance band** [[entities/resistance-band]], or a backpack for goblet squats.
- **Warm‑up**: Replaces rowing or treadmill work with brisk marching, step‑ups, or shadow‑boxing, plus bodyweight squats, hip‑hinges, and band pull‑aparts.
- **Superset A (Tuesday & Saturday)**: Uses goblet squats or split‑squat variations paired with band rows or table rows; hinge movements like dumbbell RDLs or kettlebell swings paired with band pull‑downs or rows.
- **Superset B**: Incorporates push‑ups, floor presses, or neutral‑grip dumbbell presses as shoulder‑friendly alternatives to overhead presses.
- **Core and upper‑back finishers**: Plank, dead‑bug, or scapular squeezes using only bodyweight or bands.
- **Progression**: Follows the [[concepts/progression-strategies]] outlined in the session, allowing weekly load increases, rep additions, or tempo modifications.
- **Training structure**: Relies on the [[concepts/superset-training]] model to keep each session within ~45 minutes.
- **Full‑body pattern**: Aligns with the [[concepts/full-body-training-patterns]] of squat, hinge, push, pull, and core.
