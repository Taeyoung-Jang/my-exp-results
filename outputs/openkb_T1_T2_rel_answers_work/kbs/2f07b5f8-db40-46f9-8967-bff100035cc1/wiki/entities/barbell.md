# Barbell

A **barbell** is a solid steel bar (usually 7 ft long) onto which weight plates are loaded. It is a core piece of equipment for many compound movements such as squats, deadlifts, and presses.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-15]]
- Used for the **flat barbell press** option in the Saturday shoulder‑friendly press hierarchy.
- Serves as the primary loading method for **back squats** and **Romanian deadlifts** in the Tuesday and Saturday full‑body templates.
- Integrated into the **[[concepts/superset-training]]** structure: each superset alternates a barbell lift (e.g., back squat) with a complementary pulling movement.
- Progression follows the **[[concepts/progression-strategies]]** outlined in the session: weekly load increments, rep additions, or tempo pauses.
- When shoulder comfort is a concern, the barbell press is listed as the “most strength‑carryover” option after more shoulder‑friendly alternatives.
