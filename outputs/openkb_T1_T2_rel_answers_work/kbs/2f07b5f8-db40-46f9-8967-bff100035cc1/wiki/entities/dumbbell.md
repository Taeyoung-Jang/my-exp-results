# Dumbbell

A **dumbbell** is a handheld weight commonly used for resistance training, offering a versatile, unilateral loading option for a wide range of exercises.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-15]]
- **Used in full‑body training templates** for both gym and minimal‑equipment home versions of the Tuesday and Saturday sessions.
- Serves as the primary load for:
  * Goblet squats and split‑squat variations.
  * Dumbbell Romanian deadlifts (hinge pattern).
  * Neutral‑grip and low‑incline presses that are recommended as shoulder‑friendly alternatives.
  * Floor presses and push‑ups when a bench is unavailable.
- Integrated into **superset training** structures to keep sessions under 45 minutes while providing sufficient stimulus for strength gains.
- Supports the **progression strategies** outlined in the document (weekly load increase, rep addition, tempo pauses).

Dumbbells are highlighted as the core piece of equipment for delivering **unilateral** stimulus, allowing the user to address strength imbalances and perform a variety of presses that are gentle on the shoulder.
