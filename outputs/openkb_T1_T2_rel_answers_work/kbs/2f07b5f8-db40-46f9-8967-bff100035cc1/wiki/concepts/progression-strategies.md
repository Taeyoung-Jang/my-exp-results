# Progression Strategies

**Definition**: Systematic methods to increase training load, volume, or difficulty over time, ensuring continued adaptation while preserving sustainability.

## Core Elements from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]

1. **Rep‑Based Linear Progression**
   - Add a rep or two each week until reaching the top of the prescribed range (e.g., 5‑10 reps).
   - Once the upper bound is hit, increase the weight slightly and reset reps to the lower end of the range.
2. **Weight‑Based Stepwise Increments**
   - Small weight jumps (2.5‑5 lb or 1‑2 kg) are applied after the rep range is maxed out, preserving manageable difficulty.
3. **Auto‑Regulation**
   - Adjust the day's load based on warm‑up performance: keep the weight steady or drop a little if lifts feel unusually heavy.
   - This prevents burnout and removes the need for rigid, scheduled deloads.
4. **Flexible Deloading**
   - Every 6‑8 weeks, or when motivation/joint comfort wanes, halve the working sets while keeping the same weight.
   - Provides recovery without sacrificing long‑term progress.

## Additional Strategies from [[summaries/session-38_2025-02-15]]

1. **Weekly Load Increase**
   - When a session is completed with all prescribed sets and reps cleanly, add a small amount of weight (2.5‑5 lb) to the primary lift for the next week.
2. **Rep Addition as an Alternative**
   - If weight cannot be increased, add one rep per set until the top of the 6‑10 rep range is reached, then reset to the lower end and increase load.
3. **Tempo or Pause Modifications**
   - Introduce a 2‑second pause at the bottom of a lift or slow the eccentric phase (≈3 seconds) to raise difficulty without extending session time.
4. **Simple Progression Rule Integration**
   - The above three options are applied within the 2‑set or 3‑set superset templates, ensuring the entire workout still fits a 45‑minute window.

## Why These Strategies Matter
- **Sustainability**: Simple, predictable rules make it easy to stay consistent and avoid decision fatigue.
- **Adaptability**: Auto‑regulation and tempo tweaks allow day‑to‑day tuning based on fatigue, sleep, and stress levels.
- **Progress Tracking**: Clear, incremental steps make it straightforward to log and evaluate improvements.
