# Summary of Session 38 (2025‑02‑15)

This document provides **concise, minute‑by‑minute templates** for two weekly full‑body training sessions (Tuesday & Saturday). It includes **gym and minimal‑equipment home versions**, **2‑set and 3‑set options**, exact **sets, reps, rest intervals**, and a **simple progression rule**. It also offers **shoulder‑friendly press alternatives** ranked by comfort vs. strength carryover, with selection cues and form tips. Light cardio or mobility on off‑days is suggested for recovery.

---

## Key Concepts [[concepts/full-body-training-patterns]]
- **Full‑body patterns**: squat, hinge, push, pull, core.  
- **Superset structure**: alternating two exercises to maximize efficiency and keep the session ~45 min.  
- **Progression**: weekly load increase, rep addition, tempo pause, or time under tension.  
- **Recovery cardio/mobility**: 15‑20 min low‑intensity walk, bike, or focused mobility circuit.  
- **Shoulder‑friendly pressing**: neutral‑grip dumbbell press → low‑incline dumbbell press → standard flat press.

## Detailed Minute‑by‑Minute Templates

### Tuesday – Gym Version
- **0‑5 min**: Row 3 min, 2 min bodyweight squats & hip‑hinges, 1 min band pull‑aparts.  
- **5‑20 min (Superset A)**: Back squat 6‑10 rep → 30 s rest → Cable row 6‑10 rep → 60 s rest. Repeat **2 sets** (≈15 min) or **3 sets** (≈20 min).  
- **20‑35 min (Superset B)**: Romanian deadlift 6‑10 rep → 30 s rest → Flat [[entities/dumbbell]] press (neutral grip) 6‑10 rep → 60 s rest. Same set options.  
- **35‑40 min**: Plank 60 s ×2 or dead‑bug 2 min.  
- **40‑45 min**: Light walk or stretch.

### Tuesday – Home Minimal‑Equipment
- **Superset A**: Goblet squat ([[entities/dumbbell]] or backpack) ↔ Band row or table row (using [[entities/resistance-band]]).  
- **Superset B**: Dumbbell RDL ↔ Push‑ups or floor press (using [[entities/dumbbell]]).  
- Rest/rep scheme identical to gym version.

### Saturday – Gym Version
- **0‑5 min**: Same warm‑up.  
- **5‑20 min (Superset A)**: Romanian deadlift or cable hip‑hinge ↔ Lat pull‑down or assisted pull‑up.  
- **20‑35 min (Superset B)**: Split‑squat or leg press ↔ Shoulder‑friendly press (neutral‑grip [[entities/dumbbell]] press or low‑incline [[entities/dumbbell]] press).  
- **35‑40 min**: Face‑pulls or band pull‑aparts (using [[entities/resistance-band]]).  
- **40‑45 min**: Cool‑down.

### Saturday – Home Minimal‑Equipment
- **Superset A**: Dumbbell/Kettlebell swing or RDL (using [[entities/dumbbell]] or [[entities/kettlebell]]) ↔ Band pull‑down (or high‑anchor row with [[entities/resistance-band]]).  
- **Superset B**: Split‑squat ↔ Neutral‑grip floor press or incline push‑up (using [[entities/dumbbell]] if available).  
- Finish with band scapular squeezes.

## Shoulder‑Friendly Press Options [[concepts/shoulder-friendly-presses]]
| Rank | Exercise | Why Shoulder‑Friendly | Strength Carryover |
|------|----------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Neutral‑grip dumbbell press** (flat or slight incline) | Palms‑in keep humerus neutral, minimal anterior glide. | Moderate |
| 2 | **Low‑incline dumbbell press** (≈30‑45°) | Slightly more shoulder activation, still natural elbow path. | High |
| 3 | **Standard flat press** ([[entities/barbell]] or [[entities/dumbbell]]) | Greatest loading on chest/shoulders; only if pain‑free. | Highest |

**Selection cues**:  
- Pinching or forward roll on warm‑up → choose Rank 1.  
- Mild caution, no pain → Rank 2.  
- Full comfort, stable scapular control → Rank 3.

**Form cues**: elbows tucked ~45° from torso, grip just outside shoulder width, press to a point where scapula remains retracted, avoid lowering past the point where shoulders start to protract. Stop the press an inch or two above the point where the shoulder begins to glide forward, keep forearms vertical at the bottom, and maintain a gentle squeeze of the shoulder blades.

## Progression Rules [[concepts/progression-strategies]]
1. **Load increase**: add 2.5‑5 lb to the main lift each week if all sets were completed cleanly.  
2. **Rep addition**: if load cannot increase, add 1 rep per set until reaching 10 rep ceiling, then reset to 6‑7 rep and increase load.  
3. **Tempo/pauses**: alternatively, insert a 2‑second pause at the bottom of the lift or slow the eccentric (3‑sec).

---

## Off‑Day Recovery
- 15‑20 min low‑intensity cardio (walk, easy bike, light jog).  
- 5‑10 min mobility focus: hip flexor stretch, thoracic rotations, shoulder band pull‑aparts.
