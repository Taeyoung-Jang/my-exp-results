# Summary of session-13_2024-06-15

**Key Factors for Choosing a Strength Plan**  
- Time availability, equipment access, experience level, and mental energy required.  
- Structured programs provide preset progression; self‑designed routines allow flexibility.  
- Sustainability hinges on realistic training frequency and a simple, repeatable framework.  

**Sustainable Upper/Lower Split (Minimum‑Effective Version)**  
- Four‑day split: two upper, two lower days.  
- Each session: 1 main lift, 2–3 supportive movements, 1 optional “feel‑good” exercise.  
- Sets/reps: main lifts 3 × 5‑10, accessories 2‑3 × 8‑15.  
- Progression: add reps each week until the top of the range, then increase weight and reset reps. See [[concepts/progression-strategies]].  
- Auto‑regulation: keep weight steady or drop slightly if warm‑ups feel heavy; no formal deload needed each session.  
- Deload strategy: every 6‑8 weeks (or when motivation/joint comfort wanes) cut working sets in half for a week.  

**Sample Week with Swap Options**  
- **Upper Day 1**: Barbell bench press → dumbbell bench or push‑ups; cable row → dumbbell row or machine row; lateral raises → machine laterals or incline dumbbell raises; rope pushdowns → dips or overhead extensions.  
- **Lower Day 1**: Back squat → goblet squat or hack‑squat; Romanian deadlift → dumbbell RDL or hip‑hinge machine; walking lunges → split squats or leg press; core: hanging knee raises → cable crunches.  
- **Upper Day 2**: Overhead press → dumbbells or seated machine; pull‑ups → lat pulldowns or assisted pull‑ups; incline dumbbell press → machine press; biceps curls → cable curls.  
- **Lower Day 2**: Deadlift → trap‑bar or kettlebell deadlift; leg press → front squat or step‑ups; hamstring/glute work: leg curls or hip thrusts; core: planks → back extensions.  

**Signals to Adjust the Routine**  
- Stagnant main‑lift progress for 6‑8 weeks → tweak reps, volume, or accessories.  
- Persistent dread or pain from specific movements → swap for a variation.  
- Excessive fatigue or sessions exceeding scheduled time → reduce volume (sets/reps) or add a lighter week.  
- Life‑schedule changes, new goals, or equipment limitations → consider a different split or exercise selection.
