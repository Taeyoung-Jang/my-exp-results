# Summary of Session 34 (2025‑02‑13)

**Topic:** Navigating the final funding phase of a residential real‑estate closing.

## Key Points

1. **Common last‑minute deal‑breakers**  
   - Sudden changes in the buyer’s financial picture (new credit, job change, large deposits).  
   - Title report surprises (unpaid liens, missing signatures).  
   - Lender delays or missing documents.  
   - Property‑related issues discovered during the final walk‑through.  
   > These align with the broader [[concepts/closing_process]] risk factors.

2. **What counts as “liquid funds”**  
   - Money readily available in checking, savings, or money‑market accounts.  
   - Excludes assets that require liquidation (stocks before settlement, retirement accounts, etc.).  
   - Lenders scrutinize large, late‑stage deposits, especially from new sources or gifts.  
   > Relates to the concept of [[concepts/liquid_funds]] and fund source verification.

3. **Handling brokerage‑to‑checking transfers**  
   - Acceptable if you can provide trade confirmations and settlement statements.  
   - Prefer waiting until the trade settles (T+1/T+2) so the transfer shows as cleared cash.  
   - If timing is tight, communicate the plan to the [[entities/loan_officer]]; a clear paper trail usually prevents delays.  
   > Ties into fund transfer verification.

4. **Cashier’s checks vs. wires**  
   - Both are delivery methods for the final closing payment.  
   - Neither replaces the need to have the funds documented in the borrower’s account beforehand.  
   - Verify with the [[entities/title_company]] which method they prefer and the cut‑off times.  
   > Connected to closing payment methods.

5. **Coordination hierarchy**  
   - **First:** Notify the [[entities/lender]] (underwriting) of any fund movements.  
   - **Second:** Follow lender approval before informing the [[entities/title_company]], which mainly cares about delivery logistics.  
   > Supports the broader [[concepts/closing_process]].

6. **48‑hour action checklist**  
   - Gather brokerage statements, trade confirmations, and pending transfer receipts.  
   - Obtain recent bank statements and a balance screenshot after the transfer posts.  
   - Email the [[entities/loan_officer]] with:  
     - Note of intent to move funds.  
     - All supporting documents.  
   - Ask the [[entities/title_company]]:  
     - Preferred payment method (wire vs. cashier’s check).  
     - Deadline for funds to arrive.  
     - Final amount required.  
   > This forms the basis of a closing‑funds checklist.

7. **Impact on underwriting**  
   - Large incoming transfers from the borrower’s own accounts usually trigger only a quick verification, not a full re‑underwrite.  
   - Gifts or unexplained deposits close to closing are the most likely to restart underwriting.  
   > Links to [[concepts/underwriting_triggers]].

## Practical Takeaway
Maintain a clear, traceable source of funds, communicate proactively with the [[entities/lender]] (especially the [[entities/loan_officer]]) before moving money, and confirm payment logistics with the [[entities/title_company]]. Using the checklist above will help meet the “funds in account” deadline without reopening underwriting.
