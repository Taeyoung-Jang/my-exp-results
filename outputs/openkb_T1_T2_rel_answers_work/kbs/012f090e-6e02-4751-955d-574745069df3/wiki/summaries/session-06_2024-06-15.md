## Summary

- **Core idea**: Separate finances into a *cash buffer* for short‑term needs and *long‑term investments* for growth.  
- **Emergency fund rule of thumb**: Keep 3‑6 months of essential expenses in a highly liquid, high‑yield savings account. Adjust to 6‑9 months if income is unstable.  
- **Example**: With $6,000/month expenses and $60,000 saved, a typical split is $24,000 cash (4 months) and $36,000 invested; with unstable income, shift to $36‑54 k cash and less invested.  
- **Liquidity requirement**: Only truly liquid assets (cash, high‑yield savings) count toward the emergency fund. Locked‑up or volatile investments should not be relied on.  
- **Investment pause vs balance**: If the cash target feels insufficient, pause new investing until the buffer is reached; otherwise, allocate a portion of new savings to both cash and investments.
