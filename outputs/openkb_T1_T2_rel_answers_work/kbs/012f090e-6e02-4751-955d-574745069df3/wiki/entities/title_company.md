# Title Company

**Type:** organization

**Role in real‑estate transactions**
- Acts as a neutral third‑party that conducts title searches, issues title insurance, and coordinates the closing logistics.
- Provides the final closing statement, verifies that all liens are cleared, and ensures the deed is properly recorded.

**Key interactions in session‑34 (2025‑02‑13)**
- The buyer is advised to coordinate first with the **[[entities/loan_officer]]** (lender) before informing the title company about fund movements.
- The title company’s primary concern is the method and timing of the final payment (wire vs. cashier’s check) rather than the source of the buyer’s liquid funds.
- The buyer should ask the title company about:
  - Preferred payment method for closing day.
  - Deadline for the wire or cashier’s check to arrive.
  - Exact final amount required for settlement.

**Relevant concepts**
- [[concepts/closing_process]] – the broader workflow in which the title company operates.
- [[concepts/liquid_funds]] – the buyer must demonstrate sufficient liquid funds before the title company can issue the final settlement statement.
- [[concepts/underwriting_triggers]] – while the title company does not trigger underwriting, it relies on the lender’s underwriting decisions.
