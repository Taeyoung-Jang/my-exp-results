# Underwriting Triggers

**Definition**: Specific events or conditions that prompt the lender’s underwriting team to pause, request additional documentation, or re‑evaluate a mortgage application, potentially delaying or jeopardizing the closing.

## Common triggers identified in [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-13]]

1. **Large, last‑minute deposits** – Unexpected incoming funds, especially from new sources or gifts, trigger verification to ensure the money is not borrowed.
2. **Changes to the borrower’s financial profile** – New credit inquiries, opening of credit lines, job changes, or significant fluctuations in debt‑to‑income ratios.
3. **Title issues discovered late** – Unpaid liens, missing signatures, or other title defects that arise during the final title review.
4. **Unsettled brokerage proceeds** – Transfers from a brokerage that have not yet cleared (e.g., T+1/T+2) may be viewed as pending and require proof of settlement.
5. **Documentation gaps** – Missing or incomplete bank statements, trade confirmations, or proof of liquid assets.
6. **Gift money without proper documentation** – Gifts must be accompanied by a gift letter and proof that the funds are not a loan.

## Relationship to other concepts

- [[concepts/liquid_funds]]: Lenders require documented liquid assets; any discrepancy can become an underwriting trigger.
- [[concepts/closing_process]]: Triggers directly impact the timeline and success of the closing.

## Key parties involved

- **[[entities/lender]]** – Sets underwriting guidelines and reviews trigger reports.
- **[[entities/loan_officer]]** – Communicates with the borrower about potential triggers and gathers required documentation.
- **[[entities/title_company]]** – May flag title‑related triggers during the final title clearance.

## Mitigation strategies

- **Proactive communication** – Inform the loan officer of any planned fund movements before they occur.
- **Maintain stable finances** – Avoid new credit accounts, large deposits, or job changes close to closing.
- **Provide clear documentation** – Supply trade confirmations, settlement statements, and bank statements promptly.
- **Understand timing** – Wait for brokerage proceeds to settle before transferring to checking, unless the lender approves an early transfer.

By anticipating and addressing these triggers early, borrowers can reduce the risk of re‑underwriting and keep the closing on schedule.
