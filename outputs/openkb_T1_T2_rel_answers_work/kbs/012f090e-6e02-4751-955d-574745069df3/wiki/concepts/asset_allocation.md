# Asset Allocation

Asset allocation refers to the process of distributing an individual's or portfolio's wealth across different categories of assets—such as cash, bonds, equities, and other investment vehicles—to achieve a desired balance between risk tolerance, time horizon, and financial goals.

## Key Principles

- **Risk Management**: By spreading money across assets that behave differently under market conditions, investors can reduce the overall volatility of their portfolio.
- **Return Optimization**: Different asset classes offer varying expected returns; an appropriate mix can improve the chance of meeting long‑term objectives.
- **Time Horizon Alignment**: The proportion allocated to higher‑risk, higher‑return assets (like equities) typically increases with a longer investment horizon, while a shorter horizon favors more stable, liquid assets (like cash).
