# App Clutter Management

App clutter management is the practice of reducing the number of installed applications on a mobile device to the smallest set that reliably fulfills each functional need. It combines systematic evaluation of apps with a minimalist home‑screen layout, aiming to improve productivity, conserve battery, and lower cognitive load.

## Core Components

1. **App Evaluation** – Uses a weighted scorecard to compare candidates within a functional category. The scorecard assesses:
   - Essential function fit (30 %)
   - Speed & ease of use (20 %)
   - Reliability & sync (20 %)
   - Focus & lack of clutter (15 %)
   - Trust & longevity (15 %)
   Apps scoring **≥ 70/100** are retained, the rest are hidden or removed. This method is described in detail in weighted scorecard evaluation.

2. **Category‑Specific Must‑Haves** – Each category (notes, tasks, calendar, email, files, passwords, scanning, budgeting) has a defined set of core features that the chosen app must support. These criteria ensure the selected app truly replaces multiple redundant tools.

3. **Home‑Screen Organization** – After selecting the “one best” app per category, the device’s home screen is streamlined into:
   - A **dock** with anchor apps (phone, messaging, browser, primary productivity hub).
   - A first page showing the 3‑4 most‑used apps without folders.
   - Two to three broad folders (e.g., Work Essentials, Personal/Admin, Utilities) for the remaining tools. This layout is outlined in [[concepts/iphone-home-screen-organization]].

4. **Browser‑First Strategy** – For categories where a native app offers little advantage, use Safari home‑screen shortcuts instead of installing an app. This reduces storage, background activity, and update noise while keeping access fast and familiar. The approach aligns with the "browser‑first phone" mindset described in recent discussions.

5. **iOS Settings Tuning** – Disable unnecessary background activity and notifications to keep the device responsive:
   - **Background App Refresh:** Settings → General → Background App Refresh – turn off globally or per‑app.
   - **Siri Suggestions:** Settings → Siri & Search – disable top‑level suggestions and per‑app learning.
   - **Notification Management:** Keep alerts only for essential communication, calendar, and security‑related apps (e.g., messaging, phone, maps, 2FA). 
   - **Location Permissions:** Prefer “While Using the App” for most apps.
   These tweaks reduce battery drain and improve perceived speed.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

App clutter management draws on the principles of productivity app selection—choosing the most effective tool for a task—and integrates them with a minimalistic home‑screen strategy tailored for the [[entities/iphone]]. The approach also leverages built‑in services such as [[entities/icloud]] for seamless sync and storage.
