# Summary of Session 33 (2025‑02‑14)

The conversation tackles the perception of **app bloat** on smartphones and proposes a **[[concepts/browser-first-phone]]** strategy, especially for iPhone users. It outlines a practical **[[concepts/minimal-iphone-setup]]**, recommending which native apps to retain, which categories to shift to Safari shortcuts, and which iOS settings to tweak for lower background activity and fewer notifications. It also evaluates **2FA methods** for a low‑maintenance workflow, favoring **[[concepts/passkey-authentication]]** and **[[entities/hardware-security-key]]**.

### Core Apps to Keep (≈10‑15)
- [[entities/apple-messages]], Phone, [[entities/facetime]]
- [[entities/apple-maps]], Camera, Photos
- [[entities/apple-wallet]], Settings, [[entities/app-store]]
- 2FA authenticator (or use passkeys)
- Preferred email client (if used frequently)
- Music/Podcast player (optional)
- Files or [[entities/apple-notes]] for iCloud sync

These apps are retained because they integrate tightly with **[[entities/ios]]** system features and provide a smoother experience than web equivalents.

### Categories Better as Safari Shortcuts
- Social media platforms
- Shopping & e‑commerce sites
- News and content aggregators
- Banking portals (except for push‑based alerts)
- Video streaming services
- General reference, note‑dumping, task lists

Creating home‑screen shortcuts follows the principles of **[[concepts/iphone-home-screen-organization]]**, giving an app‑like feel without occupying storage or background resources.

### iOS Settings Optimizations
- **Background App Refresh:** `Settings → General → Background App Refresh` – turn off globally or per‑app for non‑essential apps.
- **Siri Suggestions:** `Settings → Siri & Search` – disable the top suggestion toggles and turn off “Learn from this App” for each non‑essential app.
- **Notifications:** Keep only communication, calendar, and essential alerts; disable banners, badges, and sounds for others.
- **Location Permissions:** Set most apps to “While Using the App.”

These adjustments reduce background processing while preserving essential functionality, aligning with **[[concepts/app-clutter-management]]**.

### 2FA Recommendations for a Strict Minimal Setup
- **Passkeys:** Built‑in iCloud Keychain support; no extra app needed, works seamlessly in **[[entities/safari]]**.
- **Hardware Security Keys:** Physical tap (e.g., YubiKey) for sites lacking passkey support; no app installs.
- **Authenticator Apps:** Viable but break the “minimal apps” rule.
- **SMS Codes:** Universally supported but less secure and can be noisy.

**Personal pick:** Use passkeys wherever possible, supplemented by a **[[entities/hardware-security-key]]** for the remaining services.
