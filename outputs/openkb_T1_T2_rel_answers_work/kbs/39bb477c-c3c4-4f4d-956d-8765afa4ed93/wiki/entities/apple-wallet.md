# Apple Wallet

Apple Wallet is a built‑in [[entities/ios]] application that lets users store credit, debit, transit, boarding passes, tickets, and loyalty cards in a digital format. In the context of the **minimal iPhone** approach discussed in [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-14]], Apple Wallet is identified as one of the essential native apps to keep installed because it integrates tightly with system services such as Apple Pay and passes that have no practical web equivalent.

## Why Keep It Installed?
- Provides secure, NFC‑based payment via Apple Pay.
- Stores time‑sensitive passes (boarding, event tickets) that need to be available offline.
- Syncs automatically through iCloud, linking to [[entities/icloud]].
- Notifications for payment confirmations and pass updates are handled natively.

## Relation to Suggested Practices
- Fits the [[concepts/minimal-iphone-setup]] recommendation to retain core apps that rely on system‑level features.
- Complements the [[concepts/browser-first-phone]] strategy by handling use‑cases that browsers cannot replicate (e.g., contactless payments).
- Appears in the suggested list of essential apps alongside [[entities/apple-messages]], [[entities/apple-maps]], and the [[entities/app-store]].

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- When decluttering, Apple Wallet remains untouched while other categories shift to Safari shortcuts as described in [[concepts/iphone-home-screen-organization]].
- It does not conflict with [[concepts/passkey-authentication]], which focuses on web‑based credential storage, but both rely on the security foundation provided by [[entities/ios]] and iCloud.

## Related Entities
- [[entities/apple]] – the company behind the product.
- [[entities/ios]] – the operating system on which Apple Wallet runs.
- [[entities/icloud]] – the cloud service that syncs wallet data across devices.
- [[entities/app-store]] – the distribution channel for updates to Apple Wallet.
