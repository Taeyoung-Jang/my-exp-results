## Safari

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** Apple’s native web browser on iOS and iPadOS.

Safari is the default browser on [[entities/ios]] devices such as the [[entities/iphone]]. In the context of a **browser‑first phone** strategy (see [[concepts/browser-first-phone]]), Safari is used to create home‑screen shortcuts that emulate lightweight apps, helping to reduce [[concepts/app-clutter-management]] and support a [[concepts/minimal-iphone-setup]].

**Key points from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-14]]:**

- Safari shortcuts replace many social, shopping, banking, and media apps, keeping the device lean.
- Turning off autoplay and notification permissions within Safari limits background activity, complementing iOS settings like Background App Refresh.
- Safari does not support push notifications for most services, so essential apps (e.g., messaging, 2FA) must remain installed.
- Passkey authentication (see [[concepts/passkey-authentication]]) works directly in Safari without extra apps.

Safari integrates with other Apple services like [[entities/icloud]] for syncing bookmarks and the reading list, and it benefits from the overall ecosystem of [[entities/apple]].
