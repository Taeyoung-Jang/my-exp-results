# iPhone (product)

The iPhone is Apple's line of smartphones that run iOS and integrate tightly with services such as [[entities/icloud]] and other Apple hardware. It serves as the central device for many productivity workflows, including the home‑screen organization and app‑clutter‑management strategies described in [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]].
