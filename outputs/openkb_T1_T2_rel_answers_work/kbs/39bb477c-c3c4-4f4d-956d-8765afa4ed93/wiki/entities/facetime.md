# FaceTime

FaceTime is Apple’s native video‑ and audio‑calling application bundled with **iOS** and pre‑installed on all **iPhone** devices. It tightly integrates with the Contacts and Messages apps, allowing seamless one‑tap calls and FaceTime links.

## Key facts from session‑33
- Recommended as an essential native app in a **minimal iPhone setup** because it provides system‑level call functionality that a browser cannot replace. [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-14]]
- Keeps low background overhead and respects the user’s decision to limit app clutter. [[concepts/app-clutter-management]]
- Works offline and does not require a Safari shortcut, fitting the **browser‑first phone** philosophy for everything else. [[concepts/browser-first-phone]]
