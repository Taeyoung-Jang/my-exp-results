# Apple Messages

Apple Messages is the default messaging application on iPhone that supports SMS, MMS, and iMessage communication. It is pre‑installed, tightly integrated with iOS, and syncs conversation data via [[entities/icloud]].

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-15]]
- Recommended as one of the four anchor apps placed in the **dock** for quick access in the suggested [[concepts/iphone-home-screen-organization]] layout.
- Serves as a core productivity tool, illustrating the principles of [[concepts/app-clutter-management]] by being a built‑in solution that eliminates the need for third‑party messaging apps.
- When applying the [[concepts/weighted-scorecard-evaluation]] for app selection, Apple Messages scores high on reliability, sync, and trust, making it the natural “one‑best” choice for the messaging category.
- Its inclusion reflects the broader [[concepts/productivity-app-selection]] strategy of preferring native, well‑maintained apps over redundant alternatives.

## Relationships
- Developed by [[entities/apple]].
- Runs on [[entities/iphone]].
- Works seamlessly with [[entities/apple-notes]] and other iOS productivity apps through the shared iOS ecosystem.

## Usage Tips
- Keep Apple Messages in the dock alongside Phone, Browser, and your primary productivity hub to maintain a clutter‑free home screen.
- Leverage iCloud sync to access your message history across all Apple devices.
