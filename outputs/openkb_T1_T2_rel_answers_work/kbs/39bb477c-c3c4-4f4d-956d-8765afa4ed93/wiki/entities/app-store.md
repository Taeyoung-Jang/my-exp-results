## App Store

**Type:** Product (official iOS app marketplace by Apple)

The **App Store** is the central hub on iOS for discovering, installing, and updating applications. In the *minimal iPhone* recommendation from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-14]], the App Store is listed among the essential native apps that should remain installed because it provides system‑level integration for obtaining and maintaining other apps when needed.

### Why it matters in a browser‑first setup
- Serves as the sole source for installing the handful of core apps (e.g., [[entities/apple-messages]], [[entities/facetime]], [[entities/apple-maps]], [[entities/apple-wallet]], [[entities/apple-notes]]) that the minimal iPhone strategy deems essential.
- Enables occasional updates or new essential app installations without requiring a third‑party app store.
- Keeps the device secure through Apple’s review process and sandboxing, aligning with the goal of a low‑maintenance, fast phone.
