# iCloud

**Type:** Product

iCloud is Apple’s cloud‑based storage and synchronization platform that integrates tightly with iPhone and other Apple devices. It provides seamless file backup, document syncing, and cross‑device data continuity.

## Role in session‑04_2024‑06‑15
The session recommends using iCloud as the preferred file‑management solution on iPhone because of its native integration, reliable sync, and offline access. In the proposed scorecard, the **Files** category lists “seamless iCloud integration” as a must‑have feature, positioning iCloud as the benchmark for evaluating competing file‑manager apps.
