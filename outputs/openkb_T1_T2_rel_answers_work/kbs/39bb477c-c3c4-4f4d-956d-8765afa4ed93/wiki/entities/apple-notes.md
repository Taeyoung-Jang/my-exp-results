# Apple Notes (product)

Apple Notes is the built‑in note‑taking application that ships with iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It allows users to create plain‑text notes, checklists, sketches, and rich‑media entries that sync across devices via iCloud.

## Key facts from session‑04_2024-06-15
- Recommended as the **primary notes app** in the scorecard‑driven decluttering workflow.
- Scores highly on the *Essential function fit* and *Speed & ease* criteria because it captures thoughts instantly and integrates tightly with the iOS ecosystem.
- Benefits from **reliable sync** via [[entities/icloud]], satisfying the *Reliability and sync* weight.
- Offers a focused experience without the bloat of third‑party alternatives, aligning with the *Focus and lack of clutter* metric.
- Regular updates from [[entities/apple]] ensure trust and longevity, meeting the *Trust and longevity* requirement.
