## Benefits Enrollment Checklist

**Definition**: A structured list of items a new hire should verify in the company benefits portal to ensure correct payroll processing, timely enrollment, and proper documentation.

### Core Elements
1. **Personal Profile Verification**
   - Confirm name, address, phone, emergency contacts, and Social Security number.
2. **Direct‑Deposit Details**
   - Check routing and account numbers for accuracy.
3. **Tax Withholding (W‑4)**
   - Review filing status and allowances to match onboarding intent.
4. **Dependents**
   - Ensure each dependent is listed correctly and required verification documents are uploaded.
5. **Plan Elections**
   - Review health, dental, vision, HSA/FSA, life and disability insurance selections and note enrollment deadlines.
6. **Retirement Contributions**
   - Verify contribution percentage and enrollment in any employer‑matched plan.

### Record‑Keeping Recommendations
- **Today**: Capture screenshots or PDFs of the personal info page, direct‑deposit page, W‑4 page, dependents list, and any “elections saved” confirmation.
- **After First Payroll**: Save the final benefits enrollment confirmation PDF, retirement contribution confirmation, and the first pay stub.

### Timing
| Phase | Actions | Documentation |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| Day 1‑2 | Verify profile, direct‑deposit, W‑4, dependents, initial plan selections. | Screenshots of each page; note enrollment deadline. |
| Day 3‑5 | Review time‑off policy, retirement options, upload required documents. | Save any “upload complete” confirmations. |
| Post‑first payroll | Compare first pay stub to expected deductions and contributions. | Save first pay stub and final enrollment PDF. |
