# First‑Week Logistics

**Definition**: A structured set of actions and checks new hires should complete in their first days to establish reliable payroll, benefits, system access, and daily work routines.

## Core Elements (derived from [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]])

1. **Complete hiring paperwork**
   - Verify personal information (name, address, SSN, emergency contacts).
   - Submit tax forms (W‑4) and direct‑deposit details.

2. **System access**
   - Ensure email, calendar, internal tools, shared drives, and role‑specific applications are functional.
   - Flag any access issues early.

3. **Benefits portal setup**
   - Review personal profile, direct‑deposit, and W‑4.
   - Confirm dependents, health/dental/vision, HSA/FSA, and insurance elections.
   - Note enrollment deadlines.

4. **Establish daily routines**
   - Define communication cadence with manager and team.
   - Clarify short‑term priorities and workflow expectations.

5. **Record‑keeping**
   - Capture screenshots/PDFs of profile, direct‑deposit, W‑4, dependents, and benefits‑election summaries.
   - After first payroll, save the official enrollment confirmation and first pay stub.

## Timeline Overview
| Phase | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| Day 1‑2 | Paperwork, system access, initial benefits checks, immediate screenshots |
| Day 3‑5 | Review time‑off policy, retirement options, upload any required documents |
| Post‑first payroll | Verify deductions, contributions, and save final confirmations |
