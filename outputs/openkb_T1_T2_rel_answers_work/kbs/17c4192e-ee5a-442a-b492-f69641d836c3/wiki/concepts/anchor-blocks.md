# Anchor Blocks

**Definition**: Anchor blocks are small sets of recurring, purpose‑focused time slots that act as structural "pins" in a weekly schedule, providing consistency without imposing rigid constraints.

## Core Characteristics
- **Limited Quantity**: Typically 2–4 anchors per week, enough to give direction while preserving flexibility.
- **Fixed Purpose, Flexible Content**: Each block has a clear intent (e.g., planning, deep work, wrap‑up) but no predefined tasks; the specific work can vary.
- **Regular Timing**: Anchors occur at the same time each week (e.g., Monday 9 am planning, Friday 3:30 pm review) to create a reliable rhythm.
- **Scalable**: Users can add or remove anchors based on workload, moving between a lightweight (2 anchors) and a more structured (4 anchors) approach.

## Example from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]]
- **Monday Planning Session** – 9:00 am, 20‑30 min to map priorities.
- **Wednesday Focus Block** – 10:00‑12:00 mid‑week deep‑work period.
- **Thursday Focus Block** – 14:00‑16:00 additional deep‑work slot.
- **Friday Wrap‑up** – 15:30 review, inbox clearing, and week‑end reset.

A starter setup may begin with just the Monday planning and Friday wrap‑up anchors, providing clear week bookends while leaving the middle days fluid.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/weekly-themes]]**: Anchors define when themes apply; themes guide the type of work placed around the anchors.
- **[[concepts/flexible-scheduling]]**: Anchor blocks enable a schedule that can shift (e.g., moving focus blocks to accommodate heavy client‑call weeks) while keeping core structure intact.

## Practical Tips
1. **Identify Core Needs**: Decide what recurring activities are essential (planning, review, deep work).
2. **Choose Consistent Times**: Pick slots that align with natural energy peaks.
3. **Keep It Light**: Start with two anchors; add more only if you feel the week is too chaotic.
4. **Adjust as Needed**: If a week is call‑heavy, slide focus blocks to early mornings or late afternoons; retain the anchor rhythm.
5. **Pair with Reviews**: Use short daily or weekly reviews to evaluate anchor effectiveness and iterate.
