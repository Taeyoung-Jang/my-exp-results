# Payroll Verification After Onboarding

Ensuring that your first payroll cycle reflects the information you entered during onboarding is crucial for catching errors early and avoiding downstream issues. This concept outlines the steps new employees should take to verify that taxes, deductions, benefits, and direct‑deposit details are processed correctly.

## Why It Matters
- Mis‑entered direct‑deposit information can delay paycheck delivery.
- Incorrect tax withholding (W‑4) may lead to unexpected tax liabilities.
- Benefit elections (health, dental, vision, HSA/FSA, retirement) need to be reflected in deductions.
- Early detection allows HR or payroll teams to correct mistakes before the next cycle.

## Verification Checklist (derived from [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-16]])
1. **Obtain the First Pay Stub**
   - Save a PDF or screenshot of the complete stub for record‑keeping.
2. **Confirm Direct‑Deposit Details**
   - Verify that the net amount matches the expected amount based on your salary and the banking information you provided.
3. **Review Tax Withholdings**
   - Check federal, state, and local tax amounts against your W‑4 selections.
4. **Validate Benefit Deductions**
   - Ensure health, dental, vision, HSA/FSA, and any other elected benefits appear as deductions.
5. **Check Retirement Contributions**
   - Confirm the contribution percentage and that any employer match is being applied.
6. **Cross‑Reference Enrollment Confirmation**
   - Compare the stub against the final benefits enrollment PDF you saved after the enrollment window closed.

## Record‑Keeping
- Store the pay stub alongside the screenshots you captured during benefit portal setup (direct‑deposit page, W‑4 page, benefits election summary).
- Keep these files in a secure, organized folder (e.g., `Onboarding/Payroll`) for future reference.

## Follow‑Up Actions
- If discrepancies are found, contact **HR or Payroll** within the first payroll cycle to request a correction.
- Re‑verify the next paycheck after any adjustments are made.
