## Summary of Session 14 (2024-06-15)

In this conversation the assistant helps the user design a **sustainable, flexible weekly work schedule** for a self‑employed professional. The advice centers on:

- **Anchor blocks** – a small set (2‑4) of recurring time slots that act as structural “pins” for the week (e.g., Monday planning, mid‑week focus sessions, Friday wrap‑up). [[concepts/anchor-blocks]]
- **Weekly themes** – broad categories (creative/strategy, client‑facing, admin/maintenance) assigned to portions of the week to guide task selection without rigid timing. [[concepts/weekly-themes]]
- **Flexibility tactics** – moving focus blocks around when weeks are call‑heavy vs. solo‑work heavy, scaling anchors up or down, and using short daily/weekly reviews as reset moments. [[concepts/flexible-scheduling]]
- **Concrete example** – a 4‑anchor schedule (Mon 9 am plan, Wed 10 am‑noon focus, Thu 2‑4 pm focus, Fri 3:30 pm review) with three simple themes (early‑week creative, mid‑week outward‑facing, late‑week admin). 
- **Starter recommendation** – begin with just two anchors: a Monday planning session and a Friday wrap‑up, providing clear week bookends while keeping the middle fluid.
