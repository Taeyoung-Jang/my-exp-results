## Summary of Session 38 – First‑Week Onboarding Checklist (2024‑12‑16)

This document offers a step‑by‑step guide for new hires to secure essential logistics in the first few days, with a special focus on verifying information in the benefits portal and preserving proof of completion.

### Key Areas Covered

1. **Core Onboarding Logistics**  
   - Complete all hiring paperwork (personal info, tax forms, direct‑deposit details).  
   - Verify access to daily tools: email, calendar, internal platforms, shared drives, and role‑specific applications.  
   - Establish communication routines with manager and team, and clarify first‑week priorities. [[concepts/first-week-logistics]]

2. **Benefits Portal Priorities**  
   - **Personal profile**: name, address, phone, emergency contacts, SSN.  
   - **Direct‑deposit**: routing and account numbers must match bank records.  
   - **W‑4 / tax withholding**: confirm selections align with onboarding intent.  
   - **Dependents**: ensure each dependent is listed correctly and required documents are uploaded.  
   - **Plan elections**: review health, dental, vision, HSA/FSA, life/disability options and note enrollment deadlines.  
   - **Retirement**: verify contribution percentage and enrollment in any employer‑matched plan.  
   - See also [[concepts/benefits-enrollment-checklist]] for a detailed item list.

3. **Screen‑capture & Record‑Keeping Strategy**  
   - **Today (immediate)**: capture screenshots or PDFs of:  
     - Personal info page  
     - Direct‑deposit page  
     - W‑4 page  
     - Dependents list  
     - Benefits‑election summary / “elections saved” notice  
   - **After first payroll**: capture:  
     - Official final benefits enrollment confirmation (often a PDF generated by the system)  
     - Retirement contribution confirmation  
     - First pay stub to verify taxes, deductions, and contributions. [[concepts/payroll-verification]]

### Timeline
| Phase | Actions | Documentation |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Day 1‑2** | Confirm profile, direct‑deposit, W‑4, dependents, initial plan selections. | Screenshots of each page; note enrollment deadline date. |
| **Day 3‑5** | Review time‑off policy, retirement options, and any required uploads. | Save any “upload complete” confirmations. |
| **Post‑first payroll** | Validate that payroll reflects correct deductions and contributions. | Save first pay stub and final enrollment PDF. |
