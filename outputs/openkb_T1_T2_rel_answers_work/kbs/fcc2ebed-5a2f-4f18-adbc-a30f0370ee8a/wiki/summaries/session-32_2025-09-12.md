# Summary of Session 32 (2025-09-12)

**Main contribution:** A practical overview of the pros and cons of automatic cloud backups, the most critical privacy settings to review, and a prioritized checklist for securing mobile backups while minimizing cloud footprint.

## Key Points

- **Pros of automatic backups:** effortless recovery of photos, messages, and settings; no manual intervention needed.
- **Cons/Risks:** reliance on the provider’s security, potential exposure if the account is compromised, and unwanted data processing for personalization or ads.

## Critical Privacy & Security Settings

1. **Encryption** – enable any available [[concepts/end-to-end-encryption]] for device backups, messages, and photos.
2. **Account protection** – turn on two‑factor authentication and keep recovery contact info up to date.
3. **Device & app access** – regularly audit signed‑in devices and revoke unnecessary app permissions.
4. **Data type selection** – review which categories (app data, location history, voice recordings, analytics) are included in the backup and disable those you don’t need.

## Prioritized Checklist (step‑by‑step)

1. Turn on [[concepts/end-to-end-encryption]] for backups, messages, and photos.
2. Enable two‑factor authentication and verify recovery details.
3. Remove unknown or unused devices from your cloud account.
4. Revoke unnecessary third‑party app access.
5. Open backup settings; disable non‑essential data (e.g., app data, call history, detailed analytics).
6. Adjust photo backup options – keep the backup but disable shared‑album syncing or automatic sharing.

## Minimal Cloud Footprint Strategy

- Keep **device backups** on for restoring settings, contacts, and basic preferences.
- Keep **photo and contacts syncing** (iCloud Photos/Contacts, Google Photos, Google Contacts) enabled for easy recovery of media and address books.
- Turn off backup of large or sensitive **app data** and any extra services (location history, voice recordings, usage logs).

## iPhone vs Android: What Counts as "Device Backups" vs "App Data"

- **[[entities/iphone]]**: Device backup includes system settings, keychain, messages, and general preferences. App data refers to data stored inside each third‑party app. Photos and contacts sync separately via [[entities/icloud]] Photos/Contacts.
- **[[entities/android]]**: Device backup covers system settings, Wi‑Fi passwords, call history, some SMS, and limited app data via Google Backup. Apps may also store data independently. Photos and contacts sync via [[entities/google-photos]] and [[entities/google-contacts]].

## Recommendations

- Enable device backup, but limit app‑specific backups to only essential apps.
- Rely on platform‑native photo and contact sync services for media and address‑book recovery.
- Regularly audit encryption, authentication, and access permissions to mitigate exposure and [[concepts/risk-assessment]] concerns.
