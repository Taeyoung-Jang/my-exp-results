# Google LLC

Google LLC is a multinational technology company providing cloud services such as Google Backup, Google Photos, and Google Contacts.

## Role in session-32

- Mentioned as the provider of Android backup services and synchronization for photos and contacts.
- Users are advised to review Google account security settings, enable two‑factor authentication, and check which apps have access.

## Relevant Settings & Concepts

- Enable [[concepts/end-to-end-encryption]] where available for Google backups.
- Apply [[concepts/data-minimization]] by disabling non‑essential app data sync.
- Conduct [[concepts/risk-assessment]] of Google account exposure.
