# Summary of session-33_2025-02-13

**Date:** 2025-02-13 14:30  

## Core Topic
- Strategies for intentionally simplifying personal tech while staying reachable, especially around **group chat** and **navigation** needs.

## Key Findings & Recommendations
1. **Upsides of tech reduction**  
   - Less scattered attention, calmer daily routine, more intentional use of time.  
   - Facilitates noticing what truly matters instead of reacting to every ping. [[concepts/attention-control]]

2. **Common drawbacks**  
   - Initial feeling of disconnection, fear of missing urgent updates, and friction when quick actions (e.g., sending a photo) are needed.

3. **Transition tactics**  
   - Gradual rollout rather than a sudden digital cleanse.  
   - Create explicit “device rules” (e.g., *“use this app on [[entities/laptop]] only”*). [[concepts/device-separation]]  
   - Communicate your new habits to friends/family to manage expectations.

4. **Minimal‑but‑reachable setup**  
   - Keep **calls**, **texts**, and a **single messaging app** for essential group chats.  
   - Mute all non‑essential notifications; allow alerts only for direct mentions or a short‑list of priority contacts. [[concepts/notification-management]]  
   - Schedule fixed “check‑in” windows (e.g., morning & after dinner) to avoid constant vigilance.

5. **Maps & navigation**  
   - Recommended to keep a basic map app on the primary phone; it’s low‑risk and essential for last‑minute changes.  
   - Stand‑alone GPS devices are a viable alternative if the phone lacks navigation capability. [[entities/dedicated-gps-device]]  
   - Printed directions are only suitable for static, familiar routes.

6. **Low‑friction chat handling**  
   - Use a secondary device (old smartphone, tablet, or computer) dedicated to the group chat, accessed only at home.  
   - Ensure urgent communication falls back to **calls** or **texts** on the primary (e.g., [[entities/flip-phone]]) phone.

7. **Rule‑of‑thumb for “slippery‑slope” avoidance**  
   - Separate *must‑reach‑me* channels (calls/texts) from *nice‑to‑check* channels (social/chat apps) by device or strict notification settings.
