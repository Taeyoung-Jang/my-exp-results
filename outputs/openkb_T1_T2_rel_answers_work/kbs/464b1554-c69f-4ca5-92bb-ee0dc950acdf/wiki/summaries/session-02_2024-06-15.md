## Overview

A concise guide for organizing apps, optimizing battery/performance, and setting up a two‑page home‑screen layout on both iPhone and Android.

## iPhone Settings

- **Notifications**: Keep banners & badges for work/chat apps; badges‑only for social, shopping, finance; disable sounds for non‑urgent apps. Use *Scheduled Summary* for social, shopping, media, and most finance apps.
- **Background App Refresh**: Enable only for messaging, maps, health, email, and widgets you use. Turn off for social, shopping, media, and most utilities.
- **Location Services**: Set most apps to *While Using*; *Always* only for Maps, rideshare, weather.
- **Widgets**: One medium widget (e.g., Calendar or Weather) on the top of the home screen.

## Android Settings

- **Notification Channels**: Activate main message channels for work/chat; disable promotional or status channels for social, shopping, finance.
- **Battery/Background**: Set *Unrestricted* for messaging, navigation, health; *Optimized* for most apps; *Restricted* for rarely used utilities.
- **Location**: Choose *While Using* for the majority; *Always* for navigation‑related apps.
- **Widgets**: Same recommendation – one medium widget on top.

## Two‑Page Home Screen Template

**Page 1 – Daily Essentials**  
- Top: Medium widget (Calendar/Weather)  
- Row 1: 4 core icons (e.g., Messages, Browser, Camera, Music)  
- Row 2: 4 secondary daily icons (e.g., Mail, Calendar, Notes, Wallet)  
- Dock: 4 always‑available apps (Messages, Browser, Camera, Music)

**Page 2 – Weekly Rotation & Catch‑All**  
- Row 1: 4 weekly‑use apps (rotating)  
- Row 2: 4 additional rotating apps  
- Bottom: Two folders  
  - **Everyday Stuff** – Social, media, shopping, finance, travel apps used often but not daily.  
  - **Extras** – Work tools, utilities, experimental apps, or any temporary installs.

All other apps reside in the [[entities/app-library]] or [[entities/app-drawer]].
