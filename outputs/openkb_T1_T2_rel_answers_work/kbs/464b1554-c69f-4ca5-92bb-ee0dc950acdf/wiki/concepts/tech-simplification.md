# Tech Simplification

**Definition**

Tech simplification is the purposeful trimming of digital tools and services to reduce cognitive load, improve attention, and create a calmer daily routine while preserving essential communication and navigation capabilities.

**Motivations**
- **Upsides**: Less scattered attention, calmer evenings, more intentional use of time, and clearer awareness of what truly matters.
- **Drawbacks**: Initial fear of missing urgent updates, feeling disconnected, and occasional friction when quick actions are needed.

**Core Strategies**
1. **Gradual rollout** – Instead of a sudden digital cleanse, phase out non‑essential apps over time.
2. **Device separation** – Keep *must‑reach* channels (calls, texts) on a primary device (e.g., a [[entities/flip-phone]]) and move optional services (social apps, news) to a secondary device such as a [[entities/laptop]] or an old smartphone.
3. **Notification management** – Mute all non‑essential notifications; allow alerts only for direct mentions or a short list of priority contacts. See [[concepts/notification-management]] for detailed tactics.
4. **Attention control** – Use fixed “check‑in” windows (morning, after dinner) to limit spontaneous checking, reinforcing focus. Related ideas are covered in [[concepts/attention-control]].
5. **Device‑level rules** – Define explicit rules like “use this app on laptop only” to keep the primary device free of distracting apps.

**Practical Example (Session 33)**
- The user switched to a minimalist [[entities/flip-phone]] and kept only calls, texts, and a single messaging app for essential group chats.
- Group chat notifications were limited to direct mentions, and the app was accessed only during scheduled windows.
- Navigation was handled either by a basic map app on the flip phone or by a [[entities/dedicated-gps-device]] when the phone lacked navigation capability.
- This setup illustrates the interplay of [[concepts/device-separation]] and [[concepts/notification-management]] to avoid the “slippery slope” of constant checking.
