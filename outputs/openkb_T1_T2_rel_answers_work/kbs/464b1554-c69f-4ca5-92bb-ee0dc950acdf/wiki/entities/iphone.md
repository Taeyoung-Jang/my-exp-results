# iPhone

The iPhone is Apple’s flagship smartphone running the [[entities/ios]] operating system. It provides a range of built‑in tools for [[concepts/phone-organization]] and [[concepts/battery-optimization]], including notifications, background app refresh, location services, and widgets.

## Key Settings Highlighted in Session 02 (2024‑06‑15)

- **Notifications** – Use banners and badges for work/chat apps, badge‑only for social, shopping, and finance, and turn off sounds for non‑urgent apps. Leverage *Scheduled Summary* to bundle non‑critical alerts.
- **Background App Refresh** – Enable only for messaging, maps, health, email, and the widget you keep on the home screen; disable for social, shopping, media, and most utilities.
- **Location Services** – Default most apps to *While Using*; keep *Always* only for Maps, rideshare, and weather.
- **Widgets** – Place a single medium widget (e.g., Calendar or Weather) at the top of the home screen for quick glance information.

## Recommended Two‑Page Home Screen Layout

- **Page 1 (Daily Essentials)**
  - Top: medium widget (Calendar/Weather) [[entities/widgets]]
  - Row 1: 4 core icons (Messages, Browser, Camera, Music)
  - Row 2: 4 secondary daily icons (Mail, Calendar, Notes, Wallet)
  - Dock: 4 always‑available apps (Messages, Browser, Camera, Music)
- **Page 2 (Weekly Rotation & Catch‑All)**
  - Row 1: 4 weekly‑use apps (rotating)
  - Row 2: 4 additional rotating apps
  - Bottom: two folders
    - **Everyday Stuff** – social, media, shopping, finance, travel apps used often but not daily
    - **Extras** – work tools, utilities, experimental or temporary apps

All other apps reside in the built‑in [[entities/app-library]] for quick search and access.
