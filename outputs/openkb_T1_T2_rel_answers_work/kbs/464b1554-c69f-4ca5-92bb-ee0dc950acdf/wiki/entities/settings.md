## Settings

**Type:** Other

**Description:** The Settings app or menu on a mobile device where users adjust system preferences such as notifications, background app refresh, battery optimization, and location services.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]]
- Provides access to **notification management** options, allowing users to toggle banners, badges, sounds, and schedule summaries for different app categories.
- Controls **battery optimization** settings, including background activity limits and restrictions for individual apps.
- Allows configuration of **phone organization** features like app folders, widget placement, and the arrangement of home‑screen pages.
- Hosts location permissions, typically set to “While Using” for most apps and “Always” for navigation‑related services.
- Central hub for managing **widgets**, including adding, resizing, and removing them from the home screen.
