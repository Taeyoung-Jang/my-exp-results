# Android (product)

Android is a widely used mobile operating system that powers a large variety of smartphones and tablets.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]]
- **Notification management**: Android allows granular control via *Settings > Apps > Notifications* and per‑app notification channels, enabling users to silence promotional or status alerts while keeping essential messaging channels active. [[concepts/notification-management]]
- **Battery & background optimization**: Users can tune background activity through *Settings > Battery > Background Usage* or *Battery Optimization*, setting apps to *Unrestricted* (e.g., messaging, navigation), *Optimized* (most apps), or *Restricted* (rare utilities) to improve battery life. [[concepts/battery-optimization]]
- **Location services**: The OS provides a *Location* setting where most apps are set to *While Using*, with *Always* reserved for navigation‑related apps.
- **App organization**: Android’s built‑in *App Drawer* serves as the overflow area for all installed applications, complementing a minimal home‑screen layout with two pages and two catch‑all folders for rotating apps. [[entities/app-drawer]]
- **Widgets**: One medium widget (e.g., Calendar or Weather) is recommended on the top of the primary home screen to provide quick information without excess background work. [[entities/widgets]]
- **Settings navigation**: The primary configuration paths referenced are under the *Settings* app, covering notifications, battery, location, and widget placement. [[entities/settings]]
