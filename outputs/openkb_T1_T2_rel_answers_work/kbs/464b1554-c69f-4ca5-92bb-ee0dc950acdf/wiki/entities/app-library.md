# App Library

**Type:** other

The **App Library** is a built‑in iOS feature that aggregates every installed app into a searchable, automatically grouped collection, acting as an overflow area for apps that are not placed on the home‑screen pages. It allows users to keep their home screens minimal while still accessing the full app set, supporting the [[concepts/phone-organization]] workflow described in [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]].

## Key facts from the session document
- Suggested as the primary way to hide unused or rarely accessed apps on the iPhone, complementing a two‑page home‑screen layout.
- Works together with the **App Drawer** concept on Android; both serve as centralized app repositories, but the App Library is native to [[entities/ios]] and [[entities/iphone]].
- By moving non‑essential apps to the App Library, background activity and notification checks are reduced, contributing to better [[concepts/battery-optimization]] and smoother performance.
- The document recommends keeping only the most frequently used icons on the two visible pages, while the rest reside in the App Library.
