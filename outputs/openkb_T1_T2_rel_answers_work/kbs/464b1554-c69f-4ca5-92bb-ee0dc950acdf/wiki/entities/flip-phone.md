# Flip Phone (Product)

A **flip phone** is a compact, typically non‑smartphone device with a clamshell design that emphasizes basic communication (calls and texts) while omitting most apps and internet features. In the context of [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-13]], the user adopted a flip phone to achieve a quieter, more intentional daily routine.

## Key Facts from Session 33
- **Purpose:** Implements [[concepts/tech-simplification]] by stripping away social media, news feeds, and other “infinite scroll” distractions.
- **Communication:** Handles essential calls and texts; all urgent contact happens via these channels.
- **Chat Management:** The user keeps a single group chat off the flip phone, accessing it on a secondary device (e.g., an old [[entities/laptop]] or another phone) to avoid slipping back into constant checking.
- **Navigation:** If the flip phone includes a basic map app, it is kept for low‑risk navigation; otherwise, the user relies on a [[entities/dedicated-gps-device]] for reliable directions.
- **Device Boundaries:** By separating “must‑reach‑me” (calls/texts on the flip phone) from “nice‑to‑check” (social or news apps on other devices), the user leverages [[concepts/device-separation]] to enforce discipline.
- **Notification Strategy:** All non‑essential notifications are muted, using [[concepts/notification-management]] principles to ensure only direct mentions or priority contacts generate alerts.
- **Attention Benefits:** The reduced screen time improves focus and reduces scattered attention, aligning with [[concepts/attention-control]].
