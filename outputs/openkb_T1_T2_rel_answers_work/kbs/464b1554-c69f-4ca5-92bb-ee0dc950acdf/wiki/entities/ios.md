# iOS

Apple's proprietary mobile operating system that powers the iPhone and iPad. It provides a tightly integrated ecosystem of apps, settings, and services.

## Key Settings Highlighted in Session 02 (2024‑06‑15)

- **Notification Management** – Use [[concepts/notification-management]] to keep work/chat apps on banners & badges, switch social, shopping, and finance apps to badges‑only, and disable sounds for non‑urgent notifications. Leverage *Scheduled Summary* for non‑critical apps.
- **Background App Refresh** – Turn off for most apps; keep it enabled only for messaging, maps, health, email, and active widgets to improve [[concepts/battery-optimization]].
- **Location Services** – Set most apps to *While Using*; allow *Always* only for Maps, rideshare, and weather.
- **Widgets** – Place a single medium widget (e.g., Calendar or Weather) on the top of the home screen; limit to one or two to reduce background activity.

## Home Screen Organization

- **App Library** – Acts as an overflow drawer, automatically collecting all apps not placed on the home screens. Use it to hide rarely used apps and keep the home screens minimal.
- **Two‑Page Layout** –
  - *Page 1*: Daily essentials with a medium widget, two rows of four icons each, and a dock of four core apps (messages, browser, camera, music).
  - *Page 2*: Weekly rotation with two rows of four rotating apps and two folders: **Everyday Stuff** (social, media, shopping, finance, travel) and **Extras** (work tools, utilities, experimental apps).
