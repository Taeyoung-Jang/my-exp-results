# Widgets

**Entity type:** other

Widgets are customizable mini‑apps that sit on a phone’s home screen and provide at‑a‑glance information or quick actions without opening the full app. In the *session‑02_2024‑06‑15* conversation, widgets are recommended as part of a minimal two‑page home‑screen layout for both iPhone and Android devices.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]]
- Use a single **medium widget** (e.g., Calendar or Weather) on the top of the primary home screen to keep essential data visible.
- Limit widgets to one or two per screen to avoid unnecessary background activity that can affect [[concepts/battery-optimization]].
- Widgets should complement the **notification‑management** strategy by surfacing only truly needed info.

## Related entities and concepts
- Works together with [[entities/ios]] and [[entities/android]] home‑screen setups.
- Often accessed via the **App Library** on iOS or the **App Drawer** on Android, which act as overflow containers for apps and widget selection.
- Configured through the device’s **Settings** menu (see [[entities/settings]]).
