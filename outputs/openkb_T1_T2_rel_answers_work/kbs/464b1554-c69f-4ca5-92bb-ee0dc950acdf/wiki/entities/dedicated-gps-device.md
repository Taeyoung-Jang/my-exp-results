# Dedicated GPS Device

A **dedicated GPS device** is a standalone navigation unit that provides turn‑by‑turn directions without the distractions of a full‑featured smartphone. In the context of minimal‑tech lifestyles, it serves as a low‑risk alternative to keeping a map app on a primary phone, especially when that phone is a **flip phone** with limited functionality.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-13]]
- **Purpose:** Offers reliable navigation for outings while keeping the primary phone free of potentially distracting apps.
- **Device Separation:** Aligns with the principle of [[concepts/device-separation]] by assigning navigation to a separate hardware piece, preventing the phone from becoming a gateway to other apps.
- **Simplicity:** Provides a single, focused function (maps) without the temptation of social feeds, news, or other infinite scroll content.
- **Reliability:** Works offline and is less prone to sudden software updates or battery‑draining background processes, supporting overall [[concepts/tech-simplification]] goals.
- **Use Cases:** Recommended when the primary phone lacks a built‑in navigation app or when the user wants to avoid any additional digital clutter on the phone.
