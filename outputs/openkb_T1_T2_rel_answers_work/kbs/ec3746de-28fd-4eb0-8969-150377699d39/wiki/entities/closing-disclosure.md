# Closing Disclosure

**Type:** Work (legal/financial document)

**Description:** A standardized settlement statement that outlines the final loan costs, payment schedule, and escrow details for a home purchase.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-05-17]]:**
- Provides a line‑item breakdown of closing costs, loan terms, and estimated monthly payments.
- Indicates whether property taxes and insurance are being paid through an escrow account; look for sections titled “Estimated Taxes, Insurance & Assessments” and “Escrow Account” (see [[concepts/property-tax-escrow]]).
- Used by buyers to verify that numbers on the lender’s final paperwork match earlier estimates; any discrepancy can delay closing.
- Serves as a reference for post‑closing tasks, such as confirming escrow setup in the [[entities/mortgage-servicer-portal]] and ensuring insurance start dates.
- Buyers should retain a copy alongside other essential documents like the settlement statement, mortgage note, deed of trust, and the recorded deed (see [[entities/recorded-deed]]).
