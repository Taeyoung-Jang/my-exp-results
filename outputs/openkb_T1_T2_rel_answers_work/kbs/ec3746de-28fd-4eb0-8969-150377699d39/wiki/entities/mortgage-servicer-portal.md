# Mortgage Servicer Online Portal

**Type:** Product

**Brief description:** The Mortgage Servicer Online Portal is a web‑based platform that allows homeowners to view and manage their mortgage account, including payment schedules, escrow balances, and tax/insurance handling.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-05-17]]
- **Escrow verification:** Users can log in to see an “Escrow” section that lists property taxes and insurance. If this section is present, it confirms that taxes are being paid through escrow; its absence indicates the borrower must pay taxes directly.
- **Payment breakdown:** The portal shows the monthly payment split (principal, interest, escrow). This helps homeowners ensure the “Estimated Taxes, Insurance & Assessments” line from the closing disclosure matches the portal data.
- **Customer service:** The portal’s contact information (often listed on the closing packet) can be used to call the lender quickly for confirmation of escrow status.
- **Timing:** The portal typically becomes active a few days after closing, once the loan “boards.” Homeowners are advised to check it within the first week to avoid missed tax payments.

## Usage Tips
1. **Log in immediately after closing** – verify that the escrow section appears and matches the closing disclosure.
2. **Check monthly statements** – confirm that property tax and insurance amounts are being accrued correctly.
3. **Set up alerts** – most portals allow email or SMS notifications for upcoming escrow draws or tax due dates.
4. **Contact support** – if the escrow section is missing or numbers seem off, call the lender using the contact info on the closing packet.
