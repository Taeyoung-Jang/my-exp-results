# Recorded Deed

The **Recorded Deed** is the official legal instrument that transfers title ownership from the seller to the buyer and is filed with the local land records office after a real‑estate closing. It serves as public proof of who holds the fee simple interest in the property.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-38_2025-05-17]]
- **Timing:** Typically recorded within **2‑4 weeks** after closing; busy counties may take longer.
- **Verification:** The fastest way to confirm recording is to search the **[[entities/county-recorder-office]]** online portal (or call the office) using the buyer’s name or property address.
- **Related Documents:** Should be kept alongside the **[[entities/closing-disclosure]]**, settlement statement, mortgage note, and homeowner’s insurance declarations.
- **Importance in Post‑Closing Tasks:** Appears in the **[[concepts/post-closing-checklist]]** as a required document to confirm that the transfer is legally complete before finalizing escrow, tax, and HOA setups.

## How It Connects to Other Home‑Buying Steps
- **Property Tax & Escrow:** Once the deed is recorded, the new owner’s name appears on the tax assessor’s roll, which influences whether property taxes are paid through **[[concepts/property-tax-escrow]]** or billed directly.
- **HOA Onboarding:** Many homeowners associations require a copy of the recorded deed to update ownership records and grant access; see **[[concepts/hoa-onboarding]]**.
- **Mortgage Servicing:** The **[[entities/mortgage-servicer-portal]]** may display the recorded deed as part of the loan file for verification.

## Practical Tips
1. **Check Recording Status:** Within the first month, log into the county recorder’s portal or call the office to ensure the deed has been indexed.
2. **Secure Copies:** Keep both the original (if provided) and a certified copy in a safe place with other closing documents.
3. **Notify Stakeholders:** Share a copy with your mortgage servicer, HOA, and insurance provider to avoid delays in tax billing or coverage updates.
