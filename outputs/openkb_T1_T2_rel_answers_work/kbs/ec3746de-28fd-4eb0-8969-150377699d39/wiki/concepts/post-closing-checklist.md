# Post‑Closing Checklist

**Definition:** A structured list of urgent and essential tasks for new homeowners in the first weeks after closing.

---

## Overview
The post‑closing checklist guides homeowners through the most time‑sensitive actions after taking ownership of a property. It prioritizes utilities, escrow/property‑tax verification, homeowner’s insurance, HOA onboarding, address changes, and document collection. The checklist is derived from the practical advice in [[summaries/session-38_2025-05-17]].

---

## Time‑Sensitive Tasks (First Week)
1. **Utility Transfer** – Register electric, water, gas, trash, and internet in your name and confirm start dates to avoid service lapses.
2. **Escrow / Property‑Tax Verification** –
   - Log into the **mortgage servicer portal** and look for an “Escrow” section showing property taxes and insurance.
   - Review the **[[entities/closing-disclosure]]** for language such as *Estimated Taxes, Insurance & Assessments* and an *Escrow Account* section.
   - If no escrow is shown, plan to pay taxes directly according to the county’s billing cycle.
3. **Homeowner’s Insurance Confirmation** – Ensure the policy effective date matches the closing date and that coverage meets replacement‑cost requirements.
4. **HOA Onboarding** – Provide ownership details, obtain the payment schedule, gate codes, and portal login. See [[concepts/hoa-onboarding]] for best practices.
5. **Address Change & Mail Forwarding** – Set up USPS forwarding within 7 days and update banks, employer, DMV, and credit‑card providers.
6. **Document Collection** – Gather copies of the **[[entities/closing-disclosure]]**, settlement statement, mortgage note, **[[entities/recorded-deed]]** (once received), insurance declarations, inspection report, and any warranty paperwork.

---

## Ongoing 30‑Day Checklist
- Review and register any home‑repair warranties (appliances, HVAC, roof) within the first two weeks.
- Confirm that the recorded deed has been filed; typical recording time is 2‑4 weeks. Use the **[[entities/county-recorder-office]]** online portal or phone call for rapid verification.
- Establish a **[[concepts/home-repair-fund]]** or incorporate the **[[concepts/maintenance-budgeting-rule-of-thumb]]** to budget for routine upkeep.
- Begin budgeting for irregular home expenses; see [[concepts/budgeting-for-irregular-home-expenses]].

---

## Essential Documents to Keep
- **Closing Disclosure** (entity) – shows escrow and tax details.
- Final Settlement Statement (HUD‑1).
- Mortgage Note & Deed of Trust.
- Recorded Deed (entity) – proof of ownership.
- Homeowner’s Insurance Declarations Page.
- Inspection Report & Repair Agreements.
- HOA Governing Documents and payment schedule.
- Warranty registrations and receipts.

---

## Verification Steps
- **Escrow vs. Direct Tax Billing:** Look for *In Escrow* on the closing disclosure and the escrow section in the mortgage portal.
- **Recorded Deed Status:** Search the **[[entities/county-recorder-office]]** portal by property address or owner name; if unavailable after four weeks, contact the title/escrow company.
