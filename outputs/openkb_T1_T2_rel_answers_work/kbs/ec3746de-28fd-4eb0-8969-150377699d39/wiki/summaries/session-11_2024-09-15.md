## Summary of Session 11 (2024-09-15)

The conversation provides practical advice for planning and saving for irregular, high‑cost home expenses without feeling financially constrained.

### Key Strategies
- **Treat irregular expenses as monthly**: Allocate a small, consistent amount each month to a dedicated *home repair fund* rather than waiting for a large expense to arrive.
- **Separate financial buckets**: Use one general bucket for unexpected repairs and, if motivating, an additional bucket for predictable large projects (e.g., roof replacement, HVAC). The goal is to keep money isolated from everyday spending.
- **Rule‑of‑thumb for initial target**: Save **1‑2 % of the home’s value per year** for maintenance, then divide by 12 for a monthly contribution. Adjust upward as you learn actual costs.
- **Prioritise big goals with a safety valve**: While focusing on a specific goal like a roof, still contribute a modest amount (e.g., $20‑$30/month) to the general repair bucket to avoid derailing the primary target.
- **Distinguish from emergency fund**: An *emergency fund* covers major life‑disrupting events (job loss, medical crises). A dedicated home‑repair cushion handles routine‑but‑unpredictable fixes, preserving the emergency fund for true emergencies.
