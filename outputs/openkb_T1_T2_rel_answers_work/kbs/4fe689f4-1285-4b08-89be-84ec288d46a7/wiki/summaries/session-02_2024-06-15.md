# Summary of Session 02 (2024‑06‑15)

This conversation provides a practical framework for newcomers and experienced users who want to keep a [[entities/smartwatch]] useful **without becoming a distraction**.

## Core priorities for a first (or reset) wearable
- **Reliable activity & sleep tracking** – step count, total sleep time, resting heart rate.
- **Battery life & comfort** – essential for consistent wear.
- **Minimal notifications** – only calls (and optionally a few key contacts) to avoid constant interruptions. See [[concepts/wearable-notifications]].

## “Noise” health metrics for beginners
- Advanced scores such as heart‑rate variability, stress, readiness, and detailed sleep stages are often non‑actionable.
- Focus on basic step counts, overall sleep duration, and resting heart rate trends before adding finer‑grained data. See [[concepts/health-metrics]].

## Notification rules (distraction management)
- Enable **calls** (and optionally texts from 2‑3 priority contacts).
- Disable all other app alerts, emails, social notifications, and strong haptic prompts.
- Use **Do Not Disturb** / quiet mode during sleep and set hours. See [[concepts/distraction-management]].

## 30‑day “quiet mode” reset plan
| Week | Notification settings | Daily metrics | Additional actions |
|------|----------------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **1** | Allow only phone calls. Turn off all other notifications, screen wake, and strong haptics. | Check steps, total sleep time, resting heart rate once in the evening. | Enable Do Not Disturb for sleep. |
| **2** | Same as Week 1. | Same three metrics, continue once‑daily check. | Observe habit shifts; no new features added. |
| **3** | Add text alerts from 2–3 priority contacts only. | Same three metrics; add a brief weekend reflection on any trend changes. | Keep other apps silent. |
| **4** | Decide on minimal additions (e.g., a vibration reminder for scheduled workouts or a daily summary screen). | Maintain daily check‑in on steps, sleep duration, resting heart rate. | Evaluate which features felt helpful vs. noisy. |

By the end of the month the user should have a clear sense of which **[[concepts/wearable-notifications]]**, **[[concepts/health-metrics]]**, and **[[concepts/distraction-management]]** truly support their health goals and which constitute clutter.
