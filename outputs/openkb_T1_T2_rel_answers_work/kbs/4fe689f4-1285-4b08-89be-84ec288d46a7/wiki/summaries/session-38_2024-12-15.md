# Summary of session-38_2024-12-15

**Main topic:** Deciding whether to wear an analog watch, a fitness tracker, or nothing, with detailed guidance on swim‑specific trackers and a step‑by‑step quiet‑setup for the Garmin Swim 2.

## Options Overview
- **Analog watch** – low‑maintenance, style‑focused, provides time only, no workout data, no notifications.
- **Fitness tracker** – records swims, runs, sleep, heart‑rate; useful for motivation but adds charging and potential notifications.
- **No watch** – freedom of unencumbered wrists; downside is needing the phone for time and data.

## Swim‑Specific Tracker Features & Annoyances
- **Key features**: automatic lap counting, distance & pace, simple interval timer, optional heart‑rate (variable reliability underwater).
- **Common annoyances**: wet‑hand screen responsiveness, mis‑counted laps on irregular strokes, menu navigation in cold/wet conditions, frequent charging.

## Minimal‑Feel Trackers
- **Garmin Swim 2** – slim, dedicated swim focus, accurate lap/distance, long battery, minimal smart‑watch feel.
- **Fitbit Inspire series** – very thin band, basic swim metrics, more lifestyle prompts (can be turned off).
- **Garmin Vivosport** – similar to Swim 2 but slightly bulkier.

## Comfort‑Testing Tips
1. Wear any lightweight band (fabric or silicone) for a few days to reacclimate the wrist.
2. Borrow a friend’s tracker or buy from a return‑friendly retailer; wear at home and in short workouts before committing.

## Quiet Setup Checklist for Garmin Swim 2
1. **On‑watch settings**: 
   - Settings → Phone → turn off all smart notifications.
   - Disable *Move Alerts* and other activity reminders.
   - System → Vibration only for button presses.
   - Activity Profiles → keep only *Pool Swim*.
2. **Garmin Connect app**: 
   - Device Settings → turn off call, text, app notifications.
   - Disable daily goals, intensity alerts, Body Battery, stress reminders.
3. **Display**: In *Pool Swim* activity settings, set *Display Timeout* to “Stay On” (or longest) to keep screen lit during sets without extra alerts.

## Interval Training on the Swim 2
- For a manual set like **10 × 100 m with 20 s rest**:
  1. Start *Pool Swim* activity.
  2. At each wall, press the lap button – watch shows an upward‑counting rest timer.
  3. After 20 s, press lap button again to begin the next 100 m.
- The summary automatically separates *Swim* and *Rest* segments, providing splits for both.

## Additional Considerations
- **Battery life** – both Garmin Swim 2 and Fitbit Inspire offer multi‑week battery spans when swim‑only mode is used.
- **Nags vs Quiet** – Garmin Swim 2 is inherently less prone to lifestyle nudges than Fitbit devices.
- **Testing interval UI** – try a short manual interval set before programming structured workouts to ensure the timer display meets your preference.
