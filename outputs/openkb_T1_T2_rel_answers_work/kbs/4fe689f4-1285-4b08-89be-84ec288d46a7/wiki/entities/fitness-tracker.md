# Fitness Tracker (product)

A **fitness tracker** is a type of **wearable-device** designed to monitor physical activity, sleep, and basic health metrics such as steps and heart rate.  In the *session-02_2024-06-15* conversation, the assistant advises users who are new to wearables—or who want to “reset” an existing device—on how to prioritize useful features while limiting distraction.

## Key points from the session
- **Core metrics to focus on**: step count, total sleep time, and resting heart‑rate trends. These are presented as the most actionable data for beginners, while more advanced metrics like heart‑rate variability, stress scores, and detailed sleep stages are described as “noise.”
- **Distraction management**: The guidance emphasizes a minimalist notification setup—allowing only phone calls (and optionally texts from a few priority contacts) and disabling all other alerts. This aligns with the broader concept of [[concepts/distraction-management]].
- **30‑day quiet‑mode reset**: A week‑by‑week plan shows how to strip a wearable down to essentials, gradually re‑introducing limited notifications and a daily check‑in on the core metrics.
- **Battery life and comfort**: Mentioned as critical factors for consistent use, influencing the decision to choose a smaller, less‑feature‑rich device.
