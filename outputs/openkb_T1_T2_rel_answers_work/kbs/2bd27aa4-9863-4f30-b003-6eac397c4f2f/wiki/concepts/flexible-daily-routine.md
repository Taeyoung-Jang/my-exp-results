# Flexible Daily Routine

**Definition**
A scheduling framework that organizes daily activities by the amount of time elapsed since waking, rather than by fixed clock times. This allows the routine to shift naturally with irregular sleep patterns while preserving the order of core activities.

**Core Elements** (derived from [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]])
- **Startup ( +0:00‑+1:00 )** – hydrate, light snack, brief movement.
- **Focus block ( +1:00‑+4:00 )** – deep work or high‑priority tasks.
- **Workout** – placed either at the end of the focus block or just before lunch, lasting 30‑45 min.
- **Meals** – first meal within the first hour, second around the midpoint of the waking period, final meal a few hours before wind‑down.
- **Daily tidy** – a 10‑15 min slot after lunch or mid‑afternoon.
- **Wind‑down** – begins roughly +14 hours after wake time.

**Adaptations**
- **Minimum viable day** – a compressed version for wake‑times after 10 am that still meets a 9‑5 availability window, using a hard stop at 6 pm. See [[concepts/minimum-viable-day]] and [[concepts/hard-stop-rule]].
- **Weekend mode** – same rhythm without work blocks, turning the focus period into personal projects. See [[concepts/weekday-weekend-mode]].

**Benefits**
- Consistency of habit order despite fluctuating sleep.
- Clear boundaries (e.g., no work after 6 pm) prevent drift into late night.
- Simple arithmetic (W + offset) makes the routine easy to copy‑paste for any wake time.

**Implementation template**
    Wake time = W
    W‑W+0:10 — Startup
    W+0:10‑W+? — Focus block (or responsive mode)
    … etc.
