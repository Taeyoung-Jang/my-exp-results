# Minimum Viable Day

**Definition**: A streamlined daily schedule designed for wake‑times after 10 am that still fulfills a mandatory 9 – 5 work availability while enforcing a hard stop at 6 pm.

---

## Core Principles
- **Time‑since‑waking framework** – Activities are anchored to the moment of waking (W) rather than clock time.  
- **Fixed work cutoff** – No work after 6 pm to prevent drift into the night.  
- **Prioritised tasks** – Only essential responsiveness during the 9 – 5 window; deep work is condensed into a one‑hour block.
- **Minimal routine** – A short 10‑minute startup, a 20‑25 minute workout, and a quick tidy keep habit continuity without extending the day.

---

## Step‑by‑Step Checklist (applicable to any W > 10 am)
1. **W → W+0:10** – Startup: hydrate, small bite, quick stretch.
2. **W+0:10 → 5:00 pm** – *Responsive mode*: stay available for 9 – 5 duties, handle essentials, skip deep work. First real meal around W+1:30 → W+2:00.
3. **5:00 pm → 6:00 pm** – Mini focus block (1 hour) to preserve the deep‑work habit.
4. **6:00 pm → 6:30 pm** – Short workout (20–25 min).  
5. **6:30 pm onward** – Dinner, then a 10‑minute daily tidy.
6. **8:00 pm** – Begin wind‑down routine.

### Fallback when 5‑6 pm is blocked
- As soon as the schedule clears, perform a **20‑minute micro‑focus** session followed immediately by the **20‑minute workout**.  
- Keep the total under 45 minutes and respect the 6 pm hard stop when possible; if meetings run past 6 pm, finish the combined session within 45 minutes after the last meeting, then cease work.
