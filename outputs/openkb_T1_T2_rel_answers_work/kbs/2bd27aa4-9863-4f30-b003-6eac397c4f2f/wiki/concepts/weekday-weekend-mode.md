# Weekday/Weekend Mode

**Definition** – A scheduling pattern that applies a structured, work‑oriented routine on weekdays and a looser, activity‑focused routine on weekends, while preserving the same underlying timing principles.

---

## Overview
Weekday/Weekend Mode builds on the **time‑since‑waking scheduling** approach, keeping the order of daily blocks (startup, focus, exercise, meals, tidy, wind‑down) consistent across all days. The key difference is the content of the *focus* block:

* **Weekdays** – The focus block is dedicated to professional tasks (9‑5 availability) and may include a **minimum viable day** fallback when wake‑times are late.
* **Weekends** – The focus block shifts to personal projects, hobbies, or quiet time, with no mandatory work segment.

This dual‑mode design helps maintain rhythm and sleep hygiene while allowing flexibility for leisure.

---

## Weekday Mode
- Follow the **flexible‑daily‑routine** template.
- Include a **hard‑stop rule** (no work after 6 p.m.) to prevent drift into night.
- If wake‑time is after 10 a.m., apply the **minimum viable day** checklist: short startup, responsive mode for 9‑5, a 5‑6 p.m. mini focus block, then workout and wind‑down.
- Meals, exercise, and tidying are placed relative to wake‑time offsets, not clock time.

---

## Weekend Mode
- Retain the same offset structure (e.g., +0:00‑+1:00 startup, +1:00‑+4:00 personal focus, workout, meals, tidy, wind‑down).
- Replace the work‑focused focus block with personal activities (reading, hobbies, errands).
- Keep the **hard‑stop rule** for evening wind‑down to protect sleep consistency, even though there is no formal work.
- Exercise and chores remain in the same relative windows, preserving habit strength.
