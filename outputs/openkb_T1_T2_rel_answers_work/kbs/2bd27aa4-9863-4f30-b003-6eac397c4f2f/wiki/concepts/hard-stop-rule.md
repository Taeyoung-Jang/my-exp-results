# Hard‑Stop Rule

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A firm daily deadline after which no work is performed, used to keep routines from extending into late evenings and protect sleep consistency.

## Core Idea
The hard‑stop rule establishes a non‑negotiable time (e.g., 6 p.m.) at which all work‑related activities must cease.  Even when a day starts late or meetings run over, the rule forces a transition to evening routines (exercise, dinner, wind‑down) so the overall schedule does not drift later.

## How It Appears in Session 15
- The "minimum viable day" checklist includes a **hard stop at 6 p.m.** after a 5‑6 p.m. focus block and a short workout.
- A fallback is provided: if the 5‑6 p.m. block is blocked by meetings, a 20‑minute micro‑focus followed by a 20‑minute workout must finish within ~45 minutes, still respecting the 6 p.m. cutoff when possible.
- The rule is paired with a **wind‑down start around 8 p.m.**, creating a clear evening boundary.

## Benefits
- Prevents **schedule creep** when wake‑times vary.
- Guarantees a predictable **evening routine**, supporting better sleep hygiene.
- Provides a decision point for **what to drop** on late‑wake days (deep work, optional chores, etc.).

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- Works with [[concepts/time-since-waking-scheduling]] to anchor activities to wake‑time offsets while still respecting a fixed clock‑time cutoff.
- Complements [[concepts/flexible-daily-routine]] by adding a non‑flexible anchor point.
- Integral to the [[concepts/minimum-viable-day]] template for late‑wake workdays.
- Influences the design of [[concepts/weekday-weekend-mode]] by keeping weekday evenings bounded while weekends remain more fluid.

## Practical Application Steps
1. **Set a hard‑stop time** (e.g., 6 p.m.) that aligns with personal energy limits.
2. **Schedule the last work block** to end by that time (e.g., a 1‑hour focus block 5‑6 p.m.).
3. **Plan a post‑stop routine** (short workout, dinner, tidy, wind‑down) that fits before the desired sleep window.
4. **Create a fallback**: if the main block is unavailable, do a brief micro‑focus + workout combo that still respects the cutoff.
5. **Enforce the rule** consistently; treat any work after the cutoff as optional or deferred to the next day.
