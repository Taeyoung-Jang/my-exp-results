# Summary of session-15_2024-06-15

**Date:** 2024-06-15  

### Core Contribution
A flexible scheduling framework that adapts to irregular wake times, with concrete fill‑in‑the‑blank templates, worked examples, and a **minimum viable day** checklist for late‑wake workdays that must still cover a 9‑to‑5 availability window.

### Key Concepts
- **Time‑since‑waking scheduling** – organise activities by offsets (e.g., +0:00‑+1:00) rather than clock time.  
- **Flexible daily routine** – a copy‑paste checklist that can be applied to any wake time, using simple arithmetic (W + offset).  
- **Minimum viable day** – a compressed routine for wake‑times after 10 am that maintains a hard stop at 6 pm and includes a fallback for back‑to‑back meetings.  
- **Weekend mode** – same rhythm without work blocks, keeping meals, exercise, and chores consistent.  
- **Hard‑stop rule** – enforce a firm end to work at a set time to avoid night‑time drift.

### Practical Checklists
1. **General flexible template** (fill‑in‑the‑blanks) with sections such as Startup (+0:00‑+1:00), Focus block (+1:00‑+4:00), Workout, Lunch, Light tasks, Daily tidy, Wind‑down (+14:00).  
2. **Worked examples** for wake‑times 6:30, 9:15, and 11:20 showing concrete clock times for each segment.  
3. **Minimum viable day checklist** – exact durations (10 min startup, 5‑6 pm mini focus, 20‑25 min workout, hard stop at 6 pm, dinner, tidy, wind‑down).  
4. **Universal formula** for any wake time > 10 am:  
   - `W to W+0:10` – Startup  
   - `W+0:10 to 5:00 pm` – Responsive mode only  
   - `5:00‑6:00 pm` – Mini focus block  
   - `6:00‑6:30 pm` – Short workout  
   - …followed by dinner, tidy, wind‑down.  
5. **Fallback plan** – if the 5‑6 pm focus slot is blocked, perform a 20‑min micro‑focus then the 20‑min workout, keeping total time ≤ 45 min and preserving the 6 pm hard stop when possible.
