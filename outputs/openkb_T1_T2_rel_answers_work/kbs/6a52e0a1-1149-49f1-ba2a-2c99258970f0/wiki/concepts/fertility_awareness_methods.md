# Fertility Awareness Methods

Fertility awareness methods (FAM) are a collection of natural tracking techniques that help a person identify the timing of ovulation and the **[[concepts/fertile_window_timing]]**. The primary signals used are:

1. **Cycle tracking** – noting the length and regularity of menstrual cycles.
2. **Cervical mucus observation** – monitoring changes in mucus consistency and stretchiness as a sign of approaching ovulation.
3. **Basal body temperature (BBT) monitoring** – recording daily morning temperature to detect the **[[concepts/basal_body_temperature_shift]]**, which occurs after ovulation.

## Reliability
- Cycle tracking alone is the least reliable, especially with **irregular cycles**, because ovulation does not always occur at a fixed point in the cycle.
- Cervical mucus provides a real‑time cue that ovulation is approaching, but can be affected by infections, semen, lubricants, or hormonal fluctuations.
- BBT confirms ovulation after it happens; a sustained rise lasting about three days signals that ovulation occurred roughly one day earlier.
- Combining all three signals markedly improves accuracy, even when cycle length varies.

## Practical Use
- **Advance notice**: Cervical mucus patterns and **[[entities/opk]]** (ovulation predictor kits) give advance warning of the fertile window.
- **Confirmation**: BBT validates that ovulation actually took place, helping avoid missed fertile days.
- **Tools**: Many people use a **[[entities/fertility_app]]** to log temperature, mucus, and cycle data, receiving visual cues such as a “green light” when a post‑ovulation temperature rise is detected.

## Recommendations for Irregular Cycles
- Start OPK testing early (around day 8‑10 after period onset) and test once daily, switching to twice daily if a surge is suspected.
- Observe cervical mucus daily during bathroom visits.
- Record a single basal temperature each morning before moving.
- Use the combination of mucus, OPK, and BBT to maximize the chance of identifying the fertile window without overwhelming daily routines.
