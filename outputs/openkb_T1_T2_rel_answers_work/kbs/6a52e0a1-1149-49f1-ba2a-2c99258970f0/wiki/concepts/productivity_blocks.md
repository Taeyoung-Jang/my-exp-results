# Productivity Blocks

**Definition**: A set of deliberately sized work intervals (e.g., 20, 30, 45, 60 minutes) designed to match a person’s current energy state, especially during hormonal cycle phases.

## Core Principles
- **Energy‑aligned sizing**: Choose shorter blocks (20–30 min) on low‑energy days and longer blocks (45–60 min) only when feeling better than expected.
- **Task type matching**: Pair brief blocks with low‑focus activities (quick admin, light planning) and reserve longer blocks for modest progress tasks that still avoid deep concentration.
- **Flexibility**: If cramps or fatigue spike, drop to the smallest block or skip work entirely, mirroring the approach used for movement in [[concepts/low_energy_workout]].

## Practical Template from [[summaries/session-02_2025-06-15]]
| Day | Movement Duration | Work‑Block Options |
|-----|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1   | 10–15 min gentle movement | One or two 20–30 min blocks (quick messages, admin) |
| 2   | 15–20 min yoga/mobility | Two‑three 30 min blocks (light planning, outlining) |
| 3   | 20–30 min low‑intensity activity | Two‑three 30–45 min blocks (modest progress, short focused bursts) |
| 4*  | Repeat day 3 pattern | Same block options, with optional upgrades if energy improves |

**Upgrade rule**: When energy feels better than expected, extend the movement time by 5–10 minutes and add an extra work block of the next longer duration, but stop before reaching a high‑intensity grind.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works in tandem with the overall [[concepts/energy_rhythm]] to create a rhythm‑aware daily plan.
- Complements the gentle movement strategies outlined in [[concepts/low_energy_workout]].

## Usage Tips
1. **Check in** each morning: rate energy (low, average, high).
2. **Select** the appropriate block length based on that rating.
3. **Adjust** on the fly if symptoms change, always favoring shorter, low‑stress intervals on low‑energy days.

By structuring work into these adaptable blocks, you maintain steady progress without overtaxing your body or mind during fluctuating energy cycles.
