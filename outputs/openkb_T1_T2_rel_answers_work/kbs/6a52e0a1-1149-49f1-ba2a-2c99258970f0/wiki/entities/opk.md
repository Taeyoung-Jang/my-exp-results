## Ovulation Predictor Kit (OPK)

An **Ovulation Predictor Kit** (often abbreviated OPK) is a over‑the‑counter product that measures the surge of luteinizing hormone (LH) in urine, providing a forward‑looking signal that ovulation is likely to occur within the next 24‑36 hours. 

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-29]]
- **Purpose**: Gives advance notice of the fertile window, complementing other [[concepts/fertility_awareness_methods]] such as cervical mucus observation and basal body temperature tracking.
- **Start Timing**: For irregular cycles, begin testing around **day 8‑10 after the period starts** (or day 8 if cycles vary widely).
- **Testing Frequency**: Typically once per day; switch to **twice‑daily** once a faint line appears to avoid missing a short‑lived surge.
- **Interpretation**: A clear positive line indicates the LH surge and predicts ovulation **≈ 1 day later**, helping users time intercourse before the [[concepts/fertile_window_timing]] closes.
- **Limitations**: After the BBT shift (see [[concepts/basal_body_temperature_shift]]) the LH surge has already passed, so OPKs no longer provide useful information for that cycle.

### How OPKs Fit Into a Fertility Awareness Routine
1. **Early Cycle** – Start OPK testing on the recommended day.
2. **Combine With** – Observe cervical mucus daily; a fertile‑type mucus pattern plus a positive OPK gives strong confidence that ovulation is imminent.
3. **Confirm With** – Record basal body temperature each morning; a sustained rise after the predicted ovulation confirms the event.
