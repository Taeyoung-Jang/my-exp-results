# Fertility App

**Type:** product

The *Fertility App* is a digital tool that helps users monitor various fertility awareness signals—including basal body temperature, cervical mucus characteristics, and cycle dates—to identify the fertile window and confirm ovulation.

## Key Features Mentioned in [[summaries/session-36_2025-06-29]]
- **BBT tracking**: Records daily basal body temperature and alerts the user when a sustained temperature rise, known as the [[concepts/basal_body_temperature_shift]], suggests that ovulation has already occurred.
- **Cervical mucus observation**: Allows users to log mucus quality, providing advance clues about approaching ovulation as part of broader [[concepts/fertility_awareness_methods]].
- **Cycle tracking**: Stores period start dates to estimate possible ovulation windows, though the app notes that cycle‑tracking alone is the least reliable method, especially with [[concepts/fertile_window_timing]] variability.
- **Green‑light indicator**: When the app detects a consistent post‑shift temperature elevation, it signals that ovulation likely happened a day or two earlier, advising users that they are past the peak fertile days.

## Usage Guidance
- Users are encouraged to combine the app’s BBT confirmation with **OPK** tests (see [[entities/opk]]) and mucus monitoring in future cycles for more reliable advance detection.
- The app’s recommendations stress that a steady temperature climb generally means the fertile window is closed, but external factors (sleep quality, illness, environment) can affect readings.

## Relationship to Concepts
- Relies on the principles of [[concepts/fertility_awareness_methods]] to integrate multiple biomarkers.
- Interprets the [[concepts/basal_body_temperature_shift]] as a post‑ovulation confirmation.
- Helps users understand and time the [[concepts/fertile_window_timing]] for conception attempts.
