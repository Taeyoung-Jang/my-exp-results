# Summary

- **Context**: Managing daily energy swings tied to hormonal cycles.  
- **Core Idea**: Treat energy fluctuations as a natural rhythm and create separate “high‑energy” and “low‑energy” plans, often called *maintenance mode*.  
- **Low‑Energy Workout Template**: 10–30 min gentle movement options (slow walk, stretching, easy yoga, very light strength) with optional upgrades if energy is better than expected.  
- **Productivity Blocks**: 20–60 min work intervals, matched to energy level, focusing on low‑focus or light‑planning tasks.  
- **3–4 Day Schedule**: Day‑by‑day layout specifying movement duration, work‑block length, and conditional swaps for cramps or fatigue, plus “if better than expected” upgrades.  
- **Printable Menu**: Consolidated list of movement choices (10, 20, 30 min), work‑block choices (20, 30, 45, 60 min), and a simple rule for selecting options based on daily feeling.
