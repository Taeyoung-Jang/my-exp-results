# Micro Breaks

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Short, frequent pauses taken while working to relax the eyes, reset posture, and prevent fatigue.

## Overview
Micro breaks are tiny, intentional interruptions—usually a few seconds long—integrated into a continuous task. In a pottery studio, they can be as simple as looking up from the wheel, blinking deliberately, or reaching for a tool while maintaining movement. Unlike full‑length rests, micro breaks keep the workflow flowing while giving the visual and musculoskeletal systems a brief recovery period.

## Why They Matter
- **Eye‑strain mitigation:** Regularly shifting focus away from the clay reduces the build‑up of ocular fatigue [[concepts/eye-strain]].
- **Postural support:** Small adjustments in stance or seat height during a micro break help avoid prolonged neck or back strain.
- **Improved concentration:** Brief mental resets can enhance attention and precision when shaping.
- **Synergy with lighting:** When paired with proper [[concepts/ergonomic-lighting]], micro breaks help the eyes adapt to subtle changes in illumination, further lowering strain.

## Practical Tips from [[summaries/session-38_2025-07-22]]
1. **Look up for a second** while centering or pulling; this lets the eyes refocus on a distant point.
2. **Sponge your hands** and glance away for a moment—acts as a micro pause without stopping work.
3. **Shift the wheel** slightly to change the light angle, providing a visual reset.
4. **Blink deliberately** every few minutes to keep the ocular surface lubricated.

## Implementing Micro Breaks
| Situation | Micro‑break action |
|------------|--------------------|
| While centering clay | Look up to a distant point for 2–3 seconds |
| During pulling | Pause, sponge hands, blink deliberately |
| After 30 minutes of continuous wheel time | Stand, stretch shoulders, glance toward a window or far wall |

## Relation to Other Concepts
- **Ergonomic Lighting:** Soft, even lighting complements micro breaks by reducing the intensity of visual demand.
- **Eye Strain:** Micro breaks are a primary preventive strategy against the symptoms listed in [[concepts/eye-strain]].
- **Warm Lighting for Migraines:** When combined with warm‑tone bulbs, micro breaks can further lessen the likelihood of migraine triggers.
