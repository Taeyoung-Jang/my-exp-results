# Flicker‑Free Dimming

Flicker‑free dimming is the practice of using lighting components that eliminate perceptible flicker when the light level is adjusted.  In migraine‑prone users, even low‑level flicker can provoke headaches, so a stable, smooth dimming curve is a core requirement for comfortable residential lighting.

## Why it matters
- **Migraine mitigation** – Consistent, non‑flickering illumination reduces the visual stress that can trigger attacks, linking directly to [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]].
- **Comfort and glare** – Smooth dimming avoids sudden jumps in brightness that create harsh glare and eye strain.
- **Noise reduction** – Properly matched dimmers and LEDs eliminate the buzzing sound that some LED‑dimmer combos produce.

## Achieving flicker‑free operation
1. **Choose LEDs labeled “flicker‑free” or “compatible with dimmers.”**  These LEDs are engineered to maintain a constant AC waveform when dimmed.
2. **Pair with a dimmer on the Lutron compatibility list.**  Refer to [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] for guidance on checking Lutron’s LED compatibility charts before purchase.
3. **Verify fixture compatibility.**  The fixture should be marked “LED‑compatible, dimmable” and use a diffuser (fabric shade, acrylic or frosted‑glass drum) to soften the light.
4. **Avoid problem styles.**  Exposed‑filament “Edison” LEDs, ultra‑cheap unbranded LEDs, and shallow recessed cans with visible diodes tend to flicker or produce glare.

## Checklist for flicker‑free dimming
- ✅ LED is listed on Lutron’s dimmer compatibility chart.
- ✅ Dimmable fixture with a diffuser (fabric shade, acrylic drum, frosted globe).
- ✅ No visible LED die; use diffusing lenses or covers.
- ✅ Tested for silent operation (no buzzing).
- ✅ Desired color temperature (2700 K–3000 K) matches migraine‑friendly guidelines.

By following these guidelines, users can create lighting environments that dim cleanly, stay quiet, and minimize migraine triggers.
