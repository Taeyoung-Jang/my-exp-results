# LED‑Dimmer Compatibility

**Definition** – The practice of selecting LED bulbs that work reliably with a given dimmer, avoiding flicker, buzzing, and uneven light output.

---

## Why Compatibility Matters

People with light‑sensitivity, especially migraine sufferers, benefit from **flicker‑free dimming** [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] and gentle, consistent light quality. Incompatible LED‑dimmer pairings can cause:
- Visible or invisible flicker that triggers headaches
- Audible buzzing from the dimmer
- Stiff dimming curves that prevent low‑light settings
- Premature dimmer or bulb failure

## Core Compatibility Checks

1. **Manufacturer Compatibility Lists**
   - Consult the dimmer maker’s LED compatibility chart before purchase. For example, both the **Lutron Caseta PD‑6WCL‑WH** and the **Lutron Diva DVCL‑153P‑WH** have published lists of approved LEDs.
2. **LED Labeling**
   - Choose LEDs explicitly marked *dimmable* and, if possible, *flicker‑free*.
3. **Match Dimmer Type**
   - **Smart dimmers** (e.g., [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]]) often provide smoother curves and may support a broader range of LEDs.
   - **Regular LED‑rated dimmers** (e.g., [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]]) are simpler but still require compatible bulbs.
4. **Verify Fixture Compatibility**
   - Ensure the light fixture is rated for dimmable LEDs; avoid integrated‑LED kits unless they specifically claim dimmer compatibility.
5. **Test Before Full Installation**
   - If possible, install a single bulb in the dimmer circuit to confirm quiet, flicker‑free operation before wiring multiple fixtures.

## Proven LED‑Dimmer Pairings

The following products were confirmed in [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]] to work well with both Lutron dimmers:
- **Philips Warm Glow 2700 K A19** – listed under [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]].
- **Philips 3000 K Dimmable A19** – referenced as [[entities/philips-3000k-led]].
- **GE Reveal HD+ 3000 K A19** – represented by [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]].

These bulbs appear on Lutron’s compatibility lists for the **Caseta** and **Diva** models, providing smooth dimming without buzz.

## Practical Tips for Migraine‑Friendly Lighting

- Pair compatible LEDs with a **warm color temperature** (2700 K) for living spaces; 3000 K is acceptable in work areas if diffused [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]].
- Use fixtures with diffusers (fabric shades, acrylic drums) to further reduce glare.
- Re‑check compatibility when switching to newer LED generations, as updates to dimmer firmware or LED driver technology can affect performance.
