# Lutron Caseta PD‑6WCL‑WH

**Product type:** Smart in‑wall dimmer (Lutron Caseta line).

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]]**
- Designed for use with dimmable LED bulbs, especially those noted for smooth, quiet dimming such as [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]] and [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]].
- Recommended for environments where migraine‑triggering flicker and glare must be avoided.
- Pairs well with Lutron’s broader Caseta Wireless ecosystem, enabling voice, app, and preset control.
- Must be cross‑checked against Lutron’s [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] list to confirm bulb compatibility.

**Why it matters for migraine‑sensitive lighting**
- Supports [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] by using a low‑frequency, smooth dimming curve compatible with flicker‑free LEDs.
- When used with warm‑temperature LEDs (e.g., 2700 K), it helps maintain the conditions described in [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]].

**Related products**
- Companion non‑smart dimmer: [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]].
- Parent manufacturer: [[entities/lutron]].

**Typical installation**
- Fits standard single‑pole switch boxes.
- Works with fixtures that are labeled “LED‑compatible, dimmable.”
- Often paired with diffusing fixtures (fabric shades, frosted drum lights) to further reduce glare.

**Usage tips**
1. Verify the LED bulb appears on Lutron’s compatibility list before purchase.
2. Combine with warm‑tone bulbs such as Philips Warm Glow for the most migraine‑friendly environment.
3. Use the Caseta app or a smart home hub to set dimming presets for different activities (e.g., watch TV, work).
