# GE Reveal HD+ 3000K LED

**Product overview** – The GE Reveal HD+ 3000K LED is a 60 W‑equivalent, dimmable A19 bulb designed to deliver a steady warm‑white illumination at 3000 K. It is marketed for residential use where consistent color temperature and low flicker are important.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]]**
- Recommended by the assistant as a reliable 3000 K option that works smoothly with Lutron dimmers.
- Listed as one of three primary bulb suggestions for migraine‑sensitive lighting setups.
- Emphasized for its compatibility with both the **Lutron Caseta PD‑6WCL‑WH** smart dimmer and the **Lutron Diva DVCL‑153P‑WH** regular dimmer.

**Compatibility & performance**
- Falls under the [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] umbrella; appears on Lutron’s official LED compatibility list for the above dimmers, ensuring flicker‑free operation.
- Supports [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] which is crucial for users prone to light‑triggered migraines.
- Works well with diffusing fixtures (fabric shades, frosted drum diffusers) to reduce glare, aligning with [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]].

**Typical use cases**
- Overhead ceiling fixtures in home offices where a 3000 K tone provides sufficient task lighting without harshness when diffused.
- Table or floor lamps paired with Lutron dimmers for living‑room environments seeking a warm, steady light.

**Installation notes**
- Verify the fixture is marked *LED‑compatible, dimmable*.
- Avoid recessed cans with shallow housings that expose LED points; instead favor fixtures with acrylic or frosted‑glass diffusers.
- No special wet‑rating needed for standard indoor use; damp‑rated fixtures are acceptable.
