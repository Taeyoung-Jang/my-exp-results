# Philips Warm Glow 2700K LED

**Product overview**
- Manufacturer: [[entities/philips]]
- Model description: 60 W‑equivalent A19 LED, color temperature 2700 K, marketed as *Warm Glow* for a soothing, warm light shift when dimmed.
- Primary use case: residential lighting where reducing migraine triggers is important.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]]**
- Recommended as a reliable, flicker‑free option for both smart and regular dimmer setups.
- Works smoothly with Lutron dimmers, specifically the [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]] smart dimmer and the [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]] non‑smart dimmer.
- Provides the suggested lumen range (≈ 800 lm) for living‑room fixtures and pairs well with diffusing shades or drum fixtures.

**Compatibility & performance**
- Listed on Lutron’s **LED‑dimmer compatibility** chart, ensuring smooth dimming without buzzing or flicker – relevant to [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] and [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]].
- Emits a warm spectrum that aligns with [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]], helping to minimise light‑induced headaches.

**Typical installations**
- Table lamps, floor lamps, and ceiling fixtures with fabric or acrylic diffusers.
- Often combined with other recommended products such as [[entities/philips-3000k-led]] for office lighting or [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]] for a cooler 3000 K option.

**Why choose this bulb?**
- Warm color shift while dimming enhances comfort.
- Proven track record for low‑flicker performance when paired with compatible dimmers.
- Widely available under the retail name “Philips LED Warm Glow 60W Equivalent A19, 2700K.”
