# GE

General Electric (GE) is a global corporation that manufactures a variety of products, most notably in the lighting sector. In the context of residential lighting for migraine sensitivity, GE is referenced for its **Reveal HD+ 3000K LED** bulbs, which are noted for smooth dimming performance and compatibility with popular dimmers.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]]
- GE’s **Reveal HD+** bulb (see [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]]) is recommended as a reliable, dimmable 3000 K LED that works well with Lutron dimmers.
- The bulb is highlighted for its **flicker‑free** operation, aligning with the [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] principle important for migraine sufferers.
- Compatibility with Lutron’s smart and regular dimmers ([[entities/lutron]], [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]], [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]]) is emphasized, tying into the [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] concept.
- Using warm‑white LEDs (such as GE’s 3000 K offering) contributes to the [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]] guideline for reducing light‑triggered headaches.

## Products
- **GE Reveal HD+ 3000K LED** – a dimmable, warm‑white LED bulb widely cited for its stable performance with Lutron dimmers.

## Relevance
GE’s LED offerings are part of a broader strategy to create migraine‑friendly lighting environments by providing bulbs that combine appropriate color temperature, low flicker, and reliable dimmer compatibility.
