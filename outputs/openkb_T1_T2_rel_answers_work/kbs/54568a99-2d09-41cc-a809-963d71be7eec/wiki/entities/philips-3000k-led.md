# Philips 3000K LED

**Entity type:** Product

The **Philips 3000K LED** is a 60 W‑equivalent A19 LED bulb that emits a warm‑white light at a color temperature of 3000 K. It is fully dimmable and designed to work with modern LED‑compatible dimmers, making it suitable for environments where flicker and harsh glare can trigger migraines.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]]
- Recommended as a reliable 3000 K option for a home office lighting setup.
- Works smoothly with both smart and regular Lutron dimmers, specifically [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]] and [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]].
- Listed on Lutron’s LED compatibility list, ensuring low‑flicker performance and quiet operation.
- Paired with diffusing fixtures (fabric‑shade floor lamps, frosted‑glass drum fixtures) to reduce glare.
