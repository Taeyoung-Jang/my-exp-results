# Philips (Organization)

Philips is a Dutch multinational corporation that designs and manufactures a broad range of electronic products, most notably lighting solutions. In the context of [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]], Philips was recommended for its dimmable, flicker‑free LED bulbs suitable for users with migraine sensitivity.

## Key Products Mentioned
- **Philips Warm Glow 2700K LED** – a dimmable, warm‑white bulb that shifts to an even lower color temperature as it dims, helping to reduce glare and migraine triggers. See [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]].
- **Philips 3000K LED** – a steady‑warm white LED recommended for home office lighting. See [[entities/philips-3000k-led]].
- **Philips Hue White Ambiance** – smart bulbs that allow adjustable color temperature and are praised for stable, flicker‑free operation. See [[entities/philips-hue-white-ambiance]].

## Relevance to Migraine‑Friendly Lighting
The advice in the session highlights the importance of [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]] and [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] when selecting lighting for migraine sufferers. Philips’ Warm Glow line directly addresses these needs by providing warm color temperatures (2700 K) and smooth dimming without noticeable flicker.

## Compatibility
Philips LED bulbs are listed on the [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] lists for popular dimmers such as [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]] and [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]], ensuring reliable performance in both smart and regular dimming setups.
