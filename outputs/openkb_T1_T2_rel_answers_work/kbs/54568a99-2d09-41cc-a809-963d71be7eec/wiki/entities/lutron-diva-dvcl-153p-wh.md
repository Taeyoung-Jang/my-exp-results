# Lutron Diva DVCL‑153P‑WH

**Type:** Product

**Manufacturer:** [[entities/lutron]]

**Description:** A non‑smart, LED‑compatible wall dimmer designed for smooth, flicker‑free dimming of residential lighting.

**Key features cited in [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]]:**
- Works with dimmable LEDs such as [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]] and [[entities/philips-3000k-led]].
- Recommended for migraine‑sensitive environments because it supports [[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]] and avoids buzzing.
- Compatibility should be verified against Lutron’s [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] list.

**Typical use cases:**
- Living‑room and home‑office lighting where adjustable brightness and warm light are needed.
- Pairing with fixtures that use diffusers to reduce glare, supporting [[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]].

**Related products:**
- Companion smart dimmer: [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]].
- Compatible bulbs: [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]], [[entities/philips-3000k-led]], [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]].

**Installation notes:**
- Must be installed in a standard US‑rated single‑pole or 3‑way box.
- Verify that the connected LEDs are listed on Lutron’s compatibility chart.
