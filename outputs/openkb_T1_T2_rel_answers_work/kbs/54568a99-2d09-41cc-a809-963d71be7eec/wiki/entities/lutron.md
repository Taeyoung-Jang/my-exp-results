# Lutron

Lutron is a leading **organization** that designs and manufactures lighting control devices, most notably the **Caseta** and **Diva** dimmer families. Their products are frequently recommended for environments where minimizing flicker and glare is essential.

## Key Products Referenced
- **Lutron Caseta PD‑6WCL‑WH** – a smart in‑wall dimmer praised for smooth, flicker‑free dimming with compatible LED bulbs. See [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]].
- **Lutron Diva DVCL‑153P‑WH** – a non‑smart LED‑rated dimmer known for reliable operation and low buzzing. See [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]].

## Relevance to Migraine‑Sensitive Lighting
The document [[summaries/session-13_2023-07-22]] highlights Lutron dimmers as central to achieving:
- **[[concepts/flicker-free-dimming]]** by pairing labeled flicker‑free LEDs with compatible dimmers.
- **[[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]]** through Lutron’s published compatibility lists for LED bulbs.
- **[[concepts/warm-lighting-for-migraines]]** by enabling precise control of warm color temperatures (2700 K‑3000 K) without harsh glare.

## Typical Use Cases
- Residential living rooms and home offices where users need dimmable, warm lighting to reduce migraine triggers.
- Integration with smart home ecosystems (e.g., Lutron Caseta) for presets, voice control, and app‑based dimming.
- Replacement of older mechanical dimmers with LED‑rated models to eliminate buzz and flicker.
