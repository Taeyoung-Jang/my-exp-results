# Summary of Session 38 – Pottery Studio Lighting & Ergonomics

**Date:** 2025‑07‑22  

## Core Ideas
- A comfortable studio setup hinges on **soft, even lighting** and **proper posture** at the wheel.  
- Small, frequent **micro‑breaks** (looking up, stretching) reduce eye strain and headaches.  
- Monitoring subtle **warning signs** helps catch emerging lighting issues before they become painful.  
- Simple, low‑risk adjustments (lamp angle, distance, adding a secondary lamp) are preferred over modifying fixtures.  
- Covering lamps with fabric or parchment is a fire hazard; use built‑in diffusers or lower‑watt bulbs instead.

## Warning‑Sign Checklist
| Symptom | Likely Issue | Quick Check |
|---|---|---|
| Dull forehead ache after a session | Light too bright or too direct | Reduce lamp intensity or increase distance |
| Frequent blinking, dry eyes | Glare from wet clay or water bucket | Adjust lamp angle to skim the surface |
| Clay looks “off‑color” or overly contrasty | Bulb color temperature mismatch | Switch to a neutral or warm LED bulb |
| Light‑headedness when looking up from wheel | Sudden contrast change between work area and overhead skylight | Add a side lamp for balanced illumination |
| General fatigue after normal‑length session | Cumulative eye strain | Incorporate micro‑breaks and a mid‑session stretch |

## Safest/Easiest Adjustments (first‑line fixes)
1. **Re‑angle the task lamp** – aim the beam from the side so it washes across the clay rather than shining straight at you.  
2. **Move the lamp farther away or raise it** – spreads light, reducing harshness.  
3. **Shift your stool a few inches** – often eliminates a glare hotspot.  
4. **Add a second, low‑watt side lamp** – balances shadows without extra heat.  
5. **Use a lamp with a built‑in diffuser** or replace the bulb with a lower‑brightness, warm‑tone LED.  

## Safety Note on Diffusing Light
- **Avoid draping fabric or parchment** over any lamp; high‑watt bulbs can overheat and cause fire.  
- Prefer **commercial lamp diffusers**, **soft‑white LED bulbs**, or **adjusted positioning** to achieve a gentler light.
