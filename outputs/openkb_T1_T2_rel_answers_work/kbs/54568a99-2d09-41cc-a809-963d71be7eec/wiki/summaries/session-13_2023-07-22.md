# Summary of session‑13 (2023‑07‑22)

**Main focus** – Selecting lighting solutions that minimize migraine triggers by avoiding flicker and harsh glare, while providing suitable brightness and color temperature for a living room and home office.

## Key Recommendations
- **Color temperature**: 2700 K for living areas (most migraine‑friendly); 3000 K is acceptable in a home office if diffused.  
- **Brightness targets**: ~700‑900 lm per living‑room fixture; ~800‑1100 lm for office work lights. Dimmer flexibility makes exact numbers less critical.
- **Flicker‑free dimming**: Use LEDs labeled *flicker‑free* and pair with compatible dimmers. Preferred dimmers are [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]] (smart) and [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]] (regular). Always verify the bulb appears on the [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] list.
- **Smart vs. regular**: Smart dimmers (Caseta) enable presets, voice/app control, and smoother curves. Regular LED‑rated dimmers (Diva) are reliable and simpler.
- **Bulb recommendations**:
  1. **[[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]]** – consistently dimmable, warm‑shifting. 
  2. **[[entities/philips-3000k-led]]** – solid warm white for office tasks. 
  3. **[[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]]** – proven compatibility with both Lutron dimmers.
- **Fixture choices**: Favor diffusing elements – fabric or acrylic shades, frosted glass bowls, or drum fixtures with a bottom diffuser. Avoid exposed‑LED point sources, shiny reflectors, and shallow recessed cans.
- **Flush‑mount drum specs**: Acrylic or frosted‑glass diffuser, 13‑16 in diameter, 4‑6 in depth, 2‑bulb (adequate for an 8 ft ceiling) or 3‑bulb for larger spaces. Damp‑rated is fine; wet‑rated only needed for bathrooms.

## Suggested Set‑ups
### Living Room
1. **Smart** – [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]] + [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]] bulbs in table lamps and a fabric‑shade floor lamp.
2. **Smart (bulb‑centric)** – [[entities/philips-hue-white-ambiance]] bulbs + standard on/off switch.
3. **Non‑smart** – [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]] + [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]] bulbs in lamps and a frosted‑glass drum ceiling fixture.

### Home Office
1. **Smart** – [[entities/lutron-caseta-pd-6wcl-wh]] + [[entities/philips-warm-glow-2700k-led]] (or 3000 K) bulbs for overhead fixture and desk lamp.
2. **Smart (bulb‑centric)** – [[entities/philips-hue-white-ambiance]] bulbs, set to ~3000 K.
3. **Non‑smart** – [[entities/lutron-diva-dvcl-153p-wh]] + [[entities/ge-reveal-hd-plus-3000k-led]] bulbs in a drum fixture with fabric shade.

## Compatibility Checks
- Verify each LED bulb is listed on the [[concepts/led-dimmer-compatibility]] list for the chosen dimmer model.
- Ensure fixtures are marked *LED‑compatible, dimmable*.
- Confirm no exposed LED diodes are visible; use diffusers to soften light.
