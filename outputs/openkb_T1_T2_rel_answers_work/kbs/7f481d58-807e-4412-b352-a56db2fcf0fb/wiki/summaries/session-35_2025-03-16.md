# Summary of Session 35 (2025‑03‑16)

**Purpose**: Provide a concrete, time‑boxed morning routine that combines gentle stretching, a short sunrise walk, and repeatable photography practice using an iPhone.

## 1. Minute‑by‑Minute Routines

### 30‑minute version
- **00:00‑02:00** – Breath in, shoulder rolls, neck loosening.  
- **02:00‑05:00** – Gentle forward fold & side stretch.  
- **05:00‑10:00** – Begin walk toward horizon view.  
- **10:00‑12:00** – **Photo Stop 1** – first sunrise shot.  
- **12:00‑18:00** – Continue walking.  
- **18:00‑20:00** – **Photo Stop 2** – second shot (e.g., leading line).  
- **20:00‑28:00** – Walk back home.  
- **28:00‑30:00** – End with a short stretch at the doorway.

### 45‑minute version
- **00:00‑02:00** – Breath & posture reset.  
- **02:00‑05:00** – Stretch sequence (forward fold, side stretch).  
- **05:00‑12:00** – Walk, reach first viewpoint.  
- **12:00‑14:00** – **Photo Stop 1**.  
- **14:00‑22:00** – Explore, then **Photo Stop 2** (22:00‑24:00).  
- **25:00‑35:00** – Walk back; **Photo Stop 3** around 32:00 if light shifts.  
- **35:00‑45:00** – Cool‑down walk + deeper stretch before entering home.

## 2. Five Repeatable Photo Prompts
1. **Silhouette** – Frame a single tree, roofline, or person against the brightening sky.  
2. **Leading Line** – Use a path, fence, or road that draws the eye toward the sunrise.  
3. **Reflection** – Capture the sky reflected in a window, car roof, or puddle.  
4. **Foreground Detail** – Photograph a close object (leaf, railing) with the soft sky blurred behind.  
5. **Minimalist Sky** – Place a small subject in a wide expanse of clear sky for a clean, airy feel.

## 3. iPhone ProRAW Settings & Exposure Control
- **Resolution**: Prefer **48 MP ProRAW** for maximum detail; switch to 12 MP only when file size or speed is a concern.  
- **HDR**: Keep **ON** to balance bright sky and darker foreground.  
- **Night Mode**: **OFF** at sunrise – it softens the crisp light.  
- **Exposure Workflow**:  
  - Tap the brightest part of the sky.  
  - Drag the exposure slider **≈ ⅓ down** from default to retain sky color and texture.  
  - If the foreground becomes too dark, raise the slider slightly while keeping the sky tapped.  
  - If the sky looks washed out, repeat tap‑expose and lower exposure a bit more.  

### If/Then Guide
- *If the sky is blown out → tap sky, lower exposure until texture returns.*  
- *If foreground is under‑exposed → keep sky tapped, raise exposure just enough for shape detail.*  
- *If image is flat → lower exposure a touch more; ProRAW will allow later shadow lift.*
