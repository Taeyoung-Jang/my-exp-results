# Sunrise Photography

**Definition**: A focused approach to photographing the early morning light that combines a brief, repeatable [[concepts/morning-routine]] with specific composition ideas and iPhone camera techniques.

## Core Elements from Session 35

- **Time‑boxed walk**: 30‑ or 45‑minute sunrise walk that includes two to three short photo stops.
- **Repeatable prompts**:
  1. Silhouette of a tree, roofline, or person against the sky.
  2. Leading line (path, fence, road) drawing the eye toward the sun.
  3. Reflection in windows, car roofs, or puddles.
  4. Close foreground detail (leaf, railing) with soft sky background.
  5. Minimalist subject in wide, clear sky.
- **Stretch integration**: Light neck, shoulder, and forward‑fold stretches at the start and end to keep the routine mindful and low‑impact.

## iPhone Settings & Workflow

- Use **[[entities/iphone]]** in **ProRAW** mode; prefer **48 MP** for maximum detail.
- Keep **HDR on** and **Night Mode off** at sunrise.
- Tap the brightest part of the sky and drag the exposure slider about one‑third down to avoid washed‑out highlights.
- Adjust exposure upward slightly if foreground detail disappears, following the [[concepts/exposure-control]] guidelines.

## Exposure Control Tips (iPhone ProRAW)

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Sky blown out | Tap sky, lower exposure until texture and color return. |
| Foreground too dark | Keep sky tapped, raise exposure just enough for shape detail. |
| Image flat | Lower exposure a bit more; ProRAW allows later shadow lift. |
| Colors dull | Expose for sky, rely on editing to restore hue. |

## Integration with a Morning Routine

The sunrise walk doubles as a **[[concepts/morning-routine]]**: a brief stretch, mindful breathing, and a purposeful walk that ends with a final stretch. This structure makes the practice repeatable and reinforces a habit loop.
