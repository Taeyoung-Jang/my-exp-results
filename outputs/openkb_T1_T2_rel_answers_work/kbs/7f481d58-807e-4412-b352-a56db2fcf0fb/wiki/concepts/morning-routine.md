# Morning Routine

A **morning routine** is a repeatable, time‑boxed sequence that prepares the body and mind for the day while capturing the unique qualities of sunrise light. The routine described in [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-16]] blends gentle stretching, a short walk, and five repeatable photo prompts, all optimized for clear, crisp early‑morning conditions.

## Core Elements

1. **Stretching (0–5 min)** – Breath work, shoulder rolls, neck loosening, and a forward fold with side stretch. This awakens muscles and improves posture before stepping outside.
2. **Sunrise Walk (5–30 min for 30‑min version; 5–35 min for 45‑min version)** – A relaxed pace toward a clear horizon view, allowing the eye to adjust to the changing light.
3. **Photo Stops** – Integrated at specific minutes (e.g., 10‑12 min, 18‑20 min in the 30‑min plan) to capture sunrise scenes using the five repeatable prompts.
4. **Cool‑down Stretch (final 2‑5 min)** – A short stretch at the doorway to transition smoothly into the day.

## Time‑Boxed Example (30‑minute version)
- **00:00‑02:00** – Breath & shoulder rolls
- **02:00‑05:00** – Forward fold & side stretch
- **05:00‑10:00** – Begin walk
- **10:00‑12:00** – **Photo Stop 1** (silhouette)
- **12:00‑18:00** – Continue walking
- **18:00‑20:00** – **Photo Stop 2** (leading line)
- **20:00‑28:00** – Walk back home
- **28:00‑30:00** – End stretch

## Repeatable Photo Prompts
1. **Silhouette** – Frame a tree, roofline, or person against the bright sky.
2. **Leading Line** – Use a path, fence, or road that draws the eye toward the sun.
3. **Reflection** – Capture the sky reflected in a window, car roof, or puddle.
4. **Foreground Detail** – Shoot a close object (leaf, railing) with the sky softly blurred behind.
5. **Minimalist Sky** – Place a small subject in a wide expanse of clear sky.

## Technical Guidance
- **Device**: [[entities/iphone]]
- **Format**: [[entities/proraw]] (prefer 48 MP for detail)
- **Exposure**: Follow [[concepts/exposure-control]] – tap the brightest sky area, lower exposure ~⅓, adjust to keep foreground visible.
- **HDR**: Keep on; **Night Mode**: Off at sunrise.
