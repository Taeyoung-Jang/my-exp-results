# ProRAW

**Type:** Product (Apple iPhone camera feature)

**Overview**
ProRAW is Apple’s proprietary RAW capture mode that combines the flexibility of a RAW file with the computational photography benefits of the iPhone camera pipeline. It allows photographers to retain maximum sensor data—especially useful for challenging lighting such as sunrise—while still applying smart noise reduction and tone mapping in‑camera.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-16]]**
- Recommended resolution: **48 MP** ProRAW for the highest detail; 12 MP optional for smaller files.
- Workflow tip: tap the brightest part of the sky and pull the exposure slider down about a third to preserve sky texture while keeping the foreground usable.
- Suggested HDR setting: keep **HDR on**; turn **Night mode off** at sunrise.
- Exposure "if/then" guide (see [[concepts/exposure-control]]) for handling blown‑out skies or dark foregrounds.
