# iPhone

**Product**: Apple iPhone (smartphone series)

## Overview
The iPhone is a line of smartphones designed and marketed by Apple. It includes advanced camera hardware and software that support features such as HDR, ProRAW, and fine‑grained exposure control, making it a popular tool for mobile photography.

## Sunrise Photography Guidance (Session 35)
- **ProRAW**: Enable ProRAW for maximum detail; the 48 MP setting is recommended for sunrise shots.
- **HDR**: Keep HDR turned on to balance bright sky and darker foreground.
- **Night Mode**: Turn off at sunrise to avoid a muddy look.
- **Exposure Control**: Tap the brightest part of the sky and drag the exposure slider about a third down to preserve sky color and texture. Adjust upward slightly if the foreground becomes too dark. This workflow follows the recommendations in [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-16]].
- **Settings Summary**: HDR on, Night Mode off, ProRAW 48 MP, exposure adjusted per [[concepts/exposure-control]].

## Integration with Routines
The iPhone is suggested as the primary device for the [[concepts/morning-routine]] and [[concepts/sunrise-photography]] plan, providing quick, high‑quality images without extra gear.
