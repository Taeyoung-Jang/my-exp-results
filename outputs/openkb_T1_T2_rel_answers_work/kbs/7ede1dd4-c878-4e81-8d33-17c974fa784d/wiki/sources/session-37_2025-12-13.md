**User:** I’m trying to get better about managing my money week to week—do you have a simple budgeting approach you’d recommend that doesn’t feel overly restrictive?

**Assistant:** You might like a “pay‑yourself‑first” style approach because it’s simple and doesn’t feel like you’re policing every dollar. The idea is to decide on just a few priorities—like savings, bills, and spending money—then move the important pieces out of your checking account as soon as your paycheck arrives. Whatever’s left is your guilt‑free spending for the week.

For most people, it works well to set aside a fixed amount for savings or debt, cover the non‑negotiable bills, and then give yourself a weekly spending allowance that fits what’s left. Since you only have to watch that one number, it feels flexible but still keeps you on track.
