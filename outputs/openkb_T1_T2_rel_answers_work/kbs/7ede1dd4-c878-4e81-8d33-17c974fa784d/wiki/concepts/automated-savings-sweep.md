# Automated Savings Sweep

**Definition**: An *Automated Savings Sweep* is a routine that transfers surplus cash from a checking account to a savings or investment vehicle, either on a fixed schedule or when certain balance conditions are met, to keep idle cash to a minimum while preserving a predefined buffer.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Purpose**
   - Reduce idle cash that could earn higher returns elsewhere.
   - Maintain a safety **buffer** for day‑to‑day expenses and unexpected costs.
2. **Trigger Types**
   - **Scheduled Transfer** – a fixed‑date movement (e.g., every Wednesday after payday).  
   - **Conditional Transfer** – executes only if the checking balance stays above a pre‑set floor (the *checking‑buffer*).
3. **Key Parameters**
   - **Target Buffer** – the minimum balance to retain in checking; often derived from a [[concepts/cash-flow-analysis]] of recent spend patterns.
   - **Transfer Amount** – the excess over the buffer, typically calculated as *post‑payday balance – buffer*.
4. **Implementation Steps** (as illustrated in [[summaries/session-08_2025-06-15]])
   - Review the last 2‑3 months of balances to find the lowest point between pay periods.
   - Add a comfort margin (e.g., $200‑$500) → set the *checking‑buffer*.
   - Choose a transfer day that follows major bill dates (rent, credit‑card) and allows the paycheck to settle.
   - Determine a fixed transfer amount based on typical post‑payday balances minus the buffer.
   - Monitor for 30‑60 days, then adjust the amount or buffer as needed.
5. **Banking Constraints**
   - Some banks only support **scheduled transfers**; in that case, pick a conservative amount that safely stays above the buffer.
   - If conditional sweeps are available, they can automatically adjust the amount each cycle, preventing overdrafts.

## Practical Example (from the source session)

- **User scenario**: $3,000 checking floor, bi‑weekly payday, large bills early in the month.
- **Solution**: Schedule a transfer for the Wednesday/Thursday after each payday.  
  Calculate the transfer amount as: *average post‑payday balance – $3,000*.  
  Adjust after a month of observation.

---

## Benefits

- Keeps idle cash low, improving overall portfolio efficiency.
- Provides a disciplined, low‑effort way to build savings.
- Reduces the risk of accidental overdrafts because the buffer is respected.

---

## Limitations

- Requires regular monitoring when only scheduled transfers are possible.
- May not capture unexpected cash inflows/outflows between transfer dates.
- Dependent on the bank’s transfer capabilities; some institutions lack conditional logic.
