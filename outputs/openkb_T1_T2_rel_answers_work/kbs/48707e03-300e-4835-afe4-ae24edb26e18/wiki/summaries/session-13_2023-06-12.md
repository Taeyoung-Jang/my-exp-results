- **Picture optimization** – Set the TV to a calibrated picture mode (e.g., *Movie* or *Cinema*), disable motion smoothing, and adjust brightness/contrast for accurate colors and detail. This gives the biggest visual improvement with minimal effort. [[concepts/picture_mode]]

- **Audio foundation** – Prioritize clear dialogue by moving from a [[entities/soundbar]] to an [[entities/av_receiver]] with a dedicated [[entities/center_speaker]]. A real center channel provides separate control over speech and anchors voices to the screen. If you stay with a soundbar, choose one that has a true center driver rather than a virtual dialogue mode. [[concepts/audio_clarity]]

- **Dialogue‑enhancing settings** – Use mild [[concepts/dialogue_enhancement]] or light dynamic range compression to keep speech consistent without flattening explosions and music. Avoid aggressive “night mode,” which can make the mix sound mushy.

- **Center speaker characteristics** – For intelligibility, avoid horizontal MTM designs (e.g., [[entities/mtm_center]]) because they can lob off‑axis. Prefer a 3‑way horizontal center with a dedicated midrange, or better yet a [[entities/coaxial_center]] (concentric) point‑source design for off‑axis consistency. Cabinet size matters less than a smooth, even response across the vocal range.

- **Placement & angling** – When the center sits below the TV, angle it upward so the tweeter points toward ear height, keep it as close to the screen as possible, and mount it on a solid surface to reduce vibrations. This helps voices feel anchored to the on‑screen actors.

- **AVR features to consider** – Look for individual speaker room‑correction (EQ) that doesn’t over‑flatten highs, a dialogue‑lift option for raising perceived voice height, and independent center‑channel level control. Features like “center spread” are optional and mainly affect front‑stage width.

- **Off‑center listening** – If regular seating is off‑center, a [[entities/coaxial_center]] provides the most consistent dialogue clarity across the listening area. A well‑designed horizontal center can still perform adequately but may show dips in clarity at wider angles.

Overall, the recommended upgrade path is: accurate picture mode → dedicated [[entities/av_receiver]] + [[entities/center_speaker]] → fine‑tune [[concepts/dialogue_enhancement]] settings → optimize speaker placement and AVR room‑correction, with special attention to speaker design for off‑axis listening.
