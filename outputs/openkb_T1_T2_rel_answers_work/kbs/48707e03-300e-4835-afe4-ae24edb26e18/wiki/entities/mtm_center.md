# MTM Center Speaker

A **MTM (Mid‑Tweeter‑Mid)** center speaker is a horizontal three‑driver layout that places two mid‑range drivers on the sides of a central tweeter. It is marketed as a dedicated center channel for home‑theater systems.

## Key Characteristics
- **Horizontal arrangement** of drivers, which can create lobing issues when the listening position moves off‑axis.
- Often chosen for its symmetry with other front‑left/right speakers.
- Typically larger than coaxial designs, but size does not guarantee better dialogue intelligibility.

## Findings from [[summaries/session-13_2023-06-12]]
- The document advises **avoiding horizontal MTM designs** for dialogue clarity because they may suffer from off‑axis “lobing” that degrades speech intelligibility.
- For off‑center seating, a **coaxial/"concentric" center speaker** is recommended over an MTM design.
- When an MTM center is used, the recommendation is to ensure precise placement and angling, though the inherent design limitations remain.

## Practical Recommendations
1. **Prefer coaxial or dedicated point‑source centers** when dialogue intelligibility across a wide seating area is a priority.
2. If an MTM center must be used, **place it as close to the screen as possible** and angle the tweeter toward ear height to minimize lobing effects.
3. Use **light [[concepts/dialogue_enhancement]] or dynamic range compression** to assist speech clarity, acknowledging that processing cannot fully compensate for driver layout issues.
