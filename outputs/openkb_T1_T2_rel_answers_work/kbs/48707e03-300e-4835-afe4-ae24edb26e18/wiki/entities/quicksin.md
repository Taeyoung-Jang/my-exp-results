# QuickSIN

**Type:** other

QuickSIN (Quick Speech-in-Noise) is a widely used, standardized test that measures the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) required for a listener to correctly repeat 50 % of sentences. It provides a single numeric score that can be compared to age‑matched normative data and is often referenced in audiology reports to assess real‑world listening ability.
