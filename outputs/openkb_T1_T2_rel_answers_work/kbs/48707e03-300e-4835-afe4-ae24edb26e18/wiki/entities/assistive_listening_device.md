## Assistive Listening Device

**Type:** product

An Assistive Listening Device (ALD) is a technology used to enhance the **audio clarity** of speech, especially in noisy environments such as meetings, conferences, and public events.  ALDs capture sound close to the source—often via a microphone or a direct audio feed—and transmit it wirelessly or through a wired connection to a listener's headphones or hearing‑aid‑compatible receiver.  This can significantly improve a user’s performance on [[concepts/speech_in_noise_assessment]] tests and support fitness‑for‑duty decisions such as clearance for a [[entities/conference_interpreting_roster]].
