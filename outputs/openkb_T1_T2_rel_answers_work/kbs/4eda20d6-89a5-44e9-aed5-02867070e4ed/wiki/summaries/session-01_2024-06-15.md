## Overview
A concise, printable plan for a 3‑serving weekly dinner‑plus‑lunch routine. It covers **meal‑planning templates**, a **Mon‑Thu dinner schedule**, a **tight grocery list with exact amounts**, a **60‑90 min Sunday prep workflow**, and detailed **portion, container, and fridge‑life guidance**.

## Meal‑Planning Templates
- **Stir‑fry template** – protein ([[entities/chicken-thighs]] or [[entities/tofu]]), [[entities/bell-peppers]], [[entities/broccoli]], soy‑ginger sauce.  
- **One‑pan roasted protein & potatoes** – chicken thighs, [[entities/potatoes]], carrots or green beans.  
- **Tomato‑basil pasta** – pasta, [[entities/marinara-sauce]], spinach.  
- **Bean‑and‑grain bowl** – [[entities/rice-quinoa]], [[entities/black-beans]], roasted veg, yogurt‑lime dressing.

## Example Schedule (3 servings per dinner)

| Day | Dinner | Lunch (next day) |
|-----|--------|------------------|
| **Mon** | Chicken or tofu stir‑fry | Leftover stir‑fry |
| **Tue** | One‑pan roasted chicken thighs + potatoes | Leftover roasted chicken (reheat or salad) |
| **Wed** | Tomato‑basil pasta with spinach | Leftover pasta |
| **Thu** | Bean‑and‑grain bowl (rice/quinoa, beans, roasted veg) | Bowl built from remaining grains/beans/veg |
| **Fri** | – | Any leftover bowls |

## Grocery List (1–2 people, 3‑serving portions)

- 1 lb [[entities/chicken-thighs]]
- ½ lb chicken breast **or** 14‑16 oz [[entities/tofu]]
- 3–4 [[entities/bell-peppers]]
- 1 head [[entities/broccoli]]
- 1 lb [[entities/potatoes]]
- ½ lb carrots **or** ½ lb green beans
- 5–6 oz fresh spinach
- 1 small bunch basil
- 1 jar (≈24 oz) [[entities/marinara-sauce]] **or** 2 medium tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) [[entities/black-beans]] or chickpeas
- 8 oz dry pasta
- 1½ cups uncooked [[entities/rice-quinoa]] (≈3 servings)
- 2–3 limes or lemons
- 1 head garlic
- Soy sauce (a few tbsp) [[entities/soy-sauce]]
- Olive oil (a few tbsp) [[entities/olive-oil]]

## Sunday Prep Plan (60–90 min) – [[concepts/food-prep-workflow]]

1. **Cook grain** – Rinse & cook 1½ cups rice/quinoa, cool, store in a 32‑oz container.  
2. **Prep veg** – Slice 3 bell peppers and cut broccoli; store together in a 32‑oz container.  
3. **Carrots/green beans** – Chop 8 oz, store in a 16‑oz container.  
4. **Spinach & basil** – Store spinach in a 16‑oz container with a dry paper towel; basil in an 8‑oz container wrapped slightly damp.  
5. **Proteins** – Dice 10–12 oz chicken breast or tofu, store in a 16‑oz container; season 1½ lb chicken thighs, store in another 16‑oz container.  
6. **Sauces** – Mix soy‑ginger sauce (8‑oz jar) and yogurt‑lime dressing (8‑oz jar), label.  
7. **Roast veg** – Toss 2 cups mixed veg, roast 425°F 20 min, cool, store in a 16‑oz container.  
8. **Garlic** – Mince 2 cloves, store in a 4‑oz container.  

## Portion & Container Guidance – [[concepts/container-sizing]]

- **Grain**: 32‑oz container – keeps 4‑5 days.  
- **Stir‑fry veg**: 32‑oz container – 3‑4 days.  
- **Carrots/green beans**: 16‑oz container – 4‑5 days.  
- **Spinach**: 16‑oz container – 3‑4 days.  
- **Basil**: 8‑oz container – ~3 days.  
- **Chicken breast/tofu**: 16‑oz container – 2 days raw.  
- **Chicken thighs**: 16‑oz container – 2 days raw.  
- **Sauces**: 8‑oz jars – 4‑5 days.  
- **Roasted veg**: 16‑oz container – 4‑5 days.  
- **Mince garlic**: 4‑oz container – 3 days.
