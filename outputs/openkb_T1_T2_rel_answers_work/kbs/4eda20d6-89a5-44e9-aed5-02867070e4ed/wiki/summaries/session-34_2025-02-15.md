# Summary of session‑34 (2025‑02‑15)

This conversation provides a practical framework for **healthy eating while traveling**, especially when limited to late‑night hotel bars or lobby shop grab‑and‑go options. The assistant distills the advice into two reusable lists and highlights a set of **meal‑tweaking strategies** that can be applied across venues.

## Core Concepts [[concepts/healthy-travel-eating]]
- **Anchor approach** – Look for a protein source and a vegetable on any menu.  
- **Meal‑tweaks** – Extra veggies, swap fries for salad, sauces/dressings on the side, grilled instead of fried.  
- **Flexible balancing** – Adjust later meals based on earlier intake; focus on overall daily balance rather than perfection.  

## Hotel Bar Default Orders (hotel bar defaults)
1. **Grilled chicken sandwich** – bun lightly toasted, sauce on the side, swap fries for a side salad, add extra lettuce/tomato.  
2. **Basic burger** – no cheese, extra veggies, fries → side salad or steamed veg, sauce on the side.  
3. **Chicken Caesar salad** – dressing on the side, extra greens, grilled chicken if possible.  
4. **Grain / power bowl** – half sauce, load up on available veggies.  
5. **Turkey or chicken wrap** – added veggies, dressing on the side, optionally warmed.  
6. **Chili or soup bowl** – paired with steamed/roasted vegetables.  
7. **Flatbread or small pizza** – light cheese, extra vegetables.  
8. **Additional tip** – request protein‑rich additions (e.g., extra chicken, boiled egg, [[entities/grilled-chicken-strips]]) when available.  

These defaults are chosen for their prevalence in hotel bars and their ability to be **customised for higher protein and fiber** while keeping calories moderate.

## Lobby Shop Grab‑and‑Go Meal Combos [[concepts/grab-and-go-meal-combinations]]
1. **[[entities/yogurt]] + [[entities/mixed-nuts]] + fruit** – works without a fridge; yogurt is pre‑chilled; fruit can be an [[entities/apple]] or [[entities/banana]].  
2. **Turkey/chicken sandwich + [[entities/baby-carrots]] (optional cheese stick)**.  
3. **Microwaveable soup or noodle bowl + [[entities/protein-bar]]**.  
4. **[[entities/hummus]]‑and‑[[entities/crackers]] pack + veg sticks + single‑serve [[entities/tuna-packet]]**.  
5. **Bagged salad kit + [[entities/cottage-cheese]] or [[entities/grilled-chicken-strips]] (or hard‑boiled egg pack)**.  
6. **[[entities/rotisserie-chicken-snack-pack]] + whole‑grain [[entities/crackers]] + an [[entities/apple]]** – easy, filling, no fridge needed.  
7. **Microwaveable rice cup + [[entities/tuna-packet]] + any veggie side**.  
8. **[[entities/instant-oatmeal]] made with hot water + [[entities/peanut-butter]] + [[entities/banana]]** – great for a warm, satisfying late‑night meal.  

All combos aim for a **balanced macro profile** (protein, carbs, healthy fats) and are designed for settings without a refrigerator or full kitchen.

## Meal‑Tweak Strategies [[concepts/meal-tweaks]]
- **Extra vegetables** – boosts volume and fiber with minimal calories.  
- **Sauce/dressing on the side** – gives control over fat/sugar intake.  
- **Swap fried sides for salads or steamed veg**.  
- **Ask for grilled or baked preparations** instead of fried.  
- **Add protein boosters** (extra meat, cheese sticks, boiled eggs, [[entities/tuna-packet]], [[entities/grilled-chicken-strips]]) when the base dish is light.  

## Practical Takeaways
- Keep a mental checklist: *protein, veg, controlled sauce*.  
- Maintain flexibility: adjust later meals based on earlier choices.  
- Use the provided default lists as a quick reference when time and options are limited.  
- Apply the tweak principles to any menu item to improve nutritional quality.  

These guidelines can be referenced in future travel‑nutrition planning and can inform the creation of additional concept pages.
