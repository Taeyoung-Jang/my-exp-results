# Rice / Quinoa

**Type:** product

Rice and quinoa are interchangeable whole‑grain staples that serve as the primary carbohydrate in the weekly meal‑planning templates. The plan calls for **1½ cups uncooked rice or quinoa**, which yields about three servings for the Thursday bean‑and‑grain bowls.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Quantity:** 1½ cups uncooked (≈ 3 servings).
- **Storage:** Cool and transfer to a **32‑oz container**. 
- **Fridge life:** 4‑5 days, making it safe for the whole week.
- **Uses:**
  - Base for the **bean‑and‑grain bowl** (with black beans, roasted vegetables, and yogurt‑lime dressing).
  - Side for the **stir‑fry** and **one‑pan roasted chicken** meals.
  - Can be swapped with quinoa for added protein.

## Related Concepts & Workflow
- This grain fits into the broader **[[concepts/meal-planning-templates]]** for quick, balanced meals.
- It is prepared during the **[[concepts/food-prep-workflow]]** on Sunday, alongside other prepped items.
- Proper container selection follows the **[[concepts/container-sizing]]** guidelines (32‑oz container recommended).

- Used together with sauces that include **[[entities/soy-sauce]]**.
