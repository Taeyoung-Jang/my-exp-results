# Mixed Nuts

Mixed Nuts are a pre‑packaged blend of various nuts (e.g., almonds, cashews, peanuts) that provide a convenient source of protein, healthy fats, and satiety, especially useful for travelers.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- Recommended as a **grab‑and‑go** addition to yogurt for a balanced, portable meal when staying in hotel lobby shops.
- Serves as a quick way to boost protein and healthy fat intake without needing refrigeration.
- Often paired with other travel‑friendly items such as [[entities/yogurt]], [[entities/banana]], and [[entities/baby-carrots]] to create a more complete meal.

## Practical tips
- Keep a small resealable bag in your luggage for easy portion control.
- Combine with a dairy or plant‑based protein (e.g., yogurt, cottage cheese) for a balanced snack.
- Choose unsalted or lightly salted varieties to control sodium intake.
