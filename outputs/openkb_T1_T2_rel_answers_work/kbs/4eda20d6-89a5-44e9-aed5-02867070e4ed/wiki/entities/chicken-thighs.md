# Chicken Thighs

**Type:** product

Chicken thighs are the primary protein for the one‑pan roasted dinner in the weekly meal‑planning schedule (Mon‑Thu) outlined in [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]].

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Quantity:** 1 lb (approximately 1½ pounds when packaged) for the week.
- **Portioning:** Provides three servings – dinner for two people plus one lunch portion.
- **Preparation:** Seasoned lightly with salt and stored raw in a 16‑oz container.
- **Storage Life:** Keeps safely in the refrigerator for up to **2 days** before cooking.
- **Cooking Use:** Roasted together with potatoes and carrots/green beans on Tuesday night.

## Related Concepts & Entities
- Part of the **[[concepts/meal-planning-templates]]** (one‑pan roasted protein & potatoes).
- Stored using guidance from **[[concepts/container-sizing]]** (16‑oz container recommendation).
- Prepared during the **[[concepts/food-prep-workflow]]** Sunday prep block.
- Paired with other ingredients such as [[entities/potatoes]], [[entities/broccoli]] (optional side), [[entities/olive-oil]], and [[entities/soy-sauce]] for seasoning.

## Context in the Weekly Plan
The chicken thighs are cooked on Tuesday, providing leftovers for Wednesday lunch. The roasted vegetables and protein are also used in the Thursday bean‑and‑grain bowl, maximizing ingredient utilization across meals.
