# Marinara Sauce

**Type:** product

Marinara Sauce is a ready‑to‑use tomato‑based pasta sauce referenced in the weekly meal‑planning guide. It serves as the primary sauce for the **Tomato‑basil pasta** dinner in the schedule.

## Key facts from the session‑01_2024‑06‑15 document
- Recommended quantity: one 24 oz jar (approximately 1 jar) for the week.
- Used in the **Wednesday** dinner: tomato‑basil pasta with spinach.
- Paired with other pantry staples such as [[entities/olive-oil]], garlic (if present) and fresh basil.
- Stored at room temperature until opened; once opened, keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days.
