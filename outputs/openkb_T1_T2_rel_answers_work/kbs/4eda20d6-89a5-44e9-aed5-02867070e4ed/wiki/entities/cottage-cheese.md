# Cottage Cheese

Cottage cheese is a fresh, soft cheese curd with a mild flavor and a high protein to fat ratio. It is commonly sold in small, single‑serve containers that make it convenient for **grab‑and‑go** scenarios.

## Relevance in *session‑34_2025‑02‑15*
- Suggested as a protein boost for a **bagged salad kit** in the “lobby shop only” meal combos.
- Highlighted as a fridge‑light option that pairs well with other portable items such as baby‑carrots, mixed‑nuts and fruit.
- Mentioned in the context of **healthy travel eating**, offering a quick, satiating addition without needing extensive preparation.

## Typical Uses for Travelers
- **Meal‑tweaks**: Add cottage cheese to salads or grain bowls to increase protein while keeping the meal light (see meal‑tweaks).
- **Grab‑and‑go meal combinations**: Works well with pre‑packaged veggies, crackers, or fruit as part of balanced travel meals (see grab‑and‑go‑meal‑combinations).
- **Portable snack**: Small containers can be stored in a hotel mini‑fridge or consumed within a few hours at room temperature.
