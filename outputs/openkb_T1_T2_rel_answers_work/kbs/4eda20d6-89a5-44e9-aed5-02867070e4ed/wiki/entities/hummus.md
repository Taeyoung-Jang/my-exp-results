# Hummus

Hummus is a ready‑to‑eat, protein‑rich spread made from mashed chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, and olive oil. In the *session‑34* conversation it is listed as a convenient component for building balanced grab‑and‑go meals from a hotel lobby shop.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- Suggested as part of a **hummus‑and‑cracker pack** paired with fresh veggies and fruit for a quick, balanced meal.
- Provides plant‑based protein and healthy fats, making it a useful alternative to meat‑based snacks when refrigeration is limited.
- Can be combined with other listed items such as [[entities/crackers]], [[entities/baby-carrots]], [[entities/bell-peppers]], and a [[entities/banana]] for a complete, portable meal.

## Typical use cases
- Spread on crackers or flatbread for quick carbs.
- Dipped with raw vegetables like baby carrots, bell peppers, or broccoli.
- Mixed into a grain bowl for added creaminess and protein.
