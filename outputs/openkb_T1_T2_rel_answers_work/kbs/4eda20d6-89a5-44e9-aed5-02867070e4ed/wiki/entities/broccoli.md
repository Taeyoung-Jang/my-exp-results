# Broccoli

**Type:** product

## Overview
Broccoli is a cruciferous vegetable featured prominently in the *session‑01_2024‑06‑15* meal‑planning guide. It provides a nutritious, quick‑cook component for several dinner templates.

## Role in the Meal Plan
- **Stir‑fry template** – paired with bell peppers, chicken or tofu, and a soy‑ginger sauce.
- **One‑pan roasted protein** – roasted alongside chicken thighs, potatoes, and carrots/green beans.
- **Prep workflow** – chopped and stored on Sunday for up to four days of use.

## Quantity & Preparation
- **Amount:** 1 head of broccoli (approximately 1 lb) for the week, enough for three servings per dinner.
- **Prep steps:** Cut into bite‑size florets; store in a 32‑oz container as part of the [[concepts/food-prep-workflow]].
- **Container sizing:** Recommended 32‑oz container to keep the florets fresh for 3‑4 days, aligning with [[concepts/container-sizing]].

## Storage & Shelf Life
- Keep in the refrigerator, sealed container, for **3–4 days**.
- Works well with other pre‑pped vegetables such as [[entities/bell-peppers]] and [[entities/potatoes]].
