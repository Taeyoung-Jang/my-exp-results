## Tofu
A block of firm tofu is a plant‑based protein made from coagulated soy milk, pressed into solid cubes. It is praised for its neutral flavor and ability to absorb sauces, making it a staple in quick, healthy meals.

### Role in the session‑01_2024‑06‑15 plan
- Suggested as an alternative protein for the **Monday stir‑fry** template alongside chicken.
- Included in the grocery list with a quantity of **14–16 oz** for a 3‑serving dinner plus lunch.
- Pre‑cut into stir‑fry‑size pieces during the **Sunday food‑prep workflow**, stored in a 16‑oz container.
- Paired with the **soy‑ginger sauce** and **yogurt‑lime dressing** across multiple meals.
