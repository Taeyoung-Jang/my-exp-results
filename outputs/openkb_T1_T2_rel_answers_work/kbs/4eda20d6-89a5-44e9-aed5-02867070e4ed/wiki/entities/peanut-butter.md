# Peanut Butter

Peanut butter is a creamy or crunchy spread made from ground peanuts. It is prized by travelers for its **long shelf life**, **high protein and healthy fat content**, and **versatility** in quick, no‑cook meals.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- Recommended as a topping for the **instant oatmeal** combo (hot water from the lobby coffee station, peanut butter, and a banana) to add flavor, calories, and satiety.
- Cited as a **grab‑and‑go** component that pairs well with other shelf‑stable items such as [[entities/instant-oatmeal]], [[entities/banana]], and [[entities/mixed-nuts]].
- Highlighted as a **protein‑rich addition** that helps turn simple snacks into a more balanced meal, aligning with the principles of [[concepts/healthy-travel-eating]] and [[concepts/grab-and-go-meal-combinations]].
- Suggested as a **meal‑tweak** (see [[concepts/meal-tweaks]]) to boost the nutritional profile of meals without requiring refrigeration or cooking.

## Typical uses for travelers
- **Oatmeal boost** – Stir a spoonful into instant oatmeal for a creamy texture and extra protein.
- **Snack dip** – Pair with [[entities/crackers]] or sliced fruit like [[entities/apple]] or [[entities/banana]].
- **Energy bite** – Mix with [[entities/mixed-nuts]] for a quick, calorie‑dense snack during long flights or layovers.
- **Sandwich spread** – Add to a turkey or chicken sandwich from a hotel lobby shop for extra richness.

## Benefits
- **Shelf‑stable**: No refrigeration needed, making it ideal for hotel rooms and airport lounges.
- **Nutrient density**: Provides protein, healthy fats, and micronutrients such as vitamin E and magnesium.
- **Versatility**: Works in both sweet (with fruit or oatmeal) and savory (with crackers or as a dip) contexts.
