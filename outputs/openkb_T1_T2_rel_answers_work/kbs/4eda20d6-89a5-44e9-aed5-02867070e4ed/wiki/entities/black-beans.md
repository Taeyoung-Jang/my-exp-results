# Black Beans

Canned black beans are a shelf‑stable, protein‑rich legume featured in the **session‑01_2024‑06‑15** meal‑planning guide. They serve as the primary plant‑based protein for the Thursday bean‑and‑grain bowl and can be combined with other pantry staples such as [[entities/rice-quinoa]] and vegetables.

## Role in the Meal Plan
- **Template**: Part of the [[concepts/meal-planning-templates]] “bean‑and‑grain bowl” that provides a quick, balanced dinner.
- **Quantity**: One 15 oz can (≈1 cup cooked) is listed in the grocery list, enough for three servings (dinner for two + one lunch).
- **Preparation**: Rinse and drain the beans, then store them ready to mix with cooked grain and roasted vegetables.

## Prep & Storage
- **Container**: Store the drained beans in a 16‑oz container, aligning with the [[concepts/container-sizing]] recommendations for this prep workflow.
- **Fridge Life**: Keeps 4–5 days refrigerated after opening.
- **Workflow**: Integrated into the Sunday [[concepts/food-prep-workflow]] alongside other items like [[entities/potatoes]] and [[entities/broccoli]].

## Pairings & Uses
- Combined with [[entities/rice-quinoa]] for a complete grain‑protein combo.
- Tossed with a drizzle of [[entities/olive-oil]] or the yogurt‑lime dressing for flavor.
- Can be swapped with chickpeas for variety without changing the overall template.
