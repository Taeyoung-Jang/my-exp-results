# Potatoes

**Type:** Product

Potatoes are a starchy tuber included in the meal‑planning schedule from the [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]] document. They are listed as a core ingredient for the one‑pan roasted dinner on Tuesday.

## Role in the plan
- Used as the main carbohydrate for the roasted chicken thighs dinner.
- Quantity specified: **1 lb** of potatoes, sufficient for three servings (dinner for two + one lunch portion).

## Prep & Storage
- Cooked on the Sunday prep day along with other vegetables.
- Stored in a **16‑oz container** as part of the [[concepts/food-prep-workflow]].
- Recommended fridge life: **4–5 days** when kept in an airtight container.

## Related items
- Paired with [[entities/chicken-thighs]] and [[entities/broccoli]] in the roasted dinner.
- Complemented by [[entities/olive-oil]] for roasting.
- Serves alongside [[entities/rice-quinoa]] in the bean‑and‑grain bowls.
- Fits the [[concepts/meal-planning-templates]] for one‑pan meals.
- Container recommendations align with [[concepts/container-sizing]].
