# Apple (product)

Apple is a widely available fresh fruit that serves as a convenient, non‑perishable snack option for travelers. In the context of **healthy travel eating**, it is cited as a simple side that pairs well with protein‑rich items like a rotisserie‑chicken snack pack and crackers, creating a balanced, portable meal.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- **Grab‑and‑go combo:** Apple is recommended alongside a [[entities/rotisserie-chicken-snack-pack]] and [[entities/crackers]] to form a quick, satisfying meal without needing refrigeration.
- **Portability:** Its firm texture and natural packaging make it ideal for late‑night hotel lobby purchases.
- **Nutritional role:** Provides natural sugars, fiber, and vitamins that complement protein and carbohydrate sources, supporting the [[concepts/healthy-travel-eating]] approach.
