# Crackers

Crackers are a lightweight, shelf‑stable snack that can serve as a vehicle for spreads, proteins, or vegetables in on‑the‑go meal combos. In the **session‑34 (2025‑02‑15)** conversation, crackers appear as a component of several **grab‑and‑go** suggestions for hotel lobby shops.

## Key points from the document
- Used alongside **hummus** to create a quick, balanced snack.
- Paired with **baby carrots** and fruit for a more complete meal without needing a fridge.
- Combined with a **rotisserie‑chicken‑snack‑pack** and an **apple** to form a filling, protein‑rich option.
- Listed under the broader theme of **healthy‑travel‑eating**, emphasizing portable, low‑prep foods suitable for late‑night travel situations.

## Typical uses in travel meals
1. **Spread base** – Pair with hummus, peanut‑butter, or cheese for added protein and flavor.
2. **Crunch texture** – Adds a satisfying bite when combined with soft items like yogurt or cottage‑cheese.
3. **Meal enhancer** – When mixed with **mixed‑nuts**, **tuna‑packet**, or **grilled‑chicken‑strips**, crackers help round out macronutrient balance.
