# Grilled Chicken Strips

**Type:** Product

Grilled chicken strips are ready‑to‑eat, pre‑cooked pieces of chicken that have been seasoned and grilled, providing a convenient source of lean protein for travelers and anyone needing a quick, portable protein boost.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- Frequently available in hotel lobby shops as a snack‑size, **no‑refrigeration‑required** option.
- Recommended as a protein addition for meals such as **bagged salads**, **wraps**, or **grain bowls** to increase satiety without adding much weight.
- Pairs well with other grab‑and‑go items like [[entities/mixed-nuts]], [[entities/baby-carrots]], or a **protein bar** for a balanced travel meal.
- Fits within the **healthy travel eating** framework, emphasizing a protein anchor and extra vegetables.

## Usage Tips
- Add the strips to a **bagged salad kit** (see [[concepts/grab-and-go-meal-combinations]]) for a quick, balanced lunch.
- Include in a **wrap** with extra veggies and dressing on the side, supporting the **meal‑tweaks** approach of extra greens and controlled sauces.
- Combine with a **microwaveable rice cup** and a [[entities/tuna-packet]] for a hearty, portable dinner.
