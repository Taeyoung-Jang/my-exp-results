# Soy Sauce

Soy sauce is a fermented soy‑based condiment that provides salty, umami flavor. In the **session‑01_2024‑06‑15** meal plan it appears in the grocery list and is a core ingredient of the **soy‑ginger sauce** used across multiple dinners.

## Key facts from the document
- Listed under the pantry staples needed for the weekly plan.
- Combined with lime juice, grated ginger, and a splash of olive oil to create a quick **soy‑ginger sauce** that flavors the stir‑fry and can be drizzled on grain bowls. [[concepts/meal-planning-templates]]
- Used alongside other ingredients such as [[entities/chicken-thighs]], [[entities/tofu]], [[entities/bell-peppers]], [[entities/broccoli]], and [[entities/potatoes]].
- Stored in an 8‑oz jar after preparation; keeps 4–5 days in the refrigerator. [[concepts/food-prep-workflow]]
- The amount needed is “a few tablespoons” for the weekly sauces.
