# Protein Bar

A **protein bar** is a portable, shelf‑stable snack designed to provide a concentrated source of protein, carbohydrates, and sometimes fats.  In the context of [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]], the protein bar is highlighted as a key component of **grab‑and‑go meal combinations** for travelers who lack kitchen facilities.

## Role in the Session‑34 guidance
- Paired with a microwaveable soup or noodle bowl to create a quick, balanced meal when only a lobby shop is available.
- Serves as a reliable protein boost without requiring refrigeration, making it ideal for late‑night hotel stays.
- Complements other items such as [[entities/yogurt]], [[entities/mixed-nuts]], and fruit to round out macro‑nutrient intake.

## Why it fits [[concepts/healthy-travel-eating]]
- **Convenient**: Easy to carry and consume on the go.
- **Predictable nutrition**: Provides a known amount of protein, helping travelers meet daily protein goals.
- **Flexibility**: Can be combined with hot or cold foods to adjust satiety and energy levels.

## Suggested tweaks (see [[concepts/meal-tweaks]])
- Choose bars with minimal added sugars and natural ingredients.
- Pair with fresh fruit (e.g., [[entities/banana]]) or a small side of nuts for added fiber and healthy fats.
- If the bar is dense, add a side of vegetables like [[entities/baby-carrots]] to increase volume without many extra calories.

## Practical tip
Keep a few different flavors on hand to avoid taste fatigue and rotate between bars that emphasize protein versus those that add extra fiber or micronutrients.
