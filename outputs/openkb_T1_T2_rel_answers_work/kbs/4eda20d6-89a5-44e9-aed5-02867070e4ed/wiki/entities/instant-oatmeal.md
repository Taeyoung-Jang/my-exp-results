# Instant Oatmeal

Instant oatmeal is a pre‑measured, dry oatmeal cup that only requires hot water to become a warm, ready‑to‑eat meal. It is a staple of **grab‑and‑go** nutrition because it is lightweight, shelf‑stable, and needs no refrigeration.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-15]]
- Recommended as one of the eight **lobby‑shop meal combos** for travelers without a fridge.
- Paired with hot water from the hotel coffee station, **peanut butter** ([[entities/peanut-butter]]) and a **banana** ([[entities/banana]]) to create a balanced, warming option.
- Highlights the strategy of adding protein/fat (peanut butter) and fruit to boost satiety while keeping preparation minimal.

## Related concepts
- Fits within the framework of [[concepts/grab-and-go-meal-combinations]], emphasizing quick assembly and portability.
- Supports the broader approach of [[concepts/healthy-travel-eating]] by providing a warm, nutrient‑dense base that can be customized.
- Can be enhanced with the [[concepts/meal-tweaks]] principle of adding extra nutrients (e.g., fruit, nut butter) without extra cooking.

## Typical usage tips
1. **Preparation:** Empty the cup into a bowl, add hot water, stir, and let sit for 2‑3 minutes.
2. **Customization:** Mix in a spoonful of **peanut butter** for protein and healthy fats; add sliced **banana** for natural sweetness and potassium.
3. **Portability:** The package is self‑contained, making it ideal for hotel rooms, airport lounges, or any travel setting where kitchen facilities are limited.

Instant oatmeal exemplifies a simple, nutritious solution for travelers seeking a quick, warm meal without relying on full‑service dining.
