Bell peppers are a versatile vegetable featured prominently in the **session-01_2024-06-15** meal‑planning guide. They are sliced for the Monday stir‑fry template and stored for quick use throughout the week.

- **Storage:** Keep in a 32‑oz container as part of the Sunday food‑prep workflow.
