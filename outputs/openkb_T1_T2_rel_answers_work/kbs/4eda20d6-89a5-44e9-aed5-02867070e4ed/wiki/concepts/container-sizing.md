# Container Sizing

**Definition**: Container sizing is the practice of selecting storage containers with appropriate volume (e.g., 16 oz, 32 oz) for pre‑prepared ingredients and meals so that they stay fresh, are easy to portion, and fit typical refrigerator space.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents over‑filling that can crush delicate greens like spinach.
- Reduces excess air space that accelerates spoilage of cut vegetables such as bell peppers, broccoli, and carrots.
- Makes it simple to grab a single‑serving portion for lunches without reheating the entire batch.

## Guidance from [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]]
The weekly prep plan recommends the following container sizes for the listed items:

| Item | Recommended container | Approx. volume | Expected fridge life |
|------|-----------------------|----------------|----------------------|
| Cooked grain (rice/quinoa) | 32‑oz container | 32 oz | 4‑5 days |
| Stir‑fry vegetables (bell peppers, broccoli) | 32‑oz container | 32 oz | 3‑4 days |
| Carrots or green beans | 16‑oz container | 16 oz | 4‑5 days |
| Spinach | 16‑oz container | 16 oz | 3‑4 days |
| Fresh basil | 8‑oz container | 8 oz | ~3 days |
| Diced chicken breast or tofu | 16‑oz container | 16 oz | 2 days (raw) |
| Seasoned chicken thighs | 16‑oz container | 16 oz | 2 days (raw) |
| Sauces (soy‑ginger, yogurt‑lime) | 8‑oz jars | 8 oz each | 4‑5 days |
| Roasted mixed vegetables | 16‑oz container | 16 oz | 4‑5 days |
| Minced garlic | 4‑oz container | 4 oz | 3 days |

These sizes balance the typical three‑serving portions needed for each dinner (two dinner servings + one lunch serving) while keeping containers compact enough for most home refrigerators.

## Practical Tips
1. **Use shallow containers** for grains and roasted vegetables to cool food quickly and limit bacterial growth.
2. **Label each container** with the contents and date prepared.
3. **Group similar‑volume items** (e.g., 16‑oz containers) on the same shelf for easy inventory.
4. **Choose BPA‑free, airtight containers** to maintain freshness of items like [[entities/olive-oil]]‑based sauces.
