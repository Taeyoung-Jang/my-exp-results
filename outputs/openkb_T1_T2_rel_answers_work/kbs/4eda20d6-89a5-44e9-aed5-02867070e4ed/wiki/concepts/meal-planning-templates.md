# Meal Planning Templates

**Definition**: A set of repeatable culinary frameworks that let you swap proteins, vegetables, and sauces while keeping preparation time short and meals varied.

## Core Templates from [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]]

| Template | Typical Components | Typical Uses |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Stir‑fry** | Protein ([[entities/chicken-thighs]] or [[entities/tofu]]), sliced [[entities/bell-peppers]], [[entities/broccoli]] florets, quick soy‑ginger sauce ([[entities/soy-sauce]] + lime) | Weeknight dinner, leftover lunch |
| **One‑pan roasted protein & starch** | Whole [[entities/chicken-thighs]], halved [[entities/potatoes]], carrots or green beans, drizzled with [[entities/olive-oil]] | Hands‑off dinner, easy reheating |
| **Tomato‑basil pasta** | Dry pasta, [[entities/marinara-sauce]] (or fresh tomatoes), fresh basil, handful of spinach | Fast dinner, extra serving for lunch |
| **Bean‑and‑grain bowl** | Cooked [[entities/rice-quinoa]], canned [[entities/black-beans]], roasted mixed veg, yogurt‑lime or olive‑oil dressing | Flexible vegetarian option, lunch bowl |

## Why Templates Help
- **Structure over recipe**: You keep the cooking steps identical (e.g., stir‑fry = heat, add protein, add veg, finish with sauce) while changing the main ingredients.
- **Time efficiency**: Prep once (chopping, cooking grains) and reuse across several meals, fitting the <30‑minute weeknight goal.
- **Reduced waste**: Shared ingredients across templates (bell peppers, broccoli, olive oil) mean fewer single‑use purchases.
