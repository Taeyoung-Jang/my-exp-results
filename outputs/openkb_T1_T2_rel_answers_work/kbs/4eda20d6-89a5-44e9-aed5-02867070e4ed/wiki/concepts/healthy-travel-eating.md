# Healthy Travel Eating

**Definition**: A set of practical strategies for finding and assembling nutritious, satisfying meals when away from home, especially in hotels, airports, and other transient environments.

## Core Principles [[concepts/healthy-travel-eating]]
1. **Anchor Approach** – Identify a source of protein and a vegetable on any menu or in any grab‑and‑go selection.
2. **Meal Tweaks** – Use simple modifications to boost nutrition without adding excess calories:
   - Request extra vegetables (e.g., [[entities/baby-carrots]], [[entities/bell-peppers]], [[entities/broccoli]]).
   - Swap fried sides for salads or steamed veggies.
   - Keep sauces and dressings on the side (e.g., [[entities/olive-oil]], [[entities/soy-sauce]]).
   - Choose grilled or baked preparations over fried.
3. **Flexible Balancing** – Adjust later meals based on earlier intake; aim for overall daily balance rather than perfection.

## Hotel Bar Default Orders
| Order | Typical Tweaks |
|------|----------------|
| Grilled chicken sandwich | Bun lightly toasted, sauce on the side, swap fries for a side salad, add extra lettuce/tomato. |
| Basic burger | No cheese, extra veggies, fries → side salad or steamed veg, sauce on the side. |
| Chicken Caesar salad | Dressing on the side, extra greens, grilled chicken if possible. |
| Grain / power bowl | Half the usual sauce, load up on available veggies. |
| Turkey or chicken wrap | Added veggies, dressing on the side, optionally warmed. |
| Chili or soup bowl | Paired with steamed/roasted vegetables. |
| Flatbread or small pizza | Light cheese, extra vegetables. |
| Rotisserie‑style chicken snack pack | Combine with whole‑grain [[entities/crackers]] and a fruit (e.g., [[entities/apple]]). |

These defaults are chosen for their common availability in hotel bars and their capacity to be customized using the **Meal Tweaks** principles.

## Lobby Shop Grab‑and‑Go Meal Combinations [[concepts/grab-and-go-meal-combinations]]
1. **Yogurt + mixed nuts + fruit** – [[entities/yogurt]], [[entities/mixed-nuts]], [[entities/banana]] (or [[entities/apple]]). Works without a fridge.
2. **Turkey/chicken sandwich + baby carrots** – Add a cheese stick if needed.
3. **Microwaveable soup/noodle bowl + protein bar** – [[entities/protein-bar]].
4. **Hummus‑cracker pack + veggies + tuna packet** – [[entities/hummus]], [[entities/crackers]], [[entities/tuna-packet]].
5. **Bagged salad kit + cottage cheese or grilled chicken strips** – [[entities/cottage-cheese]], [[entities/grilled-chicken-strips]].
6. **Rotisserie‑style chicken snack pack + whole‑grain crackers + apple** – [[entities/rotisserie-chicken-snack-pack]], [[entities/crackers]], [[entities/apple]].
7. **Microwaveable rice cup + tuna packet + veggie side** – [[entities/rice-quinoa]], [[entities/tuna-packet]].
8. **Instant oatmeal cup + peanut butter + banana** – [[entities/instant-oatmeal]], [[entities/peanut-butter]], [[entities/banana]].

All combos aim for a balanced macro profile (protein, carbs, healthy fats) and are suitable for settings without a refrigerator or full kitchen.
