## Food Prep Workflow

The **Food Prep Workflow** describes a repeatable Sunday routine that sets up all components needed for a 3‑serving dinner‑plus‑lunch plan. It breaks down the week‑long meal plan from [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]] into a series of short, high‑impact tasks, each paired with recommended container sizes and storage times.

### Core Steps
1. **Cook the grain** – Rinse and cook 1½ cups of [[entities/rice-quinoa]] (rice or quinoa). Cool in a shallow dish and transfer to a **32‑oz container** (see [[concepts/container-sizing]]). Keeps 4–5 days.
2. **Prep stir‑fry vegetables** – Slice 3 [[entities/bell-peppers]] and cut 1 head of [[entities/broccoli]] into bite‑size pieces. Store together in a 32‑oz container. Fresh 3–4 days.
3. **Prep side vegetables** – Chop 8 oz carrots or trim 8 oz green beans. Store in a **16‑oz container**. Fresh 4–5 days.
4. **Prepare greens** – Wash 5–6 oz fresh spinach; dry and pack in a 16‑oz container with a dry paper towel. Keeps 3–4 days. Store basil in an 8‑oz container wrapped slightly damp (≈3 days).
5. **Protein preparation** –
   - Dice 10–12 oz of [[entities/chicken-thighs]] or [[entities/tofu]] for the stir‑fry; store in a 16‑oz container (raw 2 days).\
   - Season 1½ lb of [[entities/chicken-thighs]] for the one‑pan roast; store in a separate 16‑oz container (raw 2 days).
6. **Make sauces** –
   - **Soy‑ginger sauce**: 3 Tbsp soy sauce, 1 Tbsp lime juice, 1 tsp grated ginger, splash of [[entities/olive-oil]]. Store in an 8‑oz jar (4–5 days).\
   - **Yogurt‑lime dressing**: ½ cup plain yogurt, juice of 1 lime, drizzle of [[entities/olive-oil]], pinch of salt. Store in another 8‑oz jar (4–5 days).
7. **Roast mixed vegetables** – Toss 2 cups of a mix (remaining [[entities/bell-peppers]], [[entities/broccoli]], carrots) with [[entities/olive-oil]] and salt. Roast at 425 °F 20 min, cool, store in a 16‑oz container (4–5 days).
8. **Mince garlic** – Mince 2 cloves of garlic and keep in a 4‑oz container (3 days).

### Integration with Meal‑Planning Templates
The workflow supplies the building blocks for the four **meal‑planning templates** described in the source: 
- Stir‑fry (protein + bell peppers + broccoli + soy‑ginger sauce) 
- One‑pan roasted protein & potatoes ([[entities/potatoes]] + carrots/green beans) 
- Tomato‑basil pasta (using [[entities/marinara-sauce]] from the pantry) 
- Bean‑and‑grain bowl ([[entities/black-beans]] + grain + roasted veg + yogurt‑lime dressing)

These templates rely on the pre‑cooked grain, chopped veggies, sauces, and protein portions prepared during the workflow, allowing each dinner to be assembled in under 30 minutes.

### Benefits
- **Time efficiency**: 60–90 minutes total prep for a full week.
- **Portion control**: Each dinner yields exactly three servings (2 dinner + 1 lunch).
- **Reduced waste**: Container sizing aligns with expected consumption and fridge‑life.
- **Flexibility**: Ingredients can be swapped within the same template without re‑doing the workflow.
