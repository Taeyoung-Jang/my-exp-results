# Evening Routine

An **evening routine** is a set of purposeful actions performed in the hours before sleep to signal the body that it is time to wind down, improve relaxation, and protect sleep quality.  The routine typically combines a **[[concepts/wind-down-ritual]]**, mindful timing of stimulants, light snacks, and consistent environmental cues.

## Core Elements

1. **Wind‑down ritual** – A short, calming sequence (e.g., dimming lights, putting devices away, reading a few pages) that creates a predictable cue for the brain.  [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]]

2. **Drink selection** – Warm, low‑caffeine or caffeine‑free beverages about an hour before bed, such as:
   - Herbal teas like [[entities/herbal-tea]] or plain water.
   - Warm milk alternatives (e.g., [[entities/oat-milk]], [[entities/almond-milk]]).
   - Decaf coffee options like [[entities/decaf-espresso]] or coffee substitutes such as [[entities/roasted-barley]] or [[entities/chicory]].

3. **Snack choices** – Light, easy‑to‑digest foods that satisfy hunger without disrupting sleep.  Examples include a handful of nuts, a banana, whole‑grain crackers, or a small piece of [[entities/dark-chocolate]].

4. **Caffeine timing** – Limiting caffeine intake to earlier in the day and switching to decaf after mid‑afternoon helps avoid sleep‑interfering stimulation.  [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]

5. **Alcohol considerations** – Small, low‑sugar pours (e.g., a splash of [[entities/amaretto]], [[entities/whiskey]], or [[entities/wine]]) can be enjoyed if they are taken well before the intended sleep time, as alcohol can fragment later sleep cycles.  [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]]

6. **Hydration timing** – Consuming most of the day’s fluids earlier and limiting large volumes close to bedtime prevents nighttime awakenings for bathroom trips.  [[concepts/hydration-timing]]

## Benefits
- Enhances **[[concepts/sleep-hygiene]]** by establishing consistent pre‑sleep cues.
- Reduces sleep latency and nighttime awakenings.
- Supports overall wellbeing through balanced nutrition and reduced stimulant exposure.

## Example from Session 33
In the conversation documented in [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]], the user sought a relaxing yet sleep‑friendly evening ritual.  Recommendations emphasized:
- Replacing regular espresso with [[entities/decaf-espresso]].
- Pairing the drink with light snacks (nuts, dark chocolate, banana, crackers).
- Optional small alcohol additions (amaretto, whiskey, red wine) taken early enough to avoid sleep disruption.
- Consistent cues such as dim lighting and phone‑free time.

These suggestions illustrate how a well‑designed **evening routine** can preserve the enjoyable aspects of a nightly ritual while minimizing factors that impair sleep.
