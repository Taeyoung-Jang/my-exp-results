# Sleep Hygiene

Sleep hygiene refers to a set of behavioral and environmental practices that support consistent, high‑quality sleep. Good sleep hygiene helps the body’s circadian rhythms, reduces nighttime awakenings, and improves overall restorative value of sleep.

## Core Elements

- **Consistent wind‑down ritual** – Engaging in a predictable routine (e.g., dimming lights, putting devices away, reading) signals the brain that bedtime is approaching. See [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]].
- **Timing of stimulants** – Limit caffeine intake, especially in the late afternoon or evening, to avoid sleep‑disrupting effects. Refer to [[concepts/caffeine-timing]].
- **Alcohol considerations** – Small amounts of alcohol can initially promote sleepiness but tend to fragment later‑night sleep. Managing portion size and timing is essential. See [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]].
- **Evening fluid intake** – Drinking large volumes close to bedtime can cause nocturnal awakenings for bathroom trips. Moderate timing of hydration promotes uninterrupted sleep. See [[concepts/hydration-timing]].
- **Light exposure** – Dimming lights and reducing blue‑light exposure an hour before sleep supports melatonin production.
- **Physical environment** – Cool, dark, quiet rooms improve sleep onset and continuity.

## Application from Session 33 (2025‑02‑12)

The conversation in [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]] outlines a practical sleep‑hygiene routine:
1. **Replace caffeinated espresso with decaf** after mid‑afternoon to preserve the ritual without the stimulant impact.
2. **Shift the timing** of the evening drink to about an hour before bed, allowing the body time to wind down.
3. **Limit alcohol** to a small pour, consumed well before sleep, to avoid fragmented sleep patterns.
4. **Choose light snacks** (nuts, banana, whole‑grain crackers) that are easy to digest and do not spike blood sugar.
5. **Use consistent cues** such as dimming lights and putting the phone away, reinforcing the wind‑down ritual.

By integrating these steps—adjusting caffeine, managing alcohol, selecting appropriate snacks, and employing consistent cues—individuals can enhance their overall sleep hygiene and enjoy more restorative sleep.
