# Hydration Timing

Hydration Timing refers to the strategic distribution of fluid intake throughout the day to support optimal physiological function, steady energy levels, and healthy sleep. The concept is built on the idea that not all drinks are equal at every hour, and that the type, amount, and timing of hydration can influence cortisol rhythms, workout performance, and bedtime preparation.

## Core Principles
- **Baseline Still Water** – Begin each day with 12‑16 oz of still water (e.g., [[entities/water]]) soon after waking to re‑hydrate after sleep.
- **Mid‑Morning to Early Afternoon** – Introduce sparkling water in a moderated way (pour half a can, let bubbles settle, sip slowly) between roughly 11:00 am and 2:00 pm. This window minimizes bloating because the body is upright and active.
- **Pre‑ and Post‑Workout** – Prior to a late‑afternoon workout, stick to still water only; after exercise, replenish with still water or a mild herbal tea (e.g., [[entities/herbal-tea]]) to aid recovery.
- **Evening Wind‑Down** – From about 6:00 pm onward, emphasize still water or non‑caffeinated teas, and taper total fluid volume after 9:00 pm to reduce nighttime awakenings.

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/caffeine-timing]]** – Caffeine is limited to the morning; once caffeine is finished, the focus shifts to pure hydration without stimulants.
- **[[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]]** – Alcohol is reserved for late evening; a small water glass alongside the drink helps maintain hydration balance before the final wind‑down period.

## Practical Tips from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]]
1. **Sip Slowly** – For sparkling water, small sips over 10‑15 minutes reduce the perception of bloating.
2. **Half‑and‑Half Mix** – Combine equal parts sparkling and still water to keep the refreshing fizz while cutting down carbonation intensity.
3. **Upright Consumption** – Drink carbonated beverages while standing or moving (e.g., during morning tasks) to aid digestion.
4. **Consistent Small Amounts** – Aim for 8‑12 oz of still water per hour from mid‑morning through late afternoon, adjusting based on activity level.

## Summary Schedule (Illustrative)
| Time | Drink | Amount | Goal |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| 7:00 am | Still water | 12‑16 oz | Re‑hydration after sleep |
| 11:00 am – 2:00 pm | Sparkling water (mitigated) + still water | Up to 12 oz total | Refresh without bloating |
| 3:00 pm – 5:00 pm (pre‑workout) | Still water | 8‑12 oz | Hydration without carbonation |
| 5:30 pm – 7:00 pm (post‑workout) | Still water or [[entities/herbal-tea]] | 8‑12 oz | Recovery |
| 8:30 pm – 9:00 pm (alcohol) | Alcoholic drink + water | 1 serving + 4 oz water | Balanced intake before sleep |
| 10:30 pm onward | No drinks | – | Prepare for sleep |

Hydration Timing thus provides a flexible framework that can be adapted for workout days, rest days, or any personal routine, ensuring that fluid intake supports both daily performance and nighttime recovery.
