# Summary of Session 2 (2024‑06‑15)

## Overview
This document provides a practical **daily beverage schedule** that balances hydration, energy, and sleep quality. It outlines general principles and then presents two concrete timelines—one for a **late‑afternoon workout day** and another for a **rest day**—while respecting the user’s constraints (caffeine finished by early afternoon, alcohol only late evening) and offering strategies to enjoy **[[entities/sparkling-water]]** without bloating.

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/hydration-timing]]** – steady water intake throughout the day, with still water as the baseline.  
- **[[concepts/caffeine-timing]]** – caffeine intake limited to the morning hours (finish by early afternoon) to align with natural cortisol peaks and avoid sleep disruption.  
- **Sparkling‑water bloating mitigation** – pour half a can into a glass, let bubbles settle, sip slowly, or mix half‑and‑half with still water.  
- **[[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]]** – moderate alcohol consumed late evening (after 8:30 pm) with water to minimize sleep impact.  
- **Workout hydration** – prioritize still water before and after exercise; place sparkling water earlier in the day when upright.  
- **Rest‑day hydration** – similar water schedule, but sparkling water can be introduced slightly earlier.  
- **Sleep hygiene** – taper fluid intake after 9 pm and finish all drinks by 10:30 pm to reduce nighttime awakenings.

| Time | Drink | Amount | Notes |

| **7:00 am** | Still [[entities/water]] | 12–16 oz | Re‑hydrate after sleep. |

| **10:30 pm** | No more drinks | – | Begin sleep preparation. |

## Version 1: Workout Day (Late‑Afternoon Session)
- **7:00 am** – Still [[entities/water]] (12–16 oz).  
- **8:00 am** – [[entities/coffee]] (1 cup).  
- **10:30 am** – Second coffee (optional, 1 cup) – caffeine done by **12 pm**.  
- **11:00 am – 2:00 pm** – [[entities/sparkling-water]] via mitigation (half‑can poured, sip slowly) plus still [[entities/water]].  
- **3:00 pm – 5:00 pm** – Pre‑workout still [[entities/water]] only; avoid carbonation.  
- **5:30 pm – 7:00 pm** – Post‑workout hydration: still [[entities/water]] or mild [[entities/herbal-tea]].  
- **8:30 pm – 9:00 pm** – One alcoholic serving + small water glass.  
- **10:30 pm** – Stop liquids.

## Version 2: Rest Day
- **7:00 am** – Still [[entities/water]] (12–16 oz).  
- **8:00 am** – [[entities/coffee]] (1 cup).  
- **10:30 am** – Optional second coffee (1 cup) – caffeine wraps up by **noon**.  
- **10:30 am – 1:00 pm** – [[entities/sparkling-water]] (half‑can, let settle) mixed with still [[entities/water]] if desired.  
- **2:00 pm – 5:00 pm** – Still [[entities/water]] or [[entities/herbal-tea]].  
- **6:00 pm – 7:00 pm** – Calming non‑caffeinated tea or flavored water.  
- **8:30 pm – 9:00 pm** – One alcoholic drink + water.  
- **10:30 pm** – No further intake.

## Practical Tips for Sparkling Water
1. **Half‑and‑half** – Mix equal parts [[entities/sparkling-water]] and still [[entities/water]] to reduce carbonation intensity.  
2. **Let it sit** – After pouring, wait ~60 seconds for bubbles to lessen before sipping.  
3. **Sip slowly** – Small sips over 10‑15 minutes avoid rapid gas expansion.  
4. **Upright posture** – Consume while moving or standing (e.g., during morning tasks) to aid digestion.

## Takeaways
- Align caffeine with natural cortisol peaks; finish by early afternoon.  
- Keep total liquid intake moderate after evening to protect **sleep hygiene**.  
- Use the described sparkling‑water techniques to enjoy carbonation without bloating.  
- Tailor the schedule to activity level: prioritize still water around workouts and allow a mid‑day sparkling‑water window when the stomach is settled.
