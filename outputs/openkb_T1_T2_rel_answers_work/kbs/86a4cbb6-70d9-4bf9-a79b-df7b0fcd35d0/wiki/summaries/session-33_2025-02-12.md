# Summary of Session 33 (2025‑02‑12)

- The user wants a relaxing **[[concepts/evening-routine]]** that doesn’t harm sleep quality.  
- Core recommendation: adopt a simple **[[concepts/wind-down-ritual]]** about an hour before bed, focusing on warm, caffeine‑free drinks (herbal tea [[entities/herbal-tea]], warm milk) and light, easy‑to‑digest snacks.  
- For coffee lovers, the first step is to **switch to decaf espresso** [[entities/decaf-espresso]] after mid‑afternoon, preserving the aroma and ritual while removing most [[entities/caffeine]]. Alternatives include roasted barley [[entities/roasted-barley]] or chicory [[entities/chicory]] “mock‑espresso” drinks that are naturally caffeine‑free.  
- Suggested snack options: a handful of nuts, a banana, whole‑grain crackers, yogurt, or a small square of dark chocolate [[entities/dark-chocolate]] – all light enough to avoid overnight digestion issues.  
- **Alcohol considerations**: alcohol can cause fragmented sleep; see [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]]. If included, keep the pour small, low‑sugar, and sip it well before the intended sleep time.  
- Sample night‑friendly pairings:  
  1. **Decaf espresso** with a splash of warm milk + dark chocolate or almonds.  
  2. **Decaf cappuccino** made with oat milk [[entities/oat-milk]] + banana slices or crackers with nut butter.  
  3. **Decaf macchiato** + a tiny splash of amaretto [[entities/amaretto]] or whiskey [[entities/whiskey]] (or a chicory faux‑espresso + a small pour of red wine [[entities/wine]]) paired with nuts or cheese.  
- **Prioritizing changes**:  
  1. Switch to decaf espresso.  
  2. Move the drink earlier in the evening to give the body more time to settle (see [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]).  
  3. If sleep is still disrupted, consider swapping the alcohol for a non‑alcoholic alternative.  
- Emphasize consistent cues (dim lights, phone away, short reading) to reinforce the ritual as part of overall [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]].
