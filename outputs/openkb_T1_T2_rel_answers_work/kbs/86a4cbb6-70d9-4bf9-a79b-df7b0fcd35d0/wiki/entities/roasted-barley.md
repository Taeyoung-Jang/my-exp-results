# Roasted Barley

**Type:** product

Roasted barley is a grain that has been toasted until it develops a deep, nutty flavor. When brewed, it produces a dark, coffee‑like beverage that is naturally caffeine‑free, making it popular as a nighttime alternative to regular coffee.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]]
- Suggested as a **coffee alternative** for a **wind‑down ritual** because it mimics the aroma and taste of espresso without the caffeine.
- Can be prepared as a “mock‑espresso” and paired with a small pour of red wine or a snack such as nuts or cheese for a relaxing evening routine.
- Mentioned alongside other **night‑friendly drinks** like [[entities/decaf-espresso]] and [[entities/chicory]].
