# Amaretto

Amaretto is a sweet, almond‑style liqueur commonly enjoyed as a small pour in after‑dinner drinks. In the context of the **evening routine** discussed in [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]], a tiny splash of Amaretto is suggested as a night‑friendly addition to a decaf espresso‑based beverage (e.g., a decaf macchiato) to provide a treat without overwhelming caffeine.

## Key Points from Session 33
- **Sleep‑friendly pairing**: A decaf macchiato with a *tiny* splash of Amaretto, consumed well before the intended sleep time, can preserve the ritual feel while minimizing impact on sleep.
- **Portion control**: Emphasized keeping the pour small to avoid the [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]] issues that larger amounts of alcohol can cause.
- **Timing**: Recommended sipping the drink slowly and stopping well before bedtime, aligning with the broader [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]] and [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]] guidelines.
