# Oat Milk

**Type:** Product

## Overview
Oat milk is a plant‑based, oat‑derived milk alternative that provides a creamy texture and mild flavor. It is commonly used as a dairy‑free substitute in hot drinks and light snacks.

## Night‑Friendly Uses (from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]])
- Recommended as the milk component in a **decaf cappuccino** for an evening [[concepts/evening-routine]].
- Paired with a small banana slice or whole‑grain crackers with nut butter as part of a [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]] that supports [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]].
- Offers a low‑caffeine, low‑sugar option compared to dairy milk, helping maintain appropriate [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] before bed.
