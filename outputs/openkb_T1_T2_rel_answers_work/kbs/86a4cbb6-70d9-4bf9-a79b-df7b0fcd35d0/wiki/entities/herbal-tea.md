# Herbal Tea (product)

Herbal tea is a non‑caffeinated infusion made from dried herbs, fruits, or flowers. It is commonly consumed for its soothing flavors and mild health benefits.

## Role in the Daily Drink Schedule (session‑02_2024‑06‑15)
- Serves as a gentle, non‑stimulant beverage in the early afternoon (around 2:00 pm – 5:00 pm) to help wind down after caffeine has been finished.
- Recommended after workouts on a workout day, providing hydration without the diuretic effect of caffeine.
- Often paired with water intake to maintain overall [[concepts/hydration-timing]].

## Benefits Mentioned
- Supports a calm transition toward evening, complementing the [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] strategy of ending caffeine by early afternoon.
- Provides flavor variety without adding sugar or stimulants, aiding steady energy levels.

## Suggested Consumption
- **Amount:** 8 oz (about one cup) per serving.
- **Timing:** 2:00 pm – 5:00 pm on both workout and rest days, or as a soothing option around 6:00 pm – 7:00 pm before the late‑evening alcoholic drink.
- **Varieties:** Mint, rooibos, chamomile, ginger, etc.

## Notes
Herbal tea is distinct from traditional [[entities/tea]] (black, green, oolong) because it contains no *Camellia sinensis* leaves and therefore no caffeine.
