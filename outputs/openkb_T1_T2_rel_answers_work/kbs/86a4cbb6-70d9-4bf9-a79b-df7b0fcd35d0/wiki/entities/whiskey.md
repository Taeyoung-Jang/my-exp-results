# Whiskey (product)

Whiskey is a distilled alcoholic beverage made from fermented grain mash, typically aged in wooden barrels to develop flavor. In the context of [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]], whiskey is suggested as a small, optional addition to a night‑friendly coffee‑style ritual:

- **Use case**: A tiny splash added to a decaf macchiato as a treat that should be sipped slowly and finished well before bedtime.
- **Sleep considerations**: While whiskey can provide a relaxing sensation, alcohol can fragment sleep later in the night, so the recommendation emphasizes keeping the pour small and limiting consumption close to sleep time. This aligns with the broader guidance on [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]].
