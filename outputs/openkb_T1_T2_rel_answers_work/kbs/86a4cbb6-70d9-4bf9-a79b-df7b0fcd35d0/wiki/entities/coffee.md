# Coffee

Coffee is a **product** consisting of a brewed drink made from roasted coffee beans. In the context of the daily drink schedule from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]], coffee is used as the main source of caffeine.

## Role in the Schedule
- **Timing**: Consumed in the morning, typically around 8:00 am, with an optional second cup around 10:30 am.
- **Quantity**: One standard cup (≈8 oz) per serving.
- **Caffeine Management**: Aligns with the principle of [[concepts/caffeine-timing]], recommending that all caffeine intake be completed by early afternoon to avoid sleep disruption.
- **Hydration Context**: Paired with still water throughout the day, supporting the broader [[concepts/hydration-timing]] strategy.

## Related Entities
- [[entities/caffeine]] – the active stimulant provided by coffee.
- [[entities/water]] – the primary hydration baseline accompanying coffee consumption.
- [[entities/herbal-tea]] – an alternative non‑caffeinated option for the afternoon wind‑down.

## Key Takeaways
- Coffee provides a quick, familiar source of caffeine to boost morning alertness.
- Limiting coffee to the morning hours respects [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] and supports better sleep quality.
- Integrating coffee with adequate water intake aligns with overall hydration best practices.
