# Almond Milk

Almond milk is a non‑dairy, plant‑based milk made from ground almonds and water. It is commonly used as a warm beverage or as a milk substitute in coffee‑style drinks.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]]
- Suggested as a **warm milk option** for a night‑time wind‑down, offering a soothing alternative to dairy milk.
- Paired with **decaf espresso** or a **decaf cappuccino** to preserve the ritual of an evening coffee without caffeine.
- Recommended alongside light snacks such as a small square of **dark chocolate** or a handful of nuts, supporting a gentle [[concepts/evening-routine]].
- Fits within a broader [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]] aimed at improving [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]].
