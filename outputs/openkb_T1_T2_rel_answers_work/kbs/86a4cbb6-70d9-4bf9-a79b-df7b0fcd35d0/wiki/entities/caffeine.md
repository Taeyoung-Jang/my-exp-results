# Caffeine (entity)

Caffeine is a naturally occurring psychoactive stimulant commonly consumed in beverages such as [[entities/coffee]] and [[entities/tea]].  In the context of the daily drink schedule from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]], caffeine is positioned as a morning energizer and is deliberately limited to early‑day consumption.

## Key facts from the session‑02 schedule
- **Timing:** Recommended intake is concentrated between 7:00 am and 12:00 pm, with the last coffee finished by early afternoon. This aligns with the principle of [[concepts/caffeine-timing]], which suggests matching caffeine use to the body's natural cortisol peak for smoother energy and fewer crashes.
- **Amount:** One standard cup (≈8 oz) of coffee at 8:00 am, with an optional second cup around 10:30 am. Total caffeine is kept modest to avoid evening sleep disruption.
- **Interaction with other drinks:** After caffeine is wrapped up, the schedule shifts to plain [[entities/water]] and later to non‑caffeinated options such as [[entities/herbal-tea]].
- **Impact on sleep and alcohol:** By finishing caffeine early, the schedule supports better sleep quality and reduces the risk of compounding sleep‑disruptive effects when alcohol is later consumed. This reflects insights from [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]].

## Relationship to other entities and concepts
- **Beverage sources:** [[entities/coffee]], [[entities/tea]], and occasionally [[entities/cocktail]] (when caffeinated mixers are used, though the schedule advises against this).
- **Hydration context:** Caffeine intake is interleaved with regular hydration from [[entities/water]] and occasional [[entities/sparkling-water]] (the latter is scheduled later in the day to minimize bloating).
- **Alcoholic drinks:** The schedule recommends moderate alcohol (e.g., [[entities/beer]] or [[entities/wine]]) only in the late evening, after caffeine has cleared, to protect sleep.

## Practical recommendations
1. **Consume caffeine early:** Stick to the 7 am–12 pm window to sync with natural energy cycles.
2. **Limit total dose:** One to two standard cups per day keep stimulant effects moderate.
3. **Separate from alcohol:** Allow several hours between the last caffeine dose and any alcoholic intake to avoid amplifying sleep disturbances.

These guidelines derive directly from the daily drink scheduling advice in [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]].
