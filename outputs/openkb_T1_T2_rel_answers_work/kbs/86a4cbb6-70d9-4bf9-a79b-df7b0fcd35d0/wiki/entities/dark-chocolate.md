# Dark Chocolate

Dark chocolate is a type of chocolate containing a high percentage of cocoa solids and relatively low amounts of sugar and dairy. It is prized for its rich flavor, antioxidant content, and, when consumed in moderation, its minimal impact on sleep.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]]
- Suggested as a **night‑friendly snack** to pair with a [[entities/decaf-espresso]] during a [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]].
- Recommended portion is a **small square** to keep the snack light and easy to digest before bed.
- Because it is low in sugar, it aligns with good [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]] and does not spike blood glucose, which can disrupt sleep.
- Can be combined with a **tiny splash of alcohol** (e.g., [[entities/amaretto]] or [[entities/whiskey]]) for a treat‑like feel, though the amount should remain small to avoid the [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]] that leads to fragmented sleep.
- The cocoa content provides a modest amount of **theobromine**, a milder stimulant than caffeine, making it a safer option than sugary milk chocolate for late‑night consumption.
