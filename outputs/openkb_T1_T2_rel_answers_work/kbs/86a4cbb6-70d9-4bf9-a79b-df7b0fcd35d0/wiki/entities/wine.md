# Wine

**Type:** product

Wine is an alcoholic beverage produced by fermenting grapes. It is often enjoyed as part of an evening wind‑down routine, especially when paired with low‑caffeine or caffeine‑free drinks to create a calming, treat‑like experience without heavily disrupting sleep.

### Recommended intake from the schedule
- **Time:** Late evening, after caffeine and primary hydration phases have ended (approximately 8:30 pm – 9:00 pm). Aim to finish drinking at least an hour before the intended bedtime to allow the body to begin winding down.
- **Amount:** One moderate serving (≈5 oz / 150 ml). Keep the pour small to limit alcohol‑induced sleep fragmentation.
- **Accompaniment:** Pair with a small glass of [[entities/water]] to maintain overall hydration and mitigate the dehydrating effects of alcohol.
- **Snack pairing:** A handful of nuts, a piece of cheese, or a small square of dark chocolate can soften the alcohol’s impact and add a pleasant texture to the ritual.
- **Optional night‑friendly combo:** A tiny splash of wine can be enjoyed alongside a decaf espresso or a chicory‑based faux‑espresso (e.g., [[entities/chicory]]) for a coffee‑like aroma without caffeine. Sip slowly and stop well before sleep.

### Sleep impact
Limiting wine to the late‑evening window aligns with recommendations in [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]]: a modest amount is less likely to cause severe sleep fragmentation, especially when consumed with water and a light snack. Pairing wine with caffeine‑free coffee alternatives or decaf espresso respects [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] by avoiding late‑night stimulants, supporting overall [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]] and a smoother [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]].
