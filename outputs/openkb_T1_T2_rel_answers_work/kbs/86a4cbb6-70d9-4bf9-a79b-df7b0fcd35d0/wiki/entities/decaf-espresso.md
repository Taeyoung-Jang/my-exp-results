# Decaf Espresso

Decaf Espresso is a coffee beverage made from beans that have undergone a decaffeination process, retaining the traditional espresso flavor and aroma while containing minimal [[entities/caffeine]].

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]]
- **Primary recommendation**: Switch to decaf espresso as the first change to a nightly [[concepts/evening-routine]] to keep the ritual feel without disrupting sleep.
- **Timing**: Aligns with [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]—use decaf after mid‑afternoon and finish the drink at least an hour before bed to support [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]].
- **Role in wind‑down**: Serves as a central element of a [[concepts/wind-down-ritual]], providing a warm, comforting cue for relaxation.
- **Common pairings**:
  - Warm milk alternatives such as [[entities/almond-milk]] or [[entities/oat-milk]] for a creamy texture.
  - Light snacks like a small square of [[entities/dark-chocolate]], a handful of nuts, banana slices, or whole‑grain crackers.
- **Alcohol‑infused treat**: A tiny splash of [[entities/amaretto]], [[entities/whiskey]], or [[entities/wine]] can be added for a special version, but the amount should be minimal and finished well before bedtime to avoid issues highlighted in [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]].
- **Caffeine‑free alternatives**: For those who prefer to avoid espresso entirely, drinks made from [[entities/chicory]] or [[entities/roasted-barley]] provide a coffee‑like flavor without any caffeine.

## Practical tips
1. Replace regular espresso with decaf after mid‑afternoon.
2. Consume the decaf drink no later than 1–2 hours before intended sleep time.
3. Pair with light, easily digestible snacks and optional non‑alcoholic warm milk.
4. If adding alcohol, keep the pour small and sip slowly, stopping well before sleep.

By following these guidelines, decaf espresso can fit seamlessly into a relaxing evening routine while minimizing the risk of sleep disruption.
