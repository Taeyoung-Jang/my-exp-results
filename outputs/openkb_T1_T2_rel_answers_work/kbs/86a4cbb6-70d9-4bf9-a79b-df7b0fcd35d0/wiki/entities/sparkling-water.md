# Sparkling Water

**Type:** product

Sparkling water is water that has been infused with carbon dioxide gas under pressure, giving it its characteristic bubbles and a slightly tangy taste. It is often chosen as a zero‑calorie alternative to sugary sodas and can be flavored with natural extracts.

## Key Points from Session 02 (2024‑06‑15)
- Recommended as part of a **daily drink schedule** for hydration, especially between **11:00 am and 2:00 pm** on both workout and rest days.
- The document suggests mitigation strategies to reduce bloating, such as:
  1. Pouring a half‑can into a glass and letting it sit for about a minute so some carbonation softens.
  2. Sipping slowly over several minutes.
  3. Mixing half sparkling water with half still water (see [[entities/water]]).
- Suggested serving size: roughly **½ can (≈6 oz)** per occasion, spaced out rather than consumed quickly.
- Positioned after morning caffeine and before the late‑afternoon workout or rest‑day activities, aligning with the [[concepts/hydration-timing]] and avoiding the period right before sleep.
