# Chicory (product)

Chicory is a root plant whose roasted grounds are used as a **caffeine‑free coffee substitute**.  In the context of the evening routine discussed in [[summaries/session-33_2025-02-12]], chicory is recommended as a *mock‑espresso* or *faux‑espresso* drink that preserves the coffee‑like aroma and ritual without the sleep‑disrupting effects of caffeine.

## Key Points from the Session
- **Night‑friendly alternative**: Suggested as a replacement for regular espresso to maintain the cozy ritual while supporting [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]].
- **Pairings**: Paired with a small pour of red wine and a handful of nuts or cheese for a relaxing pre‑sleep treat.
- **Flavor profile**: Offers a roasted, slightly bitter taste similar to coffee, making it suitable for those who miss the coffee flavor but want to avoid caffeine.
