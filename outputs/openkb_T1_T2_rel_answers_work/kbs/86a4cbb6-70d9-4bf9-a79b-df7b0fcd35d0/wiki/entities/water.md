## Water

Water is a calorie‑free, non‑alcoholic beverage that serves as the primary source of hydration in daily drink schedules.

### Role in the "daily drink schedule" (see [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]])
- **Morning anchor** – A tall glass of still water (12–16 oz) at ~7:00 am re‑hydrates after sleep.
- **Continuous hydration** – Throughout the day, still water is consumed regularly to maintain optimal fluid balance, aligning with [[concepts/hydration-timing]].
- **Pre‑ and post‑workout** – On workout days, still water is emphasized before and after the exercise window (3:00 pm‑5:00 pm) to support performance and recovery.
- **Evening wind‑down** – After 8:30 pm, water is paired with an alcoholic drink (wine, beer, or cocktail) to moderate alcohol’s impact on sleep, reflecting the principles of [[concepts/alcohol-sleep-interaction]].

### Interaction with other beverages
- **Caffeine timing** – Water is the baseline fluid before, during, and after caffeine consumption (coffee, tea) to smooth energy spikes, complementing [[concepts/caffeine-timing]].
- **Sparkling water** – Still water can be mixed half‑and‑half with [[entities/sparkling-water]] to reduce carbonation while still providing variety, a strategy suggested to minimize bloating.
- **Herbal alternatives** – Still water is often paired with [[entities/herbal-tea]] in the late afternoon/evening as a non‑caffeinated, soothing option.

### Practical tips from the session
1. **Start the day** with 12–16 oz of still water.
2. **Sip regularly** (≈8‑12 oz per hour) from late morning through early afternoon.
3. **Use half‑and‑half** mixes with sparkling water to enjoy fizz without discomfort.
4. **Pair water with alcohol** (wine, [[entities/beer]], [[entities/cocktail]]) in the late evening to aid hydration and reduce sleep disruption.
5. **Limit fluid intake after 9:00 pm** to support uninterrupted sleep.

These guidelines position water as the foundational element of a balanced daily beverage plan.
