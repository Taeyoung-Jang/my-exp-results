# Tea (product)

Tea refers to a range of brewed beverages made from the leaves of the *Camellia sinensis* plant (such as black, green, oolong, and white tea) or from herbs and spices (herbal teas like mint or rooibos). In the context of the daily drink schedule from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]], tea is mentioned as an alternative to coffee for morning caffeine and as a non‑caffeinated option later in the day.

## Key Points from the Session
- **Morning caffeine option**: A cup of black or green tea can replace coffee around 8:00 a.m. to provide steady energy while keeping caffeine intake modest.
- **Afternoon/evening**: Herbal teas such as mint or rooibos are suggested around 2:00–3:00 p.m. to wind down without affecting sleep.
- **Timing relation**: The scheduling follows the principle of [[concepts/caffeine-timing]], ensuring tea’s caffeine is consumed early enough to avoid sleep disruption.
