# E‑Reader (product)

**Overview**

An e‑reader is a single‑purpose electronic device designed primarily for reading digital books. Its display technology (often e‑ink) mimics paper, offering good readability in bright light and low power consumption, making it a distraction‑light alternative to reading on a [[entities/smartphone]].

**Key points from [[summaries/session-35_2024-06-15]]**

- Recommended as a dedicated gadget that can **reduce distractions** compared to a phone.  
- Mentioned alongside other focused devices (analog wristwatch, basic alarm clock, paper notebook) that help users break habitual phone checking.  
- Highlighted for its calm, notification‑free experience, which can encourage more reading time.

**Benefits related to [[concepts/distraction-reduction]]**

- No push notifications or app ecosystem to pull attention away.  
- Long battery life (weeks) allows use without frequent charging, reinforcing a low‑maintenance routine.

**Comparison with other dedicated gadgets**

- Unlike a [[entities/basic-alarm-clock]], the e‑reader primarily impacts reading habits rather than sleep‑related phone use.  
- Complements a [[entities/paper-notebook]] for note‑taking, but remains purely digital.

**Related entities**

- [[entities/analog-wristwatch]] – another minimalist time‑telling device.  
- [[entities/dedicated-music-player]] – a focused audio alternative.  
- [[entities/digital-notepad]] – a simple digital note‑taking tool.

**Practical tip**

If you aim to cut phone time, keep the e‑reader in a convenient spot (e.g., bedside table) and use it for all reading sessions, reserving the phone for communication only.
