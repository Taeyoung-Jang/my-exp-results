# Smartphone

**Type:** product

**Overview**
A smartphone is a handheld computer that combines phone calls, messaging, internet access, apps, media playback, and a variety of sensors in a single device.

**Key insights from [[summaries/session-35_2024-06-15]]**
- The conversation frames the smartphone as the default tool for everyday tasks such as tracking time, setting reminders, and taking quick notes.
- While praised for convenience, the phone is also highlighted as a major source of distraction, especially at night and during wake‑up routines.
- Dedicated gadgets (e.g., an [[entities/analog-wristwatch]] or a [[entities/basic-alarm-clock]]) are suggested when they can *do one thing better* and reduce the need to check the phone.
- Practical strategies to limit smartphone use include:
  - Using a "[[concepts/dumb-alarm-clock]]" instead of a phone alarm to avoid morning notifications.
  - Placing the phone out of arm’s reach at night, following the "[[concepts/phone-placement]]" recommendation, to create friction before reaching for it.
  - Employing the "[[concepts/distraction-reduction]]" principle by substituting single‑purpose devices (e.g., an [[entities/e-reader]] for reading) for phone‑based activities.

**Related entities and concepts**
- [[entities/analog-wristwatch]] – example of a dedicated gadget that reduces reliance on the phone for timekeeping.
- [[entities/basic-alarm-clock]] – suggested alternative to a smartphone alarm for better focus.
- [[concepts/dumb-alarm-clock]] – a concept emphasizing minimal functionality to avoid distractions.
- [[concepts/phone-placement]] – a tactic for physically separating the phone from the user during sleep.
- [[concepts/distraction-reduction]] – the broader theme of minimizing attention‑draining tech.
