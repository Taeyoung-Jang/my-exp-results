# Apple Watch

Apple Watch is Apple’s flagship smartwatch that tightly integrates with the iPhone ecosystem. It offers seamless notification handling, calls, music control, and Apple Pay, and it syncs health data automatically to Apple Health.

## Key facts from session‑07_2023‑06‑15
- **Integration**: Provides the most "invisible glue" between devices, with background sync to [[entities/iphone]] and instant data flow into [[concepts/apple-health-sync]].
- **Health tracking**: Delivers solid heart‑rate, cardio‑fitness, activity, and sleep‑stage metrics, though the depth is less than Garmin’s specialized analytics.
- **Battery life**: Typically lasts about one day, requiring daily charging – a clear example of the [[concepts/battery-life-tradeoff]].
- **Smart features**: Supports Apple Pay, a wide third‑party app ecosystem, and full reply capability for notifications, unlike many Garmin models.
- **Use case**: Best suited for users who prioritize convenience, tight iPhone integration, and a broad smartwatch experience over multi‑day battery endurance.
