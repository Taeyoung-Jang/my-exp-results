# Fitbit (organization)

Fitbit is a consumer wearable brand that produces affordable fitness trackers and smartwatches focused on basic health monitoring.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-07_2023-06-15]]
- Positioned as a **simpler, lower‑cost** alternative to Apple Watch, Garmin, and Samsung Galaxy Watch.
- Provides reliable tracking of steps, sleep, heart rate, and basic stress metrics.
- Offers a more limited smart‑feature set: notifications are present but interactions are minimal, and there is no broad payment platform comparable to Apple Pay.
- The ecosystem is described as having "shrunk in depth" compared to Apple and Garmin, making it suitable for users who want health basics without a rich app marketplace.
