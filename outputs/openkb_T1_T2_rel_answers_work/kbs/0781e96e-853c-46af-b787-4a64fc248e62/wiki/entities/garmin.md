# Garmin

Garmin is a global company that designs and manufactures GPS navigation, outdoor, and fitness wearables. In the context of **session‑07_2023‑06‑15**, Garmin is discussed as a major player in the [[concepts/wearable-ecosystems]] offering distinct trade‑offs compared to Apple and other brands.

## Core Characteristics (as highlighted in the session)
- **Battery longevity** – Typical models achieve 4‑10 days of use, addressing the [[concepts/battery-life-tradeoff]] for users who dislike daily charging.
- **Health‑tracking depth** – Provides detailed sleep analysis, HRV, recovery, and Body Battery metrics, illustrating strong [[concepts/health-tracking-depth]] and especially robust [[concepts/sleep-hrv-metrics]].
- **iPhone integration** – Syncs via the Garmin Connect app and can export steps, heart rate, and sleep data to Apple Health, though the process is not as seamless as native Apple devices. This touches on [[concepts/iphone-integration]] and [[concepts/apple-health-sync]].
- **Notification & payment limitations** – Notifications appear on the watch but cannot be replied to; Garmin Pay is supported only with select banks, unlike the universal [[entities/apple-pay]] on Apple Watch. These points are covered under [[concepts/garmin-gotchas]].

## Relationship to Other Entities
- Competes with [[entities/apple-watch]] (iOS‑centric), [[entities/fitbit]] (budget‑friendly), and [[entities/samsung-galaxy-watch]] (Android‑centric). 
- Offers its own payment solution [[entities/garmin-pay]], which is more limited than Apple Pay.
