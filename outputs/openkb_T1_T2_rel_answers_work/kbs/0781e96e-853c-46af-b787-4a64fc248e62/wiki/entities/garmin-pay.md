# Garmin Pay

Garmin Pay is the contactless payment solution built into select Garmin smartwatches such as the [[entities/garmin-venu-3]] and other models. It allows users to make NFC payments directly from the watch, similar to other wearable payment services.

## Key points from the session‑07 discussion
- **Limited bank support** – The assistant noted that Garmin Pay can be “hit‑or‑miss depending on your bank,” meaning not all financial institutions are compatible.
- **No universal acceptance** – Unlike [[entities/apple-pay]], Garmin Pay is not as widely accepted across merchants and does not integrate with Apple’s broader ecosystem.
- **iPhone compatibility** – While Garmin Pay works with iPhones, users cannot rely on the same seamless experience as Apple devices; notifications and payments are less integrated.
- **Garmin ecosystem trade‑off** – The need to choose Garmin Pay over Apple Pay is part of the broader [[concepts/garmin-gotchas]] and the [[concepts/wearable-ecosystems]] discussion about balancing battery life and convenience.
