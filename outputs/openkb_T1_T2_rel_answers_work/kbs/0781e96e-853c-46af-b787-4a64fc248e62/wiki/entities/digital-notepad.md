# Digital Notepad

A **digital notepad** is a minimalist electronic device designed primarily for taking notes without the multitasking capabilities of a smartphone. In the conversation of [[summaries/session-35_2024-06-15]], it is presented as an alternative to paper that helps with [[concepts/distraction-reduction]].

## Key points from the session
- Mentioned alongside analog wristwatches, e‑readers, and basic alarm clocks as a tool that “does its job without competing for attention.”
- Suggested for users who want a focused writing experience without the temptation of apps or notifications.
- Compared to a **paper notebook** (see [[entities/paper-notebook]]) as a digital counterpart that still offers a single‑purpose workflow.

## Why it matters for attention management
A digital notepad reduces the need to pull out a [[entities/smartphone]] for quick ideas, thereby supporting the broader strategy of keeping the phone away during key moments (e.g., bedtime or morning routines) outlined under [[concepts/phone-placement]].

## Related concepts and entities
- [[concepts/distraction-reduction]] – The primary benefit of using a dedicated device for note‑taking.
- [[entities/analog-wristwatch]] – Another single‑purpose gadget discussed for its low‑distraction design.
- [[entities/e-reader]] – A comparable single‑purpose device for reading.
- [[entities/basic-alarm-clock]] – Highlighted as a high‑impact distraction‑reduction tool.
- [[entities/dedicated-music-player]] – Another example of a focused gadget.

## Practical considerations
- Choose a model with a simple interface and limited connectivity to avoid accidental app usage.
- Pair it with a routine that places the [[entities/smartphone]] out of arm’s reach (e.g., on a dresser across the room) to reinforce the distraction‑free habit.
