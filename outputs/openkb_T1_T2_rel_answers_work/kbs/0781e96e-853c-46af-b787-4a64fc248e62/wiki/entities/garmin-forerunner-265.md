# Garmin Forerunner 265

**Category**: Wearable smartwatch (product)

## Overview
The **Garmin Forerunner 265** is a lightweight, sport‑oriented GPS smartwatch that emphasizes comprehensive health monitoring—especially sleep quality, HRV trends, and everyday activity—while offering multi‑day battery life. It is positioned as a balanced alternative between pure fitness devices and full‑featured smartwatches.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-07_2023-06-15]]
- **Battery life**: Approximately a week (5–7 days) on a single charge, matching the endurance goal set by the user.
- **Health tracking depth**: Strong sleep analysis, HRV, resting heart rate, and recovery insights, aligning with the [[concepts/health-tracking-depth]] and [[concepts/sleep-hrv-metrics]] discussed in the session.
- **iPhone integration**: Works with iPhone for notifications, though you cannot reply to messages from the watch. Syncing occurs via the Garmin Connect app rather than the seamless background sync of [[entities/apple-watch]].
- **Payments**: Supports Garmin Pay, but availability depends on the user's bank; unlike [[entities/apple-pay]], it is not universally accepted.
- **App ecosystem**: Limited third‑party apps compared to Apple Watch; primarily relies on Garmin Connect for detailed analytics.
- **User experience**: Slightly more manual sync process; data flows to Apple Health via permissions but is not instant, referencing the [[concepts/apple-health-sync]] considerations.

## Positioning within Garmin’s Lineup
- Compared to [[entities/garmin-venu-3]]: the Venu 3 offers a brighter AMOLED display and a more “smartwatch‑like” UI, while the Forerunner 265 is lighter and sportier.
- Compared to [[entities/garmin-vivovactive-5]]: the vívoactive 5 is a more affordable option with similar core health features but fewer premium touches.
- Fits within the [[concepts/garmin-model-recommendations]] for users needing 5–7 day battery, strong sleep/HRV analytics, and acceptable iPhone notification support.

## Common Gotchas
- **Notification interaction**: You can receive alerts, but cannot respond directly from the watch (see [[concepts/garmin-gotchas]]).
- **Sync latency**: Data syncs when the watch reconnects or when the Garmin Connect app is opened, not in real‑time like [[concepts/iphone-integration]] with Apple devices.
- **Payment limitations**: Garmin Pay may not work with all banks, unlike the ubiquitous [[entities/apple-pay]].

## Ideal User Profile
- iPhone owners who prioritize **sleep and HRV insights** over extensive third‑party app ecosystems.
- Users who value **long battery life** and are comfortable with a slightly more manual sync workflow.
- Anyone looking for a **lightweight, sport‑focused** device that still provides solid everyday activity tracking.
