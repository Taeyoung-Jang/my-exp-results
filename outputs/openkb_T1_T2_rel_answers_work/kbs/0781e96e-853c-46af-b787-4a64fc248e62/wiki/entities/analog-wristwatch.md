## Analog Wristwatch

An analog wristwatch is a non‑digital time‑telling device that displays hours and minutes with moving hands. In the context of **session‑35_2024‑06‑15**, the user adopts an analog wristwatch to minimize reliance on a smartphone for basic time‑keeping, appreciating its simplicity, lack of notifications, and zero‑charging requirement.

### Key points from the conversation
- **Distraction reduction**: The watch provides a quiet, always‑on view of the time without app alerts, aligning with the [[concepts/distraction-reduction]] theme.
- **Battery‑free operation**: Unlike smartphones or smartwatches, the analog watch needs no charging, eliminating a common source of phone‑check behavior.
- **Catalyst for other dedicated gadgets**: The positive experience with the analog watch leads the user to explore further dedicated devices such as a [[entities/e-reader]] and a [[entities/basic-alarm-clock]].
