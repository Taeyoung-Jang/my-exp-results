# Basic Alarm Clock

A **basic alarm clock** is a stand‑alone time‑keeping device that rings at a set time without any smart features, apps, or internet connectivity. In the context of the conversation from [[summaries/session-35_2024-06-15]], it is recommended as a primary tool for cutting down on phone use, especially at bedtime and upon waking.

## Key points from the discussion
- **Distraction reduction**: A "dumb" clock prevents notification‑driven interruptions that a smartphone would otherwise provide. [[concepts/dumb-alarm-clock]] [[concepts/distraction-reduction]]
- **Phone placement**: Users are advised to keep their [[entities/smartphone]] out of arm’s reach (e.g., on a dresser across the room) to break the reflex of grabbing the phone first thing in the morning or night. [[concepts/phone-placement]]
- **Morning routine buffer**: Pair the alarm clock with a simple non‑phone task (brush teeth, make coffee) to create a friction that delays phone interaction.
- **Comparison to smart speakers**: The plain alarm clock is preferred over voice‑activated smart speakers because it lacks tempting features like news briefs or music playback.
