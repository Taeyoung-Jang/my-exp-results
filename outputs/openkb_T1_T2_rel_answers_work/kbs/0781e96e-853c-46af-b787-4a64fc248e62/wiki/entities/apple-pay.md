## Apple Pay

Apple Pay is a contactless mobile payment service that allows users to make purchases using compatible Apple devices such as the iPhone and Apple Watch.

### Key facts from session-07_2023-06-15
- Mentioned as a seamless payment option on the [[entities/apple-watch]] that works automatically with the iPhone ecosystem.
- Highlighted as a feature not available on Garmin devices, where users must rely on [[entities/garmin-pay]] if supported by their bank.
- The document contrasts the convenience of Apple Pay with the limited payment options on Garmin, framing it as part of the broader [[concepts/iphone-integration]] discussion.
