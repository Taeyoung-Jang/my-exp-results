## Garmin Venu 3

Garmin Venu 3 is a premium smartwatch in Garmin's lineup that emphasizes comprehensive health monitoring, especially sleep and HRV metrics, while offering a week‑long battery life. It is positioned as the most balanced option for users who want a smartwatch‑like experience together with deep fitness insights.

### Key characteristics
- **Display**: AMOLED screen for vivid visuals and a more polished smartwatch feel.
- **Battery life**: Approximately 7 days on typical use, making it suitable for users who dislike daily charging.
- **Health tracking**:
  - Advanced sleep stage analysis and HRV monitoring [[concepts/sleep-hrv-metrics]].
  - Recovery and body battery insights, providing context on overall readiness.
- **iPhone integration**:
  - Receives notifications from the iPhone, though replies must be made on the phone.
  - Syncs health data to Garmin Connect and can export steps, heart rate, and sleep to Apple Health [[concepts/apple-health-sync]].
- **Payments**: Supports Garmin Pay where compatible, but does not work with Apple Pay.

### Suitability
- Ideal for users prioritising **battery endurance** over instant smartwatch conveniences (see [[concepts/battery-life-tradeoff]]).
- Fits users who value **deep health tracking** more than a broad app ecosystem (see [[concepts/health-tracking-depth]]).
- Good middle ground between the tightly integrated Apple Watch experience and the more fitness‑focused Garmin devices, aligning with the trade‑offs discussed in [[concepts/wearable-ecosystems]].

### Known limitations ("gotchas")
- Cannot reply to messages directly from the watch [[concepts/garmin-gotchas]].
- Garmin Pay availability depends on the user's bank.
- Syncing relies on opening the Garmin Connect app; it is not as seamless as Apple Watch's background sync.
