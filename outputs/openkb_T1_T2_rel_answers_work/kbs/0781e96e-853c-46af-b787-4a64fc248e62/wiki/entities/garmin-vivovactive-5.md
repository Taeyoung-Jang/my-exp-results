# Garmin vívoactive 5

**Category**: Wearable smartwatch / fitness tracker

**Key facts (as discussed in session 07 2024 06 15)**
- **Price tier**: Positioning as the budget‑friendly option in Garmin’s lineup.
- **Health tracking**: Provides essential metrics such as steps, heart rate, sleep scoring, and HRV analysis.  It covers the core of [[concepts/health-tracking-depth]] needed for everyday wellness.
- **Battery life**: Typically delivers **5‑7 days** of use on a single charge, addressing the [[concepts/battery-life-tradeoff]] concern for users who dislike daily charging.
- **iPhone integration**: Syncs with the Garmin Connect app on iPhone, exporting data (steps, HR, sleep) to Apple Health.  Syncing occurs when the watch reconnects or the app is opened, which is less instantaneous than the Apple Watch but still reliable.  It supports basic iPhone notifications, though replies are not possible.
- **Smart features**: Limited compared to Apple Watch; no native Apple Pay, but can use [[entities/garmin-pay]] where supported by the bank.  Lacks a broad third‑party app ecosystem.
- **Target user**: Ideal for users who prioritize **sleep quality, HRV trends, and everyday activity** over hardcore training, and who need a watch that lasts a week between charges.
