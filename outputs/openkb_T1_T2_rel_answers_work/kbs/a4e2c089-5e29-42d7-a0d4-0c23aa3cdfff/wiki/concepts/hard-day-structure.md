# Hard‑Day Structure

**Definition**: A hard‑day combines a primary training focus (cardio or strength) with a complementary, low‑volume secondary component, all scheduled to allow a full 24‑hour low‑activity recovery window afterward.

## Core Elements
1. **Primary focus** – Either a cardio block (intervals or steady‑state) or a strength block (compound lifts).  
2. **Secondary accessory** – A short, low‑fatigue complement: light strength after cardio, a brief conditioning finisher after strength, or a mixed circuit on a dedicated mixed day.
3. **Volume caps** – Keep the main block intense but time‑limited (e.g., 6‑8 interval rounds, 20‑25 min steady cardio, or 3 × 6‑10 reps of major lifts). The accessory should be ~10‑15 minutes and stop well short of failure.
4. **Recovery alignment** – Each hard‑day is followed by a true low‑activity day as described in [[concepts/low-activity-cap]] and [[concepts/recovery-first-scheduling]].

## Example Layout from [[summaries/session-13_2025-05-03]]
| Day | Primary block | Secondary accessory |
|-----|---------------|----------------------|
| **Monday** | Cardio: 6‑8 × 1 min hard / 90 s easy **or** 20‑25 min steady‑state at challenging pace | Strength: 3 × 8‑10 goblet squats, 3 × 8 Romanian deadlifts, 2‑3 × 8‑12 push‑ups (stop ~1‑2 reps before failure) |
| **Wednesday** | Strength: 3 × 6‑10 squats, 3 × 6‑10 rows, 3 × 6‑10 deadlifts/hip thrusts | Optional 5‑8 min light bike or incline walk to cool down |
| **Saturday** | Mixed: 30‑40 min moderate cardio **or** 20‑min circuit blending body‑weight squats, light dumbbell presses, rowing intervals, core work | No additional strength; the circuit itself provides a balanced stimulus |

## Guiding Principles
- **Bundle stress**: Keep the majority of physiological load on the same day to prevent turning recovery days into additional stressors.
- **Avoid failure**: All secondary accessories stop short of muscular failure to preserve recovery capacity.
- **Consistency**: Apply the same structure each week; progression is handled by small increments (see [[concepts/weekly-progression]]).
