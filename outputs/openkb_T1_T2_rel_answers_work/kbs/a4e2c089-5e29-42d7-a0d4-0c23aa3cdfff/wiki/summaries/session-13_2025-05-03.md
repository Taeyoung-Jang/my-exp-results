# Summary of session‑13_2025‑05‑03

**Main contribution** – A practical weekly training framework for intermediate‑level athletes who need a full 24‑hour low‑activity recovery window after any hard effort. The plan outlines how to schedule three hard‑training days (Monday, Wednesday, Saturday) with true low‑activity days in‑between, concrete example exercises, intensity caps for recovery days, and a simple progression strategy over four weeks.

### Key concepts
- **Recovery‑first scheduling** – Each hard session is followed by a dedicated low‑activity day to prevent multi‑day fatigue. [[concepts/recovery-first-scheduling]]
- **Low‑activity cap** – 20–40 min of walking, gentle cycling, or mobility at an effort of 2‑3 / 10 (conversational). [[concepts/low-activity-cap]]
- **Hard‑day structure** – Cardio‑focused (intervals or steady‑state) + short strength block on Monday; strength‑focused on Wednesday; mixed cardio/strength circuit on Saturday. [[concepts/hard-day-structure]]
- **Recovery markers** – Sleep quality, resting heart rate, mood/energy used to decide whether to proceed, shorten, or skip a hard day. [[concepts/recovery-markers]]
- **Weekly progression** – Small weekly increments (add 1‑2 intervals, +2‑4 min cardio, +5 % load or 1‑2 reps on strength) while keeping the same recovery pattern. [[concepts/weekly-progression]]

### Weekly schedule example
| Day | Type | Example activity | Intensity / time cap |
|-----|------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Mon** | Hard (cardio + strength) | 6‑8 × 1 min hard/90 s easy intervals *or* 20‑25 min steady cardio; 3 × 8‑10 Goblet Squats, 3 × 8 RDL, 2‑3 × 8‑12 Push‑ups | Cardio: HR ≈ 80 % max; Strength: 3 sets, stop ≈ 1‑2 reps shy of failure |
| **Tue** | Low‑activity | Easy walk or mobility work | 20‑40 min, effort 2‑3 / 10 |
| **Wed** | Hard (strength) | 3 × 6‑10 Squats, 3 × 6‑10 Rows, 3 × 6‑10 Deadlifts/Hip‑Thrusts; optional 5‑8 min light bike cool‑down | Same set/rep scheme, moderate load, no failure |
| **Thu** | Low‑activity | Same as Tue | 20‑40 min, effort 2‑3 / 10 |
| **Fri** | Low‑activity | Same as Tue | 20‑40 min, effort 2‑3 / 10 |
| **Sat** | Hard (mixed) | 30‑40 min moderate steady cardio *or* 20‑min circuit: body‑weight squats, light dumbbell press, rowing intervals, core work | Cardio: moderate pace; Circuit: low load, continuous movement |
| **Sun** | Low‑activity | Same as Tue | 20‑40 min, effort 2‑3 / 10 |

### Decision rule for hard days
1. **Morning check** – Did you wake refreshed? (sleep quality, mood, resting HR).  
2. **Warm‑up feel** – Does the body feel ready, no lingering heaviness?  
If *both* are **yes**, proceed with the full hard session; if either is **no**, shorten the session or replace it with a low‑activity day.

### 4‑week progression (no extra hard days)
- **Week 1** – Baseline as above.  
- **Week 2** – Add 1‑2 interval rounds or +2‑4 min to steady cardio; increase strength weight by ~5 % or add 1 rep per set.  
- **Week 3** – Same increments; optionally extend mixed‑day circuit by 2‑3 min.  
- **Week 4** – Evaluate markers; if recovery is solid, repeat Week 3 load, otherwise maintain Week 2‑3 levels.

### Practical tips
- Keep strength work **bundled** with hard cardio days to avoid turning recovery days into stress days.  
- Use a simple **recovery‑marker log** (sleep, resting HR, mood) to inform daily decisions.  
- Treat low‑activity days as *reset* sessions; if walking feels refreshing, you’re within the correct intensity cap.
