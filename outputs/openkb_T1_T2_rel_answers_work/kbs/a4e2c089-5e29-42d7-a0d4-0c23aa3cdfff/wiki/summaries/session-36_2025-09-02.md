**Summary**

- Proposes a blended weekly plan that mixes **structured workouts** with **steady mileage** to build fitness without burnout. [[concepts/structured_workouts]] [[concepts/steady_mileage]]
- Typical layout:  
  - **Tuesday & Thursday:** hard interval sessions. [[concepts/interval_training]] [[concepts/hard-day-structure]]  
  - **Saturday:** longer hill‑focused workout. [[concepts/hill_training]]  
  - **Monday, Friday (and optionally Sunday):** easy endurance days kept truly easy (conversational effort) to add aerobic volume without interfering with quality days. [[concepts/easy_days]] [[concepts/endurance_training]] [[concepts/training_volume]]  
  - **Wednesday (or Sunday):** a full **rest day**; a second rest day can replace Sunday if Saturday’s hill work is especially taxing. [[concepts/rest_day]]
- Emphasizes monitoring **recovery markers**: feeling challenged but not wrecked, good sleep, gradual pace/HR improvements at the same effort, and stable resting heart rate. If fatigue or dread appears, swap an easy day for rest. [[concepts/recovery-markers]]
- **Deload week** strategy: noticeably cut overall volume and trim intensity, while retaining a very light interval to keep neural sharpness. [[concepts/deload_week]]
- Stresses flexibility: keep Wednesday as a true rest day unless a light spin truly helps recovery; adjust rest day placement based on how Thursday’s intervals feel. [[concepts/endurance_training]]
