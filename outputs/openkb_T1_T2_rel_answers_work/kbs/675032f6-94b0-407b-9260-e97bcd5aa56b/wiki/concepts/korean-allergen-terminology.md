# Korean Allergen Terminology

## Overview
Korean food packaging uses a set of standardized words and phrases to declare allergens and related additives. Recognizing these terms helps consumers quickly assess whether a product is safe for their dietary restrictions.

## Core Allergen Labels
- **알레르기 유발물질** – Primary heading for the allergen section.
- **함유** – Indicates the product *contains* the listed allergen.
- **포함될 수 있음**, **미량 포함될 수 있음** – Mean *may contain* or *trace amounts may be present*.
- **동일 제조시설에서 사용**, **같은 제조라인에서 생산**, **함께 취급하는 시설** – Warnings that the product shares equipment with other allergens (cross‑contamination warnings). See [[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]].

## Common Allergen Terms
| Allergen | Korean Terms | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| Wheat / Gluten | **밀**, **글루텐**, **보리**, **호밀**, **맥아** | Wheat may appear as 밀; barley (보리) and rye (호밀) often imply gluten. Malt (맥아) can hide wheat.
| Soy | **콩**, **대두**, **대두단백**, **대두레시틴** | Soy is frequently listed as 콩 or 대두. Protein and lecithin forms appear as 대두단백 and 대두레시틴. See [[entities/kong-soy]].
| Milk | **우유**, **유청**, **분유**, **탈지분유**, **버터**, **치즈**, **크림** | Milk derivatives include whey (유청) and skim milk powder (탈지분유). See [[entities/uyu-milk]].
| MSG | **L‑글루타민산나트륨**, **MSG**, **글루탐산** | Monosodium glutamate. See [[entities/msg-monosodium-glutamate]].
| Artificial Sweeteners | **아스파탐**, **아세설팜칼륨**, **수크랄로스** | Aspartame appears as 아스파탐. See [[entities/aspartame]].
| Preservatives | **소르빈산칼륨**, **아질산나트륨**, **산도조절제** | Potassium sorbate and sodium nitrite are common. See [[entities/potassium-sorbate]] and [[entities/sodium-nitrite]].
| Processed Starch | **~가공품** (e.g., **감자전분가공품**) | Indicates a processed ingredient; not inherently a contamination risk but warrants inspection. See [[entities/processed-potato-starch]].

## Hidden Allergen Sources
Some allergens are embedded in ingredient names that do not overtly mention the allergen:
- **맥아** (malt) can contain wheat gluten.
- **대두단백** and **대두레시틴** hide soy.
- **유청**, **분유**, **탈지분유** hide milk.
- Fish and shellfish may appear in sauces such as **멸치액젓** (anchovy sauce) or **새우젓** (shrimp sauce).

## Practical Scan Order for Korean Labels
1. **Allergen Section** – Locate 알레르기 유발물질 or 알레르기 정보; note 함유 vs 포함될 수 있음.
2. **Ingredient List** – Look under 원재료명 or 원재료 및 함량 for the terms above.
3. **Cross‑contamination Warnings** – Search for 동일 제조시설에서 사용, 같은 제조라인에서 생산, 함께 취급하는 시설.
4. **Manufacturer & Date** – Verify credibility of the producer.
5. **Certification Marks** – Check unfamiliar symbols.
