## Cross‑Contamination Warnings

**Definition**: Phrases on Korean food packaging that alert consumers the product was produced on equipment or in facilities that also handle other allergens, raising the risk of unintended trace exposure.

### Typical Korean Wording
- **동일 제조시설에서 사용** – "used in the same manufacturing facility"
- **같은 제조라인에서 생산** – "produced on the same production line"
- **함께 취급하는 시설** – "handled in a shared facility"
- **제조시설 공유** – "shared manufacturing equipment"

These statements often appear near the allergen declaration section (알레르기 유발물질) or at the bottom of the label under additional safety notes.

### How to Interpret
1. **Presence of any phrase** signals that the product *might* contain trace amounts of the listed allergens, even if they are not listed as ingredients.
2. **Severity** varies: a generic "same facility" warning is less specific than a direct "same line" notice, which suggests a higher likelihood of cross‑contamination.
3. **Consumer Action**: If you have a severe allergy, treat products with these warnings as potentially unsafe; otherwise, decide based on personal risk tolerance.
