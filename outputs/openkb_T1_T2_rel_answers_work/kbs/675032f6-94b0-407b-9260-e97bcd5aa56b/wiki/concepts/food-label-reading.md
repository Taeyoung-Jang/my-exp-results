# Food Label Reading

**Concept Overview**

Food label reading is the systematic approach to extracting key safety, nutritional, and authenticity information from packaged food products. It emphasizes a step‑by‑step scan of the label to assess ingredients, allergens, cross‑contamination warnings, manufacturer credibility, and certification marks.

---

## Core Scan Steps (derived from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]])

1. **Allergen Section** – Locate the allergen heading, typically **알레르기 유발물질** or **알레르기 정보**. Identify explicit “contains” statements (**함유**) and “may contain” notices (**포함될 수 있음**, **미량 포함될 수 있음**).
2. **Ingredient List** – Look for **원재료명** or **원재료 및 함량**. Pay attention to hidden allergen terms such as **밀** (wheat, [[entities/mil-wheat]]), **콩** (soy, [[entities/kong-soy]]), **우유** (milk, [[entities/uyu-milk]]), and additive names like **MSG** ( [[entities/msg-monosodium-glutamate]] ) or artificial sweeteners (**아스파탐**, [[entities/aspartame]]).
3. **Cross‑Contamination Warnings** – Scan for phrases like **동일 제조시설에서 사용**, **같은 제조라인에서 생산**, or **함께 취급하는 시설**, which are covered in [[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]].
4. **Manufacturer & Date** – Verify the producer’s name, address, website, and a professionally printed expiration or best‑by date. Consistent branding and intact seals increase trust.
5. **Certification Marks** – Identify any quality or safety symbols; verify them online if unfamiliar.

## Korean Allergen & Additive Terminology

A solid grasp of Korean terms speeds up the scan. Key vocabulary includes:
- **Wheat/Gluten:** 밀, 글루텐, 보리, 호밀, 맥아 (malt)
- **Soy:** 콩, 대두, 대두단백, 대두레시틴
- **Milk:** 우유, 유청, 탈지분유, 분유, 버터, 치즈, 크림
- **MSG:** L‑글루타민산나트륨, MSG, 글루탐산
- **Artificial Sweeteners:** 아스파탐, 아세설팜칼륨, 수크랄로스
- **Preservatives:** 소르빈산칼륨, 아질산나트륨, 산도조절제

These terms are central to [[concepts/korean-allergen-terminology]] and help uncover hidden allergens such as wheat in **맥아**, soy in **대두단백**, or milk in **유청**.

## Trustworthiness Cues

- Clear manufacturer details and contact information.
- Professional printing of dates and seals.
- Presence of recognizable certification logos.
- Consistency across packaging design.
