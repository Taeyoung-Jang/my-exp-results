# Low‑Budget Accessibility

**Definition**: Low‑budget accessibility refers to practical, cost‑effective strategies that enhance the inclusivity of announcements, signage, and activities for people of diverse ages, languages, and abilities.

## Core Principles
- **High‑contrast design**: Large text (title 36‑48 pt, details 24‑28 pt) on light backgrounds improves readability for low‑vision users.
- **Plain language**: Simple sentences, consistent phrasing, and clear pauses support screen readers and live captioning [[concepts/plain-language]].
- **Multiple modalities**: Pair written information with audio or video recordings that can be shared via phone or social media.
- **Universal symbols**: Use icons, arrows, and color cues that are recognisable without relying on language.
- **Bilingual presentation**: Place a Roman‑letters version (e.g., Spanish written in the Latin alphabet) first and larger, followed by an English version, ending with a brief plain‑English summary.

## Practical Tools Mentioned in [[summaries/session-32_2024-06-15]]
- **Phone recordings** for audio announcements.
- **Free design software** (e.g., Canva) to create high‑contrast flyers.
- **White‑board or poster paper** for quick, low‑cost signage.
- **Volunteer training** on speaking tips (steady pace, short sentences, face the mic) to improve live caption quality.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Enhances **[[concepts/inclusive-communication]]** by ensuring messages reach all community members.
- Relies on **[[concepts/plain-language]]** to make content easier for captioning and translation tools.

## Example Application
A community center such as [[entities/riverside-community-center]] can use the low‑budget accessibility checklist to:
1. Design flyers with large, high‑contrast text.
2. Create bilingual announcements with the Roman‑letter version first.
3. Record a short audio version for distribution.
4. Train volunteers using the speaking tips to produce cleaner live captions.

## Benefits
- **Cost‑effective**: Requires minimal financial investment.
- **Scalable**: Can be applied to any event or regular communication.
- **Empowering**: Encourages community feedback and continual improvement.
