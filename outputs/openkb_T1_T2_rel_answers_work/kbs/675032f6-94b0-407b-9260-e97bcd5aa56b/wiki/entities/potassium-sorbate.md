# Potassium Sorbate (소르빈산칼륨)

Potassium sorbate is a synthetic preservative used to inhibit mold, yeast, and fungi in a variety of food products. In Korean ingredient lists it appears as **소르빈산칼륨** and is often grouped under the broader category of preservatives.

## Appearance in Session 08
The term is called out in the summary of [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]] as one of the typical Korean‑language preservative names shoppers should recognize when scanning labels.

## Relevance to Label Reading
- It is part of the **preservative** vocabulary highlighted in [[concepts/korean-allergen-terminology]].
- Knowing this term helps with effective [[concepts/food-label-reading]], especially when checking the ingredient list (원재료명) for additives.
- While not an allergen, its presence may be noted in the same section where cross‑contamination warnings appear; see [[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]] for context on how such warnings are presented on Korean packaging.

## Related Entities
- Other common additives mentioned alongside potassium sorbate include [[entities/aspartame]], [[entities/msg-monosodium-glutamate]], and [[entities/sodium-nitrite]].
- It is unrelated to dairy (e.g., [[entities/uyu-milk]]) or wheat (e.g., [[entities/mil-wheat]]) allergens but may appear in processed blends such as [[entities/processed-potato-starch]].
