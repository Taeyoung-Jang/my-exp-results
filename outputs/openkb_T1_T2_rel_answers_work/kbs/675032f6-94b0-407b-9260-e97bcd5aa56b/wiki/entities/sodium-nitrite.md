# 아질산나트륨 (Sodium Nitrite)

Sodium nitrite (아질산나트륨) is a chemical preservative commonly used to inhibit bacterial growth and preserve color in processed foods, especially cured meats.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]]
- Appears in Korean ingredient lists under the name 아질산나트륨.
- Classified in the document as a **preservative** alongside potassium sorbate.
- Consumers are advised to spot this term when scanning labels for additives.
- Mentioned in the context of **[[concepts/korean-allergen-terminology]]** as part of the additive checklist.
- Relevant to overall **[[concepts/food-label-reading]]** strategies for assessing product safety.
