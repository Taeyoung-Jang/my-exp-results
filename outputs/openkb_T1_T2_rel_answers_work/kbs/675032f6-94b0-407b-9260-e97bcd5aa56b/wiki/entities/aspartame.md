## 아스파탐 (Aspartame)

**Type:** Artificial sweetener used in a variety of processed foods and beverages.

### Identification on Korean Labels
- Listed in Korean as **아스파탐**.
- Appears in ingredient lists under the artificial sweetener section alongside other additives such as 아세설팜칼륨 and 수크랄로스.
- Recognizable during label scanning by the specific Korean term, which is highlighted in the **원재료명** (ingredient list) portion of the label.
