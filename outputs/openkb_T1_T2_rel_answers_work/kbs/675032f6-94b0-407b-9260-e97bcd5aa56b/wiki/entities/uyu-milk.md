# 우유 (Milk)

우유 is a dairy product that appears on Korean food packaging under the term **우유** and also in derivative forms such as **유청** (whey), **분유** (milk powder), and **탈지분유** (skim milk powder).  It is a common allergen and is explicitly highlighted in the Korean allergen section **알레르기 유발물질**.

## Key facts from the document
- **Label appearance**: Directly as 우유, and indirectly as 유청, 분유, 탈지분유, as well as related dairy terms like 버터, 치즈, and 크림.
- **Allergen declaration**: Listed under the allergen heading with the Korean word **함유** for “contains” and may appear in “may contain” statements such as **포함될 수 있음** or **미량 포함될 수 있음**.
- **Cross‑contamination warnings**: If the product is made on shared equipment, Korean phrases like **동일 제조시설에서 사용**, **같은 제조라인에서 생산**, or **함께 취급하는 시설** may accompany the milk listing.
- **Relevance for label reading**: Milk listings are a focal point when applying the recommended **scan order** for Korean labels (allergen section → ingredient list → manufacturing‑line warnings).
