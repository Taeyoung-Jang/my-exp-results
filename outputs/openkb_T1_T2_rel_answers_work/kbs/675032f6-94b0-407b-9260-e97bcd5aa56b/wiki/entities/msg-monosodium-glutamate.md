# L‑글루타민산나트륨 (Monosodium Glutamate)

L‑글루타민산나트륨 is the Korean term used on food packaging for **monosodium glutamate (MSG)**, a flavor enhancer widely used in processed foods.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]]
- Listed among common additives to watch for on Korean labels.
- Appears under the allergen/additive section, often written as `L‑글루타민산나트륨` or simply `MSG`.
- Recognized as a potential irritant for some consumers, though it is not classified as a major allergen.
- Should be checked alongside other additives such as [[entities/aspartame]] and preservatives like [[entities/potassium-sorbate]] and [[entities/sodium-nitrite]].

## Relation to label‑reading practices
- Part of the broader practice of **[[concepts/food-label-reading]]**, especially for Korean‑language packaging.
- Falls under the **[[concepts/korean-allergen-terminology]]** guide, which helps shoppers identify additives quickly.
- Not directly tied to **[[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]]**, but its presence may be noted in manufacturing‑line warnings if the facility processes multiple allergens.

## Typical label appearance
- **Korean:** `L‑글루타민산나트륨` or `MSG`
- Often grouped with other flavor enhancers or listed in the ingredient list (`원재료명`).
