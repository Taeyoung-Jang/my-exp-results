# 감자전분가공품 (Processed Potato Starch)

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** Processed potato starch used as an ingredient in various Korean food products, identified on labels with the suffix “가공품”.

### Key Points from session‑08_2022‑06‑15
- Appears in ingredient lists under the heading 원재료명 or 원재료 및 함량.
- The term “~가공품” (e.g., 감자전분가공품) indicates a processed ingredient, not automatically a red flag for cross‑contamination.
- Cross‑contamination concerns are signaled by separate phrases such as 동일 제조시설에서 사용 or 같은 제조라인에서 생산; the processed‑potato‑starch label itself does not imply shared equipment [[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]].
- When scanning Korean labels, include processed ingredients like 감자전분가공품 in the ingredient review step of the label‑reading workflow [[concepts/food-label-reading]].
- Recognizing that “가공품” denotes a processed component helps avoid unnecessary alarm while still checking for hidden allergens [[concepts/korean-allergen-terminology]].
