# 콩 (Soy)

**Korean term:** 콩 (pronounced *kong*)

Soy is a major source of protein and a frequent allergen. In Korean food labeling, it appears under several names such as 콩, 대두, 대두단백 (soy protein), and 대두레시틴 (soy lecithin). The entity is highlighted in the **allergen declaration** section of Korean packages and may be listed alongside hidden forms.

## Key Points from [[summaries/session-08_2022-06-15]]
- Appears in the allergen section **알레르기 유발물질** as a direct ingredient (콩) or within derived terms.
- Recognized as an allergen requiring clear labeling; phrases like "함유" indicate presence, while "포함될 수 있음" signals possible cross‑contamination.
- Hidden behind ingredient names such as 대두단백 and 대두레시틴, which still count as soy for allergen purposes.
