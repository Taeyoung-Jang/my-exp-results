## Overview
밀 (pronounced *mil*) is the Korean term for wheat, a widely used cereal grain that can contain gluten and is a frequent allergen.

## Appearance in session-08_2022-06-15
In the June 15 2022 conversation the assistant lists 밀 as a key term to watch on Korean food labels under [[concepts/korean-allergen-terminology]]. The document advises checking the ingredient list (원재료명) for 밀 or its derivatives such as 맥아 (malt) and barley/rye mentions (보리, 호밀) that may also introduce gluten.

## Guidance for label reading
When scanning Korean labels, first locate the allergen section (알레르기 유발물질) and look for the term 밀 to determine if wheat is present. Combine this step with the broader [[concepts/food-label-reading]] workflow and be aware of cross‑contamination notes described in [[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]].
