# Riverside Community Center

**Type:** Place

Riverside Community Center is a local gathering venue that hosts a variety of community events such as free dinners, workshops, and cultural activities. The center is highlighted in [[summaries/session-32_2024-06-15]] as a case study for applying **inclusive communication** and **low‑budget accessibility** strategies.

## Key Facts from Session 32 (2024‑06‑15)
- Provides space for events like the **Community Dinner** (see [[entities/community-dinner]]).
- Uses high‑contrast, large‑text flyers and bilingual (Roman‑letter) announcements to reach diverse audiences.
- Encourages plain‑language scripts and volunteer speaking tips to improve live captions and automatic translation.
- Emphasizes simple, reusable templates for flyers, signage, and spoken announcements.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/inclusive-communication]] – applying clear visuals, simple language, and universal icons.
- [[concepts/low-budget-accessibility]] – leveraging existing tools (phone recordings, free design software) for accessibility without high costs.
- [[concepts/plain-language]] – using short sentences, consistent phrasing, and pauses to aid captions and translations.
