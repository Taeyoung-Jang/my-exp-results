# Summary of session-08_2022-06-15

## Core Advice
- When evaluating unfamiliar international food products, start with the **ingredient list** (원재료명), noting length and presence of recognizable items.
- Check the **allergen declaration** (알레르기 유발물질) for clear statements of what the product *contains* (함유) or may *contain* (포함될 수 있음, 미량 포함될 수 있음).
- Look for **manufacturer information** (name, address, website) and professional‑looking expiration dates to gauge trustworthiness.
- Scan for **certification marks** and verify them online if unknown.

These steps embody the principles of [[concepts/food-label-reading]].

## Korean‑Specific Allergen & Additive Terms
- **Wheat / Gluten:** 밀, 글루텐, 보리, 호밀, 맥아 (malt) – see [[entities/mil-wheat]] for wheat.
- **Soy:** 콩, 대두, 대두단백, 대두레시틴 – see [[entities/kong-soy]] for soy.
- **Milk:** 우유, 유청, 탈지분유, 분유, 버터, 치즈, 크림 – see [[entities/uyu-milk]] for milk.
- **MSG:** L‑글루타민산나트륨, MSG, 글루탐산 – see [[entities/msg-monosodium-glutamate]].
- **Artificial Sweeteners:** 아스파탐 (see [[entities/aspartame]]), 아세설팜칼륨, 수크랄로스.
- **Preservatives:** 소르빈산칼륨 (see [[entities/potassium-sorbate]]), 아질산나트륨 (see [[entities/sodium-nitrite]]), 산도조절제.

## Hidden Allergen Sources
- **Wheat** can appear as 맥아 (malt) or barley/rye terms (보리, 호밀).
- **Soy** may be listed as 대두단백 (soy protein) or 대두레시틴 (soy lecithin).
- **Milk** hides in 유청 (whey), 분유 (milk powder), 탈지분유 (skim milk powder).
- **Fish / Shellfish** often appear in sauces such as 멸치액젓 (anchovy sauce) or 새우젓 (shrimp sauce).

## Cross‑Contamination Warnings
- Phrases indicating shared equipment: 
  - 동일 제조시설에서 사용
  - 같은 제조라인에서 생산
  - 함께 취급하는 시설  

These reflect the ideas covered in [[concepts/cross-contamination-warnings]].

- The suffix **~가공품** (e.g., 감자전분가공품 – see [[entities/processed-potato-starch]]) simply denotes a processed ingredient; it is not itself a red flag unless accompanied by the warnings above.

## Recommended "Scan Order" for Korean Labels
1. **Allergen Section** – Locate 알레르기 유발물질 or 알레르기 정보. Identify *contains* (함유) vs *may contain* (포함될 수 있음, 미량 포함될 수 있음).
2. **Ingredient List** – Look for 원재료명 or 원재료 및 함량. Scan for hidden allergen terms listed above.
3. **Manufacturing‑Line Warnings** – Search for 동일 제조시설에서 사용, 같은 제조라인에서 생산, 함께 취급하는 시설.
4. **Manufacturer & Date** – Verify name, address, website, and expiration/best‑by dates.
5. **Certification Marks** – Note any unfamiliar symbols and check their legitimacy.

Following this flow—official allergen section, ingredient list, shared‑line warnings, manufacturer info, and certification marks—provides a reliable shortcut for Korean food label scanning.
