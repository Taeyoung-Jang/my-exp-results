# Summary of session-31_2025-03-13

**Document type:** Personal planning guide

## Core ideas
- **Time‑blocking**: A precise daily timetable (4:45 am – 9:00 pm) aligning early‑morning training, work, commute, and evening wind‑down. Includes explicit buffer periods and themed afternoon slots (chore, social, admin, errand). [[concepts/time-blocking]]
- **Themed afternoons**: Each weekday is assigned a single focus to reduce decision‑fatigue and prevent after‑work overload. Concepts: [[concepts/theme-based-scheduling]], low‑effort zones (plain text).
- **Commute integration**: Tue/Thu schedule incorporates a 35‑45 min drive, office‑side shower/change, and realistic meal timing.
- **Meal‑prep templates**: Three portable systems designed for minimal cooking time and variety.
  1. **Roasted veg + rotating protein** – base roasting step plus 4 sauce/spice variations. [[concepts/meal-prep-basics]]
  2. **Grain base with sauce rotations** – bulk‑cook rice/quinoa/farro, then switch among lemon‑tahini, soy‑ginger, pesto, tomato‑garlic. (plain text for grain bowls)
  3. **Breakfast‑focused build‑ahead** – overnight oats/chia pudding or baked oatmeal with both sweet (berries, peanut butter, cacao) and savory (egg‑spinach, avocado, feta) options. (plain text for breakfast automation)
- **Automation & habit loop**: By pre‑defining both schedule blocks and meal components, the plan creates cue‑routine‑reward loops that support consistency without nightly “productivity” pressure. [[concepts/habit-formation]]

## Outcomes
- Provides a concrete, repeatable weekly structure that respects the user’s non‑negotiables (9 pm bedtime, no night‑time work). 
- Supplies diverse, low‑effort meals to keep mornings automatic and evenings simple. 
- Offers a modular grocery list per template for quick weekly shopping.
