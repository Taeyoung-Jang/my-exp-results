# Community Health Indicators

## Definition
Community Health Indicators are observable metrics and signs that reveal how vibrant, welcoming, and sustainable an online retro gaming community is.

## Core Indicators (from the session‑13 discussion)
- **Newcomer treatment** – patient, detailed replies rather than sarcasm or gate‑keeping.
- **Moderation style** – quick spam removal and light‑handed intervention; moderators support the culture without dominating it.
- **Thread quality** – multi‑post discussions that build on each other, share personal anecdotes, and avoid one‑liners.
- **Conversation depth** – hobby‑first focus with substantive exchanges, giving the feel of an ongoing hobby club.
- **Overall vibe** – members act like a friendly regulars club, greeting newcomers and keeping tone positive.

## How to Assess a Community
1. Scan a recent week’s posts for the above signs.
2. Observe how newcomers are welcomed and whether moderators are proactive yet unobtrusive.
3. Look for threads where participants expand on each other's contributions rather than posting isolated comments.
4. Note the presence of hobby‑driven, low‑stakes discussions that encourage participation.
