# Meal‑Prep Basics

**Definition**

Meal‑Prep Basics are the fundamental techniques for planning, cooking, and storing food in bulk so that daily meals become low‑effort, nutritionally balanced, and varied without repeated decision‑making.

**Key Elements from [[summaries/session-31_2025-03-13]]**

- **Base preparation** – Cook a single versatile component (e.g., roasted vegetables, a grain batch, or an overnight‑oats breakfast) that serves as the foundation for multiple meals.
- **Variation modules** – Apply different sauces, spices, or protein choices to the base each night, creating distinct dishes while reusing the same core ingredients.
- **Compact grocery lists** – Limit shopping to a few categories (vegetables, proteins, pantry staples, sauces) to reduce cost and decision fatigue.
- **Time‑blocking integration** – Align meal‑prep sessions with dedicated weekend or evening blocks, fitting neatly into the user’s [[concepts/time-blocking]] schedule.

**Typical Workflow**

1. **Plan a weekly menu** focusing on 2–3 base components.
2. **Batch‑cook the bases** (roast a tray of veg, cook a pot of rice/quinoa, prepare overnight oats) on a chosen prep day.
3. **Create variation kits** – small containers of sauces, spices, or protein options ready to mix with the bases.
4. **Store responsibly** in portioned containers to maintain freshness and simplify grab‑and‑go meals.

**Benefits**

- Reduces daily cooking time to under 10 minutes.
- Supports consistent nutrition and weight‑management goals.
- Reinforces habit loops by pairing a cue (opening the fridge) with a quick routine (adding sauce) and a rewarding meal, linking to [[concepts/habit-formation]].
- Allows thematic afternoon planning (e.g., “meal‑prep day”) as part of [[concepts/theme-based-scheduling]].
