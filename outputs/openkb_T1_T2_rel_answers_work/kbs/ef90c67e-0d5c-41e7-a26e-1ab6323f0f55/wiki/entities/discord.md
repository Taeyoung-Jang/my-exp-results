# Discord

Discord is a voice, video, and text chat platform that many retro gaming enthusiasts use to organize discussions, share mods, and coordinate community events.

## Role in Retro Gaming Communities
In the conversation from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-12]], Discord is mentioned as one of the key places to look for active hobby‑first spaces. Users are advised to follow links from subreddits or forums to Discord servers where smaller, more personal interactions can happen.

## Related Concepts
- Community health indicators are often assessed by observing how newcomers are treated and how moderation operates within Discord servers. See [[concepts/community-health-indicators]].
- Strategies for defusing gatekeeping behavior can be applied in Discord chat channels, aligning with [[concepts/gatekeeping-mitigation]].
- Participation‑inviting thread ideas can be adapted as channel topics or discussion prompts on Discord, linking to [[concepts/participation-inviting-threads]].

## Key Facts
- Primarily a product (software service) used for real‑time communication.
- Supports text, voice, and video, facilitating both synchronous and asynchronous conversations.
- Often linked from subreddits and legacy forums as a hub for more dynamic community interaction.
