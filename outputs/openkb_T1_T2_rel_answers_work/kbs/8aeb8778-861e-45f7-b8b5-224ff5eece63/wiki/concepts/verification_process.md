# Verification Process

**Definition**: A structured procedure used by a condo board or property manager to confirm that a submission meets required standards before acceptance. It typically specifies *who* may act as a verifier, *what form* verification must take, and any *timelines* for providing that confirmation.

## Core Elements
1. **Verifier Identity** – The board defines which individuals or roles are authorized to validate a submission (e.g., a board officer, a designated manager, or an external professional).  
2. **Verification Format** – Acceptable evidence may include signed statements, official letters, email confirmations, or documented approvals. The required format is usually spelled out in the board’s policies.
3. **Deadlines** – Clear timing expectations (e.g., “by Friday”) help ensure the process stays on schedule and prevents delays in decision‑making.

## Application in Session‑31 (2023‑07‑24)
In the email drafts and advice provided for the condo board interaction, the user was instructed to:
- Request clarification on *who* counts as a verifier, the *format* for verification, and any *deadlines*.
- Phrase the request in a neutral, process‑oriented tone to avoid sounding confrontational.
- Use follow‑up and escalation emails that reinforce the procedural nature of the ask.

These steps embody the **Verification Process** concept by explicitly seeking the concrete details needed to comply with board expectations.
