# Event Planning Checklist

A **Event Planning Checklist** is a structured list of tasks that guides community organizers through the essential steps of planning a local event, ensuring nothing critical is overlooked. It is especially useful for informal, neighborhood‑scale gatherings such as block parties.

## Core Components (as used in the block party example)

- **Permits & Insurance** – Verify local requirements and submit any street‑closure permits at least six weeks before the event.
- **Budget Decision** – Choose a budget level (low, medium, high) and allocate funds across permits, food/drinks, rentals, kids’ supplies, and misc. See [[concepts/community_budgeting]] for budgeting patterns.
- **Food Planning** – Decide between potluck, grill, or food‑truck options and set up a sign‑up grid for dishes, drinks, and ice.
- **Music Arrangement** – Select a simple Bluetooth playlist or a neighbor DJ; keep volume within community guidelines.
- **Kids’ Activities** – Provide low‑maintenance supplies (chalk, bubbles, yard games) to keep children engaged.
- **Volunteer Roles** – Assign responsibilities such as setup, cleanup, music coordination, kids’ activity helper, and street/safety point‑person.
- **House Rules** – Include quiet‑hour limits, trash disposal instructions, pet policies, and alcohol guidelines.
- **Contact Information** – Provide a clear point of contact for day‑of decisions.

## Timeline Integration

These checklist items are typically sequenced according to a **timeline** that moves from six weeks out (permit application) to the day of the event (setup and safety). For a visual schedule, refer to [[concepts/timeline_planning]].

## Example Usage

The checklist was derived from the block‑party planning sheet in [[summaries/session-10_2021-07-24]], illustrating how each item maps to a concrete action:

1. **Apply for permits** – "Apply for required street‑closure permit or insurance."
2. **Set budget level** – "Set budget level (low, medium, or high)."
3. **Decide food plan** – "Decide food plan (potluck / grill / food truck)."
4. **Confirm music plan** – "Confirm music plan (playlist / neighbor DJ)."
5. **Organize kids’ supplies** – "Organize kids’ supplies (chalk, bubbles, simple games)."
6. **Distribute announcements** – "Send save‑the‑date notice to neighbors."
7. **Collect sign‑ups** – "Share potluck and volunteer sign‑up."
8. **Purchase supplies** – "Buy remaining supplies (ice, plates, cups, trash bags, tape, extension cord)."
9. **Send reminders** – "Send reminder 2–3 days before the event."
10. **Day‑of setup** – "Set up street and safety signs day‑of."

By following this **Event Planning Checklist**, community members can coordinate efficiently, maintain clarity, and create a welcoming, well‑organized gathering.
