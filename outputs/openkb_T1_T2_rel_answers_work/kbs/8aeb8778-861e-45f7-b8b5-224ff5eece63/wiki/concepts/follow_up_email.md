# Follow‑Up Email

**Definition**: A short, courteous message sent after an initial outreach that received no reply or an ambiguous answer, aimed at obtaining clarification or a timely response without escalating tension.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-31_2023-07-24]])
- **Purpose** – reaffirm the original request, remind the recipient, and keep the interaction professional.
- **Tone** – neutral, process‑oriented; avoid sounding confrontational or demanding.
- **Structure**
  1. **Brief greeting** and reference to the earlier email.
  2. **Clear reminder** of the information needed (e.g., verification details).
  3. **Optional deadline** phrased politely (e.g., “If possible, a response by Friday would help me stay on track”).
  4. **Polite close** thanking the recipient for their time.
- **Escalation** – when a second follow‑up is required, CC a higher‑level party (e.g., board president) and explicitly state that the inclusion is for transparency, not to bypass authority.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

## Practical Templates (derived from the source)
### Simple reminder (no response)
```
Hi [Name],
I wanted to check in on my note from earlier about the verification requirements. Whenever you have a moment, could you clarify what’s needed so I can ensure my next steps are correct?
Thanks again,
[Your Name]
```
### Neutral escalation (include higher‑level official)
```
Hi [Name],
I’m following up regarding the verification details I asked about previously. I’m including [Board President] so we’re all working from the same information and to be sure I’m following the process as the board intends. When you have a chance, could you clarify who may verify, what form that verification should take, and any timing expectations? If possible, it would be helpful to have guidance by Friday so I can stay on track with the board’s requirements.
Thank you,
[Your Name]
```

These templates illustrate the typical length (2‑3 short paragraphs), the use of a polite deadline, and the inclusion of a CC for transparency.
