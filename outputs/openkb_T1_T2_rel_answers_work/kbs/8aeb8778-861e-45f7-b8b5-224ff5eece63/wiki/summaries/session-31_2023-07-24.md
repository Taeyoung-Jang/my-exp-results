# Summary of session-31_2023-07-24

**Date:** 2023‑07‑24 18:30  

## Core Contribution
Provided a complete set of email templates and strategic advice for requesting clarification on a condo board’s “verification” requirement, covering initial outreach, tone variations, follow‑up, and escalation while maintaining a neutral, process‑oriented tone.

## Email Drafts
- **Base email** – concise, calm request for verification details.  
- **Alternative versions** –  
  - Formal tone (subject: *Clarification on Verification Requirements*) – linked to [[concepts/email_tone]].  
  - Direct tone (subject: *Question About Verification Process*) – linked to [[concepts/email_tone]] and [[concepts/verification_process]].  
  - Friendly tone (subject: *Following Up on Next Steps*) – linked to [[concepts/email_tone]].  
- **Follow‑up emails** –  
  1. Simple reminder if no response – linked to [[concepts/follow_up_email]].  
  2. Neutral, process‑oriented escalation that CC’s the board president and includes a polite deadline request (e.g., “by Friday”).

## Strategic Advice
- **CC decision** – Recommend starting with the property manager alone; CC the board president only if needed for transparency.  
- **Tone management** – Adjust formality to match the relationship while keeping the message firm but polite.  
- **Process framing** – Emphasize alignment with board procedures to avoid sounding confrontational.  
- **Deadline phrasing** – Use “If possible, it would be helpful to have guidance by Friday” to set expectations without pressure.
