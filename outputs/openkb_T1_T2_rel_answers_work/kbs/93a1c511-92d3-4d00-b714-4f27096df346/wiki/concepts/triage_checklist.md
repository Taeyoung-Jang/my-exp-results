# Thread Triage Checklist

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A concise set of yes/no questions that guides moderators in deciding whether to place a new forum thread into an existing category, flag it for possible new‑category creation, or merge/redirect it to an existing discussion.

---

## Origin
This checklist originates from the conversation in [[summaries/session-14_2023-06-15]], where the assistant proposed a lightweight workflow for handling the growing volume of posts in online forums.

---

## Core Questions
The checklist proceeds linearly through a series of decision points:

1. **Fit with Existing Category** – Does the thread clearly belong to one of the current high‑level categories without strain?
2. **Duplicate Check** – Is the thread essentially a repeat of an existing discussion? If yes, merge or redirect.
3. **Tag Sufficiency** – Can the topic be fully captured using tags once placed in the chosen category?
4. **Hesitation Pattern** – Does the moderator feel genuine hesitation about where it belongs, beyond a momentary pause?
5. **Repeated Friction** – Have similar hesitation moments occurred with other recent threads?
6. **Stable Grouping** – Does the grouping of these hesitant threads feel stable (i.e., a recurring theme) rather than situational?
7. **Navigation Benefit** – Would creating a new category help future readers navigate, beyond solving the moderator’s immediate uncertainty?

If the answer to questions 1‑3 is *yes*, the thread is filed in the existing category with appropriate tags. If questions 4‑7 trigger repeatedly (typically two‑three similar threads within a short span), the moderator flags the pattern for review as a potential new category.

---

## Application Guidelines
- **Speed:** The checklist is designed to be run in a few seconds per thread, keeping the moderation workload low.
- **Threshold for New Categories:** As discussed in the source, a new category is warranted only after observing a steady trickle of related threads (e.g., a few per week over several weeks) rather than isolated spikes.
- **Integration with Other Concepts:** This checklist works alongside [[concepts/category_proliferation]] to prevent uncontrolled growth of categories, and it fits within the broader [[concepts/forum_structure]] that balances stable categories with flexible tagging.

---

## Benefits
- **Consistency:** Provides a repeatable process, reducing subjective decisions.
- **Scalability:** Helps forums grow without overwhelming moderators.
- **Proactive Category Management:** Flags emerging topics before they become fragmented across tags.
