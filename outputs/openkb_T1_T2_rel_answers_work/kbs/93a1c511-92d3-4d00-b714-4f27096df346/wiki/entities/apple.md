# Apple (organization)

Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and online services. Its iOS operating system powers the iPhone and includes the **Screen Time** suite of digital‑wellness tools.

## Relevance to [[summaries/session-34_2024-06-15]]
The conversation about setting “gentle defaults” and creating “fractional friction” for healthier online habits relies on iOS features that Apple provides:
- **Screen Time** – an iOS feature that lets users set App Limits and Downtime to restrict social‑media use on weekdays while keeping weekends open. [[entities/screen_time]]
- **iPhone** – the device on which these settings are configured. [[entities/iphone]]

By using Apple’s built‑in controls, users can implement:
- [[concepts/gentle_defaults]]: default schedules (e.g., weekday Downtime) that require minimal ongoing willpower.
- [[concepts/fractional_friction]]: small barriers such as timers, passcode protection, and hidden app placement that prompt a moment of reflection before accessing an app.
- [[concepts/offline_anchors]]: designated phone‑free periods (meals, evenings) that act as offline anchors for digital balance.

## Key Points from the Document
- Apple’s **Screen Time** allows custom daily limits per app and day‑of‑week customization.
- Users can set a Screen Time passcode to prevent easy overrides, adding an extra layer of security.
- Disabling notifications and moving apps off the home screen are recommended strategies that leverage iOS UI customization.

These capabilities illustrate how Apple’s ecosystem supports practical implementations of digital‑wellness concepts discussed in the session.
