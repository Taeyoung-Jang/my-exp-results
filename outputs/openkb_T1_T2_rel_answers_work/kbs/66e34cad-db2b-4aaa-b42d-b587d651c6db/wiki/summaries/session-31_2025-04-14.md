# Summary of session‑31 (2025‑04‑14)

## Overview
The document provides a **compact, low‑energy first‑week plan** for turning a bare studio near a hospital into a warm, personal space. It focuses on **tiny daily anchors** (3–5 min on weekdays, up to 10 min on weekends), a **budget‑friendly purchase list** under $50, and **no‑cost entry‑zone tricks** using items you already have. A quick **mail‑handling script** helps prevent clutter.

## Day‑by‑Day Low‑Energy Routine (Mon–Sun)
| Day | Action (≤ 5 min weekdays, ≤ 10 min weekend) |
|-----|--------------------------------------------|
| **Monday** | Turn on the [[concepts/welcome-home-ritual]] lamp, clear the spot where you drop your bag. |
| **Tuesday** | Smooth one nearby surface (bedside table or kitchenette) — push items into a tidy line, wipe once if possible. |
| **Wednesday** | Place a personal object (photo, postcard, small memento) near the entry. |
| **Thursday** | Return one item to its proper spot (keys, charger, shoes). |
| **Friday** | Two‑minute reset: toss trash, straighten the throw blanket, set keys / water bottle in the designated spot. |
| **Saturday** | Up to 10 min: introduce a purchased warmth item (lamp, plant, blanket) and arrange it. |
| **Sunday** | Ten‑minute weekly reset: air out, quick sweep, handle dishes, play background music. |

## Sample Budget (< $50) – Where to Find Each Item
- **Thrift‑store lamp** – $15 (local [[entities/goodwill]] or similar shop)  
- **Warm‑tone bulb** – $5 ([[entities/dollar-tree]] or other dollar store)  
- **Cozy throw blanket** – $12 (discount retailer such as [[entities/tj-maxx]] or [[entities/ross-stores]])  
- **Low‑maintenance plant** – $8 (either a [[entities/pothos-plant]] or a [[entities/snake-plant]] from a grocery or home‑center)  
- **Small candle or plug‑in diffuser** – $4 ([[entities/dollar-tree]] or similar)

Total ≈ **$44**.

## No‑Cost Entry‑Zone Tricks
- Use painter’s or washi tape to mark an “entry square” on the floor, creating a visual boundary.  
- Repurpose a shallow box lid or tray as a mail/key holder; place it on the entry surface.  
- Reposition an existing lamp or chair so it faces inward, signaling a distinct zone without adding furniture.  
- Install a removable adhesive hook for coats or bags, turning a wall spot into a functional entry anchor.

These ideas embody the [[concepts/entry-zone]] principle using only what you already own.

## Mail‑Management Script (5‑second scan)
1. **Drop all incoming mail** into the designated entry tray/box lid.  
2. **Quick scan**: toss obvious junk (ads, flyers) immediately.  
3. **Leave the rest** in the tray.  
4. **Once per week**, take the tray, sort the mail, and clear it back to empty.
