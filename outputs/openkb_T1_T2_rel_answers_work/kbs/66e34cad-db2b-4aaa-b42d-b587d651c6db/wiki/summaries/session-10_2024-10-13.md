# Summary of session-10_2024-10-13

**Main contribution**: A practical framework for designing a predictable weekday‑evening routine that fits into a 45–60 minute window after the kids are asleep, with concrete schedules and concise reset checklists tailored to common pain points.

## Core Framework
- **Anchor → Reset → Unwind → Close** phases (the anchor reset unwind close sequence) provide a repeatable order regardless of clock time.
- The *anchor* shifts to the moment the household quiets down (e.g., kids in bed).
- Consistency of order, not exact timing, drives habit formation.

## Adapted Schedule (45–60 min)
1. **Anchor** – kids asleep (≈8:30 pm).
2. **Reset** – 15 min (or 5 min bare‑minimum) focusing on high‑impact tasks.
3. **Unwind** – short leisure (reading, show, tea) until ~9:15 pm.
4. **Close** – wash up, change, note next‑day items, lights out by ~9:30 pm.

### Bare‑minimum version
- Compress reset to 5 min, keep unwind as pure wind‑down, then close.

## Reset Checklists
### 15‑minute reset (3–5 steps)
1. **Clear kitchen** – wipe counters, load/rinse easy dishes.  
2. **Gather school items** – place folders, bottles, jackets in a dedicated spot.  
3. **Tidy living room** – return toys/blankets to their places.  
4. **Set out tomorrow’s essentials** – clothes, bag, lunch items.  
5. *(Optional)* Quick counter wipe or floor sweep if time permits.

### 5‑minute bare‑minimum reset (single high‑payoff step)
1. **Collect kids’ school stuff** into a ready‑to‑grab spot (the biggest ROI).  
2. *(If time allows)* Rinse the most bothersome dish(s).

## Handling Incomplete Tasks
- Treat leftover dishes or toys as *planned for tomorrow*, not a failure.  
- Reinforce the routine’s stop‑point to avoid over‑extension.
