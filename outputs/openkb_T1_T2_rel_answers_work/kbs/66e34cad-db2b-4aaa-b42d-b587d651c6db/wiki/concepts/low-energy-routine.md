# Low‑Energy Routine

## Definition
A **low‑energy routine** is a set of very short, repeatable actions performed each evening (typically 3–5 minutes on weekdays and up to 10 minutes on weekends) that help a new or small living space feel welcoming, organized, and personalized without requiring significant physical or mental effort.

## Core Principles
- **Time‑boxed**: Keep each activity under five minutes on work nights; allow a slightly longer window on weekends.
- **Consistency**: Perform the same cue‑based actions daily to build habit and signal “home” to your brain.
- **Minimal resources**: Uses items you already own or inexpensive buys (e.g., a lamp from [[entities/goodwill]], a bulb from a dollar store, a blanket from [[entities/tj-maxx]] or [[entities/ross-stores]]). 
- **Zone creation**: Small actions reinforce an entry‑zone and other micro‑zones without adding furniture, linking to [[concepts/entry-zone]].

## Example Week (adapted from [[summaries/session-31_2025-04-14]])
| Day | Action (≤ 5 min weekdays, ≤ 10 min weekend) |
|-----|--------------------------------------------|
| **Monday** | Turn on the “welcome home” lamp, clear the spot where you drop your bag. |
| **Tuesday** | Smooth one nearby surface (push items into a line, wipe once if possible). |
| **Wednesday** | Place a personal object (photo, postcard, small memento) near the entry. |
| **Thursday** | Return one item to its proper spot (keys, charger, shoes). |
| **Friday** | Two‑minute reset: toss trash, straighten the throw blanket, set keys / water bottle in the designated spot. |
| **Saturday** | Up to 10 min: introduce a purchased warmth item (lamp, plant, blanket) and arrange it. |
| **Sunday** | Ten‑minute weekly reset: air out, quick sweep, handle dishes, play background music. |

## Supporting Strategies
- **Welcome‑home ritual**: The first step each night—turning on a warm lamp and playing a calming playlist—connects to [[concepts/welcome-home-ritual]].
- **Entry‑zone cues**: Using a small rug, tape marker, or repurposed box lid for keys and mail defines a visual boundary, reinforcing the sense of an entry zone.
- **Mail‑management script**: A five‑second scan of incoming mail prevents buildup and keeps the space tidy.

## Practical Purchases (under $50)
- Thrift‑store lamp – ~$15 from [[entities/goodwill]]
- Warm‑tone bulb – ~$5 from a dollar store
- Throw blanket – ~$12 from [[entities/tj-maxx]] or [[entities/ross-stores]]
- Low‑maintenance plant – ~$8 (pothos from [[entities/pothos-plant]] or snake plant from [[entities/snake-plant]])
- Small candle or diffuser – ~$4 from a dollar store

These items support the routine while staying budget‑friendly.
