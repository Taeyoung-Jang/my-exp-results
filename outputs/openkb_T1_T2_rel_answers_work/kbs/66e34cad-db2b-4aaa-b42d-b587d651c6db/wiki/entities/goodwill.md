## Goodwill (organization)

Goodwill Industries International is a nonprofit organization that operates a network of thrift stores across the United States and other countries.  Stores sell donated, second‑hand items—clothing, furniture, home décor, and more—often at low prices, with proceeds supporting job training and community programs.

### Mention in session‑31 (2025‑04‑14)
In the **first‑week home‑comfort plan** for a studio apartment, Goodwill is suggested as a source for a **budget lamp** (approximately $15).  The recommendation highlights Goodwill’s role as an affordable place to acquire décor items that help create a welcoming home environment without exceeding a $50 total budget.
