# Snake Plant

The **Snake Plant** (also known as *Sansevieria* or *Mother‑in‑Law’s Tongue*) is highlighted in [[summaries/session-31_2025-04-14]] as a budget‑friendly, low‑maintenance plant to add warmth to a small studio.

## Key facts from the session
- **Purpose**: Serves as a cheap decor item that brings a touch of greenery and improves indoor air quality.
- **Cost**: Recommended budget is around **$8**, making it easy to include in the under‑$50 first‑week purchase list.
- **Where to find**: Can be sourced from local grocery stores, garden sections of hardware stores, or discount retailers.
- **Placement**: Works well on a windowsill, nightstand, or a small shelf to create a subtle visual anchor in the entry zone.
- **Care**: Tolerates low light and irregular watering, ideal for busy schedules and long hospital shifts.
