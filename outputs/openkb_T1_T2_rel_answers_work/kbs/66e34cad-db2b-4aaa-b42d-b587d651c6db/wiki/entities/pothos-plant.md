# Pothos Plant

**Type:** product

The pothos plant (Epipremnum aureum) is a hardy, trailing vine that thrives in low‑light indoor environments and requires minimal watering, making it ideal for small studios and busy schedules.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2025-04-14]]
- Recommended as a **cheap décor item** for creating warmth in a new studio.
- Suggested budget: **≈ $8** as part of a sub‑$50 starter kit.
- Provides a touch of greenery that contributes to a feeling of home without demanding much time or effort, fitting the [[concepts/low-energy-routine]] approach.
- Can be placed near the entry zone to help define a welcoming area, supporting the [[concepts/entry-zone]] concept.

## Practical Tips
- Position the plant where it receives indirect light, such as near a window or beside a lamp.
- Water sparingly – once the soil feels dry to the touch.
- Pair with other low‑cost items (lamp, throw, candle) to create a cohesive, cozy aesthetic.
