# Dollar Tree

Dollar Tree is a U.S. discount store chain where most items are priced at $1 (or a few dollars). It is frequently mentioned as a source for inexpensive home‑decor and routine‑support items.

## Key facts from **[[summaries/session-31_2025-04-14]]**
- Suggested as a place to buy a **warm‑tone bulb** (≈ $5) for the welcome‑home lamp.
- Recommended for **small candles** or plug‑in diffusers (≈ $3‑$5) that can be used in a **[[concepts/welcome-home-ritual]]**.
- Listed among the cheap‑decor options that help create an **[[concepts/entry-zone]]** without spending much.
- Provides budget‑friendly supplies that fit within the **under‑$50** first‑week plan, supporting a **[[concepts/low-energy-routine]]** for a busy hospital worker.

## Typical items useful for the plan
- Light bulbs (soft, warm tone)
- Small scented candles or oil diffusers
- Basic decorative trays or small storage containers

These items are low‑cost, readily available at Dollar Tree, and help establish daily anchors without adding financial strain.
