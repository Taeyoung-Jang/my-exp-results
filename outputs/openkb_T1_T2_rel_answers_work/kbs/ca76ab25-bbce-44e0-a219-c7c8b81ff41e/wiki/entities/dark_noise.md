# Dark Noise

Dark Noise is a **product** – an ambient sound application that lets users blend and fine‑tune a variety of background noises. It offers low‑volume, steady soundscapes such as fan hum, light rain, and brown noise, with EQ‑style controls that help the audio stay unobtrusive over long periods.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-05_2025-01-23]]
- Recommended as a top choice for **background sound masking** in a quiet home office.
- Allows precise tone adjustments, avoiding sudden shifts or noticeable loops.
- Provides mixes that are “invisible” yet effective at masking sharp sounds like a ticking clock, making it suitable for [[concepts/background_sound_masking]].
- Supports brown‑noise and fan‑type sounds, which are noted as preferable for masking high‑frequency ticks, tying into the discussion on [[concepts/white_noise_vs_brown_noise]].
