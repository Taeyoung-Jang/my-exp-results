# Endel

Endel is a **product** – a generative music and ambient sound app that crafts personalized soundscapes based on user preferences, time of day, and environment.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-05_2025-01-23]]
- Recommended as one of the top choices for **background sound masking** in a home office setting.
- Noted for its ability to **fine‑tune EQ** and avoid sudden loops, making it suitable for long‑duration, low‑distraction use.
- Compared alongside other apps like Dark Noise and Noisli for providing steady, broadband sounds such as soft fan, light rain, or subtle brown noise.

## Related concepts
- [[concepts/background_sound_masking]] – Endel helps mask sharp noises like a ticking clock.
- [[concepts/white_noise_vs_brown_noise]] – The app offers both white‑ and brown‑noise mixes, allowing users to choose the spectrum that best masks specific sounds.
- [[concepts/acoustic_dampening]] – By adding ambient sound, Endel contributes to the overall acoustic comfort of a space.
