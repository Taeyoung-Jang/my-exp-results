# Noisli

Noisli is a mobile and web application that lets users create and blend ambient soundscapes such as gentle wind, soft rain, or a subdued café murmur. It offers fine‑grained volume and tone controls, allowing listeners to tailor the audio to mask background distractions without becoming noticeable.

**Key facts from session 05 2024 01 23**
- Recommended as a low‑loop, EQ‑adjustable option for [[concepts/background_sound_masking]].
- The "gentle wind" and "subdued café" mixes are cited as the most "invisible" choices for long‑duration use.
- Positioned alongside other sound‑mixing products like [[entities/dark_noise]] and [[entities/endel]].

**Related concepts**
- [[concepts/acoustic_dampening]] – Noisli contributes to overall acoustic comfort by adding steady, non‑intrusive noise.
- [[concepts/white_noise_vs_brown_noise]] – While Noisli does not directly provide brown noise, it offers comparable broadband sounds that can serve a similar masking function.

**Usage notes**
- Ideal for small home offices where subtle background noise can soften stark silence.
- Users can adjust individual sound layers to avoid sudden shifts or loops, ensuring a consistent auditory backdrop.
