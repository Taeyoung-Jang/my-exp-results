# Green Flags for Stall Quality

**Definition**: Observable signs that suggest a market stall serves fresh, well‑prepared food and provides a reliable experience.

---

## Core Indicators

1. **Steady, relaxed line** – A crowd that moves smoothly signals that customers are satisfied and the service pace is manageable.
2. **Calm, practiced vendor movements** – Vendors who work with confidence and rhythm are typically experienced and attentive to food safety.
3. **Fresh‑looking ingredients** – Produce or meats that appear vibrant, not wilted or sitting in excess sauce, indicate recent preparation.
4. **Clean prep area** – Minimal clutter, no sticky residue, and organized tools suggest good hygiene practices.
5. **Consistent demand for a signature item** – When locals repeatedly order the same dish, it often reflects a proven favorite (see [[concepts/tie_breaker_local_preference]]).

---

## How to Spot Green Flags in a Noisy Market

- **Observe the line**: A short but steady queue is preferable to a chaotic crowd that constantly shifts.
- **Watch vendor posture**: Relaxed shoulders, steady hands, and purposeful gestures are positive signs.
- **Inspect displays**: Look for bright colors, neatly arranged produce, and absence of dried‑out items.
- **Listen for chatter**: Even in loud environments, satisfied customers often smile and make brief positive remarks.

These observations can be made without needing to hear the menu or read signage, aligning with the broader strategy of [[concepts/market_navigation]] and [[concepts/noise_resilient_ordering]].
