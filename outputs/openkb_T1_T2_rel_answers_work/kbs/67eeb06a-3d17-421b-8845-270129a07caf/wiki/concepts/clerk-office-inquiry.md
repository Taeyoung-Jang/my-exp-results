# Clerk Office Inquiry Script

**Definition**: A brief, polite phone script that residents can use when contacting their local clerk’s office to learn about vacancies, time commitments, and the possibility of observing meetings for local boards and commissions.

---

## Why It Matters
When starting to engage in [[concepts/local-civic-engagement]], knowing how to approach the clerk’s office efficiently saves time and projects confidence. A clear script helps the caller avoid sounding unprepared and encourages the clerk to provide the needed information.

---

## The Script (≈30 seconds)
```
Hi, I’m a resident interested in learning about openings on local boards or commissions. 
Could you tell me which ones currently have vacancies, what the usual time commitment looks like, 
and whether I’m allowed to sit in on a meeting before applying?
```
*Key points*: 
- Identify yourself as a resident.
- State the purpose (learning about openings).
- Ask three specific questions: vacancies, time commitment, and meeting observation.

---

## How to Use It
1. **Prepare**: Have a pen and paper ready to note the clerk’s answers.
2. **Call**: Use the script verbatim; pause after each sentence to let the clerk respond.
3. **Follow‑up**: If the clerk offers to email information, request the link to the board/commission list.
4. **Next Steps**: Use the details to explore [[concepts/boards-and-commissions]] that match your interests.
