# Summary of Session 14 (2024-06-12)

## Overview
This document provides practical advice for evaluating professional networking and community‑meet‑up invitations, deciding when to attend, and handling the interactions with minimal awkwardness. It covers quick **screening signals**, templates for **polite declines**, and strategies for **graceful exits** while maintaining relationships.

## Screening Criteria – “Green Flags”
- **Clear purpose**: agenda, project update, or specific cause mentioned.  
- **Named speakers or partner organizations** you respect.  
- **Explicit outcomes** (e.g., workshop, panel, collaborative session) rather than vague “networking” language.  
- **Mission‑driven language** that leads with the cause, not the crowd.  

If an invite lacks these, treat it as a likely **business‑card shuffle** and consider skipping. See [[concepts/event-screening]] for a deeper dive.

## Conversation Steering
- Set a personal goal: meet one interesting person or have a genuine chat.  
- Ask deeper questions: “What problem are you trying to solve?” or “What drew you to this event?”  
- Avoid the default “What do you do?” to move beyond small talk. See [[concepts/conversation-steering]].

## Decline Templates
### 1. Generic corporate mixer
```
Thanks for thinking of me. I’m focusing my time elsewhere right now, so I won’t be able to attend, but I appreciate the invite.
```
### 2. Vague community event
```
I’m trying to be intentional about the events I commit to, and I’m not able to make this one. Please keep me posted on future projects or gatherings with a clearer mission.
```
### 3. Mission‑driven but not aligned with my priorities
```
I’m glad you reached out. The work you’re doing matters, but I’m concentrating my volunteer time on a specific set of issues right now, so I won’t be able to join. Wishing you a great event.
```

## Graceful Exit & Follow‑up
- **In‑person line**: “I’m going to slip out, but it was really nice talking with you.”  
- **Post‑event follow‑up** (text/email):
  > “Great meeting you last night. Thanks again for the invitation—I’m glad I could stop by, even briefly.”  

## Key Concepts
- **Event screening** – Rapid assessment of event relevance.  
- **Green flags** – Indicators of substantive, mission‑driven gatherings.  
- **Polite decline** – Templates for respectful non‑attendance.  
- **Graceful exit** – Tactics to leave without burning bridges.  
- **Conversation steering** – Methods to deepen informal networking conversations.
