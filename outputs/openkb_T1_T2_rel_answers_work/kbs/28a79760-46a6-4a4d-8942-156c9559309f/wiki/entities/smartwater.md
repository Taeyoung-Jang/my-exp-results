## Smartwater

**Type:** product

Smartwater is a premium bottled water often highlighted for emergency preparedness because of its reliable sealed packaging and ready‑to‑drink convenience. In the *session‑04_2024‑06‑02* checklist, it is suggested as one of the preferred options for a **space‑efficient kit**:

- Use two 1‑liter Smartwater bottles (or similar) to meet the 2–3 gallon water requirement for a small 30–40 L emergency bin.
- The bottles are compact, stackable, and have a longer shelf life compared to some generic water containers.
- Pairing Smartwater with a larger 1‑gallon jug (e.g., from [[entities/reliance]]) ensures both volume and portability.

### Why Smartwater?
- **Sealed and sterile:** Reduces contamination risk during extended storage.
- **Consistent taste:** Encourages regular consumption, making it more likely users will keep it stocked.
- **Brand reliability:** Trusted source, which aligns with the principles of [[concepts/emergency_preparedness_basics]].

### Maintenance
- Include Smartwater bottles in the **six‑month rotation schedule** outlined in [[concepts/maintenance_schedule]].
- Check expiration dates and replace any bottles that have passed their best‑by date.

### Integration with Kit Design
- Smartwater fits the recommendations of [[concepts/space_efficient_kit_design]] by providing a high‑value, low‑volume water supply that fits tightly in a 30–40 L bin.
