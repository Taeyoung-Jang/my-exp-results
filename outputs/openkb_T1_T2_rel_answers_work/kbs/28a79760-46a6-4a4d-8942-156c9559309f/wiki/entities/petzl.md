## Petzl

Petzl is a French company that designs and manufactures outdoor equipment, most notably climbing gear and lighting solutions such as headlamps. In the context of the emergency‑preparedness checklist from [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-02]], Petzl is recommended for its **Petzl Tikkina** compact headlamp, highlighted as a high‑value lighting item for a small‑space emergency bin.

### Key Points from the Session
- **Product recommended:** Petzl Tikkina headlamp, valued for its compact size, reliability, and long battery life, making it suitable for limited‑space kits.
- **Role in the kit:** Provides hands‑free illumination, complementing a larger LED lantern (e.g., Black Diamond Moji or Goal Zero Crush Light).
- **Why it matters:** A dependable headlamp is essential for quick, low‑light tasks during power outages or evacuations.
