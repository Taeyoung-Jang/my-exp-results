# Olight

**Type:** Organization

Olight is a manufacturer of compact LED flashlights and handheld lighting tools. In the emergency‑preparedness advice of [[summaries/session-04_2024-06-02]], the **Olight i3T** (or similar tiny lantern) is recommended as the go‑bag’s “tiny flashlight” because of its small size, high lumen output, and reliable battery life.

The brand is highlighted as a high‑value item for a **space‑efficient kit** – it fits easily into a 30–40 L emergency bin or a small backpack while providing essential illumination for short‑term power outages or evacuation scenarios. Its inclusion supports the broader [[concepts/emergency_preparedness_basics]] of maintaining light sources, and aligns with the [[concepts/space_efficient_kit_design]] principles of prioritizing compact, multi‑use gear.

For ongoing readiness, the recommendation is to test the Olight flashlight during the bi‑annual [[concepts/maintenance_schedule]] check, ensuring the battery is charged and the unit is operational.
