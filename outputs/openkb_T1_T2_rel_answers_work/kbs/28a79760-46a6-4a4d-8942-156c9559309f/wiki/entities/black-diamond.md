## Black Diamond

Black Diamond is a well‑known outdoor equipment company that designs and manufactures high‑performance lighting products, including the **Moji LED lantern**. In the *session‑04_2024‑06‑02* emergency‑preparedness guide, the Moji lantern is recommended as the primary lantern for a compact, space‑efficient emergency bin.

### Key Facts from the Document
- **Product Recommendation**: The guide cites the Black Diamond Moji lantern (or a comparable model) as the preferred LED lantern for a 30–40 L emergency bin. It is highlighted for its brightness, durability, and compact size.
- **Role in Emergency Kit**: Paired with a headlamp (e.g., Petzl Tikkina) and a power bank (e.g., Anker), the lantern ensures reliable illumination during power outages or shelter‑in‑place scenarios.
