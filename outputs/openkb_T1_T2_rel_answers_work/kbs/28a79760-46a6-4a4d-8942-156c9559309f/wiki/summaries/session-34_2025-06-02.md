## Overview
This document outlines a compact, copy‑ready checklist to help anyone who has just moved into a new place (often with only an air mattress and a few carry‑on items) establish stability in the first week. It emphasizes **anchor habits**—sleep, a daily meal, and a tidy spot—and adds ultra‑short sequences for a work‑day morning and a night‑before prep to support an early commute in week two.

## Minimal First‑Week Checklist (Day‑by‑Day)
- **Day 1‑7**: Each day is broken into *Morning*, *Midday*, and *Evening* sections with **3–5 actions** maximum. The actions progressively build the core anchors while rehearsing the upcoming work‑day rhythm.
- The checklist is formatted for direct copy‑pasting into note‑taking apps.

## Workday‑Morning Sequence (10‑15 min)
1. Wake at the target commute time.  
2. Drink water.  
3. Wash face / brush teeth.  
4. Dress in clean clothes.  
5. Step outside for a minute to cue “morning mode.”

## Night‑Before Prep (5‑10 min)
- Lay out tomorrow’s clothes.  
- Set out the bowl and cup for breakfast.  
- Refill water bottle.  
- Place keys/transport card/phone together.

## Buy‑First List (6 Items, Cheapest Options)
1. Thrift‑store pillow  
2. Cheap fleece blanket  
3. Dollar‑store bowl  
4. Dollar‑store cup  
5. Thrift‑store spoon or fork  
6. Small second‑hand pot  

> Everything else (extra plates, knives, organizers, extra bedding, etc.) can be skipped until the rest of the boxes arrive.

## Takeaway
By anchoring around sleep, a consistent meal, and a tidy corner, and by rehearsing a **10‑15 minute work‑day morning** and a **night‑before prep**, the user can create a sense of control and comfort despite limited belongings and an upcoming early commute. The inexpensive buy‑first list supplies the essential tools to support these habits until the rest of their possessions arrive.
