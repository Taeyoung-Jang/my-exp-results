# Summary of session-04_2024-06-02  

**Date:** 2024‑06‑02  

## Core Idea  
Provide a concise, space‑savvy emergency‑preparedness plan for a small living space, focusing on a high‑value **emergency bin** (30–40 L) and a lightweight **go‑bag**. The plan includes prioritized items, suggested brands/types, quantities for two adults, a rotation schedule, and tips to prevent the kit from becoming clutter.

## Key Recommendations  
- **Bin contents (high‑value only):**  
  - Water: two 1‑gal jugs or a few 1‑L bottles (e.g., [[entities/reliance]] or [[entities/smartwater]]).  
  - Lighting: one LED lantern ([[entities/black-diamond]] Moji or [[entities/goal-zero]] Crush Light) and a compact headlamp ([[entities/petzl]] Tikkina).  
  - Power: one 10‑20 k mAh power bank ([[entities/anker]]) + cable.  
  - Food: 4‑6 shelf‑stable meals you actually eat (canned, pouches, curries).  
  - Medications: a few days’ supply in a labeled pouch.  
  - First‑aid: slim kit ([[entities/mymedic]] Solo or [[entities/adventure-medical]] 0.7).  
  - Tools: multitool + roll of tape.  
  - Documents: sealed envelope with copies of IDs, insurance, contacts.  

- **Go‑bag contents (grab‑and‑go):**  
  - IDs and insurance copies.  
  - Phone charger + small power bank ( [[entities/anker]] Nano‑style).  
  - One bottle of water + calorie‑dense snacks.  
  - One change of clothes (compressed in zip‑bag).  
  - Small toiletries kit.  
  - One day of essential meds.  
  - Tiny flashlight ( [[entities/olight]] i3T or similar).  

## Maintenance Schedule  
- **Every 6 months:**  
  - Check food, meds, water, flashlights, power banks for expiration or charge.  
  - Replace expired items, recharge power banks.  
  - Review and update document copies.  
  - Perform a quick visual audit of bin and go‑bag.  
- **Tie to existing bi‑annual tasks** (e.g., daylight‑saving clock change or seasonal cleaning) to keep upkeep effortless. [[concepts/maintenance_schedule]]

## Clutter‑Avoidance Tips  
- Treat the bin as a sealed “capsule”; only store items that are not used daily.  
- Keep daily‑use items (batteries, tape, tools) elsewhere to avoid frequent opening.  
- Use small, labeled pouches for each category to spot misplaced items instantly.
