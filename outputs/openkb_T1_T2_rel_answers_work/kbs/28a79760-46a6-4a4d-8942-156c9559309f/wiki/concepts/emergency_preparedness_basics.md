# Emergency Preparedness Basics

Emergency preparedness basics outline the minimal, high‑value items needed to sustain two adults for 2–3 days when normal services (power, water, ability to leave home) are unavailable. The goal is to balance safety with space constraints, ensuring the kit is easy to maintain and does not become a clutter magnet.

## Core Priorities
- **Water** – Store enough for drinking and basic hygiene. Small, sturdy containers such as two 1‑gallon jugs (e.g., [[entities/reliance]] or [[entities/smartwater]]) fit well in a compact bin.
- **Food** – Choose shelf‑stable meals you actually eat (canned goods, pouches, or ready‑to‑heat curries). Four to six meals provide sufficient calories for a short stay.
- **Lighting** – A reliable LED lantern (e.g., [[entities/black-diamond]] Moji or [[entities/goal-zero]] Crush Light) plus a compact headlamp (e.g., [[entities/petzl]] Tikkina) cover both ambient and hands‑free lighting needs.
- **Power** – One high‑capacity power bank (10 k‑20 k mAh, such as [[entities/anker]]) with a charging cable keeps phones and small devices operational.
- **First‑Aid** – A slim, comprehensive kit (e.g., [[entities/mymedic]] Solo or [[entities/adventure-medical]] 0.7) provides essential medical supplies without taking much space.
- **Tools & Miscellaneous** – A multitool, a roll of duct tape, and a sealed envelope with copies of IDs, insurance, and emergency contacts.
- **Personal Items** – Small blankets, a lightweight flashlight (e.g., [[entities/olight]] i3T), and any essential medications in a labeled pouch.

## Space‑Efficient Kit Design
The concept of a **space‑efficient kit** emphasizes packing only high‑value items in a single, clearly labeled container (30–40 L) and keeping a separate, lightweight go‑bag for rapid evacuation. See [[concepts/space_efficient_kit_design]] for detailed layout strategies.

## Maintenance Schedule
Regular checks ensure the kit remains functional:
- **Every 6 months**: Verify water freshness, rotate food, test flashlights, recharge power banks, replace expired meds, and update document copies. This aligns with the [[concepts/maintenance_schedule]] practice and can be tied to bi‑annual tasks like daylight‑saving changes.

## Clutter Prevention Tips
- Treat the bin as a sealed “capsule” – only store items that are not used daily.
- Keep daily‑use supplies (batteries, tape, tools) outside the bin to avoid frequent opening.
- Use labeled pouches for each category to quickly spot misplaced items.
