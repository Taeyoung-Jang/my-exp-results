# Minimal Checklist

**Definition**: A minimal checklist is a compact, copy‑friendly set of 3–5 actions per day (split into morning, midday, and evening) designed to help people in transition—such as after a move—establish core anchor habits quickly and with low cognitive load.

## Core Purpose
- Provides an easy‑to‑paste routine that can be stored in any notes app.
- Focuses on the three anchor habits of [[concepts/anchor_habits]]: sleep, a consistent meal, and a tidy spot.
- Adds ultra‑short sequences for work‑day mornings and night‑before preparation to support an early commute.

## Structure (as used in [[summaries/session-34_2025-06-02]])
| Day | Morning (3‑5 actions) | Midday (3‑5 actions) | Evening (3‑5 actions) |
|-----|-----------------------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| 1   | Set wake time, drink water, unpack; practice work‑day morning sequence. | Choose & set up a calm spot. | Arrange sleep area, perform night‑before prep. |
| 2   | Work‑day morning sequence; quick tidy. | Eat daily meal at work‑day time. | Short wind‑down + night‑before prep. |
| …   | … (repeat pattern, adding a short walk, light chore, stretch, grocery restock, etc.) |

Each day repeats the anchor habits while gently introducing additional micro‑routines (e.g., a brief walk at commute time, a 1‑minute stretch, a quick chore). The list caps at **3 actions per part of the day**, keeping total daily effort under 15 minutes.

## Benefits
- **Low friction**: Minimal decision‑making; actions are concrete and time‑boxed.
- **Scalable**: Works with very few possessions (air mattress, a handful of kitchen items) and can expand as more belongings arrive.
- **Prepares for work**: Includes a 10‑15 minute [[concepts/workday_morning_sequence]] and a night‑before prep routine, reducing morning scramble.
- **Cost‑effective**: Paired with a 6‑item cheap‑buy list, it requires only essential items.
