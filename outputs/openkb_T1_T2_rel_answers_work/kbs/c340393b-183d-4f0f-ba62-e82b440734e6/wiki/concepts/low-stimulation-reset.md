# Low‑Stimulation Reset

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** Gentle under‑5‑minute activities that maintain productivity without stimulating the nervous system.

## Overview
Low‑stimulation reset refers to brief, non‑screen‑based routines used in the evening to restore focus and reduce fatigue without causing further arousal. These resets are designed to bridge the gap between productive work and the wind‑down period, helping users avoid reliance on caffeine or alcohol while preserving sleep quality.

## Key Elements (from [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-13]])
- **Duration:** 5 minutes or less.
- **Goal:** Lower physiological arousal, clear mental fog, and provide a sensory cue that the work phase is ending.
- **Typical Activities:**
  - Warm shower (relaxes muscles, promotes cooling afterward).
  - Gentle stretching (neck, shoulders, back).
  - Slow walk around the house or hallway.
  - 4‑in‑6‑out breathing (four slow inhales, six slow exhales) for 1‑2 minutes.
  - Quick body‑scan stretch sequence (neck → shoulders → back).
  - 30‑60‑second workspace tidy‑up (light movement, no strong stimulation).

## How It Fits with Other Evening Strategies
- **[[concepts/evening-focus-shift]]** – By moving high‑focus tasks earlier, the low‑stimulation reset provides a smooth transition from the dip period (around 7:30 pm) to lighter work.
- **[[concepts/decision-rules]]** – The decision rule for whether to continue work at 7:30 pm often triggers a low‑stimulation reset when two of three checks indicate a stop.
- **[[concepts/chew-tapering-strategy]]** – As caffeine chews are tapered, these resets replace the brief energy boost with a calming ritual.

## Practical Example (6 pm‑11 pm schedule)
1. **6‑7 pm:** High‑focus work.
2. **7:30‑8:30 pm:** Light tasks; if the urge for a chew arises, swap it for a sensory‑rich, caffeine‑free drink.
3. **8:30‑9 pm:** Perform a low‑stimulation reset (e.g., 2‑minute breathing + quick stretch).
4. **9‑10 pm:** Finish light work, enjoy a non‑alcoholic “reward” drink.
5. **10‑11 pm:** Wind‑down, lights dim, screens off.

## Benefits
- Prevents the “wired‑but‑tired” feeling that can impair sleep.
- Maintains productivity without additional stimulants.
- Creates a consistent cue for the body to shift into relaxation mode.
