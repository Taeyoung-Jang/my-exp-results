# Decision Rules

**Definition**: Guidelines that trigger caffeine, alcohol, or evening work changes based on tracked data and cues.

## Core Elements
1. **Trigger Conditions** – measurable signals that indicate a potential problem.
   - Sleep onset >30 minutes after a day with late caffeine or alcohol.
   - Rested rating ≤5/10 the morning after alcohol.
   - Daytime energy ≤5/10 despite normal sleep.
   - Weekend spillover: feeling “off” the next day after drinking.
   - Real‑time evening cue at 7:30 pm: if two of three quick checks are true (re‑reading errors, foggy brain, task would be faster tomorrow), trigger a stop‑work rule.

2. **Action Steps** – brief, testable adjustments applied for a limited period (typically three nights or three evenings).
   - Move the last caffeine cutoff earlier by one hour (see [[concepts/chew-tapering-strategy]]).
   - Shift the last drink to at least one hour before bedtime.
   - Reduce total caffeine amount or insert a caffeine‑free day.
   - Decrease number of drinks or choose lower‑alcohol options.
   - Apply an **evening focus shift**: schedule high‑focus tasks earlier (see [[concepts/evening-focus-shift]]).
   - Use a low‑stimulation reset for 5 minutes or less when the 7:30 pm cue appears (see [[concepts/low-stimulation-reset]]).

3. **Evaluation Window** – repeat the chosen adjustment for three consecutive nights/weekends or three evenings, then reassess the same metrics.

## How It Fits Within a Habit‑Tracking System
Decision‑rules are applied after collecting data with a habit‑tracking template (see the summary of [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-15]]). Once the data are scored (e.g., using a simple bad‑sleep scoring method), the thresholds above trigger the appropriate rule. In addition, the real‑time 7:30 pm decision rule can be used without waiting for nightly scores, providing an immediate cue to stop work or switch to a low‑stimulation reset.

## Example Workflow
1. **Track** daily using the provided template.
2. **Score** each night: +1 point for >30 min sleep onset, +1 for >2 night wakings, +1 for rested ≤5/10. Nights with ≥2 points are flagged as *bad sleep*.
3. **Identify Triggers** – if a bad‑sleep night coincides with “caffeine after 2 pm” being checked, the caffeine‑timing rule applies. In the evening, at 7:30 pm, run the three quick checks; if any two are true, invoke the stop‑work rule.
4. **Apply Rule** – shift caffeine cutoff earlier for three nights, or perform a low‑stimulation reset and stop work for the night.
5. **Re‑evaluate** – after the three‑night or three‑evening trial, see if the bad‑sleep score drops or if evening productivity improves.
