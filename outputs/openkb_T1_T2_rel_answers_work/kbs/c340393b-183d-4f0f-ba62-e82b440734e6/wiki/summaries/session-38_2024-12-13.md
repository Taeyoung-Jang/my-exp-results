## Summary of session-38_2024-12-13

**Topic:** Practical strategies for reshaping a weeknight routine to stay productive while reducing reliance on caffeine‑based energy chews and evening alcohol.

### Core Recommendations
1. **Front‑load focus work** – Schedule the most demanding tasks between 6:00‑7:00 pm when natural energy peaks. [[concepts/evening-focus-shift]]
2. **Shift the chew** – Move the energy chew from 7:30 pm to around 6:00 pm (or earlier). If needed, taper over two weeks:
   * Week 1: one chew, no later than 6:30 pm.  
   * Week 2: half‑chew or lower‑caffeine alternative, then discontinue. [[concepts/chew-tapering-strategy]]
3. **Replace the drink** – Use adult‑feeling non‑alcoholic beverages (e.g., citrus seltzer, non‑alcoholic gin & tonic, spiced herbal tea) as a reward.
4. **Structured evening flow (6 pm‑11 pm)** –
   * 6‑7 pm: high‑focus work.  
   * 7‑7:30 pm: dinner/snack break.  
   * 7:30‑8:30 pm: lighter tasks (organising, planning). Replace chew urge with a sensory‑rich, caffeine‑free drink.  
   * 8:30‑9 pm: **reset** (stretch, short walk, warm shower). [[concepts/low-stimulation-reset]]  
   * 9‑10 pm: finish light work, enjoy the non‑alcoholic “reward” drink.  
   * 10‑11 pm: wind‑down (dim lights, no screens), bedtime.
5. **Low‑stimulation resets** – Under‑5‑minute activities that don’t raise heart rate:
   * Warm shower.  
   * Gentle stretching.  
   * Slow walk.  
   * 4‑in‑6‑out breathing (1‑2 min).  
   * Quick body‑scan stretch (neck → shoulders → back).  
   * 30‑60 s workspace tidy‑up. [[concepts/low-stimulation-reset]]
6. **Decision rule for 7:30 pm work continuation** – Perform three quick checks:
   * Re‑reading or making small errors?  
   * Foggy brain rather than just tired?  
   * Would the task be faster with a fresh mind tomorrow?

   **Rule:** If **any two** of the three checks are true, stop work and resume next day. [[concepts/decision-rules]]

### Additional Details
* **Reward drink ideas** – grapefruit or blood‑orange seltzer with lime, non‑alcoholic gin‑tonic style, cinnamon/ginger herbal tea.
* **Early chew placement** – Moving to 6:00 pm is effective; if still difficult, shift a bit earlier each night until comfortably before the dip.
* **Monitoring** – Watch for withdrawal symptoms (headache, irritability, excessive sleepiness) and adjust taper speed accordingly.
