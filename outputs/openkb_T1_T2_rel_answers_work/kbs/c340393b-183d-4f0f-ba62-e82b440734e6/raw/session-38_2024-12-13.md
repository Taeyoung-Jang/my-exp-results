Building in one short decompression break also makes a big difference. Ten minutes of stretching, a short walk, or even just stepping away from your workspace resets your energy without revving you up. If you taper the stimulants, swap the drink for something nonalcoholic, nudge focused work a bit earlier, and give yourself a small buffer between “work mode” and “sleep mode,” you should stay productive without the crash or the sleep disruption. If anything feels hard to adjust or you notice withdrawal-like symptoms, it’s always fine to check in with a healthcare professional for personalized guidance.
