# White Space

**Definition**: White Space refers to deliberately scheduled open blocks—often short periods like 30 minutes—within a weekly plan that act as a buffer for unexpected events, preventing the schedule from feeling overwhelming.

## Why It Matters
- Provides *flexibility* in an otherwise structured week.
- Reduces stress by offering a safe place to accommodate surprises without reshuffling all commitments.
- Supports the principle of treating the weekly plan as a *guide* rather than a rigid script (see [[concepts/weekly-planning]]).

## Key Characteristics
- **Intentional**: Placed purposefully, not left to chance.
- **Sized Appropriately**: Typically 15‑60 minutes, enough to absorb minor disruptions.
- **Distributed**: Spread across several days to create multiple safety nets.

## Practical Tips
- **Insert at least one block per day** (e.g., late morning or early evening).
- **Label it clearly** in your calendar (e.g., “Buffer – White Space”).
- **Review weekly** to ensure the blocks are providing the intended breathing room.
