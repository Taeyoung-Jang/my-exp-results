# Weekly Planning

**Definition**: Weekly Planning is a structured yet adaptable approach to organizing a seven‑day period, balancing non‑negotiable commitments with flexible tasks and intentional "white space" to accommodate unexpected events.

## Core Elements

1. **Fixed Commitments**
   - Items that are tied to other people or have hard consequences if missed (e.g., meetings, appointments, deadlines). These form the backbone of the week and remain unchanged.
2. **Flexible Tasks**
   - Activities that matter but can be moved without breaking obligations, such as exercise, personal projects, errands, or hobbies.
3. **White Space**
   - Deliberate open blocks (often 30 minutes to an hour) inserted throughout the week to absorb surprises and prevent overload. [[concepts/white-space]]
4. **Decision Checklist**
   - A rapid 1‑minute set of questions to evaluate whether a new opportunity warrants reshuffling the schedule. [[concepts/decision-checklist]]
5. **Rescheduling Safeguard**
   - When a flexible but important task (e.g., exercise) is bumped, it must be re‑scheduled within a defined window (commonly 48 hours) to avoid it disappearing.

## Process Overview

1. **Identify Fixed Items** – List all commitments that cannot move.
2. **Create Open Blocks** – Allocate 1‑2 flexible windows each day, ensuring there is sufficient white space.
3. **Populate Flexible Tasks** – Slot non‑critical activities into the open blocks, knowing they can shift.
4. **Handle Unexpected Opportunities** – Apply the decision checklist; if the opportunity scores positively, move a flexible task to a new slot, respecting the rescheduling safeguard.
5. **Review Daily** – At the end of each day, reset any unfinished flexible tasks to the next open block or the next day.

## Benefits
- Reduces overwhelm by treating the week as a guide rather than a rigid script.
- Increases resilience to interruptions and new opportunities.
- Protects important but non‑urgent activities from being perpetually postponed.
- Enhances clarity around what truly must stay fixed, reinforcing personal **commitment** to core responsibilities. [[concepts/commitment]]
