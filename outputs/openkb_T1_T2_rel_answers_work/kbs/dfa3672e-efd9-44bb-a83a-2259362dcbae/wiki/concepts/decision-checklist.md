# Decision Checklist

**Definition**: A concise, 1‑minute decision aid consisting of a few targeted questions that help you evaluate whether an unexpected opportunity warrants moving existing tasks in your weekly plan.

**Purpose**
- Provides a fast, low‑friction way to assess the trade‑off between a new opportunity and current commitments.
- Supports the [[concepts/weekly-planning]] approach by keeping the schedule flexible while protecting essential [[concepts/commitment]]s.
- Works hand‑in‑hand with intentional [[concepts/white-space]] blocks, ensuring that any reshuffle can be absorbed without overwhelming the week.

**Core Questions**
1. Does this new opportunity matter more than what I planned?
2. Will moving the original task cause real consequences or just a small shift?
3. Does this opportunity give me energy, progress, or connection I want?
4. Do I know exactly where the bumped task will go?
5. Does the reshuffle still leave my week feeling manageable?

These five prompts are derived directly from the guidance in [[summaries/session-38_2024-07-17]], where they were presented as a practical tool for handling unexpected events while maintaining a balanced schedule.

**How to Use**
- When an unexpected event arises, run through the checklist mentally (or on paper) before adjusting your plan.
- If the answers favor the new opportunity, locate a flexible block (or white‑space slot) to accommodate it.
- If a bumped task is important but not urgent (e.g., exercise), immediately assign a new specific time within a short window (e.g., 48 hours) to prevent it from being lost.

**Related Concepts**
- [[concepts/weekly-planning]] – the broader framework within which the checklist operates.
- [[concepts/white-space]] – the buffer zones that make reshuffling feasible.
- [[concepts/commitment]] – the fixed items that should remain untouched unless absolutely necessary.
