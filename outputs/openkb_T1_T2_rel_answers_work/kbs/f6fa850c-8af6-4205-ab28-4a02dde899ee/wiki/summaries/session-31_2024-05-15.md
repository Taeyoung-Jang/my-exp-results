# Summary of session-31_2024-05-15

**Main contribution:** A concrete, month‑by‑month checklist for an overseas residency move, plus a ready‑to‑paste Google Sheets CSV template, status dropdown setup, and a 15‑minute weekly review routine.

## Checklist & timeline
- **Months 1‑2:** Confirm program requirements, validate passport, start visa preparation.
- **Months 3‑4:** Submit visa, research banking, learn housing norms.
- **Months 5‑6:** Secure temporary housing, arrange healthcare, begin packing plan.
- **Months 7‑9:** Book flights, close home‑country services, network with future colleagues.
- Each item is flagged as *must‑do* or *nice‑to‑have* with typical time estimates.

## Google Sheets layout
Columns: **Task**, **Category**, **Must‑do/Nice**, **Target Deadline**, **Status**, **Notes**.  
A CSV with headers and example rows (target move November 2026) is provided, including two “visa uncertain” contingency rows.

## Status dropdown
Options to add via Data → Data validation: `Not started`, `In progress`, `Waiting on others`, `Completed`, `Blocked`.  
(Use [[entities/google-sheets]] for implementation.)

## Weekly 15‑minute review
1. Scan tasks due in the next 2‑3 weeks.  
2. Update statuses and add new tasks (≈10 min).  
3. Choose one priority for the coming week (≈4 min).  
Sorting by **Status** keeps active items at the top.

## Practical takeaways
- Use a simple Google Sheet instead of complex project tools.  
- Include contingency tasks for visa delays to avoid last‑minute stress.  
- Keep the maintenance routine under 15 minutes to ensure consistency.
