# Notion (Product)

Notion is a SaaS productivity platform that combines notes, databases, wikis, and task management in a single workspace. Users can build custom pages, tables, kanban boards, and calendars without needing separate applications.

## Mention in [[summaries/session-31_2024-05-15]]
During the relocation planning conversation, Notion was offered as a possible tool for tracking the move checklist. The assistant noted that while Notion is feature‑rich, it can become more complex than needed for a simple 15‑minute weekly review. The recommendation favored a plain Google Sheet for minimal maintenance, positioning Notion as an optional, more elaborate alternative.

## Comparison with [[entities/google-sheets]]
- **Complexity** – Notion provides rich relational databases and templates, which can be powerful but may require extra setup time. Google Sheets offers a straightforward grid with basic formulas, making it quicker to start for a simple checklist.
- **Flexibility** – Notion’s block‑based editor allows mixing text, tables, and embedded media on the same page, useful for detailed project documentation. Google Sheets excels at numeric calculations and quick filtering.
- **Collaboration** – Both support real‑time collaboration, but Notion includes versioned pages and comments directly tied to each block.

## Key Takeaways
- Notion offers a powerful, all‑in‑one workspace suitable for users who want a highly customizable tracking system.
- For a low‑maintenance, 15‑minute‑per‑week routine, a simpler tool like Google Sheets may be preferable, as highlighted in the session.
- Users can still leverage Notion’s templates for relocation planning if they prefer richer documentation or plan to expand the workspace beyond the initial move.
