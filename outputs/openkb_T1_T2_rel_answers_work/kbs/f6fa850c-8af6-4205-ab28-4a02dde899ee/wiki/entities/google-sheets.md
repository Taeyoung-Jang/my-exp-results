## Google Sheets

Google Sheets is a cloud‑based spreadsheet application that allows users to create, edit, and share spreadsheets online. In the context of the **session‑31_2024‑05‑15** relocation planning document, Google Sheets is recommended as the primary tool for tracking the month‑by‑month checklist.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-31_2024-05-15]]
- **Template provision**: A ready‑to‑paste CSV layout is supplied, with columns *Task*, *Category*, *Must‑do/Nice*, *Target Deadline*, *Status*, and *Notes*.
- **Status tracking**: The document outlines a dropdown status list (Not started, In progress, Waiting on others, Completed, Blocked) that can be implemented via Google Sheets’ data‑validation feature, directly supporting the [[concepts/task-status-tracking]] concept.
- **Timeline alignment**: The sheet’s tasks map onto the [[concepts/relocation-timeline-milestones]] outlined in the checklist, ensuring each milestone has a concrete deadline.
- **Checklist integration**: By using the sheet, users can execute the [[concepts/relocation-checklist-planning]] without needing separate project‑management software.

### Practical usage tips
1. **Import the CSV**: Copy the provided CSV into a new Google Sheet to preserve column headings and example rows.
2. **Set up the status dropdown**: Select the *Status* column, go to *Data → Data validation*, choose *Dropdown* and enter the listed status options.
3. **Weekly review**: Filter tasks due in the next two to three weeks, update statuses, and prioritize one action for the upcoming week.

Google Sheets thus serves as a simple, collaborative, and flexible platform for managing relocation tasks and timelines.
