# Umbrella Role

**Definition**: An *umbrella role* is a single, overarching job title (e.g., “Independent Consultant” or “Freelance Contractor”) that aggregates multiple freelance, contract, or part‑time engagements into one continuous entry on a resume or LinkedIn profile. This approach keeps the employment timeline clean, avoids perceived gaps, and allows selective highlighting of major projects.

## Key Elements
- **Continuous Date Range**: Covers the entire period of self‑employment (e.g., 2019–Present), regardless of overlapping contracts.
- **Unified Title**: Uses a generic yet professional title such as:
  - Independent Consultant
  - Freelance [Field] Specialist
  - Self‑Employed [Field] Professional
- **Bullet‑Point Summary**: Provides generic, transferable achievements that apply across varied client work.
- **Highlighted Contracts**: Optional sub‑entries for significant projects (e.g., a long‑term part‑time role) that are listed under the umbrella role to give them extra visibility.

## Benefits
- **Clarity**: Prevents a fragmented work history and eliminates apparent employment gaps.
- **Flexibility**: Allows you to group small contracts while still showcasing high‑impact engagements.
- **Scalability**: Works for both traditional resumes and LinkedIn profiles, adapting to each platform’s formatting conventions.

## Example Entry
**Resume**
```
Independent Consultant | Self‑Employed
2019–Present
Provide project‑based consulting across several industries, managing lifecycles and delivering tailored solutions.

Highlighted Contract
Project Consultant, BrightWave Health Systems
2021–2023 (overlapped)
Part‑time consultant supporting product and operations teams, refining workflows and delivering process recommendations while maintaining other consulting engagements.
```

**LinkedIn**
```
Independent Consultant (Self‑Employed)
2019–Present
Consult with organizations on project‑based work, handling scoping, execution, and delivery across multiple engagements.

BrightWave Health Systems – Project Consultant
2021–2023
Part‑time role alongside other projects, improving workflow clarity and delivering recommendations for product and operations teams.
```
