## Resume and LinkedIn Formatting

This concept captures the best‑practice approaches for displaying a period of self‑employment on both a traditional resume and a LinkedIn profile. It draws on the examples and recommendations from [[summaries/session-08_2024-06-15]].

### Core Principles
- **Use a single umbrella role** to keep the employment timeline continuous, avoiding gaps. The umbrella role can be titled with any of the suggested headings (e.g., *Independent Consultant*, *Freelance [Field] Specialist*). See [[concepts/umbrella-role]] for more on that approach.
- **Tailor the format** to each medium: a concise, bullet‑rich block for a resume versus a more narrative paragraph on LinkedIn, while preserving the same core information.

### Resume Formatting
1. **Header line** – Title and employment type (e.g., `Independent Consultant | Self‑Employed`).
2. **Date range** – Provide the full period (e.g., `2019–Present`).
3. **Brief description** – One or two sentences summarizing the scope across industries.
4. **Bullet points** – Use concrete, generic statements that highlight project delivery, stakeholder coordination, documentation, and adaptability. These bullets mirror the examples from the source document.
5. **Highlighted contracts** – For major part‑time engagements, add a sub‑section with the client name, dates (showing overlap), and a short narrative of responsibilities.

### LinkedIn Formatting
- **Title line** – Same title as the resume, optionally with parentheses (e.g., `Independent Consultant (Self‑Employed)`).
- **Description block** – Write in paragraph form, emphasizing the breadth of work, key activities, and ability to juggle multiple projects.
- **Clients section** – Either list a few recognizable clients, anonymize, or group by industry, following the guidance in [[concepts/client-listing-strategies]].
- **Highlighted contract entry** – Can be added as a separate role under the same company entry or as a distinct company entry; both are acceptable on [[entities/linkedin]].

### Handling Overlapping Part‑Time Contracts
- Group short or low‑impact contracts under the umbrella role to keep the section clean.
- For high‑impact, long‑duration contracts, list them as highlighted sub‑entries to give them visibility without breaking the continuous timeline.

### Related Concepts
- [[concepts/umbrella-role]] – How to frame a continuous self‑employment entry.
- [[concepts/client-listing-strategies]] – Options for naming or anonymizing clients.

By applying these formatting guidelines, job seekers can present self‑employment in a way that looks cohesive, professional, and strategically highlights their most significant projects.
