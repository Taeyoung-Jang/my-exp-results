## Overview
This conversation provides practical advice for framing a period of self‑employment on a résumé and LinkedIn profile. It covers title options, generic yet concrete bullet‑point examples, client‑listing strategies, and how to display overlapping part‑time contracts using an [[concepts/umbrella-role]] with a highlighted project.

## Title / Heading Options
- Independent Consultant  
- Freelance [Your Field] Specialist  
- Self‑Employed [Your Field] Professional  
- Contract [Your Field] Consultant  
- Owner / Principal, [Your Name] Consulting  
- Freelance Contractor (Project‑Based)  
- Independent Advisor, [Your Field]

These titles help present a single continuous role that keeps the timeline clean.

## Bullet‑Point Examples
- Managed end‑to‑end project delivery for multiple clients, from scoping through handoff.  
- Provided subject‑matter expertise to solve operational or strategic challenges.  
- Developed custom solutions that improved performance, efficiency, or outcomes.  
- Coordinated directly with stakeholders to clarify requirements and maintain communication.  
- Produced documentation, reports, and recommendations supporting leadership decisions.  
- Balanced multiple concurrent engagements while meeting deadlines and quality targets.  
- Integrated client feedback to refine deliverables and strengthen relationships.  
- Adapted quickly to different industries, tools, and workflows.

## Client Listing Strategies
- **Name full clients** if publicly disclosed and permission granted – adds credibility.  
- **Anonymize** with descriptors (e.g., “mid‑size healthcare company”) for sensitive work.  
- **Group by industry/type** (e.g., “Clients included organizations in tech, healthcare, and professional services”).  

See [[concepts/client-listing-strategies]] for guidance on choosing the best approach.

## Experience Entry Formats
### Résumé Version
```markdown
Independent Consultant | Self‑Employed
2019–Present
Provide project‑based consulting and execution support across tech, healthcare, and professional services. Manage full project lifecycles, align with stakeholders, and deliver tailored solutions. Produce reports and recommendations that aid decision‑making. Adapt quickly to varied industries and workflows.
```
### LinkedIn Version
```markdown
Independent Consultant (Self‑Employed)
2019–Present
Consult with organizations on project‑based work, covering scoping, planning, execution, and delivery while juggling multiple engagements. Collaborate with stakeholders to clarify needs and produce clear documentation and recommendations.
```
Both exemplify [[concepts/resume-linkedin-formatting]].

## Handling Overlapping Part‑Time Contracts
- **Group small/short contracts** under the umbrella role to keep the section clean.  
- **Highlight major contracts** as sub‑entries when they are long, high‑impact, or function like part‑time staff roles.

## Sample Layout: Umbrella Role + Highlighted Contract
### Résumé
```markdown
Independent Consultant | Self‑Employed
2019–Present
Provide project‑based consulting across several industries, managing lifecycles and delivering tailored solutions.

Highlighted Contract
Project Consultant, BrightWave Health Systems
2021–2023 (overlapped)
Part‑time consultant supporting product and operations teams, refining workflows and delivering process recommendations while maintaining other consulting engagements.
```
*Entity referenced: [[entities/brightwave-health-systems]]*

### LinkedIn
```markdown
Independent Consultant (Self‑Employed)
2019–Present
Consult with organizations on project‑based work, handling scoping, execution, and delivery across multiple engagements.

BrightWave Health Systems – Project Consultant
2021–2023
Part‑time role alongside other projects, improving workflow clarity and delivering recommendations for product and operations teams.
```
