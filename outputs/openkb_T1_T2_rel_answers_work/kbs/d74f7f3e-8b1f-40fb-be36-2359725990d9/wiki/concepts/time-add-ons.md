# Time Add‑Ons for Travel

**Definition** – Time Add‑Ons are fixed or range‑based buffers that travelers attach to the scheduled travel time of a mode (flight, train, or driving) to approximate the full door‑to‑door journey. They simplify the [[concepts/door-to-door-travel-time]] calculation without detailed schedule analysis.

**Typical add‑on values (from [[summaries/session-06_2022-07-02]])**

| Travel mode | Scheduled time | Add‑on (buffer) | Approx. total |
|------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| Air travel | Flight duration | ~1 h to airport + ~1 h security/boarding + ~1 h post‑landing = **≈3 h** | Flight time + 3 h |
| Train      | Train duration | 15–30 min to/from station (each end) = **≈30–60 min** | Train time + 0.5 h |
| Driving    | Map‑provided drive time | Traffic buffer 15–30 min (more at peak) = **≈0.25 h** | Drive time + 0.25 h |

**How it is used**
1. Take the quoted travel time for the chosen mode.  
2. Add the appropriate time‑add‑on from the table.  
3. Compare the resulting totals across modes using the [[concepts/travel-mode-comparison]] framework to decide which option meets a personal cutoff (e.g., 5 h).

**Benefits**
- Quick mental calculation, no need for detailed itinerary planning.  
- Provides a consistent basis for [[concepts/travel-mode-comparison]] across different trips.

**Limitations**
- Buffers are averages; actual overhead may vary with airport size, security lines, traffic conditions, or station distance. Adjust as needed for specific circumstances.
