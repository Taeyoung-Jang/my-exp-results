# Travel Mode Comparison

Travel Mode Comparison is a systematic way to decide between flying, taking a train, or driving by estimating the **door‑to‑door travel time** for each option and applying consistent **time add‑ons**. The goal is to quickly determine which mode meets a personal time threshold (e.g., five hours) without detailed schedule analysis.

## Core idea
- Start with the scheduled travel duration for each mode.
- Add predefined overhead buffers that reflect typical pre‑ and post‑travel activities.
- Compare the resulting totals to decide the most efficient mode.

## Typical add‑on values (from [[summaries/session-06_2022-07-02]])
| Mode   | Scheduled time | Add‑on buffer | Approx. total overhead |
|--------|----------------|---------------|------------------------|
| Flight | Flight duration | ~1 h to airport & check‑in + ~1 h security/boarding + ~1 h taxi‑out, deplaning, baggage | ~3 hours |
| Train  | Train duration | ~15‑30 min to/from station (both ends) | ~30‑60 min |
| Driving| Driving time (map estimate) | ~15‑30 min traffic buffer (more during peak) | ~15‑30 min |

## How to use the comparison
1. **Take the scheduled travel time** for each option.
2. **Add the appropriate overhead** using the values above (these are the [[concepts/time-add-ons]] for each mode).
3. **Sum the times** to get a door‑to‑door estimate, i.e., the [[concepts/door-to-door-travel-time]].
4. **Compare the totals** against your personal cutoff (e.g., 5 hours) to select the fastest, least stressful mode.

## Benefits
- Provides a quick, repeatable decision framework.
- Accounts for hidden time costs of air travel (airport logistics) that often make short flights longer overall.
- Offers a consistent basis for comparing disparate travel options.

## Related concepts
- [[concepts/door-to-door-travel-time]] – the total elapsed time from origin door to destination door.
- [[concepts/time-add-ons]] – standard time buffers applied to each travel mode.
