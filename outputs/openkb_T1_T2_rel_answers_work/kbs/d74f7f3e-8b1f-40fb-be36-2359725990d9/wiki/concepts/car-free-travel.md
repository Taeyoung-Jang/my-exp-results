# Car‑Free Travel

## Definition
Car‑free travel refers to journeys where the traveler moves from origin to destination without using a personal or rental automobile, relying instead on public transport, shuttles, walking, or other non‑automotive modes.

## Why it matters for low‑stress weekend planning
[[concepts/low-stress-weekend-planning]] highlights the value of minimizing logistical friction. By choosing airports and cities with direct train or shuttle links, travelers reduce door‑to‑door travel time, eliminate parking hassles, and keep the experience relaxed.

## Key considerations
- **Door‑to‑door travel time** – Assess the total time from home to the city center, not just flight duration. See [[concepts/door-to-door-travel-time]].
- **Walkable destinations** – Pick cities where attractions are within easy walking distance; [[concepts/walkable-destinations]].
- **Transit reliability** – Prefer airports with dedicated rail or shuttle services that drop you near hotels or downtown.
- **Time add‑ons** – Account for time spent transferring between modes; [[concepts/time-add-ons]].
- **Travel‑mode comparison** – Weigh car‑free options against driving or rideshare for cost, convenience, and stress; [[concepts/travel-mode-comparison]].

## Example from [[summaries/session-36_2024-07-02]]
The session suggests three car‑free friendly weekend spots from Philadelphia International Airport (PHL):
- **Providence, RI** – Direct train from the airport to downtown, fully walkable.
- **Baltimore, MD** – Train to Penn Station, walkable neighborhoods like Mount Vernon.
- **Richmond, VA** – Requires a short rideshare from the airport, but the city center is walkable.

Each option demonstrates how selecting a destination with seamless public‑transit connections enables a stress‑free, car‑free weekend.

## Practical tips
1. Check airline and airport websites for rail or shuttle schedules.
2. Choose hotels within a short walk of the nearest station.
3. Pack light to make walking and carrying luggage easy.
4. Schedule outbound flights mid‑morning (≈9:30‑11 am) and return flights late afternoon (≈4‑6 pm) to maximize on‑ground time without early wake‑ups.
