# Summary of session‑36 (2024‑07‑02)

## Core advice
- **Low‑stress weekend planning**: Choose a destination that’s easy to reach (short nonstop flight, direct airport‑to‑downtown transit) and prioritize comfort over exhaustive itineraries. [[concepts/low-stress-weekend-planning]]
- **Budget & packing**: Set a loose budget with one or two splurges (nice meal, cozy lodging). Pack light: essential clothing, one versatile pair of shoes, toiletries, and a relaxation item (book, headphones, journal). *(packing essentials)*
- **Flight timing**: Aim for a mid‑morning outbound (≈9:30‑11 am) and a late‑afternoon return (≈4‑6 pm) to maximize usable weekend hours without early‑morning stress. *(flight timing)*

## Destination selection criteria
1. **Nonstop availability** from [[entities/philadelphia-international-airport]].
2. **Walkability** – compact downtowns where most attractions are within a short stroll. [[concepts/walkable-destinations]]
3. **Car‑free access** – direct train or shuttle from airport to city center. [[concepts/car-free-travel]]
4. **Vibe match** – quiet restorative vs. lively food/arts scene.

## Recommended PHL nonstop, walkable cities
| City | Transit from airport | Walkable area | Highlights |
|------|----------------------|--------------|------------|
| **Providence** | Airport‑to‑downtown train (direct) | Downtown & East Side | [[entities/risd-museum]], vibrant food scene, fully car‑free. |
| **Baltimore** | Train to Penn Station (direct) | Mount Vernon, Harbor East, Fell’s Point | Inner Harbor, diverse restaurants, multiple museums (e.g., [[entities/baltimore-museum-of-art]]). |
| **Richmond** | Rideshare needed (no train) | The Fan, Museum District | [[entities/virginia-museum-of-fine-arts]], historic streets, good eateries. |

## Comparative snapshot for a Fri‑Sun “walk everywhere, eat well, one museum” weekend
- **Providence** – *Best convenience*: direct train, compact downtown, easy to stay car‑free. Ideal for pure relaxation. 
- **Baltimore** – *Most to do*: larger city, richer food & cultural options, occasional short rideshare. 
- **Richmond** – *Middle ground*: artsy vibe, good food, requires a rideshare from the airport but otherwise walkable.

## Actionable next steps
1. Choose the city that aligns with your desired vibe and tolerance for occasional rideshare.
2. Book a Friday outbound flight between 9:30‑11 am and a Sunday return between 4‑6 pm.
3. Pack light using the outlined essentials and plan one “splurge” activity (e.g., a special dinner or museum visit).
