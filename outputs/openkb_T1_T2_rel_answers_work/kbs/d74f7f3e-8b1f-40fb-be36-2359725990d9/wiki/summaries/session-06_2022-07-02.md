# Summary of session-06_2022-07-02

**Main contribution** – a concise, reusable checklist that lets a traveler compare the *door‑to‑door* time of **air travel**, **train travel**, and **driving** using simple time‑add‑on rules.

## Key points

- Decision factors when choosing a travel mode include total travel time, cost, hassle, luggage, and personal comfort.  
- Estimating *door‑to‑door travel time* requires adding overhead to the scheduled travel time for each mode. See [[concepts/door-to-door-travel-time]].

### Flight overhead (≈ 3 hours total)
1. **Travel to airport + check‑in** – ~1 hour  
2. **Early arrival, security, boarding** – ~1 hour  
3. **Taxi‑out, deplaning, baggage, travel from arrival airport** – ~1 hour  

These three chunks constitute the **flight overhead** that often turns a short flight into a multi‑hour journey. This is an example of [[concepts/time-add-ons]].

### Train overhead (≈ 30‑60 minutes total)
- Add 15‑30 minutes for getting to the station and settling in.  
- Add another 15‑30 minutes on arrival for exiting the station and final ground transport.

### Driving overhead (≈ 15‑30 minutes buffer)
- Use the map‑provided driving time and add a small buffer for traffic (15‑30 minutes for typical conditions, more during peaks).

## Quick comparison checklist
1. **Take the scheduled travel time** for each mode.  
2. **Add the appropriate overhead** (flight: 3 h, train: 0.5 h, driving: 0.25 h).  
3. **Compare the resulting totals** against your personal cutoff (e.g., 5 hours) to decide.

This mental model enables fast, consistent *travel mode comparison* without detailed schedule lookup. See [[concepts/travel-mode-comparison]].
