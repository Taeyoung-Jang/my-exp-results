# Baltimore Museum of Art

**Type:** place

**Description:** The Baltimore Museum of Art (BMA) is a major art museum located in the Mount Vernon cultural district of Baltimore, Maryland. It houses a broad collection ranging from ancient to contemporary works, and is noted for its substantial holdings of modern art, especially pieces by Henri Matisse.
