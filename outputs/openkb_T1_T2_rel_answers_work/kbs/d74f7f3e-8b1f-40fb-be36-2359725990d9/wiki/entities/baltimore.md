# Baltimore, Maryland

Baltimore is highlighted in [[summaries/session-36_2024-07-02]] as a short‑flight, car‑free weekend destination from [[entities/philadelphia-international-airport]]. The city is reachable by a direct train to Penn Station, making the **door‑to‑door travel time** quick and stress‑free.

## Why it fits low‑stress weekend planning
- **Walkability**: Neighborhoods such as Mount Vernon, Harbor East, and Fell’s Point are compact and pedestrian‑friendly, aligning with the [[concepts/walkable-destinations]] concept.
- **Car‑free travel**: The train connection exemplifies [[concepts/car-free-travel]], allowing travelers to arrive downtown without renting a vehicle.
- **Varied attractions**: Baltimore offers a richer selection of food, waterfront walks, and cultural sites compared to smaller towns, which can be compared using [[concepts/travel-mode-comparison]].
- **Potential rideshares**: While most activities are walkable, occasional short rideshares may be needed to bridge between neighborhoods.

## Key recommendation from the session
- **Flight timing**: Pair a mid‑morning outbound flight (≈9:30 am – 11 am) with a late‑afternoon return (≈4 pm – 6 pm) to maximize usable weekend hours, a tip rooted in the [[concepts/low-stress-weekend-planning]] guidance.

## Notable cultural spot
- The **Baltimore Museum of Art** is mentioned as a major museum option for a weekend itinerary. See [[entities/baltimore-museum-of-art]] for details.
