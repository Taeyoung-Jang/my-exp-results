## Summary

- **Goal**: design a simple [[concepts/mobile-living-system]] for a consultant rotating between client sites, using a backpack as the go‑bag and a carry‑on as the clothing module.  
- **Backpack go‑bag (24–48 hr)**: one full outfit, spare underwear/socks, permanent toiletries, meds, glasses/contacts, first‑aid, laptop + charger, phone cable, power strip, earbuds, passport/ID, badge, cards, packable tote, laundry bag, eye mask/earplugs, water bottle, snack.  
- **Carry‑on capsule wardrobe (1 week)**: 3 neutral shirts, 1 polo, 1 tee; 2 chinos, 1 dark jeans; merino crew, blazer/overshirt; business‑casual shoes + site‑appropriate shoes; 5 underwear, 5 socks, 1 workout outfit; lightweight jacket, quick‑dry towel, laundry soap sheets. See [[concepts/capsule-wardrobe]].  
- **Storage bins**: *Bin A* – Seasonal + Clothing Swaps, *Bin B* – Home‑Base Utilities + Backups, and a Long‑Term / Special Gear box. Each lid holds an inventory sheet (Item — Qty — Status) to track in/out items.  
- **Minimal duplicate strategy**: duplicate chargers, cables, toiletries, adapters, first‑aid kit; avoid duplicating bulky or expensive items. See [[concepts/minimal-duplicate-strategy]].  
- **Routines**:  
  - **Arrival**: empty backpack, sort clothes, plug chargers, restock toiletries, separate laundry, reset tech.  
  - **Departure**: fully charge devices, pack by category, verify meds/documents, refill toiletry kit, final sweep.  
- **Micro leave‑lodging checklist**: check outlets for chargers, bathroom for toiletries/meds, grab laptop/badge/passport, look under bed/closet, quick desk/nightstand sweep.  
- **Backpack size recommendation**: 20–26 L balances capacity and airline personal‑item limits.  
- **Carry‑on packing‑cube layout**: large cube for pants and shirts, medium cube for underwear, socks, workout outfit, small cube for layers, shoe bag for second pair, side pocket for towel and laundry sheets.
