# Capsule Wardrobe

**Type:** Concept
**Description:** A compact collection of versatile, neutral‑colored clothing pieces that mix‑and‑match to cover a week of business‑casual and site‑visit needs while minimizing baggage.

---

## Overview
The capsule wardrobe concept is a core component of the **[[concepts/mobile-living-system]]** described in [[summaries/session-37_2025-04-10]]. It focuses on reducing the number of items you need to carry, streamlining decisions, and ensuring that each piece can serve multiple outfit combinations.

## Core Principles
1. **Neutral Palette** – Choose colors (e.g., white, navy, charcoal, tan) that easily pair with one another.
2. **Limited Quantity** – Keep the total item count low enough to fit in a carry‑on and still allow a 24–48 hour self‑sufficiency backpack.
3. **Mix‑and‑Match** – Every top should work with every bottom; layers should be interchangeable.
4. **Laundry Frequency** – Design for one full laundry cycle per week, with a few spare items for emergencies.
5. **Minimal Duplicates** – Align with [[concepts/minimal-duplicate-strategy]] by owning only one set of each clothing type, avoiding multiple copies of bulky items.

## Typical Capsule for Mobile Living (from session‑37)
- **Tops**: 3 neutral shirts (white, navy, charcoal), 1 polo, 1 casual tee
- **Bottoms**: 2 chinos (navy, tan), 1 dark jean (site‑visit days)
- **Layers**: 1 merino crew, 1 blazer or polished overshirt
- **Shoes**: 1 business‑casual pair, 1 durable site‑appropriate pair
- **Undergarments**: 5 underwear, 5 socks (all dark neutrals)
- **Extras**: 1 workout outfit, lightweight jacket, quick‑dry towel, laundry soap sheets

These items occupy a **carry‑on** split across packing cubes (large for shirts/pants, medium for underwear and workout gear, small for layers) and leave a **backpack** with a spare change of clothes for 24–48 hours.

## Interaction with Minimal Duplicate Strategy
The capsule wardrobe minimizes duplicate clothing items while still providing redundancy for essential categories (e.g., duplicate chargers, toiletries). By limiting each garment to a single copy, the traveler reduces the risk of forgetting or misplacing items during rapid rotations.

## Implementation Tips
- **Label Packing Cubes**: Use generic labels ("Tops", "Bottoms", "Layers") to speed repacking.
- **Seasonal Swaps**: Store off‑season pieces in *Bin A* of the storage system; rotate them in as needed.
- **Quality over Quantity**: Choose durable, wrinkle‑resistant fabrics to extend wear between washes.
- **Checklist Integration**: Include the capsule list in the one‑page printable template from the source session.
