# Mobile Living System

The **Mobile Living System** is a practical methodology for maintaining a functional, low‑maintenance lifestyle while frequently moving between client sites or temporary accommodations. It organizes personal belongings into three layers—**Backpack Go‑Bag**, **Carry‑On Clothing Module**, and **Storage Bins**—and defines routines for arrival, departure, and day‑to‑day upkeep.

## Core Components

### 1. Backpack Go‑Bag (24–48 hr self‑sufficiency)
- One full outfit with spare underwear and socks
- Permanent toiletries kit
- Medications, glasses/contacts, small first‑aid kit
- Laptop + charger, phone cable, compact power strip, earbuds
- Travel wallet (passport/ID, badge, cards)
- Packable tote, laundry bag, eye mask/earplugs
- Water bottle and snack

### 2. Carry‑On Clothing Module (1‑week capsule)
- **Capsule wardrobe**: neutral shirts, polo, tee; chinos, dark jeans; merino crew, blazer/overshirt; business‑casual & site‑appropriate shoes; underwear, socks, workout outfit
- Lightweight jacket, quick‑dry towel, laundry soap sheets

### 3. Storage Bins & Inventory
- **Bin A** – “Seasonal + Clothing Swaps”
- **Bin B** – “Home‑Base Utilities + Backups”
- **Rarely‑Used Box** – long‑term or special gear
- Each lid holds a simple inventory sheet (Item — Qty — Status) to track items in or out.

## Minimal Duplicate Strategy
- Duplicate high‑use, low‑cost items: chargers, cables, toiletries, adapters, first‑aid kit.
- Avoid duplicating bulky or expensive items (shoes, jackets, work hardware). This principle aligns with [[concepts/minimal-duplicate-strategy]].

## Routines

### Arrival Routine
1. Empty backpack fully.
2. Hang/fold clothes; clear suitcase.
3. Plug in chargers; restock toiletries if needed.
4. Separate laundry.
5. Reset laptop/tech for next day.

### Departure Routine
1. Fully charge all devices.
2. Pack clothes by category (bottoms, tech, toiletries).
3. Verify meds, documents, badge, laptop.
4. Refill toiletry kit.
5. Final sweep of desk, bathroom, outlets, closet.

### Micro “Leave Lodging” Checklist
- Check outlets for chargers.
- Grab toiletries + meds from bathroom.
- Collect laptop, badge, passport/ID.
- Look under bed and in closet.
- Quick desk/nightstand sweep.
