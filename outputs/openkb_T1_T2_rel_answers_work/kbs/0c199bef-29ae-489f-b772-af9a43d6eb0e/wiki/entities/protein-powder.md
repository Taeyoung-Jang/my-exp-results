## Protein Powder

Protein powder is a dietary supplement in powdered form that provides a concentrated source of protein. It is commonly mixed into liquids such as milk, water, or yogurt to create a quick, high‑protein beverage or smoothie.

### References in session‑06_2024‑06‑11
- The document recommends adding a scoop of protein powder to a morning berry‑banana **smoothie** for a fast, balanced breakfast that helps avoid a mid‑morning energy crash.  
- This suggestion is part of the overall [[concepts/warm-up]] and [[concepts/cool-down]] routine, providing nutrition to support post‑run recovery.
