# Nut Butter

**Type:** product

Nut butter is a paste created by grinding roasted nuts into a smooth or crunchy spread. It is high in healthy fats, protein, and micronutrients, making it a popular choice for rapid, nutrient‑dense meals.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-11]]
- Suggested as a component of a **quick breakfast**: 1–2 Tbsp paired with a banana or apple and optional whole‑grain toast.
- Provides sustained energy to help avoid a mid‑morning crash after the 30‑minute run.
- Can be combined with other foods such as [[entities/berries]], [[entities/greek-yogurt]], or [[entities/protein-powder]] for a balanced snack.

## Related concepts
- Often mentioned alongside [[concepts/warm-up]] and [[concepts/cool-down]] routines as part of a **post‑run nutrition** strategy.
- Useful during a brief [[concepts/mobility-break]] for a quick bite without interrupting workflow.

## Usage tips
1. **Portion control:** Stick to 1–2 Tbsp to keep calories moderate while delivering protein and fat.
2. **Pairings:** Spread on [[entities/whole-grain-toast]] or drizzle over fruit for added texture and flavor.
3. **Storage:** Keep refrigerated after opening to maintain freshness and prevent oil separation.
