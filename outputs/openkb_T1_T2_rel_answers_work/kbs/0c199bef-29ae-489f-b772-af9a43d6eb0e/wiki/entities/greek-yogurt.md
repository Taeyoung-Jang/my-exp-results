# Greek Yogurt

Greek yogurt is a strained, high‑protein dairy product that provides a creamy base for quick, nutritious breakfasts. In the *session‑06_2024-06-11* routine it is recommended as one of three under‑5‑minute post‑run options.

## Key points from the document
- Portion: roughly **½ cup** (about 120 g) per serving.
- Paired with **½ cup mixed fruit** (e.g., berries) and a small handful of granola for carbs and texture.
- Offers sustained energy and helps avoid a mid‑morning crash after the morning run.
- Serves as a light, protein‑rich alternative to heavier meals, fitting the overall goal of staying lean and energized.
