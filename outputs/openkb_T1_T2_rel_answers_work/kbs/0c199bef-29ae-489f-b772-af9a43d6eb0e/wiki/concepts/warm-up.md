# Warm‑up

**Definition** – A short, progressive sequence of movements designed to increase blood flow, activate key muscle groups, and improve joint mobility before a physical activity (e.g., running, cycling, or a morning routine). It can be adapted to a brief 5‑minute knee‑friendly version when joint sensitivity is a concern.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-06_2024-06-11]])
| Time | Activity | Details |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0:00–2:00 | Brisk walk | Mobilises joints and raises core temperature. |
| 2:00–4:00 | Easy jog in place | Light cardio to further warm the cardiovascular system. |
| 4:00–5:00 | Leg swings (forward/back) | 10 controlled swings per leg, promoting hip flexion/extension. |
| 5:00–6:00 | Leg swings (side‑to‑side) | 10 swings per leg, opening the hip abductors. |
| 6:00–7:00 | Hip circles | 10 slow circles per leg, enhancing hip joint range of motion. |
| 7:00–8:00 | High‑knees | Easy pace, encouraging dynamic knee lift and core activation. |
| 8:00–9:00 | Butt‑kicks | Easy pace, stimulating hamstring activation. |
| 9:00–10:00 | Strides | Two 15‑second bursts at ~60‑70 % max effort, with 15‑20 s walk between to prime neuromuscular coordination. |

**Alternative 5‑minute knee‑friendly warm‑up (from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-12]])**
| Time | Activity | Details |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0:00–2:00 | Gentle marching / ankle circles | Light leg lift and ankle mobility to cue the knee without load. |
| 2:00–4:00 | Easy leg swings (forward/back) | 8‑10 controlled swings per leg, keeping range modest. |
| 4:00–5:00 | Slow static stretch of quad & calf | Hold each stretch ~10 s to prepare the knee joint. |

## Purpose and Benefits
- **Gradual heart‑rate increase** reduces cardiovascular shock.
- **Dynamic joint movement** prepares hips, knees, and ankles for the upcoming activity, reducing injury risk.
- **Neuromuscular priming** via high‑knees, butt‑kicks, and short strides improves coordination.
- **Knee‑friendly option** offers joint protection while still activating circulation, supporting consistent morning routines.
- **Habit formation aid** – a brief, repeatable block that can be anchored to existing cues (e.g., coffee maker) to strengthen [[concepts/habit-formation]] and fit into a [[concepts/morning-routine]].
