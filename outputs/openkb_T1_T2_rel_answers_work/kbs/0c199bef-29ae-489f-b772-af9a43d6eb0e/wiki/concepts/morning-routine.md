# Morning Routine

## Overview
A **morning routine** is a repeatable series of low‑intensity activities performed at the start of the day to cue the body and mind for a healthy shift into daily life. It balances light exercise, recovery, and practical cues (e.g., coffee, shower) to create a sustainable habit without over‑exertion.

## Core Elements (as described in [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-12]])
| Phase | Typical Duration | Activities |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| **Warm‑up** | 5 min (or 7 min on red days) | Gentle joint circles, leg swings, easy marching – prepares the [[entities/knee]] and increases blood flow. |
| **Main Exercise** | 20‑35 min (bike) | Light spinning on a [[entities/stationary-bike]] at easy resistance; serves as joint lubrication rather than a workout. |
| **Settle / Cool‑down** | 5‑10 min | Slow pedal deceleration, deep breathing, transition to floor. |
| **Stretch & Mobility** | 8‑15 min | Slow stretches for quads, calves, hips; can be framed as a [[concepts/mobility-break]]. |
| **Shower / Reset** | 5‑10 min | Final transition back to the day; marks the end of the unavailable window. |

## Timing Templates
- **45‑minute green**: 0‑5 min warm‑up, 5‑25 min bike, 25‑30 min settle, 30‑38 min stretch, 38‑45 min shower.
- **45‑minute yellow**: 0‑5 min warm‑up, 5‑18 min bike, 18‑23 min settle, 23‑32 min stretch, 32‑45 min shower.
- **45‑minute red**: 0‑7 min gentle warm‑up, 7‑15 min very light bike, 15‑20 min settle, 20‑30 min easy stretch, 30‑45 min shower.
- **60‑minute green**: 0‑5 min warm‑up, 5‑35 min bike, 35‑40 min settle, 40‑52 min stretch, 52‑60 min shower.
- **60‑minute yellow**: 0‑5 min warm‑up, 5‑25 min bike, 25‑30 min settle, 30‑45 min stretch, 45‑60 min shower.
- **60‑minute red**: 0‑7 min gentle warm‑up, 7‑17 min very light bike, 17‑22 min settle, 22‑40 min easy stretch, 40‑60 min shower.

## Adjustment Framework
The routine uses a **[[concepts/green-yellow-red-adjustment]]** system to modulate intensity based on knee health:
- **Green** – Knee feels smooth; follow the full “green” schedule.
- **Yellow** – Mild stiffness; shorten the bike segment.
- **Red** – Sharp pain or swelling; keep bike time minimal and focus on warm‑up and stretch.

## Habit Formation
Embedding the routine relies on **[[concepts/habit-formation]]** principles:
1. **Anchor** the start to an existing cue (e.g., coffee kettle or phone check). 
2. Keep the sequence **simple and repeatable** so it can be performed even when tired.
3. Prioritize **consistency over duration**; a short version on red days still preserves the habit loop.

## Recovery & Reset
The post‑exercise stretch and shower act as a **recovery** phase, allowing heart rate and joint load to return to baseline before the rest of the day begins. This reset reinforces the routine as a non‑stressful ritual.
