# Green‑Yellow‑Red Adjustment

**Definition**: A quick pre‑workout check that categorises knee health into three states—green, yellow, and red—and scales the morning exercise routine accordingly.

## How It Works
1. **Green** – Knee feels smooth, no pain or lingering soreness. Follow the full [[concepts/morning-routine]] as planned.
2. **Yellow** – Slight stiffness, warmth, or minor discomfort that improves with a minute of gentle motion. Reduce the central bike segment while keeping warm‑up and cool‑down intact.
3. **Red** – Sharp pain, swelling, instability, or discomfort that does not ease quickly. Switch to a very brief, low‑intensity spin and extend the warm‑up/stretch phases.

## Key Components
- **Warm‑up**: A brief, low‑impact series of leg swings, ankle circles, or marching to prepare the [[entities/knee]]. Linked to the [[concepts/warm-up]] concept.
- **Core Activity**: Primarily the [[entities/stationary-bike]] session, which serves as a gentle joint lubricant.
- **Cool‑down**: Slow stretching for quads, calves, hips, plus deep breathing—acts as a [[concepts/mobility-break]].
- **Habit Formation**: By anchoring the adjustment to existing cues (e.g., coffee kettle), the system supports consistent behavior, aligning with the [[concepts/habit-formation]] principle.

## Example Application (from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-12]])
- **45‑minute Green**: 5 min warm‑up → 20 min bike → 5 min settle → 8 min stretch → 7 min shower.
- **45‑minute Yellow**: 5 min warm‑up → 13 min bike → 5 min settle → 9 min stretch → 13 min shower.
- **45‑minute Red**: 7 min gentle warm‑up → 8 min light bike → 5 min settle → 10 min easy stretch → 15 min shower.

The same pattern scales to a 60‑minute window, simply extending each block proportionally.

## Benefits
- **Safety**: Protects the knee by matching effort to daily joint condition.
- **Flexibility**: Allows the routine to stay intact even on off‑days.
- **Consistency**: Keeps the habit loop intact, reinforcing the [[concepts/morning-routine]] habit.
