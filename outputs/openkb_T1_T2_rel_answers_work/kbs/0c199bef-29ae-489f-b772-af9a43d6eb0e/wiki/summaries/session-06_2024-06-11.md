# Summary of session‑06_2024-06-11

**Main contribution** – A concise, minute‑by‑minute routine for a morning run, including a 10‑minute [[concepts/warm-up]], a 5‑minute [[concepts/cool-down]], specific hip‑flexor and hamstring stretch cues, quick‑prep breakfast options, and a 2‑minute desk [[concepts/mobility-break]] to combat stiffness.

## Warm‑up (0:00–10:00)
| Time | Activity | Details |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0:00–2:00 | Brisk walk | Joint mobilization. |
| 2:00–4:00 | Easy jog in place | Light cardio. |
| 4:00–5:00 | Leg swings (forward/back) | 10 reps each leg. |
| 5:00–6:00 | Leg swings (side‑to‑side) | 10 reps each leg. |
| 6:00–7:00 | Hip circles | 10 slow circles per leg. |
| 7:00–8:00 | High‑knees | Easy pace, 30 s. |
| 8:00–9:00 | Butt‑kicks | Easy pace, 30 s. |
| 9:00–10:00 | Strides | Two 15‑s bursts at ~60‑70 % max effort; walk 15‑20 s between. |

## Cool‑down (0:00–5:00)
| Time | Activity | Details |
|------|----------|---------|
| 0:00–1:00 | Light jog | Slow pace. |
| 1:00–2:00 | Walk | Gradual slowdown. |
| 2:00–3:00 | Right hamstring stretch | Soft knee, long spine, hold 30 s. |
| 3:00–4:00 | Left hamstring stretch | Same cues. |
| 4:00–4:30 | Right hip‑flexor stretch (kneeling) | Pelvis tucked, ribs down, hold 30 s. |
| 4:30–5:00 | Left hip‑flexor stretch | Same cues. |

## Stretch cues (static stretching)
- **Hamstring**: hinge from hips, keep spine neutral, avoid rounding.
- **Hip‑flexor**: draw front hip upward‑forward, keep ribs down to prevent arching.
- Core principle: *“lengthen, don’t pull.”*

## Breakfast options (≤5 min)
1. **Berry‑banana protein smoothie** – 1 cup **[[entities/berries]]**, ½ banana, 1 scoop **[[entities/protein-powder]]**, ¾ cup milk or **[[entities/greek-yogurt]]** for texture.  
2. **Greek yogurt bowl** – ½ cup **[[entities/greek-yogurt]]**, ½ cup mixed fruit, small handful granola.  
3. **Nut‑butter toast** – 1 slice **[[entities/whole-grain-toast]]**, 1–2 Tbsp **[[entities/nut-butter]]**, plus a banana or apple.

All provide sustained energy and avoid a mid‑morning crash.

## Desk mobility break (2 min)
1. **March in place** – 30 s.  
2. **Shoulder/upper‑back rolls** – 30 s.  
3. **Standing hip circles** – 30 s.  
4. **Standing quad stretch** – 15 s each leg.

## Frequently asked details
- **Stride effort** – Aim for ~60‑70 % of max effort; keep stride smooth, not taxing.  
- **Stiffness after sitting** – Prefer a brief dynamic mobility sequence (hip circles, leg swings, light forward fold) before static stretching.
