# Summary of Session 32 – Gentle Morning Routine for Heart Health  

**Description:** A 15–20 minute “gentle start” morning routine tailored for cardiovascular wellbeing, especially for individuals on beta‑blockers, with a no‑stimulation checklist and fallback plan.  

## Core Routine (≈15 min)  
1. **Sit at the bedside** ~ 1 min, breathing slowly.  
2. **Sip a glass of water** ~ 2‑3 min.  
3. **Gentle movement** (neck rolls, shoulder rolls, arm stretch) ~ 5 min.  
4. **Soft light exposure** near a window ~ 5 min.  
5. **Quiet closure** ~ 2‑3 min.  

## Adjusted for [[concepts/beta_blocker_constraints]]  
- No stimulating activities in the first 30‑60 min.  
- Walk is kept very easy; lighting kept low.  

## Alternative Options for “Off” Mornings  
- **Option A:** Longer seated breathing, shorter walk.  
- **Option B:** Skip walk, extend hydration and light exposure.  

## No‑Stimulation Checklist  

| **Avoid** | **Replace** |
|---|---|
| Coffee / other caffeine | Warm (not hot) face rinse |
| Very hot shower | Soft lighting |
| Intense or fast movement | Easy stretching / slow walk |
| Loud music | Gentle background sound |
| Bright overhead lights | Soft natural light |
| Phone, news, messages | Quiet breathing |
| Rushed multitasking | Small calm action (open curtains) |

*Coffee / other caffeine* can be linked to [[concepts/caffeine_tapering]] if you wish to phase it out gradually.  

## Fallback 3‑Step Plan for Extra‑Sensitive Mornings  
1. Sit quietly + sip water.  
2. Allow soft light, breathe slowly.  
3. Move only if comfortable, keeping it brief.
