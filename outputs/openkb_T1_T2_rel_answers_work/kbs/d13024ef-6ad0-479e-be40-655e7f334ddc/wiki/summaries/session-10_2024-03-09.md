# Summary of session‑10_2024-03-09

**Main contribution**: A concrete, three‑week tapering schedule to reduce morning caffeine intake while maintaining alertness, including specific drink choices for home drip vs. café latte, rescue‑drink guidelines, and non‑caffeine strategies for mid‑day energy slumps.

## Key Concepts

- **Gradual dose reduction** – start with half‑caff, then quarter‑caff, then a mostly decaf baseline.  
- **Ritual substitution** – keep the coffee‑time ritual but swap the beverage strength or type. [[concepts/ritual_substitution]]  
- **Hydration & timing** – drink water before caffeine and delay the first sip by ~30 minutes.  
- **Rescue drinks** – low‑caffeine or decaf options (iced tea, half‑decaf cold brew, small decaf latte with a splash of regular) that prevent a full‑dose rollback.  
- **Non‑caffeine alertness tricks** – short walks, deep‑breathing, cold water, protein snacks, and micro‑stretching for 2‑3 pm meeting focus. [[concepts/non_caffeine_energy_boosts]]

## Weekly Schedule Overview

| Week | Morning drink (home) | Morning drink (café) | Afternoon rescue (if needed) |
|------|----------------------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| **1** | 12 oz half‑caff drip | 16 oz latte, 1 shot | Small iced tea or half‑decaf cold brew |
| **2** | 12 oz quarter‑caff drip | Small latte, 1 shot | Small iced tea or half‑cup of morning drink |
| **3** | 12 oz mostly decaf drip (splash of regular) | Small latte, 1 shot **or** black tea | Decaf latte or decaf cold brew with 1 oz regular coffee |

**Rescue Options**  
- Iced tea – light caffeine, familiar ritual.  
- Half‑decaf cold brew – retains coffee flavor with much less caffeine.  
- Decaf latte + splash – a treat that adds only ~10‑15 mg caffeine.

## Handling Headaches & Fog

Treat them as withdrawal signals; pause the taper or repeat the current dose level before stepping down again. Increase water intake, add a brief walk, or shift caffeine timing earlier.

## Non‑Caffeine Mid‑Day Boosts

- **Physical reset**: 2‑minute brisk walk or stair climb.  
- **Breathing**: 4‑7‑8 deep‑breath cycle.  
- **Cold water**: a full glass right before the meeting.  
- **Protein snack**: yogurt, nuts, or a boiled egg.  
- **Micro‑stretch**: shoulder rolls, upper‑back stretch.  

These techniques increase alertness without adding caffeine. [[concepts/caffeine_tapering]] can be used to track progress through the three‑week plan.
