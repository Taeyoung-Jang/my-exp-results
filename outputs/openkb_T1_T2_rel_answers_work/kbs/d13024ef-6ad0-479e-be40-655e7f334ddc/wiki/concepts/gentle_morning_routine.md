# Gentle Morning Routine

**Definition**: A low‑stress, heart‑friendly morning sequence that eases the body into activity, emphasizing hydration, gentle movement, soft light, and quiet reflection.

**Key Elements** (≈15‑20 min):
1. **Slow wake‑up** – Sit on the edge of the bed for about 1 minute, taking calm breaths.
2. **Hydration** – Sip a glass of water over the next 2‑3 minutes.
3. **Gentle movement** – 5 minutes of neck rolls, shoulder rolls, arm stretches, or a very slow walk.
4. **Soft light exposure** – Spend 5 minutes near a window or under soft lighting.
5. **Quiet closure** – 2‑3 minutes of stillness, soft music, or mindful breathing.

**Adaptation for Beta‑Blocker Users**: Avoid stimulating activities for the first 30‑60 minutes; keep the walk very easy and lighting low. See [[concepts/beta_blocker_constraints]].

**Optional Softer Versions**:
- **Option A** – Extend seated breathing (2‑3 minutes) and shorten the walk.
- **Option B** – Skip the walk entirely; extend hydration and soft‑light exposure.

**No‑Stimulation Checklist**: Items to avoid and calm replacements are outlined in [[concepts/no_stimulation_checklist]].

**Fallback Plan** (3 steps for extra‑sensitive mornings):
1. Sit quietly and sip water.
2. Allow soft light, breathe slowly until settled.
3. Move only if comfortable, keeping it brief.
