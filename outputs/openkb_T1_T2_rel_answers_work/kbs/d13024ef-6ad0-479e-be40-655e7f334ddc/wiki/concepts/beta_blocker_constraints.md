# Beta‑Blocker Constraints on Morning Activities

**Definition**

Constraints that advise avoiding any sudden physiological stimulation during the first 30–60 minutes after waking for individuals taking beta‑blockers. The goal is to protect cardiovascular stability by keeping the early‑morning routine calm, low‑intensity, and gradually progressive.

**Key Constraints**
- **No caffeine or other stimulants** (e.g., coffee, strong tea) during the initial half‑hour.
- **Avoid very hot showers** or other rapid temperature changes.
- **Skip intense or fast movement**; opt for a very slow walk or light stretching.
- **Limit loud music, bright overhead lights, and screen exposure** (phone, news, messages).
- **Do not rush or multitask**; maintain a quiet, low‑stress environment.

**Practical Adjustments to a Gentle Morning Routine**
- Follow a slowed version of the [[concepts/gentle_morning_routine]]: sit up slowly, sip water, take a short easy walk, enjoy soft natural light, then close with a brief quiet moment.
- Provide optional softer pathways for days when the heart feels “off”:
  - **Option A:** Extend seated breathing, shorten the walk.
  - **Option B:** Skip the walk entirely, focusing on hydration, soft light, and slow breathing.
