# No‑Stimulation Checklist for Early Morning

**Purpose**

A practical set of recommendations to keep the early morning low‑stress and heart‑friendly, especially for individuals on [[concepts/beta_blocker_constraints]] or those seeking a gentle start as described in [[summaries/session-32_2024-11-09]].

**Avoid**
- Coffee or other caffeine‑containing drinks
- Very hot showers
- Intense or fast movement
- Loud music or sudden noises
- Bright overhead lighting
- Phone, news feeds, or messaging apps
- Any rushed or multitasking activity

**Replace**
- Warm (not hot) face rinse or quick wash
- Soft, dimmed lighting
- Easy stretching or a slow, relaxed walk
- Gentle background sounds (e.g., soft instrumental music)
- Natural light from a window or a softly lit area
- Water or caffeine‑free herbal tea
- One small, calming action such as opening curtains or straightening the bedside table

**Relation to Other Concepts**
- Forms part of a broader [[concepts/gentle_morning_routine]].
- Complements [[concepts/caffeine_tapering]] by specifying when to hold off caffeine.
- Can be implemented through [[concepts/ritual_substitution]] by swapping stimulating habits with calm alternatives.
- Supports strategies from [[concepts/non_caffeine_energy_boosts]] for gentle energy without stimulants.

**Usage Tips**
1. Review the checklist during the first 30–60 minutes after waking.
2. Choose replacements that fit your environment and personal preferences.
3. Adjust the list as needed for health conditions or medication constraints.
