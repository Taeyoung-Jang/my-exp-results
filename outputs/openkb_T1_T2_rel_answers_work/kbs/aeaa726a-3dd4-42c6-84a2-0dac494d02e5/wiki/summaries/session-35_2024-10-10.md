# Summary of session-35_2024-10-10

**Main contribution**: Provides practical advice for a dancer choosing between structured classes and home‑video learning, and delivers a concrete 7‑day tapering schedule to optimise timing, coordination and stamina before a weekend workshop.

## Key ideas
- **Structured vs. video learning** – A hybrid approach is recommended: take an intro‑level class for feedback and habit formation, then reinforce with targeted home‑video practice. [[concepts/structured_vs_video_learning]]
- **Practice tapering** – Lightening volume while maintaining focus preserves freshness and prevents burnout. The taper involves shortening sessions and reducing intensity, especially in the last 48 hours. [[concepts/tapering_practice]]
- **Focus dimensions** – Sessions are split among *timing drills*, *full run‑throughs*, and *conditioning* (core, mobility) to balance technical precision with endurance. [[concepts/timing_drills]]

## 7‑day pre‑workshop plan (overview)
| Day | Duration | Primary focus |
|-----|----------|----------------|
| **Mon** | ~1 h | Relaxed run‑throughs, timing & transitions |
| **Tue** | ~75 min | Warm‑up, 80 % effort run‑throughs, musicality |
| **Wed** | ~90 min | Full‑power run, moderate repeat, detail polishing |
| **Thu** | ~1 h | Light marking, timing drills, gentle conditioning |
| **Fri** | ~45 min | Rhythm drills, small section work, stay loose |
| **Sat** | ~30 min | Slow walk‑through, stretch, mental review |
| **Sun** | Workshop day | Minimal warm‑up, mental rehearsal only |

The plan emphasizes a progressive reduction in volume (taper) while shifting the mental focus from “pushing” to “polishing.” This aims to keep the dancer’s timing sharp, stamina adequate, and energy reserves high for the workshop.
