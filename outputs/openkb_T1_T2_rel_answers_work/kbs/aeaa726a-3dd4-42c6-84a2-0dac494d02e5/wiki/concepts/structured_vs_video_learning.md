# Structured vs. Video Learning

**Definition**: A hybrid learning approach that combines the feedback and habit‑building of structured, instructor‑led classes with the flexibility and repeatability of self‑directed video practice.

## Why combine both?
- **Structured classes** provide:
  - Real‑time correction of form and technique.
  - Guidance on timing, coordination, and safe progression.
  - A disciplined routine that helps establish good movement habits.
- **Video learning at home** offers:
  - Unlimited ability to pause, rewind, and repeat challenging sections.
  - Low‑pressure environment to experiment and build stamina at one’s own pace.
  - Convenience for reinforcing concepts learned in class.

## Recommended strategy (from [[summaries/session-35_2024-10-10]])
1. **Start with an intro‑level class** to acquire foundational skills and receive personalized feedback.
2. **Reinforce each class** by practicing short, focused video routines at home, emphasizing timing and musicality.
3. **Adjust focus over time**: shift from “pushing” in class to “polishing” during video sessions as proficiency grows.
