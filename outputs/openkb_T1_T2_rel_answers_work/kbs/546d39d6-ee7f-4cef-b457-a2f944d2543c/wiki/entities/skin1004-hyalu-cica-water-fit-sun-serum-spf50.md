# Skin1004 Hyalu‑Cica Water‑Fit Sun Serum SPF 50

**Type:** product

**Description:** An alcohol‑free, fragrance‑free, SPF 50 water‑based sunscreen serum that provides a soft, natural finish and is suitable for tan to deeper skin tones.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]]
- **SPF level:** 50, meeting strong daily sun‑exposure needs.
- **Formulation:** Water‑fit serum with hyaluronic acid and Cica for hydration and soothing; free of denatured alcohol and added fragrance.
- **Finish:** Soft, natural (non‑shiny) finish; not fully matte but comfortable on medium/tan skin.
- **Target skin type:** Normal‑to‑dry, fragrance‑sensitive skin that prefers a lightweight, non‑greasy feel.
- **Reapplication advice:** Use a liquid or serum form for primary protection; sticks or powders can be used for quick touch‑ups but do not replace the serum.
