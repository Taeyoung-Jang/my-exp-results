## La Roche‑Posay Anthelios UVmune 400 Oil Control

**Type:** Product (sunscreen)

### Overview
La Roche‑Posay Anthelios UVmune 400 Oil Control is a high‑SPF (SPF 50) chemical sunscreen marketed for oily or combination skin. It is formulated to provide a matte finish and oil‑control benefits.

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]]
- **SPF:** 50 (broad‑spectrum UV protection).
- **Texture:** Fluid/gel‑cream that aims for a dry‑touch, matte appearance.
- **Fragrance:** Advertised as fragrance‑free.
- **Alcohol Content:** Contains denatured alcohol/ethanol, which disqualifies it from the user’s strict "alcohol‑free" requirement mentioned in the session.
- **Suitability:** Recommended initially for its matte finish, but later identified as unsuitable for fragrance‑ and alcohol‑sensitive skin.

### Usage in a [[concepts/sun-protection-routine]]
1. **Cleanse** the face.
2. Apply a lightweight, fragrance‑free moisturizer.
3. Apply a thin, even layer of Anthelios UVmune 400 Oil Control (approximately two finger‑strip lengths for face and neck).
4. Reapply every 2‑3 hours when outdoors, following [[concepts/sunscreen-reapplication-guidelines]].

### Relationship to Other Sunscreens
- Compared to **[[entities/etude-sunprise-mild-airy-finish-spf50]]**, which is fully alcohol‑free and matte, Anthelios is less suitable for users avoiding alcohol.
- **[[entities/beauty-of-joseon-relief-sun-spf50]]** and **[[entities/round-lab-birch-juice-moisturizing-sunscreen-spf50]]** are alternatives that meet the fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free criteria.

### Relevance to [[concepts/matte-sunscreen-texture]]
Anthelios UVmune 400 Oil Control targets a dry‑touch, matte finish, aligning with the concept of matte sunscreen textures. However, its alcohol content can be drying for normal‑to‑dry skin types.

### Practical Considerations
- **Pros:** High SPF, oil‑control, matte feel, fragrance‑free.
- **Cons:** Contains alcohol, which may cause stinging or dryness for sensitive skin.
- **Recommendation:** Suitable for users without alcohol sensitivity; otherwise, consider alternatives like Etude Sunprise Mild Airy Finish or Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Sunscreen.
