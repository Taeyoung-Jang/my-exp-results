# Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Sunscreen SPF 50

**Type:** Product (sunscreen)

## Key Attributes
- **SPF:** 50 broad‑spectrum protection
- **Formulation:** Fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free gel‑cream
- **Finish:** Natural, non‑shiny (not fully matte) – provides a subtle, skin‑like look
- **Cast:** No white cast on medium‑to‑tan skin tones
- **Skin Type Suitability:** Normal‑to‑dry, fragrance‑sensitive skin

## Recommended Use (as described in [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]])
- **Morning routine:** Apply after a lightweight gel‑cream moisturizer as part of a [[concepts/sun-protection-routine]].
- **Amount:** About two finger‑strip lengths (index + middle) for the face and neck; spread evenly.
- **Reapplication:** Every 2–3 hours when outdoors, following [[concepts/sunscreen-reapplication-guidelines]]. Carry a travel‑size tube for quick touch‑ups; sticks or powders are less thorough.

## Comparison to Other Options Mentioned in the Same Session
- Similar alcohol‑free, fragrance‑free profile to [[entities/etude-sunprise-mild-airy-finish-spf50]] (more matte) and [[entities/beauty-of-joseon-relief-sun-spf50]] (natural finish).
- Unlike [[entities/la-roche-posay-antihelios-uvmune-400-oil-control]] and [[entities/bioderma-photoderm-gel-cream-dry-touch]], it does **not** contain denatured alcohol.

## Relevant Concepts
- [[concepts/matte-sunscreen-texture]] – while this product offers a natural finish, it is less matte than dedicated matte formulas.
- [[concepts/sun-protection-routine]] – fits into a simple AM routine with a gel‑cream moisturizer.
