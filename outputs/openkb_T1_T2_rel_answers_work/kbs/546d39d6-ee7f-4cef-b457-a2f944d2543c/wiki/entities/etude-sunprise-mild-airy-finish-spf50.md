# Etude Sunprise Mild Airy Finish SPF 50

**Type:** product

Etude Sunprise Mild Airy Finish SPF 50 is a chemical sunscreen formulated to be fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free, and to dry down with a matte finish, making it suitable for medium to tan skin tones without leaving a white cast.

## Key Features
- **SPF:** 50, broad‑spectrum UV protection
- **Texture:** Gel‑cream / fluid that provides a matte, non‑shiny finish
- **Free of:** fragrance, denatured alcohol/ethanol, and other potential irritants
- **Finish:** Described as the most matte among the recommended options in the conversation

## Recommended Use (from [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]])
- **Amount:** Apply roughly two finger‑strip lengths (index + middle) to cover face and neck, spreading evenly.
- **Reapplication:** Every 2–3 hours when outdoors, or after sweating; a travel‑size tube can be used for quick touch‑ups.
- **Routine Placement:** Use after a lightweight, fragrance‑free moisturizer as part of a [[concepts/sun-protection-routine]].
