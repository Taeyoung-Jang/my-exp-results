# Summary of session-13_2024-07-15

**Topic:** Developing a lightweight, non‑greasy sun‑protection routine for normal‑to‑dry, fragrance‑sensitive skin, with emphasis on SPF 50, fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free, matte‑finish sunscreens that do not leave a white cast on tan skin.

## Key Recommendations
- **Sunscreen type:** Prefer modern chemical sunscreens in gel‑cream or fluid textures for quick absorption and a matte finish; mineral options can work but often leave a cast. See [[concepts/matte-sunscreen-texture]] for details.
- **SPF level:** SPF 30–50 is safe for strong daily sun; the user specifically wants SPF 50.
- **Reapplication:** Follow [[concepts/sunscreen-reapplication-guidelines]] – every 2–3 hours when outdoors; more frequent if sweating. Sticks/powders are convenient for touch‑ups but less thorough than liquids.
- **Application amount:** Approx. two finger‑strip lengths (index + middle) for face + neck, spread evenly.
- **Moisturizer:** Fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free gel‑cream with hyaluronic acid, glycerin, ceramides; oil‑free for a light feel.
- **After‑sun care:** Aloe or panthenol gel for soothing without greasiness.

## Specific Sunscreen Options (All SPF 50, fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free, no white cast)
1. **[[entities/etude-sunprise-mild-airy-finish-spf50]]** – truly alcohol‑free, very matte finish.  
2. **[[entities/round-lab-birch-juice-moisturizing-sunscreen-spf50]]** – alcohol‑free, natural (non‑shiny) finish.  
3. **[[entities/beauty-of-joseon-relief-sun-spf50]]** – alcohol‑free, fragrance‑free, natural finish (not fully matte).  
4. **[[entities/skin1004-hyalu-cica-water-fit-sun-serum-spf50]]** – alcohol‑free, fragrance‑free, soft natural finish suitable for deeper tones.

## Sunscreen Sticks & Powders
- **Sticks:** Require 10–15 swipes per area for coverage comparable to liquids; best for quick touch‑ups, not primary protection.
- **Powders:** Cannot reliably deliver full SPF; useful only for minor top‑ups over makeup or oily zones, but a liquid sunscreen layer remains essential.

## Routine Overview
- **AM:** Cleanse → lightweight gel‑cream moisturizer → matte/soft‑matte SPF 50 sunscreen (fluid or gel‑cream). This follows the [[concepts/sun-protection-routine]] framework.
- **Mid‑day:** Reapply sunscreen every 2–3 hours; use travel tube or stick for convenience.
- **PM:** Cleanse → slightly richer fragrance‑free moisturizer (optional after‑sun gel if skin feels tight).
