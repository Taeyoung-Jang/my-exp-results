## Summary

This document provides a **print‑ready, customizable checklist** for safely walking a dog on a waterfront boardwalk when fog, tide, and wind pose hazards. It outlines:

- **Key safety thresholds** (tide height, visibility distance, wind speed) with suggested starter values and blank fields for user‑specific data. [[concepts/tide-monitoring]] [[concepts/visibility-estimation]] (wind thresholds are described in plain text)
- **Step‑by‑step routine** covering:
  - *Before you leave*: check tide, visibility, wind, and attach gear.
  - *At the entrance*: inspect boardwalk condition and verify visibility to a landmark.
  - *During the walk*: monitor fog thickening and wind changes, and abort to the backup route if needed. [[concepts/dog-walk-safety]]
- **Gear recommendations** for low‑visibility conditions (dog‑light, reflective items, waterproof grip shoes). (visibility gear described in plain text)
- **Quick visibility test** methods using known landmarks, parked‑car lengths, or residential lot widths to estimate yards without tools. (field estimation techniques described in plain text)
- **Simple backup route strategy**: a predefined inland loop that avoids any reliance on seeing the waterline, automatically triggered when any threshold is exceeded or when the boardwalk appears wet or sounds of water are heard. (backup route planning described in plain text)

The template is designed as a **single‑page bullet list** with fill‑in blanks, making it easy to print, customize, and follow in real time.
