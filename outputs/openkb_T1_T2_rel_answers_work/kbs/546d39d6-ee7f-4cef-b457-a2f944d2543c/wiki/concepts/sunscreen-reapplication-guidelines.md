# Sunscreen Reapplication Guidelines

## Definition
Sunscreen reapplication guidelines describe the recommended timing, quantity, and methods for applying sunscreen again after the initial layer to maintain the intended SPF protection throughout sun exposure.

## Frequency
- **Outdoor exposure:** Reapply every **2–3 hours** when continuously outdoors, especially if sweating, swimming, or towel‑drying.
- **Intermittent exposure:** If you move between indoor and outdoor environments, aim to reapply **every 2 hours** while you are outside. Inside, a full reapplication is usually unnecessary unless you have been sweating or have rubbed the sunscreen off.
- **High‑intensity sunlight:** In regions with clear skies and intense UV (as noted in [[summaries/session-13_2024-07-15]]), err on the shorter end of the interval.

## Amount per Reapplication
- Use roughly the same amount as the initial application: **two finger‑strip lengths** (index + middle) for the face and neck combined.
- For a more precise measure, apply **0.5 mg/cm²** of skin, which translates to about **1 mL** (a pea‑sized dollop) for the entire face and neck.

## Reapplication Methods
| Method | Coverage Quality | Practical Notes |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Liquid/cream/gels** (travel tube) | Highest – uniform layer, full SPF | Preferred for primary reapplication; easy to spread evenly. |
| **Sticks** | Moderate – good for touch‑ups | Requires **10–15 swipes** per area to approach the protection level of a liquid. Best for quick fixes, not a full replacement. |
| **Powders** | Low – insufficient product to achieve full SPF | Useful only for minor top‑ups over makeup or oily zones; must be layered over a liquid sunscreen for real protection. |

## Practical Tips
1. **Apply before stepping outside**, allowing the sunscreen to absorb (usually 2–5 minutes) before exposure.
2. **Carry a travel‑size tube** of your chosen SPF 50, fragrance‑free, alcohol‑free sunscreen for easy reapplication.
3. If you use a **stick**, keep it in a purse or pocket and remember the ten‑to‑fifteen swipe rule.
4. For **powder sunscreens**, treat them as a supplemental layer; they do not replace the liquid base.
5. Reapply **after swimming, sweating, or towel‑drying**, regardless of the time interval.
