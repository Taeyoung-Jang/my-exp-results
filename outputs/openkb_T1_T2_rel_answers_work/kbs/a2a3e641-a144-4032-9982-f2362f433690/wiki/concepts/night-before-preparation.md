# Night‑Before Preparation

**Definition**: Night‑Before Preparation is the practice of assembling or portioning breakfast ingredients ahead of time (typically the evening before) so that the morning routine can be completed quickly, without cooking, and without the need for a phone.

## Why It Matters
- Reduces decision fatigue and friction early in the day.
- Supports a [[concepts/phone-free-morning]] by keeping the phone out of reach until after the meal.
- Frees mental space for a calm [[concepts/quiet-pause]] and clearer intention setting.
- Aligns with the broader habit of using gentle anchors like stretching and hydration to start the day.

## Typical Night‑Before Recipes (from [[summaries/session-02_2025-06-15]])
1. **Overnight Oats** – Combine rolled oats, milk (or plant‑based alternative), chia seeds, and sweetener in a jar; store in the fridge. In the morning, just stir and eat.
2. **Yogurt Parfait Cup** – Layer yogurt and fruit the night before; keep granola separate and add it just before eating to retain crunch.
3. **Pre‑Portioned Nut‑Butter & Fruit** – Pack single‑serve portions of peanut or almond butter with sliced banana or apple wedges in a small container.
4. **Ready‑Made Smoothie Packs** – Portion frozen fruit, greens, and protein powder in a zip‑lock bag; in the morning, blend with liquid.

## How to Implement
1. **Choose 2–3 go‑to options** that you enjoy and that meet the <5‑minute criterion.
2. **Set a reminder** (or use a habit‑stack) to assemble the components after dinner.
3. **Store in accessible containers** (e.g., mason jars, reusable snack bags) that can be grabbed straight from the fridge.
4. **Keep the phone in another room** until after you finish the pre‑prepared breakfast, reinforcing the [[concepts/phone-free-morning]] habit.

## Connection to Other Concepts
- The preparation step complements [[concepts/light-movement]] and [[concepts/gentle-anchors]] by smoothing the transition from sleep to activity.
- It also dovetails with [[concepts/intention-setting]]: after eating, you can quickly note your daily priority without distraction.
