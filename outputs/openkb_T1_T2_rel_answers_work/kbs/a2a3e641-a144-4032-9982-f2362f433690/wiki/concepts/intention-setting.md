# Intention Setting

**Definition**: A brief, focused declaration of the most important task or mindset you want to carry into the day.

**Purpose in morning routines**: After completing a phone‑free breakfast, taking a moment to name one priority helps translate the calm established by [[concepts/gentle-anchors]] and [[concepts/quiet-pause]] into actionable focus for the rest of the day.

**How it’s applied in [[summaries/session-02_2025-06-15]]**
- At minute 40‑60 of the 60‑minute routine (or the final ten minutes of the 30‑minute version), pause after getting dressed.
- Silently state or jot down one concrete goal (e.g., “complete project draft”, “stay present in meetings”).
- Use this intention as a mental cue while you transition to checking your phone.

**Relationship to other concepts**
- Works with [[concepts/gentle-anchors]] to create a smooth start.
- Complements [[concepts/light-movement]] by shifting mental energy after physical wake‑up.
- Reinforced by the [[concepts/phone-free-morning]] rule, preventing distraction before the intention is set.
- Can be paired with [[concepts/night-before-preparation]] breakfasts, allowing the intention moment to occur without hunger or haste.

**Practical tips**
1. Keep a small notebook or a note app ready for a one‑sentence entry.
2. Phrase the intention positively and specifically.
3. Review it briefly before you begin work to keep focus.
