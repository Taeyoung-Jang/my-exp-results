# Summary of session-02_2025-06-15

## Core Goal
Provide practical, low‑effort habits and a structured **first‑hour** routine that promotes a calmer, more focused morning while avoiding phone use until after breakfast.

## Key Recommendations
- **Gentle anchors**: stretch, deep breath, drink water immediately after waking. [[concepts/gentle-anchors]]
- **Movement**: light walking or simple movements for 5‑10 minutes to wake the body. [[concepts/light-movement]]
- **Quiet pause**: sit, look out a window, or breathe for a few minutes to set mental tone. [[concepts/quiet-pause]]
- **Intention setting**: briefly note one priority for the day before getting dressed. [[concepts/intention-setting]]
- **Phone‑free rule**: keep the phone in another room until after breakfast; handle urges by acknowledging, breathing, and returning to the current step. [[concepts/phone-free-morning]]

## Minute‑by‑Minute Templates
### 60‑Minute Routine
| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0‑5 | Stretch, deep breath, drink water |
| 5‑15 | Light movement (walk, gentle stretches) |
| 15‑20 | Quiet pause (sit, look outside, breathe) |
| 20‑40 | Breakfast **phone‑free** |
| 40‑60 | Get dressed, tidy up, set daily intention, then check phone |

### 30‑Minute Routine (Rushed Days)
| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0‑3 | Stretch & hydrate |
| 3‑10 | Minimal movement |
| 10‑20 | Phone‑free breakfast |
| 20‑30 | Get ready, set intention, then allow phone use |

## No‑Cook Breakfast Ideas (<5 min)
- Yogurt with fruit or granola
- Cottage cheese with honey & berries
- Peanut‑butter toast with banana slices
- Pre‑made smoothie with frozen fruit & milk
- Granola with milk or plant‑based alternative

## Night‑Before Prep Breakfast Rotations
- **Overnight oats**: jar of oats, milk, chia, and flavorings ready to grab.
- **Yogurt parfait cup**: layer yogurt & fruit night before; add crunchy granola in the morning.
- **Pre‑portioned nut‑butter & fruit**: assemble snack‑size packets for quick assembly.

These prep options keep breakfast **no‑cook**, under five minutes, and varied enough to avoid repetition. [[concepts/night-before-preparation]]
