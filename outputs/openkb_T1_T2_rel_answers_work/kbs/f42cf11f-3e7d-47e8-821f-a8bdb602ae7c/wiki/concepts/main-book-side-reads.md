# Main Book + Side Reads

**Definition**: A reading strategy that designates one primary book as the default focus and permits a small number of optional side reads to maintain momentum during low‑energy moments.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Primary Book Selection**
   - Choose a "main" book based on current curiosity and make it the default reading material.
   - This reduces decision fatigue and keeps the reading habit clear and directed.

2. **Side Reads as Pressure Release**
   - Keep side reads limited to **one** (ideal) or **two** (manageable) books.
   - Use them only when you experience internal resistance (mind drifting, dread, or lack of focus) while attempting to read the main book.
   - Side reads act as palate cleansers, allowing you to stay in the reading habit without forcing through a low‑energy segment.

3. **Implementation Rhythm**
   - No rigid schedule is required. When you sit down to read:
     - If you feel ready, continue with the main book.
     - If you notice resistance, briefly switch to a side read, then return to the primary book when the resistance eases.
   - This flexible approach maintains momentum while preserving the primacy of the main book.

4. **Limits on Side Reads**
   - **One side read** is ideal for minimizing distraction.
   - **Two side reads** can be managed but risk diluting focus.
   - More than two side reads generally undermines the main‑book focus and leads to scattered attention.
