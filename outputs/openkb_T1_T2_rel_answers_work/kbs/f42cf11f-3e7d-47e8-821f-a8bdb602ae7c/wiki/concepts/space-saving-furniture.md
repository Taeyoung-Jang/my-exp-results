# Space‑Saving Furniture

**Type**: Concept

**Description**: Strategies and specific furniture pieces designed to maximize functional floor area in small living spaces.

---

## Overview
Space‑saving furniture focuses on multi‑functional or compact designs that reduce clutter and keep movement pathways clear. In tight rooms, each piece should serve more than one purpose, be wall‑mounted, or occupy vertical space rather than floor area.

## Key Elements Highlighted in [[summaries/session-34_2025-09-16]]

1. **Wall‑Mounted Fold‑Down Desk** – A desk that pivots down from the wall and can be tucked away when not in use, freeing floor space for other activities.
2. **Murphy Bed** – A horizontal bed that folds vertically into the wall, allowing the room to serve as a workspace during the day. See also [[concepts/murphy-bed]].
3. **Beds with Built‑In Drawers** – Provide storage without needing separate dressers.
4. **Lofted or Raised Platforms** – Create usable space underneath for a dedicated office area or storage.
5. **Slim Vertical Storage Units** – Tall, narrow cabinets that keep items off the floor while minimizing visual bulk.
6. **Adjustable Monitor Arms** – Allow monitors to be moved flush against a wall, reducing desk depth.

## Design Principles (Related to [[concepts/layout-planning]])
- **Anchor Major Pieces to Perimeter Walls**: Position beds, desks, and storage along walls to preserve central floor space.
- **Maintain Clear Circulation Paths**: Keep a minimum walkway width of about 30–36 inches between entry and closet doors.
- **Use Multi‑Purpose Furniture**: Choose pieces that can transition between sleeping and working modes, such as a Murphy bed combined with a desk.
- **Prioritize Vertical Over Horizontal**: Store items upward rather than outward to avoid crowding the floor.

## Practical Tips
- Sketch a floor plan before moving furniture.
- Choose shallow desks and chairs that stay within the designated walkway.
- Opt for wall‑mounted lighting to keep surfaces clear.
- When using a Murphy bed, leave roughly 7 ft of clear space in front of the bed for safe deployment.
