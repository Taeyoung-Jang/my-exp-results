# Murphy Bed

**Type:** Concept

## Overview
A Murphy bed is a wall‑mounted, fold‑down sleeping platform that can be raised into a vertical cabinet when not in use. By “disappearing” into the wall, it converts a bedroom into a flexible living or work area, making it ideal for tight‑space environments.

## Key Features
- **Space‑saving:** Eliminates the need for a permanent bed footprint, allowing the floor area to serve other functions.
- **Dual‑functionality:** Often paired with built‑in storage, desks, or shelving to maximize utility.
- **Customizable mounting:** Can be installed on various walls depending on traffic flow and window placement.

## Layout Applications (from [[summaries/session-34_2025-09-16]])
1. **Opposite‑window placement** – Mount the bed on the wall opposite the window; the desk sits under the window, keeping the closet path clear.
2. **Entry‑door wall placement** – Install the bed on the wall that shares the entry door; a desk runs along the opposite wall, preserving a direct route to the closet.
3. **Window‑adjacent placement** – Position the bed on the window‑and‑closet wall, leaving enough clearance for the bed to fold down without blocking the closet, while the desk occupies the diagonal corner.

These configurations illustrate how a Murphy bed integrates with **[[concepts/layout-planning]]** to maintain circulation and functional zones.

## Clearance Guidelines
- **Bed deployment clearance:** Approximately 7 ft of open floor in front of the mounted wall is recommended to allow the bed to lower fully without striking furniture.
- **Walkway width:** A minimum of 30 in (ideal 36 in) should be kept clear between the entry door and the closet to avoid a cramped feel. This aligns with general **[[concepts/space-saving-furniture]]** recommendations for maintaining clear circulation paths.

## Benefits
- **Flexibility:** Transforms a bedroom into a workspace, study, or hobby area during the day.
- **Aesthetic integration:** When raised, the bed blends into the wall, preserving a clean, uncluttered look.
- **Enhanced storage:** Many models incorporate cabinets, shelves, or drawers, reducing the need for separate furniture.
