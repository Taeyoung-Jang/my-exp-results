# Summary of session-40_2025-04-16

**Main contribution:** A consolidated master checklist and 30‑minute prep timeline for a wheat‑berry salad (10–12 servings) for an office potluck.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/checklist-planning]]: Structured “Buy”, “Prep”, and “Pack” sections with checkboxes.  
- Pantry inventory: User’s existing pantry items (wheat berries, miso, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil) inform the “must‑buy” list.  
- [[concepts/grocery-shopping-list]]: Detailed quantities, optional add‑ins, and shelf‑stable vs refrigerated flags.  
- [[concepts/meal-prep-timeline]]: Two‑day schedule in 30‑minute blocks (day before 6‑7 pm, morning 8‑9 am).  
- Shelf‑stable vs refrigerated: Inline notes indicating storage requirements for each ingredient.  
- [[entities/office-potluck]]: Packing checklist (spoon, containers, napkin) for the morning.  
- Grain salad preparation: Steps for cooking wheat berries, making miso‑ginger dressing, and assembling the salad.  
- Dressing formulation: Proportions of miso, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and ginger.  

## Findings & Ideas
- Only fresh ginger [[entities/fresh-ginger]] and scallions [[entities/scallions]] are required purchases; all other staples are already on hand.  
- Optional add‑ins (cucumber [[entities/cucumber]], herbs like parsley [[entities/parsley]], dried cranberries [[entities/dried-cranberries]], toasted nuts [[entities/toasted-nuts]], bell pepper [[entities/bell-pepper]]) allow customization without extra trips.  
- A concise 30‑minute block schedule fits within the user’s available time windows (6 pm arrival, 9 am departure).  
- Shelf‑stable items can be stored at room temperature, reducing fridge space needs.  
- The master checklist can be reused for future weeks with minimal adjustments.
