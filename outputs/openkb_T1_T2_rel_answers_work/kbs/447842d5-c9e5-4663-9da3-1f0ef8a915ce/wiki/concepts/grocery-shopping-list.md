# Grocery Shopping List

**Definition**
A grocery shopping list is a concise, itemized checklist that outlines everything needed to prepare a specific meal or event, including quantities, optional add‑ins, and notes on whether each item is shelf‑stable or requires refrigeration.

**Key Characteristics**
- **Categorized sections** (e.g., must‑buy vs optional add‑ins).
- **Quantities** tailored to the intended serving size.
- **Storage flags** indicating if an ingredient is shelf‑stable or refrigerated.
- **Reusable format** that can be adapted for recurring meals.

**Relation to Other Concepts**
- Part of a broader [[concepts/checklist-planning]] workflow that organizes buying, preparing, and packing tasks.
- Often paired with a [[concepts/meal-prep-timeline]] to align shopping with cooking windows.
- When unsure about an ingredient’s suitability, the [[concepts/when-in-doubt-rule]] (choose a safe alternative or confirm before purchase) can be applied.

**Example from [[summaries/session-40_2025-04-16]]**
The following master list was created for a wheat‑berry salad (10–12 servings) for an office potluck:

### BUY (for 10–12 servings)
- [ ] Fresh ginger, 3–4 inch piece (**refrigerated**) [[entities/fresh-ginger]]
- [ ] Scallions, 1 large bunch (**refrigerated**) [[entities/scallions]]
- [ ] Cucumber, 1 large, optional (**refrigerated**) [[entities/cucumber]]
- [ ] Fresh parsley or cilantro, 1 small bunch, optional (**refrigerated**) [[entities/parsley]]
- [ ] Dried cranberries or cherries, ~1 cup, optional (**shelf‑stable**) [[entities/dried-cranberries]]
- [ ] Toasted nuts or seeds, ~1 cup, optional (**shelf‑stable**) [[entities/toasted-nuts]]
- [ ] Bell pepper, 1 medium, optional (**refrigerated**) [[entities/bell-pepper]]
- [ ] Miso, 3 Tbsp (already owned) [[entities/miso]]
- [ ] Soy sauce, 2 Tbsp (already owned) [[entities/soy-sauce]]
- [ ] Rice vinegar, 3 Tbsp (already owned) [[entities/rice-vinegar]]
- [ ] Sesame oil, 2 Tbsp (already owned) [[entities/sesame-oil]]

### Optional Add‑Ins (customizable)
- Extra vegetables, herbs, or crunchy toppings can be added based on personal preference or pantry inventory.

**Benefits**
- Prevents duplicate purchases by referencing existing pantry items.
- Saves time by grouping items into must‑buy and optional categories.
- Provides clear guidance on storage, reducing the risk of spoilage.

**Implementation Tips**
1. **Inventory first** – Review pantry contents before drafting the list (see the [[concepts/checklist-planning]] step). 
2. **Quantify precisely** – Use standard measurements (cups, tablespoons) to avoid over‑ or under‑buying.
3. **Flag storage** – Mark each item with (refrigerated) or (shelf‑stable) to streamline bagging.
4. **Reuse and adapt** – Keep a master template and adjust quantities for different serving sizes or recipes.
