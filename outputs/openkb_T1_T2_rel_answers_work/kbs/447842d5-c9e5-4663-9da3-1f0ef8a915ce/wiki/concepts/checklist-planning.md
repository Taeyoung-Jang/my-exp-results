# Checklist Planning

**Definition**: A systematic method for breaking down a goal into discrete, actionable items organized in sections (e.g., **Buy**, **Prep**, **Pack**) and often presented with checkboxes for easy tracking.

## Core Elements
- **Sectioned layout** – groups items by purpose (shopping, preparation, packing).  
- **Checkbox format** – enables quick visual confirmation of completion.  
- **Prioritization** – distinguishes *must‑buy* items from optional add‑ins.  
- **Quantitative detail** – includes serving‑size based quantities, storage notes (shelf‑stable vs refrigerated), and optional variations.

## Benefits
1. **Clarity** – users see exactly what to acquire, prepare, and bring.  
2. **Efficiency** – reduces duplicate purchases by referencing existing pantry inventory.  
3. **Reusability** – a master checklist can be adapted for future events with minimal changes.
