# Wheat Berries

**Type:** Product

Wheat berries are the whole, unprocessed kernels of wheat. In the context of the "office potluck" planning session, they serve as the primary grain component of the **[[entities/wheat-berry-salad]]** that will feed approximately 10–12 people.

## Key Facts from Session 40 (2025‑04‑16)
- The user already has wheat berries stocked in the pantry, so no purchase is required.
- They are cooked the day before the potluck (around 6 pm) and then cooled for later assembly.
- Wheat berries form the base of the salad; additional ingredients such as fresh ginger, scallions, and optional add‑ins are mixed in later.
- Quantity needed: enough to provide a hearty portion for 10–12 servings (roughly 2 cups uncooked, yielding about 4 cups cooked).
