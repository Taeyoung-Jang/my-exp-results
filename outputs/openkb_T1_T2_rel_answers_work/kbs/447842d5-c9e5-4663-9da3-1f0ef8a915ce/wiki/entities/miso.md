## Miso

Miso is a fermented soybean paste that provides a salty, umami flavor to the miso‑ginger dressing for the [[entities/wheat-berry-salad]]. In the **session-40_2025-04-16** checklist it is listed under the "Full dressing list" with a required amount of about **3 tablespoons** for 10–12 servings.

**Key points from the session**
- Already owned by the user, so it appears in the checklist as an item that does not need to be purchased.
- Combined with soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and fresh ginger to form the dressing.
- Classified as a **refrigerated** product (once opened) in the shelf‑stable vs refrigerated notes.
