# Wheat‑Berry Salad

**What it is**
A grain salad centered on [[entities/wheat-berries]] tossed with a miso‑ginger dressing, prepared for an [[entities/office-potluck]] serving about 10–12 people.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2025-04-16]]**
- **Core ingredients**: wheat berries, miso, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, fresh ginger, scallions.
- **Optional add‑ins**: [[entities/cucumber]], [[entities/parsley]], [[entities/dried-cranberries]], [[entities/toasted-nuts]], [[entities/bell-pepper]].
- **Quantity guidance**: enough for 10–12 servings; e.g., 3–4 inch piece of ginger, 1 large bunch of scallions, 1 cup of dried cranberries, 1 cup of toasted nuts, etc.
- **Shopping**: Split into “must‑buy” and “optional” sections, with shelf‑stable vs refrigerated flags (see [[concepts/grocery-shopping-list]]).
- **Prep timeline**: Two‑day schedule in 30‑minute blocks (day‑before 6:00‑7:30 pm, morning 8:00‑9:00 am) to cook wheat berries, whisk dressing, and assemble the salad (see [[concepts/meal-prep-timeline]]).
- **Packing**: Checklist includes serving spoon, large container with lid, small dressing container, napkin/towel (see [[concepts/checklist-planning]]).
