# Sesame Oil

**Type:** product

Sesame oil is a flavorful, shelf‑stable oil commonly used in Asian dressings and sauces. In the *office potluck* planning document, it appears as an ingredient for the miso‑ginger dressing of the wheat‑berry salad.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-40_2025-04-16]]
- **Quantity needed:** 2 tablespoons for 10–12 servings.
- **Storage:** Shelf‑stable (can be kept at room temperature, but often stored in the pantry).
- **Already owned:** The user already has sesame oil, so it is listed in the master checklist as a *needed item* but marked as “you already have this.”

## Role in the Checklist
- Included in the **BUY** section of the master checklist under [[concepts/grocery-shopping-list]].
- Categorized as a *shelf‑stable* ingredient, distinguishing it from refrigerated items like [[entities/fresh-ginger]] and [[entities/scallions]].

## Related Ingredients
