# Rice Vinegar

**Type:** Product

**Overview**
Rice vinegar is a mild, slightly sweet vinegar made from fermented rice. It is commonly used in Asian‑style dressings, sauces, and marinades.

**Reference in session‑40**
In the master checklist created during [[summaries/session-40_2025-04-16]], rice vinegar is listed as an ingredient for the miso‑ginger dressing of the [[entities/wheat-berry-salad]] (10–12 servings). The document notes that the user already has rice vinegar on hand, so it appears under the “already have” section of the pantry inventory.

**Quantity & Usage**
- Required amount: **3 tablespoons** for the dressing.
- Combined with [[entities/miso]] (3 Tbsp), [[entities/soy-sauce]] (2 Tbsp), [[entities/sesame-oil]] (2 Tbsp), and fresh ginger.

**Storage**
- Classified as **shelf‑stable** (room temperature) once opened, though it can be refrigerated for longer freshness. This storage note appears in the checklist under the “shelf‑stable vs refrigerated” column.
