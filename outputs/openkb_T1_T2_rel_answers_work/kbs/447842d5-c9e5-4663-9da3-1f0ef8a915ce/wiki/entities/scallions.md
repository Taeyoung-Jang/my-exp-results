# Scallions

**Type:** product

Scallions (also known as green onions) are listed as a **must‑buy** ingredient in the master checklist for the wheat‑berry salad prepared for an [[entities/office-potluck]]. The document [[summaries/session-40_2025-04-16]] specifies a need for **one large bunch**, stored **refrigerated**.

## Role in the Checklist
- Appears in the **BUY** section of the [[concepts/checklist-planning]] and [[concepts/grocery-shopping-list]] for 10–12 servings.
- Marked as a refrigerated item, differentiating it from shelf‑stable pantry staples.
- Optional add‑ins like [[entities/cucumber]] and [[entities/parsley]] are also refrigerated, reinforcing the need for cold‑storage planning.

## Interaction with Other Items
- Paired with [[entities/fresh-ginger]] in the miso‑ginger dressing.
- Complements other salad components such as [[entities/wheat-berries]] and [[entities/wheat-berry-salad]].
- Listed alongside other pantry staples ([[entities/miso]], [[entities/soy-sauce]], [[entities/rice-vinegar]], [[entities/sesame-oil]]) that the user already possesses.

## Timing
- Purchased on **Friday** as part of the grocery run before the potluck.
- No preparation required beyond washing; it is ready to slice and add during the **morning prep** block in the [[concepts/meal-prep-timeline]].
