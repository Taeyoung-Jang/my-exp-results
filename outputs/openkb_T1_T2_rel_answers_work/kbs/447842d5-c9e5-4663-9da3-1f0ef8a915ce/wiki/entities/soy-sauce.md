## Soy Sauce

Soy sauce is a dark, salty, fermented sauce made from soybeans, wheat, salt, and water. It is a staple seasoning in many cuisines and provides umami depth to sauces and dressings.

### Role in the wheat‑berry salad
- Listed in the **Buy** section of the master checklist for the office potluck as a required ingredient for the miso‑ginger dressing.
- Quantity needed for 10–12 servings: **2 tablespoons**.
- The document notes that soy sauce is **shelf‑stable** (though often kept refrigerated after opening).
