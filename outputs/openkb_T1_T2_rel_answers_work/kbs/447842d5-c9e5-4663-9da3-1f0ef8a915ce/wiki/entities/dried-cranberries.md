# Dried Cranberries

**Type:** product

Dried cranberries are sweet, dried fruit pieces often used to add a touch of sweetness and texture to grain salads and other dishes. In the *session-40_2025-04-16* conversation they appear as an **optional add‑in** for a wheat‑berry salad intended for an office potluck.

## Key facts from the document
- **Suggested quantity:** about **1 cup** for roughly **10–12 servings** of the wheat‑berry salad.
- **Storage:** **Shelf‑stable** (does not require refrigeration).
- **Role in the recipe:** Provides a sweet contrast to the savory miso‑ginger dressing and adds chewiness.
- **Categorisation in the planning workflow:** Listed under the “optional add‑ins” section of the **[[concepts/grocery-shopping-list]]** and incorporated into the **[[concepts/checklist-planning]]** master checklist.
