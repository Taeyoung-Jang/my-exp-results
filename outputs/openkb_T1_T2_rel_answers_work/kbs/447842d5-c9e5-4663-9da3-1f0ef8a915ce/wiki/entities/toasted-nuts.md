# Toasted Nuts

**Type:** product

## Overview
Toasted nuts (or seeds) are suggested as an optional crunchy add‑in for the wheat‑berry salad prepared for an [[entities/office-potluck]]. They provide texture, flavor contrast, and nutritional boost.

## Role in session‑40_2025‑04‑16
- Listed under the **optional add‑ins** section of the master checklist.
- Recommended quantity: about **1 cup** for 10–12 servings.
- Classified as **shelf‑stable** (can be stored at room temperature).
- Mentioned alongside other optional ingredients such as [[entities/cucumber]], [[entities/dried-cranberries]], and [[entities/bell-pepper]].
