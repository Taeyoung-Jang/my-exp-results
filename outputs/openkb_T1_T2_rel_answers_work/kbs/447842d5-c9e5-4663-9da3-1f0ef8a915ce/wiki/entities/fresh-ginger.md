## Fresh Ginger (product)

Fresh ginger is a rhizome commonly used as a flavoring agent in both savory and sweet dishes. In the *session-40_2025-04-16* conversation it is listed as a **must‑buy** item for preparing a miso‑ginger dressing for an office potluck grain salad.

**Key facts from the document**:
- Needed quantity: a 3–4 inch piece (≈ 1–2 tablespoons grated) for about 10–12 servings of [[entities/wheat-berry-salad]].
- Storage: refrigerated (not shelf‑stable).
- Role: Provides the main aromatic component of the miso‑ginger dressing, paired with miso, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil.
- It is the only fresh ingredient the user needed to purchase; scallions were the other required item.
