# Low-Stress Approach

The **Low-Stress Approach** is a philosophy for personal health tracking that prioritizes simplicity, consistency, and minimal mental overhead. Rather than collecting exhaustive data, it advocates using a single, easy‑to‑maintain system that provides enough insight to stay aware without becoming a source of anxiety.

## Key Principles

1. **Single‑Place Logging** – Consolidate measurements (e.g., weight) in one notes app, spreadsheet, or health‑tracking app to reduce fragmentation.
2. **Consistent, Minimal Frequency** – Choose a frequency that matches the stability of the metric. For a stable weight, weekly weigh‑ins are sufficient; daily checks are only needed when finer granularity is desired.
3. **Standardized Timing** – Weigh at the same time each week (e.g., morning after bathroom use, before food or drink) to ensure comparable data points.
4. **Accept Normal Variation** – Recognize that small fluctuations (≈0.5 kg) are normal and usually reflect hydration or other short‑term factors.
5. **Pattern Over Isolated Changes** – Focus on trends over multiple weeks rather than single out‑liers.
6. **Low‑Effort Execution** – Keep the check‑in under a minute to prevent the habit from turning into a chore.
