## Summary of session-10_2025-06-15

The conversation provides practical advice for setting up a lightweight **health‑tracking system** focused on weight and body metrics. The key recommendations are:

- **Single‑place logging** – use a notes app, basic spreadsheet, or a single health‑tracking app to keep data centralized and avoid complexity. [[concepts/health-tracking]]
- **Check‑in frequency** – for a stable weight (85 kg for a year), weekly weigh‑ins are sufficient; daily tracking is unnecessary unless the user prefers it. [[concepts/weight-monitoring]]
- **Consistent timing** – weigh each week at the same morning, right after waking, using the bathroom, and before any food or drink, to ensure comparable readings. (consistency)
- **Data granularity** – with weekly weigh‑ins, a single number per session is adequate; weekly averages are only useful when measuring daily. (granularity)
- **Normal variation** – fluctuations up to ~0.5 kg are typical and usually reflect hydration, salt, or sleep; even up to 1 kg can be inconsequential. Patterns over multiple weeks are more meaningful than isolated changes. (normal fluctuation)
- **Low‑stress mindset** – keep the routine under a minute, avoid obsessiveness, and adjust frequency if tracking feels compulsive. [[concepts/low-stress-approach]]
