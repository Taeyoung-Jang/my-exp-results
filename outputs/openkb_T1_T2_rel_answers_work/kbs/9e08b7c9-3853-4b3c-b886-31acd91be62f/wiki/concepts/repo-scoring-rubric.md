# Repo Selection Scoring Rubric

The **Repo Selection Scoring Rubric** is a lightweight framework introduced in [[summaries/session-01_2023-06-15]] to help developers prioritize which repositories to feature on their GitHub profile. It evaluates each repository across three dimensions, each scored from **1 to 5**:

1. **Relevance** – How closely the project aligns with the target job roles or desired professional narrative.
2. **Impact** – The technical depth, scale, or real‑world usage of the project (e.g., complexity, user base, innovative solutions).
3. **Clarity** – How easily a recruiter can understand the project's purpose and value at a glance (clear README, screenshots, concise description).

The total score (maximum 15) is the sum of the three criteria. Repositories with the highest totals are recommended for pinning in the **GitHub surface** as described in [[concepts/github-surface-polish]].

## How to Use the Rubric

1. List the candidate repositories you want to consider.
2. Assign a score of 1‑5 for each of the three criteria based on your self‑assessment or peer feedback.
3. Add the three scores to obtain a total.
4. Rank the repositories by total score; select the top 3‑6 for pinning.

### Example
| Repo | Relevance (1‑5) | Impact (1‑5) | Clarity (1‑5) | Total |
|------|----------------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| analytics‑dashboard | 5 | 4 | 4 | **13** |
| cli‑toolkit | 3 | 3 | 5 | 11 |
| hobby‑game | 2 | 2 | 3 | 7 |

In this example, **analytics‑dashboard** scores highest and would be a prime candidate for a pinned repo.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- The rubric is part of the broader process of **polishing the GitHub surface** [[concepts/github-surface-polish]].
- It complements the **README checklist for recruiters** [[concepts/readme-checklist-recruiters]], ensuring that the repositories you choose also have well‑crafted documentation.

By applying the Repo Selection Scoring Rubric, developers can make data‑driven decisions about which projects best showcase their skills to recruiters.
