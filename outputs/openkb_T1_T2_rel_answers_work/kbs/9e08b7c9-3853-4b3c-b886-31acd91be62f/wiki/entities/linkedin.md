## LinkedIn
LinkedIn is a product offering a professional networking platform where users maintain résumé‑style profiles, connect with colleagues, and search for job opportunities. 

### Key facts from **session‑39 (2025‑06‑15)**
- The assistant advises placing a line on a résumé or LinkedIn profile such as “Selected technical write‑ups and open‑source samples available at <link>” to give recruiters verifiable proof of work while keeping proprietary details private. 
- LinkedIn is referenced as a place to surface sanitized case studies, public side‑projects, or redacted screenshots that demonstrate measurable impact and observable artifacts without exposing confidential code.
