## Google Drive

Google Drive is a cloud‑based storage and file‑sync service that lets users back up documents, photos, and other data to the cloud and access it from multiple devices.

### Role in session‑02_2024‑06‑15
In the conversation about safe beta‑testing and update strategies, **Google Drive** is cited as a reliable cloud backup option for phones (and other devices) alongside iCloud. The document recommends ensuring that a cloud backup like Google Drive is up‑to‑date before installing a beta update, then verifying the backup before proceeding.

### Connections to key concepts
- Part of a **[[concepts/software-update-strategy]]**: using cloud backups such as Google Drive helps users weigh the risk of new software updates versus the need for new features.
- Integral to **[[concepts/beta-testing-risk-management]]**: a fresh Google Drive backup is a non‑negotiable step for beta‑first users to mitigate data‑loss risk.
- Supports **[[concepts/device-isolation]]**: by storing data in the cloud, users can safely test betas on secondary hardware while keeping critical data accessible across devices.
