# Summary of session-33_2025-03-12

**Date:** 2025‑03‑12 14:30

## Core Contribution
A concrete, copy‑edit‑focused workflow for writers under tight deadlines, presented as a day‑by‑day schedule, a sample style‑sheet, and a plain‑text decision flowchart. The guidance emphasizes separating clarity, voice, and mechanical polishing passes, and offers practical tactics to avoid scope creep.

## Key Ideas & Passes
- **Revision Passes** – First pass for *clarity*, second for *rhythm/voice* (read aloud), third for *mechanical* issues like repetition and vague phrasing. [[concepts/revision_passes]]
- **Copy‑edit Priorities** – 1️⃣ Continuity & factual consistency (character ages, timeline, name spellings). 2️⃣ Language oddities that distract but don’t reshape scenes. 3️⃣ Flag major structural concerns for the editor instead of rewriting. [[concepts/copyedit_priorities]]
- **Anti‑Spiral Techniques** – Treat edits as *touch‑ups*, use a simple decision rule (accept/query/override), and keep a running style sheet as a memory aid. [[concepts/style_sheet_usage]]

## 10‑Day Game Plan (template placeholders)
```
Day 1   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Orientation & start style sheet
Day 2   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Clarity & consistency
Day 3   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Character details
Day 4   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: World‑building terms
Day 5   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Light language polish
Day 6   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Tone check
Day 7   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Continuity scan
Day 8   Pages: ___‑___   Goal: Final main pass
Day 9   (Buffer)          Goal: Resolve flags, refine tonal fixes
Day 10  (Buffer)          Goal: Final skim, finalize style sheet, prep notes
```
The template can be filled in with total pages/chapters, aiming for ~30‑40 pages per night on a 300‑page manuscript. The two buffer days align with [[concepts/buffer_days_in_writing_schedules]].

## Sample Style Sheet Layout
- **Names** – e.g., *Elias Maren* (full name first mention), *Dr. Fallow* (title always included).
- **Places** – *Northbridge* (one word), *The Lower Docks* (capital L & D).
- **Timeline Facts** – Prologue spring; main story six years later; Ch 5‑7 one day.
- **Italics & Capitals** – Ship names italicized; internal thoughts italicized; Magic Orders capitalized, spells lower‑case.
- **Numbers** – Spell out 1‑9, numerals 10+; use numerals for times (3 a.m., 7 p.m.).
- **Hyphenation** – *long‑forgotten*, *halfhearted*, *ice‑blue*.

## Decision Flowchart (plain text)
```
1. Does the edit improve clarity or fix a clear error? → Accept.
2. Does it change tone, rhythm, or meaning?
   • Intentional original? → Override.
   • Uncertain? → Query.
3. Does it affect continuity, timeline, world‑building, or character data?
   • Correct factual error? → Accept.
   • Conflicts with established fact? → Override & note.
   • Unclear or needs editorial input? → Query.
4. Would it require a larger rewrite? → Do NOT fix now; Query.
```
The flowchart follows [[concepts/decision_flowchart_for_editing]].

## Practical Takeaways
- **Chunk the manuscript** into nightly page goals with buffer days.
- **Maintain a concise style sheet** as a quick reference for consistency.
- **Use the three‑step decision rule** to keep edits focused and avoid rewrites.
- **Read aloud** during the voice pass to preserve the author’s unique tone.
