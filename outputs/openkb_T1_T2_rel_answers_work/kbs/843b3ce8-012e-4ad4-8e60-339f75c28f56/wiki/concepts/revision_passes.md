# Revision Passes

**Definition**: A structured series of editorial passes that isolate distinct concerns—clarity, rhythm/voice, and mechanical polish—to make manuscript tightening manageable and preserve authorial voice.

---

## Core Stages (as described in [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-12]])

1. **Clarity Pass**
   - Read through the text without editing for flow. Mark any spots where you stumble, skim, or feel confused.
   - Focus on fixing omissions, ambiguous phrasing, or structural gaps that impede understanding.
   - Aim to ensure the *meaning* lands clearly for the reader.

2. **Rhythm & Voice Pass**
   - Read the revised sections **aloud**. Listen for flat, fussy, or un‑characteristic language.
   - Trim overwriting, but if a cut weakens the unique voice, restore or re‑phrase it.
   - The goal is to keep the author's tonal fingerprint intact while improving readability.

3. **Mechanical Pass**
   - Scan for repeated words, vague descriptors, duplicate sentences, and other low‑level issues.
   - Apply consistent punctuation, spelling, and formatting (see [[concepts/style_sheet_usage]] for a practical style‑sheet template).
   - This pass feels low‑pressure because structure and voice are already set.

---

## Why Separate Passes?

- **Cognitive Load Management** – Tackling one type of edit at a time prevents overwhelm and reduces the risk of *scope creep*.
- **Voice Preservation** – By isolating the rhythm/voice pass, you deliberately protect the author’s stylistic choices.
- **Efficiency** – Each pass has a clear, narrow objective, making progress measurable and deadlines more approachable.

## Practical Tips

- **Chunk the manuscript**: Combine the three passes into nightly goals (e.g., 30‑40 pages) to keep momentum.
- **Use a running style sheet**: Capture name spellings, capitalizations, and hyphenations during the mechanical pass.
- **Read aloud**: The most reliable way to hear voice drift.
- **Decision Flow**: When an edit is encountered, run it through the [[concepts/decision_flowchart_for_editing]] to decide Accept/Query/Override.
