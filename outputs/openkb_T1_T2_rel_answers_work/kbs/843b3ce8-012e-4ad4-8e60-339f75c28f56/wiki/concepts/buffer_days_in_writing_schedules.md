# Buffer Days in Writing Schedules

**Definition**: Reserved days at the end of a tight copy‑editing schedule that serve to resolve flagged items, make final refinements, and ensure consistency without introducing new extensive rewrites.

## Why They Matter
- Provide a safety net for unexpected issues that arise during the earlier passes.
- Help prevent burnout by giving writers a lighter workload after intensive daily edits.
- Allow completion of the **style sheet** and final queries before submission.

## Typical Structure (from [[summaries/session-33_2025-03-12]])
| Day | Focus | Typical Tasks |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| **Day 9** (Buffer) | Resolve flags | Review all items flagged on Days 1‑8, make small tonal tweaks, answer queries. No new page intake. |
| **Day 10** (Buffer) | Final polish | Quick full‑manuscript skim, finalize the style sheet, compile notes for the editor, confirm no lingering continuity errors. |

## Relationship to Other Writing Concepts
- Part of a broader **[[concepts/revision_passes]]** strategy, where the final pass is a low‑pressure catch‑up rather than a major rewrite.
- Complements **[[concepts/copyedit_priorities]]** by giving space to address priority‑level edits (continuity, factual consistency) without expanding scope.
- Relies on a maintained **[[concepts/style_sheet_usage]]** to quickly spot name or formatting inconsistencies during the buffer days.
- Often guided by the **[[concepts/decision_flowchart_for_editing]]** to decide whether to accept, query, or override a flagged change.

## Practical Tips for Implementing Buffer Days
1. **Schedule them early**: Build the buffer into the 10‑day plan rather than adding it last‑minute.
2. **Limit new work**: Treat the days as exclusively for resolution and review; do not start fresh pages.
3. **Use a checklist**: Verify that all flagged items have been addressed, the style sheet is complete, and any outstanding queries are documented for the editor.
4. **Allow flexibility**: If you finish early, you can use the extra time for a brief overall read‑through to catch any missed issues.
