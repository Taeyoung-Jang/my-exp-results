# Thyroid Medication (product)

**Entity type:** product

Thyroid medication refers to prescription drugs that contain synthetic thyroid hormones (e.g., levothyroxine) used to treat hypothyroidism and to maintain appropriate thyroid function.  In the context of **session-31_2025-09-14**, the user mentions a recent adjustment of their thyroid medication dose, which coincided with a gradual weight loss and subtle symptoms such as a higher resting heart rate and feeling warmer.

## Communication tip
When messaging the endocrinologist, include:
- Date of dose change.
- Approximate weight change.
- Resting heart rate values.
- Any mild symptoms.
- A clear request for repeat labs (TSH, free T4) or dose review.
