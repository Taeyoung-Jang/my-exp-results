# Functional Progress Markers

## Definition
Functional Progress Markers are low‑pressure, observational cues that signal changes in health, fitness, or nutrition without relying on precise numbers or frequent weighing. They focus on everyday experiences rather than strict metrics.

## Core Markers (as suggested in [[summaries/session-02_2025-06-15]])
- **Clothing Fit** – Noting whether clothes feel looser, snugger, or unchanged.
- **Energy / Mood** – A brief comment on overall energy levels, mood, or sleep quality.
- **Movement Ease** – Observing how regular activities (walking, stairs, routine workouts) feel: easier, harder, or the same.
- **Optional Weight** – If recorded, it is logged neutrally without interpretation (see [[concepts/neutral_weight_logging]]).

## Why Use Functional Progress Markers?
- **Low Stress**: They avoid the anxiety often associated with daily weigh‑ins or calorie counts.
- **Sustainability**: Simple, quick weekly notes are easier to maintain over the long term.
- **Holistic Insight**: They capture functional and subjective changes that numbers alone may miss.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- Part of the broader framework of [[concepts/low_stress_tracking]].
- Complementary to [[concepts/neutral_weight_logging]], offering an alternative or additional data point when weight is tracked.

## Practical Implementation
1. Choose 2–3 markers that feel most relevant to you.
2. Create a brief weekly template (e.g., the one in the session summary) and record observations.
3. Review trends over weeks rather than day‑to‑day fluctuations.

By focusing on these functional cues, you can stay attuned to genuine progress while keeping the tracking process calm and judgment‑free.
