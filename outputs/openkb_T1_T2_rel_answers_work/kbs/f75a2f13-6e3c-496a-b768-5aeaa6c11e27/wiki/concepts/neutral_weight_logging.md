# Neutral Weight Logging

**Definition**: A practice of recording body weight in a way that avoids emotional attachment or judgment, treating the number like a factual observation (e.g., checking the weather).

## Core Principles
- **Consistency**: Weigh at the same time, under similar conditions (e.g., one morning per week after waking).  Consider noting the season or any recent medication changes, as these can subtly affect weight.
- **Minimal Interpretation**: Log the number only; do not analyze or compare it immediately.  If you later review trends, do so alongside other neutral markers rather than in isolation.
- **Separation from Mood**: If the weight reading triggers any emotional response, step back and focus on other neutral markers.

## Why It Matters
Neutral weight logging aligns with [[concepts/low_stress_tracking]] by providing a single data point that does not dominate the perception of progress.  It complements other **[[concepts/functional_progress_markers]]** such as clothing fit, energy levels, and movement ease, and it fits within a broader [[concepts/health_metric_tracking]] system.  Consistent, neutral logs can also help surface seasonal patterns ([[concepts/seasonal_weight_change]]) or subtle medication effects like those described in [[concepts/thyroid_dose_effects]], giving you concrete evidence to discuss with a clinician.

## Practical Steps (as suggested in [[summaries/session-02_2025-06-15]] and expanded in [[summaries/session-31_2025-09-14]])
1. **Pick a weekly time slot** – e.g., every Monday morning after getting up.
2. **Use the same scale and minimal clothing** to keep conditions stable.
3. **Record the number** in a notes app or health log without adding comments.  Optionally add a quick note of any concurrent observations (appetite change, resting heart rate) as part of overall health metric tracking.
4. **Leave the entry untouched** for the week; revisit only when reviewing overall trends or when you need concrete data for a medical conversation.

## When to Skip
If weight has been stable and feels emotionally charged, it’s perfectly acceptable to omit this marker and rely on the other functional progress markers.
