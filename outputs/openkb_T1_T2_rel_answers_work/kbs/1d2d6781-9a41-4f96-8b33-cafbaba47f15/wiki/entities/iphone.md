## iPhone

The **iPhone** is Apple’s line of smartphones running the [[entities/ios]] operating system. In the conversation documented in [[summaries/session-39_2024-12-16]], the iPhone is the platform for implementing a **Morning Quick Check** routine aimed at reducing screen‑time inertia.

### Key Facts from Session 39
- **Focus Setup**: The user is guided to create a new Focus named *Morning Quick Check* via Settings → Focus. This Focus leverages [[concepts/focus-mode]] to allow only essential contacts and the Messages app, while silencing other notifications.
- **Allowed Contacts & Apps**: Within the Focus, only specific people (via the *People* option) and the [[entities/messages]] app are permitted, preventing distractions from apps such as Instagram.
- **Lock‑Screen Tweaks**: The routine includes hiding notification previews, disabling lock‑screen widgets, and enabling the *Dim Lock Screen* option, all of which are examples of [[concepts/attention-friction]] strategies.
- **Physical Placement**: Recommendations suggest keeping the iPhone on a dresser, kitchen counter, or hallway shelf—just out of arm’s reach from the bed—to introduce a small physical barrier that supports habit formation.
- **Duration**: The quick check is framed as a 1–2 minute scan for urgent texts, calls, or calendar events, after which the phone is set aside.
