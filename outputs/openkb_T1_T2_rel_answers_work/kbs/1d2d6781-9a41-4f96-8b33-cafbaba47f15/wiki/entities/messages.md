# Messages (iOS app)

**Type:** product

**Platform:** iOS – the operating system for iPhone and iPad devices. [[entities/ios]] [[entities/iphone]]

## Overview
Messages is the native Apple app for sending and receiving SMS, MMS, and iMessage communications. It integrates tightly with the system’s notification and Focus frameworks, making it a central tool for managing morning communication routines.

## Role in the "Morning Quick Check" Routine (Session 39)
In the conversation *session‑39_2024-12-16*, Messages is highlighted as the only app that should be permitted through a custom **Morning Quick Check** Focus mode. The recommended configuration includes:
- Allowing **only** messages from a selected group of essential contacts.
- Hiding notification previews to reduce visual temptation.
- Keeping the app enabled while silencing all other apps that tend to pull users into scrolling.

These steps are part of a broader strategy to create **attention‑friction** and support a sustainable **morning‑routine**.
