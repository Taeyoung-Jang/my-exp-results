## Calendar (iOS app)

**Type:** product

The **Calendar** app is the native scheduling application on [[entities/ios]] devices such as the [[entities/iphone]]. It provides a visual month, week, and day view for events, supports multiple calendars, invites, and integrates with alerts and notifications.

### Relevance to session‑39 (2024‑12‑16)
In the conversation, the user was advised to allow the Calendar app in a custom **Morning Quick Check** Focus so that only time‑sensitive alerts (e.g., upcoming meetings) appear during the brief morning phone check. This ties the app to the concepts of [[concepts/focus-mode]] and [[concepts/morning-quick-check]], illustrating how selective notification routing can reduce distraction.

### Key points from the session
- Included as an allowed app in the "Morning Quick Check" Focus to surface only essential schedule information.
- Paired with the [[entities/messages]] app to limit notifications to urgent communications while silencing other apps.
- Helps implement the [[concepts/attention-friction]] strategy by providing only necessary information during the brief check.
