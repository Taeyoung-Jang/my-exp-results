### Summary of Session 13 (2024‑06‑15)

This document delivers practical, **phone‑free productivity** [[concepts/phone-free-productivity]] frameworks that fit within a single hour, plus supporting night‑before preparations. It addresses regular weekdays, relaxed weekends, and anxious‑morning scenarios, and provides concrete **grounding exercises** [[concepts/grounding-exercises]] and a concise journaling prompt.

#### 60‑Minute Schedules
- **Weekday routine**: water, stretch, paper planning, quick personal‑care, breakfast, tidy‑up → phone.  
- **Weekend routine**: slower stretch, extended reflective activity (journaling/reading), relaxed breakfast, tidy‑up → phone.  
- **Anxiety‑reset weekday**: gentle wake‑up, extended grounding, brief journal unload, shower, mindful breakfast, tidy‑up.

#### Night‑Before Checklists
- Core items: lay out clothing, prep breakfast ingredients, fill water bottle, place notebook/pen, jot a top‑priority thought.  
- Weekend tweak: ensure kitchen tidiness, set out leisure items (book, journal).

#### Grounding Block (5–15 min) – Scripts
1. **Script 1** – Slow breath (4 in / 6 out) with self‑affirmation *“I don’t have to fix anything right now.”* Follow 1‑2‑3 sensory check.  
2. **Script 2** – Mantra “Here. Now.” on exhale/inhale, gentle return of attention.

#### Journaling Prompt (15–25 min)
- *“What’s the one thing on my mind that would feel lighter if I wrote it down?”* – write a sentence, close notebook, move on.
