# Summary of Session 39 (2024‑12‑16)

**Document Type:** Conversation – practical advice on managing phone use in the morning.

## Key Concepts
- **Morning phone routine** – a balanced approach that allows a brief “quick check” instead of a strict no‑phone hour. [[concepts/morning-routine]]
- **Friction techniques** – small obstacles (placement, DND, lock‑screen cues) that interrupt the habit loop. [[concepts/attention-friction]]
- **Focus / Do‑Not‑Disturb** – configuring an iPhone Focus mode to allow only essential contacts and apps. [[concepts/focus-mode]]
- **Habit formation** – treating the check like brushing teeth: clear start, defined duration, and immediate post‑check action.
- **Attention management** – limiting notification previews and lock‑screen widgets to reduce temptation.

## Practical Recommendations
1. **Define the Quick Check**
   - Duration: 1‑2 minutes.
   - Content: texts, missed calls, calendar reminders only.
   - Avoid: social feeds, news apps, any scrolling‑prone apps.

2. **Create a “Morning Quick Check” Focus** (iPhone)
   - **Path:** Settings → Focus → **+** → *Morning Quick Check*.
   - **People:** Add only the contacts whose calls or messages you need immediately.
   - **Apps:** Enable *Messages* (and optionally *[[entities/calendar]]*); leave all other apps off.
   - **Lock‑Screen Options:**
     - Turn on **Hide Notification Previews**.
     - **Customize** → choose a layout with no widgets.
     - Enable **Dim Lock Screen** for a less stimulating view.
   - **Schedule:** Auto‑activate at your wake‑up time; set duration to 1 hour.
   - [[concepts/morning-quick-check]]

3. **Physical Placement (Small Apartment)**
   - Put the phone on a dresser, kitchen counter, or hallway shelf **out of arm’s reach** from the bed.
   - Ensure it remains accessible for emergencies but requires you to **stand up** to retrieve it.

4. **Friction Rules**
   - *Rule:* “If I reach for the phone, I must stand up to get it.”
   - Use a lock‑screen reminder such as “Morning first, phone later.”
   - Enable **Do‑Not‑Disturb** with exceptions for the selected contacts.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced guilt and higher adherence compared to a strict “no phone for an hour.”
- Clear boundary between essential communication and attention‑draining apps.
- Simple, repeatable habit that integrates into daily morning flow.
