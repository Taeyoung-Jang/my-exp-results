# Morning Quick Check Routine

## Overview
The **Morning Quick Check Routine** is a concise, intentional habit that allows a brief (1‑2 minute) scan of essential notifications first thing after waking, followed by immediate disengagement from the phone. It balances the need for staying informed with the goal of preserving a calm, focused start to the day.

## Core Elements
1. **Timed Quick Scan** – Limit the check to 1–2 minutes, reviewing only:
   - Text messages and calls from a pre‑selected list of contacts.
   - Calendar reminders for the day.
2. **Focused iPhone Settings** – Create a dedicated Focus mode that:
   - Allows only the selected contacts and the **Messages** app (and optionally **Calendar**) to break through.  
   - Hides notification previews and disables lock‑screen widgets.
   - Optionally dims the lock screen for a less stimulating view.
   - Auto‑activates at the wake‑up time and runs for about an hour.
3. **Physical Friction** – Place the phone in a spot that requires standing up, such as a dresser across the room, a kitchen counter, or a hallway shelf. This extra step interrupts the impulse to continue scrolling.
4. **Post‑Check Action** – Have a predetermined activity ready (e.g., getting out of bed, making coffee, starting a shower) that you move to as soon as the scan ends.

## Implementation Steps (iPhone)
- Open **Settings → Focus** → **+** → *Morning Quick Check*.
- **People:** Add only the essential contacts.
- **Apps:** Enable **Messages** (and **Calendar** if desired); leave all others off.
- **Lock‑Screen Options:**
  - Turn on **Hide Notification Previews**.
  - Tap **Customize** and choose a layout with **no widgets**.
  - Enable **Dim Lock Screen**.
- **Schedule:** Set the Focus to turn on automatically at your wake‑up time for a duration of 1 hour.
