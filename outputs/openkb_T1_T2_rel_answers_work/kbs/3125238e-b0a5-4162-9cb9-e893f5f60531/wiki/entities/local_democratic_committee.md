# Local Democratic Committee

**Type:** Organization

## Overview
The Local Democratic Committee is a grassroots political organization that serves as a *home‑base* for Democrats in a city or district. It provides regular updates on local races, coordinates campaign activities, and mobilizes volunteers for voter outreach and policy advocacy.

In the context of the conversation from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]], the committee is presented as an example of a **home‑base organization** where politically active individuals can:
