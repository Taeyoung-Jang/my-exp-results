# Environmental Coalition

**Type:** Organization

## Overview
The Environmental Coalition is a community‑based organization that concentrates on local environmental justice, climate action, and sustainability projects. It serves as an example of an **issue‑based** group that individuals can join to translate personal values into concrete political and civic engagement.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]
- Recommended as the **one issue‑focused org** for people who want a concrete outlet for their environmental concerns.
- Suggested criteria for evaluating the coalition include:
  - Clear measurement of impact (e.g., number of green spaces created, policy changes achieved).
  - Transparent decision‑making and evidence of recent wins.
- The document advises asking the coalition about:
  - How they measure progress.
  - Recent tangible accomplishments.
  - Their collaboration with affected communities.

- Aligns with the **[[concepts/political_engagement_framework]]** by offering a structured pathway from values (e.g., environmental stewardship) to action (volunteering, advocacy).
