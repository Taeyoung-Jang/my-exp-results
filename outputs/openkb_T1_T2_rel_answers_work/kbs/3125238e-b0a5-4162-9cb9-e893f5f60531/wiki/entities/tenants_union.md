# Tenants’ Union

**Type:** organization

**Description:** A grassroots organization advocating for renters' rights and affordable housing.

## Overview
The Tenants’ Union is a local, issue‑focused group that works to improve housing conditions, protect renters from unfair evictions, and promote policies for affordable housing. It is presented in the discussion of session‑33 as a model of an **issue‑based organization** that can be paired with a broader political “home‑base” group.

## Relationship to Session‑33
- The document [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]] recommends joining one **home‑base** organization (e.g., a local party committee) and one **issue‑based** organization such as the Tenants’ Union.
- It is highlighted as an example when discussing criteria for selecting effective local groups.

## Key Characteristics (per session‑33)
- **Concrete Impact:** The Union should demonstrate measurable wins (e.g., successful tenant counseling sessions, policy changes, or eviction prevention outcomes).
- **Community Collaboration:** Works directly with affected renters to understand needs and co‑design solutions.
- **Clear Metrics:** Uses specific goals and progress reports to show effectiveness, aligning with the concept of [[concepts/organizational_effectiveness]].
- **Values Alignment:** Supports core values like fairness, responsibility, and community support, echoing the [[concepts/values_based_orientation]] framework.
