# News Audit Checklist

**Definition**: A systematic, repeatable process for individuals to assess the balance, bias, and depth of their news and social‑media intake.

**Origin**: Introduced in the conversation *session‑15_2023‑06‑15* [[summaries/session-15_2023-06-15]], where the assistant provided a practical weekly checklist to help users spot missing context, framing, and tribal influences.

## Core Components
1. **Source Diversity Check** – Ask whether most consumed items share a similar viewpoint.
2. **Depth of Consumption** – Verify if headlines were read only, or if the full article and original data were examined.
3. **Emotional Reaction Flag** – Note any strong emotional response before checking the source.
4. **Verification Before Engagement** – Confirm whether content was shared or commented on without fact‑checking.
5. **Data‑Driven Review** – Determine if original data or evidence was skipped when available.
6. **Opposing Viewpoint Exploration** – Assess whether you avoided or dismissed perspectives that conflict with your existing beliefs.
7. **Identity‑Based Bias** – Detect if agreement or dismissal was driven by who said something rather than what was said.
8. **Confirmation Bias Indicator** – Track consumption that merely confirms pre‑existing opinions.
9. **Opposing Argument Articulation** – Test your ability to explain the strongest version of the opposite view on a key issue.
10. **Content Type Balance** – Compare time spent on reactive “hot takes” versus explanatory reporting.
11. **Anecdotal Generalization Check** – Identify if single anecdotes were treated as representative of the whole story.
12. **Side‑Favoring Enjoyment** – Recognize enjoyment derived mainly from content that makes “your side” look good.

## Scoring Guide
- **0‑3 “Yes” answers**: Generally balanced; maintain current habits.
- **4‑6 “Yes” answers**: Add one or two reputable sources with differing perspectives next week.
- **7‑12 “Yes” answers**: Implement slower reading, verify origins, and deliberately seek well‑argued opposing viewpoints for each major issue.
