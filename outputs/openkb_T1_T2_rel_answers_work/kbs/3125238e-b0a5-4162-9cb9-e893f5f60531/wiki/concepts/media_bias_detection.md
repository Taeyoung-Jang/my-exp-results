# Media Bias Detection

**Overview**
Media Bias Detection refers to the set of skills and heuristics used to recognize when news, social‑media posts, or other information sources are presenting a skewed perspective. It involves spotting emotional triggers, repeated narratives, framing techniques, and identity‑based appeals that can distort understanding.

**Key Elements from [[summaries/session-15_2023-06-15]]**
- **Emotional Reaction Cue**: If a piece of content sparks instant outrage or validation, it often signals that the material is designed to provoke loyalty rather than convey balanced information.
- **Source Diversity Check**: Noticing that most consumed pieces come from similar viewpoints suggests a narrow lens; seeking contrasting reputable sources helps broaden perspective.
- **Headline‑Body Consistency**: Verifying that the headline accurately reflects the article’s body guards against sensationalist framing.
- **Identity‑Based Agreement**: Recognizing when agreement stems from the speaker’s group affiliation rather than the argument itself.
- **Opposing View Explanation**: The ability (or difficulty) to articulate the strongest version of an opposing stance indicates the depth of exposure to alternative viewpoints.

**Practical Techniques**
1. **Emotion Audit** – Pause to name the feeling a story evokes before diving deeper; label it as a potential bias indicator.
2. **Source Rotation** – Regularly include at least one source with a known divergent editorial stance.
3. **Data Verification** – Look for original data or primary documents behind claims; flag content that lacks clear sourcing.
4. **Framing Check** – Ask: *Is the story framed as a problem, a solution, a threat, or a victory?* Identify the narrative angle.
5. **Opposition Mapping** – Summarize the best counter‑argument to your current view; if you cannot, seek out a source that presents it.

Applying Media Bias Detection consistently improves critical thinking, reduces echo‑chamber effects, and supports a more balanced, evidence‑based worldview.
