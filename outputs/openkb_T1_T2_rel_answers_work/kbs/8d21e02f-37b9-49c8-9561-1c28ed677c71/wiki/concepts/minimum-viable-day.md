## Minimum Viable Day

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A lightweight fallback plan for each luteal‑phase day when energy is very low, outlining the smallest set of activities needed to stay on track.

### Core Idea
A *minimum viable day* (MVD) is a stripped‑down version of the regular luteal‑week schedule that keeps you moving, feeding, and completing essential work without over‑exerting yourself. It is meant for days when symptoms, fatigue, or unexpected demands make the full plan unrealistic. The MVD preserves consistency while honoring the body’s need for rest.

### Structure (derived from [[summaries/session-01_2025-07-15]])
| Day | Workout (MVD) | Work Priorities (MVD) | Meal / Snack (MVD) |
|-----|---------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | 10‑minute easy walk | Answer only critical messages | Simple leftovers or ready‑to‑eat breakfast; grab‑and‑go snack |
| 2 | Skip workout (or 5‑min stretch) | Complete one tiny task | Easy protein‑rich snack (e.g., cheese + crackers) |
| 3 | 5‑minute stretch or mobility | One micro‑step on a project | Apple + nut butter |
| 4 | No workout | Review to‑do list only | Warm comfort meal; crackers + nut butter |
| 5 | Skip workout | One simple maintenance task | Yogurt or cottage cheese |
| 6 | 5‑minute mobility | One easy work item | Veggies + hummus |
| 7 | No workout | Quick weekly review | Simple snack (banana + peanut butter) |

### Why It Works
1. **Energy‑aligned** – Keeps activity at a level that does not drain remaining energy.
2. **Psychological safety** – Guarantees you still accomplish *something* each day, preventing guilt.
3. **Consistency** – Even the smallest actions maintain the habit loop of movement → nourishment → task completion.
4. **Flexibility** – You can swap any MVD component for a slightly larger one if you feel better.

### How to Use It
1. **Assess your energy** each morning (e.g., fatigue rating 1‑10).
2. **Select the appropriate MVD row** for that day.
3. **Execute the three pillars** – brief movement, one essential work item, and at least one balanced snack/meal.
4. **Scale up** if you notice extra capacity; otherwise, stick with the MVD.
