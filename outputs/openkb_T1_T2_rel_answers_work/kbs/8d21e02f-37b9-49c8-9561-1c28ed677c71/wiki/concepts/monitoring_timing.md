# Monitoring Timing in Fertility Treatment

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** How clinics schedule scans and labs to align treatment steps with real‑time follicle and hormone changes.

---

## Overview
Monitoring timing refers to the dynamic, patient‑specific scheduling of ultrasounds and hormone tests during a fertility cycle. Rather than following a fixed calendar, clinics base each appointment on **what the ovaries and endocrine system are doing at that moment**. This approach ensures that interventions such as the trigger shot, intra‑uterine insemination (IUI), or egg retrieval occur when the biological markers are optimal.

## Core Components

1. **Baseline Assessment**
   - Early‑cycle ultrasound to confirm a thin endometrial lining (≈ 3‑5 mm) and small, resting follicles (≈ 5‑10 mm).
   - Baseline hormone panel (E2, LH, FSH, progesterone) should be low, confirming a quiescent state.
   - A 48‑hour follow‑up is typically scheduled to see if a **lead follicle** emerges and if estradiol begins to rise.

2. **Dynamic Ultrasound Review**
   - Follicle size, count, and growth rate are tracked.
   - The endometrial lining thickness is monitored for estrogen‑driven thickening.
   - These visual cues are interpreted alongside hormone results.
   - See [[concepts/ultrasound_assessment]] for detailed scan criteria.

3. **Hormone Monitoring**
   - **Estradiol (E2)** rises proportionally with follicle growth and is the primary driver for timing decisions.
   - **LH** remains stable until the natural or trigger‑induced surge.
   - **Progesterone** must stay low until after ovulation or trigger; an early rise is a red flag.
   - Details are linked in [[concepts/hormone_monitoring]].

4. **Decision Points**
   - **Trigger/ IUI timing** – when the lead follicle reaches ~18‑20 mm (IUI) or a cohort of follicles reaches 17‑20 mm (IVF) and the lining is ≥ 7 mm.
   - **Retrieval timing** – same follicle size criteria but coordinated with medication protocols.
   - The next step is scheduled **only after** the ultrasound picture and hormone panel are concordant.

## Typical Workflow
| Day | Action | Expected Findings | Next Step |
|-----|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| Baseline (Day 0‑2) | Ultrasound + baseline labs | Thin lining, small follicles, low E2 | Re‑scan in 48 h |
| Early Development (Day 3‑5) | Repeat ultrasound + E2 | One follicle 11‑14 mm, E2 rising | Continue monitoring |
| Pre‑Trigger (Day 6‑8) | Final scan + labs | Follicle 18‑20 mm, lining ≥ 7 mm, E2 high, progesterone low | Administer trigger → IUI or retrieval |

If any **red‑flag** appears—e.g., progesterone > 1 ng/mL, premature LH surge, cyst > 25 mm, or a lining that fails to thicken—clinics will **delay** the trigger and reassess in 24‑48 h.

## Patient‑Facing Questions
- "What is the size of my largest follicle and how many are measurable?"
- "What are my current estradiol and progesterone levels?"
- "Is my endometrial lining progressing as expected?"
- "Are there any red‑flags that would change the timing?"

These questions help patients understand why the clinic is choosing a specific interval between visits.
