## Menstrual Cycle Modes

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A framework that divides the menstrual cycle into four general "modes" – post‑period rise, ovulation peak, early luteal steadiness, and pre‑period dip – each with characteristic energy, mood, sleep, and performance tendencies.

### Overview
The concept originates from the conversation in [[summaries/session-01_2025-07-15]], where the assistant suggested using the natural fluctuations across the cycle as a planning tool. By matching daily activities to the dominant mode, a person can align workout intensity, work priorities, and nutrition with their physiological state.

| Mode | Typical timing in cycle | Key characteristics | Recommended focus |
|------|------------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Post‑period rise** | Right after menstruation (days 1‑5) | ↑ energy, motivation, clearer cognition | High‑intensity training, focused creative work, protein‑rich meals |
| **Ovulation peak** | Around ovulation (days 12‑16) | Peak physical/social energy, confidence, efficient digestion | Challenging strength/cardio, collaborative tasks, lighter meals |
| **Early luteal steadiness** | Early luteal (days 17‑21) | Generally steady, beginning of gradual shift toward stability | Consistent routines, moderate workouts, blood‑sugar‑balancing meals |
| **Pre‑period dip** | Late luteal / pre‑menstruation (days 22‑28) | Lower energy, irritability, disrupted sleep, cravings | Gentle movement (walk, yoga), low‑stress work, regular grounding meals |

### Practical Application
1. **Track personal patterns** for 1‑2 cycles to see how closely they match the generic modes.
2. **Adjust daily plans** by tilting toward the suggestions for the current mode rather than following a rigid schedule.
3. **Integrate with the luteal‑week template** (see [[concepts/luteal-week-template]]) for detailed day‑by‑day guidance during the lower‑energy phase.
4. **Use the minimum viable day concept** (see [[concepts/minimum-viable-day]]) when energy drops further than typical for a mode.
