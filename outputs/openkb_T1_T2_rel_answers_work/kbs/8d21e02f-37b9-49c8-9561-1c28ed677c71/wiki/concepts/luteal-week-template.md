# Luteal Week Template

**Type:** Concept

**Definition** – A repeatable, seven‑day framework designed for the luteal phase of the menstrual cycle, combining moderate‑intensity exercise, low‑to‑moderate work priorities, and balanced nutrition to maintain consistency while respecting lower energy levels.

---

## Overview
The template translates the broader [[concepts/menstrual-cycle-modes]] idea of a "moderate‑and‑consistent" luteal mode into a concrete daily plan. Each day lists:
- **Workout** (type and suggested duration)
- **Work priorities** (1–2 tasks aligned with steady, behind‑the‑scenes productivity)
- **Meal & snack structure** (regular meals emphasizing protein, slow‑digesting carbs, and fiber)
- **Minimum viable day** fallback for days when energy is especially low.

The structure encourages tracking personal responses for one or two cycles and adjusting the plan as needed.

---

## Daily Structure
| Day | Workout | Work Priorities | Meals & Snacks | Minimum Viable Day |
|-----|---------|----------------|----------------|--------------------|
| **1** | 20‑25 min steady strength or walk | Weekly outline; inbox cleanup | Balanced breakfast, steady lunch, protein + slow‑carb dinner; snack: yogurt + fruit | 10‑min walk; essential messages only; easy leftovers |
| **2** | 20 min light strength or gentle intervals | One focused task; one admin task | Protein‑heavy breakfast; simple lunch; grounding dinner; snack: cheese + crackers | Skip workout; one tiny task; easy regular meals |
| **3** | Brisk walk or Pilates (20‑30 min) | Advance one project; small cleanup | Consistent meals; mid‑morning snack; snack: apple + nut butter |
| **4** | Light stretching or easy walk | Check progress; clear small tasks | Same structure; warm comfort dinner; snack: crackers + nut butter |
| **5** | Slow walk or rest | One simple task; basic maintenance | Warm, steady meals; snack: yogurt or cottage cheese |
| **6** | Short strength or stretching (15‑20 min) | One low‑effort meaningful task | Simple, familiar meals; snack: veggies + hummus |
| **7** | Gentle yoga or rest | Tie up loose ends; prep for next week | Easy‑to‑digest meals; snack: banana + peanut butter |

---

## Workout Guidelines
- **Intensity:** Moderate to low; avoid high‑intensity spikes.
- **Duration:** 15‑30 minutes per session.
- **Mode options:** Strength (light weights, bodyweight), brisk walking, Pilates, yoga, or simple mobility.
- **Minimum viable:** As little as 5‑10 minutes of gentle movement or full rest if needed.

---

## Work Priorities
- Focus on **quiet, organizational, or maintenance tasks** (e.g., planning, editing, filing, low‑stress admin).
- Limit high‑creativity, high‑social‑energy, or deadline‑driven work.
- Use the **minimum viable day** to perform only one essential task.

---

## Nutrition Guidance
- **Regular timing:** Keep meals spaced evenly to avoid hunger‑driven mood dips.
- **Macro balance:** Emphasize protein + slow‑digesting carbs + healthy fats at each main meal.
- **Snacks:** Pair protein with a comforting carbohydrate (e.g., yogurt + fruit, nut butter + crackers).
- **Hydration:** Encourage consistent water intake throughout the day.
