## Summary of Session 32 (2025‑10‑13)

This document provides a practical cheat‑sheet for patients undergoing fertility treatments, outlining how clinics decide the timing of monitoring and when to move to the next step (trigger, IUI, or egg retrieval). It breaks down the typical **baseline**, **developing**, and **ready‑to‑trigger/retrieval** ranges for ultrasound measurements and hormone levels, compares medicated IUI cycles with IVF stimulation cycles, and lists common **red‑flags** that can delay progress.

### Key Concepts
- **Monitoring timing** – clinics base appointments on real‑time follicle growth and hormone trends rather than a fixed calendar. [[concepts/monitoring_timing]]
- **Ultrasound criteria** – follicle size, number of measurable follicles, and endometrial lining thickness guide decisions. [[concepts/ultrasound_assessment]]
- **Hormone panels** – estradiol (E2) is the primary driver, with LH, FSH, and progesterone providing context. [[concepts/hormone_monitoring]]
- **Cycle type differences** – medicated IUI aims for a single dominant follicle; IVF aims for a cohort of mature follicles, leading to different estradiol trajectories. (plain text)
- **Red‑flags** – early progesterone rise, premature LH surge, ovarian cysts, or a non‑responsive lining can postpone trigger or retrieval. (plain text)

### Cheat‑Sheet Table (Text Format)
| Cycle Type | Phase | Lining (mm) | Lead Follicle(s) (mm) | Estradiol (pg/mL) | LH (mIU/mL) | Progesterone (ng/mL) | Typical Action |
|------------|-------|------------|----------------------|-------------------|------------|----------------------|----------------|
| **Medicated IUI** | Baseline | 3–5 | 5–10 (multiple) | < 50 | Low early‑cycle | Very low | Start meds / baseline scan |
| | Developing | 5–7 | 11–14 (lead) | Rising, matches lead | Stable | Still low | Continue meds, repeat scan in 48 h |
| | Ready to Trigger | ≥ 7 | 18–20 (single) | Consistent with one mature follicle | Stable (unless surge) | Still low | Schedule trigger → IUI |
| **IVF Stimulation** | Baseline | 3–5 | 5–10 (multiple) | < 50 | Low | Very low | Begin stimulation |
| | Developing | 5–7+ | 10–14 (cohort) | Rapid rise (reflects many follicles) | Stable | Still low | Monitor growth, adjust meds |
| | Ready for Retrieval | ≥ 7 | 17–20 (several) | High (reflects total follicle load) | Stable | Still low | Trigger → egg retrieval |

### Suggested Patient Questions
- “What is the size of my largest follicle and how many are measurable?”
- “What are my current estradiol, LH, FSH, and progesterone levels?”
- “How does my endometrial lining compare to the expected range for the next step?”
- “What specific signs are you looking for before deciding on a trigger or medication adjustment?”
- “Are there any red‑flags today that might delay the plan?”

### Red‑Flag Summary
- Progesterone > 1 ng/mL before trigger  
- Sudden LH surge (≥ 20 mIU/mL) indicating imminent ovulation  
- Ovarian cyst or dominant follicle > 25 mm  
- Lining failing to thicken (> 0.5 mm increase over 48 h) despite rising estradiol
