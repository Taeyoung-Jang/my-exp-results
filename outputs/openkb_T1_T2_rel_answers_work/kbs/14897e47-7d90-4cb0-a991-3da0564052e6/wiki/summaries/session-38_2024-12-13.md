# Summary of session‑38 (2024‑12‑13)

## Core Reasoning behind Strict Requirements
- Banks must comply with **[[concepts/anti_money_laundering]]** and **[[concepts/know_your_customer]]** regulations, as well as international **[[concepts/tax_reporting]]** agreements (e.g., FATCA, CRS).  
- When a customer has foreign ties such as **[[concepts/citizenship_mention]]**, residency, or income, the risk profile is higher, prompting tighter document checks.

## Typical Documents Requested
- **Primary ID**: passport (foreign or domestic), national ID card, residence permit, or driver’s licence (depending on jurisdiction).
- **Proof of address**: utility bill, government correspondence, rental agreement.
- **Tax identification numbers** for each relevant country.
- **Source‑of‑funds evidence**: employment contract, tax returns, investment statements.
- **Specific tax forms** (e.g., [[entities/w_8ben]]) for non‑U.S. persons.

## Alternatives When a Foreign Passport Is Unavailable
- Acceptable substitutes can include a **national ID card**, **residence permit**, or a **combination of IDs** (e.g., driver’s licence + proof of immigration status).
- Providing **proof of passport renewal** (receipt, application confirmation) may allow a temporary grace period.

## Managing a “Pending Documentation” Status
- The account generally remains open but with **restricted functionality**:
  - Incoming deposits usually allowed.
  - Outgoing transfers, wire payments, new product enrolments, debit‑card use, or online transactions may be blocked.
- Banks often set a compliance deadline; failure to meet it can lead to account closure or further restrictions.

## Practical Steps for the Customer
1. **Request a written list** of acceptable documents and the exact compliance deadline.
2. **Ask for a written policy or regulatory citation** that mandates the foreign passport requirement.
3. **Escalate**:
   - First contact the **branch manager** for clarification.
   - If unresolved, request involvement of the **compliance department**.
4. Document all communications (secure messaging, email) to have evidence of the agreed‑upon timeline.
