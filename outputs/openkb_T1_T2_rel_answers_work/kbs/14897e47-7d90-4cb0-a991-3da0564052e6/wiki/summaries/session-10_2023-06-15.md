# Summary of session-10_2023-06-15

- **Main question**: How to state [[concepts/work_authorization_phrasing]] on a resume and cover letter without sounding awkward.  
- **Resume header recommendation**: Use a short line such as “Authorized to work in the [[entities/united-states]]” or “U.S. work authorization” placed directly beneath contact details. Optionally “U.S. citizen” (see [[concepts/citizenship_mention]]) if desired.  
- **Cover‑letter sentence**: “I am fully authorized to work in the [[entities/united-states]] and do not require sponsorship.”  
- **Sponsorship wording**: Not needed if eligibility is already clear; add only when the job posting explicitly asks for sponsorship information (see [[concepts/sponsorship_statement]]).  
- **Placement guidance**: Put the line as a separate entry in the header, below the city/email/phone line—not mixed with location or in a separate “Additional” section (see [[concepts/resume_header_placement]]).
