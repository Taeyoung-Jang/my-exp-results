# Tax Reporting

**Definition**: The systematic collection, exchange, and disclosure of taxpayer information between financial institutions and tax authorities, often mandated by international agreements.

## Core Elements
- **Regulatory Frameworks**: Includes agreements such as the Common Reporting Standard (CRS) and the U.S. Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). These frameworks require banks to identify customers with foreign tax ties and report relevant account details.
- **Bank Responsibilities**: Conduct due‑diligence, verify foreign tax identification numbers, collect appropriate documentation (e.g., passports, tax forms), and submit periodic reports to the relevant tax authority.
- **Customer Documentation**: Typical documents requested include foreign passports, national ID cards, residence permits, tax identification numbers, and forms like [[entities/w_8ben]].
- **Compliance Consequences**: Failure to report can result in penalties, account restrictions, or closure. Banks may place accounts in a "pending documentation" status, limiting functionality until proper tax reporting can be completed.
