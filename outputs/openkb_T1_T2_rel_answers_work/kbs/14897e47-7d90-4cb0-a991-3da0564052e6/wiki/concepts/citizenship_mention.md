# Citizenship Mention

**Definition**: The practice of explicitly stating that a job applicant is a United States citizen on application materials such as a resume header or cover letter.

**When to use**
- When the employer asks directly about citizenship or work eligibility.
- When you want to convey that you do not need sponsorship without adding extra wording.

**Recommended phrasing**
- Prefer concise language like **"U.S. citizen"** or **"Authorized to work in the United States"**.
- Avoid overly legalistic statements; the short label is sufficient because citizenship implies work authorization.

**Placement**
- Place the line as a separate entry in the resume header, directly beneath contact details, following the guidance in [[concepts/resume_header_placement]].
- If using a cover letter, a single sentence such as "I am fully authorized to work in the United States and do not require sponsorship" may be used, tying into the broader [[concepts/work_authorization_phrasing]] and [[concepts/sponsorship_statement]] considerations.
