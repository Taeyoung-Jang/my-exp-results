# Resume Header Placement

**Definition**: The optimal location and formatting for work‑authorization statements within the top section of a résumé.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-10_2023-06-15]]**

- Place the line **directly beneath** contact information (city, phone, email) as a standalone entry.
- Keep it short: “Authorized to work in the United States” or “U.S. work authorization”.
- Do **not** merge it with the location line or create a separate “Additional” section unless the employer explicitly requests it.
- If you prefer to note citizenship, “U.S. citizen” can be used, but the neutral phrasing is generally preferred.

**Interaction with related concepts**

- The phrasing itself is covered by [[concepts/work_authorization_phrasing]].
- When a job posting mentions sponsorship, the decision to add a sponsorship note is addressed in [[concepts/sponsorship_statement]].
- Mentioning citizenship falls under [[concepts/citizenship_mention]].

**Why placement matters**

Proper placement ensures the eligibility information is visible without disrupting the visual flow of the header, helping recruiters quickly confirm work authorization while focusing on qualifications.

**Example layout**

```
John Doe
San Francisco, CA • (555) 123‑4567 • john.doe@email.com
Authorized to work in the United States
```

This format aligns with best practices from the source discussion and maintains a clean, professional appearance.
