# W‑8BEN (Certificate of Foreign Status of Beneficial Owner for United States Tax Withholding)

**Type:** other

**Purpose**
- Provides the United States Internal Revenue Service (IRS) with certification that the holder is a non‑U.S. person and therefore eligible for reduced or exempt withholding on certain types of U.S.‑source income.
- Used by foreign individuals, corporations, and other entities to claim treaty benefits or claim exemption under FATCA.

**Key Elements**
- Beneficial owner’s name, country of citizenship, and foreign tax identification number.
- Claim of treaty benefits (if applicable) and certification of non‑U.S. status.
- Signature and date.

**Regulatory Context**
- Required by [[concepts/tax_reporting]] regulations such as the United States’ FATCA and the OECD’s CRS, which banks must adhere to when conducting [[concepts/know_your_customer]] and [[concepts/anti_money_laundering]] checks.
- Banks (e.g., [[entities/banks]]) request a completed W‑8BEN to satisfy U.S. withholding obligations for accounts that generate U.S.‑source income.
