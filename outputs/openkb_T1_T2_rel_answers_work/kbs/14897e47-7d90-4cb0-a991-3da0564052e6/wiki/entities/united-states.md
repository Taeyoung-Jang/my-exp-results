# United States (place)

The United States is a sovereign nation in North America that serves as the legal jurisdiction for employment eligibility discussed in [[summaries/session-10_2023-06-15]].  In the context of job applications, being a **U.S. citizen** signals automatic work authorization and removes the need for sponsorship.

## Relevance to Work Authorization
- **Citizenship mention** – Candidates often list "U.S. citizen" or a neutral phrase like "Authorized to work in the United States" to convey eligibility, which ties to the concept of [[concepts/citizenship_mention]].
- **Work authorization phrasing** – The preferred wording for resume headers and cover letters is covered by [[concepts/work_authorization_phrasing]].
- **Sponsorship statement** – Since a U.S. citizen does not require sponsorship, references to [[concepts/sponsorship_statement]] are typically omitted unless a posting explicitly asks.
- **Resume header placement** – The recommended location for the eligibility line is a separate line beneath contact details, as detailed in [[concepts/resume_header_placement]].

These points illustrate how the United States functions as the backdrop for the legal and practical considerations of work eligibility in the discussed session.
