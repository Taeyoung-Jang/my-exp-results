# Relationship Check‑In

A **relationship check‑in** is a recurring, low‑pressure conversation where partners openly share their current feelings, needs, and any emerging concerns. Its purpose is to keep the partnership aligned, supportive, and growing, without turning the dialogue into a performance review.

## Core Elements
- **Frequency** – Typically weekly or bi‑weekly, timed so the check‑in feels natural rather than forced.
- **Structure** – A set of 5–7 gentle prompts that invite each person to reflect on recent experiences, emotional states, and practical needs.
- **Tone** – Casual, curiosity‑driven, and framed from personal experience (e.g., “I’ve been feeling…”), which aligns with [[concepts/low-pressure-communication]].
- **Focus** – Emphasizes ***feelings and needs*** over blame, mirroring the principles of [[concepts/feelings-and-needs]].

## Example Weekly Prompts
1. What moment this week made you feel especially close?
2. Did anything leave you feeling off or disconnected?
3. Is there a need you’ve been having that I might not have noticed?
4. What did I do that you appreciated more than I realized?
5. How do you feel about the balance between time together and time apart?
6. Is there any stress you’d like me to know about?
7. What small change could we each try next week to make things smoother?

## Handling Tough Topics
- **Use “I” statements** to keep the conversation personal and non‑judgmental.
- **Ask for permission** before diving into a harder subject (“Can we talk about…?”).
- **Prioritise understanding** over winning an argument, fostering a teamwork mindset.
- **Avoid a performance‑review framing**; keep the focus on connection and growth.

## Adaptations for Long‑Term Partnerships
- Incorporate prompts about personal growth, changing life rhythms, and upcoming plans.
- Gently check in on intimacy and evolving expectations.
- Highlight mutual support for external stressors and future changes.

## Benefits
- Maintains ongoing alignment and emotional safety.
- Encourages continuous gratitude and appreciation.
- Strengthens curiosity about each other’s evolving lives.
