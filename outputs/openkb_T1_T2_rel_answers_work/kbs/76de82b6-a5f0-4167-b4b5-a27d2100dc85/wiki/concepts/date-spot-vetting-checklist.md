# Date Spot Vetting Checklist

A systematic way to evaluate potential first‑date locations, ensuring they support a low‑pressure, comfortable, and safe experience for both people.

## Core Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Questions to Ask | Why It Matters |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| **Lighting** | Is the lighting friendly (neither too dim nor overly harsh)? | Sets a welcoming tone and helps avoid awkward shadows that can affect mood. |
| **Noise Level** | Can you hold a conversation without shouting? | Enables genuine connection and reduces the need for forced small talk. |
| **Accessibility** | Is the spot easy to reach by public transit or parking? Are there clear entrances/exits? | Reduces stress about logistics and gives a natural “out” if the vibe isn’t right. |
| **Safety & Public Presence** | Is the venue well‑lit, busy, and in a mixed‑use area (cafés, bars, shops)? | Provides a secure environment, especially important for early‑stage dates. |
| **Exit Options** | Does the location have a natural endpoint (e.g., a coffee bar you can leave after 30 min, a bar with a clear closing time)? | Allows you to gracefully end the meet if needed without awkwardness. |
| **Atmosphere / Vibe** | Does the setting match the desired date length (quick meet vs. longer hang) and vibe (casual, cozy, playful)? | Aligns with the intended experience and helps convey personality. |
| **Inclusive Amenities** | Are there dietary options, wheelchair access, or quiet corners? | Shows consideration for diverse needs and can reduce anxiety. |

## How to Use the Checklist
1. **Pre‑date research** – Look up photos, reviews, and menus to gauge lighting, noise, and crowd. 
2. **Run through the questions** – Mentally or on a phone note, answer each dimension before committing.
3. **Match to date type** – For a 30–60 min meet, prioritize quick‑entry spots (indie coffee shop, bakery). For a 2–3 hr hang, consider venues that allow a natural flow (wine bar, bookstore café, board‑game lounge).
4. **Communicate the plan** – When suggesting the spot, embed the checklist implicitly by mentioning specific attributes (e.g., “Let’s meet at the bright little bakery on Main – easy parking and a cozy patio”).
