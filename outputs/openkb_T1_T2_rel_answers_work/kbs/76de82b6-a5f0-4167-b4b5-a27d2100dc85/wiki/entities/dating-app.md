# Dating App

**Type:** Product

**Description:** A platform that connects users for dating, enabling profile creation, messaging, and first‑date planning.

**Relevant Session:** [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-20]] provides guidance on using a dating app to suggest low‑pressure first‑date ideas and handling pushy requests.

**Key Concepts:**
- [[concepts/first-date-ideas]] – concrete venue suggestions for quick and longer meets.
- [[concepts/low-pressure-communication]] – wording for profiles and chat messages.
- [[concepts/public-first-date-principle]] – boundary of meeting in public first.
- [[concepts/date-spot-vetting-checklist]] – criteria for evaluating date locations.
- [[concepts/feelings-and-needs]] – addressing nervousness and personal comfort.
- [[concepts/relationship-check-in]] – later‑stage communication (mentioned in broader context).

**Usage Tips from Session 35:**
- Include profile lines like “I’m into easy first meets—coffee, a quick walk, or a cute bakery—just enough to see if we click.”
- Offer chat suggestions that are specific (e.g., indie coffee shop, boba place, cozy wine bar) and give an easy out.
- Apply the vetting checklist (lighting, noise, accessibility, exit options, safety) when selecting a meeting spot.
- Use boundary scripts ranging from playful to direct if a match pushes for private or secluded dates.
