# Breathing Pattern

A **breathing pattern** is a deliberately timed sequence of inhalation, pause, and exhalation designed to regulate the nervous system, sustain attention, and deepen bodily awareness during activities such as yoga, meditation, or any focused practice.

## Core Elements
- **Inhalation count** – typically 4 units (e.g., seconds) of gentle, steady breath.
- **Hold (pause)** – a brief retention of breath for 1 unit, allowing the inhale to settle.
- **Exhalation count** – an extended release of 5 units, encouraging relaxation and a sense of release.

This **4‑1‑5** rhythm was highlighted in [[summaries/session-34_2025-12-13]] as a practical tool to counteract [[concepts/attention_drift]] during a yoga flow.

## Nose vs. Mouth Technique
- **Nasal breathing** for the entire cycle yields a quieter, steadier flow, supporting subtle internal focus.
- **Mouth exhale** can be used when a stronger sense of release is desired, especially during moments of tension.

Choosing between the two depends on personal comfort and the desired energetic outcome of the practice.

## Relationship to Intentions
A breathing pattern often pairs with a **yoga intention** or mantra, anchoring the breath to a mental focal point. In the referenced session, the mantra *"Breathing in ease"* was paired with the 4‑1‑5 pattern to reinforce calm.

## Practical Applications
- **Yoga** – stabilizes poses, deepens proprioception, and supports mindful transitions.
- **Meditation** – grounds wandering thoughts, creating a rhythmic anchor.
- **Stress management** – short, accessible pattern that can be employed anywhere to quickly calm the nervous system.
