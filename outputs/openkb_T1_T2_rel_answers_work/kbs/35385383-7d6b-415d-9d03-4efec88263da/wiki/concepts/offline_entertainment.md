# Offline Entertainment

**Definition**: A set of audio media and offline-capable games that can be enjoyed during travel or waiting periods without requiring an internet connection.

## Core Elements

1. **Podcasts & Audiobooks**
   - Light, episodic podcasts such as *Conan O’Brien Needs a Friend* [[entities/conan-obrien-needs-a-friend]], *Stuff You Should Know* [[entities/stuff-you-should-know]], *Ologies* [[entities/ologies]], and *Armchair Expert* [[entities/armchair-expert]].
   - Gentle audiobooks like *The No. 1 Ladies’ Detective Agency* [[entities/the-no-1-ladies-detective-agency]] and *The House in the Cerulean Sea* [[entities/the-house-in-the-cerulean-sea]].
   - These selections support [[concepts/attention_drift]] by allowing listeners to pause and resume without losing narrative continuity.

2. **Offline Phone Games**
   - Primary recommendation: *Alto’s Odyssey* [[entities/altos-odyssey]] – a relaxing endless‑runner that works fully offline.
   - Backup option: *Monument Valley* [[entities/monument-valley]] – a calm puzzle adventure confirmed to function completely offline after installation.
   - Additional low‑effort titles: *Mini Metro* [[entities/mini-metro]] and classic *Solitaire* [[entities/solitaire]].
   - These games embody [[concepts/offline_entertainment]] by providing engaging diversion without network dependence.

3. **Simple Offline Activities**
   - Word/number puzzles, people‑watching, spontaneous storytelling, and note‑taking.
   - These activities reinforce [[concepts/travel_time_management]] by turning idle moments into low‑energy recreation.
