## Yoga Intention

A **yoga intention** is a concise mantra or phrase chosen at the beginning of a practice to frame the mental and emotional tone for the session. It serves as a focal point that helps practitioners stay present, counteracting **[[concepts/attention_drift]]** and deepening the mind‑body connection.

### Key Elements
- **Brevity** – Typically 3–5 words, making it easy to repeat silently.
- **Emotional Quality** – Can be *energizing* (e.g., “Here I rise,” “Power moves me”) or *calming* (e.g., “Settle into now,” “Breathing in ease”).
- **Alignment with Breath** – When paired with a **[[concepts/breathing_pattern]]** such as the 4‑1‑5 inhale‑hold‑exhale rhythm, the intention reinforces focus during each breath cycle.

### Examples from Session 34
- "I soften, I open, I arrive."
- Energizing: "Here I rise," "Power moves me."
- Calming: "Settle into now," "Breathing in ease."

These short phrases were suggested in the dialogue of **[[summaries/session-34_2025-12-13]]**, illustrating how practitioners can quickly select an intention that matches the desired energy of their practice.

### Practical Use
1. **Choose** an intention that resonates with the intended mood of the session.
2. **Repeat** it silently at the start of the practice and whenever the mind wanders.
3. **Integrate** it with your breathing pattern to create a rhythmic anchor.

By consistently applying a yoga intention, yogis cultivate a purposeful, mindful practice that supports both physical flow and mental clarity.
