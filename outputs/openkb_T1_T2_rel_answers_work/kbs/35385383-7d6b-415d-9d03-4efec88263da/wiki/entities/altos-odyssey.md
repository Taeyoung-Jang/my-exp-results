# Alto’s Odyssey

**Type:** Product

## Overview
Alto’s Odyssey is an endless‑runner sandboarding game for iOS and Android, praised for its serene visuals, gentle soundtrack, and simple swipe controls that make it easy to pick up and put down.

## Recommendation in [[summaries/session-05_2025-06-15]]
The assistant in the travel‑time document recommends Alto’s Odyssey as the top offline phone game for a three‑hour train journey. It is highlighted for its:
- Low cognitive load, allowing play in short bursts.
- Fully offline functionality once installed, fitting the need for entertainment without internet.
- Ability to turn idle travel time into a calm, enjoyable experience.
