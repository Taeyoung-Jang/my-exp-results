## Monument Valley

Monument Valley is a visually striking, low‑effort puzzle adventure game for mobile devices. It is praised for its serene atmosphere, intuitive controls, and the ability to play **completely offline** once installed, making it a reliable companion for long trips without network coverage.

- Confirmed to function fully offline, aligning with the needs of travelers seeking [[concepts/offline_entertainment]].
