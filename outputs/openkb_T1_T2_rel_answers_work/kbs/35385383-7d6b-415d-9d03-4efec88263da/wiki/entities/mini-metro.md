# Mini Metro

**Type:** product

Mini Metro is a minimalist puzzle/strategy game where players design and operate a growing subway map. It runs on iOS, Android, and desktop, and after installation it works fully offline, making it a good companion for long trips.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-05_2025-06-15]]
- Recommended as a low‑effort offline phone game to pass time during travel.
- Suggested as a backup to [[entities/altos-odyssey]] when only one game can be downloaded before a signal drops.
- Aligns with the [[concepts/offline_entertainment]] concept, providing engaging yet low‑focus gameplay.
- Supports [[concepts/travel_time_management]] by helping the perceived duration of journeys feel shorter.

## Related entities
- Alternative offline game: [[entities/altos-odyssey]]
- Other suggested offline titles: [[entities/monument-valley]], [[entities/solitaire]]

## Usage tips
- Play in short bursts; each line of the map can be expanded or paused without losing progress.
- Ideal for environments where attention drifts, complementing the [[concepts/attention_drift]] approach.
