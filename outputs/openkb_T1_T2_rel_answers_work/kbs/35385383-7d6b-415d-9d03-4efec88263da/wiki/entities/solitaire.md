- Works without an internet connection, fitting the **[[concepts/offline_entertainment]]** recommendation.
- Ideal for moments of **[[concepts/attention_drift]]**, allowing short bursts of play without demanding focus.
- Complements other travel‑time strategies such as listening to light podcasts or stretching, supporting overall **[[concepts/travel_time_management]]**.

- Keep a deck of virtual cards handy on your phone for long train rides or flights.

- Pair with a light podcast (e.g., *Stuff You Should Know*) for a balanced travel entertainment mix.
