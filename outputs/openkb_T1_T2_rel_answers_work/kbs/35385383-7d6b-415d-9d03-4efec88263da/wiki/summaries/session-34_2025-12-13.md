## Summary of Session 34 (2025‑12‑13)

- The user requested a brief **[[concepts/yoga_intention]]/mantra** to focus on at the start of a yoga flow. The assistant suggested: “I soften, I open, I arrive.” and a simple **[[concepts/breathing_pattern]]** (4‑1‑5 breath).

- The user asked for even shorter alternatives (3‑5 words), seeking one energizing and one calming option.  
  - Energizing: “Here I rise.”  
  - Calming: “Settle into now.”

- Additional requests led to more 3‑word suggestions:  
  - Energizing: “Power moves me.”  
  - Calming: “Breathing in ease.”

- The user selected the calming mantra **“Breathing in ease.”** and inquired about the mechanics of the 4‑1‑5 breath: whether to breathe entirely through the nose or exhale through the mouth.  
  - The assistant explained that the whole cycle can be done through the nose for a quieter, steadier feel, but exhaling through the mouth is also fine if a sense of release is desired.

### Key Concepts
- [[concepts/yoga_intention]] – short intention or mantra for yoga practice  
- [[concepts/breathing_pattern]] – the 4‑1‑5 inhale‑hold‑exhale rhythm  
- Nose breathing vs. mouth exhale – technique options for the breath cycle  
- Energizing mantra – brief phrase to boost energy  
- Calming mantra – brief phrase to promote calmness  
- Managing [[concepts/attention_drift]] during a yoga flow through mindful breath and short affirmations
