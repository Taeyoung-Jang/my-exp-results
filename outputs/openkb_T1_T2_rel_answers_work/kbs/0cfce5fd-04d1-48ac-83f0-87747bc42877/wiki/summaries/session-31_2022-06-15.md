# Summary of session-31_2022-06-15

**Main focus** – How towns can reduce [[concepts/regulatory-friction]] for small businesses, keep the rules that truly protect health and safety, and discard or sunset low‑value red tape.

## Key Recommendations

1. **Make the start‑up phase predictable**  
   - Clear permit requirements, realistic timelines, and transparent fees.  
   - Reduce uncertainty more than reducing the sheer number of rules.

2. **Tailor rules to business scale**  
   - Simple expansions (e.g., a café patio) should not face the same maze as a new hotel.

3. **Prioritize [[concepts/high-value-regulations]]**  
   - Fire‑suppression and building‑code requirements.  
   - Food‑handling and health‑inspection standards.  
   - Noise, odor, and nuisance limits that protect nearby residents.

4. **Identify and eliminate [[concepts/low-value-red-tape]]**  
   - Multi‑step sign approvals that duplicate review.  
   - Separate departmental approvals for minor renovations.  
   - Frequent renewals of trivial permits or paper‑only filings.

5. **Establish a regular, neutral review cycle**  
   - Mixed group of staff, business owners, and residents.  
   - Objective criteria (risk relevance, overlap, delay cost).  
   - Automatic sunset dates for administrative rules, with renewal only on demonstrated need.

6. **Create a simple regulation “scorecard”**  
   - Use the [[concepts/scoring-rubric]] to ask:  
     - Does the rule address a documented risk?  
     - Does it provide a measurable health or nuisance benefit?  
     - What is the average time/cost to comply?  
     - Does it overlap with existing rules?  
     - Is the rule applied consistently and does it cause delays?

7. **Handle aesthetic or “community‑character” rules**  
   - Use broad, predictable guidelines rather than detailed prescriptions.  
   - Offer a limited set of clear options to avoid arbitrary case‑by‑case decisions.  
   - Apply the same [[concepts/scoring-rubric]] to test whether the benefit outweighs the burden.

## Concepts for Cross‑Document Pages

- [[concepts/regulatory-friction]] – The cumulative burden of unclear, unpredictable, or duplicated rules on small businesses.  
- [[concepts/high-value-regulations]] – Rules that demonstrably protect safety, health, or reduce nuisance.  
- [[concepts/low-value-red-tape]] – Administrative steps that add cost or delay without measurable public benefit.  
- [[concepts/scoring-rubric]] – A measurable checklist for evaluating the worth of a rule.
