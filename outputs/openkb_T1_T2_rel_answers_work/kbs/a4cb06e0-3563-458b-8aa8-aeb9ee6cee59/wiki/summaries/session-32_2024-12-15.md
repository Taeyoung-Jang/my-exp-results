# Summary of session-32_2024-12-15

The user seeks low‑effort ways to increase outdoor time without it feeling like a chore, and later asks for a **weather‑based menu** that offers both short (5–10 min) and longer (45–90 min) options for nice, cold, rainy, and windy conditions.

## Key ideas
- **Micro outdoor breaks**: embedding brief activities (e.g., coffee on a balcony, short walk) into existing routines.
- **Habit rhythm** over strict rules: treating outdoor time as a flexible rhythm, using default choices rather than rigid schedules. [[concepts/habit_rhythm]]
- **Habit cueing** & comfort cues: keeping a light jacket, headphones, or a favorite café ready to reduce perceived effort. [[concepts/habit_cueing]]
- **Weather‑based menu**: a succinct “if X weather, do Y” checklist with two duration tiers for each condition. [[concepts/weather_based_menu]]
- **Low‑effort outdoor activities**: short, pleasant actions that can become habitual. [[concepts/low_effort_outdoor_activities]]
- **Movement snacks**: brief bursts of movement that fit into daily flow. [[concepts/movement_snacks]]

## Weather‑adapted checklist (phone‑friendly)
- **Nice weather**
  - *5–10 min*: slow stroll or sit in the sun with a drink.
  - *45–90 min*: neighborhood walk or park work/read session.
- **Cold weather**
  - *5–10 min*: brisk lap to warm up.
  - *45–90 min*: bundle up, hot drink, outdoor table or sunny bench.
- **Rainy weather**
  - *5–10 min*: umbrella walk, enjoy rain sounds.
  - *45–90 min*: work/read under a covered patio or café awning.
- **Windy weather**
  - *5–10 min*: short loop in a sheltered street area.
  - *45–90 min*: find a tucked‑away courtyard or protected bench for longer sit/work.
