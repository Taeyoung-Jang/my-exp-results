# Summary of session-12_2024-06-15

**Purpose**: Provide practical, low‑impact ways to add movement into a typical indoor day without creating a formal fitness routine.

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/movement_snacks]]** – Tiny 30‑90 second activities that can be done in clothing, no equipment needed.
- **[[concepts/habit_cueing]]** – Pairing movement with existing routines or environmental signals instead of phone alarms.
- **[[concepts/indoor_microbreaks]]** – Short breaks designed for apartments, quiet and space‑efficient.
- **Non‑alarm reminders** – Using task transitions, sensory cues, or natural pauses as prompts.
- **Comfort‑activity balance** – Maintaining a sense of comfort while gently increasing activity.

## Suggested “Movement Snack” Menu (30‑90 s each)
1. Ankle circles while standing at the counter  
2. Slow forward fold to loosen the back  
3. Gentle calf raises  
4. Marching in place for one minute  
5. Side‑bend stretch on each side  
6. Shoulder rolls  
7. Seated spinal twist  
8. Knee‑to‑chest lifts (standing)  
9. Hip sway to mobilize the lower back  
10. Light wall “push‑off” (lean rather than push‑up)

## Cue Ideas (No Phone Alarms)
- **Task‑completion cue** – after finishing a work block, refilling water, or switching rooms.  
- **Sensory cue** – when a kettle whistles, a microwave beeps, or a video buffers.  
- **Transition cue** – standing up to use the bathroom or fetch a drink.  
- **Environmental cue** – placing a small visual marker (e.g., a sticky note) near a frequently used spot.

## Indoor‑Focused Cue Strategies & Break Moves
- **Home‑centric cues**: tie movement to any pause in daily home activities (e.g., waiting for laundry, loading a webpage).  
- **Break‑time snacks for long sitting**:  
  - Slow spine roll from standing to a gentle forward fold  
  - Chest‑opening stretch with hands behind the back  
  - Standing leg swings or knee lifts  
  - Relaxed seated twist to loosen the spine  

## Implementation Tips
- **Integrate, don’t interrupt**: Choose a cue that naturally aligns with an existing habit.  
- **Keep it quiet**: All suggested snacks are whisper‑quiet for apartment living.  
- **Start small**: Begin with one or two cues and expand as the habit feels comfortable.  

These recommendations aim to help someone who spends most of the day indoors create a balanced routine of comfort and gentle activity through purposeful micro‑movements and cue‑based habit formation.
