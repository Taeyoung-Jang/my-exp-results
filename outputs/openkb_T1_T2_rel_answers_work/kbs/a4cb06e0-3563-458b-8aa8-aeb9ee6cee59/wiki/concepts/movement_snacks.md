# Movement Snacks

**Definition**: Small, low‑impact actions (typically 30–90 seconds) that can be done in everyday clothing, require no equipment, and fit easily into a typical indoor routine.

**Purpose**
- Provide a gentle way to increase daily movement without a formal workout.
- Enhance comfort‑activity balance by turning movement into a playful "snack" rather than a chore.
- Fit seamlessly into apartment living and quiet environments.

**Key Characteristics**
- **Duration**: 30–90 seconds per snack.
- **Space**: Minimal; can be performed in a hallway, kitchen, or seated at a desk.
- **Attire**: No need to change clothes.
- **Impact**: Low‑impact, quiet, and non‑intrusive.

**Example Menu (from [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]])**
1. Ankle circles while standing at the counter
2. Slow forward fold to loosen the back
3. Gentle calf raises
4. Marching in place for one minute
5. Side‑bend stretch on each side
6. Shoulder rolls
7. Seated spinal twist
8. Knee‑to‑chest lifts (standing)
9. Hip sway to mobilize the lower back
10. Light wall “push‑off” (lean rather than push‑up)

**Implementation Tips**
- **Pair with cues**: Use [[concepts/habit_cueing]] by linking each snack to an existing habit (e.g., refilling water, finishing a task).
- **Micro‑break integration**: Treat snacks as part of [[concepts/indoor_microbreaks]] to break up long periods of sitting.
- **Quiet and discreet**: Choose movements that are whisper‑quiet to respect shared living spaces.
- **Start small**: Begin with 2–3 snacks and expand as they become comfortable.
