# Indoor Microbreaks

**Definition**: Short, low‑impact pauses that introduce gentle movement into an indoor day, designed to be quiet, space‑efficient, and seamlessly integrated with existing routines.

## Core Idea
Indoor microbreaks are a subset of [[concepts/movement_snacks]] focused specifically on the constraints of indoor environments—limited space, shared walls, and the desire for minimal disruption. They aim to improve comfort‑activity balance by inserting brief, purposeful motions during natural pauses.

## Key Elements from [[summaries/session-12_2024-06-15]]
- **Cue‑based triggers**: Use everyday transitions (e.g., finishing a task, refilling water, a video buffering) as prompts rather than phone alarms. This aligns with [[concepts/habit_cueing]].
- **Suggested snacks** (30–90 seconds each):
  - Ankle circles at the counter
  - Slow forward fold
  - Gentle calf raises
  - Marching in place
  - Side‑bends, shoulder rolls, seated twists, knee‑to‑chest lifts, hip sways, light wall push‑offs
- **Indoor‑friendly cues**: Standing up to get a drink, waiting for the microwave, or any natural pause in home activities.
- **Benefits**: Quiet, no equipment, no clothing change, maintains a sense of comfort while gently increasing activity.

## Practical Implementation
1. **Identify natural pauses** in your day (task completion, sensory cues like a kettle whistle).
2. **Pair each pause** with a specific microbreak from the menu above.
3. **Keep it brief** (under a minute) and silent to respect apartment living.
4. **Gradually expand** by adding more cues or varying the movement snacks.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- [[concepts/movement_snacks]] provides the broader catalog of short activities; indoor microbreaks are the indoor‑specific application.
- [[concepts/habit_cueing]] offers the habit‑formation framework that underpins the cue strategy for microbreaks.

## Example Cue Flow
- **Cue**: Microwave beeps → **Microbreak**: 30 s shoulder rolls.
- **Cue**: Video buffering → **Microbreak**: 45 s ankle circles.
- **Cue**: Finish a work block → **Microbreak**: 60 s slow forward fold.
