# Weather‑based Menu

**Definition**: A set of pre‑defined, low‑effort outdoor activity options organized by weather type (nice, cold, rainy, windy) and duration (quick 5–10 min or extended 45–90 min). It serves as a decision‑making shortcut that encourages regular outdoor time without feeling forced.

**Purpose**
- Supports [[concepts/habit_rhythm]] by providing gentle defaults that fit into a daily rhythm rather than a strict schedule.
- Acts as a cue for [[concepts/habit_cueing]]: the menu itself reminds the user to step outside when the appropriate weather condition arises.
- Aligns with [[concepts/low_effort_outdoor_activities]] and [[concepts/movement_snacks]] by offering micro‑outdoor breaks that feel productive or relaxing.

**Structure**
| Weather | 5–10 min option | 45–90 min option |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Nice** | Slow stroll or sip a drink in the sun | Neighborhood walk or park work/read session |
| **Cold** | Brisk lap to warm up | Bundle up, hot drink, sit at an outdoor table or bench |
| **Rainy** | Umbrella walk, enjoy rain sounds | Work/read under a covered patio or café awning |
| **Windy** | Short loop in a sheltered street area | Tucked‑away courtyard or protected bench for longer sit/work |

**Implementation Tips**
- Keep the list on your phone for rapid reference.
- Pair each option with a comfort cue (light jacket, headphones, favorite café) to lower activation energy.
- Treat the menu as a flexible guide: if you skip a day, simply choose the next suitable option.
