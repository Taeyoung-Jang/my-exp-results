# Sunday Mass

Sunday Mass is the central weekly [[entities/catholic-church]] worship service, celebrated on Sunday mornings. In the context of the user’s schedule (see [[summaries/session-34_2024-06-13]]), it serves as a **fixed anchor** in a [[concepts/spiritual-routine]] and exemplifies a [[concepts/communal-practice]] of the local [[entities/parish]].

## Key facts from the session‑34 plan
- Treated as a **fixed** element of the weekly timetable, never moved.
- Provides a rhythmic anchor that supports other habits such as daily prayer, Scripture reading, and exercise.
- Works together with the Thursday [[entities/holy-hour]] and the weekday [[entities/weekday-mass]] to create a balanced [[concepts/flexible-schedule]].
- The user plans to attend the Sunday Mass in the morning and keep the rest of the day open for rest, planning, or light activities.

## Related concepts and entities
- [[concepts/spiritual-routine]] – the overall pattern of worship and prayer.
- [[concepts/communal-practice]] – the shared nature of attending Mass with the parish community.
- [[concepts/flexible-schedule]] – how the fixed Mass fits into a adaptable weekly plan.
- [[entities/parish]] – the local community that offers the Mass.
- [[entities/weekday-mass]] – the complementary weekday worship option.
- [[entities/holy-hour]] – the Thursday evening devotion that the user also attends.
