# Parish

**Type:** place

Parish refers to the local community and facilities of the [[entities/catholic-church]] where the user participates in regular worship and communal activities.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2024-06-13]]

- Offers a **weekday Mass** at 7:00 am (see [[entities/weekday-mass]]).
- Hosts a **holy hour** on Thursdays at 7 pm, followed by a [[entities/benediction]] (see [[entities/holy-hour]]).
- Provides the **Sunday Mass** (see [[entities/sunday-mass]]) each weekend.
- Serves as the anchor for the user's [[concepts/spiritual-routine]] and a setting for [[concepts/flexible-schedule]] adjustments.
