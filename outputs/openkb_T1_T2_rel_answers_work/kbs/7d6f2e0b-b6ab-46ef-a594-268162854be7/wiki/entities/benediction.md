# Benediction (event)

**Type:** Event

**Description:** Benediction is a short Eucharistic service that ends a holy hour with a solemn blessing, often accompanied by incense and hymns.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-34_2024-06-13]]:**
- Occurs every Thursday at 7 pm during the parish’s **holy hour**.  
- The user plans to attend it weekly and treats it as a **fixed** anchor in their [[concepts/spiritual-routine]].  
- It is part of the broader **parish** life of the [[entities/catholic-church]] and reinforces communal worship.
- Because the holy hour is a regular commitment, Benediction helps the user maintain a **flexible‑schedule** while preserving a sense of continuity.

**Related concepts:**
- [[concepts/spiritual-routine]] – integrates Benediction into daily prayer habits.
- [[concepts/flexible-schedule]] – allows other activities to move around this fixed event.
- [[concepts/communal-practice]] – highlights the communal aspect of shared worship.

**Related entities:**
- [[entities/holy-hour]] – the liturgical block that includes Benediction.
- [[entities/parish]] – the community context where Benediction is celebrated.
- [[entities/catholic-church]] – the denomination that commonly practices Benediction.

**Practical notes for the user:**
- Treat Thursday’s Benediction as a **fixed** item; other activities (e.g., workouts) can be shifted to Monday, Friday, or Saturday as swap zones.
- If a special parish event coincides with the holy hour, consider attending the special event and rescheduling the regular Benediction to the next available week, keeping the overall rhythm intact.
