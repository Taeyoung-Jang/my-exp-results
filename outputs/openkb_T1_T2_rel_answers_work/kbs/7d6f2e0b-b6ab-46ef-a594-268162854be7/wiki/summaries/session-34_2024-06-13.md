# Summary of Session 34 (2024‑06‑13)

## Context
The user wants a realistic weekly plan that blends **parish obligations** (Sunday Mass, a weekday Mass, a Thursday holy hour) with **personal habits** (daily prayer/Scripture, three workouts, social time) while keeping at least one unscheduled weeknight for rest.

## Core Schedule Proposals
### Busy Workweek (adjusted for 6:30 pm commute)
| Day | Morning | Lunch | Evening |
|-----|---------|-------|---------|
| **Mon** | Prayer (movable) | — | Workout (movable) |
| **Tue** | Prayer (movable) | — | **Rest night** (fixed) |
| **Wed** | **Weekday Mass – 7:00 am** (fixed) | — | Light evening at home (movable) |
| **Thu** | Prayer (movable) | — | **Holy hour – 7 pm (Benediction)** (fixed) |
| **Fri** | Prayer (movable) | — | Workout or easy unwind (movable) |
| **Sat** | Workout (movable) | — | Free or social (movable) |
| **Sun** | **Sunday Mass** (fixed) | — | Quiet night or gentle planning (movable) |

### Lighter Week (brief outline)
- Similar structure but with more spread‑out workouts (Mon, Wed, Sat), weekday Mass can be at midday, and social night on Tuesday.

## Flexibility Rules
1. **Protect core anchors** – Sunday Mass, Tuesday rest night, and Thursday holy hour remain *fixed*.
2. **Swap zones** – Monday, Friday, and Saturday are the primary “moveable” slots where other activities can shift.
3. **When a parish event appears** (e.g., a talk on Monday or Friday), slide the conflicted activity to the nearest open swap zone without touching the protected anchors.

## Fallback Plan for Missed Workouts
- **If Monday’s workout is displaced**: add a short, low‑intensity session on Wednesday evening and keep Saturday’s regular workout.
- **If Friday’s workout is displaced**: move it to Saturday morning and slide the usual Saturday workout to Sunday late afternoon/evening (post‑Mass), preserving Tuesday’s rest night and Thursday’s holy hour.
