# Workout Swapping

**Definition**
Workout swapping is a flexible approach to maintaining a target number of exercise sessions per week by moving missed workouts to other days, ensuring that core spiritual commitments and a designated rest night remain untouched.

**Key Elements**
- **Protected anchors**: Certain activities are kept fixed (e.g., Sunday Mass, Tuesday rest night, Thursday holy hour). Workouts are scheduled in *moveable* slots (Monday, Friday, Saturday) that can be adjusted when conflicts arise.
- **Fallback patterns**:
  - If a Monday workout is displaced by a parish event, shift a short, low‑intensity workout to Wednesday evening and keep the Saturday session.
  - If a Friday workout is displaced, move it to Saturday morning and relocate the usual Saturday workout to Sunday afternoon/evening after Mass.
- **Goal**: Preserve three weekly workouts without encroaching on the protected rest night or holy hour.

**Relation to Other Concepts**
- Ties closely to [[concepts/flexible-schedule]] by treating workouts as *swap zones* that can slide around fixed commitments.
- Supports [[concepts/habit-anchoring]] by maintaining the overall habit of three weekly exercise sessions, even when individual days change.
- Enhances a broader [[concepts/spiritual-routine]] by ensuring that physical health practices complement, rather than compete with, spiritual practices.
