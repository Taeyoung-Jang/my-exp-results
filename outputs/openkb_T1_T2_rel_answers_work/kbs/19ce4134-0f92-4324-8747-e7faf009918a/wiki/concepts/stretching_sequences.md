# Stretching Sequences

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Simple, time‑boxed movement patterns that can be performed without equipment to wake the body, release tension, and support a calm mental state.

## Core Elements
- **Overhead Reach:** Extend both arms straight up for about ten seconds, creating a gentle stretch along the spine.
- **Shoulder Rolls:** Roll shoulders forward ten times, then backward ten times to mobilize the shoulder girdle.
- **Head Tilts:** Slowly tilt the head left for ten seconds, then right for ten seconds, easing neck tension.
- **Gentle Loosening:** In heightened arousal states, drop shoulders on each exhale and turn the head slowly left‑right without forcing range.
- **Repetition:** Sequences are repeated once or twice within a two‑minute window to fit a five‑minute routine.

## Application in Session‑38
The scripts in [[summaries/session-38_2024-12-15]] use these sequences to provide structure:
- **Standard Script:** Combines a 2‑minute stretch (overhead reach, side bends, shoulder rolls) after breathing.
- **No‑Thinking Script:** Fixed 2‑minute stretch (arms up, shoulder rolls, head tilts) that requires no decision‑making.
- **Calming Script:** Two minutes of very gentle loosening (shoulder drops, slow head turns) for days when the heart is racing.

## Benefits
- **Physical Activation:** Increases blood flow and signals the brain that the body is awake.
- **Tension Release:** Targets common areas of morning stiffness (shoulders, neck).
- **Mental Cueing:** Provides a clear, repeatable action chain that reduces the need for mental planning, supporting a calm mindset.
