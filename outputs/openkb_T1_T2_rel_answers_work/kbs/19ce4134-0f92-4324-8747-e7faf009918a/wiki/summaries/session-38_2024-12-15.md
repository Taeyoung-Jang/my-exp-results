# Summary

The assistant delivers three practical five‑minute morning routines designed to wake the brain, provide grounding, and curb anxiety when time is limited.

### Core concepts
- **Breathing techniques** – paced nasal breathing, box breathing, and extended exhalations to activate the parasympathetic nervous system [[concepts/breathing_techniques]].
- **Stretching sequences** – brief overhead reach, shoulder rolls, head tilts that mobilize the body without exertion [[concepts/stretching_sequences]].
- **Mindful grounding** – sensory notice of surrounding details and hand‑on‑chest awareness to anchor attention [[concepts/mindful_grounding]].

### Scripts
1. **Standard 5‑minute script** (1 min breathing, 2 min stretch, 2 min grounding affirmation).  
2. **No‑thinking script** (1 min timed breathing, 2 min fixed stretch routine, 2 min exhale‑counting) – requires no mental choices.  
3. **Calming script for high arousal** (1.5 min box breathing, 2 min gentle loosening, 1.5 min thumb‑tracing) – recommended seated to prevent spiraling.

Each script includes precise timing cues and a simple cue‑to‑next transition, allowing execution “half‑asleep.”

### Anxiety‑focused tweaks
- Begin with a micro‑movement (jaw release, finger stretch) to break the immediate worry loop.  
- Prefer a seated posture when heart rate is high; standing can follow once calm is achieved.  
- Use repetitive, low‑decision tasks (counting exhalations, thumb tracing) to occupy the mind without judgment.

These routines illustrate how micro‑habits can regulate autonomic tone, improve focus, and set a calm intention for the day.
