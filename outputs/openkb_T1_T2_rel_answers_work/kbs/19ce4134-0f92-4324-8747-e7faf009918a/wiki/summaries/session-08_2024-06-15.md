## Overview
The document provides practical, time‑boxed morning routines that preserve a 20‑minute meditation while accommodating slow, busy, and rushed days.

## Core Concepts
- **Core rhythm** – a fixed sequence (meditation → movement → breakfast → getting ready) with variable block lengths. [[concepts/core_rhythm]]
- **Buffer time** – intentional slack between activities to prevent feeling rushed. [[concepts/buffer_time]]
- **Habit stacking** – pairing new habits with existing ones (e.g., meditation after brushing teeth). [[concepts/habit_stacking]]

## Sample Schedules
### Slow day (6:45 wake‑up)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:45‑6:50 | Wake‑up & transition |
| 6:50‑7:10 | Meditation (20 min) |
| 7:10‑7:20 | Light workout (10‑15 min) |
| 7:20‑7:35 | Breakfast |
| 7:35‑7:55 | Getting ready |
| 7:55‑8:00 | Buffer |

### Busy day (6:45 wake‑up)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:45‑6:50 | Wake‑up |
| 6:50‑7:10 | Meditation (20 min) |
| 7:10‑7:15 | 5‑minute equipment‑free workout |
| 7:15‑7:25 | Quick breakfast |
| 7:25‑7:50 | Getting ready |
| 7:50‑8:00 | 10‑minute buffer |

### Contingency plan (wake at 7:10)
- Reduce meditation to 10 min (7:10‑7:20) – keeps the habit alive.
- Micro‑workout: 1 min of bodyweight movement.
- Grab‑and‑go breakfast (5 min).
- Get ready 7:28‑7:55, leaving a 5‑min buffer.

## Minimum‑Viable Breakfast Rotation
- Overnight oats (pre‑made)
- Smoothie with pre‑portioned frozen fruit
- Toast with nut butter, cottage cheese, or pre‑sliced avocado

Rotating these three “families” prevents boredom while keeping prep under two minutes.

## Tweaks for Sustainability
1. Night‑before prep: lay out clothes, set out mat, measure breakfast ingredients.
2. Keep workouts non‑negotiable but short.
3. Pair meditation with an automatic habit (e.g., after brushing teeth).

## Takeaway
Treat the morning as a flexible sequence rather than a rigid clock‑face; adjusting block lengths and preserving core habits maintains consistency even on rushed days. [[concepts/buffer_time]] [[concepts/core_rhythm]] [[concepts/habit_stacking]]
