# Peanut Allergy‑Safe Cooking

Peanut allergy‑safe cooking focuses on selecting ingredients, products, and preparation methods that eliminate peanuts and reduce the risk of cross‑contact. The approach is especially useful for small gatherings where quick, low‑mess finger foods are desired.

## Core Strategies
1. **Ingredient vetting** – Choose whole foods (fresh fruits, vegetables, herbs) and pantry staples (chickpeas, tahini, Greek yogurt) that are naturally peanut‑free.
2. **Brand verification** – Prefer brands that explicitly label their products as peanut‑free. Notable examples from the source are [[entities/sabra]] classic hummus and [[entities/bolthouse_farms]] yogurt‑based ranch or cilantro‑avocado dressing.
3. **Label checklist** – Follow a systematic label‑checking routine (see [[concepts/allergen_label_checklist]]) to spot hidden peanuts, “may contain peanuts” warnings, and ambiguous terms like “mixed nut oils.”
4. **Cross‑contact awareness** – Use dedicated utensils, cutting boards, and storage containers for peanut‑free foods, and avoid preparing them in a space where peanut‑containing items have been processed.
5. **Simple, low‑mess options** – Combine the safety steps with easy, handheld foods that minimize utensil use, such as those listed in [[concepts/low-mess_finger_foods]].

## Practical Applications from the Source Document
- **Make‑ahead dips**: roasted red pepper hummus, herby yogurt dip, whipped feta spread – all using peanut‑free ingredients and brands.
- **Snack ideas**: fruit skewers, chocolate‑dipped strawberries, mini fruit tarts, lemon bars, cheese & charcuterie pieces, caprese skewers, mini sliders.
- **Backup snacks**: plain mini cheesecake bites and vanilla/lemon sandwich cookies that are typically peanut‑free.

## Checklist for Peanut‑Free Shopping
- Verify **no peanuts** or peanut‑derived oils in the ingredient list.
- Look for **“may contain peanuts”** or similar cross‑contact warnings.
- Avoid vague descriptors like **“mixed nut oils”** or **“natural flavor”** without clarification.
- Prefer products with clear **peanut‑free** certification or statements.
- Check the brand’s allergen policy regularly, as formulations can change.
