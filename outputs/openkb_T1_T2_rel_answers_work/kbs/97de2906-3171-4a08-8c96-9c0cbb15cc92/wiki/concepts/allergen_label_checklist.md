# Allergen Label‑Checking Checklist

**Definition**: A step‑by‑step process for inspecting food packaging to ensure products are free of peanuts and safe for people with peanut allergies.

## Core Steps
1. **Read the ingredient list** – Look first for the word *peanut* or any peanut‑derived oils.
2. **Search for allergen statements** – Many packages have a bold line such as “Contains: …” or “Allergen Information”. Verify that peanuts are not listed.
3. **Check for cross‑contact warnings** – Phrases like “may contain peanuts”, “processed in a facility that also processes peanuts”, or “shared equipment” indicate possible contamination.
4. **Watch for vague terms** – Terms such as “mixed nut oils”, “natural flavor”, or “nut blend” can hide peanuts; research the brand if unclear.
5. **Confirm brand guarantees** – Some manufacturers include a specific “peanut‑free” certification or statement; treat these as higher confidence.
6. **Document your findings** – Keep a quick note or checklist for each product you purchase, especially when buying multiple items for an event.
