## Summary of session-02_2023-06-15

**Topic:** Planning a small, peanut‑allergy‑safe get‑together with easy, low‑mess sweet and savory finger foods.

### Snack ideas
- **Sweet bites**: fruit skewers, chocolate‑dipped strawberries, mini cookies, brownies, mini fruit tarts with custard, chocolate‑covered pretzels, lemon bars, chocolate mousse cups, strawberry shortcake bites – all nut‑free.
- **Savory bites**: caprese skewers, stuffed mini peppers, cheese & charcuterie pieces, mini sliders, toasted baguette rounds (pesto‑mozzarella, smoked salmon), hummus, yogurt‑based dip, whipped feta spread.

### Make‑ahead dips/spreads
- Roasted red pepper hummus (chickpeas, roasted red pepper, tahini, lemon, garlic, olive oil).
- Herby yogurt dip (Greek yogurt, fresh dill/parsley, lemon).
- Whipped feta spread (feta, olive oil, herbs).

### Pairings (dippers)
- Cut veg: carrots, celery, bell peppers, cucumbers.
- Pita chips, crackers, toasted baguette slices.

### Shopping list (peanut‑safe)
- Fresh produce: roasted red peppers, cucumbers, carrots, celery, bell peppers, fruit for tarts.
- Canned chickpeas, tahini (peanut‑free facility), lemon, garlic, olive oil, fresh herbs, Greek yogurt, feta.
- Baguette, mini tart shells, chocolate (plain dark/milk, peanut‑free), custard/pudding mix, lemon‑bar ingredients.
- Dippers: pita chips, crackers (peanut‑free labels).
- Store‑bought backup snacks: plain mini cheesecake bites, vanilla/lemon sandwich cookies, pre‑made spinach‑artichoke dip, ranch dip.

### Peanut‑free product recommendations
- **Hummus:** [[entities/sabra]] Classic Hummus (peanut‑free label).
- **Yogurt dip:** [[entities/bolthouse_farms]] Yogurt‑Based Ranch or Cilantro‑Avocado Dressing (peanut‑free).

### Label‑checking checklist
Refer to [[concepts/allergen_label_checklist]] for a systematic approach:
1. Verify **no peanuts** or peanut‑derived oils in ingredient list.
2. Look for **"may contain peanuts"** or similar cross‑contact warnings.
3. Check for vague terms like **"mixed nut oils"** or **"natural flavor"**.
4. Confirm brand’s allergen statement guarantees **peanut‑free** production.
5. When in doubt, choose products with explicit **peanut‑free** certification.
