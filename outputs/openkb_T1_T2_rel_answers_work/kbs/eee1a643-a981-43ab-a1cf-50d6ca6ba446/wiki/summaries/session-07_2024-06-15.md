## Overview
The session offers practical advice for developing genuine camaraderie with coworkers while maintaining appropriate personal boundaries.

## Building Casual Camaraderie
- Use low‑pressure moments: share a laugh, show interest in projects, offer help.  
- Keep conversations light (weekend plans, hobbies, shows).  
- Consistency over intensity; be friendly, reliable, and easy to talk to.

## Managing Fast‑Track Intimacy
- Recognize oversharing or pushy invitations.  
- Acknowledge without deepening, then gently steer back to lighter or work‑related topics.

## Boundary‑Setting Phrases
### In‑person one‑liners
- “Got you—let’s keep it light today.”  
- “Whoa, that’s a lot. Hope you’re okay, though.”  
- “Ha, I’m not the best deep‑talk person at work.”  
- “Gonna shift gears for a sec.”  
- “Catching up is easier for me during work stuff.”

### Text one‑liners
- “Not super on my phone right now, but see you tomorrow.”  
- “I keep work chats pretty chill, just a heads‑up.”

### Firmer follow‑ups
- “I like keeping work relationships light, so I might not dive into deeper stuff, but I’m here for the day‑to‑day.”  
- “I’m trying to keep clearer boundaries with my off‑time, so I may not reply much outside work, but all good on my end.”

## Takeaway
Consistent, light‑hearted interaction fosters trust, while clear, concise phrasing lets you set and maintain comfortable boundaries without awkwardness.
