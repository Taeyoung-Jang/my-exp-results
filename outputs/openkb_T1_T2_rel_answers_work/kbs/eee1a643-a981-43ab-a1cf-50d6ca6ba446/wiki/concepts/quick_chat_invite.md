# Quick Chat Invite

**Definition**
A short, informal invitation for a 15‑minute conversation aimed at learning about teammates' work and building connections without creating a formal meeting.

**Key Characteristics**
- **Duration**: Typically 10–15 minutes, signaling low commitment.
- **Tone**: Friendly and low‑pressure, encouraging openness.
- **Purpose**: Gather information, offer support, and foster [[concepts/workplace_camaraderie]] during the onboarding period.

**Application in Session 38**
In the "session-38_2025-06-15" document, the user requested Slack intro messages that include a line inviting a quick 15‑minute chat. The assistant provided several templates that embed the quick chat invite, emphasizing that it is a casual way to learn about teammates' projects while acknowledging the new hire is still learning names.

**Relation to Other Concepts**
- Part of effective [[concepts/onboarding_communication]] strategies, helping new employees integrate.
- Complements broader [[concepts/communication_strategies]] by offering a low‑stakes interaction model.
- Can serve as a form of [[concepts/boundary_setting]] by defining a clear, limited time frame for the conversation.

**Practical Tips**
1. **Mention the time explicitly** (e.g., "quick 15‑minute chat") to set expectations.
2. **Combine with a name‑learning note** to reduce awkwardness and show humility.
3. **Use informal platforms** like [[entities/slack]] where short invites feel natural.
