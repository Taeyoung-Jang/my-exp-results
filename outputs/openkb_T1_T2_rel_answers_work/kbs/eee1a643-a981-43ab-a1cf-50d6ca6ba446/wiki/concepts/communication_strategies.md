# Communication Strategies for Light‑Weight Interaction

**Definition**: A set of concise, friendly communication techniques designed to foster workplace camaraderie without crossing personal boundaries.

## Core Principles
1. **Keep it brief and context‑appropriate** – Use short, casual phrases that suit the moment, whether in‑person, via text, or on platforms like [[entities/slack]].
2. **Maintain a light tone** – Focus on neutral topics (work, hobbies, day‑to‑day logistics) rather than deep personal disclosures.
3. **Signal boundaries gently** – Acknowledge the other person’s comment, then redirect or set a soft limit.
4. **Consistency over intensity** – Regular, low‑pressure interactions build trust more sustainably than forced, intense conversations.
5. **Invite brief, focused chats** – Offer a quick 15‑minute conversation (e.g., "quick chat invite") to learn about others’ work without framing it as a formal meeting.
6. **Support onboarding communication** – Use these tactics when introducing yourself to new teams, asking about projects, and learning names.

## Typical Phrases

  - “Got you—let’s keep it light today.”
  - “Whoa, that’s a lot. Hope you’re okay, though.”
  - “Ha, I’m not the best deep‑talk person at work.”
  - “Gonna shift gears for a sec.”
  - “Catching up is easier for me during work stuff.”
- **Text one‑liners**:
  - “Not super on my phone right now, but see you tomorrow.”
  - “I keep work chats pretty chill, just a heads‑up.”
- **Firmer follow‑ups** (when needed):
  - “I like keeping work relationships light, so I might not dive into deeper stuff, but I’m here for the day‑to‑day.”
  - “I’m trying to keep clearer boundaries with my off‑time, so I may not reply much outside work, but all good on my end.”
