# Simple Habit Tracker

**Definition**: A lightweight system for recording whether a daily outdoor routine was completed and the brief feeling afterward.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-03_2024-06-15]])
- **Code letter** – One‑character notation for the day's outcome:
  - **O** – basic routine performed (the "anchor" outdoor moments).
  - **M** – minimum version (30‑second step outside or indoor equivalent).
  - **E** – expanded version (longer walk or pleasant outing).
- **One‑word feeling** – After the activity, jot a single word (e.g., "calmer", "tired") to capture mood without full journaling.

## How It Works
1. At the end of each day, open a note in a phone app (e.g., Notes).
2. Record the code letter followed by a dash and the feeling word, e.g., `Mon: O — calmer`.
3. Optionally, keep an example week as a template to copy over:
   ```
   Mon: O — calmer
   Tue: M — foggy
   Wed: O — okay
   Thu: O — lighter
   Fri: E — good
   Sat: O — relaxed
   Sun: M — tired
   ```
4. No additional details are required; the system stays frictionless.

## Benefits
- **Simplicity** – Only two tiny actions (code + word) keep the habit from feeling like a chore.
- **Feedback loop** – The one‑word cue offers a quick sense of how the routine affects mood.
- **Flexibility** – Works alongside any outdoor routine, such as the [[concepts/simple-outdoor-routine]] or the [[concepts/daily-anchors]] discussed in the source session.
