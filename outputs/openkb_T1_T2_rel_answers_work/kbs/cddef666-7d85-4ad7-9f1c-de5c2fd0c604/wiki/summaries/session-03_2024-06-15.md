# Summary of Session 03 (2024‑06‑15)

## Goal  
Help the user create a low‑effort routine to get outside, boost energy, and track progress without pressure.

## Core Ideas  
- **Daily anchors**: three brief outdoor moments (morning, midday, evening) acting as routine “hooks”. [[concepts/daily-anchors]]  
- **Templates**: separate weekday and weekend schedules with suggested times.  
- **Versioning**: *minimum version* for low‑motivation days, *expanded version* for high‑energy days.  
- **Errand rotation**: a list of easy, no‑cost errands to keep outings varied.  
- **Go‑to places**: ten crowd‑free, nature‑leaning spots usable in most towns, including weather‑proof options.  
- **Simple habit tracker**: one‑letter code (O/M/E) plus a one‑word feeling entry, suitable for phone Notes. [[concepts/habit-tracker]]

## Practical Outputs  
- Copy‑paste schedule templates.  
- Checklist format.  
- Starter list of free places/activities.  
- Example week for the tracker.
