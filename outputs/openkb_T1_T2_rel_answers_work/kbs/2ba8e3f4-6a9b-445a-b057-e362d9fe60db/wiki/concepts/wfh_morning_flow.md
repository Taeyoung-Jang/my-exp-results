# Work‑From‑Home Morning Flow

**Definition**: A tailored version of the general morning backbone that optimizes the transition from waking to focused remote work by incorporating specific timing, attire cues, and coffee placement.

## Core Elements (Backbone Adaptation)
1. **Wake‑up & Hydration** – 0:00‑3:00 – water, brief alarm handling, no distracting apps.  
2. **Light Mobility** – 3:00‑10:00 – gentle stretches or a short walk to activate the body.  
3. **Focus Anchor** – 10:00‑15:00 – glance at the calendar, pick a single work task to start.  
4. **Work‑Ready Hygiene & Dress** – 15:00‑30:00 – quick grooming and put on clothing that signals “work mode”.  
5. **Coffee as Transition** – 30:00‑45:00 – brew coffee, sit at the desk, and sip while mentally prepping for the first task.

This sequence mirrors the [[concepts/morning_backbone]] but adds two WFH‑specific tweaks:
- **Attire cue**: dressing, even casually, signals a mental shift from home mode to professional mode, reducing the “still‑in‑bed” inertia.
- **Coffee timing**: placing coffee preparation in the final segment turns it into a reward and a cue that work is about to begin, aligning with [[concepts/coffee_as_transition]].

## Phone Compromise Rule
During the first 20 minutes, only utility checks (alarm, calendar, reminders) are permitted; all reactive apps (email, social media, news) are blocked. After 20 minutes, functional work apps may be opened, still avoiding scrolling. This rule follows the [[concepts/phone_compromise]] guideline to preserve early‑day focus.

## Minimum‑Viable Backup
If the morning is rushed, the flow can be compressed into a 10‑minute “minimum viable” version that still respects the four pillars: water + wake, quick stretch, one priority selection, fast hygiene/dress, and coffee on the way to the desk. See [[concepts/minimum_viable_morning]] for the exact steps.

## Benefits
- **Momentum preservation**: Leverages the refreshed state from consistent sleep to sustain energy into the workday, supporting [[concepts/morning_momentum]].
- **Reduced decision fatigue**: A preset sequence removes the need to plan each morning anew.
- **Seamless work start**: By the time coffee is finished, the mind is already primed for the chosen task, enabling immediate deep work.
