# Minimum‑Viable Morning Routine

**Definition**: A short, 10‑minute version of a morning routine that retains the essential pillars of the full routine—wake‑up, light movement, focus anchor, hygiene, and coffee transition—so you can still gain momentum on chaotic mornings.

## Core Structure (the "backbone")
The minimum‑viable routine mirrors the [[concepts/morning_backbone]] by compressing each pillar into a few seconds:
1. **Wake‑up & Hydrate (0‑1 min)** – Open eyes, drink a glass of water, and avoid any phone interaction beyond the alarm.
2. **Brief Stretch/Breath (1‑3 min)** – Perform a quick stretch or a couple of deep breaths to activate the body.
3. **Priority Identification (3‑4 min)** – Glance at your calendar or mental list and name one key task for the day.
4. **Fast Hygiene & Dress (4‑9 min)** – Brush teeth, wash face, and put on work‑ready clothing (even if it’s casual).
5. **Coffee Transition (9‑10 min)** – Grab a pre‑made coffee (or brew instantly) and sip while moving to your workspace.

## Why It Works
- **Momentum**: Even a brief activation triggers the [[concepts/morning_momentum]] effect, signalling the brain that the day has started.
- **Consistency**: Keeping the same sequence, albeit shortened, reinforces habit loops and reduces decision fatigue.
- **Flexibility**: Can be used on any day when time is limited without breaking the overall routine architecture.

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- **Phone Compromise**: The rule from [[concepts/phone_compromise]] still applies – only utility checks (alarm, water‑timer) are allowed in the first 20 minutes, which the minimum‑viable version comfortably satisfies.
- **Coffee as Transition**: Placing coffee at the end follows the guidance from [[concepts/coffee_as_transition]], turning the beverage into a rewarding cue that bridges preparation and work.
- **WFH Flow**: For remote workdays, the routine aligns with [[concepts/wfh_morning_flow]] by ending with coffee at the desk, ready to start focused tasks.

## Implementation Tips
- **Prep the Night Before**: Have a water bottle and coffee supplies ready to minimize friction.
- **Use a Timer**: Set a single 10‑minute timer to keep the pace tight.
- **Stay Consistent**: Even on weekends, run the minimum‑viable version when you need a quick reset.
