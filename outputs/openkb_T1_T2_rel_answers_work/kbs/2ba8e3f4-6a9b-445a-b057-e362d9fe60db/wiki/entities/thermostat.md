# Thermostat

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** A programmable device that regulates indoor temperature, often used to create a cooler bedroom environment for better sleep.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]**
- Suggested to set the thermostat a few degrees lower (mid‑60s °F) as part of the temperature experiment to reduce nighttime awakenings.
- Adjusting temperature is listed as the first experiment in the recommended sequence for improving [[concepts/sleep_quality]].
- Cooler rooms support deeper, less fragmented sleep, linking to [[concepts/environmental_sleep_factors]].

**Related concepts**
- [[concepts/environmental_sleep_factors]] – temperature is a primary environmental factor affecting sleep quality.
- [[concepts/sleep_quality]] – overall sleep quality can improve by optimizing room temperature.
- [[concepts/evening_routine]] – a consistent bedtime environment often includes a set thermostat setting.

**Related entities**
- [[entities/fan]] – can be used alongside a thermostat to enhance airflow and maintain a comfortable temperature.
- [[entities/caffeine]] – timing of caffeine is another behavioral factor to adjust after temperature changes.
- [[entities/wine]] – alcohol adjustment is the third experiment after temperature and caffeine.

**Practical tips**
- Set the thermostat to a lower temperature (e.g., 65 °F) at least 30 minutes before bedtime.
- Combine with breathable bedding and a gentle fan if needed.
- Monitor sleep using the tracking template suggested in the summary.
