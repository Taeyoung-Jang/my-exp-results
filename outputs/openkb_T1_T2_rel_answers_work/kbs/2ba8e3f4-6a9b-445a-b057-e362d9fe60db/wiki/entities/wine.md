# Wine

**Type:** Product

**Description:** An alcoholic drink often consumed with dinner that can affect sleep patterns.

**Relevance to session‑13_2024‑06‑15**

- In the conversation the user reports having a glass of wine with dinner.
- The assistant identifies wine as a **behavioral sleep factor** that can help fall asleep quickly but tends to fragment sleep in the second half of the night, reducing overall [[concepts/sleep_quality]].
- The suggested experiment is to reduce or shift wine consumption earlier to see if nighttime awakenings decrease.
