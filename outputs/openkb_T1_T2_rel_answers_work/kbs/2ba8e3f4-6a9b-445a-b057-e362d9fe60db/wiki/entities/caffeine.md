# Caffeine (product)

Caffeine is a psychoactive stimulant found in coffee, tea, soft drinks, and many energy products. It blocks adenosine receptors, reducing the feeling of drowsiness and increasing alertness.

## Mention in [[summaries/session-13_2024-06-15]]
In the *session‑13* conversation, caffeine consumption was identified as a key behavioral factor that can fragment sleep. The user typically drinks coffee as late as 2–3 pm, which may still affect nighttime sleep architecture. The assistant recommended an earlier cutoff (no later than late morning) and suggested monitoring its impact on nighttime awakenings.

## Practical recommendations (from the sessions)
1. **Cutoff time for intake:** avoid caffeine after late morning (≈10 am) to protect nighttime sleep quality.
2. **Morning placement:** brew and enjoy coffee in the final third of the routine (after hygiene) to serve as a reward and transition into work.
3. **Phone rule:** during the first 20 minutes, allow only utility checks (alarm, calendar); defer email, social media, and other distracting apps.
4. **Work‑from‑home adaptation:** dress in work‑ready attire and sip coffee at the desk to cue immediate focus.
5. **Minimum‑viable fallback:** in a 10‑minute emergency routine, allocate the last minute to grab coffee and transition to the workspace.
6. **Tracking:** log caffeine timing in a daily sleep‑tracking template to assess its impact on sleep fragmentation and daytime alertness.
