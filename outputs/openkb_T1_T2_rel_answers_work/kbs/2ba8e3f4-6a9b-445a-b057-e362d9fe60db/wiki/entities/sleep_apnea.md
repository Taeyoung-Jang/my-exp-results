# Sleep Apnea

**Type:** other

Sleep apnea is a medical condition where the airway collapses repeatedly during sleep, causing brief pauses in breathing, drops in oxygen, and frequent micro‑arousals.

## Relevance to session-13 (2024-06-15)

In the conversation, the assistant suggests that repeated nighttime awakenings, dry mouth, and feeling “wired” could be signs to consider **sleep apnea** as a possible medical factor. The user is advised to watch for snoring or breathing pauses and, if needed, seek a professional evaluation.

## Connection to broader concepts

- Part of [[concepts/medical_sleep_issues]] and can significantly reduce [[concepts/sleep_quality]] by fragmenting sleep.
- Often interacts with **environmental** and **behavioral** factors; for example, alcohol ([wine]) can worsen airway collapsibility.

## Related entities

- [[entities/wine]] – alcohol may exacerbate apnea events.
- [[entities/fan]] – a fan can help improve air circulation and comfort, though it does not treat apnea.
- [[entities/thermostat]] – cooler room temperature can reduce sleep disturbances but does not address the underlying breathing pauses.
