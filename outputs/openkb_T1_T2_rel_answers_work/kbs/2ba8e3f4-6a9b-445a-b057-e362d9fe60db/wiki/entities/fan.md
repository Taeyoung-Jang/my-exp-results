# Fan

A **fan** is a portable air‑circulating device that can lower perceived temperature and improve airflow in the sleeping environment. In the context of *session‑13_2024‑06‑15*, a fan is suggested as part of the **temperature experiment** to help maintain a cooler bedroom throughout the night, which can reduce sleep fragmentation and make it easier to fall back asleep after brief awakenings.

## Key Points from the Session
- The assistant recommends using a *gentle fan* aimed away from the sleeper to keep air moving without creating a cold draft.
- The fan is mentioned alongside other environmental tweaks such as adjusting the **[[entities/thermostat]]** and using breathable bedding.
- The goal is to create a more stable, cooler micro‑climate that supports deeper, less interrupted sleep, aligning with the broader concept of **[[concepts/environmental_sleep_factors]]**.
