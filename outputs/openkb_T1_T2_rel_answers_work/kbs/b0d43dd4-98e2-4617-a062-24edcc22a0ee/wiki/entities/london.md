# London

**Type:** place

London is the capital of England and a major global city. It is the geographic context for the commute challenges discussed in [[summaries/session-34_2024-05-15]].

## Relevance to the session-34 commute experiment

- The user lives in London and travels to work via the **Northern Line** of the London Underground, which has been experiencing frequent delays.
- The experiment proposes alternative routes, including the **London Overground** and bus segments, to mitigate these delays.
- Data collection and stress scoring are suggested to evaluate the effectiveness of different commuting options.

## Transport options mentioned

- **[[entities/northern-line]]** – the primary tube line currently causing bottlenecks.
- **[[entities/london-overground]]** – a suggested alternative rail service that can replace part of the journey.
- Bus routes and walking to nearby stations are also considered as low‑cost alternatives.

## Related concepts

- The design of the experiment follows the principles of [[concepts/commute-experiment-design]].
- Negotiating flexible hours or additional work‑from‑home days is framed using [[concepts/hybrid-work-negotiation]].
- Daily stress levels are captured with a simple template based on [[concepts/stress-scoring]].
