# Capitol Hill Library

**Type:** place

The Capitol Hill Library is a branch of the Seattle Public Library located in the Capitol Hill neighborhood. It provides public seating areas that are quiet and suitable for informal, low‑pressure meetups when cafés are crowded.

## Relevance to Session 14 (2023‑05‑15)
- Suggested as a **third fallback location** for a first‑time meetup if both Elliott Bay Book Company café and Porchlight Coffee are full.
- Described as a **public, calm space** where two people can continue a conversation without the pressure of a café environment.
- Mentioned alongside other venue options to illustrate **[[concepts/venue-suitability-criteria]]** such as noise level, seat availability, and public safety.
