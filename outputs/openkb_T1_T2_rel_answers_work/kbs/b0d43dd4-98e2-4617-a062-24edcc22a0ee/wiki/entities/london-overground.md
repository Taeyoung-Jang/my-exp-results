# London Overground

London Overground is a network of suburban rail lines that operate across Greater London, providing an alternative to the Underground for many commuters.

## Role in the *session-34_2024-05-15* commute experiment
In the **commute experiment** outlined in [[summaries/session-34_2024-05-15]], the Overground is suggested as a viable alternative segment to the unreliable Northern Line.  Swapping a leg of the journey to the Overground can reduce waiting time, lower stress, and offer a more predictable travel window, which is a key factor in the [[concepts/commute-experiment-design]] and the [[concepts/stress-scoring]] framework used to evaluate different routes.
