## Summary of Session 14 (2023‑05‑15)

**Main focus**: Providing practical advice for arranging an in‑person meetup with someone you don’t know well, emphasizing a comfortable, low‑pressure vibe, specific venue recommendations in Seattle’s Capitol Hill area, and several pre‑meetup text templates.

### Key Advice
- **Choose a public spot that offers an inherent activity** (e.g., café, bookstore, park) to reduce awkward “interview” dynamics. [[concepts/low-pressure-meetup]]
- **Set expectations clearly beforehand** with a brief, casual message that mentions a 60–90 minute window and an easy out. [[concepts/setting-expectations-via-text]]
- **During the meetup**, treat the interaction as a shared curiosity rather than a deep‑dive questionnaire; use the environment for conversation starters.

### Primary Venue Recommendations (Capitol Hill, Seattle)
1. **[[entities/elliott-bay-book-company]] café** – steady flow, quieter corners, ideal for weeknight 6:30 PM.  
2. **[[entities/victrola]] on Pike** – lively yet not chaotic; may get buzzier later.  
3. **[[entities/mr-west]]** – cozy but can fill with laptop‑workers during peak hours.

### Backup Options (short walk)
- **[[entities/porchlight-coffee]]** – calm, easy to reach from [[entities/elliott-bay-book-company]].  
- **[[entities/capitol-hill-library]] seating area** – public, quiet, non‑café fallback.

### Text Message Templates
| Tone | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Neutral / Friendly** | “Want to meet at Elliott Bay around 6:30? We can chat for about an hour before I head to something afterward. Totally chill either way.” |
| **Casual / Flirty** | “How about Elliott Bay at 6:30? We can hang out for a bit and see where the conversation goes. I’ve got something after, but I’d love to steal a little time with you.” |
| **Including Easy‑Out** | “Want to meet at Elliott Bay around 6:30 and hang out for about an hour? I’ve got something after, but it’d be great to catch up a bit and keep it low‑key.” |
| **Backup Plan Mention** | “Let’s try Elliott Bay, and if it’s crowded we can just wander to something nearby—Porchlight or even the library works. We’ll play it by ear.” |

### Decision Guidance
- For a **weeknight 6:30 PM** meetup, the assistant recommends **[[entities/elliott-bay-book-company]]** as the primary choice for best seat availability and quieter ambiance.
- If [[entities/elliott-bay-book-company]] is packed, **[[entities/porchlight-coffee]]** serves as the first backup; if both are full, the **[[entities/capitol-hill-library]]** offers a calm, public space.
