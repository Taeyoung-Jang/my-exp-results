# Rebecca (work)

**Author:** Daphne du Maurier

**Publication Year:** 1938

**Genre:** Gothic romance / mystery

**Key Themes:** identity, memory, the power of the past, and the unsettling influence of a house.

## Relevance in session-05_2024-06-15
In the conversation dated 2024‑06‑15, *Rebecca* was highlighted as the most compelling "page‑turner" among the suggested timeless classics. It was recommended to be read alongside *Crime and Punishment* as a lighter, atmospheric counterbalance, supporting a balanced reading experience.

## Why it fits the "timeless but entertaining" criteria
- **Timeless appeal:** The novel’s exploration of universal human anxieties and its haunting setting keep it resonant across generations. [[concepts/timeless_classics]]
- **Entertainment value:** Its suspenseful plot and vivid prose create a strong "just one more chapter" pull, making it an engaging read. [[concepts/balanced_reading]]
- **Reading intent:** It serves readers looking for purposeful, distraction‑free literature that still feels like a modern thriller. [[concepts/reading_intentionality]]
