## Neil Gaiman

Neil Gaiman is a celebrated British author and storyteller, renowned for his imaginative fiction and for often narrating his own books. 

### Key mention in Session 36

In the conversation about building an after‑work audiobook habit, Gaiman’s narrations were highlighted as a prime example of **narrator impact**. His own reading of *The Ocean at the End of the Lane* was described as “consistently comforting and immersive,” making it an ideal pick for listeners seeking a soothing, easy‑to‑sink‑into experience.
