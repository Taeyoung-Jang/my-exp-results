**Type:** Work (novel)

**Author:** Fyodor Dostoevsky (1866)

**Overview**
Crime and Punishment is a psychological thriller that follows the moral turmoil of the former student Raskolnikov after he commits murder. The novel explores themes of guilt, redemption, and the clash between rationalism and spirituality.
