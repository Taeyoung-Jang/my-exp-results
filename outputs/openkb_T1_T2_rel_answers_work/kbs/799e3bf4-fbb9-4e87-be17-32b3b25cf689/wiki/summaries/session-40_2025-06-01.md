## Summary of session‑40 (2025‑06‑01)

**Topic:** Minimalist kitchen setup, core pantry/fridge/freezer staples, and a lean 1‑week meal plan that maximizes leftovers.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/kitchen-organization]]** – Group items by usage frequency (eye‑level for daily items, high shelves for occasional, low for heavy). Use clear containers only if you’ll maintain them.
- **[[concepts/pantry-shelf-zoning]]** – Keep a small set of versatile grains, beans, oil, vinegar, salt, pepper, and a couple of staple spices.
- **[[concepts/core-staples]]** – Minimal pantry (rice, pasta, canned beans, olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper, garlic powder/chili flakes, tomato paste or canned tomatoes), fridge (eggs, block of cheese, lemons/limes, onions, garlic), freezer (frozen vegetables, frozen berries, shrimp or [[entities/chicken-thighs]]).
- **Meal planning** – Plan around 1–2 proteins, a few repeatable vegetables, and flexible sides; think in “themes” rather than a rigid schedule.
- **Minimalist kitchen** – Buy only what will be used that week, keep fresh produce to a handful of workhorse items, rely on a couple of backup freezer items.
- **Leftover utilization** – Design each dinner to generate a lunch‑ready portion for the next day; repurpose leftovers in fried rice, pasta, soups, or skillet meals.

### Findings & Recommendations
1. **Pantry & Fridge Setup**  
   - Store everyday staples at eye level; less‑used items higher up.  
   - Use original packaging if refilling containers feels like extra work.  
   - Keep raw proteins on the bottom shelf to avoid cross‑contamination.

2. **Core Staples List**  
   - **Pantry:** [[entities/rice]], [[entities/pasta]], canned beans, olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper, 1‑2 favorite spices, tomato paste/canned tomatoes.  
   - **Fridge:** eggs, block cheese, lemons/limes, onions, garlic.  
   - **Freezer:** frozen vegetables, frozen berries, shrimp or [[entities/chicken-thighs]].

3. **Weekly Meal Flow (Minimalist Version)**  
   - **Mon:** Roast [[entities/chicken-thighs]] + [[entities/broccoli]]; serve with rice; leftovers for Tue lunch.  
   - **Tue:** Rice bowl with leftover chicken, sautéed peppers & greens.  
   - **Wed:** Tomato‑based pasta; toss in any remaining chicken and greens.  
   - **Thu:** Shrimp stir‑fry (or frozen mixed veg) using remaining produce.  
   - **Fri:** Skillet meal with eggs, leftover veg, optional chicken bits.  
   - **Sat:** Simple omelet or scrambled eggs with toast/tortillas and any scraps.  
   - **Sun:** Easy soup or tomato‑garlic rice using pantry basics and any last veggies.

4. **Shopping List (1‑2 proteins, limited produce)**  
   - Protein: [[entities/chicken-thighs]] (main) + frozen shrimp (backup).  
   - Vegetables: broccoli, bell peppers, mixed greens, onions.  
   - Carbs: bread or tortillas.  
   - No extra spices beyond the core list.

### Practical Takeaway
By limiting the weekly grocery list to **1–2 proteins** and a few versatile vegetables, and by relying on a **core staple inventory**, you can keep the kitchen clutter‑free while still enjoying varied, nutritious meals. Each dinner naturally creates a lunch‑ready leftover, ensuring minimal waste and a streamlined cooking routine.
