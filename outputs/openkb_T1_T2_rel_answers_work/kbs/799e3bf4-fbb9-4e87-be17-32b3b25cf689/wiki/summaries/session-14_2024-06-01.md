# Summary of session-14_2024-06-01

**Document type:** Conversation – practical guide for emergency pantry organization.

## Core ideas
- **FIFO rotation** – label items with month & year, place newest to the right/back, pull oldest from the left/front. [[concepts/fifo-rotation]]
- **Shelf zoning** – top: light backups; middle: everyday staples; bottom: heavy bulk (water, large cans). [[concepts/pantry-shelf-zoning]]
- **Label legend** – write month‑year on the front or top; rewrite printed dates; if unknown, use today’s date as a start point.
- **Restock routine** – quarterly review (2‑4 × year); scan dates, move near‑expiry forward, replace used items, refresh water supply. [[concepts/restock-routine]]
- **Mystery‑item decision tree** – inspect cans/water for damage; if intact but undated, label with today’s date and prioritize use; discard if compromised.

## Printable one‑page routine
1. **Pantry map** – visual layout of top/middle/bottom shelves with left‑to‑right FIFO ordering.  
2. **Restock day checklist** – walk shelves, pull items expiring ≤ 6 months, replace used stock, straighten rows.  
3. **Labeling legend** – month & year notation.  
4. **Decision tree** for unknown‑date cans/water – safe‑use vs. discard criteria.
