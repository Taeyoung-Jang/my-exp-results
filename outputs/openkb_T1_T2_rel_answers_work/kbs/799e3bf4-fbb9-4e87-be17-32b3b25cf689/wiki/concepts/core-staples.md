# Core Staples

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A minimal collection of pantry, fridge, and freezer items that supports a wide variety of meals while keeping kitchen inventory lean.

---

## Overview
The **Core Staples** concept defines a small, versatile group of ingredients that can be combined with fresh, weekly purchases to create numerous meals without over‑stocking. It is especially useful for minimalist or small‑space kitchens where space and waste are concerns.

## Core Staples List (from [[summaries/session-40_2025-06-01]])

### Pantry
- **Grains:** [[entities/rice]] and pasta
- **Legumes:** canned beans (any variety)
- **Oils & Condiments:** [[entities/olive-oil]], vinegar, salt, pepper
- **Spices:** a couple of frequently‑used spices (e.g., garlic powder, chili flakes, cumin)
- **Tomato Base:** tomato paste or canned tomatoes for sauces and soups

### Fridge
- Eggs
- Block of cheese
- Lemons or limes
- Onions
- Garlic

### Freezer
- Frozen vegetables (mixed veg, broccoli, etc.)
- Frozen berries (optional for snacks or smoothies)
- A protein backup such as [[entities/shrimp]] or chicken thighs (see [[entities/chicken-thighs]])

## How Core Staples Enable Flexible Meal Planning
1. **Versatility** – Each item can be used across multiple cuisines (e.g., rice for stir‑fry, pasta for Italian dishes, beans for salads or soups).
2. **Meal Foundation** – They serve as the base for quick meals, allowing you to add a handful of fresh produce each week.
3. **Leftover Friendly** – Staples like rice and pasta store well, making it easy to repurpose leftovers for lunches.
4. **Minimal Waste** – By limiting the range of items, you buy only what you need, reducing spoilage.
