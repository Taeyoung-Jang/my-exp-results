# Restock Routine

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A periodic checklist for reviewing, rotating, and refilling emergency pantry supplies.

---

## Overview
The *Restock Routine* is a systematic, repeatable process for maintaining a well‑organized emergency food reserve. It ensures that items are used before they expire, that the stock level stays consistent, and that new supplies are incorporated without breaking the FIFO (first‑in‑first‑out) flow.

## Key Steps (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-01]])
1. **Schedule reviews** – perform the routine 2‑4 times per year (roughly every 3–6 months).
2. **Walk each shelf** – start at the top shelf, then middle, then bottom, checking every item’s label.
3. **Identify near‑expiry items** – pull any product that will expire within the next six months into your regular meal plan.
4. **Replace used stock** – for every item removed, add a newly‑dated replacement, placing it at the back/right side of the row.
5. **Refresh water supply** – especially if bottles are approaching the manufacturer’s recommended rotation period or are older than a couple of years.
6. **Straighten rows** – ensure the oldest items are on the left/front and newest on the right/back to keep FIFO automatic.

## Supporting Practices
- **Labeling legend** – write month and year on each can/bottle; if unknown, use today’s date as a start point.
- **Shelf zoning** – heavies on the bottom, everyday staples on the middle, light backups on the top. This layout makes the routine quicker because you know where each category lives.
- **Mystery‑item decision tree** – inspect for damage; if intact but undated, label and prioritize use; discard if compromised.

## Benefits
- Minimises waste by using items before they spoil.
- Keeps the emergency reserve reliable and ready for an event.
- Reduces the mental load of remembering when items were bought.
- Provides a clear, printable one‑page checklist that can be posted in the pantry.
