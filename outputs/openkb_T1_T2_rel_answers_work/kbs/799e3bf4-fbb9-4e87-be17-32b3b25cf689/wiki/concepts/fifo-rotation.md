# FIFO Rotation

**Type:** Concept

## Definition
FIFO Rotation (First‑In, First‑Out) is a stock‑management approach that ensures the oldest items in a reserve are used before newer ones. By labeling each item with the month and year of acquisition and arranging them so the oldest are positioned at the front or left side, you create an automatic rotation that minimizes waste and keeps emergency supplies fresh.

## Key Elements (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-01]])
- **Date labeling** – Write month & year on the front or top of every can or bottle; if no date exists, use today’s date as the start point.
- **Shelf placement** – New items go to the back/right side of a shelf; older items stay at the front/left side.
- **Pull pattern** – When retrieving supplies, always take from the front/left, ensuring the next‑oldest item is used next.
- **Review cycle** – Perform a quarterly (2‑4 times per year) check to verify dates, move near‑expiry items forward, and restock missing items.

## How it works in a pantry
1. **Label** each item with month‑year.
2. **Arrange** each category on a shelf left‑to‑right (oldest → newest).
3. **Use** items from the left/front first.
4. **Restock** by adding new items to the right/back after each use.

## Benefits
- Reduces risk of consuming expired food.
- Simplifies inventory without extra bins or containers.
- Provides a visual cue for quick checks during emergencies.
