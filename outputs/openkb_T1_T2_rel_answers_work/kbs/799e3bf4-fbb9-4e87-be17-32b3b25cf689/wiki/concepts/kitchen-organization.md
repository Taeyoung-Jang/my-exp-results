# Kitchen Organization

## Overview
Kitchen Organization is the practice of arranging pantry, fridge, and freezer spaces to streamline cooking, minimize waste, and keep the kitchen visually tidy. It emphasizes placement by usage frequency, easy access to daily items, and logical zones for different food categories.

## Key Practices from session-40
- **Usage‑based grouping**: Store everyday staples (grains, oils, frequently used spices) at eye level; place less‑used baking items higher up and heavier items lower down.
- **Container strategy**: Use clear containers only if you can consistently refill them; otherwise keep items in their original packaging.
- **Fridge layout**:
  - One shelf for quick‑grab items (leftovers, yogurt, pre‑pped veggies).
  - Lowest shelf for raw proteins to prevent drips.
  - Door for condiments (the warmest zone).
  - Separate fruits and vegetables, keeping greens washed and ready.
- **Pantry zoning**: Keep a core set of versatile ingredients within easy reach and organize the rest by how often you use them.
