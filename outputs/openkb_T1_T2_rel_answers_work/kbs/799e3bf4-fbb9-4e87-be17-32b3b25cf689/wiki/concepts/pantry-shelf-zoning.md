# Pantry Shelf Zoning

**Definition**: A practical layout that groups pantry items by their weight and how often they are used, creating three horizontal zones that support a first‑in‑first‑out (FIFO) system.

## Zones
- **Top shelf** – Light backup items (small cans, pouches, snacks, spices). These are accessed less frequently and can sit at the back of the shelf; arrange left‑to‑right with the oldest on the left.
- **Middle shelf** – Everyday staples (canned vegetables, proteins, soups, boxed goods). This is eye‑level for quick access; each category forms its own row, again ordered oldest → newest.
- **Bottom shelf** – Heavy bulk items (bottled water, large cans, bulk dry goods). Placed low for safety and ease of lifting; use front‑left positions first and add new stock to the right or behind.

## How it supports FIFO
When new items are added, they are placed on the right side or at the back of each row. Users naturally pull from the left/front, ensuring the oldest items are used first. This physical arrangement reduces the need for extra containers or complex tracking and works hand‑in‑hand with the [[concepts/fifo-rotation]] method.

## Integration with Restock Routine
During a periodic review (see [[concepts/restock-routine]]), the zoning makes it easy to scan each zone, spot near‑expiry items, and replace used stock while keeping the FIFO order intact.
