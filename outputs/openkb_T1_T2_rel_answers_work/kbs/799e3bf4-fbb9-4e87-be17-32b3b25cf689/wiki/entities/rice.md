## Rice

Rice is a long‑standing pantry staple referenced in the **session‑40** minimalist kitchen guide. It serves as a primary carbohydrate base for several meals, including:
- Monday’s roasted chicken and broccoli bowl
- Tuesday’s fried rice with leftover chicken and frozen vegetables
- Sunday’s tomato‑garlic rice soup

In the context of the document, rice is part of the **core staples** set that enables flexible meal planning with minimal shopping.

### Key Facts from session‑40
- Listed under the **core staples** for the pantry alongside pasta, canned beans, olive oil, and basic seasonings.
- Suggested to be cooked in bulk at the start of the week to provide a ready‑to‑use base for multiple dishes.
- Paired with both fresh proteins (chicken thighs) and freezer‑based options (shrimp, frozen vegetables).
