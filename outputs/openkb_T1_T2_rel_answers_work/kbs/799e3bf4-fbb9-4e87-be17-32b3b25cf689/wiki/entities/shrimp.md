# Shrimp

**Type:** product

## Overview
Frozen shrimp is a quick‑freeze seafood that provides a convenient, high‑protein backup for minimalist meal plans.

## Role in Core Staples
It appears in the [[concepts/core-staples]] list from the session-40 guide as one of the recommended freezer items that can be paired with a small set of pantry and fridge basics.

## Storage & Kitchen Organization
According to the [[concepts/kitchen-organization]] advice, keep shrimp in the freezer compartment, separate from raw meats, and label the package to support a simple [[concepts/restock-routine]].

## Meal Planning Use
In the 1‑week plan, shrimp is used for a quick stir‑fry on Thursday, allowing a protein‑rich dinner with minimal fresh produce. It pairs well with staples like [[entities/rice]], [[entities/pasta]] (if desired) and frozen vegetables.

## Related Entities
