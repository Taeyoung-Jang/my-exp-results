# Olive Oil

Olive oil is highlighted in the *session‑40* recommendations as a fundamental **core staple** for a minimalist kitchen. It is part of the small pantry inventory that enables a wide range of quick meals, from stir‑fries to dressings.

## Role in Kitchen Organization
- Stored at eye level according to [[concepts/kitchen-organization]] so it’s easy to reach for daily cooking.
- Fits within the [[concepts/pantry-shelf-zoning]] strategy of keeping everyday items on the most accessible shelf.
- Serves as a neutral base for seasoning proteins like [[entities/chicken-thighs]] and [[entities/shrimp]], and for pairing with grains such as [[entities/rice]] or [[entities/pasta]].

## Core‑Staples Context
- Listed explicitly under the **core‑staples** set in the document, alongside oil, vinegar, salt, pepper, and a few versatile spices.
- Enables flexible meal planning without requiring a large shopping list, supporting the [[concepts/restock-routine]] of buying only what will be used that week.

## Mention in Session‑40
- The entity is referenced in the summary of [[summaries/session-40_2025-06-01]], where it is recommended as part of the lean pantry foundation for the suggested one‑week meal plan.
