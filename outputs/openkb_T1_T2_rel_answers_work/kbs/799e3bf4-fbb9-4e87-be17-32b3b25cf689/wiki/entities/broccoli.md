## Broccoli

Broccoli is a green cruciferous vegetable that the session‑40 meal plan highlights as a **key workhorse** for a minimalist kitchen. It appears in the recommended shopping list as one of the few fresh produce items to buy each week, paired with protein such as [[entities/chicken-thighs]] or [[entities/shrimp]].

### Role in the Minimalist Meal Plan
- Listed as a primary vegetable for **roasting** on Monday and for quick stir‑fry on Thursday.
- Serves as a repeatable component across multiple meals, supporting the idea of **[[concepts/kitchen-organization]]** by limiting the variety of produce while maximizing nutritional coverage.
- Fits within the **[[concepts/pantry-shelf-zoning]]** approach by being stored in the fridge’s lower shelves with other raw proteins to prevent cross‑contamination.
- Aligns with the **[[concepts/restock-routine]]** of buying only a handful of versatile vegetables each week, reducing waste and clutter.
- Acts as a **[[concepts/core-staples]]** vegetable that pairs well with pantry items like [[entities/rice]] and [[entities/pasta]], enabling flexible meals such as stir‑fry, rice bowls, and soups.
