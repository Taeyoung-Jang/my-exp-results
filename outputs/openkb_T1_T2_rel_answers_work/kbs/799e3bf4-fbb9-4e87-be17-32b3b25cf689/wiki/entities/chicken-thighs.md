# Chicken Thighs

- **Type:** Product (protein)
- **Core role:** Primary fresh protein in the minimalist 1‑week meal plan from [[summaries/session-40_2025-06-01]].
- **Key uses:** Roasted for meals, used in stir‑fry, added to pasta or rice bowls, provides leftovers for lunches.
- **Storage:** Keep in the fridge, raw on the lowest shelf per [[concepts/kitchen-organization]] to prevent drips.
- **Relation to staples:** Part of the “core staples” set alongside [[entities/rice]], [[entities/pasta]], [[entities/olive-oil]], and [[entities/shrimp]] as described in [[concepts/core-staples]].
- **Shopping guidance:** Suggested as the main weekly protein; one backup freezer protein (e.g., [[entities/shrimp]]) is recommended for flexibility.
- **Meal examples:**
  - Monday roast chicken thighs with broccoli.
  - Tuesday rice bowl with leftover chicken.
  - Wednesday pasta with leftover chicken and greens.

These points illustrate how [[entities/chicken-thighs]] fits into the overall kitchen organization and restock routine outlined in the session.
