# City Council

The **City Council** is the legislative body of a municipality, responsible for enacting local ordinances, approving budgets, and representing the interests of residents.

## Role in session-03_2004-06-15
- The resident drafting a letter is addressing the City Council to express concerns about rapid [[concepts/neighborhood-change]] and to ensure the [[concepts/resident-voice]] of long‑term residents is heard.
- The council is the target audience for the communication tone discussed in [[concepts/communication-tone]].
