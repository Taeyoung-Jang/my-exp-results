# Anchor Items

**Definition:**  
Anchor items are personal objects brought into a new living space to create an immediate emotional grounding and sense of ownership.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-39_2020-06-15]]**

- Familiar objects such as a favorite blanket, a cherished lamp, or personal photos act as quick emotional anchors.
- Scent cues (favorite candle, soap, room spray) reinforce the feeling of home.
- These items shift the *feel* of a room more than its visual decor, supporting a sense of stability without major purchases.
- Anchor items can also serve as cues for functional [[concepts/zoning]], signalling areas like a reading corner or workspace.
- Pairing anchor items with simple daily [[concepts/routine]] (e.g., morning tea) strengthens the association between the space and personal habits.

**How to use Anchor Items**

1. Identify a few items that hold personal meaning and are easily portable.
2. Place them in high‑visibility spots (e.g., bedside table, entryway, favorite chair) as soon as you arrive.
3. Combine them with a consistent micro‑routine to reinforce the new environment.
4. Use scent items alongside visual anchors for a multisensory grounding effect.

By deliberately selecting and positioning anchor items, newcomers can quickly transform a transient space into a place that feels uniquely theirs.
