# Commute Routine

**Definition**: A commute routine is a repeatable, lightly‑structured plan for daily travel that provides enough predictability to reduce stress while retaining flexibility to handle unexpected disruptions.

## Core Elements
1. **Target departure window** – instead of a fixed minute, set a range (e.g., leave between 7:30 am and 7:40 am) to create natural buffer time.
2. **Limited decision points** – restrict checks to two moments: a quick status review before leaving home and a brief glance at the platform board.
3. **Time‑based thresholds** – use simple numeric cut‑offs (e.g., if the next train is > 12 min away, switch to a backup) to avoid on‑the‑spot deliberation.
4. **Low‑phone backup options** – pre‑select one or two alternatives that require minimal screen interaction, such as a familiar bus route, a nearby bike/scooter dock, or a fixed rideshare pickup spot.
5. **Pre‑planning the night before** – lay out needed items, check traffic or transit conditions, and set a realistic departure time with a small buffer. This aligns with the broader practice of [[concepts/commute-preplanning]].
6. **Consistent timing** – keeping a regular schedule helps the routine become automatic, reinforcing the benefits of a [[concepts/commute-routine]].
7. **Simple tracking** – merely log departure and arrival timestamps each day (using phone notes or a calendar) to monitor average travel time and refine the buffer, a practice described in [[concepts/low-effort-tracking]] and [[concepts/timestamp-logging]].

## Practical Checklist (derived from [[summaries/session-14_2025-04-14]])
- **Before leaving home**
  - If light‑rail delay < 10 min → proceed.
  - If delay ≥ 15–20 min → activate backup.
- **On the platform**
  - If next train arrival ≤ 8 min → stay.
  - If gap ≥ 12–15 min → exit and switch.
- **On the train** – no further checks; ride it out.

## Benefits
- Reduces cognitive load by limiting the number of decisions.
- Provides a clear, repeatable process that can be refined over time.
- Keeps the commute predictable while allowing quick pivots when needed.
- Encourages low‑effort tracking of departure/arrival times to fine‑tune buffer intervals, decreasing stress.
