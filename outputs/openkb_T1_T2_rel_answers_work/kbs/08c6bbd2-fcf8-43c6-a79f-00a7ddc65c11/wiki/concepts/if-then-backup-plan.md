# If/Then Backup Plan

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A lightweight, conditional decision framework that helps commuters handle unexpected delays by defining clear triggers and predefined alternative actions.

---

## Overview

An *If/Then Backup Plan* is a structured yet minimal set of rules that a commuter can follow without extensive phone use. The plan consists of:

1. **Pre‑departure check** – a single glance at the transit status before leaving home.
2. **On‑platform check** – a quick read of the real‑time arrival board.
3. **Decision thresholds** – specific time limits that dictate whether to stay on the planned route or switch to a backup mode.
4. **Low‑phone backup options** – pre‑selected alternatives (e.g., bus, bike/scooter, rideshare) that require little to no screen interaction.

The concept is illustrated in [[summaries/session-14_2025-04-14]], where the user’s light‑rail commute is the use‑case.

---

## Key Elements

| Element | Typical Rule | Purpose |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| **Pre‑departure check** | *If* delay < 10 min → proceed; *If* delay ≥ 15–20 min → switch now | Filters major issues early, avoiding wasted time at the station. |
| **On‑platform check** | *If* next train ≤ 8 min → stay; *If* gap ≥ 12–15 min → exit and switch | Provides a second, situational decision point while still near a pivot location. |
| **On‑train** | No further checks; ride it out | Recognises limited phone use when on board. |
| **Backup options** | Choose 1–2 low‑phone alternatives (bus route, bike/scooter dock, rideshare pickup) | Guarantees a known fallback that requires minimal decision‑making. |

---

## Benefits

- **Predictability:** Fixed time thresholds turn ambiguous delays into concrete actions.
- **Flexibility:** The plan accommodates both minor and major disruptions.
- **Low cognitive load:** Only two quick checks and one pre‑chosen alternative keep the process simple.
- **Minimal phone reliance:** Designed for commuters who cannot stay on a screen while on the platform or train.

## Application Steps (based on the source)

1. **Before leaving home**
   - Check the light‑rail status once.
   - Apply the pre‑departure thresholds.
2. **At the station**
   - Glance at the arrival board.
   - Apply the on‑platform thresholds.
3. **If thresholds trigger a switch**
   - Move to your pre‑selected low‑phone backup (e.g., walk to the nearby bus stop or grab a docked scooter).
4. **If on the train**
   - Continue to destination; no additional actions needed.

---

## Implementation Tips

- **Set a reminder** on your phone or a sticky note to perform the two checks.
- **Map out** the backup locations in advance (bus stop numbers, scooter dock IDs, rideshare pickup point).
- **Practice** the plan on a low‑stress day to internalize the thresholds.
