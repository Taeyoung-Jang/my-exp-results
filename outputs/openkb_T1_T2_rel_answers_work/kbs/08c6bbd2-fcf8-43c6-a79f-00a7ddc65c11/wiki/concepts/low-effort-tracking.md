# Low‑Effort Commute Tracking

## Overview
Low‑effort commute tracking is a minimalist approach to measuring travel time by recording only the departure and arrival timestamps each day. This method reduces friction, allowing users to gather reliable data without adding a burdensome habit.

## How It Works
- **Capture two timestamps**: note the exact time you leave home and the time you arrive at your destination. This can be done in a phone notes app, a calendar entry, or a quick screenshot of the lock‑screen clock.
- **Keep it simple**: no need to log routes, traffic conditions, or each leg of the journey. The focus is on the start and end points.
- **Review after 1–2 weeks**: calculate the average travel duration and its variability. Use the result to set a realistic [[concepts/buffer-time]] for daily planning.

## Benefits
- **Minimal friction** – requires only a few seconds of effort each day.
- **Sufficient data** – provides an accurate baseline for average commute time.
- **Supports stress reduction** – by enabling an appropriate buffer, it helps avoid rushing and reduces commute‑related anxiety [[concepts/commute-stress-management]].

## Relation to Other Concepts
- The practice relies on simple [[concepts/timestamp-logging]].
- It complements [[concepts/commute-preplanning]] by supplying the data needed to choose a realistic departure time.
- When early arrivals occur, users can engage in [[concepts/early-arrival-productivity]] activities.
