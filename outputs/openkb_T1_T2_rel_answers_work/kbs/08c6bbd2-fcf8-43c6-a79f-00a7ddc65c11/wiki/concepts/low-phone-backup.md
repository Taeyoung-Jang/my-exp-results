# Low‑Phone Backup Options

**Definition**: Low‑phone backup options are pre‑selected, easy‑to‑execute alternative travel methods that a commuter can switch to without needing to consult a phone or navigation app during the journey.

---

## Why they matter

In a typical [[concepts/if-then-backup-plan]], decision points are limited to two quick checks: one before leaving home and one while on the platform. The goal is to keep the routine predictable while allowing flexibility for unexpected delays. To honor the "low‑phone" principle, the backup options must:

1. **Require little to no screen interaction** – e.g., a familiar bus line, a nearby bike or scooter dock, or a fixed rideshare pickup spot.
2. **Be reliably available** – they should have consistent schedules or predictable locations.
3. **Fit within the time thresholds** defined in the checklist (e.g., switch if the next train is >12‑15 min away).

---

## Typical low‑phone backup options

| Category | Example | How it stays low‑phone |
|----------|---------|-----------------------|
| **Bus** | A regular city bus that stops within a minute’s walk from the station. | Fixed schedule; you can remember the route and stop without checking an app.
| **Bike / Scooter** | Docked bike or electric scooter station near the exit. | You can walk to it and unlock with a simple tap or pre‑registered account, no navigation needed.
| **Rideshare** | Designated pickup zone (e.g., a curb near the station) you’ve pre‑booked or have a favorite driver. | You know the exact spot; you can request a ride with a single tap before leaving home.

---

## Integrating with a commute routine

A robust [[concepts/commute-routine]] incorporates low‑phone backups by:

- **Pre‑planning**: Choose 1–2 backup modes in advance and practice the steps (where to go, how to unlock, etc.).
- **Embedding in the if‑then logic**: The checklist from the session uses clear thresholds (e.g., train delay > 15 min → switch). When the trigger fires, the commuter simply heads to the pre‑selected backup.
- **Minimizing decision fatigue**: Because the options are limited and well‑known, the commuter does not need to compare alternatives on the spot.

---

## Example checklist snippet (excerpt)

> **Before leaving home** – if light‑rail delay ≥ 15 min → walk to the bike dock.
>
> **On the platform** – if next train > 12 min away → exit and catch the bus.
>
> **On the train** – ride out; no phone needed.
