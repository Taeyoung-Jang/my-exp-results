# Summary of Session 39 (2025-10-14)

## Overview
The conversation provides a concise guide to making daily morning commutes smoother and less stressful through routine building, buffer‑time calculation, and low‑effort tracking.

## Key Tips
- **Pre‑planning**: prepare the night before (items, traffic check, set a realistic departure time). [[concepts/commute-preplanning]]
- **Consistent timing**: use a regular schedule to automate the routine. [[concepts/commute-routine]]
- **Focus during travel**: limit multitasking; use music, podcasts, or breathing to stay calm. [[concepts/commute-stress-management]]

## Determining the Right Buffer
- Observe actual travel times for 1‑2 weeks.
- Add 10‑15 minutes beyond the typical duration to cover common delays. [[concepts/buffer-time]]
- If early, have a small productive activity ready (checking messages, coffee). [[concepts/early-arrival-productivity]]

## Simple Tracking Method
- Log only **departure** and **arrival** timestamps each day (phone notes, calendar, or screenshot). [[concepts/low-effort-tracking]]
- After a fortnight, calculate the average and variability to refine the buffer. [[concepts/timestamp-logging]]

## When to Record Delay Causes
- Optional: note causes (traffic, parking) only if it can be done safely after parking.
- Core data (timestamps) is sufficient for most users. [[concepts/delay-cause-logging]]

The approach emphasizes minimal friction while providing enough data to improve commute efficiency and reduce stress.
