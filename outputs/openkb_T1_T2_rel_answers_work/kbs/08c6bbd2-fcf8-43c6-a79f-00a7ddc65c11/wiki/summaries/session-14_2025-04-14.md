## Summary of session-14_2025-04-14

- **Goal:** Help the user create a predictable yet flexible morning commute routine for a light‑rail‑based trip, using simple **if/then** rules and minimal phone interaction.
- **Core strategy:**
  - Establish a *target departure window* instead of an exact departure time.
  - Limit checks to two moments: a quick status check at home and a brief glance at the platform board.
  - Define clear time‑based thresholds for when to stay on the train vs. switch to an alternative.
- **Proposed checklist with thresholds:**
  1. **Before leaving home**
     - If delay < 10 min → proceed as usual.
     - If delay ≥ 15–20 min → immediately use a backup option.
  2. **On the platform**
     - If next train arrival ≤ 8 min → stay.
     - If gap ≥ 12–15 min → exit station and switch modes.
  3. **On the train** – no further checks; ride it out.
- **Low‑phone backup options** (choose 1–2):
  - Familiar bus route departing near the station.
  - Nearby bike or scooter dock you can walk to.
  - Designated rideshare pickup spot that requires no phone navigation.
- **Next steps:** User can provide their specific station and destination to fine‑tune thresholds and identify the most practical backup options.
