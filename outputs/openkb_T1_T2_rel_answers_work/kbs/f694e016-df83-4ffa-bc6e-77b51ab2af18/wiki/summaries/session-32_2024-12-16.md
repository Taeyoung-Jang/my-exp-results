# Summary of Session 32 – 16‑Week Downsizing Plan

**Purpose**: Provide a concrete, paced downsizing roadmap for a full‑time worker living in a one‑bedroom apartment, organized by room, with built‑in admin and catch‑up weeks, plus a comprehensive [[concepts/tracking_template]].

## Key Elements

- **Room‑Based 16‑Week Plan** – The schedule progresses through **Kitchen → Bedroom/Closet → Living Room → Bathroom → Storage**, dedicating specific weeks to each area while keeping the workload to **3–5 hours per week**. This follows the [[concepts/downsizing_schedule]] and [[concepts/room_based_planning]].
- **Admin Weeks** – Weeks focused on photographing, listing, price adjustments, and scheduling pickups. See [[concepts/admin_weeks]].
- **Catch‑Up Weeks** – Two buffer weeks (Weeks 9 and 15) to handle any lag without stress. Refer to [[concepts/catch_up_weeks]].
- **Time Budgeting** – Each week’s tasks are scoped to fit into a few short evening sessions, respecting a full‑time job. This aligns with [[concepts/time_budgeting]].
- **Sentimental Week** – Dedicated week (Week 13) for photos and decisions on keepsakes, using [[concepts/sentimental_decluttering]].
- **Final Wrap‑Up** – Week 16 includes trial packing of the two suitcases and final clearance.

## Tracking Template

A **TSV‑formatted sheet** is provided for easy import into [[entities/google_sheets]]. Columns include:

- `Item`
- `Category` (keep, sell, donate, trash)
- `Listing Date`
- `Sell‑By Deadline`
- `Final Status`
- `Location (Room)`
- `Estimated Value`
- `Packed (Y/N)`
- `Next action` (photograph, list, price drop, schedule pickup, donate drop‑off, pack)
- `Notes`

Example rows show items that are **donated**, **kept**, and **packed**.

## Review Routine

- **Twice‑weekly check‑ins** (start of week & end of week) – a 5‑minute glance to set priorities, update statuses, and ensure donation deadlines are met. This follows the [[concepts/review_routine]].
