# Summary of session-01_2023-06-15

**Main contribution**: Practical advice for arranging household items on shelves, a concise decision‑making checklist, and methods to keep the top shelf visible and usable.

## Shelf placement principles
- **Fragility** → place on the **top shelf** (light, delicate items).  
- **Frequency of use** → keep at **eye‑level or waist‑level** (middle shelf).  
- **Weight** → store on the **bottom shelf** (heavy or bulky items).  
- Use a **buffer zone** (open space, small bins, soft liners) to protect fragile items from being squished.  
- [[concepts/shelf_placement_rules]]

## Categorizing items
- Treat the three factors (fragile, usage frequency, heavy) as a quick mental scan.  
- Rotate items periodically to avoid “forgotten zone” buildup.  
- [[concepts/buffer_zone]]  
- rotating items (plain text)

## Printable quick checklist (5–7 yes/no questions)
1. Is it fragile or easily misshapen? → **Top**  
2. Do I use it often enough to want it at eye/waist level? → **Middle**  
3. Is it heavy enough that I’d rather not lift it above my shoulders? → **Bottom**  
4. Would it damage something else if it shifted or fell? → **Bottom**  
5. Is it light but only used occasionally or seasonally? → **Top**  
6. Does it need to stay upright or unstacked to avoid bending? → **Top or Middle**  

- This checklist can be printed and posted inside a closet door.  
- [[concepts/quick_checklist]]

## Making the top shelf visible without clutter
- Install soft upward‑facing LED lighting (e.g., hidden strip along the front lip). [[entities/led]]  
- Use clear or semi‑transparent bins for easy item identification.  
- Apply a single, small label per bin for tidy organization.  
- Leave open space around items; emptiness draws the eye and prevents a “forgotten zone.”  
- top shelf visibility (plain text)

## Overall advice
- Follow the **fragile‑use‑heavy** hierarchy for shelf placement.  
- Create a **buffer zone** around delicate items.  
- Use the **quick checklist** to decide placement instantly.  
- Enhance top‑shelf visibility with **soft lighting, clear containers, and minimal labeling**.  
- Periodically **rotate** items to keep the space functional and remembered.
