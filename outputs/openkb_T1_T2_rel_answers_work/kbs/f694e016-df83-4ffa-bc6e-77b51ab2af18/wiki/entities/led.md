# LED (Light Emitting Diode)

A **LED** (light‑emitting diode) is a semiconductor device that emits light when an electric current passes through it.  LEDs are widely used for indicator lights, displays, and low‑power lighting solutions because they are energy‑efficient, long‑lasting, and available in many colors.
