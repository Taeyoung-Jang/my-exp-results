# Google Sheets

Google Sheets is a cloud‑based spreadsheet application that lets users create, edit, and share tables online. In the context of the 16‑week downsizing plan documented in [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-16]], Google Sheets is the recommended platform for the **tracking template** that records each item’s category, location, value, and status.

## Key facts from the session‑32 document
- The plan provides a **TSV‑formatted table** that can be copied directly into Google Sheets for easy manipulation.
- Columns include *Item*, *Category*, *Listing Date*, *Sell‑By Deadline*, *Final Status*, *Location (Room)*, *Estimated Value*, *Packed (Y/N)*, and *Next action*.
- The spreadsheet supports the tracking template concept, enabling quick filtering and status updates during the weekly review routine.
- Because the downsizing schedule is paced at 3–5 hours per week, the sheet helps the user stay within the time budgeting constraints by visualizing pending actions.
- The sheet is also useful during admin weeks and catch up weeks to coordinate listings, price adjustments, and donation drop‑offs.

## Why Google Sheets?
- **Collaboration:** The user can access the sheet from any device, which is handy while juggling a full‑time job.
- **Automation‑ready:** Simple formulas can flag items approaching their sell‑by deadline, supporting the recommended twice‑weekly review routine.
- **Portability:** The TSV format ensures the data can be imported without loss of structure.

## Related concepts and entities
- downsizing schedule – the overall 16‑week roadmap that the sheet tracks.
- room based planning – the sheet records the *Location (Room)* for each item.
- sentimental decluttering – items marked as *keep* and *packed* often stem from this phase.
- [[entities/japan]] – the ultimate destination for the kept items.
- [[entities/tokyo]] – the city where the user will relocate.
