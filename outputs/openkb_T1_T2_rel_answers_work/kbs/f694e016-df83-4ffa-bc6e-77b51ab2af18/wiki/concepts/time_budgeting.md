# Time Budgeting for Downsizing

**Definition**: Time budgeting is the practice of setting a realistic weekly time cap and distributing specific downsizing activities across those hours to keep progress steady while balancing a full‑time job.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents burnout by limiting each week to **3–5 hours** of work.
- Ensures tasks are broken into bite‑size chunks that fit into evenings or short weekend slots.
- Provides a framework for integrating **admin weeks**, **catch up weeks**, and regular **review routine** checks.

## Core Principles (drawn from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-16]])
1. **Set a weekly hour ceiling** – aim for 3‑5 hours total, split across 2‑3 short sessions.
2. **Allocate time by activity type**:
   - **Decluttering** (e.g., trash sweep, kitchen clean‑out) – 1‑2 h.
   - **Administration** (photograph, list, price, schedule pickups) – 1‑2 h.
   - **Logistics** (donation drop‑offs, pickup coordination) – 0.5‑1 h.
3. **Reserve buffer zones** for unexpected delays. The plan includes two **catch up weeks** that act as safety nets.
4. **Synchronize with the overall downsizing schedule** – each week’s time budget aligns with the room‑based focus (kitchen, bedroom, living room, etc.).
5. **Track time usage** in the **tracking template** to see where you’re over‑ or under‑allocating.

## Practical Implementation
- **Weekly Planning**: At the start of each week, glance at the schedule, pick one or two focus areas, and assign a rough hour count.
- **Mid‑Week Check**: After the first session, note progress and adjust the remaining time accordingly.
- **End‑Week Review**: Use the **review routine** (brief 5‑minute glance) to log actual hours spent, update the tracker, and move unfinished items to the next week or a **catch up weeks** slot.

## Benefits
- Consistent forward momentum without overwhelming workloads.
- Clear visibility into how much time each category consumes, aiding future moves or projects.
- Flexibility to accommodate work demands while still meeting donation and listing deadlines.
