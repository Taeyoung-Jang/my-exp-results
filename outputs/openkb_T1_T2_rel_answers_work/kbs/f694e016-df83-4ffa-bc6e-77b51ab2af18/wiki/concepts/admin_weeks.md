# Admin Weeks

**Definition**: Admin Weeks are dedicated blocks in a downsizing schedule where the primary focus is on administrative logistics—photographing items, writing descriptions, posting listings, adjusting prices, and coordinating pickups or donation drop‑offs. They are intentionally low‑effort, short‑duration weeks that keep the overall decluttering flow moving without overwhelming the mover.

## Why They Matter
- **Time budgeting**: By isolating admin work, you protect the limited 3–5 hours per week you can commit while working full‑time. This aligns with the overall [[concepts/time_budgeting]] approach.
- **Momentum preservation**: Separating selling‑related tasks from physical decluttering prevents decision fatigue and keeps the physical cleanup weeks brisk.
- **Buffer creation**: Admin Weeks act as a natural buffer, reducing the need for ad‑hoc overtime and complementing [[concepts/catch_up_weeks]] and the broader [[concepts/downsizing_schedule]].

## Typical Activities
| Activity | Description |
|---|---|
| Photograph items | Use a phone or camera to capture clear images of each sellable object. |
| Write listings | Draft concise titles, bullet‑point descriptions, and price suggestions. |
| Post on platforms | Upload listings to preferred local buy/sell groups (e.g., Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist). |
| Adjust prices | Review buyer interest and lower prices for stagnant listings. |
| Schedule pickups / drop‑offs | Arrange meeting times with buyers or coordinate donation center visits. |

## Placement in the 16‑Week Plan (Session 32)
- **Week 4** – First admin week: photograph clothing and small items, write descriptions, and post them.
- **Week 6** – Second admin week: handle buyer messages, schedule pickups, and adjust prices.
- **Week 9** – Admin week after catch‑up: clear lingering listings and plan donation runs.
- **Week 12** – Admin week: final communications, set sell‑by deadlines, and flag items for donation.
- **Week 15** – Final admin week: complete any remaining pickups, donation drop‑offs, and update the tracking sheet.

These weeks are interleaved with physical decluttering weeks (kitchen, bedroom, living room, etc.) to maintain a steady rhythm.

## How to Use Admin Weeks Effectively
1. **Set a limited time budget** – aim for 1–2 hours each evening, totaling 3–5 hours for the week.
2. **Create a quick checklist** – list the items to photograph, the platforms to post on, and any follow‑up actions. This aligns with the [[concepts/quick_checklist]] practice.
3. **Update the tracking template** – after each activity, record the next action in the [[concepts/tracking_template]] sheet.
4. **Review routine** – perform the twice‑weekly review (start and end of week) to ensure listings are current and donation deadlines are met, as described in [[concepts/review_routine]].
