# Review Routine

**Definition**: A lightweight, twice‑weekly habit of scanning the downsizing tracker, confirming upcoming deadlines, and adjusting next actions so the overall [[concepts/downsizing_schedule]] stays on track.

## Why it matters
In the 16‑week plan for a one‑bedroom apartment (see [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-16]]), the author stresses that each week is limited to **3–5 hours**. A simple routine prevents small tasks—like stale listings or missed donation windows—from snowballing and turning the schedule into a stressor.

## Core steps
1. **Start‑of‑week check (5 min)**
   - Open the [[concepts/tracking_template]] sheet.
   - Identify items with a *Sell‑By Deadline* within the next 7 days.
   - Set the **Next action** for each (e.g., *photograph*, *price drop*, *schedule pickup*).
   - Note any pending admin work for the upcoming [[concepts/admin_weeks]] or [[concepts/catch_up_weeks]].
2. **Mid‑week quick glance (optional, 2 min)**
   - Verify that any scheduled pickups or donation drop‑offs have been confirmed.
3. **End‑of‑week review (5 min)**
   - Mark completed actions (e.g., *listed*, *donated*, *packed*).
   - Move items that missed their deadline to the *donate* column.
   - Update the **Packed (Y/N)** status for any items placed in the suitcases.
   - Reflect on the week’s **time budget** and note if adjustments are needed for the next week.

## Benefits
- **Consistency**: Keeps momentum without adding a heavy workload.
- **Visibility**: Deadlines and pending actions are always front‑and‑center.
- **Flexibility**: Allows quick re‑allocation of time during [[concepts/buffer_zone]] periods.

## Relations to other concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/time_budgeting]] to respect the 3–5 hour weekly limit.
- Supports the structure of [[concepts/room_based_planning]] by ensuring each room’s tasks are reviewed.
- Aligns with [[concepts/quick_checklist]] style: a minimal, actionable list rather than a long to‑do.
- Provides a safety net for the [[concepts/catch_up_weeks]] by flagging lag early.
