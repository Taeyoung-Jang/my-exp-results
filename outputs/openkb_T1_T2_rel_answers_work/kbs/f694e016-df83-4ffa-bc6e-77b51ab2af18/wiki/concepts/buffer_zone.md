# Buffer Zone

**Definition**
A buffer zone is an intentional empty space or protective barrier placed around fragile or delicate items to prevent them from being squashed, bent, or otherwise damaged.

**Key elements from [[summaries/session-01_2023-06-15]]**
- Leave open space around items on shelves.
- Use small bins, soft liners, or other containers to isolate delicate objects.
- Position fragile items on the top shelf where they are less likely to be pressed by heavier items.

**Why it matters**
Creating a buffer zone reduces the risk of accidental pressure, improves visual clarity, and makes it easier to locate items without having to move other things.

**Practical tips**
- Allocate a 1–2 inch gap between fragile items and surrounding objects.
- Place items in clear or semi‑transparent bins to keep the zone visible.
- Add a thin padding layer (e.g., a fabric liner) on the shelf surface for extra cushioning.
