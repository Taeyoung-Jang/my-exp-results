# Downsizing Schedule

**Definition**: A downsizing schedule is a sequential, time‑boxed roadmap that breaks the decluttering and relocation process into discrete weekly tasks, each with a clear focus, time budget, and outcome.

## Core Principles

1. **Room‑Based Progression** – Tasks are ordered by physical area (kitchen, bedroom/closet, living room, bathroom, storage) to keep momentum and avoid back‑tracking.  
2. **Time Budgeting** – Each week is limited to 3–5 hours of effort, making the plan realistic for a full‑time employee.
3. **Admin Weeks** – Dedicated slots for photographing, listing, price adjustments, and scheduling pickups, ensuring logistics do not pile up.
4. **Catch‑Up / Buffer Weeks** – Two built‑in catch‑up weeks provide flexibility when tasks fall behind schedule.
5. **Sentimental Decluttering** – A specific week for handling keepsakes with a photo‑capture strategy.
6. **Final Wrap‑Up** – A trial‑packing week to confirm everything fits into the two suitcases before the move.

## Example Structure (from [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-16]])

| Week | Focus | Typical Activities | Hour Estimate |
|------|-------|--------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Kitchen – trash sweep | Collect broken, expired, and trash items | 2‑3 |
| 2 | Kitchen – decision | Sort cookware, utensils, small appliances | 2‑3 |
| 3 | Bedroom/Closet – clothing | Try‑on, sort into keep/sell/donate | 3 |
| 4 | Bedroom – admin | Photograph clothing, write listings | 3‑5 |
| 5 | Living Room – sellable items | Identify furniture, electronics, décor to list | 2‑3 |
| 6 | Living Room – admin | Manage buyer messages, schedule pickups | 1‑2 |
| 7 | Bathroom – quick clear | Remove old toiletries, towels, duplicates | 1‑2 |
| 8 | Storage – hidden items | Sweep forgotten bins, decide keep/dispose | 2 |
| 9 | **Catch‑Up** | Finish any lagging tasks or extra donations | 3‑5 |
|10 | Living Room/Storage – specialty | Evaluate sports gear, instruments, hobby gear | 2‑3 |
|11 | Specialty – listing | Post listings for valuable specialty items | 2‑4 |
|12 | Admin – final logistics | Update listings, mark slow items for donation | 1‑2 |
|13 | Sentimental Decluttering | Photo capture, container limit for keepsakes | 2‑4 |
|14 | Whole‑home review | Verify suitcase contents, assign destinations | 2 |
|15 | **Catch‑Up** | Final donation runs, recycling, leftover tasks | 1‑2 |
|16 | Wrap‑Up / trial pack | Pack suitcases, mark packed items, clean home | 1‑2 |
