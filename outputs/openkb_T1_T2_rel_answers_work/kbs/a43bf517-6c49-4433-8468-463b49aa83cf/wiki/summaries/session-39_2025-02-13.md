## Overview
The document provides a practical framework for documenting water‑damage losses in a storage unit, focusing on an Excel‑compatible spreadsheet template and a concise proof‑gathering checklist.

## Spreadsheet Structure
- **Header line** (CSV‑ready):  
  `Category,Item Name,Description,Quantity,Estimated Value,Purchase Date/Year,Purchase Source,Condition Before Damage,Damage or Loss Details,Proof Available,Notes`
- Suggested **category list**: `electronics, clothing, tools, kitchen, household, miscellaneous`.
- Optional columns for cost clarity: **Original Cost** and **Replacement Cost**.

## Estimating Values
- Use **replacement cost** (current market price of a comparable mid‑range item) rather than original purchase price.  
- When exact model or price is unknown, approximate with a similar product found at a major retailer.

## Proof of Ownership
- Indicate the type of evidence in the **Proof Available** field, e.g., “photo from prior location,” “listed on unit inventory,” or “receipt uploaded.”
- Photographic checklist: item angles, damage close‑up, surrounding storage area, and empty spaces for missing items.

## Recommendations
- Keep all items in a **single sheet** and use a **Category** column for easy filtering, rather than multiple tabs.
- Maintain a **quick checklist** of required photos and documents to ensure nothing is omitted before contacting the adjuster.
