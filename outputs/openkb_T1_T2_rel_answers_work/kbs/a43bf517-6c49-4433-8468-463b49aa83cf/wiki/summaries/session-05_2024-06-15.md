## When a zoom rental is worth it
- A zoom is valuable when the shoot’s **pace** makes frequent lens swaps a liability – e.g., run‑and‑gun coverage, unpredictable action, or tight spaces where repositioning is hard. [[concepts/zoom-rental-decision]]
- Controlled shoots with time to reset favor **primes** for aesthetic and ergonomic reasons.
- Typical scenarios that justify a zoom: event coverage, documentary or corporate B‑roll, multi‑location days racing daylight. [[concepts/b-roll-workflow]]

## Specific recommendation for a small corporate doc day
- **24‑70 mm** is the only zoom that meaningfully improves workflow for office B‑roll, covering wide context to tighter details without lens swaps. [[entities/24-70mm-f4]]
- **70‑200 mm** is generally unnecessary unless distant compression shots are required. [[entities/70-200mm]]
- If you have fast primes (24/35/50/85 mm) and are comfortable moving, you can skip the zoom, but the 24‑70 adds speed and fluidity.

## Stabilization considerations
- **Lens IS** + **IBIS** gives the cleanest handheld look, especially for slow push‑ins. [[entities/lens-is]] [[entities/ibis]] [[concepts/stabilization-choice]]
- Strong in‑body stabilization reduces the advantage of a stabilized zoom, but zooms often still feel smoother due to optical IS.

## Impact of using a gimbal
- A gimbal helps but does **not** replace the convenience of a 24‑70 for off‑gimbal handheld shots and quick context changes. [[entities/gimbal]] [[entities/24-70mm-f4]]
- If you’re willing to tolerate extra lens swaps and re‑balancing, primes on the gimbal are feasible; otherwise, the zoom maintains workflow speed.

## Aperture choice for the 24‑70
- **f/4** is usually sufficient for office interviews and B‑roll; lighting can be controlled, and depth of field can be managed by distance.
- f/2.8 adds weight and is only needed for low‑light pockets or when a shallower look is essential.

## Key takeaways
- Rent a **24‑70 mm f/4** for solo corporate doc days when you need speed and flexibility. [[entities/24-70mm-f4]]
- Prioritize lens stabilization only if your camera’s IBIS is weak. [[entities/lens-is]] [[entities/ibis]] [[concepts/stabilization-choice]]
- A gimbal reduces but does not eliminate the benefit of a zoom. [[entities/gimbal]]
- Choose f/4 unless you anticipate uncontrolled low‑light situations.
