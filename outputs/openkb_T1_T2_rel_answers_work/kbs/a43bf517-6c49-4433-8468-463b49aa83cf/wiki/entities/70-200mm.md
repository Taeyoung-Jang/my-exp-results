## 70‑200 mm Zoom Lens

A versatile telephoto zoom lens that covers medium‑to‑long focal lengths, often used for events, run‑and‑gun situations, or shots that require compression.

### Relevance in session-05_2024-06-15
- Considered as a potential rental option, but deemed **generally unnecessary** for a typical small corporate documentary shoot in an office setting.
- The conversation highlighted that the 70‑200 mm shines in scenarios like event coverage or outdoor long‑lens moments, which were not the primary focus of the discussed shoot.
- Compared directly with the 24‑70 mm zoom, which was recommended as the more useful rental for the described workflow.
