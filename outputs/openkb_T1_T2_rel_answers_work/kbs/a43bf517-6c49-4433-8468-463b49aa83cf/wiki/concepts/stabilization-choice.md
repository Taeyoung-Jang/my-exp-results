# Stabilization Choice

**Scope** – This concept explains how to decide between using optical image stabilization (lens IS) and in‑body image stabilization (IBIS) for handheld and gimbal‑mounted shooting, drawing on the recommendations from [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-15]].

## Core Considerations

1. **Camera’s native IBIS quality**
   - If the camera provides strong IBIS, the incremental benefit of a stabilized lens is modest.
   - When IBIS is only mediocre, lens IS adds noticeable smoothness, especially on slow push‑ins.
   - Example entities: [[entities/ibis]], [[entities/lens-is]].

2. **Lens weight and balance**
   - Stabilized lenses can be heavier (e.g., [[entities/24-70mm-f4]] vs a non‑stabilized prime), impacting gimbal load.
   - Choosing a lighter lens (f/4) helps maintain easier gimbal balancing.

3. **Shooting style**
   - **Handheld b‑roll**: Combining any functional stabilization (IBIS or lens IS) reduces camera shake; using both often yields the smoothest result.
   - **Gimbal use**: While a gimbal provides mechanical stability, lens IS still contributes to smoother motion during rapid pans or when the gimbal is temporarily idle.

## Decision Flow

- **Step 1:** Assess IBIS performance of your camera.
  - Strong IBIS → prioritize lightweight lenses; lens IS optional.
  - Weak IBIS → favor lenses with optical IS.

- **Step 2:** Consider the focal length and aperture.
  - Longer lenses (e.g., [[entities/70-200mm]]) benefit more from lens IS due to amplified shake.
  - Mid‑range zooms like [[entities/24-70mm-f4]] gain modest benefit; weight savings may outweigh the IS advantage for gimbal work.

- **Step 3:** Factor in the overall workflow.
  - For fast‑paced, run‑and‑gun scenarios where lens swaps are minimized, a stabilized zoom supports the [[concepts/zoom-rental-decision]] by keeping footage steady without extra gear.
  - In a controlled office doc day with a gimbal, the need for lens IS lessens, but it still offers a safety net for handheld moments.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- **[[concepts/b-roll-workflow]]** – Stabilization choice directly influences how smoothly b‑roll can be captured, whether handheld or on a gimbal.
- **[[concepts/zoom-rental-decision]]** – The perceived value of renting a stabilized zoom (e.g., a 24‑70 mm) hinges on the stabilization trade‑off discussed here.
