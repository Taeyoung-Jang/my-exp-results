## B‑Roll Workflow

A **B‑Roll Workflow** describes the process and decisions involved in planning, capturing, and integrating supplemental footage that supports the primary narrative (interviews, A‑roll) in documentary, corporate, and event productions.

### Core Elements
1. **Shot Planning**
   - Identify coverage needs (context, establishing shots, cut‑aways, detail inserts).
   - Choose lenses that balance speed and visual style. For fast‑paced shoots, a versatile zoom such as a **[[entities/24-70mm-f4]]** can reduce lens‑swap time, while dedicated primes are preferred when creative control is paramount.
2. **Equipment Choice**
   - **Stabilization**: Decide between lens‑based IS, in‑body IBIS, or external stabilization like a **[[entities/gimbal]]**. The choice depends on camera capabilities and the amount of handheld movement required. See also [[concepts/stabilization-choice]].
   - **Portability**: Lighter gear (e.g., f/4 zooms) eases gimbal balancing and handheld comfort.
3. **Execution**
   - Capture a variety of angles and focal lengths to give editors flexibility.
   - When using a **[[entities/gimbal]]**, prioritize smooth motion; however, keep a zoom handy for quick handheld B‑roll that doesn’t require re‑balancing the gimbal.
4. **Post‑Production Integration**
   - Log and tag B‑roll clips during shoot for easy retrieval.
   - Match the look (aperture, depth of field) of B‑roll to A‑roll for visual consistency; an f/4 zoom often provides a suitable compromise between shallow depth and manageable weight.

### Insights from Session 05 (2024‑06‑15)
- For a solo corporate documentary day, the **[[entities/24-70mm-f4]]** was recommended as the primary zoom to improve workflow efficiency, covering a range from wide office context to tighter detail shots without frequent lens swaps.
- The decision to rent a zoom hinged on the need for speed in **[[concepts/b-roll-workflow]]**, especially when moving between locations or tight spaces.
- Stabilization considerations highlighted that strong **[[entities/ibis]]** can reduce reliance on lens‑IS, but a combination of lens‑IS and IBIS still yields smoother handheld B‑roll.
- Even with a **[[entities/gimbal]]**, a zoom remains valuable for off‑gimbal handheld shots, reinforcing the workflow’s emphasis on flexibility.
