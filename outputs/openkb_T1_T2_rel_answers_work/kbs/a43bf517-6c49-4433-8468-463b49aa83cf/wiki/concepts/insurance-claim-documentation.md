# Insurance Claim Documentation

**Definition**: The systematic collection, organization, and presentation of proof that substantiates loss or damage for an insurance adjuster, enabling a clear assessment of replacement cost and liability.

---

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-39_2025-02-13]])

1. **Spreadsheet Inventory**
   - Use a single‑sheet CSV/Excel table with the header:
     ```
     Category,Item Name,Description,Quantity,Estimated Value,Purchase Date/Year,Purchase Source,Condition Before Damage,Damage or Loss Details,Proof Available,Notes
     ```
   - Optional columns: **Original Cost** and **Replacement Cost** to avoid confusion.
   - Recommended categories: `electronics, clothing, tools, kitchen, household, miscellaneous`.
   - Row examples include entries for a [[entities/samsung-tv]], [[entities/bookshelf-speakers]], a box of clothes, and a [[entities/power-drill]].

2. **Proof of Ownership**
   - Capture the type of evidence in the **Proof Available** column (e.g., “photo from prior location,” “listed on unit inventory,” “receipt uploaded”).
   - Follow the checklist:
     - Photo of the item from multiple angles
     - Close‑up of damage
     - Wider view of surrounding storage area
     - Photo of empty space for missing items
     - Any receipts, bank statements, or inventory lists
   - See [[concepts/proof-of-ownership]] for a deeper dive.

3. **Value Estimation**
   - Focus on **Replacement Cost** (current market price for a comparable mid‑range item) rather than original purchase price.
   - When the exact model or price is unknown, approximate using a similar product from a major retailer.
   - Guidance on this process is covered in [[concepts/replacement-cost-estimation]].

4. **Documentation Consistency**
   - Keep a single sheet; add a **Category** column for easy filtering.
   - Record dates and contact person (e.g., adjuster name, call timestamps) in the **Notes** field.
   - Use consistent terminology in the **Proof Available** field to streamline adjuster review.

## Practical Checklist
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Create the spreadsheet using the header above. |
| 2 | Populate rows for each item, filling all columns. |
| 3 | Photograph each item (multiple angles, damage close‑up, context). |
| 4 | Gather receipts, bank statements, inventory lists, and note any missing items. |
| 5 | Add proof descriptors to the **Proof Available** column. |
| 6 | Review for completeness; ensure every damaged or missing item has at least one proof entry. |
| 7 | Submit the spreadsheet and supporting files to the adjuster. |
