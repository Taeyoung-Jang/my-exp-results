# Summary of session‑14_2024‑06‑15

The document offers a practical **daily routine** tailored to a 7:30 am wake‑up, emphasizing a **morning deep‑focus work period** and integrating a short **daily tidy**, a 30‑45 minute **workout**, and clear **phone‑distraction rules**. Key elements include:

## Timeline (time‑blocking)
- **7:30–8:00** – Wake up & morning prep
- **8:00–10:00** – Deep‑focus work [[concepts/deep_work]]
- **10:00–10:15** – Short break
- **10:15–12:00** – Deep‑focus work [[concepts/deep_work]]
- **12:00–13:00** – Lunch
- **13:00–13:20** – Daily tidy (dishes/laundry)
- **13:20–14:00** – Light admin tasks
- **14:00–14:45/15:00** – Workout + cooldown
- **15:30–17:30** – Lighter work & wrap‑up

## End‑of‑day workspace reset checklist
1. Clear non‑essential items from the desk.
2. Store the phone in a drawer or another room.
3. Quickly wipe the surface and close unused notebooks/tabs.
4. Place tomorrow’s starter items in view.

## Phone‑focus rules (no extra apps needed)
- **Physical distance:** Keep the phone in another room during deep‑work blocks, only accessing it during breaks.
- **Invisible screen:** Enable Do Not Disturb, flip the phone face‑down, and treat the hidden screen as “off‑limits” until the block ends.
