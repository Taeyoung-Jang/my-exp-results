### Desk setup principles
- Assign a permanent spot for every frequently‑used item to reduce visual clutter.  
- Keep the primary work surface open; store less‑used tools in drawers or out‑of‑sight containers.  
- Use simple organizers such as a small tray, a pen cup, and a drawer divider.  
- Ensure good lighting and an ergonomic chair for long sessions.  
- Route cables behind or under the desk to minimise distraction.  

### End‑of‑day reset routine (office version)
1. **Paper sweep (30–60 s)** – Collect all handouts, notes, and printouts; file needed items in a vertical file and place the rest in a drawer or recycle bin.  
2. **Shared‑printer and meeting material tidy (30–60 s)** – Return printer pickups and meeting handouts to their proper folders.  
3. **Everyday items placement (≈1 min)** – Put pens, charger, earbuds, badge, and keys back in their designated spots.  
4. **Surface wipe (30 s)** – Quickly wipe the desk to leave a clean slate for the next day.  

### Minimal organizer kit (2–4 items)
- **Slim vertical file** – Holds active papers without occupying much surface area.  
- **Small pen cup** – Keeps writing tools in one place.  
- **Shallow drawer insert** – Organises chargers, cables, and small accessories.  
- *(Optional)* **Tiny desktop tray** – For keys, badge, or other small items.  

### Practical takeaways
- A consistent, low‑effort reset (3–5 minutes) prevents clutter from accumulating.  
- Limiting organizers to essential items makes the system easy to maintain, especially in limited‑space desks.  
- Applying cable management and surface cleaning habits supports a calm, functional workspace.
