# End-of-Day Reset Routine

A structured, 3–5 minute checklist designed to quickly restore order to a desk after a workday, preventing clutter buildup and preparing the space for the next day.

## Core Principles
- **Consistency**: Perform the routine at the same time each day to form a habit.
- **Speed**: Break the process into bite‑sized steps (30–60 seconds each) so it feels effortless.
- **Minimalism**: Use a small set of organizers to keep the routine simple and sustainable.

## Typical Office Checklist (derived from [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-13]])
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 0:00 – 0:60 s | **Paper Sweep** – Gather all loose handouts, notes, and printouts. File the items you need tomorrow in a slim vertical file; place everything else in a drawer or recycle bin. |
| 1:00 – 1:60 s | **Shared Resources** – Return printer pickups and meeting handouts to their designated folders or trays. |
| 2:00 – 3:00 min | **Everyday Items** – Put pens, charger, earbuds, badge, and keys back in their dedicated spots (pen cup, shallow drawer insert, tiny desktop tray). |
| 3:30 – 4:00 min | **Surface Clean** – Quickly wipe the desk surface with a cloth or disinfecting wipe. |

## Minimal Organizer Kit (supports the routine)
- **Slim vertical file** – Holds active papers without eating desk space. [[concepts/minimalist_organizing]]
- **Small pen cup** – Keeps writing tools in one spot.
- **Shallow drawer insert** – Organises chargers, cables, and small accessories.
- *(Optional)* **Tiny desktop tray** – For keys, badge, or other small items.

## Benefits
- **Reduces visual clutter**, supporting a calmer work environment and better focus. [[concepts/desk_ergonomics]]
- **Saves time** by eliminating the need for a longer cleanup later.
- **Improves ergonomics** by keeping the primary work surface clear for proper posture and equipment placement.
