# Minimalist Desk Organizing

Minimalist Desk Organizing is the approach of equipping a workspace with only the essential tools needed for daily tasks, arranging them in clearly defined spots to reduce visual clutter and support sustained focus. The concept emphasizes simplicity, ergonomics, and quick maintenance routines.

## Core Principles
- **Limited toolset**: Use a small number of dedicated organizers (e.g., a slim vertical file, a pen cup, a shallow drawer insert) to store active papers, writing instruments, and accessories.
- **Designated homes**: Every frequently‑used item has a permanent spot, making it easy to return items after each use.
- **Open work surface**: Keep the main desk area clear for writing, typing, and ergonomic posture.
- **Quick reset**: Pair the setup with a brief [[concepts/end_of_day_reset]] routine to maintain order with minimal effort.

## Minimal Organizer Kit (as suggested in [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-13]])
1. **Slim vertical file** – Holds active documents without consuming desk space.
2. **Small pen cup** – Consolidates pens, pencils, and markers.
3. **Shallow drawer insert** – Organises chargers, cables, and small accessories.
4. *(Optional)* **Tiny desktop tray** – For keys, badge, or other small items.

## Benefits
- Reduces time spent searching for items.
- Lowers visual distraction, supporting better concentration.
- Facilitates a fast [[concepts/end_of_day_reset]] that keeps the desk tidy day after day.
- Complements [[concepts/desk_ergonomics]] by preserving space for proper posture and equipment placement.
