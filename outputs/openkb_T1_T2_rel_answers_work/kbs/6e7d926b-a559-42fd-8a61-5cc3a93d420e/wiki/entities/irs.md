# Internal Revenue Service (IRS)

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is the United States federal agency responsible for administering and enforcing the nation’s tax laws, processing tax returns, and collecting taxes.

## Connection to Session 36
- In the **[[summaries/session-36_2025-03-30]]** discussion about winding down an LLC, the IRS is mentioned as the primary authority for filing final federal tax returns and for managing payroll tax accounts such as **[[entities/eftps]]**.
- The checklist advises owners to:
  - Notify the IRS of the business’s final filing status.
  - Ensure final federal returns are filed once all numbers are settled.
  - Close payroll accounts (e.g., final Form 941) with the IRS.

## Role in Business Closure
- **[[concepts/llc-dissolution]]**: The IRS does not dissolve the legal entity but must receive the final tax filings to officially close the tax‑related side of the business.
- **[[concepts/business-closure]]**: Proper coordination with the IRS is a critical step to avoid lingering tax obligations or surprise notices after the state confirms dissolution.
- **[[concepts/emotional-impact]]**: Owners often feel anxiety about IRS correspondence; ensuring the IRS is properly notified can alleviate some of the emotional stress associated with closing a business.

## Practical Actions (from the checklist)
1. **Notify accountant** so the IRS is aware the final return will be filed.
2. **File final federal return** covering the last operating period.
3. **Close payroll tax accounts** (e.g., submit final Form 941, Form 940) via the IRS.
4. **Update business mailing address** with the IRS to receive any final notices.
5. **Monitor IRS communications** for confirmation that all tax obligations are satisfied.

## Related Entities
- **[[entities/eftps]]** – the electronic system the IRS uses for payroll tax deposits.
- Payment processors **[[entities/paypal]]**, **[[entities/square]]**, and **[[entities/stripe]]** often interact with IRS reporting for merchant tax compliance, though they are not directly managed by the IRS.
