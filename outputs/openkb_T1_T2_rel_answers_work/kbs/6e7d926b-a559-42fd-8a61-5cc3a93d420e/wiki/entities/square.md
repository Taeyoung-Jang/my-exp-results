# Square

Square is a payment‑processing organization that provides merchant services for small businesses. In the context of closing an LLC, Square accounts appear in the final‑stage checklist as one of the merchant processors that should be closed once all pending charges and refunds have cleared.

## Relevance to Business Closure
- The entity is listed under the **61–90 DAY** tasks in the [[summaries/session-36_2025-03-30]] checklist for winding down a business.
- Closing the Square account helps prevent unexpected fees and ensures that any late customer payments or charge‑backs are properly resolved before the business bank account is shut.

## Related Concepts
- The need to close Square ties into the broader [[concepts/business-closure]] process.
- It is part of the procedural steps outlined in [[concepts/llc-dissolution]].

## Associated Entities
- Other payment processors mentioned alongside Square include [[entities/paypal]] and [[entities/stripe]].
- Tax and payroll systems such as [[entities/irs]] and [[entities/eftps]] are also referenced in the same closure workflow.
