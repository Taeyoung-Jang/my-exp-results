# Stripe (Organization)

Stripe is a technology company that provides online payment processing and financial infrastructure for businesses of all sizes. In the context of the LLC closure discussed in [[summaries/session-36_2025-03-30]], Stripe appears as one of the merchant accounts that must be managed during the winding‑down process.

## Role in Business Closure
- **Merchant account closure**: The checklist advises closing merchant accounts such as Stripe, PayPal, and Square during the 61–90‑day phase to prevent lingering fees and chargeback exposure.
- **Timing**: Keep the Stripe account open until all pending refunds, chargebacks, or customer payments have cleared, then terminate it as part of the final shutdown steps.
- **Connection to concepts**: Managing Stripe ties directly into [[concepts/business-closure]] and the procedural steps of [[concepts/llc-dissolution]].

## Practical Tips from the Session
1. **Monitor pending transactions** – Review the Stripe dashboard for any unsettled charges before closing the account.
2. **Export records** – Download transaction histories for the required [[concepts/emotional-impact]]‑independent record‑retention period (typically 3‑7 years).
3. **Notify stakeholders** – Inform customers of the upcoming closure and provide alternative payment instructions if needed.
