# PayPal

**Organization** – an online payment service that enables businesses to accept and send payments electronically.

### Role in LLC dissolution (session‑36)

In the 0‑30/31‑60/61‑90 day checklist from [[summaries/session-36_2025-03-30]], PayPal is listed as a **merchant account** that should remain open until the final 60‑90‑day phase, then be closed after all pending refunds and chargebacks are resolved. Keeping the PayPal account active prevents missed payments and protects against late‑fee disputes.

### Relevant actions
- **61‑90 days:** Close the PayPal merchant account once charge‑back windows are safe.
- **Keep open until:** All customer refunds are processed and the final tax filings are complete.

### Connections
- Part of the broader [[concepts/business-closure]] process and the [[concepts/llc-dissolution]] workflow.
- Related payment processors include [[entities/stripe]] and [[entities/square]].
