# Electronic Federal Tax Payment System (EFTPS)

Electronic Federal Tax Payment System (EFTPS) is a free service provided by the **Internal Revenue Service** that allows businesses and individuals to pay federal taxes electronically.  It is commonly used for payroll tax deposits, estimated tax payments, and other federal tax obligations.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-03-30]]
- In the **LLC dissolution** checklist, the assistant advises owners to **close payroll accounts**, explicitly mentioning "IRS EFTPS" as one of the accounts to shut down once the business ceases operations.
- EFTPS is part of the **0–30 day** tasks: "Close payroll accounts if you had employees (IRS EFTPS, state unemployment, state withholding)."
- During the **61–90 day** phase, the checklist notes the need to **close IRS payroll accounts** (Form 941 final, Form 940 final) and to ensure that all tax‑related accounts, including EFTPS, are closed after final filings.
- The system remains active until the final tax returns are filed and any pending refunds or payments have cleared, so the checklist advises keeping the business bank account open until all EFTPS transactions settle.

## Related concepts and entities
- Related to the broader process of **[[concepts/llc-dissolution]]** and **[[concepts/business-closure]]**.
- Interacts with the **[[entities/irs]]** as the governing agency.
- Often used alongside merchant processors such as **[[entities/paypal]]**, **[[entities/square]]**, and **[[entities/stripe]]** when businesses need to reconcile outstanding payments before final shutdown.

## Practical considerations for closing a business
1. **Verify account status** on the EFTPS website before terminating the account.
2. **Make any final tax deposits** through EFTPS before filing the final payroll or employment tax returns.
3. **Document the closure** in your accounting records for the required **record retention** period.
4. Keep your **business email** active until you receive final confirmation that the EFTPS account is closed and no further notices are pending.
