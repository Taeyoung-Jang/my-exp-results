# Group Vibe

**Definition**: The overall atmosphere of a gathering, indicating how open, welcoming, or closed‑off the participants appear to newcomers.

## Core Indicators
- **Open, welcoming vibe**
  - Frequent eye contact and acknowledgments.
  - Conversations flow easily, allowing newcomers to join without interruption.
  - Body language is relaxed; people maintain open posture.
- **Closed‑off vibe**
  - Tight cliques, limited eye contact, and brief replies.
  - Conversations form “knots” that exclude others.
  - Participants may lean in or use intense tone, signaling discomfort.

## Practical Observation
These cues were outlined in [[summaries/session-32_2024-12-11]] where the assistant described how to differentiate between an open group and a closed‑off one during a neighborhood potluck.

## Navigation Strategies
- **Start small**: Approach one or two approachable individuals rather than addressing the whole room.
- **Use low‑stakes test questions** (e.g., “How long have you lived here?”) to gauge comfort levels.
- **Adjust body language**: Keep eye contact moderate and posture open to avoid unintentionally signaling intensity. See [[concepts/body_language]] for detailed guidance.
- **Adopt a social experiment mindset**, treating each interaction as a gentle test of the group’s receptivity. This aligns with [[concepts/social_navigation]].
