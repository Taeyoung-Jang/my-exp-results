# Low‑Effort Weekly Routine

**Definition**: A flexible, low‑stress schedule that groups activities by broad themes rather than strict time blocks, aiming to keep energy demands low while covering work, essential chores, and brief exercise.

## Core Principles
- **Theme‑Based Days** – Each day is guided by a simple theme (e.g., work focus, tiny chore, short movement) instead of a detailed minute‑by‑minute agenda.
- **Anchor Habits** – Consistent sleep, a few minutes of outdoor time, and one tiny chore per weekday prevent backlog and reduce mental load.
- **Energy‑First Adjustments** – When energy dips, optional or high‑effort tasks are the first to drop, following an [[concepts/energy-conservation-strategy]].
- **Crash‑Friendly Evenings** – Weeknight evenings are limited to a ≤10‑minute wind‑down loop, linking to [[concepts/crash-friendly-evening]].

## Practical Layout (derived from [[summaries/session-12_2024-07-15]])
| Day | Main Work Block | Chore | Light Exercise |
|-----|----------------|-------|----------------|
| Mon‑Fri | 9‑5 job (primary) | One tiny task (e.g., surface wipe, trash) | 10‑15 min walk or stretch, preferably before or during lunch |
| Sat | Flexible window for a slightly larger task (e.g., grocery run) | Optional | Optional short walk |
| Sun | Open, rest‑focused day | Light reset (e.g., prep clothes for Monday) | Optional gentle movement |

### Evening Routine (≤10 min)
1. Drop belongings & breathe (≈1 min).
2. Tiny reset task (1‑2 min).
3. Change into comfortable clothes (≈1 min).
4. Start/heat dinner (3‑4 min).
5. Brief settle‑in moment (1‑2 min).
6. Eat and switch to rest mode.

## Benefits
- Reduces decision fatigue by limiting daily choices.
- Protects recovery time, especially after a 9‑5 workday.
- Allows easy scaling up or down based on daily energy levels.
