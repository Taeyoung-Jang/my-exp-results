# Portable Workout

**Definition**: A short, no‑equipment exercise circuit designed to be performed in a hotel room or similar small space, enabling frequent travelers to maintain strength and mobility without disrupting a chaotic schedule.

---

## Core Structure (10–12 minutes)
1. **Warm‑up** – 1 minute of easy movement (march in place or light jog).
2. **Circuit (repeat 3×)**:
   - 40 seconds bodyweight squats
   - 20 seconds transition
   - 40 seconds push‑ups (or desk push‑ups if needed)
   - 20 seconds transition
   - 40 seconds plank
   - 20 seconds transition
3. **Cool‑down** – 1 minute of slow stretching.

The routine requires only body weight, a few minutes of time, and can be slotted into any morning before a flight or a mid‑day reset.

---

## Why It Works for Travelers
- **Low effort, high return** – Fits the [[concepts/low-effort-weekly-routine]] philosophy by delivering a full‑body stimulus in under 15 minutes.
- **Energy‑conserving** – Minimal equipment and space needs align with an [[concepts/energy-conservation-strategy]] for busy itineraries.
- **Supports sleep hygiene** – A brief morning activation can improve alertness, complementing the [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]] recommendations in the travel routine.
- **Jet‑lag mitigation** – Light physical activity helps reset circadian cues, reinforcing the [[concepts/jet-lag-management]] tips.

## Practical Tips
- Perform the circuit on a carpeted floor or a yoga mat in the [[entities/hotel]] room.
- If you have limited space, keep movements vertical (e.g., squat to a chair) to avoid hitting furniture.
- Pair the workout with a quick protein breakfast (e.g., [[entities/greek-yogurt]] or a [[entities/protein-box]]) to maximize recovery.
- Use the same routine on both early‑flight days and regular hotel days to build habit consistency.
