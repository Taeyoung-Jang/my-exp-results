**Goal:** Offer a flexible yet structured routine for frequent travelers.  

**Sleep hygiene:** Protect a minimum sleep window rather than a fixed bedtime; early‑flight nap; aim for total 7+ hours; shift sleep window on the plane. [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]]  

**Portable workout:** 10–12 min equipment‑free hotel‑room circuit (1 min warm‑up, 3 × 40 s squats, push‑ups, plank with short transitions, 1 min stretch). [[concepts/portable-workout]]  

**Nutrition strategy:** Stable breakfast ([[entities/greek-yogurt]], [[entities/oatmeal]], eggs); [[entities/airport]] protein‑forward orders; simple lunch; dinner with either modest alcohol or portion size; late‑night room‑service protein options (e.g., grilled chicken, fish, or a protein‑topped pasta).  

**Checklist design:** Morning, midday, evening sections with checkboxes; includes caffeine timing and contingency for late arrivals.  

**Caffeine timing:** First caffeine after breakfast; latest cutoff ≈ 2 p.m. destination time; avoid caffeine after cutoff.  

**Jet‑lag management:** Light exposure within 1 hour of arrival, naps < 20 min, adjust sleep window before boarding. [[concepts/jet-lag-management]]
