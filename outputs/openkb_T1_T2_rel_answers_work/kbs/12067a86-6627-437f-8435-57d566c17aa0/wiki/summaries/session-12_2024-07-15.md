# Summary of Session 12 (2024‑07‑15)

**Purpose:** Design a realistic, low‑stress routine that fits a 9‑5 job, minimal chores, and brief exercise while accommodating fluctuating energy levels.

**Core ideas**  
- Adopt a *broad‑theme* weekly structure instead of rigid timestamps. [[concepts/low-effort-weekly-routine]]  
- Keep weekday evenings **crash‑friendly**: a ≤10‑minute wind‑down loop. [[concepts/crash-friendly-evening]]  
- Prioritize *must‑do anchors*: steady sleep, daily short outdoor moments, one tiny chore per weekday.  
- When energy dips, drop optional/high‑effort tasks (deep cleaning, long workouts, extensive cooking). [[concepts/energy-conservation-strategy]]

## 10‑minute weekday evening routine (step‑by‑step)
1. Arrive home, drop belongings & breathe (≈1 min).  
2. Tiny reset task (trash, dish spot, countertop) (1‑2 min).  
3. Change into comfortable clothes (≈1 min).  
4. Start dinner – heat/assemble (3‑4 min).  
5. Gentle settle‑in (wash face, candle, sit) (1‑2 min).  
6. Serve, eat, and switch to rest mode.

## Vegetarian quick‑meal list (7 meals)

| # | Meal | What to buy | Assembly (≤5 min) |
|---|------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Rice‑chickpea bowl | Microwave rice, canned chickpeas, frozen mixed veg, your favorite sauce | Heat rice & veg, drain chickpeas, toss with sauce. |
| 2 | Pesto pasta with peas | Short pasta, jarred pesto, frozen peas | Boil pasta with peas, drain, mix with pesto. |
| 3 | Veggie quesadilla | Tortillas, shredded cheese, pre‑washed greens | Heat tortilla, melt cheese, fold in greens, slice. |
| 4 | Egg‑and‑toast plate | Eggs, bread, long‑lasting fruit (e.g., apples) | Scramble eggs while toast cooks, serve with fruit. |
| 5 | Grain bowl with hummus | Microwave grain (quinoa/rice), hummus, cherry tomatoes, greens | Warm grain, top with tomatoes, greens, dollop of hummus. |
| 6 | Veggie dumplings | Frozen veggie dumplings, soy sauce | Microwave or pan‑steam dumplings, drizzle with soy sauce. |
| 7 | Beans‑rice wrap | Tortillas, microwave rice, canned beans, shredded cheese, salsa | Warm rice & beans, add cheese & salsa, roll in tortilla. |

## Zero‑cook “emergency” pantry meals (open‑and‑eat)
- Canned lentil or bean soup + crackers.  
- Tortilla spread with hummus and jarred roasted peppers.  
- Yogurt bowl topped with granola and shelf‑stable fruit.

## Core staple grocery list
- Microwave rice / quinoa packets  
- Frozen mixed vegetables & peas  
- Canned chickpeas, beans, lentils  
- Jarred pesto, sauces, soy sauce  
- Eggs, shredded cheese, yogurt  
- Tortillas, bread, crackers  
- Hummus, granola, long‑lasting fruits (apples/bananas)  
- Pre‑washed greens (small bag for quick use)

## Stress‑reduction tactics
- Drop optional/high‑effort tasks first when energy is low.  
- Keep evenings under 10 minutes to protect recovery.  
- Use the “must‑do anchors” (sleep, brief outside time, one tiny chore) to prevent backlog and mental overload.
