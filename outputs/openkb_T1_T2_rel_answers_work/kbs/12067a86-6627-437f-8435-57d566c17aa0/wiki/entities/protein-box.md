# Protein Box

**Type:** product

**Overview**
The Protein Box is a pre‑packaged snack offering commonly sold at coffee‑shop chains such as Starbucks. It typically contains a mix of cheese, nuts, boiled eggs, fruit, and sometimes a small portion of crackers or dip, providing a balanced source of protein, healthy fats, and carbohydrates in a portable format.
