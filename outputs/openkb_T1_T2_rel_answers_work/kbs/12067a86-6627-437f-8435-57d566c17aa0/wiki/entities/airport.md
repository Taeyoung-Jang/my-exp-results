# Airport

## Overview
The airport is a high‑traffic transit location where travelers often need to secure nutrition, caffeine, and brief rest while managing tight schedules.

## Role in the Travel Routine (see [[summaries/session-35_2025-02-14]])
- Serves as the primary point for **early‑flight** breakfast options such as Greek yogurt, oatmeal, egg sandwiches, or a *protein box*.
- Provides quick, protein‑forward meals that support the [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]] advice to keep a stable nutrient baseline before long flights.
- Acts as a venue for the first caffeine intake of the day; the routine advises limiting caffeine intake after the **2 p.m. destination‑time cutoff** to avoid disrupting sleep.
- Offers opportunities for brief movement (e.g., walking the terminal) that complement the [[concepts/portable-workout]] approach of adding “bonus” activity throughout the day.
- Supports [[concepts/jet-lag-management]] tactics: exposure to natural light in the terminal, short walks, and avoiding long naps before boarding.

## Recommended Food Orders at the Airport
- **Chicken or tofu grain bowl** – a balanced, protein‑rich option.
- **Chicken salad** – light and easy to eat on the go.
- **Turkey sandwich with light sauce** – avoids heavy dressings.
- **Greek yogurt + banana + nuts** – a portable fallback for steady energy.
