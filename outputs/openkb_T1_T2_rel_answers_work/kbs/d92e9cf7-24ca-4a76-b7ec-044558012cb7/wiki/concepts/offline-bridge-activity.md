# Offline Bridge Activity

**Definition**: A short, screen‑free practice performed immediately after the final minutes of a wind‑down period that signals the brain it’s time to transition toward sleep.

---

## Purpose
- Lowers cognitive arousal once the device is put away.
- Reinforces the calming effects of the earlier [[concepts/low-stimulation-mode]] settings.
- Completes a [[concepts/wind-down-routine]] by providing a physical or mindful cue that the day is ending.

---

## Typical Elements (from [[summaries/session-11_2024-10-12]])
| Time after screen cutoff | Example activity | How it helps |
|--------------------------|------------------|--------------|
| **00:20 – 00:15** | Wash face with warm water or take a brief warm shower | Physical warmth promotes relaxation and drops core body temperature afterward, a cue for sleep. |
| **00:15 – 00:10** | Gentle stretch or a few yoga poses | Releases muscular tension and encourages slow, deep breathing. |
| **00:10 – 00:05** | Dim lights further, practice 3‑5 deep breaths or a short body‑scan meditation | Directly reduces sympathetic nervous system activity, easing mental chatter. |
| **00:05 – 00:00** | Slip into bed, keep the phone on DND/Night Shift/grayscale until morning | Maintains the low‑stimulus environment created by the iPhone settings (see [[entities/iphone]]). |

---

## Design Guidelines
1. **Duration**: 5–20 minutes is enough to create a clear mental break without extending bedtime.
2. **Screen‑free**: No phones, tablets, or other luminous devices; keep the device on the charger away from the bedside.
3. **Consistency**: Perform the same sequence each night to build a strong habit loop.
4. **Flexibility**: Choose an activity that feels soothing rather than obligatory—options include reading a physical book, journaling a few lines, or listening to a short calming audio track (still without a screen).

---

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Serves as the final **offline** segment of a [[concepts/wind-down-routine]].
- Complements the earlier **low‑stimulation mode** that starts the hour by adjusting iPhone settings (DND, Night Shift, grayscale).
- By keeping the device in a low‑stimulus state, the bridge activity ensures the brain does not re‑engage with digital cues at the last moment.

---

## Benefits
- Faster sleep onset and improved sleep quality.
- Reduced nighttime anxiety or boredom by providing a purposeful, calming closure.
- Supports daytime focus by preserving the efficacy of the earlier screen‑time limits.
