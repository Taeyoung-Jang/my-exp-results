# Wind‑Down Routine

A **wind‑down routine** is a deliberately timed sequence of activities performed in the hour before bedtime to help the brain transition from daytime alertness to a sleep‑ready state. The routine combines **screen‑time management** with short offline actions, creating a low‑stimulation environment that protects sleep quality and next‑day focus.

## Core Components (derived from [[summaries/session-11_2024-10-12]])

1. **Low‑Stimulation Mode** – At the start of the hour, the iPhone is set to:
   - **Do Not Disturb / Sleep Focus**
   - **Night Shift** (warm‑tone filter)
   - **Grayscale** via the Accessibility settings
   - Reduced screen brightness
   
   This visual and auditory dampening is captured by the concept [[concepts/low-stimulation-mode]].

2. **Content Selection Rules** – Two variant timelines are offered:
   - **Anxious night**: calming, predictable media (comfort show, familiar audiobook). No new content after minute 47 and no new app launches after minute 35.
   - **Bored night**: lightly engaging media (short reading, slow‑pace video essay, simple puzzle game). No new sessions after minute 45 and no app hopping after minute 35.

3. **Mid‑Hour Shift** – Around the halfway point, the routine moves from visual‑heavy media to softer audio or ambient content (podcast, lo‑fi playlist), gradually lowering cognitive load.

4. **Screen Wrap‑Up (minutes 25‑20)** – Pause the current media, lock the phone, and place it on a charger away from the bedside.

5. **Offline Bridge Activity** – A brief, non‑screen task (e.g., a minute of stretching, washing the face, a warm shower, or deep‑breathing) that signals bedtime. This step aligns with the concept [[concepts/offline-bridge-activity]].

## Typical Minute‑by‑Minute Timeline
| Time (min) | Action |
|------------|--------|
| **60‑55** | Activate low‑stimulation mode on [[entities/iphone]] (DND, Night Shift, Grayscale, dim brightness). |
| **55‑40** | Engage chosen content (calm for anxiety, light for boredom) with “no new start after 47/45 min” rule. |
| **40‑25** | Shift to softer audio/ambient media; avoid opening new apps after 35 min. |
| **25‑20** | Pause, lock, and relocate the phone to a charger/out of reach. |
| **20‑0**  | Perform an offline bridge activity (stretch, wash face, shower, etc.) before lights out. |

## Benefits
- Reduces blue‑light exposure and visual overstimulation.
- Limits the temptation to extend screen use beyond the intended hour.
- Provides a predictable cadence that calms the nervous system.
- Bridges the gap between digital engagement and sleep‑inducing physical routines.

The wind‑down routine is adaptable: users can swap specific apps or media types while preserving the timing rules and low‑stimulation settings.
