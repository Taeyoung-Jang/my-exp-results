# Screen Block

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A bounded period of intentional screen time with clear start and end cues, designed to prevent endless scrolling while preserving enjoyable digital wind‑down.

## Core Elements
- **Defined Duration** – Typically 60 or 90 minutes, set in advance.
- **Message Sweep** – A brief, purpose‑driven check of messages (e.g., 10 minutes) treated as an inbox review rather than a conversation.
- **Focused Viewing** – Choose a single video, episode, or curated playlist instead of letting the feed dictate content.
- **Transition Cue** – A physical or environmental change (turning on a lamp, making tea, stretching) that signals the end of the block.
- **Offline Bridge** – A short activity that bridges the digital phase to a calmer [[concepts/wind-down-routine]] (e.g., reading, light stretching).

## Templates from [[summaries/session-31_2025-02-11]]
### 60‑Minute Block
1. **Message Sweep** – 10 min, reply only to urgent items.
2. **Single Video** – Choose one pre‑selected video or episode.
3. **Transition** – Close apps, stand, refill water, or switch lighting.
4. **Offline Bridge** – Move to a couch with a book or do a minute of stretching.

### 90‑Minute Block
1. **Message Sweep** –10 min, inbox‑style review.
2. **Planned Viewing** – Two episodes or a batch of saved videos.
3. **Phone‑Away** – Plug the phone in, place it out of reach.
4. **Stretch** – One‑minute stretch.
5. **Offline Bridge** – Begin a calming activity such as journaling or a short meditation.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works with [[concepts/evening_anchor]] to create a clear post‑dinner transition.
- Complements a [[concepts/flexible_routine]] by offering a menu‑style option rather than a rigid schedule.
- Serves as a bridge to [[concepts/offline-bridge-activity]] and the broader [[concepts/wind-down-routine]].
- Can be combined with a [[concepts/low-stimulation-mode]] later in the evening for reduced sensory input.

## Benefits
- **Boundaries:** Prevents screen time from overtaking the night.
- **Intentionality:** Keeps digital consumption purposeful.
- **Ease of Transition:** Physical cues make shifting to offline activities smoother.
- **Mental Clarity:** Reduces the cognitive load of endless scrolling and back‑and‑forth messaging.
