# iPhone

The iPhone is a line of smartphones designed and marketed by Apple Inc. It serves as a primary device for communication, media consumption, and personal productivity. In the context of the **session-11_2024-10-12** wind‑down routine, the iPhone is leveraged as a tool for implementing a **[[concepts/low-stimulation-mode]]** during the final hour before sleep.

## Key facts from the document
- **Evening settings**: Users are instructed to enable **Do Not Disturb** (or the Sleep focus), activate **Night Shift**, lower screen brightness, and turn on **grayscale** via *Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters*. These steps collectively create a low‑stimulus visual environment.
- **Content rules**: The iPhone is used to deliver calming media (comfort shows, audiobooks, lo‑fi playlists) and to enforce timing guardrails (e.g., “no new content after 00:47”).
- **Transition**: After the screen portion ends, the iPhone is locked and placed on a charger away from the user, maintaining the low‑stimulation settings until morning.
- **Integration with routine**: The device supports the broader **[[concepts/wind-down-routine]]**, providing a controlled digital experience that blends into an **[[concepts/offline-bridge-activity]]** such as stretching or a warm shower.
