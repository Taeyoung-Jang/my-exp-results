# Summary of session-12_2023-06-15

**Purpose**: Provide a structured approach for someone planning a multi‑year relocation to a coastal area, balancing lifestyle desires with practical constraints.

## Key Concepts
- relocation checklist – a concise 10‑15 question checklist to surface priorities such as ideal day, climate, nature access, work flexibility, housing budget, walkability, community, family proximity, cultural scene, diversity, pace of life, problems to solve, and coastal importance.
- must vs nice to have – weighting of non‑negotiable (“must‑have”) criteria (coast, climate limits, job compatibility, affordable cost) against flexible (“nice‑to‑have”) preferences that can be tested later.
- relocation timeline – a 2–3 year phased plan:
  - **0‑3 months**: Identify 2‑3 coastal regions meeting must‑haves, conduct short 3‑5 day exploratory visits.
  - **3‑12 months**: Return to 1‑2 promising regions for longer stays (weeks‑month), perform remote‑work trials, start informal job market research.
  - **Year 2**: Select top contender, execute extended stay, begin serious job applications once ~70 % confidence is reached.
  - **Year 3**: Finalize move with secured housing and employment.
- decision scorecard – a weighted scoring template to quantify options across six categories:
  - Housing affordability (weight 0.25)
  - Job market fit (weight 0.30)
  - Climate + coastal feel (weight 0.15)
  - Daily‑life convenience (weight 0.10)
  - Social fit (weight 0.10)
  - Long‑term sustainability (weight 0.10)
- metrics tracking – concrete metrics to record during visits: rent ranges, commute times, seasonal weather averages, walkability scores, social interaction comfort, growth outlook.

## Process Overview
1. **Checklist Completion** – Answer the 10‑15 questions to surface deal‑breakers and preferences.
2. **Pattern Extraction** – Classify answers into must‑haves (filter) and nice‑to‑haves (ranking).
3. **Candidate Generation** – Use filters to create an initial list of coastal locations.
4. **Exploratory Visits** – Short trips to assess vibe; prune to 1‑2 candidates.
5. **Extended Trials** – Month‑long rentals or remote‑work stays; collect metric data.
6. **Scorecard Evaluation** – Populate the template, apply weights, compare totals.
7. **Job Search Integration** – Conduct informational interviews and apply in parallel with the trial phase; use market feedback to adjust weighting if needed.
8. **Decision & Move** – Choose the highest‑scoring location, secure a mid‑term lease, and transition employment.

## Illustrative Example
Two hypothetical coastal candidates were scored:
- **Cheaper Coast Town**: Total 3.35 (strong affordability, weak job market).
- **Pricier Coast City**: Total 3.80 (higher rent, strong job market).  The example shows how the heavier weight on jobs can outweigh cost advantages.

## Actionable Takeaways
- Fill out the checklist to clarify priorities.
- Follow the 2‑3 year timeline to avoid premature commitment.
- Use the provided scorecard (adjust weights to reflect personal values) to make trade‑offs transparent.
- Track the listed metrics consistently across visits to base the final decision on data, not just vibes.
