# Ad Personalization

Ad personalization refers to the process of collecting and using personal data (such as location, activity, and identifiers) to serve targeted advertisements. While it can improve ad relevance, it also raises privacy concerns because it creates detailed user profiles.

## Why Disable It?
- Reduces the amount of data shared with advertising networks.
- Limits cross‑site tracking and profiling.
- Improves overall privacy without impacting core app functionality like maps, rideshare, or weather.

## Platform‑Specific Controls
- **iPhone / iOS**: Turn off ad personalization via **Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising**. This disables the use of Apple’s identifier for ads across the device. See [[entities/apple_advertising]] for more details.
- **Android**: Disable ad personalization in the **Google Account settings** (under *Data & personalization*). This stops Google from using the Android Advertising ID for targeted ads. See [[entities/google]] for reference.

## Relationship to Other Privacy Settings
- Disabling ad personalization works alongside other privacy hardening steps such as limiting [[concepts/location_permissions]] and restricting [[concepts/background_activity_restriction]] for apps.
- These combined actions create a layered defense that cuts down unnecessary data collection while keeping essential services functional.
