# Google (Organization)

Google is a global technology corporation best known for its search engine, advertising platform, and the Android operating system. In the context of the privacy guidance from **session‑07_2024-07‑14**, Google is mentioned as the provider of ad‑personalization settings on Android devices.

## Key Points from the Session
- Users are advised to **turn off ad personalization** in the Google settings on Android to reduce tracking.
- The recommendation ties into broader privacy measures such as limiting background activity and managing location permissions.
