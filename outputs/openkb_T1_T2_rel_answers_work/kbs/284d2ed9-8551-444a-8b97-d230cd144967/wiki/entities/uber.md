## Uber (organization)

Uber provides on‑demand ridesharing services through its mobile app. In the **session‑07_2024‑07‑14** conversation, Uber was used as an example of an app whose privacy settings can be tightened without losing core functionality.

### Key privacy recommendations from the document
- **Location permissions**: Set Uber to *"while using the app"* and enable **precise location** only during an active ride or pickup. This aligns with the broader [[concepts/location_permissions]] guidance.
- **Background activity**: Disable background refresh for Uber to prevent unnecessary tracking, a practice tied to [[concepts/background_activity_restriction]].
- **General settings**: Uber, like other apps, benefits from the top non‑location privacy tweaks such as turning off ad personalization (see [[concepts/ad_personalization]]) and reviewing microphone, camera, and contacts permissions.
