# Apple (Organization)

Apple Inc. is a multinational technology company known for designing and manufacturing consumer electronics, software, and online services. It produces the iPhone, which runs iOS – the operating system that provides granular location‑services controls.

## Relevance to Session 36 (2025‑01‑14)
The session discusses how to configure iPhone location settings for privacy and battery efficiency. Apple’s iOS offers the following key controls:
- **[[concepts/ios_location_settings]]** – System‑wide toggles such as *Precise Location*, *Significant Locations*, and *Background App Refresh*.
- **[[concepts/precise_vs_approximate_location]]** – The ability to grant apps either precise GPS coordinates or an approximate area, helping balance utility with privacy.
- **[[concepts/location_privacy_tradeoff]]** – Apple’s design encourages users to limit continuous tracking, thereby reducing privacy exposure and power consumption.
- **[[concepts/geofencing]]** – Supported by iOS for automation based on entering or leaving defined zones, offering a lower‑overhead alternative to constant background tracking.

The guidance in the session recommends using Apple’s settings to allow precise location only for navigation apps, approximate location for recommendation services, and disabling background location for apps that do not need it.

## Related Entities and Concepts

- **[[concepts/location_permissions]]** – General framework for granting *Always*, *While Using*, or *Never* access, implemented in iOS.
- **[[concepts/background_activity_restriction]]** – Managing background app refresh to limit unnecessary location polling.
