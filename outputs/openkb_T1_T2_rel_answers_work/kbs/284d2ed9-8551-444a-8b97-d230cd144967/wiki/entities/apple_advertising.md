## Apple Advertising (product)

Apple Advertising is a **product** that provides ad‑personalization settings on iOS devices. It allows users to opt out of interest‑based ads served by Apple’s ecosystem, helping to reduce tracking across apps and services.

### Key facts from [[summaries/session-07_2024-07-14]]
- Mentioned as one of the top three non‑location privacy controls on iPhone.
- The recommended action is to **turn off ad personalization** via Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
- Disabling it helps limit data collection used for targeted advertising.
