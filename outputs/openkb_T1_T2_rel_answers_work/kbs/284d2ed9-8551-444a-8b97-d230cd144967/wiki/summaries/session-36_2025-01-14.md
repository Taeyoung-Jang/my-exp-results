# Summary of session-36_2025-01-14

## Trade‑offs of location access
- **Usefulness vs. privacy vs. battery** – Continuous location gives smooth navigation and hyper‑local suggestions but reveals habits and keeps GPS active, draining power. [[concepts/location_privacy_tradeoff]]

## Permission decision framework
- Ask: *Does the app need location at this moment?* If no, restrict it.
- **Always allow** – Needed for real‑time navigation, fitness/safety tracking, geofencing‑based automation.  
- **While using** – Suitable for weather, food‑delivery, ride‑share, social posting, store look‑ups.  
- **Never** – Apps that only use location for ads or optional features, or have no clear need.  
- This overall guidance aligns with [[concepts/location_permissions]].

## Checklist for auditing permissions
- **Always**: navigation, fitness/safety, smart‑home geofencing apps.  
- **While using**: weather, travel, social, retail lookup apps.  
- **Never**: ad‑targeting (see [[concepts/ad_personalization]]), games, utilities without location need.  
- Detect abuse by checking battery usage, location‑icon activity, and removing unnecessary always‑allow permissions. Use [[concepts/background_activity_restriction]] to limit unwanted background tracking.

## Reducing over‑collection
- Use **approximate** location for recommendations; reserve **precise** for navigation. [[concepts/precise_vs_approximate_location]]  
- Prefer **geofencing** over constant background tracking. [[concepts/geofencing]]  
- Limit background access to a small set of trusted apps.

## iPhone‑specific toggles
1. **App‑level**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → *App* → set to *While Using*; turn off *Precise Location* unless needed.  
2. **System Services**: Disable *Significant Locations*; keep essential services (Find My iPhone, Emergency SOS) on.  
3. **Background App Refresh**: Enable only for commute‑related apps.  

These settings provide commute and nearby suggestions while minimizing detailed tracking and battery impact. [[concepts/ios_location_settings]]
