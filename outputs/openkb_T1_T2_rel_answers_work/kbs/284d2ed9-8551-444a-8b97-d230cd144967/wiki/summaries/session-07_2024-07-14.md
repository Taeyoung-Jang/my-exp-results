## Summary of Session 7 (2024‑07‑14)

This conversation provides a practical checklist for reducing mobile tracking while keeping essential services (maps, rideshare, weather) functional. It covers **per‑app location permission strategies**, the **top non‑location privacy controls** on iOS and Android, and where to locate the relevant settings.

### Key Recommendations

- **Location permissions**  
  - Use **“While Using the App”** for Maps, Uber/Lyft, and most other apps.  
  - Switch to **approximate location** by default; enable **precise location** only when actively navigating.  
  - Turn **background refresh** off for these apps unless you need real‑time alerts.  
  - See [[concepts/location_permissions]] for the broader framework.

- **High‑impact non‑location settings**  
  1. **Ad personalization** – disable in Apple Advertising (iOS) or Google Account settings (Android). See [[concepts/ad_personalization]].  
  2. **App tracking** – on iOS, disable *Allow Apps to Request to Track*; on Android, limit background activity.  
  3. **Media access** – set photo access to **Selected Photos** and restrict camera/microphone to **While Using** or **Ask**.

- **Finding the controls**  
  - **Android**: *Settings → Apps → [App] → Battery → Restricted* to limit background activity; this relates to [[concepts/background_activity_restriction]]. Background data can be turned off under *Mobile data* for the app.  
  - **iPhone**: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos* → choose **Selected Photos**; other permissions are under *Settings → Privacy & Security* for Location, Camera, Microphone, Bluetooth.
