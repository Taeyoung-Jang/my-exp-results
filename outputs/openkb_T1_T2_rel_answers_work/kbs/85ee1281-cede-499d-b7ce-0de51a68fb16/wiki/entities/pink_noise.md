# Pink Noise (entity)

Pink noise is a type of noise whose power spectral density decreases by 3 dB per octave, resulting in a balanced, equal‑energy distribution across the audible frequency range. It is commonly used in audio engineering as a reference sound for calibrating systems, testing frequency response, and training ears.
