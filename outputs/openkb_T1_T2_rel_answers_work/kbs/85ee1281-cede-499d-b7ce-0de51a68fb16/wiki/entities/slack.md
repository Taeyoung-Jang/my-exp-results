# Slack (product)

Slack is a cloud‑based team messaging and collaboration tool that enables real‑time chat, file sharing, and integration with a wide range of services. It is commonly used in workplaces to coordinate work, share updates, and maintain ongoing conversations.

## Role in session‑40 (2025‑08‑06)
The conversation focused on how to use Slack to protect personal time while maintaining professional credibility. Key practices mentioned include:
- **Do Not Disturb scheduling** – setting a nightly DND window (e.g., 6:30 pm – 8:30 am) via *Preferences → Notifications → Schedule*.
- **Custom status** – an automatic status like “Offline—back in the morning 🌙” that signals unavailability.
- **Copy‑paste replies** for after‑hours messages (quick question, moderate urgency, true emergency, manager‑specific response).
- **Urgent channel** – a private channel (e.g., `urgent‑team‑only`) for true blockers, paired with Slack’s “notify anyway” override.
- **Emergency vs. can‑wait checklist** – a short bullet list to help teammates decide when a message warrants immediate attention.
- **Boundary communication** – concise team and 1:1 messages to set expectations without sounding like a complaint.

These tactics are part of a broader strategy for managing **[[concepts/slack_after_hours_management]]**, aligning with principles of **[[concepts/work_life_boundaries]]** and **[[concepts/urgent_communication_protocol]]**.
