# Summary of session-04_2025-06-18

**Main contribution**: a concrete, day‑by‑day practice plan (30–60 min daily) that builds ear‑training for the core [[concepts/mixing_fundamentals]] – gain staging, EQ, compression and reverb – with quick self‑tests to gauge progress.

## Key ideas
- **Professional sound** stems from intentional arrangement, performance, recording quality, and the four mixing fundamentals.
- **Learning order**: start with gain staging, then EQ, followed by compression, and finish with reverb. This mirrors the signal‑flow in a mix.
- **Simple exercises** for each tool:
  - *Gain staging*: pull all faders down, raise them slowly, note clarity (use a [[entities/DAW]]).
  - *EQ*: sweep narrow boosts to locate character frequencies, then replace with subtle cuts.
  - *Compression*: exaggerate settings for audible pumping, then dial back to transparent smoothing.
  - *Reverb*: toggle dry/wet, compare room vs. hall types.
- **Weekly schedule** (Mon–Sun) with 10‑30 min blocks and a daily self‑test (e.g., predict level adjustments, guess frequencies, identify compressor state, name reverb type, describe mix balance).
- **Progress tracking**: save versioned mixes, compare after a short break, and note faster instinctive decisions.

## Daily drill outline
| Day | Focus | Timed blocks | Self‑test |
|-----|-------|--------------|----------|
| **Monday** | Gain staging | 10 min pull‑down, 10 min rebuild | Predict level needed for a muted track |
| **Tuesday** | EQ | 15 min sweep, 10 min precise cuts | Guess harsh frequency before looking |
| **Wednesday** | Compression | 10 min over‑compress, 10 min dial‑back | Detect bypassed compressor by feel |
| **Thursday** | Reverb | 10 min add single reverb, 10 min switch types | Name reverb type (room/plate/hall) in a song |
| **Friday** | Integrated mix | 30 min apply all fundamentals | Identify one fix after a 5‑min break |
| **Saturday** | Pure ear training | 20 min pink‑noise EQ sweep (using [[entities/pink_noise]]) | Call out frequency range before checking |
| **Sunday** | Critical listening | 15 min analyze instrument placement in songs | Describe mix detail vs. a month ago |

## Expected outcomes
- Faster, more confident gain balancing.
- Ability to locate and correct frequency issues with minimal moves.
- Intuitive compression settings without over‑reliance on meters.
- Clear perception of spatial depth and appropriate reverb choice.
- Overall quicker mix decisions and measurable improvement through version comparison.
