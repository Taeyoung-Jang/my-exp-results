# Work‑Life Boundaries

**Definition**: The practice of establishing clear limits between work responsibilities and personal life to protect mental health, maintain productivity, and preserve career momentum.

## Core Principles (from [[summaries/session-40_2025-08-06]])
- **Signal availability early** – Communicate offline windows (e.g., “I won’t be around after six…”) to set expectations without appearing disengaged.
- **Deliver strong work within set hours** – Consistency and reliability during work hours reduce pressure to be constantly reachable.
- **Use structured Slack settings** – A scheduled Do Not Disturb window and a custom status convey offline status clearly.  See [[concepts/slack_after_hours_management]].
- **Create an "urgent" pathway** – A dedicated urgent channel paired with Slack’s “notify anyway” override helps differentiate true emergencies from routine queries.  Related: [[concepts/urgent_communication_protocol]].
- **Standardize after‑hours replies** – Copy‑paste scripts for quick questions, pressing but non‑emergent items, and genuine emergencies keep response tone consistent.
- **Set a clear emergency vs. can‑wait checklist** – Provides the team with guidance on when to use the urgent channel or notify anyway.
- **Frame boundaries as a productivity boost** – Messaging that highlights the benefit to the team (“so I can be fully present during the day”) positions the boundary as collaborative rather than self‑serving.

## Practical Tactics
1. **Scheduled Do Not Disturb** – 6:30 pm – 8:30 am (Slack Preferences → Notifications → Schedule).
2. **Custom Status** – “Offline—back in the morning 🌙” during DND.
3. **Urgent Channel** – Private channel (e.g., `urgent‑team‑only`) defined for real blockers.
4. **Copy‑Paste Replies** –
   - Quick question: “Just saw this—offline for the night, but I’ll jump on it first thing tomorrow.”
   - Pressing but not urgent: “Got it. I’m off for the evening, but if this is blocking anything, let me know and I’ll take a quick look in the morning.”
   - True emergency: “On it. Give me two minutes to get my laptop.”
5. **Team Norm Message** – “I’m shifting into a new rhythm with the promotion so I can be fully present during the day. If anything urgent or blocking comes up after hours, please still ping me—otherwise I’ll pick things up first thing in the morning.”
