# Evening Wind‑Down Routine

**Definition**: A short, low‑stimulus sequence of actions performed after evening activities (e.g., yoga) to signal the body and mind that it’s time to transition toward sleep.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-40_2025-09-13]])
- **Warm shower or gentle stretch** in dim lighting to lower body temperature and relax muscles.
- **10‑minute calm period**: reading a book, listening to a soothing playlist, or a brief meditation.
- **Environment control**: keep the bedroom cool, dim lights, and limit screen exposure.
- **Timing**: begin the routine shortly after returning home (around 8:30 pm) and finish before bedtime to avoid late‑night stimulation.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of the broader [[concepts/evening-routine-phases]] that include pre‑yoga nutrition and post‑yoga dinner.
- Complements [[concepts/make-ahead-meal-planning]] by ensuring meals are light and warm, reducing digestive load before sleep.
- Aligns with [[concepts/nighttime-food-avoidance]] by avoiding heavy, spicy, or sugary foods that can interfere with relaxation.
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/pre-yoga-nutrition]] (early snack) and [[concepts/post-yoga-dinner-ideas]] (quick warm meal) to create a seamless evening flow.

## Practical Steps
1. **Shower or Stretch** – 5 minutes of a warm shower or gentle yoga poses in low light.
2. **Wind‑Down Activity** – Choose one: 
   - Read a physical book (no backlit screens).
   - Play calming music or nature sounds.
   - Practice a 5‑minute guided meditation.
3. **Environment Prep** – Dim lights, set thermostat to a cool setting, and turn off electronic devices.
4. **Consistent Schedule** – Aim to start this routine within 30‑45 minutes after dinner to reinforce a regular sleep cue.

## Benefits
- Reduces sympathetic nervous system activity.
- Lowers core body temperature, facilitating sleep onset.
- Improves sleep quality by minimizing late‑night mental stimulation.
