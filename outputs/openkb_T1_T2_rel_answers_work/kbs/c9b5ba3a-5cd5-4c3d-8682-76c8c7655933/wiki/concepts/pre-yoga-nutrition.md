# Pre‑Yoga Nutrition

**Definition**: A set of lightweight, easily digestible food choices consumed roughly 90 minutes before an evening yoga session to provide steady energy without causing gastrointestinal discomfort.

## Key Elements (from [[summaries/session-40_2025-09-13]])
- **Timing**: Aim for a snack around 5:30 – 6:00 pm when the class starts at 7 pm.
- **Portion size**: Small enough to avoid feeling full, yet sufficient to prevent hunger during the workout.
- **Macronutrient balance**: Emphasize modest protein, complex carbs, and a touch of healthy fat.

### Typical Snack Options
- Yogurt with fresh fruit
- Whole‑grain toast with nut butter
- Small scoop of roasted vegetables & quinoa with hummus
- Plain overnight oats topped with berries (portion‑controlled)
- A handful of raw veggies with dip

## Make‑Ahead Strategies
Linking to [[concepts/make-ahead-meal-planning]], preparing base grains (quinoa, farro), roasted vegetables, and protein (tofu, chicken) on Sunday allows quick assembly of pre‑yoga bites throughout the week.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- After class, the same components can be expanded into a [[concepts/post-yoga-dinner-ideas]] meal.
- Choosing light snacks helps avoid the pitfalls described in [[concepts/nighttime-food-avoidance]], such as heavy or spicy foods that disrupt sleep.
- Pairing a balanced snack with a brief pre‑class stretch aligns with the broader [[concepts/evening-wind-down-routine]] for a calm transition into bedtime.

## Benefits
- Sustained energy during yoga without mid‑session fatigue.
- Improved digestion and comfort, supporting better sleep quality.
- Streamlined evening workflow when combined with make‑ahead planning.
