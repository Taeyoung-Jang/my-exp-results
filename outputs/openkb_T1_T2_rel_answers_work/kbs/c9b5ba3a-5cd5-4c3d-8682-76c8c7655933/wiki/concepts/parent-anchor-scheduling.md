# Parent Anchor Scheduling

**Parent Anchor Scheduling** is a planning approach that treats a recurring child‑care period (e.g., the kids’ evening routine) as a fixed anchor in the nightly timeline. All other activities are arranged before and after this anchor, preserving a predictable flow while allowing flexibility in block lengths.

## Core Principles
- **Anchor Identification**: Recognize the non‑negotiable time window (in the source, 5:30 – 8:30 pm) that must be dedicated to children’s routine.
- **Sequential Buffering**: Place personal tasks in a consistent order around the anchor: a short **reset**, followed by **prep for tomorrow**, a **single small chore**, and finally **downtime**. This ordering aligns with the [[concepts/evening-routine-phases]] framework.
- **Flexible Time Blocking**: Adjust the duration of each block based on energy levels or spill‑over from the anchor, but keep the sequence intact. This mirrors the ideas in [[concepts/flexible-time-blocking]].
- **Predictable Flow**: Maintaining the same order each night reduces decision fatigue and creates a calm, structured evening.

## Application from the Source Document
The session‑14 discussion demonstrates Parent Anchor Scheduling by:
1. **Treating the kids’ routine as the central anchor** (5:30–8:30 pm).
2. **Inserting a brief reset** (2–10 minutes) immediately after the anchor to shift out of parent mode.
3. **Scheduling prep tasks** (e.g., laying out clothes, packing a bag) right after the reset.
4. **Choosing one small chore** (e.g., clearing dishes, starting laundry) to keep the workload manageable.
5. **Protecting downtime** before bedtime, with length varying by personal energy.
6. **Handling late anchors** by shrinking each block proportionally while preserving the order, ensuring the overall structure remains intact.

## Benefits
- **Reduced Evening Stress**: Knowing exactly where personal time fits around the anchor prevents feeling rushed.
- **Scalable Energy Management**: Blocks can be expanded or contracted without breaking the routine.
- **Consistency for Children**: The anchor remains stable, supporting the kids’ own routine.
