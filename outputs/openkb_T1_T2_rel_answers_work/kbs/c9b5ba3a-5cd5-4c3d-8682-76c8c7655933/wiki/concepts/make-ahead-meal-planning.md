# Make‑Ahead Meal Planning

**Definition**: A systematic approach to preparing versatile, shelf‑stable foods ahead of time so that evening routines—especially pre‑ and post‑yoga meals—can be assembled quickly, stay light, and support sleep quality.

## Why It Matters
- Ensures consistent nutrient intake without daily cooking stress.
- Aligns with the timing recommendations in [[summaries/session-40_2025-09-13]] (pre‑class bite ~5:30‑6 pm, post‑class dinner ~8:30‑9 pm).
- Reduces reliance on heavy or late‑night foods that conflict with [[concepts/nighttime-food-avoidance]].

## Core Components (from the source session)
- **Base Grains**: quinoa, farro, or other whole grains cooked in bulk; keep 3‑4 days.
- **Roasted Vegetables**: carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, etc., roasted once and stored.
- **Proteins**: baked tofu, shredded rotisserie chicken, boiled eggs, or pre‑cooked lentils.
- **Soup**: simple lentil or vegetable broth (no cream) that can be portioned and reheated.
- **Overnight Oats**: plain base prepared on Sunday; fruit, nuts, or yogurt added per serving.
- **Raw Veg & Dip**: chopped veggies with hummus or tzatziki for quick pre‑class snacks.

## Typical Workflow
1. **Sunday Batch** – Allocate 60‑90 minutes to cook grains, roast veggies, and prepare protein.
2. **Portion & Store** – Divide into airtight containers (by meal type or ingredient).
3. **Combine on Demand** – Use the pre‑made ingredients to create:
   - Light pre‑yoga bites (e.g., grain + veg + hummus) – see [[concepts/pre-yoga-nutrition]].
   - Warm post‑yoga dinners (e.g., reheated veg & grain + scrambled egg or tofu, topped with pesto) – see [[concepts/post-yoga-dinner-ideas]].
4. **Adjust Timing** – Align meal prep with the evening‑routine phases discussed in [[concepts/evening-routine-phases]] and the wind‑down steps in [[concepts/evening-wind-down-routine]].

## Integration with Time Management
- Pair meal batch prep with a **flexible time‑blocking** slot on the weekend (see [[concepts/flexible-time-blocking]]) to preserve weekday evenings for yoga, wind‑down, and sleep.

## Benefits Summary
- **Speed**: 5‑10 minute assembly after class.
- **Consistency**: Stable nutrient profile each night.
- **Sleep Support**: Light, warm foods avoid the drawbacks listed in [[concepts/nighttime-food-avoidance]].
- **Mental Ease**: Reduces decision fatigue, freeing mental space for the [[concepts/evening-wind-down-routine]].
