# Post‑Yoga Dinner Ideas

Post‑yoga dinner ideas are brief, warm, and easily digestible meals that can be prepared in about ten minutes after a yoga class. They build on **make‑ahead** components prepared earlier in the week, supplying enough protein and carbs to satisfy hunger without disrupting sleep.

## Core Principles
- **Timing:** Eat within an hour of finishing class (around 8:30‑9 pm) to avoid going to bed hungry while keeping the meal light.
- **Portion size:** Modest portions that feel like a “real” dinner but aren’t heavy.
- **Warmth:** A warm plate signals the body to transition toward relaxation, supporting the [[concepts/evening-wind-down-routine]].
- **Make‑ahead foundation:** Use pre‑cooked grains, roasted vegetables, proteins, and soups prepared on Sunday (see [[concepts/make-ahead-meal-planning]]). 
- **Avoid sleep‑disrupting foods:** Steer clear of greasy, highly spicy, or sugary items that can interfere with sleep, aligning with [[concepts/nighttime-food-avoidance]].

## Quick Assembly Options (≈10 min)
1. **Roasted veg & grain bowl** – Warm a scoop of quinoa or farro with roasted veggies, stir in a scrambled egg or pre‑cooked chicken/tofu, and drizzle pesto or tahini.
2. **Lentil/vegetable soup** – Heat a cup of pre‑made soup, serve with a slice of whole‑grain toast or a small piece of cheese.
3. **Stir‑fry snack** – Sauté chopped raw vegetables and protein in a splash of broth or soy sauce; finish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
4. **Grain bowl with soft‑boiled egg** – Combine warmed grains, greens, and a halved soft‑boiled egg; add olive oil and lemon juice for flavor.
5. **Microwave‑friendly grain & protein** – Mix a portion of farro with pre‑cooked tofu, add a spoonful of hummus, and heat briefly.
