# Summary of session-40_2025-09-13

**Date:** 2025‑09‑13 18:30

## Core Advice
- **Pre‑yoga snack (5:30–6 pm):** Light, easy‑to‑digest options such as:
  - Yogurt with fruit
  - Whole‑grain toast + nut butter
  - Small portion of roasted vegetables & quinoa with hummus
  - Overnight oats (plain) with a few berries  
  See [[concepts/pre-yoga-nutrition]].

- **Post‑yoga dinner (8:30–9 pm):** Warm, modest meals that can be assembled in ~10 min using make‑ahead components:
  - Warmed roasted veg & grains + scrambled egg or pre‑cooked chicken/tofu + drizzle of pesto or tahini
  - Cup of lentil/vegetable soup + toast or cheese slice
  - Quick stir‑fry of chopped veg + protein + broth/soy sauce
  - Grain bowl with soft‑boiled egg, greens, and a splash of olive oil  
  See [[concepts/post-yoga-dinner-ideas]].

## Make‑Ahead Prep (Sunday)
- **Base grains:** quinoa, farro, or other whole grains (3–4 days)  
- **Roasted vegetables:** carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, etc.  
- **Protein options:** baked tofu, shredded rotisserie chicken, boiled eggs  
- **Soup:** simple lentil or vegetable broth (no heavy cream)  
- **Overnight oats:** plain, portioned; add fruit/nuts when serving  
- **Chopped raw veg + dip (hummus, tzatziki)** for quick snacks.  

All of these fit within [[concepts/make-ahead-meal-planning]].

## Timing Guidance
- **Pre‑class bite:** 90 min before class (5:30–6 pm) to fuel without feeling heavy. This follows a [[concepts/flexible-time-blocking]] approach.
- **Post‑class meal:** Within 60 min of finishing class (8:30–9 pm); keep light to avoid late‑night discomfort. (Meal timing for sleep quality – plain text)

## Wind‑Down Strategies
- Warm shower or a brief stretching routine in dim light.  
- Ten minutes of reading, calming music, or a short meditation.  
- Keep the bedroom cool and limit screens.  

These items belong to [[concepts/evening-wind-down-routine]] and overall [[concepts/evening-routine-phases]].

## Foods & Habits to Avoid at Night
- Heavy, greasy, or fried meals that linger in the stomach.  
- High‑sugar snacks that cause a rapid energy spike.  
- Very spicy foods that raise core body temperature.  
- Large caffeine‑containing beverages after late afternoon.  

Refer to [[concepts/nighttime-food-avoidance]] for details.
