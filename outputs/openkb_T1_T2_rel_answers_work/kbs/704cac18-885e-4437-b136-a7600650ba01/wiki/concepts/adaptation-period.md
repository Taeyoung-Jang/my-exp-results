## Adaptation Period

**Definition**: The span of time it takes a person to transition from feeling like a newcomer to living in sync with a city's everyday rhythm.

### Core elements highlighted in the source
- **Initial awareness** – Most people start noticing the local tempo within a few weeks of arrival.
- **Full adjustment** – True behavioural alignment typically occurs after **3–6 months**, when routines feel natural and the environment no longer feels foreign.
- **Factors influencing speed** – History, density, climate, dominant work‑hour patterns, and public‑space design all shape how quickly an individual adapts.

### Practical takeaways from the 30‑day pace‑reset plan
- The first month can jump‑start adaptation by deliberately adjusting mornings, social availability, and micro‑decisions.
- Consistent weekly tweaks (observing, tightening, deciding, then solidifying) help compress the longer 3–6‑month adjustment window.
