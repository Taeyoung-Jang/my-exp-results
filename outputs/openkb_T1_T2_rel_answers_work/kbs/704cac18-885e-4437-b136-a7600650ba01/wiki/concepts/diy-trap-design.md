# DIY Trap Design

**Scope** – This concept captures simple, low‑cost methods for constructing enclosed glue‑board stations that protect children and pets while remain effective against bark scorpions. The designs are drawn from the recommendations in [[summaries/session-36_2024-09-18]].

## Why DIY?
- Provides a custom fit for irregular spaces (e.g., gaps between storage bins in a garage). 
- Uses readily‑available materials – cardboard boxes, PVC pipe, tape – avoiding the need to purchase specialized hardware.
- Allows precise control over opening size to keep a medium‑sized dog and a toddler out while still admitting a scorpion.

## Core Design Principles
| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Enclosed sticky surface** | Glue board is placed inside a container so the adhesive is not directly reachable. |
| **Entry‑hole dimensions** | Approximately **½ in tall × 1 in wide**; low to the floor. This size is sufficient for bark scorpions (can flatten to finger‑width) but too small for toddler fingers or a 45 lb dog’s paw. |
| **Placement height** | Holes are cut at floor level (0 in above the surface) so the scorpion walks directly into the tunnel; hands and paws cannot get underneath. |
| **Secure anchoring** | Use strong packing tape, duct tape, or a weight (e.g., a brick) to prevent the trap from being dragged or knocked over. |
| **Material choice** | Cardboard for a cheap, disposable box; **PVC pipe** for a more durable, tamper‑resistant tunnel. |

## Two Proven DIY Models
### 1. Cardboard‑Box Station
1. Select a shoe‑box sized cardboard container (≈ 30 × 20 × 10 cm). 
2. Place a standard **glue board** flat on the bottom. 
3. Cut two opposite entry holes on the long sides: **½ in tall × 1 in wide**, with the bottom edge at floor level. 
4. Tape the lid securely; reinforce all seams with packing tape. 
5. Tape the box to a wall or to the floor so it cannot slide.

### 2. PVC‑Pipe Tunnel
1. Obtain a short length of **PVC pipe** (4 in diameter, ~12 in long). 
2. Slide the glue board inside so it rests against the interior wall. 
3. Cap both ends with cardboard or plastic pieces, each cut to leave a **½ in tall × 1 in wide** opening at the bottom. 
4. Position the pipe against a wall or between storage bins; secure with heavy tape or a small brick on top.

Both models keep the adhesive hidden, reducing the risk of accidental contact while still providing the dark, sheltered pathway scorpions favor.

## Safety Considerations
- **Pet‑safe glue removal** – Should a dog get glue on a paw, follow the steps in [[concepts/pet-safe-glue-removal]] (oil or peanut butter, gentle massage, warm soapy rinse). 
- **Avoid toxic solvents** – Do not use acetone, Goo Gone, or other harsh chemicals near pets or children.
- **Regular monitoring** – Check stations weekly (as advised in the scorpion‑proofing routine) and replace glue boards when dusty or after ~1 month.
