# Pet‑Safe Glue Removal

## Definition
Pet‑Safe Glue Removal refers to methods for detaching adhesive residues (e.g., glue‑board traps) from animal fur or skin without causing irritation or toxicity.

## Safe substances
- **Cooking oil** (vegetable, olive, canola) – breaks down the adhesive.
- **Peanut butter** – similar oil content, useful for stubborn spots.

## Step‑by‑step procedure
1. Apply a thin layer of the chosen oil or peanut butter to the glued area.
2. Gently work the substance into the fur with your fingers, allowing the adhesive to soften.
3. Once the glue releases, wash the area with warm water and mild dish soap, then rinse thoroughly.
4. Dry the skin or fur and inspect for any remaining residue.

## Substances to avoid
- **Acetone**, **Goo Gone**, mineral spirits, and other strong solvents – can irritate skin and be toxic if licked.
- Citrus‑based cleaners for the same reasons.

## When to seek veterinary help
If the glue is in a sensitive location (eyes, mouth), if the animal is highly stressed, or if removal attempts cause pain, contact a veterinarian.
