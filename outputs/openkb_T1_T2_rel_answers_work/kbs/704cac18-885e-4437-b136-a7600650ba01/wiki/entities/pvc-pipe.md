## PVC Pipe

**Type:** product

**Description:** A short length of standard 4 inch diameter PVC pipe, repurposed as a DIY enclosure for glue‑board traps to keep them out of reach of children and pets while still allowing bark scorpions to enter.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2024-09-18]]**
- Chosen for its sturdy, lightweight construction and easy availability at hardware stores.
- Used to create a tunnel‑style trap: a glue board slides inside the pipe, and the ends are capped with small openings (~½ in tall × 1 in wide) at floor level so scorpions can pass but a 45‑lb dog’s paw or a toddler’s fingers cannot.
- The pipe is secured against a wall with heavy tape or a weight (e.g., a brick) to prevent it from being dragged around the garage.

**Usage notes**
- Ensure the pipe is cut to a length of about 12 inches so it lies flat against the wall and does not protrude.
- Drill or cut the entry openings on the side of the pipe at floor level; keep the openings small enough (≈ ½ in tall × 1 in wide) to block pets and children.
- The pipe can be painted or wrapped with duct tape for added durability in a garage environment.
