# Catchmaster

Catchmaster is a commercial brand that produces a range of pest‑control products, most notably **glue‑board traps** and enclosed monitoring stations such as the **Catchmaster EZ‑Set**. These products are frequently recommended for low‑impact, home‑based pest management.

## Relevance to scorpion‑proofing
- In the advice provided in [[summaries/session-36_2024-09-18]], Catchmaster EZ‑Set monitoring stations are listed as a **store‑bought enclosed trap** option that keeps the sticky surface recessed, making it safer for children and pets.
- The stations are compatible with standard [[entities/glue-board]] inserts, allowing users to replace the adhesive component without buying a whole new device.
- They are highlighted as part of a broader [[concepts/scorpion-proofing]] strategy that emphasizes sealing entry points, reducing indoor clutter, and deploying safe traps.

## Connection to DIY trap design
- The document also discusses a DIY alternative using cardboard boxes or [[entities/pvc-pipe]] to house glue boards. Both approaches mirror the same principle of **recessed entry holes**, a concept covered under [[concepts/diy-trap-design]].
- Catchmaster’s commercial stations provide a ready‑made version of this design, reducing the need for DIY construction while still adhering to the safety guidelines.

## Pet‑safe glue removal
- While the product itself is safe for trapping, the guidance in the same session also covers how to safely remove glue from a pet’s paws using cooking oil or peanut butter, linked to [[concepts/pet-safe-glue-removal]].

## Summary of key points
- **Product type:** Enclosed glue‑board monitoring stations and disposable glue boards.
- **Primary use:** Non‑lethal trapping of insects, spiders, and scorpions (including bark scorpions).
- **Safety features:** Recessed sticky surface to prevent accidental contact by children or pets.
- **Recommended context:** Home scorpion‑proofing plans that involve minimal remodeling and prioritize safety.
