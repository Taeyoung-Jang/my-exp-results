# Protecta

**Product type:** Enclosed pest‑monitoring station that holds a standard [[entities/glue-board]] inside a recessed box.

## Overview
Protecta (often sold as *Protecta Pest Monitor*) is a small, plastic or cardboard housing designed to keep the sticky surface of a glue board out of reach of children and pets while still allowing insects and scorpions to walk through the entry slot.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2024-09-18]]
- Recommended as a **kid‑ and pet‑safe alternative** to exposed glue boards in bedrooms and garages.
- The station features side openings that are large enough for bark scorpions to enter but too small for a toddler’s finger or a medium‑sized dog’s paw.
- It can be placed **behind furniture, against walls, or in gaps between stored bins**, matching the DIY placement strategy described in the session.
- The product works well with the **DIY trap designs** outlined in [[concepts/diy-trap-design]], such as the cardboard‑box or PVC‑pipe versions.

## Recommended use
1. Insert a fresh [[entities/glue-board]] into the Protecta housing.
2. Position the station in travel corridors: under a nightstand, behind a couch, or in the garage between the wall and storage bins.
3. Secure the unit with tape or a small weight so it does not shift when the garage door opens.
4. Check the station weekly and replace the glue board as part of the regular scorpion‑proofing routine.

## Related entities & concepts
- Works in tandem with [[entities/catchmaster]] and other brand‑name monitoring stations for broader pest‑monitoring programs.
- Can be combined with a DIY PVC‑pipe tunnel (see [[entities/pvc-pipe]]) for a sturdier, semi‑permanent trap.
- Part of an overall [[concepts/scorpion-proofing]] strategy that includes sealing entry points, reducing indoor insects, and maintaining a tidy environment.
- Complements [[concepts/pet-safe-glue-removal]] guidance by minimizing the chance that pets encounter glue.
