# Glue Board

**Type:** product

A glue board is a flat, disposable sheet coated with a strong, non‑toxic adhesive. It is commonly used for passive monitoring of insects, spiders, and arachnids such as bark scorpions.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-36_2024-09-18]]
- **Purpose:** Acts as a sticky trap in a scorpion‑proofing plan without requiring bait; scorpions walk onto the board and become immobilised.
- **Placement:** Recommended in travel corridors (baseboards, behind furniture) and inside enclosed monitoring stations for child‑ and pet‑safety.
- **Safety:** Unscented glue boards are safe for homes with pets; removal of glue from a dog’s paw can be done with cooking oil or peanut butter (see [[concepts/pet-safe-glue-removal]]).
- **Enclosed stations:** The board can be housed in commercial stations like Catchmaster EZ‑Set or Protecta monitor boxes, or in DIY housings made from a cardboard box or a length of [[entities/pvc-pipe]].
- **Maintenance:** Check weekly, replace when dusty or full, typically every month.

## Usage tips
1. **Position** the board flush against a wall or baseboard where scorpions travel.
2. **Hide** it under furniture or inside a sealed box to keep it out of reach of children and pets.
3. **Replace** the board weekly or when it becomes saturated with insects.
4. **If contaminated**, clean pet paws with a small amount of cooking oil, then wash with warm, soapy water.
