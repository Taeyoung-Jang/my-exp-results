# Summary of session‑36 (2024‑09‑18)

**Key purpose** – Provide a compact, day‑to‑day [[concepts/scorpion-proofing]] plan that works without major remodels, includes safe trap deployment for a home with a medium‑sized dog and a toddler, and gives pet‑safe glue‑removal guidance.

## Checklist & nightly routine
- **Seal entry gaps** (pipes, baseboards, door thresholds, window frames).  
- **Reduce indoor hiding spots**: keep items off the floor, tidy closets, limit box piles.  
- **Control moisture** and fix minor leaks.  
- **Trim exterior vegetation** and move firewood or clutter away from walls.  
- **Place sticky traps** in travel corridors; keep indoor insects low.  
- **Keep bedding and clothing off the floor**; shake out shoes, clothes, towels each morning.  
- **Nightly 30‑second sweep** – close doors, dry sinks/showers, verify trap placement. This habit aligns with [[concepts/keystone-habits]].

## Sticky‑trap placement & schedule
| Area | Typical placement | Qty |
|------|-------------------|-----|
| Bedroom | Baseboard near door & behind nightstand | 1‑2 |
| Bathroom | Beside toilet or under sink cabinet | 1 |
| Kitchen | Behind trash can or under sink | 1 |
| Living room | Behind couch or side table | 1 |
| Garage | Near door edges and behind stored bins | 2‑3 |

- Check/replace weekly; replace when dusty, full, or after ~1 month.  
- No bait needed; unscented [[entities/glue-board]]s work fine.

## Kid‑ and pet‑safe trap solutions
### Enclosed monitoring stations (store‑bought)
- **Catchmaster EZ‑Set** – [[entities/catchmaster]]  
- **Protecta Pest Monitor** – [[entities/protecta]]  
These boxes keep the sticky surface recessed.

### DIY safe stations (see [[concepts/diy-trap-design]])
1. **Cardboard‑box version**  
   - Size: shoe‑box (~30 × 20 × 10 cm) to fit a standard glue board.  
   - Two entry holes on opposite long sides, each **½ in tall × 1 in wide**, cut at floor level.  
   - Lid taped shut; tape box to wall/floor to prevent sliding.

2. **PVC‑pipe version**  
   - 4 in diameter pipe, ~12 in long – [[entities/pvc-pipe]].  
   - Glue board slid inside; caps on ends with identical **½ in × 1 in** openings at floor level.  
   - Secure against wall with heavy tape or a weight (e.g., a brick) on top.

Both designs let bark scorpions (≈ finger‑width) pass while blocking toddler fingers and a 45 lb dog’s paw.

## Glue‑removal for pets
- **Safe options**: cooking oil, olive oil, or peanut butter – apply, work in gently, then wash with warm soapy water.  
- **Avoid**: acetone, Goo Gone, mineral spirits, or citrus‑based solvents, which can irritate skin and be ingested.  
- If removal is difficult or the dog is stressed, consult a veterinarian. This follows [[concepts/pet-safe-glue-removal]].
