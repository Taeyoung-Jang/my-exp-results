## Summary of session-35_2025-03-13

**Date:** 2025‑03‑13  

### Goal  
Create a short, sustainable morning add‑on that preserves steady energy and prevents mid‑afternoon crashes.

### 5‑minute mobility flow (with timing & form cues)  
| Time | Move & Cue |
|------|-----------|
| 0:00–1:00 | Neck circles & shoulder rolls – keep ribs relaxed, shoulders loose. |
| 1:00–2:00 | Cat‑cow (floor or standing) – soft elbows, belly drop on “cow,” gentle round on “cat.” |
| 2:00–3:00 | Hip circles or leg swings – core lightly engaged; hold a wall if needed. |
| 3:00–4:00 | Forward fold – deep knee bend, head hangs heavy, avoid pulling down; optional back‑of‑chair hamstring stretch. |
| 4:00–5:00 | Standing twist – hips forward, twist from upper back, move slowly. |

**Alternatives** for back/knee sensitivity are noted in each step. This flow aligns with [[concepts/mobility-flow]].

### Breakfast rotation (20‑25 g protein per day)  
- **Monday – Greek yogurt**: 1 cup plain [[entities/greek-yogurt]] + 2 Tbsp [[entities/nuts]] → ~22–24 g protein  
- **Tuesday – Eggs + cottage cheese**: 2 [[entities/hard-boiled-egg]] + ½ cup [[entities/cottage-cheese]] → ~24–26 g protein  
- **Wednesday – Cottage cheese**: 1 cup [[entities/cottage-cheese]] → ~22–24 g protein  
- **Thursday – Avocado toast + eggs**: 1 slice high‑protein [[entities/whole-grain-toast]] (5–7 g) + 2 [[entities/hard-boiled-egg]] (12 g) + optional spoonful [[entities/cottage-cheese]] or [[entities/greek-yogurt]] → ~20–22 g protein  
- **Friday – No‑cook, no‑fish**: 3–4 oz sliced [[entities/turkey]] or [[entities/chicken]] + fruit or crackers → ~20–25 g protein  

Each day is presented as a checklist for quick screenshotting and follows the principles of [[concepts/steady-energy-breakfast]].

### Troubleshooting afternoon crash  
1. **Undereating protein** in the morning  
2. **Carb‑heavy lunch** without balance  
3. **Insufficient hydration**  

These steps are drawn from [[concepts/energy-crash-troubleshooting]].
