# Tea (product)

**Type**: product

Tea is a drink prepared by steeping the leaves, buds, or twigs of the Camellia sinensis plant in hot water. It contains caffeine, though typically less than an equivalent serving of coffee, making it a common choice for people seeking a milder stimulant.
