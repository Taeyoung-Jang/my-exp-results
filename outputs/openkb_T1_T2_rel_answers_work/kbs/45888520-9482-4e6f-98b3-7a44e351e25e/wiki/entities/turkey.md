# Turkey (sliced)

**Type:** product

## Overview
Sliced turkey is a pre‑cooked, ready‑to‑eat deli‑style meat, typically sold in thin strips. It provides a quick source of high‑quality protein without any cooking or reheating.

## Key facts from **[[summaries/session-35_2025-03-13]]**
- Recommended as the **Friday** breakfast choice in a 5‑day rotation aimed at steady energy.
- Portion size: **3–4 oz** (about 85–115 g) of sliced turkey.
- Protein content: roughly **20–25 g** per portion, making it suitable for the target 20–25 g protein per meal.
- Paired with a piece of fruit or a few crackers for a balanced, no‑cook meal.

## Nutrition
- **Protein:** ~20 g per 3 oz, complete amino acid profile.
- **Fat:** Low, typically <2 g per serving.
- **Carbohydrates:** Negligible, unless packaged with added sugars.
- **Micronutrients:** Provides B‑vitamins (especially B12) and minerals such as selenium.

## Role in a steady‑energy routine
Including sliced turkey on Friday helps avoid the “mid‑afternoon crash” discussed in [[concepts/energy-crash-troubleshooting]] by ensuring adequate morning protein without a heavy or sugary meal. It aligns with the principles of [[concepts/steady-energy-breakfast]], which emphasize high protein, low sugar, and minimal preparation time.
