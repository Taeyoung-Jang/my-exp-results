# Whole‑Grain Toast

**Type:** Product

Whole‑grain toast is a slice of bread made from whole‑grain flour, often chosen for its higher fiber and protein content compared to refined white bread. In the breakfast rotation from [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-13]], a **higher‑protein** variety is recommended because it contributes an additional **5–7 g of protein per slice**, helping each morning meal reach the target **20–25 g protein**.

## Role in the morning routine
- Serves as the carbohydrate base for the **[[concepts/steady-energy-breakfast]]** on Thursday, paired with avocado and hard‑boiled eggs.
- Provides sustained glucose release without causing rapid blood‑sugar spikes, supporting the overall goal of steady energy throughout the day.
- Can be combined with other protein sources such as [[entities/turkey]], [[entities/chicken]], or a spoonful of [[entities/cottage-cheese]] for extra protein on days when additional protein is needed.

## Suggested pairings from the session
- **Avocado toast** – one slice of whole‑grain toast topped with mashed avocado and two hard‑boiled eggs (see [[entities/avocado]] and [[entities/hard-boiled-egg]]). This combo delivers about **17–19 g protein**, which can be nudged to the 20‑22 g range with a side of cottage cheese.
- **Turkey or chicken roll‑up** – for a no‑cook Friday option, sliced turkey or chicken can be eaten alongside the toast and a piece of fruit, contributing **20–25 g protein** total.

## Practical notes
- Choose a brand that lists **5–7 g protein per slice** to meet the protein targets without increasing portion size.
- Store in a sealed container to keep the crust fresh, especially if you prep breakfast the night before.
- Toast lightly to improve digestibility and enhance the texture for toppings.
