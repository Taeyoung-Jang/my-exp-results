# Chicken (sliced)

**Type:** product

Sliced chicken is a pre‑cooked, ready‑to‑eat protein source that can be eaten straight from the package. In the context of the morning routine from [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-13]], it is suggested as a **no‑cook, no‑reheat, non‑fish Friday breakfast** alternative.

## Key facts from the document
- **Purpose:** Provides a quick ~20–25 g of protein for a steady‑energy breakfast without requiring cooking or heating.
- **Portion:** 3–4 oz (about 85–115 g) of sliced chicken, which typically delivers 20–22 g of protein.
- **Pairing:** Recommended with a piece of fruit or a few crackers to add carbs and make the snack more satisfying.
- **Energy role:** Helps prevent mid‑afternoon energy crashes by ensuring adequate morning protein intake, linking to the broader concept of [[concepts/steady-energy-breakfast]] and the troubleshooting steps in [[concepts/energy-crash-troubleshooting]].
