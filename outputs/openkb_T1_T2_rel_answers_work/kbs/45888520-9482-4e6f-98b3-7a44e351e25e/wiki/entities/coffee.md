# Coffee

**Type:** product

Coffee is a brewed beverage made from roasted coffee beans, widely consumed for its stimulating [[entities/caffeine]] and sensory qualities. It features prominently in daily [[concepts/caffeine-ritual]]s and can become a source of [[concepts/caffeine-dependence]] when consumption leads to withdrawal symptoms such as headache and fog.

### Relevance in session-01_2024-09-14
- The user follows a routine of two 8‑10 oz coffees each day (around 8 am and 11:30 am).
- The conversation focuses on [[concepts/caffeine-tapering]] strategies, including mixing [[entities/decaf-coffee]] or switching to [[entities/tea]] to reduce dependence while preserving the ritual.
- A gradual taper plan suggests reducing total caffeine by roughly 10‑20 % every 3‑5 days and keeping the last caffeinated drink before early afternoon to protect sleep.

### Key Points
- **Ritual vs. dependence**: Coffee provides comfort (ritual) and stimulant effects (dependence).
- **Tapering methods**: Reduce cup size, blend with decaf, replace with tea, shift timing earlier.
- **Sleep impact**: Finish caffeine intake by early afternoon to avoid sleep disruption.
