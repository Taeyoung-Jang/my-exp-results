# Nuts (product)

Nuts are edible kernels of various trees and shrubs that are high in healthy fats, protein, fiber, vitamins, and minerals. In the context of the **morning routine** outlined in [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-13]], nuts serve primarily as a quick, portable protein source.

## Role in the recommended breakfast rotation
- Added **2 Tbsp** of nuts or seeds to **1 cup plain Greek yogurt** on Monday, raising the meal’s protein from ~15 g to ~22–24 g.
- Mentioned as an optional side on Thursday (avocado toast + eggs) to push the total protein into the 20–22 g range.
- Contribute to meeting the target **20–25 g protein per day** without adding much preparation time.

## Connections to broader concepts
- Part of the [[concepts/steady-energy-breakfast]] strategy that emphasizes low‑sugar, high‑protein foods to keep blood‑sugar stable and avoid mid‑afternoon crashes.
- Indirectly supports [[concepts/energy-crash-troubleshooting]] by providing sustained amino acids and preventing under‑eating.

## Related entities
- Often paired with [[entities/greek-yogurt]], [[entities/cottage-cheese]], [[entities/hard-boiled-egg]], [[entities/avocado]], [[entities/turkey]], and [[entities/whole-grain-toast]] in the suggested weekly plan.

## Practical tips
- Choose unsalted, raw or dry‑roasted varieties to keep sodium low.
- Store in an airtight container to maintain freshness and prevent rancidity.
- If you have nut allergies, seeds (e.g., pumpkin, sunflower) can provide a comparable protein boost.
