# Steady Energy Breakfast

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** A high‑protein, low‑sweetness breakfast routine designed to keep blood‑sugar stable and prevent mid‑afternoon energy crashes.

## Core Principles
- **Protein ≥ 20 g** per serving to sustain satiety and steady glucose.
- **Low simple sugars** to avoid rapid spikes and subsequent crashes.
- **Quick, no‑cook or minimal‑prep** options for consistency.
- **Portion‑specific guidance** so the user can hit target protein without guessing.

## Weekly Rotation (derived from [[summaries/session-35_2025-03-13]])
| Day | Items | Portion | Approx. Protein |
|-----|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Monday** | [[entities/greek-yogurt]] + nuts | 1 cup yogurt + 2 Tbsp nuts | 22–24 g |
| **Tuesday** | 2 [[entities/hard-boiled-egg]] + [[entities/cottage-cheese]] | 2 eggs + ½ cup cottage cheese | 24–26 g |
| **Wednesday** | [[entities/cottage-cheese]] | 1 cup cottage cheese | 22–24 g |
| **Thursday** | [[entities/whole-grain-toast]] + avocado + eggs | 1 slice toast + 2 eggs + small avocado + optional ½ cup cottage cheese | 20–22 g |
| **Friday** | Sliced [[entities/turkey]] (or chicken) | 3–4 oz turkey/chicken + fruit/crackers | 20–25 g |

### Form cues & practical tips
- **Measure by volume** (cups, ounces) rather than weight for ease.
- Pair the protein source with a **small healthy fat** (nuts, avocado) to further blunt glucose spikes.
- Keep the meal **ready‑to‑eat** (pre‑portion nuts, boil eggs in advance) to reduce friction.

## Relationship to Energy Stability
The rotation directly addresses the three common crash triggers identified in [[concepts/energy-crash-troubleshooting]]:
1. **Undereating protein** – each meal meets the 20‑25 g target.
2. **Excess simple carbs** – low‑sugar foods replace sugary cereals or pastries.
3. **Dehydration** – the routine encourages a glass of water alongside the meal.

## Integration with Morning Routine
A brief [[concepts/mobility-flow]] (5‑minute mobility sequence) precedes the breakfast, ensuring the body is awake without taxing energy reserves. The combination of gentle movement and a steady‑protein meal creates a **compound effect** for sustained alertness.

## Adaptations & Alternatives
- If dairy is an issue, swap [[entities/greek-yogurt]] for a plant‑based high‑protein yogurt.
- For vegetarians, replace turkey/chicken with **legume‑based** options (e.g., roasted chickpeas) while keeping portion sizes that hit the protein goal.
- When time is extremely limited, combine the yogurt and nuts into a **single‑serve container** prepared the night before.
