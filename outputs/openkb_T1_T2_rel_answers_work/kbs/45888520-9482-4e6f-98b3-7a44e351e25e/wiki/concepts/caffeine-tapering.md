# Caffeine Tapering

Caffeine tapering is a step‑wise approach to decreasing [[entities/caffeine]] consumption in order to reduce physiological dependence while preserving the comforting aspects of a daily [[concepts/caffeine-ritual]]. The method emphasizes small, incremental changes—typically a 10‑20 % reduction every three to five days—combined with adjustments to timing, beverage strength, and substitution with lower‑caffeine alternatives.

## Core Principles
- **One variable at a time**: Change either the amount, strength, or timing of a drink before moving to the next adjustment.
- **Gradual reduction**: Aim for roughly a 10‑20 % drop in total caffeine per step to avoid withdrawal headaches and fog.
- **Early‑day cutoff**: Keep the last caffeinated beverage before early afternoon (e.g., before 2 pm) to protect [[concepts/caffeine-tapering]]'s impact on sleep.
- **Substitution**: Use [[entities/decaf-coffee]], half‑caff blends, or milder [[entities/tea]] to maintain the ritual while lowering stimulant load.

## Sample 2–3 Week Plan (based on [[summaries/session-01_2024-09-14]])
| Week | Action | Approx. caffeine change |
|------|--------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Keep schedule (8 am & 11:30 am). Make the second cup ~25 % weaker by adding a quarter of the mug decaf. | ↓≈10 % |
| 2 | Increase decaf proportion to ~50 % of the second cup (half‑caff). | ↓≈20 % |
| 3 | Shift the second cup toward mostly decaf with a small splash of regular (≈25 % regular) and optionally move it 30 min earlier. | ↓≈30‑40 % |

## Managing Withdrawal Symptoms
If a headache or increased fog occurs on a step‑down day, temporarily raise caffeine just enough to relieve the symptom (e.g., a few extra sips of regular [[entities/coffee]] or a slightly larger regular‑to‑decaf mix). Resume the planned reduction once the symptom subsides.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/caffeine-dependence]]**: Tapering directly addresses physiological dependence by allowing the body to adapt to lower stimulant levels.
- **[[concepts/caffeine-ritual]]**: By substituting with decaf or tea, tapering preserves the habitual and sensory aspects of the ritual while reducing caffeine load.

Caffeine tapering thus offers a balanced pathway to lessen reliance on [[entities/caffeine]] without abandoning the enjoyable routine associated with coffee or tea.
