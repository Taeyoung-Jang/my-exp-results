# COVID‑19 (other)

COVID‑19 is a contagious respiratory disease caused by the SARS‑CoV‑2 virus. In the health‑self‑care checklist from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]], COVID‑19 is mentioned as a condition for which **testing** should be considered when a sudden fever with body aches or a new cough/fever combo appears.

## Relation to the Checklist
- The checklist groups COVID‑19 alongside [[entities/influenza]] under the **Cold / Flu** triage category, using the [[concepts/triage-categories]] framework.
- It advises **consider testing** for COVID‑19 (or flu) when symptoms start abruptly with fever and systemic aches, reflecting the importance of early detection.
- While COVID‑19 is not listed as a red‑flag itself, any severe symptom meeting the [[concepts/universal-red-flags]] criteria (e.g., trouble breathing) would prompt immediate care at an [[entities/emergency-room]] or [[entities/urgent-care]].

## Symptom Duration Guidance
- The checklist’s [[concepts/symptom-duration-thresholds]] inform when to monitor versus when to seek medical attention for COVID‑related symptoms, mirroring the same thresholds used for fever, cough, and other respiratory signs.

## Key Points from the Document
1. **Testing Trigger** – Sudden onset of fever with aches or a new cough/fever combo → consider COVID‑19 or flu testing.
2. **Escalation** – If any universal red flag (e.g., shortness of breath) develops, seek urgent care/ER.
3. **Monitoring** – Mild symptoms under the fever and duration thresholds can be managed at home, but watch for worsening.
