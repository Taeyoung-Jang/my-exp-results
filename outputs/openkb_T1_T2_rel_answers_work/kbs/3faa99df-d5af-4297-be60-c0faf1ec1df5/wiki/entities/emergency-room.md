# Emergency Room (ER)

The Emergency Room (ER) is a hospital department designed for **immediate, life‑saving treatment** of severe or rapidly worsening health issues. In the *session‑14_2024‑06‑15* checklist, the ER is the recommended destination for any condition that triggers the **[[concepts/universal-red-flags]]** or exceeds the thresholds outlined in [[concepts/symptom-duration-thresholds]].

## Role in the checklist
- Listed under the "Urgent care / ER" column for red‑flag situations such as:
  - Fever > 103°F unresponsive to medication.
  - Sudden, severe headache or neurological symptoms.
  - Severe respiratory distress, chest pain, or inability to breathe.
  - Significant bleeding that does not stop with pressure.
  - Large, deep, or facial burns, and severe abdominal pain with blood in vomit or stool.
- Serves as the **escalation point** beyond [[entities/urgent-care]] when symptoms indicate a potential life‑threatening emergency.

## Relationship to other concepts and entities
- Part of the broader **[[concepts/triage-categories]]** framework that groups health issues into "wait it out," "call my doctor," and "urgent care/ER."
- Works in tandem with **[[entities/covid-19]]** and **[[entities/influenza]]** testing decisions when sudden fever or respiratory symptoms arise.
- May be involved in evaluating suspected **[[entities/streptococcal-pharyngitis]]** cases that present with severe airway compromise.
