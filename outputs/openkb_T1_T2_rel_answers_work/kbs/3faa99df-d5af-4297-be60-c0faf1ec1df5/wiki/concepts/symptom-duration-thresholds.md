# Symptom Duration Thresholds

**Definition**: Concrete time‑based limits for common minor ailments that help decide whether to continue home care, contact a clinician, or seek urgent attention.

## Core thresholds (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]])
- **Fever**
  - < 102°F and < 3 days → home care
  - 102–103°F lasting > 3 days → call a doctor
  - > 103°F (or unresponsive to meds) → urgent care/ER
- **Cold/Flu symptoms**
  - Congestion, cough, mild sore throat < 7 days and improving → wait
  - No improvement by day 7 or fever 102–103°F > 3 days → call a doctor
- **Headache**
  - Usual pattern, resolves within hours → wait
  - Persists > 2 days or atypical → call a doctor
- **Stomach bug (vomiting/diarrhea)**
  - Improves within 24–48 h, fluids tolerated → wait
  - Lasts > 48–72 h or cannot keep liquids down 12 h → call a doctor
- **Sore throat**
  - Improves ≤ 3 days → wait
  - > 3 days, white patches, fever, swollen glands → call a doctor (possible [[entities/streptococcal-pharyngitis]])
- **Back pain / sciatica**
  - Improves 3–5 days → wait
  - No improvement after 1 week or interferes with sleep → call a doctor
- **Cuts**
  - Bleeding stops with pressure, clean edges → wait
  - Gaping edges or spreading redness after 1–2 days → call a doctor
- **Burns**
  - Small red burn improves in 1–2 days → wait
  - Blisters > ¼ inch or pain worsens after a day → call a doctor

## Escalation overrides
Any symptom that meets the **[[concepts/universal-red-flags]]** (e.g., sudden severe pain, breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, fainting) bypasses these duration rules and requires immediate urgent care or emergency department evaluation.

## Relation to triage workflow
These thresholds map directly onto the three tiers defined in [[concepts/triage-categories]]:
1. **Wait it out** – symptoms within safe duration limits.
2. **Call my doctor** – symptoms exceed home‑care thresholds but lack red‑flag urgency.
3. **Urgent care / ER** – either red‑flag criteria are met or duration exceeds the highest safe limit.
