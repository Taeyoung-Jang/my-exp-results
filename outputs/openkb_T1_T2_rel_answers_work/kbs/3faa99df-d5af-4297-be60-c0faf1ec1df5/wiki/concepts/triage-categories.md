# Triage Categories

**Triage Categories** is a framework for quickly deciding how to respond to common, self‑limited health complaints. The model groups symptoms into three action levels—*OK to wait it out*, *Call my doctor*, and *Urgent care/ER*—based on specific **if‑then** thresholds for duration, severity, and accompanying warning signs.

## Core Idea
The categories draw on concrete [[concepts/symptom-duration-thresholds]] (e.g., fever < 102°F for < 3 days) and the overarching [[concepts/universal-red-flags]] that override any lower‑level guidance. By matching a symptom to its appropriate bucket, users can decide whether home care, a primary‑care visit, or immediate emergency treatment is warranted.

## Typical Groupings (from [[summaries/session-14_2024-06-15]])
| Health Issue | When to *Wait* | When to *Call Doctor* | When to *Urgent Care/ER* |
|--------------|----------------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Cold / Flu** | Congestion, mild cough < 7 days; fever < 102°F < 3 days | Fever 102‑103°F > 3 days or no improvement by day 7 | Breathing difficulty, fever > 103°F, or any rapid worsening |
| **Sore Throat** | Mild pain improving in 2‑3 days | > 3 days, white patches, fever, swollen glands → consider [[entities/streptococcal-pharyngitis]] testing | Trouble swallowing, drooling, or breathing issues |
| **Headache** | Typical pattern, resolves in hours | > 2 days or atypical features | Sudden “worst ever”, vision change, weakness, confusion |
| **Stomach Bug** | Vomit/diarrhea improves 24‑48 h, fluids tolerated | > 48‑72 h or dehydration signs | Blood in vomit/stool, severe pain, marked dehydration |
| **Back Pain / Sciatica** | Improves 3‑5 days after exertion | No improvement after 1 week or functional impact | Leg weakness, loss of bowel/bladder control, groin numbness |
| **Cuts** | Small, stops bleeding, clean edges | Edge separation or spreading redness | Bleeding persists > 10 min of pressure or deep tissue visible |
| **Burns** | Small red burn improves in 1‑2 days | Blisters > ¼ inch or worsening pain | Large, deep, face/hands/genitals, or white/charred skin |

## Usage
The **Triage Categories** concept is useful for:
1. Designing quick‑reference checklists (e.g., phone screenshots).
2. Embedding decision logic into health‑app reminders.
3. Educating patients on when self‑care is safe versus when professional assessment is required.

By consistently applying these categories, individuals can reduce unnecessary medical visits while ensuring that serious conditions receive prompt attention.
