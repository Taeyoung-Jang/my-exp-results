# Inventory Documentation

**Definition**: A thorough, photo‑supported record of every personal item removed or affected by a loss, including descriptions, condition, and location, used to substantiate insurance claims.

---

## Why It Matters
In a water‑damage scenario, insurers require clear evidence of what was lost, its condition before and after the incident, and proof that the items were properly handled. A detailed inventory prevents disputes, avoids denied expenses, and speeds up claim settlement.

---

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-15]])
1. **Comprehensive List** – Every item packed out should be listed with a concise description and its pre‑damage condition.
2. **Photographic Evidence** – High‑resolution photos (or short videos) linked to each inventory entry, showing the item in‑place and after packing.
3. **Labeling System** – Boxes and items must be labeled in a way that directly maps to the inventory (e.g., box numbers, item codes).
4. **Responsibility Trace** – Clear identification of who maintains the inventory: the adjuster, the [[entities/contractor]], or the [[entities/pack-out-company]].
5. **Chain‑of‑Custody Integration** – The inventory should dovetail with the [[concepts/chain-of-custody]] log, documenting every hand‑off from home to storage.

---

## Checklist for Requesting an Inventory
- "Can you give me a full inventory of every item you packed out, **including photos and condition descriptions**?"
- "How are the boxes and items **labeled** to match the inventory list?"
- "Who is **maintaining** the inventory – your team, the pack‑out company, or the adjuster?"

---

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/pack-out-process]]** – The inventory is created during the pack‑out stage; it must align with labeling and handling procedures.
- **[[concepts/chain-of-custody]]** – A robust inventory feeds into the custody log, ensuring traceability of each item.
- **[[concepts/red-flags-contents-handling]]** – Vague entries, missing photos, or bulk line items are red flags indicating poor inventory practices.
- **[[concepts/budgeting]]** – Accurate inventories support precise budgeting for repairs, replacements, and temporary storage costs.

---

## Best Practices
- Store the inventory, photos, and related logs in a **single digital folder** for easy sharing with the insurer.
- Keep a **chronological notes log** of communications with the [[entities/adjuster]] and any vendors.
- Request **copies of the inventory and labeling system** before any boxes are opened or contents are valued.

---

## When to Escalate
If the inventory contains generic descriptions (e.g., "kitchen items"), lacks photos, or the vendor refuses to provide a copy, flag this as a **[[concepts/red-flags-contents-handling]]** issue and document the concern in writing to the adjuster.
