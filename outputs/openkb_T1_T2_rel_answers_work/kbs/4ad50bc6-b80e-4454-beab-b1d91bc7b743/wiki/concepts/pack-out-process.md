## Pack‑Out Process

**Definition**: The pack‑out process is the systematic procedure used by insurance‑related vendors to inventory, photograph, label, and move damaged personal contents from a disaster‑affected residence to a storage location, ensuring clear traceability and accountability.

### Core Steps
1. **On‑site Inventory Documentation**
   - Create a detailed inventory list that records each item’s description, condition, and location.
   - Capture clear photos or short videos for every item, linking visuals to the inventory entry.  
   - This step aligns with [[concepts/inventory-documentation]].

2. **Labeling System**
   - Assign unique identifiers (e.g., barcodes or numbered tags) to each box or item.
   - Maintain a master label‑to‑inventory mapping sheet that can be referenced later.

3. **Chain of Custody Logging**
   - Record who handled each box, the timestamps of removal, transport, and storage entry.
   - Provide a copy of the log to the homeowner and the insurer.  
   - See [[concepts/chain-of-custody]] for best‑practice details.

4. **Transport & Storage**
   - Move items to a secure, climate‑controlled storage unit.
   - Document the storage location, expected duration, and who bears the storage costs.

5. **Documentation Handoff**
   - Deliver the complete inventory, labeling map, and chain‑of‑custody record to the adjuster and, if applicable, the [[entities/adjuster]] and [[entities/contractor]].
   - Retain copies in a centralized digital folder for the claim.

### Key Considerations from Session 35
- **Ask for Full Inventory with Photos** – “Can you give me a full inventory of every item you packed out, including photos and a description of the item and its condition?”
- **Clarify Who Maintains the Inventory** – Identify whether the [[entities/pack-out-company]], the contractor, or the adjuster is responsible.
- **Request Chain‑of‑Custody Logs** – Ensure you receive a copy and that updates are shared if items are moved again.
- **Storage Fees Transparency** – Confirm policy coverage and who is billing for storage.
- **Red‑Flag Awareness** – Vague inventory entries, missing photos, generic labels, or undocumented disposals signal poor pack‑out practices.  
  See [[concepts/red-flags-contents-handling]] for a list of warning signs.

### Benefits of a Robust Pack‑Out Process
- Reduces risk of denied claim items.
- Provides clear evidence for adjusters and insurers.
- Facilitates smoother reimbursement and faster claim resolution.
