# Emergency Preparedness

## Definition
Emergency preparedness is the systematic process of identifying, acquiring, and maintaining the resources and knowledge needed to sustain safety, health, and basic comfort during short‑term crises such as storms, power outages, or being confined to home for several days.

## Core Elements (derived from [[summaries/session-02_2025-05-15]])
- **Water:** Approximately 1 gal per person per day plus extra for pets.
- **Food:** Non‑perishable meals for three days and pet food.
- **Lighting:** Reliable flashlights or headlamps with fresh spare batteries.
- **Power:** Portable power banks capable of recharging phones at least once.
- **Medical & First‑Aid:** Basic kit plus a three‑day prescription reserve.
- **Hygiene:** Wipes, soap, trash bags, and toilet‑safe bags.
- **Tools:** Manual can opener and backup eyewear (glasses/contacts).
- **Information:** Battery‑powered or hand‑crank radio for alerts.
- **Pet Care:** Food, medication, and waste bags.

## Prioritization
1. **Sustenance:** Water → Food → Pet provisions.
2. **Safety & Health:** First‑aid kit → Prescription meds → Hygiene supplies.
3. **Communication & Awareness:** Radio → Spare batteries.
4. **Comfort & Convenience:** Lighting, power bank, manual can opener, backup eyewear.

## Commonly Forgotten Items
- Fresh spare batteries (not half‑dead).
- Manual can opener.
- Radio for information when the internet fails.
- Pet medication and waste bags.
- Personal comfort items such as glasses or contacts.

## Rotation & Replacement
Regularly review and replace items to ensure readiness. See [[concepts/rotation-schedule]] for a detailed schedule, which typically includes:
- Checking shelf‑stable food twice a year.
- Testing and replacing batteries annually.
- Rotating prescription medications with each refill.
- Updating pet food supplies every 2–3 months when opening new batches.

## Budget Considerations
A basic 72‑hour kit can be assembled for **$50–$100**, while a more comprehensive version with higher‑quality gear may cost **$150–$300**. For a deeper breakdown of budgeting strategies, refer to [[concepts/budgeting]].
