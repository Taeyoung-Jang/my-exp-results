# Summary of Session 02 (2025-05-15)

## Overview
Provides a practical, prioritized 72-hour emergency preparedness plan for a two-person household with a pet, covering essential supplies, quantities, rotation schedules, and cost estimates. [[concepts/emergency-preparedness]]

## Core Checklist
- **Water:** 6 gal (2 gal/person/day) + 1.5 gal for pet.  
- **Food:** 9 non-perishable meals for people + 3 days of pet food.  
- **Light:** 2 flashlights/headlamps + spare batteries.  
- **Power:** 2 medium-size power banks (≥10 000 mAh each).  
- **Medical:** Small first-aid kit + 3‑day prescription supply.  
- **Hygiene:** Wipes, soap, trash bags, toilet-safe bags.  
- **Pet care:** Food, meds, waste bags.  
- **Information:** Battery-powered or hand-crank radio.  
- **Tools:** Manual can opener, backup glasses/contact supplies.

## Commonly Forgotten Items
- Spare batteries (not half‑dead)  
- Manual can opener  
- Information source (radio)  
- Pet medication and waste bags  
- Comfort items (glasses, contacts)

## Rotation & Replacement Schedule
- **Shelf-stable food:** inspect twice yearly; replace after 6‑12 months.  
- **Batteries:** test/replace annually.  
- **Prescription meds:** rotate with each refill.  
- **Pet food:** rotate every 2‑3 months when opening new batches.

## Shopping List (Prioritized)
| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Flashlights/headlamps | 2 | Reliable LED, waterproof |
| Spare AA/AAA batteries | 4‑6 packs | Fresh batteries |
| Power banks | 2 (≈10 000 mAh) | Can charge phone 1‑2× |
| First-aid kit | 1 | Include bandages, antiseptic, OTC pain relievers |
| Prescription meds | 3‑day supply per person | Keep in airtight container |
| Manual can opener | 1 | Sturdy metal |
| Battery-powered/hand-crank radio | 1 | NOAA alerts |
| Hygiene wipes | 1‑2 packs | Unscented |
| Small soap bottle | 1 | Antibacterial |
| Heavy-duty trash bags | 1 roll | For waste |
| Toilet-safe bags | 1 pack | Optional comfort |
| Pet food (dry/kibble) | 3 days | Match regular brand |
| Pet meds | 3‑day supply | As needed |
| Pet waste bags | 1 pack (≈30) | For litter/clean‑up |

## Budget Estimates
- **Basic setup:** $50 – $100 (economy flashlights, small power bank, basic first-aid kit, simple radio).  
- **Enhanced setup:** $150 – $300 (high‑lumens flashlights, larger power banks, comprehensive first-aid kit, rugged radio, premium hygiene items).
