# Pack‑Out Service Provider

**Type:** Organization

**Related summary:** [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-15]]

## Overview
The Pack‑Out Service Provider is a vendor that handles the removal, inventory, labeling, storage, and transportation of household contents after a water‑damage incident. It works closely with homeowners, adjusters, and contractors to ensure that all items are documented and protected for insurance claim purposes.

## Key Functions
- **[[concepts/pack-out-process]]** – Executes the physical removal of items, using a systematic labeling system that ties each box to an inventory record.
- **[[concepts/inventory-documentation]]** – Produces a detailed, photo‑supported inventory list that includes item descriptions, condition notes, and box numbers.
- **[[concepts/chain-of-custody]]** – Maintains a log tracking who handled each item, when it was moved, and where it was stored, providing a transparent chain‑of‑custody record.
- **Storage Management** – Stores packed items in a secure unit, tracks storage duration, and coordinates billing of storage fees under the homeowner’s policy.

## Interaction with Other Parties
- **[[entities/adjuster]]** – The adjuster relies on the provider’s inventory and chain‑of‑custody records to assess claim value.
- **[[entities/contractor]]** – Coordinates repair timelines and ensures that contents are returned once restoration is complete.
- **[[entities/pack-out-company]]** – Often the same entity; listed separately in the wiki for reference.

## Red‑Flag Awareness
Homeowners should watch for common issues such as vague inventory entries, missing photos, generic box labels, undocumented disposals, or delays in providing records. These are highlighted in [[concepts/red-flags-contents-handling]].

## Documentation Checklist for Homeowners
When dealing with a Pack‑Out Service Provider, request the following:
1. Complete inventory list with photos.
2. Labeling/box list that matches the inventory.
3. Chain‑of‑custody log.
4. Copies of all invoices and storage fees.
5. Scope of work outlining their responsibilities.

By securing these documents, homeowners can protect their claim and avoid disputes over missing or mis‑documented items.
