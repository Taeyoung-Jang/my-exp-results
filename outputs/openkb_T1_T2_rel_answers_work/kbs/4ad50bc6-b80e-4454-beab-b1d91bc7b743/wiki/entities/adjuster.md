## Insurance Adjuster

**Role:** An insurance adjuster evaluates loss, coordinates with contractors and pack‑out vendors, and oversees the documentation needed for a claim.

**Interaction in *session‑35 (2025‑07‑15)*:**
- Served as the primary point of contact for the policyholder dealing with extensive water‑damage.
- Provided guidance on compiling a clear claim package, emphasizing the importance of [[concepts/inventory-documentation]], [[concepts/pack-out-process]], and a thorough [[concepts/chain-of-custody]] record.
- Addressed questions about storage fees, inventory lists, labeling systems, and the homeowner’s right to be present during contents valuation.
- Agreed to supply copies of the inventory, labeling scheme, chain‑of‑custody log, and any related invoices.

**Key Expectations for the Policyholder:**
1. Receive a detailed, photo‑supported inventory of all packed‑out items.
2. Obtain the labeling system and chain‑of‑custody documentation linking each box to its contents.
3. Get confirmation on who covers storage fees under the policy.
4. Be notified before any boxes are opened or any contents valuation is performed.
5. Ensure no items are declared non‑salvageable or disposed of without written consent.
