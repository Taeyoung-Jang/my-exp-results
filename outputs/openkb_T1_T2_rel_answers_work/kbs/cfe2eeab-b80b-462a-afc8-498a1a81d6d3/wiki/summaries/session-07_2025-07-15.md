# Summary of session-07_2025-07-15

**Main focus**: Practical, low‑impact ergonomic adjustments to protect hands and wrists during daily computer, cooking, and phone tasks, with a detailed emphasis on selecting and configuring a trackball for a healing ligament.

## Key Recommendations
- **Micro‑breaks**: Every 15‑20 minutes, pause to shake out hands, rotate wrists, stretch fingers, look away from the screen, or stand briefly. [[concepts/micro-breaks]]
- **Posture & Wrist Position**: Keep forearms level, elbows close to the body, and wrists floating in a neutral line. Use a soft palm rest only during breaks. [[concepts/wrist_neutrality]]
- **Keyboard choice**: Prioritize a split or low‑profile keyboard for the greatest overall benefit; it promotes straight wrist alignment. *(concept not linked)*
- **Mouse/Trackball selection**:
  - For a healing ligament, a **trackball** is gentler than a vertical mouse because the hand rests open and the fingers move the ball, minimizing grip force. [[concepts/trackball_ergonomics]]
  - Prefer **finger‑ball** designs (e.g., Kensington Orbit or Kensington Expert) over thumb‑ball models to avoid thumb tension. [[entities/kensington-orbit]] [[entities/kensington-expert]]
  - Look for a broad, supportive housing, smooth low‑resistance ball, and large easy‑click buttons.
- **Pointer speed settings**:
  - Windows: pointer speed ~5/11, disable pointer precision.
  - macOS: tracking speed 4–5.
  - Adjust speed down if overshooting, up if frequent recentering is needed. *(concept not linked)*
- **Troubleshooting trackball issues**:
  - **Overshoot**: lower speed before tweaking acceleration.
  - **Recenter**: raise speed slightly.
  - **Finger fatigue**: ensure ball glides smoothly (clean bearings) and increase speed modestly.

## Red‑Flag Symptoms
- Gradual forearm ache, stiffness, tingling, or grip fatigue after prolonged use, even if the setup feels “fine” initially. These indicate lingering stress and a need for further adjustment. *(concept not linked)*

## Actionable First Upgrade
- Start with a **trackball** (finger‑ball) given the ligament condition, then consider a split/low‑profile keyboard if further improvement is desired. *(concept not linked)*
