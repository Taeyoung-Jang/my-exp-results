## Micro‑breaks for hand‑wrist health

Micro‑breaks are brief, regular pauses (typically 15–20 minutes apart) designed to reduce cumulative stress on the hands, wrists, and forearms during activities such as typing, mouse/trackball use, cooking, and phone handling.  The practice is grounded in the principle that **even a thirty‑second rest or gentle stretch** can interrupt the buildup of tension and prevent the gradual onset of discomfort.

### Core Elements
- **Frequency**: Every 15‑20 minutes, stop the repetitive motion for a few seconds.
- **Duration**: 10‑30 seconds is sufficient; the goal is to move and relax, not to lose workflow continuity.
- **Actions**:
  - Lower hands to the lap and shake them out.
  - Rotate wrists clockwise and counter‑clockwise.
  - Spread fingers wide, then relax.
  - Look away from the screen, roll shoulders, or stand briefly.
- **Integration**: Tie the break to natural workflow cues (e.g., after completing a paragraph, a cooking step, or a phone call).

### Benefits
- Maintains **wrist_neutrality** by preventing prolonged static positions.
- Reduces risk of forearm aching, stiffness, tingling, and grip fatigue.
- Improves circulation and reduces muscle fatigue, supporting faster recovery for injuries such as ligament tears.

### Relation to Ergonomic Tools
Micro‑breaks complement ergonomic hardware choices. For example, when using a trackball (see [[concepts/trackball_ergonomics]]), the hand remains relatively stationary, but micro‑breaks still help avoid subtle tension buildup. Pairing a well‑configured device with regular pauses yields the greatest protective effect.

### Practical Tips from the Source Session
- **Set a timer** or use software reminders to prompt the break.
- **Combine actions**: while waiting for a page to load, perform the wrist rotation.
- **Combine with posture checks**: ensure elbows stay close to the body and forearms level during the break.
- **Document reference**: Detailed guidance appears in the session summary [[summaries/session-07_2025-07-15]].

### Quick Micro‑break Routine
1. Pause typing or mouse work.
2. Place hands on thighs, shake them out for 5 seconds.
3. Flex and extend each wrist 3 times.
4. Spread fingers wide, hold 2 seconds, then relax.
5. Look up, blink, and roll shoulders.

Repeating this cycle throughout the day helps keep hand‑wrist health stable without disrupting productivity.
