# Kensington Orbit Trackball

**Type:** Product

The **Kensington Orbit** is a dome‑style, finger‑ball trackball praised for its roomy, supportive shape that lets the hand rest open with minimal grip force. In the conversation *session‑07_2025‑07‑15* it was recommended as a gentle first upgrade for users recovering from wrist ligament injuries because the device reduces thumb and wrist tension.

## Key Features
- **Finger‑ball design**: Moves the cursor with the index or middle finger, keeping the wrist neutral and avoiding the clench often required by thumb‑ball models. [[concepts/trackball_ergonomics]]
- **Broad housing**: Allows the palm to settle comfortably without needing to grip tightly.
- **Smooth, low‑resistance ball**: Enables effortless finger movement, which helps prevent fatigue during long sessions.
- **Large, easy‑click buttons**: Reduce the need for forceful presses that could aggravate a healing ligament.

## Ergonomic Benefits
- Supports **wrist neutrality** by keeping the hand in an open, relaxed posture, complementing the micro‑break strategies discussed in the session. [[concepts/wrist_neutrality]]
- Works well with the recommended **micro‑break** routine (shake out hands, stretch fingers) to further lessen strain. [[concepts/micro-breaks]]

## Usage Tips from the Session
- Pair the Orbit with pointer speed settings of roughly **5/11 on Windows** or **tracking speed 4–5 on macOS** for smooth cursor movement without overshooting.
- If you experience overshoot, lower the speed slightly; if you need to “recenter” often, raise it a notch.
- Keep the ball and bearings clean to maintain the low‑effort glide and avoid finger fatigue.
