# Kensington Expert Trackball

**Type**: Product

**Description**: The Kensington Expert is a large, dome‑shaped trackball that provides a spacious, supportive surface for the hand, allowing the fingers to move the ball while the wrist stays relaxed.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-07_2025-07-15]]**:
- Recommended as a **finger‑ball** design for users with wrist or ligament injuries because it minimizes grip force. [[concepts/trackball_ergonomics]]
- Features a broad housing, smooth low‑resistance ball, and large easy‑click buttons, which help avoid thumb tension.
- Suggested settings: moderate pointer speed, no acceleration, to reduce overshoot and finger fatigue.
- Works well with micro‑break routines such as brief hand shakes and wrist rotations. [[concepts/micro-breaks]]

**Ergonomic relevance**:
- Supports **wrist neutrality** by allowing the hand to rest open, reducing the need for a tight grip. [[concepts/wrist_neutrality]]
