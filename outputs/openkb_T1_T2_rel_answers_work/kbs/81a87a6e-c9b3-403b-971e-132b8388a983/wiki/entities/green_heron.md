# Green Heron

**Type:** other (small heron)

**Habitat:** Common in wetlands, marshes, and reed beds where it hunts for fish and amphibians.

**Identification cues (from session‑15):**
- Compact, deliberate posture; often moves slowly with short bursts.
- Bill is short and dark, typically held low but can be pointed upward when freezing.
- Leg color often yellowish, helping separate it from similar‑looking bitterns which freeze with an upward‑pointed bill and have a more stretched posture.
- Behavior distinguishes it from shorebirds: vertical stance, slower stalking rather than rapid probing.
