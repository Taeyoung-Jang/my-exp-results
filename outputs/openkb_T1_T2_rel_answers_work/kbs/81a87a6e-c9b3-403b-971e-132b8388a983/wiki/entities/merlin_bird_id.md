## Merlin Bird ID

**Type:** product

**Overview**
Merlin Bird ID is a free, user‑friendly mobile application designed to assist birdwatchers in identifying birds based on location, habitat, and observable field marks. It guides users through a series of questions about size, bill shape, leg color, behavior, and other cues, then narrows the possibilities to a short list of likely species.

**Key Facts from Session 15 (2024‑05‑08)**
- Recommended by the assistant as an ideal starter tool for beginners in [[concepts/wetland_birdwatching]].
- Allows users to filter results by habitat, making it especially useful for marshes, reeds, and other wetland environments.
- Supports quick “in‑the‑reeds” sightings by letting the observer input a few rapid notes (size, bill shape, color flash, behavior) and receive probable matches.

**How it Supports Field Identification**
The app’s workflow aligns with the core ideas in [[concepts/bird_identification]] and the emphasis on [[concepts/field_marks_and_behavioral_cues]] such as:
- Size comparison
- Bill and leg details
- Distinct coloration or pattern
- Observed behavior (e.g., stalking, tail‑bob, probing)

**Connection to Other Entities**
While the session mentions several wetland birds, Merlin Bird ID can be used to differentiate among them, including species like the [[entities/bittern]], [[entities/green_heron]], other [[entities/heron]] types, and shorebirds such as the [[entities/sandpiper]].
