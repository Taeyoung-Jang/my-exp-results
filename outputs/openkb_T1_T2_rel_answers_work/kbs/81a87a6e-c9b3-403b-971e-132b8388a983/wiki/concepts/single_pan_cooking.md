# Single‑Pan Cooking

**Definition** – Single‑pan cooking is a food‑preparation approach that confines the entire meal to a single cooking vessel (e.g., a skillet, pan, or pot).  It reduces cleanup, shortens cooking time, and is ideal for low‑energy evenings.

---

## Why It Matters
- **Speed** – Most meals can be assembled and cooked in 10–15 minutes.
- **Simplicity** – Only one pot or pan to wash, which fits well with fatigue after a night shift.
- **Cost‑Effectiveness** – Uses pantry and freezer staples that are inexpensive and have long shelf‑life.
- **Portability** – Easy to scale down for one‑person portions, avoiding leftovers.

---

## Core Elements from [[summaries/session-34_2025-01-05]]
1. **Master Ingredient List** – A set of pantry staples (tortillas, instant noodles, canned beans, tuna, etc.) and freezer items (mixed veg, shrimp, hash‑brown potatoes) that work across many single‑pan dishes.
2. **Meal Rotation Planning** – A two‑week schedule that rotates dishes like stir‑fry noodles, chickpea curry, egg fried rice, pizza wrap, and shrimp‑and‑rice, limiting cooking to five nights per week and designating Saturday as a heat‑and‑eat day.  See [[concepts/meal_rotation_planning]] for the broader scheduling idea.
3. **Flavor Boosters** – Simple additions (lemon juice, salsa, soy sauce, dried herbs) that change the taste profile without adding extra cookware.  This aligns with the principle of low‑effort pantry staples highlighted in [[concepts/low_effort_pantry_staples]].
4. **Waste Reduction** – Using full‑can portions or mini‑cans to avoid half‑opened cans, and freezing leftovers in small zip‑bags, keeping the single‑pan workflow tidy.

---

## Typical Single‑Pan Meals (examples)
- **Stir‑Fry Noodles** – Instant noodles, frozen stir‑fry veg, soy sauce, sesame oil.
- **Bean & Cheese Quesadilla** – Tortilla, canned beans, shredded cheese, optional hot sauce.
- **Garlic Butter Shrimp & Rice** – Frozen shrimp, instant rice, butter, garlic powder, peas.
- **Chickpea Curry** – Canned chickpeas, jarred curry sauce, frozen spinach, microwave rice.
- **Egg Fried Rice** – Leftover rice, frozen mixed veg, eggs, soy sauce.
- **Pizza Wrap** – Tortilla, marinara, cheese, any leftover veggies or pepperoni.
- **Sausage & Potato Skillet** – Pre‑cooked chicken sausage, hash‑brown potatoes, seasoning.
- **Black Bean Taco Skillet** – Canned black beans, frozen corn, taco seasoning, cheese.
- **Tomato Basil Gnocchi** – Shelf‑stable gnocchi, canned tomatoes, garlic powder, dried basil.

---

## Practical Tips
| Tip | How it Supports Single‑Pan Cooking |
|-----|--------------------------------------|
| **Batch prep pantry staples** | Keep core items stocked so no extra trips are needed. |
| **Use frozen, pre‑portionable veg** | Measure directly into the pan; the rest stays frozen. |
| **Choose flexible proteins** | Tofu, canned tuna, shrimp, or pre‑cooked sausage all cook in the same vessel. |
| **Limit seasonings to liquids/powders** | No extra bowls; add directly to the pan. |
| **Designate a “heat‑and‑eat” Saturday** | Freeze a complete single‑serve meal that only needs microwaving. |
