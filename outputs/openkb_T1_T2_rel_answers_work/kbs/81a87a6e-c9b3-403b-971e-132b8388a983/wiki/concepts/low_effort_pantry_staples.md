# Low‑Effort Pantry Staples

**Definition**: A curated set of long‑lasting pantry ingredients that enable single‑pan, minimal‑prep meals with little cooking time and few dishes.

## Core Items (as listed in [[summaries/session-34_2025-01-05]])
- **Tortillas** – versatile base for wraps, quesadillas, and pizza‑style wraps.
- **Instant noodles** – fast‑cooking starch that can double as a pan‑sauce carrier.
- **Canned beans** (black beans, chickpeas) – protein‑rich and ready to use straight from the can.
- **Canned tomatoes** – a quick sauce foundation for pasta, gnocchi, and curry dishes.
- **Canned tuna** – shelf‑stable protein for pasta or wrap fillings.
- **Marinara sauce** – ready‑to‑heat tomato base for pizza wraps and gnocchi.
- **Microwave/instant rice** – pre‑cooked grains that heat in minutes.
- **Pasta (dry)** – quick‑boil staple for one‑pan meals.
- **Jarred curry sauce or coconut milk** – flavor‑dense sauce for chickpea curry.
- **Soy sauce, taco seasoning, garlic powder, dried basil, olive oil** – essential seasonings that add variety without extra dishes.

## How It Supports Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/meal_rotation_planning]]** – By keeping these staples on hand, weekly meal plans can be assembled quickly, reducing decision fatigue.
- **[[concepts/single_pan_cooking]]** – The items are chosen for compatibility with one‑pan recipes, minimizing cleanup.

## Usage Tips
1. **Batch‑check inventory** each week to ensure you have at least one package of each staple.
2. **Buy in bulk** for canned beans, tomatoes, and tuna to lower cost; store extra cans in a cool, dry place.
3. **Rotate flavors** using the same base items but swapping seasonings (e.g., soy vs. taco seasoning) to keep meals interesting.
4. **Portion control** – Use whole cans when a recipe calls for a full can to avoid half‑opened waste; any leftovers can be frozen in small bags for future single‑serves.
