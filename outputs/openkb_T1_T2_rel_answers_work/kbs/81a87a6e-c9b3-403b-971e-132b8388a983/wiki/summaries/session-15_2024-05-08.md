# Summary of session-15_2024-05-08

**Main focus** – Providing practical advice for birdwatching in wetlands, especially for quick observations, and delivering a reusable one‑page notebook template with a confidence rating and a simple weekly scoring system.

## Key Ideas
- **Identify birds by a handful of reliable cues**: overall size, bill shape, leg color, movement, and standout markings. These cues work well in marshy or watery habitats where birds are only seen briefly. See [[concepts/bird_identification]] for more.
- **Behavioral distinctions** are crucial for separating similar shorebirds and small herons. Small herons move vertically and deliberately, often freezing with an upward‑pointed bill (see [[entities/bittern]] and [[entities/green_heron]] for examples), while shorebirds stay horizontal, bob or probe rapidly, and show distinct leg‑to‑body ratios. This falls under [[concepts/field_marks_and_behavioral_cues]].
- **Prioritized field marks/behaviors** (top 3‑5):
  1. Bill shape and length (long/dagger vs. short/straight).
  2. Leg length relative to body size.
  3. Posture and movement style (vertical vs. horizontal, stalking vs. rapid probing).
  4. Distinct color flashes or patterns (e.g., yellow legs, chestnut neck).
  5. Specific behavior cues (freeze, tail‑bob, steady probing).

## Note‑Taking Template
A compact, repeatable structure for weekend wetland visits, including a dedicated **“Quick Glimpse”** section and a **confidence (1–5) rating**:

```
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
WEEKLY WETLAND BIRD JOURNAL

Date and Time:
Weather and Light:
Water Level / Conditions:
Location Notes:

Full Sighting Description:
- Overall size and shape:
- Bill and leg details:
- Standout colors or markings:
- Movement and behavior:
- Position in habitat (shore, reeds, open water, mudflat, perch):

Quick Glimpse Notes (for fast disappearances):
- Immediate impression of size:
- Bill shape or length noticed:
- Any color flash or pattern:
- Behavioral clue seen in the moment:

Species Considered:
Likely ID:
Confidence (1–5):

Weekly Count Notes (including uncertain IDs):
- Estimated number of confirmed individuals:
- Estimated number of possible individuals:
- Reason for uncertainty (distance, lighting, brief view, mixed flock, etc.):

Trend Notes:
- Changes from previous weeks:
- Any unusual behavior, timing, or species:

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
```

## Simple Scoring System
- **Confirmed individual** = 1 point
- **Possible individual** = 0.5 point
- **Very brief/uncertain sighting** = 0.25 point  

These points can be summed per species each week to generate a comparable trend line, even when some observations are low‑confidence.
