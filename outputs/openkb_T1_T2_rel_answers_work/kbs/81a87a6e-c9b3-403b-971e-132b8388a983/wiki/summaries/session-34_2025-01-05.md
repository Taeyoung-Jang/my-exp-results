# Summary of Session 34 (2025‑01‑05)

**Purpose** – Provide the user with a practical, low‑effort dinner system for a Saturday‑shift schedule, emphasizing single‑pan cooking, minimal dishes, cost‑effectiveness, and a brief after‑work routine.

## 1. Always‑keep Master List
- **Pantry staples**: tortillas, instant noodles, canned black beans, canned chickpeas, canned tomatoes, tuna, marinara sauce, microwave rice, pasta, jarred curry sauce or coconut milk, soy sauce, taco seasoning, garlic powder, dried basil, olive oil. [[concepts/low_effort_pantry_staples]]
- **Freezer staples**: mixed vegetables, stir‑fry vegetables, peas, spinach, cooked shrimp, hash‑brown potatoes, pre‑cooked chicken sausage.
- **Fridge basics**: shredded cheese, eggs, butter, lemon juice.

*(Relevant concept: [[concepts/single_pan_cooking]])*

## 2. 2‑Week Rotation (5 Dinners / Week + Heat‑and‑Eat Saturday)

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| **1** | Stir‑fry noodles | Chickpea curry + microwave rice | Egg fried rice | Pizza wrap | Shrimp‑and‑rice | **Heat‑and‑eat** (freezer) | Open / leftovers |
| **2** | Tuna lemon pasta | Black bean taco skillet | Sausage & potatoes | Stir‑fry noodles (different veg) | Fried rice or curry | **Heat‑and‑eat** (freezer) | Open / leftovers |

*(Relevant concept: [[concepts/meal_rotation_planning]])*

## 3. Super‑short Flavor Boosters (no extra dishes)
- Squeeze of lemon  
- Spoon of salsa  
- Splash of soy or teriyaki sauce  
- Sprinkle of dried herbs or chili crisp  

## 4. Tips for Single‑Portion Cooking & Waste Reduction
- Use full‑can recipes (e.g., a whole can of beans as the main protein) to avoid half‑opened cans.  
- Purchase mini‑cans or pouches for beans/tuna when possible.  
- Freeze unused half‑cans in small zip‑bag portions for later single‑serve use.

## 5. Fridge‑Friendly Checklist (stick‑on format)

**Week 1 – Shopping**: tortillas, instant noodles, chickpeas, tomatoes, microwave rice, marinara, frozen mixed & stir‑fry veg, peas, shrimp, shredded cheese, eggs.  
**Week 1 – Cooking**: Mon‑Fri meals as listed; Sat – grab freezer item; Sun – optional leftovers.

**Week 2 – Shopping**: tuna, black beans, pasta, hash‑brown potatoes, chicken sausage, microwave rice, replenish any depleted items.  
**Week 2 – Cooking**: Mon‑Fri meals as listed; Sat – grab freezer item; Sun – optional leftovers.

## 6. Recommended Heat‑and‑Eat Freezer Items (filling & cheap)
1. Bean‑and‑cheese frozen burritos  
2. Microwave rice‑and‑vegetable bowls  
3. Single‑serve frozen gnocchi or ravioli trays  

## 7. After‑Work Saturday Routine (Step‑by‑Step)
1. Drop keys/coat, head straight to the microwave or stovetop.  
2. Heat the pre‑planned freezer meal (2‑5 min).  
3. While it warms, change into comfortable clothes and sip water.  
4. Eat, then spend 2 min rinsing the pan/plate and setting out tomorrow’s clothes.  
5. Done – relax.
