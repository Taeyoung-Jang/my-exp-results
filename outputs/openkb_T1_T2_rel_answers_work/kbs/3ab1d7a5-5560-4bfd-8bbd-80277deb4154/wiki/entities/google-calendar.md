# Google Calendar

Google Calendar is a web‑based calendar service provided by Google. It offers features such as automatic time‑zone conversion and a secondary time‑zone display, which are useful for [[concepts/time-zone-management]] and defining a [[concepts/safe-meeting-window]].

**Key points from [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]]**
- Recommended as a tool that “automatically shows the other person’s time zone” to reduce scheduling errors.
- Used alongside other scheduling tools like [[entities/calendly]].
- Helps users who operate in [[entities/pacific-time]] coordinate with clients in [[entities/new-york-city]] and other regions.
