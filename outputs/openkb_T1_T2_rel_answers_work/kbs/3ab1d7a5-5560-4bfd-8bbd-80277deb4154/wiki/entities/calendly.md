# Calendly (product)

Calendly is an online scheduling tool that streamlines the process of finding mutually convenient meeting times. In the context of [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]], Calendly is highlighted as a practical solution for **time zone management**, offering features such as automatic display of participants' time zones and easy generation of meeting proposals.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Time‑zone awareness:** Calendly can show the secondary time zone of invitees, helping users avoid booking meetings at undesirable hours.
- **Supports the "safe meeting window" approach:** By integrating with the user's defined availability (e.g., 8 am–6 pm Pacific Time), Calendly can restrict offered slots to those that fall within the [[concepts/safe-meeting-window]].
- **Template generation:** The tool can be used to generate consistent [[concepts/meeting-templates]] for proposing times, including example slots that respect both the user's and the client's time zones.
- **Complementary with other calendar platforms:** Calendly works alongside calendar services like [[entities/google-calendar]] to ensure all scheduled events are synchronized.

## Related Concepts and Entities
- **[[concepts/time-zone-management]]** – broader strategies for handling multi‑regional scheduling.
- **[[concepts/meeting-templates]]** – reusable phrasing for proposing meeting times, which can be exported from Calendly.
- **[[entities/google-calendar]]** – often used in tandem with Calendly for calendar integration.
- **[[entities/pacific-time]]** – the home base time zone for the user in the referenced session.

## Usage Example
A user can configure Calendly to only offer slots between 8 am and 6 pm PT, automatically converting these to the client’s time zone (e.g., New York City, which is three hours ahead). When proposing a meeting, Calendly can generate a message like:

> "I’m available between 8am–6pm Pacific Time (11am–9pm your time). For example, 9am PT / noon your time works if that’s good for you."

This aligns with the recommendations from [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]].
