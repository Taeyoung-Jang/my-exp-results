# Office Hours Communication

**Definition**: Office Hours Communication is a purposeful way of announcing regular, predictable windows for live calls and quick responses while emphasizing flexibility and client convenience.

---

## Core Elements

1. **Predictable Window**
   - Set a consistent daily block (e.g., 2 pm‑4 pm your time, which aligns with 9 am‑11 am the client’s time).
   - Communicate this as the *easiest* way for the client to reach you.

2. **Flexible Framing**
   - Phrase the window as a *convenience* rather than a restriction. Sample language (from the source):
     > "I keep a dedicated window each day for quick responses and live calls. For you, that’s typically around 9–11 am your time. I’m happy to find another slot if something urgent comes up."
   - Emphasize that exceptions are welcome, reinforcing a collaborative tone.

3. **Expectation Management**
   - Pair the office‑hours announcement with a brief response‑time promise (e.g., “I reply within this window”).
   - Align the window with your **[[concepts/time-zone-availability]]** to reduce cross‑time‑zone friction.

4. **Integration with Meeting Prep**
   - Use a short **[[concepts/meeting-preparation]]** block right before the office‑hours window to ensure you’re ready for the call.
   - Follow the meeting with a quick follow‑up block to capture actions while fresh.

---

## Benefits
- **Reduced Context Switching** – By clustering live interactions, deep‑work periods stay uninterrupted.
- **Clear Client Expectations** – Clients know exactly when they can expect a live response.
- **Time‑Zone Harmony** – Aligning windows with client working hours minimizes late‑night messages.
- **Scalable Communication** – The same phrasing can be reused across multiple clients and teams.

## Sample Email Templates (derived from the source)
1. *Flexible framing*: “To help keep our projects moving smoothly, I keep a dedicated window each day when I’m most available for live calls and quick responses. For you, that’s typically around 9–11 am your time. I’m happy to find another slot if something urgent comes up, but that window is usually the easiest way to reach me.”
2. *Convenience‑focused*: “I’ve started using regular ‘office hours’ to make scheduling simpler and ensure I’m fully focused on your work when we meet. My usual window overlaps with your morning, so feel free to message or book time then. If you ever need a different slot, just let me know and I’ll do my best to accommodate.”
3. *Light & predictive*: “For easier coordination, I generally reserve your 9 am hour for calls and quick communication. It keeps things predictable on both sides. That said, I’m flexible—so if you ever need a different time, just tell me and we’ll work it out.”
