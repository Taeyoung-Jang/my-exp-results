# Safe Meeting Window

A **Safe Meeting Window** is a self‑imposed schedule band that specifies the earliest and latest times you are willing to hold meetings, expressed in your primary time zone and automatically translated for participants in other zones. The concept helps avoid booking meetings at unreasonable hours for either party.

## Core Elements
- **Primary window** – In the example from [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]], the user sets a window of **8 am – 6 pm Pacific Time**.
- **Offset conversion** – The user’s clients are typically **3 hours ahead** (e.g., New York City). The window therefore maps to **11 am – 9 pm** for those clients.
- **Rule of thumb** – Any suggested slot outside the defined range is automatically declined.

## Practical Application
1. **Define your base zone** (e.g., [[entities/pacific-time]]) and the acceptable hour range.
2. **Calculate the corresponding range** for the most common partner zones (e.g., [[entities/new-york-city]] is +3 h).
3. **Incorporate the window into communication** using reusable [[concepts/meeting-templates]] that mention both zones and provide concrete example slots.

## Example Templates (derived from the source)
- General availability: "I’m available between 8am and 6pm Pacific Time, which is 11am to 9pm your time. For example, 9am PT (noon your time) …"
- Specific slots: "I can do 8:30am PT (11:30am your time), 11am PT (2pm your time), or 3pm PT (6pm your time)."

## Benefits
- Reduces cognitive load when juggling multiple time zones.
- Sets clear expectations for colleagues and clients.
- Prevents accidental scheduling at inconvenient hours.
