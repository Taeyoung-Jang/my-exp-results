### Key Concepts & Practices
- **Designated availability windows** – Pick recurring blocks for live meetings and quick responses; protect the rest of the day for deep work. [[concepts/time-zone-availability]]
- **Multi‑zone calendar view** – Show your primary zone plus client zones to see overlap instantly. (plain text description)
- **Pre‑meeting prep block** – 15‑20 min before the client call to review notes, gather files, and outline 2‑3 agenda points. [[concepts/meeting-preparation]]
- **Post‑meeting follow‑up block** – 10‑15 min right after the call to capture actions while fresh. (plain text description)
- **Office‑hours framing** – Phrase availability as a convenience, not a restriction, and allow exceptions. [[concepts/office-hours-communication]]
- **Scheduled async communication** – Send messages or emails to be delivered during the client’s working hours to avoid late‑night alerts. (plain text description)

### Sample Daily Schedule (centered on 2 pm meeting)
| Time (Your TZ) | Activity |
|----------------|----------|
| 08:00‑12:00 | Deep‑work block (core project tasks) |
| 12:00‑13:00 | Lunch – no screens |
| 13:30‑13:50 | Prep for 2 pm client call (review agenda, pull files) |
| 14:00‑15:00 | Client call (9 am client time) |
| 15:00‑15:15 | Immediate follow‑up notes & action items |
| 15:15‑17:30 | Light work: emails, admin, planning, async responses |

### Email Templates for Introducing “Office Hours”
1. **Flexible framing**  
   > "To help keep our projects moving smoothly, I keep a dedicated window each day when I’m most available for live calls and quick responses. For you, that’s typically around 9–11 am your time. I’m happy to find another slot if something urgent comes up, but that window is usually the easiest way to reach me."
2. **Convenience‑focused**  
   > "I’ve started using regular ‘office hours’ to make scheduling simpler and ensure I’m fully focused on your work when we meet. My usual window overlaps with your morning, so feel free to message or book time then. If you ever need a different slot, just let me know and I’ll do my best to accommodate."
3. **Light & predictive**  
   > "For easier coordination, I generally reserve your 9 am hour for calls and quick communication. It keeps things predictable on both sides. That said, I’m flexible—so if you ever need a different time, just tell me and we’ll work it out."

### Actionable Takeaways
- Set your calendar to show both your primary zone and the client’s zone.  
- Block a consistent 2 pm‑3 pm slot (client’s 9 am) for live interactions.  
- Use a short prep block (13:30‑13:50) and follow‑up block (15:00‑15:15) to minimize context‑switching.  
- Communicate the window using one of the provided templates, emphasizing flexibility.  

These practices help reduce “meeting‑in‑the‑middle‑of‑the‑day” whiplash and keep daily rhythm predictable for both you and your clients.
