# Summary of session-15_2024-06-15

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/time-zone-management]]** – Strategies to avoid scheduling errors across global locations.
- **Home base time zone** – Choose a reference zone (e.g., your own) and convert all times to it first.
- **Calendar secondary time zone** – Use calendar features (e.g., [[entities/google-calendar]], [[entities/calendly]]) that display a secondary zone automatically.
- **[[concepts/safe-meeting-window]]** – A personal rule defining allowable hours (8 am‑6 pm PT) and its conversion for clients (+3 h).
- **[[concepts/meeting-templates]]** – Pre‑written phrasing for proposing times, with concrete slot examples.
- **Email one-liner** – Ultra‑short line suitable for email signatures.
- **Casual text message** – Even more informal version for SMS.

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Adopt a home‑base zone** to reduce mental juggling of multiple clocks.
2. **Enable secondary time zone display** in your calendar to see client times at a glance.
3. **Define a safe meeting window**: 8 am–6 pm Pacific Time (PT), which translates to 11 am–9 pm for clients three hours ahead (e.g., [[entities/new-york-city]]).
4. **Create template messages** that mention both zones and provide 2‑3 example slots staying within the window.
5. **Provide concise alternatives**:
   - Email line: “I’m free between 8am–6pm PT (11am–9pm your time); for example, 9am PT / noon your time works if that’s good for you.”
   - Text line: “I’m good anytime 8–6 PT—want to do like 9 PT (noon for you)?”

## Sample Templates
### General Availability Template
> “I’m available between 8am and 6pm [[entities/pacific-time]], which is 11am to 9pm your time. For example, I could meet at 9am PT (noon your time), 1pm PT (4pm your time), or 4:30pm PT (7:30pm your time). Let me know if one of those works, or if there’s another time in that window you prefer.”

### Specific Slot Template
> “I can do something in the morning or mid‑afternoon [[entities/pacific-time]]. For instance, 8:30am PT (11:30am your time), 11am PT (2pm your time), or 3pm PT (6pm your time). Happy to adjust within my 8am–6pm PT window if another slot is better for you.”

## Practical Tips
- Add frequent client time zones to your phone’s world‑clock app.
- Set a calendar rule that flags meetings outside the safe window.
- Keep the one‑liner and text versions handy for quick copy‑paste.
