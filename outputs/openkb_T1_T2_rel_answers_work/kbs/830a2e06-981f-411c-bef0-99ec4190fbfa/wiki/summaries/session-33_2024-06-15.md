## Summary of Session 33 (2024‑06‑15)

This conversation explores how living at moderate‑high elevation (≈5,000–7,000 ft) subtly reshapes everyday household tasks, especially **cooking**, **baking**, and **bean preparation**. The assistant provides practical, low‑effort adjustments and rule‑of‑thumbs that let a newcomer adapt without over‑hauling recipes.

### Key Points
- **Altitude effects** – Lower air pressure lowers water’s boiling point, speeds leavening, and dries the air. Dry air mainly influences ingredient moisture and storage, while low pressure drives changes in cooking times and rise behavior. See [[concepts/altitude-effects-on-cooking]].
- **Cooking adjustments** –
  - Extend simmer/boil times for rice, pasta, and beans because water is cooler (~95 °C at 5,000 ft).  
  - Use a [[entities/pressure-cooker]] if possible; otherwise rely on longer cooking and full‑pot lids.
- **Baking tweaks** –
  - **Cookies**: Slightly reduce sugar, add a tablespoon of extra flour, and raise oven temperature 10–15 °F. Cool dough before baking to control spread. See [[entities/chocolate-chip-cookies]] and [[concepts/high-elevation-baking-adjustments]].
  - **Sandwich loaf**: Shorten proofing intervals rather than changing ingredient ratios first; trim yeast only if dough over‑proofs. See [[entities/sandwich-loaf]].
- **Rule‑of‑thumbs for 5,000‑7,000 ft** –
  1. Give boiling foods extra time (≈10‑20 % longer).
  2. Monitor bread rise; stop earlier to avoid over‑proofing.
  3. For cookies, tweak flour/sugar & oven heat before rewriting the full recipe.
- **Bean preparation without a pressure cooker** –
  - Overnight soak in cold water, then simmer gently with the lid fully on (crack only to control foam). See [[concepts/bean-preparation-at-elevation]].
  - Typical cooking times after soak: 1¼–1½ h for [[entities/black-beans]], ~2 h for [[entities/chickpeas]].
  - Add a pinch of [[entities/baking-soda]] only for very old or tough beans; otherwise omit.

### Practical Checklist
1. **Boiling**: Add ~10 % extra time for rice/pasta; test doneness.
2. **Bread**: Watch rise; reduce proof time by ~20‑30 %.
3. **Cookies**: +1 Tbsp flour, –1 Tbsp sugar, +10 °F oven.
4. **Beans**: Overnight soak → full‑pot simmer → check texture; add tiny baking‑soda if skins stay leathery.
