# Baking Soda (product)

Baking soda, chemically sodium bicarbonate, is a white powder commonly used as a leavening agent in baked goods and as a cleaning aid.  In the context of high‑elevation cooking (see [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]]), it is mentioned as an optional additive for softened dried beans when the skins remain tough despite extended soaking and simmering.

- **Typical uses**: leavening cookies, cakes, and breads; neutralising acidity; cleaning surfaces.
- **Altitude‑specific tip**: add only a pinch of baking soda to beans at 5,000–7,000 ft if they stay leathery after a long soak and gentle simmer; otherwise omit to preserve flavor and texture.
- **Related concepts**: [[concepts/altitude-effects-on-cooking]], [[concepts/bean-preparation-at-elevation]], [[concepts/high-elevation-baking-adjustments]].
