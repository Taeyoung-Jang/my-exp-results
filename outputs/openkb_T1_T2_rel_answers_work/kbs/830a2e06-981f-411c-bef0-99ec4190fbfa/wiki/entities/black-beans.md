# Black Beans

Black beans are a dried **product** (legume) popular in many cuisines.  At moderate‑high elevations (≈5,000–7,000 ft) they require special cooking considerations because the lower boiling temperature slows softening.

## Key facts from session‑33 (2024‑06‑15)
- **Cooking time:** After an overnight soak, black beans typically need **1 ¼–1 ½ hours** of gentle simmer.
- **Method:** Start in **cold water**, soak overnight, drain, then simmer with the **lid fully on** (crack only to control foam).  A pressure cooker would shorten this dramatically, but it was not available in the discussion.
- **Baking soda:** Use **only a tiny pinch** if beans remain leathery after extended simmering; otherwise omit.
- **Altitude impact:** Lower boiling point (≈95 °C) means longer cook times compared to sea level.
