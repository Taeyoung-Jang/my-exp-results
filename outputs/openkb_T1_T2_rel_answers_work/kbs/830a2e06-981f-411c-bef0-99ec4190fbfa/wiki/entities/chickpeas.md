# Chickpeas (product)

**Description:** Dried chickpeas are a staple legume valued for their protein content and versatility in dishes such as hummus, soups, and stews.

## Key Facts from Session 33 (2024‑06‑15)
- **Altitude cooking:** At elevations of 5,000–7,000 ft, chickpeas require longer simmer times than sea‑level cooking because water boils at a lower temperature.  After an overnight soak, expect a cooking time of roughly **two hours** or a bit more.
- **Cooking method:** Use a gentle, steady simmer with the lid **fully on** (crack briefly only to control foam).  Start with cool water for the soak, then discard and cook in fresh water.
- **Baking soda:** Add a **pinch of baking soda** only if the beans remain leathery after extended cooking; otherwise it is unnecessary.
- **Comparison:** Black beans need a shorter cook (1¼–1½ h) while chickpeas are the slower‑cooking legume at this elevation.
