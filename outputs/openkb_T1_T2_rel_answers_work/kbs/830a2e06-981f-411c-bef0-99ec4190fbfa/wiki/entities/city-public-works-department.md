# City Public Works Department

The **City Public Works Department** is the municipal agency responsible for planning, constructing, and maintaining a city’s storm‑water systems, drainage networks, pump stations, and levees. It coordinates flood‑risk projects, publishes infrastructure reports, and works with other city bodies to ensure resilient water management.

## Key Roles Highlighted in Session 05 (2022‑06‑15)
- Publishes **capital‑improvement plans** and **storm‑water master plans** that outline upgrades to pumps, levees, and drainage grading. [[entities/capital-improvement-plan]] [[entities/stormwater-master-plan]]
- Provides public dashboards and status updates on pump‑station conditions and flood‑mitigation projects, enabling residents to assess **flood‑preparedness‑indicators**. [[concepts/flood-preparedness-indicators]]
- Coordinates with the **City Council** on budgeting and policy decisions for flood resilience. [[entities/city-council]]
- Works with federal agencies such as **FEMA** and the **U.S. Army Corps of Engineers** for compliance and funding. [[entities/fema]] [[entities/us-army-corps-of-engineers]]
- Issues information relevant to **below‑sea‑level flood management** strategies, especially in low‑lying neighborhoods. [[concepts/below-sea-level-flood-management]]

## Checklist Contributions
The department is a primary source for the **pump‑levy‑maintenance‑checklist** referenced in the conversation, providing:
- Inspection schedules for pump stations and levees.
- Documentation of recent upgrades and funded projects.
