## U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE)

The **U.S. Army Corps of Engineers** (USACE) is a federal agency under the Department of Defense that designs, builds, and maintains civil engineering infrastructure, including flood control projects such as levees, dams, and pump stations. 

### Role in Flood Management
- USACE often conducts **levee inspections** and publishes **inspection summaries** that local municipalities rely on for maintaining flood protection systems. 
- The agency collaborates with city agencies like the **[[entities/city-public-works-department]]** and **[[entities/city-council]]** to develop and fund flood mitigation plans, including **[[entities/stormwater-master-plan]]** and **[[entities/capital-improvement-plan]]**.

### Relevance to Session 05 (2022‑06‑15)
- The conversation mentions that **state or federal agencies occasionally post levee inspection summaries**, with USACE being a primary source for such documents. 
- Understanding USACE’s involvement helps residents evaluate the reliability of local **pump and levee infrastructure** and locate authoritative inspection reports.
