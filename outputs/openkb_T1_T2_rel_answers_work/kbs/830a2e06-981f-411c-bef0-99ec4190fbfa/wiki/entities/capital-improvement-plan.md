# Capital Improvement Plan

**Type:** Work (city planning document)

**Description:** A formal plan that lists planned infrastructure projects, funding sources, and timelines for public works improvements.

**Key Facts from [[summaries/session-05_2022-06-15]]**:
- Used as a primary source for evaluating flood‑risk preparedness in neighborhoods.
- Lists upgrades to pump stations, levee reinforcement, and drainage improvements, which are central to the [[concepts/flood-preparedness-indicators]] discussed.
- Provides a schedule and budget that help differentiate between proactive upgrades and reactive emergency repairs (see [[concepts/pump-levy-maintenance-checklist]]).
- When combined with the [[entities/city-public-works-department]] and [[entities/city-council]] documents, it shows the governance and funding process for flood mitigation projects.
- Often referenced alongside the [[entities/stormwater-master-plan]] and FEMA flood‑zone information to ensure compliance with regional flood standards.

**Usage**:
- Residents can request the latest CIP to verify that flood‑mitigation projects are funded and scheduled.
- Planners use the CIP to align pump and levee maintenance with long‑term climate resilience goals.
