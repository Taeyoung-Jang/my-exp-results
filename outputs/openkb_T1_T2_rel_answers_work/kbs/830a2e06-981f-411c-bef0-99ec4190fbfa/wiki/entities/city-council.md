# City Council

The **City Council** is the primary legislative body of a municipality, responsible for setting policies, approving budgets, and overseeing public works and infrastructure projects.

## Role in Flood Management
- Approves and funds the **[[entities/capital-improvement-plan]]**, which outlines investments in drainage, pumps, and levees.
- Reviews and adopts the **[[entities/stormwater-master-plan]]**, guiding long‑term flood‑risk mitigation.
- Oversees the **[[entities/city-public-works-department]]**, which implements and maintains flood‑control infrastructure.
- Coordinates with state and federal agencies such as **[[entities/fema]]** and the **[[entities/us-army-corps-of-engineers]]** for funding and compliance.

## Key References from [[summaries/session-05_2022-06-15]]
- The session highlights the importance of the council’s involvement in publishing up‑to‑date **capital‑improvement plans** and ensuring funded, proactive upgrades rather than reactive fixes.
- Council meetings and minutes are cited as sources for tracking progress on pump station upgrades and levee reinforcement projects.

## Related Concepts
- **[[concepts/flood-preparedness-indicators]]** – The council’s policies influence the presence of grading, storm‑drain maintenance, and green infrastructure.
- **[[concepts/pump-levy-maintenance-checklist]]** – Council decisions determine the adoption of maintenance schedules and inspection requirements.
- **[[concepts/below-sea-level-flood-management]]** – In low‑lying cities, council actions are critical for sustained pump operation and levee funding.

## Interaction with Other Entities
- Works closely with the **[[entities/city-public-works-department]]** to execute approved projects.
- Engages with **[[entities/fema]]** and **[[entities/fema-flood-zone-maps]]** for risk assessments and grant eligibility.
- Aligns local strategies with guidance from the **[[entities/us-army-corps-of-engineers]]** on levee safety standards.
