# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies are a widely enjoyed **product** consisting of a soft‑to‑chewy dough studded with chocolate chips.  They are a staple of home baking and often serve as a reference point for altitude‑related recipe adjustments.

## High‑Elevation Adjustments
- **Reduce sugar** by about one tablespoon to prevent excess spread.
- **Add a small amount of flour** (≈1 Tbsp) to give the dough more structure.
- **Increase oven temperature** by 10–15 °F so the cookie sets before it spreads too much.
- **Cool the dough** before baking to help it hold its shape.

These tweaks are recommended for elevations around **5,000–7,000 ft** and are discussed in [[summaries/session-33_2024-06-15]].  They relate to broader guidance in [[concepts/high-elevation-baking-adjustments]] and the general impact of altitude on cooking found in [[concepts/altitude-effects-on-cooking]].

## Related Baking Items
- **Baking soda** can be used in cookie recipes for leavening; see [[entities/baking-soda]].
- The **sandwich loaf** is another baked good that requires different altitude adjustments (shorter proof times) – see [[entities/sandwich-loaf]].

## Practical Tips from the Session
1. Start with ingredient tweaks (sugar, flour) before changing oven temperature.
2. Monitor cookie spread; if they still spread too much, increase the temperature a bit more.
3. For consistent results, keep the dough chilled for at least 30 minutes before baking.
