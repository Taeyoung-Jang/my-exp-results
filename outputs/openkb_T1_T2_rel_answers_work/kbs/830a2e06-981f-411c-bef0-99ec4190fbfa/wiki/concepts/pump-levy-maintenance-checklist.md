# Pump and Levee Maintenance Checklist

**Scope**: This concept synthesizes the practical checklist from [[summaries/session-05_2022-06-15]] for assessing the reliability and long‑term planning of pump stations and levees, especially in flood‑prone or below‑sea‑level neighborhoods.

---

## 1. Core Documents & Dashboards
| What to look for | Typical source |
|------------------|----------------|
| **Capital‑Improvement Plan** – funded projects, timelines | [[entities/capital-improvement-plan]] (often posted by [[entities/city-public-works-department]]) |
| **Annual Public‑Works / Storm‑Water Report** | [[entities/city-public-works-department]] |
| **Storm‑Water Master Plan** | [[entities/stormwater-master-plan]] |
| **Pump‑Station Status Dashboard** | City GIS or infrastructure portal |
| **Levee Inspection Summaries** (state/federal) | [[entities/fema]] or [[entities/us-army-corps-of-engineers]] |
| **FEMA Flood Zone Maps** | [[entities/fema-flood-zone-maps]] |
| **Resilience or Climate Adaptation Study** | Often attached to [[entities/city-council]] meeting minutes |

---

## 2. Key Questions for City Staff
1. **When was the last major overhaul** of each pump station?
2. **How often are pumps and backup generators tested**?
3. **What is the scheduled replacement cycle** for each levee segment?
4. **Are there funded upgrades** for the next 5‑10 years, or are repairs only reactive?
5. **Has the city conducted recent resilience studies** that address increasing storm intensity?
6. **How did the system perform in the latest heavy‑rain events** (drainage speed, failures, resident reports)?

---

## 3. Red‑Flag Indicators
- Out‑of‑date or missing condition reports.
- Predominance of emergency repairs with **no funded multi‑year plan**.
- Lack of documented inspection or testing schedule.
- Prolonged standing water after recent storms.
- Silence on climate‑driven intensity projections.

---

## 4. Green‑Flag Indicators
- Up‑to‑date public dashboards and condition summaries.
- Clear, funded capital‑improvement projects for pumps & levees.
- Regular inspection logs and test results publicly available.
- Documented resilience or adaptation studies.
- Evidence of proactive budgeting and long‑term replacement cycles.

---

## 5. Special Considerations for Below‑Sea‑Level Areas
Refer to [[concepts/below-sea-level-flood-management]] for broader context. In such locales, prioritize:
- **Continuous pump operation** and **redundant backup power**.
- **Levee reinforcement** and any sea‑wall/barrier plans.
- Condition reports **within the past 1‑2 years**.
- A **long‑term modernization roadmap** rather than ad‑hoc fixes.

---

## 6. Relationship to Flood‑Preparedness Indicators
This checklist supports the broader set of [[concepts/flood-preparedness-indicators]] by providing a tangible, document‑driven method to verify that critical water‑management infrastructure is robust, maintained, and future‑proofed.
