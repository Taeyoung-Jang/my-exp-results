# Night‑Before Checklist

**Definition**: A minimal, 2–3‑step evening routine that prepares essential items and checks readiness for the next day, helping families reduce morning chaos without major reorganization.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-35_2024-06-15]])
1. **Designated departure spot** – All items that need to leave the house (bags, paperwork, special gear) are placed in a single, consistent location.
2. **Paired shoes** – Shoes intended for the next morning are kept together at that spot.
3. **Quick visual scan** – Children (and adults) perform a brief “is my stuff here?” glance before bedtime.

## Night‑Before Checklist Questions
- **Are the bags packed?**
- **Are the shoes where they should be?**
- **Is anything special needed for tomorrow?**

These three prompts can be spoken aloud as a family ritual, requiring only a few seconds even on tired evenings.

## Why It Works
- **Reduces decision fatigue**: By answering the same three questions each night, families avoid last‑minute scrambling.
- **Creates a single point of focus**: The designated spot centralizes all outbound items, eliminating scattered searches.
- **Links to the exit routine**: A smoother “out‑the‑door” process (see [[concepts/out_the_door_system]]) ripples upstream, calming the entire morning.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Part of the broader **morning optimization** workflow that starts with [[concepts/morning_timing]] and uses an [[concepts/observation_phase]] to identify bottlenecks.
- Functions as a low‑effort habit complementing the **out‑the‑door system**, which is the highest‑impact change for chaotic mornings.
- Can be incorporated into a larger **family ritual** framework (see [[concepts/family-rituals]]) for consistency across evenings.

## Implementation Tips
- Choose a visible, low‑traffic area near the front door for the departure spot.
- Keep a small container or tray for paired shoes to make the “paired” step tangible.
- Make the three questions a verbal cue, possibly set to a familiar song or rhyme to reinforce habit formation.
