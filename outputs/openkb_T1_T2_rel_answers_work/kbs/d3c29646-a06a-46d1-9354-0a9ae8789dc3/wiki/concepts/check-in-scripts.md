## Check‑In Scripts

**Check‑In Scripts** are concise, mood‑adapted question sets used during a **weekly reset evening** to foster quick, meaningful family conversation. They are designed to be low‑effort, fitting into a 10‑minute window, and can be swapped depending on the family's energy level.

### Purpose
- Provide structure without feeling like a formal meeting.
- Encourage every member to share a brief personal update.
- Adapt to differing moods: upbeat vs. tired/cranky.
- Support the broader goal of maintaining strong **family‑rituals**.

### Core Scripts
| Mood | Prompt Sequence |
|------|-----------------|
| **Good‑mood** | 1. *“Share one thing that made you smile this week.”* 2. *“What are you looking forward to in the next few days?”* 3. *“Anything you need help with before the week starts again?”* |
| **Cranky‑mood** | 1. *“Share one thing that felt hard this week – just a sentence.”* 2. *“What’s one tiny thing that would make the coming week easier for you?”* 3. *“Is there anything you want the rest of us to know before we call it a night?”* |

### How to Use
1. Choose the script that matches the family's current atmosphere.
2. Gather in a comfortable spot (living room, kitchen table) with a light snack or beverage.
3. Allocate roughly 2‑3 minutes per prompt, keeping the overall check‑in under ten minutes.
4. Allow the younger child to leave for bedtime after the first prompt if needed; the remaining family can finish the final prompt or transition to a brief, calm task (e.g., prepping backpacks).

### Benefits
- **Consistency:** Repeating the same prompts each week builds familiarity.
- **Flexibility:** Scripts can be shortened or expanded based on time constraints.
- **Emotional Awareness:** Separate scripts acknowledge both positive and challenging experiences, promoting empathy.
