# Crunchy Moments

**Definition**: Brief, high‑stress points in a morning routine where activities collide, causing delays or tension.

## Origin
The term appears in [[summaries/session-35_2024-06-15]] where families are advised to note “crunchy moments” during a short observation phase.

## Why they matter
- Highlight bottlenecks such as bathroom traffic, simultaneous shoe‑finding, or competing parental attention.
- Provide concrete data for later redesign without overwhelming the household.

## How to capture them
During a 3–5 day [[concepts/observation_phase]] log, record:
- Approximate time
- What happened
- Who was involved

Example from the source:
> “7:25 — Baby needed feeding right as the older two needed help finding shoes. Everyone stacked up in the hallway.”

## Using crunchy moments
1. Identify recurring patterns across days.
2. Prioritize the highest‑impact bottleneck (often the “out‑the‑door” step).
3. Design a tiny habit or layout tweak to resolve it, e.g., a night‑before checklist [[concepts/night_before_checklist]] or a dedicated exit spot [[concepts/out_the_door_system]].
