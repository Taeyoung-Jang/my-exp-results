# Weekly Reset Evening

**Definition**: A short, regular family gathering (typically about 10‑15 minutes) designed to create a moment of connection, reflection, and gentle transition into the weekend.

**Core purpose**
- Reinforce family bonds despite busy schedules.
- Provide a predictable touchpoint for each member to share and be heard.
- Keep the ritual low‑effort so it remains sustainable.

**Typical structure** (as described in [[summaries/session-03_2022-06-15]]):
1. **Set a consistent evening** – choose a night when most members are home.
2. **Create ambiance** – a shared snack, soft music, or a candle to signal a different mood.
3. **10‑minute check‑in** – use one of two simple scripts (see [[concepts/check-in-scripts]]):
   - *Good‑mood script*: share a smile, upcoming excitement, any help needed.
   - *Cranky‑mood script*: note a difficulty, suggest one tiny improvement, any final note.
4. **Brief shared task** (optional) – tidy a common area or prep backpacks for the next day.
5. **Flexible exit** – younger children can leave for bedtime while others finish a calm activity.

**Adaptability**
- The ritual works for families of four or more and can be trimmed if a child needs to leave early.
- The focus stays on a **cozy, non‑obligatory moment**, avoiding the feeling of a meeting or homework.

**Relation to broader ideas**
- Part of a suite of low‑effort [[concepts/family-rituals]] that sustain household cohesion.
- The check‑in scripts are a concrete example of [[concepts/check-in-scripts]] applied in a family context.

**Benefits observed**
- Increases feeling of togetherness.
- Provides a predictable space for emotional check‑ins.
- Reduces friction around bedtime routines by offering a shared wind‑down.

> “The key is choosing something easy enough that no one feels pressured, but regular enough that it becomes a familiar touchpoint everyone can count on.” – from the source conversation.
