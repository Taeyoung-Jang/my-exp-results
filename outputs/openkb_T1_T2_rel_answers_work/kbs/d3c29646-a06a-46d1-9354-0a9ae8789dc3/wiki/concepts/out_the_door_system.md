## Out‑the‑Door System

**Definition**: A small set of habits and a night‑before checklist that ensure everyone leaves the house smoothly, reducing upstream morning stress.

### Core Principles
1. **Centralized Drop‑Off Spot** – All items that need to leave (bags, coats, shoes) are placed in one designated location the night before.
2. **Paired Shoes** – Shoes stay together in that spot to avoid searching for mismatched pairs.
3. **Quick Pre‑Sleep Scan** – Each child (and adult) does a brief “is my stuff here?” glance before bed.

### Night‑Before Checklist (2–3 Questions)
- *Are the bags packed?*
- *Are the shoes where they should be?*
- *Is anything special needed for tomorrow?*

These three prompts can be spoken aloud while winding down, requiring minimal energy.

### Why It Works
- **Fixed Deadline**: The moment of leaving the house has a non‑negotiable time; smoothing this step prevents cascading delays.
- **Cognitive Off‑Loading**: By handling logistics the night before, the morning brain focuses on routines instead of last‑minute searches.
- **Low Friction**: No major rearrangement of rooms; only a tiny habit change that fits into existing family flow.
