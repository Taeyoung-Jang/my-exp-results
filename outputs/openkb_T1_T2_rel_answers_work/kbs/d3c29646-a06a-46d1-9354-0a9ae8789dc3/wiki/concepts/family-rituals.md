# Family Rituals

Family rituals are recurring, low‑effort activities that help households maintain a sense of connection and shared identity despite busy schedules. They are intentionally simple, time‑bounded, and flexible enough to accommodate different ages, bedtime routines, and fluctuating energy levels.

## Core Elements (from [[summaries/session-03_2022-06-15]])
- **Brief duration** – typically 10‑15 minutes, so the ritual feels like a pause rather than a task.
- **Shared anchor** – a snack, a candle, or soft music that signals the start of the ritual.
- **Check‑in conversation** – a short set of prompts that can be adapted for good‑mood or tired evenings. See [[concepts/check-in-scripts]] for example scripts.
- **Optional micro‑task** – a quick, playful household chore (e.g., tidying the living room) that reinforces teamwork.

## Example: Weekly Reset Evening
A common family ritual is the [[concepts/weekly-reset-evening]], a weekly “reset” where the family gathers for a brief check‑in and a light shared activity. The ritual can be split into:
1. **Opening (2‑3 min)** – gather, share a snack, set a calming ambience.
2. **Check‑in (5‑7 min)** – use one of the scripts from [[concepts/check-in-scripts]].
3. **Wrap‑up (2‑3 min)** – the younger child may head to bed while the rest finish a quick prep task.

## Benefits
- Strengthens emotional bonds and communication.
- Provides predictable structure that children can rely on.
- Reduces the feeling of “busy‑ness” by carving out intentional downtime.
- Encourages cooperative habits through the micro‑task.

## Design Guidelines
1. **Keep it optional** – no one should feel forced; the ritual should feel inviting.
2. **Adapt to schedules** – choose a time when most members are home, and allow early exit for bedtime.
3. **Make it enjoyable** – incorporate sensory cues (snack, music) to differentiate the ritual from daily routines.
4. **Iterate** – periodically adjust prompts or activities based on family feedback.

These principles, illustrated in the conversation of [[summaries/session-03_2022-06-15]], can be applied to any household seeking simple, sustainable ways to stay connected.
