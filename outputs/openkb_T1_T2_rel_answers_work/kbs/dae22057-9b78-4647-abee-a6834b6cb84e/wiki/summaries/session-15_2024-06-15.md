## Summary of session-15_2024-06-15

**Main contribution** – Provides a concrete, low‑stress weekly routine that balances work, short strength sessions, gentle mobility work, optional walks, and full rest days, along with clear checklist formats, minimum‑effort options, and decision‑making guidance for days when you feel tight or exhausted.

### Key components
- **Weekly rhythm** – 2 strength days, 2 mobility days, 2 full rest days, 1 optional easy‑walk day, plus a flexible “wild‑card” slot. [[concepts/weekly-routine-balance]]
- **Daily checklist format** – Each day lists a *Full* version, a *Minimum* version (≈5 min or less), and a *Swap‑if‑tired* alternative.
- **Strength routines** – Two specific low‑intensity circuits (Routine A and Routine B) with exact sets, reps, and effort levels (≈3‑4/10) to avoid soreness. [[concepts/low-intensity-strength]]
- **Mobility flow** – A 10‑15 min sequence covering breath, neck/shoulder rolls, cat‑cow, hip circles, forward fold, chest opener, twists, and a final stretch. [[concepts/gentle-mobility-flow]]
- **Decision guide** – Quick self‑check to choose between Full, Minimum, or full Rest based on how tight or exhausted you feel.

### Example daily checklist
- **Monday – Strength (Routine A)** – Full 15‑20 min circuit, Minimum 5 min (2 moves), Swap to Tuesday mobility.
- **Tuesday – Mobility** – Full 10‑15 min flow, Minimum 2‑3 min neck/shoulder/hip, Swap to Rest.
- **Wednesday – Rest** – Full: no structured activity; Minimum: 1 min breathing.
- **Thursday – Strength (Routine B)** – Full 15‑20 min, Minimum 5 min, Swap to Friday mobility.
- **Friday – Mobility** – Full flow, Minimum short twists, Swap to Rest.
- **Saturday – Optional easy walk** – Full walk 10‑30 min, Minimum stretch, Swap to Rest.
- **Sunday – Rest** – Full day off, anchor day.
