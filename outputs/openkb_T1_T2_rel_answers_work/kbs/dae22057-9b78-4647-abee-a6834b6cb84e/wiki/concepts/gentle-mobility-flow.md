# Gentle Mobility Flow

**Definition** – A 10–15 minute gentle movement routine that focuses on breath, joint circles, and soft stretches to reduce tension, improve range of motion, and prepare the body for the day without inducing fatigue.

**Core Elements**
- **Breathing reset** – 1 minute of quiet, diaphragmatic breathing to center attention.
- **Neck & shoulder mobility** – Slow neck circles and shoulder rolls (30–45 seconds each) to release upper‑body tightness.
- **Spinal articulation** – Cat‑cow flow for 1–2 minutes, encouraging fluid vertebral movement.
- **Hip circles** – 1 minute of controlled hip rotations to open the pelvis.
- **Forward fold & rise** – Gentle forward fold and upright roll‑up for 1 minute, stretching hamstrings and lower back.
- **Chest opener** – Light doorway or wall stretch (30–45 seconds) to counteract desk‑related rounded shoulders.
- **Torso twists** – Soft seated or standing twists each side (30–45 seconds) for rotational mobility.
- **Finishing stretch** – Easy hip or hamstring hold to conclude the flow.

**Purpose & Benefits**
- Acts as a *reset* between work blocks, mitigating accumulated stress.
- Keeps joints lubricated and muscles supple, supporting the broader [[concepts/weekly-routine-balance]].
- Complements low‑stress strength sessions by maintaining mobility without creating additional fatigue, aligning with the principles of [[concepts/low-intensity-strength]].

**Usage Guidelines**
- Perform in the morning before work or any time you feel tension building.
- If short on time, a *minimum* version can be as brief as 2–3 minutes focusing on neck, shoulders, and a single hip circle.
- When feeling overly tight or exhausted, choose the *minimum* version or swap for a full rest day, following the decision framework outlined in the weekly schedule.

**Origins**
The flow was created for the weekly schedule in the document [[summaries/session-15_2024-06-15]], which outlines a balanced plan of work, strength, mobility, and rest.
