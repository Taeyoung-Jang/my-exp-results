## Schedule Backbone

**Definition**: The *schedule backbone* is a small set of non‑negotiable routine components—typically wake‑time, morning routine, and the first work block—that provide structural stability for a daily schedule while leaving the rest of the day flexible.

### Key Characteristics
- **Predictable Anchors**: Fixed points that reliably occur each day, creating a rhythm that the rest of the schedule can revolve around.
- **Flexibility Around Anchors**: All other activities are treated as *padding* that can shift, expand, or contract without destabilising the core.
- **Morning‑Centred**: Emphasising a steady morning rather than a fixed night helps reset the system each day, making evening spontaneity less disruptive.
- **Sleep Window Integration**: The backbone works best when paired with a flexible bedtime window, ensuring the minimum sleep needed is protected without a rigid bedtime.

### Benefits
- **Sustainability**: Because the backbone is minimal, it is easier to maintain over the long term.
- **Resilience to Disruption**: When unexpected events occur, only the flexible padding needs adjustment; the backbone remains intact.
- **Energy Management**: Consistent morning anchors support circadian alignment, helping energy levels stay stable throughout the day.
