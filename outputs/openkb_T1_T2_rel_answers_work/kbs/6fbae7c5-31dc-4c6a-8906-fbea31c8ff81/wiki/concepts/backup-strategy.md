# Backup Strategy

A **backup strategy** is the plan you follow to ensure your phone’s photos, notes, messages, and other data are safely stored and can be restored when needed. The strategy described in [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]] advocates a **hybrid approach** that leverages both [[concepts/cloud-backup]] and [[concepts/local-backup]].

## Core Principles

1. **Redundancy** – Use more than one backup venue so that if one fails, the other still protects your data.
2. **Convenience vs. Control** – Cloud services provide automatic, background syncing with minimal user effort, while local backups give you full control and avoid recurring subscription fees.
3. **Regular Verification** – Periodically check that the backup is up‑to‑date and that the key data categories (photos, notes, messages) are included.

## Cloud Backup (Automatic)

- Runs continuously in the background.
- Stores data on services such as [[entities/icloud]] for iPhone and [[entities/google-photos]]/Google One for Android.
- Main trade‑offs: storage limits, subscription cost, and reliance on a third‑party provider.
- Ideal for day‑to‑day protection and quick device switches.

## Local Backup (Computer)

- Performed manually or on a scheduled basis to a personal computer or external drive.
- No subscription fees; you retain full ownership of the data.
- Requires you to remember to run the backup and to keep the backup hardware safe.
- Serves as a safety net if the cloud service is unavailable or your account is compromised.

## Recommended Hybrid Model

- **Primary**: Enable continuous [[concepts/cloud-backup]] on the phone (i.e., iCloud for Apple devices, Google One/Photos for Android).
- **Secondary**: Perform a monthly or weekly local backup to a computer or external drive.
- **Verification**: Follow a short monthly checklist (see the source summary) to confirm the last backup timestamps, storage availability, and that recent items appear in the cloud.

## Benefits of the Hybrid Strategy

- **Resilience** – Protection against both device failure and cloud‑service outages.
- **Ease of Recovery** – Quick restores from the cloud; full data recovery possible from the local copy if needed.
- **Cost Management** – Use free cloud tiers for most data, supplement with local storage for larger archives.
