# Summary of session-07_2024-06-15

## Core Advice on Phone Backup
- **Hybrid approach**: Combine [[concepts/cloud-backup]] (automatic, convenient) with [[concepts/local-backup]] (computer) for redundancy. This addresses [[concepts/backup-strategy]].
- **Trade‑offs**:
  - Cloud: storage limits, subscription costs, reliance on third‑party services.
  - Local: requires manual effort, vulnerable to computer failure, no recurring fees.
- **Recommendation**: Use cloud as primary backup, supplement with periodic computer backups.

## Monitoring Automatic Backups
- Verify that **photos**, **notes**, and **messages** are toggled on in backup settings.
- Ensure the linked cloud account has sufficient storage.
- Periodically review the backup status line or last‑backup timestamp.
- Watch for extra battery or data usage, but continuous backup is generally safe.

## Monthly Checklist (Specific to Platforms)

### iPhone (iCloud)
1. Open **Settings → \[your name\] → iCloud → iCloud Backup** and note the **Last successful backup** date.
2. Confirm **Photos** is enabled (iCloud Photos on) – see [[entities/icloud]].
3. Verify **Notes** syncing is on for the iCloud account.
4. Ensure **Messages in iCloud** is active.
5. **Restore test**: Create a new photo or note, then view it on **iCloud.com** to confirm upload.

### Android (Google)
1. Go to **Settings → System → Backup** and check the **Last backup** timestamp under **Backup by [[entities/google-one]]**.
2. In **Google Photos**, confirm **Backup** is on and see recent activity – [[entities/google-photos]].
3. For notes (e.g., Google Keep): pull‑to‑refresh in the app, then verify the note appears at **keep.google.com** – [[entities/google-keep]].
4. In **Messages**, open profile → **Messages settings → RCS settings** and ensure RCS is enabled (Google’s message backups rely on it).
5. **Restore test**: Add a new photo and a new note, then check **photos.google.com** and **keep.google.com** from another device.

## Quick Detection of Sync Issues
- Look for stale **last‑backup** dates or error messages in the backup settings.
- Compare the count of items (photos, notes) on the phone vs. in the cloud; mismatches indicate a stall.
- Spot‑check recent items via the web interface to catch silent failures.
