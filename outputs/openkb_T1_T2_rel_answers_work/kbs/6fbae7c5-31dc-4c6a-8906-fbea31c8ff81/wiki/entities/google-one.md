# Google One

Google One is a subscription‑based cloud storage and backup product offered by [[entities/google]]. It provides expanded storage tiers for Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos, and includes features such as family sharing, automatic phone backup, and dedicated support.

## Mentions in session‑07_2024‑06‑15
- The document references Google One in the Android backup checklist, noting the **"Backup by Google One"** status screen where users can see the **Last backup** timestamp.
- It is presented as the primary way to manage and verify the phone’s cloud backup on Android devices, alongside [[entities/google-photos]] and [[entities/google-keep]].
