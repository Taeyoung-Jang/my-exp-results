Apple Inc. is a multinational technology company best known for its hardware, software, and services ecosystem. In the context of the **session-07_2024-06-15** conversation, Apple is mentioned as the platform behind the iPhone’s built‑in backup solution, iCloud.

- The document advises using iCloud as the main backup and pairing it with a [[concepts/local-backup]] (e.g., computer) for redundancy, illustrating Apple’s place in a broader [[concepts/backup-strategy]].
