## Google Keep

Google Keep is a note‑taking and list‑making product offered by [[entities/google]]. It stores notes in the cloud, allowing them to sync across devices via the user's Google account.

**Key facts from the backup checklist (session‑07_2024‑06‑15):**
- The checklist recommends pulling down in the Keep app to force a sync and then confirming the new note appears at **keep.google.com**.
- Google Keep is used as the primary notes solution on Android in the document’s recommended backup strategy.
- It is part of the broader [[concepts/backup-strategy]] that includes both cloud and local backups.

**Related concepts and entities**
- Cloud backup via Google services ([[entities/google-photos]] for photos, [[entities/google-one]] for storage)
- The document’s monthly checklist links to [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]] for detailed steps.

**Usage tip**: Regularly verify that new notes appear on the web interface to ensure the backup is functioning correctly.
