# Google Photos (product)

Google Photos is a cloud‑based photo storage and backup service provided by [[entities/google]].  It allows Android users to automatically back up images taken on their device and access them from any web‑connected platform.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]]
- **Backup role**: Listed as the primary cloud‑backup solution for photos on Android devices.
- **Settings check**: Users should open the Google Photos app, confirm that **Backup** is turned on, and verify recent activity to ensure new photos are being uploaded.
- **Restore test**: Create a new photo on the phone, then view it on **photos.google.com** (or the Google Photos web UI) to confirm the upload succeeded.
- **Trade‑offs**: Benefits include automatic syncing and cross‑device access; downsides are storage limits and potential data usage.
