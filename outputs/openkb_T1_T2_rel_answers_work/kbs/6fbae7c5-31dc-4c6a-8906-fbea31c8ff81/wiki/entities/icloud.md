# iCloud

**Type:** product

iCloud is Apple’s cloud‑based storage and backup platform that lets iPhone users automatically sync and back up personal data such as photos, notes, and messages. It integrates with the iOS Settings app under the user’s Apple ID and provides features like **iCloud Photos**, **Notes syncing**, and **Messages in iCloud**.

## Key facts from session‑07_2024‑06‑15
- The backup workflow is described in the [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]] summary.
- Users should check **Settings → \[name\] → iCloud → iCloud Backup** for the *Last successful backup* timestamp.
- Within iCloud settings, **Photos** must be toggled on (iCloud Photos enabled), **Notes** syncing must be active for the iCloud account, and **Messages in iCloud** should be enabled to ensure message continuity.
- A quick restore test involves creating a new photo or note on the iPhone and confirming its appearance on **iCloud.com**.
- Continuous backup may increase battery and data usage but offers the safest, low‑effort protection strategy, aligning with the broader [[concepts/cloud-backup]] and [[concepts/backup-strategy]] discussions.
