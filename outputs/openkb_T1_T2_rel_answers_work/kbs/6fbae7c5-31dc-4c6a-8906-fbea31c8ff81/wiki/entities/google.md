# Google

Google is a global technology organization best known for its search engine and a suite of online services. In the context of mobile device management, Google provides the primary **backup infrastructure for Android** devices.

## Role in Backup Strategy
- Supports a **cloud backup** approach as part of a broader [[concepts/backup-strategy]].
- Enables automatic syncing of photos, notes, and messages, reducing reliance on manual [[concepts/local-backup]].
- Offers storage through services such as [[entities/google-one]] for overall data quota management.

## Related Services Mentioned
- **Google Photos** – Handles image backup and sync. [[entities/google-photos]]
- **Google Keep** – Notes app that can be backed up via Google services. [[entities/google-keep]]
- **Google One** – Subscription service that provides additional cloud storage for backups. [[entities/google-one]]

## Reference in Session 07 (2024‑06‑15)
The document *"session-07_2024-06-15"* outlines a monthly checklist for Android users, directing them to:
1. Verify the **Backup by Google One** status and recent timestamp.
2. Ensure **Google Photos** backup is active.
3. Use **Google Keep** for note syncing and confirm via keep.google.com.
4. Check message backup via RCS settings, which relies on Google’s ecosystem.

These steps illustrate how Google’s services are integral to a reliable phone backup routine. See the full summary for details: [[summaries/session-07_2024-06-15]].
