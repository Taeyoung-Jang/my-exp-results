# Growth Goal Setting

**Concept Definition**: A structured approach to create clear, measurable objectives that help technical professionals deepen expertise, expand impact, and advance their careers while remaining hands‑on.

---

## Overview
Technical career paths require visible progress that is not tied to people‑management responsibilities. Growth goal setting provides a way to demonstrate ambition, broaden scope, and contribute value through concrete deliverables such as project ownership, mentorship, patents, and publications. The concept is outlined in the discussion of career planning in [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]].

---

## Core Principles
1. **Hands‑On Focus** – Goals should keep the professional engaged in lab or engineering work.
2. **Measurability** – Each goal includes a clear metric or deliverable (e.g., “submit one patent”).
3. **Scope Expansion** – Goals increase the breadth or depth of influence (e.g., owning a subsystem).
4. **Visibility** – Outcomes are demonstrable to the manager and team (presentations, documents, patents).
5. **Alignment with Technical Track** – Goals reinforce the choice of a technical rather than a managerial trajectory, linking back to [[concepts/technical-vs-leadership-paths]].

## Sample Growth Goals (adapted from the session)
| # | Goal Description |
|---|-------------------|
| 1 | **Technical Ownership** – Take full responsibility for a complex subsystem or research area, delivering design specs and roadmap. |
| 2 | **End‑to‑End Delivery** – Lead a project from concept through lab validation, producing a working prototype and final report. |
| 3 | **Domain Expertise** – Become the go‑to expert on a specific technique/tool and create a documented best‑practice guide. |
| 4 | **Experimental Campaign** – Design and execute a challenging set of experiments that unlock a key team milestone. |
| 5 | **Mentorship** – Mentor a junior engineer on lab methods, conducting monthly coaching sessions and tracking progress. |
| 6 | **Intellectual Property** – File at least one patent or internal invention disclosure related to your work. |
| 7 | **Technical Presentation** – Deliver a deep‑dive talk to the group or at an internal symposium, summarizing findings and lessons learned. |
| 8 | **Publication** – Author a paper or white‑paper (where applicable) that showcases a novel methodology or result. |

## Using Goals in Career Conversations
When discussing career direction with a manager, frame these goals as evidence of a **technical growth trajectory**. Emphasize that they:
- Increase technical depth and reliability.
- Provide tangible outcomes the team can rely on.
- Demonstrate leadership **through expertise**, aligning with the ideas in [[concepts/signaling-technical-track]].
