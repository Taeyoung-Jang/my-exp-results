# Summary of session-38_2024-12-16

**Main focus** – How to quickly bring structure to a chaotic team by introducing a single, low‑overhead artifact that surfaces priorities, handoff risks, and stalled work without adding meetings.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/team-alignment]]** – early, conversational discovery of what people are doing and where friction exists.  
- **[[concepts/lightweight-dashboard]]** – a one‑page view that shows only the most actionable signals.  
- **[[concepts/handoff-risk-management]]** – any upcoming transition between owners that could become a bottleneck.  
- **priority management** – a simple three‑level (High/Medium/Low) tag that avoids score‑card debates.  
- **health signal** – objective traffic‑light status (green/yellow/red) tied to timing and blockage.  
- **async review cadence** – a brief weekly, asynchronous update rather than a formal status meeting.

## Recommended Artifact
A **single‑page priorities board** (spreadsheet or Notion table) with the following columns (8‑12 max):

```
Project | Owner | Collaborator | Priority | Phase | Next Handoff | Handoff Date | What’s Needed Next | Health
```

### Example rows
| Project | Owner | Collaborator | Priority | Phase | Next Handoff | Handoff Date | What’s Needed Next | Health |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Website Refresh | [[entities/maya]] | [[entities/jordan]] | High | Design Review | Engineering kickoff | Apr 4 | Finalize design notes | Green |
| Data Cleanup | [[entities/ravi]] | — | Medium | In Progress | QA check | Mar 29 | Confirm missing fields | Yellow |
| Mobile Onboarding | [[entities/lena]] | [[entities/chris]] | High | Dev Complete | PM review → Release prep | Apr 2 | PM feedback before handoff | Yellow |

### Health signal rules
- **Green** – handoff date realistic; no blockers.  
- **Yellow** – date at risk because something is waiting on another person or an open question exists.  
- **Red** – date will be missed unless something changes; work is blocked or no progress.

### Defining “no progress”
Treat a row as **no progress** when the **What’s Needed Next** field has not changed for **seven days**. In Notion this can be derived from the built‑in *Last Edited* property; in spreadsheets you can add a timestamp column.

## Views for Rapid Insight
1. **Upcoming Hand‑offs** – sorted by *Handoff Date*.  
2. **Conflicting Priorities** – filtered by *Owner* and highlighted where multiple *High* items exist.  
3. **Stuck Work** – rows with *Health = Red* **or** unchanged *What’s Needed Next* > 7 days.

## Review Cadence
- **Weekly async check‑in**: each owner updates their row before a set morning (e.g., Monday 9 am).  
- After updates, post a short written summary highlighting any red items or overlapping high‑priority hand‑offs.  
- No additional synchronous meeting is required unless a red item demands immediate discussion.

## Rationale
Keeping the board **lightweight** (≈ 1 minute to edit) encourages ownership, surfaces friction early, and avoids the perception of “more process.” The limited field set, objective health rules, and simple weekly rhythm make the dashboard a **transparent communication layer** rather than a new project‑management system.
