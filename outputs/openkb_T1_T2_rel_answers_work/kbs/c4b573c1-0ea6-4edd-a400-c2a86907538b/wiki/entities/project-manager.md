# Project Manager

**Category:** Work role (project coordination)

**Typical responsibilities**
- Defining project scope, timelines, and deliverables.
- Managing budgets, resources, and stakeholder expectations.
- Tracking progress, mitigating risks, and reporting status.
- Facilitating communication between cross‑functional teams.

**Relevance to career path decisions**
- In the context of [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]], a *Project Manager* role is highlighted as a title to be cautious about for engineers who wish to stay on a technical track. These roles tend to emphasize people‑management, schedule, and budget responsibilities rather than hands‑on engineering work.
- The role sits on the **leadership** side of the [[concepts/technical-vs-leadership-paths]] spectrum, where impact is measured through coordination and delivery rather than deep technical execution.

**Contrast with technical‑track signals**
- When signaling a preference for the technical track, professionals are advised (see [[concepts/signaling-technical-track]]) to emphasize impact through problem‑solving and expertise, which contrasts with the coordination‑focused impact of a *Project Manager*.

**Implications for growth planning**
- Engineers aiming for hands‑on growth may prioritize goals from [[concepts/growth-goal-setting]] such as technical ownership, patent contributions, or mentoring, which are less aligned with the typical deliverables of a *Project Manager*.

**Related roles**
- Similar coordination‑focused role: [[entities/program-manager]]
- Technical‑focused counterparts to consider instead: [[entities/senior-engineer]], [[entities/staff-engineer]], [[entities/lead-engineer]], [[entities/principal-engineer]]
