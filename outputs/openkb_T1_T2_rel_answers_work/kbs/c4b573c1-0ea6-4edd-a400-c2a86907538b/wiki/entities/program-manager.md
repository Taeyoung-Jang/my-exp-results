# Program Manager

A **Program Manager** is a professional who oversees the planning, execution, and delivery of complex programs that span multiple projects. Their responsibilities typically include coordinating cross‑functional teams, managing timelines, budgets, and stakeholder expectations, and ensuring that program objectives align with organizational strategy.

## Context from [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]]

In the discussion about choosing between technical and leadership career tracks, the **Program Manager** role was highlighted as an example of a position to be cautious about for those who wish to stay on a hands‑on technical path. The document advises:
- Avoid roles where the primary deliverables are schedules, budgets, or personnel decisions rather than technical output.
- Recognize that titles such as *Project Manager* or *Program Manager* tend to emphasize program‑level coordination over deep engineering work.

## Typical Responsibilities
- Define program scope, objectives, and deliverables.
- Create and maintain program schedules and budgets.
- Align multiple project teams to achieve program milestones.
- Communicate status and risks to senior leadership and stakeholders.
- Facilitate resource allocation across projects.

## When to Consider or Avoid
- **Consider** if you enjoy high‑level coordination, strategic planning, and stakeholder management.
- **Avoid** if your career goal is to remain deeply involved in hands‑on engineering work, as the role may limit technical depth.
