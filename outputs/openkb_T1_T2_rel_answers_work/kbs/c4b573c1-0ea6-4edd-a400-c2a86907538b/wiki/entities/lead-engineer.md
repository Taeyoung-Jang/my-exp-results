# Lead Engineer

## Overview
- **Lead Engineer** is a senior engineering position that often blends deep technical expertise with some leadership or coordination duties. The exact balance can vary by organization.

## Role Ambiguity in Career Paths
- In the discussion from [[summaries/session-11_2024-06-15]], the title “lead engineer” was highlighted as ambiguous: it can represent a purely technical track or a stepping stone toward people‑management.
- The advice suggests asking how much **hands‑on** time versus coordination the role actually involves before accepting.

## Relationship to Other Engineering Titles
- Typically positioned above [[entities/senior-engineer]] and below [[entities/staff-engineer]] or [[entities/principal-engineer]] in technical ladders.
- May be considered a parallel path to roles like [[entities/program-manager]] or [[entities/project-manager]] when the focus shifts toward project coordination.

## Guidance for Choosing the Path
- When evaluating a Lead Engineer role, consider the concepts of [[concepts/technical-vs-leadership-paths]] and [[concepts/signaling-technical-track]] to ensure alignment with personal career goals.
- Set measurable growth goals as described in [[concepts/growth-goal-setting]] to maintain a hands‑on focus if that is desired.
