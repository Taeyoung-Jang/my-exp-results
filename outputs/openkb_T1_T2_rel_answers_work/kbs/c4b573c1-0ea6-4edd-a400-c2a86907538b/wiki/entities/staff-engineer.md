## Staff Engineer

A **Staff Engineer** is a senior technical individual‑contributor role often found in engineering organizations.  It sits above senior‑level engineers and below principal or distinguished engineers, and is characterized by deep domain expertise, broad influence across multiple projects, and responsibility for architectural decisions without direct people‑management duties.

### Typical Responsibilities
- Provide technical leadership and mentorship to other engineers (e.g., senior‑engineer, lead‑engineer).
- Own the design and execution of complex subsystems or research areas.
- Drive high‑impact projects from concept through validation, often serving as the go‑to expert for a specific technique or tool.
- Contribute to technical strategy and influence product direction through expertise rather than people‑management.
- Document best practices, create internal guidelines, and may author patents or publications.
