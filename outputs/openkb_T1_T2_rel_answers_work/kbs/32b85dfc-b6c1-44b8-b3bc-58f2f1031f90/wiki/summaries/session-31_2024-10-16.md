## Summary of Session 31 (2024‑10‑16)

This document provides a concise, actionable routine for someone who leaves for work at **5:15 am**, works out before work, and feels depleted by dinner time. The focus is on **three “non‑negotiable” actions** for both morning and evening, supported by a **night‑before preparation** phase and **low‑effort wind‑down** techniques.

### Core Recommendations

1. **Night‑Before Preparation**
   - **Pack gym bag** (clothes, shoes, water bottle).
   - **Portion grab‑and‑go meals**: overnight oats or yogurt + fruit/nuts for breakfast; protein‑veg bowl or leftovers for lunch.
   - **Fill water bottle** and set it by the door.
   - Takes ~5 minutes after dinner.
   > This leverages night before preparation to reduce decision fatigue in the morning.

2. **Morning Non‑Negotiables**
   - **4:45 am – Wake & hydrate** (drink a glass of water). 
   - **4:50 am – Immediate cue**: switch into gym clothes or do a 1‑minute stretch. 
   - **5:00 am – Quick gym session** (as per personal workout plan).
   - **5:10 am – Grab pre‑prepped breakfast & lunch**, head out by **5:15 am**.
   > These are examples of non negotiables and habit stacking (linking hydration → dressing → workout).

3. **Evening Low‑Effort Wind‑Down**
   - **After dinner – 5‑minute reset**: lay out next‑day clothes, pack any needed items, tidy kitchen.
   - **Signal cue**: take a quick shower, dim lights, or brush teeth.
   - **Screen‑free buffer** (3–5 minutes) before bed; go to sleep at the first sign of tiredness.
   > Supports sleep hygiene and uses a simple routine consistency cue.

### Key Concepts Highlighted

- **Micro‑habits / Tiny habits** – keeping actions under a few minutes to ensure they stick.
- **Habit stacking** – attaching new habits to existing routines (e.g., hydrate → dress).
- **Grab‑and‑go meals** – pre‑prepared nutrition to avoid morning cooking.
- **Night‑before preparation** – front‑loading effort to smooth morning flow.
- **Sleep hygiene** – using consistent wind‑down signals to improve bedtime adherence.
- **Non‑negotiables** – a limited set of essential actions that define the routine.

### Practical Takeaways

- Keep the **morning routine** to **3 core actions** that take **≤10 minutes total**.
- Use the **evening “reset”** as both a preparation step and a wind‑down cue.
- Maintain **consistent cues** (water bottle, gym bag location, dimmed lights) to automate behavior.
- Review and adjust **non‑negotiables** monthly to keep the routine feeling fresh and sustainable.
