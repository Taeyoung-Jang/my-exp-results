# Event Clarity

**Definition**: Event Clarity is the practice of presenting information, signage, and spatial design in a way that allows participants to quickly grasp what is happening, where to go, and how to engage without feeling lost or stressed.

## Key Elements

- **Clear Signage**: Large, legible signs that indicate entrances, restrooms, activity zones, and exits.  
- **Simple Layout Maps**: Visual maps placed at key points, showing the flow of the main walkway and any auxiliary areas such as a calm pocket or live‑art corner.  
- **Consistent Messaging**: Uniform terminology and visual language across all event communications, from digital flyers to on‑site posters.  
- **Staff Visibility**: Friendly staff or volunteers wearing identifiable attire positioned at decision points to answer questions.

In the conversation from [[summaries/session-34_2025-06-15]], Event Clarity emerges as a top priority for making a large city festival welcoming.  The assistant recommends:

1. An **easy‑to‑navigate layout** with clear pathways and visual cues.

These recommendations directly embody the principles of Event Clarity, emphasizing that when attendees understand the environment instantly, the event feels less chaotic and more inviting.
