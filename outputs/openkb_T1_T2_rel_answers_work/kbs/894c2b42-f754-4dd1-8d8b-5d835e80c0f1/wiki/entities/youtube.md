# YouTube (Organization)

YouTube is a global video‑sharing service that hosts a vast library of music, ambient sound recordings, and podcasts. In **[[summaries/session-36_2024-06-15]]**, YouTube is recommended as one of the primary sources for searching "cozy evening" audio content, alongside Spotify.

## Key Points from Session 36
- The assistant provides a list of concrete search phrases that can be pasted directly into YouTube (and Spotify) to locate playlists, ambient tracks, and podcasts suitable for creating a warm, welcoming home atmosphere.
- These suggestions support the concept of **[[concepts/background-sound-selection]]**, helping users choose low‑effort soundscapes that fill silence without becoming distracting.
- By using YouTube for ambient music and sound effects, users can enhance **[[concepts/home-comfort-design]]** through auditory cues that complement lighting and scent.
