# Summary of Session 36 (2024‑06‑15)

## Main Goal  
Provide practical, low‑effort ideas for making a living space feel more welcoming and “alive” during evening after‑work routines.

## Core Recommendations  
- **Sensory touches**: fresh flowers, easy‑care houseplants, rotating throw blankets, candles, or plug‑in diffusers for scent.  
- **Lighting**: use warm‑toned lamps or string lights instead of overhead lights; switch on a single cozy lamp when you arrive. [[concepts/ambient-lighting]]  
- **Soundscape**: low‑volume playlists, ambient loops, café or rain ambience, or gentle podcasts to fill silence without demanding attention. [[concepts/background-sound-selection]]  
- **Micro‑movement**: turn on a small fan, crack a window for airflow, or do a brief stretch.  
- **Evening ritual (10‑20 min)**:  
  1. Enter, immediately start a calm playlist or ambient station.  
  2. Turn on a warm lamp.  
  3. Light a candle or start a diffuser.  
  4. Change into comfortable clothes.  
  5. Make a quick drink (tea, flavored seltzer) and settle.  
- **Energy‑level variations**:  
  - *Wiped‑out*: focus on sound and light, no preparation, just sit and unwind.  
  - *More energy*: add a tiny tidy task, a nicer drink, or a couple of stretches.  
- **Background‑sound advice**: choose music with steady rhythm and instrumental texture; avoid vocal‑heavy or highly dynamic tracks. Low volume keeps it atmospheric rather than noisy.
