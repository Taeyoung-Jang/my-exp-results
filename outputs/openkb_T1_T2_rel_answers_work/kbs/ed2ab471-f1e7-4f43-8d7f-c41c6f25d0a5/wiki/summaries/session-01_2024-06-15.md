# Summary of session-01_2024-06-15

- Advice on creating a welcoming living‑room focal point using a locked display case for vintage ceramics.  
- **Lighting**: soft, directed light above or inside the case to draw attention and signal value. [[concepts/lighting_design]]  
- **Placement**: maintain breathing room around the case; position a subtle plaque in the lower corner for visibility without distraction. [[concepts/display_placement]]  
- **Plaque wording**: provided 8‑10 short options across three tones (gentle, lightly humorous, polite‑direct). Example chosen: “Display only—thank you for caring for them with me.”  
- **Sign material & finish**: recommend a small engraved metal plate (~business‑card size) with a simple serif or clean sans‑serif font; brushed brass or matte black finish for understated elegance.  
- **Mounting**: mount on the lower corner of the case frame for seamless integration; a tiny stand is an alternative when the frame is ornate.  
- Overall recommendation: combine appropriate lighting, clear but warm signage, and a well‑designed plate to protect the ceramics while enhancing the room’s aesthetic. [[concepts/signage_etiquette]]
