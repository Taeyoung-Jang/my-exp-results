# Lighting Design for Display Cases

Effective lighting enhances the visual impact of a display case and subtly signals that its contents are valuable and not to be touched. The approach balances illumination, ambiance, and practicality.

## Purpose
- Draw attention to the displayed items without creating glare.
- Communicate that the collection is curated and should be observed, not handled.
- Contribute to the overall welcoming atmosphere of the room.

## Core Principles
1. **Soft, Directed Light** – Use a gentle, focused light source positioned just above or inside the case. This creates a halo effect that highlights the ceramics while avoiding harsh shadows.
2. **Warm Color Temperature** – Opt for warm white (2700‑3000 K) to evoke a cozy feeling that matches a living‑room setting.
3. **Even Coverage** – Ensure the light reaches all pieces evenly; multiple low‑intensity LEDs can provide uniform illumination.
4. **Minimize Glare** – Position lights at an angle that prevents reflections on glass surfaces, preserving visibility.

## Practical Tips (from [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]])
- Install a small LED strip along the inner top edge of the case, or place a discreet track light above the case.
- Use dimmable fixtures to adjust brightness based on time of day or ambient lighting.
- Combine lighting with clear **display placement** [[concepts/display_placement]] to maintain breathing room around the case, allowing the light to accentuate rather than overwhelm.

## Integration with Signage
- Complement lighting with subtle **signage etiquette** [[concepts/signage_etiquette]]: a gently illuminated plaque can reinforce the “display only” message without breaking the visual flow.

## Summary
Well‑designed lighting for display cases uses soft, warm, directed illumination to showcase items, guide viewer behavior, and harmonize with the surrounding interior design.
