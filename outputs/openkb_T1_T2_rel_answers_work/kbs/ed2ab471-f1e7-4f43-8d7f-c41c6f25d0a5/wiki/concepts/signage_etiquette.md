# Signage Etiquette and Wording for Non‑Touch Displays

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Best practices for creating friendly, effective signage that tells visitors a display is for viewing only without feeling museum‑like.

---

## Core Principles
1. **Tone Balance** – Aim for a tone that is gentle, welcoming, and clear.  The wording should invite curiosity while politely requesting that items not be handled.
2. **Brevity** – Keep the message to one or two short lines so it can be read at a glance.
3. **Visibility without Distraction** – Place the sign where it is noticeable as a visitor approaches but does not compete with the displayed objects.
4. **Material & Finish** – Choose a durable, understated material (e.g., brushed brass or matte black metal) that matches the surrounding décor.
5. **Placement** – Lower‑corner mounting on the case frame or a low‑profile stand directly in front of the case works well.

---

## Example Wording Options (from [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]])
- “Display only—thank you for caring for them with me.”
- “Please enjoy these pieces from afar.”
- “Loved and displayed, not for handling.”
- “Look with curiosity, not with hands.”
- “Admire freely, handle gently… by not handling at all.”
- “Please enjoy these treasures with your eyes.”
- “Quietly on display, happily untouched.”
- “Look all you like—hands get the day off.”
- “These pieces are shy; they prefer not to be picked up.”
- “Fragile friends inside. Viewing only, please.”

These examples illustrate three tone categories:
- **Very Gentle** – “Please enjoy these pieces from afar.”
- **Lightly Humorous** – “Look all you like—hands get the day off.”
- **Polite‑Direct** – “Display only—thank you for caring for them with me.”

---

## Design Recommendations
- **Size**: Approximate business‑card dimensions (around 5 × 3 in) – large enough to read, small enough not to dominate the case.
- **Font**: Simple serif or clean sans‑serif, medium weight.
- **Finish**: Brushed brass or matte black for an understated, permanent look.
- **Mounting**: Directly onto the lower corner of the display‑case frame for seamless integration; a tiny stand is an alternative when the frame is ornate.
