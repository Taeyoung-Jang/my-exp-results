## Summary of session-01_2025-06-15

**Date:** 2025‑06‑15  
**Topic:** Choosing between cloud‑based subscription suites and standalone licenses for design and 3D tools.

### Key trade‑offs
- **Subscription suites** – provide continuous updates, cloud storage, collaboration, and predictable monthly fees; downside is lack of ownership and potentially high cumulative cost.  
- **Standalone (perpetual) licenses** – one‑time purchase, full ownership, slower/extra‑cost updates; may miss cloud‑linked features.  
These points are explored in [[concepts/subscription_vs_perpetual]].

### Decision framework
A simple **checklist** can be turned into a **weighted scoring system** that evaluates options across four **workflow factors**:

| Criteria | Weight | How to Score (1‑5) |
|---|---|---|
| Core workflow reliance | 40% | Extent to which the option covers daily modeling, texturing, and scene assembly. |
| Cost efficiency | 25% | Incremental cost relative to value delivered. |
| Workflow integration | 20% | Fit with existing pipeline, file formats, and plugins. |
| Future flexibility | 15% | Ability to support emerging rendering methods, AI tools, and collaboration. |

The overall approach is described in [[concepts/decision_scoring_framework]].

Replace the placeholder scores with your own assessments to decide which configuration offers the best overall value.

### Takeaways
- Use the checklist to focus on actual usage patterns rather than feature lists.  
- Prioritize **core workflow reliance** when the tool is central to daily production.  
- Consider **cost efficiency** over the expected lifespan of the tool.  
- Ensure smooth **workflow integration** to avoid costly export/import steps.  
- Keep an eye on **future flexibility** to protect your investment as technology evolves.  

These points tie back to the broader [[concepts/workflow_factors]] and the overall [[concepts/decision_scoring_framework]] approach.
