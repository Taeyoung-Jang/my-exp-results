# Subscription vs Perpetual Licensing

**Overview**

The concept examines the two primary licensing approaches for cloud‑based creative and 3D design tools: *subscription suites* and *perpetual (standalone) licenses*. It highlights how each model impacts cost structure, ownership, update frequency, cloud features, and long‑term flexibility.

## Subscription Suites
- **Continuous updates** – users receive the latest features, bug fixes, and cloud‑linked capabilities without extra charge.
- **Cloud services** – integrated storage, collaborative editing, and often built‑in rendering farms.
- **Predictable expense** – monthly or annual fees spread the cost over time, useful for budgeting.
- **No ownership** – access is contingent on ongoing payment; stopping the subscription revokes usage.
- **Potential cost accumulation** – over many years the total expense can exceed the price of a perpetual license, especially if the user only needs a subset of the suite’s tools.

## Perpetual (Standalone) Licenses
- **One‑time purchase** – the software is owned outright, allowing indefinite use without further fees.
- **Slower or paid updates** – major version upgrades may require additional purchase, and minor updates may be limited.
- **Limited cloud integration** – may lack built‑in storage, collaboration, or rendering services that are standard in subscription platforms.
- **Stability** – fewer unexpected changes, which can be advantageous for pipelines that require consistency.
- **Higher upfront cost** – a larger initial outlay, but potentially lower total cost over a long horizon if the tool remains sufficient.

## Decision Factors
Choosing the appropriate licensing model depends on several workflow‑related criteria, which can be evaluated with a [[concepts/decision_scoring_framework]]:
- **Core workflow reliance** – how central the tool is to daily modeling, texturing, and scene assembly.
- **Cost efficiency** – total expense over the expected usage period versus value delivered.
- **Workflow integration** – compatibility with existing pipelines, file formats, and plugins.
- **Future flexibility** – ability to adopt emerging technologies such as real‑time rendering or AI‑assisted design.

These criteria align with the broader [[concepts/workflow_factors]] that influence software selection.

## When to Prefer Each Model
- **Subscription** is ideal when constant updates, collaborative cloud features, and multi‑tool interoperability are critical, and when the user expects to use the suite daily.
- **Perpetual** suits users who need a stable, single‑purpose tool, have predictable workflows, and wish to avoid ongoing fees.

Understanding these distinctions enables creators to apply the appropriate scoring framework and make informed licensing decisions.
