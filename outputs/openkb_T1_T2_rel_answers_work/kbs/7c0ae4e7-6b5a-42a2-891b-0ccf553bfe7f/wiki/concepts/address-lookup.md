# Address Lookup for Utilities and Internet

Understanding which providers serve a particular address is essential for a smooth move‑in. The process combines checking city‑run utility portals with the availability tools of private internet providers.

## Why It Matters
- Guarantees that you can schedule service activation before your move‑in date.
- Prevents last‑minute surprises (e.g., discovering a building is fully electric). 
- Helps you compare options like [[entities/att-fiber]], [[entities/google-fiber]] and [[entities/spectrum]] for the fastest, most reliable connection.

## Core Steps (as used in [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]])
1. **Identify the city‑run utility portal** – Austin’s utilities are managed through the **Customer Care** portal at `coautilities.com`. This site handles both [[entities/austin-energy]] (electric) and [[entities/austin-water]] (water & wastewater).
2. **Check natural gas** – For most residences in the 78704 pocket, gas is provided by [[entities/texas-gas-service]]. Their start‑service page (`texasgasservice.com/startservice`) confirms coverage.
3. **Run internet availability checks**:
   - **AT&T Fiber** – Use `att.com/availability`. If the address format is finicky, enter the USPS‑standard street address without the unit number, then select the correct unit from the drop‑down.
   - **Google Fiber** – Use `fiber.google.com`. You can search by building name (e.g., "Barton Springs Apartments") followed by "Austin, TX" to trigger autocomplete.
   - **Spectrum** – Use `spectrum.com` as a fallback when fiber isn’t available.
4. **Validate formatting** – Ensure the address follows USPS conventions (e.g., `1234 S Lamar Blvd`), and try variations (with/without unit) if the tool rejects the entry.
5. **Record results** – Note the confirmed providers and any required documentation (lease, ID) for each service.

## Tips for Successful Lookups
- **Use the building name** when unit numbers cause errors; many portals list the building and then let you pick the unit.
- **Check both city and private portals** – Austin utilities are centralized, but internet providers have independent databases.
- **Start early** – As shown in the [[concepts/service-start-timeline]], initiating requests 3‑5 days before move‑in gives a buffer for technician visits.
