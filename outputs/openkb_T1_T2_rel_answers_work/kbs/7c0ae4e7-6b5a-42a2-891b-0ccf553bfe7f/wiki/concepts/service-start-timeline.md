# Service Start Timeline

A **service start timeline** outlines the optimal sequence and timing for turning on essential utilities and related services when beginning a new residence.  The goal is to avoid gaps in power, water, gas, internet, and other municipal services while minimizing stress during move‑in.

---

## Core Elements of the Timeline

1. **Identify providers** – Determine which companies supply electricity, water, gas, and internet for the address.  For the South Lamar/Barton Springs area in Austin, the providers are typically:
   - Electricity: [[entities/austin-energy]]
   - Water & wastewater: [[entities/austin-water]]
   - Natural gas (if present): [[entities/texas-gas-service]]
   - Internet: [[entities/att-fiber]], [[entities/google-fiber]], or fallback [[entities/spectrum]]
   - Trash & recycling: [[entities/austin-resource-recovery]]
   - Parking permits: [[entities/city-of-austin-parking-division]]

2. **Gather required information** – Most providers need:
   - Lease start date
   - Government‑issued ID
   - Payment method (card or bank account)
   - Occasionally a security deposit

3. **Set the order of requests** – Follow the typical **utility‑setup** sequence:
   1. Electricity and water (city‑served) – request first.
   2. Gas (if applicable) – request next, allowing a few days for a technician.
   3. Internet – request early (about a week before move‑in) to secure a self‑install kit or technician slot.
   4. Trash/recycling – usually auto‑linked to water service, but verify pickup day.
   5. Parking permits and renter’s insurance – can be completed any time during the week.

---

## Example Timeline (Monday → Friday Move‑In)

| Day | Action | Provider | Notes |
|-----|--------|----------|-------|
| **Monday** (today) | Submit start‑service requests for electricity, water, and gas (if needed). | [[entities/austin-energy]], [[entities/austin-water]], [[entities/texas-gas-service]] | Use the City of Austin *Customer Care* portal (coautilities.com) for electric & water; use texasgasservice.com/startservice for gas. |
| | Initiate internet availability check and place order. | [[entities/att-fiber]] / [[entities/google-fiber]] / [[entities/spectrum]] | Use the address‑lookup tools (see [[concepts/address-lookup]]) and request a self‑install kit or schedule a technician. |
| **Tuesday–Wednesday** | Upload lease, ID, and payment info; confirm order numbers. | All providers | Verify that start dates are set for the upcoming Friday. |
| **Thursday** | Confirm trash/recycling pickup day; apply for parking permit if required. | [[entities/austin-resource-recovery]], [[entities/city-of-austin-parking-division]] | No extra paperwork needed for trash; parking permit needs lease proof. |
| **Friday** (move‑in day) | All utilities should be active; finalize renter’s insurance. | Lemonade, State Farm, USAA (optional) | Insurance can be signed up the same day with minimal info. |

---

## Why the Timeline Matters

- **Buffer for provider processing** – Most city utilities can activate the next day, but a 24‑hour buffer protects against unexpected delays.
- **Technician scheduling** – Gas and some internet installations require a technician; scheduling early (3‑5 days before move‑in) improves the odds of a same‑week appointment.
- **Automatic linkages** – In Austin, trash service is typically tied to the water account, so activating water also triggers trash pickup.
- **No tenant coordination needed** – Each provider handles overlapping service dates automatically; only master‑metered buildings require property‑manager involvement.
