# Summary of Session 34 (2023‑06‑15)

**Location:** Near S Lamar Blvd & Barton Springs Rd, Austin, TX 78704  
**Primary utility providers:**  
- Electricity: [[entities/austin-energy]] (city‑served, no retail choice)  
- Water & wastewater: [[entities/austin-water]] (city‑served)  
- Natural gas (if applicable): [[entities/texas-gas-service]]  

**Internet options:** [[entities/att-fiber]], [[entities/google-fiber]], and fallback [[entities/spectrum]]  

**Common additional services:** trash & recycling via [[entities/austin-resource-recovery]], residential parking permits via [[entities/city-of-austin-parking-division]], and renter’s insurance (e.g., [[entities/lemonade]], [[entities/state-farm]], [[entities/usaa]]).

## Order of Operations & Timeline (Monday → Friday move‑in)

1. **Today (Monday)** – Submit start‑service requests for:  
   - Electricity via the *Customer Care* portal (coautilities.com) or call 512‑494‑9400.  
   - Water & wastewater via the same portal (coautilities.com).  
   - Gas via [[entities/texas-gas-service]]’s start‑service page or call 800‑700‑2443 (if the building has gas).  
   - Internet availability check and order ([[entities/att-fiber]] or [[entities/google-fiber]]). If a self‑install kit is available, request it now; if a technician is needed, schedule the earliest slot.  

2. **Tuesday–Wednesday** – Confirm receipt of confirmation numbers, upload lease start date, government ID, and payment info for each provider.  

3. **Thursday** – Verify trash/recycling pickup day (usually auto‑linked to water service) and apply for any required parking permit.  

4. **Friday (move‑in day)** – All utilities should be active; finalize renter’s insurance and any remaining paperwork.

## Required Information for Each Provider

- **Lease start date** (mandatory for all utilities)  
- **Government‑issued ID** (driver’s license, state ID, or passport)  
- **Payment method** (credit/debit card or bank account)  
- **Deposit** (may be required for electricity or gas if no credit history)  
- **Proof of residence** (lease or utility bill) for parking permits  

## Contact & Quick‑Access Links

- **Austin Energy / Austin Water portal:** https://coautilities.com (phone: 512‑494‑9400) – the official “Customer Care” system for both utilities.  
- **Texas Gas Service start service:** https://texasgasservice.com/startservice (phone: 800‑700‑2443)  
- **AT&T Fiber availability:** https://att.com/availability – enter unit number; use USPS‑formatted address if needed.  
- **Google Fiber availability:** https://fiber.google.com – search by building name or address (see [[concepts/address-lookup]] for tips).  
- **Spectrum availability:** https://spectrum.com – fallback provider.

## Transfer Notes

- No direct coordination with the previous tenant is required; each provider handles overlap automatically.  
- Only master‑metered buildings (rare in 78704) would require the property management to manage the switch.
