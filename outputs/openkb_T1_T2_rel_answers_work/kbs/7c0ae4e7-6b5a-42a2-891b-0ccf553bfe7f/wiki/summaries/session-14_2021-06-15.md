## Summary of session‑14 (2021‑06‑15)

- **Core question**: How to choose a neighborhood in a rainy, coastal U.S. city for a mostly car‑light lifestyle.  
- **Prioritized factors** (in order of importance for car‑light residents):
  1. **Walkability** – short, pleasant, well‑maintained sidewalks and dense local amenities. [[concepts/walkability]]
  2. **Transit reliability** – frequent, rain‑proof options (buses, light rail). [[concepts/transit-reliability]]
  3. **Proximity to nature** – accessible green or waterfront spaces for mood‑boosting breaks.  

- **Red‑flag checklist**:
  - Poor drainage or flooding risk.
  - Narrow, uneven, or poorly lit sidewalks.
  - Infrequent or unreliable transit despite proximity.
  - Neighborhoods that become deserted after dark.
  - Steep hills with little tree cover, increasing wind and rain exposure.

- **“Rainy‑day test” methodology**:
  - Visit on a real, steady rain day.
  - On a weekday, walk a typical errands route, noting puddles, splash, shelter, and transit wait conditions.
  - On a weekend, explore further, checking for cozy indoor “third places” (cafés, bookstores, bars) and the usability of nearby nature spots.

- **Seattle‑specific neighborhood recommendations**:
  - **Capitol Hill** – strongest transit (light rail + dense bus network); high urban density ensures many “cozy third places” and constant street activity. Best micro‑area: within ~10 min of the light‑rail station but off Broadway’s busier thoroughfare. [[entities/capitol-hill]] [[entities/seattle-light-rail]]
  - **Ballard** – flat layout, clustered amenities, decent bus service; excellent for errands and indoor comfort spots. Stay near the central commercial corridor (44th St, D‑Line). [[entities/ballard]] [[entities/44th-street]] [[entities/d-line-bus]]
  - **Fremont** – charming, quirky third‑places; slightly hillier and more spread‑out, making it less reliable in heavy rain. [[entities/fremont]]
  - **Greenwood** & **Columbia City** – quieter but still lively; watch for limited transit frequency. [[entities/greenwood]] [[entities/columbia-city]]

- **Micro‑area guidance**:
  - **Capitol Hill**: stay east/southeast of the light‑rail station; avoid steep slopes north/west of Broadway.
  - **Ballard**: stay near the core around Market & Ballard Ave; avoid far‑north/eastern stretches where transit thins.
  - **Fremont**: stay around Fremont Ave/34th St; residential hills beyond become isolated after dark.

- **Overall recommendation**: For winter transit reliability and feeling constantly connected, prioritize **Capitol Hill near the light‑rail station**; **Ballard core** is a solid secondary choice, while **Fremont** works best only if you remain in its vibrant center.
