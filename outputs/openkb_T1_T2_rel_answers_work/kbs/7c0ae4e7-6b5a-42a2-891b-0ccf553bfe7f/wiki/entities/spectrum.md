## Spectrum (Cable ISP)

Spectrum provides cable television, internet, and phone services throughout the United States, including the Austin, TX market. In the context of moving to the S Lamar & Barton Springs area (78704), Spectrum is mentioned as the **fallback internet provider** when fiber options such as [[entities/att-fiber]] or [[entities/google-fiber]] are unavailable.

### Role in Utility Setup
- Listed alongside other essential services in the [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]] as a backup option for **internet connectivity**.
- Recommended as part of the overall [[concepts/utility-setup]] and [[concepts/service-start-timeline]] to ensure continuous service on move‑in day.

### Typical Use Case
1. **Check fiber availability** via AT&T or Google Fiber using an address lookup (see [[concepts/address-lookup]]).  
2. If fiber is not present, **order service from Spectrum** to cover the internet need.
3. Include standard documentation: lease start date, ID, and payment method, similar to other utilities.
