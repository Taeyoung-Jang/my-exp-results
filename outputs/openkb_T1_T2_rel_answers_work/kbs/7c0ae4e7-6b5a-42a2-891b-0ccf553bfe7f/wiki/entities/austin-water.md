# Austin Water

**Type:** organization  
**Description:** City‑run provider of potable water and wastewater services for the Austin, Texas area.

## Overview
Austin Water (often referred to simply as “Austin Water”) delivers drinking water, manages sewer collection, and provides wastewater treatment for residents and businesses within the City of Austin. It is part of the municipal utility system that also includes [[entities/austin-energy]] for electricity.

## Role in Utility Setup
In the utility onboarding process described in [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]], Austin Water is the second service to be activated after electricity. It can be started online through the City of Austin’s unified portal at **coautilities.com** and the same customer‑care phone line (512‑494‑9400) serves both electric and water accounts. Typical required information includes:

- Lease start date
- Government‑issued ID
- Payment method
- Possible small deposit (if no credit history)

Activation can occur within a day, but the recommendation is to request service 3‑4 days before move‑in to provide a buffer, as outlined in the [[concepts/service-start-timeline]].

## Interaction with Other Services
- When water service is opened, trash and recycling pickup are automatically linked through [[entities/austin-resource-recovery]].
- Parking permits are handled separately by the [[entities/city-of-austin-parking-division]].
- Internet providers (e.g., [[entities/att-fiber]], [[entities/google-fiber]], [[entities/spectrum]]) do not depend on Austin Water but often require the same address verification step, tying into the broader [[concepts/utility-setup]].

## Common Questions
- **Do I need to coordinate with the previous tenant?**  
  No. Austin Water handles overlaps automatically; you simply schedule start‑of‑service for your move‑in date.
- **What if my building is master‑metered?**  
  In that case the property management liaises with Austin Water on your behalf.
