# Seattle Light Rail

Seattle Light Rail is a **rapid transit rail system** serving the city of [[entities/seattle]]. It provides high‑frequency, weather‑proof service that is a cornerstone for a [[concepts/car-light-lifestyle]] in the region.

## Key facts from session‑14 (2021‑06‑15)
- **Transit reliability**: The document highlights the light rail as the most dependable winter‑time transit option, especially for neighborhoods like [[entities/capitol-hill]] where rainy‑day mobility is critical.  [[concepts/transit-reliability]]
- **Neighborhood impact**: Proximity to a light‑rail station on Capitol Hill is recommended as the safest bet for staying connected during harsh winter weather, reducing dependence on cars.
- **Comparison with other transport**: While the [[entities/d-line-bus]] and other bus routes serve areas like [[entities/ballard]] and [[entities/fremont]], they are more vulnerable to traffic delays in rain, making the rail line a preferred alternative for reliable travel.
- **Walkability synergy**: The presence of the light rail enhances overall [[concepts/walkability]] of nearby districts by allowing short walks to stations and then swift, dry transit to other parts of the city.
