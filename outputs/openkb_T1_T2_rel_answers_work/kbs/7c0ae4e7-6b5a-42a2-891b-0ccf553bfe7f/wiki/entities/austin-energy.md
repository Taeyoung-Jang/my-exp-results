# Austin Energy

Austin Energy is the municipal electric utility for the City of Austin, Texas. It provides electricity to nearly all residential addresses in Austin, including the 78704 area near S Lamar Blvd & Barton Springs Rd. The utility is part of the City of Austin’s unified customer‑care portal, which also handles water service.

## Role in Utility Setup
- Primary electricity provider for the address discussed in [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]].
- Service can be started online via the City of Austin Utilities portal (coautilities.com) or by calling **512‑494‑9400**.
- Required information: lease start date, government‑issued ID, payment method, and possibly a deposit.
- Activation can occur as soon as the next business day, making it the first step in the [[concepts/service-start-timeline]].

## Interactions with Other Services
- Works alongside [[entities/austin-water]] for water and wastewater service, both managed through the same portal.
- Coordination with [[entities/texas-gas-service]] is needed only if the residence uses natural gas.
- Internet providers such as [[entities/att-fiber]], [[entities/google-fiber]], and [[entities/spectrum]] are independent but often activated after electricity and water are live.
- Trash, recycling, and compost pickup are handled by [[entities/austin-resource-recovery]] and are typically linked to the water account.
- Residential parking permits are issued by the [[entities/city-of-austin-parking-division]].
