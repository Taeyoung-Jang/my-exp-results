# D‑Line Bus

The **D‑Line Bus** is a major Seattle transit route that runs along **44th Street** and connects neighborhoods such as [[entities/ballard]], [[entities/fremont]], and [[entities/greenwood]].

## Role in a car‑light lifestyle
- Provides reliable service for residents aiming to minimize car use, especially during rainy months.  
- Supports the **[[concepts/transit-reliability]]** pillar of a **[[concepts/car-light-lifestyle]]** by offering frequent stops and coverage of key corridors.

## Connection to walkability and neighborhoods
- The D‑Line’s stops are often located within short walking distance of amenities, reinforcing **[[concepts/walkability]]** in the served areas.
- In Ballard, proximity to the D‑Line (especially near the core around Market & Ballard Ave) helps avoid the “quiet after dark” red flag noted for neighborhoods farther from transit.

## Mention in session‑14
The bus line is highlighted in the discussion of Seattle neighborhoods that pass the “rainy‑day test.” It is recommended for those staying in Ballard’s core, where the D‑Line and the **[[entities/44th-street]]** corridor keep residents connected without a car.
