# Greenwood

Greenwood is a residential neighborhood in [[entities/seattle]] that often comes up in discussions of a [[concepts/car-light-lifestyle]].  It offers a relatively quiet but still lively streetscape, making it a viable option for people who want to minimize car use while still having access to daily necessities.

## Key characteristics from the session‑14 discussion
- **Walkability**: The area scores well on [[concepts/walkability]] thanks to a compact grid of sidewalks, local cafés, and small shops that are within a short stroll of each other.
- **Transit**: While not as transit‑dense as Capitol Hill, Greenwood benefits from nearby [[entities/d-line-bus]] routes that provide reasonable service.  The document notes that transit frequency can be limited, so residents should verify schedules, especially in winter.
- **Car‑light suitability**: The neighborhood’s layout supports errands on foot, and the presence of a few grocery and food options means most daily needs can be met without a car.
- **Potential red flags**: As highlighted, some parts of Greenwood may feel isolated after dark, and the limited transit options could become a drawback during rainy evenings.

## Comparison to nearby areas
- **Columbia City**: Like Greenwood, Columbia City is mentioned as another quieter neighborhood that can work for a car‑light lifestyle, but both share the challenge of less frequent transit compared to more central districts such as [[entities/capitol-hill]].
