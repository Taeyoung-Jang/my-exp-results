# City of Austin Parking Division

**Type:** organization  
**Description:** Handles residential parking permit applications for Austin residents.

**Functions:**
- Issues and manages residential parking permits.
- Applications are submitted online through the City of Austin website.
- Requires proof of residence such as a lease agreement or a utility bill (e.g., [[entities/austin-energy]] or [[entities/austin-water]]).

**Relevance to Utility Setup:**
When moving to a new address in Austin, the parking permit process is often coordinated alongside the [[concepts/utility-setup]] checklist described in [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]]. The recommended timeline suggests applying for the permit after the lease start date is confirmed, typically a few days before move‑in, aligning with the [[concepts/service-start-timeline]] for utilities.
