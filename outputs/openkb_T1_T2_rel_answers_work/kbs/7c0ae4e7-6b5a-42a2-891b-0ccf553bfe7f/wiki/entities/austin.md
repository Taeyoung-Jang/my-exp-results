## Austin, Texas

Austin is the capital city of Texas, known for its vibrant tech scene, live music, and rapid growth. It serves as the geographic context for the utilities and services outlined in [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]]. Residents in the city typically use the following providers:

- **Electricity**: [[entities/austin-energy]] (city‑served utility)
- **Water & Wastewater**: [[entities/austin-water]] (city‑run service)
- **Natural Gas**: [[entities/texas-gas-service]] for most properties that have gas hookups
- **Internet**: [[entities/att-fiber]], [[entities/google-fiber]], and fallback [[entities/spectrum]]
- **Trash & Recycling**: managed by [[entities/austin-resource-recovery]]
- **Parking Permits**: issued by the City of Austin Parking Division

The city’s utility onboarding process is captured by the [[concepts/utility-setup]] concept, and the recommended scheduling follows the [[concepts/service-start-timeline]] framework. Residents often need to perform an [[concepts/address-lookup]] to confirm provider availability for specific apartments or complexes.

Austin’s walkability and transit options, such as the D‑Line bus, influence how newcomers prioritize services like parking permits and internet choices, linking to broader discussions in [[concepts/walkability]] and [[concepts/transit-reliability]].
