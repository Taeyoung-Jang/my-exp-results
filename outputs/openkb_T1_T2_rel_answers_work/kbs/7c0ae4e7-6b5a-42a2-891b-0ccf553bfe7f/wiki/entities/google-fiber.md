## Google Fiber

Google Fiber is a high‑speed fiber‑optic broadband product offered by Google. In the Austin, TX area—specifically near S Lamar Blvd & Barton Springs Rd (78704)—Google Fiber is commonly available alongside AT&T Fiber and Spectrum, providing residents with fast, reliable internet service.

**Key points from [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]]**:
- It is one of the three major internet options mentioned for the 78704 pocket.
- Availability can be checked via the address lookup tool at **fiber.google.com**; users may need to enter the building name or use a USPS‑formatted address if the unit number causes issues.
- For many apartments in this neighborhood, Google Fiber may be wired to specific floors or units, so checking by building name is recommended.
