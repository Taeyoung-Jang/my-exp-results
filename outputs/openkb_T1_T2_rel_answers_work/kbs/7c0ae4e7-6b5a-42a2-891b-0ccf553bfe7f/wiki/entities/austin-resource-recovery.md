# Austin Resource Recovery

Austin Resource Recovery (ARR) is the city‑run organization that provides residential trash, recycling, and compost pickup services in Austin, Texas.

## Key Facts from Session 34 (2023‑06‑15)
- ARR service generally **activates automatically** when a customer opens an Austin Water account, but residents are advised to confirm their pickup schedule.
- The utility setup checklist in the conversation recommends contacting ARR after electricity, water, gas, and internet have been arranged.
- ARR is part of the broader **utility‑setup** process for new renters in the 78704 area, alongside [[entities/austin-energy]], [[entities/austin-water]], and [[entities/texas-gas-service]].
- The recommended timeline (see [[concepts/service-start-timeline]]) suggests verifying ARR pickup days on Thursday before move‑in.
