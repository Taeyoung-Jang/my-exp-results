# Texas Gas Service

**Type:** Organization

Texas Gas Service (TGS) is the primary natural‑gas provider for most residential properties in Austin, Texas. It handles service start‑ups, transfers, and billing for homeowners and renters.

## Key Facts from Session 34 (2023‑06‑15)
- **Service area:** The 78704 ZIP code (South Lamar & Barton Springs area) typically uses Texas Gas Service for natural‑gas connections. [[entities/austin]]
- **Contact details:**
  - **Website for starting service:** https://texasgasservice.com/startservice
  - **Phone:** 800‑700‑2443
- **Required information for a new account:** address, lease start date, government ID, and sometimes a deposit.
- **Timing:** Schedule gas service 3‑5 days before move‑in to allow for any necessary technician visit. This aligns with the recommended [[concepts/service-start-timeline]] for utility activation.
- **Coordination:** No direct coordination with the previous tenant is needed; Texas Gas Service handles overlaps automatically.
