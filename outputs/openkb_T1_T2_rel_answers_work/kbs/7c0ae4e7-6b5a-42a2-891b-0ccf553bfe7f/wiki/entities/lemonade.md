## Lemonade (Insurance)

Lemonade is a US‑based insurance company that uses an AI‑driven platform to sell renters, homeowners, and pet policies. It is known for its fast digital onboarding, transparent pricing, and charitable giving model where a portion of premiums is donated to causes chosen by policyholders.

### Mention in Session 34

In the utility‑setup checklist for a new renter in Austin (see [[summaries/session-34_2023-06-15]]), Lemonade is listed as one of the recommended national providers for renter’s insurance, alongside [[entities/state-farm]] and [[entities/usaa]]. The assistant suggests signing up the same day as the other utilities to ensure coverage starts on move‑in day.

### Why Renters Might Choose Lemonade

- Fully online application and claim filing.
- Quick policy issuance, often within minutes.
- Charitable component that donates unclaimed premiums.
- Competitive rates for renters in many markets, including Austin, TX.
