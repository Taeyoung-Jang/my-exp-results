## 44th Street

44th Street is a major east‑west thoroughfare in Seattle’s Ballard neighborhood.  It runs through the commercial core where most grocery stores, cafés, restaurants, and the D‑Line bus corridor are clustered, making it a key reference point for residents seeking a walkable, car‑light lifestyle.
