# Session 40 – Daycare Enrollment Checklist & Contract Review (2024‑10‑04)

## Key Takeaways
- A printable **daycare vs. nanny‑share checklist** covering routines, safety, communication, and paperwork.
- A short **tour script** and polite phrasing for asking about staff turnover.
- A **contract‑gotchas checklist** organized by fees/billing, schedule & closures, illness/health, termination/notice, and other policies.
- Added **start‑date logistics** (drop‑off routine, labeling, comfort items) and sample lines for requesting small exceptions.
- A friendly **email template** to the director for post‑enrollment clarification.

## Core Concepts
- [[concepts/childcare-selection]] – evaluating daycare and nanny‑share options.  
- [[concepts/daycare-checklist]] – questions on daily routines, safety, communication, and environment.  
- nanny‑share questions – schedule alignment, safety, expectations, and communication (plain text).  
- [[concepts/contract-gotchas]] – hidden fees, notice periods, closure policies, health exclusions, and rate‑increase clauses.  
- [[concepts/start-date-logistics]] – first‑day drop‑off, labeling, supplies, and comfort‑item handling.  
- [[concepts/turnover-red-flags]] – normal turnover vs. concerning staff changes.  
- [[concepts/parental-email-template]] – concise, non‑anxious follow‑up email to the director.

## Practical Artifacts
- **Printable checklist** with checkboxes for daycare, nanny share, paperwork, and start‑date items.  
- **Tour script** to use during on‑site visits.  
- **Email draft** to confirm tuition timing, closure notifications, contact person, and transition plans.  
- **Exception request lines** for later pickup or special food/breast‑milk needs.

These resources aim to help parents navigate enrollment, avoid common contract pitfalls, and ensure a smooth first day at a new childcare setting.
