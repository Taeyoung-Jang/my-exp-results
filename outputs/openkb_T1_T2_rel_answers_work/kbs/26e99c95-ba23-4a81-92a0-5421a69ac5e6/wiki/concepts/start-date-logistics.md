# Start‑Date Logistics

**Overview**

Start‑Date Logistics covers the practical steps parents need to take for a smooth first day at a daycare or nanny‑share. It includes the drop‑off routine, labeling requirements, what to pack versus what stays at the center, handling of comfort items, and how to request small exceptions (e.g., later pickup or special food).

---

## Key Elements

### 1. First‑day drop‑off routine
- Confirm the exact drop‑off location and any check‑in process.
- Know who will receive your child and where you should wait.
- Ask if a brief “goodbye” is encouraged and how long you may stay.

### 2. Labeling requirements
- All bottles, cups, clothing, sleep gear, and personal items must be clearly labeled with the child’s name.
- Use waterproof labels or permanent marker as recommended by the center.

### 3. Packing vs. staying at school
- **Daily pack:** diaper bag, spare change of clothes, any medication (with a signed form), and a comfort item if allowed.
- **Stay‑at‑school items:** blankets, sleep sacks, or extra toys that the center can keep in a designated cubby.

### 4. Comfort items
- Ask the center’s policy on comfort objects (e.g., lovey, pacifier). Some locations allow them during nap time but keep them stored otherwise.
- Verify how the item is cleaned and whether it must be labeled.

### 5. Breast‑milk / special food handling
- Confirm storage procedures (refrigerator vs. cooler) and who prepares the bottle.
- Ask about any labeling needed for milk or food containers.
- If you need to bring a non‑standard food item, request permission and note any labeling or storage rules.

### 6. Requesting small exceptions
- Use polite, concise language when asking for a one‑time later pickup or a special food item. Example phrasing:
  - “I totally understand if this isn’t possible, but could we arrange a later pickup on [date]?”
  - “Would it be okay to bring [breast milk/food] this week if I label it clearly and follow your storage rules?”
