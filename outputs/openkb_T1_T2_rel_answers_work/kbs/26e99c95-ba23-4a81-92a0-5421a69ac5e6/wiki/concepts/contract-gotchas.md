# Contract Gotchas

**Definition**:  A set of frequently overlooked clauses and hidden fees in daycare enrollment contracts that can lead to unexpected costs, schedule conflicts, or policy misunderstandings if not identified early.

---

## Core Areas of Concern

### 1. Fees & Billing
- **Tuition amount and due date** – Verify the exact monthly amount and the calendar day it is due.
- **Late‑payment penalties** – Note when a payment is considered “late” and the fee structure.
- **Deposit handling** – Confirm whether the enrollment deposit is refundable or credited to future tuition.
- **Additional charges** – Look for fees for late pickup, returned payments, missing paperwork, extra days, early drop‑off, or extended hours.
- **Annual registration/materials fees** – Identify one‑time or recurring costs beyond tuition.
- **Rate‑increase clause** – Understand how and when tuition can be raised, and the notice period required.
- **Tuition during closures** – Check if tuition is still owed for holidays, weather closures, or staff‑training days.

### 2. Schedule & Closures
- **Holiday and planned closure list** – Ensure all school‑wide shutdowns (holidays, teacher‑training, retreat days) are documented.
- **Weather/emergency closures** – Look for the policy on unexpected closures and whether tuition is prorated.
- **Daily hours** – Confirm any seasonal hour changes and arrival/pick‑up windows.

### 3. Illness & Health
- **Return‑to‑care rules** – Specific waiting periods after fever, vomiting, diarrhea, or antibiotics.
- **Immunization requirements** – Frequency of updates and required documentation.
- **Medication policy** – What can be administered on‑site and required parental permissions.
- **Injury reporting** – How minor injuries are communicated and the timeline for incident reports.
- **Contagious‑illness protocols** – Guidelines for conditions like conjunctivitis, hand‑foot‑mouth, RSV, etc.
- **Food & allergy rules** – Policies on outside food, labeling, and allergy management.

### 4. Termination & Notice
- **Withdrawal notice period** – Exact length of notice (often a full month) and when the notice clock starts.
- **Tuition obligation during notice** – Whether tuition is owed regardless of attendance.
- **Center‑initiated termination** – Conditions under which the center can end enrollment.
- **Spot‑holding for extended leave** – Rules for holding a child’s place during family vacations or prolonged absences.

### 5. Other Key Policies
- **Photo/video permissions** – How images are used and opt‑out procedures.
- **Communication expectations** – Preferred apps or methods for daily updates.
- **Supplies & gear** – What parents must provide (e.g., nap gear, diapers) versus what the center supplies.
- **Incident reporting** – Formal process for documenting accidents and how quickly parents receive reports.
- **Classroom transitions** – Timeline and criteria for moving a child to the next age group.

### 6. Start‑Date Logistics (linked to overall onboarding)
- **First‑day drop‑off routine** – Where to go and who to check‑in with.
- **Labeling requirements** – Bottles, clothing, and comfort items must be clearly labeled.
- **Packing guidelines** – What should be packed daily versus what can stay at the center.
- **Comfort items** – Policy on blankets, loveys, and handling them throughout the day.
- **Breast‑milk/food handling** – Storage, heating, and labeling rules.

---

## Why It Matters
Understanding these contract gotchas helps parents avoid surprise charges, ensures clear expectations about health and safety protocols, and supports smoother communication with the daycare team. Proactively addressing each area reduces stress and builds trust with the provider.
