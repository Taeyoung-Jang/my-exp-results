# Daycare Checklist

The **Daycare Checklist** is a practical, copy‑paste‑ready list of questions, observations, and paperwork items parents can use when evaluating a daycare center. It groups the most important topics—daily routines, safety protocols, communication practices, environment, and required policies—into clear checklist items that help ensure nothing is overlooked before committing to a provider.

## Core Sections (from [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-04]])

### 1. Daily Routines
- Ask about the typical schedule: naps, meals, outdoor time, and transitions.
- Inquire how the center supports children who struggle with naps or transitions.
- Verify staff‑to‑child ratios throughout the day.

### 2. Safety & Health
- Confirm background checks, CPR/first‑aid training, and staff vetting processes.
- Review illness policies, return‑to‑care guidelines, and medication handling.
- Check emergency and evacuation procedures, and how injuries are reported.

### 3. Communication
- Determine how often updates are sent (e.g., daily notes, photos, app alerts).
- Ask how incidents or behavior concerns are communicated to parents.
- Identify the primary point of contact for day‑to‑day questions.

### 4. Environment & Behavior
- Observe cleanliness, noise level, and overall atmosphere.
- Ask how behavior challenges and emotional support are handled.
- Verify policies on mixed‑age groups and classroom transitions.

### 5. Paperwork & Policies to Request
- Illness policy and symptom guidelines.
- Immunization requirements.
- Late‑pickup policy and fees.
- Payment schedule, autopay options, and refund rules.
- Holiday closures, staff‑training days, and unexpected closure notifications.
- Discipline, behavior guidance, and incident‑reporting policies.

### 6. Start‑Date Logistics (see [[concepts/start-date-logistics]])
- Confirm first‑day drop‑off routine and labeling requirements.
- Identify what items should be packed daily versus kept at the center.
- Clarify comfort‑item handling and breast‑milk/food storage procedures.

## Red Flags & Turnover Concerns
- High staff turnover without clear explanation (related to [[concepts/turnover-red-flags]]).
- Vague or defensive answers about safety or emergency procedures.
- Reluctance to allow drop‑ins or tours.
- Chaotic or understaffed appearance of the facility.

## How to Use the Checklist
1. Print or copy the list into a notes app.
2. Mark each item as you ask questions or observe the environment.
3. Follow up on any unanswered or concerning points before signing a contract.
