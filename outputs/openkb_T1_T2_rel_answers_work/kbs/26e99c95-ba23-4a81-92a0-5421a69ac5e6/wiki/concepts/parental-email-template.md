# Parental Email Template

**Definition**: A short, polite email framework parents can use to clarify contract details, logistics, or request small exceptions without sounding anxious or nitpicky.

## Purpose
- Confirm key administrative points after signing a daycare contract.
- Build a positive relationship with the director by being clear and courteous.
- Provide a reusable structure that can be adapted for different queries (fees, closures, turnover, start‑date logistics, etc.).

## Core Elements (derived from [[summaries/session-40_2024-10-04]])
1. **Greeting** – address the director by name.
2. **Brief context** – mention recent enrollment and excitement.
3. **Specific questions** – list 2‑3 items (e.g., tuition due dates, closure notifications, point of contact).
4. **Optional clarification** – ask about classroom transitions or other policies.
5. **Polite closing** – thank them and express looking forward to joining the community.

## Sample Template
```
Hi [Director’s Name],

Now that we’re officially enrolled, I just wanted to check a couple of small details so we’re fully set for the first day.

- Could you confirm when tuition is considered “late” and how you notify families about unexpected closures?
- Who will be our main point of contact for day‑to‑day updates once we start?
- I’d also love to know how classroom transitions usually work as kids move up through the program.

No rush at all – just making sure we’re aligned on the basics. Thanks so much, and we’re really excited to join the community!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## When to Use It
- After **contract signing** to verify billing cycles and notice policies (see [[concepts/contract-gotchas]]).
- When arranging **start‑date logistics** such as drop‑off routines or labeling items (see [[concepts/start-date-logistics]]).
- To ask about **staff turnover** in a neutral way (see [[concepts/turnover-red-flags]]).
- For any **minor exception requests** (later pickup, special food) while keeping tone friendly.

## Adaptation Tips
- Keep the email **short** (3‑4 lines of questions). 
- Use **bullet points** for clarity.
- Phrase requests as **inquiries** rather than demands.
- End with a **positive note** about looking forward to the program.
