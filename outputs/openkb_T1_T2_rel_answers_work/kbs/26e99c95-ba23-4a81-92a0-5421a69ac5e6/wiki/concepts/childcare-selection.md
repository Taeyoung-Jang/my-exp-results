# Childcare Selection

**Childcare Selection** is the process of comparing and deciding between different early‑childhood care arrangements—such as licensed daycares, home‑based centers, and nanny‑share agreements—to find the best fit for a family’s needs, values, and budget.

## Core Evaluation Areas

1. **Daily Routines** – Understand how the provider structures the day: nap schedules, meals, outdoor time, transitions, and support for children who struggle with any of these.
2. **Safety & Staffing** – Review staff‑to‑child ratios, background‑check procedures, emergency plans, medication policies, and how the provider handles injuries or illnesses.
3. **Communication** – Determine the frequency and method of updates (apps, daily notes, emails), incident reporting, and the openness of staff to discuss concerns.
4. **Environment** – Observe cleanliness, noise level, space layout, and how mixed‑age groups are managed.
5. **Turnover Insight** – Ask about staff stability using neutral language to gauge whether turnover is typical or a red flag. See [[concepts/turnover-red-flags]] for details.
6. **Contractual Details** – Identify hidden fees, notice periods, closure policies, health exclusions, and rate‑increase clauses. Refer to [[concepts/contract-gotchas]] for a checklist.
7. **Paperwork & Policies** – Request the illness policy, immunization requirements, late‑pickup fees, payment terms, and any other required documentation.
8. **Start‑Date Logistics** – Plan the first‑day drop‑off routine, labeling, supplies, and comfort‑item handling. See [[concepts/start-date-logistics]].

## Decision Tools

- **Daycare vs. Nanny‑Share Checklist** – Use the comprehensive list of questions and items in [[concepts/daycare-checklist]] to compare providers side‑by‑side.
- **Tour Script** – A short, focused set of questions to ask during an in‑person visit, ensuring you cover routines, safety, communication, and environment.
- **Follow‑Up Email** – After enrollment, a polite email template (see [[concepts/parental-email-template]]) helps clarify any lingering contract or logistical questions.
