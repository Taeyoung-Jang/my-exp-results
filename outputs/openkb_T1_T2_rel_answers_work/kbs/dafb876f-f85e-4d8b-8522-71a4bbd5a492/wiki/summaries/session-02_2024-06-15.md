# Summary of session-02_2024-06-15

**Main topic:** How to find and confirm up‑to‑date local event information, with a focus on Seattle.

## Key Points
- Reliable sources for event dates are official city or venue calendars, major local news outlets, and the organizers’ own pages. This aligns with [[concepts/event-calendar-reliability]].
- Cross‑checking a week before an event helps avoid last‑minute changes and follows the [[concepts/date-verification-workflow]].
- Suggested Seattle‑wide calendars:
  - [[entities/visit-seattle]] (city tourism site) – broad festivals & family‑friendly daytime events.
  - [[entities/seattle-times]] events page – news‑driven listings.
  - [[entities/the-stranger]] events – indie gigs & nightlife.
  - [[entities/seattle-met]] events – curated larger happenings.
- Recommended venue calendars:
  - [[entities/stg-presents]] (Paramount, Moore, Neptune) – accurate live‑music schedules.
  - [[entities/seattle-center]] calendar – many festivals, expos, family‑oriented activities.
- Practical tip: add calendar reminders linking to the official event page and re‑verify a week before the date.

## Concepts for Cross‑Document Pages
- [[concepts/event-calendar-reliability]] – criteria for trustworthy event listings.
- [[concepts/date-verification-workflow]] – steps to confirm event dates before planning.
- [[concepts/family-friendly-daytime-events]] – sources and filters for kid‑appropriate outings.
- live‑music venue calendars – how venue‑specific calendars differ from city‑wide listings (mentioned as plain text because no dedicated concept page exists).

## Actionable Guidance
1. Start with a city‑wide calendar ([[entities/visit-seattle]] or [[entities/seattle-center]]) to discover events.
2. Click through to the organizer’s page for the most current date/time.
3. Add a reminder in your personal calendar with the link.
4. Re‑check the event a week before attending.
