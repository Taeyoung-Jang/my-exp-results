## Visit Seattle

Visit Seattle is the official tourism organization for the city of [[entities/seattle]]. It maintains a comprehensive online events calendar (https://visitseattle.org/events) that aggregates festivals, cultural activities, and community happenings across the Seattle area.

### Role in Event Planning
- Recommended in [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]] as a primary source for reliable, up‑to‑date event listings.
- Especially useful for finding **family‑friendly daytime events**, as it highlights festivals, cultural days, and community programs that cater to families.
- Serves as a key component of a robust [[concepts/date-verification-workflow]]: users can start here, then cross‑check dates on organizers’ pages before finalizing plans.

### Reliability Factors
Visit Seattle scores high on [[concepts/event-calendar-reliability]] because:
1. It is maintained by the city’s tourism department, which receives direct updates from event organizers.
2. The calendar is refreshed frequently, reducing the risk of outdated information.
3. It links out to official event pages, enabling quick verification of dates and details.

### Connections to Other Resources
- Comparable city‑wide calendars include those of [[entities/seattle-times]], [[entities/the-stranger]], and [[entities/seattle-met]].
- Venue‑specific calendars such as [[entities/stg-presents]] (covers Paramount, Moore, and Neptune theatres) and [[entities/seattle-center]] focus on concerts and large‑scale events.

### Practical Tips
- **Start** your event search with Visit Seattle to capture a broad view of upcoming activities.
- **Filter** for family‑oriented daytime options using the site’s categories.
- **Set reminders** with links to the official event page and **re‑check** a week before the event as part of the [[concepts/date-verification-workflow]].
