# The Stranger (Organization)

**What it is**: The Stranger is an alternative weekly newspaper based in Seattle that publishes news, culture, and a comprehensive events calendar, especially known for covering live music, nightlife, and indie gigs.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-02_2024-06-15]]**:
- Recommended as one of the most reliable Seattle‑focused event calendars for up‑to‑date listings.
- Particularly useful for discovering live‑music shows and nightlife events.
- Suggested alongside other reliable sources such as [[entities/visit-seattle]], [[entities/seattle-times]], [[entities/seattle-met]], and venue calendars like [[entities/stg-presents]].

**Why it matters**:
- Aligns with the concept of [[concepts/event-calendar-reliability]] by providing frequently refreshed event information.
- Users are advised to follow the date verification workflow ([[concepts/date-verification-workflow]]) by checking The Stranger’s listings and then confirming details on the organizer’s official page.
- While not primarily a family‑focused source, it complements [[concepts/family-friendly-daytime-events]] by offering a broader view of Seattle’s cultural scene.

**Related entities**:

- [[entities/seattle-times]] – newspaper event listings.
- [[entities/seattle-met]] – magazine event guide.
- [[entities/stg-presents]] – venue group (Paramount, Moore, Neptune theaters).
