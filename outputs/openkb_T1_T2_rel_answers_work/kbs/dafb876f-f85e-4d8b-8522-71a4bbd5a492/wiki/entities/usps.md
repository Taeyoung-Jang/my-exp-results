## United States Postal Service (USPS)

The United States Postal Service (USPS) is the federal agency responsible for providing postal service in the United States. It operates a nationwide network of post offices, sorting facilities, and delivery routes, handling billions of pieces of mail each year.

### Role in Moving & Address Changes
When relocating to a new state, the first recommended step is to **forward your mail** through USPS. This service ensures that any mail sent to your old address is automatically redirected to your new residence, preventing loss of important documents and communications during the transition.

The process is straightforward and does not require any special documentation beyond providing the old and new addresses. In the context of the moving checklist from [[summaries/session-39_2025-02-15]], USPS forwarding is listed as the initial action before gathering other documents.
