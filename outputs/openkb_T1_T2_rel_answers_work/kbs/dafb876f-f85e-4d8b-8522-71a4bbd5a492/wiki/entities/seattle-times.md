# Seattle Times

Seattle Times is a leading daily newspaper serving the Seattle metropolitan area. It maintains an **events listings page** that is frequently updated and is recommended as a reliable source for local event information.

## Key Facts from Session 02 (2024‑06‑15)
- The assistant highlighted the Seattle Times events page (https://www.seattletimes.com/entertainment/events) as one of the top **[[concepts/event-calendar-reliability]]** resources for checking festival, concert, and nightlife dates.
