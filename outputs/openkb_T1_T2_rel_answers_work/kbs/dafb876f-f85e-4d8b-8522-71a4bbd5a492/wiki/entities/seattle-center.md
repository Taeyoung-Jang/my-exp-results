# Seattle Center

Seattle Center is a large public campus in the heart of [[entities/seattle]] that hosts museums, festivals, conventions, and performance venues. It maintains an official, regularly updated events calendar that is recommended for finding **family‑friendly daytime activities** and larger community gatherings.

## Key Points from session‑02_2024-06-15

- The calendar URL (https://www.seattlecenter.com/events) was provided as a direct link for quick lookup.
