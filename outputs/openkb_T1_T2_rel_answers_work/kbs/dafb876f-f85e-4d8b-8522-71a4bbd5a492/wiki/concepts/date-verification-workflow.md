# Date Verification Workflow

**Definition**: A systematic approach to ensure that the dates and times of planned events are accurate and up‑to‑date before committing to attendance.

## Why It Matters
In the *session-02_2024-06-15* conversation, the assistant highlighted that event information can become outdated quickly, especially for festivals, concerts, and museum late‑night events. Verifying dates mitigates the risk of scheduling conflicts or showing up to a cancelled event.

## Core Steps
1. **Identify a Reliable Source**
   - Start with an official or frequently updated calendar such as [[entities/visit-seattle]], [[entities/seattle-times]], [[entities/the-stranger]], [[entities/seattle-met]], or venue sites like [[entities/stg-presents]] and [[entities/seattle-center]].
2. **Cross‑Check the Organizer’s Page**
   - Follow the event link to the organizer’s own website or ticketing page (e.g., Eventbrite, Facebook Events) to confirm the listed date and time.
3. **Create a Calendar Reminder**
   - Add the event to your personal calendar with a direct hyperlink to the official page. This makes re‑verification easy.
4. **Re‑Verify Close to the Event**
   - Perform a final check about a week before the event; many venues (especially live‑music venues like the [[entities/paramount-theater]], [[entities/moore-theater]], and [[entities/neptune-theater]]) update schedules last‑minute.
5. **Document Any Changes**
   - If the date or time has shifted, update your calendar entry and, if needed, notify any companions.
