# Family Home Agreement (work)

**Type:** work

**Created:** June 2022 (session‑10_2022‑06‑15)

**Purpose:** A friendly, printable one‑page agreement that helps families living together across generations set clear, flexible expectations for shared spaces, quiet hours, guest etiquette, and conflict handling.

**Key elements**
- **Shared spaces** – Simple bullet‑point rules for the kitchen and living‑room to keep them tidy and respectful. [[concepts/shared-spaces]]
- **Kitchen reset** – Everyone cleans up after use; includes a line for grandparents’ preferred kitchen time. [[concepts/household-norms]]
- **Quiet hours** – Two optional time windows that families can choose to balance early sleepers and night‑owls. [[concepts/household-norms]]
- **Guest and overnight policy** – Heads‑up etiquette rather than formal permission. [[concepts/household-norms]]
- **Weekly check‑in** – A brief agenda for a five‑minute family meeting each week. [[concepts/household-norms]]
- **Conflict handling** – A calm four‑step process (pause, express feelings, listen, adjust). [[concepts/conflict-resolution]]

**Relation to source**: This work is the concrete output of the conversation captured in [[summaries/session-10_2022-06-15]].

**Usage**: Families can print the layout, fill in specific times (e.g., quiet‑hours window, grandparents’ kitchen slot), names, and dates, and then display it in a common area as a living document.
