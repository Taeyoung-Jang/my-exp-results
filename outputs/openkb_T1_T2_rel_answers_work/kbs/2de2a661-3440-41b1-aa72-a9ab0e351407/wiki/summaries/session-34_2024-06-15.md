## Summary of Session 34 (2024‑06‑15)

This conversation provides concrete, low‑effort **anchor routines** to help someone who has just moved into a new space build a sense of home through daily structure.

### Key Ideas
- **Small, repeatable moments** are more sustainable than trying to overhaul a life at once.  
- **Anchor routines** are brief morning and evening rituals (5–10 minutes) that signal the start and end of the day, creating mental framing and comfort. [[concepts/anchor-routine]]
- **Place‑based habits** tie a specific activity to a specific spot, reinforcing a feeling of ownership over the new environment. [[concepts/place-based-habit]]
- **Low‑energy variations** ensure the routines stay doable on days when motivation is low, preventing the habit from breaking. [[concepts/low-energy-variation]]

### Morning Anchor (5–10 min)
1. Make a warm drink (tea, coffee, or hot water).  
2. Sit in a chosen spot (window seat, favorite chair).  
3. Take five slow breaths.  
4. Spend a minute noticing the room or view.  
5. Identify one task you want to accomplish today.

**Low‑energy version**: drink water, sit briefly, set a gentle intention.

### Evening Anchor (5–10 min)
1. Dim the lights.  
2. Play a soft song or switch to quiet mode.  
3. Tidy one small surface for a minute.  
4. Sit or lie down and take five slow breaths.  
5. Put the phone away or set it to “do not disturb”.

**Low‑energy version**: dim lights, tidy a single item, sit quietly for 30 seconds before bed.
