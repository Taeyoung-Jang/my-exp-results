# Summary of Session 10 – Family Home Agreement (2022‑06‑15)

**Document type:** Printable agreement for a multi‑generational household.  

**Entity:** [[entities/family-home-agreement]]

## Core ideas
- **Shared spaces** – Guidelines for the kitchen and living‑room to keep them orderly and respectful. [[concepts/shared-spaces]]
- **Kitchen reset** – Everyone cleans and returns items after use; includes a slot for grandparents’ preferred kitchen time.
- **Living‑room/TV use** – First‑come, first‑serve with emphasis on early‑evening group viewing and low‑volume or headphones later at night.
- **Quiet hours** – Flexible window (choose A or B) to balance early sleepers and night‑owls.
- **Guest and overnight policy** – Simple heads‑up etiquette rather than formal permission.
- **Weekly check‑in** – Five‑minute family meeting to surface what works, what doesn’t, and one tweak.
- **Conflict handling** – Calm four‑step process: pause, express feelings, listen, agree on a small adjustment. [[concepts/conflict-resolution]]

## Practical output
- A one‑page bullet‑point layout with blank lines for families to fill in exact times, names, and dates.
- Friendly wording that avoids legal language while still setting clear expectations.

## Relevance
The agreement illustrates how **household norms** can be co‑created to support **privacy**, **independence**, and **respect** across generations. [[concepts/household-norms]]
