# United States

The United States is a federal republic located in North America, consisting of 50 states, the District of Columbia, and several territories. It establishes legal categories such as **U.S. citizen** and **U.S. national**, which are referenced in many official forms and documentation.

## Relevance to session‑06_2020‑06‑15
- The guidance in [[summaries/session-06_2020-06-15]] advises applicants to match their answers to the terminology used by the United States government when reporting [[concepts/citizenship-status-reporting]].
- It clarifies the distinction (or practical equivalence) between "U.S. citizen" and "U.S. national" as outlined in [[concepts/us-citizen-vs-national]].
- Consistency across job applications, school records, and other paperwork is framed as [[concepts/form-consistency]], which relies on the government‑defined categories.
