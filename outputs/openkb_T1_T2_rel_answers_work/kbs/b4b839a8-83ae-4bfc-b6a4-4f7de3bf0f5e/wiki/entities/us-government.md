# U.S. Government

The U.S. Government is the federal authority responsible for defining and administering citizenship, immigration status, and related documentation for residents and nationals of the United States.

## Relevance to session‑06_2020-06-15

In the conversation summary [[summaries/session-06_2020-06-15]], the assistant advises users to align their answers on forms with the terminology used by the issuing agency, which is typically a branch of the U.S. Government (e.g., the Department of State for passports, USCIS for immigration status). The guidance emphasizes:

- Matching the response to the exact wording required by the form, a practice promoted by [[concepts/form-consistency]].
- Reporting citizenship or immigration status truthfully, reflecting the principles of [[concepts/citizenship-status-reporting]].
- Understanding the distinction between "U.S. citizen" and "U.S. national," a distinction clarified by [[concepts/us-citizen-vs-national]].

## Key Functions (pertinent to the document)

- **Issuing official documents** such as passports, naturalization certificates, and immigration approval notices.
- **Defining status categories** used on job applications, school records, and government forms.
- **Providing verification procedures** when agencies request proof of citizenship or immigration status.
