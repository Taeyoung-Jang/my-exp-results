# Human Resources

Human Resources (HR) is the organizational unit responsible for hiring, onboarding, and employee lifecycle management. In the context of **session-31_2023-06-15**, HR provides guidance on completing new‑hire forms and ensures that critical data fields are consistent and accurate.

## Key Functions Mentioned
- **Onboarding guidance**: HR advises new hires to verify that their legal name, address, dates, and government‑issued identifiers (e.g., Social Security number, Alien Registration Number) match across all documents.
- **Form consistency**: HR recommends a simple checklist that aligns with the principles of [[concepts/form-consistency]].
- **Legal name usage**: HR stresses using the exact name from government ID, reflecting the practice described in [[concepts/legal-name-usage]].
- **Address handling**: Minor formatting differences (e.g., "Apt" vs "Unit") are acceptable as long as the location is clear, tying into [[concepts/address-formatting]].
- **Immigration identifiers**: HR notes the importance of copying numbers like the Alien Registration Number directly from the green card, connecting to [[concepts/immigration-identifiers]].

## Interaction with Other Entities
- Works with the **Social Security Administration** for SSN verification (see [[entities/social-security-administration]]).
- Coordinates with **USCIS** when immigration‑related identifiers are required (see [[entities/uscis]] and [[entities/green-card]]).
