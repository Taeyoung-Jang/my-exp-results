# Social Security Administration

The **Social Security Administration (SSA)** is a United States federal agency responsible for issuing Social Security Numbers (SSNs) and administering Social Security benefits. It is a key point of reference when new‑hire onboarding forms request your SSN.

## Role in Onboarding
- New employees are asked to provide their SSN for payroll and tax reporting. The SSA‑issued number must be copied **exactly** as it appears on the Social Security card to avoid common errors such as digit transposition or dropping leading zeros.
- Consistency of the SSN across all onboarding documents is essential for smooth processing. This ties directly into the broader practice of [[concepts/form-consistency]].

## Common Pitfalls Mentioned in [[summaries/session-31_2023-06-15]]
- **Transposing digits** or altering the formatting of the SSN.
- Confusing the SSN with other government‑issued identifiers, such as the **Alien Registration Number** managed by [[entities/uscis]].
- Failing to ensure the SSN matches the version on the card, which can cause delays in verification by [[entities/human-resources]].

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- **Legal name usage**: The SSN must be linked to the exact legal name as shown on government ID, aligning with [[concepts/legal-name-usage]].
- **Address formatting**: While minor address variations are acceptable, the SSN field itself should remain unchanged, supporting the principles in [[concepts/address-formatting]].
- **Immigration identifiers**: For non‑citizens, the SSA works alongside immigration‑related numbers (e.g., A‑Number) overseen by [[entities/uscis]], highlighted in [[concepts/immigration-identifiers]].

## Related Entities
- [[entities/green-card]] – often referenced alongside the SSN when providing immigration‑related identifiers.
- [[entities/united-states]] and [[entities/us-government]] – the SSA operates under the broader U.S. federal government structure.
