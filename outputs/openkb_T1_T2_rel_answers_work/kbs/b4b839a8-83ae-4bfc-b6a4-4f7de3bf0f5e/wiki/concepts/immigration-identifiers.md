# Immigration Identifiers

## Definition
Immigration identifiers are government‑issued numbers that uniquely identify a non‑citizen or a person with immigration status when completing onboarding or background‑check paperwork.

## Core Identifiers Mentioned in [[summaries/session-31_2023-06-15]]
- **Alien Registration Number (A‑Number)** – found on the [[entities/green-card]]; must be copied exactly as printed.
- **USCIS Online Account Number** – a separate identifier sometimes requested by HR; obtain from the USCIS portal.
- **I‑94 Arrival/Departure Record Number** – required for some verification forms.
- **Passport Number** – taken from the passport document.
- **Driver’s License Number** – from the state‑issued driver’s license.
- **Social Security Number (SSN)** – issued by the [[entities/social-security-administration]].
- **Professional or license registration numbers** – e.g., medical, legal, or engineering licenses, copied from the official license card.

## Importance for [[concepts/form-consistency]]
Accurate transcription of these identifiers is crucial for maintaining consistency across all onboarding documents. Even a single transposition error or omitted leading zero can cause processing delays or background‑check failures.

## Common Mix‑ups
- Confusing the **A‑Number** with the **USCIS card number** printed elsewhere on the green card.
- Transposing digits or adding/removing leading zeros.
- Substituting a different USCIS identifier (e.g., using the Online Account Number when the form asks for the A‑Number).

## Best Practices
1. **Use the original document** – keep the physical or digital copy of each ID open while filling the form.
2. **Copy exactly** – do not re‑format, add spaces, or remove characters.
3. **Cross‑check** – after completing the form, compare each entered identifier with the source document.
4. **Ask for clarification** – if a form’s wording is ambiguous, contact [[entities/human-resources]] for guidance.
