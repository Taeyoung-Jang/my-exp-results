# Summary of session-31_2023-06-15

## Overview
This conversation provides practical advice for filling out new‑hire onboarding paperwork, focusing on the sections most often misunderstood and offering a concise checklist to ensure data consistency across all forms.

## Commonly Overlooked Sections
- **Identity verification** – name must match the government ID exactly. [[concepts/legal-name-usage]]
- **Tax forms** – filing status and withholding choices often cause confusion. 
- **Background‑check questions** – minor disclosures can be missed; answer only what is asked. 

## Consistency Checklist
Ensure the following fields are identical across every document:
- Full legal name (including middle name/initial if on ID)  
- Current home address (same location; minor formatting differences are acceptable) – see [[concepts/address-formatting]]  
- Date of birth  
- Social Security Number (SSN) – see [[entities/social-security-administration]]  
- Employee ID (if already assigned)  
- Start/availability dates  

Read each form once to fill it, then review with fresh eyes to catch contradictions.

## Legal Name vs. Full Name
- When a field says **"legal name"**, copy the exact representation from your government ID.  
- When it says **"full name"**, it’s safest to use the same legal version to keep everything aligned. [[concepts/legal-name-usage]]

## Address Formatting
- Variations such as **"Apt"** vs **"Unit"**, or abbreviations like **"St"** vs **"Street"**, are generally fine as long as the address is clearly the same place. [[concepts/address-formatting]]
- For maximum consistency, use the version printed on a government‑issued document (e.g., driver’s license).

## Immigration Identifiers (Background‑Check Section)
- **Alien Registration Number (A‑Number)** – copy directly from your green card. [[entities/green-card]]
- Other identifiers that require exact transcription:
  - USCIS Online Account Number – see [[entities/uscis]]
  - I‑94 number
  - Passport number
  - Driver’s‑license number
  - Professional/license registration numbers  

These fields belong to the broader concept of government‑issued identifiers. [[concepts/immigration-identifiers]]

## Common Mix‑ups to Watch For
- Confusing the A‑Number with other USCIS numbers printed on the card.  
- Transposing digits or adding/removing leading zeros.  
- Using a card number instead of the requested identifier.

## Recommendations
1. Keep a **master copy** of your key IDs (driver’s license, passport, green card) beside you while completing forms.
2. Copy numbers **exactly** as they appear—no re‑formatting.
3. If unsure which identifier a form requests, ask [[entities/human-resources]] for clarification.
4. Apply the consistency checklist before submitting the packet. [[concepts/form-consistency]]
