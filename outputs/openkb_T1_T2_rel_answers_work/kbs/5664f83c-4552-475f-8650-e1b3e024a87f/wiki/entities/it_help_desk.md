# IT Help Desk

**Type:** Organization

The IT Help Desk is the hospital's central resource for troubleshooting technology problems, such as badge‑scanner malfunctions or EHR access issues.  In the night‑shift rollout plan described in [[summaries/session-38_2025-09-15]], the IT Help Desk is listed as the primary contact for technical problems or login trouble during go‑live nights.

## Key Roles in the Document
- **Technical escalation point**: Staff are directed to call the IT Help Desk for any issues that the night champion cannot resolve.
- **Support for new systems**: Provides assistance during the rollout of new badge scanners and EHR updates, ensuring that urgent problems do not halt patient care.
