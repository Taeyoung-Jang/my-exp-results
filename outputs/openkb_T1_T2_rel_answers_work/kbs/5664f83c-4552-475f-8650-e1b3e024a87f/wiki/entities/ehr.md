## Electronic Health Record (EHR)

The **Electronic Health Record (EHR)** is a digital product used by hospitals to store, retrieve, and manage patient health information and support clinical workflows. In the context of *session-38_2025-09-15*, the EHR is one of the key technologies being rolled out to night‑shift staff, requiring focused training, support structures, and concise documentation to avoid workflow disruption.

### Key Facts from Session 38 (2025‑09‑15)
- **Change focus**: The session discusses a go‑live rollout of a new EHR interface alongside a badge scanner update.
- **Training approach**: Emphasizes short, hands‑on walk‑throughs at shift start, night‑shift champions, and quick reference guides to help staff adapt without slowing care delivery. [[concepts/night_shift_training]]
- **Rollout strategy**: A week‑before awareness phase, followed by structured support during the first three night shifts, with midpoint huddles to capture feedback. [[concepts/rollout_strategy]]
- **Support contacts**: Immediate technical issues are directed to the IT help desk. [[entities/it_help_desk]]
- **Documentation**: Uses half‑page cheat sheets that highlight new steps, common hiccups, and contact info. [[concepts/cheat_sheets]]
