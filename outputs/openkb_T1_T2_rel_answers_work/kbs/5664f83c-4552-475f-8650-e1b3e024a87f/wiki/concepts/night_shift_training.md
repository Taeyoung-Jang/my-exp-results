# Night‑Shift Training

**Definition**: A set of focused, low‑impact training practices designed to help hospital night‑shift staff adopt new technologies or workflow changes without compromising patient care.

---

## Core Elements (as described in [[summaries/session-38_2025-09-15]])

1. **Brief, on‑shift walk‑throughs**
   - Conducted at the start of each night shift.
   - Last 1–2 minutes, highlighting the new step(s) (e.g., badge scanner or EHR login).
   - Delivered by a super‑user or educator who can answer immediate questions.

2. **Dedicated Night Champions**
   - Experienced nurses or techs who receive early exposure to the new system.
   - Serve as on‑site point‑people for real‑time troubleshooting.
   - Require no formal authority, just a head‑start and a brief training session.

3. **Mid‑shift “quick huddle”**
   - A 5‑minute checkpoint halfway through the shift to gather feedback, identify hiccups, and adjust workflows on the spot.
   - Encourages peer‑to‑peer learning and rapid issue escalation.

4. **Visible Quick Reference Guides**
   - Half‑page [[concepts/cheat_sheets]] placed at workstations (e.g., next to badge scanner or EHR login screen).
   - Include: what’s new, first‑step actions, common hiccups, and help contact.

5. **Real‑time Support Channels**
   - Immediate questions go to the night champion.
   - Technical or login problems are directed to the [[entities/it_help_desk]].
   - Workflow concerns are escalated to the unit manager or educator.

- **[[concepts/email_drafting]]** – Pre‑go‑live email briefs (overview, schedule, contacts) set the stage for night‑shift training.

## Practical Checklist (derived from the session)

- **Week‑before**: distribute a short overview email, run a quick demo at shift start, brief night champions, post a “what to expect” note at each workstation.
- **Night 1‑3**: assign a night champion, start shift with a 60‑second reminder, hold a midpoint huddle, use cheat sheets, log issues to the [[entities/it_help_desk]].
