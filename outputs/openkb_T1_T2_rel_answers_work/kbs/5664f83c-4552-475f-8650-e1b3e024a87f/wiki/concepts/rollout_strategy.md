# Rollout Strategy for Clinical Technology

**Definition**: A structured approach to deploying new clinical technology—such as badge scanners or electronic health record (EHR) updates—while preserving patient safety and maintaining workflow continuity, particularly during night shifts.

---

## Core Elements (derived from [[summaries/session-38_2025-09-15]])

### 1. Week‑before Preparation
- **Awareness brief**: Distribute a concise overview of the upcoming change.
- **Hands‑on demo**: Conduct a short walkthrough at the start of each shift so staff can practice in a low‑pressure environment.
- **Night champions**: Identify experienced nurses or techs, give them early access to the new system, and brief them on key differences.
- **Visible notes**: Post a “what to expect” note at every workstation.

### 2. Go‑Live Nights 1‑3
- **Assign a night champion** per shift to float, answer questions, and monitor issues.
- **Shift‑start reminder**: A 60‑second verbal cue highlighting the new steps and location of the [[concepts/cheat_sheets]].
- **Mid‑shift huddle**: Quick feedback session to capture hiccups and adjust workflow on the spot.
- **Escalation path**: Log recurring problems to the dedicated support line (often the [[entities/it_help_desk]]).

### 3. Support Contacts
- **Immediate queries** → night champion.
- **Technical/login issues** → [[entities/it_help_desk]] or the hospital’s go‑live support line.
- **Workflow concerns** → unit manager or educator.

### 4. Communication Tools
- **Cheat sheets**: Half‑page, location‑specific tip sheets covering new steps, common hiccups, and contact info. See [[concepts/cheat_sheets]].
- **Email announcements**: Use clear, concise language to outline the schedule and contacts (see [[concepts/email_drafting]] and [[concepts/professional_communication]]). 
- **Scheduling calls**: If remote support is needed, schedule brief calls with the implementation team using [[concepts/scheduling_calls]].
