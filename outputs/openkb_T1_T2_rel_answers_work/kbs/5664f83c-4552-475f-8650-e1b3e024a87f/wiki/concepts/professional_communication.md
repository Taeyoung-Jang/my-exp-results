# Professional Communication

Professional communication refers to the practice of conveying information in a business context with clarity, respect, and an appropriate tone. It balances formality with approachability, ensuring the message is both effective and relationship‑building.

## Core Elements
- **Clarity** – Use concise language; avoid ambiguity.
- **Politeness** – Courteous greetings and sign‑offs (e.g., "I hope you’re doing well.")
- **Purpose‑Driven** – State the intent early (e.g., scheduling a call, reviewing an estimate).
- **Tone Balance** – Formal enough for business credibility while remaining friendly.
- **Actionable Items** – Clear next steps, such as proposing specific time slots or requesting updates.
