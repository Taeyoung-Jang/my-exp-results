# Cheat Sheets for Rapid Workflow Adoption

## Overview
Cheat sheets are ultra‑short, printable reference guides designed to help staff quickly learn and remember new procedures when a hospital system (e.g., a new [[entities/badge_scanner]] or [[entities/ehr]] update) goes live. They focus on the *what*, *how*, and *who* of a change, allowing teams to keep patient care flowing without searching for manuals.

## Why They Matter
- Provide **just‑in‑time** information at the point of work.
- Reduce reliance on lengthy training sessions during busy shifts.
- Support the **night‑shift training** approach described in [[concepts/night_shift_training]].
- Fit naturally into a broader **rollout strategy** by offering a tangible, low‑cost communication tool (see [[concepts/rollout_strategy]]).

## Design Guidelines (derived from [[summaries/session-38_2025-09-15]])
1. **Length** – Keep to a half‑page (single‑sided) to ensure quick glanceability.
2. **Structure** – Use a consistent template:
   - **What’s new** – One‑to‑two sentence description of the change.
   - **How to start** – First steps for login, badge scan, or system entry.
   - **Common hiccups** – Two or three frequent issues and simple fixes.
   - **Help info** – Phone number or contact for the [[entities/it_help_desk]] and the designated night champion.
3. **Visual Placement** – Post at the workstation where the badge is scanned or the computer is logged on; avoid email‑only distribution because night staff are often on the move.
4. **Language** – Use plain, action‑oriented wording; avoid jargon to aid rapid comprehension.

## Integration with Other Concepts
- **Night‑Shift Training**: Cheat sheets act as a tangible supplement to the short walk‑throughs and on‑the‑fly teaching described in [[concepts/night_shift_training]].
- **Professional Communication**: When announcing a new cheat sheet, a brief email (see [[concepts/email_drafting]]) can alert staff of its location and purpose.
- **Scheduling Calls**: If a hiccup is reported, the night champion can log the issue and schedule a quick follow‑up call using [[concepts/scheduling_calls]] to ensure timely fixes.

## Example Use Case
During the week‑before go‑live for a new badge scanner, the unit placed half‑page cheat sheets at each login station. Night champions reviewed the sheet during the shift‑change huddle and used it to field questions, resulting in a smooth transition with minimal disruption.
