## Summary of session-13_2025-06-15

The conversation walks through creating a professional yet friendly email to a contractor for scheduling a call to review an estimate. Key steps included:

- Starting with a courteous opening and a clear subject line.
- Offering two specific time slots after 3 PM Eastern time.
- Requesting an updated estimate if there have been changes.
- Asking the recipient to indicate the expected call duration.
- Maintaining a tone that is formal enough for business while staying approachable.

**Key concepts addressed**

- [[concepts/email_drafting]]
- [[concepts/scheduling_calls]]
- time zone specification
- estimate review
- requesting updates
- meeting duration inquiry
- [[concepts/professional_communication]]

The final email template provided:

> **Subject:** Scheduling a Call to Review Estimate
> 
> Hi [Name],
> 
> I hope you’re doing well. I’d like to schedule a call to review the estimate and discuss next steps. I’m available after 3 PM Eastern, and this week I could do Wednesday at 3:30 PM ET or Thursday at 4 PM ET if either of those times works for you. If there have been any updates to the estimate, please feel free to send an updated version beforehand. It would also be helpful if you could let me know how long you expect the call to take so I can plan accordingly.
> 
> I look forward to speaking with you.
