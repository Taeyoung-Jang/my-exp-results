# Summary of Session 38 (2025‑09‑15)

**Main focus** – Practical advice for implementing new badge‑scanner/EHR changes on night shifts, emphasizing low‑disruption training, peer support, and concise documentation.

## Key Concepts
- Hospital workflow changes – Ongoing tech updates, automation, and staffing pressures reshape daily routines.  
- Night‑shift training – Short, focused walk‑throughs at shift start, on‑site quick reference guides, and real‑time support keep care uninterrupted. See [[concepts/night_shift_training]].  
- Rollout strategy – Phase‑in a week‑before awareness, a structured go‑live period (nights 1‑3), and midpoint huddles to capture feedback. See [[concepts/rollout_strategy]].  
- Night champions – Trusted, experienced nurses or techs who receive early exposure and act as on‑shift point‑people.  
- Cheat sheets – Half‑page, location‑specific tip sheets covering new steps, common hiccups, and contact info. See [[concepts/cheat_sheets]].  
- Communication huddles – Brief, scheduled check‑ins (e.g., shift start, mid‑shift) to share observations and adjust workflows.  

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Preparation (Week‑before)**
   - Distribute a concise overview of the upcoming change.
   - Conduct a quick hands‑on demo at the start of each shift.
   - Identify and brief night champions; give them early access to the new system.
   - Post a “what to expect” note at every workstation.
2. **Go‑Live Nights 1‑3**
   - Assign a night champion per shift to float, answer questions, and monitor issues.
   - Begin each shift with a 60‑second reminder of the key steps and cheat‑sheet location.
   - Hold a midpoint huddle to collect rapid feedback and make on‑the‑spot tweaks.
   - Escalate recurring problems to the dedicated go‑live support line.
3. **Support Contacts**
   - Immediate questions → night champion.  
   - Technical/login issues → [[entities/it_help_desk]] or the hospital’s go‑live support line.  
   - Workflow concerns → unit manager or educator.
4. **Cheat‑Sheet Design**
   - One‑to‑two sentence description of the change.  
   - First‑step actions for login or badge scan (e.g., [[entities/badge_scanner]]).  
   - Two‑three common hiccups with quick fixes.  
   - Contact information for escalation (e.g., [[entities/ehr]] support or [[entities/it_help_desk]]).
