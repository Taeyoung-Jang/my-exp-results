# Decision Checklist for Shuttle vs. Driving

**Definition**: A short, structured set of yes/no questions that help a traveller quickly determine whether to take a regional shuttle or drive a personal vehicle when cost and travel time are comparable.

## Origin
This checklist originates from the advice given in [[summaries/session-05_2024-06-09]], where the assistant distilled a practical decision‑making tool for everyday commuting between smaller towns and a city hub.

## Checklist Items
1. **Am I carrying anything bulky or heavy?**
   - Heavy luggage or large items often make personal driving more convenient.
2. **Am I traveling very early or very late?**
   - Off‑peak shuttle schedules may be sparse, favoring driving.
3. **Is the shuttle running infrequently right now?**
   - Low frequency increases wait times and uncertainty.
4. **Will parking be easy at both ends?**
   - Easy parking reduces the hassle of a car‑based trip.
5. **Is traffic likely to be light?**
   - Light traffic improves driving efficiency.
6. **Do I need schedule flexibility today?**
   - Unplanned changes are easier to accommodate with a car.
7. **Will the shuttle drop‑off point be inconvenient for this trip?**
   - If the final walk or transfer is long, driving may be preferable.

## Decision Rule
- **If three or more answers are "yes," drive**; otherwise, take the shuttle.
