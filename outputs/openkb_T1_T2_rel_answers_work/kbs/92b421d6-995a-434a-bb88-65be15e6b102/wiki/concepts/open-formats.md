# Open Formats

**Definition**: Open Formats are widely supported, non‑proprietary file types that ensure long‑term accessibility and usability across platforms.

## Recommended Open Formats (from [[summaries/session-36_2024-12-15]])

- **Text & Notes**: plain text (`.txt`) or [[entities/markdown]] (`.md`) – human‑readable and version‑control friendly.
- **Images**: [[entities/jpeg]] and [[entities/png]] with embedded [[entities/exif]] / [[entities/xmp]] metadata.
- **Video**: [[entities/mp4]] (H.264) – universal playback support.
- **Audio**: common open audio formats such as MP3 or FLAC (widely supported).
- **Documents**: PDF/A for archival‑ready PDFs.

## Why Use Open Formats?

- **Future‑proof**: Avoid vendor lock‑in; the formats are documented and widely implemented.
- **Interoperability**: Can be opened on any OS or device without specialized software.
- **Metadata Embedding**: Supports EXIF/XMP for images and front‑matter for markdown, keeping context with the file.
- **Ease of Backup**: Simple binary or text files work well with [[concepts/backup-strategy]] and integrity checks.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- Works hand‑in‑hand with a clear [[concepts/folder-structure]] to keep archives organized.
- Facilitates smooth [[concepts/migration-planning]] when moving data between systems.
- Supports reliable [[concepts/pilot-testing]] of archival workflows.
