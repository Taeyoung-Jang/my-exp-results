# PNG (type: other)

**PNG** (Portable Network Graphics) is an open, lossless image format that is broadly supported across platforms and applications. It is highlighted in [[summaries/session-36_2024-12-15]] as a preferred choice for long‑term personal archives.

## Key Facts from Session 36
- Recommended alongside JPEG as part of the **open formats** strategy for preserving photos and graphics.
- Supports embedded metadata such as [[entities/exif]] and [[entities/xmp]], allowing important context to travel with the file.
- Uses lossless compression, which preserves image quality over repeated copies or edits.
- Fully compatible with the suggested folder hierarchy (see [[concepts/folder-structure]]) and naming conventions (`YYYY‑MM‑DD‑keyword‑description.png`).

## Why PNG for Future‑Proofing?
- **Open specification**: No proprietary constraints, ensuring future software can read the format.
- **Widely adopted**: Supported by browsers, operating systems, and image editors.
- **Metadata friendly**: Enables embedding of descriptive data without needing external databases.
- **Stable**: Ideal for archiving assets that require high fidelity, such as scans of documents or graphics.

## Related Entities and Concepts
- Compared with [[entities/jpeg]] for photographic content; JPEG is suitable for photographs where lossy compression is acceptable, while PNG excels for graphics and lossless needs.
- Part of the broader [[concepts/open-formats]] recommendation that includes Markdown, PDF/A, MP4, etc.
- Works within the overall **backup strategy** outlined in [[concepts/backup-strategy]], benefiting from checksums and versioned backups.
