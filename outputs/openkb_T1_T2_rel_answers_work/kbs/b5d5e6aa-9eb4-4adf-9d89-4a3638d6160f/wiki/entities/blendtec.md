# Blendtec (product)

Blendtec manufactures high‑power countertop blenders popular among enthusiasts. In the conversation *session‑11_2025‑06‑15* the brand was highlighted for specific recurring complaints:

- **Jar cracking** – reviewers frequently mention cracks at the base of the jar or “spider” cracks that appear during ordinary blending or routine washing.
- **Bearing noise** – over time the bearings can become noticeably noisy, another pattern observed across multiple low‑star reviews.
- **Loud operation** – while Blendtec models are louder than many competitors, noise was noted as a brand characteristic rather than a deal‑breaker.

These issues align with the **brand‑specific red‑flags** outlined in [[concepts/brand-specific-issues]] and are examples of the kinds of problems flagged in the [[concepts/review-red-flags]] checklist.

When evaluating Blendtec against other brands, the document suggests focusing on the consistency of these reports (e.g., early failures under normal use) as a stronger indicator of a design flaw than isolated complaints.
