# Ninja (blender brand)

Ninja is a widely‑distributed blender brand that appears in consumer reviews for its affordability and performance. In the context of the **session‑11_2025‑06‑15** discussion, Ninja was highlighted for two recurring negative‑review patterns:

- **Leaking seals** around the blade assembly, often reported on first use or at normal speeds.
- **Motor stress or burn‑out** when handling typical smoothie‑type tasks.

These issues align with the broader **[[concepts/review-red-flags]]** checklist, where early‑failure leaks and overheating are considered deal‑breaker red flags. The brand’s problems contrast with the more annoyance‑type complaints noted for other brands such as Vitamix.
