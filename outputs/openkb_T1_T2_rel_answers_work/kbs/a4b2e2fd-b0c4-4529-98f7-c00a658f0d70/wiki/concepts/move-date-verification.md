## Move‑Date Verification

**Move‑Date Verification** is the process of determining the exact move‑in and move‑out dates for a residence by consulting authoritative records. Accurate dates are essential for rental applications, background checks, and personal address‑history audits.

### Why Verify Move Dates?
- Rental applications often require **official** dates, not the day you physically packed boxes.
- Consistent date records help prevent gaps or overlaps in an **address‑history tracking** system.
- Verified dates support **address‑audit** activities where landlords or agencies review tenancy continuity.

### Primary Sources (ordered by reliability)
| Source | Best for | Typical date reflected |
|--------|----------|------------------------|
| Lease or rental agreement | Both move‑in & move‑out | Official start & end dates |
| Landlord/property‑manager emails or texts | Both | Exact day of key pickup, move‑out confirmation |
| Rent‑payment records (bank, Venmo, Zelle, checks) | Both (move‑in stronger) | First payment after move‑in, last payment before move‑out |
| Utility start/stop notices | Both | Service start ≈ move‑in, service stop ≈ move‑out |
| USPS change‑of‑address confirmation | Move‑in | Forward‑mail start date |
| Account address updates (bank, employer, delivery apps) | Move‑in | Date the account address was changed |

When dates from multiple sources conflict, the **lease or landlord confirmation** takes priority, as defined in the [[concepts/proof-source-hierarchy]] concept.

### Verification Workflow
1. **Gather documents** – locate the lease, any landlord communications, payment histories, utility bills, and USPS change‑of‑address notices.
2. **Extract dates** – note move‑in and move‑out dates from each source.
3. **Cross‑check** – compare dates; flag any discrepancies.
4. **Prioritize** – apply the hierarchy; default to the lease or written landlord confirmation.
5. **Document** – record the final dates in a single living spreadsheet or note, citing the source used for each.
