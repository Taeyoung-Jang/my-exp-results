# ZIP+4 (entity)

**Type:** other

**Description:** ZIP+4 is the United States Postal Service's extended ZIP code format that appends a four‑digit suffix to the standard five‑digit ZIP, enabling more accurate routing of mail and packages.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-10_2024-06-15]]
- The document emphasizes using a **USPS‑verified address** that includes the ZIP+4 to serve as the single source of truth for an address audit.
- During the **address‑audit** process, confirming that the ZIP+4 is present and correct is a critical check to avoid lost mail or mis‑delivery.
- The audit template includes a column “ZIP+4 Correct?” to flag any mismatches.
- The importance of ZIP+4 is highlighted in the **risk‑prioritization** step: accounts used for billing or legal notices are high‑risk if the ZIP+4 is missing or altered.
