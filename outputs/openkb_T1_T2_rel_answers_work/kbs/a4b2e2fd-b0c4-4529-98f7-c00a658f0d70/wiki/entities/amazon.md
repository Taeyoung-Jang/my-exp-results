# Amazon (organization)

Amazon is a multinational e‑commerce and cloud computing company that operates an online marketplace, subscription services, and a logistics network.

## Relevance to the address‑audit discussion
In the *session‑10_2024‑06‑15* conversation, Amazon is mentioned as a typical vendor where users should:

- Verify that the stored address matches the [[concepts/usps-formatted-address]] exactly, including the unit number and ZIP+4.
- Watch for Amazon’s auto‑formatting behavior, which may reformat punctuation or abbreviations but should not drop critical fields such as the unit or ZIP+4.  This aligns with the recommended "Vendor Auto‑Changes?" column in the address audit template.
- Include Amazon in the high‑risk portion of the address audit because it is used for both **billing** (payment methods) and **shipping** (order deliveries).

## Key points from the source
- Amazon is listed as an example of a service that may **auto‑reformat** addresses.
- Users are advised to copy the [[concepts/usps-formatted-address]] into Amazon’s address form and verify that the unit number and ZIP+4 remain intact.
- The conversation suggests treating Amazon as a **critical account** in the address‑audit template, especially for shipping purposes.
