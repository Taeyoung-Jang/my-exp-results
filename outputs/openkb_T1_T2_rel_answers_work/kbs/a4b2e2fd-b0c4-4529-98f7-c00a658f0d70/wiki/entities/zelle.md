# Zelle (product)

Zelle is a peer‑to‑peer digital payment service that lets users send money directly between U.S. bank accounts using an email address or mobile phone number. It is often mentioned alongside other payment apps such as Venmo and PayPal when discussing how to verify rent‑payment records.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2025-06-15]]
- **Use case in address history tracking**: The summary suggests checking rent‑payment records—including Zelle transaction histories—to pinpoint the first payment after moving in and the last payment before moving out. These timestamps help reconstruct move‑in and move‑out dates when lease documents are missing.
- **Placement in proof‑source hierarchy**: Zelle appears in the list of reliable sources for confirming move‑in/out dates, ranking below the lease agreement and landlord communications but above less precise signals like USPS change‑of‑address.
