# Venmo

**Type:** Product

## Overview
Venmo is a mobile payment service that enables peer‑to‑peer money transfers. It is widely used for everyday transactions, including rent payments.

## Role in address‑history tracking
In the context of personal address history, Venmo can serve as a **proof source** for confirming move‑in and move‑out dates. Payment timestamps from Venmo transactions provide a reliable record of when rent was first paid after moving in and the last payment before moving out.

## Use for move‑date verification
- **Move‑in verification:** The date of the first Venmo rent payment often aligns closely with the actual move‑in date.
- **Move‑out verification:** The final Venmo rent payment can indicate the approximate move‑out period, though the lease or landlord confirmation remains the authoritative source.

These uses align with the broader [[concepts/move-date-verification]] and fit within the [[concepts/proof-source-hierarchy]] for reconstructing address timelines.
