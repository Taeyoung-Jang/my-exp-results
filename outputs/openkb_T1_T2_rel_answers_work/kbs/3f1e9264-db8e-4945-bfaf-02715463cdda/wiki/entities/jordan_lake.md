# Jordan Lake

Jordan Lake is a large reservoir located about an hour west of the Raleigh‑Durham (RDU) area.  It offers shoreline trails, easy hiking routes, and opportunities for kayaking or paddle‑boarding, making it a favorite spot for quick outdoor getaways.
