## Greensboro Farmers Curb Market

Greensboro Farmers Curb Market is a seasonal, open‑air market located in downtown Greensboro, NC. It features local growers, artisans, and food vendors who sell fresh produce, baked goods, prepared foods, and handcrafted items.

### Role in the itinerary
The market is the kickoff point for the **Greensboro weekend itinerary** in [[summaries/session-01_2024-06-15]]. Visitors arrive at 10:00 am on Saturday to browse stalls, sample local foods, and set a relaxed, community‑focused tone for the trip.
