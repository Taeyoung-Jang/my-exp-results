# Budget Planning for Short Trips

**Definition**: A systematic approach to estimating and controlling the total expense of a weekend‑length getaway, typically for two people, while keeping the overall cost under a modest budget (e.g., $400).

## Core Elements

1. **Lodging**
   - Identify budget‑friendly accommodations (mid‑range Airbnbs, boutique inns, or modest hotels).  
   - Use a nightly range (e.g., $110‑$170) to create a flexible cost envelope.
2. **Transportation**
   - Calculate fuel costs based on round‑trip mileage from the home hub (e.g., the [[entities/rdu_area]]).  
   - Include a small buffer for tolls or parking.
3. **Food & Drink**
   - Split meals into categories: breakfast (often covered by lodging), lunch, dinner, and optional snacks or desserts.  
   - Allocate a per‑meal range (e.g., $20‑$35 for dinner for two) to stay within budget.
4. **Activities & Attractions**
   - Prioritize free or low‑cost experiences (parks, arboretums, farmers markets).  
   - Add a modest allowance for paid entry or a small purchase (e.g., a book, museum ticket).
5. **Miscellaneous**
   - Include a contingency for toiletries, souvenirs, or unexpected fees.

## Practical Tips

| Step | Tip |
|------|-----|
| **Pre‑Trip Research** | Use price‑comparison sites for lodging and check fuel‑price apps for gas estimates. |
| **Buffer** | Add 5‑10 % of the projected total for unexpected expenses. |
| **Track Real‑Time** | Keep a simple spreadsheet or notes app to log actual spend versus planned budget. |
| **Leverage Discounts** | Look for coupons, loyalty programs, or free‑entry days at museums/parks. |

By following these guidelines, travelers can craft enjoyable short trips that respect financial limits while still delivering memorable experiences.
