# Outfit Formula

**Definition**: An *outfit formula* is a repeatable styling pattern that pairs a relaxed base garment with one or more structured elements to achieve a look that feels intentional, comfortable, and appropriate for the setting.

## Core Components

1. **Relaxed Base** – Soft, comfortable pieces such as knit tops, drapey blouses, or ribbed midi dresses that provide ease of movement.
2. **Structured Element** – Items that add shape or definition, e.g., high‑rise trousers, waist‑defining belts, built‑in dress seams, or a half‑tuck technique.  [[concepts/waist_definition]]
3. **Warm Outer Layer** – Lightweight jackets or cardigans that add warmth without bulk, keeping the silhouette polished.  [[concepts/warm_outer_layer]]
4. **Flat or Low‑Heel Footwear** – Shoes that prioritize comfort for seated or walking scenarios, such as flat ankle boots, loafers, low‑profile mules, or minimalist sneakers.
5. **Minimal Accessory** – One accent (belt, pendant, or small bag) that adds visual interest without overwhelming the outfit.

## Example Application (from [[summaries/session-39_2025-02-15]])

- **Look 1**: Cropped structured knit top + high‑rise straight jeans + thin belt + quilted bomber + flat ankle boots.
- **Look 2**: Drapey blouse half‑tucked into high‑rise tailored trousers + cropped blazer + low loafers.
- **Look 3**: Ribbed knit midi dress with waist seam + cropped cardigan + flat sneakers.

Each look follows the *outfit formula*: relaxed base → structured waist definition → warm outer layer → flat shoes → single accessory.

## Benefits
- **Comfort** – Prioritizes fabrics and fits that feel easy to wear for long periods.
- **Versatility** – Works across casual, semi‑formal, and date‑night contexts.
- **Flattering Silhouette** – Emphasizes waist definition without tightness, suitable for various heights.
- **Practicality** – Flat shoes and wrinkle‑resistant fabrics keep the wearer at ease while seated.
