# Task List Structure

**Definition** – A simple, three‑section framework for personal task management that separates work into *Today*, *This Week*, and *Later* buckets. It helps users focus on a handful of high‑priority items each day while keeping longer‑term work visible but non‑distracting.

## Core Elements

| Section | Purpose | Typical Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | Immediate focus – the only tasks you aim to finish before the workday ends. Limit to **three** items to avoid overload. | High‑urgency items, deadline‑driven work, unblockers, or tasks that give the greatest sense of progress. |
| **This Week** | Short‑term backlog – tasks that must be done within the current week but not today. | Meetings to prepare for, reports due later in the week, follow‑ups, and any work that can be pulled into the *Today* list during morning planning. |
| **Later** | Long‑term catch‑all – anything that is not time‑critical. Keeps the list from disappearing while staying out of the daily focus zone. | Administrative clean‑ups, idea‑generation, research, and future projects.

## How It Was Used in Session‑33
The assistant in [[summaries/session-33_2024-04-15]] demonstrated the structure with a concrete example:

```
Today
- Finish the quarterly report draft
- Prep for the 2 p.m. client call
- Clear out the five priority emails from yesterday

This Week
- Follow up with design team on asset approvals
- Review onboarding checklist for new hire
- Update project tracker
- Prepare slides for Friday’s meeting

Later
- Organize shared drive folders
- Draft ideas for next month’s newsletter
- Research new task‑tracking tools
```

The **Today** list is intentionally capped at three items, reinforcing the *three‑item rule* that promotes realistic daily commitments. The *This Week* section acts as a parking lot, allowing the user to pull items forward during their morning “anchor task” planning. The *Later* bucket prevents low‑priority items from cluttering the daily view while ensuring they remain accessible.

## Benefits
- **Clarity** – Separates urgent work from future commitments, reducing decision fatigue.
- **Focus** – A limited *Today* list prevents multitasking overload.
- **Flexibility** – Items can move between sections as priorities shift.
- **Visibility** – Long‑term tasks stay on the radar without hijacking daily attention.
