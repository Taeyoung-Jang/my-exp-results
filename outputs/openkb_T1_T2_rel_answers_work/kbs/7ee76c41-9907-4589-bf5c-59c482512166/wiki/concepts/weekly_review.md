# Weekly Review

**Definition**: A brief, structured weekly reset that helps you evaluate what moved forward, identify stalls, and realign tasks and goals for the coming week.

## Core Elements (as described in [[summaries/session-07_2022-04-15]])
- **Progress audit** – quickly note what was completed and what got delayed.
- **Deadline scan** – check upcoming meetings, class requirements, and research milestones.
- **Block verification** – ensure that the three protected deep‑work sessions ([[concepts/deep_work]]) and any advisor‑prep slot are still scheduled.
- **Task hygiene** – clear out finished or irrelevant items and highlight the handful of high‑impact tasks for next week.
- **Focus selection** – choose a single primary research goal for Monday to start the week with clear direction.

## Typical Timing
- Allocate 10–15 minutes.
- Common slots: Friday afternoon or Sunday evening.

## Benefits
- Prevents tasks from slipping through the cracks.
- Maintains alignment between daily actions and longer‑term goals.
- Supports the energy‑aware rhythm of [[concepts/time_blocking]] by confirming that low‑energy windows are reserved for admin work.

## How to Run a Weekly Review
1. **Gather** your calendar, task list, and notes.
2. **Review** completed items vs. planned items.
3. **Identify** any stalled or overdue work.
4. **Check** upcoming deadlines and ensure research blocks are booked.
5. **Prune** completed or obsolete tasks.
6. **Highlight** 3–5 key tasks for the next week.
7. **Select** Monday’s primary research focus.
