# Summary of session-07_2022-04-15

**Main focus:** Providing a lightweight, structured system for graduate students to stay motivated, organized, and aligned with long‑term goals while juggling coursework, research, and administrative duties.

## Key Recommendations

- **Daily check‑in** – a quick mental scan of what truly needs attention, what can wait, and any long‑term step to take.  
- **Central capture** – one place for tasks, ideas, and notes; keep calendar, task manager, and notes app with clear, separate roles.  
- **Mindset shift** – treat research and coursework as *deep work*‑style training: small, consistent steps beat occasional bursts. [[concepts/deep_work]]
- **Time‑blocking** – anchor 2‑3 *deep research* blocks each week, schedule class prep around lectures, and place admin/TA work in lower‑energy slots. [[concepts/time_blocking]]
- **Weekly review** – a brief (10‑15 min) reset to audit progress, adjust goals, and confirm that all essential blocks are scheduled. [[concepts/weekly_review]]

## Weekly Review Checklist
- Review what moved forward vs. stalled.  
- Verify upcoming deadlines, meetings, and class needs.  
- Confirm three protected research blocks and advisor‑prep slot.  
- Ensure class‑prep windows are scheduled around each class.  
- Clear completed or irrelevant tasks.  
- Highlight key tasks for the coming week.  
- Choose Monday’s primary research focus.
