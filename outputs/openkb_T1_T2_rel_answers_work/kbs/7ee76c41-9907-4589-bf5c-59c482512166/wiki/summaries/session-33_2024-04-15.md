# Summary of session‑33_2024‑04‑15

**Main focus** – The assistant offers a practical, low‑overhead system for a new full‑time worker to stay organized, avoid burnout, and keep energy levels high.

## Key Recommendations
- **Daily anchor task** – Choose one high‑impact task that, once completed, unlocks or simplifies the rest of the day. *(plain text)*
- **Three‑item “Today” list** – Limit the “must‑do today” section to three tasks. This keeps the list manageable and increases the chance of finishing everything before clock‑out. *(plain text)*
- **Three‑section layout** –  
  - **Today** (3 priority items)  
  - **This Week** (parking‑lot for tasks due later)  
  - **Later** (long‑term backlog). [[concepts/task-list-structure]]
- **Batching interruptions** – Check Slack or other messages at set intervals (e.g., hourly) unless truly urgent. Use short canned replies to acknowledge without committing. [[concepts/notification-batching]]
- **Energy‑aware scheduling** – Align demanding work with personal peak‑energy periods (often mornings) and reserve lighter tasks for low‑energy times. [[concepts/energy-management]]
- **Transition ritual** – After work, take a brief walk, listen to a podcast, or simply sit still for a few minutes to shift out of “office mode.” *(plain text)*
- **Tool simplicity** – Stick to a single, always‑open notes app, lightweight to‑do app (e.g., Todoist), or a plain document. The key is a single home for all tasks, not multiple fragmented systems. *(plain text)*

## Example Daily Page
```
Today
- Finish the quarterly report draft
- Prep for the 2 p.m. client call
- Clear out the five priority emails from yesterday

This Week
- Follow up with design team on asset approvals
- Review onboarding checklist for new hire
- Update project tracker
- Prepare slides for Friday’s meeting

Later
- Organize shared drive folders
- Draft ideas for next month’s newsletter
- Research new task‑tracking tools
```

## Prioritization Method
When faced with many items, select the top three based on:
1. **Immediate deadline** (must be done today).
2. **Unblocking others** – tasks that enable teammates to move forward.
3. **High‑impact / progress‑driving** – tasks that give a strong sense of accomplishment.

## Slack Reply Scripts
- “Got this! I’m tied up for a bit, but I’ll circle back soon.”
- “Thanks for the heads‑up — I can take a look later this afternoon.”
- “On my radar. I’ll follow up once I wrap what I’m on.”

These concise responses keep communication clear while preserving focus.
