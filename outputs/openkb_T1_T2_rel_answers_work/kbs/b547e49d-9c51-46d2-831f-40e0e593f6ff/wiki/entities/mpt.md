# Multistate Performance Test (MPT)

The **Multistate Performance Test (MPT)** is a work‑type component of the **[[entities/bar-exam]]** that assesses a candidate’s ability to apply legal knowledge in a realistic, practical task such as drafting a memorandum, brief, or client letter. Unlike the multiple‑choice **[[entities/mbe]]**, the MPT focuses on written performance and requires the organization and synthesis of legal rules.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-36_2025-01-11]]
- **Introduction timing:** First added to the study rhythm in **Week 4** of the 8‑week bar‑prep plan as a *relaxed, untimed* exercise to familiarize the learner with the format.
- **Progression:** After the initial exposure, the MPT is incorporated more formally:
  - **Weeks 5‑6:** Optional second relaxed MPT (still untimed) if workload permits.
  - **Week 6 onward:** A **timed MPT** is scheduled once per week to build stamina.
- **Placement in the weekly rhythm:** Usually placed on Thursday or Saturday, days earmarked for lighter output work, to avoid overlapping with intensive MBE timed sets.
- **Purpose within the plan:** Acts as a controlled increase in workload that respects **[[concepts/incremental-pacing]]** and supports **[[concepts/burnout-prevention]]** by providing a varied task type without overwhelming the learner.
- **Metrics for success:** Ability to complete the task within the allotted time, clarity of issue spotting, and quality of the written product compared to model answers.

## Relationship to Study Concepts
- The MPT fits into the broader **[[concepts/learning-rhythm]]** by alternating between input (reviewing rules) and output (performing tasks), ensuring a balanced cognitive load.
- Its gradual integration exemplifies **[[concepts/incremental-pacing]]**, starting with low‑stakes practice and scaling to timed performance.
- By limiting the frequency and keeping a dedicated **recovery day**, the MPT schedule aligns with **[[concepts/burnout-prevention]]**, helping candidates maintain sustainable study energy.
