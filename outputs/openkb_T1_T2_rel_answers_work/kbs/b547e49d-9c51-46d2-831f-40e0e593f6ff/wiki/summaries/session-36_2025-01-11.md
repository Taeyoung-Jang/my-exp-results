# Summary of session-36_2025-01-11

**Main contribution** – a detailed, sustainable weekly rhythm for a professional pivot into bar‑exam preparation, culminating in an 8‑week ramp‑up plan that balances *learning rhythm*, *incremental pacing*, and *burnout prevention*.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/learning-rhythm]]: alternating input (review, lectures) and output (practice, essays) days.  
- [[concepts/incremental-pacing]]: start with modest daily hours (1–2 h) and gradually increase volume.  
- Checkpoint: short weekly reviews and monthly milestones (course/module completion, timed set performance).  
- [[concepts/burnout-prevention]]: built‑in recovery day and regular self‑check‑ins to adjust load.  
- MBE practice: untimed question sets early, progressing to timed mixed sets.  
- Essay practice: mix of full‑written essays and outlines to train structure without overload.  
- [[entities/mpt]]: introduced mid‑plan, initially untimed, later timed, to build stamina.  
- Timed practice vs untimed practice: clear differentiation for scaling difficulty.  
- Recovery day: a protected weekly rest block.

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Start with a gentle rhythm** – 1–2 h per weekday, alternating subject review/MBE questions with essay writing/outlining.  
2. **Weekly checkpoints** – track MBE accuracy and essay outline speed; adjust load if sessions feel draining.  
3. **Monthly milestones** – complete a subject outline, a timed MBE set, or a set of essays to gauge real capability.  
4. **Progressive scaling** – increase total MBE questions per week, length of timed sets, and introduce mixed sets and [[entities/mpt]] in small steps.  
5. **Recovery day** – keep one full rest day untouched; use it as a mental reset before the next week.

## 8‑Week Ramp‑Up Plan (high‑level targets)

| Week | Total MBE Qs | Timed Mixed Sets | Full Essays / Outlines | [[entities/mpt]] | Recovery Day |
|------|--------------|------------------|------------------------|------------------|--------------|
| 1 | ~90 (mostly untimed) | 1 modest timed block | 1 essay + 2 outlines | – | Yes |
| 2 | ~110 | 1 timed block | 1 essay + 2 outlines | – | Yes |
| 3 | ~140 | 1 longer timed block + 1 untimed mixed set | 1 essay + 2 outlines | – | Yes |
| 4 | ~150‑160 | 1 timed mixed set | 1 essay + 2 outlines | 1 relaxed [[entities/mpt]] (untimed) | Yes |
| 5 | ~170‑180 | 1 timed mixed set (optional partial timing) | 1 essay + 2 outlines | 2nd relaxed [[entities/mpt]] (optional) | Yes |
| 6 | ~200 | 1 full timed mixed set + untimed mid‑week set | 1 essay + 2 outlines | 1 timed [[entities/mpt]] | Yes |
| 7 | ~200‑220 | 1 solid timed mixed set + 1 short timed block | 1 essay + 2 outlines | 1 timed [[entities/mpt]] | Yes |
| 8 | ~200 | 2 modest timed blocks | 1 essay + 1‑2 outlines | 1 final timed [[entities/mpt]] | Yes |

**Scaling notes** – increase question volume by ~10‑20 % each two weeks, add timed elements gradually, and only add [[entities/mpt]] after the fourth week to avoid overloading the recovery day.

## Practical Takeaway
Apply the **rhythm → checkpoint → scale** loop: establish a manageable daily routine, review weekly/monthly markers, and incrementally raise intensity while safeguarding a weekly rest block. This structure keeps progress measurable and burnout at bay throughout the [[entities/bar-exam]] journey.
