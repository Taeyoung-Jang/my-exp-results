# Summary of session-35 (2025-07-24)

## Overview
User wants a realistic weekend routine and meal‑prep plan for a six‑week Saturday ceramics class. Assistant delivers a detailed time‑blocked schedule, a one‑week chicken‑centric meal plan, grocery list, dinner recipes, lunch ideas, and reheating tips.

## Weekend Time‑Blocking
- **Friday (6:30‑8:00 pm)**: laundry, quick tidy, cook half the **[[entities/chicken-thighs]]** and roast broccoli + peppers.  
- **Saturday**: class 10 am‑3 pm; dinner assembled from pre‑cooked items (≈10‑15 min).  
- **Sunday**: grocery run (11‑11:45 am), finish laundry/clean (10‑11 am), prep remaining chicken (12‑12:20 pm).

## One‑Week Chicken Meal Plan
| Day | Dinner | Prep needed |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Mon | Roasted chicken bowl (rice, broccoli, peppers, lemon) | Reheat roasted veg & Friday chicken |
| Tue | Pesto pasta with spinach (+optional chicken) | Boil pasta, toss pesto |
| Wed | Chicken stir‑fry (veg, soy/teriyaki, rice) | Sauté veg & Sunday chicken |
| Thu | Fried‑rice skillet (veg, egg, remaining chicken) | Combine leftovers |

## Lunch Ideas (reuse same ingredients)
1. Spinach salad with leftover roasted chicken, chopped peppers, lemon‑olive‑oil dressing.  
2. Rice bowl with remaining vegetables and chicken, seasoned with soy sauce or teriyaki sauce.  
3. Pesto‑spinach egg scramble (2 eggs, pesto, spinach) – good reheated.

## Grocery List (single‑person, quantities)
- 1 lb boneless skinless **[[entities/chicken-thighs]]**  
- 1 head broccoli  
- 2 bell peppers  
- 1 container baby spinach (≈5 oz)  
- 1 lemon  
- Small jar **[[entities/pesto]]**  
- Small bottle **[[entities/soy-sauce]]** or **[[entities/teriyaki-sauce]]**  
- 12 eggs  

*(Rice, pasta, olive oil, basic spices already on hand.)*

## Cooking & Reheating Tips
- Cook chicken just to done; store airtight.  
- Reheat in microwave covered with a splash of water, or on stovetop with a little broth/sauce to retain moisture.  
- Roasted vegetables keep 4‑5 days, perfect for multiple meals.
