# Soy Sauce

**Type:** product

Soy sauce is a fermented soy‑based condiment that adds salty, umami flavor to dishes. In the [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-24]] it is listed as a small bottle on the one‑week grocery list for a chicken‑centric weekend routine. It is used in:

- the stir‑fry on Wednesday,
- the fried‑rice skillet on Thursday,
- as a quick seasoning for leftover rice bowls.

The sauce supports the overall [[concepts/meal-prep]] strategy and helps minimize [[concepts/leftover-utilization]] by giving flavor to reheated meals. It pairs well with other pantry items such as [[entities/pesto]] and [[entities/teriyaki-sauce]] and is kept on hand alongside [[entities/olive-oil]].

**Key facts from the plan**

- Quantity: a small bottle (enough for several meals).
- Role: primary seasoning for chicken dishes and a versatile component of the [[concepts/weekend-routine]] cooking schedule.
- Storage: refrigerated after opening to maintain freshness.
