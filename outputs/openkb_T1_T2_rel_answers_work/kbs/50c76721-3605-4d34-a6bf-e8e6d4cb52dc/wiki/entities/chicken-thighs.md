# Chicken Thighs (Product)

**Type:** Product

## Overview
Chicken thighs refer to boneless, skinless chicken thigh pieces purchased for the week‑long meal‑prep schedule outlined in [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-24]]. They serve as the primary protein source across four low‑effort dinners.

## Quantity & Allocation
- **Amount purchased:** 1 lb (approximately 4–5 thighs).
- **Portioning:**
  - ½ of the thighs for the **roasted chicken bowl** (Monday).
  - ¼ for the **stir‑fry** (Wednesday).
  - Remaining ¼ for the **fried‑rice‑style skillet** (Thursday).
  - Optional small pieces may be added to the **pesto pasta** (Tuesday) for extra protein.

## Role in the Meal Plan
The thighs are cooked early in the weekend to fit the [[concepts/weekend-routine]] and [[concepts/meal-prep]] structure:
- **Friday:** Roast half the thighs with broccoli and bell peppers.
- **Sunday:** Finish cooking the remaining thighs (roasted or pan‑seared) for quick dinner assembly.
- **Dinners:** Used in roasted bowls, stir‑fry, fried rice, and optionally in pesto pasta, illustrating [[concepts/leftover-utilization]].

## Cooking & Reheating Tips
- **Initial cooking:** Season with salt (and optionally pepper) and roast at 425 °F for 15‑20 minutes, or pan‑sear until just done to retain juiciness.
- **Storage:** Cool to room temperature, then store in airtight containers in the fridge (up to 4 days).
- **Reheating:**
  - Microwave: place in a covered bowl, add a splash of water, and heat on medium for 1‑2 minutes.
  - Stovetop: warm in a pan with a little [[entities/soy-sauce]] or [[entities/teriyaki-sauce]] or a drizzle of [[entities/olive-oil]] to add moisture.
- **Avoid dryness:** Do not over‑cook initially; reheat gently with moisture.
