## Olive Oil (product)

Olive oil is a staple culinary oil that the user already has in the kitchen. In the **session‑35** weekend and meal‑prep plan it is referenced as the primary dressing for salads and as a cooking fat for:

- Roasting vegetables for the chicken bowls.
- Sautéing peppers and broccoli in the stir‑fry.
- Adding a splash of moisture when reheating chicken leftovers to prevent drying.

The oil supports the overall **[[concepts/meal-prep]]** strategy by providing a quick, low‑effort way to add flavor and moisture to dishes. It also ties into the **[[concepts/leftover-utilization]]** approach, where a drizzle of olive oil helps keep reheated leftovers tender.

Relevant entities mentioned alongside olive oil in the plan include [[entities/pesto]], [[entities/soy-sauce]], [[entities/teriyaki-sauce]], and [[entities/chicken-thighs]]. The weekly schedule and grocery list are described in [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-24]].
