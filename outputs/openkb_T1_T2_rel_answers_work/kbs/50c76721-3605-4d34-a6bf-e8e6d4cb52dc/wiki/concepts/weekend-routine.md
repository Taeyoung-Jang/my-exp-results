# Weekend Routine

**Definition**: A weekend routine is a repeatable, time‑blocked plan that allocates specific periods for household chores, grocery shopping, meal preparation, and personal activities, helping to prevent task backlog and reduce reliance on takeout.

---

## Core Elements (derived from [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-24]])

1. **Friday Evening Block** (≈6:30 pm – 8:00 pm)
   - Laundry start (30 min)
   - Quick surface tidy (20 min)
   - Core meal prep: roast broccoli & bell peppers, cook half of the chicken thighs, and store components separately.

2. **Saturday** – *Class Day*
   - Minimal home work; only a short post‑class assembly of pre‑cooked items (≈10‑15 min) for dinner.

3. **Sunday Reset Block** (≈10 am – 12:20 pm)
   - Finish/fold laundry and deeper cleaning (10:30 am – 11 am).
   - Grocery run for the week (11 am – 11:45 am).
   - Finish cooking remaining chicken thighs for later meals (12 pm – 12:20 pm).

4. **Meal Flow**
   - Dinners are organized so each uses a portion of the same protein and vegetables, reducing waste and prep time.
   - Lunches recycle leftovers (salads, rice bowls, egg‑pesto scramble).

---

## Benefits
- **Task Distribution**: Moves the bulk of chores to Friday evening and Sunday, keeping Saturday free for the ceramics class.
- **Ingredient Efficiency**: Centralizes protein (chicken thighs) and vegetables, enabling [[concepts/leftover-utilization]] across four dinners and multiple lunches.
- **Time Savings**: Each weekday dinner requires 10‑15 minutes of active work, ideal for “walk‑in‑the‑door” evenings.
- **Stress Reduction**: Pre‑planned grocery list and prep steps limit decision fatigue and curb takeout reliance.

## Practical Tips
- **Batch Cook**: Roast all vegetables at once; store in airtight containers.
- **Moisture Retention**: When reheating chicken, cover with a damp paper towel in the microwave or add a splash of broth on the stovetop.
- **Flexibility**: If a class runs longer, shift the Friday prep later or use the Sunday prep window to finish any missing steps.
