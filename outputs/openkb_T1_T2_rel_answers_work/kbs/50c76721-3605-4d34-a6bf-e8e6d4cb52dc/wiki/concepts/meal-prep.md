# Meal Prep

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Systematic planning of meals to streamline cooking, shopping, and leftovers.

---

Meal prep is the practice of organizing, preparing, and sometimes partially cooking food items ahead of time so that daily meals require minimal effort.  It typically involves:

1. **Batch cooking core proteins** (e.g., chicken thighs) and grains.
2. **Roasting or chopping vegetables** that can be used across multiple dishes.
3. **Creating a reusable grocery list** that focuses on a few versatile ingredients.
4. **Designing a weekly schedule** that aligns cooking tasks with personal time blocks (e.g., Friday evening and Sunday morning).
5. **Ensuring leftovers are usable** in subsequent meals to reduce waste.

The concept is illustrated in [[summaries/session-35_2025-07-24]], where a user seeks a concrete weekend routine and a one‑week chicken‑centric meal plan.  The session outlines:

- A **weekend routine** with clear time blocks for laundry, cleaning, grocery shopping, and food preparation.
- A **four‑night dinner rotation** that reuses the same protein (chicken thighs) and vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, spinach) across different cooking methods (roasting, pasta, stir‑fry, fried‑rice skillet).
- A **streamlined grocery list** that minimizes excess items while covering all meals.
- **Lunch ideas** that repurpose the same ingredients, embodying the principle of [[concepts/leftover-utilization]].
- Tips for **reheating chicken** without drying out, reinforcing the need for proper storage and gentle reheating methods.

Key elements from the source that define the meal‑prep approach include:

- **Batch cooking on Friday**: half the chicken and all vegetables are roasted, providing ready‑to‑heat components for Monday‑Thursday dinners.
- **Second batch on Sunday**: the remaining chicken is cooked, completing the protein supply for the week.
- **Time‑efficient dinner assembly**: each night’s meal can be prepared in 10‑15 minutes from pre‑cooked components.
- **Cross‑meal ingredient synergy**: the same set of ingredients supports both dinner and lunch dishes, reducing waste and shopping complexity.
