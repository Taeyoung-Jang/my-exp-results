# New York City

**Type:** Place (city in the United States)

## Overview
New York City (NYC) is the most populous city in the United States and a global hub for finance, culture, and politics. It is located in the state of New York and serves as a key reference point in discussions of electoral reforms.

## Mention in [[summaries/session-11_2022-06-15]]
In the conversation dated 2022‑06‑15, NYC is highlighted as a real‑world example where **ranked‑choice voting** has been implemented. The discussion notes that NYC provides early evidence that ranked‑choice voting can soften candidate rhetoric and encourage broader coalitions, illustrating a practical case of [[concepts/voting_reforms]].
