# Nordic Countries

Nordic Countries refer to the group of Northern European nations—including Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, and Sweden—that share social‑democratic traditions, high levels of civic participation, and political systems that often employ proportional representation.

## Relevance to session‑11_2022‑06‑15
The conversation highlights the Nordic Countries as an example of a place where politics tends to feel less tribal. Their multi‑party, proportional‑representation systems encourage negotiation across several parties, which helps keep political debate more practical and less binary. This illustrates how **structural drivers** such as voting systems can temper the **psychological drivers** of group identity that fuel polarization.
