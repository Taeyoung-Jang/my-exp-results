# Small Wins and Incremental Strategy for Sustainable Activism

**Definition**: A tactical approach that focuses on achieving frequent, modest victories and building progress step‑by‑step to maintain momentum, prevent burnout, and keep activist movements resilient over time.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Concrete, Local Wins**
   - Target actions that have clear, observable outcomes in the community (e.g., a neighborhood zoning amendment, a school policy tweak).  These win‑examples are discussed in [[summaries/session-37_2024-06-15]] and illustrate how personal relevance fuels deeper involvement.
2. **Value‑Centred Prioritisation**
   - Separate **non‑negotiable** core values from optional policy details.  The framework aligns with the [[concepts/priority_framework]] introduced in the same session.
3. **Role Rotation & Breaks**
   - Encourage participants to hand off tasks or adopt lighter roles temporarily, keeping the movement’s momentum circulating rather than dependent on a few individuals.
4. **Incremental Progress as Strategy**
   - View compromise not as surrender but as a stepping‑stone toward the ultimate vision.  Small, deliberate advances shift the "normal" and make larger goals more attainable.

---

## Why Small Wins Matter

- **Psychological Boost**: Frequent successes reinforce a sense of agency, a key driver identified in [[concepts/personal_agency]] and [[concepts/psychological_drivers]].
- **Visibility of Impact**: Tangible outcomes counteract cynicism that often arises from focusing solely on distant, large‑scale change.
- **Community Building**: Shared victories create social bonds that strengthen collective identity and resilience.

---

## Linking to Larger Structural Goals

While the approach emphasizes modest, immediate actions, it is deliberately connected to broader structural changes.  The idea is that each small win shifts the policy landscape, making it easier to later introduce more ambitious reforms (e.g., voting reforms, systemic policy overhauls).  This synergy reflects the tension between **incremental progress** and **structural drivers** discussed across the knowledge base.

---

## Practical Checklist (derived from the session)

1. **Identify Core Values** – Define the underlying principles you want to protect.
2. **Select Concrete Wins** – Choose actions with measurable, local impact.
3. **Assess Urgency** – Determine if a win must happen now or can be phased.
4. **Plan Role Sharing** – Assign backups and schedule regular breaks.
5. **Communicate Strategically** – Frame each win as a step toward the larger vision, using language that emphasizes shared goals.
