# IRP Registration Process

**Definition**: The IRP (Immigration Residence Permit) registration process is the sequence of actions a non‑EU worker must complete after arriving in [[entities/ireland]] to obtain a formal Irish residence permit and the corresponding [[entities/irp_card]].

## Core Steps (as outlined in [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]])
1. **Book an appointment** – Usually at the Burgh Quay Registration Office (GNIB).  
   - Link: [[entities/burgh_quay_registration_office]] (often referred to as GNIB).  
2. **Gather required documentation** (see the *Proof of address* subsection).  
3. **Attend the appointment** – Submit documents, pay the IRP fee, and have a biometric photo taken.  
4. **Receive receipt and temporary permission** – Allows legal stay while the IRP card is being produced.  
5. **Collect the IRP card** – Typically within 2‑4 weeks; keep the card and receipt as proof of status.

## Required Documents
| Document | Why it matters | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| Work authorisation approval letter | Proves the legal basis for stay | Employer / immigration lawyer |
| Passport (valid) | Identity verification | Personal |
| Proof of address in Ireland | Satisfies residence requirement | Airbnb booking confirmation, host letter, or other backup (utility bill, lease) |
| Employment contract | Confirms job and sponsor | Employer |
| PPS number allocation (optional at this stage) | Needed for tax and services later | [[entities/pps_number]] |
| Fee receipt | Demonstrates payment of IRP processing fee | Payment method used |

### Proof of address alternatives (if Airbnb host won’t provide a letter)
- Full Airbnb booking confirmation that lists your name and the exact address.
- Printed Airbnb invoice/receipt showing the address.
- Email from Airbnb support confirming stay details with address.

## Common Failure Points
- **Missing appointment slot** – Slots fill quickly; delaying booking can push the registration window past the legal deadline.
- **Insufficient proof of address** – Without a verifiable document, the officer may refuse registration, forcing a reschedule.
- **Late receipt of work permit approval** – Shifts all downstream dates, possibly causing a gap between arrival and registration.
- **Failure to pay the IRP fee on time** – Results in postponed card issuance.

These pitfalls intersect with the broader [[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]] and underscore the importance of early, explicit ownership of each task.
