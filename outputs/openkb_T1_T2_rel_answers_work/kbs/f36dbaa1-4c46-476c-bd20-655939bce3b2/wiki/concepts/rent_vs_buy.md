# Rent vs. Buy Decision Framework

**Definition**: A structured approach for comparing the financial and lifestyle implications of renting versus purchasing a home, using timeline, total cost, and personal stability criteria.

## Core Elements

1. **Timeline Assessment**
   - Determine expected length of stay in a location.  
   - *Long‑term* (≥ 2‑3 years) supports buying; *short‑term* or uncertain tenure favors renting.
2. **All‑in Monthly Cost Comparison**
   - For buying, include: mortgage principal + interest (P&I), property taxes, homeowner’s insurance, maintenance, and PMI if down‑payment < 20 %.
   - For renting, include: rent, renter’s insurance, and any non‑adjustable utilities.
   - Compare the resulting monthly totals to see which option is cheaper or more predictable.
3. **Stability & Control Considerations**
   - *Financial predictability*: Fixed‑rate mortgage vs. fixed rent.
   - *Control over space*: Ownership allows renovations and customisation, which renting does not.
   - Align the option that best matches the user's definition of stability.

## Practical Steps (as used in [[summaries/session-08_2024-05-15]])

- **Gather data**: rent amount, home price, down‑payment %, interest rate, tax rate, insurance cost, maintenance % and closing‑cost %.
- **Calculate monthly housing cost** for each scenario.
- **Identify break‑even horizon** – the time needed for the upfront costs of buying to be offset by monthly savings or equity buildup (see [[concepts/break_even_analysis]]).
- **Set emergency‑fund buffer** based on total monthly expenses (see [[concepts/emergency_fund]]).

## Decision Guidance

- If the all‑in cost of buying is **lower or comparable** to rent and the timeline aligns, buying is usually preferred.
- If buying is **significantly higher** per month, or the timeline is uncertain, renting preserves flexibility.
- Adjust assumptions (price, down‑payment, interest rate) to bring the buying cost into the target range, or increase savings to improve the down‑payment, as illustrated in the source session.
