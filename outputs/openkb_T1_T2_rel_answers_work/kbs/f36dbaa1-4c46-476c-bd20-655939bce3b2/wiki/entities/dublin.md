# Dublin

Dublin is the capital and largest city of [[entities/ireland]], frequently chosen by professionals moving abroad for employment. In the context of [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]], Dublin is the relocation destination for a two‑year contract and the focal point for a series of immigration and practical onboarding steps.

## Key facts from session‑37
- **Visa logistics**: The move hinges on a work permit that must be approved before any travel. Understanding the specific permit category is essential for timing the entry date and for knowing employer‑related obligations. See [[concepts/visa_logistics]].
- **Critical timeline milestones**: filing the work authorisation, approval, earliest lawful entry date, arrival, GNIB/IRP registration (often at the Burgh Quay office), fee payment, PPS number acquisition, SIM purchase, bank account opening, health insurance/GP registration, and finally receipt of the [[entities/irp_card]].
- **IRP registration process**: Registration with Irish immigration is required shortly after arrival. The appointment is typically booked at the [[entities/burgh_quay_registration_office]] and must be completed within a statutory window to avoid status gaps. See [[concepts/irp_registration_process]].
- **Common relocation pitfalls**: Assuming the employer will handle all immigration steps, under‑estimating processing times, and forgetting essential documents for the IRP registration (proof of address, employment contract, approval letter, health insurance). See [[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]].
- **Proof of address tips**: When staying in an Airbnb, a formal booking confirmation, a printed invoice, or an email from Airbnb support can serve as acceptable proof. Backup options include a short‑term lease agreement, a notarised host letter, or a utility bill in the traveller’s name.

## Practical relevance
The Dublin entry in this session illustrates a repeatable relocation framework that can be adapted to other cities: start with clear visa strategy, map out each deadline, assign ownership, collect proof, and anticipate slip scenarios. The checklist provided in the conversation can be directly applied to a Dublin move, ensuring a smooth transition from arrival to full settlement.
