## IRP Card

The **IRP Card** (Irish Residence Permit) is a physical card that proves a non‑EU resident’s right to live and work in Ireland. It is issued after the **IRP registration process** is completed and is required for accessing many services, such as opening a bank account, obtaining a PPS number, and proving legal status at the border.

### Key Facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]]
- **Timing**: Must be obtained shortly after arrival in **[[entities/dublin]]**; failure to book the registration appointment on time can lead to status gaps.
- **Proof required**: Approval letter for the work permit, proof of address (e.g., Airbnb booking confirmation, lease, or host letter), employment contract, and passport‑sized photos.
- **Common pitfalls**: Missing the appointment window, lacking acceptable proof of address, or delayed fee payment can postpone issuance.
- **Ownership**: The applicant is usually responsible for booking the **[[entities/burgh_quay_registration_office]]** appointment and paying the fee, though some employers assist.

### Typical Documentation Checklist
| Document | Why needed |
|---|---|
| Work authorisation approval letter | Shows lawful entry and employment eligibility |
| Proof of address (Airbnb booking confirmation, lease, or host letter) | Required for registration at **[[entities/gnib]]**/*Burgh Quay* |
| Employment contract | Confirms the job tied to the visa |
| Passport‑sized photos | Needed for the card issuance |
| Fee receipt | Proof of payment for the IRP card |

### Interaction with Other Entities & Concepts
- The IRP Card is part of the broader **[[concepts/irp_registration_process]]** and is directly linked to the **[[concepts/visa_logistics]]** of the work permit.
- Obtaining the card enables the holder to apply for a **[[entities/pps_number]]**, open an Irish bank account, and register for health services.
- Delays in the IRP Card can amplify **[[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]]**, such as missed payroll setup or inability to prove residence for tax purposes.

### Practical Tips
1. **Book the Burgh Quay appointment ASAP** – slots fill quickly.
2. **Save all proofs digitally** – email confirmations, PDFs, and screenshots.
3. **Bring multiple address proofs** – if the Airbnb host won’t provide a letter, use the full booking confirmation, a printed receipt, or a short statement from the host with the address.
4. **Pay the fee before the appointment** – a receipt is required to receive the card.
5. **Track the IRP card receipt date** – it matters for travel and future visa renewals.
