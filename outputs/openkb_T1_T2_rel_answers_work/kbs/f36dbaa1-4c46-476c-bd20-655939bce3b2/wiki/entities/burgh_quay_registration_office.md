## Burgh Quay Registration Office

**Type:** Organization

**Location:** Burgh Quay, Dublin, Ireland – the main venue for the **GNIB/IRP** (Immigration Registration Permit) registration service for non‑EU workers.

**Primary Function:**
- Processes the in‑country registration of work permits for foreign nationals arriving in Ireland.
- Issues the **IRP card** after applicants provide the required documentation and pay the registration fee.
- Offers appointment scheduling (often online) for the registration interview.

**Key Facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]]:**
- The session highlights the office as a critical checkpoint: booking a "Burgh Quay IRP appointment" is essential to avoid long waiting periods that can jeopardise legal status.
- The document advises applicants to keep the **appointment confirmation** as proof and to prepare all required paperwork (approval letter, proof of address, employment contract, etc.) before the visit.
- Failure to secure an appointment promptly can lead to a status gap, since immigration law requires registration within a set timeframe after arrival.

**Typical Services and Requirements:**
- **Appointment booking:** Usually via the office’s website (e.g., https://burghquayregistrationoffice.ie).
- **Document checklist:** includes work authorisation approval, proof of address (e.g., Airbnb booking confirmation), employment contract, passport‑size photos, and payment receipt.
- **Fees:** A standard registration fee payable at the office; receipt must be retained for the IRP card issuance.

**Practical Tips for Applicants:**
1. **Book early:** Appointments fill quickly; aim to schedule within the first two weeks of arrival.
2. **Prepare proof of address:** If staying in an Airbnb, bring a booking confirmation that shows your name and the full address, or one of the backup documents (utility bill, host letter, or printed invoice).
3. **Keep all receipts:** Confirmation emails, fee receipts, and the IRP card receipt are essential for future renewals.
4. **Check the office’s website:** For updated hours, required forms, and any COVID‑related entry changes.
