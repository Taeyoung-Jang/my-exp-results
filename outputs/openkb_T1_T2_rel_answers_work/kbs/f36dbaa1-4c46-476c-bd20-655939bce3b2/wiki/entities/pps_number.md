# PPS Number (Other)

## Overview
The **PPS Number** (Personal Public Service Number) is a unique identifier issued by the Irish Department of Social Protection. It is required for accessing public services, tax registration, payroll processing, banking, and many other official interactions in [[entities/ireland]].

## Role in an International Relocation to Dublin
In the context of the relocation checklist from [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]], the PPS Number is a critical milestone after arrival in Ireland. Without it you cannot:
- Set up your tax profile in [[entities/revenue]] (via the myAccount portal).
- Receive payroll from your employer.
- Open a local bank account or obtain an Irish SIM.
- Complete the [[concepts/irp_registration_process]] for the IRP card, as the PPS number is often requested during registration.

## Key Steps to Obtain a PPS Number
| Step | Owner | Proof / Document | Typical Timeline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apply for PPS number | Me | Completed application form + ID (passport) | Usually within 1–2 weeks after booking the appointment | Appointments can be booked online via the Department of Social Protection website. |
| Receive PPS confirmation letter | Me | PPS letter (official correspondence) | Immediate on approval | Keep this letter to attach to payroll, bank account, and IRP registration. |

## Common Pitfalls
- **Delaying the application** – postponing the PPS appointment can stall payroll setup and bank account opening, causing cash‑flow issues.
- **Missing documentation** – failure to bring original passport, proof of address, or a completed form leads to re‑booking.
- **Assuming the employer will handle it** – many employers expect the employee to secure the PPS number independently; clarify responsibilities early to avoid the [[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]].
