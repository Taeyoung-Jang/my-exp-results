# Airbnb

**Type:** organization

**Description:** Airbnb is a global online platform that connects hosts offering short‑term lodging (homes, apartments, rooms) with travelers seeking temporary accommodation.

## Role in International Relocation
During the move to **[[entities/dublin]]**, the user plans to stay in an Airbnb for the first month. The platform provides a **booking confirmation** that can serve as proof of address for the **[[concepts/irp_registration_process]]** required by the Irish immigration office (GNIB/Burgh Quay). The document highlights two common issues:
- Hosts may not provide a formal letter confirming the stay, which is sometimes requested by immigration officials.
- Without a host letter, the traveler must rely on alternative proof of address, such as the full booking confirmation, a printed Airbnb invoice, or an email from Airbnb support that includes the guest’s name and the property address.

## Key Facts from [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]]
- Airbnb bookings are used to satisfy the "proof of address" requirement for the **IRP registration**.
- The platform’s confirmation documents are accepted by Irish immigration when they clearly show the guest’s name, the exact Irish address, and the dates of stay.
- The user is advised to keep these documents (PDF or screenshot) in a dedicated folder as part of the relocation checklist.
- If the host does not provide a letter, the traveler should prepare backup documents: full booking confirmation, printed invoice, or Airbnb support email.

## Interaction with Other Entities & Concepts
- **[[entities/burgh_quay_registration_office]]** – the office where the IRP appointment is booked; it requires proof of address.
- **[[entities/gnib]]** – the Irish immigration authority that issues the IRP card.
- **[[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]]** – forgetting to secure adequate proof of address is listed as a typical pitfall.
- **[[concepts/visa_logistics]]** – the broader context of visa approval and entry dates where accommodation planning is a prerequisite.

## Practical Advice
1. Save the Airbnb booking confirmation as a PDF and print a hard copy for the IRP appointment.
2. Request a written confirmation from the host; if unavailable, rely on the alternative documents mentioned above.
3. Keep all accommodation proof together with other immigration paperwork (work authorisation, PPS number, etc.) to avoid last‑minute scrambling.
