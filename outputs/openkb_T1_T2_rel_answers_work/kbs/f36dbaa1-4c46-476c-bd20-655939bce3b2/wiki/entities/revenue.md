# Revenue (Irish Tax Authority)

Revenue is the national tax collector for the Republic of Ireland. It administers taxes, collects revenues, and provides services such as the online **myAccount** portal for individuals and businesses.

## Role in the relocation process (session-37)
- In the Dublin relocation checklist, new arrivals are advised to **set up Revenue/myAccount** to register for taxes, obtain a PPS number, and manage payroll deductions.  
- The checklist links directly to the Revenue setup step, indicating that the organization is a critical point for ensuring compliant employment and tax filing after moving to [[entities/ireland]].
- Failure to register promptly can cause payroll delays and affect access to public services.

## Key interactions
- **myAccount registration** – an online account where expatriates can file tax returns, view liabilities, and request refunds.  
- **PPS number linkage** – the Personal Public Service (PPS) number, issued separately, must be entered into Revenue’s system for correct tax processing.
- **Employer cooperation** – employers often confirm payroll setup with Revenue on behalf of the employee.

## Related concepts and entities
- Related to the [[concepts/irp_registration_process]] because an IRP card holder must also be tax‑registered.
