# Garda National Immigration Bureau (GNIB)

**Type:** organization

## Overview
The Garda National Immigration Bureau (GNIB) is the Irish government agency responsible for immigration control, including the issuance of work permits, registration of non‑EEA residents, and the management of the Irish Residence Permit (IRP) system.

## Role in the Dublin relocation (session‑37_2025‑02‑14)
In the context of the relocation checklist from [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]], GNIB is the authority where newcomers must:
- Book and attend the IRP registration appointment, often at the Burgh Quay office.
- Submit proof of address, employment contract, and the approved work authorisation.
- Pay the IRP processing fee and receive the [[entities/irp_card]].

Failure to register with GNIB within the required window can lead to status gaps and complications for payroll, tax registration, and health‑care access.

## Key Facts Mentioned in the Document
