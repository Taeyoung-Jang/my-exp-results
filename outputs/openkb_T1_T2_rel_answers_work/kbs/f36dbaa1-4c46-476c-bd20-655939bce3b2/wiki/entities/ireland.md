# Ireland (Place)

Ireland is the country where the user in [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-14]] is moving for a two‑year contract in Dublin. The relocation plan highlights several Ireland‑specific administrative steps and common pitfalls.

## Key Facts from the Session
- **Visa logistics**: The move hinges on a work authorisation (visa) that must be approved before entry. Understanding the visa category is essential for timeline management and future employment flexibility. [[concepts/visa_logistics]]
- **Entry and registration**: After the visa is approved, the user must track the earliest lawful entry date and, upon arrival, register with Irish immigration at the Burgh Quay office (formerly GNIB). This is part of the IRP registration process. [[concepts/irp_registration_process]]
- **IRP registration documents**: Required proof includes the work authorisation approval letter, employment contract, proof of address (e.g., Airbnb booking confirmation or other alternatives), and private health insurance. The session notes common missing items such as proof of address and passport‑sized photos.
- **Proof of address alternatives**: If an Airbnb host does not provide a letter, the user can use a full booking confirmation showing the address, a printed Airbnb invoice, or an email from Airbnb support confirming stay details.
- **Additional Ireland‑specific tasks**:
  - Obtain an Irish SIM card. [[entities/irish_sim]]
  - Apply for a PPS number. [[entities/pps_number]]
  - Set up Revenue/myAccount for tax. [[entities/revenue]]
  - Open an Irish bank account and arrange health insurance/GP registration.
  - Receive the IRP card after registration. [[entities/irp_card]]
- **Common relocation pitfalls**: Over‑reliance on the employer’s immigration team, under‑estimating processing times, and forgetting essential documents for the IRP registration. [[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]]

Awareness of the outlined steps and potential pitfalls can help ensure a smooth transition to living and working in Dublin.
