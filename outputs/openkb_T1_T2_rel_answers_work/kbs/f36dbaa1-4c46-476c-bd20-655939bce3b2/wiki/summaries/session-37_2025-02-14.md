# Summary of session-37_2025-02-14

**Main focus:** Practical advice for an international work relocation to Dublin, with a detailed checklist of dates, responsibilities, and documentation, plus highlights of typical failure points.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/visa_logistics]] – understanding visa categories, employer obligations, and approval timelines.  
- Immigration timeline management – mapping critical dates from application filing to IRP card receipt.  
- [[concepts/common_relocation_pitfalls]] – over‑reliance on employer immigration teams, under‑estimating processing times, and missing documentation.  
- [[concepts/irp_registration_process]] – steps for registering with Irish immigration (GNIB/Burgh Quay) and required proofs.  
- Proof of address options – acceptable documents when primary proof (e.g., Airbnb letter) is unavailable.

## Findings & Recommendations
1. **Clarify the visa path first** – the specific work permit dictates all downstream actions.  
2. **Track a sequence of dates** – filing, approval, lawful entry, arrival, IRP appointment, fee payment, PPS number, bank account, health insurance, and IRP card receipt.  
3. **Assign ownership** – clearly note whether the employer, a relocation service, or you are responsible for each task.  
4. **Maintain proof** – keep emails, confirmation letters, receipts, and screenshots for every milestone; these are essential if timelines slip.  
5. **Anticipate slip scenarios** – each delayed step propagates forward, potentially causing travel postponements or status gaps.  
6. **IRP registration documentation** – primary proof of address can be an Airbnb booking confirmation, a printed invoice, or an email from Airbnb support. Backup options include a utility bill in your name (if available), a letter from a host with a notarised statement, or a lease agreement for a short‑term sublet.  
7. **Additional Ireland‑specific tasks** – obtain an [[entities/irish_sim]], apply for a [[entities/pps_number]], set up [[entities/revenue]]/myAccount, confirm payroll, open a bank account, and arrange health insurance/GP registration.

## Practical Output
- A **copy‑paste‑ready table** (provided in the conversation) that lists each task, owner, proof, consequences of delay, and useful links (e.g., Burgh Quay appointment site, Revenue myAccount).  
- Specific guidance on **proof of address** for IRP registration when staying in an Airbnb, plus 2–3 backup document alternatives.
