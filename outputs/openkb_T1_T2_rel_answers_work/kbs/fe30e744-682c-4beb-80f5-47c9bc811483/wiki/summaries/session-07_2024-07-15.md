## Overview  
A tendonitis‑friendly 3‑day upper‑body strength program that avoids overhead presses, uses neutral‑grip and low‑angle movements, and includes clear progression, rest intervals, and alternative exercise swaps.

## Day 1 – Horizontal Press + Row + Lateral Raise  
- **Main press:** Neutral‑grip dumbbell press (flat or slight‑decline) – 3‑4 × 5‑8 reps, 2 min rest. *Swap:* decline dumbbell press or neutral‑grip machine chest press.  
- **Row:** Chest‑supported row, neutral/underhand grip – 3‑4 × 6‑10 reps, 90 s rest. *Swap:* single‑arm cable row or land‑mine row.  
- **Finisher:** Cable lateral raises, slight bend, raise to comfortable height – 2‑3 × 12‑15 reps, 60 s rest. *Swap:* light dumbbell scapular‑plane raise or reduced range.

## Day 2 – Low‑Incline / Landmine Press + Pull‑Down + Rear‑Delts  
- **Main press:** Very low‑incline neutral‑grip dumbbell press or low‑angle landmine press – 3‑4 × 6‑10 reps, 90 s rest. *Swap:* low‑angle machine press.  
- **Pull:** Neutral‑grip lat‑pull down – 3‑4 × 6‑10 reps, 90 s rest. *Swap:* high‑angle cable row or band‑assisted neutral‑grip pull‑up.  
- **Finisher:** Rear‑deltoid cable pull (cross‑body) – 2‑3 × 12‑15 reps, 60 s rest. *Swap:* prone bench rear‑deltoid raise with light dumbbells.

## Day 3 – Close‑Grip Neutral Press + Single‑Arm Row + External Rotation  
- **Main press:** Close‑grip neutral bench press or neutral‑grip push‑up handles – 3‑4 × 5‑8 reps, 2 min rest. *Swap:* narrow‑grip machine chest press.  
- **Row:** Single‑arm cable or dumbbell row – 3‑4 × 8‑10 reps, 90 s rest. *Swap:* chest‑supported row (higher bench) or low‑cable row.  
- **Finisher:** Cable external‑rotation (elbow tucked) – 2‑3 × 12‑15 per side, 60 s rest. *Swap:* light resistance band rotation or reduced range.

## Pain Management Rules  
- Mild, transient discomfort is acceptable; any sharp or lingering pain → shorten range, reduce load, or use an alternate swap.  
- Keep elbows closer to the torso and avoid long levers to protect the rotator cuff and tendons.

## Progression & Flare‑up Strategy  
- **Standard weeks:** Add one rep per set until the top of the rep range, then increase weight 2‑5 % and return to the lower rep end. See [[concepts/progression-strategies]].  
- **Flare‑up weeks:** Cut load to ~50 % of regular weight, use slower tempo, and stay strictly pain‑free; resume normal loading once symptoms subside. This follows the principles of [[concepts/tendonitis-friendly-training]] and [[concepts/shoulder-health]].
