# University Registrar Office

The **University Registrar Office** is the central administrative unit responsible for maintaining official academic records, confirming degree conferrals, issuing diplomas, transcripts, and sealed transcripts, and updating student information.

## Key Functions (as referenced in [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-11]])
- **Document issuance**: Provides final official transcripts, sealed transcripts, and diplomas after graduation.
- **Record correction**: Handles name‑spelling errors and other inaccuracies through the [[concepts/document-correction-workflow]]. Corrections require legal proof such as a passport or birth certificate.
- **Verification support**: Updates the university’s verification system so external parties (employers, licensing boards) can confirm degrees.
- **Student account transition**: Coordinates the shutdown of student email and portal access, ensuring graduates retrieve needed files before accounts close.

## Interaction with Graduates
Graduates are advised to contact the Registrar Office promptly when:
- A name is misspelled or missing diacritics on a diploma or transcript.
- They need sealed transcripts for job applications, visas, or further study.
- They want confirmation that their corrected information propagates to all university systems.
