# Alumni Portal

**Type:** Product  
**Description:** Online platform for graduates to stay connected and access alumni services.

## Overview
The Alumni Portal is a dedicated online system provided by universities for former students after they graduate. It replaces the student account for many services and serves as a hub for networking, career resources, event information, and continued access to certain university records.

## Role in Post‑Graduation Administration
According to [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-11]], graduates should **join the alumni portal within the first three to six months after graduation**. The portal helps maintain a link to the institution, allowing alumni to:
- Receive updates on credential verification and document correction processes.
- Access alumni‑only versions of transcripts or request updates to official records.
- Stay informed about [[concepts/post-graduation-administration]] tasks and deadlines.

## Key Actions Mentioned in Session 37
1. **Join the portal** – “If you haven’t joined your alumni portal yet, do that now,” the assistant advises, highlighting its importance for continued communication.
2. **Use the portal for document updates** – While the session focuses on correcting a misspelled name, the alumni portal can serve as a channel to request new sealed transcripts that match the corrected name.
3. **Leverage alumni networking** – Though not detailed in the session, the portal typically offers networking events and mentorship opportunities valuable for recent graduates.
