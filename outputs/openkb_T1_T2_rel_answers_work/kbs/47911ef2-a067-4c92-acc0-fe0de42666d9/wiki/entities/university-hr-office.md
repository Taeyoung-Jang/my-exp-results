# University HR/Benefits Office

The University HR/Benefits Office is the administrative unit responsible for managing human resources functions and benefits programs for students, staff, and faculty at the university. It handles inquiries about eligibility for the student health plan, student discount programs, and related documentation requirements.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-11_2024-05-12]]
- Receives email inquiries about **student health plan eligibility** and **student discount programs**.
- Provides guidance on required documentation such as enrollment letters or advisor notes, linking to the concept of [[concepts/documentation-requirements]].
- Answers questions about coverage start/end dates, pro‑rated plans, and potential gaps if a dissertation defense date shifts, relating to [[concepts/student-health-plan-eligibility]].
- Interacts with the user through drafted email communications, illustrating the practice of [[concepts/email-drafting]].
