## Summary

This document records a dialogue in which the user requests assistance creating short, polite email drafts to a university HR/benefits office. The goal is to confirm eligibility for the [[entities/student-health-plan]] and any [[entities/student-discount-programs]] during the interim period before the dissertation defense. The assistant provides:

1. An initial concise email (4–5 sentences) with a subject line.
2. A more formal alternative version.
3. A refined formal draft that:
   - Asks about coverage start/end dates and whether the plan is pro‑rated.
   - Inquires about potential coverage gaps if the defense date shifts.
   - Includes placeholders for student ID and expected defense date.
   - Swaps “related benefits” for “student discount programs” and moderates the tone to be less stiff while remaining formal.
   - Is formatted as a single paragraph for easy copy‑pasting.
