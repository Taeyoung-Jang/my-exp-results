# Document Correction Workflow

This concept outlines the systematic actions needed to fix mistakes—such as misspelled names or incorrect dates—on official academic records like diplomas and sealed transcripts. It is derived from the guidance given in [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-11]].

## Key Steps
1. **Identify the Error Early**
   - Review the diploma, unofficial transcript, and any previously issued sealed transcripts for inconsistencies.
2. **Contact the Registrar**
   - Reach out to the [[entities/university-registrar-office]] promptly. Explain the mistake and request a correction.
3. **Provide Legal Proof**
   - Submit a copy of a government‑issued ID (passport, national ID, or birth certificate) that shows the correct spelling, including any accent marks.
4. **Request Updated Documents**
   - Ask for a corrected diploma, updated official transcript, and new sealed transcripts that reflect the accurate name.
5. **Verify System‑Wide Updates**
   - Confirm that the student record, degree conferral record, and any verification databases used by employers or licensing boards have been updated.
6. **Archive the Corrected Versions**
   - Scan the corrected documents, store high‑quality copies in multiple locations (cloud, external drive), and keep the original safe.
7. **Notify External Stakeholders**
   - Use an [[concepts/email-drafting]] approach to inform employers, graduate programs, or credential evaluators of the update, attaching the corrected files.

## Practical Tips
- **Act Quickly** – Corrections become harder once records are archived.
- **Keep Proof of the Request** – Save email threads with the registrar as evidence of the correction process.
- **Order New Sealed Transcripts** – Even if you have old ones, future recipients should see the corrected name.
- **Sync Digital Profiles** – Update your name on alumni portals, professional networking sites, and any university‑linked services.
