# Student Health Plan Eligibility

**Definition**: The set of conditions, documentation, and timelines that determine whether a currently‑enrolled student can access the university's health insurance and associated discount programs.

## Core Elements

1. **Enrollment Status**
   - The student must be officially enrolled, even if the dissertation defense is pending. This interim status often requires confirmation from the university.
2. **Coverage Period**
   - Eligibility inquiries typically include questions about **coverage start and end dates**, and whether the plan is **pro‑rated** to the exact enrollment window.
3. **Potential Gaps**
   - If the defense date shifts, students need to know whether a gap in coverage will occur.
4. **Required Documentation**
   - Common proofs include an **enrollment letter**, a note from an academic advisor, or other verification as outlined in [[concepts/documentation-requirements]].

## Related Activities

- Drafting concise, polite inquiries to the HR/benefits office is essential. See [[concepts/email-drafting]] for best practices on tone, structure, and placeholders (e.g., student ID, expected defense date).
