# Documentation Requirements

Documentation requirements describe the specific paperwork a university HR or benefits office needs to verify a student's eligibility for the **student health plan** and any **student discount programs**.  They also encompass the broader set of records graduates must secure immediately after completing a degree, as outlined in the [[concepts/post-graduation-administration]] timeline.

## Core Items for Current Students
- An enrollment verification letter or official enrollment status confirmation.
- A note or endorsement from the academic advisor confirming ongoing student status.
- The student's identification number (student ID) for record matching.
- The expected dissertation defense date, when relevant to eligibility timelines.

These items enable the [[entities/university-hr-office]] to determine coverage start and end dates, assess whether the plan is pro‑rated, and identify any potential gaps in benefits.  The process is closely tied to the creation of concise, polite requests, which falls under [[concepts/email-drafting]].

## Additional Documentation for Recent Graduates
When a student graduates, the same principle of providing clear, official documentation applies, but the focus shifts to confirming the finalized degree and correcting any record errors.  Key steps include:
- Requesting the final official transcript and confirming that the degree has been formally conferred.
- Verifying that the official diploma (including any corrected name accents) matches the name on the student record.
- Ordering new sealed transcripts if previous versions contain misspellings or outdated information.
- Providing legal proof (passport, ID, birth certificate) when correcting name errors with the [[entities/university-registrar-office]].

These graduate‑focused documents are essential for later verification by employers, licensing boards, and credential evaluators, and they support a smooth transition from student to alumni status.

## Relationship to Eligibility Concepts
- **Student Health Plan Eligibility** – The documentation supplied directly influences whether a student can access health coverage, as detailed in [[concepts/student-health-plan-eligibility]].
- **Post‑Graduation Administration** – Maintaining accurate records and correcting errors ensures that alumni can still prove their credentials for future professional or educational opportunities, tying back to the broader [[concepts/post-graduation-administration]] workflow.

By understanding and assembling the proper documentation, students and recent graduates can efficiently satisfy institutional requirements, avoid unnecessary delays, and ensure continuous access to health, discount, and professional verification services.
