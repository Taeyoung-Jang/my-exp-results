# Employer Communication Strategy

## Definition
A set of best‑practice tactics for notifying current or prospective employers, graduate programs, and other stakeholders about updates or corrections to official academic documents (e.g., name spelling, accent marks) in a clear, professional manner.

## Core Elements
1. **Timing** – Initiate communication as soon as corrected documents are in hand.
2. **Clarity** – Keep the message brief, factual, and focused on the administrative nature of the correction.
3. **Documentation** – Attach the updated diploma, sealed transcript, or other relevant file; if the portal blocks new uploads, email the appropriate contact directly.
4. **Tone** – Frame the update as an *institutional* correction (e.g., registrar’s error) rather than a personal issue to avoid any perception of inconsistency.
5. **Follow‑up** – Confirm receipt and that the new file replaces the previous one in the recipient’s records.

## Recommended Communication Template
> Subject: Updated Academic Document – [Your Name]
>
> Dear [Contact Name/Admissions Team/HR],
>
> My university recently corrected the spelling of my legal name (added the missing accent). Please find the updated diploma (and/or transcript) attached so that your records are accurate.
>
> Thank you for updating my file.
>
> Best regards,
>
> *Your Name* – [Phone] – [Email]

This template follows the principles outlined in [[concepts/email-drafting]] and aligns with the broader [[concepts/post-graduation-administration]] workflow.

## Connection to Document Correction
When a name or other detail is amended, the **[[concepts/document-correction-workflow]]** should be completed first (contact registrar, provide legal ID, obtain corrected copies). Only after the corrected documents exist does the employer communication step commence.

## Documentation Requirements
Employers often request **sealed transcripts** or official diplomas; ensure the corrected versions meet the same standards as outlined in [[concepts/documentation-requirements]].

## Practical Example from Source
In [[summaries/session-37_2025-02-11]], the user received a corrected diploma missing an accent mark and was advised to:
- Draft a concise update email.
- Attach the new diploma.
- Explain that the change was an administrative correction.
- Expect that background checks will later align with the corrected record.
