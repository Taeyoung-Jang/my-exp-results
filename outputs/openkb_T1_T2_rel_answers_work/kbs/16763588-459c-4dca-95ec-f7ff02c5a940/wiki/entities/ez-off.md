## EZ Off Jar Opener

**Type:** product

The EZ Off Jar Opener is a wall‑mounted, V‑shaped device designed to open jars with a single hand. Once installed under an upper cabinet, a jar is placed into the opener’s teeth and twisted using the good hand, eliminating the need for the other hand to brace or apply wrist pressure. This makes it especially useful for users recovering from wrist injuries.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]]**:

- Suggested mounting location: front underside of an upper cabinet near the main prep area for easy reach without twisting the body.
