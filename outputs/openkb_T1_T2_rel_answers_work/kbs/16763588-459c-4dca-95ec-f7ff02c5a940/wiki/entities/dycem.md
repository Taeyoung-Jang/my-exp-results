# Dycem

Dycem is a **non‑slip mat** that provides a high‑friction surface without being sticky. It is commonly placed under bowls, cutting boards, and containers to keep them from sliding, making it especially useful for **one‑handed cooking** and other single‑hand tasks.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]]
- Recommended as part of a “set‑it‑and‑forget‑it” kitchen kit for users with limited wrist use.
- Works well with the **Swedish cutting board** and **Sammons Preston** boards, allowing food to stay steady without wrist pressure.
- Pairs with other wrist‑friendly tools such as the **EZ Off** jar opener and **rocker knife** to create a fully **wrist‑friendly tool design**.
- Provides a stable base for **OXO Good Grips** mixing bowls and other kitchenware.
