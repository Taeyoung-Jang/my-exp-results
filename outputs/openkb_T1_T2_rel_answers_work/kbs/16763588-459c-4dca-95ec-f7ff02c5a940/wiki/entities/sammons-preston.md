## Sammons Preston Cutting Board

**Type:** Product (kitchen tool)

### Overview
The *Sammons Preston* cutting board is a specialty kitchen accessory featuring built‑in spikes or corner guards that hold food securely in place. It is marketed for users who need to perform kitchen tasks with a single hand, especially when wrist support is limited.

### Key Features
- **Spiked surface** that grips vegetables, fruit, bread, or meat without the need for a stabilizing hand.
- **Corner guard** provides additional stability for irregularly shaped items.
- Constructed from durable wood or composite material, resistant to cuts and warping.
- Thin enough to fit comfortably on standard countertops yet sturdy enough to stay flat on a non‑slip mat.

### Relevance to One‑Handed Cooking
In the context of [[concepts/one-handed-cooking]], the board eliminates the need to press down with the injured wrist, allowing the user to focus on the cutting motion with a single hand. It aligns with [[concepts/wrist-friendly-tool-design]] by minimizing required wrist pressure and supporting ergonomic workflow.

### Integration in a One‑Handed Kitchen Setup
The board is recommended alongside other wrist‑friendly tools such as the [[entities/ez-off]] under‑cabinet jar opener, a [[entities/rocker-knife]] for rocking cuts, and a [[entities/swedish-cutting-board]] as alternative spiked options. Placing the board on a [[entities/dycem]] non‑slip mat further enhances stability without extra hand effort.
