# OXO Good Grips

**Type:** product

OXO Good Grips is a brand of kitchen utensils and accessories known for their ergonomic, non‑slip handles and user‑friendly designs. In the context of one‑handed cooking, the brand is highlighted for two specific items:

- **Rubber‑cone jar opener** – a simple, rubber‑based tool that increases grip on jar lids, allowing a single hand to twist open containers without extra force.
- **Mixing bowls with rubberized bases** – bowls that stay stationary on a non‑slip surface, useful when the user cannot apply stabilizing pressure with an injured wrist.

These products are recommended in [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]] as part of a “set‑it‑and‑forget‑it” kitchen kit for people needing wrist‑friendly, one‑handed solutions.
