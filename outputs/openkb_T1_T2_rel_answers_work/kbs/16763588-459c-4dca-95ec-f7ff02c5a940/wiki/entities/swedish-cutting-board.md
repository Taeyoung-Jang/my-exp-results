## Swedish Cutting Board

The **Swedish Cutting Board** is a kitchen work surface designed with built‑in spikes or a corner guard that securely holds food items in place. It is marketed as a *wrist‑friendly* tool, allowing users to cut vegetables, fruits, bread, and other foods without needing to press down with the wrist or hand that holds the board.

### Key features highlighted in [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]]
- **Stabilization**: Spikes grip produce, eliminating the need for a second hand to steady items.
- **Wrist‑friendly design**: Users can rest their forearm or elbow for balance; the board does not require weight or pressure on the injured wrist.
- **Compatibility**: Works well with a **rocker knife** for efficient one‑handed slicing.
- **Portability**: Light enough to move on a non‑slip mat (e.g., [[entities/dycem]]) but sturdy during use.

### Recommended use cases
- Preparing meals with a single functional hand, especially during rehabilitation where wrist loading is limited.
- Pairing with other one‑handed tools such as the **OXO Good Grips** jar opener, the **EZ Off** under‑cabinet jar opener, and a **rocker knife** for streamlined cutting workflows.

### Purchasing considerations
- Look for boards with sturdy, stainless‑steel spikes and a non‑slip base.
- Ensure the board size fits your workspace and can be easily placed on a [[entities/dycem]] mat for extra stability.
