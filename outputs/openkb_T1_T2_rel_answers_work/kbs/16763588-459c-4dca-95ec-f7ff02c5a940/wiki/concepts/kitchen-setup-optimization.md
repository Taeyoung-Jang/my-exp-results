# Kitchen Setup Optimization

**Definition**: Kitchen Setup Optimization is the practice of arranging tools, surfaces, and storage in a kitchen to enable efficient one‑handed tasks while minimizing strain on injured or limited wrists. It draws on principles of [[concepts/one-handed-cooking]] and [[concepts/wrist-friendly-tool-design]] to create a workflow that is both safe and fast.

## Core Principles
1. **Stabilize Every Surface** – Use a high‑friction mat (e.g., [[entities/dycem]]) to keep bowls, cutting boards, and containers from sliding.
2. **Hands‑Free Mechanisms** – Install under‑cabinet devices such as the [[entities/ez-off]] jar opener to eliminate the need for a second hand or wrist pressure.
3. **Spike or Guard Cutting Boards** – Boards with built‑in spikes or corner guards (e.g., [[entities/swedish-cutting-board]] or [[entities/sammons-preston]]) keep food stationary, allowing a rocker‑style knife to cut without downward wrist force.
4. **Ergonomic Tools** – Choose tools designed for single‑hand operation, such as a [[entities/rocker-knife]] for slicing and a one‑handed can opener (e.g., [[entities/kohn-rikon]]) that clamps and turns with one grip.
5. **Strategic Placement** – Position frequently used items within easy reach of the good hand: mount the EZ Off near the front underside of an upper cabinet, keep the non‑slip mat movable, and locate the cutting board near the counter edge so the forearm can provide balance without wrist load.
6. **Minimal Setup, Maximum Return** – Aim for a “set‑it‑and‑forget‑it” kit of 5‑7 core items that together cover most prep, opening, and cleaning tasks.

## Example Kit (derived from [[summaries/session-39_2025-03-14]])
- **Dycem non‑slip mat** – universal grip surface.
- **EZ Off under‑cabinet jar opener** – hands‑free jar opening.
- **Swedish (or Sammons Preston) spiked cutting board** – food‑holding board.
- **Rocker knife** – one‑handed slicing.
- **One‑handed can opener** (OXO or [[entities/kohn-rikon]]) – clamping opener.
- **Rubber‑base mixing bowl (OXO)** – stays put on the mat.
- **Pump‑style soap dispenser** – hands‑free dispensing.

## Benefits
- **Reduced wrist strain** – No need to brace or press down with the injured wrist.
- **Increased speed** – Stabilized surfaces and hands‑free tools cut down on extra motions.
- **Greater independence** – Users can perform a full range of kitchen tasks with a single functional hand.
