# Summary of Session 32 – Boston Knee‑Friendly Weekend Planning

**Context** – User is recovering from knee surgery and needs low‑impact weekend activities within a 30‑45 minute drive of Cambridge/Somerville. They want options for both rainy and nice weather, walking estimates, parking/entrance advice, and ultra‑low walking variants (< 0.25 mi/day).

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/low_impact_travel]]** – Activities that minimize walking and stairs, suitable for physical‑therapy schedules.  
- **[[concepts/rainy_day_planning]]** – Indoor‑focused alternatives and how to adapt outdoor sites.  
- **[[concepts/ultra_low_walking]]** – Plans keeping total walking under ~0.25 mi per day, often driven‑only with one indoor destination.  
- **Walking estimates** – Rough mileage ranges (0.25–0.5 mi, 0.5–1 mi) to help manage knee strain.  
- **Accessibility notes** – Elevators, ramps, benches, and restroom locations.  
- **Parking tips** – Preferred lots, valet options, and low‑stress entry points.

## Ultra‑Low Walking Variants (< 0.25 mi/day)

- **Concord/Lexington**: Saturday – Concord Museum only (parking adjacent, benches throughout). Sunday – [[entities/decordova_museum]] (step‑free lot, elevator, short café visit).  
- **Arboretum/Harvard**: Saturday – Harvard Museum of Natural History (52 Oxford St garage, elevators). Sunday – [[entities/harvard_art_museums]] (Broadway Garage, elevator, café right off lobby).  
- **Gloucester/Salem**: Saturday – [[entities/beauport_hotel]] all‑day (valet, lobby/restaurant on main floor). Sunday – [[entities/peabody_essex_museum]] (Church Street lot, elevators, café near atrium).  
- **Staycation**: Saturday – [[entities/whitney_hotel]] stay (valet, elevators, lounge). Sunday – [[entities/porter_square_books]] (shopping‑center lot, level entrance, café inside).

## Key Takeaways
- Each weekend includes **rainy** and **nice** versions, allowing flexibility without exceeding the user’s walking limit.  
- Parking recommendations focus on **direct‑to‑entrance lots**, **valet**, or **garage** locations to reduce steps.  
- Accessibility notes highlight **elevators, ramps, benches, and restroom proximity** for all indoor sites.  
- The **ultra‑low walking** options provide a mostly drive‑and‑indoor experience, ideal during high‑pain therapy days.  

These curated itineraries enable the user to enjoy food, books, nature, and culture while protecting their knee recovery.
