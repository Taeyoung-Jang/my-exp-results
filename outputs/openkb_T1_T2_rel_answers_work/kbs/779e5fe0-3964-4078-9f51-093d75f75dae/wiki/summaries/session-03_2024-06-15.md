# Summary of session-03_2024-06-15

**Main contribution**: A practical framework for staying organized when alternating between several living spaces. It introduces a minimal **essential kit** that always travels, a **duplication strategy** for items kept at each location, and a tight **weekly switching routine** with leave and arrive checklists (≤10 items each).

## Core concepts
- **[[concepts/essential_kit]]** – a small, consistently packed pouch containing items that must travel with you (e.g., meds, primary charger, wallet, keys, comfort items).  
- **[[concepts/duplication_strategy]]** – keep full‑size versions of bulk or location‑specific items (toiletries, clothing, chargers) at each site, minimizing what needs to be moved.  
- **[[concepts/anchor_routines]]** – a few portable rituals (morning check‑in, nightly wind‑down, 10‑minute reset) that create a sense of “home” wherever you are.  
- leave checklist – a repeatable, 10‑step list executed before departing a space to ensure nothing essential is left behind and the place is reset.  
- arrive checklist – a complementary 10‑step list for settling into the new location, confirming that travel items are present and the environment is ready.  
- portable home base – a tiny set of comfort objects (e.g., earplugs, favorite pen, eye mask) and a digital space (playlist, notes folder) that signal “home” upon arrival.

## Two‑column item split
| Keep at each place | Always travel |
|---|---|
| Full toiletries & shower items | Work laptop |
| Sleepwear & basic clothing | Work folder / gear |
| Backup phone & laptop chargers | Medication / supplements |
| Hair tools / grooming items | Primary charger & battery pack |
| Gym clothes & shoes | Wallet & keys |
| Water bottle | Earbuds / headphones |
| Snacks / breakfast basics | Small comforts pouch (eye mask, pen, lip balm) |
| Extra cables / adapters | Any non‑duplicated clothing |
| (etc.) | Personal tech that isn’t duplicated |

## Tight leave checklist (10 items)
1. Take laptop  
2. Take meds  
3. Take wallet & keys  
4. Grab travel pouch  
5. Pack any non‑duplicated clothes  
6. Pack work gear  
7. Verify chargers & cables you brought  
8. Quick room reset (trash, dishes)  
9. Lights off  
10. Door locked  

## Tight arrive checklist (10 items)
1. Set bag down in one spot  
2. Unpack laptop & meds  
3. Check travel pouch completeness  
4. Plug devices to charge  
5. Restock low items at this location  
6. Store brought clothes  
7. Review planner for the day  
8. Set out comfort items  
9. Confirm keys & wallet location  
10. Quick space scan to locate everything  

## Practical takeaways
- **Minimize decision load** by keeping the travel pouch unchanged and only restocking when needed.  
- **Standardize the routine**; repeating the same short sequence builds automaticity and reduces mental overhead.  
- **Leverage duplication** to keep bulk items local, freeing the travel pouch for truly essential, non‑duplicable items.  
- **Use the checklists** as screenshot‑ready references to ensure consistency across weekly location swaps.
