# Essential Kit

**Definition**: A small, consistently packed pouch containing the few items that must accompany you wherever you go, regardless of which location you are staying in.

## Core Idea
The Essential Kit is the anchor of an **[[concepts/anchor_routines]]** system. By limiting the number of items you ever move, you reduce decision fatigue and ensure you always have the most critical personal and functional items on hand.

## Typical Contents (as outlined in [[summaries/session-03_2024-06-15]])
| Category | Example Items |
|---|---|
| Health | Medication, supplements |
| Power | Primary phone charger, battery pack |
| Essentials | Wallet, keys |
| Comfort | Earbuds/headphones, small comfort pouch (eye mask, pen, lip balm) |
| Work‑specific | Work laptop, work folder/gear (if not duplicated) |
| Personal tech | Any device that cannot be duplicated |
| Clothing | Any non‑duplicated clothing you brought |

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/duplication_strategy]]**: The Essential Kit works hand‑in‑hand with duplication. Bulk or easily replaceable items (toiletries, full‑size chargers, extra clothing) stay at each location, leaving the Essential Kit to contain only the irreplaceable, travel‑only items.
- **[[concepts/anchor_routines]]**: Using the same kit each time creates a reliable ritual cue. Opening the pouch signals the start of a routine (e.g., a quick morning check‑in) that reinforces a sense of home across sites.

## Benefits
1. **Decision reduction** – No need to rethink what to bring each move.
2. **Consistency** – Same items, same place, everywhere you go.
3. **Speed** – Packing and unpacking become a single, repeatable action.
4. **Reliability** – Critical items (meds, power, wallet) are never left behind.

## Practical Tips from the Source
- Keep the pouch **always packed**; only restock when you notice something is low.
- Choose a **compact, durable zip pouch** that fits comfortably in any bag.
- Review the contents weekly using the **leave/arrive checklists** to ensure nothing is missing.
