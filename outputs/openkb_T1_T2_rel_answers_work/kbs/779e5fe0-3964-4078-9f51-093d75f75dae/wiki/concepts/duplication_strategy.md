# Duplication Strategy

**Definition**: A practical approach that separates items into two groups – those that stay permanently at each living space and those that always travel in a compact essential kit. By duplicating bulk or location‑specific items, the traveler reduces the number of things that need to be moved, minimizing decision fatigue and ensuring consistency across multiple homes.

## Core Principles
- **Location‑specific stock**: Keep full‑size versions of toiletries, clothing basics, chargers, and other bulk items at each site. This eliminates the need to pack and unpack these repeatedly.
- **Travel‑only essentials**: Pack a small, consistently assembled pouch (see [[concepts/essential_kit]]) that contains truly non‑duplicable items such as medication, primary chargers, wallet, keys, and comfort items.
- **Clear division**: Decide upfront which items *never move* and which *always travel*. This binary split creates a reliable mental model and a predictable packing routine.

## Implementation from Session 03
The strategy was outlined in the conversation on 2024‑06‑15, where the assistant suggested:
- Maintaining **full‑size toiletries, sleepwear, basic clothing, backup chargers, gym gear, water bottle, snacks, and extra cables** at each location.
- Keeping a **travel pouch** with a work laptop, work folder, medication, primary charger, wallet, keys, earbuds, a small comforts pouch, and any non‑duplicated clothing.

These lists demonstrate the concrete application of the duplication strategy, enabling the user to switch weekly between two homes without forgetting essential items.

## Benefits
1. **Reduced mental load** – Fewer decisions about what to pack each time.
2. **Consistency** – The same items are always in the same place, reinforcing routine and stability.
3. **Efficiency** – Quick packing/unpacking flows integrate with the [[concepts/anchor_routines]] for seamless transitions.
