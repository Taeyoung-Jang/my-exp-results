# Low‑Impact Travel

**Definition**

Low‑Impact Travel refers to a set of travel strategies that keep walking distances short, avoid stairs or steep terrain, and prioritize easy parking and accessible entrances. It is especially useful for people in physical‑therapy or with mobility constraints.

**Key Principles**

- **Walking ≤ 0.5 mi per day** (or even ≤ 0.25 mi for ultra‑low‑walking variants).  
- **Flat, paved paths** or indoor venues with elevators and ramps.  
- **Proximity parking**: lots or garages located right next to the entrance, or valet service.  
- **Bench and restroom availability** near entrances for rest breaks.  
- **Rain‑or‑shine flexibility**: indoor alternatives for bad weather while still staying within the walking limit.
