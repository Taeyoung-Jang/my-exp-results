# Ultra‑Low Walking

**Type:** Concept

**Description:** Planning trips that require virtually no walking, typically under 0.25 mi per day, by relying on driving, indoor venues, and highly accessible sites.

## Core Idea
Ultra‑low walking itineraries are designed for individuals who need to limit foot‑based activity because of injury, recovery, or mobility constraints. The goal is to keep total walking distance for a whole day well below a quarter of a mile while still providing a sense of a getaway. This is achieved by:

1. **Choosing a single indoor destination** (museum, hotel lounge, café) that offers ample seating, benches, and on‑site amenities.
2. **Parking as close as possible to the entrance**, often using valet or dedicated garage lots that place the vehicle within a few steps of the venue.
3. **Ensuring full accessibility** – elevators, ramps, flat pathways, and nearby restrooms.
4. **Limiting extra excursions** to brief, step‑free stops (e.g., a quick coffee or hotel lobby).

## Tips for Implementing Ultra‑Low Walking Plans
1. **Map Parking First** – Verify that a lot or valet service is within a 1‑2 minute flat walk to the venue entrance.
2. **Check Accessibility Features** – Confirm elevators, ramps, and bench locations via venue websites or phone calls.
3. **Pre‑Select a “Linger‑Friendly” Café** – Choose spots with ample seating and power outlets; this maximizes rest time.
4. **Limit Extra Stops** – If a secondary destination is desired, pick one that shares the same parking area or is reachable via a short, level walk.
5. **Schedule Around Therapy Sessions** – Align travel times so you arrive early, reducing the need for rushed walking.
