# Tatte Bakery & Café

Tatte is a Boston‑area bakery‑café chain renowned for its pastries, coffee, and spacious seating that makes it a favorite spot for **low‑impact travel** and **rainy‑day planning**.  In the *session‑32_2024-11-15* recommendations, Tatte was highlighted as a “linger‑friendly” option for both weekend and stay‑cation itineraries.

---

## Key Facts from Session 32

- **Locations referenced**: Near Harvard Art Museums on Brattle Street in **[[entities/cambridge]]**, and on Charles Street in **[[entities/cambridge]]**/Beacon Hill (accessible from the Whitney Hotel stay‑cation).
- **Why it’s suggested**:
  - Easy parking nearby (street or garage) and a level entrance.
  - Plenty of indoor seating, ideal when the user wants to rest the knee.
  - Close to other low‑impact destinations such as the **Arnold Arboretum**, **Harvard Art Museums**, and the **Charles River Esplanade**.
- **Accessibility notes**: Ground‑floor entry, no stairs required, and restrooms are on the same level as the café area.
- **Fit with planning concepts**:
  - Supports [[concepts/low_impact_travel]] by offering a quiet base for short, gentle outings.
  - Serves as a fallback for [[concepts/rainy_day_planning]] when outdoor activities are limited.
  - Can be part of an [[concepts/ultra_low_walking]] itinerary where the user spends most of the day seated.
