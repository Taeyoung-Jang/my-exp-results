## Beauport Hotel

Beauport Hotel is a boutique property located on the waterfront in **Gloucester, Massachusetts**.  It offers a range of amenities that make it a strong fit for anyone recovering from injury or looking for a gentle weekend, including:

- **Parking & Access**: On‑site garage and valet service provide step‑free entry to the lobby, eliminating the need for long walks from street parking.
- **Indoor Spaces**: A spacious lobby, a main‑floor restaurant, and a lounge with comfortable seating allow guests to linger for hours with minimal movement.
- **Rooftop & Views**: Elevators serve the rooftop lounge, giving panoramic ocean views without requiring stairs.
- **Accessibility**: Restrooms and elevators are located on the main level; benches are placed throughout public areas.
- **Travel Fit**: Highlighted in the [[summaries/session-32_2024-11-15]] as an **ultra‑low‑walking** option and a recommended spot for **rainy‑day planning**.  Its convenient access and low‑impact design align with the [[concepts/low_impact_travel]] and [[concepts/ultra_low_walking]] concepts, making it a go‑to choice for low‑strain weekend itineraries.

**Key Takeaways**
- Ideal for weekend plans that keep walking under ~0.25 mi per day.
- Perfect for rainy‑day indoor relaxation or quick waterfront views with minimal steps.
- Easily paired with nearby dining venues such as **Sugar Magnolias** or **Castaways Coffee** (mentioned in the same session).
