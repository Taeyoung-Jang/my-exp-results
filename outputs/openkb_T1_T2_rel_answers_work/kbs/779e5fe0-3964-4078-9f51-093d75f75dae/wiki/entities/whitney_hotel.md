## Whitney Hotel

**Type:** Place (Boutique hotel in Beacon Hill, Boston)

### Overview
Whitney Hotel is a historic boutique hotel located in Beacon Hill, Boston, just a short drive from Cambridge and Somerville. It offers valet parking, elevators to all guest floors, and comfortable lounge spaces that make it an ideal low‑impact base for visitors recovering from knee surgery.

### Accessibility
- **Parking:** Valet service eliminates the need to walk from a distant lot.
- **Elevators:** Available from the lobby to all guest rooms and public areas.
- **Walking distance:** The hotel is within walking distance of Charles Street cafés and the Charles River Esplanade; the recommended staycation itinerary keeps total walking under 0.25 mi per day.

### Role in Session 32 Weekend Plans
In the “Boston/Cambridge Staycation” weekend, Whitney Hotel serves as the overnight anchor:
- **Rainy‑day option:** Guests can spend the day inside the hotel’s lobby, restaurant, and lounge, requiring minimal movement.
- **Nice‑weather option:** A short, step‑free walk to the nearby Charles River Esplanade uses the Charles/MGH ramps, staying within the low‑impact walking limits.
