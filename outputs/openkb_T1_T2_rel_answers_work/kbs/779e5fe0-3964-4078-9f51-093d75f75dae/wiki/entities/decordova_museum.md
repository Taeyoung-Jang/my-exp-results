# DeCordova Museum

**Type:** Organization (contemporary art museum)  
**Location:** Lincoln, Massachusetts, about 30‑45 min drive north of Cambridge/Somerville.

**Key facts from session‑32**
- Recommended as a low‑impact outing for both rainy and nice‑weather weekends.
- Offers indoor galleries that are fully accessible: elevators, ramps, benches, and restrooms on the main floor.
- Parking is a dedicated lot right beside the entrance, providing a short, flat walk to the doors.
- Suggested as a “rainy‑day” activity with a walking estimate of 0.25–0.4 mi and as part of the “nice‑weather” plan (central flat sculpture park area) with 0.5–1 mi of walking.
- Paired with nearby **Twisted Tree Café** for a linger‑friendly meal.

**Why it fits low‑impact travel**
The museum’s layout and proximity to parking align with [[concepts/low_impact_travel]] guidelines, keeping knee strain minimal.

**Rainy‑day planning**
Because the galleries are indoors and the lot is step‑free, it is a prime example of [[concepts/rainy_day_planning]].

**Ultra‑low walking option**
For days when walking should stay under ~0.25 mi, the DeCordova Museum can serve as the sole destination, matching the [[concepts/ultra_low_walking]] approach.
