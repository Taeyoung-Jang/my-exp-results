# Peabody Essex Museum

## Overview
Peabody Essex Museum (PEM) is a major art and cultural institution located in Salem, Massachusetts. It houses extensive collections of American, Asian, and maritime art, and provides a range of indoor galleries and café spaces.

## Role in [[summaries/session-32_2024-11-15]]
In the weekend‑planning session the museum was highlighted as a **rainy‑day friendly** destination for the Gloucester‑Salem weekend.  The plan notes:
- Parking in the **Church Street lot** offers a flat, short walk to the entrance.
- The museum is fully **elevator‑served** and provides benches throughout the galleries.
- The on‑site **PEM Café** is recommended for lingering meals or coffee breaks.
