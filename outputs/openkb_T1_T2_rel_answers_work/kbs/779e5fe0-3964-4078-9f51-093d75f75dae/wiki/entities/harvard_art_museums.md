## Harvard Art Museums

The **Harvard Art Museums** are a trio of galleries—Fogg Museum, Busch-Reisinger Museum, and Arthur M. Sackler Museum—located on Harvard University's campus in Cambridge. They provide a low‑impact cultural destination that is highly accessible for visitors recovering from mobility challenges.

### Key Facts from Session 32
- **Accessibility**: The main entrance on Broadway is step‑free. The adjacent **Broadway Garage** offers direct, elevator‑served entry to the museum, minimizing walking to roughly **0.25–0.4 mi** on rainy‑day visits and up to **0.5–1 mi** if you add a brief Harvard Square stroll.
- **Indoor Benefits**: Ideal for [[concepts/rainy_day_planning]] because galleries, cafés, and restrooms are all under one roof.
- **Low‑Impact Suitability**: Fits [[concepts/low_impact_travel]] recommendations—short, flat routes, plenty of benches, and easy‑to‑find restrooms.
- **Ultra‑Low Walking Option**: When paired with a valet or garage drop‑off, the museum can be part of an [[concepts/ultra_low_walking]] day with total walking under **0.25 mi**.
- **Nearby Linger‑Friendly Cafés**:
  - **Museum Café** (on‑site, wheelchair‑accessible seating)
  - **[[entities/tatte]]** on Brattle Street, a short two‑minute walk from the museum entrance.
- **Parking & Entrance Tips**:
  1. Use the **Broadway Garage** (address: 150 Broadway, Cambridge) for the shortest path.
  2. Arrive early to avoid peak traffic and ensure a spot close to the elevator.
  3. Ask staff for the nearest wheelchair‑accessible restroom; they are located on the ground floor near the main lobby.
