# Summary of session-07_2025-06-14

### Core advice
- **Consistency over modality** – pick the cardio you’ll repeat 3‑4 times / week, whether outdoor running or lower‑impact cycling/rowing. [[concepts/consistent-cardio-practice]]
- **Progression principle** – start easy (conversational effort), keep sessions 20‑30 min, add 5 min each week, then introduce one harder day. [[concepts/progressive-overload-in-endurance]]

### Two 4‑week plans
| Week | Running plan (days / min) | Cycling/Rowing plan (days / min) |
|------|---------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Mon 20 min easy, Wed 20 min easy, Fri 20 min run‑walk (2 min jog/1 min walk), Sat 20 min easy | Mon 30 min easy, Wed 30 min easy, Fri 20 min steady, Sat 30 min easy |
| 2 | Mon 25 min easy, Wed 25 min easy, Fri 20 min run‑walk, Sat 25 min easy | Mon 35 min easy, Wed 35 min easy, Fri 20 min steady, Sat 35 min easy |
| 3 | Mon 25 min easy, Wed 25 min easy, Fri 20 min continuous run, Sat 25 min easy | Mon 40 min easy, Wed 40 min easy, Fri 25 min steady, Sat 40 min easy |
| 4 | Mon 30 min easy, Wed 30 min easy, Fri 25 min steady, Sat 30 min easy | Mon 40 min easy, Wed 40 min easy, Fri 30 min steady, Sat 40 min easy |

### Warning signs of over‑reaching
- Unusual pre‑session fatigue  
- Easy pace feels suddenly hard  
- Prolonged elevated heart rate post‑session  
- Persistent minor aches  

*If two or more appear → cut next session ½ duration or replace with easy activity.* [[concepts/overreaching-warning-signs]]

### Warm‑up & cool‑down (running plan)
- **Warm‑up:** 2 min walk → 2 min light jog; optional leg swings/ankle rolls.  
- **Cool‑down:** 1‑2 min easy jog → walk until breathing normal; gentle stretching optional.

### Strength work (non‑cardio days)
- Short (≈20 min) sessions twice weekly.  
- Core stability, glutes/hip, lower‑leg resilience: bodyweight squats, lunges, single‑leg balance, calf raises, planks.

### Adjusted plan for existing 5 km every‑other‑day runner
- Keep two weekly runs as normal easy 5 km.  
- Third run varies weekly:  
  1. **Progression run** – gradual pace increase to steady conversational pace by final km.  
  2. **Pick‑up intervals** – 2 × 30‑40 s gentle fast segments with easy jog recovery.  
  3. **Recovery week** – all three runs easy 5 km.  
  4. **Steady‑effort run** – full 5 km at comfortably hard but not breathless pace.  

- Strength sessions remain 2 × 20 min, adding single‑leg work (split‑squat, step‑ups) and calf/shin work.  
- Same over‑reaching signals apply; back‑off if they appear.
