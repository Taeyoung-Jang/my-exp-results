# Staging Essentials

**Definition**: A *staging essentials* approach involves creating small, self‑contained kits of frequently needed items on each level of a home or workplace so that a person with limited mobility can stay on one floor without repeatedly climbing stairs.

**Key components**
- **Identify high‑use items** such as toiletries, medication, chargers, snacks, water, and pain relievers.
- **Duplicate where practical**; keep a copy of each item on every floor you need to occupy.
- **Portable organizer**: use a cross‑body bag or backpack to transport the entire kit in a single trip, leaving hands free for handrails or crutches.
- **Clear, accessible storage**: place items at waist or counter height to avoid bending or over‑reaching.
- **Bundle trips**: when a move between floors is unavoidable, carry the full kit at once to avoid multiple short trips.

**How it ties to other concepts**
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/one-level-living]] by turning a single floor into a temporary “base camp.”
- Relies on safe handling of mobility aids as described in [[concepts/mobility-aid-safety]].

**Practical example from [[summaries/session-37_2025-09-11]]**
- At home, the user was advised to set up a “down‑stairs kit” containing a change of clothes, toiletries, chargers, and a small tote for meals, allowing them to sleep and live on the ground floor.
- At work, a desk‑side stash of snacks, a water bottle, pain relievers, and necessary documents was suggested to reduce trips to the restroom floor.

**Benefits**
1. **Reduced stair exposure** – fewer ascents/descents lower the risk of falls or aggravating injuries.
2. **Increased independence** – users can manage daily tasks without constant assistance.
3. **Energy conservation** – less physical strain means faster recovery and less fatigue.

**Implementation steps**
1. List items you reach for most often on each floor.
2. Gather duplicates and store them in labeled containers or bags.
3. Choose a sturdy, lightweight bag for any planned inter‑floor moves.
4. Review and adjust the kits weekly as needs change.
