# Thermometer‑Hygrometer

A **Thermometer‑Hygrometer** is a compact, inexpensive instrument that simultaneously displays ambient temperature and relative humidity. It is commonly carried in a pocket or attached to a bag for quick, on‑the‑go environmental checks.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-39_2024-07-15]]
- Recommended as the simplest low‑tech upgrade for personal weather sensing. [[concepts/low-tech-environment-monitoring]]
- Allows users to correlate numeric humidity values with the subjective feeling of "heavy" or "crisp" air discussed in the conversation. [[concepts/humidity-perception]]
- Particularly useful for coastal walks where humidity is high and sea‑salt aerosols are present, helping to distinguish between coastal and inland moisture conditions.

## Typical usage
1. **Read temperature and humidity** during a walk.
2. **Compare the numbers** to personal sensory cues (skin stickiness, hair frizz, breath temperature).
3. **Log observations** alongside notes on the presence of ocean breezes, sea‑salt smell, or inland plant odors to build an intuitive sense of air quality.

## Related entities and concepts
- Often used alongside observations of the **[[entities/ocean]]** and **[[entities/coastline]]** environments where sea‑salt particles may affect perception. [[entities/sea-salt]]
- Part of a broader set of simple tools for monitoring ambient conditions without relying on digital weather apps.
