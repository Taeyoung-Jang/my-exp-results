# Sea Salt

Sea salt refers to tiny aerosolized particles of sodium chloride that originate from the ocean and become suspended in the atmosphere, especially near coastlines. These particles contribute to the distinctive texture and scent of coastal air and can subtly affect how heavy or “thick” the air feels.

## Key points from [[summaries/session-39_2024-07-15]]
- The document notes that sea‑salt aerosols add a slightly sharper, fresher edge to humid coastal air compared to inland humidity.
- Simple DIY observations, such as a faint tacky film on a glass surface after a walk, can hint at the presence of sea salt in the air.
- Metal objects may develop a dullness or minor rust over time due to salt exposure.
