# Automation for Interns

**Definition**
Automation for interns refers to the set of low‑effort, repeatable systems that handle routine tasks so that trainees can preserve mental bandwidth for clinical work.

**Key Strategies from [[summaries/session-36_2024-07-10]]**

1. **Scheduled grocery delivery** – set a same‑day weekly delivery so you never need to shop during a busy rotation.
2. **Recurring calendar reminders** – automate bill payments, laundry days, and other chores with repeat events.
3. **Default meal system** – keep a bulk, ready‑to‑eat dish (e.g., pasta, rice‑veggie, chili) in the fridge at all times; supplement with a stash of non‑perishable snacks (nuts, jerky, granola bars, instant oatmeal).
4. **Morning bag‑check routine** – adopt a consistent checklist that runs each night so you never scramble for badge, charger, pens, or socks in the early hours.
5. **Call‑bag checklist** – maintain a pre‑packed bag containing scrubs, a warm layer, deodorant, charger, spare socks, pens, earplugs, and shelf‑stable snacks.  See [[concepts/call-bag-checklist]] for the detailed list.
6. **Link to sleep hygiene** – a reliable automation framework supports the [[concepts/post-call-recovery]] routine by ensuring you have food, light exposure, and a calm environment ready when you get home.

**Why It Works**
Automation reduces the number of daily decisions, which helps protect cognitive resources during long, unpredictable shifts. By turning essential tasks into “set‑and‑forget” actions, interns can focus on patient care and personal well‑being.

**Related Concepts**
- [[concepts/consistent-presence]] – consistent small touchpoints with friends and family become possible when routine tasks are automated.
- [[concepts/adaptability]] – automations provide a stable base that lets interns adapt more easily to shifting schedules.

**Implementation Tips**
- Start with one automation (e.g., grocery delivery) and add another each week.
- Use phone reminders or a simple spreadsheet to track inventory of bag items and pantry staples.
- Review the system every month and adjust to fit new rotation demands.
