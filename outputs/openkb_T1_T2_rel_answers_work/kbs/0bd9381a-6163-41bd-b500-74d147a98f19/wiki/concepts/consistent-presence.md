# Consistent Presence

**Definition**: Consistent presence is the practice of regularly showing up for a friend in reliable, low‑effort ways, creating a sense of trust and stability over time.

## Core Elements
- **Reliability**: Being someone a friend can count on, even through brief check‑ins or simple gestures.
- **Regularity**: Maintaining a rhythm of contact—whether a quick message, a periodic call, or scheduled get‑togethers—without demanding constant high‑frequency interaction.
- **Low‑Effort Investment**: Small, natural touches (e.g., a "thinking of you" text) that keep the connection warm without feeling forced.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- **[[concepts/honest-communication]]**: Trust built through consistent presence is reinforced when communication is open and sincere.
- **[[concepts/adaptability]]**: Consistent presence must be flexible, allowing the friendship to evolve as life circumstances change.

## Practical Tips
1. **Set lightweight routines** – a weekly or bi‑weekly message reminding a friend you’re thinking of them.
2. **Prioritize reliability** – respond when you say you will, even if the exchange is brief.
3. **Adjust frequency** – match the level of contact to each friend’s preferences while keeping a steady pattern.

Consistent presence forms the backbone of long‑lasting friendships, providing the dependable foundation upon which deeper emotional bonds can grow.
