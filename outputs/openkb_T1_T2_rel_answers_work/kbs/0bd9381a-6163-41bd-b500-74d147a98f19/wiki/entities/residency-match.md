# Residency Match

**Type:** Event

The Residency Match is the nationwide system that pairs graduating medical students with residency training programs, marking the transition from medical school to the demanding environment of intern year.

## Connection to Session 36 (2024‑07‑10)

The conversation in [[summaries/session-36_2024-07-10]] centers on life after the Residency Match, focusing on how new interns can manage post‑call fatigue, automate routine tasks, and protect personal relationships.

## Themes Highlighted in the Session

- **Post‑call recovery:** Strategies such as short naps, light exposure, and caffeine cut‑offs are recommended to help interns regain a healthy rhythm after the long 28‑hour shifts that often follow the match period. [[concepts/post-call-recovery]]
- **Automation for interns:** The session proposes consistent grocery deliveries, calendar reminders for bills, and a default meal system to reduce decision‑fatigue in an unpredictable schedule. [[concepts/automation-for-interns]]
- **Call‑bag preparation:** A “always packed” bag checklist is suggested, ensuring essentials are always on hand during on‑call rotations. [[concepts/call-bag-checklist]]
- **Maintaining relationships:** Small, friction‑free touchpoints—micro‑check‑ins, drop‑in communication, and brief rituals—are advised to preserve friendships despite limited free time. [[concepts/consistent-presence]] and [[concepts/honest-communication]]

These concepts illustrate the practical challenges and adaptive habits that often begin immediately after the Residency Match.
