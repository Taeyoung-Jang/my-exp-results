## Overview
This document provides concrete, day‑to‑day advice for physicians entering intern year. It focuses on **post‑call sleep recovery**, **automation of routine tasks**, and **maintaining personal relationships** despite unpredictable, long shifts.

## Post‑call Sleep Recovery
- Treat sleep as a protected appointment: set a cutoff for caffeine (avoid after the latter half of the shift) and aim for a short nap (≈1–1.5 h) when you get home.  
- After the nap, get **light exposure** (open blinds or step outside for ~10 min) and eat a small meal to reset circadian cues.  
- Aim for a normal bedtime that night; the full reset usually occurs by the next morning.  
- If you oversleep (e.g., 4 h), immediately get sunlight, eat, stay awake until a reasonable bedtime (e.g., midnight) and let the schedule drift back over a day or two.  
- When too wired to nap, lie in a dark, quiet room for 20‑30 min to give the brain a partial reset.

## Sample Post‑call Schedule (adjustable for arrival time)
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| Arrival (11 am‑2 pm) | Drop items, hydrate, quick shower if helpful |
| 30 min after arrival | Lie down for a **short nap** (1 h max) |
| Wake (≈12:30‑3:30 pm) | Open blinds/step outside for 10 min light exposure |
| Immediately after | Eat a simple, grounding meal (protein + carbs) |
| Afternoon | Light activities (laundry, short walk, low‑stakes TV) |
| Evening (9‑10 pm) | Begin wind‑down routine, aim for regular bedtime |
| Next morning | Normal wake‑up, resume routine |

## Automation Strategies
- **Groceries**: schedule same‑day weekly delivery.  
- **Bills & chores**: set repeating calendar reminders.  
- **Default meals**: keep a ready‑to‑eat bulk dish (pasta, rice‑veggie, chili) in the fridge.  
- **Snack stash**: non‑perishable items (nuts, jerky, granola bars, instant oatmeal) in the work bag.  
- **Morning bag‑check**: a consistent routine to ensure badge, charger, pens, etc., are packed.

## Call Bag Checklist (always packed)
- Scrubs & warm layer  
- Deodorant & lip balm  
- Charger & spare socks  
- Pens (2‑3)  
- Small toiletries kit  
- Foam earplugs  
- Non‑perishable snacks (nuts, jerky, granola bars, instant oatmeal)

## Protecting Relationships
1. **Micro‑check‑ins**: send a quick voice note or meme each shift, regardless of timing.  
2. **Drop‑in access**: let a close friend know they can text anytime; reply when you have a quiet moment.  
3. **Scheduled tiny ritual**: a 2‑minute phone call on the walk from hospital to car, or a shared coffee break when schedules align.  

These low‑friction touchpoints create consistent connection despite limited free time.
