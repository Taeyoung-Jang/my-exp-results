# Notion (product)

Notion is a versatile workspace application that lets users create databases, notes, and task boards. In the context of the **session‑14_2022‑06‑15** workflow, Notion is recommended as one of two lightweight options for a one‑page weekly dashboard.

## Key facts from the document
- **Weekly Projects database**: a simple table called *Weekly Projects* with the properties **Name**, **Deadline**, **Priority**, **Current Deliverable**, and **Next Action**.
- **Purpose**: to keep a concise view of active client projects, their deadlines, and the single next step needed, supporting the rotating‑project deep‑work schedule.
- **Comparison**: presented alongside Trello as an alternative lightweight tracking tool.
