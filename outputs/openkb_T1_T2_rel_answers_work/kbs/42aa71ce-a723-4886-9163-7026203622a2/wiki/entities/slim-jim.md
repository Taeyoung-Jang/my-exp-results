# Slim Jim

**Type:** Product

**Description:** Slim Jim is a pre‑packaged meat stick snack known for its convenience, high protein content, and low mess, making it a popular choice for nurses working long 12‑hour shifts.

**Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]]:**
- Included in the list of 6–8 exact snack ideas that fit in a small lunch bag for med‑surg nurses.
- Valued for durability and quick protein boost during micro‑breaks on the floor.
- Suggested as part of a broader [[concepts/nutrition-for-nurses]] approach to sustain energy without causing sugar crashes.
- Used in the **during‑shift** phase of the [[concepts/shift-routine]] to keep stamina when formal breaks are scarce.
- Complements other recommended snacks such as [[entities/chobani]], [[entities/kind]], [[entities/rxbar]], [[entities/sabra]], and [[entities/blue-diamond]].

**Practical tips:**
- Keep a single Slim Jim in the lunch bag for a fast, mess‑free protein snack.
- Pair with a water bottle or a small amount of caffeine (see [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]) to avoid an energy dip later in the shift.
