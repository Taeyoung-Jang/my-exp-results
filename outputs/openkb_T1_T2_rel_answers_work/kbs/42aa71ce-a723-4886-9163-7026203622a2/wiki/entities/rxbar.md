# RXBar

RXBar is a grain‑free protein bar that packs a solid amount of protein and fiber in a compact, shelf‑stable package. In the context of long 12‑hour med‑surg nursing shifts, it is highlighted as one of the ideal grab‑and‑go snacks because it:

- **Stays fresh** without refrigeration, making it safe to sit in a lunch bag all day.
- **Provides steady energy** without the sugar crash that can come from candy or pastries, supporting the [[concepts/nutrition-for-nurses]] recommendations for sustained fuel.
- **Fits easily** into a small bag alongside other snacks such as [[entities/kind]] bars, [[entities/oikos]] yogurt, and cheese sticks.

### How it fits into the shift routine

The summary for [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]] suggests a three‑phase routine (before, during, after) for new med‑surg nurses. RXBar is listed among the six‑to‑eight specific snack options to carry for **micro‑breaks** during the shift, helping nurses maintain energy between meals and avoid the need for excessive caffeine spikes, which aligns with the [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] guidance.

### Practical tips

- **Pack a single bar** per shift in a resealable pouch to keep it from crushing other items.
- **Pair with a piece of fruit** (e.g., a clementine) or a handful of nuts for a more balanced snack.
- **Use it during a brief reset**: eat while walking back to the nurses’ station or during a 10‑minute break to refuel quickly.
