# Chomps

**Type:** Product

Chomps is a brand of meat‑based snack sticks marketed as a high‑protein, low‑carb alternative to traditional jerky. In the **session‑40_2024‑06‑15** summary, Chomps is listed as one of several durable snack options for med‑surg nurses working 12‑hour shifts.

## Key facts from [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]]
- Suggested as a compact, non‑perishable snack that fits easily in a small lunch bag.
- Recommended alongside other snack choices such as [[entities/kind]], [[entities/rxbar]], [[entities/blue-diamond]] almonds, and [[entities/slim-jim]].
- Fits the broader nutrition guidance for nurses outlined in [[concepts/nutrition-for-nurses]], emphasizing protein‑rich foods that sustain energy without causing sugar crashes.
- Can be part of a **shift‑routine** that includes micro‑breaks for hydration and quick bites, as discussed in [[concepts/shift-routine]].

## Typical use case for nurses
1. **Before shift:** Pack a few Chomps sticks with a main lunch (e.g., chicken‑and‑rice bowl) to ensure a steady protein source.
2. **During shift:** Eat a stick during brief micro‑breaks or while charting to maintain energy levels.
3. **After shift:** No special post‑shift handling needed; the snack is already consumed.
