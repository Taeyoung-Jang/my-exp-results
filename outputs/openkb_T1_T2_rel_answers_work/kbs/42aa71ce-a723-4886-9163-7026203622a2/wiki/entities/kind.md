## Kind (Product)

Kind is a popular brand of protein bars often cited as a convenient, shelf‑stable snack for healthcare workers on long shifts. In the context of a new med‑surg nurse’s routine (see [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]]), Kind bars are suggested as part of a **nutrition‑for‑nurses** strategy to maintain steady energy without causing a sugar crash.

### Key Facts from the Session
- **Suggested Use:** One Kind bar can serve as a mid‑morning or mid‑afternoon snack, fitting into the caffeine timing plan that spreads caffeine doses across a 12‑hour shift ([[concepts/caffeine-timing]]).  
- **Portability:** The bars are individually wrapped, resistant to crushing, and do not emit strong odors, making them ideal for a small lunch bag.
- **Nutritional Profile:** Typically provides 15‑20 g of protein, modest carbs, and a blend of nuts and dried fruit, supporting satiety between meals.
- **Complementary Snacks:** Can be paired with other recommended items such as [[entities/blue-diamond]] almonds, [[entities/chobani]] yogurt, [[entities/oikos]] Greek yogurt, [[entities/rxbar]] bars, [[entities/sabra]] hummus singles, and [[entities/slim-jim]] beef sticks for variety.

### Relation to Nursing Shift Routine
Kind bars fit into the **shift‑routine** framework by offering a quick, mess‑free fuel source during micro‑breaks. They help nurses avoid the energy dip that can occur after the first caffeine dose, supporting sustained focus throughout the day.

### Practical Tips
1. **Pack One Per Shift:** Place the bar in an outer pocket of your bag for easy access.
2. **Pair with Hydration:** Drink a glass of water when you eat the bar to aid digestion and maintain hydration.
3. **Timing:** Eat the bar around the 10‑11 am window (after the first caffeine dose) or again around 2‑3 pm if you need a secondary energy boost.
