# Blue Diamond

Blue Diamond is a brand of flavored and roasted almonds that is often used as a portable, protein‑rich snack. In the **session‑40_2024-06-15** summary, it is listed among the recommended snack options for a new med‑surg nurse’s lunch bag because it stays fresh, does not create strong odors, and provides steady energy throughout a 12‑hour shift.

## Key Facts from the Document
- **Purpose**: Serves as a quick, non‑perishable snack to prevent energy crashes during long clinical days.
- **Placement in Packing List**: Suggested alongside other sturdy items such as Kind bars, RXBar, Chobani yogurt, and Slim Jim beef sticks.
- **Nutritional Role**: Supplies healthy fats and protein, supporting the [[concepts/nutrition-for-nurses]] approach of maintaining steady blood sugar and satiety during busy shifts.
- **Practical Benefits**: Compact size fits in a small lunch bag, resists sogginess, and does not emit strong smells that could bother coworkers.

## Related Concepts and Entities
- **Snack Strategy**: Part of a broader shift routine that balances meals, caffeine timing, and micro‑breaks (see [[concepts/shift-routine]] and [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]).
- **Other Recommended Snacks**: Often paired with [[entities/kind]], [[entities/rxbar]], [[entities/chobani]], and [[entities/slim-jim]] for variety.
