- **Before shift**: quick stretch, protein‑rich breakfast, and a packed meal that stays fresh all day. Default lunches include a **chicken‑and‑rice bowl** or a **turkey‑cheese wrap**. Suggested snacks (6‑8 items) that survive a small bag: [[entities/chobani]] or [[entities/oikos]] yogurt, [[entities/kind]] or [[entities/rxbar]] bar, baby carrots with [[entities/sabra]] hummus singles, [[entities/sabra]] cheese stick, [[entities/blue-diamond]] almonds, a small bunch of grapes, a clementine, and a [[entities/slim-jim]]/[[entities/chomps]] beef stick. Bring a large water bottle and a preferred caffeinated drink. [[concepts/nutrition-for-nurses]]

- **Caffeine schedule** for a 7 a–7:30 p shift: first dose (6–7 a) with breakfast, second dose (10–11 a) to smooth the mid‑morning lull, optional third dose (2–3 p) – a small amount to avoid evening crash. [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]

- **During shift**: use micro‑breaks – 20‑second breathing pauses, sip water while walking, nibble a snack while charting. When a lull appears, take a 10‑minute reset: sit, hydrate, eat a bite, relax shoulders. Request coverage with concise scripts such as:  
  - “When you have a minute, could you keep an eye on my patients so I can take ten?”  
  - If a preceptor seems busy: “I know it’s busy, but I need two minutes to use the restroom—who can cover my patients right now?”  
  - For low energy: “I’m feeling light‑headed; can someone watch my call lights for five minutes?”

- **After shift**: change out of scrubs immediately, take a quick shower, have a light dinner, and engage in a low‑effort wind‑down (music, short show, mindfulness). Avoid caffeine after mid‑afternoon to protect sleep. [[concepts/shift-routine]]

- **Emergency meal options** when lunch is forgotten: cafeteria grilled‑chicken sandwich or wrap, rice‑and‑protein bowl, cup of chili; vending‑machine protein bar paired with nuts and a piece of fruit. Choose options that are filling but not greasy to prevent energy crashes.

- **Foot and back care**: wear supportive shoes, change socks mid‑shift if they become damp, perform calf stretches while charting, maintain upright posture, and replace insoles regularly. These small habits reduce soreness by day’s end. [[concepts/shift-routine]]
