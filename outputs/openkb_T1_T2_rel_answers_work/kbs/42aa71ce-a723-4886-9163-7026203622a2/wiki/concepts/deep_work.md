# Deep Work

**Definition**: Deep Work is a scheduled period of protected, distraction‑free focus aimed at tackling the most cognitively demanding design tasks. It maximizes creative output and reduces context‑switching overhead.

## Origin in Session 14
The weekly workflow proposed in [[summaries/session-14_2022-06-15]] designates **9:00 – 12:00** each workday as the Deep Work block. During this time the designer:
- Works on new client projects, heavy creative tasks, or anything requiring full concentration.
- Turns off email, chat, and other low‑value interruptions.
- Treats the slot as *non‑negotiable* to protect mental bandwidth.

## Practical Implementation
1. **Schedule it** – Add a recurring calendar event titled “Deep Work” from 9 am to 12 pm, Monday‑Friday.
2. **Set boundaries** – Use a status message (e.g., “In deep work, will respond after lunch”) and the quick response script from the source document for mid‑morning pings.
3. **Pair with triage** – Any incoming request is filtered through the [[concepts/triage_rule]]; non‑urgent items are queued for the afternoon.
4. **Link to tracking** – Record the day’s primary project and the specific goal accomplished in the [[concepts/weekly_dashboard]] so progress is visible at a glance.

## Benefits Highlighted
- **Higher quality output**: uninterrupted time allows for complex visual problem‑solving.
- **Reduced burnout**: clear work boundaries prevent over‑extension.
- **Predictable cadence**: coworkers know when to expect rapid replies versus delayed responses.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- The **triage rule** determines which requests can safely wait until after the Deep Work block.
- The **weekly dashboard** (Trello or Notion) captures which project is the primary focus for each Deep Work session, ensuring balanced attention across multiple clients.
