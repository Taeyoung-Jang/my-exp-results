# Caffeine Timing

**Definition**: A purposeful plan for when and how much caffeine to consume during a 12‑hour nursing shift to maintain steady alertness, avoid energy crashes, and protect sleep quality.

---

## Why Timing Matters

Long med‑surg shifts often include periods of high demand followed by quieter lulls. Consuming caffeine all at once early in the shift can lead to a mid‑day crash, while taking it too late can interfere with post‑shift sleep. Proper timing aligns caffeine peaks with natural dips in energy, supporting both patient safety and personal well‑being.

---

## Recommended Schedule (Based on [[summaries/session-40_2024-06-15]])

| Time | Action | Reason |
|------|--------|--------|
| **6:00‑7:00 am** | First dose (e.g., a cup of coffee or tea) with breakfast | Sets a baseline alertness for the start of the shift.
| **10:00‑11:00 am** | Second, moderate dose (half the first dose) | Smooths the typical mid‑morning dip without causing a spike.
| **2:00‑3:00 pm** (optional) | Small “micro‑dose” (e.g., a caffeine chew or half‑cup of coffee) | Provides a gentle lift for the afternoon lull while still allowing evening wind‑down.
| **After 4:00 pm** | No caffeine (or only decaf) | Prevents interference with the post‑shift wind‑down and sleep.

> **Tip:** Use a reusable travel mug to measure portions and avoid over‑consumption.

---

## Practical Tips for Implementation

- **Pair with Hydration**: Drink a glass of water with each caffeine dose to stay hydrated.
- **Choose Low‑Sugar Options**: Black coffee, unsweetened tea, or a small amount of milk keep blood‑sugar stable.
- **Track Your Response**: Note how you feel after each dose; adjust timing or amount for personal sensitivity.
- **Avoid Energy‑Guzzling Snacks**: Pair caffeine with protein‑rich snacks (see [[concepts/nutrition-for-nurses]]) to sustain the effect.

---

## Integration with the Overall Shift Routine

Caffeine timing is a sub‑component of a broader **[[concepts/shift-routine]]** that includes pre‑shift nutrition, micro‑breaks, and post‑shift recovery. When coordinated, these elements create a seamless flow:

1. **Before Shift** – Breakfast + first caffeine dose.
2. **During Shift** – Micro‑breaks, hydration, and the second caffeine dose.
3. **After Shift** – No caffeine, focus on wind‑down activities.
