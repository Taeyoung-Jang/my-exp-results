# Shift Routine

**Shift Routine** is a structured, three‑phase approach that helps new med‑surg nurses maintain energy, manage stress, and preserve personal time during long 12‑hour shifts. It integrates nutrition, micro‑breaks, and post‑shift wind‑down into a repeatable pattern.

---

## Phases of the Routine

### 1. Before Shift
- **Quick stretch** and mental reset.
- **Protein‑rich breakfast** (e.g., eggs, yogurt, peanut‑butter toast).
- **Pack a durable lunch**: default options such as a chicken‑and‑rice bowl or a turkey‑cheese wrap.
- **Snack arsenal** (fits in a small lunch bag):
  - [[entities/chobani]] or [[entities/oikos]] yogurt cup
  - [[entities/kind]] bar or [[entities/rxbar]]
  - Baby carrots with [[entities/sabra]] hummus singles
  - [[entities/blue-diamond]] almonds
  - Small bunch of grapes or a clementine
  - [[entities/slim-jim]] or [[entities/chomps]] beef stick
- **Caffeine schedule** (see [[concepts/caffeine-timing]]):
  1. First dose with breakfast (6–7 am).
  2. Second dose mid‑morning (10–11 am).
  3. Optional tiny dose early afternoon (2–3 pm) to avoid evening crash.
- Bring a large water bottle and a preferred caffeinated drink.

### 2. During Shift
- **Micro‑breaks**: 20‑second breathing pauses, sip water while walking, nibble a snack while charting.
- **10‑minute reset** when a lull appears: sit, hydrate, eat a bite, relax shoulders.
- **Coverage scripts** for quick breaks:
  - “When you have a minute, could you keep an eye on my patients so I can take ten?”
  - If the preceptor seems busy: “I know it’s busy, but I need two minutes to use the restroom—who can cover my patients right now?”
  - For low energy: “I’m feeling light‑headed; can someone watch my call lights for five minutes?”
- **Emergency meal options** (cafeteria or vending) when lunch is forgotten: grilled‑chicken sandwich, rice‑and‑protein bowl, cup of chili, or a protein bar with nuts and fruit.

### 3. After Shift
- **Immediate transition**: change out of scrubs, quick shower, hydrate.
- **Light dinner** and avoid caffeine after mid‑afternoon to protect sleep.
- **Wind‑down routine**: 10‑minute low‑effort activity (music, short show, mindfulness).
- **Foot and back care**: supportive shoes, change socks mid‑shift, calf stretches while charting, upright posture, replace insoles regularly.
