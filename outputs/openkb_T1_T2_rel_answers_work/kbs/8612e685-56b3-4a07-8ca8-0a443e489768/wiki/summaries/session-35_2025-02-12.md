# Summary of session-35_2025-02-12

**Main contribution** – A set of practical, printable tools for structuring a commuter’s weekday routine, including [[concepts/time_blocking]] templates for normal and errand days, a weekly schedule with three capped evening workout sessions, a concise “Pack for Tomorrow” checklist, and a 5‑minute nightly [[concepts/nightly_reset]] routine to prevent buildup.

## Key Elements

1. **Daily time‑blocking templates**  
   - Normal workday: wake‑up at 6:00 am, departure 6:45 am, commute 1.5 h, evening window for dinner, optional workout, wind‑down, and a bedtime window targeting 7.5–8 h of sleep (≈9:45‑10:00 pm).  
   - Errand day: same morning, but after the 6:30 pm commute allocate 6:30‑7:30 pm for errands, 7:30‑8:00 pm quick dinner, then relax.

2. **Weekly overview**  
   - Workouts scheduled Mon, Wed, Thu (45 min each) with 10‑15 min buffer before and after.  
   - Friday designated as a “wiped‑out” recovery day with ultra‑low‑effort dinner ideas and earlier lights‑out.  
   - Saturday/Sunday left un‑blocked for flexibility.

3. **Pack for Tomorrow checklist** (8‑10 items) – designed for a 15‑20 min prep block and formatted for one‑page printing:  
   - Work bag (laptop, charger, notebook)  
   - Keys, wallet, transit card  
   - Filled water bottle  
   - Lunch/snacks  
   - Clothes laid out  
   - Workout gear (when needed)  
   - Papers or packages to drop off  
   - Medications/supplements  
   - Headphones/often‑forgotten items  

4. **Low‑effort Friday dinner options** – rotisserie‑chicken bowl with microwaved rice and bagged greens, or quick pasta with jarred sauce and pre‑washed spinach. Both under 15 min, providing a “real” meal feel.

5. **5‑minute nightly reset** – a simple sequence to keep the environment tidy even on exhausted evenings: toss visible trash & load dishes, reset one high‑impact surface, set out morning clothes, place packed bag by the door, dim lights, take a slow breath, and finish.
