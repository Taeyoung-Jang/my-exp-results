# Early‑Morning Workout Routine

An **early‑morning workout routine** is a systematic plan for exercising shortly after waking, designed to minimize fatigue and sustain energy throughout the day. The concept emphasizes three pillars:

1. **Sleep hygiene** – go to bed 15–20 minutes earlier and keep a consistent wake‑up time to reduce the shock of early activity.
2. **Gradual activation** – use a simple pre‑workout ritual (hydration, light stretching, familiar music) to ease the body and mind into movement.
3. **Energy management** – adjust workout intensity, time caffeine, and choose light snacks to avoid mid‑morning crashes.

## Core Elements

- **Consistent schedule**: Regular bedtime and wake‑up times help the body adapt after about a week.
- **Pre‑workout routine**: A few sips of water, a short stretch, and a playlist create a smooth transition into exercise.
- **Workout length**: Shorten or lighten sessions on low‑energy days to preserve overall stamina while reinforcing habit formation.
- **Caffeine timing**: Take a modest dose (≈40–80 mg) immediately after waking; effects start in 10–15 minutes, aligning with a 20–30 minute window before a swim or other activity. See [[concepts/caffeine-timing]] for details.
- **Pre‑workout snack**: Opt for easily digestible carbs such as half a banana, a piece of toast, crackers, a sip of juice, or a glucose chew. See [[concepts/pre-workout-snack]] for options.

## Benefits
- Reduces perceived “zombie” fatigue later in the day.
- Improves focus and performance during the workout.
- Supports a sustainable habit by balancing effort and recovery.
