# Summary of session-07_2025-06-15

## Overview
This document provides a practical framework for eating **two anchor meals per day** (plus an optional snack) that delivers steady energy and balanced nutrition without turning meals into a stressful “diet project.” It includes:
- A simple portion‑based template for each anchor meal
- A minimal‑prep grocery list
- A one‑day‑batch **weekly preparation** checklist
- A 7‑day meal checklist that can be screenshot‑ready
- Swap ideas for variety

## Anchor Meal Template
- **Protein** – roughly the size of your palm (eg. eggs, tofu, chicken, beans)  
- **Vegetables/Fruit** – about half the plate (fresh or frozen)  
- **Carbs or Healthy Fats** – a fist‑sized portion of grains/potatoes or 2‑3 tbsp of avocado, olive oil, nuts, cheese

This three‑part structure underpins all meals in the plan and aligns with the concept of **anchor meals**.

## Grocery List (Pantry/Freezer‑Friendly)
- Proteins: eggs, tofu block, chicken thighs, canned beans (or canned salmon)  
- Carbs: microwave rice packets, quinoa, pasta, tortillas, frozen mixed veggies, frozen potatoes  
- Fresh: a small selection of spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers  
- Snacks: fruit, nuts, yogurt, cheese sticks, hummus, crackers  
- Flavor helpers: soy sauce, stir‑fry sauce, olive oil, hot sauce, shredded cheese

These items support the **pantry friendly meals** concept.

## Weekly Prep Checklist (do once)
- Cook a batch of rice (or have packets ready)  
- Roast or sauté a large tray of mixed vegetables  
- Hard‑boil 6–8 eggs **or** press & slice tofu  
- Keep canned beans, sauces, and noodles on hand

This aligns with **batch preparation**.

## 7‑Day Meal Checklist (snap‑ready)
**Day 1**
- Meal 1: Scrambled eggs or tofu + leftover veggies + rice
- Meal 2: Frozen‑veg stir‑fry + chicken/beans/tofu
- Snack: Fruit or nuts (optional)

**Day 2**
- Meal 1: Rice bowl with sautéed greens, egg/tofu, olive oil/hot sauce
- Meal 2: One‑pan roast using prepped veggies + protein
- Snack: Yogurt or cheese

**Day 3**
- Meal 1: Veg‑and‑bean skillet + bread or tortillas
- Meal 2: Pasta + frozen veggies + protein
- Snack: Fruit or nuts

**Day 4**
- Meal 1: Eggs scrambled with leftover roasted veggies
- Meal 2: Quesadilla with beans or chicken + raw veggies side
- Snack: Fruit or hard‑boiled egg

**Day 5**
- Meal 1: Grain bowl with veggies + egg or tofu
- Meal 2: Quick noodle broth + frozen veg + protein
- Snack: Hummus and crackers

**Day 6**
- Meal 1: Savory wrap (eggs or tofu + veggies + cheese)
- Meal 2: 15‑minute chili (canned beans + tomatoes + veggies)
- Snack: Fruit or nuts

**Day 7**
- Meal 1: Veggie omelet or egg‑veg sauté over rice
- Meal 2: Sheet‑pan protein + veg leftovers
- Snack: Optional

## Swap Ideas (for variety)
- **Protein swaps:** eggs ↔ tofu ↔ beans ↔ canned salmon
- **Carb swaps:** rice ↔ tortillas ↔ pasta ↔ frozen potatoes
- **Veg swaps:** roasted veg ↔ frozen stir‑fry mixes ↔ bagged greens

These swaps support the broader **meal variation strategy**.

## Key Concepts
- anchor meals – balanced, substantial meals that anchor the day
- batch preparation – cooking once to power multiple meals
- pantry friendly meals – using long‑shelf and freezer items for flexibility
- meal variation strategy – systematic swaps to prevent boredom
- steady energy nutrition – meal composition that smooths blood‑sugar curves
