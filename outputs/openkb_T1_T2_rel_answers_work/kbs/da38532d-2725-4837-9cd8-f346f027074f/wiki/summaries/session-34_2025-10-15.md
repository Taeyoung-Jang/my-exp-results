# Summary of session-34_2025-10-15

**Main contribution:** Practical advice for sustaining an early‑morning workout routine without feeling depleted later in the day.

## Key Recommendations

- **Sleep hygiene**: Go to bed 15–20 minutes earlier and keep a consistent wake‑up time. This reduces the shock of early activity after about a week.
- **Pre‑workout routine**: Keep it simple – hydrate, do a few minutes of light stretching, and play a familiar playlist to ease the brain into movement.
- **Workout duration**: Shorten or lighten the session on low‑energy days to preserve overall stamina while reinforcing the habit.
- **Caffeine timing & dosage**:
  - Take a small amount (≈40–80 mg, roughly half a normal cup of coffee) **immediately after waking**. Effects begin in 10–15 minutes, aligning with a 20–30 minute window before a pool session. See [[concepts/caffeine-timing]].
  - Split the dose: keep the pre‑workout amount modest to avoid jitters, then consume the remainder after the swim to maintain steady energy and prevent a crash.
- **Pre‑workout snack**: Choose easy‑digestible carbs such as half a banana, a small piece of toast, a couple of crackers, a sip of juice, or a glucose chew. The goal is a light fuel boost without heaviness. See [[concepts/pre-workout-snack]].
