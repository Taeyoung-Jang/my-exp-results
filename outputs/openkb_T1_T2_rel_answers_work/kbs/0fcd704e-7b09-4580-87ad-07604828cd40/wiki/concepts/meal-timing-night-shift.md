# Meal Timing for Night Shift

**Definition**: A structured approach to timing meals for people who work evening or overnight shifts, designed to stabilize energy levels, support a consistent [[concepts/shifted-sleep-window]], and avoid sleep disruption.

---

## Core Principles
1. **Late Breakfast / Early Brunch** – Eat soon after waking (e.g., 9:00 – 9:30 for a 9:00 a.m. wake‑up) to break the overnight fast and jump‑start metabolism.
2. **Mid‑Afternoon Main Meal** – Schedule the largest meal around the middle of the waking period (≈ 14:00 – 15:00). This provides sustained fuel for errands, errands, or daytime activities.
3. **Light Pre‑Work Dinner** – Take a modest, easily digestible dinner 2‑3 hours before the shift starts (≈ 18:00 – 18:30). This keeps energy steady without causing post‑meal drowsiness during work.
4. **Post‑Shift Snack (Optional)** – A small protein‑rich or carbohydrate snack after returning home (≈ 1:00 – 1:30) can prevent hunger spikes without shifting the bedtime.
5. **Avoid Heavy Late‑Night Meals** – Large, rich meals close to bedtime can impair the [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]] process and delay the onset of the wind‑down routine.

---

## Why Timing Matters
- Aligning meals with a consistent [[concepts/shifted-sleep-window]] helps the body’s circadian rhythm adapt to a non‑traditional schedule.
- Stable blood‑sugar levels reduce cravings and the temptation to over‑eat late at night, supporting better sleep quality.
- Predictable eating windows make it easier to plan errands and social activities during the “daytime” portion of the shifted schedule, aiding [[concepts/social-sync]].

| Time (approx.) | Meal / Action |
|----------------|----------------|
| 09:00 – 09:30 | Wake & **late breakfast** (protein, whole grain, fruit) |
| 11:00 – 14:00 | Errands / social; **mid‑afternoon main meal** (larger portion, balanced macros) |
| 18:00 – 18:30 | **Light dinner** before shift (e.g., salad with lean protein) |
| 00:30 – 01:00 | End shift, **post‑shift snack** if needed (yogurt, nuts) |
| 01:30 – 02:00 | Begin **wind‑down‑routine** and sleep |

---

## Tips for Implementation
- **Batch prep** meals on days off to reduce cooking time on work nights.
- Keep a **food journal** to notice how meal timing affects energy and sleep; adjust portions if you feel sluggish.
- Pair meals with hydration; avoid caffeine after the mid‑afternoon meal to protect the upcoming wind‑down period.
- If a shift runs later (home at 02:00), shift the snack later but keep the bedtime window consistent; a shorter wind‑down (15 min) can still maintain rhythm.
