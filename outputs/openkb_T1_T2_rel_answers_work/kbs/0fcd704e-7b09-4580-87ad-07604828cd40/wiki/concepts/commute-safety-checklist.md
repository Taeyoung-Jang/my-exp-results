# Commute Safety Checklist

A concise, repeatable routine for night‑shift workers that minimizes risk and uncertainty when traveling home after a late closing. The checklist is designed to be quick, low‑effort, and adaptable to different transport options.

## Core Elements
1. **Phone Ready** – Fully charge your phone before leaving work; keep a portable charger handy.
2. **Notify a Contact** – Send a brief message (or set an automatic check‑in) to a trusted friend or family member indicating your expected arrival time.
3. **Keys & Wallet** – Place keys, wallet, and any transit cards in a designated pocket or pouch before stepping out.
4. **Route Confirmation** – Stick to a well‑lit, familiar route to the bus stop or rideshare pickup point; avoid shortcuts through isolated areas.
5. **Visibility** – Wear reflective clothing or a light‑colored jacket; consider a small flashlight or phone’s torch.
6. **Backup Awareness** – Review your [[concepts/transport-backup-plans]] (Plan A/B/C) before you leave, ensuring you know the next steps if the last bus is missed.
7. **Environment Scan** – Perform a quick visual scan of the surroundings at the stop or curb to ensure it feels safe before boarding.

- The checklist supports the **[[concepts/last-bus-buffer]]** by prompting workers to stop extra tasks early enough to start the safety routine.
- Maintaining a consistent routine feeds into overall **[[concepts/shifted-sleep-window]]** stability, as reduced stress improves sleep quality.
