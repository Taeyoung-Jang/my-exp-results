# Transport Backup Plans (Plan A/B/C)

**Definition** – A structured set of three sequential options for getting home after a late‑night shift, designed to keep the commute reliable and safe while preserving a consistent sleep window.

## Components
| Plan | Description | When to use |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Plan A** | Catch the scheduled **last bus** on the regular route. | Normal nights when the bus is still running on time. |
| **Plan B** | Pre‑arranged **rideshare** (e.g., Uber, Lyft) or a coworker’s lift. | When the bus is missed, running late, or the stop is unsafe. |
| **Plan C** | Emergency fallback: stay inside the store until a trusted friend/family member arrives, or call a safe pickup service. | When both the bus and rideshare are unavailable or feel unsafe.

## Safety Guidelines (linked to [[concepts/commute-safety-checklist]])
1. **Stay inside** the venue until the backup ride is confirmed.
2. **Verify vehicle details** (license plate, driver name) before stepping out.
3. **Notify a contact** of your expected arrival time and backup plan.
4. Keep your **phone fully charged** and within reach but out of sight.
5. Use **well‑lit, familiar waiting spots** near the exit.

## Timing Buffer (related to [[concepts/last-bus-buffer]])
- **Buffer start**: cease non‑essential closing tasks **≈ 25 minutes** before the last bus departure.
- This creates a predictable “leaving mode” window, giving you time to assess whether Plan A will work and, if not, to activate Plan B or C without rushing.

## Interaction with Other Concepts
- The backup plans support a stable **[[concepts/shifted-sleep-window]]** by preventing large delays that would push bedtime later.
- They complement the **[[concepts/wind-down-routine]]**, ensuring you can still begin your wind‑down shortly after arriving home, even if you used Plan B or C.
- Consistent use of these plans reduces anxiety and helps maintain **[[concepts/social-sync]]** by keeping your evening routine predictable.
