# Summary of session‑07_2025-06-15

**Main contribution** – A set of three interchangeable, 20‑30 minute evening wind‑down routines designed to help the user fall asleep more easily while avoiding melatonin supplements and screen exposure.

## Key concepts
- **Wind‑down window** – A consistent start time (10:00 PM) leading to lights‑out at 10:30 PM. wind down window
- **Soft lighting** – Dimming bright lights an hour before bed to cue the brain for sleep.
- **Slow breathing** – A few minutes of longer exhalations to calm the nervous system.
- **Journaling** – Brief written reflection to unload mental chatter.
- **Gentle stretching / self‑massage** – Physical relaxation for tense muscles.
- **Quiet activity** – Non‑screen, low‑arousal options such as calm reading, instrumental music, ambient sounds, or a body‑scan meditation. [[concepts/quiet-activity]]
- **Sleep hygiene** – Consistent bedtime, routine predictability, and environmental cues that support natural sleep onset. [[concepts/sleep-hygiene]]

## Three template outlines (10:00‑10:30)
### 1. Wired / Anxious night
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 10:00‑10:05 | Dim lights, tidy the space |
| 10:05‑10:10 | Slow breathing (5 min) |
| 10:10‑10:20 | Brief journaling |
| 10:20‑10:30 | Quiet activity (e.g., calm book or soft instrumental music) → lights out |

### 2. Normal night
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 10:00‑10:10 | Soft lighting, bathroom routine (teeth, sleep clothes) |
| 10:10‑10:15 | Light stretching or slow walk |
| 10:15‑10:30 | Quiet activity (e.g., mellow book, ambient soundscape) → lights out |

### 3. Physically tense night
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 10:00‑10:10 | Warm shower / warm rinse |
| 10:10‑10:20 | Gentle stretching or self‑massage (e.g., massage ball) |
| 10:20‑10:30 | Quiet activity (e.g., short body‑scan, familiar low‑stakes reading) → lights out |

## Non‑screen quiet‑activity suggestions
- Reading a calm, low‑plot book  
- Listening to soft instrumental or ambient music  
- Performing a silent body‑scan meditation  
- Holding a warm mug of herbal tea while breathing slowly  

These templates can be rotated based on the user’s mental and physical state, keeping the overall sleep schedule stable while providing targeted relaxation techniques.
