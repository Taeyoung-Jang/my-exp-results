# Simple Tracking

**Type:** Concept

**Definition**: Simple Tracking is an ultra‑lightweight habit‑logging approach that captures a learning session in a single, quick entry, keeping the tracking effort to a few seconds.

## Core Principles
- **Minimal friction** – The log takes less than 5 seconds to complete.
- **Single‑line entry** – Record the date and a brief note about what was done (e.g., “read 5 pages of *X*” or “watched video on topic Y”).
- **Consistent location** – Use the same place each day: a pinned note on the phone, a recurring calendar event, or an X on a paper calendar.
- **No extra tools** – Avoid complex spreadsheets or dedicated habit‑tracker apps; the goal is to keep the process invisible to the day’s workflow.

## How It Was Applied in [[summaries/session-32_2025-03-14]]
In the 20‑minute lunch‑break routine, the user is advised to:
1. Open a single note (e.g., phone notes app) at the start of the session.
2. After the study block, add a one‑sentence line with the date and activity.
3. Alternatively, mark an X on a paper calendar for each day the session occurs.

Both methods require only a few taps or a quick pen stroke, reinforcing the habit without adding mental load.

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Works hand‑in‑hand with [[concepts/micro_learning_habit]], providing the logging side of a tiny, repeatable learning habit.
- Complements the [[concepts/course_vetting]] process by offering an easy way to note the outcome of each quick vetting session.

## Practical Tips
- **Set a reminder**: A silent timer that alerts when the 20‑minute block ends can also prompt the quick log.
- **Use abbreviations**: If you’re pressed for time, shorthand like “V‑vid” (video) or “R‑book” (reading) keeps the entry brief.
- **Review weekly**: Spend 2‑3 minutes at the end of each week scanning the entries; look for patterns rather than counting days.

Simple Tracking turns habit logging into a negligible part of the day, ensuring that the focus stays on the learning activity itself.
