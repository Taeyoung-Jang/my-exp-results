# Course Vetting Checklist

**Concept**: A brief, structured set of yes/no questions that can be completed in a single 20‑minute lunch break to determine whether an online course or learning topic is suitable for a micro‑learning habit.

## Why Use a Vetting Checklist?
- Prevents investing time in content that is too long, dense, or misaligned with immediate goals.
- Keeps the decision‑making process fast and frictionless, matching the low‑effort mindset of a tiny daily habit.
- Helps maintain consistency with the **[[concepts/micro_learning_habit]]** approach by ensuring the chosen material can be consumed in 10‑20 minute bursts.

## The 20‑Minute Checklist (Yes/No)
Run through the following prompts sequentially, marking **Yes** or **No**. Aim to complete all five in about twenty minutes.
1. **Clarity** – Do I understand the instructor’s speech without needing to replay the segment?
2. **Pacing** – Is the delivery calm enough that I could follow it even when I’m tired?
3. **Relevance** – Do the first few lessons directly address a skill or knowledge I want to apply in the next 1‑3 months?
4. **Length Suitability** – Are most lessons short enough to fit into a 10‑ to 20‑minute lunch‑break window?
5. **Motivation Spark** – Does the content give me at least a small spark of curiosity to watch the next video?

**Decision Rule**: If you answer **Yes** to the majority (≥ 3) of the questions, the course is likely a good fit for a month‑long, weekday‑only learning plan.

## How to Apply the Results
- **Select the course**: Add it to your learning queue and schedule the first video for the next lunch break.
- **Track the outcome**: Use a **[[concepts/simple_tracking]]** method (e.g., a one‑line daily note or an X on a calendar) to record that the course passed the vetting.
- **Iterate**: If a course fails the checklist, repeat the process with another option; the goal is to find material that seamlessly integrates with your habit.

## Relation to Other Concepts
- This checklist supports the creation of a **[[concepts/micro_learning_habit]]** by confirming that the chosen material matches the short, repeatable format.
- After vetting, the chosen course can be tracked with the ultra‑simple methods described in **[[concepts/simple_tracking]]**.
