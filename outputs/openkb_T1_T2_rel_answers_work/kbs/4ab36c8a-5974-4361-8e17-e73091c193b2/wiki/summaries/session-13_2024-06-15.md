# Summary of session-13_2024-06-15

**Main contribution**: A practical framework for designing a long‑term, burnout‑free study schedule that totals ~8 hours per week, broken into three sessions (3 h + 2 h + 3 h) with concrete, retention‑focused activities.

## Key Concepts
- **study routine** – Assess realistic weekly study capacity, anchor sessions to high‑energy windows, start small and scale.
- **session structure** – Each session follows a repeatable flow: warm‑up review, focused new learning, active‑recall checks, practice problems, and end‑of‑session summary.
- **active recall** – Embed self‑question prompts (e.g., “What are the three main ideas?”) after each chunk of material.
- **spaced review** – Quick skim of notes from the previous session and from a week ago; use “teach‑back” style without flashcard apps.
- **practice problems** – Sequence problems: easy → medium → challenging to avoid repetition and maintain difficulty balance.
- **burnout prevention** – Start with shorter sessions, keep total weekly hours sustainable, include brief pauses, and monitor energy.
- **missed session adjustment** – If a session is missed, fold essential review into the next session, reduce new material load, and keep practice component.

## Session Templates
### 3‑hour session (twice a week)
1. **15 min spaced review** – skim notes from last week & previous session, ask self‑recall questions.
2. **90 min new learning** – break into 20‑25 min blocks, intersperse active‑recall prompts.
3. **30 min practice problems** – start with a confidence‑boosting problem, then medium, then a challenging one.
4. **15 min summary** – write a few sentences summarizing key takeaways for future reference.

### 2‑hour session (once a week)
1. **20 min stronger spaced review** – deeper “teach‑it‑back” of earlier material.
2. **60 min focused practice** – diagnostic problems, note persistent gaps.
3. **10 min reflection** – identify shaky concepts, plan brief revisit.
4. **10 min quick recap** – update session summaries.

## Adjusting the Plan
- **If a session is missed**: extend the review at the start of the next session, limit new content to one chunk, keep the practice segment to preserve momentum.
- **Flexibility**: shift session order or duration as long as the overall rhythm (review → new → practice → summary) stays intact.

## Takeaway
Following this plug‑and‑play script helps maintain a sustainable study rhythm, maximizes retention through active recall and spaced review, and provides clear guidance for handling missed sessions without burnout.
