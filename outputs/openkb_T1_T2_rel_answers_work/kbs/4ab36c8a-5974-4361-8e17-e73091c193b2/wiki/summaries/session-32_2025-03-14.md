# Summary of Session 32 (2025‑03‑14)

## Core Idea
A practical framework for creating **tiny, repeatable learning habits** that survive busy, unpredictable days. The focus is a 20‑minute weekday lunch‑break routine, a quick course‑vetting checklist, and a simple 4‑week schedule that accommodates 10‑, 15‑, or 20‑minute sessions and missed days.

## Key Concepts
- **Micro‑learning habit** – Choosing a single, almost‑effortless action (e.g., read a short passage, watch a brief video, or do one drill) that can be executed even on the worst days. [[concepts/micro_learning_habit]]
- **Focus selection** – Prioritize what *matters now* to you, not what you think you should learn. Align the choice with a concrete benefit you expect in the next 1‑3 months. 
- **Lunch break flow** – 1 min cue, 15 min focused study, 2 min quick note, 2 min wrap‑up. This structure turns a fixed block into a lightweight “bubble.” 
- **Ultra‑simple tracking** – One‑line daily note, a calendar X, or a quick phone‑note; the goal is < 5 seconds of friction. [[concepts/simple_tracking]]
- **Course vetting checklist** – A 20‑minute yes/no script to decide if a course fits the lunch‑break format. [[concepts/course_vetting]]
- **Weekday study schedule** – Repeating template that treats each day as a *touch* rather than a deadline, with explicit handling for 10‑, 15‑, 20‑minute days and missed days. 

## Concrete Outputs Provided
1. **Study‑block examples** for reading, video courses, and skill practice, each framed as a single 15‑minute bite.
2. **Tracking method** options: a pinned note with date + activity, or an X on a paper calendar.
3. **Course‑vetting checklist** (5 yes/no questions) that can be run in one lunch break.
4. **4‑week plan template**:
   - *Every weekday*: open next video, play until time runs out.
   - *10‑minute day*: play, stop when timer ends.
   - *15‑minute day*: play, add a quick mental recap.
   - *20‑minute day*: finish short lesson or pause where you are.
   - *Missed day*: treat as a neutral zero; resume where you left off.

## Recommendations for Implementation
- **Pick the first skill/course** by asking: *What will make my day‑to‑day easier in the next two months?*
- **Set a silent timer** for 20 minutes to enforce the bubble.
- **Log in the same place** each day (e.g., phone notes) to keep the habit frictionless.
- **Review weekly** (5‑minute glance) to note any emerging patterns or needed adjustments, not to “catch up.”
