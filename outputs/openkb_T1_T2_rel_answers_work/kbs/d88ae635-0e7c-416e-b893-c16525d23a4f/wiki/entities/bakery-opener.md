# Bakery Opener

**Type:** Person

**Role:** First crew member to arrive (≈6 am) in a two‑person bakery team. Supports the owner in production, handles the counter during the morning rush, and maintains shop stability while the owner takes the protected break.

**Key Responsibilities (from session‑34):**
- Arrives at 6 am, takes over the front counter so the [[entities/bakery-owner]] can focus on “air‑traffic control” of ovens and dough.
- Shapes pastries, glazes breads, restocks the coffee bar, and ensures display cases are filled.
- Holds the floor during the owner’s [[concepts/protected-break]] (≈9:30‑10:00 am).
- Keeps the shop running while the owner performs the admin block (inventory, orders, email) after the [[entities/bakery-closer]] arrives at 11 am.

**Interaction with Other Roles:**
- Works directly with [[entities/bakery-owner]] and hands off tasks to [[entities/bakery-closer]] later in the day.
- Part of the [[entities/two-person-team-workflow]] that defines the daily rhythm for small bakeries.

**Relevant Concepts:**
- The opener’s schedule enables the owner to protect a break and to allocate the [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]] block for tomorrow’s doughs.
- By freeing the owner from front‑of‑house duties, the opener indirectly supports the [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]] for inventory and ordering.
