## Bakery Closer

**Entity Type:** Person

**Key Facts (from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-10]])**
- **Arrival Time:** Starts the shift at **11:00 am**.
- **Primary Responsibilities:**
  - Takes over the front‑of‑house while the owner focuses on [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]] tasks such as inventory checks, vendor orders, and customer email replies.
  - Provides coverage for the register, dishes, and general cleanup during the early‑afternoon period.
  - Ensures a smooth handoff from the morning crew to the owner’s [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]] block.
- **Impact on Workflow:** By handling admin and closing duties, the Bakery Closer creates the time window that lets the owner protect a short [[concepts/protected-break]] after the morning rush and dedicate the early afternoon to prep for the next day.

**Relationships**
- Works with the **Bakery Owner** ([[entities/bakery-owner]]) and the **Bakery Opener** ([[entities/bakery-opener]]) as part of the [[entities/two-person-team-workflow]].
- Supports the [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]] phase by freeing the owner to focus on scaling ingredients, mixing long‑ferment doughs, and labeling tomorrow’s batches.

**Context**
The Bakery Closer role was highlighted in the discussion about optimizing a small bakery’s daily rhythm, emphasizing the need for a dedicated staff member to handle mental‑intensive tasks during a low‑traffic period.
