# Two‑Person Team Workflow

**Type:** other

**Description:** A structured daily routine for a bakery staffed by the owner, a morning opener, and an afternoon/evening closer.

## Core Elements
- **Anchor alarm strategy** – the owner wakes at 3:45 am and is in the shop by 4:15 am, using the early hours for solo production. [[concepts/anchor-alarm-strategy]]
- **Consistent wake‑up time** – the same wake‑up time each day stabilises sleep hygiene and energy levels. [[concepts/consistent-wake-up-time]]
- **Protected break** – a short, non‑negotiable rest after the morning rush (≈9:30‑10:00 am) to reset focus. [[concepts/protected-break]]
- **Early‑afternoon prep block** – dedicated time (≈12:30‑2:00 pm) for scaling tomorrow’s ingredients, mixing long‑rise doughs, and labeling. [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]]
- **Admin‑scheduling block** – admin tasks (inventory, ordering, email) are moved to the period when the closer is on‑site (≈11:15‑12:30 pm). [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]]

## Team Roles
- **[[entities/bakery-owner]]** – runs solo production before staff arrive, oversees timing, takes the protected break, handles admin and prep blocks.
- **[[entities/bakery-opener]]** – arrives at 6:00 am, assists with shaping, glazing, and front‑of‑house during the opening rush.
- **[[entities/bakery-closer]]** – arrives at 11:00 am, provides front‑of‑house coverage, enabling the owner to focus on admin and prep.

## Typical Timeline (illustrative)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 3:45 am | Alarm (anchor alarm strategy) |
| 4:15 am | Owner solo production |
| 6:00 am | Opener joins – finish pastries, stock coffee |
| 7:00‑9:30 am | Morning rush – both serve customers |
| 9:30‑10:00 am | Protected break |
| 10:15‑11:00 am | Light tasks (quick restock, cleanup) |
| 11:00 am | Closer arrives |
| 11:15‑12:30 pm | Admin‑scheduling block |
| 12:30‑2:00 pm | Early‑afternoon prep block |
| 2:00‑3:00 pm | Closing walk‑through and shutdown |

## Relevance
This workflow is referenced in the summary of [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-10]] and serves as a practical template for small bakeries seeking to balance production, staff handoffs, and owner well‑being.
