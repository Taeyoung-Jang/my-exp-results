# Summary of Session 34 (2025-02-10)

## Core Habit Themes
- **Sleep hygiene** – Treat bedtime as non‑negotiable to sustain early‑morning energy.  
- **Prep planning** – Use the previous day to pre‑scale ingredients and map production order.  
- **Timing consistency** – Keep mixing, proofing, and oven slots choreographed daily.  
- **Staffing delegation** – Build a small, reliable crew; let them run the counter and closing duties.  

## Typical Day Flow (single owner)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 3:45 am | Alarm (linked to [[concepts/anchor-alarm-strategy]]). |
| 4:15 am | Owner arrives: solo production – mix doughs, start long fermentations, load early bakes. |
| 6:00 am | First team member arrives – shift to “air‑traffic control” (shape, glaze, monitor bakes). |
| 7:00 am | Doors open. |
| 7:00‑9:30 am | Morning rush: serving, pulling trays, quick restocks. |
| 9:30‑10:00 am | **Protected break** – sit, eat, hydrate; no prep, no admin. [[concepts/protected-break]] |
| 10:00‑11:00 am | Light tasks only (quick restock, cleanup). |
| 11:00 am | Closer arrives – shop coverage for admin. [[entities/bakery-closer]] |
| 11:15‑12:30 pm | **Admin block** – inventory checks, vendor orders, email replies. [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]] |
| 12:30‑2:00 pm | **Early‑afternoon prep block** – scale tomorrow’s ingredients, mix long‑rise doughs, portion pastries, label tubs. [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]] |
| 2:00‑3:00 pm | Final service, register close, cleaning, walk‑through. |
| 3:00‑4:00 pm | Owner leaves to rest and sleep. |

## Tasks to Ban from Morning Production
1. Detailed **admin work** (orders, inventory, emails).  
2. **Deep cleaning** projects.  
3. **New recipe experimentation**.

## Tailored Timeline for a Two‑Person Team + Closer
- **4:15‑6:00** – Owner only: core production (no inbox). [[entities/bakery-owner]]  
- **6:00‑7:00** – Opener joins: finish pastries, stock coffee, prep for opening. [[entities/bakery-opener]]  
- **7:00‑9:30** – Both front‑of‑house; owner stays out of admin.  
- **9:30‑10:00** – Owner’s protected break; opener holds the floor. [[concepts/protected-break]]  
- **10:15‑11:00** – Light tasks (quick restock, cleanup).  
- **11:00‑12:30** – Closer on duty; owner handles **inventory, ordering, email** (admin block). [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]]  
- **12:30‑2:00** – Owner runs **tomorrow‑prep** while staff covers front. [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]]  
- **2:00‑3:00** – Closing walk‑through and shutdown.  

## Key Takeaways
- Protect a short break right after the morning rush to reset energy. [[concepts/protected-break]]  
- Reserve the early‑afternoon window for heavy tomorrow‑prep. [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]]  
- Funnel all mental‑intensive admin tasks to a dedicated block when the shop is staffed and customer flow is low. [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]]  
- Explicitly ban admin, deep cleaning, and recipe tests from the early production window to maintain rhythm.
