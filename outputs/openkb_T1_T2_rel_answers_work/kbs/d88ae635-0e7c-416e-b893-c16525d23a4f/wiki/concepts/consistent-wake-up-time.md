# Consistent Wake‑Up Time

**Definition**: A stable daily wake‑up time that anchors the circadian rhythm across weekdays and weekends, fostering reliable energy, mood, and sleep quality for demanding schedules such as bakery operations.

## Core Principles
1. **Single Anchor Point**
   - Choose one realistic wake‑up time to repeat every day, including weekends. This anchor reduces the need for extensive adjustments when the week transitions.
2. **Flexibility Margin**
   - Small deviations (≈ 60–90 minutes) are tolerable; larger shifts act like a mini‑time‑zone change and can disrupt the rhythm.
3. **Reinforcement Techniques**
   - Pair the anchor with brief morning light exposure or a short “get‑up‑briefly” routine. See [[concepts/anchor-alarm-strategy]] for a practical method.
4. **Evening Discipline**
   - Treat bedtime as a non‑negotiable appointment; limit screens, caffeine, and late‑night work so the morning anchor can be met without sleep debt.
5. **Weekend Management**
   - Limit oversleep on weekends; if more rest is needed, shift bedtime earlier rather than relying on a later wake‑up.
6. **Consistency Over Perfection**
   - The goal is a sustainable pattern, not an inflexible schedule. Small, regular anchors are more effective than occasional large shifts.

## Implementation Steps (derived from [[summaries/session-09_2024-06-13]])
1. **Track Natural Sleep Window** – Observe when you naturally feel sleepy without an alarm.
2. **Select a Realistic Wake‑Up Time** – Align it with your weekday schedule (e.g., 6:30 am) and the needs of your bakery routine.
3. **Set a “Half‑Way” Weekend Alarm** – For a 6:30 am weekday and 9:00 am weekend pattern, an alarm around 7:45 am works well (see [[concepts/anchor-alarm-strategy]]).
4. **Brief Morning Activation** – Get out of bed for 5‑10 minutes, expose yourself to light, then optionally return to sleep.
5. **Adjust Bedtime When Needed** – If more sleep is required, go to bed earlier rather than sleeping in excessively.
6. **Maintain a Consistent Wind‑Down Cue** – Dim lights, turn off screens, and engage in a short pre‑bed ritual each night.
7. **Link to Day‑Level Rhythm** – A reliable wake‑up anchor enables you to schedule a protected break after the morning rush and an early‑afternoon prep block without risking fatigue. See [[concepts/protected-break]] and [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]].
8. **Separate Admin Work** – Use the admin‑scheduling block in the mid‑day (when a closer is on duty) to keep early mornings free for production. Refer to [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]].
