# Early Afternoon Prep

**Definition**: A focused time block, typically from about 12:30 pm to 2:00 pm, dedicated to scaling ingredients, mixing long‑ferment doughs, portioning pastries, labeling containers, and otherwise preparing the workflow for the next day’s bakery operations.

## Why It Matters
- **Reduces morning chaos** – Having tomorrow’s doughs and ingredient ratios ready means the early‑morning production can start immediately without ad‑hoc prep.
- **Protects owner energy** – By moving heavy mental tasks out of the 4:15 am‑7:00 am window, the owner can preserve sleep and stay focused on baking.
- **Leverages staffing lull** – Customer traffic dips in the early afternoon, providing a quiet environment for precise work.

## Core Activities (from [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-10]])
1. **Scale tomorrow’s flour, water, and other bulk ingredients**.
2. **Mix any long‑rise or overnight doughs** that will be proofed overnight.
3. **Portion and label pastry components** (e.g., croissant butter blocks, tart fillings).
4. **Organize trays, pans, and bake sheets** for the next morning’s schedule.
5. **Update production notes** (e.g., adjustments to fermentation times or bake orders).

## Placement in the Daily Rhythm
- Follows the [[concepts/protected-break]] (the short rest after the morning rush).
- Precedes the closing and final walk‑through, ensuring the shop is ready for the next day.
- Works in tandem with the [[concepts/admin-scheduling-block]] where inventory, ordering, and email handling occur later in the late morning/early afternoon.

## Interaction with Team Roles
- The **[[entities/bakery-owner]]** typically leads the prep, using the quiet period.
- The **[[entities/bakery-closer]]** handles front‑of‑house duties, allowing the owner to focus on prep.
- In a **[[entities/two-person-team-workflow]]**, the opener may finish front‑of‑house tasks while the owner executes the prep block.

## Benefits Summary
- Guarantees that the first bakery shift starts with all ingredients and doughs pre‑prepared.
- Creates a predictable, repeatable rhythm that shields the owner from burnout.
- Aligns with the overall rhythm of the day described in [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-10]].
