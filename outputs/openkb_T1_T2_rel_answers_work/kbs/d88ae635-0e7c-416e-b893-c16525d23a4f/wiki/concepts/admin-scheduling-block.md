# Admin Scheduling Block

**Definition**: A fixed, non‑negotiable period during the bakery day reserved exclusively for mental‑intensive administrative work (inventory, ordering, email) so that production‑focused time remains uninterrupted.

## Why It Matters
- Prevents admin tasks from creeping into early‑morning production, which would disrupt the rhythm of mixing, baking, and serving.
- Provides a predictable window when the shop is adequately staffed, allowing the owner to focus without juggling customers.
- Helps avoid burnout by separating physical labor from cognitive load.

## Typical Implementation (based on [[summaries/session-34_2025-02-10]])
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **11:15 – 12:30** | Owner handles inventory checks, supplier orders, and replies to customer emails. The shop is covered by the [[entities/bakery-closer]] and the [[entities/bakery-opener]], so front‑of‑house duties are maintained while admin work proceeds.
| **12:30 – 2:00** | Transition to the [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]] block for tomorrow’s production.

## Key Elements
1. **Staff Coverage** – The presence of a reliable [[entities/bakery-closer]] (and optionally an opener) creates the staffing buffer needed to free the owner for admin work.
2. **Fixed Timing** – The block is scheduled after the morning rush and before the early‑afternoon prep, mirroring the rhythm of the day.
3. **Task Scope** – Includes inventory reconciliation, placing ingredient orders, handling vendor communications, and answering emails. It explicitly excludes any production or deep‑cleaning activities.
4. **Integration with Other Concepts** – The block follows the [[concepts/protected-break]] (morning reset) and precedes the [[concepts/early-afternoon-prep]] window, creating a clean handoff chain.

## Benefits
- **Predictable Workflow**: Production teams know that admin will not interrupt the early bake schedule.
- **Mental Clarity**: The owner can concentrate on paperwork without the distraction of customers.
- **Energy Management**: By compartmentalising admin, the owner preserves stamina for both physical and cognitive demands of the day.
