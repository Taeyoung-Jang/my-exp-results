**User:** I’ve been trying to get better at handling big life transitions without letting my home routine fall apart. Do you have any practical strategies for making evenings feel calmer and less “off” when your schedule suddenly changes?

**Assistant:** You can make evenings feel steadier by anchoring them with just one or two simple, repeatable cues. Think of them as small “reset points” rather than full routines. For example, choose a predictable wind‑down moment—like dimming the lights, putting on the same playlist, or making a warm drink—and let everything else stay flexible. That little ritual helps signal to your mind and body that the day is closing, even if the rest of your schedule has been unpredictable.

It also helps to shrink your expectations. Instead of trying to recreate your ideal evening routine, pick the smallest version of it that still feels grounding. When big changes are happening, consistency matters more than completeness.

And if things still feel off, treat the evening as a chance to check in with yourself. A quick pause to notice how you’re feeling and what you need can keep the night from spiraling, even if the day was chaotic.
