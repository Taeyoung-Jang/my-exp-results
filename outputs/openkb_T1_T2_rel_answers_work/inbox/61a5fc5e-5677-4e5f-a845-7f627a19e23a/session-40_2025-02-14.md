**Assistant:** You can keep it really simple by choosing a small set of foods you actually enjoy and can use in more than one way. For quick meals, choose two or three base ingredients you can mix and match, like rice or pasta plus a protein and a frozen veggie. Make sure they’re things you won’t get tired of. The goal is to reduce decisions, not variety forever. Once you have a few reliable options you can whip up without thinking, everything gets easier and cheaper, and you’re way less likely to end up hungry.
