**User:** I’ve been trying to refine my evening routine lately—something that feels relaxing but doesn’t wreck my sleep. Do you have any ideas for a simple wind-down ritual, especially around what to drink or snack on at night?

**Assistant:** You can keep it really simple. About an hour before bed, try switching to something warm and calming to drink—herbal teas like chamomile, peppermint, or rooibos are usually gentle on the system and won’t interfere with sleep. If you want something with a little more substance, warm milk (dairy or non‑dairy) can be surprisingly soothing.

For snacks, think light and easy to digest. A small handful of nuts, a banana, a few whole‑grain crackers, or a little yogurt can take the edge off hunger without keeping you awake. The trick is to avoid anything sugary, heavy, or spicy late at night.

Pair that with a quick, consistent wind‑down cue—maybe dimming the lights, putting your phone away, and reading a few pages of something mellow—and your body will start to recognize the routine as a signal to settle down.

**Assistant:** You can absolutely keep the cozy “evening espresso” vibe without the caffeine hit. Some people also like doing a small “half‑caf” earlier in the evening and then going fully decaf once they’re getting close to bedtime.

If you want a coffee‑style drink that’s even more sleep‑friendly, you could try a warm coffee alternative like roasted barley or chicory blends. They taste close to coffee, especially when pulled as a mock‑espresso, but they’re naturally caffeine‑free.

As for having a drink with your show, the main thing is keeping it light and not too close to when you actually want to fall asleep. Alcohol can make you feel sleepy but tends to fragment sleep later in the night. If you do have something, stick to a small pour and sip it slowly, and try to avoid anything very sweet or very strong right before bed. That way you keep the relaxing ritual without paying for it at 2 a.m.
