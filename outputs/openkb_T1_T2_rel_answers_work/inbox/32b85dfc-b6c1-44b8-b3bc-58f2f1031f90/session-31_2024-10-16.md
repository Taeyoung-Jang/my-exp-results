**User:** I’ve been trying to get more consistent with my mornings and evenings lately—what are some practical ways to build a routine that actually sticks without feeling miserable after a week?

**Assistant:** One of the easiest ways to make a routine stick is to start ridiculously small. Most people try to overhaul everything at once, then burn out. If you pick just one or two things for morning and evening that take only a few minutes, it’s much easier to stay consistent and build from there.

It also helps to tie each habit to something you already do. For example, stretch right after you brush your teeth, or prep tomorrow’s clothes right after dinner. The less you have to think about it, the more automatic it becomes.

And don’t aim for perfection. If you miss a day, just pick it up again the next. Routines stick when they feel doable, not when they feel strict.
