**User:** I’ve been thinking about how meal timing affects energy and focus during the day. How do you usually recommend structuring meals and snacks for someone with a busy schedule, and what are some simple go-to options that don’t take much prep?

**Assistant:** For a busy schedule, I usually suggest keeping meals on a loose rhythm rather than a strict schedule. Eating something every three to four hours tends to help keep energy and focus steady without big crashes. That can look like a decent breakfast, a satisfying lunch, a lighter dinner, and one or two simple snacks in between if you feel your energy dipping.

Go‑to options that work with almost no prep include things like yogurt with fruit, nut butter on whole‑grain toast, a piece of cheese with crackers, hummus with pre‑cut veggies, or a piece of fruit paired with nuts. For meals, quick choices like eggs, a microwavable grain bowl with pre‑washed greens, or a simple wrap with protein and veggies usually come together in a few minutes.

The key is keeping a few easy staples on hand so you can put something together quickly without thinking too much.

If you can’t take a real break until late afternoon, keep something you can eat while working right at your desk. Sippable or one‑hand snacks help: a small smoothie, drinkable yogurt, trail mix, or a protein shake.
