**User:** That makes sense. If I only have about 20–30 minutes from waking up to walking out the door, what would you prioritize in that window (and what would you skip)? Also, is it better to do caffeine before or after breakfast?

**Assistant:** With just 20–30 minutes, I’d prioritize whatever helps you wake up, feel steady, and head out without stress. They’re simple, calm on your system, and fit easily into that short morning window.
