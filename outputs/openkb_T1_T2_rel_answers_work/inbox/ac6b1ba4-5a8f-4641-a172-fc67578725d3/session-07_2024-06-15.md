**User:** I’ve been thinking about how tricky it can be to stay close with friends when everyone’s on different schedules—like, even when you all care, timing makes it weird. Do you have any good strategies for keeping friendships strong when coordinating hangouts and chats is hard?

**Assistant:** It definitely gets harder when everyone’s moving at a different pace, but you can still keep friendships strong without constant coordination.

A big one is lowering the pressure on what staying in touch “has” to look like. Quick check‑ins—like sending a random thought, a meme that reminded you of them, or a short voice note—can keep the connection warm without needing a full conversation.

Being honest about schedules helps too. Saying something like, “I’m slammed this week, but I’m thinking of you,” lets them know they matter even when you can’t talk much.

You can also set up loose, low‑maintenance routines. For example, a weekly or monthly call with no exact time, just whenever both of you happen to be free that day. It keeps things flexible but intentional.

And when you do get time together, even if it’s rare, being present and engaged goes a long way. Good friendships don’t need constant attention—just consistent, genuine touchpoints that remind each other you’re still there.
