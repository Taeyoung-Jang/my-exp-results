The real trick is moving anything recreational or infinite—social apps, news feeds, shopping—off your phone entirely and only using them on a laptop. That way you’re reachable, but you’re not carrying the temptations around in your pocket. It feels surprisingly freeing once you get used to it.

For the group chat, the least slippery option is using a single-purpose, low-friction setup. You only use it when you’re out, and the moment you’re done, it goes back in the glovebox and never follows you inside.

The whole idea is separating “must reach me” from “nice to check.” Calls and texts go to the phone you always have. Everything optional stays on devices that never live in your pocket. That boundary does most of the discipline for you.
