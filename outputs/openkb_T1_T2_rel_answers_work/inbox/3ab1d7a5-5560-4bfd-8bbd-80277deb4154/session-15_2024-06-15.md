# Conversation session 15

Date: 2024-06-15 10:00

**User:** I’ve been juggling meetings with people in different parts of the world lately, and it’s making my schedule feel like a puzzle. Do you have a good system for managing time zones—like tools, habits, or rules of thumb to avoid accidentally booking something at a terrible hour?

**Assistant:** One simple approach is to pick a “home base” time zone in your mind and convert everything to that first, no matter where the other person is. It keeps you from mentally juggling multiple clocks. Using a calendar that automatically shows the other person’s time zone—like Google Calendar’s secondary time zone option or a scheduling tool such as Calendly—removes most of the guesswork. I also find it helps to set a personal rule about acceptable hours, so when you propose or accept a meeting, you already know your boundaries and can check quickly whether it lands outside them. And if you’re working with the same people repeatedly, adding their time zones to your phone’s world clock makes it easy to glance and confirm before committing.

**User:** Could you help me set up a “safe meeting window” rule for myself and then translate it into a couple of templates I can use when proposing times? Most of my clients are 3 hours ahead of me, and I’d like to avoid anything before 8am my time or after 6pm my time.

**Assistant:** You can frame your safe meeting window like this: you’re available only between 8am and 6pm your time, and because most clients are three hours ahead, that translates to 11am to 9pm for them. Anything outside that range is an automatic “not available.”

For suggesting times, you could say something like: “I’m available between 8am and 6pm my time, which is 11am to 9pm your time. Would any slot in that range work for you?” Or, if you want to offer specific options: “I can do something between 8am and noon my time, or between 2pm and 5pm. Let me know what fits on your end.”

**User:** I’m on Pacific Time, so my local clock is running three hours behind my NYC clients. Can you rewrite those two templates so they explicitly mention Pacific Time and also include 2–3 concrete example slots (with both PT and their time) that stay within my 8am–6pm PT rule?

**Assistant:** Here are rewritten versions that mention Pacific Time and keep everything within your 8am–6pm PT window.

You could say: “I’m available between 8am and 6pm Pacific Time, which is 11am to 9pm your time. For example, I could meet at 9am PT (noon your time), 1pm PT (4pm your time), or 4:30pm PT (7:30pm your time). Let me know if one of those works, or if there’s another time in that window you prefer.”

Or, if you want something a little more specific: “I can do something in the morning or mid‑afternoon Pacific Time. For instance, 8:30am PT (11:30am your time), 11am PT (2pm your time), or 3pm PT (6pm your time). Happy to adjust within my 8am–6pm PT window if another slot is better for you.”

**User:** Can you also give me a super short one-liner version I can drop into an email, plus a text-message version that’s even more casual?

**Assistant:** A quick email line could be: “I’m free between 8am–6pm PT (11am–9pm your time); for example, 9am PT / noon your time works if that’s good for you.”

A casual text version could be: “I’m good anytime 8–6 PT—want to do like 9 PT (noon for you)?”
